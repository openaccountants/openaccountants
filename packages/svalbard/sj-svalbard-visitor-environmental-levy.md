# Svalbard: visitor environmental levy in 2026 v0.1

> **General reference only.** This Guide is general tax/accounting reference material for AI-assisted workflows. It has not been reviewed for any specific person's facts, documents, elections, deadlines, residency, filing status, or local procedures. Do not rely on it to file, pay, amend, or take a tax position without review by a qualified professional in the relevant jurisdiction.

**Source-cited draft.** Independent AI review is not accountant attestation.

## Who this applies to

Travellers to Svalbard's land and internal waters fall within [environmental-levy regulation FOR-2006-12-22-1532 §§1–3](https://lovdata.no/dokument/SF/forskrift/2006-12-22-1532). This is a separate environmental levy paid to Svalbard's Environmental Protection Fund. It is not the Svalbard income tax, VAT or an airline's commercial service charge. Jan Mayen is outside this regulation's stated territory.

[Svalbard Environmental Protection Act §78](https://lovdata.no/dokument/NL/lov/2001-06-15-79/KAPITTEL_7) prohibits imposing this levy on permanent residents. Regulation §4 provides their refund mechanism. The [Fund's procedure](https://www.miljovernfondet.no/refusjon-av-avgift/) identifies permanent residents through the Svalbard population register; income-tax residence alone is not proof of registration for this refund.

## Rates, bands and thresholds

| Item | Applicable 2026 rule | Official source |
|---|---|---|
| Ordinary visitor levy | NOK 150 per liable person | [Regulation §3](https://lovdata.no/dokument/SF/forskrift/2006-12-22-1532) |
| Air-ticket collection | On purchase of travel from Svalbard; check the aviation exceptions incorporated by §3 | same |
| Private-boat traveller's payment | NOK 150 in connection with the required notification to the Governor | same |
| Permanent-resident refund | Refund of the levy actually paid for qualifying travel | [Regulation §4](https://lovdata.no/dokument/SF/forskrift/2006-12-22-1532); [Fund procedure](https://www.miljovernfondet.no/refusjon-av-avgift/) |
| Refund claim time limit | Within three years after actual travel date | [Fund procedure, Tidsfrist](https://www.miljovernfondet.no/refusjon-av-avgift/) |

The regulation also allows Ministry dispensation for special reasons (§5). Obtain an actual dispensation before treating the charge as waived. Do not infer a waiver from age, a business purpose or nationality. Section 3 incorporates specified aviation-tariff exceptions; the ordinary examples below assume no such exception.

## The base

Build a person-and-journey schedule, not a percentage of the ticket price. Record traveller, travel date, transport, permanent-resident status, who paid, levy actually charged, evidence and any refund already received. Compute the ordinary charge as liable persons multiplied by the statutory amount.

Under [regulation §3](https://lovdata.no/dokument/SF/forskrift/2006-12-22-1532), collection is attached to the ticket; for air passengers the regulation specifies the outward journey from Svalbard. The [Governor's travel guidance](https://www.sysselmesteren.no/ferdsel-pa-svalbard/) confirms that airlines and cruise operators include the levy in ticket prices. Do not charge it again merely because the passenger crosses the boundary on both legs of the same paid trip. Reconcile to the actual ticket collection.

Private-boat visitors arrange their own payment. The [Governor's Fund page](https://www.sysselmesteren.no/svalbards-miljovernfond/) publishes the Fund's payment account and international bank details. Use those current official details for the payment workpaper and retain payment evidence; do not use an unrelated income-tax payment reference. The [Governor's travel page](https://www.sysselmesteren.no/ferdsel-pa-svalbard/) separately explains the trip/crew notification route. Paying the levy does not replace travel permission or border notification.

For a registered permanent resident, reconcile the levy collected through the ticket to the exemption/refund. The [Fund procedure](https://www.miljovernfondet.no/refusjon-av-avgift/) requires evidence of completed travel from Svalbard, accepting a boarding pass or another confirmation. An unused booking alone is not the specified completed-travel evidence.

Choose the claimant route before calculating the receivable:

- **Privately paid journey:** the permanent resident uses the personal electronic refund form linked by the Fund.
- **Employer, NAV, Pasientreiser or similar payer:** do not claim through that personal form. Employers can enter written refund agreements and use the separate employer refund form submitted to the tax office. Establish the agreement and claimant before recording an employer refund entitlement.

Keep the charge, a valid refund claim and the actual refund separately reconciled. Do not net an unsupported refund against the carrier's collection.

## Deadlines and filing

For ticket travel, check the item collected on the relevant ticket. For private-boat travellers, complete the required notification and arrange the direct payment described above ([regulation §3](https://lovdata.no/dokument/SF/forskrift/2006-12-22-1532)).

For a personal refund:

1. Establish population-register eligibility, payer and levy paid.
2. Retain boarding pass or other completed outward-travel confirmation, dates and the charge evidence.
3. Open [Refusjon av avgift](https://www.miljovernfondet.no/refusjon-av-avgift/) and its linked [Governor refund form](https://skjema.no/sysselmesteren/ref_miljoavgift). The form's title is **Refusjon miljøavgift for fastboende på Svalbard**. Its initial screen presents a privacy declaration and ID-porten, username/password or without-login access choices. The filer must choose the appropriate access and consent route. No private form data or post-access fields were inspected in preparing this Guide.
4. Submit the supported personal claim within the stated three-year limit, retain the submission record, then reconcile the tax office's refund to the claimed trips. Do not record a published guaranteed processing time: none is supplied by the cited procedure.
5. For employer-paid journeys, follow the separate written-agreement/employer-form route instead. The Fund page identifies Svalbard tax office as the administrator and provides its contact route for this procedure.

## Penalties and interest

[Regulation §§6–7](https://lovdata.no/dokument/SF/forskrift/2006-12-22-1532) permits coercive fines under Environmental Protection Act §96 and fines for intentional or negligent infringements. It does not provide a flat automatic late-payment charge or a daily amount in these provisions. Do not copy income-tax additional-tax percentages or interest rates into this environmental levy. Follow the actual enforcement decision and its legal basis.

## Cross-border points

The visitor charge is attached to the Svalbard journey and specified territory. A foreign passport, mainland address or foreign payer does not by itself establish an exemption. For residents, use the register and payer tests above. Mainland airport duties and destination-country taxes are separate analyses; this Guide does not treat a mainland flight duty as this environmental levy.

## Two worked examples that recompute

**Ordinary visitor group.** Twelve adult visitors make an ordinary cruise journey, with no resident exemption or dispensation. The operator collects NOK 1,800 through their tickets. No additional direct payment is due on the assumption that this is the full levy actually collected for these journeys.

```oa-check
name: Svalbard environmental levy ordinary visitor tickets 2026
inputs:
  liable_visitors: 12
  levy_per_person: 150
steps:
  ticket_levy: liable_visitors*levy_per_person
expected:
  ticket_levy: 1800
```

**Choose the correct resident refund route.** A registered permanent resident completed three privately paid outward journeys and two employer-paid journeys. NOK 150 was charged for each. All are within three years and have the required travel evidence. The personal form supports a NOK 450 claim for the private journeys, not NOK 750 for all five. The employer-paid NOK 300 requires the separate employer route; this example does not assume its agreement or approval.

```oa-check
name: Svalbard resident private versus employer refund route 2026
inputs:
  private_trips: 3
  employer_trips: 2
  levy_per_trip: 150
steps:
  total_paid: (private_trips+employer_trips)*levy_per_trip
  personal_claim: private_trips*levy_per_trip
  separate_employer_route: employer_trips*levy_per_trip
expected:
  total_paid: 750
  personal_claim: 450
  separate_employer_route: 300
```

## Ask the client first

- What journey, date, transport and actual levy collection does each ticket/payment show?
- Is the traveller registered as a permanent resident of Svalbard?
- Who paid each journey, and is there an employer refund agreement?
- Is completed outward-travel evidence available, and has a refund already been claimed or received?
- Does an aviation-specific exception or an actual Ministry dispensation apply?
