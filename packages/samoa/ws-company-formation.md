# Samoa Company Formation and Entity Choice v0.1

> **General reference only.** This skill supports AI-assisted working papers. It is not approval to file, pay or take a tax position for a particular person. Obtain qualified local review of the facts, applicable law and filing instructions.

## Who this applies to

Domestic private/public companies under the Companies Act 2001, and the initial choice between a company, sole trader, partnership or overseas-company branch. MCIL maintains the Samoa Register of Companies; company registration is distinct from the Ministry of Revenue business licence and tax registration. International companies under the International Companies Act 1988 require a separate SIFA/trustee-company route and restrictions. The old 1987 title and unsupported New Zealand-model comparison are not instructions for incorporation. [company registration and foreign investment requirements](https://mcil.gov.ws/services/business-registration); [registration process, director/share/address/application](https://www.businessregistries.gov.ws/companies/how-to-register-a-company/the-registration-process/); [section 249(2), exemptions and exclusions](https://sifa.ws/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/International-Companies-Act-1988.pdf).

## The base

Choose the legal form before using tax rates: a company has its own ordinary tax computation, while a sole trader’s ordinary profit uses individual bands and the proprietor’s own salary is not a separate deductible business wage. Partnership filing and allocation require the agreement; an overseas branch is not a new domestic company. Load [personal](ws-income-tax.md), [company](ws-corporate-income-tax.md), [VAGST](ws-vat-gst.md) and [payroll](ws-payroll-social.md) as the facts require. [Business Tax; printed 28 April 2020, republished October 2026](https://revenue.gov.ws/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/IS01_Income-Tax-1-2026.pdf); [section 2, resident company](https://www.ag.gov.ws/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/Income-Tax-Act-2012.pdf).

Registration sequence: create a Samoa Company Registry account; select a compliant unique company name; state private/public status and whether model rules apply; enter directors’ names/residential/postal addresses, shareholders and shares, registered office and postal address; obtain director consents, attach different rules if used, review and pay the fee. A name must satisfy section 10; do not promise a separate reservation stage without current portal evidence. The Registrar registers a compliant application and issues the certificate. [section 6](https://www.ag.gov.ws/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/Companies-Act-2001.pdf); [section 7](https://www.ag.gov.ws/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/Companies-Act-2001.pdf); [registration process, director/share/address/application](https://www.businessregistries.gov.ws/companies/how-to-register-a-company/the-registration-process/).

Then obtain any Foreign Investment Certificate/sector approvals and the business licence/TIN; register VAGST and PAYE when required and register the relevant contribution schemes when employing staff. MCIL says foreign shareholders need a current FIC; reserved/restricted activities and foreign directors working in Samoa need additional checks. A foreign director’s immigration/work permission is not a tax-residence conclusion. [company registration and foreign investment requirements](https://mcil.gov.ws/services/business-registration); [foreign investment certificate and permits](https://mcil.gov.ws/services/business-registration); [section 19](https://www.npf.ws/_files/ugd/2b7bf6_424e99682c134c08aa7789baf544d3c3.pdf).

International companies are a separate regime under the International Companies Act 1988, not the draft’s 1987 title. Section 249(2) provides an exemption, not a general domestic 0% rate: it excludes licensed international banks and income from carrying on business in Samoa. The separate dividend/interest limb has ownership and trustee-company-distribution exceptions. A resident-owned domestic company cannot elect this treatment merely by earning foreign income. [section 249(2), exemptions and exclusions](https://sifa.ws/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/International-Companies-Act-1988.pdf); [section 6, shareholder restrictions](https://sifa.ws/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/International-Companies-Act-1988.pdf).

The enacted 2026 removal Act commences on 1 January 2028 and preserves the removed exemptions until that date. It amends the income/direct-tax wording, so do not assume every indirect-tax or stamp-duty exemption also disappears. Do not extend a 2026 exemption calculation into 2028 or infer future filing mechanics from the Act’s title. [sections 1-2 and 6, enacted deferred commencement](https://sifa.ws/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Miscellaneous-Removal-of-Tax-Exemption-for-International-Companies-Amendment-Act-2026-Eng.pdf).

## Deadlines and filing

File the company’s annual registry return in the allocated month. MCIL describes the incorporation month, but section 124 permits the Registrar to alter the allocated month; no annual return is needed in the incorporation calendar year and a subsidiary may use the approved holding-company month. Confirm the registry record rather than treating incorporation anniversary as every company’s deadline. Income tax, VAGST and payroll returns are separate jobs. [section 124](https://www.ag.gov.ws/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/Companies-Act-2001.pdf); [company registration and foreign investment requirements](https://mcil.gov.ws/services/business-registration).

Business licences renew annually by 31 January, using the current renewal requirements including beneficial-owner information. The annual income tax deadline and approved balance-date exception belong to [company tax](ws-corporate-income-tax.md#deadlines-and-filing); VAGST/PAYE apply as registered. Do not give the old 1-2-week incorporation estimate as a guaranteed service time; no primary service standard was established. [due dates, annual licence renewal](https://revenue.gov.ws/inland-revenue-services/); [IR25, December 2025 update, licence class and fee](https://revenue.gov.ws/wp-content/uploads/2022/09/Business-License-Renewal-Form-IR25_17-12-25-1.pdf); [section 80](https://www.ag.gov.ws/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/Income-Tax-Act-2012.pdf).

## Penalties and interest

Registry, licence and tax late-filing charges are separate. MCIL publishes late-document fees of WST 50 within 25 working days and WST 150 later; section 124 also creates director liability for annual-return failure. Refer tax penalties to the [income-tax rules](ws-income-tax.md#penalties-and-interest). Do not price a late application without its document type, actual deadline and applicable notice. [current registration and maintenance fees, 9 March 2026](https://mcil.gov.ws/services/business-registration); [section 124](https://www.ag.gov.ws/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/Companies-Act-2001.pdf).

## Cross-border points

International-company ownership and domestic dealing are restricted. Section 6 bars Samoan citizen/resident/domiciled natural persons and domestic companies other than trustee companies from the specified ownership/guarantee membership. Section 249 restricts business/assets/property dealings with ordinary Samoa residents/domestic companies, land interests, and unlicensed banking/trust/insurance activities. It preserves limited local banking/professional contacts, records, meetings and qualifying office leases. Do not describe it as an unrestricted domestic trading company or assume every local contact breaches the restriction. [section 6, shareholder restrictions](https://sifa.ws/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/International-Companies-Act-1988.pdf); [section 249(4)-(5), domestic restrictions](https://sifa.ws/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/International-Companies-Act-1988.pdf).

## Two worked examples that recompute

### Budget for a single domestic activity

Assume a Samoan-owned domestic company with one full-year licensed activity, no FIC, special permit or agent fee, and no proration. Domestic registry fee WST 250 plus business licence WST 640 gives WST 890 initial statutory/administrative fee subtotal, derived. It excludes share funding and professional costs; an annual registry return is not required in the incorporation year.

```oa-check
name: Budget for a single domestic activity
inputs:
  registration: 250
  business_fee: 640
steps:
  - initial_fees: registration + business_fee
expected:
  initial_fees: 890
```

### Sole trader or ordinary company at the same profit

Assume an existing resident business with WST 40,000 ordinary taxable profit after allowable annual expenses, retained in the business, no wages, credits or special exemption, one full-year licensed activity, and a company annual return due. The identical taxable-profit input is after any deductible fees; fees below are shown separately only to compare cash outlays, without deducting them from profit again. Sole-trader tax is WST 6,050 plus licence WST 282 = WST 6,332. Company tax is WST 10,800 plus licence WST 640 and registry annual return WST 50 = WST 11,490. Difference WST 5,158, derived. This narrow tax/fee comparison favours the sole trader on the assumed facts; limited liability, finance, ownership and remuneration can change the decision and require separate advice.

```oa-check
name: Sole trader or ordinary company at the same profit
inputs:
  profit: 40000
  sole_licence: 282
  company_licence: 640
  annual_return: 50
steps:
  - sole_tax: min(max(profit - 15000, 0), 10000) * 0.20 + max(profit - 25000, 0) * 0.27
  - company_tax: profit * 0.27
  - sole_total: sole_tax + sole_licence
  - company_total: company_tax + company_licence + annual_return
  - difference: company_total - sole_total
expected:
  sole_total: 6332
  company_total: 11490
  difference: 5158
```

## Ask the client first

- Who will own/control the entity, with what citizenship/residence and foreign investment status?
- What activities, locations, sector permissions and expected turnover/staff?
- Which directors, consents, rules, share terms and addresses?
- Which tax balance date, allocated registry month, licence activities and formation-cost quotes?
