# Timor-Leste Payroll & Social Contributions

## Scope

Compute ordinary employee Wage Income Tax (WIT), social deductions, net pay and employer contributions/cost for 2026. Employers withhold WIT for ATTL and remit both social shares to the Instituto Nacional de Segurança Social (INSS), also presented as Segurança Social Timor-Leste. Public/private employers and subordinate workers are ordinarily subject to mandatory registration. Petroleum employment has separate tax rules; ENIN/self-employed and voluntary social insurance have different bases outside these calculations. This is a source-cited draft, not an accountant attestation. [Tax Act, articles 20–25 and 68–72](https://www.mj.gov.tl/jornal/public/docs/2019/serie_1/SERIE_I_NO_33_B.pdf); [DL 20/2017 republished by DL 30/2021, articles 1–4 and 8](https://www.mj.gov.tl/jornal/public/docs/2021/serie_1/SERIE_I_NO_49.pdf); [2026 Budget, article 3](https://www.mj.gov.tl/jornal/public/docs/2025/serie_1/SERIE_I_NO_48_B.pdf).

## Ask the client first

Obtain employer TIN/NISS, worker NISS, contracts/work locations, period/start/end dates, remuneration components/benefits, travel/residence and permanent-abode evidence, concurrent employment, foreign social certificates, petroleum/cooperation agreements and prior returns/payments. For relief obtain monthly headcount, nationalities, contribution-clearance status and evidence all workers were declared and contributions paid monthly.

## The method, step by step

1. **Confirm coverage and residence separately.** Tax residence is not nationality or automatic social exemption. WIT residence uses at least 183 days in any 12-month period beginning or ending in the year, unless the permanent abode is outside Timor-Leste; a Timor-Leste government official posted abroad is also resident. Obtain a coverage decision for temporary foreign postings; tax non-residence alone does not remove a worker from INSS. [Tax Act, article 1(nn)](https://www.mj.gov.tl/jornal/public/docs/2019/serie_1/SERIE_I_NO_33_B.pdf); [DL 20/2017, articles 2, 6-A, 6-B and 85-C](https://www.mj.gov.tl/jornal/public/docs/2021/serie_1/SERIE_I_NO_49.pdf).
2. **Build two bases.** WIT generally covers all employment remuneration, subject to legal wage exemptions. Test official-function exemptions, qualifying taxed foreign-government pay, UN/specialized-agency pay and the statutory Trust Fund exemption individually. The Budget also exempts qualifying cooperation agents working under agreements with Timor-Leste. A social-base exclusion does not automatically exclude pay from WIT. [Tax Act, articles 20–21 and 67](https://www.mj.gov.tl/jornal/public/docs/2019/serie_1/SERIE_I_NO_33_B.pdf); [2026 Budget, article 3(3)(d)](https://www.mj.gov.tl/jornal/public/docs/2025/serie_1/SERIE_I_NO_48_B.pdf).

The social base is gross remuneration due for work, including performance/productivity pay, legally due annual subsidy, shift/night supplements, remote-location and special-career supplements, other legally/contractually due work supplements and qualifying unjust-dismissal compensation before a fixed-term contract ends. Apply statutory public-service conditions where relevant. Exclude work-relocation/travel cost allowances, company-economic-performance gratuities/profit shares, overtime, food subsidies, other extraordinary employer benefits and representation expenses. Classify the actual payment's nature. [DL 20/2017, articles 8–9 as amended](https://www.mj.gov.tl/jornal/public/docs/2021/serie_1/SERIE_I_NO_49.pdf); [INSS base guidance](https://segurancasocial.gov.tl/cidadaos/deveres/contribuicoes).

3. **Compute each liability.** Calculate WIT before deducting the worker social share, consistent with INSS's wage examples. The employer bears its own contribution in addition to gross wages.

| Charge and applicable group | 2026 rate or threshold | Official source |
|---|---|---|
| Ordinary resident employee, full-month taxable wages | 0% through USD 500; 10% of the excess over USD 500 | [ATTL WIT](https://attl.gov.tl/wage-income-tax/) |
| Ordinary non-resident employee, taxable wages | 10% without a tax-free threshold | [ATTL WIT](https://attl.gov.tl/wage-income-tax/) |
| Mandatory employee social contribution | 4% of the legally defined contribution base, withheld by employer | [2026 Budget, article 3(5)](https://www.mj.gov.tl/jornal/public/docs/2025/serie_1/SERIE_I_NO_48_B.pdf) |
| Standard employer social contribution | 6% of that base; standard combined contribution 10% | [2026 Budget, article 3(5)](https://www.mj.gov.tl/jornal/public/docs/2025/serie_1/SERIE_I_NO_48_B.pdf) |
| Eligible private small employer in 2026 | At most 10 workers, at least 60% nationals, regularized contributions and continued monthly payment/declaration for all workers: 10% reduction of employer share; derived employer rate 6% × 90% = 5.4%, plus unchanged 4% worker share = 9.4% combined | [DL 20/2017, article 86](https://www.mj.gov.tl/jornal/public/docs/2021/serie_1/SERIE_I_NO_49.pdf); [2026 Budget, article 3(6)](https://www.mj.gov.tl/jornal/public/docs/2025/serie_1/SERIE_I_NO_48_B.pdf) |

4. **Retest relief monthly.** The employer-share reduction covers all workers while all conditions hold. It ceases when headcount exceeds the limit, monthly contributions are unpaid or the monthly declaration omits workers. It can resume from the month after regularization for the remaining legal period. From 2027 the general rate applies under the existing schedule. [Article 86](https://www.mj.gov.tl/jornal/public/docs/2021/serie_1/SERIE_I_NO_49.pdf).
5. **Reconcile gross, net and cost.** Net cash pay equals gross cash remuneration minus worker social contribution, WIT and separately authorized deductions. Employer cash cost equals gross cash pay plus employer contribution and other employer-paid benefits. Reconcile non-cash benefits separately; do not pay them out twice. The worked cases contain cash remuneration only.
6. **Reconcile separate authorities.** WIT goes to ATTL and combined social contributions to INSS. Match payroll to each declaration, guide and bank evidence. Correct WIT discharges the wage obligation without a statutory single-employer restriction; incorrect withholding may be assessed/refunded. [Tax Act, articles 23–25](https://www.mj.gov.tl/jornal/public/docs/2019/serie_1/SERIE_I_NO_33_B.pdf); [DL 20/2017, article 7](https://www.mj.gov.tl/jornal/public/docs/2021/serie_1/SERIE_I_NO_49.pdf).

## Boundary and exception table

| Situation | Treatment before computing | Official source |

## Worked cases

Both hypothetical cases assume ordinary full-month cash wages, established residence and mandatory employee coverage, with no exemption, petroleum activity, other deduction or rounding dispute.

**Different WIT and social bases.** A resident receives USD 900 basic salary, USD 60 shift supplement, USD 80 overtime and USD 40 food subsidy. Taxable wages are USD 1,080; the social base is USD 960 because overtime/food subsidy are excluded. Standard employer social is USD 57.60, worker social USD 38.40, WIT USD 58, net pay USD 983.60 and employer cost USD 1,137.60. [Articles 8–9](https://www.mj.gov.tl/jornal/public/docs/2021/serie_1/SERIE_I_NO_49.pdf); [Tax Act, article 21 and Annex V](https://www.mj.gov.tl/jornal/public/docs/2019/serie_1/SERIE_I_NO_33_B.pdf).

```oa-check
name: Separate WIT and social bases
inputs:
  basic: 900
  shift: 60
  overtime: 80
  food: 40
steps:
  gross_cash: basic + shift + overtime + food
  social_base: basic + shift
  worker_social: social_base * 0.04
  employer_social: social_base * 0.06
  wit: max(gross_cash - 500, 0) * 0.10
  net_pay: gross_cash - worker_social - wit
  employer_cost: gross_cash + employer_social
expected:
  gross_cash: 1080
  social_base: 960
  worker_social: 38.40
  employer_social: 57.60
  wit: 58
  net_pay: 983.60
  employer_cost: 1137.60
```

**Relief changes employer cost, not worker deductions.** A private employer has 10 workers, 6 nationals, regularized contributions and complete on-time monthly declarations/payments in 2026. All are resident and each earns USD 800 for the full month. Eligibility holds at exactly 60% nationals. On the USD 8,000 wage/contribution base, employer social is USD 432, worker social USD 320, WIT USD 300, net pay USD 7,380 and employer cost USD 8,432. The saving against standard employer social is USD 48. If headcount exceeds the limit or another condition fails, apply the unreduced share until the statutory restoration condition is satisfied.

```oa-check
name: Conditional small employer relief in 2026
inputs:
  workers: 10
  nationals: 6
  wage_each: 800
steps:
  national_percent: nationals / workers * 100
  gross: workers * wage_each
  standard_employer: gross * 0.06
  reduced_employer: standard_employer * 0.90
  worker_social: gross * 0.04
  wit_total: workers * max(wage_each - 500, 0) * 0.10
  net_total: gross - worker_social - wit_total
  cost: gross + reduced_employer
  saving: standard_employer - reduced_employer
expected:
  national_percent: 60
  gross: 8000
  reduced_employer: 432
  worker_social: 320
  wit_total: 300
  net_total: 7380
  cost: 8432
  saving: 48
```

## When to refuse or refer

Hold a filing-ready computation where residence, base classification, coverage, part-month proration or relief evidence is missing. Refer petroleum, international postings, ENIN/voluntary bases, disputed exemptions and arrears/sanctions. Do not claim a universal ceiling or absence of ceilings from the repeal of one special rule. Do not file or transfer funds without separate client authorization.

## Filing and payment

**Register.** Register the employer with INSS when admitting the first worker, and each worker by the first remuneration declaration including them. The official employer page identifies the registration form, TIN and commencement documents, with in-person or signed scanned delivery through the published INSS contact route. Retain the NISS. [DL 20/2017, articles 3–4](https://www.mj.gov.tl/jornal/public/docs/2021/serie_1/SERIE_I_NO_49.pdf); [INSS employer page](https://segurancasocial.gov.tl/empregadores).

**Declare, obtain guide, then pay INSS.** Deliver the **Declaração mensal de remunerações (DR)** by the 10th of the following month, identifying each worker and showing contributory remuneration, work time and rate. A full-month contract/work period is declared as 30 days regardless of calendar length. Obtain/reconcile the **Guia de Pagamento** and pay both shares between the 10th and 20th of the following month, after guide issuance. Retain declaration, guide and bank evidence and reconcile the credit. [Articles 7 and 11–12](https://www.mj.gov.tl/jornal/public/docs/2021/serie_1/SERIE_I_NO_49.pdf); [INSS workflow](https://segurancasocial.gov.tl/empregadores); [payment guidance](https://segurancasocial.gov.tl/cidadaos/deveres/pag_cont).

The site links **Moduló de Contribuiçóes** at [contribuicoes.segurancasocial.gov.tl](https://contribuicoes.segurancasocial.gov.tl/). Older generic employer guidance still labels several online functions as under construction. The newer **INSS Circular 22/2026**, signed 26 October and published 30 October, calls employers to regularize contributions by 31 December 2026 and makes regularized status indispensable for automatic access to the contribution module. It states that unregularized employers face the statutory sanctions from 1 January 2027. Keep the normal monthly declaration/payment dates alongside this regularization call; the circular does not state a blanket waiver of arrears or relief-eligibility conditions. No authenticated screens/current DR box numbering were available. Obtain the current DR/payment guide through the authority's published route; hold screen-specific instructions rather than inventing buttons. [Circular 22/2026, points 1–4](https://www.mj.gov.tl/jornal/public/docs/2026/serie_2/SERIE_II_NO_44.pdf).

**Monthly WIT.** Declare and pay by the 15th of the following month. Continue subsequent nil declarations unless ATTL grants a written waiver. The public [eTax guide](https://attl.gov.tl/uncategorized/etax-guideline/) gives these steps using earlier sample periods:

1. Log in through [ATTL e-Tax](https://e-tax.mof.gov.tl/). Choose **Declarations → Consolidated Declarations → Domestic Monthly Taxes → File** for the actual month.
2. Select **Domestic Monthly Wages Income Tax for Resident**; enter gross wages at line 5 and manually computed WIT total at line 10. **Calculate → Save → Mark as Complete**.
3. Complete **Domestic Monthly Wages Income Tax for non-Resident** separately if applicable. Enter gross wages at line 5 and reconcile calculated withholding at line 10. **Calculate → Save → Mark as Completed**. Resolve missing required tax-type registration with ATTL.
4. Select associated tax types, **Submit Declaration**, check the consolidated list and **Submit**. Retain each assessment number and the unique declaration number. Separately applicable service tax is completed only where the employer has that liability.
5. Pay using [domestic payment instructions](https://attl.gov.tl/how-to-pay-taxes/), retain bank evidence and send it through **Mailbox → Compose Mail → Category: Payment** with tax account, TIN, period, amount/type, attachment and **Send**. Reconcile the credit. The paper alternative uses 3 copies of the **Monthly Taxes Form** at BNU and a stamped retained copy. Payment and declaration are distinct obligations.

**Annual and employee records.** Submit the **Employer Wage Income Tax Withholding Form** by 31 March after year-end; ATTL specifies the next business day if that date is a weekend/public holiday. Supply a requested employee withholding statement within 21 days after year-end or termination during the year. No authenticated annual-employer-form screen was inspected. [Tax Act, articles 23–24](https://www.mj.gov.tl/jornal/public/docs/2019/serie_1/SERIE_I_NO_33_B.pdf); [ATTL WIT](https://attl.gov.tl/wage-income-tax/).

## Completion checklist

Confirm coverage/residence, separate both bases, document exclusions/relief, reconcile gross/net/cost and each share, file DR/WIT returns, obtain the INSS guide, pay each authority on time and retain/reconcile confirmations/bank credits. Complete annual employer and requested employee statements separately.
