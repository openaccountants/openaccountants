# Timor-Leste Company Formation & Entity Choice

**General reference only.** This skill is not accountant-approved for a particular formation. Use the actual documents and activity permissions before signing or trading. Checked against the cited provisions on 2026-11-15; tax year 2026.

## Who this applies to

Domestic and foreign applicants choosing an ordinary Timor-Leste business form. A private company is a sociedade por quotas (Lda.); its single-member form is a sociedade unipessoal por quotas. A sociedade anónima (S.A.) uses shares. An empresário em nome individual (ENIN) is an individual trading in their own name, rather than a separate limited company. Law 10/2017 annex arts. 1, 167, 199 and 202 and registry arts. 9 and 11 distinguish these forms. Law 10/2017 enactment art. 10 expressly repeals the former Law 4/2004. The official texts are linked below.

Foreign branches, regulated businesses, incentives and sector ownership restrictions require separate analysis. A company certificate does not establish every permission to operate. Quick answer: Lda. capital is freely fixed subject to each quota's minimum; the larger amount sometimes described as a company minimum is a contribution-deferral condition. The S.A. has its own capital floor. Obtain the form, owners, activity, capital and payment plan before choosing.

## The base

Record each nominal quota/share, actual cash/in-kind contribution and unpaid balance. A single-member company has a single initial quota and the unipessoal designation. Limited liability does not remove contribution obligations: Lda. members answer for completing contributions under arts. 167 and 173; art. 168 permits stipulated additional liability. Pre-registration acts have separate consequences under arts. 15 to 17. Capital is committed to the company, not a government fee or a tax deduction. Mixed contributions need statutory valuation/proof; the examples below assume cash only.

SERVE is the registration authority under republished registry art. 3. Under art. 38, the unique business number corresponds to the TIN and is issued on first registration through the shared tax-administration system. SERVE can also issue missing TINs for the specified ENIN, shareholders and officeholders. Reconcile any existing TIN; do not assume a separate company-TIN visit must always precede SERVE registration.

## Deadlines and filing

1. Select ENIN, single-member Lda., multi-member Lda. or S.A. Use the [SERVE checklist for that form](https://serve.gov.tl/requirements/), rather than a foreign-branch checklist. Identify owners, activity, office, directors and legal representative.
2. Prepare the constitutive act/articles, appointments and acceptances, identity and relevant marital-property documents, shareholder TINs, contribution proof and location map. Law 10/2017 annex art. 7 and registry art. 12 control. The constitutive act must be in an official language; private drafting is permitted subject to any more solemn form required by contributed property.
3. Use the currently published [SERVE_F_1-3 application](https://serve.gov.tl/wp-content/uploads/2022/02/Application-Form-for-Business-Registration-and-Licensing_updated19-11-2021.pdf). The applicant page separates name approval, registration and activity-authorisation boxes. The registration page asks for enterprise name/type, local/foreign status, office/contact, objects/activity, USD capital, existing TIN, shareholder identities/TINs/quotas and officeholders. Enter the agreed nominal amounts and actual facts; retain signed form and attachments. Its older licensing page is not proof of the current sector-licence procedure.
4. Submit to SERVE within the applicant deadline; retain the submission/process reference. Obtain an itemised current fee assessment before payment. The published free-registration FAQ and fee regulation do not establish a current all-in formation price; the initial-fee conflict is held below.
5. On approval, reconcile certificate, unique business number/TIN, capital, members and officeholders to the application. Registry arts. 37 and 38 specify the particulars; certificates are valid for 1 year (in words on the page). Distinguish renewal from original registration. Retain official receipts and correct errors through SERVE.
6. Establish activity permissions separately and the tax/employer registrations needed for actual operations. Load [corporate income tax](tl-corporate-income-tax.md), [income tax](tl-income-tax.md), [indirect taxes](tl-vat-gst.md) and, for staff, [payroll/social contributions](tl-payroll-social.md). Registration alone is not permission for every activity.

Annual income tax follows the deadline above. Applicable monthly WIT, services tax, domestic sales tax and withholding are due by the 15th of the following month under Tax Act arts. 9, 18, 23 and 58 (in words on the page); follow the separate return requirements of each tax. Import sales tax follows its customs payment procedure under art. 18(2); importing alone does not turn it into an ordinary monthly domestic return. Use each tax's actual scope, exemptions and deadline. Live e-tax fields and payment references are outside this formation workflow.

## Penalties and interest

Registry art. 11(6) provides fines for failure to register required company facts: USD 50 to USD 500 for natural persons and USD 500 to USD 5,000 for legal persons. Identify the obligation and offender; this is not an automatic charge on every application. Under art. 29 the registry may refuse missing evidence, insufficient authority or nonpayment of an actually due fee, with written reasons. These are registration consequences, not a tax-penalty or interest calculation. Detailed tax procedure remains held below.

## Cross-border points

A foreign company carrying on permanent activity is subject to registration and local representative/capital requirements under Law 10/2017 annex art. 3. A branch is not an incorporated Lda. or S.A.; do not apply this company capital table as a branch rule. SERVE's branch checklist asks for origin-country corporate documents and the board decision. Current branch capital, sector ownership and immigration particulars require separate confirmation. Registration, tax residence and permanent establishment are separate tests.

## Two worked examples that recompute

**Lda.: full payment or cash deferral.** Assume 2 members each subscribe USD 3,000 cash. Paying half gives USD 3,000 total, below the art. 172 deferral floor. Paying USD 2,500 each meets both conditions: unpaid USD 500 per quota is below half, and total paid is USD 5,000. The USD 1,000 deferred balance needs an agreed date within the statutory period. Full payment instead costs another USD 1,000. This does not make USD 5,000 a universal formation minimum.

```oa-check
name: tl-lda-cash-deferral-choice
inputs:
  members: 2
  quota: 3000
  half_fraction: 0.5
  deferral_paid_floor: 5000
steps:
  subscribed: members * quota
  half_paid: subscribed * half_fraction
  required_paid: max(half_paid, deferral_paid_floor)
  paid_per_member: required_paid / members
  unpaid_per_member: quota - paid_per_member
  deferred: subscribed - required_paid
expected:
  subscribed: 6000
  half_paid: 3000
  required_paid: 5000
  paid_per_member: 2500
  unpaid_per_member: 500
  deferred: 1000
```

**S.A.: the cash floor changes the percentage result.** Assume 3 ordinary private shareholders subscribe USD 100,000 cash. Article 203's percentage alone gives USD 25,000. Electing art. 218 cash deferral also requires USD 50,000 paid, so pay USD 50,000 and defer USD 50,000 within its statutory period. Full payment needs another USD 50,000. Subscribing only USD 50,000 would not permit merely USD 12,500 paid with the remainder deferred: the cash floor would fail.

```oa-check
name: tl-sa-cash-deferral-floor
inputs:
  subscribed: 100000
  minimum_fraction: 0.25
  cash_floor: 50000
steps:
  percentage_paid: subscribed * minimum_fraction
  required_paid: max(percentage_paid, cash_floor)
  deferred: subscribed - required_paid
  full_payment_extra: subscribed - required_paid
expected:
  percentage_paid: 25000
  required_paid: 50000
  deferred: 50000
  full_payment_extra: 50000
```

## Ask the client first

- Actual form, transaction date, activity and sector permissions.
- Owners/ownership chain, nationalities, residence and existing TINs.
- Office, directors, legal representative and signing authority.
- Each contribution, cash/in-kind split, payment proof and deferral plan.
- Tax year, existing operations, registrations, turnover and staff.
- Current application, fee assessment, submission reference and certificate.
