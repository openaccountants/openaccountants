# Uzbekistan Tax Optimization v0.2

> **General reference only.** Prepare a documented comparison for qualified professional review. A lower isolated tax amount does not establish a lawful business structure, regime election or entitlement to an incentive. Amounts are UZS unless explicitly USD.

## Who this applies to

For an existing or proposed individual entrepreneur (IE), self-employed person, small company or qualifying IT business considering lawful tax choices in Uzbekistan during 2026. Start with actual legal status, activity, timing, turnover/profit, ownership and distributions, employment and VAT position. Keep business substance and the statutory eligibility alongside every comparison.

The old skill claimed all individual dividends were exempt. That was wrong: ordinary resident dividends generally bear **5%**, while the temporary exemption concerns qualifying **shares**. A normal LLC distribution does not inherit the share exemption. [Tax Code, arts. 381 and 483](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/4674902).

Do not convert the BCV multiplier into an undated fixed soum limit; actual crossing and notice decisions need the applicable date's BCV and transition history.

## The base

### Compare the lawful alternatives

| Alternative | What to compare | Evidence required before selection |
|---|---|---|
| Genuine IE/self-employed business under turnover tax | Tax on turnover, separate social-tax obligations, excluded activities, compliance and VAT position. A low profit margin does not reduce turnover-tax base automatically. | Registration/status, actual business receipts and activity, period, threshold history, invoices and eligibility. |
| Company under turnover tax or profit tax | Turnover tax on its statutory base versus profit tax on Code income less permitted expenses; then any dividend tax, payroll/social tax, VAT and business compliance costs. | Actual election/registration, admissible expenses, accounts, prior regime and distributions. |
| Retain company profit or distribute it | Company tax is distinct from the owner's dividend tax. Retention does not turn company funds into tax-free personal spending. | Approved accounts and corporate distribution restrictions, ownership/instrument and recipient status. |
| IT Park | Exact tax covered and activity/status conditions, resident obligations and the appropriate period. A payment from an IT customer alone proves no exemption. | Resident approval, permitted activity, service contracts, income segregation and any required export/education evidence. |

For turnover classification, apply the later decree's removal of import-only compulsory VAT alongside the remaining statutory exclusions; do not assume every small importer must be on general tax throughout 2026. If already registered for VAT/profit tax, apply the notice and next-period transition rather than switching from a spreadsheet comparison. The optional simplified VAT regime has a separate eligibility and credit trade-off; use [VAT](uzbekistan-vat.md) before recommending it.

For dividends, first apply the company's correct regime and compute lawful distributable profit. Then establish whether the recipient is an individual or corporation, resident or nonresident, and whether it holds shares or an LLC interest. The temporary share exemption does not make a blanket “company plus dividend extraction is tax-free” strategy. Check [corporate income tax](uz-corporate-income-tax.md), [income tax](uzbekistan-income-tax.md) and [formation](uz-company-formation.md) for their respective jobs.

For IT Park, preserve the distinction between the resident's own relief and the nonresident supplier's income exemption. The original Uzbek Tax Code and PF-157 attach the USD 10,000,000 customer export threshold and non-treaty-country requirement to the foreign-service rule. Reject an exemption based on the supplier's export sales alone. Later-period extension criteria and professional approval are required before using a post-2027 plan.

## Deadlines and filing

For tax filing, payment and notice deadlines expressed in days, count working days unless the provision expressly says calendar days. If the final date is a statutory nonworking day, apply the next-working-day rule. [Tax Code, art. 5](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/4674902).

Document the comparison and actual election/notification before relying on a new regime. Under the opened Tax Committee guidance, an existing VAT payer below the applicable boundary may return to turnover tax from the next year after notifying no later than **10 days before the next tax period begins**. That is not an immediate conversion when the June decree is published. The VAT option's separate next-month process belongs in the VAT skill. [Tax Committee, existing-payer questions](https://gov.uz/oz/soliq/news/view/183078).

The **31 December 2028** share-dividend window and the IT Park periods are different. Model the appropriate post-window ordinary position for long-term planning; do not assume relief continues merely because another incentive lasts longer. Return, payment and payroll duties continue according to the actual regime. Preserve application/notice acknowledgements, approvals, supporting computations and the tax-account reconciliation.

## Penalties and interest

**AUDIT FLASH POINT**: do not split receipts across nominal businesses, misdescribe employment as self-employment, backdate elections, label private consumption as business expenditure, or move non-qualifying revenue into an IT Park exemption. Statutory relief has specific exclusions/clawbacks; a low headline rate is not authority for artificial arrangements. Article 337 expressly excludes income-splitting/liquidation and specified taxpayers from its transition reliefs. [Tax Code, art. 337](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/4674902).

No generic penalty percentage is used in the decision examples. For an actual failed condition, omitted withholding or late payment, identify the specific breach and date, restore the correct tax and compute statutory interest/sanctions from the applicable provision with professional review.

## Cross-border points

Foreign IT providers from treaty states do not qualify under the specific non-treaty-state exemption merely by having an IT Park customer; analyse the actual treaty instead. Nonresident dividends, royalties and corporate recipients have their own rules. A foreign owner or export invoice does not prove foreign-source income, IT Park eligibility, beneficial entitlement or an exemption. Keep the currency and exchange-date evidence where a USD threshold is involved.

## Two worked examples that recompute

### Example 1: turnover tax versus ordinary company tax on different margins

Illustrative **January–May 2026 eligibility comparison**: genuine IE turnover UZS 800,000,000 would produce UZS 8,000,000 turnover tax. Compare only that tax component with a resident-owned ordinary company **validly on profit tax** and distributing all its after-CIT profit as an ordinary LLC dividend. At UZS 30,000,000 taxable profit the company's two tax components are lower; at UZS 200,000,000 they are higher. This does not include VAT, IE social tax, employer taxes, fees or compliance, and does not establish a legal-form recommendation. A company's possible turnover-tax alternative also needs evaluation. [Tax Code, arts. 337, 381, 461 and 467](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/4674902).

```oa-check
name: uz-planning-margin-decision
inputs:
  turnover: 800000000
  ie_rate: 0.01
  low_profit: 30000000
  high_profit: 200000000
  cit_rate: 0.15
  resident_dividend_rate: 0.05
steps:
  ie_turnover_tax: turnover * ie_rate
  low_cit: low_profit * cit_rate
  low_dividend: low_profit - low_cit
  low_dividend_tax: low_dividend * resident_dividend_rate
  low_total_tax: low_cit + low_dividend_tax
  high_cit: high_profit * cit_rate
  high_dividend: high_profit - high_cit
  high_dividend_tax: high_dividend * resident_dividend_rate
  high_total_tax: high_cit + high_dividend_tax
expected:
  ie_turnover_tax: 8000000
  low_cit: 4500000
  low_dividend: 25500000
  low_dividend_tax: 1275000
  low_total_tax: 5775000
  high_cit: 30000000
  high_dividend: 170000000
  high_dividend_tax: 8500000
  high_total_tax: 38500000
```

The decision reverses with the margin: UZS 5,775,000 is below UZS 8,000,000, but UZS 38,500,000 is above it. The rates alone do not decide without the bases and omitted costs.

### Example 2: distinguish an LLC dividend from a qualifying share dividend

Assume an individual resident receives UZS 10,000,000 in 2026, either as an ordinary LLC dividend or as a documented qualifying share dividend within the statutory window. For this comparison the amount is already a lawful distribution after the payer's own taxes. [Tax Code, arts. 381 and 483](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/4674902).

```oa-check
name: uz-planning-dividend-instrument
inputs:
  gross_dividend: 10000000
  llc_rate: 0.05
  qualifying_share_rate: 0
steps:
  llc_pit: gross_dividend * llc_rate
  llc_net: gross_dividend - llc_pit
  share_pit: gross_dividend * qualifying_share_rate
  share_net: gross_dividend - share_pit
  difference: share_net - llc_net
expected:
  llc_pit: 500000
  llc_net: 9500000
  share_pit: 0
  share_net: 10000000
  difference: 500000
```

The old blanket exemption would understate LLC dividend PIT by UZS 500,000 in this case. Do not relabel an LLC interest as a share, or change legal form solely from this isolated comparison.

## Ask the client first

Obtain actual legal status and registration; period, activity and regime/election history; turnover and profit with documentary expenses; ownership and distribution instrument; recipient residence and actual tax already borne; staff/social-tax obligations; VAT/credit position; IT Park approval/permitted activity and export evidence; nonresident provider's treaty state; planned timing and post-incentive horizon. Refuse unsupported exemptions, false business splitting and any comparison that omits material taxes while claiming an all-in saving.
