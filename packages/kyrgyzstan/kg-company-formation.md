# Kyrgyzstan company formation and entity choice (2026)

> **General reference only.** This skill is general tax/accounting reference material for AI-assisted workflows. It has not been reviewed for any specific person's facts, documents, elections, deadlines, residency, filing status, or local procedures. Do not rely on it to file, pay, amend, or take a tax position without review by a qualified professional in the relevant jurisdiction.

An ordinary limited liability company (OsOO) can have 1 or more founders, but its participant limit and contribution obligations matter. Use the registration edition in force on the actual application date.

## Who this applies to

Founders choosing an ordinary Kyrgyz business vehicle and assembling its registration file. This is a reference to entity and registration rules, not a completed application. Financial institutions, regulated activities, complex single-member ownership chains and foreign branches require additional review.

The registration reference below uses the 17 April 2026 Regulation257 edition in force on 15 November 2026. Amendment746, published 10 November, takes effect after 10 days, on 20 November 2026 (derived); do not apply its revised procedures early. Recheck an application on or after that date.

## The base

| Option | What changes the choice | Official source |
|---|---|---|
| OsOO | Separate company, divided participation interests and ordinary limited liability under art127; art128 adds ownership/participant constraints | https://cbd.minjust.gov.kg/3-1/edition/latest/ru |
| Open joint-stock company | Shares can be transferred without other shareholders' consent and public subscription is permitted under arts139-140; securities and governance requirements are separate | https://cbd.minjust.gov.kg/3-1/edition/latest/ru |
| Closed joint-stock company | Shares distributed among founders/a predetermined group; no public subscription under art141; do not import its participant rule into an OsOO | https://cbd.minjust.gov.kg/3-1/edition/latest/ru |
| Individual entrepreneur | Business without forming an organisation under Tax Code art26. Patent/self-work and unified-tax possibilities depend on their own statutory eligibility, not automatic election by every sole trader | https://cbd.minjust.gov.kg/112340/edition/latest/ru |
| Foreign branch/representative office | Not a separate legal person; operates under parent-approved regulations and authorised management, Civil Code art90. Tax PE depends on actual activity under Tax Code art28, not the label alone | https://cbd.minjust.gov.kg/3-1/edition/latest/ru; PE: https://cbd.minjust.gov.kg/112340/edition/latest/ru |

For an OsOO, document founders, ownership, contribution amount/timing and governance. Civil Code art129 governs constitutive documents, including each interest and contribution terms; the registration regime below governs which documents are actually submitted. Single-member corporate chains have art128(2) restrictions and liability consequences: obtain the complete ownership chain and check that article before proceeding. Do not promise limited liability without the unpaid-capital and statutory exceptions. Net assets and later capital changes also require art130 review.

## Deadlines and filing

1. Check the proposed name for identity/similarity through the public registration register; reservation is optional under para10. Prepare the required Kyrgyz/Russian names and authentic meaning. The applicant may be a founder, executive head or authorised representative under para9.
2. Prepare the prescribed registration application with beneficial-owner information, and the founders' creation decision under paras11,66-68. The decision includes name/address, founders, capital and each share, executive and beneficial owners; confirm truth and compliance with contribution procedures. Use the applicable model charter under the operative para16. Ordinary charters/agreements are not submitted to the register under para15, except the financial-credit category. Distinguish internal approval from documents physically filed.
3. Supply identity documents and legal-address evidence, and any foreign-corporate extract/translation, under paras32-33,44,52. Under para33, identity documents are not submitted for digital registration. Foreign-language documents need notarised Kyrgyz/Russian translation. The operative para44 also requires a notarised translation into a language understood by a foreign founder, head or representative who cannot understand the Kyrgyz/Russian document. Paper founder signatures follow para68's notarisation/seal rules; digital submissions have the stated exception. Confirm regulated-sector consents before filing.
4. File with the authorised registration body/territorial office under paras6,8, with levy/service-charge payment evidence under para66. Digital registration is permitted under its prescribed procedure; live portal fields and the precise current office have not been verified. Use the official registration framework cited here; do not present bank-account opening as part of legal registration itself.
5. Obtain and check the digital registration certificate and register entry under paras26,69-71. Registration date is the order date. Single-window registration simultaneously creates tax, statistics and Social Fund registration, and assigns registration number, TIN and OKPO under paras62-64; separate duplicate tax/Social Fund registration is not required.
6. Arrange operational banking as needed after registration; bank-specific KYC/account requirements are unconfirmed here. A seal/stamp is a right under para28, not a universal compulsory formation step. Registration alone does not authorise licensed activity under para26.

Maintain accounting records and annual financial statements under Accounting Act arts1,1-1,2,10,12-13. Public-interest/large entities use IFRS, medium entities IFRS for SMEs (with the statutory election), and small entities the Cabinet's simplified rules (with the statutory election). Do not prescribe IFRS to every OsOO. Art13 permits a non-budget entity created after 1 October to have its first accounting year end on 31 December of the following year; this does not itself extend tax deadlines. Confirm actual size, sector and reporting recipients before selecting standards or audit requirements. Tax compliance is separate: applicable annual profit declaration/payment, ordinary monthly VAT and payroll/social reports remain under the linked tax skills. A new certificate does not create an exemption from filing.

## Penalties and interest

Unpaid agreed capital can require reduction or liquidation under Civil Code art130(3); too many participants invokes art128(1). Incomplete/incorrect registration documents can interrupt or defeat registration under Regulation257 paras125-126. Tax arrears and late reports have separate Tax Code arts82,163 consequences; do not treat a registration fee as a penalty or quote a universal formation fine.

## Cross-border points

Obtain foreign founder existence/authority evidence and the required translation. Branches and representative offices are not independent limited-liability companies. Check actual PE activity and treaty rules before treating branch income or withholding, and review the current ownership-chain restrictions rather than assuming all foreign-controlled single-member chains qualify.

## Two worked examples that recompute

Illustrative agreed capital amounts, not statutory minima or fee quotations. Two founders agree capital KGS 1000000 in a 60%/40% split. At the end of the first year, the first has paid KGS 400000 and the second KGS 250000. Unpaid contributions total KGS 350000; the art127 unpaid-contribution liability and art130 non-payment remedy must be addressed. The arithmetic does not approve a capital reduction.

```oa-check
name: Agreed capital and unpaid contributions at first-year deadline
inputs:
  agreed_capital: 1000000
  first_paid: 400000
  second_paid: 250000
steps:
  - first_due: agreed_capital * 0.60
  - second_due: agreed_capital * 0.40
  - first_unpaid: first_due - first_paid
  - second_unpaid: second_due - second_paid
  - total_unpaid: first_unpaid + second_unpaid
expected:
  first_unpaid: 200000
  second_unpaid: 150000
  total_unpaid: 350000
```

A different OsOO agrees KGS 2000000 split 75%/25%, and both founders pay their full agreed amount in time. The unpaid-contribution shortfall is zero; this removes that particular unpaid-capital exposure, not all statutory liability or ongoing compliance. Compare this fully funded case with the first before advising that an unpaid contribution is harmless.

```oa-check
name: Fully funded agreed capital removes contribution shortfall
inputs:
  agreed_capital: 2000000
steps:
  - first_contribution: agreed_capital * 0.75
  - second_contribution: agreed_capital * 0.25
  - total_paid: first_contribution + second_contribution
  - unpaid: agreed_capital - total_paid
expected:
  first_contribution: 1500000
  second_contribution: 500000
  total_paid: 2000000
  unpaid: 0
```

## Ask the client first

- Activities, licences, planned entity form and reason for incorporation versus branch/sole trade.
- Every founder, citizenship/legal status, beneficial owner and full ownership chain.
- Agreed capital, each interest, contribution evidence and intended funding timetable.
- Name, legal-address rights, executive and applicant authority.
- Application date, paper/digital route, foreign documents and translations.
- Expected reporting category, employees, tax regime and VAT/import activity.
