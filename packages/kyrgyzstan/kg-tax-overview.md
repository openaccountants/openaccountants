# Kyrgyzstan tax overview (2026)

> **General reference only.** This skill is general tax/accounting reference material for AI-assisted workflows. It has not been reviewed for any specific person's facts, documents, elections, deadlines, residency, filing status, or local procedures. Do not rely on it to file, pay, amend, or take a tax position without review by a qualified professional in the relevant jurisdiction.

Use the taxpayer's citizenship, legal form, income source and tax regime before selecting the ordinary rates. The Tax Code has applied since 1 January 2022; this reference uses its consolidated 2026 text.

## Who this applies to

Individuals, domestic organisations and foreign businesses with Kyrgyz income or activity. The State Tax Service administers the taxes through the statutory tax-authority system in art55. Use the detailed [personal income tax](kg-income-tax.md), [profit tax](kg-corporate-income-tax.md), [VAT](kg-vat-gst.md), [payroll](kg-payroll-social.md) and [formation](kg-company-formation.md) skills for the relevant job.

For PIT, arts185-186 use citizenship and residence-permit/kairylman status as well as residence and source. Kyrgyz citizens and non-citizen residents with the specified permit/status fall within worldwide-income rules; non-citizen residents without it and non-citizen non-residents generally fall within Kyrgyz-source rules. There is a specific foreign-branch employee exception. A registered individual entrepreneur is not an ordinary PIT taxpayer on business profit. Domestic companies generally include worldwide-source income; foreign PEs use Kyrgyz-source rules under arts209,247. Do not use a blanket residents-worldwide/non-residents-source-only slogan.

## The base

The PIT and profit-tax periods are the calendar year under arts188,211. Ordinary VAT and sales-tax periods are monthly under arts313,369, with express special-period exceptions. PIT uses statutory income exclusions/deductions and salary floors; profit tax uses income less allowed expenses/losses, not cash receipts; VAT uses output tax less eligible credits, not profit.

Sales tax is a separate turnover tax under arts363-371, not automatically a substitute for VAT. Art366 ordinarily uses business sales revenue excluding VAT and sales tax; banks, securities/asset sales, insurance and other listed cases have different bases. Arts364-365 contain exemptions and non-objects: exports, outside-Kyrgyz sales and international transport are not ordinary taxable sales; qualifying agriculture, charitable and other specified activities have their own relief. Distinguish a statutory exemption/non-object from a zero rate. Do not treat the seed's approximate rate range as permission to choose a rate by payment method alone.

Check whether a special regime displaces a general-regime tax before computing. HTP's qualifying registered activity has the arts432-435 exemptions, while ordinary payroll/agent obligations can remain. Patent and unified-tax eligibility/rates are outside this overview; do not apply ordinary profit tax merely because the supplier is an individual entrepreneur.

Use the prescribed electronic tax reports under art100 with TIN, addresses, period, tax obligation and signature. Reconcile calculations with invoices/payroll/ledgers, save filed return/submission evidence, and separately settle and reconcile payments. This overview does not supply current portal field numbers or payment references. Art18 moves a non-working last day to the next working day. An art102 filing extension does not extend payment.

## Penalties and interest

Art82 ordinary late-tax interest is 0.09% per calendar day from the day after the due date, capped at 100% of arrears, with statutory exceptions including qualifying deferral, force majeure, bankruptcy and reliance on official explanations. Art163 has separate non-filing and understatement sanctions. Do not use one late-filing amount for all taxpayer/return categories or assume social-insurance sanctions are identical.

## Cross-border points

Check treaty priority under art3, source and PE under art28 before taxing foreign payments. Profit-tax withholding, VAT on foreign services and payroll withholding are distinct obligations. Residence alone does not decide source, and payment abroad does not eliminate Kyrgyz source. Specialist treaty rates and foreign credits require the applicable primary treaty/procedure.

## Two worked examples that recompute

Illustrative KGS inputs. For an ordinary company with statutory taxable income KGS 5000000, allowed expenses KGS 3500000 and no losses/credits/exemption, taxable profit is KGS 1500000 and profit tax KGS 150000. This does not determine VAT or sales-tax liability.

```oa-check
name: Ordinary corporate profit from allowed income and expenses
inputs:
  taxable_income: 5000000
  allowed_expenses: 3500000
steps:
  - profit: taxable_income - allowed_expenses
  - tax: profit * 0.10
expected:
  profit: 1500000
  tax: 150000
```

For VAT-taxable trading goods sold to an ordinary identified buyer outside the special buyer/sector categories, suppose sales revenue excluding both taxes is KGS 1000000. Ordinary sales tax at 1% is KGS 10000, even though the sale also falls within VAT. Compare a legally VAT-exempt trading sale with the same sales-tax base and no sales-tax exemption: its ordinary sales tax is KGS 20000 at 2%. These are alternative classified transactions, not a free choice of regime.

```oa-check
name: Sales tax differs with VAT classification of trading goods
inputs:
  sales_tax_base: 1000000
steps:
  - vat_taxable_trade_sales_tax: sales_tax_base * 0.01
  - vat_exempt_trade_sales_tax: sales_tax_base * 0.02
  - difference: vat_exempt_trade_sales_tax - vat_taxable_trade_sales_tax
expected:
  vat_taxable_trade_sales_tax: 10000
  vat_exempt_trade_sales_tax: 20000
  difference: 10000
```

## Ask the client first

- Citizenship, residence/permit status, legal form and PE/source facts.
- Business activity, tax regime, HTP/investment status and registration dates.
- Income, expenses, invoices, taxable/exempt transactions and buyer/payment categories.
- Employees, remuneration and special payroll categories.
- Calendar period, prior returns, withheld/preliminary taxes and payments.
- Foreign counterparties, treaty claims, export/import and digital-service facts.
