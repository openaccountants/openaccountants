# Montenegro Company Formation & Entity Choice

> **General reference only.** This skill is general tax/accounting reference material for AI-assisted workflows. It has not been reviewed for any specific person's facts, documents, elections, deadlines, residency, filing status, or local procedures. Do not rely on it to file, pay, amend, or take a tax position without review by a qualified professional in the relevant jurisdiction.

**Quick answer.** A d.o.o. needs EUR 1 of capital and at most 30 members; an a.d. EUR 25,000. Sign the founding act and statute (on paper with certified signatures, or electronically with qualified e-signatures), pay in the capital, and file one electronic application with the CRPS at the Tax Administration, paying EUR 15 (d.o.o.) or EUR 50 (a.d.). The CRPS decides a complete application within 10 working days (5 for natural-person founders using the published model acts) and issues the registration number and PIB. Then enter beneficial owners within 8 days. All figures were read on 2026-11-14 in the Official Gazette (sluzbenilist.me), except the two tax-law rows marked "not reopened".

## Scope

- **Who this is for:** founders and advisers forming a business in Montenegro in 2026, foreign parents opening a branch, and companies registered before 2026 that had to align with the new law.
- **The new laws.** A new Law on Companies (Zakon o privrednim društvima, Official Gazette 90/25) and a new Law on Registration of Business and Other Entities (Zakon o registraciji privrednih i drugih subjekata, 92/25) apply from 1 January 2026 _(Law on Companies art. 635; Registration Law art. 48)_. The Law on Companies replaced the 2020 law (65/20, 146/21 and 4/24) from that date _(art. 634)_. Both were amended by 121/25 and again by Official Gazette 44/26 of 27 March 2026, in force on publication.
- **Forms.** Business activity is carried on by a company, an entrepreneur (preduzetnik) or a part (branch) of a foreign company. The company forms are the general partnership (ortačko društvo), the limited partnership (komanditno društvo), the joint-stock company (akcionarsko društvo, a.d.) and the limited liability company (društvo sa ograničenom odgovornošću, d.o.o.) _(Law on Companies art. 2(1), (4))_.
- **d.o.o.** The most common vehicle. Capital is divided into shares (udjeli) held by one or more members; at most 30 members _(art. 358)_; a single-member d.o.o. is allowed _(art. 360)_. Members are not liable for the company's obligations unless the law says otherwise; the company is liable with all its assets _(art. 16(1), (3))_.
- **a.d.** A share-capital company for larger ventures, minimum capital EUR 25,000 _(art. 137(2))_; shareholders are not liable for the company's obligations _(art. 16(3))_.
- **Partnerships.** Partners in a general partnership, and general partners in a limited partnership, are liable jointly and without limit with all their assets _(art. 16(2))_.
- **Entrepreneur (preduzetnik).** A natural person with legal capacity who carries on a business for profit on his or her own account, as a main activity (an unemployed person) or a supplementary one (a person who is employed); a supplementary entrepreneur may not employ anyone _(art. 84)_. The entrepreneur is liable with all of his or her property, and the liability does not end on striking off the CRPS _(art. 93)_. Tax: [montenegro-income-tax](montenegro-income-tax.md).
- **Branch of a foreign company (dio stranog privrednog društva).** A part of a company founded and registered outside Montenegro that does business in Montenegro through it; registered in the CRPS _(art. 546(1)-(3))_ within 30 days of being set up _(art. 547(1))_, and must do business under Montenegrin rules _(art. 546(4))_. Tax as a permanent establishment: [me-cross-border](me-cross-border.md).
- **Founders.** Any natural or legal person with legal capacity, "domaće ili strano" (domestic or foreign) _(art. 7(1))_. Art. 7 sets no nationality or residence condition, so a d.o.o. can be 100% foreign-owned.
- **Tax by form.** A d.o.o. or a.d. pays corporate profit tax at 9%, 12% and 15% (bands EUR 100,000 and 1,500,000): [me-corporate-income-tax](me-corporate-income-tax.md). An entrepreneur with a main activity pays personal income tax at 0%, 9% and 15% on annual bands of EUR 8,400 and 12,000, or 15% flat on a supplementary activity: [montenegro-income-tax](montenegro-income-tax.md). The comparison is in [montenegro-tax-optimization](montenegro-tax-optimization.md).
- **Register.** All companies, entrepreneurs and branches register in the Central Register of Business and Other Entities (CRPS) _(Law on Companies art. 5(1))_, kept by the Tax Administration (Poreska uprava) _(Registration Law art. 4)_.

## Ask the client first

1. Which form: d.o.o., a.d., partnership, entrepreneur, or branch of a foreign company?
2. How many founders, natural or legal persons, domestic or foreign? Will they use the CRPS model founding act and statute (faster decision)?
3. Capital: amount, cash or in kind; is real estate contributed (then the founding act cannot be electronic)?
4. Can every founder sign with a qualified electronic signature, or will the acts be on paper with certified signatures?
5. Expected supplies in the first 12 months (VAT registration at EUR 30,000)?
6. Who are the beneficial owners, and who in Montenegro will be designated to keep the register entry?

## The method, step by step

1. **Reserve the name (optional).** A reservation is registered for 90 days from the registration decision and can be transferred while it lasts _(Registration Law art. 30)_.
2. **Sign the founding act and statute.** The founding act is a founding contract where two or more persons found the company, or a founding decision where one person does. It can be on paper or electronic. On paper, all founders sign with certified signatures under the law on certification of signatures; electronically, all founders sign with a qualified electronic signature or seal. Where real estate is contributed, the founding act cannot be electronic. A d.o.o. and an a.d. also have a statute; an a.d. statute on paper also needs certified signatures. Both acts are registered in the CRPS _(Law on Companies art. 8, as replaced by 44/26 art. 2)_.
3. **Pay in the capital.** Payment can be made electronically without the founder being present, unless the contribution is in kind _(art. 10(7), as replaced by 44/26 art. 3)_. Paying into a bank in an EU member state and filing an electronic bank proof of payment (art. 10(8) and (9)) apply only from Montenegro's accession to the EU _(art. 633, as amended by 44/26 art. 7)_.
4. **File with the CRPS.** One electronic application on a single form with the founding documents _(Registration Law art. 19(1), (2), (5))_. A d.o.o. or a branch can be formed fully online with no physical presence at any stage; the authority can ask for presence only on concrete signs of identity misuse, money laundering or doubt about capacity or authority _(Law on Companies art. 10(1), (10)-(11), as replaced by 44/26 art. 3)_. The fee is due on the day the application is filed _(Registration Law art. 42(2))_.
5. **Registration and PIB.** Before registering, the CRPS checks the founding act and statute, the minimum capital, the activity, the number of members and the filer's authority _(Law on Companies art. 5(9), as replaced by 44/26 art. 1)_. The registration decision gives the registration number and the tax identification number (PIB) _(Registration Law art. 27(7))_. The company is a legal person from the day of registration _(Law on Companies art. 6(1))_.
6. **Beneficial owners.** Enter them in the Register of Beneficial Owners within 8 days of CRPS registration (see Filing and payment).
7. **VAT.** Register when supplies pass EUR 30,000; voluntary registration is possible below it: [montenegro-vat](montenegro-vat.md).
8. **Changes later.** Register any change within 7 days of it _(Registration Law art. 19(7))_.

## Boundary and exception table

| Situation | Rule |
|---|---|
| Real estate contributed | Founding act cannot be electronic; paper with certified signatures _(art. 8 as replaced by 44/26)_ |
| Contribution in kind | Electronic payment without presence does not apply _(art. 10(7))_ |
| Payment through an EU bank, electronic bank proof | Only from Montenegro's EU accession _(art. 633 as amended by 44/26)_ |
| Signs of identity misuse or money laundering | The CRPS may require physical presence _(art. 10(10)-(11))_ |
| Supplementary entrepreneur | May not employ anyone _(art. 84)_ |
| Company or entrepreneur registered before 2026 | Had to align with the new Law on Companies and register the changes by 15 June 2026 _(art. 630(1), as amended by 44/26 art. 6)_; one that has not, or has not filed its annual accounts, is marked "registered inactive" on the CRPS list _(Registration Law art. 12)_ |
| Entrepreneurs and listed joint-stock companies | Excluded from the Register of Beneficial Owners _(AML Law art. 43)_ |

## Worked cases

**Two founders form a d.o.o. online with EUR 1,000 capital and want an extract.** They sign the founding contract and statute with qualified e-signatures, pay the capital electronically and file one CRPS application: fee EUR 15. They also order an electronic extract: EUR 10. Total CRPS fees EUR 25. The CRPS must decide within 10 working days (5 if both used the model acts). If they later raise the capital, that change costs EUR 50 and must be filed within 7 days.

## When to refuse or refer

- Regulated activities (banks, insurers, investment firms, gambling): refer; sector licences are outside this skill.
- Who certifies paper signatures (notary or another body): not confirmed, refer to a notary.
- A company marked "registered inactive" or one that missed the 15 June 2026 alignment: refer to a Montenegrin lawyer.
- Do not quote how long a bank account or post-registration setup takes: no official source.

## Filing and payment

- **Annual financial statements.** Filed with the Tax Administration on paper and electronically by 31 March for the previous year _(Accounting Law 84/25, art. 42(1))_. Micro and small entities file a balance sheet, an income statement, simplified notes and a statistical annex _(art. 42(7))_. Consolidated statements by 31 May _(art. 42(2))_. The Tax Administration publishes them _(art. 42(6))_.
- **Corporate income tax return.** Filed, and the tax paid, within three months after the end of the tax period: 31 March 2027 for 2026, with the balance sheet and income statement _(Corporate Profit Tax Law art. 40(3)-(4))_. Detail: [me-corporate-income-tax](me-corporate-income-tax.md).
- **Register of Beneficial Owners.** Kept by the Tax Administration. Companies, branches of foreign companies and other legal persons enter their beneficial owners within 8 days of registration in the CRPS or the taxpayer register, and every change within 8 days; they confirm the data once a year by 31 March _(AML Law 110/23, art. 43(2)-(5), as amended by 59/26 art. 27)_. Each must name at least one natural person employed by it and living in Montenegro, or a Montenegrin-registered company that is an obligor under art. 4(2)(13) indent 2, authorised to enter the data and answer the authorities _(art. 43(9), added by 59/26 art. 27)_.
- **VAT.** Monthly returns electronically by the 15th of the following month, even with no VAT due _(VAT Law art. 35(2)-(3))_: [montenegro-vat](montenegro-vat.md).
- **Payroll.** A company with employees files the monthly IOPPD by the 15th for the previous month: [montenegro-payroll](montenegro-payroll.md).

## Completion checklist

- [ ] Form chosen; capital at or above the minimum; members within 30 (d.o.o.).
- [ ] Founding act and statute signed (e-signatures, or paper with certified signatures).
- [ ] Capital paid; CRPS application filed with the fee.
- [ ] Registration decision with registration number and PIB received.
- [ ] Beneficial owners entered within 8 days; designated person named.
- [ ] VAT threshold watched; IOPPD set up if there are employees.
- [ ] 31 March diarised (annual statements, tax return, beneficial owner confirmation).
