# Argentina: official source index 2026 v1.1

> **General reference only.** This skill is general tax/accounting reference material for AI-assisted workflows. It has not been reviewed for any specific person's facts, documents, elections, deadlines, residency, filing status, or local procedures. Do not rely on it to file, pay, amend, or take a tax position without review by a qualified professional in the relevant jurisdiction.

## The method, step by step

**Quick answer:** load the tax job's Guide, then read its official source and date-specific table. This Tier 2 router has no professional approval. Establish jurisdiction, taxpayer, tax, fiscal/payment period and the exact question first.

1. Use [tax overview](ar-tax-overview.md) and the [pack map](README.md) to select the job. [Individual income tax](argentina-income-tax.md), [corporate tax](ar-corporate-income-tax.md), [Monotributo](ar-monotributo.md), [IVA computation](argentina-iva.md), [IVA filing](ar-vat-return.md), [social contributions](ar-social-contributions.md), [payroll](argentina-payroll.md), [TP](argentina-transfer-pricing.md), [cross-border](argentina-cross-border.md) and [formation](ar-company-formation.md) each own their figures and legal conditions.
2. Read the cited ARCA Biblioteca Electrónica law/regulation, distinguishing **Texto vigente** from embedded earlier versions. The current consolidated LIG and its decree govern income-tax classification, deductions, source and withholding; the IVA text governs IVA rather than a bank's transaction label. An article number in a search snippet does not establish its operative text.
3. For indexed parameters use the original official table named for the applicable period, not the statute's original nominal amounts or an older table increased by a guessed index. Corporate fiscal-opening years, payroll payment/settlement tables, Monotributo subperiods and monthly autonomous contributions have different boundaries. The owning Guide supplies the exact official document and read scope.
4. Read definitions, caps, exemptions, effective dates and transition articles around the quoted amount. Match the transaction's facts and date before computing. Record the original URL, portion read, short exact original-language quotation and read date. Preserve an original HTML/PDF and extraction outside the published Guide when doing research.
5. If an official URL returns an unavailable page, do not count its HTTP response as reading the law. Use the substantive official alternative cited by the owning Guide, or mark the proposition unconfirmed. A downloaded compressed PDF must be extracted/rendered and read before its figures are called verified.
6. Use the official filing regulation and current ARCA instructions for portal/form/field and confirmation/payment steps. Old manuals identify historical workflow only; they do not prove a current screen. Provincial and municipal matters need the actual local authority's sources in addition to national law.
7. Hand off the relevant Guide, source passage/table, dates and unresolved facts. Tax information from another country's folder, an open-source calculator, a commercial summary or another AI is not primary Argentine evidence.

## When to refuse or refer

Stop the affected figure when the primary text or period-specific table cannot be read. Record it under the owning Guide's Not yet confirmed line and the PR's omissions. Refer disputed facts, treaty-country matrices, special regimes, provincial/municipal matters and unsupported historical periods to their own current official texts and qualified professional.

The former file contained Vietnam repositories and Vietnamese PIT legislation. Those are unrelated to Argentina and are removed, not translated into Argentine rules. This file makes no compatibility, licensing or tax-accuracy claim about those repositories. Repository licensing is preserved in frontmatter and does not itself establish tax accuracy.
