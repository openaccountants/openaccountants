# Laos Company Formation & Entity Choice v0.1

> **General reference only.** This skill is general tax/accounting reference material for AI-assisted workflows. It has not been reviewed for any specific person's facts, documents, elections, deadlines, residency, filing status, or local procedures. Do not rely on it to file, pay, amend, or take a tax position without review by a qualified professional in the relevant jurisdiction.

Written by Codex. Not reviewed by an accountant.

## Scope

Use this skill to select the company form and prepare a registration and capital-evidence brief for a Laos business in 2026. It does not establish that a particular foreign investment, sector, branch or concession is permitted. Separate legal registration, operating permission and actual capital contribution.

**Quick reference:** an ordinary limited company normally has two to thirty shareholders; a sole limited company has one; a public company has at least three founding shareholders and an auditor from registration. The ordinary-company exception for more than thirty requires the special resolution in Article 90, not the old fifty-shareholder shortcut. [Enterprise Law 33/NA, Articles 3, 90, 171 and 180](https://laoofficialgazette.gov.la/kcfinder/upload/files/33%E0%BA%AA%E0%BA%9E%E0%BA%8A2022.pdf).

## Ask the client first

- How many actual shareholders, of what nationality and legal form, and is a public offer intended?
- What exact activities, location, controlled-sector status, SEZ status and government concession apply?
- What registered capital, share subscriptions, cash, owned assets and enterprise borrowing are proposed?
- Is foreign capital coming from abroad or from documented Lao-source funds, and when were the registration and investment/operating permissions issued?
- What name, office, managers/directors, ownership documents and authorizations are available?
- Is this a new registration, existing entity change or older entity subject to transitional contribution rules?

## The method, step by step

### Choose the legal form [https://laoofficialgazette.gov.la/kcfinder/upload/files/33%E0%BA%AA%E0%BA%9E%E0%BA%8A2022.pdf](https://laoofficialgazette.gov.la/kcfinder/upload/files/33%E0%BA%AA%E0%BA%9E%E0%BA%8A2022.pdf)

| Option | Conditions and consequence | Official source |
|---|---|---|
| Ordinary limited company | Normally 2 to 30 shareholders. More than 30 can retain the form only through the Article 90 special-resolution route; otherwise convert or dissolve. If only one remains, change to a sole limited company or dissolve. No public solicitation of subscriptions under Article 91. | Enterprise Law Articles 3(7), 90 and 91: https://laoofficialgazette.gov.la/kcfinder/upload/files/33%E0%BA%AA%E0%BA%9E%E0%BA%8A2022.pdf |
| Sole limited company | One individual or legal-entity founder. If ownership becomes plural, change to ordinary limited-company form or dissolve. | Articles 171 and 175: https://laoofficialgazette.gov.la/kcfinder/upload/files/33%E0%BA%AA%E0%BA%9E%E0%BA%8A2022.pdf |
| Public company | At least 3 founding shareholders and an auditor from registration; the public-offer and securities rules apply. This is not an ordinary limited company with a public advertisement. | Articles 180 and 183: https://laoofficialgazette.gov.la/kcfinder/upload/files/33%E0%BA%AA%E0%BA%9E%E0%BA%8A2022.pdf |

A company is a separate legal person; its branch is not. Shareholders' company-debt liability under Articles 3(6) and 83 is limited to the unpaid portion of their subscribed shares; that does not erase their separate guarantees or wrongdoing. Ordinary-company directors may number one or more as the bylaws/meeting determine (Article 123). The old public-company director count is not cleared as a current securities-law instruction. [Enterprise Law Articles 21, 83, 84, 123 and 188](https://laoofficialgazette.gov.la/kcfinder/upload/files/33%E0%BA%AA%E0%BA%9E%E0%BA%8A2022.pdf).

### Establish real capital and its permitted composition [https://laoofficialgazette.gov.la/kcfinder/upload/files/2025.9.9.2025.pdf](https://laoofficialgazette.gov.la/kcfinder/upload/files/2025.9.9.2025.pdf)

Do not issue a blanket “foreign investors have no minimum capital” conclusion. General-investment capital follows the Enterprise Law and applicable sector law; necessary sector minima can be set with government approval. Concessions have their own minimum-capital rules. [Enterprise Law Article 20](https://laoofficialgazette.gov.la/kcfinder/upload/files/33%E0%BA%AA%E0%BA%9E%E0%BA%8A2022.pdf); [Investment Promotion Law 62/NA Articles 51 and 52](https://laoofficialgazette.gov.la/kcfinder/upload/files/62%20%E0%BA%81%E0%BA%BB%E0%BA%94%E0%BB%9D%E0%BA%B2%E0%BA%8D%20%E0%BA%A7%E0%BB%88%E0%BA%B2%E0%BA%94%E0%BB%89%E0%BA%A7%E0%BA%8D%E0%BA%81%E0%BA%B2%E0%BA%99%E0%BA%AA%E0%BA%BB%E0%BB%88%E0%BA%87%E0%BB%80%E0%BA%AA%E0%BA%B5%E0%BA%A1%E0%BA%81%E0%BA%B2%E0%BA%99%E0%BA%A5%E0%BA%BB%E0%BA%87%E0%BA%97%E0%BA%B6%E0%BA%99%20%E0%BA%AA%E0%BA%B0%E0%BA%9A%E0%BA%B1%E0%BA%9A%E0%BA%9B%E0%BA%B1%E0%BA%9A%E0%BA%9B%E0%BA%B8%E0%BA%87%2028-06-2024.pdf).

Registered capital must actually exist and be contributed. Enterprise borrowing in the enterprise's own name cannot be turned into its registered capital. Under the current contribution decision, cash is transferred into the enterprise's Lao commercial-bank account; non-domiciled foreign owners transfer from abroad and obtain the Bank of the Lao PDR capital-import confirmation. The separate domiciled-foreign-owner route requires documented local funds and bank confirmation, not a nationality-based assumption. [Decision 2025/IC of 9 September 2025, Articles 2, 4 and 7](https://laoofficialgazette.gov.la/kcfinder/upload/files/2025.9.9.2025.pdf).

| Contribution amount or deadline | Condition for a new entity | Official source |
|---|---|---|
| Full contribution within 1 year after enterprise registration | General rule; another applicable law or regulation can provide otherwise. It applies across the forms specified in Articles 4 and 5. | Decision 2025/IC Article 6: https://laoofficialgazette.gov.la/kcfinder/upload/files/2025.9.9.2025.pdf |
| In-kind contribution at most 50% of registered capital | Value the owner's assets in money, transfer ownership to the enterprise and obtain applicable foreign-capital confirmation; exception only if another applicable law/regulation provides otherwise. | Decision Article 8: https://laoofficialgazette.gov.la/kcfinder/upload/files/2025.9.9.2025.pdf |
| Foreign general investment: at least 30% imported within 90 days of operating/investment permission; remainder within 1 year | Separate investment-law obligation, unless relevant law provides otherwise. Registration and permission dates are different triggers; do not replace the earlier applicable obligation with the later one. | Investment Promotion Law Article 53: https://laoofficialgazette.gov.la/kcfinder/upload/files/62%20%E0%BA%81%E0%BA%BB%E0%BA%94%E0%BB%9D%E0%BA%B2%E0%BA%8D%20%E0%BA%A7%E0%BB%88%E0%BA%B2%E0%BA%94%E0%BB%89%E0%BA%A7%E0%BA%8D%E0%BA%81%E0%BA%B2%E0%BA%99%E0%BA%AA%E0%BA%BB%E0%BB%88%E0%BA%87%E0%BB%80%E0%BA%AA%E0%BA%B5%E0%BA%A1%E0%BA%81%E0%BA%B2%E0%BA%99%E0%BA%A5%E0%BA%BB%E0%BA%87%E0%BA%97%E0%BA%B6%E0%BA%99%20%E0%BA%AA%E0%BA%B0%E0%BA%9A%E0%BA%B1%E0%BA%9A%E0%BA%9B%E0%BA%B1%E0%BA%9A%E0%BA%9B%E0%BA%B8%E0%BA%87%2028-06-2024.pdf |

Ordinary-company share contributions follow the shareholders' agreed call, subject to the current contribution deadline and applicable investment/sector law. The law allows a debt set-off only with the required special resolution; that is not permission to label an enterprise's own borrowed money as capital. Sole-company Article 173 says contribution **after registration** in the native Lao text; the unofficial English rendering that says “before” is not used here. [Enterprise Law Articles 101 and 173](https://laoofficialgazette.gov.la/kcfinder/upload/files/33%E0%BA%AA%E0%BA%9E%E0%BA%8A2022.pdf); [Decision Articles 2 and 6](https://laoofficialgazette.gov.la/kcfinder/upload/files/2025.9.9.2025.pdf).

### Prepare and submit the registration dossier [https://laoofficialgazette.gov.la/kcfinder/upload/files/33%E0%BA%AA%E0%BA%9E%E0%BA%8A2022.pdf](https://laoofficialgazette.gov.la/kcfinder/upload/files/33%E0%BA%AA%E0%BA%9E%E0%BA%8A2022.pdf)

1. Select a lawful available name and the entity suffix. Earlier registration has name priority; Article 28 prohibits conflicting names, with its individual-enterprise exception. A live name-reservation form and reservation-validity period have not been confirmed; do not invent them. [Articles 21, 27 and 28](https://laoofficialgazette.gov.la/kcfinder/upload/files/33%E0%BA%AA%E0%BA%9E%E0%BA%8A2022.pdf).
2. For an ordinary limited company, prepare the written formation contract and bylaws, subscribe all shares without a public offer, hold the formation meeting, elect the first directors and submit the registration. Article 91 normally requires submission within 30 days after that meeting closes, unless otherwise agreed. Article 95 requires the formation report and subscriber details 10 working days before the meeting. Apply Article 96's voting and conflict-of-interest conditions. [Articles 86, 87 and 91–97](https://laoofficialgazette.gov.la/kcfinder/upload/files/33%E0%BA%AA%E0%BA%9E%E0%BA%8A2022.pdf).
3. The ordinary dossier includes the prescribed application, formation contract, shareholder/director identity documents, legal-entity shareholder consent, authorization and other necessary documents (Article 97). The sole-company dossier uses its prescribed application, owner/manager identity documents, legal-entity consent where relevant and authorization (Article 172); the sole-company exception to a formation contract does not remove its bylaws (Articles 86 and 176). [Enterprise Law](https://laoofficialgazette.gov.la/kcfinder/upload/files/33%E0%BA%AA%E0%BA%9E%E0%BA%8A2022.pdf).
4. Submit to the competent industry-and-commerce registrar directly or through the permitted electronic channel. The statutory certificate consideration period is 3 working days after a correct, complete application, not a guaranteed whole-project timetable. Keep the dated receipt and certificate. The certificate includes the enterprise identifier and TIN, so inspect it before assuming a separate TIN is absent. [Articles 15–18](https://laoofficialgazette.gov.la/kcfinder/upload/files/33%E0%BA%AA%E0%BA%9E%E0%BA%8A2022.pdf).
5. Obtain the activity-specific investment and/or operating permission before doing the activity. Controlled activities require the investment-permission route; non-controlled activities still require applicable operating permission. [Enterprise Law Article 19](https://laoofficialgazette.gov.la/kcfinder/upload/files/33%E0%BA%AA%E0%BA%9E%E0%BA%8A2022.pdf); [Investment Promotion Law Articles 31–39](https://laoofficialgazette.gov.la/kcfinder/upload/files/62%20%E0%BA%81%E0%BA%BB%E0%BA%94%E0%BB%9D%E0%BA%B2%E0%BA%8D%20%E0%BA%A7%E0%BB%88%E0%BA%B2%E0%BA%94%E0%BB%89%E0%BA%A7%E0%BA%8D%E0%BA%81%E0%BA%B2%E0%BA%99%E0%BA%AA%E0%BA%BB%E0%BB%88%E0%BA%87%E0%BB%80%E0%BA%AA%E0%BA%B5%E0%BA%A1%E0%BA%81%E0%BA%B2%E0%BA%99%E0%BA%A5%E0%BA%BB%E0%BA%87%E0%BA%97%E0%BA%B6%E0%BA%99%20%E0%BA%AA%E0%BA%B0%E0%BA%9A%E0%BA%B1%E0%BA%9A%E0%BA%9B%E0%BA%B1%E0%BA%9A%E0%BA%9B%E0%BA%B8%E0%BA%87%2028-06-2024.pdf).
6. Open the appropriate enterprise account, complete documented contributions, retain valuation/title and capital-import evidence, and issue share evidence. Do not treat issuance of the registration certificate as proof that money or assets were paid. [Decision Articles 7, 8 and 11](https://laoofficialgazette.gov.la/kcfinder/upload/files/2025.9.9.2025.pdf).

## Worked cases

### Case 1: cash and owned assets within the composition limit

Assume a new ordinary limited company, two shareholders, registered capital LAK 100,000,000, owned assets properly valued at LAK 40,000,000 and cash LAK 60,000,000 transferred through the required account. No enterprise borrowing or special sector rule applies. The Article 8 ordinary asset ceiling is LAK 50,000,000, so the composition passes by LAK 10,000,000. This checks composition only; actual payment, title, dates and permits still have to pass. [Decision Articles 2, 6–8](https://laoofficialgazette.gov.la/kcfinder/upload/files/2025.9.9.2025.pdf).

```oa-check
name: New ordinary company contribution composition
inputs:
  registered_capital: 100000000
  assets: 40000000
  cash: 60000000
steps:
  - asset_ceiling: registered_capital * 0.50
  - contribution: assets + cash
  - asset_headroom: asset_ceiling - assets
expected:
  contribution: 100000000
  asset_ceiling: 50000000
  asset_headroom: 10000000
```

### Case 2: decide how to fix excessive assets

For the same permitted activity and ordinary rule, a proposal of LAK 60,000,000 owned assets plus LAK 40,000,000 cash against LAK 100,000,000 capital fails the composition limit. Option A keeps capital unchanged and replaces LAK 10,000,000 assets with cash, leaving LAK 50,000,000 each. Option B retains all assets, increases genuine registered capital to LAK 120,000,000 and cash to LAK 60,000,000, requiring LAK 20,000,000 more cash and the lawful registration change. Neither an artificial valuation nor an enterprise loan cures the failure. For one owner, select the sole-company form; the same contribution-decision conditions still apply. [Decision Articles 2, 4, 6, 8 and 9](https://laoofficialgazette.gov.la/kcfinder/upload/files/2025.9.9.2025.pdf); [Enterprise Law Article 171](https://laoofficialgazette.gov.la/kcfinder/upload/files/33%E0%BA%AA%E0%BA%9E%E0%BA%8A2022.pdf).

```oa-check
name: Decide between replacing assets and increasing genuine capital
inputs:
  capital: 100000000
  assets: 60000000
  cash: 40000000
steps:
  - option_a_assets: capital * 0.50
  - option_a_cash: capital - option_a_assets
  - option_a_extra_cash: option_a_cash - cash
  - option_b_capital: assets / 0.50
  - option_b_cash: option_b_capital - assets
  - option_b_extra_cash: option_b_cash - cash
expected:
  option_a_extra_cash: 10000000
  option_b_capital: 120000000
  option_b_extra_cash: 20000000
```

## When to refuse or refer

Refuse a completed incorporation/legal-permission conclusion where the activity classification, foreign-ownership restriction, contribution origin, capital exception or live dossier is unknown. Do not use the legacy “2–8 weeks” estimate as a commitment. Securities offerings, foreign branches, concessions and SEZ incentives need their own operative instruments. This skill prepares a brief, not a filed application.

## Filing and payment

After formation, load [la-tax-overview](la-tax-overview.md) for the current income-tax and financial-statement calendar and [laos-vat](laos-vat.md) for VAT-system entry and evidence. The old annual-profit-tax “20 January” statement is superseded by the income-tax rules in that overview. A financial-statement deadline does not mean every company has the same audit obligation. The applicable registration form, live electronic fields, fees and acknowledgment/payment procedure remain unconfirmed; do not invent a portal walkthrough.

## Completion checklist

Match the certificate to the lawful name, actual owners, office, managers/directors, registered capital and TIN. Reconcile subscriptions to the account and asset/title documents, each applicable clock, foreign-capital confirmation and operating permission. Record unresolved sector/branch/SEZ issues explicitly and obtain qualified professional review before registering or operating.
