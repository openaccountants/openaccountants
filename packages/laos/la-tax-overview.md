# Laos Tax Overview v0.2

> **General reference only.** This skill is general tax/accounting reference material for AI-assisted workflows. It has not been reviewed for any specific person's facts, documents, elections, deadlines, residency, filing status, or local procedures. Do not rely on it to file, pay, amend, or take a tax position without review by a qualified professional in the relevant jurisdiction.

Written by Codex. Not reviewed by an accountant.

## Scope

Use this skill for a 2026 Laos income-tax scoping and computation brief. Income Tax Law 88/NA of 25 June 2025 replaces the old 2019 law. Its Article 74 states 1 September 2025 commencement after promulgation and Gazette publication, while the Gazette record shows publication on 19 June 2026. Do not resolve that timing difference by silently applying the new rules to pre-publication transactions or returns; those periods need specific operative confirmation. The calculations below assume the revised law applies. The Law on Making Legislation, Articles 107–110, governs publication, effectiveness and retroactivity. [Law on Making Legislation](https://laoofficialgazette.gov.la/index.php?id=2375&r=site%2Fdisplay). [Native law, Article 74](https://laoofficialgazette.gov.la/kcfinder/upload/files/88-25-6-2025_0001.pdf); [Gazette record](https://laoofficialgazette.gov.la/index.php?r=site%2Fsearchtip&s=%E0%BA%AD%E0%BA%B2%E0%BA%81%E0%BA%AD%E0%BA%99%E0%BA%A5%E0%BA%B2%E0%BA%8D%E0%BB%84%E0%BA%94%E0%BB%89).

**Quick reference:** ordinary profit tax is 20% of the established taxable profit, not automatically 20% of bank receipts; the salary schedule reaches 25%, but that is not a flat payroll rate. [Income Tax Law Articles 13–16 and 38](https://laoofficialgazette.gov.la/kcfinder/upload/files/88-25-6-2025_0001.pdf). VAT has its own supply, registration, credit and filing rules in [laos-vat](laos-vat.md); do not treat an income-tax conclusion as VAT classification.

## Ask the client first

- Which income dates and accounting period, including any Ministry-approved non-calendar close, are involved?
- Is the person an individual, Lao-established entity, non-domiciled enterprise, permanent establishment or unregistered overseas supplier?
- What home, livelihood/business establishment and income-source facts establish domicile and the Lao connection, and is a treaty relevant?
- What activity, investment holiday, special sector, listed status, micro-business regime or multinational-group status applies?
- Are complete compliant accounts, tax adjustments, supporting documents and prior instalments available?
- Is the payment salary, service revenue, dividend, rent or another category, and who must withhold?

## The method, step by step

### Determine the income and taxpayer [https://laoofficialgazette.gov.la/kcfinder/upload/files/88-25-6-2025_0001.pdf](https://laoofficialgazette.gov.la/kcfinder/upload/files/88-25-6-2025_0001.pdf)

The native law's domicile definition looks to a permanent address and residence/livelihood/business in Laos; non-domicile includes absence of a Lao residence or establishment. Do not use the legacy presence-only “183 days in twelve months” statement as a universal residence test. The law covers Lao-established businesses and specified non-domiciled earners, including Lao revenue without a Lao entity. Establish the actual permanent establishment, source and treaty facts before choosing a base; “domestic worldwide, foreign territorial” alone is insufficient. [Articles 2, 3, 7, 8 and 12–13](https://laoofficialgazette.gov.la/kcfinder/upload/files/88-25-6-2025_0001.pdf).

### Establish the base, then choose the applicable rate [https://laoofficialgazette.gov.la/kcfinder/upload/files/88-25-6-2025_0001.pdf](https://laoofficialgazette.gov.la/kcfinder/upload/files/88-25-6-2025_0001.pdf)

| Item | 2026 condition and amount | Official source |
|---|---|---|
| Ordinary profit-tax rate | 20% of actual or applicable deemed taxable profit; special activities, investment relief and multinational minimum-tax rules must be checked first. | Income Tax Law Articles 15–16: https://laoofficialgazette.gov.la/kcfinder/upload/files/88-25-6-2025_0001.pdf |
| Ordinary actual-profit base | Proper compliant accounts: annual taxable revenue less allowable expenses, or accounting profit reconciled for statutory adjustments. Do not assume every bookkeeping expense is deductible. | Articles 13–14: https://laoofficialgazette.gov.la/kcfinder/upload/files/88-25-6-2025_0001.pdf |
| Compulsory deemed-profit base for ordinary trade/services | 15% of revenue is the deemed profit; the applicable profit-tax rate is then applied. This includes the relevant no-Lao-entity/no-PE foreign supplier branch, not every foreign business. Other activity ratios differ. | Articles 12–16: https://laoofficialgazette.gov.la/kcfinder/upload/files/88-25-6-2025_0001.pdf |
| Salary rate ceiling | Progressive schedule reaches 25%; it does not tax the whole salary at that rate. The schedule's monetary bands can change with the government minimum wage. | Article 38: https://laoofficialgazette.gov.la/kcfinder/upload/files/88-25-6-2025_0001.pdf |

Use actual profit where the statutory accounting conditions are met. Compulsory deemed profit also concerns deficient/incomplete accounts or noncooperation; the law limits the Lao-registered deficient-accounting route to the specified period before proper books are required. A nonresident with a Lao permanent establishment starts in the actual-profit branch. Article 13(2.1) requires the compulsory deemed-profit method if failure to cooperate or provide information prevents actual-profit calculation. Separate activity records matter; do not choose a convenient ratio. [Articles 13–14](https://laoofficialgazette.gov.la/kcfinder/upload/files/88-25-6-2025_0001.pdf).

For a payment to an unregistered overseas business in the relevant branch, the Lao payer withholds the profit tax; failing to withhold can make the payer liable. The actual contract, activity, establishment and any treaty determine the treatment. The service ratio is not a universal withholding rate for dividends, royalties, rent, goods or transport. [Articles 12–16, 20 and 22](https://laoofficialgazette.gov.la/kcfinder/upload/files/88-25-6-2025_0001.pdf).

### Apply the correct calendar [https://laoofficialgazette.gov.la/kcfinder/upload/files/88-25-6-2025_0001.pdf](https://laoofficialgazette.gov.la/kcfinder/upload/files/88-25-6-2025_0001.pdf)

| Obligation | Statutory trigger and time | Official source |
|---|---|---|
| Standard business accounting close | 31 December; approved non-calendar periods and cessation/transfer cases differ. | Article 24: https://laoofficialgazette.gov.la/kcfinder/upload/files/88-25-6-2025_0001.pdf |
| Annual profit declaration, proper-accounting branch | By 31 March following the year. A Ministry-approved non-calendar period uses 3 months after its close. | Article 20: https://laoofficialgazette.gov.la/kcfinder/upload/files/88-25-6-2025_0001.pdf |
| Annual financial statements and tax reconciliation/appendices | Normally by 31 March following the year; listed companies by 30 April; approved non-calendar periods use 3 months after close. Branch/group reporting conditions also apply. | Article 21: https://laoofficialgazette.gov.la/kcfinder/upload/files/88-25-6-2025_0001.pdf |
| Ordinary actual-accounting profit-tax instalments | Twice in the current year, by 20 July and 31 December; based on the specified actual/estimated/prior-year information, then annual reconciliation. | Article 22(1): https://laoofficialgazette.gov.la/kcfinder/upload/files/88-25-6-2025_0001.pdf |
| Relevant nonresident business payment | Profit-tax declaration within 15 days of payment; remit within 15 days from the applicable withholding or supplier payment trigger in Article 22(3). Do not substitute the annual return clock. | Articles 20(2) and 22(3): https://laoofficialgazette.gov.la/kcfinder/upload/files/88-25-6-2025_0001.pdf |
| Employer salary withholding/report | Withhold and report ordinary domiciled salary by the 20th of the following month. For non-domiciled pay, apply the distinct Article 49 clock. | Articles 35, 39 and 49: https://laoofficialgazette.gov.la/kcfinder/upload/files/88-25-6-2025_0001.pdf |

The law uses Lao kip in its monetary schedule; retain LAK amounts and source documents. The law identifies the tax administration for declarations, financial-statement reconciliation and salary reports. This is not a social-security calculation. [Articles 20–22, 38–39](https://laoofficialgazette.gov.la/kcfinder/upload/files/88-25-6-2025_0001.pdf).

## Worked cases

### Case 1: ordinary company after the new law's publication

Assume post-publication 2026 ordinary trading activity, proper accounts and an already reconciled taxable profit of LAK 50,000,000. No special-sector rate, investment holiday, listed-company relief or multinational adjustment applies. Profit tax is LAK 10,000,000. This does not establish which accounting expenses are allowed or the final balance after instalments. [Articles 13–16](https://laoofficialgazette.gov.la/kcfinder/upload/files/88-25-6-2025_0001.pdf).

```oa-check
name: Ordinary reconciled taxable profit
inputs:
  taxable_profit: 50000000
steps:
  - profit_tax: taxable_profit * 0.20
expected:
  profit_tax: 10000000
```

### Case 2: choose the correct nonresident service base

Assume a post-publication 2026 LAK 100,000,000 payment for ordinary services to a non-domiciled overseas business with no Lao entity and no Lao permanent establishment, within Articles 12–14's compulsory deemed-profit branch. There is no special activity or treaty reduction. The deemed profit is LAK 15,000,000 and ordinary profit tax withheld by the Lao payer is LAK 3,000,000. Taking 20% of gross would produce LAK 20,000,000 and overstate this particular profit tax by LAK 17,000,000. If the supplier has a Lao permanent establishment, check whether actual profit can be calculated with the required records. Apply Article 13(2.1)'s compulsory deemed-profit branch if noncooperation or missing information prevents that calculation. VAT is a separate analysis and is not included in these figures. [Articles 12–16, 20 and 22](https://laoofficialgazette.gov.la/kcfinder/upload/files/88-25-6-2025_0001.pdf).

```oa-check
name: Decision on no-PE overseas ordinary service profit base
inputs:
  service_revenue: 100000000
steps:
  - deemed_profit: service_revenue * 0.15
  - withheld_profit_tax: deemed_profit * 0.20
  - incorrect_gross_tax: service_revenue * 0.20
  - overstatement: incorrect_gross_tax - withheld_profit_tax
expected:
  deemed_profit: 15000000
  withheld_profit_tax: 3000000
  overstatement: 17000000
```

## When to refuse or refer

Do not give a filing-ready income-tax result without the income classification, taxable-base evidence, applicable period and rate exceptions. Refer pre-publication timing disputes, residence/treaty/PE questions, special-income categories, micro regimes, incentives and minimum-tax cases for their specific legal treatment. Do not invent current payroll bands or social-security contributions from a top rate.

## Filing and payment

Prepare a working paper identifying the taxpayer/TIN, period, actual or deemed branch, tax adjustments, tax, withholding and instalment credits. The annual packet includes financial statements, reconciliation and the statutory appendices; listed/non-calendar cases need the correct separate clock. The current electronic declaration screen, form/box identifiers, acceptance receipt, treasury payment reference and late-interest/penalty procedure have not been read in operative current instructions. Do not fabricate them or call the packet submitted. [Articles 20–22](https://laoofficialgazette.gov.la/kcfinder/upload/files/88-25-6-2025_0001.pdf).

## Completion checklist

Separate profit tax, salary withholding, other income tax, VAT and social security. Show the source next to each rate and deadline, reconcile instalments/withholding, retain the unknowns, and obtain qualified professional review before any filing or payment.
