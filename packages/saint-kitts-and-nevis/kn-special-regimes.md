# Saint Kitts and Nevis special regimes

> **General reference only.** This skill is general tax/accounting reference material for AI-assisted workflows. It has not been reviewed for any specific person's facts, documents, elections, deadlines, residency, filing status, or local procedures. Do not rely on it to file, pay, amend, or take a tax position without review by a qualified professional in the relevant jurisdiction.

## Scope

Regimes that change a business's obligations in Saint Kitts and Nevis in 2026, beyond the ordinary taxes: virtual asset businesses, investment and sector concessions, the former Nevis offshore exemptions and Nevis entity records, and gaming. Amounts are EC$. Ordinary corporation tax: [kn-corporate-income-tax](kn-corporate-income-tax.md). Forming the entity: [kn-company-formation](kn-company-formation.md).

## Ask the client first

1. What does the business do, for whom, and from where (in or from the Federation, or offshore serving residents)?
2. Which grant, licence or registration does it hold (virtual asset registration, approved enterprise order, small business status, hotel approval, zone agreement, gaming licence)?
3. For a Nevis IBC or LLC: when was it formed, and has it acquired new assets or started new activities since?
4. Client funds held (virtual assets) and the financial year end.
5. Any change in owners, directors or senior management (gaming, virtual assets)?

### Who is in and who is out

**Virtual asset businesses.** The Act covers, for another person: fiat or virtual asset exchange, transfers, custody or administration, and financial services for issuance or sale, offered to residents or from the Federation (ss.2 to 4; s.4 replaced in March 2021). Holding for one's own account is outside; digital fiat and securities are excluded from "virtual asset". An offshore provider serving residents must settle its position before starting. Applications use the Schedule to the Virtual Asset Business Regulations, SRO 13 of 2026 (published 22 June 2026, replacing the 2022 forms): ownership and fitness evidence, constituting documents, business plan, capital, liquidity, resources and controls (reg.3). The Authority decides within 90 days of a complete application; answer requests or ask for more time within 15 days. The June 2026 amendments (Act 8 of 2026) add senior management, beneficial owner fitness and proliferation financing controls; since June 2024 a significant shareholder includes holdings or votes above 10%. An offshore registrant keeps an ordinarily resident principal representative (s.9(9)). Records are kept 7 years from when obtained or produced (reg.27). Act 8 of 2026 also adds an independent test of operations and financial crime controls (s.9(11)) and lets the Authority appoint an external auditor at the registrant's cost. Public issues: prospectus rules in ss.10 to 12.

**Approved manufacturing (Fiscal Incentives Act, Cap. 20.14).** An approved enterprise may get full or partial income tax exemption from the production day for the period in its order, within the Third Schedule limit; apportion a period spanning expiry; separate import licence and duty relief with use and disposal limits. Act 13 of 2019 (published 30 December 2019) revised the enclave definition, applied Income Tax Act s.4A and ended the export allowance after 31 December 2022.

**Small businesses.** St Kitts: apply to the Small Business Development Centre for Approved Small Business status and the specific concession (checks with IRD, Social Security and the electricity company; 2022 amendments). Nevis: declaration and register under Cap. 6.14(N); a conditional declaration before incorporation needs incorporation within 3 months.

**Hotel projects.** Construction and import concessions under the Hotels Aid Act and the project approval; check income tax, customs duty, Customs Service Charge and VAT separately.

**Special Sustainability Zones (Act 21 of 2025, published 25 August 2025).** Development agreement and Gazette declaration with an area and effective date; s.7 requires legislative ratification (and Nevis Assembly ratification for an area in Nevis); concessions only as granted, from the s.8 date. The proposed Destiny project in Nevis was under review in July 2026.

**Nevis entity records.** Business corporations (s.101) and LLCs (s.67A, 2023 amendments): where a register copy is at the agent's office, tell the agent of changes within 15 days and record where the original is; tell the agent within 15 days if the original moves; records at the agent's office are kept 6 years after the entity ends. The November 2025 amendments add name, status, office, agent and governing-document records. The LLC exemption ended on the same terms (Cap. 7.04(N) s.96).

**Gaming.** Federal Gaming Control Act 2021: keep records (s.36); 21-day change notices. Nevis Online Gaming Ordinance 2 of 2025: separate authority; regulations (SRO 5 of 2025) in effect from 1 July 2025 with fees and application evidence; keep financial records 5 years after the relationship ends; file financial statements within 3 months of year end.

## Worked cases

### Case 1: a virtual asset provider holding EC$200,000 of client funds

Escrow 30,000; first-year application and registration fees 189,000.

```oa-check
name: St Kitts virtual asset escrow on 200,000 and first fees
inputs:
  client_funds: 200000
steps:
  - escrow: client_funds * 0.15
  - first_fees: 54000 + 135000
expected:
  escrow: 30000
  first_fees: 189000
```

### Case 2 (decision): renew on time, or 20 days late?

Renewing by 31 January costs the 135,000 fee. Paying on 20 February adds 20 days at 100, 2,000. Unpaid three months after the due date, the registration lapses.

```oa-check
name: St Kitts virtual asset renewal 20 days late
inputs:
  fee: 135000
  days_late: 20
steps:
  - late_fee: days_late * 100
  - total: fee + late_fee
expected:
  late_fee: 2000
  total: 137000
```
