# Georgia tax overview v1.1

> **General reference only.** This Guide is general tax/accounting reference material for AI-assisted workflows. It has not been reviewed for any specific person's facts, documents, elections, deadlines, residency, filing status, or local procedures. Do not rely on it to file, pay, amend, or take a tax position without review by a qualified professional in the relevant jurisdiction.

Georgia (the country in the South Caucasus, not the US state) runs its tax system under one Tax Code, collected by the Revenue Service. The ordinary personal income tax rate is 20%, with statutory exceptions. Ordinary company profit tax covers distributions and other statutory payments, not just declared dividends. The standard VAT rate is 18%. The following is a routing and headline reference, not a full return computation. The current consolidated Code, including the 28 October 2026 publication, was checked on 16 November 2026; Georgian wording controls over English translation differences.

In effect, residents are taxed on Georgian-source income only _(Tax Code art. 82(1)(ფ), shown as (u) in the English translation)_. Non-residents are taxed on Georgian-source income _(Tax Code art. 80(2), (3))_ | https://matsne.gov.ge/ka/document/view/1043717 |
| Headline personal income tax rate | 20% flat _(Tax Code art. 81(1))_. Rent of residential space for living, with no deductions claimed: 5% _(art. 81(2))_. Detail: [georgia-income-tax](georgia-income-tax.md). | https://matsne.gov.ge/ka/document/view/1043717 |
| Small business status | an individual entrepreneur pays 1% of taxable income, which is Georgian-source income other than salary and the excluded income types _(Tax Code art. 90(1), (3))_. Once gross income for the year passes GEL 500,000 (GEL 700,000 for wine and agro tourism), the rate is 3% from the start of the month the limit is passed to the end of the year _(art. 90(2))_. A monthly return and payment are due by the 15th _(art. 93(1^1))_. Detail: [georgia-tax-optimization](georgia-tax-optimization.md). | https://matsne.gov.ge/ka/document/view/1043717 |
| Micro business status | an individual with no employees and gross income up to GEL 30,000 a year pays no income tax _(Tax Code arts. 84(1), 86)_ | https://matsne.gov.ge/ka/document/view/1043717 |
| Profit tax (companies) | 15% _(Tax Code art. 98(1))_. It is charged on distributed profit, costs unrelated to the business, free supplies and entertainment over the cap, each divided by 0.85 _(art. 97(1), (10))_. Merely retaining ordinary trading profit is not a tax trigger; nonbusiness payments, deemed distributions and the other article 97 triggers can still be taxable. Banks, credit unions, microfinance organisations and loan providers pay 20% on annual profit _(arts. 97(12), 98(4))_. Detail: [ge-corporate-income-tax](ge-corporate-income-tax.md). | https://matsne.gov.ge/ka/document/view/1043717 |
| Withholding on dividends and interest | generally 5% for the recipient/payment classes in articles 130(1) and 131(1), subject to their exemptions. Domestic enterprise dividends, licensed-financial-institution interest and the other statutory exceptions require a separate check. The article 134(1)(e) residual Georgian-source nonresident-payment rate is 10%; the preferential-tax-country rate is 15% specifically for interest, royalties and residual other payments under article 134(1)(b), (b1), (e), not every payment. Check the payee, source, permanent-establishment attribution and treaty relief _(arts. 130, 131, 134)_ | https://matsne.gov.ge/ka/document/view/1043717 |
| VAT | standard rate 18% _(Tax Code art. 166)_. Registration is required within 2 business days of exceeding GEL 100,000 of taxable supplies in any 12 consecutive months _(art. 165(1))_. Detail: [georgia-vat](georgia-vat.md). | https://matsne.gov.ge/ka/document/view/1043717 |
| VAT filing | the tax period is a calendar month; the return and payment are due by the 15th of the following month _(Tax Code arts. 167, 168(1))_ | https://matsne.gov.ge/ka/document/view/1043717 |
| Profit tax filing | a monthly return by the 15th of the following month _(Tax Code art. 153(10))_. The tax is paid by the same date _(art. 62(2))_ | https://matsne.gov.ge/ka/document/view/1043717 |
| Payroll | employers withhold income tax and file a monthly return on salaries paid and tax withheld by the 15th of the following month _(Tax Code art. 153(5))_. For cash salary, withheld tax goes to the budget when paid; for a nonmonetary payment, remittance is due on the last day of the relevant month _(art. 154(3)(a))_. Detail: [georgia-payroll](georgia-payroll.md); funded pension contributions: [georgia-social-contributions](georgia-social-contributions.md). | https://matsne.gov.ge/ka/document/view/1043717 |
| Annual income tax return | residents whose income was not taxed at source, and others outside the monthly profit tax regime, file before 1 April of the following year (Georgian text: "1 აპრილამდე", so by 31 March) _(Tax Code art. 153(1))_ | https://matsne.gov.ge/ka/document/view/1043717 |
| Current (advance) income and profit tax payments | for calendar-year enterprises and entrepreneur natural persons to whom article 155 applies, 25% of last year's tax each by 15 May, 15 July, 15 September and 15 December _(Tax Code art. 155(1))_. Micro/small businesses do not pay these current advances under article 94(1); check article 155 exceptions or properly notified reductions separately | https://matsne.gov.ge/ka/document/view/1043717 |
| Property tax (companies) | up to 1% a year under the ordinary rule, set locally. The base is the statutory average annual net book value, with article 202 adjustments, revaluation exceptions and separate leasing rules; do not multiply unadjusted book value automatically _(Tax Code arts. 6(6), 202)_ | https://matsne.gov.ge/ka/document/view/1043717 |

## Ask the client first

- Is this Georgia the country, and which tax year, entity and special-status certificate apply?
- What residence, source-of-income and permanent-establishment facts apply, including work location and travel days?
- What are business income, deductible costs, distributions and other payments, and who receives them?
- What are taxable supplies for every rolling 12-month window and the place of supply?
- Which employees, pension participants, property, registrations and filed returns exist?

## Worked example that recomputes

Assume an ordinary article 97 company makes a GEL 85 distribution before dividend withholding to an individual, with no exemption or treaty reduction. The profit-tax base is 85 / 0.85 = GEL 100, profit tax GEL 15 and dividend withholding GEL 4.25. The shareholder receives GEL 80.75. Total tax on the GEL 100 pre-profit-tax amount is GEL 19.25. This illustration does not determine entity or recipient eligibility. [Tax Code arts. 97(10), 98(1), 130(1)](https://matsne.gov.ge/ka/document/view/1043717).

```oa-check
name: Georgia ordinary distribution and individual dividend
inputs:
  distribution_before_withholding: 85
  profit_gross_up: 0.85
  profit_rate: 0.15
  dividend_rate: 0.05
steps:
  profit_tax_base: distribution_before_withholding / profit_gross_up
  profit_tax: profit_tax_base * profit_rate
  dividend_withholding: distribution_before_withholding * dividend_rate
  shareholder_cash: distribution_before_withholding - dividend_withholding
  total_tax: profit_tax + dividend_withholding
expected:
  profit_tax_base: 100
  profit_tax: 15
  dividend_withholding: 4.25
  shareholder_cash: 80.75
  total_tax: 19.25
```
