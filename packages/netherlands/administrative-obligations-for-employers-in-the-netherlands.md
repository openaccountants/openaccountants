# Administrative obligations for employers in the Netherlands — 2026

## Ask the client first

Tax year 2026. Use this method for employer registration, onboarding, payroll records, payslips, returns, corrections, annual statements and retention in the European Netherlands. It is an administrative workflow, not a substitute for the employee-specific tax calculation. Identify the actual employer and each employment relationship; a director, contractor, agency worker and ordinary employee do not automatically have the same status. Refer uncertain employment status, cross-border taxation, social insurance and permission to work before finalising payroll. [Payroll Handbook, chapters one and two](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t61fd.pdf)

- Obtain the legal entity, payroll tax number and first employment date.
- Obtain the return invitation, deadlines, sector/Whk notices, opening balances and earlier returns/corrections.
- Obtain employee contracts, collective agreements, identity evidence and employee data statements.
- Establish residence and work locations; obtain wage and hours records, benefits, reimbursements, pension arrangements and relevant decisions.
- Identify missing evidence and who approves payroll and payment.

The official download page listed the **March 2026 Payroll Handbook** when checked. Read later official updates as well as the handbook; a handbook's publication year does not prove that every figure remains current. For detailed wage-tax, employee-insurance, Zvw, WKR and company-car calculations use the applicable current payroll guidance and recorded decisions. [Current handbook editions](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/themaoverstijgend/brochures_en_publicaties/handboek-loonheffingen)

## The method, step by step

1. Establish the withholding entity and register or notify changes.
2. Complete identity, employee-data and cross-border checks before work begins.
3. Set up payroll records and income-relationship identifiers from evidence.
4. Reconcile each payroll, issue the required payslip and approve payments.
5. File the return, check acceptance and separately confirm payment.
6. Correct errors using the route for the year, deadline and software.
7. Deliver annual information and retain accessible records securely.

The checks below explain these steps. Reconciliation and access-control suggestions are operational controls, not invented statutory forms.

## Register and keep the employer position current

Register once the first employee's start date is known, no later than that first working day. Use the Dutch-employer registration form or the foreign-enterprise form according to the employer's establishment. Retain the payroll tax number and annual return invitation. Where employee-insurance contributions apply, obtain the sector and differentiated Whk information. If already registered without employee-insurance contributions, notify the authority when first employing someone for whom these premiums become due. Do not assume hiring the first non-director is necessarily the first time a BV has payroll obligations. [Employer registration](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/personeel-en-loon/content/aanmelden-als-werkgever)

A change of legal form, transfer of activities, merger, demerger or cessation needs an actual notification review. A change of legal form can require a new payroll tax number. For a qualifying transfer of undertaking, the transferring and receiving employers notify the transfer; assess the actual effective date and implications for sector/Whk and income relationships. Do not simply copy the old employer's identifiers into the new entity. Obtain advice for the legal transfer and insurance consequences. [Handbook, employer changes and transfer sections](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t61fd.pdf)

Check employer notices before entering them: correct entity, applicable year, sector, Whk percentage and underlying information. Investigate unexplained attributed benefits or an unexpected percentage. Retain the notice and evidence of any challenge; do not invent a substitute percentage. [Handbook, payroll setup and Whk](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t61fd.pdf)

## Identify and onboard the employee

For current employment, establish identity before the person starts work and retain a readable copy of an acceptable valid identity document with all personal details, including the reverse side where the BSN is printed. A driving licence used for identification elsewhere is not enough for this payroll onboarding check. Keep the distinction between payroll identity verification and workplace identification. The handbook permits adequately controlled remote identity verification, but a casually emailed image alone does not demonstrate the required checks. Retain how identity was established. [Identity verification](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/vraag-en-antwoord/identificatieplicht/wat-moet-ik-als-werkgever-doen-om-te-voldoen-aan-de-identificatieplicht) [Handbook, remote verification](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t61fd.pdf)

For a foreign employee, separately establish permission to work/reside where required, actual tax residence, work locations and applicable social-security legislation. Obtain relevant permits, decisions and an A1 where applicable; an identity card alone does not establish the tax or insurance outcome. Resolve cross-border uncertainty with a specialist rather than infer it from a Dutch postal address. Former-employment payments and officially recognised groups of withholding agents can have different identity/data rules; verify the exception before omitting a check. [Handbook, employee data and international employment](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t61fd.pdf)

Collect name/initials, birth date, BSN, address/postcode/place, and foreign country/region where applicable. Obtain these before the first working day; if the person is hired on that day, obtain them before work begins. The employee's submission must be dated and signed. The official model, a complete own form, or a compliant digital equivalent may be used; electronic signatures need the handbook's authenticity/integrity safeguards. Verify the facts rather than only copying fields. [Handbook, employee data](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t61fd.pdf)

Record the employee's dated, signed request before applying the payroll tax credit, subject to specific statutory exceptions. Explain that applying it concurrently at multiple income sources may produce under-withholding. Determine residence from the actual circumstances: a temporary Dutch address does not establish Dutch residence. Track changes and the effective date. For a missing BSN, the handbook provides for a personnel number in the return until the BSN is supplied; this does not itself waive the anonymous-rate rules. [Handbook, employee data and tax credit](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t61fd.pdf)

Check whether the authority has specifically imposed a first-day notification obligation. It is not a routine additional notification for every employer. [Handbook, first-day notification](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t61fd.pdf)

## Decide the anonymous rate and later correction

The anonymous rate is **52%** wage tax/national insurance. It can apply to missing required name/address/residence/BSN information, deficient identity verification or retention, information known or reasonably recognisable as wrong, and missing required residence/work authorisation. Some conditions specifically concern current employment; former-employment payments have their own data deadline. Under this rate, do not apply the payroll tax credit or the normal employee-insurance/Zvw contribution ceilings. This is not a statement that every payroll charge equals 52%. [Anonymous-rate conditions](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontenten/belastingdienst/business/payroll_taxes/you_are_not_established_in_the_netherlands_are_you_required_to_withhold_payroll_taxes/when_you_are_going_to_withhold_payroll_taxes/obtain_the_data_required_for_the_payroll_taxes/anonymous_rate) [Handbook, anonymous rate](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t61fd.pdf)

**Missing-BSN exception:** a pending application alone is not enough, but the employer may omit the anonymous rate where it can prove the missing BSN is outside the employer's and employee's control, such as municipal processing delay. Keep the evidence; if uncertain, request written agreement from the authority. Other identity/data failures are not cured by this exception. [Official BSN exception](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontenten/belastingdienst/business/payroll_taxes/you_are_not_established_in_the_netherlands_are_you_required_to_withhold_payroll_taxes/when_you_are_going_to_withhold_payroll_taxes/obtain_the_data_required_for_the_payroll_taxes/anonymous_rate)

Distinguish an administrative error despite information received on time from information actually supplied late. Correct an erroneous application as an error. For late complete/correct information, use normal treatment going forward; the authority permits correcting earlier anonymous-rate wage-tax/national-insurance withholding **within the same calendar year** after receipt. Send the relevant correction reports and settle the difference with the employee. Do not extend this late-information concession automatically to an earlier year; assess other payroll components separately. [Handbook, anonymous-rate correction](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t61fd.pdf)

## Build records and supporting files

Create a payroll record (*loonstaat*) annually before the first payment for each employment relationship, not just one combined record per person irrespective of their employments. Apply the handbook's specific exceptions only where established. Where one employment has multiple income relationships, separate records per income relationship are recommended. Keep the income-relationship number stable for that relationship, including through software migration; use a new number for a new relationship and follow the restrictions on reuse. Reconcile any required combined tax calculation separately. [Handbook, payroll records and income relationships](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t61fd.pdf)

Retain the source documents behind the payroll record: contract and changes, credit request, identity/data evidence, A1/permits where relevant, rulings, pension/collective-agreement information, company-car records, expenses and substantiation, WKR classifications, wage attachments, notices, returns, corrections and correspondence. A fixed net allowance is not tax-free merely because software labels it that way. Record its basis and refer unsupported exemption or valuation decisions. [Handbook, general and special administrative duties](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t61fd.pdf)

Each cycle check joiners/leavers, salary and contract changes, paid hours, overtime, bonuses, holiday allowance, reimbursements, pension deductions, company cars, garnishments, sickness and leave. Reconcile payroll totals to employee bank payments, the payroll journal, tax liabilities and returns. Document unresolved differences and approval; software correctness does not establish that the input facts or tax treatment are correct. Preserve the calculations behind the reconciliation. [Handbook, payroll records and return controls](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t61fd.pdf)

## Issue a compliant payslip

Give a payslip for the first payment and whenever a payment differs from the previous one. Issuing one every cycle is a useful control. Include employer/employee names, period, contractual hours, gross money wages and their breakdown, deductions/settlements, the applicable statutory minimum wage information and required written-permanent/on-call contract indicators. Include applicable components such as overtime, holiday allowance, pension, tax, Zvw and wage attachments. Do not present only an unexplained gross-to-net total. [Required payslip information](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontenten/belastingdienst/business/payroll_taxes/you_are_not_established_in_the_netherlands_are_you_required_to_withhold_payroll_taxes/when_you_are_going_to_withhold_payroll_taxes/payroll_records/issuing_pay_slips_to_employees)

Digital delivery requires employee agreement and a form the employee can store and read later, including after leaving. Arrange downloads or retained copies before closing a portal account. An ordinary payslip does not need a BSN merely because payroll records do. **If it also serves as the annual income statement, however, include every mandatory annual field, including the BSN.** Protect it appropriately rather than removing a required annual field. [Payslip rules](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontenten/belastingdienst/business/payroll_taxes/you_are_not_established_in_the_netherlands_are_you_required_to_withhold_payroll_taxes/when_you_are_going_to_withhold_payroll_taxes/payroll_records/issuing_pay_slips_to_employees) [Annual statement fields](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontenten/belastingdienst/business/payroll_taxes/you_are_not_established_in_the_netherlands_are_you_required_to_withhold_payroll_taxes/when_you_are_going_to_withhold_payroll_taxes/payroll_records/issuing_annual_income_statements_to_employees)

## File, confirm acceptance and pay separately

Use the periods and deadlines in the return invitation, generally monthly or four-weekly; do not assume the pay period equals the return period. Include both collective totals and complete required individual income-relationship data. These feed UWV's policy administration, so a wrong number of paid hours can matter even when tax is unchanged. Retain submission and acceptance evidence and resolve rejection messages. [Return procedure](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/personeel-en-loon/content/loonaangifte-aangifte-loonheffingen) [Handbook, payroll returns](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t61fd.pdf)

Continue filing required returns even when there is no payroll. Distinguish a nil return with no personnel from a zero return where personnel remain but no wages were paid, using the authority's rules. Deregistration is a separate process: keep filing until the authority confirms that it has withdrawn the return obligation. [Nil/zero returns and deregistration](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/personeel-en-loon/content/loonaangifte-aangifte-loonheffingen)

Separately verify the correct payment reference, amount and actual receipt by the payment deadline. A successfully submitted return is not payment. Use the current official payment instructions rather than a historic saved bank-account template. Reconcile corrections included with the return to its payment balance. [Handbook, payment and correction balances](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t61fd.pdf)

## Correct the right information through the right route

Record the affected employee/relationship, periods, original and corrected facts, discovery date and filing-deadline status. Correct non-financial employee-data errors as well as tax differences. Recompute any later cumulative calculations affected by the change. [Handbook, corrections](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t61fd.pdf)

| Situation | Route and key control |
|---|---|
| Filing deadline still open | Resend the **complete** return, including unchanged employees and collective totals. A complete replacement overwrites the previous return. |
| Deadline open and software supports a supplementary return | Use the explicit supplementary designation; include full collective totals and complete data for affected/added relationships. The authority's online form does not support this route. |
| Deadline passed; ordinary current-year correction | Include the correction with the next or following return, unless an official correction direction sets the deadline or a standalone route applies. |
| Prior year after its final return deadline, or another qualifying standalone situation | Send a standalone correction; when self-discovered, ordinarily within eight weeks. Check the official instruction for the particular year and filing software. |

For corrections after the deadline, the official online form also requires all employee/income-relationship data, including unchanged records. Commercial software can send only the affected relationships, but each affected record must be complete and the collective totals must cover everyone. For a standalone correction, follow the same software-specific distinction. [Handbook, correction contents](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t61fd.pdf)

A partial employee file accidentally marked as a full return can erase unchanged employee records. Preserve previously submitted correction balances when a replacement return requires them. For standalone corrections, follow the resulting assessment/refund process rather than paying an invented standalone amount. A substantive disagreement with the tax treatment may require objection within **six weeks after payment**; do not assume correcting a clerical error preserves an objection deadline. [Official correction/objection routes](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/personeel-en-loon/content/hoe-maak-ik-bezwaar-tegen-mijn-aangifte-loonheffingen) [Handbook, correction methods and timing](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t61fd.pdf)

## Annual information and year-end controls

After year-end give annual income information without waiting for a request, including to leavers; it may already be issued on departure. Provide it per employment, handling multiple income relationships under the handbook's rules. No universal February deadline is asserted here. Reconcile annual taxable wages, tax withheld, applied employment credit, employee BSN, employee/employer names, employee Zvw withholding, employer Zvw and employee-insurance premiums as applicable. Use the current official model as the completeness checklist. [Handbook, annual statements](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t61fd.pdf)

A final payslip can serve as the annual statement only with all mandatory cumulative annual fields and an explanation identifying those fields. Do not give only the final month's amounts or omit the BSN. At year-end also reconcile cumulative returns, payments, corrections, WKR processing, Whk notices and employee data; prepare the next year's setup from current official information. [Handbook, annual statements and payroll administration](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t61fd.pdf)

## Retain, protect and keep control when outsourcing

Core payroll administration has a **seven-year** tax retention period, including after business closure or the last employee leaves. The starting point depends on the continuing relevance of a record; do not assume every record's clock begins on creation. Specific employee-data statements, identity copies and qualifying employee decisions/declarations have their own minimum: generally **five calendar years after the calendar year employment ends**. Preserve replaced statements as required and check longer duties or a dispute hold before deletion. Do not apply one employee-departure deletion date to every document. [Handbook, record retention](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t61fd.pdf) [Retention clock](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/administratie/)

Keep digital files and the means of reading and checking them, not just printed totals. Before changing provider or software, export records, attachments, income identifiers and audit trails and confirm they remain readable. The employer remains responsible when a provider keeps the files; agree retrieval, retention, exit transfer and incident responsibilities. Check permission where records are kept at a location other than the business premises. [Handbook, storage and service providers](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t61fd.pdf)

Payroll data include sensitive identifiers and financial information. Apply the GDPR/AVG: identify the processing basis, necessary data, retention and permitted recipients. As practical controls, restrict access by role, log access where appropriate, protect transfers and storage, periodically review permissions and document processor arrangements. Do not make every employee's salary and identity file visible to the whole business. Statutory retention does not authorise unrelated disclosure or indefinite retention of every HR note. [KVK privacy guidance](https://www.kvk.nl/wetten-en-regels/privacywetgeving-avg-wanneer-ben-je-compliant/)

## Worked and decision checks

The following are hypothetical checks, not complete payroll calculations or professional attestation.

- **A — Anonymous-rate arithmetic.** For €4,000 of wages subject to 52% withholding, the illustrated wage-tax/national-insurance withholding is €2,080. This does not calculate net pay or other employer charges. [Anonymous rate](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontenten/belastingdienst/business/payroll_taxes/you_are_not_established_in_the_netherlands_are_you_required_to_withhold_payroll_taxes/when_you_are_going_to_withhold_payroll_taxes/obtain_the_data_required_for_the_payroll_taxes/anonymous_rate)
- **B — Municipal BSN delay.** Identity and all other data are valid; the BSN application is delayed by the municipality and the employer retains evidence. Evaluate the documented exception; do not impose 52% merely because the BSN field is empty. [BSN exception](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontenten/belastingdienst/business/payroll_taxes/you_are_not_established_in_the_netherlands_are_you_required_to_withhold_payroll_taxes/when_you_are_going_to_withhold_payroll_taxes/obtain_the_data_required_for_the_payroll_taxes/anonymous_rate)
- **C — Separate employments.** One individual has distinct employments with the same employer. Set up separate payroll records for the employments; separately assess how wages must be combined for calculation. [Handbook, payroll records](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t61fd.pdf)
- **D — Annual payslip.** The final payslip is intended to be the annual statement but lacks the BSN and cumulative annual Zvw information. It is not complete: add all mandatory annual information, with secure delivery. [Annual statement fields](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontenten/belastingdienst/business/payroll_taxes/you_are_not_established_in_the_netherlands_are_you_required_to_withhold_payroll_taxes/when_you_are_going_to_withhold_payroll_taxes/payroll_records/issuing_annual_income_statements_to_employees)
- **E — Replacement risk.** Before the filing deadline, an employer corrects one person's hours. With the online form, resubmit the complete return, including unchanged employees. Do not send only the changed person as a full replacement. [Correction methods](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/personeel-en-loon/content/hoe-maak-ik-bezwaar-tegen-mijn-aangifte-loonheffingen)
- **F — Retention boundary.** An employee leaves during 2026. For documents subject to the five-calendar-year-after-departure-year rule, the subsequent years run through 2031; deletion during 2031 is premature. Review other retention duties before any later deletion. [Handbook, retention](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t61fd.pdf)
- **G — No money difference.** Paid hours are wrong but tax totals are correct. Correct the employee data; tax reconciliation alone does not prove a correct return. [Handbook, policy administration and corrections](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t61fd.pdf)
- **H — Payment control.** The return is accepted but the bank payment failed. Filing passed; payment did not. Resolve payment before its deadline and retain both confirmations. [Handbook, filing and payment](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t61fd.pdf)

## When to refuse or refer

- Deliver a dated employer setup file, complete onboarding checklist, payroll/return/payment reconciliation, correction register, annual-statement checklist and document-specific retention schedule. Identify the period, source version, unresolved facts and responsible approver. Obtain approval before filing or paying.

- Stop for missing identity/data without an established lawful exception, unresolved work permission, uncertain residence/insurance or employment status, unsubstantiated tax-free allowances, unexplained payroll balances, rejected returns or a threatened deadline. Refer these to the responsible payroll specialist, accountant or tax adviser; do not manufacture values or silently assume eligibility. Keep a record of the resolution and whether earlier periods also need correction.
