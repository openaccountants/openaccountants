# Norway Company Formation & Entity Choice

## Scope

Use for ordinary AS formation and its follow-up in 2026. The alternative entity routes remain below; complex restructurings, regulated businesses and foreign-branch facts need their own legal/tax work. A capital contribution is funding retained by the company, not a registration expense.

## Ask the client first

- Which legal vehicle and activity, with which founders and liability expectations?
- Cash or non-cash capital; proposed directors/general manager and their residence?
- Who can sign in Altinn, and can every required role acceptance and capital confirmation be obtained?
- Expected registration date, accounting year, group status and audit thresholds?
- Beneficial owners, tax residence/management and expected taxable sales?

## The method, step by step

### Entity choice and legal parameters

- **Overview**: use this filing method for an ordinary Norwegian private limited company (aksjeselskap, AS). It preserves the alternative AS, ASA, ENK, partnership and NUF routes below. Register an AS in the Register of Business Enterprises (Foretaksregisteret) and the Central Coordinating Register for Legal Entities (Enhetsregisteret) through Brønnøysundregistrene. The current Business Register Act is lov 20 June 2025 nr. 106; its § 13-1 repealed the 1985 Act from 1 January 2026. [Business Register Act §§ 2-1, 13-1](https://lovdata.no/dokument/NL/lov/2025-06-20-106), [2026 register categories](https://www.brreg.no/en/how-can-we-help-you/fees-for-registration/).

- **Private limited company (Aksjeselskap, AS)**: limited liability; minimum share capital NOK 30,000, divided into one or more shares of equal amount _(Aksjeloven § 3-1)_. An AS is a taxpayer in its own right _(Skatteloven § 2-2 first paragraph letter a)_ [Aksjeloven §§ 1-2, 3-1](https://lovdata.no/dokument/NL/lov/1997-06-13-44/*); [Skatteloven § 2-2(1)(a)](https://lovdata.no/dokument/NL/lov/1999-03-26-14/*).

- **Public limited company (Allmennaksjeselskap, ASA)**: minimum share capital NOK 1,000,000 (in words on the page) _(Allmennaksjeloven § 3-1)_ [Allmennaksjeloven § 3-1](https://lovdata.no/lov/1997-06-13-45/§3-1).

- **Sole proprietorship (Enkeltpersonforetak, ENK)**: not one of the companies that are taxpayers in their own right _(Skatteloven § 2-2 first paragraph)_; the owner files a personal return, due by the end of May, and is taxed on the business income _(Skatteforvaltningsforskriften § 8-2-3 first paragraph letter a)_. See [no-income-tax](no-income-tax.md). The owner has full personal liability for the business's debts and obligations _(Brønnøysundregistrene, Enkeltpersonforetak)_. An ENK currently has a right to register in the Register of Business Enterprises under the 2025 Act § 2-2; it must register there when operating as a state-authorised accountant or auditor. Registration in the Central Coordinating Register only costs NOK 2,181 digitally and NOK 2,746 on paper; registration in both registers costs NOK 3,883 digitally and NOK 4,398 on paper _(Brønnøysundregistrene, Fees for registration 2026)_ [Skatteloven § 2-2](https://lovdata.no/dokument/NL/lov/1999-03-26-14/*); [Tax Administration Regulation § 8-2-3](https://lovdata.no/dokument/SF/forskrift/2016-11-23-1360/*); [Business Register Act § 2-2](https://lovdata.no/dokument/NL/lov/2025-06-20-106); [ENK liability](https://www.brreg.no/enkeltpersonforetak/); [mandatory professional registration](https://www.brreg.no/enkeltpersonforetak/registreringsplikt-i-foretaksregisteret/); [2026 fees](https://www.brreg.no/en/how-can-we-help-you/fees-for-registration/).

- **Partnerships (ANS, DA, KS)**: not taxpayers in their own right; each partner is taxed on its share _(Skatteloven § 2-2 second and third paragraphs)_. In an ANS or DA the partners have unlimited personal liability for the partnership's obligations, in an ANS jointly and in a DA each for a share _(Selskapsloven § 1-2 first paragraph letter b)_. A creditor must first claim against the partnership and can go to the partners if not paid within 14 days of the demand _(Selskapsloven § 2-4 second paragraph)_. A KS has at least one partner with unlimited liability and at least one whose liability is limited to a fixed sum _(Selskapsloven § 1-2 first paragraph letter e)_. General partnerships (ANS, DA) pay NOK 3,883 digitally and NOK 4,398 on paper to register; a KS is in the limited-liability band and pays NOK 6,825 digitally and NOK 7,912 on paper _(Brønnøysundregistrene, Fees for registration 2026)_ The creditor can also proceed directly where the partnership obviously cannot pay or cannot be found; § 2-4(3) permits agreement variations subject to its registration/knowledge conditions. [Selskapsloven §§ 1-2, 2-4](https://lovdata.no/lov/1985-06-21-83/§2-4); [Skatteloven § 2-2(2)-(3)](https://lovdata.no/dokument/NL/lov/1999-03-26-14/*); [2026 fees](https://www.brreg.no/en/how-can-we-help-you/fees-for-registration/).

- **Norwegian-registered foreign enterprise (NUF)**: a foreign enterprise that carries on business in Norway or on the Norwegian continental shelf must register in the Register of Business Enterprises _(2025 Foretaksregisterloven § 2-1(2))_. The foreign enterprise itself is responsible for the business in Norway _(Brønnøysundregistrene, Norskregistrert utenlandsk foretak (NUF))_. A NUF can be registered only on the paper form. Registration in both registers costs NOK 4,398 on paper _(Brønnøysundregistrene, Fees for registration 2026)_. [2025 Business Register Act § 2-1(2)](https://lovdata.no/dokument/NL/lov/2025-06-20-106); [NUF responsibility](https://www.brreg.no/andre-organisasjonsformer/norskregistrert-utenlandsk-foretak-nuf/); [fees and paper-only initial registration](https://www.brreg.no/en/how-can-we-help-you/fees-for-registration/).

- **Minimum share capital, AS, paid before registration**: NOK 30,000 _(Aksjeloven § 3-1)_. The share contributions must be paid in full before the company is reported to the register. A cash contribution can be confirmed by an auditor, a financial institution in Norway or another EEA state, a lawyer or a state-authorised accountant; a contribution in kind needs an auditor _(Aksjeloven § 2-18 second paragraph)_. The board members and the person who confirmed are jointly liable for any shortfall _(Aksjeloven § 2-19)_ The shortfall liability does not cover a shortfall caused by valuing non-cash contributions under § 2-19(2). [Aksjeloven §§ 2-18, 2-19, 3-1](https://lovdata.no/dokument/NL/lov/1997-06-13-44/*).

- **Residence of general manager and board**: the general manager and at least 50% (in words on the page: minst halvdelen) of the board members must live in an EEA state, the United Kingdom or Switzerland. The ministry can grant an exemption _(Aksjeloven § 6-11 first paragraph)_ [Aksjeloven § 6-11](https://lovdata.no/dokument/NL/lov/1997-06-13-44/*).

- **Registration authority**: Brønnøysundregistrene, through the Coordinated register notification (Samordnet registermelding) in Altinn, or through one of six digital company formation providers that work with Brreg _(Brønnøysundregistrene, Register a limited company)_ [current AS registration routes](https://www.brreg.no/en/limited-company/register-a-limited-company/).

- **Registration fee, AS**: NOK 6,825 if filed digitally, NOK 7,912 on paper _(Brønnøysundregistrene, Fees for registration 2026, last updated 4 August 2026)_. Most later changes are free; some, such as a reduction of share capital or a change of name, cost NOK 1,276 digitally and NOK 2,509 on paper _(same page)_ [2026 fees](https://www.brreg.no/en/how-can-we-help-you/fees-for-registration/).

- **Registration deadline**: the AS must be reported to the Register of Business Enterprises within 3 months (in words on the page) after the memorandum of association is signed. If it is not, it cannot be registered and the memorandum no longer binds the founders _(Aksjeloven § 2-18 first and third paragraphs)_ [Aksjeloven § 2-18(1),(3)](https://lovdata.no/dokument/NL/lov/1997-06-13-44/*).

- **Incorporation timeline**: a processing queue is not a promised completion time. Brreg says manual registration is usually completed within 3 weeks, but varies by case type and workload; postal cases can take significantly longer. Check the live queue before planning completion. The former dated queue observation is held below. [Brreg processing guidance, read 15 November 2026](https://www.brreg.no/en/how-can-we-help-you/when-will-my-case-be-processed/).

- **Core incorporation steps**: the founders sign a memorandum of association (stiftelsesdokument) that contains the articles of association _(Aksjeloven § 2-1 first paragraph)_. The memorandum states the founders, the shares each subscribes for, the price per share and when it is paid _(Aksjeloven § 2-3)_. It can be made on paper or through an electronic formation service approved by the registrar _(§ 2-1 first paragraph)_. The share capital is paid in and confirmed, and the board, any deputy, the general manager, auditor and accountant sign the registration form to accept their roles _(Brønnøysundregistrene, Register a limited company)_ [Aksjeloven §§ 2-1 to 2-3](https://lovdata.no/dokument/NL/lov/1997-06-13-44/*); [required attachments and role acceptances](https://www.brreg.no/en/limited-company/register-a-limited-company/).

- **Tax residence of the company**: under the text applicable in 2026 a company incorporated under Norwegian company law is resident in Norway and taxed on worldwide income, as is a foreign company with its effective management in Norway, unless a treaty makes it resident elsewhere _(Skatteloven § 2-2 sixth to eighth paragraphs)_. See [no-corporate-income-tax](no-corporate-income-tax.md) [Skatteloven § 2-2(6)-(8)](https://lovdata.no/dokument/NL/lov/1999-03-26-14/*).

- **VAT registration**: required once taxable sales and withdrawals exceed NOK 50,000 in a 12-month period _(Merverdiavgiftsloven § 2-1 first paragraph)_. See [norway-mva](norway-mva.md) [VAT Act § 2-1(1)](https://lovdata.no/dokument/NL/lov/2009-06-19-58/*).

- **Annual general meeting**: within 6 months (in words on the page) after the end of each financial year; it approves the annual accounts and any dividend _(Aksjeloven § 5-5)_ [Aksjeloven § 5-5](https://lovdata.no/dokument/NL/lov/1997-06-13-44/*).

- **Annual accounts filing**: send the annual accounts and the other reports required for the entity to the Register of Company Accounts within 1 month (in words on the page) after approval under Regnskapsloven § 8-2. For an ordinary calendar-year AS, Brreg requires a complete set by 31 July to avoid a late filing penalty and cannot extend that deadline. The tax return is a separate obligation. Beneficial-owner registration has its own event-based deadline below. [Accounting Act § 8-2](https://lovdata.no/lov/1998-07-17-56/§8-2); [2026 annual-accounts guidance](https://www.brreg.no/en/submission-of-annual-accounts/).

- **Late filing penalty**: NOK 1,345 a week for the first 8 weeks, NOK 2,690 a week for the next 10 weeks and NOK 4,035 a week for the last 8 weeks; a started week counts as a whole week, and it runs for at most 26 weeks (so at most NOK 69,940). It is based on the court fee of NOK 1,345 _(Brønnøysundregistrene, How much is the late filing penalty)_ The 2026 maximum is derived: 8 × 1,345 + 10 × 2,690 + 8 × 4,035 = NOK 69,940. If weeks cross a year end, use the court fee applying to those weeks. [Brreg penalty calculation](https://www.brreg.no/en/submission-of-annual-accounts/late-filing-penalty/how-much-is-the-late-filing-penalty/).

- **Statutory audit exemption, small AS**: the general meeting can decide that the accounts need not be audited if operating income is under NOK 7,000,000 (in words on the page), the balance sheet total is under NOK 27,000,000 (in words on the page), and the average number of employees does not exceed 10 full-time equivalents (in words on the page) _(Aksjeloven § 7-6 first paragraph; forskrift 3 January 2018 nr. 7 § 1)_. A new company can opt out at formation if it has no more than 10 employees (in words on the page) and the share contributions are under NOK 27,000,000 (in words on the page) _(Aksjeloven § 7-6 second and fifth paragraphs; forskrift § 2)_. If an annual account shows operating income, balance sheet total or employees above a threshold, the next year's accounts must be audited _(§ 7-6 third paragraph)_. A parent company tests the thresholds for the group as a whole _(§ 7-6 first paragraph)_. The decision only takes effect once registered with the Register of Business Enterprises _(§ 7-6 sixth paragraph)_ No exemption can be chosen if another law or regulation requires audit. [Aksjeloven § 7-6](https://lovdata.no/dokument/NL/lov/1997-06-13-44/*); [threshold regulation §§ 1-2](https://lovdata.no/dokument/SF/forskrift/2018-01-03-7).

- **Annual corporate tax return**: the company tax return (skattemelding for formues- og inntektsskatt) is due by the end of May in the year after the income year, so 31 May 2027 for 2026 _(Skatteforvaltningsforskriften § 8-2-3 first paragraph letter a)_. Businesses must file it electronically _(Skatteforvaltningsforskriften § 8-1-2 second paragraph letter a)_. Owners of sole proprietorships have the same end-of-May deadline _(same)_ [Tax Administration Regulation §§ 8-1-2, 8-2-3](https://lovdata.no/dokument/SF/forskrift/2016-11-23-1360/*).

### Submit and follow up

1. Confirm the vehicle and activity before using the AS route. Prepare the signed memorandum and articles, and record subscribers, shares, subscription amounts, payment date, board and any auditor.
2. Pay the subscribed capital in full. Attach the authorised confirmation; non-cash contributions need the founder statement and auditor confirmation. If an EEA financial institution confirms the contribution, Brreg requires the entire notification by post.
3. Complete Coordinated register notification in Altinn, or the supported digital formation service. Attach the documents and any activity-specific approval/appointment evidence listed by Brreg. Obtain the required role acceptances. Submit within the statutory period and preserve the receipt. These are the [actual registration instructions](https://www.brreg.no/en/limited-company/register-a-limited-company/).
4. Check the returned decision and registered details before treating the filing as completed; resolve a rejection or request for evidence. Record the organisation number and hand tax, VAT and payroll work to the owning skills. The live portal field layout and case-specific authority are not inferred here.
5. For an organisation within the beneficial-owner duty, submit the separate Altinn form within 14 days of registration in a public register, and within 14 days of a new beneficial owner or other change. Filing also applies with no beneficial owners, no activity or dissolution before strike-off. Retain the decision/transcript and Sent form in Altinn. [current beneficial-owner instructions](https://www.brreg.no/en/beneficial-owners/).
6. Set the AGM, accounts and tax-return calendar separately. Submit the complete annual accounts through the Brreg-linked Altinn form; retain the decision sent to the Altinn inbox. A timely filing has a 2-week correction period for errors/insufficiencies under the current registry guidance; it does not extend the original filing deadline. [annual-accounts instructions](https://www.brreg.no/en/submission-of-annual-accounts/).

## Worked cases

### Digital or paper AS registration

Assume an ordinary cash-funded AS with exactly NOK 30,000 capital and no other formation costs. The digital fee is NOK 6,825; the paper fee is NOK 7,912. Initial funding plus the fee is NOK 36,825 digitally or NOK 37,912 on paper, a derived NOK 1,087 difference. The capital remains company funding. Where Brreg requires post, such as confirmation by a foreign EEA bank, the cheaper digital route is unavailable. [2026 fees](https://www.brreg.no/en/how-can-we-help-you/fees-for-registration/); [postal condition](https://www.brreg.no/en/limited-company/register-a-limited-company/).

```oa-check
name: Norway AS 2026 digital versus paper initial funding
inputs:
  capital: 30000
  digital_fee: 6825
  paper_fee: 7912
steps:
  - digital_funding: capital + digital_fee
  - paper_funding: capital + paper_fee
  - fee_difference: paper_fee - digital_fee
expected:
  digital_funding: 36825
  paper_funding: 37912
  fee_difference: 1087
```

### Late accounts: file now or delay another week

Assume all penalty weeks occur in 2026 at the published court fee. Filing in the 9th commenced penalty week costs 8 × NOK 1,345 + NOK 2,690 = NOK 13,450. Waiting into the 10th week costs NOK 16,140, a derived extra NOK 2,690. The full 26-week maximum is NOK 69,940. A commenced week counts fully; a later year may have a different fee. [weekly tiers and duration](https://www.brreg.no/en/submission-of-annual-accounts/late-filing-penalty/how-much-is-the-late-filing-penalty/).

```oa-check
name: Norway 2026 accounts penalty ninth versus tenth week
inputs:
  first_week_rate: 1345
  second_week_rate: 2690
  third_week_rate: 4035
steps:
  - file_week_nine: 8 * first_week_rate + second_week_rate
  - file_week_ten: 8 * first_week_rate + 2 * second_week_rate
  - delay_cost: file_week_ten - file_week_nine
  - maximum: 8 * first_week_rate + 10 * second_week_rate + 8 * third_week_rate
expected:
  file_week_nine: 13450
  file_week_ten: 16140
  delay_cost: 2690
  maximum: 69940
```

## When to refuse or refer

- Missing capital/payment evidence, signatures, identity, activity approval or beneficial-owner information: stop that submission until established.
- Non-cash valuation, special rights, regulated activity, group audit, restructurings or an uncertain foreign-branch/residence position: obtain the relevant specialist analysis.
- A filing receipt or indicative queue does not establish registration approval; do not promise a completion date.
- Do not treat company capital as a fee, the accounts return as the tax return, or the old July beneficial-owner notice as the current event-based deadline.
