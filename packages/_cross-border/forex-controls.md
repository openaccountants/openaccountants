# Forex controls and cross-border money movement

## Scope

Use this Guide to separate exchange permission, bank evidence, investment registration, tax collection and information reporting for founders, freelancers and other individuals or businesses moving money across borders. Nationality alone does not decide the answer: establish the person's relevant residence, entity, transaction purpose, direction, currency conversion and applicable period. A declaration threshold is not necessarily a transfer ceiling, and a bank's commercial acceptance does not establish tax compliance.

> **Based on work by [Artin (@ar-gen-tin)](https://github.com/ar-gen-tin/panrise)**, licensed under MIT. Adapted for the OpenAccountants format.

Not yet approved by an accountant. Rules were checked on 16 November 2026. It retains the original country, offshore-income and inquiry-response topics. The specific unresolved propositions in Sources cannot support a transfer, return, exemption or entity recommendation.

## Ask the client first

Read the existing case, contracts, bank correspondence and returns before asking for missing facts. Establish:

- The sender, owner and beneficiary, the relevant residence of each, and whether the sender is an individual, partnership or company.
- The actual economic purpose: salary, independent services, goods, gift or family support, dividend, loan, investment, capital contribution or liquidation. Distinguish personal from company money.
- Origin, destination, currencies, conversion into local currency, payment channel, dates, amount and relevant cumulative prior transactions; identify any physical cash separately.
- Contracts, invoices, employment and dividend records, ownership, source of funds, prior foreign-exchange declarations/approvals, tax paid and the exact bank or tax-authority request and deadline.

Do not substitute a foreign company, another person's quota or an inaccurate payment description for the correct legal route. A foreign account does not decide where income is taxable or discharge reporting duties.

### Mainland China: personal, trade and capital routes

The SAFE explanation distinguishes personal foreign-currency settlement and resident-individual purchases within the USD 50,000 annual convenience amount from documented genuine current-account transactions above it. Education, travel and medical purposes are examples; exceeding the convenience amount does not itself prohibit a genuine current-account payment. Personal purchases must not be used for the listed closed capital purposes, including overseas property, securities or the specified life/investment-return insurance. A blanket statement that every business-related receipt is forbidden is also inaccurate: the explanation expressly includes overseas labour income among current-account settlement examples. [SAFE Shanghai personal exchange explanation](https://www.safe.gov.cn/shanghai/2019/1213/1198.html)

Use true purpose and evidence. Do not submit false documents, borrow or lend another person's quota, split transactions to evade quota/authenticity management, or use underground exchange channels. These prohibitions are not advice to keep each payment just below an amount. [SAFE Shanghai personal exchange explanation](https://www.safe.gov.cn/shanghai/2019/1213/1198.html) [SAFE Current Account Guidelines2020, watch-list clauses](https://www.safe.gov.cn/beijing/file/file/20250901/f03a39d891024861a8ce859dab124f06.pdf)

For company trade, distinguish the genuine underlying transaction and the bank's document/authenticity review from capital-account registration or approval. The Foreign Exchange Administration Regulations Articles 12-14 require genuine lawful current-account transactions and relevant payment documents. Article 17 requires outward direct/securities investment registration under the applicable rules and prior approval or filing where the State requires it; it does not establish the old universal ODI dollar threshold. Article 22 makes capital-payment approval conditional on the rules applicable to that payment. This foundational 2008 instrument is not a complete current ODI procedure or evidence that every company transaction requires SAFE approval. [China Foreign Exchange Administration Regulations, Order532](https://www.safe.gov.cn/neimenggu/file/file/20170728/b663448ec2f74289b194838d47b8df95.pdf)

A Hong Kong company can receive its own genuine revenue under Hong Kong's capital framework, but that does not determine a mainland founder's investment, remuneration, service, dividend, residence or tax obligations. For a proposed salary retain the employment/work-location record; for a dividend retain ownership and the resolution; for services retain the contract, performance and actual invoice; for family support retain the true relationship and purpose. These are evidence prompts, not verified mandatory Chinese payment codes or clearance of those arrangements. The old provider and offshore-retention recommendations remain individually held in Sources. [Hong Kong Basic Law Articles111–112](https://www.basiclaw.gov.hk/en/basiclaw/chapter5.html) [China Foreign Exchange Administration Regulations, Order532](https://www.safe.gov.cn/neimenggu/file/file/20170728/b663448ec2f74289b194838d47b8df95.pdf)

### India: LRS is not company or ODI clearance

RBI's LRS direction permits resident individuals, including minors under its guardian/Form A2 procedure, to remit up to USD 250,000 per financial year for permitted purposes. It does not extend the individual scheme to corporate, partnership, HUF or trust remitters. Prohibited FEMA/current-account transactions, prohibited destinations or persons, and overseas margin/margin-call remittances do not become permissible because they fit the amount. Family consolidation for capital investment requires compliance with the co-owner/co-partner conditions; one resident cannot use a gift to replenish another resident's overseas foreign-currency account. [RBI Master Direction, Liberalised Remittance Scheme](https://rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_ViewMasDirections.aspx?id=10192)

Paragraph 7 contains purpose-specific facilities: medical treatment above the ordinary amount can use the specified doctor/hospital estimate under general permission, and studies can use the foreign institution's estimate without prior RBI permission. Qualifying employer-borne business visits are treated outside LRS subject to bona fides. Emigration follows its prescribed-country amount/ordinary amount and incidental-expense conditions, excluding investment simply to earn immigration points. Other excess releases require the applicable RBI permission. [RBI Master Direction, Liberalised Remittance Scheme](https://rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_ViewMasDirections.aspx?id=10192)

PAN and the authorised dealer's Form A2 process form part of the direction. The direction refers overseas direct and portfolio investment to the 2022 overseas-investment rules: an LLC or Pte. Ltd. is not automatically an eligible investment. Do not treat a proposed foreign-company capital contribution, loan, round-trip structure or retention of foreign business receipts as cleared by the LRS amount alone. Paragraph 17 allows an LRS investor to retain/reinvest investment income, but requires the received/realised/unspent/unused foreign exchange, unless reinvested, to be repatriated and surrendered within its 180-day rule; resident-individual ODI must instead comply with the specified 2022 investment framework. That is not general permission to retain all foreign business revenue. The remaining transaction-specific ODI and Indian foreign-income/credit rules are listed in Sources. [RBI Master Direction, Liberalised Remittance Scheme](https://rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_ViewMasDirections.aspx?id=10192)

For a normal LRS remittance under Income-tax Act 2025 section 394(1), Table 7, as amended by Finance Act 2026 with effect from 1 April 2026, the authorised dealer collects 2% for education/medical purposes and 20% for other purposes on the amount/aggregate exceeding INR 1,000,000. Collection is at debit or receipt, whichever is earlier. Section 394(4) excludes qualifying education remittances funded by a loan from the specified section 129(3)(b) financial institution and prevents dealer collection on the same amount on which the overseas tour-package seller has already collected tax. Section 394(5) also addresses amounts on which the buyer is liable to deduct, and has deducted, tax under the other provisions. The tour-package seller is a separate Table 8 category; do not confuse its rate with the LRS threshold. [Income-tax Act2025 as amended by Finance Act2026](https://www.incometaxindia.gov.in/documents/d/guest/income_tax_act_2025_as_amended_by_fa_act_2026-pdf)

These are ordinary statutory rates for a correctly classified remittance, with no higher-rate identifier/default issue and no lower-rate certificate assumed. Section 395 permits the specified lower-collection certificate route and requires the tax-collection certificate. Reconcile that certificate and the actual return/credit position; the present source read does not promise an automatic refund or a final tax cost equal to the collection. [Income-tax Act2025 as amended by Finance Act2026](https://www.incometaxindia.gov.in/documents/d/guest/income_tax_act_2025_as_amended_by_fa_act_2026-pdf)

### Brazil: payment channel, documentation and IOF

The Banco Central's 2026 exchange booklet directs the sender to an authorised foreign-exchange institution or correspondent and requires identification of sender and beneficiary. For an outward payment order, up to R$10,000 may be delivered through the described payment instruments, including cash reais; above it the booklet specifies debit from an eligible deposit/payment account or cheque. The inward counterpart has its corresponding account-credit/cheque rule. This is a reais settlement-method boundary, not the old USD 10,000 daily no-document allowance. [Banco Central do Brasil, Cartilha de Câmbio2026](https://aprendervalor.bcb.gov.br/content/estabilidadefinanceira/politica_cambial/Cartilha_cambio_2026.pdf)

Ask for the Valor Efetivo Total (VET): the booklet explains that it includes the exchange rate, IOF and fees/commissions and must be supplied by the institution and shown on the exchange contract or receipt. The exact IOF rate and current transaction-specific documentation depend on the operation; the old numerical IOF range and “US LLC + Mercury avoids the rules” proposition cannot be used from this Guide. [Banco Central do Brasil, Cartilha de Câmbio2026](https://aprendervalor.bcb.gov.br/content/estabilidadefinanceira/politica_cambial/Cartilha_cambio_2026.pdf)

### Taiwan: three different questions

First, distinguish foreign-currency movements without NTD conversion from settlement involving NTD. The CBC FAQ describes the former as free; for the latter, each exchange transaction reaching TWD 500,000 is subject to the declaration regulations. Articles 2-3 of the regulations govern the declaration and relevant person categories; do not substitute nationality or the former annual TWD amount for this per-transaction test. [CBC current inward/outward funds FAQ](https://www.cbc.gov.tw/tw/cp-1179-101135-49647-1.html) [CBC declaration regulations,26December2022revision](https://www.cbc.gov.tw/tw/dl-171561-a2f2e7bbd39d4e2c8abc05b2f3e5bc7c.html)

Second, distinguish the annual direct-to-bank route. CBC's 31 October 2024 Order under Article 7 raised the Article 4(1)(3)/6(1)(1) company amount from USD 50,000,000 to USD 100,000,000 and individual/group amount from USD 5,000,000 to USD 10,000,000, effective 1 November 2024. The 26 December 2022 regulation PDF still prints the previous amounts; apply the later order rather than treating the two documents as competing current answers. The current FAQ gives those raised annual amounts and USD 100,000 per transaction for nonresidents, with supporting documents and CBC approval through the bank above the relevant amount. These are distinct categories, not a uniform resident transfer cap. [CBC31October2024annual-settlement adjustment order](https://www.cbc.gov.tw/tw/dl-212210-8b20d63a34cf4b098a130e529e3f0e45.html) [CBC declaration regulations,26December2022revision](https://www.cbc.gov.tw/tw/dl-171561-a2f2e7bbd39d4e2c8abc05b2f3e5bc7c.html) [CBC current inward/outward funds FAQ](https://www.cbc.gov.tw/tw/cp-1179-101135-49647-1.html)

Third, identify transactions and documents independently. Article 4 and the FAQ separately address goods/services and approved investment. Article 5 requires supporting documents for specified cases, including company transactions of USD 1,000,000 or more and individual/group transactions of USD 500,000 or more, as well as the other listed investment/underlying-transaction cases. Investment approval, OBU tax treatment and CFC status are not resolved by passing the exchange declaration test. [CBC declaration regulations,26December2022revision](https://www.cbc.gov.tw/tw/dl-171561-a2f2e7bbd39d4e2c8abc05b2f3e5bc7c.html) [CBC current inward/outward funds FAQ](https://www.cbc.gov.tw/tw/cp-1179-101135-49647-1.html)

### South Korea and Japan

The Bank of Korea's English procedural explanation separates current transactions, capital transactions and payment methods. It describes evidential documents for current payments, specified notification/confirmation cases, and capital routes requiring their applicable procedures. Its numerical donation/other examples are not a sufficient current consolidated 2026 clearance for the former country matrix. Establish the actual remitter, investment/payment type and current designated-bank/authority procedure before using a numerical exception. The exact old Korean amounts are preserved as non-usable research items in Sources. [Bank of Korea, foreign-exchange transaction procedures](https://www.bok.or.kr/eng/main/contents.do?menuNo=400191)

BOJ's reporting explanation says FEFTA payment/receipt reporting captures resident/nonresident payments exceeding JPY 30,000,000, subject to its stated exceptions, including payments for goods whose export/import has been cleared through customs. Capital-transaction reporting under Article 55-3 is separate. This is reporting scope, not proof that every remittance is unlimited, every filing is post-transaction, or FEFTA restrictions concern only sensitive-sector investment. [BOJ, balance-of-payments source-report explanation](https://www.boj.or.jp/en/statistics/outline/exp/exbpsm6.htm)

### Hong Kong, Singapore and the UAE

Hong Kong Basic Law Article 112 provides no foreign-exchange controls, free convertibility and free capital flow. HKMA explains the linked-rate system's HKD 7.75-7.85 per USD band; the band is not a guaranteed customer dealing rate or evidence that Hong Kong is the best jurisdiction for a particular founder. Formation, fees, tax and payment-provider eligibility require their own facts. [Hong Kong Basic Law Articles111–112](https://www.basiclaw.gov.hk/en/basiclaw/chapter5.html) [HKMA Linked Exchange Rate System](https://www.hkma.gov.hk/eng/key-functions/money/linked-exchange-rate-system/)

For Singapore, the original statements about unrestricted MAS transactions, no reporting for most transfers, founder suitability and the interaction with another country's CFC rules remain separate non-usable propositions in Sources. A Singapore entity or account does not by itself answer the sender's home-country exchange/tax questions.

The UAE government's tax page expressly states that it does not levy income tax on individuals and distinguishes 5% VAT from corporate tax on corporations/businesses. The old “5% personal income tax from January 2026” is corrected. This does not exempt a natural person's business from all corporate-tax questions or substantiate the original blanket capital/profit repatriation and any-currency transfer statements. [UAEgovernment taxation overview](https://u.ae/en/information-and-services/finance-and-investment/taxation)

### European Union: capital freedom, cash and payment charges

TFEU Article 63 prohibits capital and payment restrictions within the framework of its chapter, including movements with third countries. Article 65 permits the specified tax distinctions, anti-infringement/prudential measures, administrative/statistical declarations and public-policy/security measures, subject to its conditions and prohibition on arbitrary discrimination or disguised restriction. It is not a universal waiver of national tax/account reporting or all other chapter exceptions. [TFEU Article63](https://publications.europa.eu/resource/cellar/9912914b-f9a7-4f78-b34e-d3e6a2d71f67.0005.01/DOC_1) [TFEU Article65](https://publications.europa.eu/resource/cellar/f07ba7d3-e51a-4b86-915f-771a9e18ece7.0005.01/DOC_1)

Regulation 2018/1672 Articles 1-3 requires a carrier entering or leaving the Union with defined cash worth EUR 10,000 or more to declare it and make it available for control; incorrect/incomplete information or failure to make it available does not fulfil the duty. “Cash” is the regulation's defined category, including relevant bearer instruments and listed stores of value, rather than every electronic bank balance. The rule is an external-Union border rule; do not restate it as all intra-EU crossings or only amounts strictly greater than the threshold. Separate national controls and unaccompanied-cash rules need their own applicable scope. [RegulationEU2018/1672cashcontrols](https://publications.europa.eu/resource/cellar/71d1af91-e611-11e8-b690-01aa75ed71a1.0006.03/DOC_1)

Regulation 2021/1230 Article 3 equalises the provider's charges for cross-border euro payments with corresponding national payments; it extends to another national currency only under the regulation's notification framework. The provider must identify the corresponding national payment. Currency-conversion charges are excluded from that equality rule. Article 3(5), added by Regulation 2024/886, disapplies Article 3(1) for a cross-border instant credit transfer in euro by a provider in a non-euro Member State where Regulation 260/2012 Article 5b(1) would require a lower charge for the same transfer. Article 5b(1) caps instant-transfer charges at those for other credit transfers of corresponding type; its ordinary provider compliance dates are 9 January 2025 in euro Member States and 9 January 2027 in non-euro Member States. Check the applicable transition, including Article 16(9) for a state introducing the euro; do not assume a uniform 2026 instant-transfer fee cap. Under amended Article 6(2), failure to communicate the IBAN and, where appropriate under Regulation 260/2012, the related BIC can permit additional charges only if appropriate, in line with costs, agreed and disclosed in good time before the user is bound. SEPA or the euro label is not a promise that all transfers/FX are free or that all domestic and cross-border procedures are identical. [RegulationEU2021/1230cross-borderpayments](https://publications.europa.eu/resource/cellar/2b7c281b-f0d1-11eb-a71c-01aa75ed71a1.0006.03/DOC_1) [RegulationEU2024/886, current cross-border charge amendments](https://publications.europa.eu/resource/cellar/d395dbf7-e597-11ee-8b2b-01aa75ed71a1.0006.03/DOC_1)

### China: foreign income, credit and the actual notice

The official resident-individual foreign-income announcement distinguishes comprehensive income, business income and other categories. Comprehensive income is combined with domestic comprehensive income; business is separately combined under its rules, with foreign losses not offset against domestic or another country's business income and carried within the relevant country rules. Other categories use their separate calculation. A single gross-income ratio for every kind of income is therefore incorrect. [China resident-individual foreign-income announcement2020No3](https://12366.chinatax.gov.cn/bzds/082/082-5-4.html)

For the country credit limit, the announcement separately allocates computed comprehensive-income tax by that country's comprehensive-income amount over total comprehensive-income amount, business-income tax by the relevant taxable-business-income ratio, and the Chinese-law tax for other categories, then sums the category limits for that country. Creditable foreign income tax must be legally due and actually paid; treaty-not-due or wrongly paid tax, interest/penalties, compensated tax and tax on exempt foreign income are excluded under the stated rules. Conditional treaty tax sparing is separate. Credit is limited to the lower eligible foreign tax or country limit; excess can be carried to the following five tax years within the rule, not refunded by China merely because it exceeds that year's credit limit. [China resident-individual foreign-income announcement2020No3](https://12366.chinatax.gov.cn/bzds/082/082-5-4.html)

A resident individual with foreign income files from 1 March to 30 June of the following year under the announcement: for 2025 income that is 1 March-30 June 2026. Apply the announcement's tax-year, competent-office and documentary rules; retain foreign assessments/certificates and payment evidence, or its permitted alternative evidence. Its limited prior-year credit/correction route requires the actual conditions. The old mobile-app click path is not a verified current filing procedure. [China resident-individual foreign-income announcement2020No3](https://12366.chinatax.gov.cn/bzds/082/082-5-4.html)

CRS is an information-exchange framework, not an exemption from a person's return or proof that every foreign account is automatically reported to China. The independently reviewed EU Annex I definitions distinguish reportable accounts, persons, controlling persons and reportable jurisdictions; that EU source is a framework example, not evidence of every national exchange relationship. [EU Directive 2011/16/EU, consolidated 1 January 2026](https://publications.europa.eu/resource/cellar/4328aa6c-1209-11f1-8870-01aa75ed71a1.0002.02/DOC_1) Determine the reporting institution/account/holder or controlling-person status, the relevant reporting-jurisdiction list and actual exchange relationship under the applicable year's law. Use the separately reviewed `fatca-crs-automatic-exchange.md` for those tests; specific country-list/relationship and the old wealth-band-enforcement assertions remain held here. Neither a US account nor an offshore company establishes secrecy or non-taxability.

For a compliance reminder, risk/interview notice or formal audit notice, preserve the exact document, identity of the issuing office, tax years, requested records, service date and stated response date. Reconcile bank receipts, company ownership, salary/fees/dividends, returns and foreign tax evidence. Preserve documents and obtain representation appropriate to the notice; do not assign a universal low/medium urgency or invent a fixed response period from the notice's informal label. These are case-handling steps, not a verified Chinese statutory notice taxonomy or guaranteed penalty concession.

### Enforcement: classify the offence before quoting a sanction

The Current Account Foreign Exchange Business Guidelines (2020), Articles 62-63 and Annex 6, address evasion of quota/authenticity controls. The 2026 effective-law catalogue and the official amendment notes were checked for the used clauses; the earlier Notice 2015/49 was repealed by the 2020 instrument and is not the operative authority here. It distinguishes quota-lending warnings and repeat conduct from borrowing/other evasion that enters the watch list. The stated monitoring period is the listing calendar year plus the next two consecutive years; listed people use identity and transaction-value supporting documents, with strict authenticity review. It is not a uniform two-year prohibition on all exchange purchases or automatic account-freeze rule. The later official catalogue and the used amendment scopes do not change this monitoring/document rule. [SAFE Current Account Guidelines2020, watch-list clauses](https://www.safe.gov.cn/beijing/file/file/20250901/f03a39d891024861a8ce859dab124f06.pdf)

The Foreign Exchange Administration Regulations distinguish offences. Article 39 unlawful outward transfer/evasion and Article 40 unlawful exchange acquisition provide ordinary fines up to 30% of the offence amount, and serious-case fines from 30% up to that amount, alongside their correction/return and possible criminal-liability provisions. Other offences have their own provisions; a 30% figure is not a universal mandatory fine, and these clauses do not establish the old fixed imprisonment or income-tax-penalty claims. [China Foreign Exchange Administration Regulations, Order532](https://www.safe.gov.cn/neimenggu/file/file/20170728/b663448ec2f74289b194838d47b8df95.pdf)

## Worked cases

### Case 1: ordinary India LRS collection, two purposes

Assume an eligible resident individual makes a single permitted INR 1,600,000 LRS remittance in the relevant financial year after 1 April 2026, with no earlier LRS remittance, qualifying education loan, tour-package overlap, tax-deduction exclusion, higher-rate identifier/default issue or lower certificate. The threshold excess is INR 1,600,000 - INR 1,000,000 = INR 600,000. For qualifying education/medical use, normal collection is INR 600,000 × 2% = INR 12,000; for another purpose it is INR 600,000 × 20% = INR 120,000. This compares statutory collection under the assumptions; it does not establish ODI eligibility, the FX conversion, the person's final tax, or a refund. [Income-tax Act2025 as amended by Finance Act2026](https://www.incometaxindia.gov.in/documents/d/guest/income_tax_act_2025_as_amended_by_fa_act_2026-pdf)

```oa-check
name: India LRS ordinary education and other purpose collection
inputs:
  remittance: 1600000
  threshold: 1000000
  education_rate: 0.02
  other_rate: 0.20
steps:
  excess: max(0, remittance - threshold)
  education_collection: excess * education_rate
  other_collection: excess * other_rate
expected:
  excess: 600000
  education_collection: 12000
  other_collection: 120000
```

### Case 2: China credit limit keeps income categories separate

Assume a Chinese resident has only comprehensive income for the illustrated computation, with hypothetical Chinese-law computed comprehensive tax of RMB 90,000, total comprehensive-income amount RMB 600,000 and the relevant country's comprehensive-income amount RMB 200,000. Assume eligible legally due foreign income tax of RMB 40,000 actually paid, no treaty tax-sparing or other-category amount, and no prior carry-forward. The category/country limit is RMB 90,000 × RMB 200,000 / RMB 600,000 = RMB 30,000. Current credit is the lower of RMB 40,000 and RMB 30,000 = RMB 30,000; excess RMB 10,000 is a potential carry-forward subject to the following-five-tax-years rule and actual evidence/remaining-limit conditions. The RMB 90,000 and income amounts are supplied assumptions, not derived Chinese rates or a gross-worldwide-income shortcut. Other income categories would require their separate components. [China resident-individual foreign-income announcement2020No3](https://12366.chinatax.gov.cn/bzds/082/082-5-4.html)

```oa-check
name: China comprehensive income country credit limit
inputs:
  computed_comprehensive_tax: 90000
  country_comprehensive_income: 200000
  total_comprehensive_income: 600000
  eligible_foreign_tax: 40000
steps:
  country_limit: computed_comprehensive_tax * country_comprehensive_income / total_comprehensive_income
  current_credit: min(eligible_foreign_tax, country_limit)
  excess: max(0, eligible_foreign_tax - country_limit)
expected:
  country_limit: 30000
  current_credit: 30000
  excess: 10000
```

## When to refuse or refer

Stop a transfer recommendation or filing conclusion if residence/entity/purpose, current route, decisive documents or an actual notice deadline is missing. Refer transactions needing investment approval, sanctions/AML review, a disputed foreign-tax credit or offence-specific advice to the appropriate qualified adviser. A company in Hong Kong, Singapore or the US, an OBU account, a provider name or offshore retention is not an exemption. Do not divide payments, lend quotas, misdescribe a salary/service/dividend as family support, or destroy records.

For a reviewable output, record each obligation separately: permission/registration, bank declaration and documents, collection and final tax, annual account reporting, physical-cash reporting, and the relevant source/period. An amount cleared for one question does not answer the others.

The original personal/company, investment, offshore-income, CFC, payment-provider, penalty and inquiry topics remain within the research record. The precise unresolved items below require actual source work before use; they are not alternative current rules.
