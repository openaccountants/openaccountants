# EU and Nordic treaty withholding corridors

## Quick reference

Use the payer’s country first: an arrow means **source payer → treaty-resident beneficial recipient**. The limits below concern source tax on the gross ordinary payment, before any more favourable domestic or EU exemption. They do not determine residence-country tax or clear a filing.

Coverage retains Germany–France, Germany–Netherlands, Germany–Italy, Germany–Spain, France–Italy, France–Spain, France–Belgium, Netherlands–Belgium, Spain–Portugal, Italy–Malta, Austria–Germany, and the Sweden–Denmark, Sweden–Norway and Denmark–Norway corridors. The corresponding reverse direction is identified where materially different.

## Ask the client first

Obtain the payment date, amount and currency; actual payer/source state and treaty residence; legal form and tax status of each company; beneficial owner and any transparent entity; direct/indirect capital and voting percentages; dated holding history and reorganisations; exact debt/IP rights; PE/fixed-base attribution; related-party terms and deductibility; residence certificates and relief documents; and commercial purpose/substance. Treat pension funds, property funds, profit-participating debt and royalties by category separately.

## The method, step by step

1. Classify the payment and its legal source. A royalty label in an invoice does not decide whether copyright, industrial IP, equipment, services or business profits apply.
2. Test domestic exemption first, then the applicable EU relief and treaty entitlement independently. A treaty caps an existing source charge; it does not create a charge where domestic law exempts it.
3. Read the direction and the complete ownership/holding condition in the corridor section. Resolve any protocol, MLI, PE, special-relationship and anti-abuse exception before calculating.
4. Compute gross source tax only after eligibility is supported. A denied Directive exemption does **not** automatically establish a treaty entitlement: the same conduit or abuse facts can prevent treaty relief.
5. Record residence-country relief separately. Confirm the payer’s relief-at-source or refund procedure, evidence and deadline under the applicable national Guide; the table is not a substitute for submitting a form.

## EU Directive quick reference and where it fails

| Framework | Complete starting test | Official basis |
|---|---|---|
| Parent–Subsidiary Directive (PSD) | Minimum 10% capital; voting rights only by the bilateral-agreement option. Both companies need listed forms, qualifying EU residence and liability without option/exemption to the listed corporate tax. Qualifying distributions are exempt from source withholding; this is not an exemption for individuals or portfolio holdings automatically. | [Arts 1–3, 5–7, Annex I](https://publications.europa.eu/resource/cellar/1d1ad431-c8c4-11e4-bbe1-01aa75ed71a1.0020.02/DOC_1) |
| PSD holding period | A member state may refuse relief where the holding is not maintained uninterrupted for at least 2 years. This is a national option, not a universal two-year prerequisite or automatic retroactive refund. | [Art 3(2)(b)](https://publications.europa.eu/resource/cellar/1d1ad431-c8c4-11e4-bbe1-01aa75ed71a1.0020.02/DOC_1) |
| Interest and Royalties Directive (IRD) | Minimum direct 25% capital in either direction, or a third qualifying EU company directly holding at least 25% of each; a state may substitute voting rights. Recipient company/PE must satisfy the beneficial-owner, subject-to-tax, residence and listed-form tests. | [Arts 1, 3 and Annex](https://publications.europa.eu/resource/cellar/108ba38b-c91c-4625-87f8-2a7fb03fbb38.0006.03/DOC_2) |
| IRD holding/PE/payment boundaries | A state may refuse relief without an uninterrupted 2-year association. Paying and receiving PE rules are distinct; third-state PEs are excluded. Only the arm’s-length amount qualifies; profit distributions and specified equity-like/long-dated debt can be excluded. | [Art 1(2)–(10), arts 2–4](https://publications.europa.eu/resource/cellar/108ba38b-c91c-4625-87f8-2a7fb03fbb38.0006.03/DOC_2) |
| Anti-abuse | PSD denies non-genuine arrangements with a main tax-advantage purpose defeating its object; IRD preserves fraud/abuse measures. Unrelated payments and individuals need independent domestic/treaty analysis. | [Art 1(2)–(4)](https://publications.europa.eu/resource/cellar/1d1ad431-c8c4-11e4-bbe1-01aa75ed71a1.0020.02/DOC_1) |

IRD also requires its own [art 5 anti-abuse test](https://publications.europa.eu/resource/cellar/108ba38b-c91c-4625-87f8-2a7fb03fbb38.0006.03/DOC_2). Legal entitlement and administrative relief are separate: [art 1(11)–(16)](https://publications.europa.eu/resource/cellar/108ba38b-c91c-4625-87f8-2a7fb03fbb38.0006.03/DOC_2) permits attestation/prior-decision/refund procedures. Norway is outside these EU Directives; its EEA domestic exemption is a different route. Third-country PEs, post-Brexit UK recipients, below-threshold holdings and non-qualifying funds must not be assumed eligible.

## Nordic Convention: Sweden–Denmark–Norway

The current official consolidation is dated 26 May 2026. The multilateral convention covers Denmark, Finland, Iceland, Norway and Sweden, and separately includes the Faroe Islands within its definition of a contracting state; Denmark expressly excludes the Faroe Islands and Greenland; the mainland Nordic corridors here retain the original scope. It is not an EU Directive.

| Payment direction | Ordinary dividend cap | Qualifying company dividend | Ordinary interest / royalties | Official basis |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sweden → Denmark | 15% | 0%: beneficial-owner company other than partnership/estate, directly owning at least 10% payer capital | 0% / 0%, subject to effective PE/fixed-base connection and related-party excess | [Arts 10(3), 11–12](https://www.regjeringen.no/contentassets/89a3e0b0ec98420b955ff0e65502676d/nordiske-avtale-konsolidert-26-mai-2026-.pdf) |
| Sweden → Norway | 15% | 0%: beneficial-owner company other than partnership/estate, directly owning at least 10% payer capital | 0% / 0%, subject to effective PE/fixed-base connection and related-party excess | [Arts 10(3), 11–12](https://www.regjeringen.no/contentassets/89a3e0b0ec98420b955ff0e65502676d/nordiske-avtale-konsolidert-26-mai-2026-.pdf) |
| Denmark → Norway | 15% | 0%: beneficial-owner company other than partnership/estate, directly owning at least 10% payer capital | 0% / 0%, subject to effective PE/fixed-base connection and related-party excess | [Arts 10(3), 11–12](https://www.regjeringen.no/contentassets/89a3e0b0ec98420b955ff0e65502676d/nordiske-avtale-konsolidert-26-mai-2026-.pdf) |

Article 10(3) has its own look-through rule for corporate holdings through partnerships; do not substitute a universal MLI holding period. Article 26(4) contains the treaty’s PPT. ## Worked case

Assume an ordinary EUR 100,000 dividend is paid by a German capital company to a qualifying French capital company in 2026. The recipient owns 12% capital throughout the required 365-day period including payment; treaty residence/beneficial entitlement, documentary requirements and anti-abuse tests are satisfied, there is no PE connection, property fund or deductible profit-participating instrument, and no more favourable domestic/PSD relief is established for this illustration.

The German-source treaty cap is EUR 5,000 and the net payment at that cap is EUR 95,000. If the recipient instead has only 8% throughout, the ordinary cap is EUR 15,000 and the net is EUR 85,000. Difference: EUR 10,000. These are separate factual alternatives, not a recommendation to restructure. Actual source tax may be lower under domestic/PSD relief; French taxation/credits are excluded. [Art 9(2),(5) and MLI 8](https://www.bundesfinanzministerium.de/Content/DE/Standardartikel/Themen/Steuern/Internationales_Steuerrecht/Staatenbezogene_Informationen/Laender_A_Z/Frankreich/2025-01-01-Abkommen-Synthetisierter-Text.pdf?__blob=publicationFile&v=3).

```oa-check
name: Germany to France dividend source caps
inputs:
  gross: 100000
  qualifying_rate: 0.05
  ordinary_rate: 0.15
steps:
  - qualifying_cap: gross * qualifying_rate
  - qualifying_net: gross - qualifying_cap
  - ordinary_cap: gross * ordinary_rate
  - ordinary_net: gross - ordinary_cap
  - difference: ordinary_cap - qualifying_cap
expected:
  qualifying_cap: 5000
  qualifying_net: 95000
  ordinary_cap: 15000
  ordinary_net: 85000
  difference: 10000
```

## When to refuse or refer

Refer to a treaty/withholding specialist where residence, beneficial ownership, transparent-entity treatment, fund status, source/PE attribution, abuse, historical credit refunds or a payment category is disputed. Do not issue a withholding instruction until entitlement and the local relief procedure are supported. Never assume a treaty rate is a complete recipient tax rate, and never promise a Directive fallback after an abuse denial.
