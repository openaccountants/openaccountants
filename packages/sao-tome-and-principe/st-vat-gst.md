# Sao Tome and Principe VAT v0.4

> **Not yet approved. Held: the VAT rules here come from a bill.** Tax year 2026. Every VAT rule below is taken from the final committee text of Bill 05/XI/2.a/2019, which the Assembly published on 15 October 2019 with a committee report saying the text still had to go to the plenary final global vote. The enacted Code, its promulgation and publication, its commencement and its later amendments were not read on an official host (the Finance Ministry download links returned 404 and the newer Ministry site refused access), so no rule taken from the bill is usable for a 2026 return until it is checked against the enacted text. The 2025 Budget Law rows are published law for 2025; whether they continue in 2026 is not confirmed. Held points sit on the `Not yet confirmed:` line. No accountant has signed this version.

## Who this applies to

In the 2019 bill text (held), taxable persons are any person or entity that independently carries on economic activity, including production, trade, services, extraction, farming, forestry, livestock and fishing, and government bodies and other public bodies so far as they make taxable supplies ([VAT Code art. 3(1)](https://www2.camara.leg.br/saotomeeprincipe/diarios-da-an/xi-legislatura-22-11-2018/ii-serie-a/2.a-sessao/DAN30-IIS.pdf)). Employees are not taxable persons (art. 3(2)). The Code applies to the national territory (art. 2).

The older skill named the approving law Law 13/2019 and said Law 2/2023 and Decree-Law 12/2023 amended the Code; no official text read names these numbers, so they are unverified. The bill's approving law set entry into force on 1 March 2020 (approving law art. 6). The report printed with the published 2025 Budget Law says "Com a entrada em vigor da Lei do IVA em Junho de 2023" (with the entry into force of the VAT law in June 2023), and the 2026 Budget Bill report says the same; the operative text of the 2025 Budget Law (art. 8(15) and (17)) also refers to the Código do IVA and to the normal and special VAT regimes. These statements show that a VAT law applies, as the older skill said (1 June 2023), but they are not the enacted text: the start date and the Code's content stay held.

**Taxes replaced (held, bill text).** The bill's approving law revokes the consumption tax of Decree-Law 20/76 and the consumption tax on services of Decree-Law 35/2000, with their amendments, and the stamp duty provisions on invoicing (approving law art. 2). The 2025 Budget Law report says the consumption tax left the tax system when the VAT law entered into force in June 2023. The older skill also listed taxes on alcohol, spirits and cigarettes, telecoms and hotel tax, and restaurant tax; those were not in the text read and stay held.

## The base

Held, not usable: every point in this section is the 2019 bill text (Bill 05/XI/2.a/2019), not the enacted Code.

- **Tax due** for a month is taxable operations times the rate, less deductible input tax under arts. 21 to 26 ([art. 28(3)](https://www2.camara.leg.br/saotomeeprincipe/diarios-da-an/xi-legislatura-22-11-2018/ii-serie-a/2.a-sessao/DAN30-IIS.pdf)).
- **Input tax.** The right to deduct arises when the input tax becomes chargeable (art. 21(1)). No deduction for pleasure boats, helicopters and aircraft, or tobacco (art. 22(1)); none for non-residents on the simplified registration (art. 22(2)); 50% for the items in the table (art. 22(3)).
- **Exemption regime.** Turnover of associated persons counts towards the Db 1,000,000 test (art. 44(3)). A business on the exemption regime has no right to deduct (art. 44(4)). It must still file start and cessation declarations, pay the art. 48 tax, write "IVA - Regime especial de isenção" on its invoices and keep purchase and turnover records (art. 47).
- **Leaving and joining.** Opting out of the exemption is by electronic start or change declaration; the business must then stay in the normal regime for at least five years (art. 46(3)). A normal-regime business that falls below the limit and wants the exemption files a change declaration in January of the next year and adjusts input tax on remaining stock (art. 45). The Direccao dos Impostos can call in a business it believes has passed the limit, and tax is due from the month after the declaration (art. 49).
- **Internal exemptions** (art. 17): life insurance and reinsurance; financial intermediation (Annex II), except where a specific fee is charged; investment gold; sales of residential property other than the first sale; residential lettings.
- **Import exemptions** (art. 18): imports of goods whose home supply is exempt; gold, coins and notes imported by the Central Bank; imports under treaties and diplomatic relations. Special customs regimes are in art. 19.
- **Exports** (art. 20): goods sent abroad; goods for hydrocarbon operators in the economic zone; ship and aircraft supplies; international transport of people, goods and mail from abroad.
- **Reduced and zero rates.** The older skill said reduced and zero rates exist; the bill text read has one rate plus the half-base rule, with art. 20 relief for exports.

## Penalties and interest

Held, bill text: if a monthly return is missing, the Direccao dos Impostos assesses the tax from past returns or other information, including income tax data, after notice; the tax is due within 30 days of notice (arts. 28(4), 50(1) to (3)). Non-payment starts the procedure in art. 79 of the Tax Process Code and can lead to suspension of taxable-person status or a frozen bank account until paid (art. 50(3) and (4)). Filing the missing return and paying the tax with compensatory interest within that period cancels the assessment, without prejudice to any penalty ([VAT Code art. 50(5)](https://www2.camara.leg.br/saotomeeprincipe/diarios-da-an/xi-legislatura-22-11-2018/ii-serie-a/2.a-sessao/DAN30-IIS.pdf)). Omissions or errors in a return are corrected by additional assessment (art. 51). Compensatory interest, from the General Tax Code (published law), runs at the Central Bank reference rate plus three percentage points a year ([General Tax Code art. 38](https://ministerio.financas.st/phocadownload/Imposto/legislacao/Lei_N6_2007_Lei_Que_Aprova_Codigo_Geral_Tributario.pdf)). Penalty amounts were not read.

## Cross-border points

Held, not usable: the points below are the 2019 bill text.

- **Place of supply of services** (art. 10(1)): for a taxable buyer, where the buyer has its seat or the establishment receiving the service; for a non-taxable buyer, where the supplier is established. Services on property in STP, work on goods in STP, cultural, sporting and educational events in STP, hotel and restaurant services in STP and some transport are always in STP (art. 10(2)).
- **Reverse charge.** A taxable buyer accounts for the tax on services located in STP when the supplier is non-resident with no permanent establishment ([VAT Code art. 27(2)](https://www2.camara.leg.br/saotomeeprincipe/diarios-da-an/xi-legislatura-22-11-2018/ii-serie-a/2.a-sessao/DAN30-IIS.pdf)).
- **Non-resident suppliers** without a permanent establishment file electronically and appoint a resident taxable representative, jointly liable, unless they use the simplified registration set by the Direccao dos Impostos (art. 30).
- **Imports** are taxed on the importer (art. 27(3)).

## Two worked examples that recompute

**Example 1, held case: normal month under the 2019 bill text.** Not usable for a 2026 return until the enacted Code is read. Standard sales of Db 100,000, Annex I food invoices of Db 20,000, deductible input tax of Db 8,000.

```oa-check
name: Monthly VAT
inputs:
  standard: 100000
  food: 20000
  input_vat: 8000
steps:
  - output: standard * 0.15 + food * 0.5 * 0.15
  - payable: output - input_vat
expected:
  output: 16500
  payable: 8500
```

**Example 2, held case, decision: stay on the exemption regime or opt out.** Under the 2019 bill text, not usable until the enacted Code is read. A trader with sales of Db 800,000 pays 7% with no deduction. Compare with normal VAT on the margin, if customers can bear the 15% on top: with purchases carrying VAT of Db 500,000, normal VAT is lower by Db 11,000 a year. Opting out locks the trader into the normal regime for at least five years (art. 46(3)), with monthly returns. The art. 48 text is the 2019 bill version, so confirm the enacted and current art. 48 first.

```oa-check
name: Exemption regime against normal VAT
inputs:
  sales: 800000
  purchases: 500000
steps:
  - special: sales * 0.07
  - normal: (sales - purchases) * 0.15
  - saving: special - normal
expected:
  special: 56000
  normal: 45000
  saving: 11000
```

## Ask the client first

- Turnover in the last calendar year, and legal form
- Any sales of Annex I food
- Any services bought from non-residents
- Imports or exports, and any oil and gas customers
- Whether it opted out of the exemption regime, and when
- Whether it buys cocoa, coffee, vanilla or other crops from small sellers
