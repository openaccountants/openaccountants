# Isle of Man Company Formation & Entity Choice v0.2

> **General reference only.** Prepare a formation and compliance workpaper for professional review before incorporation or filing.

## Scope

This Guide covers the existing 2026/27 entity-choice, registration and recurring compliance topics below. It does not establish the unconfirmed capital, audit, excepted-company or protected-cell conditions listed under Sources.

## Ask the client first

- Which legal form, business activity and regulatory permissions are intended?
- Who will own, vote, control and manage the entity, and where do they live and work?
- Which director, secretary, registered agent and registered office arrangements are proposed?
- Is this a new incorporation, foreign registration, re-registration or restoration?
- What are the incorporation anniversary, return made-up date, accounting period and current filing status?
- Are there staff, taxable supplies, a relevant substance activity, charity registration or an excepted-company claim?

## The method, step by step

1. Choose the actual legal regime and check activity/licensing and tax residence separately.
2. Obtain name approval and use the appropriate incorporation documents, qualified officers, registered office and licensed agent where required.
3. Reconcile subscribers, capital contributions and ownership/control evidence to those documents; apply the beneficial-owner procedure below.
4. Select the filing service and correct fee category. Retain the Registry's incorporation decision and filing receipt.
5. Diary Registry annual returns separately from income tax, substance, employer and VAT workstreams. Use the topic Guides for their detailed conditions.

### Entity types and documents

The Isle of Man has two company law regimes running side by side: companies under the Companies Act 2006 (often called New Manx Vehicles) and companies under the Companies Acts 1931 to 2004. It also offers limited liability companies, foundations and limited partnerships. The Companies Registry receives filings and fees. Income tax treats 1931 Act and 2006 Act companies the same. Amounts are in pounds sterling (GBP).

- **2006 Act company: who can incorporate:** Only the person named in the memorandum as the first registered agent can file the application. A registered agent must hold a Class 4 (corporate services) licence from the Financial Services Authority under the Financial Services Act 2008 ([Companies Registry, 2006 Act Companies](https://www.gov.im/categories/business-and-industries/companies-registry/registries/2006-act-companies)).
- **2006 Act company: memorandum and articles:** The memorandum sets out the name, first registered office, first registered agent, each subscriber's name and address, and the shares each takes and pays for. Companies may file their own articles or adopt the Model Articles where prescribed for their company type under part 6(1). Names need approval first; an approval lasts 3 months (ss.11 and 12 govern names) ([Companies Registry, 2006 Act Companies](https://www.gov.im/categories/business-and-industries/companies-registry/registries/2006-act-companies)).
- **2006 Act company: officers and members:** At least one director, appointed within one month of incorporation; the director can be a corporate body (s.91(7)(a)). A single member, individual or corporate. A registered agent at all times (s.74(3)). A seal is optional ([Companies Registry, 2006 Act Companies](https://www.gov.im/categories/business-and-industries/companies-registry/registries/2006-act-companies)).
- **1931 Act company:** Public or private. At least two directors, who must be individuals (corporate directors are not permitted for these companies), and at least one company secretary. Under Companies Act 1982 s.19(4), the directors of a public company having share capital must appoint a secretary with adequate knowledge and experience and a listed qualification or experience route. This statutory qualification test is not a blanket rule for every private company; regulated entities require their separate checks. One of the directors may also be the secretary, and a corporate secretary is allowed subject to other restrictions. Under s.19(3), a requirement for an act by both director and secretary cannot be satisfied by the same person acting in both capacities. The registered office must be in the Island. A public company must have at least two members and delivers audited accounts each year with its annual return; a private company does not, unless it is a subsidiary of a Manx public company. Changes to directors go on form 9N within 1 month ([Companies Registry, 1931 Act Companies](https://www.gov.im/categories/business-and-industries/companies-registry/registries/1931-act-companies/); [Companies Act 1982, s.19](https://legislation.gov.im/cms/images/LEGISLATION/PRINCIPAL/1982/1982-0002/1982-0002_4.pdf)).
- **Limited liability company (LLC):** Formed under the Limited Liability Companies Act 1996 by filing Articles of Organisation, form L6 and the fee. It is a legal person; members' liability is limited to their capital contribution; management sits with the members; the name ends in "Limited Liability Company", "LLC" or "L.L.C.". Its profits are taxed as the members' income ([Companies Registry, LLCs](https://www.gov.im/categories/business-and-industries/companies-registry/registries/limited-liability-companies/); [im-corporate-income-tax](im-corporate-income-tax.md)).
- **Other vehicles:** Foundations under the Foundations Act 2011 (established on form MF01), limited partnerships (Partnership Act 1909), business names, and foreign companies registering a place of business in the Island, each with its own Registry fees. Protected cell companies are formed under the Protected Cell Companies Act 2004, whose Eligibility Regulations 2010 the Registry lists; their eligibility conditions are not covered here ([Registry fees](https://www.gov.im/categories/business-and-industries/companies-registry/fees/), [beneficial ownership](https://www.gov.im/categories/business-and-industries/companies-registry/beneficial-ownership/), [Acts and Regulations](https://www.gov.im/categories/business-and-industries/companies-registry/acts-and-regulations/)).
- **Incorporation fees and timing:** Standard incorporation within 48 hours of receipt of the documents: GBP 100. Two-hour service (documents in before 2:30pm on a business day): GBP 250. While-you-wait service (documents in before 4pm): GBP 500. The same three fees apply to 1931 Act and 2006 Act companies, LLCs, foundations and limited partnerships. A foreign company registers for GBP 100 and files an annual declaration costing GBP 380. Fees may not be refunded if the registration does not go through ([Registry fees](https://www.gov.im/categories/business-and-industries/companies-registry/fees/); [2006 company fee schedule](https://www.gov.im/categories/business-and-industries/companies-registry/fees/2006-companies/)).
- **Annual return:** Every company files an annual return, a snapshot on the anniversary of incorporation, within 1 month of its made-up date, whether or not it trades. 2006 Act companies use form IMAR, 1931 Act companies form AR, foreign companies form ARF. Failing to file can lead to prosecution of the officers or striking off ([File an annual return](https://www.gov.im/categories/business-and-industries/companies-registry/file-an-annual-return/?iomg-device=Mobile)).
- **Annual return fees:** Standard companies: GBP 380 on time; GBP 480 in the 1 month and 1 day to 3 months band measured from the return's made-up date; GBP 630 more than 3 months after that date. Excepted companies and LLCs: GBP 95, GBP 195, GBP 345. Companies registered as charities: GBP 0, GBP 100, GBP 250. The same made-up-date clock applies to the annual-return scales above. Foundations follow the standard scale (GBP 380, 480, 630). Limited partnerships file an annual statement at GBP 95, GBP 195, GBP 345. Foreign companies file an annual declaration at GBP 380 ([Registry fees](https://www.gov.im/categories/business-and-industries/companies-registry/fees/); [File an annual return](https://www.gov.im/categories/business-and-industries/companies-registry/file-an-annual-return/?iomg-device=Mobile)).
- **Late fees on other documents:** GBP 100 if filed 1 month and 1 day to 3 months after the due date; GBP 250 if more than 3 months. A document is registered only once checked; a rejected document can attract late fees ([Registry fees](https://www.gov.im/categories/business-and-industries/companies-registry/fees/); [2006 company fee schedule](https://www.gov.im/categories/business-and-industries/companies-registry/fees/2006-companies/)).
- **Other Registry fees (examples):** Registration of a charge GBP 25; re-registration of a 1931 Act company as a 2006 Act company GBP 100; application to restore a 2006 Act company under s.187 GBP 1,185 ([Registry fees](https://www.gov.im/categories/business-and-industries/companies-registry/fees/); [2006 company fee schedule](https://www.gov.im/categories/business-and-industries/companies-registry/fees/2006-companies/)).
- **Beneficial ownership:** Under the Beneficial Ownership Act 2017 every legal entity in scope must have a nominated officer: an Island-resident individual or a corporate services licence holder. Changes of nominated officer are notified within 21 days on form NO/CSP. The nominated officer files the registrable beneficial owners on the Isle of Man Database of Beneficial Ownership. From 2026 a registrable beneficial owner is a natural person who owns or controls 25% or more, or who controls the entity by other means (no threshold). Where there is none, the nominated officer files a statement and the senior managing official's details (Beneficial Ownership Act 2017 (Amendment) Order 2026; Beneficial Ownership Information Regulations 2026) ([Beneficial ownership](https://www.gov.im/categories/business-and-industries/companies-registry/beneficial-ownership/)).
- **Annual corporate tax return:** Every corporate taxpayer files an income tax return for each accounting period by the day after 12 months from its end, online, and pays any tax by the same date, even at the 0% rate. A company with no income still files and states why (for example dormant) ([ITA 1970, ss.A66, B66](https://legislation.gov.im/cms/images/LEGISLATION/PRINCIPAL/1970/1970-0003/1970-0003_62.pdf); [GN58](https://www.gov.im/media/1386923/gn58-company-income-tax-return-20241231_compressed.pdf); [im-corporate-income-tax](im-corporate-income-tax.md)).
- **Economic substance:** A company carrying on a relevant activity must meet the economic substance requirements in Part 6A of the Income Tax Act 1970 and answer the substance questions in its return (s.A66(4A)). The rules were extended to partnerships and LLCs in 2021 ([Income Tax Division, Economic Substance](https://www.gov.im/categories/tax-vat-and-your-money/income-tax-and-national-insurance/economic-substance/)).
- **Employer and VAT registration:** A company that pays staff registers as an employer within 14 days of the first payment (form T30c). A company whose taxable supplies pass GBP 90,000 in 12 months registers for VAT with Customs and Immigration ([im-payroll-social](im-payroll-social.md); [isle-of-man-vat](isle-of-man-vat.md)).

## Worked cases

### Standard company: two filing bands

Assume an ordinary standard 2006 Act company, with no excepted or charity status. If its annual return is accepted within one month of its made-up date, the fee is GBP 380. If instead accepted two months after that same made-up date, the published middle band is GBP 480, an increase of GBP 100. This is the fee charged in that band, not GBP 480 plus another GBP 100. An extra document-rejection delay can change the band. [Registry fee schedule](https://www.gov.im/categories/business-and-industries/companies-registry/fees/2006-companies/).

## When to refuse or refer

Do not complete formation on an assumed agent licence, officer qualification, beneficial-owner category, fee exception or regulated permission. Refer unresolved control, foreign management, public offers, foundations, protected cells, accounts/audit and economic-substance cases. Hold the specific unconfirmed claims under Sources out of usable advice until their operative conditions are established.
