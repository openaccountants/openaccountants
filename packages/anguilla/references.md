# Anguilla pack: sources and remaining coverage

This pack contains two source-cited drafts for General Services Tax and monthly employer and employee Universal Social Levy. Both await professional review and period-specific legal verification. This register also covers selected income tax scope, tax residence and Social Security contributions.

The GST guide describes the enacted 2025 baseline. The USL guide uses an unofficial online consolidation hosted by the government, stating that it shows the law as at 31 December 2022. Sources were read on 11 October 2026, but complete operative provisions and later amendment chains remain unverified. Each companion stops an engagement calculation until its applicable law and source conditions are established.

## Income tax and the tax residence programme

The Government's 2021 GST white paper describes Anguilla as having no general personal or business income tax. It identifies separate charges on labour. Select Anguilla, the government-appointed residence agency, describes the same ordinary income tax scope. Its separate tax residence programme requires an annual lump-sum payment. Check Universal Social Levy and Social Security separately for work and self-employment. [Government white paper, pp 6–7][I1] · [Select Anguilla, Business & Tax][I2] · [Inland Revenue, Universal Social Levy][U2]

The Government's 2026 estimates group the Bank Deposit Levy, ANGLEC Gross Revenue Tax and Petroleum Gross Revenue Tax under corporate revenue within 'Taxes on income, profits and capital gains'. The estimates show projected receipts for the Bank Deposit Levy in 2026. Check the operative charging instruments for a business in those sectors. A budget heading or a zero estimate does not settle a company's liability. [2026 estimates, recurrent revenue][I3]

The Economic Residence Act and its regulations were read in the government-hosted revision showing the law as at 31 December 2022. Section 4 applies to applicants for Residence for Tax Purposes. The regulations' Schedule prescribes an annual lump-sum tax of US$75,000 and US$400,000 of approved real estate for up to a family of 4. Section 4 requires genuine links to Anguilla, at least 45 days in Anguilla each calendar year and an annual declaration of fewer than 183 days in each other country and that the person's centre of vital interest is in Anguilla. The application goes through an authorised agent. Section 5 waives the s 4(1)(b) property condition for applicants qualifying under the specified combined route. [Anguilla Economic Residence Act, ss 4–5][I4] · [Economic Residence Regulations, Schedule][I5]

The 2022 text requires first-year prepayment to Inland Revenue, with possible proration based on final approval. It requires payment on 31 March in subsequent years and annual certificate renewal for at least 4 taxation years after the year of approval. Inland Revenue issues the certificate once the conditions for final approval under s 11(6) are met. Confirm the 2024 amendment, subsequent law, the current property and fee schedule and agency instructions before applying these rules. A certificate does not determine residence or income tax in another country. [Anguilla Economic Residence Act, ss 4(2)–(3), 11(4) and (6)][I4] · [Select Anguilla, Tax Residency][I6] · [OECD, Tax residency][I7]

## Social Security contribution guidance

The Social Security Board gives a combined employee and employer rate of 11.5% of wages from 1 January to 31 December 2026. It describes a 5.75% employee deduction and an equal employer share, subject to the applicable wage ceiling. Its employer guidance calls for a CR-1 contribution certificate with each payment. Use the Board's current form. [Social Security Board rate notice][S1] · [Employer contributions][S2] · [Board forms][S3]

Confirm the operative contribution regulations, current ceilings, assessable wages, age and other exemptions, self-employed and special classes and remittance requirements before calculating or filing. The Board's employee page directs readers to check current ceilings. [Employee contributions][S4]

## Verification scope

The GST Act's 75 OCR page segments were retained and checked for sequential presence. Selected original pages were visually read for the rate, registration, period, commencement, invoice evidence, input allocation and payment provisions. Its printed section order differs from numeric order on physical pages 40 and 41. The original page images confirm that sequence. The complete smaller GST regulations, Goods Tax Act and return extraction were read. The USL consolidation produced native text with no pages flagged for OCR, and its complete extract was read.

Both companion guides provide 5 hypothetical positions. The dated verification log records the review and remaining gaps. The 2026 Social Security guidance and the tax residence references do not qualify a current engagement calculation or application.
