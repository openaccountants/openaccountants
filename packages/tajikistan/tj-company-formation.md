# Tajikistan company formation and entity choice v0.1

> **General reference only.** This skill prepares a working paper for review by a qualified Tajikistan professional. Confirm the facts, source version and filing procedure before filing, paying or taking a tax position.

**Built from the law. Not yet approved.** Tax year 2026; checked on 2026-11-15.


## Scope

Use for an ordinary limited liability company (LLC) and the state-registration handoff. An LLC is a company whose participants generally risk their contributions rather than answer for its obligations; the unpaid-contribution and other statutory liability exceptions still matter. Formation in regulated banking, insurance or investment sectors needs the sector law. [LLC Law arts. 1-2](https://investcom.tj/uploads/post_docs/492/ZYRjrIc2yg5e.pdf).

Choose the legal vehicle before preparing this dossier: LLC, joint-stock company, individual entrepreneur, or foreign branch/representative office. This skill executes the ordinary LLC route only. The Registration Law separately covers entrepreneurs and foreign branches/representative offices; it is not a substitute for their legal-form, licensing, immigration or tax analysis. [Registration Law arts. 1-2, 11(1)(4)](https://investcom.tj/uploads/post_docs/511/FtMoBDqW4mvU.pdf) and [LLC Law art. 2](https://investcom.tj/uploads/post_docs/492/ZYRjrIc2yg5e.pdf).


## Ask the client first

- Proposed legal form, activities, licences and whether the activity is a regulated sector.
- Every founder and ultimate beneficial owner, citizenship/incorporation country, ownership chain and representatives.
- Proposed name, Tajik registered address, executive manager and evidence of authority/residence/work permission where relevant.
- Capital, each founder's nominal share, cash/non-cash contributions, valuation and payment timetable.
- Foreign documents, translation/notarisation and treaty-based legalisation exceptions.
- Intended registration date, employees, projected income, VAT exposure and accounting framework.


## The method, step by step


### Select founders, governance and capital

| Requirement or boundary | Ordinary LLC rule | Official provision |
|---|---|---|
| Founders | 1 founder permitted; maximum 30 participants (in words on the page). A single-member company cannot itself be the sole participant | [Art. 8(2)-(3)](https://investcom.tj/uploads/post_docs/492/ZYRjrIc2yg5e.pdf) |
| Minimum charter capital | TJS 500 (in words on the page), in national currency; no approximate US-dollar equivalent is a legal threshold | [Art. 16(2)](https://investcom.tj/uploads/post_docs/492/ZYRjrIc2yg5e.pdf) |
| Contribution deadline | Each founder pays the full agreed contribution within the constitutive timetable, no later than 1 year after state registration (in words on the page) | [Art. 18(1)-(2)](https://investcom.tj/uploads/post_docs/492/ZYRjrIc2yg5e.pdf) |
| Registration charge | TJS 0, derived from the statutory no-state-duty/no-registration-payment rule; external professional/translation costs are separate | [Registration Law art. 6(1)](https://investcom.tj/uploads/post_docs/511/FtMoBDqW4mvU.pdf) |
| Complete incorporation application | Registration and certificate within 24 hours after submission of the statutory documents; not the old 5-working-day estimate | [Registration Law art. 10(1)-(2)](https://investcom.tj/uploads/post_docs/511/FtMoBDqW4mvU.pdf) |

Individuals and legal entities can be participants, subject to art. 8 restrictions. The ordinary sole executive director is a natural person elected by the participants; the law separately permits a management delegation arrangement if the charter allows it. Do not equate a legal-entity shareholder with an ordinary legal-entity director. Obtain local legal review of any delegated/collective management arrangement. [LLC Law arts. 8, 45-47](https://investcom.tj/uploads/post_docs/492/ZYRjrIc2yg5e.pdf).

Founders approve the charter; a sole founder uses its approved charter, while multiple founders also conclude the founding agreement. The documents set shares, contributions, timing and governance. Non-cash contributions require the statutory valuation process, including independent expert valuation under art. 17(2). Prepare these corporate documents even though the charter is not submitted with the registration application. [LLC Law arts. 13-14, 17](https://investcom.tj/uploads/post_docs/492/ZYRjrIc2yg5e.pdf) and [Registration Law art. 11(3)](https://investcom.tj/uploads/post_docs/511/FtMoBDqW4mvU.pdf).


### Build the statutory dossier

Use the tax authority as the single-window registrar at the company address. Art. 4 permits paper or electronic documents. The approved application states legal form, full/state-language company name, address, formation/reorganisation method, founders/executive and beneficial-owner information, principal activities, date and applicant signature. [Registration Law arts. 4-5, 11(1)(1)](https://investcom.tj/uploads/post_docs/511/FtMoBDqW4mvU.pdf).

Attach the founder decision/minutes, executive/applicant identity and authority documents, founder identity information, beneficial-owner evidence, and registered-address evidence. Check art. 11(1) for the conditional additions: foreign legal-entity register extract, legal-entity founder tax-clearance evidence, shareholder-register information, free-economic-zone administration certificate and media-regulator opinion as applicable. This is a conditional list, not a request for every item for every LLC. [Registration Law art. 11(1)](https://investcom.tj/uploads/post_docs/511/FtMoBDqW4mvU.pdf).

For a foreign executive, the statutory dossier includes evidence permitting residence and work, including the specified visa/registration/residence documentation with validity of at least 3 months (in words on the page). Foreign-founder documents require the stated Tajik translations and notarised translations; foreign register extracts require legalisation or apostille unless an applicable recognised treaty removes that requirement. Provide offshore-founder beneficial-owner information where art. 11(8) applies. The old no-resident-director sentence does not remove these document checks. [Registration Law art. 11(1)(3), (5)-(8)](https://investcom.tj/uploads/post_docs/511/FtMoBDqW4mvU.pdf).

Do not add a charter-copy or state-fee receipt to the mandatory ordinary submission list: the law says the charter is not required for submission and registration is free. Evidence of formation of capital is not required at registration, but must be presented to the registrar within 1 year; failure to form the minimum capital can lead to liquidation proceedings. Keep this obligation on the post-registration checklist. [Registration Law arts. 6, 11(3)-(4), (9)](https://investcom.tj/uploads/post_docs/511/FtMoBDqW4mvU.pdf).


### Submit, obtain the certificate and reconcile registrations

Submit the complete paper dossier to the competent tax registrar, or use its current approved electronic process after obtaining the live instructions. Retain a dated receipt. The 24-hour rule runs from submission of the statutory documents; if the registrar fails to decide or provide the document within that period, art. 10(2) requires immediate entry and issue/sending of the certificate. Keep any refusal and its reasons for local legal review. [Registration Law arts. 4, 10](https://investcom.tj/uploads/post_docs/511/FtMoBDqW4mvU.pdf).

Single-window registration is conducted without a legal examination of submitted documents, but the applicant remains responsible for their accuracy and completeness. This does not waive beneficial-owner, immigration, bank, licensing or professional due diligence. Obtain the registration certificate, unified identifier and confirmations covering tax, statistics and state social-insurance registration; reconcile names, addresses and identifiers before opening the company records. [Registration Law art. 4(1)-(4)](https://investcom.tj/uploads/post_docs/511/FtMoBDqW4mvU.pdf).

After registration, retain the charter, founding agreement/decision, capital records and certificate; calendar the contribution/evidence deadlines and open accounting records. Annual corporate returns and accounting statements, including the balance sheet, are covered by [tj-corporate-income-tax](tj-corporate-income-tax.md); use [tj-vat-gst](tj-vat-gst.md) and [tj-payroll-social](tj-payroll-social.md) where VAT or employees create duties. Registration does not establish a tax exemption. [Tax Code art. 241(8)](https://www.andoz.tj/docs/kodex/TaxKodex.pdf).


## Worked cases


### Sole founder: minimum capital is not the registration fee

Illustrative sole-founder LLC chooses the statutory minimum TJS 500 capital and has paid TJS 300 by an internal review date before the payment deadline. TJS 200 remains to contribute. Registration duty is zero, so the TJS 500 capital must not be described as a fee paid to the registrar. Retain evidence and ensure full payment within the statutory timetable.

```oa-check
name: Sole-founder outstanding contribution
inputs:
  agreed_capital: 500
  capital_paid: 300
steps:
  outstanding: max(0, agreed_capital - capital_paid)
  statutory_registration_charge: 0
  remaining_capital_and_registration_charge: outstanding + statutory_registration_charge
expected:
  outstanding: 200
  statutory_registration_charge: 0
  remaining_capital_and_registration_charge: 200
```


### Multiple founders: reconcile each share separately

Illustrative TJS 1,000 cash charter capital split 60% / 40% under the founding documents. Founder A owes TJS 600 and has paid TJS 450; founder B owes and has paid TJS 400. Total outstanding is TJS 150. Total paid already exceeds the statutory minimum, but that does not discharge founder A's unpaid agreed share. Obtain the founding agreement and track the full contribution deadline.

```oa-check
name: Two-founder contribution reconciliation
inputs:
  capital: 1000
  share_a: 0.60
  paid_a: 450
  paid_b: 400
steps:
  nominal_a: capital * share_a
  nominal_b: capital - nominal_a
  outstanding_a: max(0, nominal_a - paid_a)
  outstanding_b: max(0, nominal_b - paid_b)
  total_outstanding: outstanding_a + outstanding_b
expected:
  nominal_a: 600
  nominal_b: 400
  outstanding_a: 150
  outstanding_b: 0
  total_outstanding: 150
```


## When to refuse or refer

Refer joint-stock/entrepreneur/foreign-branch legal-form comparisons, regulated businesses, complex/foreign ownership, disputed beneficial owners, in-kind contributions, delegated management, immigration and licensing. Do not certify a document's validity merely because registration is declaratory, or promise bank opening within the registration period. Stop if the statutory dossier is incomplete.
