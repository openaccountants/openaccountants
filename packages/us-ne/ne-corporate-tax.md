# Nebraska corporate and franchise tax, 2026

> **Built from the law. Not yet approved.** Written by Codex; this version has not been reviewed by an accountant. General reference only. Under Circular 230 section 10.37, apply the cited authorities to the actual facts and obtain qualified professional review before filing or acting.

**Answer first:** Nebraska taxes ordinary 2026 corporate taxable income at 4.55%, replacing 2025's 5.20%. Financial institutions have a separate deposits-based franchise regime; a blanket no-franchise statement is not sufficient. [77-2734.02(1)(f)-(h)](https://nebraskalegislature.gov/laws/statutes.php?statute=77-2734.02); [actual 2026 corporate estimate booklet](https://revenue.nebraska.gov/sites/default/files/doc/tax-forms/2025/f_1120N-ES.pdf); [77-3802](https://nebraskalegislature.gov/laws/statutes.php?statute=77-3802).

## Scope

Ordinary C-corporation computation once Nebraska nexus, income base and factor are established. Federal computation belongs to `us-form-1120-c-corp`; P.L.86-272 boundaries to `us-pl-86-272-income-tax-nexus`; employer taxes to `ne-payroll`. Unitary/combined reporting, industry/special sourcing, financial-institution franchise base/limitation, insurance and NOL/credit calculations are partial specialist areas.

## Ask the client first

1. Entity/federal classification, Nebraska business/source-income activities, tax-year beginning date and federal taxable income/return?
2. Related corporations, unitary activities, consolidated federal return, ownership and prior state filing method?
3. Federal-to-Nebraska additions/subtractions, asset/research basis, NOL/capital-loss carryovers and credit certifications?
4. Nebraska/everywhere sales, delivery/service receipts, nonapportionable income and actual supported factor?
5. Financial-institution/insurance status, estimates/prior-year tax, state identification/e-file mandate and extension evidence?

## The method, step by step

1. **Choose the taxpayer/regime.** The opened **2025-2026 filing cycle** booklet requires 1120N for corporations doing business or having Nebraska-source income and subject to federal corporate tax, including relevant cooperatives and unrelated-business-income entities. Ordinary S corporations use 1120-SN; financial institutions use 1120NF. Investigate nexus and P.L.86-272 before concluding source sales alone require income tax. No sales/use nexus threshold is imported into corporate tax. [2025 corporate booklet, Who must file](https://revenue.nebraska.gov/sites/default/files/doc/tax-forms/2025/f_Corp_Booklet.pdf).
2. **Reconcile federal taxable income.** Add/subtract Nebraska modifications under current authorities, with state asset/research and loss schedules. The dated booklet uses Schedule A additions/subtractions and expressly says federal deduction for state income tax is not an ordinary addback. Section 77-2716 includes bond and other modifications; section 77-27,242 provides conditional state expensing without duplication. Do not use historical bonus-addback years as current law. Final 2026 corporate general instructions were not found; detailed line map remains partial. [77-2716](https://nebraskalegislature.gov/laws/statutes.php?statute=77-2716); [77-27,242](https://nebraskalegislature.gov/laws/statutes.php?statute=77-27,242); [2025 instructions lines 2-6](https://revenue.nebraska.gov/sites/default/files/doc/tax-forms/2025/f_Corp_Booklet.pdf).
3. **Allocate/apportion income.** Ordinary multistate/unitary business uses adjusted federal taxable income times the Nebraska sales factor under 77-2734.05, with nonapportionable allocation and special rules where applicable. In the dated form process, state capital-loss/NOL carryovers follow the Nebraska income computation and require separate supported balances. Determine the factor under the actual sourcing rules, not a guessed gross-receipts ratio. [77-2734.05](https://nebraskalegislature.gov/laws/statutes.php?statute=77-2734.05); [Schedule I and carryover instructions](https://revenue.nebraska.gov/sites/default/files/doc/tax-forms/2025/f_Corp_Booklet.pdf).
4. **Apply the correct year rate.** For a taxable year beginning in 2026, gross ordinary corporate income tax = final Nebraska taxable income x 0.0455. Rate follows the beginning of the tax year for fiscal filers. 2025 uses 5.20%, 2027 separately 3.99%; do not blend years. [77-2734.02](https://nebraskalegislature.gov/laws/statutes.php?statute=77-2734.02); [2026 estimate worksheet lines 1-5](https://revenue.nebraska.gov/sites/default/files/doc/tax-forms/2025/f_1120N-ES.pdf).
5. **Apply supported credits and reconcile payments.** Certification, eligibility, ordering, carryforward and refundability must be established. Reconcile estimates and state identification consistently for unitary groups. Do not apply individual personal-exemption credits or treat income-tax payment as franchise registration. [2026 corporate estimates](https://revenue.nebraska.gov/sites/default/files/doc/tax-forms/2025/f_1120N-ES.pdf); [2025-2026 payment/credit procedures](https://revenue.nebraska.gov/sites/default/files/doc/tax-forms/2025/f_Corp_Booklet.pdf).

## Worked cases

### All-Nebraska corporation

A calendar 2026 ordinary C corporation has $100,000 final Nebraska taxable income, no credits and no estimates. Gross tax is $4,550. This uses a supplied finalized state base, not a federal number assumed to need no adjustments.

```oa-check
name: NE all-state corporate tax 2026
inputs:
  ne_taxable: 100000
steps:
  - tax: ne_taxable * 0.0455
expected:
  tax: 4550
```

### Established multistate factor

Adjusted apportionable federal income is $500,000 and the correctly computed Nebraska factor is 40%, with no separate allocations/loss carryovers. Nebraska taxable income is $200,000; gross tax is $9,100. A supported $500 nonrefundable credit reduces the illustrated income liability to $8,600 before estimates/payment reconciliation. The factor and credit are assumed verified facts, not inferred from this example.

```oa-check
name: NE apportioned corporate income 2026
inputs:
  adjusted: 500000
  factor: 0.4
  credit: 500
steps:
  - ne_taxable: adjusted * factor
  - gross: ne_taxable * 0.0455
  - tax: max(0, gross - credit)
expected:
  ne_taxable: 200000
  gross: 9100
  tax: 8600
```

## When to refuse or refer

Refer disputed nexus, unitary reporting, nonstandard factors, financial-institution franchise/insurance, PTE elections, losses/reorganizations, certified incentive claims, asset/research basis and amended/short-year returns. Do not present the franchise routing note as a complete 1120NF computation.

## Filing and payment

The **2025-2026 filing cycle** booklet uses 1120N, Schedule A adjustments, I apportionment and II foreign-dividend/special adjustments, with related-group schedules III/IV where required. It states the return is due when the related federal return is due. Final 2026 corporate return/general instructions and exact 2027 filing calendar were not found; do not relabel the dated form map current. [2025 corporate booklet](https://revenue.nebraska.gov/sites/default/files/doc/tax-forms/2025/f_Corp_Booklet.pdf); [current corporate forms index](https://revenue.nebraska.gov/about/forms/corporation-and-s-corporation-income-tax-forms); [2026 final forms index](https://revenue.nebraska.gov/tax-professionals/2026-final-income-tax-forms).

Current 7004N grants qualifying federal-extension carryover or a timely state request, with a maximum seven-month corporate filing extension. Attach the relevant evidence and follow electronic-payment/application instructions; payment is due by the original date and unpaid tax accrues interest. [7004N instructions](https://revenue.nebraska.gov/sites/default/files/doc/tax-forms/2026/f_7004n.pdf).

Use the actual 2026 Form 1120N-ES in the 2025 directory, not the 2027 voucher in the 2026 directory. Expected net tax at least $400 triggers ordinary estimates, due 15th of 4th/6th/9th/12th months. Complete the actual worksheet and examine 2220N before asserting a prior-year safe harbor; this skill does not invent one. Cash-device distributors/operators have separate dates and should refer. [2026 estimate instructions](https://revenue.nebraska.gov/sites/default/files/doc/tax-forms/2025/f_1120N-ES.pdf).
