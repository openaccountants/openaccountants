# Switzerland Company Formation & Entity Choice v1.0

## Scope

The two main Swiss capital companies are the GmbH/Sàrl (limited liability company) and the AG/SA (stock corporation); sole proprietorships and partnerships are also common. These are the rules in force for 2026, taken from the federal acts on fedlex.admin.ch. This file takes a founder through forming a GmbH or AG in the order they meet it, with the 2026 fees and the first compliance points. Taxes after formation are in `ch-corporate-income-tax`, `ch-cantonal-tax`, `switzerland-vat` and `ch-ahv-iv`.

## Ask the client first

- GmbH or AG, or a sole proprietorship or partnership? How much capital, in cash or in kind?
- In which canton will the seat be? The notary fee and the cantonal taxes follow the canton.
- Who will be the Swiss-resident representative (board member, managing officer or director)?
- Will turnover pass CHF 100,000 a year (VAT) or CHF 500,000 (full books for a sole proprietorship)?
- Will the company have more than ten full-time positions on annual average? That decides whether the limited audit can be opted out of.

## The method, step by step

- **GmbH / Sàrl (limited liability company)**: members hold capital contributions (Stammanteile), each with a nominal value above zero. Founded by public deed _(OR arts. 774(1), 777(1))_
- **AG / SA (stock corporation)**: capital divided into shares (registered or bearer). Founded by public deed _(OR arts. 622(1), 629(1))_
- **Other forms**: sole proprietorship (Einzelunternehmen), general partnership (Kollektivgesellschaft) and limited partnership (Kommanditgesellschaft). A sole proprietor whose business had turnover of at least CHF 100,000 in the last business year must register it in the commercial register; liberal professions and farmers not run on commercial lines are exempt, and anyone may register voluntarily _(OR art. 931(1), (3))_
- **GmbH minimum share capital**: CHF 20,000, or the equivalent in a foreign currency essential to the business. On formation, a contribution equal to the issue price must be paid in full for each capital contribution _(OR arts. 773(1), (2), 777c(1))_
- **AG minimum share capital**: CHF 100,000, or a foreign-currency equivalent at formation. At least 20% of the nominal value of each share must be paid in at formation, and at least CHF 50,000 in total _(OR arts. 621(1), (2), 632(1), (2))_
- **Capital deposit**: cash contributions are deposited with a Swiss bank for the company's exclusive use. The bank releases them only after the company is entered in the commercial register. The same rules apply to a GmbH _(OR arts. 633(1), (2), 777c(2))_
- **Incorporation steps**: 1) check the name and draft the articles; 2) deposit the cash contributions with a bank; 3) the founders sign the public deed, which adopts the articles and appoints the officers; 4) file the application with the commercial register office with the deed, the articles, the officers' acceptances, the auditor's acceptance where needed and the bank's deposit certificate (unless the deed names the bank); 5) the bank releases the capital after registration _(OR arts. 629, 633, 777; HRegV arts. 43(1), 71(1))_
- **Commercial register fees**: new registration of a capital company CHF 420; general or limited partnership CHF 160; sole proprietorship CHF 80. Changes to the articles cost CHF 200; registering an opt-out from the limited audit costs CHF 30. Where the schedule gives no flat fee, the office charges CHF 100 to 250 an hour, and may add up to 50% for unusual, difficult or urgent work _(GebV-HReg art. 3 and Annex items 1.1 to 1.3)_
- **Notary fees**: the public deed is drawn up by a notary under cantonal law, so the fee is set by each canton. In the canton of Zurich the fee for the deed founding an AG or GmbH is 1 per mille of the capital, within CHF 500 to 4,000 for companies that are neither public companies nor over the ordinary-audit size limits (CHF 500 to 12,000 and CHF 500 to 8,000 for those), and VAT is added. A Zurich GmbH with CHF 20,000 capital pays the CHF 500 minimum, as 1 per mille is CHF 20 _(Zurich NotGebV (LS 243) § 2 and Annex item 4.4.3.1)_
- **Swiss residency requirement**: the company must be able to be represented by a person resident in Switzerland. For an AG this person must be a board member or a director; for a GmbH a managing officer or a director. The person must have access to the share register (AG) or the register of contributions (GmbH); the wording of that sentence was last amended with effect from 1 October 2026 _(OR arts. 718(4), 814(3))_
- **Capital issue stamp duty**: 1% federal issuance stamp duty on the amount the company receives for new shares or contributions, at least the nominal value. Shares issued for consideration on the formation or capital increase of an AG or GmbH are exempt to the extent the members' contributions do not exceed CHF 1 million in total; only the amount above CHF 1 million is taxed _(StG arts. 5(1)(a), 6(1)(h), 8(1)(a))_
- **VAT registration**: an enterprise is exempt while its turnover from supplies in Switzerland and abroad is under CHF 100,000 a year. It may waive the exemption and register voluntarily, for at least one tax period. Rates, methods and returns are in [switzerland-vat](switzerland-vat.md) _(MWSTG arts. 10(2)(a), 11)_
- **Bookkeeping and annual accounts**: companies (legal persons), and sole proprietorships and partnerships with turnover of at least CHF 500,000 in the last business year, must keep full books and prepare annual accounts under the OR. Smaller sole proprietorships and partnerships keep only income, expenses and the asset position _(OR art. 957(1), (2))_
- **Annual meeting**: the shareholders' meeting of an AG, and the members' meeting of a GmbH, takes place within six months of the end of the business year _(OR arts. 699(2), 805(2))_
- **Audit requirement**: ordinary audit for listed companies, companies that must prepare group accounts, and companies that exceed two of: balance sheet total CHF 20 million, revenue CHF 40 million, 250 full-time positions, in two consecutive business years. Otherwise a limited audit. With the consent of all shareholders, a company with no more than ten full-time positions on annual average can opt out of the limited audit. Since 1 January 2025 the opt-out applies only to future business years and must be filed with the commercial register before the year begins, together with the last annual accounts. These rules apply to a GmbH too _(OR arts. 727(1), 727a(1), (2), (2bis), 818(1))_
- **Annual tax filing**: the cantons assess and collect the direct federal tax, and each canton names one office to assess companies. The cantons also levy their own profit and capital tax on companies. A company must file a return even if it received no form. Federal, cantonal and communal profit tax, capital tax, deadlines and payment are in [ch-corporate-income-tax](ch-corporate-income-tax.md); the owners' personal cantonal tax is in [ch-cantonal-tax](ch-cantonal-tax.md); VAT returns in [ch-vat-return](ch-vat-return.md); social security in [ch-ahv-iv](ch-ahv-iv.md) _(DBG arts. 2, 104(2), 124(1); StHG art. 2(1)(b))_

For the whole system on one page, see [ch-tax-overview](ch-tax-overview.md).

## Worked cases

### Case 1: what a Zurich GmbH costs to register

A founder forms a Zurich GmbH with CHF 20,000 of capital. The register fee is CHF 420 (GebV-HReg Annex 1.1). The notary fee is 1 per mille of capital, CHF 20, lifted to the CHF 500 minimum, plus 8.1% VAT (CHF 40.50). Official costs: CHF 960.50, before bank and adviser fees. No stamp duty: contributions are under CHF 1 million.

```oa-check
name: Switzerland 2026 Zurich GmbH official formation cost
inputs:
  capital: 20000
  register_fee: 420
  notary_rate: 0.001
  notary_min: 500
  vat: 0.081
steps:
  - notary_fee: max(capital * notary_rate, notary_min)
  - notary_vat: notary_fee * vat
  - total: register_fee + notary_fee + notary_vat
expected:
  notary_fee: 500
  notary_vat: 40.50
  total: 960.50
```

### Case 2: stamp duty on a larger AG (a decision on capital)

An AG is formed with CHF 1,500,000 paid in. Contributions up to CHF 1 million are exempt (StG art 6 para 1 let h), so 1% is due on CHF 500,000: CHF 5,000. The law counts the members' contributions "gesamthaft" (in total) against the CHF 1 million; before splitting a capital plan into formation plus a later increase to use the allowance twice, confirm the ESTV practice on the cumulative count.

```oa-check
name: Switzerland 2026 AG issuance stamp duty above CHF 1 million
inputs:
  contributions: 1500000
  allowance: 1000000
  rate: 0.01
steps:
  - taxable: max(contributions - allowance, 0)
  - duty: taxable * rate
expected:
  taxable: 500000
  duty: 5000
```

## When to refuse or refer

- Notary fees in a canton other than Zurich: each canton sets its own; refer to the canton's notary tariff.
- Contributions in kind, or a takeover of assets on formation: these need a founders' report and audit confirmation; refer to a notary.
- Tax rulings, holding or IP structures, and the corporate tax rate of a canton: `ch-corporate-income-tax`.
- Whether a specific company must have an ordinary audit: refer to an auditor.
