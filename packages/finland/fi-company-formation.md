# Finland company formation and entity screening v0.3

> **General reference only.** This Guide is general tax/accounting reference material for AI-assisted workflows. It has not been reviewed for any specific person's facts, documents, elections, deadlines, residency, filing status, or local procedures. Do not rely on it to file, pay, amend, or take a tax position without review by a qualified professional in the relevant jurisdiction.

## Scope

| Quick reference, 2026 | Rule | Official authority |
|---|---|---|
| Ordinary Oy guided setup | EUR 300 if every guided eligibility condition is met | https://www.prh.fi/en/companiesandorganisations/yrityksen_perustaminen/osakeyhtio.html |
| Drafted-document setup | EUR 400 | https://www.prh.fi/en/companiesandorganisations/yrityksen_perustaminen/osakeyhtio.html |
| Private Oy share capital | No statutory minimum; zero-capital registration permitted | Companies Act ch. 1 s. 3, https://www.finlex.fi/fi/lainsaadanto/2006/624; PRH https://www.prh.fi/en/companiesandorganisations/yrityksen_perustaminen/osakeyhtio.html |
| Incorporation filing | Within three months after signed memorandum or incorporation lapses | Companies Act ch. 2 s. 8, https://www.finlex.fi/fi/lainsaadanto/2006/624 |


Use this Guide to prepare an ordinary private limited company (Oy). The company becomes a separate legal person on registration. A public limited company (Oyj) has a EUR 80,000 minimum share-capital rule. A private Oy has no minimum share capital; PRH records zero share capital as EUR 0. Private-trader, partnership and cooperative routes need their own registration/liability analysis before comparing tax outcomes. A sole trader (toiminimi) has no separate legal personality and the owner is personally liable without limit ([Suomi.fi, private-trader liability](https://www.suomi.fi/company/changes-and-crisis-situations/closing-down-a-business/guide/bankruptcy/closing-down-a-company-in-bankruptcy-proceedings)); a general partnership (Ay) and a limited partnership (Ky) are governed by the Partnerships Act 389/1988, where general partners are personally liable. [Companies Act, chapter 1 sections 2–3 and chapter 2 section 9](https://www.finlex.fi/fi/lainsaadanto/2006/624); [PRH startup instructions](https://www.prh.fi/en/companiesandorganisations/yrityksen_perustaminen/osakeyhtio.html); [Partnerships Act 389/1988](https://www.finlex.fi/fi/lainsaadanto/1988/389)

Connect entity choice to the [individual](fi-income-tax.md), [corporate](fi-corporate-income-tax.md), [YEL](fi-yel-social.md) and [payroll](fi-employer-payroll.md) skills. A registration route does not decide the tax residence, worker status or optimal entity for particular facts.

This Guide covers ordinary Oy preparation and ongoing formation-related controls. It does not complete an entity tax comparison or specialist incorporation. Registration, tax residence, tax registrations and professional approval are separate decisions.

## Ask the client first

- Which entity, activities, ownership and tax-residence facts apply?
- Who will own shares and serve as ordinary/deputy directors or managing director, and where do they live?
- Are standard articles sufficient, and do the specified people have Finnish identity codes?
- What subscription price/capital, payment evidence and signing date apply?
- What do both financial periods, articles and group/holding-company facts show for audit?
- Which registrations, permits, meeting decisions and filing receipts already exist?

## The method, step by step
### 1. Choose the filing route and authority fee

| Route | Authority handling fee | Eligibility/source |
|---|---:|---|
| Guided setup package | EUR 300 | No share subscription price, zero share capital, adult shareholders, Finnish identity codes for the specified people, standard articles sufficient. [PRH startup instructions](https://www.prh.fi/en/companiesandorganisations/yrityksen_perustaminen/osakeyhtio.html) |
| Start-up notification with drafted documents | EUR 400 | Use where share capital, entity shareholders, missing Finnish identity codes, tailored articles or public-company status require it. [PRH startup instructions](https://www.prh.fi/en/companiesandorganisations/yrityksen_perustaminen/osakeyhtio.html) |

PRH's 2026 instructions require online filing, in Finnish or Swedish, and permit an Oy with no share capital. Recheck route eligibility and the live authority fee before payment. Share capital is company funding, not another PRH fee. [PRH startup instructions](https://www.prh.fi/en/companiesandorganisations/yrityksen_perustaminen/osakeyhtio.html)

### 2. Screen officers and permits

The ordinary statutory board has one to five members unless the articles provide otherwise. Fewer than three members requires at least one deputy; multiple members require a chair. [Companies Act, chapter 6 section 8](https://www.finlex.fi/fi/lainsaadanto/2006/624)

PRH checks EEA residence separately for ordinary and deputy members: an EEA-resident ordinary director does not settle the deputy group's permit requirement. The managing director and their deputy have their own residence/permit test. Citizenship does not determine residence. Obtain the applicable permit before treating the structure as compliant. [PRH foreign-person permits](https://www.prh.fi/en/companiesandorganisations/ulkomaalaisluvat.html); [Companies Act, chapter 6 sections 10 and 19](https://www.finlex.fi/fi/lainsaadanto/2006/624)

### 3. Prepare, sign, submit and monitor

File within **three months after the memorandum is signed** or incorporation lapses. Shares submitted for registration must be fully paid; non-cash subscription requires the statutory supporting auditor statement. Preserve the memorandum, articles, payments/declarations, officer details and registration result. [Companies Act, chapter 2 section 8](https://www.finlex.fi/fi/lainsaadanto/2006/624)

Use the current PRH route and monitor the submitted notification in Virre. Its registration extract establishes the recorded result; a draft saved in a service is not a completed registration. No fixed processing duration is supplied here. [PRH filing instructions](https://www.prh.fi/en/companiesandorganisations/muutokset_rekisteritietoihin/yleisohje.html)

The PRH startup notification can also carry Tax Administration register details; PRH handles the Trade Register part and Vero handles its own registers. Select the actual VAT, prepayment and employer registrations from the business facts. Beneficial-owner details, officers and contact information must be kept current. [PRH startup instructions](https://www.prh.fi/en/companiesandorganisations/yrityksen_perustaminen/osakeyhtio.html)

### 4. Apply the audit-size screen

Under Auditing Act chapter 2 section 2, the ordinary size-based omission rule requires **at most one** of these conditions to be met in **each** of the concluded and immediately preceding financial periods:

| Condition | Exceedance threshold | Official authority |
|---|---:|---|
| Balance-sheet total | Over EUR 100,000 | Auditing Act ch. 2 s. 2, https://www.finlex.fi/fi/lainsaadanto/2015/1141 |
| Net sales or corresponding revenue | Over EUR 200,000 | Auditing Act ch. 2 s. 2, https://www.finlex.fi/fi/lainsaadanto/2015/1141 |
| Average employee count | Over 3 | Auditing Act ch. 2 s. 2, https://www.finlex.fi/fi/lainsaadanto/2015/1141 |

Equality does not exceed a threshold. Startup entities without both periods have a separate statutory rule. Articles can require an audit, and the specified securities-holding/significant-influence case always requires an auditor. Check group and other-law requirements separately. [Auditing Act, chapter 2 section 2](https://www.finlex.fi/fi/lainsaadanto/2015/1141)

## Worked cases

### 1. Registration fee versus company funding

Assume an ordinary own-document Oy route with EUR 10,000 agreed cash share capital and the published EUR 400 authority fee. Cash funding is EUR 10,400, of which EUR 10,000 is company capital. This is not a tax deduction calculation or a quote for professional/bank services. The zero-capital guided route is not assumed to be available.

```oa-check
name: Finland assumed Oy capital and authority fee separation
inputs:
  agreed_cash_share_capital: 10000
  own_document_authority_fee: 400
steps:
  combined_cash_funding: agreed_cash_share_capital + own_document_authority_fee
  authority_fee_only: combined_cash_funding - agreed_cash_share_capital
expected:
  combined_cash_funding: 10400
  authority_fee_only: 400
```

### 2. Count each year's established audit triggers

Assume current balance EUR 110,000, sales EUR 190,000 and average employees 4: two exceedances. Previous figures EUR 90,000, EUR 150,000 and 2 give zero. The ordinary omission test requires no more than one in each period, so these facts do not satisfy it. The stated raw inputs establish the classified flags; the arithmetic control recomputes each period separately. It is not a complete legal decision engine.

```oa-check
name: Finland assumed separate period audit trigger counts
inputs:
  current_balance: 110000
  current_sales: 190000
  current_average_employees: 4
  previous_balance: 90000
  previous_sales: 150000
  previous_average_employees: 2
  balance_threshold: 100000
  sales_threshold: 200000
  employee_threshold: 3
  current_balance_trigger: 1
  current_sales_trigger: 0
  current_employee_trigger: 1
  previous_balance_trigger: 0
  previous_sales_trigger: 0
  previous_employee_trigger: 0
steps:
  current_trigger_count: current_balance_trigger + current_sales_trigger + current_employee_trigger
  previous_trigger_count: previous_balance_trigger + previous_sales_trigger + previous_employee_trigger
  highest_period_count: max(current_trigger_count, previous_trigger_count)
expected:
  current_trigger_count: 2
  previous_trigger_count: 0
  highest_period_count: 2
```

### 3. Choose between eligible zero-capital routes

Assume all guided-route conditions are met and no share subscription price or capital is agreed. The guided route costs EUR 300; using drafted documents costs EUR 400. The authority-fee difference is EUR 100. Choose the guided route only if every eligibility fact is satisfied; a EUR 100 saving cannot cure a missing identity code or tailored-articles requirement. [PRH startup instructions](https://www.prh.fi/en/companiesandorganisations/yrityksen_perustaminen/osakeyhtio.html)

```oa-check
name: Finland eligible guided versus own-document authority fee
inputs:
  guided_fee: 300
  drafted_fee: 400
  agreed_share_capital: 0
steps:
  guided_cash: guided_fee + agreed_share_capital
  drafted_cash: drafted_fee + agreed_share_capital
  fee_difference: drafted_cash - guided_cash
expected:
  guided_cash: 300
  drafted_cash: 400
  fee_difference: 100
```

## When to refuse or refer

Foreign ownership, EEA officer residence, Finnish tax residence, immigration permission, bank onboarding and regulated-activity approval are distinct checks. The permit screen above does not resolve the others. Collect the activity, ownership and management facts and obtain the relevant authority decision before relying on a particular route. [PRH permit guidance](https://www.prh.fi/en/companiesandorganisations/ulkomaalaisluvat.html)

Do not submit an ineligible guided notification, treat unregistered incorporation as completed, or decide an audit exemption from one period alone. Refer unusual entities, regulated sectors, non-cash capital valuation, group/sustainability obligations and unconfirmed exceptions before action.

## Filing and payment

An Oy keeps double-entry books and prepares annual financial statements under the Accounting Act. Prepare the financial statements and any required annual report within four months after period end. [Accounting Act 1336/1997, chapter 1 sections 1–2 and chapter 3 sections 1 and 6](https://www.finlex.fi/fi/lainsaadanto/1997/1336) Hold the ordinary general meeting within six months of period end; it adopts the accounts and decides on the use of profit. Company-law registration of the financial statements/annual report is due within two months after adoption. [Companies Act, chapter 5 section 3 and chapter 8 section 10](https://www.finlex.fi/fi/lainsaadanto/2006/624)

PRH's general outer timetable is eight months after period end; filing within that period is free. Earlier adoption still starts the statutory two-month clock. Tax-return timing is separate: the corporate income tax return is due four months after the end of the calendar month in which the period ends (see the [corporate skill](fi-corporate-income-tax.md)); do not wait for the meeting/PRH filing. VAT and payroll reports run on their own periods ([VAT](finland-vat-return.md), [payroll](fi-employer-payroll.md)). [PRH financial statements](https://www.prh.fi/en/companiesandorganisations/financial_statements/limited_liability_companies_co-operatives_and_other_companies/digital.html); [Vero, corporate tax return](https://www.vero.fi/en/businesses-and-corporations/taxes-and-charges/limited-companies-and-cooperatives/tax-return/)

PRH's 2026 notice describes a late-submission fee from EUR 150, rising to EUR 600 for more than four months' delay, with doubling for two or more consecutive periods. Actual period, delay and statutory notice/decision conditions must be checked; this Guide does not compute every fee. Inactive companies still have a filing duty. [PRH 2026 late-filing notice](https://www.prh.fi/en/presentation_and_duties/uutislistaus/news/2026/financial-statements-submitted-late-27.5.2026.html)

## Completion checklist

- Match the entity, route and articles to the documented facts.
- Check board/deputy/CEO residence and any permits separately.
- Preserve signed documents, payment evidence, filing receipt and register result.
- Diary incorporation, accounts preparation, adoption, PRH and Vero deadlines separately.
- Keep both periods and all statutory/articles exceptions in the audit workpaper.
