# Bosnia and Herzegovina Tax Overview

> **General reference only.** This skill is general tax/accounting reference material for AI-assisted workflows. It has not been reviewed for any specific person's facts, documents, elections, deadlines, residency, filing status, or local procedures. Do not rely on it to file, pay, amend, or take a tax position without review by a qualified professional in the relevant jurisdiction.

## Who this applies to

Use this overview to choose the right tax regime before computing. Bosnia and Herzegovina has state-level VAT, customs and excise administered by the Indirect Taxation Authority (Uprava za indirektno oporezivanje, UIO). Direct profit tax, individual income tax and contributions require separate Federation of BiH (FBiH), Republika Srpska (RS) or Brčko District (BD) analysis. UIO's [institutional remit](https://www.uino.gov.ba/portal/bs/o-nama/), the [FBiH law](https://www.pufbih.ba/v1/public/upload/zakoni/9080b-zakon-o-porezu-na-dobit-precisceni.pdf), [RS law and residence amendment](https://www.narodnaskupstinars.net/sites/default/files/upload/dokumenti/akti/usvojeni_zakoni/Zakon%20o%20izmj%20i%20dop%20Zakona%20o%20porezu%20na%20dobit_la.zip) and [BD law](https://skupstinabd.ba/3-zakon/ba/Zakon%20o%20porezu%20na%20dobit/05B17-22%20Zakon%20o%20porezu%20na%20dobit%20-%20prec--is--eni%20tekst.pdf) are separate sources, not interchangeable rates.

Establish the company's registered seat and effective management, each establishment's location, the person's residence and actual work, the income category and the relevant period. A low headline rate does not establish the taxable base. This overview retains the old pack's core figures and edge cases; the linked domain skills own detailed computations and return preparation.

- Company residence and source: FBiH resident companies are within worldwide-profit taxation, with separate nonresident permanent-establishment and withholding rules; an establishment of a company from elsewhere in BiH is assessed where the relevant local rules put it. See [FBiH arts. 3-5](https://www.pufbih.ba/v1/public/upload/zakoni/9080b-zakon-o-porezu-na-dobit-precisceni.pdf). RS residence includes a registered seat **or** effective management and supervision in RS; resident worldwide profit, inter-entity establishments/property and nonresident source profit are distinguished in [arts. 3, 3a and 3b](https://www.narodnaskupstinars.net/sites/default/files/upload/dokumenti/akti/usvojeni_zakoni/Zakon%20o%20izmj%20i%20dop%20Zakona%20o%20porezu%20na%20dobit_la.zip), with the 2026 share-disposal addition in [114/25 art. 2](https://www.narodnaskupstinars.net/sites/default/files/upload/dokumenti/akti/usvojeni_zakoni/la/Zak%20o%20izmj%20i%20dop%20Zak%20o%20porezu%20na%20dobit.zip). BD distinguishes local companies, inter-entity establishments, foreign establishments and immovable-property/source income in [art. 3](https://skupstinabd.ba/3-zakon/ba/Zakon%20o%20porezu%20na%20dobit/05B17-22%20Zakon%20o%20porezu%20na%20dobit%20-%20prec--is--eni%20tekst.pdf). Do not treat every nonresident receipt as establishment profit. Detail: [ba-corporate-income-tax](ba-corporate-income-tax.md).
- Individuals: an FBiH resident's income includes income earned inside and outside FBiH; the nonresident categories include local establishment, employment, property, rights and capital income, subject to the actual classification in [income-law art. 2](https://www.pufbih.ba/v1/public/upload/zakoni/d9bf6-zakon-o-porezu-na-dohodak-ispravan-tekst.pdf). Its residence rules and special inter-entity employment provisions must be read before applying a residence label. RS and BD residence/category detail belongs in [bosnia-income-tax](bosnia-income-tax.md).
- Tax authorities: [FBiH art. 4(3)](https://www.pufbih.ba/v1/public/upload/zakoni/9080b-zakon-o-porezu-na-dobit-precisceni.pdf) identifies the FBiH Tax Administration; [RS art. 53](https://www.narodnaskupstinars.net/sites/default/files/upload/dokumenti/akti/usvojeni_zakoni/Zakon_o_porezu_na_dobit_8.zip) distinguishes ministry supervision from Tax Administration inspection. BD Tax Administration is within its Finance Directorate under [BD Tax Administration Law arts. 1, 3](https://skupstinabd.ba/3-zakon/ba/Zakon%20o%20Poreznoj%20upravi%20Brc--ko%20Distrikta%20BiH/011B11-20%20Zakon%20%20o%20poreznoj%20upravi%20Brcko%20distrikta%20BiH.pdf). The old RS art. 24(2) citation concerned capital-gain valuation, not the identity of the authority.

## The base

Profit tax is applied after the relevant jurisdiction's tax adjustments to accounting profit. Do not multiply turnover by the profit-tax rate. A resident's worldwide scope does not itself give an unrestricted foreign-tax credit; nonresident/establishment attribution and inter-entity relief belong in the [corporate](ba-corporate-income-tax.md) and [cross-border](ba-cross-border.md) skills.

Salary bases differ even where the nominal rates look similar. FBiH salary-tax deductions include qualifying employee contributions and resident tax-card deductions. RS ordinary salary tax starts with gross salary, applies the eligible exemption/deduction and does **not** subtract the employee contribution charge from its income-tax base. BD deductions and contributions need their own facts. Load [bosnia-payroll](bosnia-payroll.md), [bosnia-social-contributions](bosnia-social-contributions.md) and [bosnia-income-tax](bosnia-income-tax.md) before a real payroll computation; do not import an FBiH base into RS.

The RS statute's education wording is literally **“najmanje stečeno srednje obrazovanje u trajanju od tri godine”**, alongside jobs requiring no specified education. The authority's implementation notice specifies NK, PK, KV and lower registered qualifications. The former overview's free paraphrase “at most three-year secondary schooling” is not the wording of the statute. Use the actual statutory job definition and the registration classification together; refer an uncertain classification instead of treating every secondary-school graduate as eligible. The notice's opening Gazette reference says 114/26, while the actual enacted amending law is 114/25 and commences on 1 January 2026; the statute controls. [Statute](https://www.narodnaskupstinars.net/sites/default/files/upload/dokumenti/akti/usvojeni_zakoni/la/Zak%20o%20izmj%20i%20dop%20Zak%20o%20porezu%20na%20dohodak_1.zip), [implementation and exceptions](https://poreskaupravars.org/izmjene-u-podnosenju-obrasca-1002-od-01-01-2026-godine-2/?lang=lat).

## Cross-border points

- Keep a foreign company recipient separate from a foreign individual. The corporate withholding rows above cannot be applied to everyone receiving money abroad. FBiH withholding is on the gross listed payment and has PE/statutory exclusions; the payment and following-month reporting/payment rules are distinct. RS's former 5% dividend withholding exception was deleted by 1/17; current art. 46 exemptions also require the 48/24 and 114/25 amendments. BD's dividend exclusion does not exempt every other foreign payment. [FBiH art. 38](https://www.pufbih.ba/v1/public/upload/zakoni/9080b-zakon-o-porezu-na-dobit-precisceni.pdf), [RS 2017 amendment](https://www.narodnaskupstinars.net/sites/default/files/upload/dokumenti/akti/usvojeni_zakoni/Zakon%20o%20izmj%20i%20dop%20Zakona%20o%20porezu%20na%20dobit_la.zip), [2024 exemption amendment](https://www.narodnaskupstinars.net/sites/default/files/upload/dokumenti/akti/usvojeni_zakoni/lat_zakon_o_izmj_i_dop_zakona_o_porezu_na_dobit_hp_la.zip), [2026 RS amendment](https://www.narodnaskupstinars.net/sites/default/files/upload/dokumenti/akti/usvojeni_zakoni/la/Zak%20o%20izmj%20i%20dop%20Zak%20o%20porezu%20na%20dobit.zip), [BD arts. 29-30](https://skupstinabd.ba/3-zakon/ba/Zakon%20o%20porezu%20na%20dobit/05B17-22%20Zakon%20o%20porezu%20na%20dobit%20-%20prec--is--eni%20tekst.pdf).
- Individual dividend treatment is separate: FBiH dividends/profit shares are excluded from income under [PIT art. 5(1)(1)](https://www.pufbih.ba/v1/public/upload/zakoni/d9bf6-zakon-o-porezu-na-dohodak-ispravan-tekst.pdf). RS company dividends/profit shares remain exempt: the original [60/15 art. 8(3)(13)](https://www.narodnaskupstinars.net/sites/default/files/upload/dokumenti/akti/usvojeni_zakoni/Prijedlog_zakona_o_porezu_na_dohodak_0.zip) became paragraph 4 under [105/19 art. 1](https://www.narodnaskupstinars.net/sites/default/files/upload/dokumenti/akti/usvojeni_zakoni/Zak%20izmj%20dop%20Zak%20porezu%20na%20dohodak_la.zip), then point 14 under [112/23 art. 2](https://www.narodnaskupstinars.net/sites/default/files/upload/dokumenti/akti/usvojeni_zakoni/lat_zakon_o_dop_zakona_o_porezu_na_dohodak_hp_la.zip); the [114/25 amendment](https://www.narodnaskupstinars.net/sites/default/files/upload/dokumenti/akti/usvojeni_zakoni/la/Zak%20o%20izmj%20i%20dop%20Zak%20o%20porezu%20na%20dohodak_1.zip) does not remove this dividend point. BD excludes dividends, profit shares and gains from disposing of financial instruments under [PIT art. 6(a)](https://skupstinabd.ba/3-zakon/ba/Zakon%20o%20porezu%20na%20dohodak/Nesluz--bena%20prec--is--c-ena%20verzija%20Zakona%20o%20porezu%20na%20dohodak%20B.pdf). Do not confuse an income-tax exemption with a corporate withholding exemption or a right to distribute profits.
- Treaties can reduce or exclude domestic withholding only with the applicable income/recipient, residence, entitlement and documentary conditions. No treaty-specific rate was verified in this overview. Route to [ba-cross-border](ba-cross-border.md) and [ba-transfer-pricing](ba-transfer-pricing.md) for their actual jobs; no treaty rate or transfer-pricing safe harbour is invented here.
- For planning across legal forms and jurisdictions, load [bosnia-tax-optimization](bosnia-tax-optimization.md). Do not choose a location from the salary or profit headline alone.

## Two worked examples that recompute

### Ordinary FBiH salary with and without a tax card

Assume an ordinary FBiH-resident employee under PIT art. 2(3), monthly salary KM 2,000, no art. 2(4)/27(5) inter-entity employment exception, no special contribution category, no other personal deduction, and the current employer rates. Employee contributions are KM 620; employer contributions are KM 100. With an eligible card, taxable salary is KM 1,080, tax KM 108 and net KM 1,272. Without payroll card entitlement, the KM 300 basic deduction is not used at withholding: tax KM 138 and net KM 1,242. The KM 30 difference is derived; it is not an automatic final-year refund. Ordinary employer cost is KM 2,100 either way. [PIT arts. 9, 24, 27](https://www.pufbih.ba/v1/public/upload/zakoni/d9bf6-zakon-o-porezu-na-dohodak-ispravan-tekst.pdf), [Contributions art. 10](https://www.pufbih.ba/v1/public/upload/zakoni/2dcc6-zakon-o-doprinosima-precisceni-tekst-25.pdf).

```oa-check
name: FBiH ordinary resident salary card decision
inputs:
  gross: 2000
steps:
  - pension: gross * 0.17
  - health: gross * 0.125
  - unemployment: gross * 0.015
  - employee: pension + health + unemployment
  - employer: gross * (0.025 + 0.02 + 0.005)
  - card_tax: max(0, gross - employee - 3600 / 12) * 0.10
  - no_card_tax: (gross - employee) * 0.10
  - card_net: gross - employee - card_tax
  - no_card_net: gross - employee - no_card_tax
  - employer_cost: gross + employer
  - withholding_difference: no_card_tax - card_tax
expected:
  employee: 620
  employer: 100
  card_tax: 108
  no_card_tax: 138
  card_net: 1272
  no_card_net: 1242
  employer_cost: 2100
  withholding_difference: 30
```

### RS authority salary fixture with the qualifying relief

Preserve and recompute the original authority case: gross KM 1,800, eligible ordinary-base employee, KM 50 exemption and full KM 1,000 monthly allowance. Contribution base KM 1,750 produces KM 542.50 contributions; salary-tax base KM 750 produces KM 60 tax and net KM 1,197.50. Do not deduct those contributions again when calculating RS salary tax. If the otherwise identical ordinary employee is ineligible for the relief, contributions are KM 558, tax KM 64 and net KM 1,178: the relief changes net by KM 19.50, derived. This comparison does not cover seconded/textile special contribution bases, part-time deduction scaling or an unconfirmed qualification. [PURS actual case and exceptions](https://poreskaupravars.org/izmjene-u-podnosenju-obrasca-1002-od-01-01-2026-godine-2/?lang=lat), [statutory relief](https://www.narodnaskupstinars.net/sites/default/files/upload/dokumenti/akti/usvojeni_zakoni/la/Zak%20o%20izmj%20i%20dop%20Zak%20o%20porezu%20na%20dohodak_1.zip), [salary rate/base](https://www.narodnaskupstinars.net/sites/default/files/upload/dokumenti/akti/usvojeni_zakoni/lat%20Zakon%20o%20izmj%20i%20dop%20Zakona%20o%20porezu%20na%20dohodak_la.zip).

## Ask the client first

1. Which entity/District has the company's seat, effective management and each establishment, and where is the work actually done?
2. Which income category, recipient type and residence apply, and is there a nonresident establishment or treaty claim?
3. Which accounting/tax period and payment date apply, including a new business, cessation or approved different year?
4. For pay, what are gross remuneration, actual hours/payers, tax-card entitlement, registered qualification and any special contribution base?
5. What turnover counts for VAT, including exempt-with-credit and related-control amounts, and is registration compulsory or voluntary?
6. Which return/payment has already been filed or paid, and what dated notice or debt statement supports any penalty, interest or relief?
