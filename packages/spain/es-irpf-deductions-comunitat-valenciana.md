# Spain: Comunitat Valenciana regional IRPF credits, 2026

**Sourced from the law.** This revision has not had an independent whole-pack review or accountant sign-off. Calculate regional credits after establishing tax residence and the taxpayer's facts. This Guide holds the Comunitat Valenciana catalogue of [es-irpf-deductions](es-irpf-deductions.md), moved here unchanged on 6 November 2026, so that each Guide stays within the size the site can deliver. Specific unresolved interactions are listed in Sources.

## Who this applies to

An ordinary IRPF resident whose community of residence for 2026 under [LIRPF art. 72](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2006-20764#a72) is Comunitat Valenciana. A regional credit reduces regional tax, not the income base. Before applying any row below, use [es-irpf-deductions](es-irpf-deductions.md) for the residence test, the deduction ledger, the state/regional order of credits, the national credits, deadlines, penalties and the questions to ask, and [es-income-tax](es-income-tax.md) for the bases, scales and minima. Where a row below refers to "the attributable-tax worksheet under The base", that worksheet is [es-irpf-deductions-attributable-tax](es-irpf-deductions-attributable-tax.md); national credits, the deduction ledger and any calculation "above" outside this catalogue are in [es-irpf-deductions](es-irpf-deductions.md#the-base). Basque and Navarre residents use [es-foral-income-tax](es-foral-income-tax.md); an IRNR taxpayer or an art. 93 incoming worker does not take these credits.

## Rates, bands and thresholds

All amounts are EUR. `BI` means the specified sum of general and savings **taxable income before base reductions** (base imponible); `BL` means base liquidable after the relevant reductions. Use the definition in each row: an income limit based on BI cannot be replaced with gross salary, BL or take-home pay. The table year is 2026 unless a transition is stated.

### Comunitat Valenciana: applicable text, income tests and family credits

Use **Ley 13/1997 art. 4** for 2026. The health/sport amendments in **DL 1/2026 arts. 2–3** and the music credit in **DL 2/2026 art. 2** expressly apply to accruals from **1 January 2025**, so they are already available throughout 2026, including the increased income limits. Both decrees were validated by the Corts. For the six provisions replaced by **DL 14/2025 art. 65**, that text was effective from **30 December 2025 through 2 July 2026**; **Ley 3/2026 art. 82** replaces it from **3 July 2026**. Its disability-event wording and tenant compatibility cross-reference differ as described below. Do not apply the year-end wording automatically to an earlier short period ending on death ([current art. 4](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-1998-8202#a4); [DL 14/2025 art. 65/final provision 4](https://www.boe.es/buscar/doc.php?id=DOGV-r-2025-90320); [Ley 3/2026 art. 82/derogation/final provision 4](https://www.boe.es/buscar/doc.php?id=BOE-A-2026-15683); [DL 1/2026](https://www.boe.es/buscar/doc.php?id=DOGV-r-2026-90037), [validation](https://www.boe.es/buscar/doc.php?id=DOGV-r-2026-90079); [DL 2/2026](https://www.boe.es/buscar/doc.php?id=DOGV-r-2026-90041), [validation](https://www.boe.es/buscar/doc.php?id=DOGV-r-2026-90069)).

Here **B = general BL + savings BL**, without subtracting personal/family minima. `I/J` means individual/joint filing. The following factors phase the stated **credit amount or credit limit**, not every expenditure base or percentage indiscriminately. At an upper endpoint the factor is zero. Test each claimant's filing position; do not double a joint-return limit without authority.

Birth/adoption/foster relief requires descendant-minimum entitlement. For the second/third-and-later fostering or delegated-guardianship amount, the preceding children counted for ordering must have been held for **more than 183 days**. Exclude a child adopted during the year from the foster route; use the birth/adoption route where entitled. Share the applicable amount equally between entitled declaring taxpayers. The birth/adoption amount continues for two following years; the fostering amount follows actual qualifying days. Multiple adoption requires at least two adoptions on the same date. The event credits a/b/c are compatible with one another and d under the express statutory conditions ([art. 4.Uno.a–d](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-1998-8202#a4)).

For c, disability is at least **65%**, or intellectual/mental disability at least **33%**. For accruals through **2 July 2026**, DL 14/2025 expressly requires permanent recognition of that intellectual/mental disability; Ley 3/2026's replacement c omits that adjective from **3 July**, although the separate permanent-recognition wording remains in h/i/l/n. Retain the competent disability certificate and apply the judicial/pension assimilations in DA 6 where they actually fit ([DL 14/2025 art. 65](https://www.boe.es/buscar/doc.php?id=DOGV-r-2025-90320); [Ley 3/2026 art. 82](https://www.boe.es/buscar/doc.php?id=BOE-A-2026-15683); [DA 6](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-1998-8202#dasexta)).

For d, require the recognised large/monoparental category at accrual and cohabitation with the other family members; share equally between entitled declaring taxpayers. For e, the parents/adopters/carers must undertake employment or self-employment producing employment/economic income. Use regular custody in a qualifying nursery/first-cycle infant centre, not occasional babysitting ([art. 4.Uno.d/e](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-1998-8202#a4)).

For f, count the **third-birthday month through the month before the fifth birthday**, provided the descendant-minimum and other conditions hold at each month end; a birthday on the month's last day has the same result. Work registration on any day satisfies that month's registration condition. Start with `460 × qualifying months / 12`, apply F, and limit the deduction to gross Social Security/alternative-mutual dues accrued **from the third birthday through the day before the fifth birthday**, ignoring contribution bonuses. The contribution ceiling is global for the whole deduction: count gross dues accrued in the current tax period within the applicable third-to-fifth-birthday intervals, without counting the same dues twice for overlapping children; monthly testing determines each child’s entitlement. For two children qualifying all year and 500 of eligible dues, the ceiling gives `min(920,500) = 500`, before any other applicable limit. Thus a third birthday on 31 March gives ten age-eligible months, March–December: `460 × 10 / 12 = 383.33` before F and the contribution ceiling. A fifth birthday on 31 March gives two months, January–February: `460 × 2 / 12 = 76.67` before those limits ([art. 4.Uno.f, Cuatro and Cinco](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-1998-8202#a4); [AEAT reconciliation calculation](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/ayuda/manuales-videos-folletos/manuales-ayuda-presentacion/irpf-2025/10-cumplimentacion-irpf-deducciones-autonomicas/10_15-comunitat-valenciana/10_15_6-conciliacion-trabajo-vida-familiar.html)). The normal claimant is the working mother/female foster carer; the father succeeds to the pending right on her death or exclusive custody, and a male foster carer can qualify where there is no female foster carer. Share equally if several declaring taxpayers qualify. Adoption/guardianship instead uses the **fourth and fifth years after Civil Registry registration**, irrespective of the minor's age; fostering uses those years after the administrative resolution and requires the arrangement still to exist at accrual. ([art. 4.Uno.f](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-1998-8202#a4); [AEAT reconciliation guidance](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/ayuda/manuales-videos-folletos/manuales-practicos/irpf-2025-deducciones-autonomicas/comunitat-valenciana/conciliacion-trabajo-vida-familiar.html)).

For h, the direct ascendant by blood, affinity or adoption must be **over 75**, or **over 65** with disability at least **65%** or permanently recognised intellectual/mental disability at least **33%**; non-exempt annual income cannot exceed **8,000**, and an IRPF return with income **over 1,800** blocks the claim. Require cohabitation for at least half the period; a dependent disabled ascendant in a specialist institution counts under the statute. Determine personal/family circumstances at accrual. Equal-degree entitled claimants share equally; the nearer degree takes priority unless its non-exempt income is at most 8,000, in which case the next degree can qualify. For i, require an indefinite household-employment contract, actual contributions, claimant employment/economic income, and a qualifying under-five minimum-entitled child or qualifying older/disabled minimum-entitled ascendant on the last day of each relevant month. **i is incompatible with e, f and h**. ([art. 4.Uno.h/i](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-1998-8202#a4)).

For i, calculate `min(50% × eligible paid contributions, F × (child cap + ascendant cap))`. Count contributions for months whose last day meets the statutory care and work-income conditions. Use a child cap of 660 for one qualifying under-five child, or 1,100 for two or more or a qualifying monoparental family; use an ascendant cap of 330 for one, or 550 for two or more. Use zero for a care category that does not qualify. Add the category caps for mixed care, including where the same worker cares for both generations. Apply the 50% rate once to the eligible contribution amount. For one child and one ascendant the cap is 990; for two children and one ascendant it is 1,430. A sole registered employer who pays the contributions keeps the whole applicable cap even if both parents claim the descendant minimum. If two taxpayers each employ carers for the same qualifying relatives, split the cap equally. The same cap amounts apply on a joint return. The credit is incompatible with e, f and h ([art. 4.Uno.i, Cuatro and Cinco](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-1998-8202#a4); [AEAT household-worker calculation and example](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/ayuda/manuales-videos-folletos/manuales-practicos/irpf-2025-deducciones-autonomicas/comunitat-valenciana/contratar-manera-indefinida-personas-afiliadas.html)).

The two-descendant credit t requires **aggregate BI no more than 30,000** for the entitled parents, qualifying descendants and other members of the relevant joint family units. This is not the claimant's B or salary. Apply 10% after the other regional deductions and statutory state-credit allocations, excluding t itself. School relief v requires a minimum-entitled child/ward in primary, compulsory-secondary or special education at a public/publicly funded private school, and unemployment with registration as a jobseeker of the claimant or cohabiting partner. Prorate by qualifying unemployment and fostering days; combined parents' unemployment days cannot exceed period days. Share equally between entitled claimants. For aa, share only the descendant addition where appropriate; it is incompatible with a/b/c **for the same descendant**, whereas the basic 330 is not excluded by that sentence ([art. 4.Uno.t/v/aa](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-1998-8202#a4)).

For j, the housing tenancy must begin during the period; where a previous tenancy lasted less than three years, the new tenant must differ. The monthly rent cannot exceed the official private-rental reference price and the deposit must be lodged before period end. Arts. j/k/l are expressly compatible. **Art. 4.Dos applies its net-wealth increase condition to j as well as k/l/m/o**: compare closing and opening checked wealth, disregard changes in value of assets retained at year end, and require the statutory increase covering the investment. Do not silently remove j from that express list. For m, require actual application of the grant money to the habitual home. If grant income is spread over several years under national timing rules, split the 112 across those years. The alternative average-rate route is confined to the specified demolition/replacement/reurbanisation/temporary-rehousing programme. Do not use the same grant money again in k/l; the distinct regional-general-rate aid method is supplied in the attributable-tax worksheet under The base ([art. 4.Uno.j–m/Dos](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-1998-8202#a4)).

For n, the enhanced conditions are **age at most 35**, the specified disability (65% or permanently recognised intellectual/mental 33%), and statutory gender-violence-victim status. Require an actually occupied habitual home, a contract **after 23 April 1998** lasting at least a year, and a receipt separating the use-assignment rental component where applicable. For at least half the tax period, neither the taxpayer nor the relevant family unit can own, individually or together, the whole ownership or real right to use another home **less than 50 km** away, unless judicial/administrative restrictions prevent its use. The statute preserves the specified over-65 owner's EVHA social-letting/collaborative-housing exception. A victim's aggressor spouse and the former shared home receive the specific exclusions in n. No entitlement to a home-investment deduction for the same period is allowed. Prorate the cap by tenancy days and days satisfying the enhanced circumstances; divide it equally between entitled declaring taxpayers, including community-property spouses paying with common funds even if only one is named on the lease ([art. 4.Uno.n](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-1998-8202#a4)).

For ñ, the rented regional home must be **more than 50 km** from the previous home, the employment in a different municipality, and the rent not reimbursed by the employer. Apply the ordinary income gate, day proration and shared cap. From **3 July 2026**, n expressly confirms compatibility with **ñ**. The operative DL 14/2025 wording through **2 July** instead refers to **o**; do not substitute ñ into that earlier cross-reference. The compatibility of n/ñ for such a short period is listed as unresolved ([DL 14/2025 art. 65](https://www.boe.es/buscar/doc.php?id=DOGV-r-2025-90320); [Ley 3/2026 art. 82](https://www.boe.es/buscar/doc.php?id=BOE-A-2026-15683)).

For o, require the relevant registered grid self-consumption, certified off-grid solar/wind low-voltage installation, or solar/biomass/geothermal thermal installation; exclude installations legally mandatory under the building technical code and business-use property. Keep the invoice, registration/certification and non-cash payment evidence. Allocate collective installations by the community ownership coefficients. Track the **four following periods** for insufficient-tax carry independently from each year's 8,800-per-home investment base. For u, compare actual current/prior-year mortgage interest, not principal. Where the loan began in the preceding year, the statutory positive comparison is `current interest × days from the day after granting through prior 31 December / 360 − prior interest`, capped at actual current interest before applying the 50% and 100/F credit cap. This is not an assumed annualisation of last year's interest ([art. 4.Uno.o/u](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-1998-8202#a4)).

For w, require ownership or a real right to use the habitual home/building and works within the incorporated state/regional housing-plan criteria. Exclude ordinary garages, gardens, pools/sports annexes unless the statutory disabled-access exception applies, o-qualifying renewables and publicly financed expenditure. Apply the 5,500 base by ownership share and retain the provider NIF and non-cash payment evidence. For x, the pass must be issued by an entity adhering to the specified programme ([art. 4.Uno.w/x](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-1998-8202#a4)).

For y, the category maximum under **Order 5/2020 plus 10%** gives **550 urban conventional bicycle / 770 family cargo bicycle / 1,540 electric urban bicycle / 660 electrification kit / 495 personal-mobility vehicle**. Electric bicycles/kits must meet the authority's 250 W/25 km/h cut-off, approximately 50 km range, 36 V lithium-ion battery with minimum 9 Ah and the cited EPAC standard; personal-mobility vehicles require maximum 25 km/h, minimum 30 km range and minimum 7.8 Ah lithium-ion battery. Only the invoice-named purchaser claims; apportion the vehicle base if several taxpayers are entitled. A joint return does not create extra vehicles, but each actual acquiring taxpayer retains their one-vehicle entitlement. Article y excludes publicly financed cost from the category ceiling. The AEAT page's example subtracts aid from the enhanced category ceiling, but another sentence describes subtracting aid from actual cost and then capping: the differing-result aid-order case remains unconfirmed. Do not use a more favourable ordering without resolution ([art. 4.Uno.y](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-1998-8202#a4); [AEAT vehicle specifications, limits and conflicting aid wording](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/ayuda/manuales-videos-folletos/manuales-practicos/irpf-2025-deducciones-autonomicas/comunitat-valenciana/adquisicion-vehiculos-nuevos-pertenecientes-categorias-2020.html)).

**Preserved housing routes.** DT 1 retains former art. 3 bis for taxpayers entitled under national **DT 18**; the preserved article refers to the national complementary percentages rather than creating an unrestricted new-purchase credit. Apply the national grandfathering/base calculation in this Guide's legacy-home section and keep this route distinct from the additional regional k/l/m credits; detailed combined-claim allocation remains unresolved ([regional DT 1](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-1998-8202#dtprimera); [preserved art. 3 bis](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-1998-8202&b=10&tn=1&p=20021231#a3bis)).

**DA 13 remains a payment-year route for works actually performed in 2014/2015**, not a credit for newly performed 2026 works and not an automatically expired balance. Qualifying 2026 payments for those old works receive **10% for 2014 works / 25% for 2015 works**. The annual base is `4,500 × clamp(1-(B-23000)/2000,0,1)` I or `4,500 × clamp(1-(B-37000)/3000,0,1)` J, subject to a **5,000 cumulative base per home** and ownership shares. The AEAT implementation therefore limits positive claims to B below **25,000 I / 40,000 J**. Require qualifying habitual-home conservation/quality/sustainability/accessibility works, provider NIF and non-cash payment; exclude the specified annexes, o-eligible renewables and publicly financed cost. ## Worked example

### Comunitat Valenciana second child: factor F phases the amount

Hypothetical 2026 facts: a Valencian resident is the only entitled declaring taxpayer for her second child, born in 2026, and files individually with `B` (general BL + savings BL) **28,000**, within the **30,000** individual ordinary gate. Factor F is `1 − (28,000 − 27,000)/3,000 = 0.6667`. The second-child amount **750** is phased by F: `750 × (1 − 1,000/3,000) = 500` ([Ley 13/1997 art. 4.Uno.a.1 and 4.Cuatro](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-1998-8202#a4)).

```oa-check
name: Comunitat Valenciana second-child birth, phased by factor F
inputs:
  b: 28000
  amount: 750
steps:
  f: clamp(1 - (b - 27000) / 3000, 0, 1)
  credit: round(amount * f, 2)
expected:
  credit: 500.00
```
