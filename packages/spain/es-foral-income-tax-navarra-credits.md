### Navarra benefits credited after the differential quota

For a taxable, nonexempt qualifying birth-and-care benefit, compute the art 68 quater credit on the qualifying benefit amount, limited to the maximum Social Security benefit per child; where received across 2 taxyears allocate that maximum in proportion to receipts. The percentage is 25% at claimant income≤30,000; above 30,000 use `max(0,25−10×(income−30,000)/30,000)` percent, rounded 2 decimals. Income includes the benefit and exempt income, using 64.5's grossing-back rules. Equivalent qualifying public-service/mutuality and cooperative EPSV benefits follow the listed equivalents. Deduct this credit after the differential quota; do not both exempt the benefit and credit it. [Navarra DFL4/2008, current revision20April2026](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BON-n-2008-90013&p=20260420&tn=1)68 quater.

Art 68 widow/contributory-retirement/SOVI relief targets the 2026 annual threshold 15,400. Select the statutory category first: with entitlement to minimum supplements use the relevant minimum annual pension including specified gender-gap/maternity supplements; without that entitlement use qualifying actual annual pension amounts; concurrent pensions use the statutory sum. The provisional credit is the positive shortfall from 15,400. No member of the claimant's family unit may be obliged to file wealth tax. Total pension, other counted income and the credit must not exceed 21,619.59 individual or 30,800 for the family; reduce the credit by any excess, to zero. Exclude the specifically protected energy-poverty/home-loss grants. Annualise part-year pensions to determine the shortfall, then prorate to entitlement days. Guaranteed-income/minimum-vital-income receipts are incompatible for overlapping days; for part-year receipt prorate to days without those benefits. Widow-with-minimum-supplement and widow-SOVI categories permit the statutory advance application; already advanced amounts cannot be credited again in the return. Other widow/retirement categories prohibit an advance. Validate statutory category/pensionminimum, actual days and other income before giving the final amount. [Navarra DFL4/2008, current revision20April2026](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BON-n-2008-90013&p=20260420&tn=1)68(A)–(C), as amended for 2026.

### Navarra Emanzipa and DAVID: complete income and monthly method

The advance application is constitutive: a tax-return-only claim creates no right. Determine the latest income-tax period whose ordinary filing deadline has ended at the application date; if the latest campaign remains open, use the preceding campaign. Match the SARA denominator to **that same income year**. The official calculator expressly uses `income of year n × N × 0.94 / SARA of year n`. In October 2026, the latest ended campaign is income 2025, so use **10,919.71**. Resolution 357/2026 establishes **11,201.44 for income year 2026**; use it when 2026 is the relevant income year, including after that campaign ends in2027. Do not substitute the newest published SARA into a different income year. [P](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BON-n-2008-90013&p=20260420&tn=1) art 68 quinquies B.5/C.1; [HC](https://www.navarra.es/es/tramites/on/-/line/calcular-los-ingresos-familiares-ponderados); [S26](https://bon.navarra.es/es/anuncio/-/texto/2026/179/7).

For **Emanzipa**, age 23–35 inclusive at year-end, calculate income as general taxable base plus exempt income, without the household N/T weighting used for DAVID. The upper limit is22,000 for an individual outside a family unit or33,000 for the combined family unit. OF 187E/2026 increases the minimum from3,000 to **5,000**, enters into force on **18 September 2026** (day after BON publication17September), and expressly applies to annual renewals approved from entry into force. New applications from18September use5,000; do not retroactively apply it to awards/renewals approved before that change. Rest of the statutory conditions remain. Monthly household benefit is `min(50% × monthly rent,280)`. Divide that amount equally by **every contract holder**, including holders who cannot qualify, to compute each eligible holder's benefit. A620-rent contract with two holders gives140 each; one eligible holder cannot absorb the other's140. [P](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BON-n-2008-90013&p=20260420&tn=1) art 68 quinquies A/C.7, DA67; [E](https://www.lexnavarra.navarra.es/detalle.asp?r=58954); [ES](https://www.navarra.es/es/tramites/on/-/line/ayudas-al-alquiler-joven-emanzipa).

For **DAVID**, calculate the household's weighted income `IFP=BI×N×0.94`; calculate `r=IFP/SARA_same_income_year`. BI adds the applicants' and their spouses'/registered partners' general taxable bases (a negative general base is treated as zero) and actually received exempt income. Recast another tax administration's return on the same Navarra basis. Exclude judicial child-maintenance payments received by parents and nonperiodic public emergency/extraordinary social assistance. In the housing access/census calculation also include a cohabiting parent living with common children, even without marriage/registered partnership, as housing-law art 18.3 expressly requires. [HR](https://www.lexnavarra.navarra.es/detalle.asp?r=32564) arts7–8, AnnexII; [HL](https://www.lexnavarra.navarra.es/detalle.asp?r=29980) art 18.3.

For a widowed/separated/divorced applicant or dissolved registered partnership where the last return still covered the relationship, use only the applicant's income under separation of property; otherwise use the greater of own individual income and50% of the couple's joint income. Family membership follows the PIT family-unit definition with the regulation's shared-parental-authority presumption, not every economically dependent adult child. The wider inclusion of every cohabitant in AnnexII is expressly for protected-rent subsidy art 28; do not indiscriminately add unrelated housemates to the ordinary DAVID family unit. [HR](https://www.lexnavarra.navarra.es/detalle.asp?r=32564) art 7, AnnexII.

For the **VAIS** branch, prior recognised participation before1January of the application/renewal year is required. Its benefit is `min(60%×rent,360)` for a maximum of two consecutive years; the statutory scheme still awards/reviews the advance as prescribed. The minimum-income test is the one for access to VAIS, not automatically the ordinary5,000 census minimum.
