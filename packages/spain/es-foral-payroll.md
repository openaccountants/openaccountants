# Spain: foral wage withholding for2026

## Who this applies to

[Basque Economic Agreement art. 7](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2002-9969#a7) assigns ordinary employment withholding by where work/services are performed. Where common/Basque work is mixed or its location cannot be determined, use the assigned work centre; telework, foreign work and the specified sea-platform work follow that centre. Apply the competent Basque territory's rules, rather than the employee's annual IRPF residence alone. Pensions/public-system benefits generally follow recipient residence. Directors have payer-domicile and joint corporate-tax allocation rules. State public-sector pay has its separate State allocation and listed institutional exceptions. An employee living in Madrid but assigned to a Bilbao centre for telework does not acquire the State payroll algorithm simply from that residence.

[Navarra Economic Agreement art. 10](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-1990-31117#a1-13) similarly assigns ordinary work in Navarra, mixed/indeterminate work by assigned centre and telework/foreign work to that centre. It separately assigns Navarra/local public employees, route transport workers by payer domicile, pensions and directors under their specified limbs, with State public-sector exceptions and compensation provisions. Keep the final annual-residence tax calculation and the withholding administration as separate determinations. Obtain actual work, centre-assignment, payer and residence records.

### Basque computation, family facts and prospective changes

1. Determine expected **gross annual cash plus valued in-kind pay**, fixed and foreseeable variable pay, for the calendar year from this payer. Include extra pay; apply the jurisdiction's actual non-integration percentages and caps to eligible irregular/benefit income. Exclude only the qualifying employer pension/EPSV and specified RETA payments identified by that regulation. Do not subtract employee social contributions, the State's 2,000 employment expense or its payroll employment reduction before selecting this table. Those are separate annual-tax/payroll rules.
2. Count descendants for whom the applicable NF art. 79 deduction is available, using circumstances on **1 January or employment commencement**. The age gate is not more than 30 in that tax period, except the art. 82 disability/dependency case. Establish cohabitation/statutory maintenance, the child's non-exempt income no more than annual SMI, other-family-unit and own-return exclusions from the annual PIT procedure. A shared parent's annual credit fraction does not turn a qualifying child into half a child in the table. Keep signed family declarations; absent communication apply the rate without the uncommunicated facts.
3. Select the one percentage for annual forecast and descendant column, then apply the active-disability point reduction below and the applicable statutory minimum. The ordinary percentage applies to the **whole relevant payment**, including the integration-adjusted amount where the statute requires it. In-kind plus cash has the local combined-withholding/ingreso-a-cuenta treatment; the Basque regulations charge the resulting aggregate against cash. Retain the tax certificate/in-kind ledger rather than applying the common-territory forecast exclusions by analogy.
4. Where remuneration changes in-year, recalculate the table percentage using the changed annual forecast; apply it **prospectively from the change**. Contract continuation/re-engagement within the same calendar year counts both already paid and expected further pay. These provisions do not direct the State quota-minus-prior-withholding divided by remaining-pay algorithm. Preserve the old and new annual forecasts, table row, rate and effective payment.
5. A relationship **under one year** has a **2% minimum**; other special dependent relationships generally have **15%**, with the statutory prisoner/disability exceptions. Gipuzkoa expressly includes the specified artists/technical-assistant relationship in the 2% floor; do not infer an identical special-artists override from Bizkaia/Álava wording. Part-time employment alone is not stated as the Basque 2% trigger. A requested higher rate must be written, timely for payroll and retained under the jurisdiction's continuation/change rules.

## Cross-border points

Wage-withholding allocation follows the actual Concierto/Convenio points of connection described above. It is separate from final annual IRPF residence. A nonresident worker, treaty-exempt work or elected specialist impatriate regime needs the corresponding substantive law before replacing an ordinary percentage. Keep foreign tax and social-insurance certificates separately; wage withholding does not settle social contributions.

## Two worked examples that recompute

**Basque ordinary and disability control.** Full-year ordinary indefinite employee with30,000 forecast, two qualifying descendants and2,500 monthly taxable cash: table13%, withholding325. With category(a) active disability the reduction is7points, producing6%, withholding150. This does not reduce the base by social contributions or use the State employment reduction.

```oa-check
name: Basque2026 flat table and active disability points
inputs:
  annual_forecast: 30000
  monthly_payment: 2500
  selected_table_rate: 13
  disability_points: 7
steps:
  ordinary_withholding: monthly_payment * selected_table_rate / 100
  disability_rate: max(selected_table_rate - disability_points, 0)
  disability_withholding: monthly_payment * disability_rate / 100
expected:
  ordinary_withholding: 325
  disability_rate: 6
  disability_withholding: 150
```

**Navarra boundary and part-time control.** At30,000 forecast and two qualifying children, choose the threshold27,500 row (12.6%), giving315 on2,500 payment. At exactly30,250 the same12.6% row still applies; at30,250.01 the rate becomes13.7%. A separate lawful indefinite part-time employment with16,000 forecast and1,000 payment has zero from the general table but the2% incomplete-time floor produces20 withheld.

```oa-check
name: Navarra2026 table and incomplete-time minimum
inputs:
  monthly_payment: 2500
  table_rate: 12.6
  parttime_payment: 1000
  parttime_table_rate: 0
  incomplete_time_floor: 2
steps:
  ordinary_withholding: monthly_payment * table_rate / 100
  parttime_rate: max(parttime_table_rate, incomplete_time_floor)
  parttime_withholding: parttime_payment * parttime_rate / 100
expected:
  ordinary_withholding: 315
  parttime_rate: 2
  parttime_withholding: 20
```
