# Spain: Cantabria regional IRPF credits, 2026

**Sourced from the law.** This revision has not had an independent whole-pack review or accountant sign-off. Calculate regional credits after establishing tax residence and the taxpayer's facts. This Guide holds the Cantabria catalogue of [es-irpf-deductions](es-irpf-deductions.md), moved here unchanged on 6 November 2026, so that each Guide stays within the size the site can deliver. Specific unresolved interactions are listed in Sources.

## Who this applies to

An ordinary IRPF resident whose community of residence for 2026 under [LIRPF art. 72](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2006-20764#a72) is Cantabria. A regional credit reduces regional tax, not the income base. Before applying any row below, use [es-irpf-deductions](es-irpf-deductions.md) for the residence test, the deduction ledger, the state/regional order of credits, the national credits, deadlines, penalties and the questions to ask, and [es-income-tax](es-income-tax.md) for the bases, scales and minima. Where a row below refers to "the attributable-tax worksheet under The base", that worksheet is [es-irpf-deductions-attributable-tax](es-irpf-deductions-attributable-tax.md); national credits, the deduction ledger and any calculation "above" outside this catalogue are in [es-irpf-deductions](es-irpf-deductions.md#the-base). Basque and Navarre residents use [es-foral-income-tax](es-foral-income-tax.md); an IRNR taxpayer or an art. 93 incoming worker does not take these credits.

## Rates, bands and thresholds

All amounts are EUR. `BI` means the specified sum of general and savings **taxable income before base reductions** (base imponible); `BL` means base liquidable after the relevant reductions. Use the definition in each row: an income limit based on BI cannot be replaced with gross salary, BL or take-home pay. The table year is 2026 unless a transition is stated.

**Individual current-share-loss and progressivity-exempt extension, observed administrative method.** Keep all existing eligibility, documented contract-holder payment, strict10%test and regional-tax conditions. The loss must actually qualify for current integration, including the [33.5(f) homogeneous-share restriction](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2006-20764#a33); an economic loss with deferred tax recognition is not an eligible `L`.

For this observed population, the rent-burden denominator is `R=W+B−C`. Do not add `E` to this bounded denominator or subtract pension/base-liquidable reductions or personal/family minima. Exempt-progressivity income remains relevant to the tax-rate calculation under [DA20](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2006-20764#da-20); excluding it from this observed rent test does not eliminate that separate tax effect. This combines the statutory integration rules with actual regional software observations; neither the regional statute nor its manual expressly defines a universal `R=BI` formula or a clamp for every signed category.

For arts. 2.11.1–11.4, retain the required invoices/receipts and bank-payment evidence; cash is excluded. Do not impose the ordinary rent route's age gate on the municipality-specific rent provision. The rural work move requires residence in the move year and **three following years**, although the credit is claimed only in the first **two**. Joint filing allows 500 per qualifying mover, subject to the combined attributable-tax ceiling for those qualifying contributors; the mixed general/savings, minimum-spillover and base-reduction calculation is supplied in the attributable-tax worksheet under The base. Breach triggers the specified tax-and-interest recapture ([art. 2.11](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOCT-c-2008-90028#a2); [AEAT rural-move interpretation](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/ayuda/manuales-videos-folletos/manuales-practicos/irpf-2025-deducciones-autonomicas/comunidad-autonoma-cantabria/gastos-traslado-residencia-municipio-afectado-despoblamiento.html)).

The separate new-resident route requires no Cantabria habitual residence during the preceding **four years**, and residence maintained for the **three years following** arrival. AEAT includes arrivals from Ceuta/Melilla but excludes direct foreign arrivals for this route. Qualifying costs are necessary family travel/moving, descendants' compulsory/bachillerato schooling, habitual-home rent and **connection/contracting costs**, excluding ongoing utilities/service consumption. Test actual expenses in each of the four claim years. For joint returns apply each contributor's own rate/cap; AEAT treats an accompanying spouse as having the work motivation of the working spouse and allocates community-property costs equally. Unabsorbed credit generated in each year has **four following utilisation years**, oldest first, and both carried/current art. 2.16 amounts take priority over other regional credits. Do not confuse that utilisation clock with the four generation years. Vacant-home letting requires at least **one year** without habitation, letting, use or business allocation before the qualifying LAU lease, effective letting for at least **three years**, rent including annexes no more than **1,000 monthly**, and ownership of no more than **three homes**. The tenant cannot be spouse or a relative through the **third degree**. The statute allows a replacement lease within **six months** where the combined effective letting periods reach three years; maintain evidence of availability during the gap. Allocate the 500 credit by property participation instead of granting every co-owner the whole amount. The timing of repeated claims for one lease and compatibility with the separate new-resident/rural expense credits remain to confirm ([art. 2.17](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOCT-c-2008-90028#a2)).

Foundation gifts require the Cantabrian domicile, permitted purposes, regional registration, accounts rendered and the protectorate's ordered deposit. Disability-association gifts require Cantabrian domicile, Ley 49/2002 eligibility and the disability-support purpose. The 10% ceiling applies to the **combined base**, not just the regional nominal credit. The first art. 2.13 inflation provision is limited expressly to **2022**; distinguish it from the separately numbered education paragraph, which the consolidated text also labels 13 ([art. 2.4/13](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOCT-c-2008-90028#a2)).

SME investment covers formation/increase subscriptions in SA, SL or their labour-company equivalents meeting the EU SME definition. Combined capital/votes with spouse and relatives through the **third degree** must not exceed **40% on any day**; hold at least **three years**, with Cantabrian registered/tax offices, genuine activity and the required Cantabrian-resident full-time employee from the first year for formation. Capital increase must be within **three years** of incorporation, add at least one full-time-equivalent employee in the **two following fiscal years** compared with the prior 12 months, and maintain that increase another **24 months**. The investor cannot hold executive/management functions or employment in the entity. Retain a public deed identifying the investment. Preserve the stated entity/ownership/role conditions for three years; breach recaptures the tax saved plus interest ([art. 2.6](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOCT-c-2008-90028#a2)).

Social-economy recipients need the statutory status/registration, Cantabrian registered/tax offices and annual-average full-time general-regime employment, maintained at least **three years**. Family-through-third-degree participation must not exceed **40%**. Keep the public deed, gift certificate/value evidence and bank-payment proof. Membership equity must remain in the taxpayer's assets at least **five years**, a different clock from the entity-condition period. Do not deduct one amount under several social-economy modalities or combine the same investment with the foundation/Coopera or SME-equity routes; breach recaptures tax and interest ([art. 2.12](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOCT-c-2008-90028#a2)).

Inbound-investor assets under the current wording are non-housing/non-tourist-housing real estate **not allocated to an economic activity**, situated in Cantabria, or the specified debt/equity securities of entities with Cantabrian registered/tax offices and genuine activity. For securities, family aggregation is through the **second degree**, capital/votes at most **40% throughout maintenance**, with no executive/management role or employment. The claimant must become an ordinary Cantabria IRPF taxpayer after **five years** without Spanish residence. Invest in the arrival year or next; qualifying Spanish-entity debt/equity can also be bought in the preceding year. Hold for **six years**, permitting an onerous sale only with **full proceeds reinvested within one month**, and keep Cantabria IRPF residence through the final maintenance year. Insufficient-tax amounts have **five following claim years**, starting with arrival instead if the eligible investment preceded it. The article applies to qualifying arrivals from **1 January 2025** and is incompatible with the SME credit for the same investment; breach of residence/holding/full-reinvestment conditions loses the credit. Do not import Madrid's asset geography or family-degree tests into this provision ([art. 2.15](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOCT-c-2008-90028#a2)).

For the10% burden test, keep a separate denominator `R` and the underlying general/savings income, BI, BL, exempt-income and household-allocation records. **The current statute and retrieved AEAT2025 rental page do not define whether `R` is BI, BL or another net-income measure, or expressly resolve its aggregation on joint filing.** DA1 defines home/family/disability concepts but not this income. Do not import the health-credit `BL−minimum` test or set `R` equal to gross salary. Once the legally applicable `R` is established, eligibility is `eligible_rent > 0.10×R` and credit is `min(0.10×eligible_rent,300 or600)`. At `R=20,000`, rent2,000 fails and2,000.01 passes; these are **conditional comparator controls**, not certification that a particular person's denominator equals20,000. [CurrentCantabria art.2.1](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOCT-c-2008-90028#a2); [exactAEATrent guidance](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/ayuda/manuales-videos-folletos/manuales-practicos/irpf-2025-deducciones-autonomicas/comunidad-autonoma-cantabria/arrendamiento-vivienda-habitual-jovenes-mayores-discapacidad.html).

## Worked example

### Cantabria birth credit: second year of an earlier birth plus a new birth with disability

Hypothetical 2026 facts: a Cantabrian resident is the only taxpayer entitled for two children and meets every condition this Guide states for art. 2.10. Child A was born in **2025**, so 2026 is the first of the two following years: **1,400**. Child B is born in **2026** with a recognised disability of at least **33%**: the amount is raised to **1,600**. The 2026 credit is `1,400 + 1,600 = 3,000` ([Cantabria DL 62/2008 art. 2.10](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOCT-c-2008-90028#a2)).

```oa-check
name: Cantabria birth/adoption, continuing year plus disability amount
inputs:
  child_a_amount: 1400
  child_b_amount: 1600
steps:
  credit: child_a_amount + child_b_amount
expected:
  credit: 3000
```
