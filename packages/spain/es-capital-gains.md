# ES Capital Gains

## Quick reference

**Quick reference**

| Item | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Capital gains type | "Savings income" (rentas del ahorro) — no distinction for holding period |
| Rates | 19%–28% (progressive savings income scale) |
| Annual exemption | None |
| Legislation | LIRPF Art. 46, 49 |
| Form | IRPF Model 100 (residents), Model 210 (non-residents) |

## Savings income tax rates (2025)

**Savings income tax rates (2025)**

| Band | Rate |
| --- | --- |
| First €6,000 | 19% |
| €6,001 – €50,000 | 21% |
| €50,001 – €200,000 | 23% |
| €200,001 – €300,000 | 27% |
| Above €300,000 | 28% |

- **Holding period irrelevance** — Capital gains are taxed at these rates regardless of how long the asset was held. (Spain removed the distinction between short-term and long-term gains in 2015.)

## Main residence exemption (reinvestment relief)

- **Habitual residence reinvestment exemption** — Gain from sale of habitual residence is fully exempt if the entire proceeds are reinvested in a new habitual residence within 2 years (before or after the sale).
- **Partial reinvestment** — If only part of the proceeds are reinvested, a proportional exemption applies.
- **Age 65+ exemption** — Taxpayers aged 65+ are fully exempt from CGT on sale of their habitual residence regardless of reinvestment.

## Capital losses

- **Loss offset restriction — financial assets** — Losses on financial assets can only offset gains from financial assets.
- **Loss offset restriction — other assets** — Losses on other assets (real property) can only offset gains from other assets.
- **Loss carryforward period** — Unused losses carry forward 4 years.

## Non-residents

- **CGT rate for EU/EEA non-residents** — 19%
- **CGT rate for other non-residents** — 24%
- **Buyer withholding obligation** — Buyer must withhold 3% of the purchase price for real property sales by non-residents.
