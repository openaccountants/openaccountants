# Spain: Aragón regional IRPF credits, 2026

**Sourced from the law.** This revision has not had an independent whole-pack review or accountant sign-off. Calculate regional credits after establishing tax residence and the taxpayer's facts. This Guide holds the Aragón catalogue of [es-irpf-deductions](es-irpf-deductions.md), moved here unchanged on 6 November 2026, so that each Guide stays within the size the site can deliver. Specific unresolved interactions are listed in Sources.

## Who this applies to

An ordinary IRPF resident whose community of residence for 2026 under [LIRPF art. 72](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2006-20764#a72) is Aragón. A regional credit reduces regional tax, not the income base. Before applying any row below, use [es-irpf-deductions](es-irpf-deductions.md) for the residence test, the deduction ledger, the state/regional order of credits, the national credits, deadlines, penalties and the questions to ask, and [es-income-tax](es-income-tax.md) for the bases, scales and minima. Where a row below refers to "the attributable-tax worksheet under The base", that worksheet is [es-irpf-deductions-attributable-tax](es-irpf-deductions-attributable-tax.md); national credits, the deduction ledger and any calculation "above" outside this catalogue are in [es-irpf-deductions](es-irpf-deductions.md#the-base). Basque and Navarre residents use [es-foral-income-tax](es-foral-income-tax.md); an IRNR taxpayer or an art. 93 incoming worker does not take these credits.

## Rates, bands and thresholds

All amounts are EUR. `BI` means the specified sum of general and savings **taxable income before base reductions** (base imponible); `BL` means base liquidable after the relevant reductions. Use the definition in each row: an income limit based on BI cannot be replaced with gross salary, BL or take-home pay. The table year is 2026 unless a transition is stated.

### Aragón: ordinary credits and rural enhancements

Apply the ordinary credit first, then any qualifying rural enhancement. `BI` is general plus savings base imponible; `BL` is the corresponding sum of bases liquidables. Where specified, `adjusted BI` means **BI less only the taxpayer minimum and descendant minimum**, not every personal/family minimum. Income comparisons, ages and holding periods in these rows are statutory conditions, not general defaults.

For dependent care, share equally where several taxpayers qualify, with the closer-degree relative taking priority unless that relative's non-exempt annual income is at most 8,000, when the next degree can qualify. For first/second-child relief, share between entitled cohabiting taxpayers and do not also claim art. 110-3 for that same child. Childcare requires a minimum-entitled descendant and an authorised centre; share the credit equally when the child lives with several entitled taxpayers. The childcare and extreme-depopulation-residence tests use **BL** for their main threshold and separately restrict **savings BI**; do not apply a generic total-BI test ([arts. 110-5, 110-16 and 110-17](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOA-d-2005-90006#a11017)).

The gift recipient must be Aragón or its dependent public body with the specified environmental/research purpose, or a qualifying registered nonprofit whose exclusive/principal aim is environmental protection or scientific/technical research and development. Investment arts. 110-8, 110-9 and 110-19 are incompatible for the **same investment**. The social-economy route requires the entity to qualify under Ley 5/2011, registered/tax offices in Aragón, at least one full-time general-regime employee and a public deed identifying investor/amount. Count spouse and relatives through the third degree for its 40% ownership test; maintain the contribution and prescribed entity conditions five years. Breach brings recapture plus interest ([arts. 110-6, 110-8, 110-9 and 110-19](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOA-d-2005-90006#a11019)).

The art. 110-9 new-company route incorporates current **LIRPF art. 68.1**: national maximum base **100,000**; eligible unlisted SA/SL/labour-company subscription, genuine economic means/activity, company/group own funds at acquisition-year start no more than **400,000**, formation or capital increase within **five years**, or **seven** for a qualifying emerging company; holding **more than three and less than twelve years**. The ordinary 40% combined spouse/second-degree-family ownership ceiling has the statutory qualifying startup-founder exception. Exclude continuation of the same activity under a different ownership vehicle and obtain the investee's certificate. Aragón additionally requires registered/tax offices there and prohibits the investor's executive, management or employment role, while allowing board membership. Account for the statutory reinvestment-exemption restriction and prevent the same subscription amount entering both state and regional bases. For one otherwise qualifying subscription amount `I`, with exempt-reinvestment disposal proceeds `P` (zero if that restriction does not apply), the two minimum-investment floors give regional base `max(0, I − max(100,000, P))`, then credit `min(4,000, 20% × regional base)`. Both floors constrain the same investment; they are not blindly added together. A small subscription does not receive the Aragón credit merely because it is a new company ([LIRPF art. 68.1](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2006-20764#a68); [Aragón art. 110-9](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOA-d-2005-90006#a1109)).

For arts. 110-7/110-10, use the incorporated pre-2013 home rules, including the **9,040 annual acquisition/rehabilitation base cap**, eligible acquisition/financing costs, prior-home/reinvestment adjustments and the end-versus-start net-wealth check, excluding interest/other financing costs from the investment amount tested and excluding mere valuation movements. Ordinary habitual residence requires three continuous years and actual occupation within twelve months, with the historical necessary-move/death exceptions. Qualifying rehabilitation is protected work or the prescribed structural reconstruction exceeding 25% of the relevant acquisition/market value excluding land; it is not every repair. Rural art. 110-10 applies to acquisitions/rehabilitations from 1 January 2012, with a home in a municipality below 3,000 inhabitants or a legally recognised local/population entity separate from the municipal capital. These incorporated definitions do not impose the national DT 18 pre-2013 acquisition gate on every new Aragón regional purchase ([Aragón arts. 110-7/110-10](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOA-d-2005-90006#a11010); [historical LIRPF arts. 68.1/70](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2006-20764&p=20121031&tn=1#a68); [historical RIRPF arts. 54–55](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2007-6820&p=20111220&tn=1#a54)).

For post-dación rent, establish settlement of the **whole outstanding mortgage debt** by the habitual home and a lease with a purchase option over that same home under art. 121-10, alongside the bond and income conditions. Ordinary young-tenant rent does not automatically qualify for this special credit. The social-housing landlord route requires placing the property at the disposal of the regional government or authorised programme manager, then the actual reduced rental-income and attributable-tax calculation. Use the attributable-tax worksheet under The base for mixed general/savings income, minima and ordinary base reductions; preserve its precise signed-income allocation boundary ([arts. 110-12/110-13 and 121-10](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOA-d-2005-90006#a12110)).

The following caps apply **per descendant**, not to a pooled household education budget. Books/material use the primary/compulsory-secondary curriculum and school-approved equipment/material definition. Support tuition covers the prescribed infant, compulsory basic or basic-vocational subjects outside school hours, including properly IAE-registered private tutors. Both tuition/training and books require descendant-minimum entitlement, actual payment, evidence and public-aid reconciliation. Compute the capped deduction first, then subtract the public aid specified by the article, with no negative credit. The paying ascendant claims, with the statutory equal expenditure attribution for spouses under the Aragón marital-property consortium or an analogous regime ([arts. 110-11/110-21/110-22](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOA-d-2005-90006#a1-35)).

#### Aragón differentiated rural regime

Arts. 160-1/160-2 apply the following enhancements, without replacing the ordinary credit's other conditions. Establish the actual settlement rank and **ISDT below 100**: eligible high-depopulation-risk settlements are ranks **VIII/IX**, and extreme-risk settlements rank **X**. Residence ordinarily must continue through the tax-accrual calendar year and the next **four years**; on a married joint return the general residence condition can be satisfied by either spouse. Property-based benefits also require the property in a qualifying settlement. The statutory acquisition/starting-business relocation alternative has its own four-year timing rule. Obtain the settlement classification evidence and maintain a future-condition/recapture schedule ([arts. 160-1/160-2](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOA-d-2005-90006#a1601)).

| Affected credit | Differentiated regime adjustment | Tax year | Official source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Third/subsequent child, disabled-child event, international adoption | Increase the respective ordinary deduction by 20% | 2026 | [Art. 160-2.1–160-2.3](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOA-d-2005-90006#a1602) |
| Dependent-person care | Replace 150 with 300 | 2026 | [Art. 160-2.4](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOA-d-2005-90006#a1602) |
| Rural habitual-home acquisition/rehabilitation | Replace 5% with 7.5% | 2026 | [Art. 160-2.5](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOA-d-2005-90006#a1602) |
| Books/material cap | Increase each ordinary cap by 20%: joint 120 / 60 / 45; individual 60 / 45 / 30; large-family joint 180 / individual 90 | 2026 | [Art. 160-2.6](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOA-d-2005-90006#a1602); [AEAT cap interpretation](https://www3.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/ayuda/manuales-videos-folletos/manuales-practicos/irpf-2025-deducciones-autonomicas/comunidad-autonoma-aragon/adquisicion-libros-texto-material-escolar.html) |
| Childcare caps | Increase 250 / 125 by 20%, giving 300 / 150; the 15% expense rate remains | 2026 | [Art. 160-2.7](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOA-d-2005-90006#a1602) |
| Qualifying extreme-risk residence | 600 per qualifying taxpayer, including each entitled taxpayer on a joint return; rank X, BL strictly below 35,000 individual / 50,000 joint and savings BI at most 4,000 | 2026 | [Art. 160-2.8](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOA-d-2005-90006#a1602) |

Do not use the below-3,000 home-location test or below-10,000 child-event test as a substitute for the rank/ISDT test. Do not add a general 20% uplift to support tuition or independent-living training: those articles are not among the enumerated enhancements. The art. 160-2.8 residence credit is specifically for rank X, not every rank VIII/IX claimant. Its joint-return per-person entitlement still requires that person's conditions ([arts. 160-1/160-2](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOA-d-2005-90006#a1602)).

Art. 110-15 health-insurance relief has no content from 2016; art. 110-18 flood aid is repealed. Art. 110-20 describes personal public-transport/bicycle-system passes up to **50**, but expressly conditions use on implementing rules entering into force. Their operative application for 2026 is not confirmed here, so no transport claim is calculated from the headline amount ([arts. 110-15, 110-18 and 110-20](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOA-d-2005-90006#a11020)).

## Worked example

### Aragón third child: income-tested amount and the differentiated rural regime

Hypothetical 2026 facts: a married couple resident in Aragón file a joint return with adjusted BI **30,000**, within the **35,000** joint limit, so the third-child birth credit is **600** rather than 500. They live in a settlement that meets every condition of the differentiated rural regime stated in this Guide (rank, ISDT below 100 and continued residence), which increases the ordinary deduction by **20%**. The credit on the joint return is `600 × 1.20 = 720` ([DL 1/2005 arts. 110-2 and 160-1/160-2](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOA-d-2005-90006#a1-2)). Outside a qualifying settlement it stays at 600.

```oa-check
name: Aragón third child, joint return, rural 20% increase
inputs:
  adjusted_bi_joint: 30000
  rural_increase: 0.20
steps:
  ordinary: 600
  credit_rural: ordinary * (1 + rural_increase)
expected:
  ordinary: 600
  credit_rural: 720.00
```
