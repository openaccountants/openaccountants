# Ethiopia Company Formation & Entity Choice v0.3

## Scope

Use this Guide for initial entity choice and incorporation in Ethiopia in 2026. Confirm the number and nationality of owners, activity and capital before choosing a form. An ordinary PLC and a one-member PLC are separate forms. This is partial formation guidance, not a sector-licence opinion or a completed filing.

## Ask the client first

- Who are the owners, how many are there, and are any investors foreign or treated as domestic under investment law?
- What activity and location will the business have, and is sector permission already documented?
- What cash and in-kind capital is available, and is this a new project, acquisition or reinvestment?
- Is limited liability required, and will shares be offered to the public?
- Are the manager, address, identity documents and proposed names ready?
- Will the business employ staff, make taxable supplies or use a non-standard accounting year?

## The method, step by step

### 1. Choose the legal form

The Commercial Code recognises general partnership, limited partnership, limited liability partnership, joint venture, share company, PLC and one-person PLC (art. 174). Investment Proclamation art. 8 also permits a sole proprietorship and an enterprise established abroad. A foreign branch therefore remains an option, but its detailed registration documents require a separate EIC check. [Commercial Code arts 172 to 177](https://justice.gov.et/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/%E1%8A%A0%E1%8B%8B%E1%8C%85-%E1%89%81%E1%8C%A5%E1%88%AD-1243-2013.pdf); [Investment Proclamation arts 3, 8](https://ldi.gov.et/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Investment-Proclamation-Proc-No.-1180_2020.pdf).

Do not rank one form as the most common without evidence. Choose from the actual ownership and capital constraints. A PLC cannot offer shares for public subscription (art. 495(2)); the one-member form has separate legal personality and limited liability conditional on fully making the contribution (art. 534). [Commercial Code](https://justice.gov.et/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/%E1%8A%A0%E1%8B%8B%E1%8C%85-%E1%89%81%E1%8C%A5%E1%88%AD-1243-2013.pdf).

### 2. Check foreign-investment permission before paying capital

For ordinary projects within its scope, the capital requirement is additional to the chosen company's capital rules. EIC flags separate restricted export/import/wholesale/retail trade rules under Directive 1001/2024 and special economic/free-trade-zone rules under Proclamation 1322/2024; those branches are non-usable holds here. See [EIC current requirements](https://investinethiopia.gov.et/requirement). Investment Proclamation art. 9(4) excludes reinvested profits/dividends from an existing enterprise in an area open to foreign investment, specified board members following PLC-to-share-company conversion, and a foreign investor buying an existing foreign-owned enterprise or shares in it. Do not treat these as general waivers for all acquisitions. Foreign investors and joint foreign/domestic investors require an investment permit; an existing-enterprise or share acquisition needs EIC prior approval under art. 10(3). Sector reservations are governed by the regulations and Board decisions under art. 6. [Investment Proclamation arts 6, 8 to 10](https://ldi.gov.et/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Investment-Proclamation-Proc-No.-1180_2020.pdf).

### 3. Name, constitutive document, TIN and commercial registration

1. Use the Ministry of Trade and Regional Integration or the competent registration body for the domestic route. Under Directive 935/2022 art. 5, submit business-name Form 01 with at least three alternatives. The registering body checks the central register, then arranges memorandum authentication and TIN issuance by letters or linked electronic service. The cleared name must be registered within six months from issue after those steps (art. 5(4)); the old draft's approximate 30-day reservation is replaced. [Directive 935/2022 arts 2(4), 5](https://www.motri.gov.et/sites/default/files/resource/COMMERCIAL%20REGISTRATION%2C%20LICENSING%20AND%20POST%20LICENSING%20INSPECTION%2C%20NO.%209352022_0.pdf).
2. Prepare the memorandum with the owners, nationality and addresses, company name and address, business purpose, fully paid capital and each contribution, management and other required particulars (Commercial Code art. 500). For a one-member PLC use the authenticated declaration and nominee acceptance (arts 536, 537), rather than an agreement between several members. The permitted document language and a universal domestic articles requirement are not confirmed here. [Commercial Code](https://justice.gov.et/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/%E1%8A%A0%E1%8B%8B%E1%8C%85-%E1%89%81%E1%8C%A5%E1%88%AD-1243-2013.pdf); [Directive art. 5(3)](https://www.motri.gov.et/sites/default/files/resource/COMMERCIAL%20REGISTRATION%2C%20LICENSING%20AND%20POST%20LICENSING%20INSPECTION%2C%20NO.%209352022_0.pdf).
3. Fund the formation account and retain bank evidence. For an ordinary PLC the cash shares must be deposited before registration (art. 499(2)). [Commercial Code](https://justice.gov.et/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/%E1%8A%A0%E1%8B%8B%E1%8C%85-%E1%89%81%E1%8C%A5%E1%88%AD-1243-2013.pdf). For the investment route use [EIC's current requirements page](https://investinethiopia.gov.et/requirement) and its eInvest service. It distinguishes sole proprietor, branch, one-member PLC and PLC/share-company documents; provide the identity/visa, contribution and draft constitutional documents for the actual route. Corporate shareholders need the stated authenticated incorporation/resolution/constitutional/representation documents.
4. Apply for commercial registration using Form 02 as identified in Directive art. 13. Retain the commercial-registration certificate issued after the applicable requirements are met (art. 13(5)). A one-member PLC also submits its unilateral declaration and authenticated trust/nominee document (art. 13(7)). Legal personality of business organisations other than joint ventures arises on commercial registration (Commercial Code art. 175). [Directive](https://www.motri.gov.et/sites/default/files/resource/COMMERCIAL%20REGISTRATION%2C%20LICENSING%20AND%20POST%20LICENSING%20INSPECTION%2C%20NO.%209352022_0.pdf); [Commercial Code](https://justice.gov.et/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/%E1%8A%A0%E1%8B%8B%E1%8C%85-%E1%89%81%E1%8C%A5%E1%88%AD-1243-2013.pdf).
5. Apply for the activity business licence using Form 03 under Directive art. 18, satisfying the underlying Regulation arts 23 to 26. Keep the licence and actual permitted activities. Renewal is a separate Form 03 service under Directive art. 19. Do not treat name clearance or commercial registration as completion of licensing. [Directive arts 18, 19](https://www.motri.gov.et/sites/default/files/resource/COMMERCIAL%20REGISTRATION%2C%20LICENSING%20AND%20POST%20LICENSING%20INSPECTION%2C%20NO.%209352022_0.pdf).

The printed directive and EIC requirements above were read on 16 November 2026. Authenticated portal screens, current fees, live submission fields and service completion dates were not tested. No application has been submitted.

## Worked cases

**Preserved VAT case, non-usable registration conclusion pending directive confirmation.** A new company expects taxable sales of ETB 180,000 monthly. Annualised sales are ETB 2,160,000, ETB 160,000 above the ETB 2,000,000 amount in Proc. 1341/2024 art. 12. That article also permits a Ministry directive to change the amount; Directive 1104/2025 was not read. The arithmetic below does not establish a current registration obligation. Keep this case on hold until that directive and the supplies/related-person conditions are checked. **Preserved first-employee case.** An eligible Ethiopian-national employee of an ordinary private PLC receives regular-hours monthly salary ETB 10,000. Assume the employee is engaged for at least 45 days under the statutory employee definition, the worker is not in an excluded/consent-only category, and there are no other remuneration components or other scheme issues. Employer pension is ETB 1,100 and salary plus employer pension is ETB 11,100. This is not net pay or a complete employer-cost quotation. [Proc. 1268/2022 arts 2(1), 2(7), 3, 10](https://www.hopr.gov.et/admin/Files/2026/2/25/fc53f560-b228-4314-b372-da2b26ef4b53_Upploads_04012023090127_%E1%8B%A8%E1%8C%8D%E1%88%8D%20%E1%8B%B5%E1%88%AD%E1%8C%85%E1%89%B5%20%E1%88%A0%E1%88%AB%E1%89%B0%E1%8A%9E%E1%89%BD%20%E1%8C%A1%E1%88%A8%E1%89%B3%20%E1%8A%A0%E1%8B%8B%E1%8C%85%20%E1%89%81%E1%8C%A5%E1%88%AD%201268%20%281%29.pdf).

**Decision both ways.** For a domestic non-regulated business with ETB 15,000 cash capital, one owner can meet the one-member PLC capital minimum, but cannot satisfy the ordinary PLC member count. If a second owner joins and total cash capital remains ETB 15,000, the ordinary PLC minimum capital is also met; allocating ETB 7,500 to each leaves no capital shortfall. Authentication, nominee requirements on the single-owner route, registration and licensing still apply. This is a formation comparison, not a tax-saving recommendation. [Commercial Code arts 495, 496, 499, 534 to 537](https://justice.gov.et/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/%E1%8A%A0%E1%8B%8B%E1%8C%85-%E1%89%81%E1%8C%A5%E1%88%AD-1243-2013.pdf).

```oa-check
name: Domestic one-owner or two-owner PLC capital
inputs:
  capital: 15000
  one_member_minimum: 15000
  ordinary_minimum: 15000
  two_owners: 2
steps:
  - single_shortfall: max(one_member_minimum - capital, 0)
  - ordinary_shortfall: max(ordinary_minimum - capital, 0)
  - each_contribution: capital / two_owners
expected:
  single_shortfall: 0.00
  ordinary_shortfall: 0.00
  each_contribution: 7500.00
```

## When to refuse or refer

Pause formation advice where the owners, sector permission, foreign-investor classification, source of capital or constitutive documents are unknown. Refer foreign branches, regulated activities and complex in-kind contributions for their specific requirements. Do not promise the former draft's domestic incorporation turnaround; it has no operative confirmation.

## Filing and payment

Keep commercial registration, business licence renewal, statutory accounts and tax obligations as separate completion items. Use [Directive 935/2022 art. 19](https://www.motri.gov.et/sites/default/files/resource/COMMERCIAL%20REGISTRATION%2C%20LICENSING%20AND%20POST%20LICENSING%20INSPECTION%2C%20NO.%209352022_0.pdf) for the licence-renewal service and the [tax overview](et-tax-overview.md) for income-tax/VAT timing. Start [payroll](ethiopia-payroll.md) and [social contributions](ethiopia-social-contributions.md) when staff are hired; do not assume all pension categories are covered. The current filing portals, form fields, payment references and licence-renewal due date remain on hold below.

## Completion checklist

- Record the chosen legal form, owners, activity and capital evidence.
- Keep the approved name and authenticated constitutive instrument as applicable.
- Keep TIN, commercial registration, investment approval/permit if required, and activity licence separately.
- Record the accounting period, licence-renewal task and tax/payroll handoffs.
- Leave every unresolved item below open; a draft checklist does not authorise trading.
