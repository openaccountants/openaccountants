# Jordan company formation and entity choice v0.1

> **General reference only.** Prepare a formation working paper from the actual owners, activities and documents. Obtain qualified Jordan professional review before submission or acting. Unconfirmed items in Sources cannot be used as default legal positions.

## Scope

Choose between the existing LLC, private shareholding company, public shareholding company, general or limited partnership, and foreign-company branch routes. Companies Law art. 6 identifies the domestic forms; the Ministry's company-type guidance also distinguishes operating and non-operating foreign companies. The detailed application below follows an LLC, while the other original choices remain part of the entity decision. Do not transfer LLC capital, liability or procedure rules to another form. [Companies Law arts. 2, 6, 53](<https://www.jsc.gov.jo/Uploads/Files/companies_jordan-2026.pdf>); [Ministry of Investment company-type FAQ](https://invest.jo/ar/faqs).

The Companies Control Department (CCD), linked to the Minister of Industry, Trade and Supply, administers company registration under the law. Registration is a distinct step from activity permissions, foreign-investment screening, tax registration and employee social-security onboarding. The earlier whole-Guide hold had no primary company-law research; the confirmed extracts below now have actual official readings. Detailed current foreign-activity restrictions and live registration/payment screens remain held.

## Ask the client first

- Which exact activities, sectors, municipalities and operating locations are proposed?
- Who are the legal and beneficial owners, their nationalities, proposed interests and signing representatives?
- Which legal form is proposed, and why is a branch, partnership or shareholding company being considered instead of an LLC?
- What cash and in-kind capital, payment dates, bank evidence and valuations are available?
- What constitutional documents, manager authorities and sector/foreign-investment approvals exist?
- When will trading and employment begin, and what tax/social-security registrations already exist?

## The method, step by step

### 1. Establish the form, owners and capital

Do not use the former assertion of no LLC statutory minimum or the approximate JOD 1,000 practice figure as a legal floor. The Ministry FAQ's broad no-minimum wording does not displace the dedicated LLC regulation or the private-shareholding minimum. Likewise, its foreign-investor guidance reports removal of a general investment-project capital condition; it does not establish that every sector, entity or licence has no capital condition. The old blanket JOD 50,000 foreign contribution remains unconfirmed and must not be assumed. ### 2. Prepare the name and constitutional application

Check that the proposed name is not fraudulent, unlawful or confusingly similar to a registered company. Include the LLC legal-form suffix, capital and registration details on its business papers as required. The application goes to the Controller with the approved-form memorandum and articles. Sign before the Controller/authorized delegate, a notary or a licensed lawyer as permitted by art. 57; notarization is not the only statutory signing route. [Companies Law arts. 5, 55, 57](<https://www.jsc.gov.jo/Uploads/Files/companies_jordan-2026.pdf>).

Record the name, purposes and head office; partner names, nationalities and service addresses; capital and each interest; in-kind assets/contributors/values; and additional legally required information. The articles also address management/powers and borrowing limits, transfers, profit/loss allocation, meetings/quorums/decision procedures and liquidation. Do not treat an informal commercial summary as the signed constitutional document. [Art. 57(b)–(c)](<https://www.jsc.gov.jo/Uploads/Files/companies_jordan-2026.pdf>).

Identify the activity's actual foreign-investment, sector and premises permissions. The original Regulation 7/2023 arts. 10–13 contains exceptions, restricted activities, Cabinet relaxation and prohibited activities; it is not a blanket foreign-ownership approval. Its full current list after the listed 2026 amendment has not been cleared here, so do not approve an activity or ownership percentage from that old list. The Ministry links its [investment-services portal](https://portal.moin.gov.jo/) for registration/licensing support; no authenticated form or submission has been tested. [Original Regulation arts. 10–13](<https://opendata.gov.jo/dataset/MIGRATED-2050-2023/resource/e89c6e18-8c08-41be-8abf-9b7c88f8a9fe/download/-7-2023.pdf?increment=true>); [Ministry FAQ, registration steps](https://invest.jo/ar/faqs).

### 3. Obtain the decision, registration evidence and capital records

Article 59(a) provides a registration decision within 15 days after submission/signing by the partners, subject to legal compliance and correction of defects. Refusal may be challenged to the Minister within 30 days of notification, then an adverse ministerial decision to the Administrative Court within 30 days of notification. This is not the old two-to-six-week service promise and does not settle other approvals. [Companies Law art. 59(a)](<https://www.jsc.gov.jo/Uploads/Files/companies_jordan-2026.pdf>).

After approval and collection of registration fees, the Controller issues the registration certificate and publication follows in the Gazette and CCD website. Record the actual registration date, certificate, fee receipt and capital-payment deadlines. Supply the bank evidence for the first required payment and track the unpaid balance. Article 59(c) restricts return of formation deposits without the Controller's abandonment certificate. [Art. 59(b)–(c)](<https://www.jsc.gov.jo/Uploads/Files/companies_jordan-2026.pdf>).

**Source corroboration:** the JSC-posted consolidation and the official AML Unit's consolidation both contain the amended 50%-within-60-days rule and the two-year balance rule. Those actual clauses support the method above. The earlier Guide's claimed April 2024 change date is not established. [JSC consolidation art. 59](<https://www.jsc.gov.jo/Uploads/Files/companies_jordan-2026.pdf>); [AML Unit consolidation art. 59](<https://www.amlu.gov.jo/ebv4.0/root_storage/ar/eb_list_page/%D9%82%D8%A7%D9%86%D9%88%D9%86_%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B4%D8%B1%D9%83%D8%A7%D8%AA_%D8%B1%D9%82%D9%85_%2822%29_%D9%84%D8%B3%D9%86%D8%A9_1997_%D9%88%D8%AA%D8%B9%D8%AF%D9%8A%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%AA%D9%87.pdf>).

### 4. Establish the annual records and hand over tax/payroll

The LLC manager/board prepares annual financial statements, notes, cash flows and the annual business report, audited by a licensed auditor under the recognized accounting/audit standards. Submit them to the ordinary annual general meeting and supply the Controller with the documents/recommendations before the end of the first three months of the new financial year. The ordinary annual meeting occurs within the first four months. These are separate duties, not interchangeable deadlines. [Companies Law art. 62](<https://www.jsc.gov.jo/Uploads/Files/companies_jordan-2026.pdf>); [art. 64(a), AML Unit-posted consolidation](<https://www.amlu.gov.jo/ebv4.0/root_storage/ar/eb_list_page/%D9%82%D8%A7%D9%86%D9%88%D9%86_%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B4%D8%B1%D9%83%D8%A7%D8%AA_%D8%B1%D9%82%D9%85_%2822%29_%D9%84%D8%B3%D9%86%D8%A9_1997_%D9%88%D8%AA%D8%B9%D8%AF%D9%8A%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%AA%D9%87.pdf>).

Maintain the partner register and record ownership changes. Separately assemble the ISTD income-tax/GST and SSC onboarding facts. Do not assume every newly incorporated company is immediately required to register for GST or that company registration itself completes tax/social-security obligations. Use [corporate income tax](jo-corporate-income-tax.md), [GST](jordan-gst.md) and [payroll/social security](jo-payroll-social.md) for the applicable scope, thresholds, returns and continuing holds. The old fourth-month income-tax deadline, periodic GST returns and monthly SSC filings require the corresponding current tax/social-security rules; no company-registration renewal rule is inferred from the annual accounts. [Partner-register duty, art. 71](<https://www.jsc.gov.jo/Uploads/Files/companies_jordan-2026.pdf>).

## Worked cases

### LLC cash-capital payment reconciliation

Assume a newly registered ordinary cash-capital LLC declares JOD 10,000. Its bank evidence shows JOD 3,000 paid, and the example is evaluated before the 60-day limit. The required first payment is at least JOD 5,000, so a further JOD 2,000 is needed within that period. After that top-up, JOD 5,000 remains to be paid within two years of registration. Sector permissions and entity capital adequacy are independently established; the arithmetic neither grants an extension nor approves the activity. [Companies Law art. 59(b)(2)](<https://www.jsc.gov.jo/Uploads/Files/companies_jordan-2026.pdf>).

```oa-check
name: Jordan LLC initial capital payment and balance
inputs:
  declared_cash_capital: 10000
  evidenced_paid_capital: 3000
  first_payment_fraction: 0.50
steps:
  required_first_payment: declared_cash_capital * first_payment_fraction
  additional_first_payment: max(0, required_first_payment - evidenced_paid_capital)
  paid_after_top_up: evidenced_paid_capital + additional_first_payment
  remaining_capital: declared_cash_capital - paid_after_top_up
expected:
  required_first_payment: 5000
  additional_first_payment: 2000
  paid_after_top_up: 5000
  remaining_capital: 5000
```

## When to refuse or refer

Refer uncertain foreign participation or activity permissions, conflicting current law, disputed in-kind valuations, branch eligibility, regulated/public-offer structures and missing shareholder/representative documents. Do not file from an approximate capital figure, an untested portal path or the old universal tax-registration statement. Legal, tax and social-security approvals are separate evidence items. Keep every unresolved item open for a qualified Jordan professional.

## Filing and payment

Prepare the approved company-law application and signed constitutional documents, identify the competent CCD/investment-service channel and current activity-specific attachments, then obtain the actual decision, certificate, fee receipt and bank-capital evidence. Current portal field labels, fee schedules, payment references, confirmation screens and adjustment of calendar deadlines have not been tested. No amount is invented for them.

## Completion checklist

Keep the form/entity decision, owners and nationalities, activity/licensing evidence, name check, signed constitution, authorized manager/representative, capital allocation/valuation/bank proof, registration date/certificate and payment calendar together. Create a separate annual-audit/accounts/meeting calendar and explicit tax/social-security handovers. Preserve open permissions and source holds rather than marking incorporation complete from a calculation.
