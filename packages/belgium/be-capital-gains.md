# BE Capital Gains

## Core rule: generally no CGT for private investors

- **General absence of CGT for private investors** — Belgium does not have a general capital gains tax for private investors who manage their own portfolio in the normal course. Gains from disposal of shares, bonds, and other securities by a private individual acting as a normal investor are not taxed.  _(Wetboek van de Inkomstenbelastingen 1992 (WIB), Art. 90)_

## When gains ARE taxable

- **Speculative behaviour trigger** — Gains on financial assets become taxable as miscellaneous income (diverse inkomsten) if the person is engaged in speculative behaviour (frequent trading with short holding periods, use of leverage).  _(Wetboek van de Inkomstenbelastingen 1992 (WIB), Art. 90)_
- **Professional activity trigger** — Gains become taxable if the activity rises to the level of a professional activity (beroepsactiviteit).  _(Wetboek van de Inkomstenbelastingen 1992 (WIB), Art. 90)_
- **Shares in Belgian companies sold to non-EEA purchasers (significant participation >25%)** — 16.5% (Significant participation (>25%) sold to a non-EEA entity)  _(Wetboek van de Inkomstenbelastingen 1992 (WIB), Art. 90)_
- **Default position for buy-and-hold private investors** — For the vast majority of buy-and-hold private investors: no CGT.  _(Wetboek van de Inkomstenbelastingen 1992 (WIB), Art. 90)_

## Taxe sur les Opérations Boursières (TOB)

Belgium imposes a stock exchange tax (TOB / beurstaks) on transactions in Belgian or foreign securities by Belgian residents:

**TOB rates and caps by transaction type**  _(Wetboek diverse rechten en taksen (TOB))_

| Transaction type | Rate | Cap |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Belgian listed shares — purchase/sale | 0.35% | €1,600 per transaction |
| Foreign listed shares — purchase/sale | 0.35% | €1,600 per transaction |
| Capitalising investment funds (acc.) | 1.32% | €4,000 per transaction |
| Bonds | 0.12% | €1,300 per transaction |

- **TOB collection responsibility** — TOB is charged by the broker. Foreign brokers are required to collect it; if not, the Belgian investor must self-declare and pay.  _(Wetboek diverse rechten en taksen (TOB))_

## Real property gains

- **Real property gains within 5 years — built property** — 16.5% (Within 5 years of acquisition, built property)  _(Wetboek van de Inkomstenbelastingen 1992 (WIB), Art. 90)_
- **Real property gains within 5 years — land** — 33% (Within 5 years of acquisition, land)  _(Wetboek van de Inkomstenbelastingen 1992 (WIB), Art. 90)_
- **Real property gains after 5 years** — After 5 years: generally not taxed.  _(Wetboek van de Inkomstenbelastingen 1992 (WIB), Art. 90)_
- **Primary residence exemption** — Primary residence: gains are exempt if the property has been the taxpayer's primary residence since at least 6 months after purchase.  _(Wetboek van de Inkomstenbelastingen 1992 (WIB), Art. 90)_
