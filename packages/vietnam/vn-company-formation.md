# Vietnam Company Formation and Entity Choice v2.0

> **General reference only.** This skill is general tax/accounting reference material for AI-assisted workflows. It has not been reviewed for any specific person's facts, documents, elections, deadlines, residency, filing status, or local procedures. Do not rely on it to file, pay, amend, or take a tax position without review by a qualified professional in the relevant jurisdiction.

## Who this applies to

Founders, local or foreign, setting up a business entity in Vietnam in 2026 under the Enterprise Law 59/2020/QH14 as amended by Law 03/2022/QH15 and Law 76/2025/QH15 (in force 1 July 2025). Foreign investors also need investment registration under the Investment Law before or with enterprise registration, for most projects; the investment procedure is outside this skill. Tax after formation: [vn-corporate-income-tax](vn-corporate-income-tax.md), [vietnam-vat](vietnam-vat.md), [vn-payroll-social](vn-payroll-social.md).

| Entity | Owners | Liability | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Single-member LLC (công ty TNHH một thành viên) | One organisation or individual | Limited to charter capital | Enterprise Law Art. 74 |
| Multi-member LLC (công ty TNHH hai thành viên trở lên) | 2 to 50 members | Limited to capital contributed | Art. 46 |
| Joint-stock company (công ty cổ phần) | At least 3 shareholders, no maximum; capital divided into shares; may issue securities | Limited | Art. 111 |
| Private enterprise (doanh nghiệp tư nhân) | One individual | Unlimited | Enterprise Law |

## The base

The steps, in the order a founder meets them:

1. **Choose the entity** from the table above. One founder with limited liability: single-member LLC. Two to 50 partners: multi-member LLC. Plans to raise outside equity or list: joint-stock company.
2. **Foreign investors:** obtain investment registration for the project where the Investment Law requires it, then register the enterprise.
3. **Prepare the file:** registration request, charter, list of members or founding shareholders, the **list of beneficial owners** (Art. 3(35) defines them as the individuals who in fact own charter capital or control the enterprise; Law 76/2025 added the duty to collect, update and keep this information and to provide it on request), and identity documents.
4. **File** with the provincial business registration office, online through the national business registration portal (dangkykinhdoanh.gov.vn) or in person. The office checks the file and issues the enterprise registration certificate within 3 working days, or says in writing what to correct (Art. 26(5)). The enterprise code is also the tax code.
5. **Contribute capital** in full and in the promised assets within 90 days of the certificate (Art. 47(2), Art. 75). A member who has contributed nothing stops being a member; a partial contributor keeps rights pro rata; the unpaid part may be offered for sale (Art. 47(3)).
6. **If capital is short,** register the reduced charter capital and ownership ratios within 30 days after the 90-day deadline (Art. 47(4)). Until then the defaulting members are liable, in proportion to the capital they promised, for the company's obligations.
7. **Tax start-up:** open a bank account, register for electronic invoices, choose the CIT period (calendar or notified financial year), register employees for social insurance, and check the CIT exemptions (new SME three-year exemption; the VND 1 billion revenue exemption in [vn-corporate-income-tax](vn-corporate-income-tax.md)). No licence fee return is due for 2026 onward.

## Deadlines and filing

| Step | Deadline |
| --- | --- |
| Registration decision | 3 working days from a complete file (Art. 26(5)) |
| Capital contribution | 90 days from the certificate (Art. 47(2)) |
| Charter capital adjustment after shortfall | 30 days after the contribution deadline (Art. 47(4)) |
| First CIT provisional payment | Last day of the first month after each quarter; none if revenue is expected to be VND 1 billion or less (Decree 141/2026 Art. 2) |

## Penalties and interest

Members who fail to contribute bear liability for the company's obligations, in proportion to the capital they promised, until the capital change is registered (Art. 47(4)). Administrative fines for late registration of changes are in the enterprise registration penalty decree, not read (see "Not yet confirmed"). Late tax payment interest is 0.03% per day (Law 108/2025/QH15 Art. 16(2)(a)).

## Cross-border points

- Foreign ownership caps and conditions apply in conditional sectors (for example banking, insurance, some services); check the market access list before choosing the structure.
- Capital contributed by foreign investors must go through a direct investment capital account at a bank in Vietnam (foreign exchange rules, not read here).
- Treaty and withholding questions on dividends and service fees: [vn-corporate-income-tax](vn-corporate-income-tax.md).

## Two worked examples that recompute

### Example 1: two-member LLC, charter capital VND 2 billion, only 1.2 billion contributed by day 90

The company must register charter capital of 1.2 billion within 30 days after the 90th day; the 0.8 billion shortfall is removed from charter capital or offered for sale. With the certificate dated day 0, the last day to register the change is day 120.

## Ask the client first

1. Number and type of owners (individuals or companies), and whether any is foreign.
2. Business lines, and whether any is a conditional sector.
3. Charter capital and the assets to be contributed, with dates.
4. Who the beneficial owners are, through every layer.
5. Expected first-year revenue (for the VND 1 billion CIT exemption and e-invoice duties).
