# Vietnam: official source index 2026 v2.0

> **General reference only.** This skill is general tax/accounting reference material for AI-assisted workflows. It has not been reviewed for any specific person's facts, documents, elections, deadlines, residency, filing status, or local procedures. Do not rely on it to file, pay, amend, or take a tax position without review by a qualified professional in the relevant jurisdiction.

## The method, step by step

1. Pick the Guide that owns the job from the [tax overview](vn-tax-overview.md): [personal income tax](vietnam-pit.md), [corporate income tax](vn-corporate-income-tax.md), [VAT](vietnam-vat.md), [payroll and social insurance](vn-payroll-social.md), [company formation](vn-company-formation.md).
2. Open the law as published in the Official Gazette (congbao.chinhphu.vn, signed PDFs on congbaocdn.chinhphu.vn) or the Government legal document system (vanban.chinhphu.vn, vbpl.vn). Prefer a consolidated text (văn bản hợp nhất, VBHN) when one exists, and check its date against later amending laws.
3. Check the effective date and the tax period it applies from. In 2026 this matters: Law 109/2025/QH15 (PIT) applies to salaries and business income from tax period 2026 but to other income from 1 July 2026; Law 09/2026/QH16 changed thresholds with effect from 1 January 2026; Law 108/2025/QH15 (tax administration) took effect on 1 July 2026.
4. Where the law delegates a figure to the Government, find the decree (for example Decree 141/2026 for the VND 1 billion thresholds; Decree 252/2026 for filing deadlines) and read the decree, not a news summary, where possible. Government portal articles (xaydungchinhsach.chinhphu.vn, baochinhphu.vn) may be used to locate and summarise a decree; say so in the Sources row.
5. Record the URL, the articles read and the read date in the owning Guide's Sources table.

## When to refuse or refer

Stop a figure when the official text cannot be opened or its effective date is unclear, and list it on the owning Guide's "Not yet confirmed" line. Commercial law-database sites (for example thuvienphapluat.vn, luatvietnam.vn), firm summaries, open-source calculators and other AI answers are not official evidence. The former version of this file listed two open-source PIT calculators on GitHub; they were not official sources and one of them carried the wrong 2026 band rates, so they are no longer cited.
