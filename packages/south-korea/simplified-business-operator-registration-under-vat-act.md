# Simplified Business Operator Registration under VAT Act v0.1

> **General reference only.** This skill is general tax/accounting reference material for AI-assisted workflows. It has not been reviewed for any specific person's facts, documents, elections, deadlines, residency, filing status, or local procedures. Do not rely on it to file, pay, amend, or take a tax position without review by a qualified professional in the relevant jurisdiction.

## Scope

Use this Guide for the foreign electronic-services registration regime, 간편사업자등록, in South Korea for 2026. It covers registration, filing and payment. It does not compute the separate domestic small-business regime, 간이과세. The operative VAT Act text is effective 2 January 2026; the implementing decree and rule read here are effective 1 April 2026. [VAT Act art. 53-2](https://www.law.go.kr/LSW/lsLinkCommonInfo.do?lsJoLnkSeq=1031740015); [Decree art. 96-2](https://www.law.go.kr/LSW/lsLinkCommonInfo.do?lspttninfSeq=112680).

## Ask the client first

Obtain the foreign supplier's legal and trading names, representative, contact and website details, home-country registration and business address, electronic-service categories, first Korean supply date, customer business-registration evidence, any Korean establishment, marketplace contracts and payment flows. Obtain the tax manager's particulars if appointed and the refund account if one exists. These are the registration inputs under [Decree art. 96-2(3)](https://www.law.go.kr/LSW/lsLinkCommonInfo.do?lspttninfSeq=112680) and [Rule art. 66-2(1)](https://law.go.kr/LSW/lsLinkCommonInfo.do?chrClsCd=010202&lspttninfSeq=74183).

Before a return, obtain the simplified registration number, filing period, gross receipts, VAT-inclusive/exclusive pricing terms, refunds, payment and delivery dates, currency, NTS-notified exchange rate, and supporting documents for any claimed input VAT. Do not infer B2B status merely from a company-looking customer name or register both a platform and underlying supplier for the same deemed supply. [VAT Act arts. 29, 38-39](https://www.law.go.kr/LSW/lsLinkProc.do?chrClsCd=010202&joLnkStr=%EB%B2%95+%EC%A0%9C8%EC%A1%B0&lsNm=%EB%B6%80%EA%B0%80%EA%B0%80%EC%B9%98%EC%84%B8%EB%B2%95&mode=10); [art. 53-2](https://www.law.go.kr/LSW/lsLinkCommonInfo.do?lsJoLnkSeq=1031740015).

## The method, step by step

1. **Determine the liable supplier.** A foreign business within art. 52's definition supplying qualifying electronic services directly to Korean consumers follows art. 53-2(1). For an open market or similar operator enabling the transaction, or an intermediary receiving the buyer's payment and paying the seller, apply the deemed-supplier rule in art. 53-2(2). Its exclusion also covers cases governed by art. 53. A foreign establishment or agency structure needs classification before registration. [VAT Act arts. 52-53](https://www.law.go.kr/LSW/lsLinkProc.do?chrClsCd=010202&joLnkStr=%EB%B2%95+%EC%A0%9C8%EC%A1%B0&lsNm=%EB%B6%80%EA%B0%80%EA%B0%80%EC%B9%98%EC%84%B8%EB%B2%95&mode=10); [art. 53-2(1)-(2)](https://www.law.go.kr/LSW/lsLinkCommonInfo.do?lsJoLnkSeq=1031740015).
2. **Classify the service.** The categories are digitally processed games, audio/video, electronic documents and software, associated improvements; online advertising; cloud computing; and prescribed domestic goods/services intermediation. The last category excludes intermediation whose consideration is already included in the goods/services consideration on which the art. 3 taxpayer reports and pays VAT. Delivery may be stored or streamed. [VAT Act art. 53-2(1)](https://www.law.go.kr/LSW/lsLinkCommonInfo.do?lsJoLnkSeq=1031740015); [Decree art. 96-2(1)-(2)](https://www.law.go.kr/LSW/lsLinkCommonInfo.do?lspttninfSeq=112680).
3. **Separate registered-business supplies.** Supplies for a customer's registered taxable or exempt business fall outside art. 53-2. Retain registration and business-use evidence and keep the excluded transactions in the records. This exclusion does not decide any separate reverse-charge liability under art. 52. [VAT Act art. 53-2(1),(2),(6)](https://www.law.go.kr/LSW/lsLinkCommonInfo.do?lsJoLnkSeq=1031740015); [art. 52](https://www.law.go.kr/LSW/lsLinkProc.do?chrClsCd=010202&joLnkStr=%EB%B2%95+%EC%A0%9C8%EC%A1%B0&lsNm=%EB%B6%80%EA%B0%80%EA%B0%80%EC%B9%98%EC%84%B8%EB%B2%95&mode=10).
4. **Open the official registration route.** On the [NTS English homepage](https://www.nts.go.kr/english/main.do), use **Home Tax Service: Simplified Business Operator Registration & Filing Value Added Tax**. It links to the English [HomeTax simplified-business service](https://www.hometax.go.kr/websquare/websquare.html?w2xPath=/ui/pp/a/a/UTXPPADA01.xml&lang=en). Enter the statutory registration fields: business/representative names, contacts and website, trading name if different, foreign registration country/address/number, electronic-service types and Korean commencement date. Include the appointed tax manager's name, resident or business-registration number, address and telephone, and a refund account if opened. Submit the registration within the statutory deadline; NTS allocates a simplified number and notifies the business and appointed manager. [Decree art. 96-2(3)-(4)](https://www.law.go.kr/LSW/lsLinkCommonInfo.do?lspttninfSeq=112680); [Rule art. 66-2(1)](https://law.go.kr/LSW/lsLinkCommonInfo.do?chrClsCd=010202&lspttninfSeq=74183).
5. **Build the return workpaper.** Use the earlier of delivery and completed payment as supply time. Aggregate Korean taxable electronic-service supply value for the reporting period. Exclude VAT itself from the base. For foreign-currency consideration the decree permits the NTS-notified basic exchange rate at the end of the taxable/preliminary period. Save that notification with the calculation; this Guide does not substitute a private currency site for it. [VAT Act art. 29](https://www.law.go.kr/LSW/lsLinkProc.do?chrClsCd=010202&joLnkStr=%EB%B2%95+%EC%A0%9C8%EC%A1%B0&lsNm=%EB%B6%80%EA%B0%80%EA%B0%80%EC%B9%98%EC%84%B8%EB%B2%95&mode=10); [Decree art. 96-2(7),(11)](https://www.law.go.kr/LSW/lsLinkCommonInfo.do?lspttninfSeq=112680).
6. **Compute and document VAT.** Multiply the VAT-exclusive base by the statutory rate. Deduct only electronic-service-related input VAT allowed by arts. 38 and 39. Check valid supporting tax invoices and business purpose and exclude blocked items; a number appearing in the input field is not proof of entitlement. [VAT Act arts. 38-39](https://www.law.go.kr/LSW/lsLinkProc.do?chrClsCd=010202&joLnkStr=%EB%B2%95+%EC%A0%9C8%EC%A1%B0&lsNm=%EB%B6%80%EA%B0%80%EA%B0%80%EC%B9%98%EC%84%B8%EB%B2%95&mode=10); [art. 53-2(5)](https://www.law.go.kr/LSW/lsLinkCommonInfo.do?lsJoLnkSeq=1031740015).
7. **Enter the simplified VAT return.** Sign in to the simplified service using the registered account and assigned number. Select the VAT return and correct preliminary/final period. The statutory fields are **business name**, **simplified registration number**, **total electronic-service supply value in Korea during the reporting period**, **deductible input VAT**, and **VAT payable**. Reconcile those fields to the workpaper before submitting. Preserve the submission result and return copy. The legal fields are confirmed; the authenticated 2026 screen labels and receipt format were not accessed. [Decree art. 96-2(5)](https://www.law.go.kr/LSW/lsLinkCommonInfo.do?lspttninfSeq=112680).
8. **Pay and reconcile.** Payment is by deposit to the foreign-exchange-bank account designated by NTS. Use the account-specific NTS instructions supplied for that registration, verify the credited amount and save the bank remittance and payment confirmation. A return submission does not establish that payment arrived. Do not copy another taxpayer's bank account. [Decree art. 96-2(6)](https://www.law.go.kr/LSW/lsLinkCommonInfo.do?lspttninfSeq=112680).

## Boundary and exception table

| Situation | Treatment | Official source |
|---|---|---|
| Direct consumer supply | Supplier follows simplified registration and return procedure. | [VAT Act art. 53-2(1)](https://www.law.go.kr/LSW/lsLinkCommonInfo.do?lsJoLnkSeq=1031740015) |
| Qualifying intermediary handles supply | Test deemed-supplier conditions and exceptions before assigning liability. | [VAT Act art. 53-2(2)](https://www.law.go.kr/LSW/lsLinkCommonInfo.do?lsJoLnkSeq=1031740015) |
| Registered taxable or exempt business customer | Outside this special rule for that business supply; retain its details and assess other applicable rules separately. | [VAT Act art. 53-2(1),(2),(6)](https://www.law.go.kr/LSW/lsLinkCommonInfo.do?lsJoLnkSeq=1031740015) |
| Platform fee already included in another taxable supply | Apply the decree's exception to the prescribed intermediation category. | [Decree art. 96-2(2)](https://www.law.go.kr/LSW/lsLinkCommonInfo.do?lspttninfSeq=112680) |
| Foreign currency | Period-end basic exchange rate is permitted; NTS must notify/publish it. | [Decree art. 96-2(7)](https://www.law.go.kr/LSW/lsLinkCommonInfo.do?lspttninfSeq=112680) |
| NTS transaction-detail request | Provide the required statement within 60 days after the request; prescribed statement is Rule form 37-3, with permitted electronic-record substitution at NTS discretion. | [VAT Act art. 53-2(7)-(8)](https://www.law.go.kr/LSW/lsLinkCommonInfo.do?lsJoLnkSeq=1031740015); [Rule art. 66-2(2)-(3)](https://law.go.kr/LSW/lsLinkCommonInfo.do?chrClsCd=010202&lspttninfSeq=74183) |

## Worked cases

### Foreign software supplier, quarterly consumer supplies

Illustrative inputs: foreign software supplier has no relevant Korean establishment or deemed-supplier intermediary, all customers are consumers, and supplies in the April-June 2026 reporting period total KRW 11,000,000 including VAT. Eligible documented input VAT is KRW 50,000. The base is KRW 10,000,000, output VAT KRW 1,000,000 and payable KRW 950,000. Enter those values in the supply/input/payable fields; exclude already reported January-March supplies. [VAT Act arts. 29(7), 30, 38-39, 49](https://www.law.go.kr/LSW/lsLinkProc.do?chrClsCd=010202&joLnkStr=%EB%B2%95+%EC%A0%9C8%EC%A1%B0&lsNm=%EB%B6%80%EA%B0%80%EA%B0%80%EC%B9%98%EC%84%B8%EB%B2%95&mode=10); [art. 53-2(5)](https://www.law.go.kr/LSW/lsLinkCommonInfo.do?lsJoLnkSeq=1031740015).

## When to refuse or refer

Stop if customer business status, establishment attribution, intermediary responsibility, price inclusion, currency conversion or input entitlement cannot be evidenced. Refer disputed domestic-establishment, agency, exemption or reverse-charge questions to a Korean practitioner. Do not apply domestic small-business concessions to this foreign registration regime. Do not invent filing screen numbers or payment details.

## Filing and payment

The statutory ordinary schedule follows the half-year periods in art. 5 and arts. 48 and 49 through art. 53-2(4). Nominal deadlines below require any applicable holiday extension or NTS-specific extension to be checked for the actual filing date; they are not an adjusted 2026 calendar.

| Return | Supply period accounted for | Ordinary filing and payment deadline | Official source |
|---|---|---|---|
| First preliminary | January-March | April 25 | [VAT Act art. 48(1)-(2)](https://law.go.kr/LSW/lsLinkCommonInfo.do?lsJoLnkSeq=1033432981) |
| First final | April-June, excluding already reported preliminary supplies | July 25 | [VAT Act art. 49(1)-(2)](https://law.go.kr/LSW/lsLinkCommonInfo.do?lsJoLnkSeq=1033432991) |
| Second preliminary | July-September | October 25 | [VAT Act art. 48(1)-(2)](https://law.go.kr/LSW/lsLinkCommonInfo.do?lsJoLnkSeq=1033432981) |
| Second final | October-December, excluding already reported preliminary supplies | January 25 of the following year | [VAT Act art. 49(1)-(2)](https://law.go.kr/LSW/lsLinkCommonInfo.do?lsJoLnkSeq=1033432991) |

Keep transaction type, supply value, VAT, delivery/payment timing and customer identifiers in the transaction records. Electronic retention is permitted. [Decree art. 96-2(8)-(9)](https://www.law.go.kr/LSW/lsLinkCommonInfo.do?lspttninfSeq=112680). The five-year retention period and NTS request procedure above also cover excluded registered-business supplies. [VAT Act art. 53-2(6)-(8)](https://www.law.go.kr/LSW/lsLinkCommonInfo.do?lsJoLnkSeq=1031740015).
