# Mozambique VAT

## Who this applies to

Persons whose Mozambique VAT (IVA) status and supplies have been established, including actual taxable domestic sales, imports and non-resident/digital-service transactions. Currency is MZN/Metical; AT is the Autoridade Tributaria de Mocambique. A bank statement can support a classification workpaper, not a filed VAT return or automatic input recovery. The original contributor credit **Open Accounting Skills Registry** is preserved as historical credit, not approval of this revision.

Do not confuse IVA, ISPC, IRPS, IRPC or INSS. Each has its own registration, base and obligations. Normal IVA is not the old generic quarterly regime; ISPC remains a separate enacted regime under Law 9/2025. This Guide preserves the original short pack's topics and cases, but does not claim a complete 2016/2017 base-code read, recovered enacted 2025 exemption-extension text, tested return boxes or live portal.

## The base

### Correct source version and regime

The original 22/2022 Gazette was withdrawn and replaced by the second supplement, as stated in its own footnote and the official catalogue. Use the actual AT-host replacement cited here, not the superseded copy. It changes 9/10/12/15/17/20/21 and adds 17-A. The document previously saved as `CIVA.pdf` is a training presentation, not the 2016 enacted Code; it is excluded as operative proof.

Law 10/2025 supplies the actual 2026 changes to 3/6/12/18/19/21/23/25/26/27/31/32/33/34, adds 25-A/53 and revokes the stated small-taxpayer regimes and old 21(8). Do not carry forward the old twelve-month mandatory 50% refund request as if still operative, or its superseded 30 day refund deadline. Existing credits, suspensions, security and full eligibility/procedure need their applicable clauses and records; the short amended text is not a complete consolidated refund manual.

Record actual taxable consideration, time/place/status and whether an invoice price is net or inclusive. For a confirmed standard-rate net supply, IVA = net x 16%; gross = net + IVA. For an inclusive amount use 34's actual base formula and rounding rule. A zero-rate, exemption, reduced base, reduced rate, ISPC election and a non-supply are different classifications. Do not post a gross bank debit as if its tax base, tax point or deduction were established.

The replacement's recovered 9(1)(a) addresses public hospital/dispensary and similar medical services,9(3) the specified public recognised education/vocational cases,9(4) banking/financial operations subject to stamp duty,9(5) housing rental, and 9(6) insurance/reinsurance and related intermediary services subject to stamp duty. Article 10 has cumulative public/nonprofit conditions. These are operation-specific provisions, not a blanket public/private, bank-name or business-label exemption. Rice/bread in 9(10) are a specified-goods case, not all basic food. Public transport, agricultural-input/annex classifications and 9(13)'s unread 2025 extension require the actual complete operative text and current conditions before use.

### Input tax, mixed activities and documents

Current 18(2) requires legally issued invoices/equivalent documents from IVA-registered taxable persons or lawful customs import documents, in the claimant's possession. It does not say a bank debit alone qualifies. Current 18(6) limits deduction proportionally where the listed 15(2)(j)-(m) reduced-base provisions apply;19(2) excludes the specified 15(6)(c) and 17-A operations. Do not assume 5% activities have ordinary full recovery.

Replacement 20(3) includes specific exceptions for relevant business trading/exploitation of goods and commercial travellers' professional lodging/food, with its fuel qualification. The full base 20(1) blocked-item text, mixed-activity 22/pro-rata calculation, complete invoice 27 requirements and implementing procedure were not recovered/read as a current consolidation. The old under-nine-seats vehicle rule, entertainment, personal-use/fuel blocks and 'invoice without NUIT' are retained as document/classification issues requiring the actual operative clause and exceptions, not blanket decisions made from these extracts. NUIT and registration evidence matter; a NUIT printed on a bank narrative alone does not establish an admissible tax invoice.

Only deduct input tax after establishing the actual taxable activity, claimant/status, transaction, invoice/customs proof, limitations and any allocation. Never promise a net-zero imported-service transaction or automatic recovery on an export from two multiplication lines. Carry-forward credit, adjustments and payable/refund balances require the actual account and return, not assumed old box numbers.

The old proposed output 1-8, input 9-14 and net 15-17 box map is **not verified** and must not be copied into a filing. Preserve those required data groups without pretending the numbers identify current official fields.

## Deadlines and filing

Use the distinct 32 monthly deadlines in the table, including 15th for credit/nil, last day for payment and 10th for 26(4)digital acquisitions. The old monthly-for-large/quarterly-for-standard and last-working-day-next-quarter wording is rejected. The amended text does not itself establish all deadline carries or the exact current electronic form; obtain the operative calendar/process for an actual filing.

Law 10/2025,27(10)requires submission of invoices/equivalents for each transaction in terms to be regulated. Article 31's invoice dispensation does not eliminate authorised printed/numbered sales-receipt requirements. Multi-establishment 25-A calls for sales by establishment and other transactions, in terms to be regulated. Do not invent reporting formats, rollout dates, authentication or live portal paths. The old e-fatura address is not proof that it is the IVA-return portal.

Under Law 9/2025, actual ISPC has quarterly turnover, declaration/payment and invoice obligations. Eligible ISPC invoices must not include IVA under 12(6). Confirm the actual election, annual activity band, mixed activities and prescribed process; the ordinary VAT calendar is not automatically its ISPC calendar.

## Penalties and interest

The old IVA late-filing 5,000-50,000 range and 2%-per-month late-payment assertion were not supported by a current operative IVA penalty law and are **not usable**. RSSO 98's 2% monthly-or-fraction rule belongs to INSS; it does not prove a VAT rate. Obtain the applicable tax/infraction law, official notices, dates and account before quantifying a fine or interest bill. Do not fabricate a penalty from turnover or a bank narration.

## Cross-border points

### Digital and other non-resident supplies

Amended 3 defines digital goods and 6(7)(k)/6(9)digital services including relevant software/SaaS, cloud, media/streaming, digital intermediation and analogous remote functionality. Actual provider/customer location and status matter. The 6(8)foreign-customer wording must be read with the complete territorial rules before a real export conclusion; no universal export exemption follows from it alone.

Current 26(4)places obligations for digital goods/services supplied by non-residents on a taxable customer located in Mozambique, in terms to be regulated, with 25(7)/32(1)(c)reporting. Current 23(2)separately addresses 6(7)services **excluding(k)** and 26(3)cases, with 33's isolated-operation declaration for its stated situations. Do not collapse digital 26(4), represented/other non-resident 26(1)-(3), ordinary imported services and every foreign supplier into the old universal 'autoliquidacao 16%, net zero' instruction. No tested supplier collection or current implementation is claimed.

Exports, import relief/agricultural inputs and the enacted 2025 time extension need their actual operative classification/documents. LNG, Sasol, mining concessions and other megaproject regimes remain the original R-MZ-1 escalation: individually negotiated/special fiscal terms cannot be supplied by this ordinary VAT Guide. No company IRPC32%, IRPS10%-32% or employee/employer INSS4%/3% liability is computed here; route those original adjacent topics to their owners.

## Two worked examples that recompute

### Original ordinary net sale

Original facts: net 1,000,000. If the actual supply is established as standard 16%, IVA160,000 and gross 1,160,000. This is supply arithmetic, not evidence that the bank receipt alone proves the rate.

```oa-check
name: Original 1000000 confirmed standard net supply
inputs:
  net: 1000000
  established_rate: 0.16
steps:
  iva: net * established_rate
  gross: net + iva
expected:
  iva: 160000
  gross: 1160000
```

### Original South African services case: no automatic input credit

Original service amount 3,000,000. If a legally established ordinary standard-rate self-assessment route applies, output arithmetic 480,000. The old matching 480,000 input and zero net were conditional claims without input-eligibility proof. With no established deductible amount, this example cannot claim a credit; the illustrative comparison assumes zero allowed input, leaving 480,000. It is not a conclusion on actual South African/digital treaty or collection facts.

```oa-check
name: Original 3000000 imported-service arithmetic without assumed recovery
inputs:
  net: 3000000
  established_rate: 0.16
  independently_established_input: 0
steps:
  output_arithmetic: net * established_rate
  difference_arithmetic: output_arithmetic - independently_established_input
expected:
  output_arithmetic: 480000
  difference_arithmetic: 480000
```

### Other original cases and tests retained

- Original small-retailer ISPC quarter sales 400,000:3% arithmetic is 12,000 only **if** the actual election/activity/annual band establishes that rate. Quarter turnover alone supplies none of those facts. Other current bands/services differ; do not retain the obsolete universal 3%/2,500,000 rule or assume an exact tax/input-recovery result. ISPC invoices themselves must not include IVA.
- Original purchase 500,000 plus IVA80,000: multiplication at 16% is correct; actual 80,000 input recovery remains conditional on registration, lawful documents, activity and every applicable limitation/allocation. No invoice/status proof means no certified credit.
- Original export 50,000,000: retain the exact amount and export topic; do not declare zero output or recoverable input until current export eligibility and documents are checked. It is not the same as domestic exemption.
- Original rice/bread case: the recovered 9(10)expressly names those goods. Actual goods/supply classification and any applicable later provisions must be checked; do not extrapolate to all foods, all agricultural inputs or an automatic mixed-activity deduction result.
- Original entertainment/vehicle/personal-use/fuel cases: hold exact blocked-input classification until 20's complete current rule and exceptions are established. No supplier narration alone creates or destroys a credit.
- Original turnover above 2,500,000: not an automatic current ordinary-IVA registration conclusion. Determine actual regime after Law 9/10 changes, including possible ISPC up to 4,000,000 with all conditions and separately governed VAT obligations.
- Original 'zero-rated/input recoverable', 'autoliquidacao/net zero' and 'escalate' regressions survive as classification checks with these missing conditions; no unsupported unconditional result is retained. Arithmetic is recomputed explicitly; the old 'never compute numbers' sentence is not a prohibition on a checked worked example.

## Ask the client first

Obtain actual registration/ISPC election, annual and quarterly activity/turnover, period, supply nature/location, supplier/customer status, net/inclusive price, lawful invoice/import proof, taxed/exempt/mixed activity, prior credit/adjustments and filings. For foreign services obtain actual provider/representative and customer facts and current collection process. A bank-only record permits a provisional workpaper and missing-document requests, not a filing, blanket exemption, guaranteed credit, megaproject conclusion or exact liability. Preserve the original prohibitions against confusing IVA/ISPC, charging IVA on an ISPC invoice, using ordinary rules for an unresolved megaproject and accepting unsupported invoice claims.
