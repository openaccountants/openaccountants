# Bahamas: starting a business and its tax registrations

> **General reference only.** This skill is general tax/accounting reference material for AI-assisted workflows. It has not been reviewed for any specific person's facts, documents, elections, deadlines, residency, filing status, or local procedures. Do not rely on it to file, pay, amend, or take a tax position without review by a qualified professional in the relevant jurisdiction.

## Scope and who this is for

Anyone setting up a business in The Bahamas in 2026: sole proprietors, home-based businesses, partnerships, limited liability partnerships and companies, private and public companies, and international business companies (IBCs). It covers the tax registrations in the order they are met. The company registration itself (Registrar General's Department) is described only in outline because its pages and fees could not be opened. Amounts are BSD.

## Ask the client first

1. Is the applicant a Bahamian citizen or permanent resident with the right to work? If not, Bahamas Investment Authority approval is needed first.
2. What vehicle: sole proprietor, partnership, domestic company, IBC?
3. What activity (liquor, insurance, financial services, family office, farming or fishing change the licence rules)?
4. Expected turnover in the first 12 months (BSD 100,000 for both the VAT test and the licence exemption).
5. Will it employ staff (NIB employer registration)?

### Steps in order

1. **Approval for non-Bahamians.** Apply to the Bahamas Investment Authority before applying for a licence (DIR annual licence page).
2. **Form the entity.** Register the company or IBC with the Registrar General's Department. An IBC needs a Bahamian registered agent and registered office. Fees and timelines: see Not yet confirmed.
3. **Bahamas Identification Number.** Register for a BIN at login.onetaxbahamas.gov.bs (the DIR "One Tax" system; required before DMTT registration and used for DIR accounts).
4. **Business licence.** Apply to DIR before trading, through the business licence application links on the DIR site. Liquor businesses first get a Certificate of Endorsement from the Liquor Review Board (from 1 July 2026). Some activities need approvals from other agencies (DIR "Agency Approval Forms").
5. **VAT.** Register within 14 days once taxable activity in the past or next 12 months exceeds BSD 100,000, or voluntarily. Registration requires the business licence (VAT Act s. 22A). See `bahamas-vat`.
6. **NIB.** Register as an employer before paying wages, or as self-employed; see `bs-payroll-social`.
7. **Economic substance.** Entities in scope of the Commercial Entities (Substance Requirements) Act report through DIR's substance reporting (CESRA) channel; contact the competent authority at the Ministry of Finance (DIR substance reporting page).
8. **Each year.** Renew by 31 January; file the turnover return and pay by 31 March; notify DIR within 14 days of stopping trading, and no later than 31 December.

### What happens after

DIR issues the licence for the calendar year. The first licence tax for a new business is BSD 100 under DIR's page (Taxes, fines and penalties, BL); later years follow the turnover bands, with the BSD 100,000 exemption where it applies. Each year's estimated tax rests on the prior year's turnover.

## Worked cases

### New sole trader, first two years

Maria opens a home-based design business on 1 February 2026. She pays the BSD 100 new business licence tax. Her 2026 turnover is BSD 85,000; she is not VAT registered and her 2027 licence tax is nil because turnover is not over BSD 100,000. She renews by 31 January 2027 and files her 2026 turnover return by 31 March 2027.

## When to refuse or refer

- Forming the company or IBC, registered agent services, Bahamas Investment Authority applications: refer to a Bahamian attorney or licensed corporate service provider.
- Freeport (Grand Bahama Port Area) licensing: refer.
- Financial services entities, family offices and proprietary trading businesses: specialist licensing; refer.
