# Réunion: octroi de mer for local producers

## Scope

This Guide covers identifying a local producer's octroi obligations and the national compliance framework for Réunion in 2026. It separates the producer threshold from import taxation. It does not supply a current regional tariff, exemption entitlement, deduction/refund calculation or clearance to file.

The July 2025 national customs circular supplies the compliance explanations below. Its production definition predates the February 2026 amendment; use the amended law for the period to which it applies. The retained Légifrance consolidation is dated 14 November 2026. This is a bounded source read, not a claim to have checked every provision in the amendment chain. [Law 2004-639, arts. 1, 2 and 27](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/section_lc/JORFTEXT000000253374/LEGISCTA000006099294/); [national circular](https://www.douane.gouv.fr/sites/default/files/2025-07/16/Circulaire-octroi-de-mer10072025.pdf).

## Ask the client first

- When did production start, and what period did the business actually operate?
- What does the preceding calendar year's production-sales ledger show, separately from services, resales and immovable property?
- What production was sold locally or exported, and what establishments operate in other overseas departments?
- Which product classifications and activities support production status?
- What imports, regional rate/exemption decisions and effective dates are involved?
- What identification, return, payment and customs-account records already exist?

## The method, step by step

### Identify production and the relevant turnover

| Rule | Threshold, conditions and period | Official source |
|---|---|---|
| Inclusive production threshold: EUR 550,000 | Independent production, exclusive or alongside another activity; preceding calendar-year production turnover has reached or exceeded the threshold, regardless of legal form or treatment under other taxes | [Law 2004-639, art. 2](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000053545622/2026-06-23) |
| Production definition from 21 February 2026 | Manufacture, transformation or renovation of tangible movable goods, extractive activities and activities deemed agricultural under Rural Code L.311-1 | [Art. 2, version since 21 February](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000053545622/2026-06-23) |
| Transformation classification: SH 4 | The transformed good changes tariff heading compared with the inputs; test the first four digits of the combined nomenclature | [Art. 2, final paragraph](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000053545622/2026-06-23) |
| Partial reference year | Adjust the threshold to actual operating time; do not compare a partial-year producer automatically with the full-year amount | [Art. 2](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000053545622/2026-06-23), [circular, paragraph 19](https://www.douane.gouv.fr/sites/default/files/2025-07/16/Circulaire-octroi-de-mer10072025.pdf) |

The earlier article 2 reproduced in the circular used agricultural and extractive operations; the February amendment expressly refers to activities deemed agricultural under L.311-1. Do not apply the new wording retroactively to an earlier period or decide a particular agricultural activity from the cross-reference alone. Obtain the period and the activity's qualification. [Circular, annex II art. 2](https://www.douane.gouv.fr/sites/default/files/2025-07/16/Circulaire-octroi-de-mer10072025.pdf); [amended art. 2](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000053545622/2026-06-23).

Measure production sales, not total business revenue. The circular excludes services, goods purchased for resale without production and production of immovable property. Include local and exported production sales, aggregate the producer's relevant establishments across the five French overseas departments, and exclude VAT, octroi de mer and regional octroi from the turnover measure. Legal form and another tax regime do not change this threshold test. [Circular, paragraphs 16 to 22](https://www.douane.gouv.fr/sites/default/files/2025-07/16/Circulaire-octroi-de-mer10072025.pdf).

Article 1 distinguishes imports from remunerated deliveries of goods by the producer. The production-turnover threshold is not a blanket exemption for goods imported by a small business. No import tariff or exemption entitlement is established here. [Law arts. 1 and 2, retained consolidated section](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/section_lc/JORFTEXT000000253374/LEGISCTA000006099294/).

### Liability and identification

| Obligation | Trigger and nominal timing | Official source |
|---|---|---|
| Producer liability starts | 1 January following the calendar year in which the applicable production threshold was reached or exceeded | [Circular, paragraph 19 and annex III, Decree 2015-1077 art. 1](https://www.douane.gouv.fr/sites/default/files/2025-07/16/Circulaire-octroi-de-mer10072025.pdf) |
| Customs identification documents | **Before 31 January** of that following year, although liability already begins on 1 January | [Circular, paragraph 102](https://www.douane.gouv.fr/sites/default/files/2025-07/16/Circulaire-octroi-de-mer10072025.pdf) |
| Changes in production/business circumstances specified by the circular | Notify within 30 **working** days; do not replace this with calendar days | [Circular, paragraph 102](https://www.douane.gouv.fr/sites/default/files/2025-07/16/Circulaire-octroi-de-mer10072025.pdf) |

Identify with the territorially competent customs office. The circular specifies the business existence declaration or, failing that, a K bis extract, plus the preceding year's production turnover broken down by product nomenclature. It distinguishes NC8 and the applicable TARIC subdivisions. The current electronic submission channel, login, fields and acceptance receipt have not been verified; the circular's business-formality-body references are not a current portal instruction. [Law art. 34](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/section_lc/JORFTEXT000000253374/LEGISCTA000006099294/); [circular, paragraph 102](https://www.douane.gouv.fr/sites/default/files/2025-07/16/Circulaire-octroi-de-mer10072025.pdf).

Businesses with establishments in different overseas departments identify in each relevant department, reporting the local and aggregate production turnover. Within a department, the principal establishment handles identification with the other establishments listed. A producer becoming taxable again must re-identify under the circular's conditions. [Circular, paragraphs 102 and 103](https://www.douane.gouv.fr/sites/default/files/2025-07/16/Circulaire-octroi-de-mer10072025.pdf).

### Quarterly returns, invoices and records

| Rule | Conditions and limits | Official source |
|---|---|---|
| Quarterly return: nominal deadline the 24th of the following month | Calendar quarter, electronic declaration, with payment where due; no weekend, holiday or exceptional-extension conclusion is supplied | [Circular, paragraphs 104 to 106 and annex III, Decree 2015-1077 art. 7](https://www.douane.gouv.fr/sites/default/files/2025-07/16/Circulaire-octroi-de-mer10072025.pdf) |
| Nil returns | Declare even if there were no sales during the quarter | [Circular, paragraph 104](https://www.douane.gouv.fr/sites/default/files/2025-07/16/Circulaire-octroi-de-mer10072025.pdf) |
| Customs records: three years under the circular | Customs accounting/support records; this does not determine longer retention under other accounting or tax laws | [Circular, paragraph 107](https://www.douane.gouv.fr/sites/default/files/2025-07/16/Circulaire-octroi-de-mer10072025.pdf) |

The return annexes separate sales by product code and rate from exports by product code. File in each department where the producer is taxable; the principal establishment consolidates the establishments within the same department. The final-quarter declaration includes annual production turnover under the implementing decree. These are national content requirements; exact current screens and fields remain on hold. [Circular, paragraphs 104 to 106 and annex III art. 7](https://www.douane.gouv.fr/sites/default/files/2025-07/16/Circulaire-octroi-de-mer10072025.pdf).

For a local taxable delivery to another taxable producer, invoices must show product nomenclature and separately the relevant rates and amounts of octroi and regional octroi. Where deliveries are wholly exempt under the specified regional exemption provisions, including a zero rate in that setting, use the prescribed phrase **“livraison exonérée d’octroi de mer”**. Do not extend that wording rule to every export or outside-scope sale. Keep taxed operations, including those at a zero rate, exempt operations and outside-scope operations separately identifiable, with supporting records available to customs. [Law arts. 35 to 37](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/section_lc/JORFTEXT000000253374/LEGISCTA000006099294/); [circular, paragraph 107](https://www.douane.gouv.fr/sites/default/files/2025-07/16/Circulaire-octroi-de-mer10072025.pdf).

### Regional rates are a separate decision

Article 27 assigns rate setting to Réunion's regional authority by product nomenclature. The 2026 Finance Law amended the communication/effective-date framework from **1 July 2026**; the current article refers to implementing rules and limits the latest effective date by reference to communication. This does not prove a particular product decision's validity or rate. Obtain the actual decision and its operative date before calculating tax. [Art. 27 and commencement note citing Finance Law 2026 art. 99 III](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/section_lc/JORFTEXT000000253374/LEGISCTA000006099294/).

## Boundary and exception table

| Situation | Treatment in this Guide | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Full-year production turnover exactly at the threshold | Included, because the law says reached or exceeded | [Art. 2](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000053545622/2026-06-23) |
| Services/resales alongside production | Exclude separately identified non-production receipts from this threshold; support classification from transactions | [Circular, paragraphs 17 to 21](https://www.douane.gouv.fr/sites/default/files/2025-07/16/Circulaire-octroi-de-mer10072025.pdf) |
| Imports by a producer below the local-delivery threshold | Analyse the separate import charge and any actual exemption; do not infer a small-business import exemption | [Arts. 1 and 2](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/section_lc/JORFTEXT000000253374/LEGISCTA000006099294/) |
| More than one DOM or establishment | Aggregate for threshold purposes while preserving the department-specific identification and return obligations | [Circular, paragraphs 19, 103 and 106](https://www.douane.gouv.fr/sites/default/files/2025-07/16/Circulaire-octroi-de-mer10072025.pdf) |
| No sales in the quarter | Nil-return obligation continues for the taxable producer | [Circular, paragraph 104](https://www.douane.gouv.fr/sites/default/files/2025-07/16/Circulaire-octroi-de-mer10072025.pdf) |
| Regional exemption or zero rate | Depends on the actual product, legal basis, decision and period; never infer it from turnover alone | [Arts. 27, 35 and 37](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/section_lc/JORFTEXT000000253374/LEGISCTA000006099294/) |

## Worked cases

The original inputs are preserved. These are threshold tests only, with no invented reference year, filing cycle, regional tariff or tax liability. [Art. 2](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000053545622/2026-06-23); [circular, paragraphs 16 to 22](https://www.douane.gouv.fr/sites/default/files/2025-07/16/Circulaire-octroi-de-mer10072025.pdf).

**Partial year.** Six months of actual operation gives EUR 550,000 × 6/12 = EUR 275,000. Production sales of EUR 280,000 exceed that adjusted threshold by EUR 5,000. Under the circular's rule, liability starts on 1 January of the following calendar year, not at the sale that crossed the threshold.

```oa-check
name: Original six-month threshold and EUR 280000 production
inputs:
  annual_threshold: 550000
  operating_months: 6
  full_year_months: 12
  production_sales: 280000
steps:
  - adjusted_threshold: annual_threshold * operating_months / full_year_months
  - excess: production_sales - adjusted_threshold
expected:
  adjusted_threshold: 275000
  excess: 5000
```

**Mixed business.** Full-year production sales of EUR 300,000 plus separately classified services of EUR 400,000 total EUR 700,000 of business receipts. Only EUR 300,000 enters this production test, which is EUR 250,000 below the threshold. This does not decide VAT, imports or the services' legal classification.

```oa-check
name: Original production and separate service receipts
inputs:
  production_sales: 300000
  separate_services: 400000
  production_threshold: 550000
steps:
  - total_business_receipts: production_sales + separate_services
  - production_shortfall: production_threshold - production_sales
expected:
  total_business_receipts: 700000
  production_shortfall: 250000
```

**Exact boundary.** A full-year independent producer at exactly EUR 550,000 meets the inclusive threshold. The arithmetic shows equality; inclusion follows from the statutory wording, not from a positive excess.

```oa-check
name: Original inclusive full-year boundary
inputs:
  full_year_production: 550000
  production_threshold: 550000
steps:
  - difference: full_year_production - production_threshold
expected:
  difference: 0
```

## When to refuse or refer

Hold an actual filing or tax calculation if the relevant period, production qualification, product code, production ledger or regional decision is missing. New-business capital-input relief, deductions/refunds, suspension, special production cases, penalties and interest are not established here. Do not infer their treatment from the general producer threshold.

## Filing and payment

The national framework above identifies the competent customs office, supporting documents, quarterly return content, nominal due day and payment association. It does not establish the current electronic service, registration/login steps, return boxes, payment reference or confirmation process. Keep execution on hold until those operational requirements and any extensions are verified for the actual period.

## Completion checklist

Reconcile production receipts to accounts, separating services, resales, immovables and taxes. Aggregate relevant establishments, adjust for actual operating time, and record why the threshold is or is not met. Preserve identification and nil-return obligations separately from any amount payable. Obtain the applicable product decisions before computing a liability.
