### Enacted asset and loss relief

- **Enacted asset and loss relief** -- For 2025–26, eligible small business entities using simplified depreciation can deduct assets costing less than $20,000 when first used or installed ready for taxable use. The permanent threshold was enacted on 26 August 2026; schedule 2 commences on 1 October 2026 and applies to specified first use or installation from 1 July 2026. The new corporate loss carry-back rules were enacted in the same Act; distinguish the October commencement from their income-year application.  _([Tax Reform No. 2 Act 2026, schedules 1–2](https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2026A00071/asmade/text))_

- **2026-27 thresholds.** The indexed minimum is $69,528 and the second threshold is $129,717. Apply 15% to repayment income above the minimum up to the second threshold and 17% to repayment income above the second threshold. For a person liable to repay, the compulsory repayment is the least of that calculation, 10% of total repayment income and the repayable debt.  _([2026-27 indexation notice](https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2026G00249/asmade/text) and [Higher Education Support Act 2003, ss 154-1 and 154-20](https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2004A01234/2026-10-17/2026-10-17/text/original/epub/OEBPS/document_1/document_1.html))_

- **One-off 20% reduction** -- The one-off 20% reduction concerns eligible student-loan debts at 1 June 2025, with the associated 2025 indexation adjustment. It is not a new reduction at 1 June 2026. Use the current repayment-year thresholds separately.  _([Department of Education](https://www.education.gov.au/higher-education-provider-updates/higher-education-provider-updates-december-2025))_

## Worked examples

For 2026-27, assume repayment income of $70,000 and a repayable HELP debt of $50 under section 154-1. The first marginal band gives $70.80 and 10% of repayment income is $7,000. The repayable debt caps the compulsory repayment at $50. [Higher Education Support Act 2003, ss 154-1 and 154-20](https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2004A01234/2026-10-17/2026-10-17/text/original/epub/OEBPS/document_1/document_1.html) and [2026-27 indexation notice](https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2026G00249/asmade/text).

```oa-check
name: HELP repayment capped by repayable debt (2026-27)
inputs:
  repayment_income: 70000
  minimum: 69528
  repayable_debt: 50
steps:
  - marginal_amount: pct(max(repayment_income - minimum, 0), 15)
  - income_cap: pct(repayment_income, 10)
  - repayment: min(marginal_amount, income_cap, repayable_debt)
expected:
  marginal_amount: 70.80
  repayment: 50
```

For 2026-27, a member's $3.1 million closing total superannuation balance includes a $200,000 limited recourse borrowing amount added under paragraph 307-230(1)(d). The Division 296 balance is $2.9 million and the member is below the $3 million threshold. [ITAA 1997 ss 296-30 and 296-260](https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2026A00008/asmade/2026-03-13/text/original/epub/OEBPS/document_1/document_1.html).

```oa-check
inputs:
  ordinary_closing_balance: 3100000
  lrba_add_back: 200000
  threshold: 3000000
steps:
  - division_296_balance: ordinary_closing_balance - lrba_add_back
  - excess: max(division_296_balance - threshold, 0)
expected:
  division_296_balance: 2900000
  excess: 0
```
