# Contributor terms

Plain terms for anyone who contributes to OpenAccountants, developer or accountant. There is nothing to sign. Opening a pull request means you accept them.

## What you confirm

1. **It is yours to give.** You wrote the contribution, or you have the right to submit it. If it includes someone else's material, you say where it comes from and under what licence.
2. **It is shared under this repository's licences.** Code is under AGPL-3.0-only. Skills are under the [Open Accountants Skills License v2.0](LICENSES/LicenseRef-OA-Guide-License-2.0.txt). See [LICENSING.md](LICENSING.md). The same terms apply to your contribution as to everyone else's.

## What happens to your skill

1. **It is published.** It appears on openaccountants.com and in this repository.
2. **It is used by AI.** The OpenAccountants plugin serves it to AI tools such as ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Cursor when they answer tax questions.
3. **Accountants can check and improve it.** A licensed accountant may edit it, correct it and approve it under their name. Other contributors may improve it too.

## What stays yours

1. **Your authorship.** You keep your copyright and the credit for what you wrote.
2. **No extra rights are taken.** These terms do not let OpenAccountants relicense your work under other terms. If we ever want to include your contribution in a paid licence, we will ask you first.

## Earlier contributions

Before 6 November 2026, contributions were made under the [Contributor License Agreement](CLA.md), accepted by a signature comment or, earlier, by a ticked checkbox in the pull request template. Those acceptances remain valid for the contributions they covered. New contributions do not need it.
