# Delaware payroll withholding and unemployment insurance, 2026

## Scope

2026 Delaware wage withholding, employer unemployment insurance(UI), separate operations/technology and training assessments, plus paid-leave registration/contribution boundaries. Federal FICA/FUTA, deposits and federal wage reporting belong to `us-form-941-940-payroll`. City wage tax is separate. Withholding computation uses the currently posted **2025-2026 filing cycle** employer method, effective 2025 with unchanged underlying schedule; 2026 deposit thresholds/calendar are expressly published. [State source](https://revenue.delaware.gov/employers-guide-withholding-regulations-employers-duties/); [State source](https://revenue.delaware.gov/business-tax-forms/lookback-period/).

**Circular 230 §10.37 disclosure:** This skill supplies general state-tax working papers from the sources below. It is not a professional approval. A qualified tax professional must review the facts, assumptions and outputs before filing.

## Ask the client first

1. Employer/entity identity, FEIN, start date, Delaware payroll, DOR withholding and DOL UI account status?
2. Worker classification, residence, physical/required work locations, corporate officers and special/military exemptions?
3. Wage period, state taxable pay, DE-W4 allowances/status, additional withholding and year-to-date wages per employee?
4. Withholding reported July 1, 2024-June 30, 2025, assigned frequency and any new-employer default?
5. Actual 2026 UI rate notice, acquisition/successor history, operations/training bills, payments and delinquencies?
6. Delaware employee headcount/time, approved private paid-leave plan/waivers, cost sharing and contribution records?

## The method, step by step

1. **Open both accounts.** Use One Stop/DOR for withholding and DOL's liability registration UC-1 for UI; FAQ says submit within 20 days of first business day. A FEIN alone does not open either state account. UI ordinary liability is wages of at least $1,500 in a quarter or one worker in 20 weeks in current/prior year; agriculture, household, nonprofit and government have different coverage rules. Do not deduct employer UI from workers. [State source](https://onestop.delaware.gov/); [State source](https://labor.delaware.gov/divisions/unemployment-insurance/employer-faqs/); [State source](https://laborfiles.delaware.gov/main/dui/handbook/UI%20Employer%20Handbook.pdf).
2. **Locate the wages and get DE-W4.** Withholding generally reaches Delaware services by nonresidents and resident wages subject to the employer's duty; relief for tax withheld by another state needs the exact employer instructions. Delaware has a convenience-of-employer sourcing rule: a nonresident's chosen home days do not automatically remove Delaware wages. Military-spouse exemption requires WTH-EXM facts. Corporate officers may be UI employees; sole proprietor/partner draws are not wages. Multi-state UI uses localization/base/direction/residence rules, not just withholding situs. [State source](https://revenue.delaware.gov/employers-guide-withholding-regulations-employers-duties/); [State source](https://labor.delaware.gov/divisions/unemployment-insurance/employer-faqs/); `de-income-tax`.
3. **Compute withholding using the state method.** Annualize wages (weekly 52, biweekly 26, semimonthly 24, monthly 12, daily 300); subtract state standard deduction ($3,250 single/separate, $6,500 joint), apply the `de-income-tax` rate schedule, subtract $110 per valid allowance and divide by pay periods; floor at zero and add written additional withholding. Wage-bracket tables already incorporate their allowances/deduction: do not subtract them again. The published method is labelled effective 2025; no separate 2026 replacement was found. Use the actual posted table/form and reconcile before finalizing unusual facts. [State source](https://revenue.delaware.gov/employers-guide-withholding-regulations-employers-duties/); [State source](https://revenue.delaware.gov/employers-guide-withholding-tables/).
4. **Assign 2026 withholding frequency** from the DOR lookback, independently of UI/GRT. Withholding up to $6,020 is quarterly; over $6,020 through $33,460 monthly; above $33,460 eighth-monthly. No prior record defaults monthly. For 2026 the lookback is July 1, 2024-June 30, 2025. Use the actual 2026 due-date table; eighth-monthly is not twice monthly. [State source](https://revenue.delaware.gov/business-tax-forms/lookback-period/).
5. **Compute UI by employee.** Limit 2026 taxable wages to remaining $14,500 per employee per employer, subject to any recognized successor wages. Multiply by assigned basic rate; new employers including construction are 1.0%. The official merit table publishes 0.3%-5.4% and delinquent 6.3% for 2026. Add the separate 0.2% operations assessment and the handbook training rate 0.11% at this wage base, reconciling actual notices. The older FAQ still prints $18,500/$10,500 elsewhere; use the expressly dated 2026 wage base instead. Fiscal notebook and web merit minima differ, so the individual rate notice controls; do not choose the lowest rate without assignment. [State source](https://labor.delaware.gov/divisions/unemployment-insurance/employer-services/); [State source](https://delcode.delaware.gov/title19/c033/sc03/index.html); [State source](https://laborfiles.delaware.gov/main/dui/handbook/UI%20Employer%20Handbook.pdf).
6. **Keep billed components separate.** DOL's August 2026 FAQ says operations/technology 0.2% was previously included but is now billed separately:Q1 due August 31, Q2 due September 30 and later letters for remaining quarters. Reconcile whether already paid before adding another payment. Training is billed semiannually. DOL's November notice says OES can still take reports but cannot take payments from November 1 during the upgrade; use the stated UI payment lockbox and current account notice. Operations has its own lockbox. Do not send both assessments to one assumed account. [State source](https://labor.delaware.gov/divisions/unemployment-insurance/employer-faqs/); [State source](https://labor.delaware.gov/divisions/unemployment-insurance/).
7. **Assess Paid Leave separately.** Under Chapter 37, 10-24 employees generally require parental leave only; 25+ all three lines; smaller employers can opt in, with statutory seasonal/federal exclusions and approved private-plan alternatives. 2025-2026 public rates are 0.32% parental, 0.40% medical, 0.08% family caregiving/QE, total 0.80%. Employers owe the contribution but may recover up to 50% from employees; covered wages follow in-state FICA wages up to the federal Social Security base, whose amount remains in the federal skill. Register/report in LaborFirst, not the UI withholding account. Check employee localization, eligibility/waivers and private-plan status before charging. Benefits began January 1, 2026. [State source](https://delcode.delaware.gov/title19/c037/); [State source](https://labor.delaware.gov/delaware-paid-leave/employers/).

## Worked cases

**Case 1:** Single monthly wages $5,000, one valid allowance, no other adjustments. Annual taxable wages $56,750; tax $2,763.125 minus $110=$2,653.125; monthly withholding $221.09 by exact method. A $6,020 lookback is quarterly; at $6,020.01 it becomes monthly. A new employer with no history is monthly regardless of its first wage amount.

```oa-check
name: DE withholding and filing frequency
inputs:
  monthly_wage: 5000
  periods: 12
  deduction: 3250
  allowances: 1
steps:
- taxable: monthly_wage * periods - deduction
- annual_tax: 1001 + (taxable - 25000) * 0.0555 - allowances * 110
- withholding: round(max(annual_tax, 0) / periods, 2)
expected:
  taxable: 56750
  annual_tax: 2653.125
  withholding: 221.09
```

**Case 2:** New employer employee has $13,500 prior 2026 wages and earns $3,000 this quarter. Only $1,000 is UI taxable:basic $10, operations $2, training $1.10, total $13.10 economic cost. If prior wages were already $14,500, all three incremental amounts would be zero. Report all $3,000 gross wages; reconcile separate bills before payment.

```oa-check
name: DE UI cap and separate assessments
inputs:
  prior: 13500
  current: 3000
  base: 14500
steps:
- taxable: min(current, max(base - prior, 0))
- basic: taxable * 0.01
- operations: taxable * 0.002
- training: taxable * 0.0011
- total: basic + operations + training
- at_cap: min(current, max(base - 14500, 0))
expected:
  taxable: 1000
  basic: 10
  operations: 2
  training: 1.1
  total: 13.1
  at_cap: 0
```

## When to refuse or refer

Refer worker status/successor wages, agricultural/household/nonprofit/government coverage, disputed location/convenience sourcing, military cases, rate-notice conflicts and paid-leave private plans/waivers. Paid-leave benefit adjudication and local Wilmington wage tax are outside this computation. Do not treat the older handbook's historical UI base or online-payment text as current when a 2026 notice supersedes it.

## Filing and payment

Use WTH-TAX and the 2026 calendar: monthly generally 15th of following month, quarterly last day of following month, eighth-monthly the printed period dates. Initiate electronic transactions at least one day before the due date. Annual WTH-REC/W-2 reconciliation is due January 31(next business day); for 2026 wages that is February 1, 2027. The current form instructions require electronic Delaware W-2/1099 when federally required; quarterly reconciliations are not accepted. [State source](https://revenuefiles.delaware.gov/2025/2026_WH_DateTable_IRAS.pdf); [State source](https://revenuefiles.delaware.gov/2025/BusinessTaxForms_Instructions/WTH-REC_Instructions_2025-01.pdf).

UI gross/taxable wages and assessments use UC-8/UC-8A, quarterly even with zero wages, due last day of the month after quarter-end:April 30, July 31, October 31, January 31, subject to the state's actual weekend/holiday/payment notice. As of read date November 13, 2026, follow the OES upgrade notice for payment and continue filing reports. Paid Leave uses separate quarterly Hours & Wage Reports and contributions in LaborFirst under the applicable account calendar. Retain DE-W4, work locations, per-person year-to-date bases, separate component notices and confirmations. [State source](https://laborfiles.delaware.gov/main/dui/handbook/UI%20Employer%20Handbook.pdf); [State source](https://labor.delaware.gov/divisions/unemployment-insurance/); [State source](https://labor.delaware.gov/delaware-paid-leave/employers/).
