# Montserrat customs duty, consumption tax and hotels tax

> **General reference only.** This skill is general tax/accounting reference material for AI-assisted workflows. It has not been reviewed for any specific person's facts, documents, elections, deadlines, residency, filing status, or local procedures. Do not rely on it to file, pay, amend, or take a tax position without review by a qualified professional in the relevant jurisdiction.

## Scope

Montserrat's indirect taxes in 2026. There is no VAT or GST. Goods imported for consumption bear customs duty and consumption tax under the Customs (Control and Management) Act, Cap. 17.04, and the Customs Duties and Consumption Tax Act, Cap. 17.05, collected by the Customs and Excise Unit of Montserrat Customs and Revenue Services (MCRS Enabling Act, Cap. 17.06, ss.2 to 5). Paid accommodation bears hotels tax under the Hotels Tax Act, Cap. 17.13. Amounts are EC$. Customs incentives for approved enterprises and hotels: [ms-investment-incentives](ms-investment-incentives.md).

## Ask the client first

1. What are the goods, their HS commodity code and origin (Common Market origin changes the duty)?
2. The invoice price, freight, loading and handling, and insurance to the port of import.
3. Does the importer hold an exemption, an approved-enterprise or hotels aid licence, or a Business Progressive Fund approval?
4. Do the goods need an import licence under the External Trade Act?
5. For accommodation: hotel (six or more bedrooms), guest house, villa or other rental, and the guest's length of stay.

### The base

- **Entry.** For ordinary air or sea cargo the importer registers an entry in the form the Comptroller directs; a home-use declaration generally takes the duty rate in force on initial registration (Cap. 17.04 ss.26 and 66), with different rules for unentered and warehoused goods. For an ASYCUDA entry MCRS asks for the purchase invoice or receipt, freight documents and evidence of other costs such as insurance. Keep the declaration and its itemised calculations.
- **Customs value.** Where the transaction value method is available, start from the price paid or payable for goods sold for export to Montserrat and add the Schedule 2 items (freight, loading, handling, insurance). Otherwise follow the sequence in paras.2 to 7. SRO 30/2025 overrides para.8(1)(e) for transport and handling only; it says nothing on insurance.
- **Tariff.** The 2021 order (SRO 27/2021) replaced the tariff and conditional-exemption schedules from 12 April 2021; MCRS publishes the tariff book on the 2017 HS nomenclature. Check later orders.
- **Domestic production.** Producers and manufacturers account for consumption tax at production or manufacture unless another time is prescribed; goods made in Montserrat and exported are exempt.
- **Rounding.** Cap. 17.04 s.69(3) disregards fractions of a cent for duty and specified associated amounts. The older MCRS scissors example takes 15% of 8.05 (1.2075) as 1.20.
- **Hotels tax.** A hotel has six or more bedrooms; a guest house more than three and fewer than six. A guest is someone over 14 in paid accommodation; permanent residents are excluded, including someone staying at least 60 consecutive days in a hotel or guest house in the calendar year (s.2). Section 10 has separate rules for other rented accommodation.

### Steps

1. **Licence:** check the External Trade Act and Import Restriction Order for the goods and origin; apply in advance (digital applications since 22 September 2025; the licence is issued as a PDF copied to MCRS).
2. **Value:** price plus the Schedule 2 additions (50% of freight and handling under SRO 30/2025 in its period).
3. **Duty:** customs value x the tariff rate (Common Market origin preference under Cap. 17.05 ss.11 and 12, with proof of origin).
4. **Consumption tax:** (customs value + duty) x the scheduled rate.
5. **Exemptions:** apply the order, then the processing fee if both taxes are exempt.

## Worked cases

### Case 1: qualifying cement in 2026

Invoice price 1,000, freight 200, loading and handling 40, no insurance, both orders applying. Customs value is 1,000 + 100 + 20 = 1,120; duty at 5% is 56; consumption tax at 5% of 1,176 is 58.80; total 114.80.

```oa-check

inputs:
  price: 1000
  freight: 200
  handling: 40
steps:
  - customs_value: price + freight * 0.5 + handling * 0.5
  - duty: customs_value * 0.05
  - ct: (customs_value + duty) * 0.05
  - total: duty + ct
expected:
  customs_value: 1120
  duty: 56
  ct: 58.8
  total: 114.8
```

### Case 2 (decision): hotel or villa for a 10-night stay of EC$3,000?

A guest pays 3,000 for accommodation. In a hotel (six or more bedrooms) the tax is 10%, 300. In a villa the 7% rate on gross rent gives 210. A guest staying 60 or more consecutive days in a hotel or guest house in the year is a permanent resident and outside the guest charge.

```oa-check
name: Montserrat hotels tax, 3,000 in a hotel vs a villa
inputs:
  charge: 3000
steps:
  - hotel_tax: charge * 0.10
  - villa_tax: charge * 0.07
  - difference: hotel_tax - villa_tax
expected:
  hotel_tax: 300
  villa_tax: 210
  difference: 90
```

## When to refuse or refer

- Valuation outside the transaction value method; insurance under the SRO 30/2025 concession.
- The domestic consumption tax assessment base.
- Conditional exemptions and processing-fee bases beyond the orders named.
- Hotels tax returns and payment days (they depend on the Comptroller's direction and the 2025 Bill).

## Filing and payment

1. Register the entry in ASYCUDA with the invoice, freight and cost evidence; pay duty and consumption tax when duty is payable.
2. Exports of mined aggregate: declare before loading; pay within 14 days of export.
3. Hotels tax: register the premises, keep the guest register, and pay on the day the Comptroller's direction sets.

## Completion checklist

- Licence checked; customs value built with the right freight share.
- Duty, then consumption tax on value plus duty; fractions of a cent dropped.
- Exemption and processing fee applied; accommodation class settled.
