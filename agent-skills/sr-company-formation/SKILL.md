# Suriname company formation and registration (2026) v2.0

> **General reference only.** This skill is general reference material for AI-assisted workflows. It has not been reviewed for any specific person's facts or documents. A Surinamese notary and a qualified professional in Suriname must review the work before anyone signs a deed or files a registration.

## Scope

This skill covers setting up a company in Suriname in 2026: the choice of form, the deed of incorporation, the KKF trade register and the tax registrations that follow. The tax on the company's profit is in [sr-corporate-income-tax](sr-corporate-income-tax.md); BTW in [sr-vat-gst](sr-vat-gst.md); employees in [sr-payroll-social](sr-payroll-social.md); the overview in [sr-tax-overview](sr-tax-overview.md).

- **The NV is the only company form.** Suriname keeps the naamloze vennootschap as the one company form; there is no besloten vennootschap (BV) as in the Netherlands or the Dutch Caribbean (Burgerlijk Wetboek Boek 2, S.B. 2024 no. 164, art. 1(1) and the explanatory notes to the new Boek 2). A client who asks for a "BV" needs an NV.
- **Other vehicles.** A sole trader (eenmanszaak) registers directly in the trade register; foundations (stichting), associations and cooperatives are separate legal persons under Boek 2 titles 2 to 4.
- **Which text applies.** Today's NV rules are in the Wetboek van Koophandel (WvK) arts. 33 and following, as amended by S.B. 2016 no. 103 (which introduced the standard model deed); the new Burgerlijk Wetboek Boek 2 (Wet van 5 december 2024, S.B. 2024 no. 164) restates them in arts. 100 and following. Its commencement date was not confirmed (see Sources). The steps below hold under both texts unless marked.

## Ask the client first

1. What will the company do, and is the business licensed (vergunning) activity? The KKF One Stop Window handles licence requests with registration.
2. Who are the founders and first directors, with names and places of residence? Will anyone sign by written power of attorney?
3. How many shares, of which classes, and how will each be paid up: in cash, or in kind (which needs an opening balance sheet)?
4. Will a standard model deed do, or are tailored articles needed (then a notary is required)?
5. Will turnover exceed SRD 1,000,000 a year, and will there be employees from the start?

## The method, step by step

1. **Choose the form.** For limited liability and outside shareholders, an NV. Do not offer a BV: Surinamese law has none (Boek 2 art. 1(1)).
2. **Check and reserve the name.** Apply for name approval (naamgoedkeuring) through the KKF e-services before registering. The name of an NV begins or ends with "Naamloze Vennootschap" or "N.V." (WvK art. 36).
3. **Prepare the deed.** Use the Minister's standard model deed if its fixed articles fit; otherwise a Surinamese notary draws up a notarial deed in Dutch (Boek 2 art. 4(1), (3)). The deed contains at least: the articles (statuten), with the name, seat in Suriname and objects (WvK art. 36); the names and places of residence of the first directors (Boek 2 art. 4(2)); the number and classes of shares issued at formation and who took them; and each payment and how the duty to pay up is met (Boek 2 art. 101(1)). Enough shares must be issued at formation to vote on every matter, and at least one share must share in profit (art. 100(3)).
4. **Capital and opening position.** Under Boek 2, attach a statement by all founders that the company's equity at formation is not negative (art. 101(2)); for a contribution in kind, also an opening balance sheet signed by all founders, dated no more than three months before the deed (art. 101(3)). Every founder and share-taker signs in person or by written power of attorney (art. 101(5)).
5. **Register in the trade register.** Enter the company in the KKF trade register (Handelsregisterwet). Under the WvK, directors are jointly and severally liable for acts before publication and trade-register entry, or before at least 10% of each share issued at formation is paid up (WvK art. 40).
6. **Register with the Tax Administration.** Register in the online portal for income tax and, with staff, for wage tax and AOV. Register for BTW at once when turnover passes SRD 1,000,000 (Wet BTW art. 21); the BTW registration asks for the KKF extract (sr-vat-gst).
7. **First filings.** Provisional income tax return by 15 April (or within two and a half months of a book year's start) and the definitive return after the year end (sr-corporate-income-tax); monthly BTW returns once registered; dividend tax within one month of any distribution.

## Worked cases

### Case 1: a trading NV and the BTW threshold

Two founders form an NV with a standard model deed in March 2026. In its first calendar year the NV expects taxable sales of SRD 1,150,000 excluding BTW. They order two trade register extracts, one for the bank and one for the BTW registration.

- BTW: 1,150,000 is more than SRD 1,000,000, by SRD 150,000, so the NV must register for BTW as soon as the threshold is passed (Wet BTW art. 21) and file monthly.
- Extracts: 2 x SRD 300 = SRD 600.
- Deed: no notary is needed for the standard model deed; a tailored deed needs a notary and must be in Dutch.

## When to refuse or refer

- Tailored articles, share classes, shareholder agreements or contributions in kind: refer to a Surinamese notary.
- Licensed activities (financial services, mining, petroleum, gaming) and foreign investment approvals: refer.
- Converting a foreign company, a merger or a branch of a foreign company: refer.
- Any question on whether the old WvK rules or the new Boek 2 apply to a deed signed in 2026: refer to a notary until the commencement date is confirmed.
