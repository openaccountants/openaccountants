# West Virginia corporate net income tax (2026)

**Circular 230 §10.37 disclosure:** This skill supplies general state-tax working papers from the sources below. It is not a professional approval. A qualified tax professional must review the facts, assumptions and outputs before filing.

**Answer first:** West Virginia corporate net income tax is 6.5% of the state taxable base. Start with federal taxable income, apply state adjustments, allocation/apportionment and the state loss deduction, then credits. A corporate return can be required with no tax due. [§11-24-4(8)](https://code.wvlegislature.gov/11-24-4/); [CIT-120 instructions](https://tax.wv.gov/Documents/CIT/2025/cit120.instructions.2025.pdf).

## Scope

West Virginia domestic/foreign corporations, including entities taxed as corporations federally, with business or sources in the state; ordinary separate entities, with combined-reporting requirements identified for specialist completion. Tax year 2026. The latest instructions read cover the 2025-2026 filing cycle, including fiscal periods beginning in 2025 and ending in 2026; final 2026 forms/conformity are not assumed. Federal corporation rules belong to `us-form-1120-c-corp`. Individual proprietors use `wv-income-tax`; payroll uses `wv-payroll`.

## Ask the client first

1. Federal entity classification, fiscal year, WV activities/property and any federal exemption or unrelated business income?
2. Federal taxable income and detailed state adjustment schedules?
3. Unitary/related entities, ownership, federal consolidation and any surety/group election?
4. Business vs nonbusiness receipts and state destination/source data?
5. Loss origin years, prior state usage, credits, REIT/foreign/intangible transactions?
6. Prior returns, current estimates/payments, extensions, federal changes and notices?

### Computation and schedules

1. Establish nexus and entity classification. The instructions cover corporations enjoying the state's protection or deriving WV income; federal-exempt organizations generally exempt but unrelated business taxable income can be taxable. Federal net-income protection is a separate fact-sensitive inquiry, not a sales-tax threshold. [CIT-120 p.4](https://tax.wv.gov/Documents/CIT/2025/cit120.instructions.2025.pdf).
2. Start from Form 1120 federal taxable income or the required pro forma amount. Schedule B increases include deducted income taxes, federal NOL, relevant other-state obligation interest, related REIT/intangible items and foreign adjustments. Decreases include qualifying government-obligation income and income-tax refunds, with supported expenses/modifications. Do not use federal taxable income times the state rate without Schedule B. [CIT-120 pp.8-13](https://tax.wv.gov/Documents/CIT/2025/cit120.instructions.2025.pdf).
3. Wholly WV separate corporations use Schedule 1: federal amount plus increases minus decreases, state NOL, REIT/other inclusion, tax, then credits. Multi-state entities use Schedule 2 and CIT-120APT: remove nonbusiness income, apportion business income, add WV-allocated nonbusiness income, subtract allowable state NOL, and add the specified inclusions. The read regular-entity method is a single sales factor; motor carriers and financial organizations have special methods. Round the sales factor to six decimal places. [CIT-120 pp.8-9,16-18](https://tax.wv.gov/Documents/CIT/2025/cit120.instructions.2025.pdf).
4. Add back the federal NOL and calculate the WV NOL separately on Schedule NOL. The read 2025-2026 filing cycle instructions distinguish post-2017 losses from earlier vintages, apply the 80% limit, and limit losses by entity and combined-group history. Do not offset another member's income with a pre-combination separate loss without the required analysis. [CIT-120 pp.14-15,19](https://tax.wv.gov/Documents/CIT/2025/cit120.instructions.2025.pdf).
5. Unitary groups must provide combined reporting; either separate combined returns or a group combined return with designated surety can apply. Complete UB-CR and required federal attachments, and use the surety account for specified combined payments. Federal consolidation alone does not decide the state group. Refer water's-edge and group elections. [CIT-120 pp.5,19-24](https://tax.wv.gov/Documents/CIT/2025/cit120.instructions.2025.pdf).
6. Apply CIT-120TC credits within the applicable tax limits, then reconcile estimates, extension payments, carryforward and withholding to the balance/refund. On the latest read layout, Schedule 1 line 12 or Schedule 2 line 18 transfers to page 2 line 9; payments/credits reach lines 13-18 and balance plus additions reaches line 22. These are 2025-2026 filing cycle field references; use the final 2026 form before transcribing. [CIT-120 pp.8-10](https://tax.wv.gov/Documents/CIT/2025/cit120.instructions.2025.pdf).

### Register, file and pay

Register the entity/tax account through the state business-registration process and access MyTaxes at https://mytaxes.wvtax.gov for payment/account management. Use the current CIT-120 with approved e-file software, attaching Schedule B, applicable 1/2/APT, TC, NOL, combined forms and federal support as required. Sign the return; an incomplete unsigned return is not timely completion. Keep acceptance, payment reference and bank match. Even a zero-tax corporation must file if required. [CIT-120 pp.3-7](https://tax.wv.gov/Documents/CIT/2025/cit120.instructions.2025.pdf).

The statutory fourth-month annual due date makes a calendar 2026 ordinary corporate return due April 15, 2027, subject to the final 2026 instructions. The latest read instructions accept the federal filing extension with attachment and entered extended date, or CIT-120EXT for a state extension; payment remains due originally. Estimates use CIT-120ES. A federal final adjustment must be reported on an amended/RAR return within 90 days under the read instructions. [CIT-120 pp.4-7](https://tax.wv.gov/Documents/CIT/2025/cit120.instructions.2025.pdf).

Interest runs on unpaid tax from the original due date using the Tax Commissioner's period rate; the 2025 rate in the old instructions is not a verified 2026 rate. Estimated-tax additions use the applicable interest rate and the read instructions' 90% annual-liability rule. Do not claim a filing extension extends payment. [CIT-120 p.6](https://tax.wv.gov/Documents/CIT/2025/cit120.instructions.2025.pdf).

## Worked cases

**Case 1:** A wholly WV corporation has federal taxable income $200,000, deductible state income tax addback $10,000 and qualifying government-interest subtraction $2,000. With no NOL, special inclusion or credits, WV base is $208,000 and tax $13,520. This is a working-paper computation before the final 2026 return layout is confirmed.

**Case 2:** Applying the read 2025-2026 filing cycle loss rule, assume post-apportionment income before NOL of $100,000 and an eligible post-2017 same-entity loss pool $90,000, no older loss or credit. Deduct $80,000, leave $20,000 taxable, tax $1,300 and carry $10,000. If the eligible pool is only $30,000, deduct $30,000, tax $4,550 and carry zero. Validate 2026 law/conformity before final filing.

## When to refuse or refer

Refer unitary group composition/elections, financial organizations, motor carriers, foreign/REIT/intangible addbacks, federal immunity, mergers, pre-2018 loss history and credit certification. Current 2026 federal-conformity/depreciation changes are not confirmed by the prior-cycle booklet; do not assume federal changes flow through unchanged.

Prepare a state reconciliation and actual-year form set, signed and supported, with paid estimates and credit evidence. Resolve the 2026 conformity/form gaps before producing a filing-ready return. This new core skill does not claim full specialized corporate coverage.
