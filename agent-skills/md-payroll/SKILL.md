# Maryland employer withholding and unemployment insurance, 2026

> **General reference only.** Source-cited draft, not approved by an accountant. For US Treasury Circular 230 §10.37 purposes this is general reference material; a qualified professional must check the facts, current law and forms before filing or taking a position.

**Answer first.** Maryland withholding includes the state component plus the applicable county or special nonresident component. It is not the final annual income-tax computation. Withholding goes through Maryland Tax Connect; employer UI goes through BEACON. Federal payroll belongs to `us-form-941-940-payroll`, and annual state/local rates belong to `md-income-tax`.

## Who this applies to

Employers under Maryland jurisdiction paying resident wages or nonresident wages for Maryland services, subject to exemptions. Obtain actual residence/abode, work locations, dates and MW507 rather than defaulting to the employer's county. Register with the Comptroller and determine covered UI employment separately. A contractor label or Form 1099 does not establish exemption. [2026 employer manual pp.4-6](https://www.marylandcomptroller.gov/content/dam/mdcomp/tax/instructions/withholding/2026/withholding-guide.pdf).

## The base

For withholding, start with taxable wages, including taxable cash/noncash compensation and supplements. Subtract the pay-period standard allowance and MW507 allowances, then apply the published combined table and add requested extra withholding. Use the table equal to or next above the actual local rate when manual tables require grouping; graduated counties need their actual table/worksheet instructions. Annual brackets/exemptions are in `md-income-tax`; do not replace payroll tables with the annual return computation. The Maryland-resident/Delaware-or-other-nonreciprocal-state table addresses other-state withholding credits. [Manual pp.5-6, 9-10 and tables](https://www.marylandcomptroller.gov/content/dam/mdcomp/tax/instructions/withholding/2026/withholding-guide.pdf).

The signed MW507 county entry is in the address area, not line 4. Line 1 is allowances, 2 additional withholding, 3 no-liability exemption, 4 DC/VA/WV reciprocity, 5 Pennsylvania state exemption, 6-7 Pennsylvania local exemption conditions, 8 military-spouse relief plus MW507M. Keep the certificate; submit it when the form requires Comptroller review, including nonresidence claims. A certificate normally stays effective until changed; do not invent a universal annual-expiry rule. [2026 MW507](https://www.marylandcomptroller.gov/content/dam/mdcomp/tax/forms/2026/mw507.pdf); [manual pp.5-6](https://www.marylandcomptroller.gov/content/dam/mdcomp/tax/instructions/withholding/2026/withholding-guide.pdf).

UI uses covered gross wages and per-employee taxable/excess wage tracking; report gross amounts even after the cap. Use actual BEACON assessment components so the new fee is not added on top of an unreduced total rate. Reimbursable government/nonprofit employers follow their separate status. [DOL](https://www.labor.md.gov/unemployment-insurance/employer-agent/tax-rate.shtml); [L&E 8-605.1](https://mgaleg.maryland.gov/mgawebsite/Laws/StatuteText?article=gle&section=8-605.1&enactments=false).

For worker status, L&E 8-205 requires freedom from control, an independently established same-nature business/occupation, and work outside the customer's usual business OR outside all its places of business. All statutory elements must be satisfied; usual-course overlap alone does not defeat the alternative location limb. Construction/landscaping workplace-fraud proceedings are a separate regime and are not resolved merely by an affidavit. [L&E 8-205](https://mgaleg.maryland.gov/mgawebsite/Laws/StatuteText?article=gle&section=8-205&enactments=false).

The existing sick/safe-leave topic is retained: qualifying employers with at least 15 employees provide paid leave; 14 or fewer provide at least unpaid leave, based on prior-year average monthly employees. Accrual is at least one hour per 30 worked; permitted annual earning cap 40 hours, use/total cap 64, carryover cap 40, with frontload and other statutory exceptions. Use may be restricted for the first 106 calendar days; low-hours pay periods have specified exclusions. No mandatory unused sick-leave payout; rehire within 37 weeks and successor retention have specific rules. Final wages are due on the normal payday; an accrued-leave forfeiture requires the written-policy, notice and entitlement conditions in L&E 3-505. [L&E 3-1304](https://mgaleg.maryland.gov/mgawebsite/Laws/StatuteText?article=gle&section=3-1304&enactments=false); [3-505](https://mgaleg.maryland.gov/mgawebsite/Laws/StatuteText?article=gle&section=3-505&enactments=false). Other eligibility/purpose/damages details remain pending below.

## Deadlines and filing

1. Register via Combined Registration Application/Maryland Tax Connect at `mdtaxconnect.gov`; retain the eight-digit CRN and FEIN. The employer remains responsible when a payroll provider acts. Register UI in the Maryland Unemployment Insurance Portal BEACON and retain its separate employer account, coverage decision and rate notice. [Manual pp.4,6-7](https://www.marylandcomptroller.gov/content/dam/mdcomp/tax/instructions/withholding/2026/withholding-guide.pdf); [DOL new employers](https://www.dllr.state.md.us/unemployment-insurance/employer-agent/new-employer-get-started.shtml).
2. **Apply the statutory withholding frequency.** Less than USD 250 expected annual withholding: annual MW506 by 31 January after the year. Otherwise quarterly unless expected quarterly withholding is USD 700 or more, in which case monthly. Quarterly and monthly returns are due on the 15th of the following month. USD 15,000 or more required withholding in the preceding year triggers accelerated filing within three business days of each payroll that makes accumulated withholding at least USD 700; an approved monthly waiver may apply. Accelerated filers still file at least monthly until closed. [Tax-General 10-822](https://mgaleg.maryland.gov/mgawebsite/Laws/StatuteText?article=gtg&section=10-822&enactments=false). This statute resolves the manual's annual-date table error: its text says 31 January but one table prints 15 January. The statute also resolves its more-than-USD-700 wording: exactly USD 700 meets monthly status.
3. Log in to Maryland Tax Connect, select Business Tax and withholding, the correct period and MW506 or MW506M. Accelerated filings include the actual pay date. Enter liability, review and submit, schedule payment, save acknowledgement/reference and reconcile the ledger. Zero reports remain due; avoid duplicate paper filings after electronic submission. In 2026 electronic filing is the default with specified paper arrangements; the broad statutory mandatory rule starts with periods after 31 December 2026, not all 2026 periods. [Manual pp.6-8](https://www.marylandcomptroller.gov/content/dam/mdcomp/tax/instructions/withholding/2026/withholding-guide.pdf); [May 2026 tax alert](https://www.marylandcomptroller.gov/content/dam/mdcomp/tax/legal-publications/alerts/tax-alert-sales-and-use-tax-updates-2026-2027.pdf).
4. File MW508 and statements by 31 January after the calendar year, adjusted for closed days. Reconcile all payroll providers and MW506 payments. Maryland combines state/local tax in W-2 box 17; do not split local withholding into box 19 as a separate Maryland amount. Use current modified EFW2/Publication 1220 files; 25 or more forms require electronic filing. Correct period liability on MW506A/MW506AM, annual reconciliation on MW508A; individual W-2C corrections use the stated paper procedure. Use MW506FR to close the account. [Manual pp.7-8](https://www.marylandcomptroller.gov/content/dam/mdcomp/tax/instructions/withholding/2026/withholding-guide.pdf). Final 2026 file layout remains pending below.
5. File BEACON quarterly gross/taxable/excess wages and contribution report, using employee detail entry or accepted CSV/XML/ICESA/EFW2. Review the rate/fee components, submit, pay and retain receipt; Payment History verifies payment. Due within one month after quarter end. No-payroll reporting and account closure must follow the account's obligations. [DOL reporting](https://www.labor.md.gov/unemployment-insurance/employer-agent/tax-rate.shtml).

## Penalties and interest

Withholding nonfiling/nonpayment can incur a penalty up to 25% of unpaid tax. The manual also specifies USD 30 for a dishonoured instrument and separate civil/criminal wilful-failure provisions; do not substitute the old unsupported USD 100 per W-2/USD 50,000 cap. [Manual pp.4-5](https://www.marylandcomptroller.gov/content/dam/mdcomp/tax/instructions/withholding/2026/withholding-guide.pdf). UI late reports incur USD 35 each; unpaid contributions/reimbursements accrue 1.5% monthly interest. Rate notices can be challenged within 30 days. [DOL](https://www.labor.md.gov/unemployment-insurance/employer-agent/tax-rate.shtml). The current withholding annual interest percentage is unconfirmed below, rather than copying the old 11.5% figure.

## Cross-border points

DC, Virginia and West Virginia reciprocity uses MW507 line 4; DC/VA Maryland abode can change resident treatment, while the form explains West Virginia wage relief regardless of time spent. Pennsylvania line 5 removes state withholding but can leave local withholding at the Maryland work county unless line 6 or 7 applies. Do not say that every Pennsylvania commuter owes zero Maryland local tax or that Maryland automatically collects Pennsylvania local EIT. Military-spouse exemption requires MW507/MW507M and the form's actual conditions. [MW507](https://www.marylandcomptroller.gov/content/dam/mdcomp/tax/forms/2026/mw507.pdf).

Priya remains subject to Maryland resident withholding using her actual Montgomery County certificate and phase-out worksheet. Sam and Kavita, the existing Virginia examples, may claim relief only on their documented facts; a move to Maryland needs an updated certificate and dated wage records. The DOL new-employer page states FAMLI contributions begin January1, 2027 and requires localized employers to register separately; do not collect a 2026 FAMLI contribution merely because the account opens. [DOL new employers](https://www.dllr.state.md.us/unemployment-insurance/employer-agent/new-employer-get-started.shtml). Nonreciprocal multi-state work, localization for UI, and disputed abode require individual analysis. The old claim that reciprocity makes every multi-state sourcing issue disappear is not retained.

## Two worked examples that recompute

**Case 1, Jamal's payroll versus final annual tax.** Single, Baltimore City resident, one certified allowance, annual pay USD 52,000 in weekly periods. The published annual percentage table uses USD 3,400 plus USD 3,200 allowances: taxable payroll USD 45,400 x 7.95% = USD 3,609.30, approximately USD 69.41 weekly. This is payroll withholding; the annual tax is computed separately under `md-income-tax`. At a hypothetical published UI notice schedule rate 2.6%, contribution is 2.45% plus the 0.15% administrative fee on USD 8,500, total USD 221. A first USD 1,000 wage payment has USD 24.50 contribution plus USD 1.50 fee. If already beyond the base, both are zero. Eligible 40-hour weeks accrue 1.3333 leave hours, subject to caps. [Manual 3.20% annual table](https://www.marylandcomptroller.gov/content/dam/mdcomp/tax/instructions/withholding/2026/withholding-guide.pdf); [UI statute](https://mgaleg.maryland.gov/mgawebsite/Laws/StatuteText?article=gle&section=8-605.1&enactments=false).

```oa-check
name: Maryland 2026 payroll and UI fee allocation
inputs:
  annual_wages: 52000
  allowances: 1
  ui_wages: 8500
steps:
  - taxable_payroll: annual_wages - 3400 - allowances * 3200
  - annual_withholding: taxable_payroll * 0.0795
  - weekly: round(annual_withholding / 52, 2)
  - contribution: ui_wages * (0.026 - 0.0015)
  - fee: ui_wages * 0.0015
  - total_ui: contribution + fee
  - weekly_leave: 40 / 30
expected:
  taxable_payroll: 45400
  annual_withholding: 3609.3
  weekly: 69.41
  contribution: 208.25
  fee: 12.75
  total_ui: 221
  weekly_leave: 1.333333
```

**Case 2, classification decision.** Diego's existing facts are USD 4,500 monthly for 11 months, tools/schedule controlled by the construction company, no independent business and work at its jobsites. Those facts fail the statutory contractor test; wages are USD 49,500 and at an assumed actual total notice rate of 2.6%, taxable UI is USD 8,500, total USD 221. A genuinely independent business satisfying every 8-205 condition has zero covered wages from that contract. At a schedule/notice rate of 5.4% the arithmetic would be USD 459, but that old rate is not asserted as a 2026 construction default. Civil penalties, wage remedies and any federal settlement are separately determined; the worker cannot elect tax status. [L&E 8-205](https://mgaleg.maryland.gov/mgawebsite/Laws/StatuteText?article=gle&section=8-205&enactments=false).

```oa-check
name: Maryland employee versus genuine contractor
inputs:
  monthly_pay: 4500
  months: 11
steps:
  - wages: monthly_pay * months
  - covered_ui_wages: min(wages, 8500)
  - employee_ui: covered_ui_wages * 0.026
  - contractor_ui: 0 * wages
  - legacy_assumed_rate_result: covered_ui_wages * 0.054
expected:
  wages: 49500
  covered_ui_wages: 8500
  employee_ui: 221
  contractor_ui: 0
  legacy_assumed_rate_result: 459
```

## Ask the client first

1. Pay dates/frequency, wages/benefits, certified MW507 allowances and extra withholding, year-to-date taxable UI wages?
2. Domicile, Maryland abode/day count, county, all work locations, Pennsylvania local conditions and military-spouse documents?
3. CRN/FEIN, BEACON account, actual rate/fee notice, prior-year withholding and current assigned frequency?
4. Entity/industry, first hire, predecessor, household/agriculture/nonprofit facts, worker-control and independent-business evidence?
5. Filed periods/payments, payroll-provider changes, W-2 totals, amendments, notices and final account status?
6. Employee counts, leave policy/accrual/carryover, hire/rehire/separation dates and written accrued-leave terms?
