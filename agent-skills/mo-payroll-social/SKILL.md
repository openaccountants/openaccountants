# Macau Payroll and Social Contributions v0.1

> **General reference only.** These instructions support working papers. Obtain qualified local review before filing, paying or adopting a position for particular facts.


## Who this applies to

Employers with Macau employees and ordinary professional-tax withholding obligations, and employers liable for mandatory FSS or non-resident employment fees. Professional tax covers Macau work, including cash/in-kind remuneration and resident/non-resident employees; FSS resident coverage is a different test. This skill computes ordinary regular wages and statutory flat contributions, not every benefit, self-employed return or special non-resident service payment. [article 5](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/78/08/lei02.asp?printer=1); [current group classification, calculation and employer obligations](https://www.dsf.gov.mo/en/tax/tax_introduction/salaries_tax); [article 10](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/2010/34/lei04.asp?printer=1); [nonresident amount, exceptions, permit dates and quarterly payment](https://www.fss.gov.mo/en/sites/outax).

Mandatory FSS covers resident employees working under an employer’s authority/direction for remuneration, including qualifying overseas branches of Macau-registered enterprises. Public staff are covered subject to the existing pension-scheme exclusion. Check the exclusions for the employer personally, qualifying spouse/de-facto/family arrangements, absence of subordination, apprentices/training, relevant minors and the pension scheme; these are not an automatic resident-headcount charge. FSS pension recipients still contribute where employment liability continues. [article 10](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/2010/34/lei04.asp?printer=1); [article 11](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/2010/34/lei04.asp?printer=1); [article 16](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/2010/34/lei04.asp?printer=1); [obligatory FAQs 1-6, exclusions and edge months](https://www.fss.gov.mo/en/social/social-faq).

## The base

FSS is a fixed monthly contribution, not a wage percentage or a salary-cap calculation. The employer remits both shares and deducts only the employee’s share from wages. Distinguish long-term start/end-month exclusion from fixed-term half contributions; neither permits skipping the required beneficiary enrollment. Non-resident workers do not enter ordinary resident mandatory FSS solely because they work in Macau; test the separate employment fee. [article 10](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/2010/34/lei04.asp?printer=1); [article 12](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/2010/34/lei04.asp?printer=1); [article 16](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/2010/34/lei04.asp?printer=1); [article 18](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/2010/34/lei04.asp?printer=1); [article 19](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/2010/34/lei04.asp?printer=1); [obligatory FAQs 1-6, exclusions and edge months](https://www.fss.gov.mo/en/social/social-faq); [nonresident amount, exceptions, permit dates and quarterly payment](https://www.fss.gov.mo/en/sites/outax).

For the employment fee, use the worker-card issue and invalidity/abolition dates, not an unsupported daily proration. Issue on days 1-15 charges from that month; issue on days 16-31 charges from the next month. Invalidity/abolition on days 1-15 stops charging from that month; on days 16-31 it stops from the next month. Apply the manufacturing reduction or domestic-helper exemption only when proved. [nonresident amount, exceptions, permit dates and quarterly payment](https://www.fss.gov.mo/en/sites/outax).

For ordinary regular-wage professional tax, identify all remuneration and the article 4 exclusions, then apply the separate income allowance; do not double-deduct unsupported expenses. Apply the appropriate annual exemption and each marginal excess band, then the annual liability rebate and upward rounding. The old approximate 6.3% description is not an average tax rate: under the ordinary 25% income-allowance/30% tax-rebate conditions, 12% times 75% times 70% is a derived 6.3% upper marginal share of otherwise taxable gross wages. It is not a flat rate or a total payroll-cost percentage. [article 4](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/78/08/lei02.asp?printer=1); [article 4(o), income allowance](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/78/08/lei02.asp?printer=1); [article 7](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/78/08/lei02.asp?printer=1); [article 8](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/78/08/lei02.asp?printer=1); [2026 Budget Law 13/2025 article 18](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/2025/52/lei13.asp?printer=1).

Withholding uses the statutory annualization: assalariados use daily remuneration times 300 (in words on the page); empregados use monthly remuneration times the number of certain/permanent paid months under law or contract. Reconcile differences in January of the following year through the accepted payment slip. Bonuses, irregular pay, special exemptions, multiple employers and non-resident professional services need their actual statutory treatment; the ordinary wage computation is not a universal services-withholding calculator. [article 32](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/78/08/lei02.asp?printer=1); [article 36](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/78/08/lei02.asp?printer=1); [2026 Budget Law 13/2025 article 18](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/2025/52/lei13.asp?printer=1).


## Deadlines and filing

Register the employer and first-time beneficiary with FSS within the contribution month immediately following the employment start. For long-term employees, remit the preceding quarter during January, April, July and October. For fixed-term employees, pay during the month following the working month. Non-resident employment fees are paid for the preceding quarter in January, April, July and October; a monthly fee is not a monthly remittance rule. [article 12](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/2010/34/lei04.asp?printer=1); [article 19](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/2010/34/lei04.asp?printer=1); [coverage, enrollment, rate splits and payment periods](https://www.fss.gov.mo/en/social/social-mandatory); [nonresident amount, exceptions, permit dates and quarterly payment](https://www.fss.gov.mo/en/sites/outax).

Use the Business and Associations Platform/Macao One Account or the FSS accepted payment channel and correct Contribution Payment Advice or non-resident Employment Fee Advice. Verify staff changes, deductions, beneficiary/employer number and the accepted payment receipt. A missing advice is not a reason to omit payment; obtain its replacement through the accepted FSS service. Current live field mapping is not cleared here. [coverage, enrollment, rate splits and payment periods](https://www.fss.gov.mo/en/social/social-mandatory); [obligatory FAQs 1-6, exclusions and edge months](https://www.fss.gov.mo/en/social/social-faq); [nonresident amount, exceptions, permit dates and quarterly payment](https://www.fss.gov.mo/en/sites/outax).

For professional tax, submit employee M/2 within 15 days of hiring. DSF lists M/2A for departure by the end of the following month. Withhold at payment and remit the preceding quarter by 15 January,15 April,15 July and 15 October; the Budget expressly carries the last 2026 quarter into 15 January 2027. Submit the annual employee/remuneration M3/M4 during January and February, including remuneration even where no tax was deducted. The old 31 January-only annual deadline is incorrect. Use current DSF Electronic Services or accepted paper forms, keep acknowledgments and reconcile withholding to the annual statement. [article 29](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/78/08/lei02.asp?printer=1); [article 32](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/78/08/lei02.asp?printer=1); [article 13](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/78/08/lei02.asp?printer=1); [current group classification, calculation and employer obligations](https://www.dsf.gov.mo/en/tax/tax_introduction/salaries_tax); [2026 Budget Law 13/2025 article 18](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/2025/52/lei13.asp?printer=1).

For a listed self-employed profession, annual M/5 without organized accounts is within January-February; organized accounts use 15 April under article 11. A single-payer Group 1 employee is generally excused from M/5, but the statute does not excuse every multi-payer employee. These are filing boundaries, not a complete self-employed calculation; the full individual-tax skill is still missing. The old 31 March self-employed assertion is not used. [article 5](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/78/08/lei02.asp?printer=1); [article 10](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/78/08/lei02.asp?printer=1); [article 11](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/78/08/lei02.asp?printer=1).


## Penalties and interest

FSS registration, beneficiary enrollment, contribution payment and remitting amounts deducted from employees are distinct obligations. Late/non-payment may produce interest, fines or more serious consequences for appropriation. Do not quote a generic penalty without the actual due date, contribution class and period. Employment-permit consequences are separate from the professional-tax assessment. [coverage, enrollment, rate splits and payment periods](https://www.fss.gov.mo/en/social/social-mandatory); [nonresident amount, exceptions, permit dates and quarterly payment](https://www.fss.gov.mo/en/sites/outax).


## Cross-border points

Separate FSS residence/contract coverage from professional tax on Macau work and the employer’s non-resident levy. Authority to work and worker-card validity must be proved. Overseas payment of Macau work income does not automatically remove professional tax; treaty, social-security coordination and the special non-resident-services rule require further review. [article 10](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/2010/34/lei04.asp?printer=1); [article 5](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/78/08/lei02.asp?printer=1); [article 36](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/78/08/lei02.asp?printer=1); [nonresident amount, exceptions, permit dates and quarterly payment](https://www.fss.gov.mo/en/sites/outax); [current group classification, calculation and employer obligations](https://www.dsf.gov.mo/en/tax/tax_introduction/salaries_tax).


## Two worked examples that recompute

### Ordinary regular wages with separate FSS

Assume a resident long-term employee, ordinary exemption, 12 equal monthly payments of MOP 24,000, no other exclusions/benefits, no second payer and an ordinary liable full FSS month. Annual gross is MOP 288,000; after the 25%allowance, MOP 216,000 remains. Excess over the 2026 exemption is MOP 72,000, giving MOP 5,880 band tax and MOP 4,116 after the 30%rebate. Monthly withholding is MOP 343, employee FSS MOP 30, net MOP 23,627 and employer wage-plus-FSS MOP 24,060. All results are derived; this subtotal excludes other labour obligations. [article 4(o), income allowance](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/78/08/lei02.asp?printer=1); [article 7](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/78/08/lei02.asp?printer=1); [article 8](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/78/08/lei02.asp?printer=1); [article 32](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/78/08/lei02.asp?printer=1); [2026 Budget Law 13/2025 article 18](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/2025/52/lei13.asp?printer=1); [coverage, enrollment, rate splits and payment periods](https://www.fss.gov.mo/en/social/social-mandatory).

```oa-check
name: Ordinary regular wages with separate FSS
inputs:
  monthly_gross: 24000
  paid_months: 12
  allowance_rate: 0.25
  exemption: 144000
  rebate_rate: 0.3
  employee_fss: 30
  employer_fss: 60
steps:
  - annual_gross: monthly_gross * paid_months
  - taxable_income: annual_gross * (1 - allowance_rate)
  - excess: max(0, taxable_income - exemption)
  - band_tax: min(excess, 20000)*0.07 + min(max(excess-20000,0),20000)*0.08 + min(max(excess-40000,0),40000)*0.09 + min(max(excess-80000,0),80000)*0.10 + min(max(excess-160000,0),120000)*0.11 + max(excess-280000,0)*0.12
  - annual_tax: ceil(band_tax * (1 - rebate_rate))
  - monthly_withholding: ceil(annual_tax / paid_months)
  - monthly_net: monthly_gross - monthly_withholding - employee_fss
  - employer_subtotal: monthly_gross + employer_fss
expected:
  annual_gross: 288000
  taxable_income: 216000
  excess: 72000
  band_tax: 5880
  annual_tax: 4116
  monthly_withholding: 343
  monthly_net: 23627
  employer_subtotal: 24060
```

### Fixed-term and non-resident charges are different

Assume two resident long-term staff liable for all 3 quarter months, one separate resident fixed-term employee working 10 days in one month, and one ordinary non-resident whose card is issued 16 January and invalidated 14 March. The non-resident fee charges only February: MOP 200. Employer FSS is MOP 390, employee FSS MOP 195, employer FSS-plus-levy MOP 590 and total remitted MOP 785, derived. The casual contribution is paid in the following month; the long-term and non-resident quarter amounts are paid in April. No salary/PAYE or other employer-cost amount is inferred. [coverage, enrollment, rate splits and payment periods](https://www.fss.gov.mo/en/social/social-mandatory); [obligatory FAQs 1-6, exclusions and edge months](https://www.fss.gov.mo/en/social/social-faq); [article 19](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/2010/34/lei04.asp?printer=1); [nonresident amount, exceptions, permit dates and quarterly payment](https://www.fss.gov.mo/en/sites/outax).

```oa-check
name: Fixed-term and non-resident charges are different
inputs:
  long_term_staff: 2
  quarter_months: 3
  long_term_employer_monthly: 60
  long_term_employee_monthly: 30
  fixed_term_employer: 30
  fixed_term_employee: 15
  nonresident_chargeable_months: 1
  nonresident_monthly_fee: 200
steps:
  - employer_fss: long_term_staff * quarter_months * long_term_employer_monthly + fixed_term_employer
  - employee_fss: long_term_staff * quarter_months * long_term_employee_monthly + fixed_term_employee
  - nonresident_fee: nonresident_chargeable_months * nonresident_monthly_fee
  - employer_subtotal: employer_fss + nonresident_fee
  - total_remittance: employer_subtotal + employee_fss
expected:
  employer_fss: 390
  employee_fss: 195
  nonresident_fee: 200
  employer_subtotal: 590
  total_remittance: 785
```


## Ask the client first

- Resident/non-resident status, valid permit/card dates, contract type, workdays and start/end dates?
- Any employer-family, apprenticeship, public-pension or minor exclusion?
- All cash/non-cash remuneration, paid months, other payers and proved exclusions; age/disability evidence?
- Correct contribution/payment advice, manufacturing eligibility, M/2/M/2A and annual statement receipts?
