# Wyoming entity formation: LLC or corporation, fees, annual report and registered agent (2026)

> **Source-cited draft.** Built from Wyoming Statutes Title 17 (chapters 16, 28 and 29), Title 34 (34-1-139 and chapter 29) and Title 4 (4-10-510), and the Wyoming Secretary of State's Business Division fee schedule effective 1 July 2026 and business FAQs, read on 2026-11-14. No accountant has reviewed it. All outputs must be reviewed by a qualified professional before filing.

**Answer first.** A Wyoming LLC or corporation costs **USD 100** to form. From the next year it files an annual report and pays a license tax of **USD 60 or two-tenths of one mill (USD 0.0002) on each dollar of assets located and employed in Wyoming, whichever is greater**, on or before the first day of its anniversary month. Up to USD 300,000 of Wyoming assets the tax is USD 60; USD 1,210,000 of Wyoming assets gives USD 242. Wyoming has no general state business license and no state income tax. Miss the report by 60 days and the entity is administratively dissolved; it can be reinstated within 2 years.

## Scope and who this is for

This skill covers forming and keeping in good standing a Wyoming LLC (W.S. Title 17, chapter 29) or profit corporation (chapter 16): the filing, its fees, the registered agent, the annual report and license tax, dissolution and reinstatement, and the Wyoming rules people form here for (the charging order, series, long-duration and spendthrift trusts). It is for founders and the accountants who advise them.

It links, and does not restate:

- Sales and use tax: `wy-sales-tax`; unemployment insurance: `wy-payroll`. Wyoming has no individual or corporate income tax (pack README).
- Choice of state against Delaware, Nevada or the home state, foreign registration in other states and what other states charge, series recognition elsewhere, and beneficial ownership (BOI) reporting: `us-state-formation-matrix`; Delaware: `de-formation`; Nevada: `nv-formation`.
- Federal classification: `us-form-8832-entity-classification`; S election: `us-s-corp-election-decision`; foreign-owned single-member LLC and Form 5472: `us-form-5472-foreign-owned-us`; Schedule C: `us-schedule-c-and-se-computation`; crypto: `us-crypto-tax`; trusts: `us-form-1041-trust-and-estate-income`.

Out of scope (refer): close LLCs, DAO LLCs (W.S. 17-31), private trust companies, captive insurers, special purpose depository institutions, veil-piercing litigation, and trust drafting.

## Ask the client first

- Where does the owner live, and where will the business actually be run? Forming in Wyoming does not change the owner's tax home. An LLC run from a home office in another state is usually doing business there and must register there (`us-state-formation-matrix`).
- What will the entity hold or do: an operating business, real estate (where?), a holding company, digital assets?
- What assets will be located and employed in Wyoming? This sets the license tax.
- LLC or corporation? Number of authorized shares (corporation)? Series wanted, and how many?
- Who will be the registered agent and at what yearly price? Will they act as organizer?
- Is any owner a non-US person (Form 5472, EIN by phone, fax or mail)?
- Venture finance planned? If so, Delaware is usually the vehicle (`de-formation`).
- Any known creditor or pending claim? Forming and funding after a claim arises does not protect the assets (below).

## The method, step by step

1. **Choose the entity and the state** from the answers above, and record why.
2. **Check the name.** An LLC name must include "limited liability company" or "LLC" or "L.L.C.", "limited company" or "LC" or "L.C.", or another listed form, may not imply another purpose or a corporation, and must be distinguishable on the Secretary of State's records (W.S. 17-29-108). A name may be reserved for 120 days (17-29-109). A corporation's name follows W.S. 17-16-401.
3. **Appoint a registered agent** with a physical Wyoming street address where the agent (or a natural person in an agency relationship with it) is physically present to accept service. An agent for more than ten entities must register as a commercial registered agent (W.S. 17-28-101). The agent signs a written consent filed with the articles (17-29-201(c)).
4. **File the articles online at wyobiz.wyo.gov** (articles of organization and of incorporation can be filed electronically), or on paper. LLC articles state only the name and "The street address of the limited liability company's initial registered office and the name of its initial registered agent at that office" (W.S. 17-29-201(b)). Corporation articles state the name, the number of authorized shares (which may be unlimited), the registered office and agent, and each incorporator's name and address (W.S. 17-16-202). Pay USD 100. For a faster paper review pay the expedite fee (next business day USD 700, same business day USD 1,400; some documents are excluded). [Fee schedule](https://sos.wyo.gov/Business/Docs/BusinessFees.pdf)
5. **What happens next.** The LLC is formed when the articles become effective, unless they state a delayed date (17-29-201(e)). Get an electronic certificate of good standing free at wyobiz.wyo.gov (it carries a validation number); a manually signed certificate costs USD 20. [FAQ](https://sos.wyo.gov/faqs.aspx?root=BUS)
6. **Sign the operating agreement or bylaws.** The operating agreement is not filed. Members are not named in the LLC articles.
7. **Get an EIN from the IRS** (the Secretary of State does not issue it) and decide the federal classification.
8. **Register as a foreign entity** in each other state where the entity does business, before starting there (`us-state-formation-matrix`).
9. **Diarise the annual report**: due on or before the first day of the anniversary month every year. An entity formed on 15 January has its report due 1 January each year. It may be filed up to 120 days early. Courtesy emails come 60, 30 and 10 days before. [FAQ](https://sos.wyo.gov/faqs.aspx?root=BUS)
10. **Keep Wyoming records**: books needed to work out the license tax are kept for 3 years at the principal place of business (17-29-209(e)); the registered agent keeps the names and addresses of managers, officers and directors and of a contact person (17-28-107).

### The annual report and license tax

Every Wyoming LLC, and every foreign LLC with a certificate of authority, files "on or before the first day of the month of organization of every year" a certification of "its capital, property and assets located and employed in the state of Wyoming", gives its principal office address, and pays "sixty dollars ($60.00) or two-tenths of one mill on the dollar ($.0002), whichever is greater" (W.S. 17-29-209(a)). Corporations file the same report and tax on the first day of the month of registration, and the report also names the officers and directors (W.S. 17-16-1630(a)). Assets used in interstate carriage count only to the extent used intrastate; mines are valued at the assessed value of the previous year's gross product; property is valued at actual value (17-29-209(b)). Financial figures are as at the end of the last fiscal year (17-29-209(c)). [Title 17](https://wyoleg.gov/statutes/compress/title17.pdf)

**Late.** The entity is delinquent on the second day of the month after the due date. "If the annual report is not filed within sixty (60) days following the due date, the entity will be administratively dissolved." [FAQ](https://sos.wyo.gov/faqs.aspx?root=BUS). The statute runs the 60 days from the Secretary of State's notice: an LLC that has not paid the fee is "deemed to be transacting business within this state without authority", and unless it complies within 60 days of the notice it is "deemed defunct" (W.S. 17-29-705(b)).

**Reinstatement.** Within 2 years of forfeiture, pay the delinquent fees (and the USD 100 reinstatement fee on the schedule). Reinstatement "relates back to and takes effect as of the effective date deemed defunct", and the LLC carries on "as if it had never been deemed defunct" (17-29-705(b)). After 2 years there is no reinstatement. [FAQ](https://sos.wyo.gov/faqs.aspx?root=BUS)

**Other forfeiture grounds** (17-29-705(a), (c)): no registered agent or office (60 days after notice; reinstatement within 2 years with a USD 250 statutory penalty plus the fee); a member signing a knowingly false filing; ignoring a valid subpoena; failing to give the registered agent required records; false information; being unservable; or ownership or control by a foreign adversary listed in 15 C.F.R. 791.4(a) without CFIUS approval.

### Registered agent duties

The registered office is a physical Wyoming street address where the agent or its natural-person agent "is physically present" to accept service (W.S. 17-28-101(a)). The agent must keep, current within 60 days of a change, the names and addresses of the entity's directors, officers or LLC managers and the name, address and phone of a natural person authorized to receive its communications (17-28-107(a)(v)); those records can be examined by the Secretary of State (17-28-108). An agent resigning must notify the entity at least 30 days before filing; if no successor is appointed, the entity files a statement of change within 30 days of receiving the resignation (17-28-103). [Title 17](https://wyoleg.gov/statutes/compress/title17.pdf)

### The charging order (W.S. 17-29-503)

A court may charge a judgment debtor's transferable interest; the LLC then pays the creditor any distribution that would have gone to the debtor (17-29-503(a)). "This section provides the exclusive remedy by which a person seeking to enforce a judgment against a judgment debtor, including any judgment debtor who may be the sole member, dissociated member or transferee, may, in the capacity of the judgment creditor, satisfy the judgment from the judgment debtor's transferable interest or from the assets of the limited liability company. Other remedies, including foreclosure ... are not available ... and may not be ordered by the court." (17-29-503(g)). The debtor may end the order by paying the judgment; the LLC or other members may pay it and take over the order (17-29-503(d), (e)). [Title 17](https://wyoleg.gov/statutes/compress/title17.pdf)

What it does not do:

- protect the LLC's assets from the LLC's own creditors, or the member from a lender holding a personal guarantee;
- stop a court disregarding a sham LLC (commingled funds, no separate books or bank account, formalities ignored);
- save assets moved into the LLC after a claim arose: a transfer meant to hinder, delay or defraud creditors can be undone;
- bind the IRS, whose federal lien and levy reach the interest regardless of state law, or federal forfeiture.

Operating agreement points that support the order: a manager other than the debtor; distributions at the manager's discretion, not mandatory; transfer restrictions; no personal guarantees; adequate capital and separate records.

### Series (W.S. 17-29-211)

An operating agreement may establish series of members, managers, transferable interests or assets. A series' debts are enforceable against its assets only, and not against the LLC, other series or any member, if (i) its records account for its assets separately, (ii) the operating agreement "specifically provides for the limitations on liabilities", and (iii) "Notice of the limitations on liabilities of the particular series is included in the articles of organization" (17-29-211(c)). A series can contract, hold title, grant liens and sue and be sued in its own name, but may not act as a financial institution or insurer (17-29-211(e)). The Secretary of State charges USD 10 per series designated. How other states, banks and the IRS treat a series: `us-state-formation-matrix`. [Title 17](https://wyoleg.gov/statutes/compress/title17.pdf)

### Privacy on the public record

LLC articles name the registered agent, not the members or managers; the LLC annual report gives the principal office address and the asset figure. A corporation's annual report names its officers and directors (17-16-1630(a)). The members are still known to the registered agent (which must hold manager names and a contact person, 17-28-107), to the IRS (the EIN responsible party), to banks under customer due diligence, and to any court or agency with a subpoena or summons. Say "private on the public record", never "anonymous". A registered agent service may sign the articles as organizer; a third party who serves as manager takes on a manager's duties and should be covered by a written nominee agreement.

### Trusts that pair with a Wyoming LLC

- **Up to 1,000 years.** A trust created after 1 July 2003 holding property other than, or in addition to, real property may last up to 1,000 years if it is governed by Wyoming law, the trustee has a place of business, administers the trust or resides in Wyoming, and powers of appointment end within 1,000 years (W.S. 34-1-139(b)). Interests in real property held directly stay under the common-law rule (21 years after a life in being, 34-1-139(a)). [Title 34](https://wyoleg.gov/statutes/compress/title34.pdf)
- **Qualified spendthrift trust.** A settlor may create a self-settled qualified spendthrift trust with a qualified trustee; the instrument must say it is one, adopt Wyoming law, include a spendthrift provision and be irrevocable, though the settlor may keep a veto over distributions, powers of appointment, income rights, up to 5% a year, discretionary principal and the right to change trustees (W.S. 4-10-510). [Title 4](https://wyoleg.gov/statutes/compress/title04.pdf)
- **Digital assets.** Wyoming classifies digital consumer assets and digital securities as intangible personal property and virtual currency as money for UCC article 9 purposes (W.S. 34-29-102). An ordinary LLC is the usual vehicle for holding digital assets; a DAO LLC is a separate statute (out of scope). [Title 34](https://wyoleg.gov/statutes/compress/title34.pdf)

Trust drafting and federal income, gift, estate and generation-skipping tax are out of scope: refer.

### Red flags to raise with the client

- Marketing that calls a Wyoming LLC "anonymous" or "tax-free".
- A foreign owner with no Form 5472 filed.
- An LLC run from another state with no foreign registration there.
- Personal guarantees of LLC debts by the member.
- Assets moved in after a claim arose.
- Annual report anniversary not diarised, or the entity shows delinquent on wyobiz.

## Worked cases

**Case 1, first two years of a Wyoming LLC, and the asset test.** A founder forms an LLC online (USD 100) and pays a commercial registered agent USD 150 a year (the agent's quote). Year 1: USD 250. Year 2: the annual report with no Wyoming assets is USD 60, plus the agent: USD 210. If the LLC instead held USD 1,210,000 of assets located and employed in Wyoming, the year-2 tax would be the greater of USD 60 and 1,210,000 x 0.0002 = USD 242.

```oa-check
name: Wyoming LLC first two years and asset-based license tax (2026)
inputs:
  articles: 100
  agent_fee_quoted: 150
  wyoming_assets_none: 0
  wyoming_assets_large: 1210000
steps:
  - year_1: articles + agent_fee_quoted
  - tax_none: max(60, wyoming_assets_none * 0.0002)
  - year_2: tax_none + agent_fee_quoted
  - tax_large: max(60, wyoming_assets_large * 0.0002)
expected:
  year_1: 250.00
  tax_none: 60.00
  year_2: 210.00
  tax_large: 242.00
```

**Case 2, decision: four property LLCs or one series LLC with four series.** An investor wants one vehicle per rental property, all in Wyoming. Four separate LLCs cost 4 x USD 100 = USD 400 to form and file four annual reports. One LLC with four series costs USD 100 plus 4 x USD 10 = USD 140 to form: USD 260 less at formation, with one annual report on the LLC. Against that, the series shield depends on separate records, the limitation in the operating agreement and notice in the articles (17-29-211(c)), and on how other states and lenders treat series. If any property is outside Wyoming, or a lender insists on a separate entity, choose separate LLCs.

```oa-check
name: Wyoming separate LLCs against one series LLC, formation cost (2026)
inputs:
  properties: 4
  articles: 100
  series_fee: 10
steps:
  - separate_llcs: properties * articles
  - series_llc: articles + properties * series_fee
  - saving: separate_llcs - series_llc
expected:
  separate_llcs: 400.00
  series_llc: 140.00
  saving: 260.00
```

## When to refuse or refer

- The owner wants Wyoming to avoid tax owed in their home state or to the IRS: explain the rules; refuse to build the structure for that purpose.
- A claim has already arisen or is foreseeable, and the client wants to move assets into a new LLC or trust: refer to counsel; do not facilitate.
- Trust drafting (1,000-year or qualified spendthrift trust), DAO LLCs, private trust companies, captive insurers and special purpose depository institutions: refer to a Wyoming attorney.
- Non-resident alien owners: US source and treaty questions, and home-country tax, go to a cross-border specialist (`us-form-5472-foreign-owned-us` for the filing).
- Venture finance: refer to startup counsel (`de-formation`).

## Completion checklist

- [ ] Owner's residence and the real place of business recorded; no home-state tax saving promised
- [ ] Name compliant; registered agent at a physical Wyoming address, consent filed, price recorded
- [ ] Articles filed; good standing certificate on file
- [ ] Operating agreement signed: manager, discretionary distributions, transfer restrictions; series limitation and articles notice if series are used
- [ ] Separate bank account and books; no personal guarantees without advice
- [ ] EIN and federal classification done; Form 5472 diarised if foreign-owned
- [ ] Wyoming assets measured; annual report diarised for the first day of the anniversary month
- [ ] Foreign registration in each state of business
- [ ] Trust structures, if any, referred to Wyoming counsel
- [ ] Credentialed adviser sign-off before delivery
