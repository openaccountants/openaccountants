# Slovenia Tax Overview (2026) v0.3

## Who this applies to

Start here for the Slovenian tax system in 2026, then open the Guide for the income, entity or filing involved. Establish residence, income type, business form, turnover, insurance history and the event date before using the figures. The normed-expense rules below are the ZPZR rules operative at the read date; the conditional ZIURS change remains a hold.

- **Overview.** Slovenia is a euro-area EU member state. It has a progressive personal income tax (dohodnina), a corporate income tax (davek od dohodkov pravnih oseb, DDPO) and VAT (davek na dodano vrednost, DDV) under the EU VAT directive. Taxes are administered by the Financial Administration of the Republic of Slovenia (Finančna uprava Republike Slovenije, FURS). Returns are filed on eDavki.
- **Tax year, individuals.** The calendar year ([ZDoh-2](https://pisrs.si/api/datoteke/integracije/358727637) art. 15(1)). Income tax is set under the law in force on 1 January of the year ([ZDoh-2](https://pisrs.si/api/datoteke/integracije/358727637) art. 2).
- **Tax period, companies.** The calendar year. A company may choose a business year that differs from the calendar year, of no more than 12 months ([ZDDPO-2](https://pisrs.si/api/datoteke/integracije/393614825) art. 10(1) and (2)).
- **Currency.** Euro (EUR).
- **Tax authority.** FURS, with returns filed through eDavki.
- **Individuals: who is taxed on what.** A resident is taxed on income from Slovenian and foreign sources. A non-resident is taxed only on income with a source in Slovenia ([ZDoh-2](https://pisrs.si/api/datoteke/integracije/358727637) art. 5). A person is resident if, among other tests, they have a registered permanent residence in Slovenia, have their habitual abode or centre of personal and economic interests there, or are present in Slovenia for more than 183 days in the tax year ([ZDoh-2](https://pisrs.si/api/datoteke/integracije/358727637) art. 6). Detail: [slovenia-income-tax](slovenia-income-tax.md) and [si-cross-border](si-cross-border.md).
- **Companies: who is taxed on what.** A resident company is taxed on income from all sources. A non-resident is taxed on income with a source in Slovenia ([ZDDPO-2](https://pisrs.si/api/datoteke/integracije/393614825) art. 4). A company is resident if it has its seat or its place of effective management in Slovenia ([ZDDPO-2](https://pisrs.si/api/datoteke/integracije/393614825) art. 5(1)).

## The base

- **Corporate tax stays at 22%.** The temporary rate under the flood reconstruction law applies for 2024 to 2028 ([ZORZFS](https://pisrs.si/api/datoteke/integracije/586948650) art. 64(1)). See [si-corporate-income-tax](si-corporate-income-tax.md).
- **Normed-expense sole proprietors (normiranci).** From tax year 2026, [ZPZR](https://pisrs.si/api/datoteke/integracije/486867865) replaces the [ZDoh-2](https://pisrs.si/api/datoteke/integracije/358727637) entry and exit tests and the flat 20% final tax ([ZPZR](https://pisrs.si/api/datoteke/integracije/486867865) arts. 6, 7 and 21(2)):
  - An s.p. may use normed expenses if the average revenue of the two previous years is not over EUR 120,000 (full-time self-insured for at least nine months in each year), EUR 50,000 (not full-time self-insured in those years) or EUR 85,000 (full-time self-insured in only one of the two years). The same averages force an exit to actual expenses ([ZPZR](https://pisrs.si/api/datoteke/integracije/486867865) art. 6(3) and (13)). An s.p. counts as full-time self-insured for nine months if insured for at least 75% of the minimum annual hours of full-time self-employed insurance ([ZPZR](https://pisrs.si/api/datoteke/integracije/486867865) art. 6(16)). For entry only, part-year trading requires the proportional adjustment in [ZPZR](https://pisrs.si/api/datoteke/integracije/486867865) art. 6(4). Do not annualise part-year revenue for the compulsory exit test in art. 6(13). For both tests a prior year with no trading counts as zero under art. 6(14). Check the business-transfer and related-party rules in arts. 6(8)-(9), (13), rather than assuming that a new registration alone establishes eligibility.
  - The normed expenses themselves are unchanged: 80% of revenue up to EUR 60,000 for an s.p. full-time self-insured for at least nine months of the tax year; 80% up to EUR 12,500 and 40% from EUR 12,500 to 30,000 for others ([ZDoh-2](https://pisrs.si/api/datoteke/integracije/358727637) art. 59(1) and (2); [ZPZR](https://pisrs.si/api/datoteke/integracije/486867865) art. 20).
  - The final tax is 20% up to a base of EUR 72,000 (EUR 33,000 if not full-time self-insured for nine months of the tax year) and 35% above, as a final tax ([ZPZR](https://pisrs.si/api/datoteke/integracije/486867865) art. 7(1) and (3)).
  - FURS confirmed on 16 October 2026 that [ZPZR](https://pisrs.si/api/datoteke/integracije/486867865) governs 2026. It said that a separate law, ZIURS, would change these rules from 1 January 2026 if it enters into force after a referendum (FURS news, 16 Oct 2026). The National Assembly has called that referendum for Sunday 6 December 2026 (OdZIURS, Uradni list RS 1692/2026 of 16 Oct 2026). As read on 17 November 2026, [ZPZR](https://pisrs.si/api/datoteke/integracije/486867865) applies. The referendum date alone does not establish whether ZIURS enters into force; do not use proposed ZIURS figures as operative law.
  - Planning detail: [slovenia-tax-optimization](slovenia-tax-optimization.md).
- **Winter allowance.** Employees are entitled to a winter allowance of half the minimum wage, paid within 18 days after the November pay period ends ([ZPZR](https://pisrs.si/api/datoteke/integracije/486867865) arts. 2(1) and 3(1)). It is not taxed up to that amount, counted together with any business-performance bonus within the [ZDoh-2](https://pisrs.si/api/datoteke/integracije/358727637) art. 44(1)(12) cap ([ZPZR](https://pisrs.si/api/datoteke/integracije/486867865) art. 4(1)). For part-year employment the entitlement is proportional; for part-time work it is proportional except the [ZDR-1](https://pisrs.si/api/datoteke/integracije/358535670) arts. 67/67.b cases. A non-working-day due date moves to the next working day. An activity-level collective agreement may defer payment for illiquidity to 31 March of the following year; this deferral does not apply to the public sector ([ZPZR](https://pisrs.si/api/datoteke/integracije/486867865) arts. 2(2)-(3), 3(1)-(3)). See [slovenia-payroll](slovenia-payroll.md).

Apply that rule to the statutory 31 July date and to any delivery-based objection deadline; do not treat the date table as an exception to the carry rule.

## Two worked examples that recompute

**Annual scale.** Assume the already determined net annual taxable base is EUR 30,000 for 2026. It falls in the 33% band: EUR 6,461.89 + (EUR 30,000 − EUR 28,592.44) × 33% = EUR 6,926.38, rounded to cents. Gross income, eligibility for allowances and any separately taxed income have already been resolved; this is not a payroll withholding calculation. Source: [FURS 2026 scale](https://www.fu.gov.si/fileadmin/Internet/Davki_in_druge_dajatve/Podrocja/Dohodnina/Letna_odmera_dohodnine/Opis/Lestvica_za_leto_2026.docx).

```oa-check
name: Slovenia 2026 net annual base EUR 30,000
inputs:
  net_base: 30000
  lower_limit: 28592.44
  tax_at_limit: 6461.89
steps:
  - excess: net_base - lower_limit
  - tax: round(tax_at_limit + excess * 0.33, 2)
expected:
  excess: 1407.56
  tax: 6926.38
```

**Eligible full-time normiranec.** Assume the election and prior-year eligibility tests are met, the person meets the current-year full-time insurance condition, and 2026 revenue is EUR 60,000. Normed expenses are EUR 48,000, the base EUR 12,000 and final business-income tax EUR 2,400. Contributions, VAT and other income are separate; this is not a total-tax figure. Sources: [ZDoh-2 art. 59(1)](https://pisrs.si/api/datoteke/integracije/358727637) and [ZPZR arts. 6-7](https://pisrs.si/api/datoteke/integracije/486867865).

```oa-check
name: Slovenia eligible full-time normiranec EUR 60,000
inputs:
  revenue: 60000
steps:
  - normed: min(revenue, 60000) * 0.80
  - base: revenue - normed
  - tax: min(base, 72000) * 0.20 + max(base - 72000, 0) * 0.35
expected:
  normed: 48000
  base: 12000
  tax: 2400
```

## Ask the client first

- Which tax year, transaction date and filing period?
- Where are the person and company resident, and where is the income sourced?
- What income category and business form are involved?
- What were turnover and trading periods in the two previous years and the current year?
- What are the election record, self-employment insurance hours, related-party dealings and business-transfer history?
- Are there employees, foreign activities, capital disposals or a received FURS assessment and delivery date?
