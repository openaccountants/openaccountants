# Wisconsin corporate franchise or income tax, 2026

> **General reference only.** Not accountant approved. Circular 230 section 10.37: apply the cited law to verified facts, state assumptions and unresolved issues, and obtain professional review before filing or acting.

## Scope

Wisconsin (US-WI) chapter 71 corporate franchise/income tax, ordinary separate-return computation, combined-group boundary, economic development surcharge and actual 2026 estimates. The DOR corporate FAQ states law as of November 5, 2026. Final 2026 Form 4 instructions have not been confirmed; published 2025 boxes/procedures below remain the **2025-2026 filing cycle**. The 2026 Corp-ES instructions apply to years beginning in 2026.

Federal calculations/numerical rules belong in `us-form-1120-c-corp`. Use `wi-income-tax` for individual owners, `wi-payroll` for employee withholding/UI and `wisconsin-sales-tax` for sales/use tax. Specialized entities and combined groups need their own calculations beyond these routing gates.

## Ask the client first

- What entity, federal tax classification, Wisconsin incorporation/licensing and year-end apply, including first/final/short years?
- What property, people, sales, services, intangible receipts or partnership/disregarded interests connect it to Wisconsin?
- What ownership, unitary operations and foreign entities determine combined reporting or an election?
- What federal income before NOL/special deductions, Wisconsin adjustments, asset bases, related expenses and state loss records are supported?
- What Wisconsin/total receipts, allocated items and gross receipts from all activities determine the factor and surcharge?
- What prior-year liability, credits, estimates, extensions, payments and notices apply?

## The method, step by step

### 1. Choose tax and return

Wisconsin has franchise and income tax but imposes only one against a corporation for a taxable year. Domestic corporations with nonexempt income and foreign corporations doing business generally pay franchise tax. Certain foreign corporations not subject to franchise tax but owning Wisconsin property or exclusively in interstate/foreign commerce pay income tax. Both rates are 7.9%. U.S. obligation income is included for franchise tax and excluded for income tax. Federal-law protections and statutory exemptions require facts. [DOR questions 2-4](https://www.revenue.wi.gov/Pages/faqs/ise-crpginfo.aspx).

Wisconsin-incorporated/licensed corporations generally file even if no business was transacted; unlicensed corporations can have nexus through activities. Corporate-classified LLCs and corporate partners need testing. A disregarded single-owner entity does not file its own Form 4; its owner files if required. Completely inactive corporations need applicable Form 4H treatment before assuming no return. [DOR questions 3-4](https://www.revenue.wi.gov/Pages/faqs/ise-crpginfo.aspx).

The cycle-specific Form 4 instructions list nexus through premises, real estate, inventory, employees/representatives, Wisconsin-benefited services, intangible receipts, secured loans and partnership/LLC interests. Their regular-activity examples use fifteen person-days, including combinations of people/days, excluding travel/weekends unless business occurred. P.L. 86-272 can protect qualifying tangible-goods solicitation; it is not a universal services/intangibles exemption. [Form 4 pp.2-4](https://www.revenue.wi.gov/TaxForms2025/2025-Form4-Inst.pdf).

### 2. Screen combined reporting first

The 2025-2026 filing-cycle instructions describe three combined-group conditions: common control, unitary business and inclusion under water's-edge rules. Common control generally requires more than 50% voting power including attribution, rather than exactly 50% or a federal consolidated-return percentage. Integration and flows of value determine unitary operations. A controlled-group election generally binds ten years. Tax-option S corporations, REITs, RICs, REMICs and FASITs are excluded from that combined group. Resolve the gate before using separate Form 4. Federal consolidation does not establish the Wisconsin election. [Form 4 pp.5-8](https://www.revenue.wi.gov/TaxForms2025/2025-Form4-Inst.pdf).

### 3. Reconcile base and apportionment

In the **2025-2026 filing-cycle** map, Form 4 line 1 starts with federal taxable income before NOL/special deductions, ordinarily federal Form 1120 line 28. Line 2 adds Schedule 4V adjustments, line 4 subtracts Schedule 4W adjustments. Remove nonapportionable/separately apportioned items on line 6/Form N before applying the factor, then restore Wisconsin allocated income on line 10. [Form 4 pp.25-27](https://www.revenue.wi.gov/TaxForms2025/2025-Form4-Inst.pdf).

Reconcile asset basis/depreciation, conformity, state taxes, dividends and related-entity expenses through the appropriate schedules. Related interest, rent, management and intangible expenses require an addition and, if deductibility tests are met, a subtraction. Schedule RT is generally required when relevant post-apportionment expenses exceed $100,000. Below that disclosure threshold, substantive adjustment rules still apply. [Form 4 p.25](https://www.revenue.wi.gov/TaxForms2025/2025-Form4-Inst.pdf).

Most nonspecialized unitary corporations with nexus in Wisconsin and another state/foreign country use single sales factor: Wisconsin sales divided by total sales under Schedule A-01 sourcing. Corporate partners normally include their share of partnership/LLC numerator/denominator, subject to the passive/nonoperational investment exception. Carriers, financial institutions, telecommunications, insurers and other listed industries use special schedules. Unitary separate accounting requires DOR approval; nonunitary business may require separate allocation. [Form 4 pp.20-22](https://www.revenue.wi.gov/TaxForms2025/2025-Form4-Inst.pdf).

Reconcile modified income, the apportionable pool, factor and Wisconsin allocated items. Form 4 line 8 reports factor percentage to four decimal places. Line 14 uses allowable Wisconsin loss carryforward from Form 4BL, limited to line 13 income; never substitute federal NOL. Line 15 is Wisconsin net income after state loss. The current FAQ says state net business losses may carry forward up to twenty years, with records retained through use; vintage/group/ownership limits require their own schedules. [Form 4 pp.26-27](https://www.revenue.wi.gov/TaxForms2025/2025-Form4-Inst.pdf); [DOR question 14](https://www.revenue.wi.gov/Pages/faqs/ise-crpginfo.aspx).

### 4. Rates, credits and surcharge

| Item / condition | Rate, amount or threshold | Official source |
|---|---|---|
| Corporate franchise/income tax | 7.9% of Wisconsin net income | https://www.revenue.wi.gov/Pages/faqs/ise-crpginfo.aspx |
| Economic development surcharge gate | Gross receipts from all activities at least $4 million, subject to exclusions | https://www.revenue.wi.gov/Pages/faqs/ise-crpginfo.aspx |
| Surcharge when subject | 3% of gross tax before credits, minimum $25, maximum $9,800 | https://www.revenue.wi.gov/TaxForms2025/2025-Form4-Inst.pdf |
| 2026 estimate trigger | Net tax plus surcharge at least $500 | https://www.revenue.wi.gov/TaxForms2026/2026-Form-Corp-ES-inst.pdf |
| 2026 estimate exception | Twelve-month 2025 year with no tax liability, and 2026 net income less than $250,000 | https://www.revenue.wi.gov/TaxForms2026/2026-Form-Corp-ES-inst.pdf |
| Prior-year estimate alternative | 100% of qualifying twelve-month 2025 tax, only below $250,000 Wisconsin net income | https://www.revenue.wi.gov/TaxForms2026/2026-Form-Corp-ES-inst.pdf |

In the cycle-specific map, line 16 is 7.9% of line 15, with applicable industry adjustments before line 18 gross tax. Schedule CR nonrefundable credits enter line 19; line 20 net tax is floored at zero. Line 21 surcharge uses line 18 **before credits**. Credit ordering/carryforwards require their schedules. [Form 4 pp.27-28](https://www.revenue.wi.gov/TaxForms2025/2025-Form4-Inst.pdf).

The surcharge receipts test includes gross sales, dividends, interest, rents, royalties, gross disposition prices and relevant pass-through receipts included for Wisconsin purposes. It is not just Wisconsin sales or net income. The FAQ excludes domestic corporations without Wisconsin business activity, foreign corporations without Wisconsin nexus (with a combined-group caveat), and nuclear decommissioning trusts. A zero-income-tax year still requires testing the surcharge minimum. [DOR question 9](https://www.revenue.wi.gov/Pages/faqs/ise-crpginfo.aspx); [Form 4 p.5](https://www.revenue.wi.gov/TaxForms2025/2025-Form4-Inst.pdf).

### 5. Actual 2026 estimated payments

The trigger includes net tax plus surcharge. The exception requires all three conditions: a twelve-month 2025 year, no 2025 liability and 2026 net income less than $250,000. Equality fails the exception. Exempt corporations with unrelated business income use Corp-ES; exempt trusts follow the specified 1-ES rule. [2026 Corp-ES](https://www.revenue.wi.gov/TaxForms2026/2026-Form-Corp-ES-inst.pdf).

Ordinary calendar-year installments are April 15, June 15, September 15 and December 15, 2026. Fiscal years generally use the fifteenth of the fourth, sixth, ninth and twelfth months; a fiscal year beginning in April has its first installment in the third month, June 15. Weekend/legal-holiday deadlines move to the next business day. [2026 Corp-ES p.1](https://www.revenue.wi.gov/TaxForms2026/2026-Form-Corp-ES-inst.pdf).

For calendar-year liability first met after February 28 but before June 1, June includes one-half the annual amount; after May 31 but before September 1, September includes three-quarters; after August 31 December includes all. A new corporation's first return is treated as twelve months for installment-date purposes. The official March-start example has June/September/December installments at one-half, one-quarter and one-quarter. [2026 Corp-ES p.1](https://www.revenue.wi.gov/TaxForms2026/2026-Form-Corp-ES-inst.pdf).

A short year of one month or less has no estimated installment. The source gives one, two, three and four installments for two-three, four-six, seven-nine and ten-eleven-month short years, with its fourth/sixth/ninth/last-month date table and April-start exception. Do not divide every short/new year's liability by four. [2026 Corp-ES p.1](https://www.revenue.wi.gov/TaxForms2026/2026-Form-Corp-ES-inst.pdf).

Required-payment alternatives include 90% of current tax/surcharge, the qualifying prior-year method below $250,000 Wisconsin net income, and 90% under annualization or adjusted seasonal methods. Apply the source's income/apportionment conditions and Form U; do not import federal individual safe harbours. Recompute after substantial changes and retain required cumulative payments versus actual payment dates. [2026 Corp-ES pp.2-3](https://www.revenue.wi.gov/TaxForms2026/2026-Form-Corp-ES-inst.pdf).

## Worked cases

### Case 1: surcharge before credits and threshold decision

An invented separate Wisconsin corporation has $400,000 reconciled Wisconsin net income, $2,000 allowable credits, no special-industry rules and $5 million gross receipts. Gross tax is $31,600; surcharge is $948 on gross tax; net tax is $29,600, totaling $30,548 before payments. An alternative with receipts below $4 million and no other trigger has zero surcharge and $29,600 total. Numeric flags below are documented scenario facts, not an automatic legal determination.

```oa-check
name: Wisconsin surcharge before credits
inputs:
  wi_net_income: 400000
  credit: 2000
  surcharge_applies: 1
  below_threshold_applies: 0
steps:
  - gross_tax: wi_net_income * 0.079
  - net_tax: max(gross_tax - credit, 0)
  - surcharge: min(max(gross_tax * 0.03, 25), 9800) * surcharge_applies
  - alternative_surcharge: min(max(gross_tax * 0.03, 25), 9800) * below_threshold_applies
  - total: net_tax + surcharge
  - alternative_total: net_tax + alternative_surcharge
expected:
  gross_tax: 31600
  net_tax: 29600
  surcharge: 948
  total: 30548
  alternative_total: 29600
```

### Case 2: new corporation starts March 1

An invented calendar-year corporation begins Wisconsin operations March 1, 2026 with a $4,000 required estimated tax/surcharge obligation, no prior payment and no exception. The 2026 rule gives $2,000 due June 15 and $1,000 each September 15 and December 15. Paying only $1,000 in June leaves a $1,000 installment shortfall even if later payments reach the annual total.

```oa-check
name: Wisconsin new corporation installments
inputs:
  annual_required: 4000
  june_paid_wrong: 1000
steps:
  - june_due: annual_required * 0.5
  - september_due: annual_required * 0.25
  - december_due: annual_required * 0.25
  - june_shortfall: max(june_due - june_paid_wrong, 0)
  - total: june_due + september_due + december_due
expected:
  june_due: 2000
  september_due: 1000
  december_due: 1000
  june_shortfall: 1000
  total: 4000
```

## Filing and payment

The November 2026 FAQ says returns generally follow federal due dates, ordinarily the fifteenth of the fourth month after year-end. The retained **2025-2026 filing-cycle** instructions separately specify a June 30 fiscal-year third-month exception. Use the actual year's calendar for June 2026/later year-ends rather than silently carrying that older exception forward. Form 4T has its separate fifth-month date; federal short returns need corresponding state handling. [DOR question 7](https://www.revenue.wi.gov/Pages/faqs/ise-crpginfo.aspx); [Form 4 p.9](https://www.revenue.wi.gov/TaxForms2025/2025-Form4-Inst.pdf).

A federal extension extends Wisconsin to thirty days after the federal extended date; include a copy with the filed return. Without a federal extension, the FAQ gives an automatic seven-month state extension or until the original corresponding federal due date, whichever is later. Payment is not extended; use Corp-ES by the unextended payment date. Check disaster relief separately. [DOR question 8](https://www.revenue.wi.gov/Pages/faqs/ise-crpginfo.aspx); [2026 Corp-ES](https://www.revenue.wi.gov/TaxForms2026/2026-Form-Corp-ES-inst.pdf).

Corporate returns must be electronic through federal/state e-file unless DOR approved a waiver; paper without a waiver is returned. MTA can arrange payment, which is not an accepted return. Preserve acceptance, reconciliation and separate payment confirmation. Permitted paper payments use current C-PV/Corp-ES instructions. Final returns need the final indicator and applicable liquidation documents; they do not close all tax accounts. [DOR questions 6,12-13](https://www.revenue.wi.gov/Pages/faqs/ise-crpginfo.aspx).

Amend with the appropriate return checkbox, Schedule AR and corrected support. Federal amended returns affecting Wisconsin tax/credit/loss generally require a state amendment within 180 days after filing; final IRS adjustments affecting Wisconsin must be reported within 180 days after finality. Keep asset/loss/credit records through applicable use/assessment periods. [DOR questions 10,14](https://www.revenue.wi.gov/Pages/faqs/ise-crpginfo.aspx); [Form 4 p.26](https://www.revenue.wi.gov/TaxForms2025/2025-Form4-Inst.pdf).

The 2026 Corp-ES instructions distinguish installment underpayment interest from final unpaid balances. They describe 12% annual interest during applicable extension periods, 18% on delinquent tax/surcharge and a stated 12% condition for certain timely-return extension situations. Apply exact timing/return facts, using Form U for estimates. Full late-filing/negligence/fraud penalties remain unconfirmed. [2026 Corp-ES pp.2-3](https://www.revenue.wi.gov/TaxForms2026/2026-Form-Corp-ES-inst.pdf).

## Completion checklist

- Establish classification, nexus, year and separate/combined return first.
