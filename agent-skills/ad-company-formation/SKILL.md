# Andorra Company Formation & Entity Choice

The laws below are read from the consolidated texts on the Portal Jurídic d'Andorra, which are marked "Text refós sense caràcter oficial". The BOPA text governs. This skill covers forming and keeping an Andorran company. The tax computations are in the domain skills it links to.

**Quick answer (2026).** SL (or SLU with one member): minimum capital EUR 3,000. SA: minimum capital EUR 60,000. Both paid in full on subscription. A foreign investor who will hold more than 10% needs prior authorisation from the ministry before the deed; the ministry has two months (one extension of up to half that), and silence means refusal. The notary sends the deed to the Registre de Societats within 15 days and the registry decides within 15 days. The company exists from registration. Company tax is 10%. Ask first: who owns it and where they live, the foreign percentage, the capital available, the activity, and whether directors will be paid.

## Scope

- **Covered.** Forming a Societat de Responsabilitat Limitada (SL or SLU) or a Societat Anònima (SA) under Llei 20/2007; the foreign-investment authorisation or declaration under Llei 10/2012; tax and CASS registration; the yearly company-law and tax filings that follow; dissolution causes.
- **Who it is for.** Founders (resident or not), foreign investors buying into or setting up an Andorran company or branch, and their advisers.
- **Not covered here.** The tax computations (`ad-corporate-income-tax`, `andorra-igi`, `ad-payroll-social`, `ad-tax-overview`); immigration and residence permits; sector licences and regulated financial entities; notary fees (not read); reorganisations and company migrations.

## Ask the client first

- Who will own the company, and is each owner an Andorran resident with at least three years of uninterrupted residence since the immigration permit? (Decides whether the investment is foreign.)
- What percentage of capital or votes will each foreign owner hold, directly or indirectly? (More than 10% needs prior authorisation.)
- SL or SA, and how much capital is available now, in cash or in kind?
- What activity will the company carry on? (The registry approves the activities that form the object.)
- When was the company registered, will the directors be paid, what was their net income last year, and do they have other self-employed activities or sole-member/family holdings? (Decides the CASS base.)
- Expected turnover, assets and staff, and the year end. (Decide IGI frequency, audit and filing dates.)

### Step 1. Choose the form

- **Andorran corporate forms overview.** Llei 20/2007 sets one regime for both main company forms: the Societat Anònima (SA) and the Societat de Responsabilitat Limitada (SL) _(Llei 20/2007, preamble: "una disciplina única per a la societat anònima i per a la societat de responsabilitat limitada")_. The company name must carry "SA" or "SL" _(art 2(1))_.
- **Societat Limitada (SL/SLU).** Limited-liability company with its capital divided into participacions _(art 15(1))_. A company with a single member is a "societat unipersonal" and adds "unipersonal", or a "U" at the end of the abbreviation (SLU) _(art 2(2))_. If a company becomes single-member later, the sole member has one month to apply to register that fact. If it is not registered in time, the sole member becomes personally, jointly and without limit liable with the company for the company's debts incurred while it was single-member; debts after registration do not attach to the member _(art 2(2))_.
- **Societat Anònima (SA).** Company with its capital divided into accions _(art 15(1))_.
- **Minimum share capital, SL/SLU.** EUR 3,000 _(art 14(1))_. A notary may not authorise a deed of incorporation below the minimum _(art 14(2))_.
- **Minimum share capital, SA.** EUR 60,000 _(art 14(1))_.
- **Which to pick.** The law sets the capital floor, not a use case. Where the founders cannot put in EUR 60,000 in full on day one, only the SL is open to them (worked case 1). Which form is more common in practice is not from an official source (see `Not yet confirmed:`).

### Step 2. Check the foreign-investment position

- **Foreign ownership.** Llei 10/2012 liberalised foreign investment in full, with prior authorisation kept for certain cases _(Llei 10/2012, preamble: "es preveu una liberalització total de cadascuna")_. A foreign investor may hold 100% of an Andorran company once authorised. Exception: a foreign credit or financial entity investing in an Andorran non-financial entity may not exceed the holding the financial-system rules allow _(art 10(4))_.
- **Who is a foreign investor.** Among others: individuals not resident in Andorra; foreign legal persons; and Andorran companies in which foreign holders own 50% or more of the capital or voting rights. For these purposes a holding is foreign if its holder is not resident, or has fewer than three years of uninterrupted residence since the immigration permit _(Llei 10/2012, art 1(1) and (2))_.
- **Foreign-investment authorisation.** Prior authorisation from the ministry is needed for a direct investment that leaves the investor holding more than 10% of the capital or voting rights of an Andorran company. A holding of 10% or less needs no prior authorisation but must be declared afterwards to the Registre d'Inversions Estrangeres _(Llei 10/2012, art 10(1))_. Setting up or enlarging a branch or other permanent establishment also needs prior authorisation _(art 10(2))_.
- **After authorisation.** Once authorised, these also need prior authorisation: a real change of the company object, a capital increase not charged to voluntary reserves, and a rise in the foreign percentage where foreign holders already have 50% or more _(art 10(5))_.
- **Procedure.** The application goes to the Govern _(art 18(1))_. The ministry must give a reasoned decision within two months; it may extend that once, by no more than half the original period. No decision by the end of the period, including any extension, means the authorisation is refused _(Llei 10/2012, art 18(3))_. For a foreign credit or financial entity, the ministry first needs a report from the Autoritat Financera Andorrana _(art 18(2))_.

### Step 3. Reserve the name and the activities

- **Company-name reservation.** The Registre de Societats Mercantils approves the name and the business activities that will form the company's object, and issues the name-reservation certificate _(Llei 20/2007, art 2(3))_. The certificate binds all later registry steps until the company is registered _(art 2(5))_. A name identical to an existing one cannot be used _(art 2(4))_.

### Step 4. Pay in the capital

- **Paid in full.** Every share and participation must be subscribed at least at nominal value, and the nominal value must be paid in full by an actual contribution to the company _(art 15(2))_. Contributions may be in cash or in kind; work and services cannot be contributed _(art 17)_.
- **Contributions in kind.** The deed describes each non-cash contribution and its value in euros, with an independent expert's report where required, and states the number and nominal value of the shares or participations each contributor takes _(art 18(1))_. No expert report is needed for transferable securities or money-market instruments valued at the one-year weighted average price, for assets contributed to an SL with prior-year income of EUR 600,000 or less that has deposited its accounts, or for technology equipment up to EUR 3,000 in total and at most 12 months old with its purchase invoices _(art 18(2))_. A required report must be dated within the year before the contribution _(art 18(3))_.

### Step 5. Sign the deed and register

- **Incorporation steps.** (1) Name reservation and approval of the activities. (2) Foreign-investment authorisation where needed. (3) Payment of the capital in full. (4) Deed of incorporation signed before an Andorran notary, who must check the legal requirements and attach the name certificate _(art 7(1)-(2))_. (5) The notary sends a copy of the deed to the Registre de Societats within 15 days of signing _(art 7(2))_. (6) Registration: the company gets legal personality on registration _(art 7(3))_. (7) Tax registration (see below) and CASS registration before hiring. The registry fee is deposited with the notary at signing _(fourth additional provision)_.
- **Statutory time limits.** The ministry must decide a foreign-investment application within two months; it may extend that once, by no more than half the original period. No decision by the end of the period, including any extension, means the authorisation is refused _(Llei 10/2012, art 18(3))_. The Registre de Societats must register or refuse within 15 days of filing; no decision in time counts as a tacit refusal for appeal purposes _(Llei 20/2007, art 6 bis(1)-(2))_. These periods are counted in calendar days _(art 6 bis(5))_.
- **Refusal.** A refusal and its reason are notified to the parties and the notary; it can be appealed administratively and then in the courts, and the notary may also appeal _(art 6 bis(3)-(4))_. The minister refuses registration where the law's requirements are not met _(art 7(3))_.
- **Acts before registration.** Members and directors are jointly and without limit liable for acts done in the company's name before registration, unless the company takes them over once registered _(Llei 20/2007, art 12)_.

### Step 6. Register for tax and CASS

- **Tax registration (NRT).** A company subject to company tax must enrol in the Registre d'Empresaris i Professionals del Cens d'Obligats Tributaris _(Llei 95/2010 IS, art 47)_. The tax register number (número de registre tributari, NRT) can be revoked on provisional deregistration _(IS arts 48, 52)_. Provisional deregistration follows debts declared uncollectable, or no returns for two consecutive periods after a one-month warning; it is published in the BOPA and noted in the company registry, and the NRT is restored only once the company is up to date _(IS arts 48 to 52)_.
- **Accounting basis.** Company tax starts from the accounting result under Llei 30/2007 de la comptabilitat dels empresaris and the Pla general de comptabilitat _(IS art 9(3))_.
- **CASS.** Register before hiring; see `ad-payroll-social` for registration and the contribution bases.

## Worked cases

### 1. SL or SA with EUR 20,000 of cash (a decision)

Three founders have EUR 20,000 between them, all in cash. An SL needs EUR 3,000 paid in full, so they can form an SL today and keep EUR 17,000 in the company or in hand. An SA needs EUR 60,000 paid in full, so they are EUR 40,000 short. With this cash only the SL is open. If the three take equal stakes at the minimum, each pays EUR 1,000.

```oa-check
name: Andorra SL or SA, capital headroom with EUR 20,000
inputs:
  cash_available: 20000
  sl_minimum: 3000
  sa_minimum: 60000
  founders: 3
steps:
  sl_headroom: cash_available - sl_minimum
  sa_shortfall: max(0, sa_minimum - cash_available)
  sl_minimum_each: sl_minimum / founders
expected:
  sl_headroom: 17000
  sa_shortfall: 40000
  sl_minimum_each: 1000
```

### 2. Year-two cash calls for a calendar-year SL

An SL with a 31 December year end had a 2025 company-tax liquidation quota of EUR 18,000. In September 2026 (the ninth month of the 2026 period) it pays 50% of that on account: EUR 9,000. Its 2025 accounts were deposited four months after the deadline, so the art 106(2.2)(b) fine of EUR 1,000 applies (more than three months, not more than twelve). The cash out for these two items is EUR 10,000.

```oa-check
name: Andorra SL payment on account and late-deposit fine
inputs:
  prior_liquidation_quota: 18000
  payment_on_account_rate: 0.50
  late_deposit_fine: 1000
steps:
  payment_on_account: prior_liquidation_quota * payment_on_account_rate
  total_cash: payment_on_account + late_deposit_fine
expected:
  payment_on_account: 9000
  total_cash: 10000
```

## Filing and payment

- **Annual accounts.** The board must prepare and sign the annual accounts (balance sheet, profit and loss, statement of changes in equity, cash-flow statement and notes) and the proposal for the result within six months of the year end _(Llei 20/2007, art 71(1)-(2))_.
- **Annual general meeting.** One ordinary general meeting a year, within the period set by the statutes, which may not exceed six months from the start of the new financial year _(art 30(1)-(2))_.
- **Audit.** Required where, for two consecutive years, at least two of these apply: total assets over EUR 3,600,000; annual turnover over EUR 6,000,000; more than 50 employees. Also required where turnover exceeds EUR 10,000,000 for two consecutive years _(art 72(1))_. The general meeting appoints the auditor _(art 72(2))_.
- **Deposit of accounts.** The approved accounts, the resolution on the result and any audit report are filed with the registry as set by regulation _(art 74(1))_. Failure is noted and published by the registry _(art 74(2))_. Late deposit: EUR 500 fine up to three months late, EUR 1,000 over three months, EUR 2,000 over twelve months _(art 106(2.2))_.
- **Beneficial owners.** The company keeps a register of its beneficial owners and provides it to the Registre de Societats as regulation sets out, so the registry always holds current information _(Llei 20/2007, art 21 bis(1), (3))_. Fines: EUR 2,500 for not filing the beneficial-owner declaration in time or not updating it after a change; EUR 5,000 for incomplete or plainly incorrect information; EUR 15,000 for false information or hiding the beneficial owner _(art 106(2.1))_.
- **Company tax (IS).** 10% general rate _(IS art 41(1))_. Payment on account in the ninth month of the tax period, 50% of the previous year's liquidation tax _(IS art 45)_; none is due in the first year of activity _(IS Regulation 2023 art 37(3))_. Return in the month after the six months that follow the year end: July for a 31 December year end _(IS Regulation 2023, art 45(1))_. Detail: `ad-corporate-income-tax`.
- **IGI returns.** Frequency by the previous year's net turnover: below EUR 250,000 in July and January; below EUR 3,600,000 in April, July, October and January; EUR 3,600,000 or more monthly. A business in its first year files in April, July, October and January _(Llei 11/2012 IGI, art 78)_, or in July and January if the simplified regime applies _(same)_. Detail: `andorra-igi`.
- **CASS.** Contributions are settled by calendar month and paid within 15 calendar days after the month ends _(Llei 17/2008, art 96(1)-(2))_. Employer 15.5% and employee 6.5% _(arts 100, 103)_. A director who contributes as self-employed qualifies for the 25% base during the first 12 months from company registration _(art 101(2)(a)(v))_; if there are several self-employed activities, all must meet that condition _(art 101(5))_. Outside that start period, no pay for the role or prior-year net income under EUR 6,000 can qualify, subject to the additional turnover conditions for sole-member and family companies _(art 101(2)(a)(i)-(iii))_. Apply to CASS for the reduced base _(art 101(7))_. Whether a director counts as employed or self-employed for CASS is in `ad-payroll-social`.
- **Dissolution.** The general meeting must resolve to dissolve when, among other causes, net equity falls below half of share capital and is not remedied _(Llei 20/2007, art 85(5)(c))_; directors must call the meeting within two months of the cause, or become jointly liable for later debts _(art 86(2), (4))_. Since Llei 17/2026, a company found after an adversarial file to be an empty structure with no real business may be dissolved administratively without liquidation by ministerial resolution, published in the BOPA _(arts 85(6), 86(6))_. Late deposit fines do not stop that procedure _(art 106(2.3))_.
