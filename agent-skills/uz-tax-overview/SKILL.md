# Uzbekistan Tax Overview v0.1

> **General reference only.** This is a headline rates card and routing aid, not a complete return, regime election or professional approval. Establish the actual taxpayer, period, income/activity and regime before using a rate. Figures are UZS (Uzbekistani soum); do not substitute a neighbouring country's tax system.

## Who this applies to

Use to identify the relevant Uzbekistan tax job for individuals, companies and employers in 2026. The Tax Committee administers the taxes; the taxpayer's tax cabinet is used for its registrations, reporting and account history. Tax year is the calendar year; tax-specific reporting periods differ. [Tax Code, system and period provisions](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/4674902).

For an individual, determine residence and source. The ordinary presence test is **more than 183 calendar days** in a consecutive **12-month period starting or ending in the tax year**, not simply “183+ days ending in the year” (figures in words on the original). The Code also has an alternative shorter-presence rule where Uzbekistan exceeds every other country's presence, early recognition on evidence, special absence/status rules and treaty provisions. Do not make a final residence decision from a single undifferentiated day count. Residents ordinarily have worldwide-income scope; nonresidents have Uzbek-source scope, subject to exemptions and treaty rules. [Tax Code, arts. 30, 364, 366 and 368](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/4674902).

## The base

PIT, corporate profit, turnover and VAT do not share a tax base. First separate employment, business, investment, foreign-source and non-income transfers. For a company, profit tax ordinarily starts with Code income less allowable documented expenses; turnover tax starts with its own revenue measure. VAT concerns the taxable supply/import and credit conditions, not every incoming payment. Employer social tax is a separate employer-funded obligation.

## Deadlines and filing

For tax filing, payment and notice deadlines expressed in days, count working days unless the provision expressly says calendar days. If the final date is a statutory nonworking day, apply the next-working-day rule. [Tax Code, art. 5](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/4674902).

Use the calendar in the table only after confirming the obligation. Source withholding does not create a universal exemption from an annual declaration. Article 393 has specific exceptions and requires a foreign individual who is resident or becomes resident by 1 April of the current year to file, irrespective of its first two parts; an ordinary company's return calendar is not the monthly payroll calendar. Retrieve the tax cabinet's current forms, registration, earlier submissions and payment history. Reconcile the draft to books/source records, retain the filed return and acceptance, and reconcile payments to the tax account. Current form-field mappings are not invented here. Apply the Code's nonworking-day rule and any applicable special filing provision.

## Penalties and interest

Do not infer fines from the headline rates. Late-payment interest, failure to file, failure to withhold and understatement are different matters. Route the actual violation, dates, tax debt and payments to the relevant skill and current law; the overview does not quote a fixed penalty rate or imply that a disclaimer cures a missed deadline.

## Cross-border points

Individual residence and corporate residence/PE are different tests. Residents' worldwide scope, nonresident source rules, statutory exemptions, beneficial entitlement and treaty conditions must be established before a cross-border calculation. A foreign payee, bank account, invoice currency or number of days alone does not settle the result. The special foreign-citizen regime is a separate election/approval analysis covered by the income-tax skill.

## Two worked examples that recompute

### Example 1: headline salary and employer cost

Assume ordinary resident employment gross UZS 5,000,000, no deduction/relief and a standard employer; social-tax base also UZS 5,000,000. PIT allocations within withholding do not create an extra employee charge. [Tax Code, arts. 381, 405 and 407](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/4674902).

```oa-check
name: uz-overview-salary
inputs:
  gross: 5000000
  pit_rate: 0.12
  employer_rate: 0.12
steps:
  pit: gross * pit_rate
  net: gross - pit
  employer_social: gross * employer_rate
  employer_cost: gross + employer_social
expected:
  pit: 600000
  net: 4400000
  employer_social: 600000
  employer_cost: 5600000
```

Employee net is UZS 4,400,000; employer cash cost is UZS 5,600,000. Employer social tax is not subtracted again from net pay.

### Example 2: corporate profit differs from VAT turnover

Assume an ordinary general-regime company has UZS 100,000,000 net domestic standard-rated sales, UZS 60,000,000 allowable expenses and no other adjustments/reliefs. Ignore input VAT solely to isolate the two different bases. [Tax Code, arts. 258, 295, 305 and 337](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/4674902).

```oa-check
name: uz-overview-different-bases
inputs:
  net_sales: 100000000
  allowable_expenses: 60000000
  cit_rate: 0.15
  vat_rate: 0.12
steps:
  profit: net_sales - allowable_expenses
  cit: profit * cit_rate
  output_vat: net_sales * vat_rate
  gross_invoice: net_sales + output_vat
expected:
  profit: 40000000
  cit: 6000000
  output_vat: 12000000
  gross_invoice: 112000000
```

CIT is UZS 6,000,000 on profit; output VAT is UZS 12,000,000 on the net supply. VAT payable still needs the actual allowable credits and adjustments.

## Ask the client first

Confirm jurisdiction/currency; person/company/employer role; exact tax and payment dates; residence/source/PE; activity and sector; gross versus net amounts; actual tax regime and election history; turnover/crossings; exemptions/deductions and evidence; previous returns, withholding and payments. If these facts are missing, route and flag the gap instead of choosing the lowest headline rate.
