# Greenland mandatory pension: 2026 minimum working paper

**Built from law. Not yet approved.** This Guide prepares a bounded statutory-minimum working paper. It does not establish full Greenland coverage, professional attestation or authority to file.

## Scope

Use **9% for ordinary 2026 savings bases**, not the older consolidation's printed 10% trajectory: the May amendment ends the annual rise at 9% in 2025, with effect from 2026 onward. Qualifying self-employment profit has a separate 6% rule. Establish the person's liability, base and contribution measurement before computing a minimum. [Amendment 19, ss 1–2](https://nalunaarutit.gl/groenlandsk-lovgivning/2026/inatsisartutlov-nr-19-af-29_05_2026?sc_lang=da); [consolidation 67, s 3](https://nalunaarutit.gl/groenlandsk-lovgivning/2025/selvstyrets-lovbekendtgoerelse-nr-67-af-25_09_2025?sc_lang=da).

## Ask the client first

Obtain date of birth and applicable pension age; full-tax-liability dates; A-income, B-income and statutory additions; each excluded-income category; self-employment profit; civil-service benefits and how they are earned; pension-provider domicile and regulation; any foreign-scheme permission; employer and personal contributions; tax treatment and pre-tax conversion evidence; prior credited contributions and any historical shortfall notice. Reconcile income records to pension statements. Contractual pension promises are a separate question.

## The method, step by step

### 1. Establish personal and scheme eligibility

Section 1 applies to persons aged 18 and fully taxable in Greenland, until their pension age under the old-age-pension law. Contributions must start no later than the income year after the 18th birthday; the person may stop four years before the income year in which pension age is reached. Confirm the actual dates and applicable pension age before applying this rule; this Guide does not assume a universal pension age. [Section 1(1)–(2)](https://nalunaarutit.gl/groenlandsk-lovgivning/2025/selvstyrets-lovbekendtgoerelse-nr-67-af-25_09_2025?sc_lang=da).

Ordinary eligible arrangements are the specified Greenland/Danish pension funds or life-insurance companies, qualifying banks domiciled or established in Greenland, and the named CS/HKKF funds, subject to the statutory regulatory conditions. A foreign bank account is not automatically eligible. A temporary foreign-scheme permission must actually be granted: section 1(4) concerns Norway, the Faroes, Iceland or EU schemes, an application within three months of full-tax liability, a pre-existing scheme connected with the employment move, an expected stay of at most two income years and permission for at most two income years. Stop if those facts or the permission are missing. [Section 1(3)–(4)](https://nalunaarutit.gl/groenlandsk-lovgivning/2025/selvstyrets-lovbekendtgoerelse-nr-67-af-25_09_2025?sc_lang=da).

### 2. Establish the savings base

Start with A- and B-income and the additions cross-referenced to income-law sections 41(2) and 47b(6). Classify all exclusions under section 2(2)–(4), rather than treating gross wages or taxable income as the base automatically. The exclusions cover the specified civil-service payments, disability support, child-related payments, public assistance, old-age/disability pensions, housing and education support and listed municipal allowances subject to their exceptions; parental/public-maintenance support and other pension payments also require the statutory classification. Use the actual provision and records for each component. [Section 2](https://nalunaarutit.gl/groenlandsk-lovgivning/2025/selvstyrets-lovbekendtgoerelse-nr-67-af-25_09_2025?sc_lang=da).

From the 2026 amendment, section 2(4) excludes benefits a civil servant earns as part of the employment. Do not continue using the older wording about benefits used to determine the civil-service pension. Exact civil-service classification, statutory additions and income exclusions must be settled before the numerical cases apply. [Amendment 19, s 1(1) and s 2](https://nalunaarutit.gl/groenlandsk-lovgivning/2026/inatsisartutlov-nr-19-af-29_05_2026?sc_lang=da).

### 3. Apply the applicable rate to the established base

For an ordinary base between the taper endpoints, compute `rate = 0.09 × (base − 125000) / 50000`, then `minimum = base × rate`. At the lower endpoint the rate is zero; at or above the upper endpoint it is the full ordinary rate. The taper changes the percentage on the whole base; it is not 9% of only the excess. The cases below do not decide eligibility or the base. [Section 3(1), (3), amended](https://nalunaarutit.gl/groenlandsk-lovgivning/2025/selvstyrets-lovbekendtgoerelse-nr-67-af-25_09_2025?sc_lang=da); [amendment 19](https://nalunaarutit.gl/groenlandsk-lovgivning/2026/inatsisartutlov-nr-19-af-29_05_2026?sc_lang=da).

Self-employment uses taxable business profit and section 3(2), (4), not an automatic employment-base percentage. The full rate is 6%, with its own linear taper between the stated endpoints. Stop for mixed employment/business income unless the allocation and contribution treatment have been established; this Guide supplies no combined formula. Special buyer-withholding rules may be made for fishing, hunting, sheep farming and craft proceeds under section 4 and are not computed here. [Sections 3–4](https://nalunaarutit.gl/groenlandsk-lovgivning/2025/selvstyrets-lovbekendtgoerelse-nr-67-af-25_09_2025?sc_lang=da).

### 4. Measure contributions and remaining obligation

Count only eligible contributions on the legally required basis. Section 5 requires contributions taxable on payment or non-deductible contributions to be converted to pre-tax amounts when measuring section 3 savings. Do not subtract a bank payment before establishing eligibility and the conversion. Reconcile employer and personal contributions and apply the annual cessation rule separately. Conversion factors, disputed scheme classification, mixed-income cases and interaction with historical collections are not guessed here. [Sections 3(5), 5](https://nalunaarutit.gl/groenlandsk-lovgivning/2025/selvstyrets-lovbekendtgoerelse-nr-67-af-25_09_2025?sc_lang=da).

### 5. Separate saving from collection machinery

The May amendment suspends section 7(3)–(6) for income years 2026–27 and bars the specified extra tax-card percentages/tax-bill increases when cards/bills for those years are issued. New section 7a also deals with unfulfilled differences due no later than 1 January 2026. This does not repeal the savings obligation or resolve an individual's historical account. No extra tax-card percentage is not proof that no saving is due. Retain the assessment, payment and pension statements and refer historical shortfalls for individual review. [Amendment 19, s 1(3)–(4), s 2](https://nalunaarutit.gl/groenlandsk-lovgivning/2026/inatsisartutlov-nr-19-af-29_05_2026?sc_lang=da).

Assume an eligible adult, a fully established ordinary savings base, no self-employment component, no conversion adjustment and no contributions yet credited. All original inputs and outcomes are retained.

### Case 2: contributions already established on the correct basis

Keep the original DKK 300,000 ordinary base and DKK 20,000 qualifying contributions, with the same eligibility and measurement assumptions. The statutory minimum is DKK 27,000 and remaining minimum DKK 7,000. This does not establish that a DKK 20,000 bank transfer qualifies.

```oa-check

  - remaining: max(0, minimum-qualifying_contributions)
expected:
  minimum: 27000
  remaining: 7000
```

## When to refuse or refer

Stop the computation if full-tax liability, precise start/stop ages, base exclusions, civil-service benefits, scheme eligibility, foreign permission, conversion or contribution reconciliation is unresolved. Refer mixed employment/self-employment income, special buyer withholding and historical shortfalls. Do not infer liability from a tax card alone or present a consolidation without its 2026 amendment as current authority. A qualified local reviewer must check the actual facts and current law before filing or payment; no professional attestation is claimed.

## Completion checklist

Retain the source versions, age/tax-liability decisions, classified income components, scheme evidence, conversions, credited-contribution reconciliation, computations, unresolved items and reviewer handoff. Keep contractual obligations separate from this statutory minimum. Do not mark a held fact as established.
