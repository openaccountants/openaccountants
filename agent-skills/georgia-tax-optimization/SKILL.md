# Georgia tax optimization v0.3

> **General reference only.** This Guide is general tax/accounting reference material for AI-assisted workflows. It has not been reviewed for any specific person's facts, documents, elections, deadlines, residency, filing status, or local procedures. Do not rely on it to file, pay, amend, or take a tax position without review by a qualified professional in the relevant jurisdiction.

## Scope

Prepare a comparison for Georgia the country for 2026. This Guide loads the Georgian income-tax, company-tax, VAT and payroll Guides for complete computations. The output is a planning workpaper and unresolved-facts list. It does not grant or assume a special status, replace residence/source analysis or approve a filing. No Georgian tax adviser has approved this revision.

## Ask the client first

- What legal form, status certificate and effective date, residence, work location and Georgian-source receipts apply?
- Which precise activities, customers, contracts and excluded income types exist, including consulting, trading and construction?
- What are gross income, costs and distributions for each month and both relevant years, and are wine/agro-tourism rules relevant?
- What are VAT-taxable supplies in each rolling 12-month period, registrations, stock and supporting invoices?
- Which employees, payroll amounts, prior-year income, pension duties and genuine employment facts apply?
- For international/virtual-zone status, what approval, activity experience, Georgian creation/resources, ancillary income and overlapping status history are documented?

## The method, step by step

### 1. Establish small-business eligibility and taxable receipts


**Small Business Status feature table**

| Feature | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Who | An entrepreneur natural person, that is a registered individual entrepreneur (Tax Code art. 88(1)). Article 88 sets no residence or citizenship condition; Georgian-source taxation and the actual status certificate are still required.  https://matsne.gov.ge/ka/document/view/1043717 |
| Rate | **1%** of taxable income (art. 90(1)). **3%** from the start of the month in which gross income passes GEL 500,000 (GEL 700,000 for wine tourism and agro tourism operators) until the end of that calendar year (art. 90(2))  https://matsne.gov.ge/ka/document/view/1043717 |
| Taxable income | Georgian-source income, excluding salary and the income types excluded by the Government (art. 90(3))  https://matsne.gov.ge/ka/document/view/1043717 |
| Income not taxed under the regime and not counted toward the limit | Rent of property; loan income; gambling winnings; gifts; gains on real estate, vehicles and securities; inheritance; dividends; interest; royalties; debt forgiveness; gains on selling a partner's share (Resolution 415, annex 5, items 1 to 11, including the immovable-rent clarification in Resolution 544/2016). From 1 February 2025, also income from construction services (National Classifier SEK 006-2016 group 41.2 buildings, division 42 civil engineering, division 43 specialised construction) supplied to an enterprise, an organisation or an individual entrepreneur (annex 5, item 12, added by Resolution No 436 of 23 December 2024). These do not receive the small-business rate; apply the general rules and any separate exemption. [Construction amendment](https://matsne.gov.ge/ka/document/view/6344539); [rent amendment](https://matsne.gov.ge/ka/document/view/3459221); https://matsne.gov.ge/ka/document/view/1164635 |
| Employees | Allowed. Salaries up to GEL 6,000 in total for the year are not taxed at source if the person registered as an individual entrepreneur and obtained small-business status in the same year, or had gross income of no more than GEL 50,000 the year before (art. 94(4))  https://matsne.gov.ge/ka/document/view/1043717 |
| Loss of status | If gross income exceeds GEL 500,000 (GEL 700,000 for wine and agro tourism) in each of 2 calendar years, the status ends from the start of the next year (art. 89(2)(a), (3)). It also ends for a barred activity, for 3 or more cash-register fines in a year (from the start of that year), or on the person's own request (from the next month) (art. 89(2), (3^1), (4)).  https://matsne.gov.ge/ka/document/view/1043717 |

### 2. Screen micro-business eligibility and customer effects


- **Micro Business Status eligibility and rate**: an individual with no hired staff who works independently. Gross income must not exceed GEL 30,000 in the calendar year (Tax Code art. 84(1)). The Government lists some activities to which the GEL 30,000 limit does not apply (art. 84(2); Resolution 415, annex 1). A micro business pays no income tax (art. 86). The Code says "natural person" here, not "entrepreneur natural person" as for small business (arts. 84(1), 88(1)). [Official Tax Code](https://matsne.gov.ge/ka/document/view/1043717).
- **Barred activities**: those needing a licence or permit, except the Tbilisi taxi (M1) permit; activities whose income could exceed GEL 30,000 a year; currency exchange; medical work (except a village doctor or nurse/paramedic in the state programme), architectural, advocacy or notary, audit and consulting work (including tax consultants); gambling; and trading, except processing purchased goods and supplying the resulting goods (Resolution 74/2011 replacing annex 2, item 6). Do not treat a reseller as a qualifying micro business. (Resolution 415, annex 2, as amended by Resolutions No 368 of 11 July 2018 and No 98 of 11 March 2021). [Micro trading amendment](https://matsne.gov.ge/ka/document/view/1213287); [current medical exception](https://matsne.gov.ge/ka/document/view/5120243). [Official Tax Code](https://matsne.gov.ge/ka/document/view/1043717).
- **Loss of status**: on the person's request; if a stock count shows inventory over GEL 45,000; or on VAT registration (art. 85(2)). A person who passes GEL 30,000 must apply for small business status within 15 days, or loses the status (art. 85(3)). [Official Tax Code](https://matsne.gov.ge/ka/document/view/1043717).
- **Return**: an annual return before 1 April of the following year (art. 93(1); Georgian "1 აპრილამდე", so by 31 March). [Official Tax Code](https://matsne.gov.ge/ka/document/view/1043717).
- **Effect on business customers**: a company that buys from a micro business is taxed on that cost as a cost unrelated to its business. This does not apply where the micro business is taxed under the general rule, or where a consignment note or prescribed document is issued for the supply/purchase of goods (art. 98^2(1)(c)). Expect business customers to ask for the document. [Official Tax Code](https://matsne.gov.ge/ka/document/view/1043717).

### 3. Compare VAT and ordinary taxation


- **VAT registration threshold**: a person must apply to register within 2 business days of exceeding GEL 100,000 of VAT-taxable supplies in any 12 consecutive months. VAT is due from the transaction that crosses the threshold (Tax Code art. 165(1), (2)). The rate is 18% (art. 166). Most exempt supplies are left out of the GEL 100,000 count. Exempt supplies of financial or immovable things as the main activity, exports of goods and the art. 172(1), (2) supplies are counted (art. 165(7)). Whether a service supplied to a non-resident is a Georgian taxable supply depends on the place-of-supply rules: see [georgia-vat](georgia-vat.md). [Official Tax Code](https://matsne.gov.ge/ka/document/view/1043717).
- **Standard system rates outside the special regimes**: personal income tax 20% (art. 81(1)). Profit tax 15% on distributed profit, costs unrelated to the business, free supplies and entertainment over the cap, each divided by 0.85 (arts. 97(1), (10), 98(1)). generally 5% on dividends to the article 130(1) recipient classes, subject to exceptions. Retaining ordinary trading profit alone is not a tax trigger, but nonbusiness payments and other article 97 triggers remain taxable. Detail: [ge-corporate-income-tax](ge-corporate-income-tax.md). [Official Tax Code](https://matsne.gov.ge/ka/document/view/1043717).
- **Company vs small business**: a company owner who takes profit as dividends bears 15% profit tax on the grossed-up amount plus 5% withholding. Per GEL 100 of pre-tax profit paid out, that is GEL 15 + GEL 4.25 = GEL 19.25. A small business pays 1% of gross income (up to GEL 500,000) but cannot deduct costs. For this simplified tax-only comparison, an eligible 1% small business and a fully distributing ordinary company break even at a profit margin of 1% / 19.25%, approximately 5.1948%. Low margins can favor the company tax result; retained profit creates a timing comparison. Liability, excluded activities, salary treatment, the 3% rate, VAT, pension, compliance and legal form can change the decision. Do not infer an overall recommendation from this one ratio. [Official Tax Code](https://matsne.gov.ge/ka/document/view/1043717).

### 4. Test international-company status


- **What it is**: a Georgian enterprise carrying on the Government-listed activities, subject to the permitted related-ancillary-income rule below. The Government grants the status (Tax Code art. 23(1), (2), (15)). [Tax Code](https://matsne.gov.ge/ka/document/view/1043717).
- **Profit tax**: 5% on the same distributed-profit base as other companies, grossed up by dividing by 0.95 (art. 23(9) to (11)). Expenses listed by the Government and borne in Georgia can reduce the taxable amount (art. 23(12)). [Tax Code](https://matsne.gov.ge/ka/document/view/1043717).
- **Salaries**: employees' income from working for an international company is taxed at 5% (art. 23(7)). [Tax Code](https://matsne.gov.ge/ka/document/view/1043717).
- **Dividends**: not taxed at source, and not included in the recipient's gross income (art. 23(8)). [Tax Code](https://matsne.gov.ge/ka/document/view/1043717).
- **Property tax**: exempt, except on land, for property used for the permitted activities (art. 23(13)). [Tax Code](https://matsne.gov.ge/ka/document/view/1043717).
- **Limits**: it cannot be set up in a Free Industrial Zone (art. 23(4)). The current Ordinance 619 conditions also apply. Annex 2 art. 2 permits only ancillary income related to permitted activities, excluding VAT, up to 2% of permitted-activity income for the year. This is not a general allowance for unrelated business. Outside permitted activities and that ancillary exception, cancellation for the activity breach operates from 1 January of the year it began. If the breach results from an amendment to Ordinance 619, cancellation instead starts from that amendment's effective date. Separately, failure to meet the requirements of the status rules (annex 1 article 3(1)(c), as amended by Ordinance 416/2021) causes cancellation retroactively from the original status-grant date; where that failure is caused by an amendment, use the amendment's effective date (Code art. 23(3); Ordinance 619 annex 1 art. 3(1)(b)–(c), (4)(b)–(b1), amended by Ordinance 279/2023). [Tax Code](https://matsne.gov.ge/ka/document/view/1043717).

The status is an approval, not an automatic IT-company election. Ordinance 619 annex 1 art. 2(3) limits status to a person taxed on the objects in Tax Code article 97(1). Article 2(1) requires the relevant two-year permitted-activity experience through one of three routes: the Georgian enterprise itself; its representation in Georgia of a nonresident enterprise with the relevant experience; or qualifying partner enterprise(s) holding more than 50% of its shares, with each qualifying partner independently having that experience. Article 2(2) also requires core income-generating activities in Georgia, qualified human resources and appropriate operating costs. The work cannot be merely or mainly auxiliary. Match the precise activities to current annex 2, including the service-contract condition for the listed IT services; maritime carriage itself is excluded. The permitted expense reductions in annex 3 require actual qualifying Georgian expenditure. [Core-activity and ancillary-income amendment](https://matsne.gov.ge/ka/document/view/5155134); [current cancellation timing](https://matsne.gov.ge/ka/document/view/5886927). Obtain the status decision and its effective period before using the reduced rates. [Ordinance 619 and amendment history](https://matsne.gov.ge/ka/document/view/5012362).

International-company and virtual-zone status do not accumulate. Granting international-company status to a virtual-zone person cancels its virtual-zone status under Ordinance 366/2021. If an international company is granted virtual-zone status, international-company status is cancelled from the moment the virtual-zone status is granted. Under Ordinance 420/2025, innovative-startup status causes international-company cancellation from the first day of the month following the decision granting innovative-startup status. These interactions require a status-history check; this Guide does not calculate the innovative-startup regime. [Ordinance 366/2021](https://matsne.gov.ge/ka/document/view/5221309); [Ordinance 420/2025](https://matsne.gov.ge/ka/document/view/6635309).

### 5. Test virtual-zone status and qualifying supply


- **Who**: a legal person engaged in IT activities that holds virtual zone status (Tax Code art. 8(35); Law on Information Technology Zones art. 2(b)). IT means the study, support, development, design, production and introduction of computer information systems that produce software products (Law on IT Zones art. 2(c)). An individual cannot hold the status. [Tax Code](https://matsne.gov.ge/ka/document/view/1043717); [IT-zone law](https://matsne.gov.ge/ka/document/view/1152405).
- **Relief**: no profit tax on profit, or its distribution, from IT the company developed in Georgia and supplied outside Georgia (Tax Code art. 99(1)(p); Law on IT Zones arts. 3(2)(a), 6). Profit from IT supplied inside Georgia is taxed under the general rules. Costs are split between the two in proportion to income (Law on IT Zones art. 3(3)). [Tax Code](https://matsne.gov.ge/ka/document/view/1043717); [IT-zone law](https://matsne.gov.ge/ka/document/view/1152405).
- **Status**: granted by a person the Government designates, within 10 working days of the application. An electronic certificate follows within 2 working days (Law on IT Zones art. 5). [Tax Code](https://matsne.gov.ge/ka/document/view/1043717); [IT-zone law](https://matsne.gov.ge/ka/document/view/1152405).
- **Not covered**: the relief is for profit tax on qualifying exports. Dividends paid to individuals or nonresident enterprises generally bear 5% withholding, subject to the recipient/payment exemptions in article 130 and applicable treaty relief (Tax Code art. 130(1)); Article 130 has no virtual zone exception. Employees' salaries are taxed at 20% (art. 81(1)). [Tax Code](https://matsne.gov.ge/ka/document/view/1043717); [IT-zone law](https://matsne.gov.ge/ka/document/view/1152405).

Article 6 requires IT creation activity in Georgia. A virtual-zone certificate and a foreign customer alone do not establish the profit-tax exemption. Preserve development, personnel, contracts, source location, qualifying product and cost-allocation evidence; refer any uncertain creation or supply facts before applying relief. [Law on IT Zones arts. 2, 3, 6](https://matsne.gov.ge/ka/document/view/1152405).

## Worked cases

### A. Preserve the ordinary GEL 100 distribution comparison

Assume GEL 100 of pre-profit-tax ordinary company profit is fully used for a distribution to an individual, with no exemption, special status or treaty reduction. Profit tax is GEL 15, the declared dividend GEL 85, dividend withholding GEL 4.25 and total tax GEL 19.25. Compare a valid small business only on its gross taxable receipts, not on this profit amount. [Code arts. 97(10), 98(1), 130(1)](https://matsne.gov.ge/ka/document/view/1043717).

```oa-check
name: Georgia original GEL 100 pre-tax profit distribution
inputs:
  pre_tax_profit: 100
  profit_rate: 0.15
  dividend_rate: 0.05
steps:
  company_tax: pre_tax_profit * profit_rate
  dividend: pre_tax_profit - company_tax
  dividend_withholding: dividend * dividend_rate
  shareholder_cash: dividend - dividend_withholding
  total_tax: company_tax + dividend_withholding
expected:
  company_tax: 15
  dividend: 85
  dividend_withholding: 4.25
  shareholder_cash: 80.75
  total_tax: 19.25
```

### B. Decide the simplified 1% versus fully distributing company comparison both ways

Assume valid small-business status, all receipts taxable at 1%, no salary or excluded activity, no VAT/pension difference and a resident individual receiving all ordinary company profit. Both alternatives receive GEL 100,000. At GEL 90,000 costs, the company tax burden is GEL 1,925 versus GEL 1,000 small-business tax. At GEL 97,000 costs, the company burden is GEL 577.50 versus GEL 1,000. This is a tax-only comparison with all profit distributed. The break-even profit margin is approximately 5.1948%; changing legal or retention facts can reverse the practical choice. [Code arts. 90, 97, 98, 130](https://matsne.gov.ge/ka/document/view/1043717).

```oa-check
name: Georgia eligible small business versus ordinary company both margins
inputs:
  revenue: 100000
  higher_margin_costs: 90000
  lower_margin_costs: 97000
  small_business_rate: 0.01
  company_profit_rate: 0.15
  dividend_rate: 0.05
steps:
  small_business_tax: revenue * small_business_rate
  combined_company_rate: company_profit_rate + (1 - company_profit_rate) * dividend_rate
  higher_margin_profit: revenue - higher_margin_costs
  lower_margin_profit: revenue - lower_margin_costs
  higher_margin_company_tax: higher_margin_profit * combined_company_rate
  lower_margin_company_tax: lower_margin_profit * combined_company_rate
  higher_company_extra_tax: higher_margin_company_tax - small_business_tax
  lower_company_tax_saving: small_business_tax - lower_margin_company_tax
  break_even_margin_percent: small_business_rate / combined_company_rate * 100
expected:
  small_business_tax: 1000
  combined_company_rate: 0.1925
  higher_margin_profit: 10000
  lower_margin_profit: 3000
  higher_margin_company_tax: 1925
  lower_margin_company_tax: 577.5
  higher_company_extra_tax: 925
  lower_company_tax_saving: 422.5
  break_even_margin_percent: 5.1948051948
```

### C. Crossing the small-business threshold changes the whole month's rate

Assume an ordinary eligible small business has GEL 490,000 of counted receipts before October, then GEL 20,000 of taxable October receipts. The year reaches GEL 510,000. The whole GEL 20,000 October taxable amount is at 3%, giving GEL 600, not a progressive tax only on the GEL 10,000 excess. Later taxable months also use 3% through year end. The separate two-year status-cancellation test still needs both years. [Code arts. 89(2)(a), 90(2)](https://matsne.gov.ge/ka/document/view/1043717).

```oa-check
name: Georgia threshold crossing whole-month rate
inputs:
  prior_counted_income: 490000
  october_taxable_income: 20000
  ordinary_limit: 500000
  crossing_month_rate: 0.03
steps:
  year_income: prior_counted_income + october_taxable_income
  excess_over_limit: year_income - ordinary_limit
  october_tax: october_taxable_income * crossing_month_rate
expected:
  year_income: 510000
  excess_over_limit: 10000
  october_tax: 600
```

## When to refuse or refer


- **Prohibited activities**: Small Business Status is barred for: [Official Tax Code](https://matsne.gov.ge/ka/document/view/1043717).
  - activities that need a licence or permit, except the Tbilisi taxi (M1) permit under art. 24(26^1) of the Law on Licences and Permits (Resolution No 368 of 11 July 2018);
  - activities that need significant investment (production of excisable goods);
  - currency exchange;
  - medical, architectural, advocacy or notary, audit and consulting work, including tax consultants;
  - gambling;
  - staffing (provision of personnel);
  - production of excisable goods.
  
  (Resolution 415, annex 4; Tax Code arts. 88(2), 89(2)(c).) Carrying on a barred activity ends the status from the start of the year and draws a GEL 500 fine (arts. 89(4), 288). **AUDIT FLASH POINT**.
- **Construction for business customers**: since 1 February 2025, construction services (SEK 006-2016 group 41.2, divisions 42 and 43) supplied to an enterprise, organisation or individual entrepreneur are outside the 1% regime and taxed under the general rules (Resolution 415, annex 5, item 12). Construction for a private individual who is not receiving the service as an entrepreneur is not excluded by annex 5 item 12 alone; the 1% or 3% rate still depends on valid status, taxable source and the income threshold. **AUDIT FLASH POINT**. [Official Tax Code](https://matsne.gov.ge/ka/document/view/1043717).
- **Genuine self-employment**: salary is not taxable income under Small Business Status (art. 90(3)). If a single-client contract is in substance employment, the income is salary taxed at 20% (art. 81(1)). Check the facts. [Official Tax Code](https://matsne.gov.ge/ka/document/view/1043717).
- **Two-year cap manipulation**: do not move income between years or between related people to stay under GEL 500,000. For ordinary small businesses the rate rises to 3% from the month GEL 500,000 is passed in any year; the statutory wine/agro-tourism threshold is GEL 700,000 (art. 90(2)), and the status ends after two such years (art. 89(2)(a)). [Official Tax Code](https://matsne.gov.ge/ka/document/view/1043717).
- **Monthly filing**: a small business files and pays monthly by the 15th (art. 93(1^1)). A return up to 2 months late costs 5% of the tax due. More than 2 months late, the fine is 10% (art. 274(1), (2), Georgian text; the English translation still shows an older per-month wording). There is no fine where the tax due is zero (art. 274). Late payment carries penalty interest of 0.05% a day (art. 272(4)). [Official Tax Code](https://matsne.gov.ge/ka/document/view/1043717).
- **International company and virtual zone scope**: apply the current permitted-activity and related-ancillary-income tests above before deciding international-company cancellation (art. 23(3); Ordinance 619). Virtual zone relief does not cover IT supplied inside Georgia. [Official Tax Code](https://matsne.gov.ge/ka/document/view/1043717).

- **Fixed-tax activities**: do not apply micro/small treatment to activities assigned to the fixed-tax regime by Ordinance 415 art. 1¹ and annex 6 without checking the transitional grandfather rules in the relevant amendments. Fixed-tax rates and individual grandfather entitlement are outside this Guide. [Resolution 58/2012](https://matsne.gov.ge/ka/document/view/1593310) and [Resolution 415](https://matsne.gov.ge/ka/document/view/1164635). [Official Tax Code](https://matsne.gov.ge/ka/document/view/1043717).


- **Assume Small Business Status without checking prohibited list/cap**: NEVER assume Small Business Status applies without checking the Resolution 415 lists (consulting is barred) and the GEL 500,000 limit.
- **1% on profit misrepresentation**: NEVER present the 1% as applying to profit. It is on gross Georgian-source income.
- **Ignore VAT trigger**: NEVER ignore the GEL 100,000 VAT registration trigger. Check the place of supply of each service before leaving it out of the count.
- **Contradict other skill rates**: NEVER contradict the rates in `ge-corporate-income-tax.md`, `georgia-income-tax.md` or `georgia-social-contributions.md`.
- **Present research gaps as confirmed**: NEVER present the items on the "Not yet confirmed" line as confirmed, nor optimisation as definitive advice. Route to a licensed Georgian tax adviser.
