# Falkland Islands transfer pricing 2026 v0.1

> **General reference only.** This Guide is general tax/accounting reference material for AI-assisted workflows. It has not been reviewed for any specific person's facts, documents, elections, deadlines, residency, filing status, or local procedures. Do not rely on it to file, pay, amend, or take a tax position without review by a qualified professional in the relevant jurisdiction.

## Who this applies to

Section 156 gives effect to Schedule 2A. It is an applicable tax domain in the Falkland Islands. A transaction or series may include informal understandings, arrangements and mutual practices. Common control and attributed rights matter as well as direct ownership. The operative tests are participation, difference from independent dealings, and potential tax advantage, with statutory exceptions. [§156; Sch 2A ¶¶1-5](https://legislation.gov.fk/download/pdf/05a29220-06c3-4560-867c-99d6c5200d0d/15f327cc-37ae-4304-a080-e3d54a80bd43/fiord-1997-14_2026-06-12.pdf#page=247).

Quick reference: there is no universal prescribed markup in the text read. Price the actual functions and risks using evidence, then apply the legal tests. Do not import the UK's SME exemption or treat current OECD guidance as automatically incorporated: ¶2 defines the recognised guidelines and permits later designation by order.

## The base

Start with the actual provision: identify counterparties, actual conduct, property, services, funding, consideration, timing and connected arrangements. Map control under ¶4, ¶12 and §209. Include indirect and future rights and relevant connected-person rights, and examine any linked series under ¶3. [Schedule 2A](https://legislation.gov.fk/download/pdf/05a29220-06c3-4560-867c-99d6c5200d0d/15f327cc-37ae-4304-a080-e3d54a80bd43/fiord-1997-14_2026-06-12.pdf#page=247).

Compare what independent enterprises would agree for those functions, assets and risks. Keep contracts, benefit evidence, invoices, allocation keys, financing terms and reliable comparable evidence. These are evidence steps, not a claim that the statute prescribes a particular master-file template. Reconcile any adjustment to the tax computation and prevent double deduction. A service charged at cost is not automatically acceptable or unacceptable.

Under ¶5(1), smaller taxable profits or larger tax losses are a potential advantage. Under ¶5(2)-(4), the advantage is excluded only where **both** affected persons meet **both** conditions: each is taxable on the relevant activities without an exemption, an individual is resident for those periods, and neither has an actual or potential foreign-tax-credit entitlement for the relevant profits. Do not reduce this to “domestic parties are exempt.” Ring-fence rules can disapply this exception. [¶¶5, 10](https://legislation.gov.fk/download/pdf/05a29220-06c3-4560-867c-99d6c5200d0d/15f327cc-37ae-4304-a080-e3d54a80bd43/fiord-1997-14_2026-06-12.pdf#page=250).

| Boundary | Treatment |
|---|---|
| Unconnected independent transaction | ¶1 participation condition must still be established; do not adjust merely because the price seems unusual |
| Actual arrangement that independents would not make | ¶1(3) expressly includes this case |
| Both fully taxable domestic parties with no relevant foreign-credit entitlement | Test every ¶5(3)-(4) condition before applying the exclusion |
| Oil valued under §151 | ¶9 excludes that transaction from Schedule 2A; statutory petroleum valuation applies |
| Ring-fence and non-ring-fence activities | ¶10 can remove the domestic exclusion or deem separate persons; capital allowances remain protected by ¶11 |

[Sch 2A ¶¶1, 5, 9-11](https://legislation.gov.fk/download/pdf/05a29220-06c3-4560-867c-99d6c5200d0d/15f327cc-37ae-4304-a080-e3d54a80bd43/fiord-1997-14_2026-06-12.pdf).

## Deadlines and filing

Make the arm's-length computation in the ordinary income-tax or corporation-tax return. For company filing and payment use [fk-corporate-income-tax](fk-corporate-income-tax.md); Schedule 2A does not replace those obligations. Retain the pricing reconciliation with the supporting computation. Do not assume that a foreign parent's master file satisfies the local return or evidence request.

Where ¶6 applies, obtain the advantaged person's return computation or relevant assessment notice, confirm the disadvantaged person's eligibility, make a consistent written claim, and record the two-year claim clock. ¶7 adjusts the disadvantaged person's double-tax credit as necessary. A foreign corresponding adjustment requires the other jurisdiction's law or treaty procedure; a local claim does not bind it. [Sch 2A ¶¶6-7](https://legislation.gov.fk/download/pdf/05a29220-06c3-4560-867c-99d6c5200d0d/15f327cc-37ae-4304-a080-e3d54a80bd43/fiord-1997-14_2026-06-12.pdf#page=251).

## Penalties and interest

An adjustment changes the ordinary tax base; tax and interest follow the underlying tax. §§11 and 33 prescribe interest at three percentage points above the statutory base lending rate for overdue individual and company tax respectively. Ordinary late-filing and inaccurate-return sanctions may also apply. No separate transfer-pricing documentation fine or mandatory CbCR deadline is established by the provisions read, so none is invented here. [§§11, 33, Part IX](https://legislation.gov.fk/download/pdf/05a29220-06c3-4560-867c-99d6c5200d0d/15f327cc-37ae-4304-a080-e3d54a80bd43/fiord-1997-14_2026-06-12.pdf).

## Cross-border points

There need not be a foreign counterparty for Schedule 2A to require an examination. For overseas transactions also test withholding, income source and foreign credit separately in [fk-cross-border-tax](fk-cross-border-tax.md). UK treaty associated-enterprise and mutual-agreement provisions may assist eligible residents, but do not provide an automatic refund. Petroleum disposals and finance-charge restrictions need their specific provisions as well as general pricing analysis. [Sch 2A; Part VIII](https://legislation.gov.fk/download/pdf/05a29220-06c3-4560-867c-99d6c5200d0d/15f327cc-37ae-4304-a080-e3d54a80bd43/fiord-1997-14_2026-06-12.pdf).

## Two worked examples that recompute

**Overpriced service.** Assumed evidence supports GBP 100,000, while a controlled foreign supplier charged GBP 140,000. The Falkland buyer's book profit is GBP 50,000. Assume ¶1 participation and advantage, no exception, and a fully deductible arm's-length service. Add back GBP 40,000, producing taxable profit GBP 90,000 before other adjustments. This is a hypothetical benchmark, not a statutory markup.

```oa-check
name: Service pricing adjustment
inputs:
  charged: 140000
  independent: 100000
  book_profit: 50000
steps:
  addback: charged - independent
  revised_profit: book_profit + addback
expected:
  addback: 40000
  revised_profit: 90000
```

**Decision at the domestic boundary.** Assume the same GBP 20,000 overcharge. If both ordinary domestic parties satisfy every ¶5(3)-(4) condition and ¶10 does not apply, ¶5(2) excludes potential advantage and the pricing adjustment under this Schedule is zero. If one party instead has an exemption on the relevant activity, that exclusion fails; assuming all other ¶1 conditions, the payer adds GBP 20,000. Ordinary deductibility still needs checking in either case.

```oa-check
name: Domestic exclusion versus exempt recipient
inputs:
  charged: 70000
  independent: 50000
steps:
  price_difference: charged - independent
  excluded_adjustment: price_difference * 0
  exception_failed_adjustment: price_difference
expected:
  excluded_adjustment: 0
  exception_failed_adjustment: 20000
```

## Ask the client first

- Full ownership, voting, management and future-rights map, including connected people.
- All linked contracts, actual conduct and transaction dates.
- Counterparty residence, exemptions and relevant actual or possible foreign tax credits.
- Functions, assets, risks and reliable independent comparable evidence.
- Ring-fence activity, oil transactions and capital-asset implications.
- Existing returns, assessments, pricing adjustments and compensating claims.
