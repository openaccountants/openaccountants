# UA Crypto Tax

## Who this applies to

Ukrainian tax-resident individuals holding virtual assets/cryptocurrency personally. This is not a business mining/trading, FOP, company, exchange or service-provider computation. Start from income-tax-workflow-base, establish residence and each receipt's source, then obtain a Ukrainian professional's review.

**As read on 15 November 2026, bill 10225-д remains “preparing for second reading.”** The [official bill card](https://itd.rada.gov.ua/billinfo/Bills/Card/56271) records registration on 24 April 2025 and acceptance at first reading on 3 September 2025. Inclusion in the September 2026 agenda is not final adoption, signature or commencement. The old description's assertion that the new framework was legislated for 1 January 2026 was wrong. The original bill PDF's target date is a proposal, not current law.

For current treatment, the [current tax-authority answer](https://zir.tax.gov.ua/main/bz/view/?id=37299&src=ques), corroborated by its [6 October 2026 explanation](https://ck.tax.gov.ua/media-ark/news-ark/print-1056526.html), includes crypto-sale receipts in annual income: foreign income if the payment source is foreign, or other income for the stated domestic resident-payer case. It expressly refers to the **amount of money received**, and says each case depends on its documents. Do not replace this with the old draft's annual net-gain model.

Currency: UAH (₴). Tax year: calendar year. Authority: ДПС / State Tax Service. Filing portal: Electronic Cabinet (cabinet.tax.gov.ua). Contributor: Open Accountants Community. Version: 1.1. The old references to NSSMC/NBU market regulation and the 2022 Law “On Virtual Assets” remain research context; they do not establish an in-force tax exemption or automatic permission for a business activity.

Keep the documents even where no current deduction has been established.

## The base

### Current receipts: establish the transaction before applying a rate

1. Reconcile fiat receipts to sale records and bank/exchange statements. The authority's ordinary treatment concerns money received from crypto transactions; it does not approve an automatic purchase-cost deduction. Do not assume current taxable income is proceeds minus documented acquisition price, or that missing purchase records alone determine a different legal base.
2. Identify the actual payer/source and any Ukrainian tax agent. A domestic exchange interface or the holder's own wallet location alone does not establish payment source. The authority distinguishes foreign income from the stated domestic resident-payer other-income case. Its answer expressly requires the facts/documents of the individual case.
3. Where income is in foreign currency or foreign-currency-denominated assets,164.4 uses the NBU rate at accrual/receipt as legally applicable. Keep the original currency, date, quantity, rate evidence and UAH equivalent. Do not assume a crypto market price is an NBU fiat quotation. Actual daily NBU rates and noncash valuation/timing require evidence and review.
4. Separate same-owner transfers from disposals using ownership and transaction evidence; do not count the same sale twice because money passes through two own accounts. A mere wallet label does not establish no ownership change. This is a reconciliation safeguard, not a blanket current exemption for swaps, rewards or crypto spending.
5. Review mining, staking/validator receipts, airdrops, forks, gifts, issuance and tokens exchanged for personal data individually. The old disposal-only defaults are withdrawn. Current receipt timing, valuation, income category, cost and later-sale interaction for these special receipts are **not yet confirmed** here. Do not defer them automatically until cash-out.

[Current official answer](https://zir.tax.gov.ua/main/bz/view/?id=37299&src=ques); [Tax Code 163.1,164.2.20,164.3-164.5](https://tax.gov.ua/nk/).

## Deadlines and filing

For ordinary taxable personal income received from a non-tax-agent payer or from abroad, the current authority answer requires the annual **Декларація про майновий стан і доходи** (property and income declaration) and payment under 168.2.1/179. Establish any withholding, actual exemptions and other income before preparing the return. The ordinary statutory return rule is **before 1 May of the following year** under 49.18.4, with 49.20 calendar rules;179.7 requires self-assessed payment **before 1 August of the following year**. The old 30 April/31 July shorthand must not override a calendar adjustment or individual special rule. Exact 2027 filing/payment calendar and final 2026 form identifiers remain unconfirmed here. Use ua-income-tax/ua-return-assembly for the applicable declaration; do not claim a new crypto-specific form from an unimplemented bill. [Current ZIR answer](https://zir.tax.gov.ua/main/bz/view/?id=37299&src=ques); [Tax Code 49.18.4,49.20,179.7](https://tax.gov.ua/nk/).

## Penalties and interest

No new crypto-specific bill penalty is treated as in force. Current omission, late declaration, late payment or inaccurate reporting can require compliance review under the applicable general rules. Exact penalty/interest amounts and personal relief grounds are **not yet confirmed** in this skill. Do not infer a wartime amnesty or use proposed service-provider penalties for an individual investor.

## Cross-border points

The current authority answer applies the resident's worldwide-income framework and distinguishes foreign-source receipts. Foreign exchange custody/self-custody alone does not remove Ukrainian residence taxation, nor establish the payer's source or a foreign tax credit. Confirm residence first and route treaty, double-tax, foreign-account reporting, currency-control and AML questions to ua-cross-border/a qualified professional. Obtain foreign tax documents before considering a credit; no credit or reporting exemption is computed here. Business-scale mining/trading, legal entities and single-tax eligibility require separate review.

## Two worked examples that recompute

### Current case 1: documented taxable money received from a crypto sale

Assume a resident personal holder received **UAH 100,000** taxable sale income in 2026 from a non-tax-agent payer; the professional has confirmed the authority's ordinary receipts treatment, no exemption/foreign credit, and no tax already withheld. No purchase-cost deduction is established. 18% PIT=**UAH 18,000** and 5% levy=**UAH 5,000**; combined **UAH 23,000**, derived. This does not decide receipt classification, token rewards or the treatment of an unresolved actual case.

```oa-check
name: Current ordinary taxable crypto receipts 100000
inputs:
  taxable_receipts: 100000
  pit_rate: 0.18
  levy_rate: 0.05
steps:
  - pit: taxable_receipts * pit_rate
  - levy: taxable_receipts * levy_rate
  - total_tax: pit + levy
expected:
  pit: 18000
  levy: 5000
  total_tax: 23000
```

### Current case 2: records do not establish the draft's gain concession

Assume **UAH 300,000** taxable money received in 2026 under the same ordinary-case conditions, with **UAH 100,000** of purchase records. The purchase record alone does not establish a current deduction. On the expressly confirmed receipts base, 18% PIT=**UAH 54,000** and 5% levy=**UAH 15,000**; combined **UAH 69,000**, derived. The economic difference of **UAH 200,000** is not silently used as the tax base. This is not approval of the old legacy-asset transition.

```oa-check
name: Current receipts treatment distinct from proposed net gain
inputs:
  taxable_receipts: 300000
  purchase_records: 100000
  pit_rate: 0.18
  levy_rate: 0.05
steps:
  - economic_difference: taxable_receipts - purchase_records
  - pit: taxable_receipts * pit_rate
  - levy: taxable_receipts * levy_rate
  - total_tax: pit + levy
expected:
  economic_difference: 200000
  pit: 54000
  levy: 15000
  total_tax: 69000
```

## Ask the client first

Confirm residence, personal-versus-business activity, actual receipt dates, assets/quantities, transaction types and ownership. Obtain per transaction: buy/sell/swap/transfer/reward date; counterparty/exchange; quantity, fiat value and UAH conversion evidence; acquisition invoices/exchange statements/on-chain references; fees; running holdings and any previously used cost method; withdrawal/bank records; withholding and foreign tax documents. Preserve the old cost-method history without assuming it is legally accepted.

### The ten original reviewer questions, retained

1. **In-force status:** bill card, final enactment/signature/publication and actual commencement, not a target date. Original 246 of 450 vote claim is not verified by the accessible card; voting iframe was unavailable and that tally is not evidence of final adoption.
2. **Cost method:** no FIFO, weighted-average or specific-identification default; current legal deduction and any eventual regulations must be established.
3. **Staking timing:** establish actual source, receipt/disposal timing and business status before deciding.
4. **Business versus investor:** frequency, scale, organization and intent are flags for professional analysis, not a statutory safe-harbor test invented here.
5. **De-minimis:** preserve the proposed annual 1 January minimum-wage condition, with no current exemption.
6. **Transition levy:** no confirmed special reduced/waived levy or enacted 2026 legacy-window computation.
7. **Records:** collect every item above; no unsupported cost or double-counted receipt.
8. **Foreign holdings:** residence/worldwide-income/source/treaty/foreign-reporting/currency-control review, not exemption from offshore custody.
9. **FOP interaction:** separate permitted-activity/settlement review; do not promise single-tax eligibility.
10. **Anti-avoidance/wash sales:** no invented near-repurchase safe harbor or automatic loss harvesting; draft related-party and authorization disallowances are distinct, and final/current rule remains case-specific.

### Original eight quick tests, corrected

1. BTC sold for UAH: current taxable-receipts review under the authority answer; no assumed 23% net gain.
2. BTC swapped for ETH: no current blanket exemption; proposal excludes pure swaps but treats top-up swaps differently.
3. ETH moved between own wallets: establish unchanged ownership and reconcile; no double-counting or automatic tax conclusion from a label.
4. BTC bought in 2021 sold 2026: no enacted 5% legacy concession established; actual proposal uses gross receipts and conditions.
5. No purchase record: do not invent a current net-gain cost rule; ordinary money-received guidance still requires the case's documents.
6. Net crypto loss: no current carry-forward confirmed from the proposal.
7. Rate from 2027: no automatic 23% net-gain framework from a bill's target date.
8. Definitive 2026 enactment: card still preparing for second reading; do not say final adoption is verified.

### Prohibitions

Do not claim final enactment, fabricate a rate/threshold/loss limit/cost method/levy waiver, recommend cashing out legacy assets for an unimplemented concession, handle business-scale mining/trading or legal entities here, infer VAT/customs/ЄСВ/residence/AML/currency-control conclusions, or skip professional review. All six original prohibitions remain. Proposed service-provider/regulatory rules are not current individual tax instructions.
