# Saint Kitts and Nevis property tax and stamp duty

> **General reference only.** This skill is general tax/accounting reference material for AI-assisted workflows. It has not been reviewed for any specific person's facts, documents, elections, deadlines, residency, filing status, or local procedures. Do not rely on it to file, pay, amend, or take a tax position without review by a qualified professional in the relevant jurisdiction.

## Scope

Annual property tax and the stamp duty on transferring land in 2026. St Kitts administers property tax under the Property Tax Act, Cap. 20.32; Nevis under the Property Tax Ordinance, Cap. 6.13(N). Stamp duty is federal, under the Stamps Act, Cap. 20.40 (2020 revision, reflecting Act 5 of 2015). Non-citizens also need a licence under the Aliens Land Holding Regulation Act, Cap. 10.01. Amounts are EC$. Establish the island, valuation list entry, land and improvement values, class or mixed-use split, any exemption, the demand notice and its dates first.

## Ask the client first

1. Which island, and the valuation on the list or notice (land and building)?
2. Residential, commercial or mixed use; any concession on the notice?
3. For a transfer: consideration, market value, the parties (spouses, parent and child), and whether the land is in a Special Development Area or the South East Peninsula.
4. Is the buyer a non-citizen (alien landholding licence)?
5. Any arrears from before the 2026 SMARTS notices?

### The base

- **St Kitts.** Apply the assessed taxable valuation and the rate for each class; the IRD example deducts EC$80,000 for a residence and applies 0.2%. The June 2026 SMARTS notices show the current year only: get a separate arrears balance.
- **Nevis.** IRD's undated rates table still prints 0.156% for a residential component. The Nevis 2025 budget (para.173) describes 0.2% on residential property worth EC$2 million or more from 1 June 2025. The Premier's 8 June 2026 statement describes an EC$80,000 exemption and a proposal to raise it during the revaluation. Read the principal rates regulations and the assessment before choosing a rate.
- **Stamp duty.** Sections 60 to 62 cover the principal instrument, shares in land-rich companies and passing the vendor's duty to the purchaser. Condominium sales: 10% of consideration on the vendor or transferor. Separate entries cover public vendors (para.(k), National Housing Corporation exception). The 2009 revision showed 12% on ordinary land sales; Act 5 of 2015 changed it to 10%.

### Steps

1. **Annual tax:** taxable value (after any exemption) x the island's rate for the class.
2. **Transfer:** stamp duty on the S-10 form by type of transfer; the vendor pays unless the contract passes it on (ss.60 to 62).
3. **Non-citizen buyer:** obtain the alien landholding licence (Cap. 10.01 ss.3 and 4).

## Worked cases

### Case 1: St Kitts home valued at EC$330,000

Taxable 250,000 after the 80,000 deduction; tax 500.

```oa-check
name: St Kitts residential property tax on 330,000
inputs:
  value: 330000
steps:
  - taxable: value - 80000
  - tax: taxable * 0.002
expected:
  taxable: 250000
  tax: 500
```

### Case 2 (decision): sell below value, or gift to a child?

A parent sells land worth 320,000 to an unrelated buyer for 300,000: duty is 10% of the value, 32,000, on the vendor. A gift of the same land to the parent's child costs $100. A gift to anyone else costs 6% of value, 19,200. In a Special Development Area the sale would cost 12% of market value, 38,400.

```oa-check
name: St Kitts stamp duty, sale at 300,000 of land worth 320,000
inputs:
  price: 300000
  value: 320000
steps:
  - sale_duty: max(price, value) * 0.10
  - gift_other: value * 0.06
  - sda_duty: value * 0.12
expected:
  sale_duty: 32000
  gift_other: 19200
  sda_duty: 38400
```

## When to refuse or refer

- Nevis rates until the principal regulations and assessment are read.
- Land-rich company shares, condominium and public vendor cases.
- Alien landholding licences and their fees.
- Capital gains and estate questions.

## Filing and payment

1. St Kitts: pay the SMARTS demand by 30 June; reconcile arrears separately.
2. Nevis: get the demand notice from the e-government portal; pay by 30 September 2026; object within 30 days.
3. Transfers: file S-10 with IRD and pay the duty before registration.

## Completion checklist

- Island and class settled; exemption and rate from the notice.
- Stamp duty by transfer type; vendor or purchaser burden recorded.
- Licence for a non-citizen buyer.
