# Sao Tome and Principe Corporate Income Tax v0.3

> **Sourced from the law, in part.** Tax year 2026. The IRC code was read in its 2009 republication on the Ministry of Finance legacy site, and the 2026 minimum tax in the Assembly's record of 11 February 2026. Later amendments to the code, Decree-Law 6/2016 and the published 2026 Budget Law were not read on an official host: see `Not yet confirmed:` under Sources. No accountant has signed this version.

## Who this applies to

IRC taxpayers are ([IRC code art. 2](https://ministerio.financas.st/phocadownload/Imposto/legislacao/Lei_N10_2009%20_Codigo_Imposto_Sobre_Rendimento_Pessoas_Colectivas_IRC.pdf)):

- commercial companies, civil companies in commercial form, cooperatives, public enterprises and other public or private entities with a seat or effective management in STP;
- entities without legal personality with a seat or effective management in STP whose income is not taxed directly on the owners (for example estates in abeyance, companies before final registration);
- entities with no seat or effective management in STP, on income obtained there not subject to IRS, including permanent establishments of non-residents.

The State, the Autonomous Region of Principe and local authorities are outside IRC unless they carry on business (art. 3). Residents are taxed on worldwide income, non-residents only on STP income (art. 4). The code is Law 16/2008 as amended and republished by Law 10/2009 of 8 October 2009.

**Exempt:** international organisations STP belongs to, as their conventions provide; religious bodies with legal personality on income from worship; non-profit associations and bodies (art. 16). Bodies exempt under art. 16 must still file annual returns (2025 Budget Law and 2026 Budget Bill art. 8(14)). **Agriculture:** 50% of the taxable profit of companies with an exclusively agricultural activity is exempt, after losses (art. 17).

**Groups** (arts. 18 to 21):

- **Group 1** (general regime, organised accounts, taxed on actual profit): residents with turnover in the prior year, or expected in the current year, at or above the threshold; and, whatever the turnover, public enterprises, SA companies, partnerships limited by shares, and non-residents with a permanent establishment in STP (art. 19). The 2009 code set the threshold at Dbs. 500.000.000,00 (pre-redenomination dobras); the law approving the VAT Code changed it to Db 1,000,000 (art. 4 of that law, Assembly final text of 15 October 2019).
- **Group 2:** everyone else, and those carrying on an activity occasionally or temporarily. They use simplified bookkeeping or the simplified regime for taxable profit (art. 20).
- A Group 2 taxpayer whose turnover reaches the threshold moves to organised accounts from the following year (art. 21(3)).

## The base

1. **Taxable profit, Group 1.** Net result for the year plus positive and negative equity changes not in that result, from accounts kept under the accounting rules in force, corrected under the code (art. 24). Taxable income is taxable profit less tax losses (art. 23).
2. **Losses.** Tax losses are deducted from taxable profits of one or more of the next five years (art. 52).
3. **Group 2.** Taxable income follows the simplified bookkeeping or simplified regime rules (arts. 20 and following); indirect methods can apply to both groups (art. 22).
4. **Tax.** Apply 25% (art. 81(1)). Then deduct, in this order: international double taxation, tax benefits, the special payment on account, and tax withheld at source. Only withholding can produce a refund (art. 83). Group 2 has no double taxation deduction (art. 84).
5. **Double taxation credit.** The lower of the foreign tax and the IRC on that income; unused credit carries forward five years; a certified foreign statement is required with the return (art. 85).
6. **Minimum IRC.** 2/1000 of turnover, at least Db 2,400 (2026 Budget art. 8(10), as amended in the Assembly). How the minimum interacts with normal IRC (whether it is a floor on total tax or a separate charge) is in Decree-Law 6/2016, which was not read: hold the interaction.
7. **Billing software and cash.** Billing software cost is written off in one year (2025 Law art. 8(13)). Cash payments above the limit are not deductible (art. 9(5)).

History of the minimum: the floor was Db 1,244, rose to Db 2,544 under the 2024 Budget and returned to Db 1,244 after a Constitutional Court ruling, as the Minister said in the debate; the 2026 Bill proposed 2/1000 with a Db 5,000 minimum and the Assembly replaced it with Db 2,400. A deputy said in the same debate that Db 2,544 was Db 2,400 plus 6% stamp duty that fell away with VAT. These are statements in debate, not law read.

Incentives rest on Decree-Law 15/2016 (Tax Benefits Code) and Decree-Law 19/2016 (Investment Code), per the [Ministry presentation of 2025](https://www.britacom.org/zt/ThemeDay/Tenth/SEMINAR/202510/P020251021566751705880.pdf). Their rates were not read.

Forms: IRC Modelo 2 and its annexes are in the Ministry forms folder ([IRC Modelo 2](https://ministerio.financas.st/phocadownload/Imposto/espaco_contribuinte/Minutas_Formularios/IRC/IRC%20_%20Modelo2.pdf)). Self-assessment is made in the return when filed on time; if not, the Direccao dos Impostos assesses by 31 August of the following year from the nearest year it has and other information (art. 82).

The older skill said returns were commonly due by 31 March; the code gives 30 April for Group 1 and February for Group 2 with simplified books.

## Penalties and interest

- Late filing counts as delayed assessment and carries compensatory interest under General Tax Code art. 38, plus any fine (IRC art. 91): the Central Bank reference rate plus three percentage points a year, daily ([General Tax Code art. 38](https://ministerio.financas.st/phocadownload/Imposto/legislacao/Lei_N6_2007_Lei_Que_Aprova_Codigo_Geral_Tributario.pdf)).
- Late payments on account or balance carry default interest from each due date (IRC arts. 92(4), 94(5)) at 1/6 of the reference rate a month (General Tax Code art. 54).
- The Direccao dos Impostos is the first instance for tax enforcement, per the Ministry presentation. Fine amounts were not read.

## Cross-border points

- Non-residents with a permanent establishment are in Group 1 whatever their turnover (art. 19(1)(b)(ii)).
- Payments to non-residents without a permanent establishment for royalties, equipment rental, capital income, board fees, prizes, performers and services suffer final 20% withholding (arts. 81(2), 86(3)).
- Foreign tax on income included in the base is credited under art. 85.
- Treaties: see [income tax](st-income-tax.md). Transfer pricing has no skill yet.

## Two worked examples that recompute

**Example 1, minimum IRC on a large turnover.** Turnover of Db 5,000,000 with a tax loss.

```oa-check
name: Minimum IRC, large
inputs:
  turnover: 5000000
steps:
  - minimum: max(turnover * 0.002, 2400)
expected:
  minimum: 10000
```

**Example 2, edge: the floor.** Turnover of Db 500,000: 2/1000 gives Db 1,000, so the Db 2,400 floor applies. Whether the floor sits on top of or instead of normal IRC depends on Decree-Law 6/2016, which was not read.

```oa-check
name: Minimum IRC, floor
inputs:
  turnover: 500000
steps:
  - minimum: max(turnover * 0.002, 2400)
expected:
  minimum: 2400
```

**Example 3, Group 1 profit with losses brought forward, and next year's payments on account.** Taxable profit of Db 1,000,000, losses of Db 300,000 from the previous five years. IRC at 25% on Db 700,000. Each of next year's three payments on account is 25% of this year's tax.

```oa-check
name: Group 1 IRC and payments on account
inputs:
  profit: 1000000
  losses: 300000
  rate: 0.25
steps:
  - taxable: max(profit - losses, 0)
  - irc: taxable * rate
  - each_payment: irc * 0.25
expected:
  taxable: 700000
  irc: 175000
  each_payment: 43750
```

## Ask the client first

- Legal form, seat and place of effective management
- Turnover last year and expected this year (Group 1 or Group 2)
- Profit or loss for the year, and losses of the last five years
- Any exempt status, agricultural activity or incentive certificate
- Payments to non-residents, and foreign tax paid
- Cash payments of Db 10,000 or more
