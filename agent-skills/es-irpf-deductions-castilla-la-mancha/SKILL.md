# Spain: Castilla-La Mancha regional IRPF credits, 2026

**Sourced from the law.** This revision has not had an independent whole-pack review or accountant sign-off. Calculate regional credits after establishing tax residence and the taxpayer's facts. This Guide holds the Castilla-La Mancha catalogue of [es-irpf-deductions](es-irpf-deductions.md), moved here unchanged on 6 November 2026, so that each Guide stays within the size the site can deliver. Specific unresolved interactions are listed in Sources.

## Who this applies to

An ordinary IRPF resident whose community of residence for 2026 under [LIRPF art. 72](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2006-20764#a72) is Castilla-La Mancha. A regional credit reduces regional tax, not the income base. Before applying any row below, use [es-irpf-deductions](es-irpf-deductions.md) for the residence test, the deduction ledger, the state/regional order of credits, the national credits, deadlines, penalties and the questions to ask, and [es-income-tax](es-income-tax.md) for the bases, scales and minima. Where a row below refers to "the attributable-tax worksheet under The base", that worksheet is [es-irpf-deductions-attributable-tax](es-irpf-deductions-attributable-tax.md); national credits, the deduction ledger and any calculation "above" outside this catalogue are in [es-irpf-deductions](es-irpf-deductions.md#the-base). Basque and Navarre residents use [es-foral-income-tax](es-foral-income-tax.md); an IRNR taxpayer or an art. 93 incoming worker does not take these credits.

## Rates, bands and thresholds

All amounts are EUR. `BI` means the specified sum of general and savings **taxable income before base reductions** (base imponible); `BL` means base liquidable after the relevant reductions. Use the definition in each row: an income limit based on BI cannot be replaced with gross salary, BL or take-home pay. The table year is 2026 unless a transition is stated.

### Castilla-La Mancha: family, education, housing and rural credits

Separate the income measure and the order of calculation for each credit. Here `BI` means total general plus savings base imponible, `BL` their bases liquidables, and `education/rent income` means **BI less the descendant minimum only**. The common art. 13 income gate and incompatibilities below are part of these tables. Apply these tables together with [Ley1/2026 art.18/DF6](https://docm.jccm.es/portaldocm/descargarArchivo.do?ruta=2026/03/30/pdf/2026_2465.pdf&tipo=rutaDocm): the official March2026 amendment changes the ordinary rental caps, adds12octies/12nonies and adds13.3(g). DF6 expressly applies the IRPF amendments to tax periods beginning1January2025. The linked older BOE consolidation has not incorporated those changes; it remains evidence for the unchanged clauses, not authority for the superseded450 caps.

**Common family-income gate:** total BI must be at most **27,000 individual / 36,000 joint** for arts. **1, 2, 2 bis, 3 bis, 4, 5 and 6**. Do not extend this gate to every other credit or replace the narrower education/rent tests with it. Art. 13 prescribes application in the statutory article order. Use the minimum-entitlement/sharing rules it incorporates; for the listed shared birth, large-family, disabled-relative and older-ascendant credits, an ineligible co-claimant does not transfer their share to the eligible claimant ([art. 13](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2014-1368#a13)).

Compare eligible alternatives before claiming. Large-family art. 2 cannot be combined with large-family rent art. 9 ter; single-parent art. 2 bis with art. 9 quater; taxpayer disability art. 4 with disabled rent art. 9 quinquies. All five regional rent routes are mutually incompatible. Arts. 4/5 cannot both apply for the same disabled person; on a joint return, use art. 5 for the qualifying disabled descendant. For the same over-75 person, the age credit cannot also be taken with disability arts. 4/5; the statute directs the qualifying disability route instead. Under the new13.3(g), art.12 first-home variable-loan interest and art.12ter rural-home relief are also **mutually incompatible**; compare the eligible alternatives instead of adding them. If the same claimant otherwise qualifies for150 interest credit and1,500 rural-home credit, the larger eligible choice is1,500, not1,650. This is the incompatibility enacted by [Ley1/2026 art.18/DF6](https://docm.jccm.es/portaldocm/descargarArchivo.do?ruta=2026/03/30/pdf/2026_2465.pdf&tipo=rutaDocm), and it applies even though interest is part of the otherwise eligible rural-home payment base. The national rent credit still has its separate state-half entitlement test ([art. 13.3](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2014-1368#a13)).

Single-parent status requires the defined separated/unmarried parent and cohabiting minor or adult child with the specified civil-law support measures, exclusive economic dependency evidenced by full descendant-minimum entitlement, and no child-maintenance annuities received. Apply the statutory exclusion for a final intentional-homicide conviction involving the former spouse/analogous partner. Older-person relief is excluded where the person stays more than **30 calendar days** in a regional/publicly contracted/subsidised older-person residential place, except properly certified temporary convalescence. For foster-child ordering, count only minors satisfying the more-than-183-day condition and exclude children adopted during the year; prescribed individual-return fostering amounts are split equally between spouses/partners. The older-person fostering exclusion for relatives through the fourth degree concerns the over-65 route; the competent department must certify the absence of related regional aid ([arts. 2 bis and 6–8](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2014-1368#a7)).

#### Castilla-La Mancha education and rent calculations

The education base includes textbooks for the statutory education-system stages and 15% of: extra-school language tuition; qualifying external support/refresher teaching; initial internet signup and the **first month's line fee only**, excluding a provider change or a business line; and qualifying study/residence away from the family municipality where the required educational offer is unavailable locally, or regionally for the specified out-of-region study. It does not grant 15% of every computer or every monthly internet bill. Obtain payment evidence and descendant-minimum entitlement. Add the eligible textbook amount to the rate-based educational amounts, cap that combined credit per child, then subtract the article's public aid, never below zero ([art. 3](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2014-1368#a3)).

Childcare instead starts with eligible costs **net of the child's public aid**, then applies 30% and the applicable 500/250 ceiling. Include qualifying registration/enrolment, full-month attendance and meals in an authorised nursery/first-cycle infant centre; exclude costs treated as the specified exempt employment benefit. Split consistently with shared descendant-minimum entitlement ([art. 3 bis](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2014-1368#a3-5)).

For the young-tenant enhanced rate, the municipality must have at most **2,500** inhabitants, or more than 2,500 and at most **10,000** while more than **30 km** from a municipality above **50,000** inhabitants, using the prescribed 1 January population. The four other regional rent routes in arts. 9 bis–9 quinquies have a **500** ceiling prorated by days the lease is in force and divided equally between entitled declarants for the same home. Under [Ley1/2026 art.18/DF6](https://docm.jccm.es/portaldocm/descargarArchivo.do?ruta=2026/03/30/pdf/2026_2465.pdf&tipo=rutaDocm), the changed15% ordinary cap is500; the separately enacted20% young-tenant rural/remote cap remains612. Eligible full-year ordinary rent4,000 gives `min(4,000 ×15%, 500) =500`. Do not automatically double those caps on a joint return or stack two qualifying categories ([arts. 9–9 quinquies](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2014-1368#a9)).

#### Castilla-La Mancha first-home savings and assistance dogs

For new **art.12octies**, the taxpayer must be under36, expressly including contributions made in the **year they turn36**. Eligible deposits must fund legal ownership/full title (which can be shared) of their **first main home in Castilla-La Mancha**, by acquisition or construction, **before six years have elapsed from account opening**. Apply the main-home definition in12ter.6(a), including actual occupation/continuity and its justified exceptions. This is a savings-stage deduction; an unrestricted bank deposit or investment account is not enough.

Keep one separately identified qualifying account per taxpayer, separate from other deposits. The balance at tax accrual must exceed the previous tax-accrual balance by at least the amount generating the deduction. Identify the account, opening date and year's balance increase on the return. Full restoration/transfer before tax accrual to a qualifying account with the same purpose and conditions preserves the specified withdrawal rule. Death before the destination deadline does not itself breach the destination requirement. Do not deduct the same eligible deposits again when buying the home. Total general-plus-savings **BI must not exceed27,000 individual/36,000 joint**; use BI, not BL or BI less descendant minima. The **joint annual credit cap is750, without automatic multiplication by the number of contributors**; allocate the allowed joint deduction in proportion to each qualifying taxpayer's contributions under12octies.3(b) and [LIRPF84.2](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2006-20764#a84). Keep the deductions previously attributed to each qualifying account, including a qualifying replacement, instead of resetting the total cap each year.

For an **individual return**, with all entitlement and balance-growth conditions satisfied, calculate `credit = min(15% ×eligible_deposits, 750, max(3000 −prior_deductions, 0))`, before available regional-tax limits. Eligible deposits6,000 with no previous deduction give750, not900. A previous750 deduction in2025 leaves2,250 of total capacity, so the same qualifying2026 deposits still give750. A qualifying36th-birthday year is included; the year they turn37 is not. On a joint return with two qualifying contributors, no prior use and otherwise sufficient credit/tax capacity, eligible deposits4,000 and2,000 produce one750 deduction, allocated500/250; two6,000 deposits produce one750, allocated375/375. The deduction starts in2025, so an ordinary two-contributor2026 joint return has at most1,500 of combined valid earlier2025 deductions, leaving enough total capacity for the750 annual cap even on the more restrictive aggregated-history reading. A claimed earlier2,800 deduction history would not be a feasible2026 fact pattern under this new relief. Breach causing loss of earlier deductions triggers regularisation under the incorporated State IRPF rules; retain actual destination and replacement-account evidence. Source: [Ley1/2026 art.18/DF6](https://docm.jccm.es/portaldocm/descargarArchivo.do?ruta=2026/03/30/pdf/2026_2465.pdf&tipo=rutaDocm), new12octies.1–6.

For **art.12nonies**, use a legally qualifying assistance dog under the incorporated national/regional definitions, with the taxpayer-and-dog assistance unit formally recognised by the competent social-welfare authority in Castilla-La Mancha or another autonomous community. Eligible actual costs are the specified vaccinations, deworming, sterilisation and other treatments **mandatory under the applicable assistance-dog rules**. Do not grant30% of every pet or optional veterinary bill. Require an invoice from a legally authorised veterinary professional/centre, **no public or private subsidy of the claimed costs**, and the art.13.2 BI limits27,000 individual/36,000 joint. Apply `min(eligible_unsubsidised_costs ×30%, 100)` per taxpayer per year throughout qualifying ownership. An otherwise eligible400 invoice yields100, not120. A merely registered pet or an unsupported assertion of assistance status does not establish entitlement; the no-subsidy condition is not an automatic net-of-aid formula. Source: [Ley1/2026 art.18/DF6](https://docm.jccm.es/portaldocm/descargarArchivo.do?ruta=2026/03/30/pdf/2026_2465.pdf&tipo=rutaDocm), new12nonies.1–5.

For residence relief, use the approved zone classification and **effective stay**, rather than a mailing address alone. Ley 2/2021 art. 5 requires municipal-register certification matching the specified local health-area registration/certification and, where applicable, compulsory-school-age children's reference-school enrolment. It includes the specified mutual-insurance healthcare certification route. DA 6 extends the risk-zone treatment to the specified agriculture-dominant intermediate-zone municipalities below 2,000 inhabitants that lost population over the five years before 1 January 2021. Do not classify a town from population alone or assume that every rural municipality qualifies ([Ley 2/2021 arts. 5, 12–13 and DA 6](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2021-11513#a5)).

For arts. 12 bis–12 quater, regional **DA 2** uses each year's 1 January municipal population but preserves the conditions/amounts from the 2021 register where later population change would reduce or remove the deduction. Check both records; crossing a modern population threshold does not automatically remove the protected entitlement. The residence deduction uses the selected percentage of **regional gross tax**, with remaining available tax controlled under the ordinary liquid-tax floor. It is not a reduction of income ([DA 2](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2014-1368#da-2)).

Rural home relief requires a home in a qualifying **Ley 2/2021 art. 12** zone, population below **5,000**, and acquisition/rehabilitation from **1 January 2021**. This is narrower than simply every municipality eligible for risk-zone residence relief. The eligible base includes acquisition costs, financing repayment/interest and permitted hedge costs net of hedge proceeds. For a single home before the separate prior-home/reinvestment adjustments, the overall ceiling is the lesser of **180,000** and the qualifying acquisition/rehabilitation amount **after** the relevant regional grants. The 180,000 ceiling belongs to the home and is allocated by ownership share; annual base cannot exceed **12,000**, including across the members of a joint return. Keep a cumulative-used-base schedule, apply prior-home deducted-investment and exempt-reinvestment restrictions, then calculate `15% × eligible annual base`. For that single-home case, compute remaining overall base after previously used amounts, then annual base `min(eligible current payments, 12,000, remaining overall base)`. The annual cap alone gives a derived maximum **1,800** before available-tax limits; it is not an unconditional annual credit ([art. 12 ter.1–12 ter.3](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2014-1368#a1-5); [AEAT clarification of the unchanged ceiling](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/ayuda/manuales-videos-folletos/manuales-practicos/irpf-2025-deducciones-autonomicas/comunidad-autonoma-castilla-mancha/adquisicion-rehabilitacion-vivienda-habitual-zonas-rurales.html)).

The regional home definition ordinarily requires **three years** of continuous use and actual occupation within **twelve months**, with the listed death/necessary-change exceptions; ownership can be shared. The article expressly includes qualifying construction/amplification, with a final construction certificate within **four years** from investment start. Structural rehabilitation requires costs exceeding **25%** of the relevant acquisition/market value excluding land, with the specified two-year acquisition comparison. Apply the end-versus-start wealth-increase test excluding financing costs and mere valuation movements. The specified separated/divorced parent's former-family-home payments have their own rule ([art. 12 ter.4–12 ter.6](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2014-1368#a1-5)).

Relocation requires an actual work-related move to the prescribed art. 12 zone, initial-year BL below the table's threshold and continued residence through the move year and next three years. On a joint return, each qualifying mover has the statutory 500 amount but only within the regional tax attributable to that mover's employment/business income. The attributable-tax worksheet under The base supplies the mixed-income/minimum and base-reduction cap calculation, with its precise signed-income boundary. Breach of the applicable rural conditions triggers statutory recapture plus interest ([arts. 12 bis/12 quater](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2014-1368#a1-6)).

The new-company route requires eligible SA/SL/labour-company/cooperative subscriptions, ownership with spouse/relatives through the third degree no more than **40%** on any day, at least **three years** holding and no executive/management role. The entity needs regional registered/tax offices and genuine economic activity. Formation requires, from the first fiscal year, one full-time employee or two part-time employees whose total hours reach one full-time job, with social registration and employment conditions maintained **24 months**. Capital increases must be within **three years** of incorporation, increase average staffing by the prescribed full-time equivalent over the preceding 12-month baseline in the next two fiscal years and maintain that increase a further **24 months**. Record the public deed identifying investor/amount and the recapture conditions. Do not import another region's different employee or investor-employment restriction into this article ([art. 12 sexies](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2014-1368#a1-8)).

The social-economy route instead needs a qualifying Ley 5/2011 entity, regional registered/tax offices, at least one full-time general-regime employee, a public deed, family ownership through the third degree at most 40% and a **five-year** holding/entity-condition period. It is incompatible with the new-company route for the same investment. Its statute specifies the recapture treatment for breached conditions ([art. 12 septies](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2014-1368#a1-9)).

For gifts, verify the precise recipient and certificate: art. 10 requires qualifying regionally registered nonprofit purposes and, for foundations, the deposited-account condition; art. 11 covers the stated regional public/research or registered nonprofit research/innovation entities. Art. 11 bis concerns inventoried regional cultural property, qualifying conservation/restoration and the specified cultural-patronage plan/recipients, with an aggregate base ceiling rather than a ceiling on tax. Detailed in-kind cultural valuations/patronage-credit interactions remain to confirm. Art. 12 quinquies' inflation relief is expressly limited to **2022** and is not claimed for 2026 ([arts. 10–12 quinquies](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2014-1368#a1-7)).

## Worked example

### Castilla-La Mancha childcare: costs net of aid, 30% and the 500 cap

Hypothetical 2026 facts: an individual resident of Castilla-La Mancha with total BI **25,000** (within the **27,000** individual common family-income gate) is the only person entitled to the descendant minimum for a one-year-old. She pays **2,400** of qualifying nursery costs and the child receives **600** of public aid for them. Start with costs net of aid: `2,400 − 600 = 1,800`; `30% × 1,800 = 540`, capped at **500** per child ([Ley 8/2013 arts. 3 bis and 13](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2014-1368#a3-5)). In the year the child turns three the cap is 250.

