# Manitoba retail sales tax v2.0

## Scope

Prepare the separate Manitoba Retail Sales Tax workpaper under The Retail Sales Tax Act, C.C.S.M.c.R130, as amended, its retail regulation and applicable service regulations. This is a retail tax, not a value-added tax; there is no general input tax credit. A specific resale/use exemption, statutory refund or permitted internal correction is not the same as a GST input credit. Manitoba Finance administers the account through TAXcess. [Act](https://web2.gov.mb.ca/laws/statutes/ccsm/r130.php?lang=en), [vendor instructions](https://www.gov.mb.ca/finance/taxation/pubs/bulletins/004.pdf).

`canada-gst-hst` owns the separate federal return, registration, supply/place, exemptions/zero-rating and input-credit mechanics. A provincial exempt sale is not automatically federal zero-rated. Reconcile explainable sales/timing/base differences instead of forcing both returns to agree. No live registration, login, return submission, payment or production-data write is performed here. [CRA distinction](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/rc4022/general-information-gst-hst-registrants.html).

| Quick reference | Entry |
|---|---|
| Currency/account | CAD; actual 7-digit RST number differs from the 15-digit tax return/account reference |
| Period | Current calendar 2026 transactions plus expressly identified historical comparisons |
| Portal | TAXcess; authorised representative and actual account assignment required |
| Filing | Actual assigned monthly, quarterly or annual period; monthly filers use electronic TAXcess |
| Main documents | Actual prescribed return and statement, sales/purchase tax reconciliation, supported refunds/adjustments and payment evidence |
| Preserved example | Acme Software Inc., Winnipeg, all original revenue and purchase facts retained |

## Ask the client first

1. Obtain legal/entity/trade name, RST/account numbers, actual registration/start/closure date, current filing assignment, premises/inventory, purchase imports, old assessments/credit balances, representative and approval authority.
2. Obtain dated invoice-level goods/service/software/cloud type, use/device/user location, customer/delivery and actual statutory Manitoba connection. Reconcile gross, provincial taxable/exempt, outside-province, marketplace and federal classifications separately.
3. For software/cloud, obtain hosting/download/access and control facts, ordinarily situated device/users, licence/user allocation, bundled optional services, custom source-code/development terms, payment date and service period crossing January 2026. Do not classify every SaaS/vendor brand identically.
4. For vendors/small businesses, obtain preceding/current expected **calendar-year taxable retail sales**, all input tax paid, resale inventory, tobacco/liquor and platform sales. For remote sellers obtain orders/solicitation/delivery/inventory and actual service relationship; a website alone is not the entire legal test.
5. Obtain tax collected/collectable, credit sales, purchases/inventory withdrawals for own use, imports with tax unpaid, exemptions/resale number/use certificates, shipping, refunds, write-offs/recoveries and supporting previous tax remittance.
6. Obtain actual accommodation, insurance, contractor/property, natural-resource/production, utility, vehicle/aircraft and First Nations facts before accepting a specialist exception. For professional services obtain Manitoba relationship, other jurisdictions, related-party and standalone/exempt-service evidence.

### Registration, small businesses and platforms

1. Establish vendor/purchaser duties under the actual Act rather than automatically registering every person or applying a federal threshold. The statute defines retail sale in the province, vendor, purchaser, platform and source/use conditions. A person importing goods for own business use can need an account even without taxable sales; the vendor bulletin describes its separate repeated-import test. [Act ss.1,5,9](https://web2.gov.mb.ca/laws/statutes/ccsm/r130.php?lang=en), [vendor registration/import instructions](https://www.gov.mb.ca/finance/taxation/pubs/bulletins/004.pdf).
2. For an outside-province goods seller, test the actual solicitation/orders/delivery or inventory conditions. The old fictional general CAD 10,000 economic nexus test is not the law read here. Service/software/cloud location and collection rules have their own actual tests. Don't let an unsupported old revenue safe harbour suppress a genuine obligation. [Act s.1(1.2) and vendor definition](https://web2.gov.mb.ca/laws/statutes/ccsm/r130.php?lang=en), [out-of-province instructions](https://www.gov.mb.ca/finance/taxation/pubs/bulletins/004.pdf), [current software/cloud guidance](https://www.gov.mb.ca/finance/taxation/pubs/bulletins/033.pdf).
3. Apply the small-business alternative from the law. Its prior and estimated current calendar-year thresholds are **not greater than** the statutory amount, with tobacco/liquor and platform restrictions, rather than a rolling 12-month universal relief. The administrative notice uses “under” the amount; for the exact boundary use the statutory did-not-exceed wording and test all the other conditions. It changes collection mechanics, not whether taxable inputs carry tax. [Act ss.5(7),9(2.3)](https://web2.gov.mb.ca/laws/statutes/ccsm/r130.php?lang=en), [notice](https://www.gov.mb.ca/finance/taxation/pubs/bulletins/noticerst2401.pdf).
4. Under that route, pay applicable tax on own-use and resale/production inputs, keep sales records and use the required tax-included invoice presentation rather than itemising an output tax not collected under the route. Test the government notice's exclusions for untaxed out-of-province inputs, liquor/tobacco and out-of-province resale inputs. If cancelling an existing account, reconcile tax-free inventory and actual cancellation; do not simply stop filing. [Actual notice](https://www.gov.mb.ca/finance/taxation/pubs/bulletins/noticerst2401.pdf), [vendor guide](https://www.gov.mb.ca/finance/taxation/pubs/bulletins/004.pdf).
5. Monitor the real threshold and expected sales. The government's one-month implementation instruction on crossing the boundary must be applied to the actual timeline and statutory obligations, not turned into unlimited retroactive grace. A platform operator and platform sales are excluded from the statutory small-business provision. [Act s.9(2.3.1)](https://web2.gov.mb.ca/laws/statutes/ccsm/r130.php?lang=en), [notice](https://www.gov.mb.ca/finance/taxation/pubs/bulletins/noticerst2401.pdf).
6. An online platform and seller can have different duties. Establish the actual statutory platform/payment role, taxable sale and collector/remittance evidence. The law allows a seller selling only through a platform to avoid its registration/remittance for those transactions where the operator remits the tax, and prevents duplicating tax already remitted on another's behalf. Direct-channel and own-use obligations remain separate. Do not assume a familiar brand or marketplace statement proves all tax was remitted. [Act ss.9(2.2),21.1-21.2](https://web2.gov.mb.ca/laws/statutes/ccsm/r130.php?lang=en).
7. Prepare the actual TAXcess/application evidence and keep approval, account and assigned period. The current vendor guide says application is free and numbers are not transferable. Preserve name/address/entity/ownership/start/cessation changes and the regulation's notice/closure duties. The old invented universal application identifier is not a substitute for the current actual document. [Vendor instructions](https://www.gov.mb.ca/finance/taxation/pubs/bulletins/004.pdf), [Reg.s.5](https://web2.gov.mb.ca/laws/regs/current/075-88r.php?lang=en).

### Supply, base, source and exemptions

8. Classify tangible goods, actual taxable service or insurance separately. Use the real selling/fair value and included charges under the Act, excluding GST as required; don't tax a vague location or uncertain service just to avoid research. Tax collected in error can still require remittance until a proper refund/correction, so overcharging is not a harmless default. [Act ss.1-4,9(2.8)](https://web2.gov.mb.ca/laws/statutes/ccsm/r130.php?lang=en).
9. Preserve the original goods/exemption jobs: groceries/food context, prescription/other medicines/medical devices, books, baby/children's clothing, residential rent/accommodation, farms/fishing, production machinery/direct agents, resale, reserve/First Nations and goods exported with supported delivery. Their actual conditions differ from federal tax and from each other. The July 2026 summary records changes to food and qualifying manufacturing/processing items; do not repeat an older blanket “all prepared food taxable” or obsolete machinery rule. Obtain the relevant statutory use, certificate, delivery and product facts. [Current Act exemptions](https://web2.gov.mb.ca/laws/statutes/ccsm/r130.php?lang=en), [July 2026 summary](https://www.gov.mb.ca/finance/taxation/pubs/bulletins/030.pdf).
10. Children's clothing has size/price/age/certification conditions, not an automatic exemption for any garment bought by a parent. Medical/prescription, school/book and farm/production goods each need their actual definition and evidence; an ISBN, verbal intended use or a business account is not sufficient on its own. Do not equate a new general machinery exemption with every production facility's inputs. [Current exemption summary and required use documents](https://www.gov.mb.ca/finance/taxation/pubs/bulletins/030.pdf).
11. Utilities are not simply “residential all exempt, commercial all taxed.” The summary distinguishes heating/exempt use, mixed-use reduced rate and approved production use. Retain actual metering, fuel/utility, use percentage and approval records; this guide does not certify every special utility amount or election. [Current utility distinctions](https://www.gov.mb.ca/finance/taxation/pubs/bulletins/030.pdf).
12. Accommodation is a taxable service subject to its actual duration, small-establishment/platform and other exclusions. The Act's lodging exemption and timeshare provisions must be considered. The old additional universal 5% provincial “lodging tax” field was not established by the RST law/current return instructions; don't add it. A real municipal accommodation levy requires its own jurisdiction, bylaw, collector, base and return, separate from the RST computation. [Act s.4(1),(1.0.1)-(1.1)](https://web2.gov.mb.ca/laws/statutes/ccsm/r130.php?lang=en).
13. Retain specialist liquor/tobacco/fuel/cannabis, insurance, mining/oil/gas/electricity, contractor/property and private vehicle/aircraft work. The presence of another tax does not itself prove an RST exemption. Apply the actual product/service and separate statute rather than asserting all tobacco or fuel treatment from a commodity label. Vehicle valuation/Autopac and insurance premium scope require their own evidence; don't reuse the ordinary software return example. [Act ss.2,2.2-2.4,4.1](https://web2.gov.mb.ca/laws/statutes/ccsm/r130.php?lang=en), [current summary](https://www.gov.mb.ca/finance/taxation/pubs/bulletins/030.pdf).

### Current professional services and software/cloud

14. The statutory taxable professional services include legal, accounting, architectural, geoscientific/engineering, security/monitoring and private investigation to the extent of the prescribed Manitoba relationship. The old claim that architecture and engineering are universally exempt is wrong. Apply the actual service definition, relationship, partial allocation and specific exemptions under M.R.22/2005. Pure general consulting or standalone teaching/financial-planning work can have different treatment; not every accountant's invoice is fully taxable. [Act s.4(1)(i)](https://web2.gov.mb.ca/laws/statutes/ccsm/r130.php?lang=en), [Services Reg.ss.2-9](https://web2.gov.mb.ca/laws/regs/current/022-2005.php?lang=en).
15. For a mixed professional engagement, trace whether accounting is provided in conjunction with a taxable/non-taxable service and the actual rule. The service regulation contains an accounting-in-nontaxable-service threshold of **less than 20%**, not the old universal 10%-taxes-everything bundle rule. Other services and software bundles have their own conditions. Obtain actual fee/optional-service allocation and do not export this one exception to all mixed supplies. [Services Reg.s.4(2) and ss.5-9](https://web2.gov.mb.ca/laws/regs/current/022-2005.php?lang=en).
16. For software, distinguish prewritten/right-to-use/system software, actual source-code modifications, custom software designed solely for a specific person and later copies/licences to others. The bulletin's comparative price tests and separate itemisation matter for qualifying modifications; mere “customised” or a single client does not prove the exemption. Retain development, ownership/code and price evidence. Correct official software bulletin is 033, not the old accounting bulletin 057. [Act software/TPP/use definitions](https://web2.gov.mb.ca/laws/statutes/ccsm/r130.php?lang=en), [bulletin 033 sections 1-5](https://www.gov.mb.ca/finance/taxation/pubs/bulletins/033.pdf).
17. From January 2026, the current statute and bulletin cover cloud access, computational capacity, storage/API and rights, including SaaS, PaaS and IaaS under their actual definitions. Test ordinarily situated users/devices and supported allocation of licences/users across jurisdictions. The same invoice cannot be assumed taxable or exempt solely by provider location. [Current statutory definitions](https://web2.gov.mb.ca/laws/statutes/ccsm/r130.php?lang=en), [bulletin 033](https://www.gov.mb.ca/finance/taxation/pubs/bulletins/033.pdf).
18. Preserve the effective-date boundary: the bulletin states that a payment **before**1January 2026 for a service period **beginning before** that date is not taxed under the cloud change even if the period extends beyond it. Before 2026, mere access to software installed on a server outside Manitoba is distinguished from a download to a Manitoba computer. Keep payment, service start and delivery/access evidence; do not retrospectively impose a2026 rule on Q1 2025. [Bulletin 033 sections 3-4](https://www.gov.mb.ca/finance/taxation/pubs/bulletins/033.pdf).
19. Optional separately described data processing, backup, retrieval, consulting, studies, procurement, training or help-line services are not all taxable merely because sold by a software business; contractual condition/optionality and the new cloud definition matter. Services to taxable software, configuration/installation, maintenance/warranty and support contracts have their own rules. A single bundled contract cannot be allocated just by renaming its components. [Bulletin 033 sections 3-4](https://www.gov.mb.ca/finance/taxation/pubs/bulletins/033.pdf).
20. The separately read amendment M.R.119/2025 adds the specified **related-corporation/partnership software-service** exemption from January 2026. It is not a blanket exemption for the software or every affiliate transaction. Verify the federal relationship/partnership test and service scope. [Actual amendment](https://web2.gov.mb.ca/laws/regs/annual/2025/119.php?lang=en), [current software services explanation](https://www.gov.mb.ca/finance/taxation/pubs/bulletins/033.pdf).
21. A Manitoba cloud provider retains control of hardware/software used to supply access and ordinarily pays the applicable tax on its own inputs rather than treating them as goods resold outright to customers. Obtain actual contract and use evidence, specific exceptions, and self-assess supported untaxed inputs. A taxable output does not automatically make every purchased input exempt. [Bulletin 033 cloud-provider rules](https://www.gov.mb.ca/finance/taxation/pubs/bulletins/033.pdf).

### Actual return, adjustments, period and payment

22. Reconcile sales and invoices to **tax collectable**, including credit/instalment sales at the applicable legal time; don't wait for cash if the tax is already due. Distinguish exemption/outside-province, platform-collected and direct supplies with their evidence. Purchases for own use, inventory withdrawal and imports with tax unpaid are separate liability rows, not an input credit offset. [Vendor guide](https://www.gov.mb.ca/finance/taxation/pubs/bulletins/004.pdf), [Act ss.2,9](https://web2.gov.mb.ca/laws/statutes/ccsm/r130.php?lang=en).
23. Use the actual customised return/statement and approved form. The vendor bulletin describes a working copy retained and a remittance copy, pre-populated account adjustments and current tax collectable/own-use purchase work. Do not invent a nine-line gross/exemption/commission/lodging form. Retain gross/net classification as a **workpaper**, transferring only the actual requested fields. [Actual vendor return instructions](https://www.gov.mb.ca/finance/taxation/pubs/bulletins/004.pdf), [Reg.s.7(8)](https://web2.gov.mb.ca/laws/regs/current/075-88r.php?lang=en).
24. Current commission is **not** a discretionary deduction for timely 2026 filing. The official 2024budget ended commissions for periods ending afterApril 2024, and the current vendor guide revision removed that feature. The old Acme assumed CAD 58 deduction was unsupported even on its own stated tax amount. Do not make a cash payment smaller because an old description mentions commission. [Budget measure](https://www.gov.mb.ca/asset_library/en/budget2024/budget2024.pdf), [vendor revision summary](https://www.gov.mb.ca/finance/taxation/pubs/bulletins/004.pdf).
25. For PST/RST paid in error, establish actual exempt purchase and original tax. The vendor guide permits specified internal adjustment against sales tax collectable, with a floor and remaining recovery in later periods, or a separate refund application. This is a corrective mechanism, not a general VAT input credit. The law/general two-year claim and documentary/authorisation requirements apply. [Act s.26](https://web2.gov.mb.ca/laws/statutes/ccsm/r130.php?lang=en), [vendor adjustment/refund instructions](https://www.gov.mb.ca/finance/taxation/pubs/bulletins/004.pdf).
26. For bad debt, retain original invoice/remitted tax, part-payments, actual write-off and proportional tax calculation, and use the proper claim period/time limit. Later recovery requires reinstating the tax attributable to recovery. A customer refund is not automatically a complete write-off or an excuse to duplicate a prior credit. [Actual bad-debt example and recovery](https://www.gov.mb.ca/finance/taxation/pubs/bulletins/004.pdf).
27. Follow the account's assigned period under Reg.7, not a table mislabelled “annual” figures that are actually monthly. Exact CAD 5,000 belongs in monthly;4999.99 remains the stated quarterly range, subject to assignment. Nil returns remain required. Monthly filers must use TAXcess; quarterly/annual paper options remain under current instructions. [Reg.s.7](https://web2.gov.mb.ca/laws/regs/current/075-88r.php?lang=en), [vendor instructions](https://www.gov.mb.ca/finance/taxation/pubs/bulletins/004.pdf).
28. Calendar the actual deadline, approval and bank cut-off. The vendor guide says no later than 4:30p.m.on the twentieth, next working day if weekend/statutory holiday. Mail must arrive; a postmark is not the receipt date. Financial-institution payment is separate from the filed return. The annual period's ordinary due date is the following January twentieth. Cessation has the regulation's early 15-day return/payment, not the normal wait. [Reg.s.7(3),(5)](https://web2.gov.mb.ca/laws/regs/current/075-88r.php?lang=en), [vendor guide](https://www.gov.mb.ca/finance/taxation/pubs/bulletins/004.pdf).
29. Reconcile account assessments, prior credit, arrears/interest and current return before authorised settlement. The guide says interest compounds monthly and its rate is set each January/July; a universal 10% penalty and a guessed “current” interest rate are not established for every failure. Obtain the specific statute/notice and effective rate, distinguish tax/interest/penalty, and refer arrears/disclosure/audit work with actual dates. [Vendor debt/receipt instructions](https://www.gov.mb.ca/finance/taxation/pubs/bulletins/004.pdf).
30. Retain invoice/contract, tax/location/exemption/number and shipping evidence, input/own-use and credit/recovery records, filed return and statement, authority, accepted acknowledgement and payment. The guide requires current fiscal year **plus** immediately preceding six years and general ledgers indefinitely; “six years total then delete” is not that rule. Retain other required litigation/claim evidence as applicable. [Vendor record-retention section](https://www.gov.mb.ca/finance/taxation/pubs/bulletins/004.pdf).

## Worked cases

### Case 1: preserve Acme Software Inc., correct the period and unsupported commission

The original Winnipeg corporation was called **Acme Software Inc.** and its stated period Q1 2025. Keep its records: Manitoba SaaS 80,000, Ontario SaaS 120,000, one-client custom software 40,000, Manitoba off-the-shelf software 15,000, Manitoba telecom 5,000 and Saskatchewan sales 10,000; total 270,000. Foreign cloud hosting for Manitoba use was 8,000. A historical 2025 return cannot be signed from these labels: server/download/access, payment/start dates and actual prior law determine the SaaS/cloud outcome. The old automatic 2025 SaaS/cloud tax and CAD 58 commission were wrong assumptions. [Actual transition](https://www.gov.mb.ca/finance/taxation/pubs/bulletins/033.pdf), [current commission elimination](https://www.gov.mb.ca/asset_library/en/budget2024/budget2024.pdf).

For a **new 2026 workpaper using the same figures**, explicitly assume ordinary taxable Manitoba SaaS/cloud beginning and paid in 2026, supported single-client custom-software exemption, outside-Manitoba use/delivery on the other streams, taxable telecom, no other adjustment, and the actual assigned reporting period. Gross 270,000 less supported excluded 170,000 gives taxable 100,000; collectable 7,000; own-use cloud tax 560; current commission zero; total 7,560. This is a supported-input scenario, not a declaration that every Acme 2025 invoice has that tax. [Current software/cloud](https://www.gov.mb.ca/finance/taxation/pubs/bulletins/033.pdf), [general rate](https://web2.gov.mb.ca/laws/statutes/ccsm/r130.php?lang=en), [vendor instructions](https://www.gov.mb.ca/finance/taxation/pubs/bulletins/004.pdf).

```oa-check
name: Manitoba 2026 Acme supported cloud and software return workpaper
inputs:
  mb_saas: 80000
  ontario_saas: 120000
  supported_exempt_custom: 40000
  mb_prewritten: 15000
  mb_telecom: 5000
  saskatchewan_sales: 10000
  mb_taxable_untaxed_cloud_input: 8000
steps:
  - gross_sales_workpaper: mb_saas + ontario_saas + supported_exempt_custom + mb_prewritten + mb_telecom + saskatchewan_sales
  - supported_exclusions: ontario_saas + supported_exempt_custom + saskatchewan_sales
  - taxable_sales: gross_sales_workpaper - supported_exclusions
  - sales_tax_collectable: taxable_sales * 0.07
  - own_use_tax: mb_taxable_untaxed_cloud_input * 0.07
  - current_commission: 0
  - current_liability_before_other_adjustments: sales_tax_collectable + own_use_tax - current_commission
expected:
  gross_sales_workpaper: 270000
  supported_exclusions: 170000
  taxable_sales: 100000
  sales_tax_collectable: 7000
  own_use_tax: 560
  current_commission: 0
  current_liability_before_other_adjustments: 7560
```

Do not assume that corporate revenue makes the company a valid quarterly filer; obtain its real assignment and ordinary monthly collectable amount. Keep custom-development/source-code/price evidence, Ontario/Saskatchewan device/use/delivery support, and the cloud allocation and payment/service dates. Prepare the separate federal workpaper and explain differences rather than forcing its sales line to 270,000.

### Case 2: ordinary taxable licence and proportional bad debt

For an ordinary non-exempt CAD 1,000 licence with a separately supported federal standard 5% taxable supply, provincial tax is 70 and federal tax 50, each on the stated exclusive base, total 1,120. No provincial input credit follows merely because the buyer uses the software in business. [Act/base](https://web2.gov.mb.ca/laws/statutes/ccsm/r130.php?lang=en), [CRA separate tax](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/rc4022/general-information-gst-hst-registrants.html).
