## Bulgarian tax system at a glance

Bulgaria taxes personal and corporate income at a flat 10%. Bulgaria adopted the euro on 1 January 2026. Amounts that the tax laws still print in leva are read as euro at BGN 1.95583 = EUR 1: the lev amount is divided by the full rate and the result is rounded to the cent (Euro Introduction Act arts. 11(2), 12, 13). This overview gives the headline figure for each tax and points to the skill that holds the working rules. Where a figure here differs from the domain skill, the domain skill wins.

Tax year covered: 2026 (calendar year). Every figure below was read in the consolidated texts of the acts that the National Revenue Agency publishes on its legislation page (https://nra.bg/wps/portal/nra/zakonodatelstvo), or in the State Gazette or Parliament copy named on the row.

## Who is taxed, and on what

- **Individual tax basis.** A resident individual is taxed on income from Bulgaria and abroad (ZDDFL art. 6). A non-resident is taxed only on Bulgarian-source income (art. 7). An individual is resident if they have a permanent address in Bulgaria, stay in Bulgaria more than 183 days in any 12-month period, are sent abroad by the Bulgarian state or a Bulgarian enterprise, or have their centre of vital interests in Bulgaria (art. 4(1)). See bulgaria-income-tax and bg-cross-border.
- **Corporate tax basis.** A company incorporated under Bulgarian law is resident and taxed on its profits and income from all sources in Bulgaria and abroad (ZKPO art. 3). A foreign company is taxed on the profits of a permanent establishment in Bulgaria and on listed Bulgarian-source income (art. 12). See bg-corporate-income-tax and bg-cross-border.
- **Tax authority.** The National Revenue Agency (Национална агенция за приходите, НАП, nra.bg). Corporate returns are filed with the NRA territorial directorate where the company is registered (ZKPO art. 92(2)). Social and health contributions are covered in bulgaria-social-contributions.
- **Social security framework.** Pension, illness and maternity, unemployment and work accident contributions under the Social Insurance Code (KSO art. 6(1)), plus health insurance, on insurable income between a monthly floor and ceiling (table above). Contribution rates and splits are in bulgaria-social-contributions; payroll withholding is in bulgaria-payroll.
- **Transfer pricing.** Where related persons deal on terms that differ from those between unrelated persons and that affect the tax base, the base is set and taxed on the terms unrelated persons would have agreed (ZKPO art. 15). This covers domestic as well as cross-border deals. See bg-transfer-pricing.

## Tax year and deadlines

- **Tax year.** The calendar year for companies (ZKPO art. 21(1)). A company formed during the year has a first period from formation to 31 December (art. 21(2)). Individuals are taxed on the income of the calendar year and file in the following year (ZDDFL art. 53(1)).
- **Currency.** Euro from 1 January 2026. Old lev amounts convert at BGN 1.95583 = EUR 1 (Euro Introduction Act arts. 11(2), 12, 13). For example, the BGN 500 cap on the early-filing discount is EUR 255.65. The annual personal tax is rounded to the whole euro after the deductions and any discount (ZDDFL art. 48(8)).
- **Annual personal tax return.** 10 January to 30 April 2027 for 2026 income. Traders, including sole traders, and art. 29a farmers file from 1 March to 30 June 2027 (ZDDFL arts. 51(1), 53(1)). The 5% early-filing discount is in the table above (art. 53(6)).
- **Annual corporate tax return and payment.** Return from 1 March to 30 June of the following year (ZKPO art. 92(2)). The tax, less advance payments, is due by 30 June (art. 93(1)). Companies make monthly or quarterly advance payments (art. 83(1)), except the companies exempted in the table above (art. 83(2)).
- **VAT returns.** The VAT tax period is the calendar month (ZDDS art. 87(2)). Returns are filed by the 14th of the month after the period (art. 125(5)). See bulgaria-vat-return.

## Worked example

A resident freelance consultant (liberal profession, not a lawyer or notary) has 2026 fees of EUR 40,000 and owes EUR 6,000 of her own social and health contributions for the year. She paid no advance tax. She files electronically by 31 March 2027, pays by then and has no debts under enforcement.

1. Statutory cost deduction 25%: taxable income 40,000 x 75% = EUR 30,000 (ZDDFL art. 29(1)(3)).
2. Less her own contributions: annual tax base 30,000 - 6,000 = EUR 24,000 (art. 30, item 1).
3. Tax at 10%: EUR 2,400 (art. 48(1)).
4. Early-filing discount 5% of 2,400 = EUR 120, under the EUR 255.65 cap (art. 53(6)).
5. Tax to pay 2,400 - 120 = EUR 2,280, already a whole euro (art. 48(8)).

## Ask the client first

1. Is the person an individual or a company, and is the individual resident in Bulgaria (permanent address, 183 days, centre of vital interests)?
2. Which kind of income: employment, freelance, sole trader, rent, dividends, or company profit?
3. For a company: net sales in the year before the preceding year, and is it part of a group with EUR 750 million revenue?
4. Bulgarian turnover for the calendar year, for the VAT threshold.
5. Does the person plan to file electronically and pay by 31 March?
