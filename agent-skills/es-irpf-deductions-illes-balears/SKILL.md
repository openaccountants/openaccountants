# Spain: Illes Balears regional IRPF credits, 2026

**Sourced from the law.** This revision has not had an independent whole-pack review or accountant sign-off. Calculate regional credits after establishing tax residence and the taxpayer's facts. This Guide holds the Illes Balears catalogue of [es-irpf-deductions](es-irpf-deductions.md), moved here unchanged on 6 November 2026, so that each Guide stays within the size the site can deliver. Specific unresolved interactions are listed in Sources.

## Who this applies to

An ordinary IRPF resident whose community of residence for 2026 under [LIRPF art. 72](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2006-20764#a72) is Illes Balears. A regional credit reduces regional tax, not the income base. Before applying any row below, use [es-irpf-deductions](es-irpf-deductions.md) for the residence test, the deduction ledger, the state/regional order of credits, the national credits, deadlines, penalties and the questions to ask, and [es-income-tax](es-income-tax.md) for the bases, scales and minima. Where a row below refers to "the attributable-tax worksheet under The base", that worksheet is [es-irpf-deductions-attributable-tax](es-irpf-deductions-attributable-tax.md); national credits, the deduction ledger and any calculation "above" outside this catalogue are in [es-irpf-deductions](es-irpf-deductions.md#the-base). Basque and Navarre residents use [es-foral-income-tax](es-foral-income-tax.md); an IRNR taxpayer or an art. 93 incoming worker does not take these credits.

## Rates, bands and thresholds

All amounts are EUR. `BI` means the specified sum of general and savings **taxable income before base reductions** (base imponible); `BL` means base liquidable after the relevant reductions. Use the definition in each row: an income limit based on BI cannot be replaced with gross salary, BL or take-home pay. The table year is 2026 unless a transition is stated.

### Illes Balears: income gates, housing and island conditions

Test each credit's own income limit, family status and island conditions. `BI` is general plus savings base imponible; `I/J` means individual/joint filing. The table uses the current wording for an ordinary **31 December 2026** accrual. [DL 1/2026 final provision 1](https://www.boe.es/buscar/doc.php?id=BOE-A-2026-9525) expressly renews mortgage-interest relief for 2026 and substitutes the Middle East-war aid route. [Ley 4/2026 final provision 67](https://www.boe.es/buscar/doc.php?id=BOE-A-2026-15579) amended rental/landlord rules and increased birth/adoption amounts; its general entry date is **14 June 2026**, confirmed by [ATIB's notice](https://atib.es/General/Novedad.aspx?idTexto=16987&idTipoTexto=1&lang=es). Do not apply this current wording automatically to a short period ending before that date; the transition remains to confirm.

Sustainability relief covers the stated renewable-energy and water-saving/reuse/sanitation investments in a Balearic habitual home of the taxpayer or an LAU tenant. **At least one energy-rating level of improvement** and registered before/after certificates are required; do not grant it for an invoice alone or waive that condition for water works. A landlord must have at least a one-year contract with the same tenant, the IBAVI deposit, declared property-rental income and no passing of the investment cost to the tenant ([art. 3](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2014-6925#a3)).

The enhanced rent groups are age **under 30**, disability at least **33%**, entitlement to an ascendant/descendant disability minimum, the qualifying cohabiting large/single-parent family, or qualifying self-employed registration at least **183 days** in the year. AEAT treats these as their own eligibility alternatives; do not impose the ordinary under-36/over-65 condition again on a disabled or self-employed claimant. Require actual occupation and a contract of at least **one year**. For at least half the period, neither the claimant nor a family-unit member can hold any full-ownership/use/enjoyment share in another home less than **70 km** away, except another island, outside the Balearics or a home yielding property-rental income that year. There must be no entitlement to a habitual-home investment deduction for the same period. Only the named tenant's eligible payments count; joint filing does not make a non-tenant spouse a tenant ([art. 3 bis](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2014-6925#a3bis); [AEAT rent interpretation](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/ayuda/manuales-videos-folletos/manuales-practicos/irpf-2025-deducciones-autonomicas/comunidad-autonoma-illes-balears/arrendamiento-vivienda-habitual.html)).

For rent and textbooks, qualifying general large/single-parent families raise the ordinary **33,000 I / 52,800 J** income gate by **20%**, to **39,600 I / 63,360 J**; special-category large families raise it by **30%**, to **42,900 I / 68,640 J**. The particular family references matter: [Ley 8/2018 art. 6](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2018-13193#ar-6) includes the stated one-disabled-child single-parent family, while [art. 7.7](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2018-13193#ar-7) concerns single-parent families with at least two children. Obtain the recognised category and the article's cohabitation/parental-authority facts rather than treating every unmarried parent as eligible ([arts. 3 bis and 4](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2014-6925#a4)).

Mortgage-interest relief concerns a variable-rate loan/credit financing the habitual home, requires the bank's comparison certificate and is incompatible with the national DT 18 home-investment credit. A fall in interest does not create a negative credit. The landlord routes require a whole-home letting in **one contract**, at least a one-year initial term, the IBAVI deposit, declared property income and BI at most **52,800 I / 84,480 J** under current art. 4 quater.4. Insurance relief is compatible with either other landlord route, but those two routes are mutually incompatible. For the unused-home route, the home must not have been let in either of the **two preceding fiscal years** and rent per tenant must not exceed **15 per useful square metre per month**. Its eligible costs are damage insurance, conservation/repair, applicable community/tax costs, lease formalisation and energy certificates, paid that year. For renewal/replacement relief, the new lease must begin before **six months** from the prior lease ending; rent must stay unchanged or rise no more than the statutory annual-update index. Use the bounded net-yield/time method below where its facts apply; early-accrual and mixed-contract attribution remain specifically unconfirmed ([arts. 3 quater and 4 quater](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2014-6925#a4-13)).

**Renewal/replacement net-yield method, labelled statutory inference.** For a sole owner with a full 2026 calendar-year tax period, a qualifying new lease beginning after 14 June 2026 and no 2026 rent from an earlier contract, compute that home's rental income under [LIRPF 22](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2006-20764#a22), less its supported deductible expenses and depreciation under [23.1](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2006-20764#a23). This is net yield **before** the separate housing-rental percentage reduction in 23.2, rather than gross rent or reduced net yield. With positive eligible net yield `N_h`, calculate `min(50%×N_h,800)` for each qualifying home, then cap their sum at 2,000 per taxpayer, within available regional gross tax. Retain actual income, expense and ownership records; the example does not create an expense-allocation shortcut.

The 800 wording refers to the **tax period**. [LIRPF 12–13](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2006-20764#a12) make that period the calendar year, shortened on death. On this labelled reading, a midyear lease does not itself shorten a surviving taxpayer's tax period: use the 800 ceiling in this full-year branch, rather than silently multiplying it by rental days. [Ley 4/2026 DF 67.3/DF 72](https://www.boe.es/buscar/doc.php?id=BOE-A-2026-15579) supplies the amended wording and 14 June 2026 commencement. Neither provision expressly rules on this numerical time-allocation example; a contrary administrative lease-day interpretation has not been confirmed. Short tax periods and transactions predating the amendment need their own date analysis.

**Separating example.** The sole owner's previous lease ended 31 December 2025. A replacement whole-home lease runs from 15 June 2026 for an initial year, before six months have elapsed; the monthly rent remains 800, and the deposit, income, documentary and other 4 quaterconditions pass. It charges 400 for 15–30 June and 4,800 forJuly–December:5,200 total 2026 rent. Verified expenses/depreciation attributable to that qualifying rental income are 4,000, giving 1,200 net yield and **600 credit**, subject to available regional gross tax. The 50%rate applies to 1,200; it is not applied again after a national housing-rental percentage reduction. This full-tax-period reading does not replace 800 with `800×200/365`, which would instead reduce this particular 600 result to 438.36 under the competing lease-day approach. With three independently qualifying homes and net yields 1,200/2,000/3,000, the home amounts 600/800/800 sum to 2,200; the taxpayer ceiling gives **2,000**, not 2,200. These are derived controls, not published administrative worked examples.

This supplies the stated positive-net-yield, sole-owner, post-commencement full-year branch. Allocation between earlier and replacement contracts in the same 2026 income stream, co-owner/joint-return cap attribution, short-period proportional mechanics and early-accrual transition remain unconfirmed. Do not infer zero relief or a general lease-day rule from those limits ([art 4 quater.3–4](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2014-6925#a4-13)).

Temporary island relocation requires the **same employment relationship**, actual rented-home occupation, IBAVI deposit, landlord identification and evidence, and a move lasting no more than **three years**; each eligible joint-return member can claim their own credit. The occupation/eviction route covers IBI, waste tax, home utilities, ordinary community costs, conservation/maintenance and recovery-of-possession court costs. In a vulnerability-based suspended eviction, the landlord must also qualify for the rental bad-debt expense in that same fiscal year ([arts. 4 quinquies–4 sexties](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2014-6925#a4-16)).

Textbooks must support the second infant-education cycle, primary, compulsory secondary, bachillerato or specific vocational education, and the child must generate the descendant minimum. The 350 cap uses the enhanced rent groups above, except that the textbook age route is **under 30**. Foreign-language relief concerns extra-curricular language learning by children in those stages; do not import the textbook family income uplift into art. 4 bis. Split these child credits equally between cohabiting parents filing individually and retain invoices. Higher-education relief requires economic dependence, the descendant minimum, study outside the taxpayer's Balearic home island, at least a complete academic course or **30 credits**, and descendant income no more than **8,000**; claim in the year the academic course starts and share between cohabiting entitled individual ascendants ([arts. 4–4 ter](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2014-6925#a4-12)).

The disability credit expressly allows each individually filing spouse entitled to the descendant minimum to claim the **full** art. 6 amount. This is not the equal-sharing rule used for the education credits. Under-six care requires income-generating employment/self-employment, qualifying zero-to-three nursery costs, three-to-six school custody/meals/activities, or an employed carer registered in household Social Security. Pay by card, transfer, named cheque or credit-institution deposit. Enhanced care groups match rent except the age route is **under 36**; family income gates rise by 20% or 30% as stated above. The statutory day-proration and sharing rules are applied below ([arts.6–6bis](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2014-6925#a6-12)).

**Under-six child-day calculation.** Keep a separate record for each eligible child: supported expense, paid service period, birth/adoption/fostering date, sixth birthday, qualifying days and number of entitled individual filers. The exact [AEAT return instructions10.4.18](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/ayuda/manuales-videos-folletos/manuales-ayuda-presentacion/irpf-2025/10-cumplimentacion-irpf-deducciones-autonomicas/10_4-illes-balears/10_4_18-gastos-relativos-descendientes-acogidos-anos.html) administer the660/900 limit **per child** and require child-by-child inputs. For2026 use the child's actual qualifying days `d/365` when birth/adoption/fostering or age-out requires proration. Count days from birth/placement and only days before the sixth birthday, and exclude the post-birthday part of that month's expense. Include only expense for the months in which the claimant undertakes the required income-generating work/activity. Calculate the eligible percentage against each child's supported cost, subject to that child's prorated cap, then add the child amounts. Do not replace two overlapping children's periods with a single union of days: that would discard a separately entered child's cap. This follows AEAT's expressly stated per-child implementation of [art6bis](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2014-6925#a6-12), whose practical-manual summary uses plural/sum-of-days wording.

For two entitled parents filing separately, divide the child's applicable maximum equally as the practical manual instructs; on a joint return do not divide it between the two spouses. For the ordinary same-rate/equal-payment case, each parent uses their own half of qualifying cost and half the child's prorated maximum. Thus a full-year child with2,000 eligible shared cost gives660 on the joint return or330 to each equally paying entitled individual parent. Two full-year children each with2,000 eligible cost give1,320 on the joint return, or660 to each such individual parent. With twins born1July2026, each has184 qualifying days: each ordinary child cap is `660×184/365=332.7123…`; sufficient qualifying expense gives665.42 across both children on the joint return. At the enhanced rate the corresponding combined cap is `2×900×184/365=907.3972…`, or907.40. Keep full precision until the return's monetary display; these amounts do not prescribe a special statutory rounding convention.

**Unequal payments and different personal rates on individual returns.** Apply the published AEAT administrative worksheet separately to each entitled claimant and each child. Establish that both taxpayers meet the existing work, income, child/service and payment conditions and file individual returns. Use the claimant's **own evidenced eligible payments** `Eij`, their own rate `ri` (40% or50%) and their own annual maximum `Li` (660 or900). With that child's qualifying-day fraction `fj` established above, calculate `creditij = min(ri × Eij, Li × fj /2)`; add the child results for that claimant. If only one taxpayer is entitled, the two-entitled-person sharing condition does not apply. Do not divide the claimant's own payment by two again, transfer the other payer's expense, or reallocate an unused half-cap. A shared bank account does not itself prove an unequal personal-payment allocation. The [practical manual](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/ayuda/manuales-videos-folletos/manuales-practicos/irpf-2025-deducciones-autonomicas/comunidad-autonoma-illes-balears/gastos-descendientes-acogidos-menores-seis-conciliacion.html) expressly divides the **maximum** for two entitled individual filers; the existing return instructions expressly require payments by the titular, entitled-person count and child-day inputs. This supplies the ordinary published administrative method under current6bis, continued from the2025 instructions where the relevant statutory rate/sharing provisions are unchanged; it does not assume a2027 form box or procedure.

For one full-year child and two ordinary40%claimants, actual own payments of1,500and500give `min(600,330)=330` and `min(200,330)=200`, **530total**. Pooling2,000then dividing a660credit into330each would give the smaller payer130more than this own-payment method. If the500payer independently qualifies for the50%/900enhancement through at least183days in the relevant self-employed Social Security regime and the1,500payer remains ordinary, their results are `min(250,450)=250` and `min(600,330)=330`, **580total**. Reversing which payer has the enhancement gives `min(750,450)=450` and `min(200,330)=200`, **650total**. These are individual-return household totals before available regional quota, with all separate eligibility gates assumed established. The statutory enhancement is tested for the actual claimant; one person's age/registration condition does not automatically upgrade the other person's rate. Actual public Renta2025OPEN1.19 inputs corroborate each of the330/200/250/450claimant amounts; this is2025 software evidence, not a binding ruling or a2026-income simulator claim.

**Precise sharing interpretation boundary.** Current [6bis(4)](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2014-6925#a6-12) refers to the deduction being shared equally, while the practical manual expressly shares the maximum and the form asks for own payments. The positive worksheet above states that published administrative application; it does not claim that the statutory wording was amended or that a contrary whole-credit-sharing challenge has been authoritatively decided. The existing same-rate/equal-payment and joint examples remain as stated above. A mixed-personal-rate joint return needs its own supported joint attribution and cannot be computed merely by pooling individual rates or transferring half-caps; that narrower joint interaction remains unconfirmed here.

The original equal-payment sharing controls above assume equally paying, equally entitled parents at the same rate. For unequal payments or different personal rates on individual returns, use the supplied individual-return worksheet above. Neither set of controls transfers enhanced status between parents or establishes entitlement for a non-paying parent. The mixed-rate joint-return and statutory-interpretation boundaries remain as stated above.

Birth requires Balearic fiscal residence in the **preceding year**; adoption does not copy that condition. Child order counts cohabiting natural/adopted children generating the minimum at accrual. Birth's current sharing rule halves the amount for each entitled parent when two parents are involved and do not file jointly together, **even if only one qualifies**. Each parent's pre-existing children can give a different half-amount; a joint return in that case adds those two halves. Adoption's sharing provision instead refers to two taxpayers with entitlement, and excludes adoption of a spouse/partner's child. Claim adoption in the Civil Registry inscription year. Both credits take priority over other regional credits and are incompatible with the regional birth aid specified in their articles. Apply the current birth/adoption amounts above only after establishing the relevant period and sharing facts ([arts. 6 ter–6 quater](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2014-6925#a6-14)).

Insufficient regional tax does not make these two family credits an automatic negative regional liability. Their articles authorise a **separate regional payment request**. For birth, [Order 37/2024 arts. 2–7](https://www.caib.es/seucaib/ca/arxiuServlet?id=6261230) supplies **IB50** advance payment, normally within **15 working days** of birth, and **IB52** insufficient-tax payment, from the day after the annual-return filing window ends through **30 September of that filing year**. Missing the advance deadline does not remove the ordinary return claim. Test prior-year income for the advance and reconcile actual birth-year entitlement and any advance on the return. IB51/IB53 handle the corresponding repayment, with art. 10's one-month deadlines and triggers; current statutory entitlement controls where an older form/order predates an amendment. The current adoption payment procedure has not been confirmed here; do not present the birth-only forms as adoption forms ([arts. 6 ter–6 quater](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2014-6925#a6-14); [Order 37/2024](https://www.caib.es/seucaib/ca/arxiuServlet?id=6261230)).

Older-person care covers the taxpayer or minimum-entitled ascendants over **65**; disability relief extends to the taxpayer or dependants with at least **33%** disability and entitlement to an art. 60 disability-minimum component. Eligible services are residential/day-centre stays, day-centre custody/meals/activities and an employed carer registered in Social Security. Require the specified bank payment methods. For a shared ascendant each claimant can claim their own full qualifying deduction; for shared descendants on individual returns divide equally. Art. 6 quinquies is incompatible with the under-six care credit. ALS relief covers the taxpayer and minimum-entitled ascendants/descendants, requires invoices and an ALS causal connection, excludes health-insurance premiums and unrelated costs, and is expressly compatible with the older/disabled-care credit ([arts. 6 quinquies–6 sexties](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2014-6925#a6-16)).
