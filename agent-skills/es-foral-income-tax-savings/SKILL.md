# Spain: foral resident savings income, gains and rental income, 2026

This Guide holds part of [es-foral-income-tax](es-foral-income-tax.md), moved here unchanged on 6 November 2026 so that each Guide stays within the size the site can deliver. Built from the law; not yet approved by an accountant.

## Who this applies to

An ordinary resident whose foral IRPF is Bizkaia, Álava, Gipuzkoa or Navarra under the residence tests in [es-foral-income-tax](es-foral-income-tax.md#who-this-applies-to), with savings income, a disposal or rental income. Apply those residence tests and the competent territory first; the general and savings scales and the annual settlement stay in es-foral-income-tax.

## The base

### Basque resident property-gain measurement

**Basque resident ordinary non-business property disposals:** under local PIT44–45, compute net proceeds as the actual sale amount, subject to the statutory normal-market-value floor, less qualifying seller-paid disposal expenses and taxes. Acquisition value comprises purchase cost plus qualifying acquisition expenses/taxes and investment/improvement costs, excluding interest, less the depreciation required by the local rule, including its minimum depreciation. Apply [the2026 PIT coefficient table](es-foral-non-resident-tax.md#complete2026-non-pe-property-gain-coefficients) separately to acquisition/cost components by payment year and depreciation by corresponding year; preserve the31December1994 exception and any applicable historic relief. If there are improvements, distinguish the proceeds attributable to each component under44.2. This is the same gain-measurement rule incorporated by non-PE IRNR24. For a **resident**, place the resulting non-business disposal gain/loss in the local savings-disposal sub-basket, apply local66 netting/carryforward rules and then the resident savings scale in76 and applicable credits. The IRNR flat19% tax and nonresident purchaser-advance example do not determine the resident's liability. Business-asset disposals retain the business-income route in this Guide.

Authorities: [Bizkaia NF13/2013 arts.25,44–45,63(c),66,76](https://www.bizkaia.eus/documents/880307/15187815/ca_13_2013.pdf/ce52e403-4fca-9ef7-bf85-2505ba29f921?t=1784098584068), [Álava NF33/2013 same articles](https://web.araba.eus/documents/d/araba/indice_norma-foral_irpf_cas-4-pdf), [Gipuzkoa NF3/2014 same articles](https://www.gipuzkoa.eus/documents/2456431/8.0759126E7/NF%203-2014%20(2026-0).pdf/8ab5c95c-3f89-8fdf-5cb8-01950e3983cc).

### Ordinary home and small-disposal gain exemptions

Calculate the gain under the selected territory's disposal rules before applying an exemption. In **Bizkaia, Álava and Gipuzkoa**, local article42(b)/(c) exempts an onerous disposal of the habitual home by a person in recognised severe or great dependency, or by a person **older than65**. The age-based route covers the **first400,000 of gain and one disposal only**. It is not a400,000 sale-proceeds ceiling. The dependency route is a separate paragraph without that age-based400,000 limit. For a qualifying first age-based disposal with gain450,000, exempt400,000 and retain50,000 for the applicable savings computation. A second disposal does not obtain a fresh age-based allowance. A person aged66 can meet the Basque age condition; that age alone does not meet Navarra's different condition. [Bizkaia42](https://www.bizkaia.eus/documents/880307/15187815/ca_13_2013.pdf/ce52e403-4fca-9ef7-bf85-2505ba29f921?t=1784098584068), [Álava42](https://web.araba.eus/documents/d/araba/indice_norma-foral_irpf_cas-4-pdf), [Gipuzkoa42.1/2](https://www.gipuzkoa.eus/documents/2456431/8.0759126E7/NF%203-2014%20(2026-0).pdf/8ab5c95c-3f89-8fdf-5cb8-01950e3983cc).

In **Navarra**, article39.5(b) covers the **first onerous disposal** of the habitual home by a person **older than70**, or in recognised severe/great dependency. Its exempt gain is capped at **300,000**, with the excess taxable; the provision expressly denies a further claim to taxpayers who already used this exemption in an earlier tax period. A qualifying gain350,000 therefore leaves50,000 taxable, including under the Navarra dependency route. Article39.5(d) separately covers onerous disposals whose **aggregate proceeds for the calendar year do not exceed3,000**: exempt gain up to50% of those aggregate proceeds and tax the excess. With annual proceeds3,000 and gain1,800, exempt1,500 and retain300 taxable; with proceeds3,000 and gain1,200 the whole gain is exempt. Annual proceeds3,000.01 fail the proceeds condition, so this small-disposal paragraph gives no exemption. Aggregate all relevant disposals before testing the annual ceiling. [Navarra39.5](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BON-n-2008-90013&p=20260420&tn=1).

Habitual-home status is an evidence condition, not the address label on the sale deed. The ordinary Basque definition requires at least **three years' continuous residence** and effective permanent occupation within **twelve months** of acquisition/completion, subject to the stated necessary-move, death and employment-housing exceptions. For the age/dependency and reinvestment exemptions, a home qualifying at disposal or on any day within the previous **two years** remains within the specific former-home rule. B67.4 and G64.4 also preserve status after a required move to specified relatives' homes or residential care because of dependency/serious illness, provided there is no different use of the old home; do not insert that additional text into A61.4. B/A/G have their own annex/single-registered-property and family-main-home conditions. [B regulation67](https://www.bizkaia.eus/documents/880307/15187815/ca_47_2014.pdf/c17e2f7e-e4dd-789c-235e-21add02afe5e?t=1710491329241), [A regulation61](https://web.araba.eus/documents/d/araba/indice-reglamento_irpf_cas-2-pdf), [G regulation64](https://www.gipuzkoa.eus/documents/2456431/8.0755457E7/DF%2033-2014%20(2026-2).pdf/7e4c25eb-a139-fa5f-d310-deaa4c4bcffd). Navarra's own regulation40.1/6 supplies its three-year, twelve-month and previous-two-year conditions and necessary-move exceptions; its reinvestment definition includes a simultaneously acquired storage room and at most two garages, rather than the Basque single-registered-property test. [Navarra regulation40](https://www.navarra.es/es/web/normativa-hacienda/-/reglamento-del-impuesto-sobre-la-renta-de-las-personas-f%C3%ADsicas-df-174/1999-de-24-de-mayo-?print=true).

Basque42(e) also exempts qualifying habitual-home mortgage dation in payment and judicial/notarial foreclosure, including the stated debtor/guarantor cases. It requires the specified professional mortgage creditor and insufficient other assets/rights to pay the whole debt and avoid disposal. It is not an exemption for every distressed sale. A42 and G42.2 contain additional former-family-home provisions for judicial separation/partnership dissolution; apply their actual residence conditions. [B42(e)](https://www.bizkaia.eus/documents/880307/15187815/ca_13_2013.pdf/ce52e403-4fca-9ef7-bf85-2505ba29f921?t=1784098584068), [A42](https://web.araba.eus/documents/d/araba/indice_norma-foral_irpf_cas-4-pdf), [G42](https://www.gipuzkoa.eus/documents/2456431/8.0759126E7/NF%203-2014%20(2026-0).pdf/8ab5c95c-3f89-8fdf-5cb8-01950e3983cc).

### Replacement-home reinvestment method

Use **B/A/G law49 with B regulation49, A46 or G45**, or **Navarra law45.1 with regulation40**. Establish both homes' local habitual-home conditions and qualifying acquisition/rehabilitation evidence. Álava and Gipuzkoa law49 expressly require full ownership of both homes, allowing shared full ownership with other co-owners; bare ownership/usufruct alone does not satisfy that sentence. B/A law49.3 separately admits the specified former family home left under a judicial separation/partnership-dissolution decision, subject to continuing habitual-home status for the former spouse/partner and, in B, the applicable descendants. Obtain that judicial/residence evidence before using the exception. For an ordinary positive-gain case, let `G` be the properly measured gain, `P` the tax transfer value and `L` outstanding principal of the loan used to acquire the sold home. The reinvestment target is `T = P − L`, **not the gain and not the original mortgage amount**. Let `R` be the eligible timely amount reinvested. If `R ≥ T`, the gain is fully exempt; if `0 ≤ R < T`, `exempt_gain = G × R / T` and `taxable_gain = G − exempt_gain`. These formulas assume a positive target and gain; a zero/negative-target case needs its own actual transaction treatment rather than division by zero. A sale with P300,000, outstanding acquisition principal100,000, gain100,000 and eligible reinvestment150,000 gives target200,000 and exempt gain75,000, leaving25,000 taxable. Dividing by gross300,000 would incorrectly leave50,000 taxable. [B law49](https://www.bizkaia.eus/documents/880307/15187815/ca_13_2013.pdf/ce52e403-4fca-9ef7-bf85-2505ba29f921?t=1784098584068), [A law49](https://web.araba.eus/documents/d/araba/indice_norma-foral_irpf_cas-4-pdf), [G law49](https://www.gipuzkoa.eus/documents/2456431/8.0759126E7/NF%203-2014%20(2026-0).pdf/8ab5c95c-3f89-8fdf-5cb8-01950e3983cc), [N45.1](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BON-n-2008-90013&p=20260420&tn=1).

The ordinary window is **two years after disposal**; the local regulations also admit the stated qualifying new-home acquisition within the **two preceding years**. Keep the actual acquisition/payment dates and destination of proceeds. For an instalment-price disposal, apply the rule requiring each receipt to be devoted to the qualifying purpose within the tax period in which it is received. If reinvestment is deferred beyond the disposal year, state the intention to reinvest in that gain year's return. The Basque regulations assimilate qualifying rehabilitation under local law87.6. Navarra40.3 includes acquisition/rehabilitation payments and buyer-borne acquisition costs;40.8 requires the competent body's certificate for qualifying rehabilitation. Its40.7 construction route requires completion within four years from investment commencement, with a further four years for the stated developer-insolvency case and investment/insolvency documents attached to the initial-breach-year return. For other exceptional construction delays beyond the taxpayer’s control, request an extension within thirty days after the initial deadline is missed, with reasons and the necessary completion period (at most four additional years); silence for three months permits treating the request as refused. The twelve-month occupation clock in the insolvency case starts on delivery; do not treat an unfinished build as an unconditional completed acquisition. [B49](https://www.bizkaia.eus/documents/880307/15187815/ca_47_2014.pdf/c17e2f7e-e4dd-789c-235e-21add02afe5e?t=1710491329241), [A46](https://web.araba.eus/documents/d/araba/indice-reglamento_irpf_cas-2-pdf), [G45](https://www.gipuzkoa.eus/documents/2456431/8.0755457E7/DF%2033-2014%20(2026-2).pdf/7e4c25eb-a139-fa5f-d310-deaa4c4bcffd), [N40](https://www.navarra.es/es/web/normativa-hacienda/-/reglamento-del-impuesto-sobre-la-renta-de-las-personas-f%C3%ADsicas-df-174/1999-de-24-de-mayo-?print=true).

**Basque acquisition-date gate:** the new law49 justification-of-move condition applies to habitual homes **acquired from1January2026**, under B NF2/2025 DF3, A NF3/2025 DF1 and G NF1/2025 DF2/current law49 effect note143. Do not turn that acquisition gate into a blanket rule for every2026 sale. The statutory causes include family inadequacy, employment relocation/first or new employment, marriage/registered partnership or separation/dissolution, inability to pay and analogous circumstances. B's actual DF133/2025 art1(Tercero1) aligns regulation49 from2026; A's regulation46 older opening does not displace the later statute's applicable condition. [B enactment](https://www.bizkaia.eus/lehendakaritza/Bao_bob/2025/04/22/I-441_cas.pdf), [A enactment](https://www.araba.eus/BOTHA/Boletines/2025/044/2025_044_01182_C.pdf), [G current law49/effect note143](https://www.gipuzkoa.eus/documents/2456431/8.0759126E7/NF%203-2014%20(2026-0).pdf/8ab5c95c-3f89-8fdf-5cb8-01950e3983cc), [B implementing amendment](https://www.bizkaia.eus/lehendakaritza/Bao_bob/2025/12/30/I-1406_cas.pdf).

**Gipuzkoa's new regulation45.3** requires a qualifying circumstance arising **after acquisition of the sold home**. It gives concrete family-size, recognised disability/dependency or medical-inadequacy cases; first/new work in a different municipality, changed work centre/job conditions; and marriage/partnership or separation/dissolution. Its economic-payment cases include job loss, work-preventing incapacity, insolvency, acquisition-mortgage commitments **greater than30%** of available income (sum of taxable bases less liquid quotas), or the home's share of planned community rehabilitation/improvements **greater than5%** of its cadastral value. Its business cases include cessation through economic unviability or net income in **two consecutive tax periods** reduced **more than20%** against the average of the **three preceding years**; the activity must represent **more than50%** of total net business and employment income. Exactly30%,5%,20% or50% does not meet its respective strict numerical limb, although another proved statutory cause can still apply. For example, commitments12,000 with available income40,000 are exactly30%;12,000.01 exceed30%. Do not import these Gipuzkoa regulatory numerical limbs into B/A by analogy. The enacted DF27/2025 art1(Segundo1)/DF2 applies the new45 to homes acquired from1January2026; the consolidation's embedded former45 text is historical. G45.2 separately admits the specified judicial-separation former family home while it is the family home or within two years after that status ceases, with spouse/partner and applicable descendants' residence evidence. [G actual amendment and commencement](https://egoitza.gipuzkoa.eus/gao-bog/castell/bog/2025/12/30/c2508991.pdf), [G current45](https://www.gipuzkoa.eus/documents/2456431/8.0755457E7/DF%2033-2014%20(2026-2).pdf/7e4c25eb-a139-fa5f-d310-deaa4c4bcffd).

**Later breach:** B49.4, A46.4 and G45.6 restore the corresponding nonexempt gain to its **original gain year**, with delay interest, within the period from breach to the end of the ordinary filing period for the breach year; A/G expressly prescribe a supplementary self-assessment. In **Navarra**, use current law83.4 instead: add the full tax corresponding to the lost incentive, plus delay interest, **with the breach-year quota**. The annotated regulation40.9 showing an original-year supplementary return is expressly **repealed from31July2019**. It is not the current Navarra filing method. [B49](https://www.bizkaia.eus/documents/880307/15187815/ca_47_2014.pdf/c17e2f7e-e4dd-789c-235e-21add02afe5e?t=1710491329241), [A46](https://web.araba.eus/documents/d/araba/indice-reglamento_irpf_cas-2-pdf), [G45](https://www.gipuzkoa.eus/documents/2456431/8.0755457E7/DF%2033-2014%20(2026-2).pdf/7e4c25eb-a139-fa5f-d310-deaa4c4bcffd), [N83.4](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BON-n-2008-90013&p=20260420&tn=1), [N annotated40](https://www.navarra.es/es/web/normativa-hacienda/-/reglamento-del-impuesto-sobre-la-renta-de-las-personas-f%C3%ADsicas-df-174/1999-de-24-de-mayo-?print=true).

### Disposal proceeds reinvested in an insured life annuity

For a person **older than65**, B42(f)/regulation49bis, A42(f)/46bis, G42.1(f)/45bis and N45.3 provide their own asset-disposal-gain exemption where proceeds constitute an **insured life annuity in that taxpayer's favour**. This is a disposal-gain method, separate from the pension/annuity-yield exemptions later in this Guide. Constitute the annuity within **six months of disposal**, contract with an insurer with the taxpayer as beneficiary, notify the insurer that it is a disposal-proceeds reinvestment, start payments within **one year of constitution**, pay at least annually, and ensure annual payments do not decrease by **more than5%** from the preceding year. The provisions permit the stated reversion, certain-payment-period or death-counterinsurance mechanisms. An annual payment falling from10,000 to9,500 meets the5% condition;9,499 does not. A periodic investment product without an insured life-annuity contract does not satisfy these provisions. [B49bis](https://www.bizkaia.eus/documents/880307/15187815/ca_47_2014.pdf/c17e2f7e-e4dd-789c-235e-21add02afe5e?t=1710491329241), [A46bis](https://web.araba.eus/documents/d/araba/indice-reglamento_irpf_cas-2-pdf), [G45bis](https://www.gipuzkoa.eus/documents/2456431/8.0755457E7/DF%2033-2014%20(2026-2).pdf/7e4c25eb-a139-fa5f-d310-deaa4c4bcffd), [N45.3](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BON-n-2008-90013&p=20260420&tn=1).

Track the **240,000 aggregate reinvestment ceiling**, subtracting prior qualifying reinvestments. B/A/G expressly share that ceiling with their respective **law9.37/9.43/9.38** qualifying annuity exemption. Do not grant a new240,000 for each disposal or for each of those two Basque routes. Navarra45.3(e) likewise subtracts previous reinvestments for its own240,000 total. For a positive gain and proceeds, calculate `eligible_R = min(actual_qualifying_reinvestment, max(240000 − prior_counted_reinvestment,0), disposal_proceeds)` and `exempt_gain = gain × eligible_R / disposal_proceeds`. **Do not deduct an acquisition mortgage from this annuity denominator.** For proceeds300,000, gain120,000, prior counted reinvestment100,000 and new qualifying reinvestment200,000, only140,000 counts: exempt56,000 and retain64,000 taxable. With no prior use and240,000 reinvested, exempt96,000 and retain24,000. [B42(f)](https://www.bizkaia.eus/documents/880307/15187815/ca_13_2013.pdf/ce52e403-4fca-9ef7-bf85-2505ba29f921?t=1784098584068), [A42(f)](https://web.araba.eus/documents/d/araba/indice_norma-foral_irpf_cas-4-pdf), [G42.1(f)](https://www.gipuzkoa.eus/documents/2456431/8.0759126E7/NF%203-2014%20(2026-0).pdf/8ab5c95c-3f89-8fdf-5cb8-01950e3983cc), [N45.3(e)](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BON-n-2008-90013&p=20260420&tn=1).

If the gain is subject to withholding and **all proceeds net of that withholding** constitute the annuity within six months, the named local provisions extend the time for reinvesting the **withheld amount only** to the end of the calendar year following disposal. This is not a general extension for uninvested net proceeds. With proceeds100,000 and withholding10,000, investing all90,000 within six months opens the extension for10,000; investing only89,000 does not. State the intention in the disposal-year return where constitution occurs in a later year, and retain the insurance contract, amounts, dates, notice and payment schedule. Breach of any condition or total/partial anticipation of economic rights recaptures the corresponding gain. B49bis5/A46bis5/G45bis5 require an **original-gain-year supplementary return plus interest**, filed between breach and the ordinary deadline for the breach-year return. N45.3(f) instead expressly applies **law83.4's breach-year tax-plus-interest method**. Do not copy the Basque filing route into Navarra. [B49bis](https://www.bizkaia.eus/documents/880307/15187815/ca_47_2014.pdf/c17e2f7e-e4dd-789c-235e-21add02afe5e?t=1710491329241), [A46bis](https://web.araba.eus/documents/d/araba/indice-reglamento_irpf_cas-2-pdf), [G45bis](https://www.gipuzkoa.eus/documents/2456431/8.0755457E7/DF%2033-2014%20(2026-2).pdf/7e4c25eb-a139-fa5f-d310-deaa4c4bcffd), [N45.3/83.4](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BON-n-2008-90013&p=20260420&tn=1).

### Ordinary resident dividends and financial-income expenses

In **Bizkaia, Álava and Gipuzkoa**, article9.24 exempts eligible article34(a)/(b) dividends/profit participations up to **EUR1,500 annually in aggregate**, not for every company, payment or account. Establish the gross entitlement **before withholding**, classify the actual distribution and subtract only the remaining exemption capacity. It does not cover IIC/fund distributions, interest paid by a cooperative on members' contributions, or the specified dividend-capture transactions: securities acquired within two months before payment followed by a disposal of homogeneous securities within two months after payment. For securities outside the provision's defined listed-market population, those before/after windows are **one year**. Obtain acquisition, payment and later-disposal dates before using the exemption; a broker's net receipt does not prove eligibility.

For an otherwise eligible ordinary dividend, calculate `exempt = min(eligible_gross_dividend, max(1500 −exemption_already_used_this_year, 0))`, then `taxable_dividend = gross_dividend −exempt`. Do not apply the exemption to a specifically excluded payment. Under B/A99.2 and G98.2, the quantitative limit remains **1,500 on a joint return**, without multiplication by the number of family members. Under each territory's article39.1, ordinary dividend net income equals the relevant gross income after the exemption: the article39.2 necessary-expense exception is for its named technical-assistance/movable-property/business/mine/subletting income, **not a general custody-fee deduction from dividends**. Do not copy the State or Navarra expense rule into a Basque return.

For example, assume one eligible6,000 ordinary dividend, unused annual exemption, no other savings income/losses or applicable credit. The taxable financial-income amount is4,500. It is within the supplied first7,500 savings band, giving `4,500 ×19% =855` gross savings quota. If500 of annual exemption capacity has already been used, only1,000 remains and taxable dividend is5,000, producing950 on the same stipulated first-band facts. An excluded6,000 IIC distribution has no article9.24 exemption and, on otherwise identical ordinary savings facts, gives1,140 gross savings quota. If two qualifying joint filers each receive3,000 ordinary dividends, total eligible6,000 less the **single1,500 joint ceiling** again leaves4,500, not3,000. Actual payments on account are credited at the differential-tax stage; they do not reduce the declared gross dividend. Sources: current B/A/G articles9.24/34/39 and B/A99.2/G98.2: [Bizkaia](https://www.bizkaia.eus/documents/880307/15187815/ca_13_2013.pdf/ce52e403-4fca-9ef7-bf85-2505ba29f921?t=1784098584068), [Álava](https://web.araba.eus/documents/d/araba/indice_norma-foral_irpf_cas-4-pdf), [Gipuzkoa](https://www.gipuzkoa.eus/documents/2456431/8.0759126E7/NF%203-2014%20(2026-0).pdf/8ab5c95c-3f89-8fdf-5cb8-01950e3983cc).

In **Navarra**, establish ordinary dividend gross income under28(a), allowable net-income expenses under32, then the financial-income savings basket under54.1(a)/54.2 and the article60 scale. Do not import the Basque1,500 exemption or use a historical preamble as the current charging rule. ### Ordinary listed and unlisted share disposals

For ordinary nonbusiness equity disposals, use **B/A/G article47.1(a)/(b) and47.2**, or **Navarra article43.1(a)/(b) and43.2**. Confirm the actual statutory market population before treating a security as listed. For listed shares in that population, the transfer reference is the **higher of quotation at disposal and agreed price**. For unlisted shares, actual proceeds alone do not displace the local minimum-valuation rule. Establish acquisition value and qualifying transfer expenses under the selected territory's own general rules; retain acquisition dates/lots and any applicable local cost update rather than borrowing a State basis by analogy. The following examples stipulate an already established tax acquisition value and zero expenses.

Absent evidence that the actual price is what independent parties would agree in normal market conditions, the unlisted transfer-value floor is the greater of **the holding's net-asset value from the last closed financial year before tax accrual** and **its share of average results for the three closed financial years before tax accrual capitalised at20%**. Thus the second amount is `average_results /0.20`. Count distributed dividends and reserve allocations in those results, excluding the specified regularisation/revaluation reserves. Use the actual agreed amount if higher than the floor. Valid independent-price proof permits the actual-price route. All four current provisions use the **tax-accrual reference date**, not an assumed last balance before the disposal date. The calculated statutory transfer value is also relevant to the acquirer's acquisition value.

For example, with tax acquisition value EUR5,000, actual sale EUR7,000, proportional net assets EUR10,000 and attributable three-year average results EUR3,000, the capitalised amount is EUR15,000. Without the required independent-price proof, transfer value is EUR15,000 and gain **EUR10,000** in each of the four territories on these stipulated facts. Verified EUR7,000 independent-party pricing instead gives gain EUR2,000. A EUR18,000 actual price gives EUR13,000 gain even though the computed floor is only EUR15,000. Apply the territory's savings-disposal netting and scale after measurement; the common/nonresident flat-rate examples do not replace the resident scale.

Use FIFO for homogeneous securities. Partially paid bonus shares take the actual paid amount; fully paid bonus shares divide the original cost across old and new shares and retain the source shares' seniority. Subscription-right proceeds follow the named local gain rule (B/A/G47.1(a)/(b), N43.1(k)). Before using a loss, apply B/A/G43(g)/(h) or N39.6(f)/(g): acquisition of homogeneous listed securities within the two months before/after disposal, or unlisted securities in the year before/after, defers the corresponding loss until the retained securities are disposed of under the local rule. Keep the blocked-loss ledger instead of deducting the same loss and carrying it again. Patrimonial-company shares, actual worker/labour-company price concessions, funds and tax-neutral restructurings use their own named provisions; this ordinary method does not establish those special populations' entitlement.

**Basque small listed-disposal option:** B DA24 and A/G DA20 allow the alternative **3% levy on transfer value**, only where the annual aggregate transfer value of the relevant article47.1(a) securities is **strictly below EUR10,000**. It is not a3% rate on the gain and not a separate EUR10,000 allowance for each broker or sale. ## Two worked examples that recompute

### Example 1: resident dividends, Basque exemption against Navarra expenses

These are the controls stated under "Ordinary resident dividends and financial-income expenses", recomputed. **Basque territory:** an eligible ordinary dividend of **6,000** with the unused annual exemption of **1,500** leaves **4,500** taxable; inside the first 7,500 savings band at **19%** that is **855**. If 500 of the exemption was already used, **5,000** is taxable and the quota is **950**. An excluded IIC distribution of 6,000 has no exemption: **1,140**. **Navarra:** a taxable 6,000 dividend at **20%** gives **1,200**; with 250 of qualifying administration charges the expense ceiling is `6,000 × 3% = 180`, net **5,820**, quota **1,164**.

```oa-check
name: Foral dividends, Basque exemption and Navarra expense ceiling
inputs:
  dividend: 6000
  basque_exemption: 1500
  exemption_used: 500
  navarra_charges: 250
steps:
  basque_taxable: dividend - basque_exemption
  basque_quota: basque_taxable * 0.19
  basque_taxable_part_used: dividend - (basque_exemption - exemption_used)
  basque_quota_part_used: basque_taxable_part_used * 0.19
  iic_quota: dividend * 0.19
  navarra_quota: dividend * 0.20
  navarra_expense: min(navarra_charges, dividend * 0.03)
  navarra_quota_after_expense: (dividend - navarra_expense) * 0.20
expected:
  basque_taxable: 4500
  basque_quota: 855.00
  basque_taxable_part_used: 5000
  basque_quota_part_used: 950.00
  iic_quota: 1140.00
  navarra_quota: 1200.00
  navarra_expense: 180.00
  navarra_quota_after_expense: 1164.00
```

### Example 2: unlisted-share floor and a crypto swap

These are the controls stated under "Ordinary listed and unlisted share disposals" and "Ordinary nonbusiness crypto sales, swaps and loss timing", recomputed. **Unlisted shares:** tax acquisition value **5,000**, actual sale **7,000**, net-asset value **10,000** and three-year average results **3,000**. The capitalised amount is `3,000 / 0.20 = 15,000`; without independent-price proof the transfer value is the greater of the floor and the price, **15,000**, and the gain **10,000** in each territory. With verified independent pricing the gain is **2,000**. **Crypto swap:** outgoing acquisition value **1,000**, market value given **4,000**, market value received **4,500**: `M = max(4,000, 4,500)` and the gain is **3,500**, the same if the two market values are reversed.

```oa-check
name: Unlisted-share transfer-value floor and fee-free crypto swap
inputs:
  share_basis: 5000
  actual_price: 7000
  net_assets: 10000
  average_results: 3000
  token_basis: 1000
  value_given: 4000
  value_received: 4500
steps:
  capitalised: average_results / 0.20
  transfer_value: max(net_assets, capitalised, actual_price)
  share_gain_no_proof: transfer_value - share_basis
  share_gain_with_proof: actual_price - share_basis
  swap_gain: max(value_given, value_received) - token_basis
expected:
  capitalised: 15000
  transfer_value: 15000
  share_gain_no_proof: 10000
  share_gain_with_proof: 2000
  swap_gain: 3500
```

## Ask the client first

1. Which territory is competent for your IRPF in 2026, and were you resident there all year?
2. What did you sell, swap or let, when and how was each item acquired, and what were its acquisition value, expenses and any earlier reinvestment?
3. For a home: was it your habitual home, are you over 65 or 70 or dependent, and do you intend to reinvest or buy an annuity?
4. For dividends and shares: are the shares listed, and what were the net assets and three-year results of an unlisted company?
5. For crypto: which units were sold, swapped or held abroad, with dates, market values and fees?
