# Rhode Island corporate and entity minimum tax, tax year 2026

> **General reference only.** Written by Codex. Not reviewed by an accountant. Apply the official sources to the taxpayer's facts and obtain qualified professional review before filing, paying or taking a tax position.

> **Circular 230 section 10.37:** Consider relevant facts, reasonable assumptions and applicable law. Evaluate federal tax implications using the federal owner skills and obtain professional review where needed.

**Answer first.** An ordinary RI C corporation files RI-1120C and pays the greater of 7% of RI taxable net income after permitted credits and the USD 400 minimum. LLCs taxed as partnerships/disregarded entities and S corporations generally have their separate USD 400 entity filing obligation; pass-through treatment does not erase it. Source: [Notice 2026-01, entity filing table](https://tax.ri.gov/sites/g/files/xkgbur541/files/2026-02/Notice_2026_01_LLC_filing_requirements.pdf).

## Scope

Ordinary calendar-year corporations doing business or deriving taxable income in RI, with separate identification of LLC/partnership/S corporation returns. Federal corporate taxable income is the starting point, not necessarily the state base. `us-form-1120-c-corp` owns federal corporate computations, `ri-income-tax` individual owners, `ri-sales-tax` sales/use, and `ri-payroll` employee taxes. Combined groups, financial institutions and insurance have specialist rules outside this ordinary calculation.

## Ask the client first

1. Legal entity, federal tax classification/elections, formation/qualification and dissolution dates.
2. Federal return, taxable income, accounting year and RI activity/nexus, including related entities.
3. State additions/subtractions, losses and HR1 adjustment workpapers.
4. RI and everywhere sales/apportionment records and combined-reporting information.
5. Credit certificates, minimum-tax members, prior-year tax and estimated payments.
6. Nonresident owners, PTE election/withholding, RI registration and electronic-filing obligation.

## The method, step by step

### Rates, bands and thresholds

| Item | Amount and condition | Official source |
|---|---|---|
| Ordinary C corporation | 7% of RI taxable net income, minimum USD 400 | [2026 entity table](https://tax.ri.gov/sites/g/files/xkgbur541/files/2026-02/Notice_2026_01_LLC_filing_requirements.pdf) |
| LLC treated as partnership | RI-1065, annual USD 400 | [2026 LLC table](https://tax.ri.gov/sites/g/files/xkgbur541/files/2026-02/Notice_2026_01_LLC_filing_requirements.pdf) |
| Disregarded single-member LLC | RI-1065 annual USD 400; deadline depends on owner classification under notice footnotes | [single-member LLC row and footnotes](https://tax.ri.gov/sites/g/files/xkgbur541/files/2026-02/Notice_2026_01_LLC_filing_requirements.pdf) |
| S corporation | RI-1120S annual USD 400; additional applicable income taxes require separate testing | [S corporation row](https://tax.ri.gov/sites/g/files/xkgbur541/files/2026-02/Notice_2026_01_LLC_filing_requirements.pdf) |
| Corporate estimates, verified filing cycle | Required when estimated annual tax exceeds USD 400; four 25% installments | [general instructions, section 6](https://tax.ri.gov/sites/g/files/xkgbur541/files/2026-01/2025%20Business%20Corp%20General%20Instructions.pdf) |
| Electronic mandate | Combined annual RI tax liability at least USD 5,000 or annual gross income over USD 100,000 | [larger business registrants mandate](https://tax.ri.gov/sites/g/files/xkgbur541/files/2026-01/2025%201120C%20Instructions%20with%20Credit%20Worksheets.pdf) |

1. Determine classification and filing obligation before applying a rate. Notice 2026-01 covers LLCs, including single-member LLCs, and annual minimum payment even where the entity exists for only part of the year. A disregarded entity's owner income return and entity RI-1065 minimum are separate obligations. Source: [notice and table](https://tax.ri.gov/sites/g/files/xkgbur541/files/2026-02/Notice_2026_01_LLC_filing_requirements.pdf).
2. Start from federal taxable income and document RI Schedule B deductions and Schedule C additions. The verified **2025-2026 filing cycle** RI-1120C path uses line 1 federal taxable income, line 2 deductions, line 3 additions and line 4 adjusted income. HR1 differences for federal interest, research and expensing require applicable adjustment schedules; final 2026 conformity/line mapping is Not yet confirmed. Source: [RI-1120C line instructions and HR1 update](https://tax.ri.gov/sites/g/files/xkgbur541/files/2026-01/2025%201120C%20Instructions%20with%20Credit%20Worksheets.pdf).
3. Apply the correct apportionment and allocation, then losses/adjustments, to derive RI taxable net income. The filing-cycle form uses Schedule H apportionment feeding line 5/6, later adjustment/loss lines and tax at line 11. Do not assume a sole office makes 100% RI sourcing where sales/activity facts differ, or use a pass-through apportionment method for a C corporation. The simple examples below assume already established RI taxable income; final 2026 apportionment details are Not yet confirmed. Source: [Schedule H and lines 5 to 11](https://tax.ri.gov/sites/g/files/xkgbur541/files/2026-01/2025%201120C%20Instructions%20with%20Credit%20Worksheets.pdf).
4. Multiply the final apportioned/adjusted RI taxable income by 7%, apply only approved supported credits and preserve the minimum. For combined groups, the filing-cycle minimum worksheet requires USD 400 for each member with RI nexus, rather than treating the entire group as one minimum. Source: [tax, credits and minimum worksheet](https://tax.ri.gov/sites/g/files/xkgbur541/files/2026-01/2025%201120C%20Instructions%20with%20Credit%20Worksheets.pdf).
5. For partnerships/S corporations/LLCs, determine entity minimum, nonresident pass-through withholding and any voluntary PTE election separately. Notice 2026-01 says electing PTEs may be exempt from pass-through withholding subject to its conditions. The filing-cycle general instructions discuss withholding on nonresident distributive shares, with exemptions including a member share below USD 1,000; do not assume every owner is covered or exempt. Source: [PTE/PTW discussion](https://tax.ri.gov/sites/g/files/xkgbur541/files/2026-02/Notice_2026_01_LLC_filing_requirements.pdf); [nonresident withholding section](https://tax.ri.gov/sites/g/files/xkgbur541/files/2026-01/2025%20Business%20Corp%20General%20Instructions.pdf).
6. Reconcile estimates, withholding and credits with return tax/minimum; pay balance and retain proof. Credits are not an automatic reduction below minimum. The 2026 change advisory extends R&D expense-credit carryforward from 7 to 15 years and sunsets several other incentives. Do not assume a 2025 incentive survives in 2026 simply because it appears on an older schedule. Source: [2026 credit changes table](https://tax.ri.gov/sites/g/files/xkgbur541/files/2026-01/ADV_2026_02_Tax-Changes.pdf).

## Boundary and exception table

| Situation | Action |
|---|---|
| Loss-making ordinary corporation | Minimum generally still applies; do not report zero solely due to a loss |
| Disregarded LLC | Test separate RI-1065 minimum despite federal disregarded treatment |
| Combined/unitary group | Refer group composition, apportionment and member-by-member minimum |
| Bank, insurer, utility or exempt organization | Refer applicable separate tax regime/return |
| Nonresident owners/PTE election | Obtain member records and current PTW/PTE schedules; keep owner credit/add-back consistent |
| Federal HR1 deduction or expiring state incentive | Reconcile current state treatment and effective dates; final 2026 schedule needed |

## Worked cases

**Case 1, ordinary corporation.** Invented RI C corporation has established RI taxable income USD 200,000, no credits and USD 12,000 estimates. Seven percent gives USD 14,000, above USD 400 minimum; balance USD 2,000. Federal-to-state adjustments and apportionment must already be complete.

```oa-check
name: Rhode Island C corporation ordinary 2026 tax
inputs:
  ri_taxable_income: 200000
  estimates: 12000
steps:
  - income_tax: ri_taxable_income * 0.07
  - tax_due: max(income_tax, 400)
  - balance: tax_due - estimates
expected:
  income_tax: 14000
  tax_due: 14000
  balance: 2000
```

**Case 2, minimum-tax decision.** An invented corporation with RI taxable income USD 5,000 computes USD 350, so USD 400 controls. At USD 6,000 income, computed USD 420 controls. A loss does not by itself avoid the USD 400 minimum; entity classification/exemptions must still be checked.

```oa-check
steps:
  - lower_computed: lower_income * 0.07
  - lower_due: max(lower_computed, 400)
  - higher_due: max(higher_income * 0.07, 400)
expected:
  lower_computed: 350
  lower_due: 400
  higher_due: 420
```

## When to refuse or refer

Refer combined groups, multistate apportionment, financial/insurance regimes, exempt entities, complex losses, incentive certification and disputed nexus. Do not give a final 2026 filing answer from a filing-cycle form where conformity or schedules remain unconfirmed.

## Filing and payment

Use https://taxportal.ri.gov or authorized corporate e-file, choose RI-1120C/RI-1120S/RI-1065 and correct period/classification, complete the federal reconciliation and applicable state schedules, submit and retain acceptance; pay using the correct corporation/entity account and retain payment proof. The 2026 notice gives calendar C corporations 15 April, partnerships/S corporations 15 March, and disregarded LLC due dates by owner classification. Apply holidays and final-year instructions rather than guessing dates. Source: [filing table and footnotes](https://tax.ri.gov/sites/g/files/xkgbur541/files/2026-02/Notice_2026_01_LLC_filing_requirements.pdf).

The verified **2025-2026 filing cycle** general instructions require corporate estimates exceeding USD 400, with 25% installments on the 15th of months 4, 6, 9 and 12. Filing extensions do not extend payment. Its underpayment comparison, penalties and interest are period-specific; confirm final 2026 estimate/extension instructions. Source: [general instructions, extensions and section 6](https://tax.ri.gov/sites/g/files/xkgbur541/files/2026-01/2025%20Business%20Corp%20General%20Instructions.pdf).

## Completion checklist

- Classification, nexus, year and any entity minimum identified separately from owner tax.
- Federal reconciliation, state adjustments, apportionment and losses supported.
- 7% tax compared with minimum and certified credits; estimates reconciled.
- Correct return/account submitted with acceptance and payment; final-2026 gaps resolved.
