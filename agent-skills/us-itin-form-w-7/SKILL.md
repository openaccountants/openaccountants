# Getting an ITIN on Form W-7, tax year 2026

> **Source-cited draft.** Built from the Instructions for Form W-7 (revised December 2024) and the IRS ITIN Acceptance Agent Program page, read on 2026-11-13. No accountant has reviewed it. All outputs must be reviewed by a qualified tax professional before filing.

**Answer first.** An ITIN is a nine-digit tax number for a person who must have a US taxpayer identification number and who does not have, and cannot get, a Social Security number. Apply on Form W-7, attached to the front of the federal return that needs the number, unless an exception in the instructions applies. A valid passport is the only document that stands alone. An ITIN that was not on a return for three consecutive tax years expires on 31 December of that third year. Renew it only if it will be on a return.

## Scope and who this is for

In scope: a first ITIN application, or a renewal, filed with a 2026 federal return or under an exception in the Form W-7 instructions. That includes spouses and dependents claimed for an allowable tax benefit, nonresidents filing a return or claiming a treaty benefit, and applications sent by mail, a Taxpayer Assistance Center, or an acceptance agent.

## Ask the client first

1. **Does the person already have an SSN, or a pending SSN application?** If yes, do not file Form W-7. If the Social Security Administration has denied an SSN, get the denial letter.
2. **New or renewal,** and the old ITIN and the name it was issued under, if any.
3. **Why the number is needed.** The Form W-7 reason box (a through h). A spouse or dependent needs an allowable tax benefit, or their own return.
4. **Which original documents** they can part with for up to 60 days, or whether they can go to a Taxpayer Assistance Center or a certifying acceptance agent so the documents are returned at once.
5. **The last tax year the ITIN was on a filed return,** if this is a renewal.

### Who files, and in what order

1. **Stop if an SSN is available.** Do not file Form W-7 if the person has an SSN or is eligible for one (a US citizen, or a person admitted for permanent residence or for US employment). If an SSN application is pending, wait. File Form W-7 only after the Social Security Administration says the person is not eligible, and attach the denial letter. Students, professors, and researchers on an F-1, J-1, or M-1 visa who will not work may attach a letter from the designated school official or responsible officer instead of a denial letter. Once an SSN is issued, stop using the ITIN. Source: [Instructions for Form W-7](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/iw7).
2. **Pick the reason box.** Check one box even on a renewal. Use the box that best fits: (a) nonresident claiming a treaty benefit, (b) nonresident filing a return, (c) resident alien under the substantial presence test, (d) dependent of a US citizen or resident, (e) spouse of a US citizen or resident, (f) nonresident student, professor, or researcher, (g) dependent or spouse of a nonresident visa holder, or (h) other, including an exception for a third party who must file an information return. A spouse is never a dependent.
3. **Attach the return, unless an exception applies.** Put Form W-7 on the front of the federal return. Leave the SSN box blank for each person applying. More than one Form W-7 (spouse, dependents) can go on the same return. The IRS assigns the numbers, then processes the return. Exceptions, listed in the instructions, replace the return. Name the exception on the dotted line by box h (for example, Exception 1a-Partnership Income). A treaty claim under box a or f also checks box h and names the treaty country and article.
4. **Renew only if the number will be on a return.** Check "Renew an existing ITIN" and complete lines 6e and 6f. If the ITIN is used only on information returns filed by a bank or other third party, do not renew until a return is filed. An expired ITIN on a return can delay processing.
5. **Documents.** Send originals, or copies certified by the issuing agency. A certified copy is not a notarized copy, except for the military cases the instructions allow. At least one document must have a photograph, unless the applicant is a dependent under 14 (under 18 if a student). A current passport is enough by itself. Without it, send at least two of the listed documents that together show identity and foreign status. Documents must be unexpired on the day the form is submitted (the postmark, in general). A civil birth certificate does not expire. A dependent under 18 who has no passport needs a birth certificate. Dependents who must prove US residency need a passport with a US entry date, or one of the extra residency documents in the instructions, unless they are a military dependent overseas or a Canadian or Mexican dependent claimed for a benefit other than the credit for other dependents.
6. **Where it goes.** Do not use the address in the Form 1040 or 1040-NR instructions. Mail the package to Internal Revenue Service, ITIN Operation, P.O. Box 149342, Austin, TX 78714-9342. A private delivery service uses Internal Revenue Service, ITIN Operation, Mail Stop 6090-AUSC, 3651 S. Interregional Hwy 35, Austin, TX 78741-0000. A Taxpayer Assistance Center that authenticates documents does so by appointment (844-545-5640) and returns the documents at the visit. An acceptance agent helps complete the form and still sends the originals to the IRS. A certifying acceptance agent reviews the originals and returns them at once, except foreign military identification, and for dependents can authenticate only a passport and a civil birth certificate. The agent attaches Form W-7 (COA). From 1 June 2026, a certifying acceptance agent claiming Exception 1(a) also attaches the partnership or LLC agreement extract described in the table above.
7. **After filing.** The IRS mails the ITIN notice and returns the documents. Allow 7 weeks, or 9 to 11 weeks in peak season or from overseas. A return cannot be e-filed with an ITIN in the calendar year the ITIN is assigned. Later years can be e-filed. Estimated tax (Form 1040-ES or 1040-ES (NR)) and an extension (Form 4868) do not wait for the ITIN and do not get a Form W-7 attached. Write "ITIN TO BE REQUESTED" where the number is asked.

### When the ITIN expires

If the ITIN was not on at least one federal return for the last three consecutive tax years, it expires on 31 December of that third year. Count tax years, not calendar years since the last notice. A renewal for a 2026 return goes out with that return.

### Boundary and exception table

| Situation | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Person has or can get an SSN | Do not file Form W-7 |
| SSN application still open | Wait for the denial letter |
| Spouse or dependent, no allowable tax benefit and no own return | Not eligible for a new ITIN or a renewal |
| ITIN used only on a third party's information return | Do not renew until a tax return will include it |
| Passport with no US entry date, for a dependent who must prove residency | Passport is not enough. Add the residency document the instructions require |
| Need the passport within 60 days | Use a Taxpayer Assistance Center or a certifying acceptance agent, not the mail |
| First year the ITIN is issued | Paper file. E-file that calendar year is not available |
| Deceased person | The instructions list the surviving spouse, the court-appointed executor, and Form 1310 when neither is filing. Follow that list |

## Worked cases

**Renew, or wait?** An applicant's ITIN was last included on a federal return for tax year 2022. It was not on the 2023, 2024, or 2025 returns. It expired on 31 December 2025, the end of the third consecutive tax year without it. They will file a 2026 return that needs the number, so they renew: Form W-7 checked as a renewal, lines 6e and 6f completed, attached to the front of that return, with a current passport or two other documents. If instead a bank is the only one using the number, on an information return, they do not renew yet. The decision is whether a 2026 tax return will carry the number.

| R-W7-EXCEPT | They claim an exception and will not attach a return | Use the exception tables in the Form W-7 instructions. Do not invent the exception number |
| R-W7-TREATY | Box a or f and a treaty claim | Name the treaty article from the treaty, not from memory. See Publication 901 |
| R-W7-DEP | A dependent, and residency or the allowable benefit is unclear | Stop. The passport, entry date, and benefit rules differ for Canada, Mexico, and military families |
| R-W7-CAA | The signer wants to become an acceptance agent | That is the electronic agent application, with training, not Form W-7 |

## Filing and payment

There is no tax to pay with Form W-7. The package is the form, the return or the exception papers, and the documents.

Mail: Internal Revenue Service, ITIN Operation, P.O. Box 149342, Austin, TX 78714-9342.

Private delivery: Internal Revenue Service, ITIN Operation, Mail Stop 6090-AUSC, 3651 S. Interregional Hwy 35, Austin, TX 78741-0000.

In person: a designated Taxpayer Assistance Center, appointment on 844-545-5640. The center returns authenticated documents at the visit and sends the form on.

Through an agent: an acceptance agent, or a certifying acceptance agent who returns the documents and attaches Form W-7 (COA). The agent must interview the applicant in person. Video may be used for the interview only if the agent has the original documents during it.

What happens next: the IRS assigns the ITIN, or renews it, and then processes the attached return. The notice and the documents come by mail. Call if the notice is not there after 7 weeks, or after 9 to 11 weeks in peak season or from overseas, and call if original documents are not back within 60 days. From inside the United States the help line in the instructions is 800-829-1040. From outside, 267-941-1000.

A first-time application should go in by the due date of the return it belongs to. If the return cannot be filed by then, file the extension by that due date. The instructions warn that a late return with an incomplete Form W-7 can cost refundable credits the person otherwise qualifies for.

## Completion checklist

- SSN eligibility checked, and a denial letter attached if one was required.
- Reason box checked, including on a renewal.
- Return attached to the front, SSN line left blank, or a named exception with its papers.
- Passport, or two other current documents, with the photograph rule and the dependent residency rule applied.
- Renewal lines 6e and 6f filled if the old number is known.
- Austin address used, not the address on the tax return.
- Applicant told they cannot e-file in the calendar year the ITIN is assigned.
