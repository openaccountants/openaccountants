# Albania Tax Optimization

## Scope

**Built from the law. Not yet approved.** This guide supports a documented planning comparison for Albanian self-employed individuals, individual traders and ordinary resident small companies in calendar tax year 2026, using ALL (lek). The tax year runs from January through December ([Law 29/2023 art. 6](https://tatime.gov.al/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Ligj-Nr.-29-2023-Per-Tatimin-mbi-te-Ardhurat-i-ndryshuar.pdf)). It is not professional approval or a complete Albanian tax computation. A licensed Albanian tax adviser must review the facts and proposed action.

### Quick reference

| Planning switch | Rule and conditions for 2026 | Official source |
|---|---|---|
| Temporary business income-tax rate | **0%** for individual traders, self-employed individuals and entities whose annual **gross income is no more than ALL 14,000,000**, through **31 December 2029**. Test classification first; this is not an exemption for employment income or for a shareholder's dividends. | [Law 29/2023 arts. 12, 24(3), 69(1)(dh)](https://tatime.gov.al/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Ligj-Nr.-29-2023-Per-Tatimin-mbi-te-Ardhurat-i-ndryshuar.pdf) |

There is no zero-rate slice after failing the gross-income test. | [Law 29/2023 arts. 24(2), 41, 69(1)(dh)](https://tatime.gov.al/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Ligj-Nr.-29-2023-Per-Tatimin-mbi-te-Ardhurat-i-ndryshuar.pdf) |
| Self-employed client concentration | Employment-income test: **80% or more** directly or indirectly from one client, or **90% or more** from fewer than **three clients**; apply the exceptions and facts below before choosing a business-tax route. | [Law 29/2023 art. 12(1)(ç), Annex 1](https://tatime.gov.al/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Ligj-Nr.-29-2023-Per-Tatimin-mbi-te-Ardhurat-i-ndryshuar.pdf) |

## Ask the client first

1. Which calendar year, Albanian tax residence, legal form, activity and business registration apply? Is the proposed company an ordinary taxable resident entity, or does a special regime apply?
2. What is the person's or entity's complete annual gross income, including every activity, and the independently reconciled taxable profit? Keep these separate; do not assume invoiced turnover alone answers the gross-income test.
3. For self-employment, who pays each invoice, directly and indirectly? Obtain annual client shares, residence and Albanian permanent-establishment facts for every customer, contracts, and evidence of control, equipment, hours, substitution and financial risk.
4. What are the calendar-year and rolling turnover records, VAT status, start date and profession? Is voluntary registration being considered?
5. What contribution coverage, active periods, chosen social base and other work apply? Are agriculture, family work or other special coverage rules involved?
6. How much profit will the owner retain or distribute, to whom, and when? Obtain other personal income, deductions already used, dependants, losses, credits, salary proposals and incorporation/compliance costs before ranking structures.

## The method, step by step

### 1. Establish business treatment before applying a concession

For a self-employed individual, calculate both client-concentration tests in the quick reference. Business income under art. 13 excludes income classified as employment under art. 12. Passing a client-count test does not undo an actual employment relationship under art. 12(1)(a), nor turn directors' or partners' remuneration for work into business profit ([Law 29/2023 arts. 12–13](https://tatime.gov.al/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Ligj-Nr.-29-2023-Per-Tatimin-mbi-te-Ardhurat-i-ndryshuar.pdf)).

| Route when a concentration test is met | Required evidence and limit | Official source |
|---|---|---|
| Genuine independent work supported by status declaration | The individual declares that the facts are not employment, using the law's Annex 1 model, lodged with the registered regional tax directorate alongside the annual personal business return in March of the following year. It is an assessment tool; the administration may reclassify an inaccurate declaration or tax-avoidance arrangement. Own equipment alone is not sufficient. | [Law 29/2023 art. 12(1)(ç), Annex 1](https://tatime.gov.al/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Ligj-Nr.-29-2023-Per-Tatimin-mbi-te-Ardhurat-i-ndryshuar.pdf); [Instruction 26/2023 para. 12.8.1 and its examples](https://tatime.gov.al/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Udhezimi-nr-26-date-8.9.2023-Per-tatimin-mbi-te-ardhuart.pdf) |
| Statutory foreign-customer exception | Art. 12(1)(ç)'s final paragraph says services **only to non-residents of Albania OR only to entities without an Albanian permanent establishment** remain business income. Annex 1 point 6 says no status declaration is needed in that case. Instruction 12.9 describes non-resident customers without an Albanian permanent establishment. Obtain all residence/establishment facts; if the result depends on the difference in wording, refer for a documented legal resolution and do not automatically apply the exception. Mixed domestic and foreign customers are not an all-foreign exception. | [Law 29/2023 art. 12(1)(ç), Annex 1(6)](https://tatime.gov.al/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Ligj-Nr.-29-2023-Per-Tatimin-mbi-te-Ardhurat-i-ndryshuar.pdf); [Instruction 26/2023 para. 12.9](https://tatime.gov.al/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Udhezimi-nr-26-date-8.9.2023-Per-tatimin-mbi-te-ardhuart.pdf) |
| Neither exception is supported | Treat the affected income as employment for tax purposes and route the base/deductions to the personal-income-tax owner. The business concession is unavailable to that employment income. Employment tax is **13%** on the annual taxable base up to **ALL 2,040,000**, then **23%** on the excess; these are taxable-base bands, not gross invoices. | [Law 29/2023 arts. 12, 21–24(1)](https://tatime.gov.al/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Ligj-Nr.-29-2023-Per-Tatimin-mbi-te-Ardhurat-i-ndryshuar.pdf) |

Retain the Annex 1 facts on each relevant contractor: invoicing excluding VAT, duties and duration, reporting and instructions, personal service/substitutes, marketing, materials and equipment, workplace and hours, who sets payment, loss risk and delay penalties. Do not replace that assessment with a checklist score or a declaration filed merely to obtain the concession ([Law 29/2023 Annex 1](https://tatime.gov.al/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Ligj-Nr.-29-2023-Per-Tatimin-mbi-te-Ardhurat-i-ndryshuar.pdf); [Instruction 26/2023 para. 12.8.1](https://tatime.gov.al/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Udhezimi-nr-26-date-8.9.2023-Per-tatimin-mbi-te-ardhuart.pdf)).

### 2. Apply the annual gross-income cliff, then compare ordinary tax

The zero rate applies to the whole qualifying business profit while the gross-income condition is met, not to the first part of gross income. Above the limit, compute the whole taxable profit under the ordinary rates, after lawful adjustments and personal deductions where applicable. Do not use the same number for gross income and taxable profit ([Law 29/2023 arts. 13(2), 21–24, 43, 69(1)(dh)](https://tatime.gov.al/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Ligj-Nr.-29-2023-Per-Tatimin-mbi-te-Ardhurat-i-ndryshuar.pdf)).

| Comparison | Treatment and boundary | Official source |
|---|---|---|
| Individual trader or genuine self-employed individual | Apply the ordinary individual scale in the quick reference if the concession fails. Resolve deductible expenses and personal deductions in the income-tax owner; a personal deduction is claimed only once. | [Law 29/2023 arts. 13(2), 21–24](https://tatime.gov.al/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Ligj-Nr.-29-2023-Per-Tatimin-mbi-te-Ardhurat-i-ndryshuar.pdf) |
| Ordinary resident company | Apply corporate tax to its entire taxable profit when the gross-income concession fails. A distribution to a resident individual shareholder then bears **8%** dividend tax on the gross dividend, with no investment-expense deduction; dividend withholding is also **8%**. Corporate tax and dividend tax are separate. The business zero rate does not make individual dividends tax-free. | [Law 29/2023 arts. 24(3)(a)–(4), 41, 58–59, 69(1)(dh)](https://tatime.gov.al/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Ligj-Nr.-29-2023-Per-Tatimin-mbi-te-Ardhurat-i-ndryshuar.pdf) |
| Retained versus distributed company profit | Show the corporate tax first, then the additional tax on the actual distribution. Undistributed profit is company money, not the individual's net cash. Owner remuneration for work requires a separate employment/payroll analysis. | [Law 29/2023 arts. 3(19), 12(1)(c), 24, 40–41, 58–59](https://tatime.gov.al/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Ligj-Nr.-29-2023-Per-Tatimin-mbi-te-Ardhurat-i-ndryshuar.pdf) |

The old simplified profit tax in Chapter III of Law 9632/2006 was repealed by art. 71(2). Art. 69(1)(e) preserved old forms and responsibilities only for the 2024 tax period; do not use those transitional forms to justify a current exemption. The business concession ends on **31 December 2029** under current art. 69(1)(dh). For planning after that date, model the current ordinary individual/company rules subject to any future amendment; do not promise an extension or present a future year's rates as settled ([Law 29/2023 arts. 24, 41, 69, 71–72](https://tatime.gov.al/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Ligj-Nr.-29-2023-Per-Tatimin-mbi-te-Ardhurat-i-ndryshuar.pdf)).

### 3. Keep VAT and contributions in the cash-flow comparison

| Obligation | Planning check, with conditions | Official source |
|---|---|---|
| Mandatory VAT registration | Standard threshold **ALL 10,000,000** calendar-year turnover; the law also tests **12 consecutive months**, including crossing before that period ends. Apply within **15 days** of crossing, deemed the last day of the calendar month of excess. Notaries and state/private judicial bailiffs have a zero registration threshold regardless of turnover, even for exempt activities. Do not infer the position of other professions from this non-exhaustive wording. | [VKM 953/2014 art. 11](https://tatime.gov.al/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/VKM-Nr.953-date-29.12.2014-i-ndryshuar.pdf); [Law 92/2014 art. 122(1)](https://tatime.gov.al/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Ligji-Nr.-92-2014-Per-tatimin-mbi-vleren-e-shtuar-ne-Republiken-e-Shqiperise-i-ndryshuar.pdf) |
| VAT turnover and scope | Use VAT-law turnover, not the income-tax gross-income figure: taxable supplies including the specified zero-rated supplies, excluding VAT; investment-asset disposals and qualifying incidental financial/property transactions are excluded, while main-business financial/property supplies count. New registrants use a proportionate threshold. Small-business treatment has exclusions, including non-established suppliers' Albanian-taxable services; fiscal evasion is excluded too. Read the VAT owner before applying the standard threshold. | [Law 92/2014 arts. 117–119](https://tatime.gov.al/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Ligji-Nr.-92-2014-Per-tatimin-mbi-vleren-e-shtuar-ne-Republiken-e-Shqiperise-i-ndryshuar.pdf) |
| Voluntary VAT registration | Available only where annual turnover exceeds **ALL 5,000,000** under the decision. The normal regime then lasts at least **two calendar years**; returning to the small-business regime takes effect **six months** after the request. Small-business treatment has no input-credit right and does not permit charging VAT on invoices. Do not recommend an election without modelling input credits and customer prices in the VAT owner. | [VKM 953/2014 art. 11(1)](https://tatime.gov.al/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/VKM-Nr.953-date-29.12.2014-i-ndryshuar.pdf); [Law 92/2014 arts. 120–121](https://tatime.gov.al/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Ligji-Nr.-92-2014-Per-tatimin-mbi-vleren-e-shtuar-ne-Republiken-e-Shqiperise-i-ndryshuar.pdf) |
| Standard non-agricultural self-employed contribution floor | Social insurance is **23%** of a chosen monthly base not below **ALL 50,000** in 2026, with a statutory maximum base; health is **3.4%** of twice that minimum, **ALL 100,000**. At the minimum this is **ALL 11,500 + ALL 3,400 = ALL 14,900 monthly (computed)**, even if business income tax is zero. These are compulsory insurance charges, not a percentage of income-tax liability. Special coverage and detailed payment rules belong to the contributions owner. The old currency amounts printed in VKM 77 are not the current minimum. | [VKM 77/2015 ch. III(6)–(7)](https://www.issh.gov.al/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/VENDIM-NR.-77-DATE-28.1.2015-PER-KONTRIBUTET-E-DETYRUESHME-DHE-PERFITIMET-NGA-SISTEMI-I-SIGURIMEVE-SHOQERORE-DHE-SIGURIMI-I-KUJDESIT-SHENDETESOR.pdf); [GDT notice, effective 1 January 2026](https://tatime.gov.al/nga-1-janari-2026-rritet-paga-minimale-dhe-maksimale/) |

## Worked cases

### Case 1: the gross-income cliff does not remove the contribution floor

An ordinary non-agricultural self-employed individual has genuine independent business income, no concentration-rule problem, no other personal income or dependants, no special sector relief, and VAT registration already addressed. Compare two alternative complete annual gross-income facts: **ALL 14,000,000** and **ALL 14,000,001**. Each scenario has **ALL 4,000,000** agreed lawful business profit before the personal deduction. Do not achieve the lower scenario by suppressing earned income or splitting a business.

At the cap, business income tax is **ALL 0**. Above it, the whole business profit leaves the concession. For this individual, the **ALL 360,000** personal deduction leaves **ALL 3,640,000** taxable, giving **ALL 546,000** tax at **15%**. The latter is not tax on the excess gross-income lek. The contribution floor for one ordinary full active month is still **ALL 14,900** in either scenario. The decision is to budget the whole-profit tax exposure when income crosses the cap and retain the separate insurance budget, not to manufacture eligibility ([Law 29/2023 arts. 22(1)(c), 23, 24(2), 69(1)(dh)](https://tatime.gov.al/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Ligj-Nr.-29-2023-Per-Tatimin-mbi-te-Ardhurat-i-ndryshuar.pdf); [VKM 77/2015 ch. III(6)–(7)](https://www.issh.gov.al/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/VENDIM-NR.-77-DATE-28.1.2015-PER-KONTRIBUTET-E-DETYRUESHME-DHE-PERFITIMET-NGA-SISTEMI-I-SIGURIMEVE-SHOQERORE-DHE-SIGURIMI-I-KUJDESIT-SHENDETESOR.pdf); [2026 GDT notice](https://tatime.gov.al/nga-1-janari-2026-rritet-paga-minimale-dhe-maksimale/)). The profit input is agreed before this calculation; this example does not decide the held deduction of the individual's own contributions.

```oa-check
name: Gross-income cap, whole-profit exposure and monthly contribution floor
inputs:
  gross_at_cap: 14000000
  gross_above_cap: 14000001
  business_profit: 4000000
  personal_deduction: 360000
  social_minimum_base: 50000
steps:
  excess_gross: gross_above_cap - gross_at_cap
  tax_at_cap: business_profit * 0
  taxable_above_cap: max(0, business_profit - personal_deduction)
  tax_above_cap: min(taxable_above_cap, 14000000) * 0.15 + max(0, taxable_above_cap - 14000000) * 0.23
  social_minimum: social_minimum_base * 0.23
  health_minimum: social_minimum_base * 2 * 0.034
  monthly_contributions: social_minimum + health_minimum
expected:
  excess_gross: 1
  tax_at_cap: 0
  taxable_above_cap: 3640000
  tax_above_cap: 546000
  social_minimum: 11500
  health_minimum: 3400
  monthly_contributions: 14900
```

### Case 2: individual versus company, with and without distribution

Compare an independent resident individual business and an ordinary resident company, each with **ALL 20,000,000** complete annual gross income and **ALL 16,000,000** agreed taxable business profit before the individual's personal deduction and before income tax. Neither qualifies for the gross-income concession. Assume no losses, credits, special relief, other personal income, dependants or other distributions. For the company, the sole shareholder is a resident individual; no salary is modelled. This is an income-tax comparison only, conditional on a lawful remuneration arrangement, not a conclusion that a working owner has no payroll duties. Contributions, any own-contribution deduction, formation/compliance costs and other differences must be resolved separately before implementation.

The individual deducts **ALL 360,000** once, leaving **ALL 15,640,000** taxable: **ALL 2,100,000** on the first taxable band plus **ALL 377,200** on the excess, total **ALL 2,477,200**. Cash after this income tax is **ALL 13,522,800**.

The company pays **ALL 2,400,000** corporate tax. If it retains the remaining **ALL 13,600,000**, this is available in the company; it is not the owner's cash. If it distributes that entire amount lawfully as a dividend, **ALL 1,088,000** dividend tax leaves the individual **ALL 12,512,000**. Combined company/dividend tax is **ALL 3,488,000**, or **21.8% (computed)** of the original profit. Under these assumptions full distribution leaves the individual-business route **ALL 1,010,800** more after income tax. Retaining company profits instead defers that distribution cost, but does not establish an overall saving after the unresolved costs and obligations ([Law 29/2023 arts. 22–24, 40–41, 58–59, 69(1)(dh)](https://tatime.gov.al/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Ligj-Nr.-29-2023-Per-Tatimin-mbi-te-Ardhurat-i-ndryshuar.pdf)).

```oa-check
name: Ordinary resident individual versus company, retained and fully distributed profit
inputs:
  gross_income: 20000000
  business_profit: 16000000
  personal_deduction: 360000
steps:
  taxable_individual: max(0, business_profit - personal_deduction)
  individual_first_band: min(taxable_individual, 14000000) * 0.15
  individual_excess_band: max(0, taxable_individual - 14000000) * 0.23
  individual_tax: individual_first_band + individual_excess_band
  individual_cash: business_profit - individual_tax
  corporate_tax: business_profit * 0.15
  company_retained: business_profit - corporate_tax
  dividend_tax: company_retained * 0.08
  shareholder_cash: company_retained - dividend_tax
  combined_tax: corporate_tax + dividend_tax
  combined_percent: combined_tax / business_profit * 100
  individual_cash_advantage: individual_cash - shareholder_cash
expected:
  taxable_individual: 15640000
  individual_first_band: 2100000
  individual_excess_band: 377200
  individual_tax: 2477200
  individual_cash: 13522800
  corporate_tax: 2400000
  company_retained: 13600000
  dividend_tax: 1088000
  shareholder_cash: 12512000
  combined_tax: 3488000
  combined_percent: 21.8
  individual_cash_advantage: 1010800
```

## When to refuse or refer

- Never present the zero rate without its gross-income cap, expiry and business-classification conditions. Never describe single-client freelancing as safe merely because an invoice or status declaration exists.
- Never ignore VAT or compulsory contributions when income tax is zero. Never recommend a structure based on corporate tax alone when the owner needs personal cash.
- Never recommend concealing turnover, fictitious expenses, sham contracts or fragmentation without substance. Obtain a licensed Albanian tax adviser's documented view where aggregation, classification or a held point changes the answer.
- Never contradict the income-tax or contributions owners without reconciling the primary law. Never turn a research gap or a “Not yet confirmed” item into usable guidance; no definitive optimization recommendation follows from this guide alone.

A qualified professional, such as a licensed Albanian tax adviser, must review and sign off all outputs before action.

## Filing and payment

The annual business-income/corporate return is due **31 March of the following year**, including business income taxed at zero; art. 67(2) requires the personal business return. The supported self-employed status declaration accompanies the personal business return at the registered regional tax directorate in that period. For the 2026 business year this means **31 March 2027 (calendar application)**. Instruction 12.8.1 expressly clarifies the following-year timing, while the added wording of art. 12 refers to March of the tax year. Do not read that isolated wording as permission to omit the return or declaration ([Law 29/2023 arts. 12, 61, 67](https://tatime.gov.al/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Ligj-Nr.-29-2023-Per-Tatimin-mbi-te-Ardhurat-i-ndryshuar.pdf); [Instruction 26/2023 paras. 12.8.1, 61](https://tatime.gov.al/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Udhezimi-nr-26-date-8.9.2023-Per-tatimin-mbi-te-ardhuart.pdf)).

Use the linked income/corporate owners for the return fields, instalments, payment and penalties; use the VAT/contributions owners for their separate forms and deadlines. This planning decision does not replace filing either return.

## Completion checklist

- Record the residence, activity, year, legal person, complete gross-income schedule and separate taxable-profit reconciliation.
- Document both concentration tests, employment facts and any supported exception; retain the declaration and filing evidence where required.
- Calculate income tax for each genuinely available route, showing company funds separately from the shareholder's cash and adding separately resolved costs/obligations.
- Check VAT eligibility, registration/election timing and contribution coverage even where business income tax is zero.
- Stop on held facts or legal issues; obtain licensed local review before implementing the recommendation. Do not project the temporary concession beyond its statutory expiry.
