# District of Columbia corporate and unincorporated franchise tax (2026)

**Circular 230 §10.37 disclosure:** This skill supplies general state-tax working papers from the sources below. It is not a professional approval. A qualified tax professional must review the facts, assumptions and outputs before filing.

**Answer first:** DC imposes corporate and unincorporated business franchise taxes at 8.25%, with minimum tax $250 when DC gross receipts are $1 million or less, or $1,000 above that. DC nonresident wage immunity does not exempt a separate business franchise-tax obligation. D-20 and D-30 are separate return routes within this one business-tax owner. [OTR rates](https://otr.cfo.dc.gov/page/dc-business-franchise-tax-rates); [unincorporated definition](https://code.dccouncil.gov/us/dc/council/code/sections/47-1808.01).

## Scope

2026 DC business franchise tax: corporations including S corporations on D-20, and taxable unincorporated businesses on D-30. Latest annual form mechanics are the 2025-2026 filing cycle; 2026 estimate instructions are published. Group/industry details and final annual conformity remain partial. Federal corporation computation belongs to `us-form-1120-c-corp`, partnership federal work to `us-form-1065-partnership`; individual state tax to `dc-income-tax`.

## Ask the client first

1. Legal/federal classification, DC activities/receipts, fiscal year and existing accounts?
2. For an unincorporated activity, total gross income, personal services actually rendered by owners, and whether capital materially produces income?
3. Federal income, DC modifications, depreciation, NOL vintages and credits?
4. Unitary related entities, ownership, group/agent elections and disregarded-entity disclosures?
5. Sales destinations, property income, nonbusiness items and apportionment evidence?
6. Prior-year tax, estimates, extensions, federal changes and tax notices?

### Determine the return and base

D-20 applies to corporations/financial institutions doing business or receiving DC-source income, including S corporations and Q-Subs treated as C corporations for DC. OTR-approved exemptions may apply; exempt organizations with taxable unrelated business income can still file. Do not assume a federal S election removes DC entity tax. [D-20 p.5](https://otr.cfo.dc.gov/sites/default/files/dc/sites/otr/publication/attachments/2025_D-20_Book_Final_wLinks_02272026.pdf).

For D-30, test each separate business. The statute excludes specified nonincorporable/professional businesses, the more-than-80% personal-service/no-material-capital category and specified investment activities with exceptions. A return not required due to $12,000-or-less gross income can require D-30N disclosure. A disregarded entity may be disclosed on the owner's D-20/D-30 rather than file separately if conditions are met. A partnership outside D-30 may still file D-65. Check income routing to D-40; do not tax the same franchise-taxed income again without the allowed individual subtraction. [§47-1808.01](https://code.dccouncil.gov/us/dc/council/code/sections/47-1808.01); [D-30 pp.6-7](https://otr.cfo.dc.gov/sites/default/files/dc/sites/otr/publication/attachments/2025_D30_Book_Final_wLinks_02232026.pdf); `dc-income-tax`.

Use the applicable return's federal-income starting records and DC additions/subtractions. The 2025-2026 filing cycle instructions address depreciation differences, disallowed bonus/production-property expensing and limited state expensing, bond interest, section 1202, business-interest and grant adjustments. These prior-cycle directions are not a final determination of the 2026 emergency/successor-law chain. Preserve federal/state asset bases and refer 2026 conformity before final computation. [D-20 pp.8-12](https://otr.cfo.dc.gov/sites/default/files/dc/sites/otr/publication/attachments/2025_D-20_Book_Final_wLinks_02272026.pdf); [D-30 pp.8-12](https://otr.cfo.dc.gov/sites/default/files/dc/sites/otr/publication/attachments/2025_D30_Book_Final_wLinks_02232026.pdf).

Allocate nonbusiness income and apportion business income on the prescribed schedules. Separate filers complete Schedule F even if wholly DC. Combined entities use the combined reporting schedules instead, including member-level factors/minimums. A unitary business can require combined reporting even when the federal group differs; consolidation is not a substitute. [D-20 pp.5,11-15](https://otr.cfo.dc.gov/sites/default/files/dc/sites/otr/publication/attachments/2025_D-20_Book_Final_wLinks_02272026.pdf); [D-30 pp.7,11-15](https://otr.cfo.dc.gov/sites/default/files/dc/sites/otr/publication/attachments/2025_D30_Book_Final_wLinks_02232026.pdf).

D-30 has a distinct owner salary allowance and exemption before the state loss deduction. Do not give it to a corporation or subtract the entire owners' draws. In the 2025-2026 filing cycle instructions, reasonable compensation for actual owner services is reduced by independent management/collection fees and limited in aggregate to 30% of line 31 DC total net income; draws alone are not services. Line 33 allows up to $5,000 exemption for a full year, denied where the specified loss condition applies and prorated by days for a short period; complete line 33a or the instructions deny it. These are explicitly prior-cycle mechanics pending final 2026 confirmation. Compute the allowable DC NOL separately from federal NOL; preserve vintages and state usage. The read prior-cycle corporate booklet disallows carrybacks; D-30 line 35 limits post-2017 losses to 80% of DC taxable income before that deduction. Its Schedule F uses single-sales-factor apportionment for ordinary multistate business. After apportionment and allowed deductions, apply the rate, credits and minimum-tax floor; receipts determine the minimum tier, not profit. [D-20 pp.8,10-15](https://otr.cfo.dc.gov/sites/default/files/dc/sites/otr/publication/attachments/2025_D-20_Book_Final_wLinks_02272026.pdf); [D-30 instructions for lines 32-40](https://otr.cfo.dc.gov/sites/default/files/dc/sites/otr/publication/attachments/2025_D30_Book_Final_wLinks_02232026.pdf).

### Register, file, pay

Register online FR-500 through MyTax.DC.gov; accounts do not auto-register from a return/payment. Use D-20 or D-30 for the actual tax year, approved e-file or the prescribed paper process, federal support, required credit/loss/apportionment/group schedules and an authorized signature. The latest annual layout puts rate tax on line 37, credits on 38, minimum-tax gross receipts on 39 and net tax on 40, then payments and balance/refund; final 2026 line numbers remain unconfirmed. Save acceptance and bank confirmation. [D-20 pp.10-15](https://otr.cfo.dc.gov/sites/default/files/dc/sites/otr/publication/attachments/2025_D-20_Book_Final_wLinks_02272026.pdf); [D-30 pp.10-15](https://otr.cfo.dc.gov/sites/default/files/dc/sites/otr/publication/attachments/2025_D30_Book_Final_wLinks_02232026.pdf).

The read 2025-2026 filing cycle annual deadline is the fifteenth day of the fourth month; exempt organizations with UBI use the fifth month. Do not file a 2025 form merely relabeled 2026. Apply for the DC extension (FR-128 corporate; FR-130 unincorporated) by the original deadline with payment; a federal extension alone does not extend DC filing. 2026 D-20ES/D-30ES pay estimates by the published dates. Payments over $5,000 per period must be electronic. [annual booklets](https://otr.cfo.dc.gov/sites/default/files/dc/sites/otr/publication/attachments/2025_D-20_Book_Final_wLinks_02272026.pdf); [2026 D-30ES](https://otr.cfo.dc.gov/sites/default/files/dc/sites/otr/publication/attachments/2026_D30ES_Book_wLinks03272026.pdf).

The prior-cycle booklets impose late filing/payment additions, 10% annually compounded daily interest and further collection/fraud consequences. Underpayment of 2026 business estimates uses the published current/prior-year safe harbors and period calculations. Apply the actual notice and no double-counted overlap; these are separate from individual estimates (whose last date is January). [2026 D-30ES p.1](https://otr.cfo.dc.gov/sites/default/files/dc/sites/otr/publication/attachments/2026_D30ES_Book_wLinks03272026.pdf); [D-20 pp.7-8](https://otr.cfo.dc.gov/sites/default/files/dc/sites/otr/publication/attachments/2025_D-20_Book_Final_wLinks_02272026.pdf).

## Boundary and exception table

| Situation | Treatment |
| --- | --- |
| Federal S corporation | D-20 route, not automatic state pass-through exemption |
| Exactly 80% owner personal services | Does not satisfy the more-than-80% exclusion |
| More than 80%, but material capital | Exclusion still fails |
| Loss year | Minimum and return may remain due |
| DC gross receipts exactly $1 million | Lower minimum tier; only amounts above it use higher tier |
| Business closure or asset sale | Final return and disposition income may still be required |
| Sports facilities fee, estate, personal/real property | Separate obligations; not discharged by franchise return |

## Worked cases

**Case 1:** Assume final allowable DC taxable income $2,000, no credits, and DC minimum-tax receipts $1 million. Rate tax is $165, but pay the $250 minimum. If receipts are $1,000,001, the minimum becomes $1,000. If income is $40,000, rate tax is $3,300 and exceeds either minimum. Do not derive the minimum from net profit.

```oa-check
name: DC business franchise minimum both tiers
inputs:
  small_income: 2000
  larger_income: 40000
steps:
- computed_small: small_income * 0.0825
- low_tier: max(computed_small, 250)
- high_tier: max(computed_small, 1000)
- computed_larger: larger_income * 0.0825
expected:
  computed_small: 165
  low_tier: 250
  high_tier: 1000
  computed_larger: 3300
```
**Case 2, both routes:** A business projects $12,000 current-year tax and had $8,000 tax for the prior full year. Current route is $10,800; prior route $8,800, or $2,200 quarterly. If projected current tax instead falls to $6,000, current route is $5,400, or $1,350 quarterly. The smaller permitted route changes with the facts; timely paid credits/deposits must be included.

```oa-check
name: DC 2026 business estimates choice
inputs:
  prior: 8000
  high_current: 12000
  low_current: 6000
steps:
- prior_route: prior * 1.1
- high_required: min(high_current * 0.9, prior_route)
- low_required: min(low_current * 0.9, prior_route)
- high_quarter: high_required / 4
- low_quarter: low_required / 4
expected:
  prior_route: 8800
  high_required: 8800
  low_required: 5400
  high_quarter: 2200
  low_quarter: 1350
```

## When to refuse or refer

Refer owner-service/material-capital disputes, special professional exclusions, combined group/elections, industry apportionment, federal immunity, 2026 depreciation/conformity, special credits, NOL vintage/ownership restrictions and D-30 salary allowance/exemption before filing. No final tax computation can omit an allowable but unconfirmed deduction silently.

## Filing and payment

Use the correct entity account and tax year. Reconcile rate tax, minimum, credits and payments; obtain the actual-year forms and resolve the disclosed partials before filing. Extensions defer filing only.

## Completion checklist

- D-20/D-30/exclusion decision documented and D-65/D-30N considered.
- Federal/state reconciliation and group status complete.
- Apportionment, deductions, NOL and minimum tier separately supported.
- Correct estimates, extension, signed return and payment confirmations retained.
