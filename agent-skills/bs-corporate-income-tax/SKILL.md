# Bahamas company taxation: no corporate income tax, business licence tax and DMTT

> **General reference only.** This skill is general tax/accounting reference material for AI-assisted workflows. It has not been reviewed for any specific person's facts, documents, elections, deadlines, residency, filing status, or local procedures. Do not rely on it to file, pay, amend, or take a tax position without review by a qualified professional in the relevant jurisdiction.

## Who this applies to

Companies, partnerships and other entities carrying on business in or from The Bahamas in 2026: domestic companies, international business companies (IBCs), branches and constituent entities of multinational groups. Authority: Department of Inland Revenue (DIR). Amounts are BSD.

There is no corporate income tax on profits, and no Bahamian Act imposes withholding tax on dividends, interest or royalties. A company's direct tax is the business licence tax on turnover. Only constituent entities of large multinational groups pay the DMTT.

## The base

### Business licence tax

Turnover is all revenue from business activities, in money and money's worth, without deductions (DIR). The estimated tax for a year is the band rate applied to the prior year's turnover; the difference against actual is settled the next 31 March (Business Licence Act 2023, ss. 26 and 35). Companies with turnover of BSD 250,000 or more file an accountant's report, a BICA review report up to BSD 4.99 million, and audited statements from BSD 5 million (DIR renewal page).

### DMTT

The DMTT applies the OECD GloBE Model Rules as defined in the Act to Bahamian constituent entities of in-scope groups. Profit tax computation follows those rules; this skill does not compute the GloBE effective tax rate. Refer.

| VAT returns | 21 days after each period, see `bahamas-vat` |
| DMTT | As in the table |

## Penalties and interest

- Business licence: late renewal or return BSD 100; late cessation notice BSD 100; late payment 10% of the tax; 5% a year on payments 30 days or more late (Business Licence Act 2023, Fourth Schedule).
- Advance ruling not acted on in time through the applicant's fault: the lesser of BSD 1,000 and 0.1% of the assessment that would have arisen (s. 37(5) as replaced in 2025).
- DMTT: penalties and interest under the Act; instalment shortfalls under new s. 7A.

## Cross-border points

- No withholding tax on payments abroad of dividends, interest or royalties.
- Non-Bahamians need Bahamas Investment Authority approval to carry on business (DIR annual licence page).
- The DMTT is a qualified domestic top-up for a group's Bahamian entities; the parent's jurisdiction may apply its own rules.
- Economic substance reporting (CESRA) is separate; see `bs-company-formation`.

## Two worked examples that recompute

### Bahamian trading company, turnover BSD 2 million

A domestic company with no foreign group members had turnover of BSD 2,000,000 in 2025. Corporate income tax: none. DMTT: out of scope (purely domestic). Business licence tax for 2026: 0.75% × 2,000,000 = BSD 15,000, due by 31 March 2026 or in four instalments of BSD 3,750. It files a BICA review report with its renewal.

```oa-check
name: BS company 2026 business licence, turnover 2m
inputs:
  turnover: 2000000
  rate: 0.0075
steps:
  - corporate_income_tax: 0
  - bl_tax: turnover * rate
  - instalment: bl_tax / 4
expected:
  bl_tax: 15000
  instalment: 3750
```

### Decision: is the group in DMTT scope?

A group with a foreign parent and a Bahamian subsidiary had consolidated revenue of EUR 800 million, 700 million, 760 million and 600 million in the four years before 2026. Two of the four years are at least EUR 750 million, so it is in scope for 2026. Had only one year reached EUR 750 million it would be out.

```oa-check
name: BS DMTT revenue test, two of four years
inputs:
  y1: 800
  y2: 700
  y3: 760
  y4: 600
  threshold: 750
steps:
  - hit1: min(floor(y1 / threshold), 1)
  - hit2: min(floor(y2 / threshold), 1)
  - hit3: min(floor(y3 / threshold), 1)
  - hit4: min(floor(y4 / threshold), 1)
  - years_meeting: hit1 + hit2 + hit3 + hit4
  - in_scope: min(floor(years_meeting / 2), 1)
expected:
  years_meeting: 2
  in_scope: 1
```

## Ask the client first

1. Entity type: domestic company, IBC, partnership, branch, family office, financial services entity?
2. Prior year and current year turnover.
3. Is it part of a group with any entity outside The Bahamas, and what was consolidated revenue in each of the last four years?
4. Fiscal year end, and has it a BIN and a DMTT registration?
5. Does it sell liquor or carry on insurance (2026 licence changes)?
