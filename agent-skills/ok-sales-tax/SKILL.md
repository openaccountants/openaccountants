# Oklahoma sales and use tax, tax year 2026

> **Source-cited draft.** Written by Codex. Not reviewed by an accountant. This skill supports a professional working paper; review the taxpayer facts and current forms before filing. Circular 230 section 10.37: this material is not a legal opinion or a substitute for reasonable factual and legal inquiry.

**Answer first.** Oklahoma normally charges 4.5% state sales tax plus applicable local tax. Qualifying food and food ingredients have a 0% state rate but remain locally taxable; bottled soft drinks and packaged candy can qualify, unlike the old blanket full-rate treatment.

## Scope and who this is for

Oklahoma retail sales and use tax, physical/remote sellers, product classification and filing. `us-sales-tax` owns the federal/multistate framework. Current OTC pages and sales return are read; the consolidated rules cited below are the published 2025 edition, used as a **2025-2026 filing cycle** baseline. Exact local rates, later amendments and identified special cases require confirmation rather than a guessed universal combined rate.

## Ask the client first

1. What products/services/software delivery modes, packaging, food preparation, utensils and prepared-food sales percentage apply?
2. Where are inventory, workers, business locations, pickup and delivery addresses; what physical presence exists?
3. What Oklahoma taxable sales occurred this and the preceding calendar year, and what sales were facilitated by marketplaces?
4. What exemptions, manufacturer/agricultural permits, resale certificates and other-state tax evidence are available?
5. What OkTAP account, assigned frequency, prior-fiscal-year average monthly tax, semimonthly payments and current location codes/rates apply?
6. For a holiday sale, what date, article type and actual sales price including coupon conditions apply?

### Nexus, registration and sourcing

Physical presence is separate from the remote-seller economic threshold. Remote taxable Oklahoma sales must **exceed $100,000** in the preceding or current calendar year; there is no transaction-count alternative. The old July 2018 date is not evidence that the current threshold applied then. Marketplace-facilitated and direct sales need separate records: the agency FAQ describes a distinct Marketplace Sales Act collection-versus-notice/reporting regime, so do not assume every facilitator has the same $100,000 test or that all platform sales create a second tax collection. Current facilitator threshold/election details remain a listed gap. Source: [official text](https://oklahoma.gov/tax/helpcenter/businesses.html).

Register for the sales/use permit through [OkTAP](https://oktap.tax.ok.gov) using the business registration service, identify locations and activity, and retain the assigned account/permit. A sales permit allows genuine purchases for resale; it does not make the buyer’s own equipment tax-free. Source: [official text](https://oklahoma.gov/tax/helpcenter/businesses.html).

At the place of business, source to the pickup/sale location. For delivered goods, source where the purchaser receives them. Apply the actual city/county **sales or use** rate and effective date for that address; do not assume those local rates are identical. The former approximate 11.5% ceiling and 600-jurisdiction count are not verified 2026 inputs. Source: [official text](https://oklahoma.gov/tax/businesses/sales-use-tax.html).

### Food classification and holiday

The dedicated OTC food page and STS-20002-C both establish **August 29, 2024**, not November 1. An older general help FAQ still uses August 30; use the dedicated guidance, return and current food-rule baseline consistently. Grocery food and ingredients qualify for 0% state but local tax remains. **Packaged candy and bottled/canned soft drinks can qualify**; they are not automatically treated like restaurant food. Dietary supplements, alcohol, pet food and ordinary OTC medications remain outside the grocery exemption. Sources: [official text](https://oklahoma.gov/tax/businesses/state-sales-tax-on-food-and-food-ingredients.html), [candy FAQ](https://oklahoma.gov/tax/helpcenter/businesses.html), [STS-20002-C current instructions](https://oklahoma.gov/content/dam/ok/en/tax/documents/forms/businesses/general/STS-20002-C.pdf).

Determine whether the seller heats food, mixes ingredients or provides utensils. A seller above 75% prepared-food sales that makes utensils available generally treats food as prepared, subject to the four-or-more-serving/package exception and its conditions. Other exceptions include food requiring cooking, only sliced/repackaged food, qualifying unheated variable-weight/volume sales without utensils, and qualifying third-party manufactured food. Do not use “all prepared food always taxable” or “all grocery-store items exempt” as a substitute for the decision. Source: [official text](https://oklahoma.gov/tax/businesses/state-sales-tax-on-food-and-food-ingredients.html).

Ordinary clothing is taxable outside the holiday. Under OAC 710:65-13-511, the first-Friday-to-Sunday rule gives **August 7-9, 2026**. Eligible clothing/footwear must be **less than $100**, not $100 or less. Accessories, rentals and special athletic/protective articles are excluded. The FAQ requires participating retailers not to charge tax on eligible sales; the old statement that local jurisdictions may simply opt out is withdrawn. Confirm final 2026 administration notices before a retrospective claim. Sources: [OTC Chapter 65, 2025-2026 filing cycle](https://oklahoma.gov/content/dam/ok/en/tax/documents/resources/rules-and-policies/agency-rules/2025/Chapter_65-Sales_and_Use_Tax-2025.pdf), [holiday FAQ](https://oklahoma.gov/tax/helpcenter/businesses.html).

### Software, medical and production exemptions

| Category | Treatment supported by the opened rule baseline |
| --- | --- |
| Prewritten software on tangible media / load and leave | Taxable. |
| Electronically delivered prewritten software / electronic music, video, books | Not subject to the sales-tax levy under OAC 710:65-19-156(b), contrary to the former taxable assertion. |
| Custom program | Service transaction, normally not taxable; hardware/software bundles and modifications require separate pricing and evidence. |
| Hosted access/SaaS | Establish whether there is a transfer of software or a separately taxable bundled transaction. Do not classify every contract from the label alone. |
| Human prescription drugs, insulin/medical oxygen | Exempt under the specific medical rule; ordinary OTC drugs do not receive that drug exemption. |
| Prosthetics/DME/mobility equipment | Apply the practitioner/administered/distributed/prescribed conditions and purchaser/work-order documentation; not every general medical purchase qualifies. |
| Manufacturing equipment/materials/repair supplies | Qualifying production purchases can be exempt with manufacturer permit evidence. Administration/distribution and extractive oil/gas drilling are outside the manufacturing operation. |
| Farm machinery/feed/seed/fertilizer | Requires qualifying for-profit production and actual direct agricultural use plus exemption evidence. Highway vehicles, personal lawn/home goods and pets are not blanket farm exemptions. |
| Livestock purchase | Do not infer exemption merely from “breeding/production.” Resale/private-treaty/special-sale and other specific facts control under the agricultural rule. |

Sources: [OTC Chapter 65, 2025-2026 filing cycle](https://oklahoma.gov/content/dam/ok/en/tax/documents/resources/rules-and-policies/agency-rules/2025/Chapter_65-Sales_and_Use_Tax-2025.pdf), OAC 710:65-19-64, 19-156, 13-170/171, 13-150.1, 13-15 and 7-8/9. These are the expressly identified **2025-2026 filing cycle** rule baseline, not a claim that the 2026 consolidated rules were read.

### Exemption evidence and use tax

Resale evidence needs purchaser name/address, business type, permit number and signature. Manufacturer transactions need the manufacturer permit or the prescribed verified information. For general vendor relief, the opened OAC 710:65-7-6 baseline allows timely exemption documentation within **90 days after sale**, with conditions; the old absolute “all certificates at or before sale” statement was too broad. Renewal intervals and statutory category exceptions must still be tracked. Do not confuse a three-year renewal interval with a universal retention period. Source: [OTC Chapter 65, 2025-2026 filing cycle](https://oklahoma.gov/content/dam/ok/en/tax/documents/resources/rules-and-policies/agency-rules/2025/Chapter_65-Sales_and_Use_Tax-2025.pdf).

Taxable goods brought into Oklahoma for storage, use or consumption incur applicable use tax if not sufficiently taxed. Separate consumer-use and vendor-use accounts/forms as appropriate; reconcile any allowable other-state tax credit with evidence and the statutory cap. **Vehicles are a different tax system**: OAC 710:65-19-215 prescribes 1.25% state sales/use without ordinary local tax for sales subject to vehicle excise. Do not calculate a car at an ordinary 8.5% combined rate and subtract Texas tax. Source: [official text](https://oklahoma.gov/tax/businesses/sales-use-tax.html), [official text](https://oklahoma.gov/tax/helpcenter/businesses.html), [OTC Chapter 65, 2025-2026 filing cycle](https://oklahoma.gov/content/dam/ok/en/tax/documents/resources/rules-and-policies/agency-rules/2025/Chapter_65-Sales_and_Use_Tax-2025.pdf).

## Worked cases

**Case 1, classification decision.** Assume a documented 4.5% local rate. A $200 qualifying grocery purchase owes **$9** local tax and no state tax; the same $200 sold as taxable prepared food owes $9 state + $9 local = **$18**. A qualifying $10 packaged candy purchase owes **$0.45**, not the old assumed full state-plus-local tax. These are assumed local rates, not verified 2026 Tulsa rates.

```oa-check
name: OK qualifying food versus prepared product
inputs:
  price: 200
  candy: 10
  local_rate: 0.045
  state_rate: 0.045
steps:
- food_tax: price*local_rate
- prepared_tax: price*(state_rate+local_rate)
- candy_tax: candy*local_rate
expected:
  food_tax: 9
  prepared_tax: 18
  candy_tax: 0.45
```

**Case 2, holiday decision.** Assume an eligible general-use shirt bought during the qualifying holiday and an illustrative combined ordinary rate of 8.625%. At $99.99 the shirt qualifies and tax is **$0**. At exactly $100 it fails the strict less-than test and tax is **$8.625**, rounded to **$8.63** for this transaction. An accessory at $99.99 does not qualify merely because its price is low.

```oa-check
name: OK holiday less than100 boundary
inputs:
  under_price: 99.99
  at_price: 100
  rate: 0.08625
  under_exempt: 1
  at_exempt: 0
  accessory_exempt: 0
steps:
- under_tax: under_price*rate*(1-under_exempt)
- at_tax: at_price*rate*(1-at_exempt)
- at_rounded: floor(at_tax*100+0.5)/100
- accessory_tax: under_price*rate*(1-accessory_exempt)
expected:
  under_tax: 0
  at_tax: 8.625
  at_rounded: 8.63
  accessory_tax: 8.6241375
```


**Preserved prior examples, corrected:** $800 furniture at an assumed 8.625% produces $69 tax and $869 total. A remote seller with $110,000 relevant taxable sales in the prior calendar year exceeds the $100,000 test. A $50,000 production machine can have zero tax only with manufacturer qualification, qualifying use and permit support. $100,000 ordinary drilling equipment at an assumed 8.5% produces $8,500 tax/$108,500 total absent a proved exemption; the old $1 million scenario is $85,000 tax under the same assumptions. A $100 hotel room with assumed 8.5% sales and 3.5% lodging tax produces $12 tax/$112 total, subject to verified separate tax bases. The old $10 candy/$0.86 case is withdrawn for a qualifying packaged grocery sale: at its assumed 4.125% local rate, tax is $0.4125, rounded $0.41. The $20,000 Texas vehicle/$1,650 prior-tax example does **not** establish $50 Oklahoma tax; the separate 1.25% sales/use component would start at $250 before any permitted credit, while vehicle excise, title and credit rules must be established separately. No final vehicle balance is asserted.


## When to refuse or refer

Refer uncertain service/digital bundles, tribal/member/compact sales, oilfield exceptions, vehicle credits, multi-state nexus, facilitator elections, audit/assessment, voluntary disclosure and penalty abatement. Keep all unconfirmed legacy claims out of a filed return until resolved.

## Filing and payment

Use OkTAP to select the correct sales/use account and period, reconcile gross receipts, exempt receipts, inventory withdrawals/use purchases, state food adjustment and each city/county schedule. The current sales form is **STS-20002-C**, with **STS-20021** for local detail: lines 1/2 gross sales and withdrawals; 3 exemptions; 4 eligible food; 5 state-taxable base; 6 state tax; 7 local tax; 8 combined tax; 9 interest; 10 penalty; 11 payment total. Qualifying food is removed from the state base, while its local base remains taxable. Save acceptance and payment confirmations and file required zero periods. Sources: [STS-20002-C current instructions](https://oklahoma.gov/content/dam/ok/en/tax/documents/forms/businesses/general/STS-20002-C.pdf), [official text](https://oklahoma.gov/content/dam/ok/en/tax/documents/forms/businesses/general/STS-20021.pdf).

Monthly returns are due the **20th of the following month**, with the rule’s next-business-day treatment when the office is closed. Semiannual status requires qualification, written OTC authorization and the $50 monthly conditions; approved periods end June/December with July 20/January 20 due dates. Vendors averaging at least **$2,500 monthly** tax in the prior fiscal year must make semimonthly electronic payments: first-half sales by the 20th of the same month, second half by the 20th of the next month, plus the monthly return. The first-half rule permits the specified 90%-of-actual or 50%-of-same-month-prior-year alternative; it is not a blanket quarterly allowance. Source: [OTC Chapter 65, 2025-2026 filing cycle](https://oklahoma.gov/content/dam/ok/en/tax/documents/resources/rules-and-policies/agency-rules/2025/Chapter_65-Sales_and_Use_Tax-2025.pdf), OAC 710:65-3-1/3.

The current form has **no generic 1% vendor-discount line**. Do not deduct the old unqualified allowance. Interest is 1.25% per month under the return instructions; a 10% penalty follows the 15-day delinquency condition. The 25% rule applies to failure/refusal after written demand, not every late return; fraud has a separate 50% rule. Sources: [STS-20002-C current instructions](https://oklahoma.gov/content/dam/ok/en/tax/documents/forms/businesses/general/STS-20002-C.pdf), [OTC Chapter 65, 2025-2026 filing cycle](https://oklahoma.gov/content/dam/ok/en/tax/documents/resources/rules-and-policies/agency-rules/2025/Chapter_65-Sales_and_Use_Tax-2025.pdf).
