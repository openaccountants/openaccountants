# Spain: foral corporate tax credits for investment, research, employment and environment, 2026 v0.8

This Guide holds part of [es-foral-corporate-tax](es-foral-corporate-tax.md), moved here unchanged on 6 November 2026 so that each Guide stays within the size the site can deliver. Built from the law; not yet approved by an accountant.

## Who this applies to

An ordinary trading company taxed under Bizkaia, Álava, Gipuzkoa or Navarra corporate tax law under the tests in [es-foral-corporate-tax](es-foral-corporate-tax.md#who-this-applies-to). Compute the base, the nominal rate, the minimum taxation and the credit sequence there first; the rows below generate credits that sequence then limits.

## Rates, bands and thresholds

### Investment, research and employment

Determine eligibility before applying the quota limits above. For every claim retain invoices, asset commissioning dates, project/worker certification, grants, related-party information and continued-eligibility evidence.

The Basque art.67.3 general credit base excludes interest, indirect taxes/surcharges, construction margin, tendering technical costs, dismantling/removal costs and land. Cap it at market value, require accounting recognition and ordinarily subtract the grant amount multiplied by (100% minus the company's rate). Apply express special overrides: Álava/Gipuzkoa art.65.3 environmental credits and the applicable bicycle provisions subtract grants in full. Bizkaia art.65 does not contain that environmental subsidy override, so its ordinary art.67.3 reduction is the grant multiplied by (100% minus the company's tax rate). Álava art.62.3 expressly overrides art.67 for the funded investments within its R&D base. B/G art.64 and A art.64 also exclude listed travel, training, project-documentation/management-certification, administration and overhead costs from R&D/innovation bases. Investment credits normally arise on commissioning; the long-order payment election has the one-month contract-notification requirement. Maintain eligible assets for five years, or three for movables, unless useful life is shorter, with the specified three-month replacement exception; earlier disqualification recovers the credit plus interest. Navarra chapter-III bases instead subtract grants in full under art.67.2 and use its own retention/time rules. ### Ordinary social, commuting and environmental credits

The separate women/under-36 indefinite-hire method is B art.66 decies and A/G art.66 octies. For contracts formalised in 2026 the emergency instruments raise the salary percentage from 35% to **45%**, retaining the annual-SMI credit cap. Starting pay must exceed SMI by at least 10%, and the statutory three-year follow-through must reach pay exceeding then-current SMI by at least 70%. Increase and maintain the required indefinite workforce for the next three concluding periods; apply the part-time fractions, suspension/replacement rules, related-entity anti-duplication and recapture plus interest. Gipuzkoa expressly makes this credit incompatible with art.66; B/A art.67.6 also prevents two chapter-III credits for the same expense. Do not add this to the ordinary-hire credit on the same salary. [B art.66 decies; A/G art.66 octies; B26 art.5; A26 art.2; G26 art.5.](https://www.bizkaia.eus/documents/880307/15187815/ca_11_2013.pdf/5a463c1d-a59b-7e48-6429-80fd22f30bc6?t=1768908575887), [Álava NF37/2013](https://web.araba.eus/documents/d/araba/norma-foral-del-impuesto-sobre-sociedades_cas-3-pdf), [Gipuzkoa NF2/2014](https://www.gipuzkoa.eus/documents/2456431/8.0761374E7/NF%202-2014%20%282026%29.pdf/f1231a01-1f83-ee15-e569-7b9b84d44d54), [Bizkaia DFN1/2026](https://www.bizkaia.eus/lehendakaritza/Bao_bob/2026/04/20/I-441_cas.pdf), [Álava DNUF2/2026](https://www.araba.eus/botha/Boletines/2026/045/2026_045_01089_C.pdf), [Gipuzkoa DF-Norma2/2026](https://www.gipuzkoa.eus/documents/2456431/8.813641E7/DF-Norma%202-2026%20medidas%20para%20mitigar%20Crisis%20Oriente%20Medio.pdf/925d814a-7f73-6e58-f249-a1437b1d76bf)

All three provinces provide a new conciliation-plan credit: 5% of liquid quota in the introduction year capped EUR 2,500; 15% in the first year at least one third of staff use a qualifying measure capped EUR 7,500. Micro/small companies increase the two percentages by five points to 10%/20%, retaining those caps. The plan must contain new flexible-hours, continuous-day, telework, leave/reduced-hours, free childcare or commuting measures beyond legally mandatory minimums, available to every eligible worker; supply the required responsible declaration. Calculate the two separate statutory trigger years and apply the ordinary art.67 bucket/minimum. [B art.66 sexies; A art.66 quinquies; G art.66 quater; their current implementing regulations.](https://www.bizkaia.eus/documents/880307/15187815/ca_11_2013.pdf/5a463c1d-a59b-7e48-6429-80fd22f30bc6?t=1768908575887), [Álava NF37/2013](https://web.araba.eus/documents/d/araba/norma-foral-del-impuesto-sobre-sociedades_cas-3-pdf), [Gipuzkoa NF2/2014](https://www.gipuzkoa.eus/documents/2456431/8.0761374E7/NF%202-2014%20%282026%29.pdf/f1231a01-1f83-ee15-e569-7b9b84d44d54)

Preferred EPSV employer contributions under collective bargaining generate 15% credit when imputed contributions are at least 1.5% but below 2.5% of annual gross pay, 20% from 2.5% to below 4%, and 25% at least 4% or for the specified under-36 employee contributions. Other eligible employment social-provision systems generate 10% where contributions reach at least 1.5% of annual gross pay or favour under-36 employees, including qualifying EU/EEA equivalents. Use the local regulatory annual-gross-pay denominator and the age-at-accrual provisions; the base is qualifying contributions actually imputed from collective bargaining. These permanent methods apply in 2026; the larger credits expressly confined to 2025 do not. [B arts.66 septies/octies and DA 31–33; A arts.66 sexies/septies; G arts.66 quinquies/sexies and implementing regulations.](https://www.bizkaia.eus/documents/880307/15187815/ca_11_2013.pdf/5a463c1d-a59b-7e48-6429-80fd22f30bc6?t=1768908575887), [Álava NF37/2013](https://web.araba.eus/documents/d/araba/norma-foral-del-impuesto-sobre-sociedades_cas-3-pdf), [Gipuzkoa NF2/2014](https://www.gipuzkoa.eus/documents/2456431/8.0761374E7/NF%202-2014%20%282026%29.pdf/f1231a01-1f83-ee15-e569-7b9b84d44d54)

For tax periods **beginning from 1 January 2025 through 31 December 2029**, qualifying tax-imputed collective-bargaining contributions **below 1.5%** of the employer's annual gross pay generate a **10% credit for preferred EPSV plans** or **5% for other employment systems**. Apply articles 59 and 67, including the quota limits and the prohibition on claiming two chapter-III credits for the same expense. Keep the permanent under-36 treatment when applicable; do not add a transitional credit on the same contribution. A plan outside the preferred EPSV form, including an EU/EEA employment system, follows the preferred permanent bands and preferred 10% transitional route if its specifications satisfy the statutory preferred-plan requirements. Foreign location alone does not establish that equivalence. Sources: B DA 31–33, A DA 28–30 and G DA 26–28 in the current laws linked above.

For example, assume a 2026 period, an employee aged 40, an established annual-gross-pay denominator of EUR 100,000 and EUR 1,000 of qualifying imputed employer contributions. The contribution ratio is 1%, so the generated credit is EUR 100 for the preferred or qualifying preferred-equivalent route, and EUR 50 for an ordinary other employment system. At exactly EUR 1,500 of contributions, the permanent 1.5% threshold is met: preferred credit is EUR 225 and the other-system credit is EUR 150. These are generated amounts before the separate articles 59/67 usage limits; a below-1.5% contribution is not automatically a zero-credit case.

Commuter bicycles in all three provinces give 10% of qualifying fixed-asset investment: per-bike base maximum EUR 3,000 for the prescribed electric-assisted bicycle and EUR 500 otherwise; total bike base EUR 15,000 for micro/small companies, EUR 20,000 otherwise. Deduct grants in full and record these assets separately. Bizkaia/Gipuzkoa also include parking installations at 10%, base capped EUR 10,000. Álava's art.61 bis covers bicycles and its separate art.61 ter charging-point credit is 15%, plus five points for public access, plus two points for 7.4–22 kW or five for over 22–under 50 kW; the access and applicable power uplift can cumulate. Charging base is capped EUR 5,000 normal/EUR 25,000 high-power per point and total annual credit EUR 10,000. Observe the technical commissioning rules. [B art.66 nonies; A arts.61 bis/61 ter; G art.66 septies.](https://www.bizkaia.eus/documents/880307/15187815/ca_11_2013.pdf/5a463c1d-a59b-7e48-6429-80fd22f30bc6?t=1768908575887), [Álava NF37/2013](https://web.araba.eus/documents/d/araba/norma-foral-del-impuesto-sobre-sociedades_cas-3-pdf), [Gipuzkoa NF2/2014](https://www.gipuzkoa.eus/documents/2456431/8.0761374E7/NF%202-2014%20%282026%29.pdf/f1231a01-1f83-ee15-e569-7b9b84d44d54)

The environmental art.65 credit in each province is 35% for qualifying new renewable/hydrogen/renewable-fuel generation, consumption, distribution, clean-technology-list equipment, carbon capture for the named permitted destinations, and material recycling/circularity investments. The separate 15% branch covers approved contaminated-soil work and the named applied projects (energy recovery where material recovery is unavailable, voluntary ecosystem regeneration beyond compulsory measures, water saving/treatment, best/emerging techniques and eligible sustainable transport). Match the project to a statutory category, obtain the specified regional/provincial certification or reasoned report, apply Bizkaia's art.67.3 subsidy reduction or Álava/Gipuzkoa's express full-grant reduction in art.65.3, prevent incompatible category overlap and apply the 50%-of-residual-quota bucket/minimum exception. Bizkaia and Álava state a EUR 40,000 clean-list passenger-car base cap; Gipuzkoa excludes principal waste-management businesses from the named energy-recovery branch. Financed-credit transfers under art.65 bis need their own signed contract, certification, communication and recipient limit and do not let both project owner and financer claim the transferred amount. [B/A/G arts.65–65 bis; G26 art.8 for revised timing of financed costs.](https://www.bizkaia.eus/documents/880307/15187815/ca_11_2013.pdf/5a463c1d-a59b-7e48-6429-80fd22f30bc6?t=1768908575887), [Álava NF37/2013](https://web.araba.eus/documents/d/araba/norma-foral-del-impuesto-sobre-sociedades_cas-3-pdf), [Gipuzkoa NF2/2014](https://www.gipuzkoa.eus/documents/2456431/8.0761374E7/NF%202-2014%20%282026%29.pdf/f1231a01-1f83-ee15-e569-7b9b84d44d54), [Gipuzkoa DF-Norma2/2026](https://www.gipuzkoa.eus/documents/2456431/8.813641E7/DF-Norma%202-2026%20medidas%20para%20mitigar%20Crisis%20Oriente%20Medio.pdf/925d814a-7f73-6e58-f249-a1437b1d76bf)

For the environmental subsidy calculation, assume EUR 100,000 of otherwise eligible non-vehicle investment, a EUR 20,000 funding grant and a company taxed at 24%. Bizkaia's ordinary article 67.3 reduction is `20,000 × (100% - 24%) = EUR 15,200`; the base is EUR 84,800 and its generated 35% credit is EUR 29,680. Álava/Gipuzkoa article 65.3 instead subtract the full EUR 20,000: base EUR 80,000 and generated credit EUR 28,000. The EUR 1,680 difference is a territorial base rule, before quota limits. Sources: each province's article 65 and Bizkaia article 67.3, in the current corporate laws linked above.

Bizkaia and Álava art.66 bis give 25% of eligible new/recent SME-share investment, increased to 35% for the statutory innovative or silver-economy company category. Only the investment portion within 25% total investor/related-person ownership can form the credit base. Require an eligible unlisted company form, real new business and its people/assets, acquisition at formation or in the following five years (seven for the innovative route, with its high-initial-risk-financing exception), retention **longer than five years**, the issuing company's certificate and innovation evidence where relevant. Exclude continuation/restructuring without a real new activity, enterprises in difficulty, pending aid-recovery orders and excess risk-finance cases. The same funds cannot generate two claims; recover the credit plus interest on failure. Bizkaia expressly makes this a return election that cannot be changed after the voluntary deadline. Do not give Gipuzkoa this corporate credit by copying art.66 bis, which there is a culture provision. [B/A art.66 bis.](https://www.bizkaia.eus/documents/880307/15187815/ca_11_2013.pdf/5a463c1d-a59b-7e48-6429-80fd22f30bc6?t=1768908575887), [Álava NF37/2013](https://web.araba.eus/documents/d/araba/norma-foral-del-impuesto-sobre-sociedades_cas-3-pdf)

Bizkaia's separate silver/care-economy vocational-training credit is 10% of qualifying employee training expense, plus 15% of the excess over the previous two-year average. It requires the statutory sector/activity classification and skills/update/reskilling purpose; exclude employee remuneration and follow the implementing subject/course conditions. [B art.66 ter and BR.](https://www.bizkaia.eus/documents/880307/15187815/ca_11_2013.pdf/5a463c1d-a59b-7e48-6429-80fd22f30bc6?t=1768908575887), [Bizkaia DF203/2013](https://www.bizkaia.eus/documents/880307/15187815/ca_203_2013.pdf/5d2f80fc-9d5b-0f71-b578-56da0a5a0c50?t=1679483614806)

Navarra art.62's R&D financing method requires an unrelated financer, a prior signed financing contract identifying parties, project, budget and funding, no financer ownership of project-result IP, a requested/obtained technical report before contract signature, and joint communication of the report/request and contract before the end of the period generating the credit. The financer claims the transferred qualifying credit limited to **1.20 times its paid financing**; the project company retains the excess and cannot also claim the transferred portion. The financer brings the positive excess of credit used over financing paid into taxable income. Apply the art.53 minimum and art.67 sequencing to the actual claims. [N arts.39,61–62.](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2017-2356)

Navarra foreign-tax credits in arts.56–57 have a **ten-year** insufficient-quota carry, unlike chapter-III investment credits' fifteen years. Credit the lower of actually paid equivalent foreign tax (within treaty entitlement) and Navarra tax attributable to the foreign income; gross up the foreign tax in the income and separate country baskets and each PE as required. Any eligible undeducted foreign tax treated as a business expense follows art.56.2. Do not claim participation/PE exemption and foreign credit for the same income. [N arts.35–36,56–57.](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2017-2356)

Culture, credit financing and specialised IP/reinvestment routes remain additional methods, not universal deductions. Use the separately identified eligibility, contract and communication requirements before applying a specialist claim.

## Worked example

### The text's own control: environmental investment subsidy, Bizkaia against Álava and Gipuzkoa

This is the control stated under "Ordinary social, commuting and environmental credits", recomputed. Otherwise eligible non-vehicle environmental investment **100,000**, a **20,000** funding grant and a company taxed at **24%**.
