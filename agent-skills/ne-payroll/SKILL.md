# Nebraska employer withholding and unemployment, 2026

> **Built from the law. Not yet approved.** Written by Codex; this version has not been reviewed by an accountant. General reference only. Under Circular 230 section 10.37, apply the cited authorities to the actual facts and obtain qualified professional review before filing or acting.

**Answer first:** Nebraska wage withholding uses 2026 Circular EN's period/status tables and allowance/minimum rules, not final-return income-tax bands. UI generally caps annual taxable wages at $9,000 per employee, but category 20 uses $24,000. [2026 Circular EN instructions](https://revenue.nebraska.gov/sites/revenue.nebraska.gov/files/doc/business/Cir_En_2025/2026_instruct.pdf); [2026 percentage tables](https://revenue.nebraska.gov/sites/default/files/doc/business/Cir_En_2025/2026_percent.pdf); [2026 UI rate publication](https://dol.nebraska.gov/webdocs/Resources/Items/2026%20UI%20guide%20to%20understanding.pdf).

## Scope

Ordinary Nebraska employee wage withholding and contributory employer UI. Federal FICA/FUTA, federal 941/940 and federal deposit figures belong to `us-form-941-940-payroll`; do not reproduce them here. Nonresident/military allocation, contractor personal-service withholding, nonwage payments, special UI localization and reimbursing employers are partial boundaries.

## Ask the client first

1. Employer/state accounts, employees/work states, legal employer and successor or PEO facts, UI coverage determination?
2. Pay frequency/status, Nebraska-taxable wages, valid W-4N or eligible before 2020 federal certificate, allowances and extra withholding?
3. Documentation supporting unusually low withholding/exemption and any nonresident 9N allocation or military certificate?
4. Each employee's prior annual covered wages, actual 2026 combined UI rate/category and correct annual wage base?
5. First/second-month withholding totals, quarterly/annual-filer assignment, electronic mandate, annual statement counts and payroll/payment records?

## The method, step by step

1. **Establish accounts and coverage.** Apply for Nebraska withholding using Form 20 and UI through NEworks; federal registration does not establish either state obligation. The employer UI publication requires applying when operations/employment start even if liability is not yet determined. Ordinary liability generally arises with one or more covered workers in 20 weeks or $1,500 quarterly wages; successor/FUTA, agricultural/domestic/nonprofit routes differ. [Circular EN registration](https://revenue.nebraska.gov/sites/revenue.nebraska.gov/files/doc/business/Cir_En_2025/2026_instruct.pdf); [MDOL employer publication, liability](https://dol.nebraska.gov/webdocs/Resources/Items/Employer%20Guide%20to%20Unemployment%20Insurance_Rebrand.pdf).
2. **Select the correct certificate/pay period.** Use W-4N for federal W-4s completed from 2020; eligible before 2020 federal certificates can remain in use. If a post-2019 federal W-4 exists without W-4N, use single/no allowances regardless of marital status. Exempt certificates renew annually under the instructions, and additional withholding can be requested. Calculate from Nebraska-taxable wages for the actual payroll period; do not substitute resident final-return deductions. [certificate and percentage-method instructions](https://revenue.nebraska.gov/sites/revenue.nebraska.gov/files/doc/business/Cir_En_2025/2026_instruct.pdf).
3. **Subtract period allowances, then apply that period's table.** Adjusted wages=max(0, period taxable wages - allowances x period value). Weekly table is reproduced below; for biweekly/monthly/other periods use the exact corresponding official table. Annualization/average/cumulative/part-year alternatives are allowed only under Circular EN's stated federal-method conditions; do not scale weekly dollar bands by guesswork. [determining withholding and allowance table](https://revenue.nebraska.gov/sites/revenue.nebraska.gov/files/doc/business/Cir_En_2025/2026_instruct.pdf); [2026 tables 1-8](https://revenue.nebraska.gov/sites/default/files/doc/business/Cir_En_2025/2026_percent.pdf).
4. **Check the special minimum.** If the percentage-method result is below 1.5% of taxable wages, the instructions require adjustment to at least 50% of the corresponding single/one-allowance or married/two-allowance withholding; substantiated lower withholding is subject to the instructions' documentation rules. Obtain the employee evidence before using a low/shaded result. Add requested extra withholding. This special comparison is not the final income-tax liability. [2026 percentage/wage-bracket minimum instructions](https://revenue.nebraska.gov/sites/revenue.nebraska.gov/files/doc/business/Cir_En_2025/2026_instruct.pdf).
5. **Compute UI separately by employee.** Identify assigned category/base first. Current-period taxable wages=max(0, min(current covered wages, annual base-prior annual taxable wages)); combined tax=taxable wages x assigned combined rate. Show gross/excess/taxable separately. The published 2026 new-employer rates are 1.25% nonconstruction and 5.40% construction, but the actual notice/category governs. The 20% SUIT figure is an **allocation from combined tax**, not another 20% tax on wages. Employer pays UI; do not deduct it from worker pay. [2026 combined-rate table](https://dol.nebraska.gov/UITax/UnemploymentInsuranceTax/CombinedTaxRates); [2026 UI publication](https://dol.nebraska.gov/webdocs/Resources/Items/2026%20UI%20guide%20to%20understanding.pdf); [computing combined tax](https://dol.nebraska.gov/webdocs/Resources/Items/Employer%20Guide%20to%20Unemployment%20Insurance_Rebrand.pdf).

## Worked cases

### Weekly employee above the minimum trigger

Single employee, $1,000 weekly Nebraska-taxable wages, two valid allowances, no additional withholding. Adjusted wages are $906.16. Weekly raw withholding=$31.860368, which is above 1.5% of gross wages ($15); no special minimum adjustment is triggered. Illustrated withholding is $31.86 to cents before the payroll system's permitted rounding convention. This uses withholding bands, not 4.55% final-return tax.

```oa-check
name: NE weekly withholding 2026
inputs:
  wages: 1000
  allowances: 2
steps:
  - adjusted: max(0, wages - allowances * 46.92)
  - raw: 25.85 + (adjusted - 772) * 0.0448
  - minimum_trigger: wages * 0.015
expected:
  adjusted: 906.16
  raw: 31.860368
  minimum_trigger: 15
```

### Ordinary UI cap versus category 20

Two separate contributory employers each pay a worker $3,000 after $8,000 prior annual taxable wages. One has an actual 1.25% rate/$9,000 base: only $1,000 new wages are taxable, giving $12.50. The other has an actual category 20 notice at 5.40%/$24,000 base: all $3,000 are taxable, giving $162. The same wage history does not produce the same cap result.

```oa-check
name: NE UI category wage bases 2026
inputs:
  prior: 8000
  wages: 3000
steps:
  - ordinary_taxable: max(0, min(wages, 9000 - prior))
  - ordinary_tax: ordinary_taxable * 0.0125
  - category20_taxable: max(0, min(wages, 24000 - prior))
  - category20_tax: category20_taxable * 0.054
expected:
  ordinary_taxable: 1000
  ordinary_tax: 12.5
  category20_taxable: 3000
  category20_tax: 162
```

## When to refuse or refer

Refer unsupported low withholding, missing certificates/rate notices, disputed worker status/localization, nonresident/military/contractor rules, reimbursing or successor arrangements and special nonwage payments. Do not infer the UI category from the tax rate alone or add SUIT twice.

## Filing and payment

**Withholding:** Ordinary quarterly 941N is due the last day of the month after the quarter, including zero returns; approved annual filers use a single 941N due January 31. For month 1 of a quarter, deposit if withholding is more than $500; for month 2, deposit if more than $500 was withheld in either month 1 or month 2. Monthly deposits are due the 15th after the month; the quarterly return reconciles remaining liability. Form 501N is the paper-check transmittal when permitted, not an extra filing for electronic deposits. [2026 filing/payment instructions](https://revenue.nebraska.gov/sites/revenue.nebraska.gov/files/doc/business/Cir_En_2025/2026_instruct.pdf).

Annual W-3N reconciliation is separate from 941N and due January 31 following the year, with required W-2/other covered statements. More than 50 covered forms requires electronic filing under this 2026 publication; obey an actual electronic-payment mandate. Obtain current file specifications and actual weekend/holiday calendar before submission. [annual reconciliation/electronic instructions](https://revenue.nebraska.gov/sites/revenue.nebraska.gov/files/doc/business/Cir_En_2025/2026_instruct.pdf).

**UI:** File combined tax and employee wage reports each quarter and pay via NEworks or the permitted assigned route. Ordinary deadlines are April 30, July 31, October 31 and January 31; report gross wages even after the cap and file required zero reports. The employer publication describes electronic reporting/payment when payroll exceeded $100,000 in either of the two preceding years. Reimbursers retain wage-report duties with a different payment basis. Confirm current notices/calendar rather than assuming withholding and UI forms are interchangeable. [reporting/paying and quarterly wage reports](https://dol.nebraska.gov/webdocs/Resources/Items/Employer%20Guide%20to%20Unemployment%20Insurance_Rebrand.pdf).

## Completion checklist

Confirm accounts/coverage; retain correct certificate and minimum-rule evidence; select actual period/status/allowance table; apply UI category/base/combined rate per worker; reconcile deposits/quarterly returns and annual statements; preserve confirmations and resolve special facts before filing.
