# Guyana tax overview (2026) v1.0

> **General reference only.** This skill is general tax/accounting reference material for AI-assisted workflows. It has not been reviewed for any specific person's facts, documents, elections, deadlines, residency, filing status, or local procedures. Do not rely on it to file, pay, amend, or take a tax position without review by a qualified professional in the relevant jurisdiction.

**Which text of the law.** The figures below come from the statutes as the Guyana Revenue Authority (GRA) publishes them: the Income Tax Act, Cap. 81:01 (ITA) and the Corporation Tax Act, Cap. 81:03 (CTA), both revised to 1 April 2026; the Value-Added Tax Act, Cap. 81:05 and the Value-Added Tax Regulations 2005, revised to 27 March 2026; and the Capital Gains Tax Act, Cap. 81:20 (CGTA), revised to 15 September 2022. The 2026 consolidations already include the Fiscal Enactments (Amendment) Act 2026 (Act No. 3 of 2026), which set the 2026 personal allowance and band.

**Year of income and year of assessment.** Income tax and corporation tax are charged for a year of assessment on the income of the preceding calendar year, the year of income (CTA s. 2, 6(1); ITA s. 69). Income earned in 2026 is year of assessment 2027, and its return is due on 30 April 2027. A GRA table labelled "YA 2026" is about 2025 income.

**Ask the client first.** Before using any figure below, find out:
1. Is the person an individual or a company, and are they resident in Guyana (see the residence rows)?
2. Which year of income is the question about (2026 income is year of assessment 2027)?
3. For a company: does at least 75% of its gross income come from trading in goods it did not make (commercial company)?
4. For VAT: taxable supplies in the last 12 months, or expected in the next 12, against G$15,000,000.
5. Any payments to or from non-residents, or any sale of property or shares (capital gains tax).

**How the bands work.** The personal allowance is a deduction from income, not a credit. Above total income of G$5,040,000 (three times G$1,680,000), one third of income is the larger allowance. Chargeable income is then taxed in two slices: 25% on the first G$3,360,000 and 35% on the rest. The GRA's own PAYE and income tax rate pages still show earlier years; the Act wins (see [gy-income-tax](gy-income-tax.md)).

**Other taxes not covered here.** Property tax on net property (individuals are exempt from the 2025 year of income, Property Tax Act s. 6(1)(y); see gy-income-tax), excise tax and customs duty.

### Worked example: a resident consultant and her VAT

Maya is resident in Guyana and self-employed. Her 2026 profit is G$6,000,000 and she has no other deductions. She is VAT-registered and in one month invoices G$1,000,000 plus VAT.

- Allowance: the greater of G$1,680,000 and one third of G$6,000,000 (G$2,000,000), so G$2,000,000.
- Chargeable income: 6,000,000 - 2,000,000 = G$4,000,000.
- Tax: 25% of 3,360,000 = 840,000, plus 35% of 640,000 = 224,000, total G$1,064,000, due by 30 April 2027 less the advance tax she paid in 2026.
- VAT on the invoice: 14% of G$1,000,000 = G$140,000, so the client pays G$1,140,000. Working back from a VAT-inclusive price uses the tax fraction 14/114 (VAT Act s. 20(4)), which gives the same G$140,000. It goes on the return due by the 21st of the next month.
