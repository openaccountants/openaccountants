# Bangladesh virtual-assets refusal and referral v1.1

> **General reference only.** This skill is general tax/accounting reference material for AI-assisted workflows. It has not been reviewed for any specific person's facts, documents, elections, deadlines, residency, filing status, or local procedures. Do not rely on it to file, pay, amend, or take a tax position without review by a qualified professional in the relevant jurisdiction.

## Scope

For questions about Bitcoin, crypto trading, virtual assets or export payment in crypto involving Bangladesh. This routes the user to appropriate legal/tax review and gives no trading or settlement instructions.

## Ask the client first

- Connection to Bangladesh, transaction dates and residence.
- Whether the question concerns payment, purchase, trading, transfer or existing historical exposure.
- Contracts, bank/platform records and any authority notice.

## The method, step by step

1. Apply Bangladesh Bank's prohibition to obtaining virtual assets or currencies in/from/to Bangladesh and facilitating their exchange, transfer or trading. The current foreign-exchange circular compilation explicitly says those restrictions remain in force (https://www.bb.org.bd/mediaroom/circulars/fepd-2/oct292026fepd-209e.pdf, Part H and enclosure B).
2. Do not describe virtual currencies as approved currency, authorized investments or an approved settlement method. Digital representations of recognized fiat, securities or other recognized financial assets are distinguished in the underlying circular; do not classify them solely by a marketing label.
3. For an exporter proposing crypto receipt, do not provide a settlement route. Preserve contracts and existing receipts and refer the foreign-exchange position. Load [freelancer relief](bd-it-freelancer-tax.md) for lawful banking evidence and separate incentive eligibility.
4. For past gains or receipts, do not infer tax-free status from the prohibition or invent a crypto rate. Refer income recognition, disclosure and possible liabilities under the applicable law. The old claim that there is absolutely no possible tax consequence is not a conclusion this router makes.

## When to refuse or refer

Refuse instructions for restricted crypto settlement or facilitation. Refer historical exposure to qualified Bangladeshi legal/tax advisers. Do not present paying tax as authorization for prohibited activity or state a settled special crypto-tax regime.
