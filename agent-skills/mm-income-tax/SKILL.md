# Myanmar Individual Income Tax v1.0

> **General reference only.** This skill is general tax/accounting reference material for AI-assisted workflows. It has not been reviewed for any specific person's facts, documents, elections, deadlines, residency, filing status, or local procedures. Do not rely on it to file, pay, amend, or take a tax position without review by a qualified professional in the relevant jurisdiction.

## Who this applies to

Individuals with income in Myanmar in the financial year 2026-27 (income year 1 April 2026 to 31 March 2027; returns due by 30 June 2027). The rates come from the Union Taxation Law 2026 (National Defence and Security Council Law No. 18/2026 of 15 March 2026, in force from 1 April 2026); the charging rules from the Income Tax Law, both published by the Internal Revenue Department (IRD), Ministry of Finance and Revenue. For the 2025-26 year ending 31 March 2026 (returns due 30 June 2026) the Union Taxation Law 2025 applied; the IRD's own pages still quote it, with the same brackets, reliefs and salary exemption as below.

**Residence (Income Tax Law s 3).** A foreigner is a resident foreigner if he resides in Myanmar for at least 183 days in the income year. A resident citizen or resident foreigner is taxed on income within and outside Myanmar. A non-resident citizen is a citizen who resides and earns income outside Myanmar during the year. A non-resident foreigner is taxed on Myanmar income.

**Out of scope:** companies and other associations of persons ([mm-corporate-income-tax](mm-corporate-income-tax.md)); commercial tax ([myanmar-ct](myanmar-ct.md)); social security ([mm-payroll-social](mm-payroll-social.md)); undisclosed income under Union Taxation Law 2026 s 25 (refer); oil and gas.

## The base

**Order of computation (resident, salary over 4.8 million or any business, professional or other income):**

1. Total income from all heads (salary, profession, business, property, other sources). Rental income from land and buildings is taxed separately at 10% and not combined; capital gains are separate at 10%.
2. Deduct reliefs under Income Tax Law s 6: basic relief (20%, cap 10 million); parents living with the taxpayer, one spouse, children (UTL 2026 s 21); life insurance premiums for the taxpayer and spouse; contributions to prescribed savings funds (the IRD lists Social Security Fund contributions, taxpayer only).
3. Deduct donations under s 6A to approved religious or charitable organisations or relevant funds.
4. Apply the brackets to what remains.
5. Credit tax withheld (salary) and advance tax paid.

**The 4.8 million cliff.** Salary of 4.8 million kyats or less is wholly exempt. Once salary exceeds 4.8 million, the whole salary is taxed after reliefs (s 19(b)).

**Salary withholding.** The employer deducts tax from salary in as nearly equal instalments as possible over the year and pays it to the Union Government within the prescribed period (Income Tax Law s 16(a), (c)). Excess or short deductions are adjusted in later instalments. A salary-only employee does not register or file a return; the employer files the annual salary statement within 3 months after year end (s 18).

**Self-employed and landlords** pay advance income tax quarterly within 10 days after each quarter (s 16-A(a)) and file a return within 3 months after year end. Landlords with rent over 50 million kyats a year also charge 5% commercial tax ([myanmar-ct](myanmar-ct.md)).

**New micro, small and medium enterprises** (start-ups, cottage industries, small-scale domestic production): net profit up to 20 million kyats a year exempt for 3 consecutive years including the year of commencement; tax on the excess only (UTL 2026 s 29).

## Deadlines and filing

1. **Income year:** 1 April 2026 to 31 March 2027.
2. **Salary:** employer withholds monthly; annual salary statement within 3 months after year end (by 30 June 2027).
3. **Other income:** register as a taxpayer at the township revenue office (individuals not yet taxpayers file there); pay advance tax quarterly within 10 days of each quarter end; file the return (Form PaTaKha (WinNga)-1) by 30 June 2027.
4. **Capital gains:** separate return and payment within 30 days of the sale.
5. **Filing methods** (IRD notice of 28 April 2026): in person (date stamped), by post (postmark date) or electronically through the e-Filing Management System (date received). Large and Medium Taxpayer Office taxpayers must file electronically. An extension can be requested in advance from the revenue office with sufficient reason.
6. **Assessment notices:** tax demanded on Form (WinNga)-8 is paid within 21 days (IRD individual page).

## Penalties and interest

- Late return: the greater of 5% of tax owing plus 1% for each month or part month until assessment, and K 100,000 (IRD notice, 28 April 2026).
- Advance tax short or unpaid: 10% of the shortfall (Income Tax Law s 16-A(c)).
- Failure to deduct or pay salary tax: the employer is deemed a defaulter; penalty fixed by notification (s 16(d)).

## Cross-border points

- Resident citizens and resident foreigners: worldwide income.
- Citizens residing abroad: 10% on non-salary foreign income; salary at the lesser method (UTL 2026 s 22), in the foreign currency; foreign tax set off.
- Non-resident foreigners: no reliefs; salary at brackets on total income; other income 22%.
- A non-resident claiming a double taxation agreement on income subject to withholding applies on Form IRD (IT)-11-Q1 (IRD Notification 108/2023). The treaty list is not restated here.
- Income earned in foreign currency (other than capital gains) is taxed in that currency (UTL 2026 s 30).

## Two worked examples that recompute

**Example 1. Resident citizen employee, salary 1,000,000 kyats a month, married with one child, FY 2026-27.** Total 12,000,000. Basic relief 20% = 2,400,000; spouse 1,000,000; child 500,000. Taxable 8,100,000. Tax 5% of (8,100,000 minus 2,000,000) = 305,000 a year. (Any SSB contribution and life insurance premium would reduce it further.)

```oa-check
name: Myanmar employee 12m kyats, spouse and one child, FY 2026-27
inputs:
  salary: 12000000
  spouse: 1000000
  children: 1
steps:
  - basic: min(salary * 0.20, 10000000)
  - taxable: salary - basic - spouse - children * 500000
  - b5: clamp(taxable - 2000000, 0, 8000000) * 0.05
  - b10: clamp(taxable - 10000000, 0, 20000000) * 0.10
  - b15: clamp(taxable - 30000000, 0, 20000000) * 0.15
  - b20: clamp(taxable - 50000000, 0, 20000000) * 0.20
  - b25: max(taxable - 70000000, 0) * 0.25
  - tax: b5 + b10 + b15 + b20 + b25
expected:
  taxable: 8100000
  tax: 305000.00
```

**Example 2 (decision). Accept a raise from 4.8 million to 4.9 million kyats a year? Single, no other reliefs.** At 4,800,000 the salary is exempt: tax nil, take-home 4,800,000. At 4,900,000 the whole salary is taxed: basic relief 980,000, taxable 3,920,000, tax 5% of 1,920,000 = 96,000; take-home 4,804,000. The raise of 100,000 nets only 4,000. The figure that decides is the 4.8 million cliff; the raise is worth taking only if it adds more than the tax it triggers (or the employee has spouse, child or parent reliefs that cut the tax).

```oa-check
name: Myanmar 4.8 million cliff, salary 4.9 million vs 4.8 million
inputs:
  low: 4800000
  high: 4900000
steps:
  - tax_low: 0
  - taxable_high: high - min(high * 0.20, 10000000)
  - tax_high: clamp(taxable_high - 2000000, 0, 8000000) * 0.05
  - net_low: low - tax_low
  - net_high: high - tax_high
  - gain_from_raise: net_high - net_low
expected:
  taxable_high: 3920000
  tax_high: 96000.00
  gain_from_raise: 4000.00
```

## Ask the client first

1. Citizen or foreigner? For a foreigner, days in Myanmar in the income year.
2. Salary only, or also business, professional, rental or other income?
3. Annual salary (is it over 4.8 million kyats)?
4. Spouse, children and parents living with the taxpayer; life insurance; SSB contributions.
5. Any asset sold in the year, and the total value of assets sold?
6. For citizens abroad: country, currency and foreign tax paid.
