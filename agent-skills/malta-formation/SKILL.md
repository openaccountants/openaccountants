# Malta company formation, 2026

## Scope

The workflow for setting up a Maltese company in 2026 under the Companies Act, Cap. 386, with the Malta Business Registry (MBR) through BAROS. It does not repeat the tax work: company tax and refunds [mt-corporate-tax](mt-corporate-tax.md); accounts and audit [malta-financial-statements](malta-financial-statements.md); VAT registration and its thresholds [malta-vat-return](malta-vat-return.md); employer registration and payroll [malta-payroll](malta-payroll.md); the owner's personal tax [malta-income-tax](malta-income-tax.md); selling the shares [selling-a-company-in-malta](selling-a-company-in-malta.md); residence and substance [mt-company-residence-and-substance](mt-company-residence-and-substance.md); sole trader or company [malta-tax-optimization](malta-tax-optimization.md).

Built from the law: not yet approved by an accountant. Forming companies by way of business is a regulated company service (Company Service Providers Act, Cap. 529).

## Ask the client first

1. Who will the shareholders and beneficial owners be, and where do they live? One owner or several?
2. Who will be director and who company secretary?
3. What will the company do, and does that activity need a licence?
4. How much authorised capital, and paid in cash or in assets?
5. Will it employ staff or make taxable supplies in Malta soon?
6. Will profits be paid out or kept (feeds [mt-corporate-tax](mt-corporate-tax.md))?

## The method, step by step

1. **Choose the entity** (comparison below). Default for a trading business with outside customers: a private limited company.
2. **Name.** A private company ends with "private limited company", "limited" or "ltd." (art. 70(2)); a public company with "public limited company" or "p.l.c." (art. 70(1)). The Registrar refuses a name the same as or confusingly close to another partnership's, offensive or undesirable, or reserved (art. 70(4)); "fiduciary", "nominee" or "trustee" needs trustee authorisation (art. 70(5)). Reservation: EUR 10 a name.
3. **Memorandum** (art. 69(1)): private or public; name and residence of each subscriber; company name; registered office in Malta and the company's e-mail address; the objects (not just "any lawful purpose", art. 71); authorised capital, its division into shares, shares taken up by each subscriber and the amount paid up; number of directors, the first directors and how the company is represented; the first company secretary. At the minimum capital it must be fully subscribed (art. 72(2)). Articles are optional; the First Schedule model applies where silent (art. 75). A private writing is enough; a public deed is optional (art. 76(2)).
4. **Pay up the capital.** At least 20% of each share taken up (private) or 25% (public) on signing (art. 72(3)). Non-cash consideration must be "capable of economic assessment", never future services (art. 73(1)), with an independent expert's report approved by the Registrar, or a director's declaration where it does not exceed EUR 50,000, before registration (art. 73(4) to (6)).
5. **Officers.** Each director signs the memorandum or a written consent (art. 139(1)). Every company has a secretary (art. 138(1)); the sole director cannot be secretary except in an exempt company (arts 138(2)(a), 211(4)); a body corporate can be secretary only if it is a registered company service provider (art. 138(2)(c)). No residence rule for directors or secretary.
6. **Register with MBR.** Deliver the memorandum and articles (art. 76) on BAROS or paper, with the fee and the beneficial owner declaration (S.L. 386.19). The company exists from the date on its certificate (art. 77(1)); anyone trading in its name before then is personally liable unless agreed otherwise (art. 78). Registration does not grant any activity licence (art. 77(3)).
7. **Tax.** A company incorporated in Malta is resident from incorporation (Cap. 123 art. 2(1)). Get the tax number; a registered tax practitioner files its return after the company authorises them on form MTCA02 ([mt-corporate-tax](mt-corporate-tax.md)).
8. **VAT**: register under the right article within the time the VAT Act sets ([malta-vat-return](malta-vat-return.md)).
9. **Employer**: register as an FSS payer within fifteen days of the first emoluments accruing and report employees to Jobsplus ([malta-payroll](malta-payroll.md)).
10. **Bank account**: the bank runs its own due diligence. Expect the certificate of registration, memorandum and articles, a board resolution, identity and address documents for every director, shareholder and beneficial owner, and a business description.

### Entity types

| Feature | Sole trader | Partnership en nom collectif | Private limited company | Public limited company |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Legal personality | No | Yes, distinct from the partners (art. 4(4)) | Yes | Yes |
| Liability | Unlimited | Unlimited, joint and several, partnership assets first | Limited to the amount unpaid on shares | Same |
| Members | 1 | 2 or more | At least 2; 1 for a single member company (an exempt company whose objects state its main activity, art. 212); at most 50 | At least 2 |
| Directors | n/a | n/a | At least 1 | At least 2 (art. 137) |
| Secretary | No | No | Yes | Yes (art. 138) |
| Tax | Individual rates | Each partner on their share, unless the partnership elected company treatment (Cap. 372 art. 27) | 35% with refunds | Same |
| Audit | Outside this workflow | Do not infer a blanket audit exemption | Required unless an exemption applies ([malta-financial-statements](malta-financial-statements.md)) | Required |

## Worked cases

### Case 1: EUR 20,000 authorised capital, electronic

Fully subscribed, 20% paid up, filed on BAROS. Band: over EUR 10,000 to EUR 50,000: EUR 379 plus EUR 17 for each EUR 2,500 or part over EUR 10,000. Excess EUR 10,000 = 4 blocks: EUR 68. Registration fee EUR 447; paid up EUR 4,000; annual return fee EUR 300 a year. At the smallest common set-up, EUR 1,200 authorised, the fee is EUR 100, paid up EUR 240, annual return EUR 85.

## When to refuse or refer

- **R-MT-F1, shell company.** Refuse to help form a company whose stated purpose is to hold no real activity in order to hide income or ownership.
- **R-MT-F2, hiding beneficial ownership.** Beneficial owners must be declared; refuse if the purpose is to hide who owns the company.
- **R-MT-F3, regulated activity.** Financial services, gaming, crypto, insurance and investment activities need their own authorisation: refer.
- **R-MT-F4, sole director as secretary**, **R-MT-F5, single member without the conditions**: fix the memorandum before filing.
- Formation by way of business for third parties: Cap. 529 licensing; refer.

## Filing and payment

1. Reserve the name on BAROS (EUR 10).
2. Upload the signed memorandum and articles, the directors' consents, evidence of paid-up capital, the beneficial owner declaration and identity documents; pay the registration fee online.
3. Receive the certificate of registration: the company exists from its date.
4. After registration: tax number and MTCA02; VAT; FSS payer and Jobsplus.
5. Each year: annual return and beneficial owner confirmation within 42 days after the anniversary; a beneficial owner change within 14 days after it is recorded with the company (S.L. 386.19 reg. 6(1)); director, secretary or representation changes within 14 days (art. 146); accounts laid within ten months and delivered to the Registrar within 42 days after that period (art. 183); company tax return by the later of nine months after year end and 31 March of the year of assessment ([mt-corporate-tax](mt-corporate-tax.md)); provisional tax from the second year of assessment ([mt-estimated-tax](mt-estimated-tax.md)).
