# Timor-Leste Personal Income Tax

## Scope

Calculate ordinary individual income tax in USD for 2026. Separate Wage Income Tax (WIT), withheld by employers, from annual non-wage income tax. Correctly withheld wages are final for WIT and excluded from the ordinary annual income base. Petroleum employment and petroleum-contract income require their special regime. This is a source-cited draft, not an accountant attestation. [Tax Act, articles 20–28 and 68–72, republished by Law 5/2019](https://www.mj.gov.tl/jornal/public/docs/2019/serie_1/SERIE_I_NO_33_B.pdf).

The ordinary tables were checked against the republication, Law 17/2023 and the 2026 Budget as amended in June 2026. The 2023 amendment changes the permanent-establishment definition, not these individual bands. The Budget also exempts qualifying foreign cooperation agents working under agreements with Timor-Leste; obtain the agreement before treating their income as exempt. [Law 17/2023, article 8](https://www.mj.gov.tl/jornal/public/docs/2023/serie_1/SERIE_I_NO_32_A.pdf_); [Law 8/2025, article 3](https://www.mj.gov.tl/jornal/public/docs/2025/serie_1/SERIE_I_NO_48_B.pdf); [Law 8/2026, article 2](https://www.mj.gov.tl/jornal/public/docs/2026/serie_1/SERIE_I_NO_22_B.pdf).

## Ask the client first

Obtain a travel calendar covering overlapping periods around the year, permanent-abode evidence, government posting details, every employer's wages/withholding, employment dates and any petroleum/cooperation agreement. For non-wage income obtain source-country details, accounts, invoices, asset register, private-use allocations, withholding certificates, any election out of final service withholding, prior losses and instalments. Confirm the tax year and accounting method; receipts are not necessarily taxable profit.

## The method, step by step

1. **Determine residence.** A natural person is resident if present for at least 183 days in any 12-month period beginning or ending in the year, unless their permanent abode is outside Timor-Leste. A Timor-Leste government official posted abroad at any time in the year is also resident. A permanent home in Timor-Leste is not a separately stated alternative test. Do not replace the rolling test with days in the calendar year. [Tax Act, article 1(nn)](https://www.mj.gov.tl/jornal/public/docs/2019/serie_1/SERIE_I_NO_33_B.pdf).
2. **Classify receipts first.** Residents' ordinary gross income includes domestic and foreign sources; non-residents' includes only Timor-Leste sources. Business income, property income, prizes and other taxable increases in wealth enter the ordinary base unless exempt or finally withheld. Wages subject to WIT do not. Compute each individual's income separately. [Articles 28–29 and 46](https://www.mj.gov.tl/jornal/public/docs/2019/serie_1/SERIE_I_NO_33_B.pdf).
3. **Reconcile final withholding.** Correct WIT discharges the wage obligation; the statute does not restrict this to a single employer. Qualifying service withholding is final unless the recipient makes the irrevocable written election out. Prizes and specified non-resident payments are final, and royalty/rent withholding is final for individual recipients. Exclude finally taxed receipts and their associated expenses from the annual computation. Check the category/certificate before claiming a withholding credit. [Articles 25 and 60–61](https://www.mj.gov.tl/jornal/public/docs/2019/serie_1/SERIE_I_NO_33_B.pdf).
4. **Compute ordinary taxable income.** Reconcile accounting profit to tax rules. Deduct expenses connected with deriving taxable income, subject to statutory restrictions. Personal/dependant expenses, income tax, penalties, partner salaries and interest outside the financial-institution exception are not deductible. Capital expenditure with a useful life exceeding a year follows depreciation/amortization rules; land is excluded from the cost of depreciable buildings. An expense requiring withholding is deductible only after that withholding is remitted. Apply approved-pension-fund and bad-debt deductions only with evidence of their conditions. [Articles 26 and 30–39](https://www.mj.gov.tl/jornal/public/docs/2019/serie_1/SERIE_I_NO_33_B.pdf).
5. **Apply the applicable table.** These are ordinary non-petroleum rates. The annual resident threshold concerns taxable non-wage income, not an additional wage deduction.

| Tax category and period | Rate or threshold and conditions | Official source |
|---|---|---|
| Resident individual's ordinary annual non-wage taxable income | 0% up to USD 6,000; 10% on the excess, computed from the marginal bands | [Tax Act, Annex VI](https://www.mj.gov.tl/jornal/public/docs/2019/serie_1/SERIE_I_NO_33_B.pdf); [ATTL return instructions, line 165](https://attl.gov.tl/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/2023_Income_Tax_Form_Instructions_Final_Port.pdf) |
| Non-resident individual's ordinary annual taxable income | 10% on Timor-Leste-source taxable income without the resident threshold; test final withholding first | [Tax Act, article 28, Annex VI](https://www.mj.gov.tl/jornal/public/docs/2019/serie_1/SERIE_I_NO_33_B.pdf) |
| Resident employee's ordinary full-month taxable wages | 0% up to USD 500; 10% of the portion above USD 500 | [ATTL WIT](https://attl.gov.tl/wage-income-tax/) |
| Non-resident employee's ordinary taxable wages | 10% without a tax-free threshold | [ATTL WIT](https://attl.gov.tl/wage-income-tax/) |
| Ordinary income-tax instalments | 0.5% of relevant turnover; quarterly when prior-year turnover is at most USD 1,000,000, otherwise monthly; exclude exempt and withholding-taxed receipts under the statutory turnover rule | [Tax Act, article 64](https://www.mj.gov.tl/jornal/public/docs/2019/serie_1/SERIE_I_NO_33_B.pdf) |

6. **Reconcile credits and balance.** Deduct allowable foreign-tax credit first, then instalments, then creditable withholding. Foreign credit is limited to Timor-Leste tax on that foreign income and computed separately for each country; foreign business losses cannot offset domestic income. Final withholding stays outside this reconciliation. Excess instalments are an overpayment. [Articles 26(4), 50–51 and 64](https://www.mj.gov.tl/jornal/public/docs/2019/serie_1/SERIE_I_NO_33_B.pdf).

## Worked cases

Both hypothetical cases assume ordinary non-petroleum income, established residence, valid records, no special exemption and no disputed withholding.

**Resident sole trader with separate wages.** Annual taxable business receipts are USD 24,000 and allowable expenses USD 9,000. Separately, USD 12,000 of wages were correctly subjected to WIT and stay outside the annual business base. Prior-year turnover permits quarterly instalments; USD 120 was paid. Taxable non-wage profit is USD 15,000, annual tax USD 900 and the balance USD 780. Expenses exclude private/finally taxed activities. These inputs are hypothetical, not statutory thresholds.

```oa-check
name: Resident business with final wages excluded
inputs:
  business_receipts: 24000
  allowable_expenses: 9000
  final_wages: 12000
  instalments_paid: 120
steps:
  taxable_nonwage: business_receipts - allowable_expenses
  annual_tax: max(taxable_nonwage - 6000, 0) * 0.10
  wages_added_to_base: final_wages * 0
  balance: annual_tax - instalments_paid
expected:
  taxable_nonwage: 15000
  annual_tax: 900
  wages_added_to_base: 0
  balance: 780
```

**Same profit, different established residence.** Two individuals have USD 8,000 ordinary taxable non-wage income, wholly from Timor-Leste and not finally withheld. The resident owes USD 200 before credits; the non-resident USD 800. Residence is a legal fact, not an election to choose the cheaper result.

```oa-check
name: Resident and nonresident ordinary income comparison
inputs:
  taxable_income: 8000
steps:
  resident_tax: max(taxable_income - 6000, 0) * 0.10
  nonresident_tax: taxable_income * 0.10
  difference: nonresident_tax - resident_tax
expected:
  resident_tax: 200
  nonresident_tax: 800
  difference: 600
```

## When to refuse or refer

Do not give a filing-ready result without residence evidence, income classification or records supporting deductions/credits. Refer petroleum/Bayu-Undan/Greater Sunrise cases, contested foreign permanent establishments, pension approval, loss restrictions, special agreements and concurrent-employer allowance allocation. Do not present a missing current portal field or unverified penalty computation as confirmed.

## Filing and payment

For the ordinary calendar year, the annual return and balance are due by the last day of the third month after year-end: 31 March 2027 for 2026. Required filers include persons with tax liability and business losses, plus designated classes. Business returns require income statement, balance sheet and cash-flow statement. A written filing extension does not extend payment. Instalments are due by the 15th day after the month/quarter. Employer WIT return/payment is due by the 15th of the next month, with an annual employer withholding return by the end of March. [Tax Act, articles 23 and 62–64](https://www.mj.gov.tl/jornal/public/docs/2019/serie_1/SERIE_I_NO_33_B.pdf).

The public [ATTL eTax guide](https://attl.gov.tl/uncategorized/etax-guideline/) gives this workflow using an earlier sample year. No taxpayer account was accessed.

1. Obtain the TIN/eTax account, prepare accounts and reconcile instalments in the **Account Module**. Log in through ATTL's **e-Tax Login** at [e-tax.mof.gov.tl](https://e-tax.mof.gov.tl/).
2. Select **Declarations → Declaration → Domestic Income Tax → File** for the actual year. Check individual-enterprise/residence indicators at lines 1–3; enter gross income at line 5 and applicable expenses. The guide identifies depreciation at line 15, amortization at line 20 and other deductible expenses at line 110. A field's existence does not establish deductibility.
3. Reconcile calculated expenses at line 135, net income at line 140, taxable income/loss at line 150 and tax at line 165. Check foreign credit at line 170 and instalments at line 175; use withholding-credit fields only where legally creditable.
4. **Calculate**, correct discrepancies, **Save**, attach the profit/loss, balance sheet, cash flow and required schedules, then **Submit Declaration**. Retain the submitted record. If the actual 2026 screen or calculation differs, obtain current form instructions rather than inventing mappings.
5. Pay using [ATTL payment instructions](https://attl.gov.tl/how-to-pay-taxes/). A bank transfer alone does not submit the return. The manual route uses 3 copies of the relevant form at BNU and a stamped retained copy. For eTax bank evidence use **Mailbox → Compose Mail → Category: Payment**, identify the tax account, TIN, period and tax type, attach bank evidence and **Send**. Reconcile the credit; do not use petroleum payment details for domestic tax.

Use **tl-payroll-social** for wage and social-contribution filing. Correctly taxed wages do not become ordinary non-wage income because an annual employer form exists.

## Completion checklist

Record residence/period, classify every receipt, separate WIT/final withholding, reconcile profit and capital allowances, apply the applicable annual band, verify credits, reconcile instalments/balance, and retain the declaration, accounts and payment evidence. Record unresolved items before filing.
