# Samoa Value Added Goods and Services Tax v0.1

> **General reference only.** This skill supports AI-assisted working papers. It is not approval to file, pay or take a tax position for a particular person. Obtain qualified local review of the facts, applicable law and filing instructions.

## Who this applies to

Samoa’s VAGST is the credit-invoice consumption tax under the Value Added Goods and Services Tax Act 2015. A taxable activity is continuous/regular supplies for consideration, including businesses and public-authority activity, subject to the Act’s exclusions. Registration, place/time of supply, exemptions and documentary evidence come before charging tax. [section 3](https://www.ag.gov.ws/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/Value-Added-Goods-and-Services-Tax-Act-2015.pdf); [section 8](https://www.ag.gov.ws/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/Value-Added-Goods-and-Services-Tax-Act-2015.pdf); [section 9](https://www.ag.gov.ws/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/Value-Added-Goods-and-Services-Tax-Act-2015.pdf).

## Rates, bands and thresholds

| Item | Rule or amount | Official source |
|---|---|---|
| Ordinary taxable supply/import | 15% of statutory value. Exempt imports and supplies are outside this ordinary charge; zero-rated supplies are taxable at 0% (in words on the page). | [section 8](https://www.ag.gov.ws/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/Value-Added-Goods-and-Services-Tax-Act-2015.pdf) |

| Compulsory registration | WST 130,000 threshold: required when taxable supplies exceed the threshold in a past 12-month or shorter period, or reasonably are expected to exceed it in a coming 12-month period. Public-authority activity has a separate registration trigger. Apply within 15 working days of becoming required. | [section 9](https://www.ag.gov.ws/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/Value-Added-Goods-and-Services-Tax-Act-2015.pdf) |

| Threshold adjustments | Ignore capital-asset sales and supplies solely from sale/cessation of the activity; Commissioner may consider associates’ supplies. Voluntary registration below threshold requires satisfying the Commissioner of an existing/intended taxable activity, not automatic entitlement to charge VAGST. | [section 9](https://www.ag.gov.ws/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/Value-Added-Goods-and-Services-Tax-Act-2015.pdf) |

| Exports | 0% (in words on the page), only within Schedule 3 conditions. Export goods must not be re-imported and must leave within 28 days or an allowed longer period; the secure-export-scheme evidence condition also applies. Export services need use/consumption outside Samoa and documentary proof acceptable to the Commissioner. | [section 8](https://www.ag.gov.ws/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/Value-Added-Goods-and-Services-Tax-Act-2015.pdf); [Schedule 3 Part 1 clauses 1-3](https://www.ag.gov.ws/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/Value-Added-Goods-and-Services-Tax-Act-2015.pdf) |

| Exempt supplies | Financial services as defined in Schedule 2 and the other listed exempt supplies do not generate input credits to the extent acquisitions serve exempt output. Zero rating takes precedence if a supply satisfies both categories. | [Schedule 2, exempt supplies and financial services](https://www.ag.gov.ws/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/Value-Added-Goods-and-Services-Tax-Act-2015.pdf); [section 26](https://www.ag.gov.ws/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/Value-Added-Goods-and-Services-Tax-Act-2015.pdf) |

## The base

For ordinary supplies use statutory price, with the associate undervalue/fair-market-value exception where full input credit is unavailable. If the total price already includes tax, extract VAGST at 15/115 (derived tax fraction), not 15% of the inclusive price. Imports use customs value plus relevant transport/insurance and non-VAGST duties, levies and charges not already included; special repaired/re-imported goods have their own valuation condition. [section 18](https://www.ag.gov.ws/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/Value-Added-Goods-and-Services-Tax-Act-2015.pdf); [section 25](https://www.ag.gov.ws/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/Value-Added-Goods-and-Services-Tax-Act-2015.pdf); [section 8](https://www.ag.gov.ws/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/Value-Added-Goods-and-Services-Tax-Act-2015.pdf).

Input credit is limited to a creditable acquisition for making taxable supplies, in the period input tax is paid and the required documents are held. Keep the tax invoice for domestic purchases, import documents for imports and the appropriate debit/credit note for adjustments. No full credit for private/exempt use. Net tax is output tax plus any legally operative imported-services liability less allowed input credit; an excess credit is subject to the Act’s credit/refund rules, not an assumed immediate cash refund. [section 26](https://www.ag.gov.ws/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/Value-Added-Goods-and-Services-Tax-Act-2015.pdf); [section 30](https://www.ag.gov.ws/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/Value-Added-Goods-and-Services-Tax-Act-2015.pdf).

The imported-services provisions are expressly subject to a Minister-nominated commencement date. The Ministry says certain imported services are taxed and the form has an imported-services field, but a commencement instrument and a complete operational recipient/scope test were not recovered. Preserve the statutory reverse-charge topic without assuming every overseas service invoice is chargeable or creditable; refer it for instrument and transaction checks before adding it to the return. [section 1](https://www.ag.gov.ws/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/Value-Added-Goods-and-Services-Tax-Act-2015.pdf); [section 8](https://www.ag.gov.ws/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/Value-Added-Goods-and-Services-Tax-Act-2015.pdf); [VAGST001, boxes 3-15](https://revenue.gov.ws/wp-content/uploads/2022/09/VAGST-001.pdf).

Exempt categories include defined financial services, donated goods/services supplied by a non-profit body, passenger bus/taxi services (excluding exclusive hire), departure charge, resident telecom interconnection, domestic ship passenger carriage and qualifying overseas-aid-project supplies, but excluding supplies to or by subcontractors or other suppliers to the head contractor. Financial-service definitions exclude debt collection/factoring and securities custody; do not exempt every finance-related fee. For zero-rated categories beyond exports, apply the full Schedule 3 description, including defined education, hospital medical supplies and water, rather than treating an industry name as an exemption. [Schedule 2, exempt supplies and financial services](https://www.ag.gov.ws/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/Value-Added-Goods-and-Services-Tax-Act-2015.pdf); [Schedule 3 Part 1 clauses 1-3](https://www.ag.gov.ws/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/Value-Added-Goods-and-Services-Tax-Act-2015.pdf).

## Deadlines and filing

Ordinary periods are 2 months: Category A ends January/March/May/July/September/November; Category B ends February/April/June/August/October/December. Commissioner may assign a shorter Category C for noncompliance. The old blanket monthly claim is corrected. Section 41 requires filing within 15 working days after period end, with particular-case/non-profit alternatives; payment is due on the filing due date. Import tax is paid at import and collected by Customs. [section 29](https://www.ag.gov.ws/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/Value-Added-Goods-and-Services-Tax-Act-2015.pdf); [section 41](https://www.ag.gov.ws/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/Value-Added-Goods-and-Services-Tax-Act-2015.pdf); [section 42](https://www.ag.gov.ws/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/Value-Added-Goods-and-Services-Tax-Act-2015.pdf); [section 43](https://www.ag.gov.ws/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/Value-Added-Goods-and-Services-Tax-Act-2015.pdf).

The Ministry’s summary also says the 21st following the period and labels filing bi-monthly, while another table loosely says every month. That is not the same test as 15 working days. Record the statutory period and obtain the actual taxpayer’s current return/notice and holiday-adjusted deadline before filing; do not promise a universal 21st or resolve the conflict by guessing. Registration is IR31; the published return is VAGST001. [Tax Due Dates and footnote](https://revenue.gov.ws/inland-revenue-services/); [section 41](https://www.ag.gov.ws/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/Value-Added-Goods-and-Services-Tax-Act-2015.pdf); [VAGST001, boxes 3-15](https://revenue.gov.ws/wp-content/uploads/2022/09/VAGST-001.pdf).

VAGST001 shows total sales inclusive of VAGST at box 3, zero-rated supplies at box 4, taxable balance at box 5 and extracted tax at box 6; output adjustments lead to box 8. Documented taxable purchases and input tax/adjustments lead through boxes 9-12, net tax/credit at box 13, carried credit at box 14 and payable/credit at box 15. The printed extraction divisor is rounded; use the statutory fraction for working papers and reconcile the actual filing instructions. [VAGST001, boxes 3-15](https://revenue.gov.ws/wp-content/uploads/2022/09/VAGST-001.pdf); [section 18](https://www.ag.gov.ws/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/Value-Added-Goods-and-Services-Tax-Act-2015.pdf).

## Cross-border points

Separate imported goods from imported services. Export evidence, actual destination/use and re-import facts control zero rating. An overseas customer or foreign-currency invoice alone is insufficient; non-residents carrying taxable activity in Samoa need their own registration/representative analysis. Treat the imported-services instrument gap above as a stop before computation. [Schedule 3 Part 1 clauses 1-3](https://www.ag.gov.ws/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/Value-Added-Goods-and-Services-Tax-Act-2015.pdf); [section 9](https://www.ag.gov.ws/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/Value-Added-Goods-and-Services-Tax-Act-2015.pdf); [section 1](https://www.ag.gov.ws/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/Value-Added-Goods-and-Services-Tax-Act-2015.pdf).

## Two worked examples that recompute

### Domestic inclusive price and documented inputs

Assume a registered business has only ordinary domestic supplies: WST 23,000 VAGST-inclusive receipts and WST 11,500 inclusive purchases wholly for taxable supplies, paid with valid tax invoices in the period. Output is WST 3,000, input WST 1,500 and net tax WST 1,500, derived. No imported-service, mixed-use or carried-credit adjustment is assumed.

```oa-check
name: Domestic inclusive price and documented inputs
inputs:
  sales_inclusive: 23000
  purchases_inclusive: 11500
steps:
  - output: sales_inclusive * 15 / 115
  - input_tax: purchases_inclusive * 15 / 115
  - net: output - input_tax
expected:
  output: 3000
  input_tax: 1500
  net: 1500
```

### Export zero rating compared with failed export evidence

Assume WST 100,000 exclusive-value goods supplies and WST 6,000 fully documented eligible input tax. If goods satisfy all export/28-day/no-re-import evidence conditions, output is zero and excess input credit WST 6,000. If zero rating fails and the supply is ordinary domestic taxable at the same exclusive value, output is WST 15,000 and net tax WST 9,000. Derived alternatives show why zero rating is different from exemption and why export proof matters.

```oa-check
name: Export zero rating compared with failed export evidence
inputs:
  supply_value: 100000
  eligible_input: 6000
steps:
  - export_output: supply_value * 0
  - export_credit: eligible_input - export_output
  - domestic_output: supply_value * 0.15
  - domestic_payable: domestic_output - eligible_input
expected:
  export_credit: 6000
  domestic_payable: 9000
```

## Ask the client first

- Which legal person, taxable activity, registration effective date and Commissioner period/category?
- What actual/expected supplies, capital disposals, associates, exemptions and export evidence?
- Which invoice/import documents, payment dates, private/exempt use and carried credits?
- What current return/notice, deadline and imported-service commencement/scope evidence?
