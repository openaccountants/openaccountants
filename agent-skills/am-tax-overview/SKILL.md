# Armenia tax overview v0.3

## Start with the taxpayer and activity

Armenia's tax year is the calendar year. The State Revenue Committee administers tax filings. Begin by identifying whether the taxpayer is an individual, individual entrepreneur, resident commercial organisation, non-resident with Armenian-source income or a permanent establishment. Then classify the receipt and activity, check source and residence, and test any special regime before applying a headline rate. The individual entrepreneur's business income does not automatically use the ordinary personal 20% method. [Tax Code, Articles 11, 13, 20, 103 and 140](https://www.arlis.am/hy/acts/215611/latest)

The Armenian dram (AMD) is the currency used in the domestic tax rules. Residence, source, treaty relief, permanent establishment, tax-agent duties and the correct tax base depend on the actual facts. Use the relevant method below; this overview does not determine those facts. [Tax Code](https://www.arlis.am/hy/acts/215611/latest)

## Main routes

| Question | Starting point | Use this method | Official basis |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Individual salary, passive income, property or annual declaration | Category-specific PIT rules; ordinary employment income is generally taxed at 20% | [Individual income tax](armenia-income-tax.md) | [Tax Code, 143, 150](https://www.arlis.am/hy/acts/215611/latest) |
| Resident company or Armenian permanent establishment | Ordinary profit tax is generally 18%, subject to the taxpayer, base, deductions and special rules | [Corporate income tax](am-corporate-income-tax.md) | [Tax Code, 103, 125](https://www.arlis.am/hy/acts/215611/latest) |
| Goods or services subject to VAT | Ordinary VAT rate is 20%; registration, place, exemptions, recovery and timing still matter | [VAT](armenia-vat.md) | [Tax Code, 59–63](https://www.arlis.am/hy/acts/215611/latest) |
| Resident business considering a special regime | Check business form, prior or expected turnover, activity, exclusions and election deadline | [Tax planning](armenia-tax-optimization.md) | [Tax Code, 253–254, 267](https://www.arlis.am/hy/acts/215611/latest) |
| Employer or payer making individual payments | Keep PIT withholding separate from pension, stamp and health contributions | [Payroll](armenia-payroll.md) and [social contributions](armenia-social-contributions.md) | [Tax Code, 151–158](https://www.arlis.am/hy/acts/215611/latest) |
| Cross-border company, owner or related party | Establish source, permanent establishment, withholding, treaty and credit treatment | [Corporate cross-border](am-cross-border.md) and [transfer pricing](am-transfer-pricing.md) | [Tax Code, 19–27, 107, 132; Chapter 73](https://www.arlis.am/hy/acts/215611/latest) |

Ordinary corporate profit tax returns and payment are generally due by 20 April after the tax year. The annual individual declaration has different dates for the transition year: the 2025-income declaration window is 2 March through 1 November 2026; the general 2026-income window is 2 March through 1 July 2027. The latter date applies to the declaration population and rules in the Code, not as a universal filing obligation for every person. Check the individual's filing status and any amended deadline before using it. [Tax Code, Articles 125, 156 and 158](https://www.arlis.am/hy/acts/215611/latest); [HO-76-N](https://www.arlis.am/hy/acts/206337)
