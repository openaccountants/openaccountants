# San Marino company formation and entity choice v0.4

> **General reference only.** This Guide is general tax/accounting reference material for AI-assisted workflows. It has not been reviewed for any specific person's facts, documents, elections, deadlines, residency, filing status, or local procedures. Do not rely on it to file, pay, amend, or take a tax position without review by a qualified professional in the relevant jurisdiction.

The ordinary company-law provisions below were read in the official collection updated to 27 November 2024, with Law 80/2022, and checked against the relevant later official acts identified through 16 November 2026. Company registration remains with the Economic Activities Office (UAE) under Regulation 6/2025 article 2(4); operating authorisations are a separate procedure. The 2025 UAE service charter confirms the ordinary capital-registration route, and DD 43/2025 supplies the reserved-activity screen. The accounting-book exception effective from tax period 2026 is set out below. Special regimes and activity-specific licensing conditions remain outside this ordinary formation scope (see Sources).

## Scope

This skill covers:

- Choosing between a general partnership, an SRL (società a responsabilità limitata) and an SPA (società per azioni).
- Capital, contributions and their timing.
- The incorporation deed, registration and the start of legal personality.
- Directors, ownership disclosure and the link to tax.

It does not cover: regulated financial, insurance, fiduciary, cooperative, professional-company or technology regimes; activity licences; residence permits; fees.

## Ask the client first

1. What will the company do, and is the activity regulated?
2. Owners and beneficial owners, their nationality and residence, and any similar business in the last twelve months (for the tax incentive).
3. Proposed directors and where the board will meet.
4. Capital route (25,500 SRL, 9,000 SRL or SPA) and cash or guarantee.
5. Premises and hiring plan.

## The method, step by step

### Entity choice

- **General partnership:** partners are jointly and without limit liable ([Law 47/2006, art 4](https://www.consigliograndeegenerale.sm/on-line/documento17113696.html)). Profit is taxed in the partners' hands ([IGR art 10(3)](https://www.consigliograndeegenerale.sm/on-line/documento17086436.html)).
- **SRL and SPA (capital companies):** company obligations belong to the company, subject to special liability rules. Individuals and legal persons can hold shares ([Law 47/2006, arts 2, 4](https://www.consigliograndeegenerale.sm/on-line/documento17113696.html)).
- **Sole member:** a capital company can have one member, at formation or later. Sole-member status must be registered. If the company becomes insolvent, the sole member is liable without limit for debts arising while they held all the shares, where registration of sole-member status was late, or (for a company formed by unilateral act) capital was not paid half within 120 days and in full within three years ([Law 47/2006, art 12, as amended by Law 80/2022](https://www.consigliograndeegenerale.sm/on-line/documento17113696.html)).
- **Professional companies** have their own eligibility and activity limits ([Law 47/2006, art 3](https://www.consigliograndeegenerale.sm/on-line/documento17113696.html)).
- Sole traders and professionals register as economic operators under the activity laws, not company law.

| Minimum capital, SPA | 77,000 | [Law 47/2006, art 13(1)](https://www.consigliograndeegenerale.sm/on-line/documento17113696.html) |
| Alternative SRL capital | 9,000, all paid within 60 clear days after registration, in cash at a San Marino bank and blocked as security for company debts until liquidation, or by bank guarantee | [Law 47/2006, art 13(1-bis), added by Law 80/2022, art 4](https://www.consigliograndeegenerale.sm/on-line/home/archivio-leggi-decreti-e-regolamenti/documento17132480.html) |
| Ordinary payment timing | At least half within 120 clear days after registration; all within three years | [Law 47/2006, art 10(3)-(4), as amended by Law 80/2022, art 2](https://www.consigliograndeegenerale.sm/on-line/home/archivio-leggi-decreti-e-regolamenti/documento17132480.html) |
| Form of cash contributions | Deposit at a San Marino bank, or a bank guarantee to the company valid for at least three years | [Law 47/2006, art 10(8)](https://www.consigliograndeegenerale.sm/on-line/documento17113696.html) |
| Proof of payment | Directors file payment/date evidence within 30 days: a declaration in the statutory forms under Law 159/2011 arts 13 and 24, or a notarised sworn declaration | [Law 47/2006, art 10(5)](https://www.consigliograndeegenerale.sm/on-line/documento17113696.html) |
| Contributions in total | Not less than share capital; in cash unless the statutes allow otherwise; in kind needs valuation evidence | [Law 47/2006, art 10(1)-(2), (7)](https://www.consigliograndeegenerale.sm/on-line/documento17113696.html) |

Failure to pay contributions on time is a cause of dissolution and liquidation; the court can give directors up to 60 days to regularise ([Law 47/2006, art 10(6)](https://www.consigliograndeegenerale.sm/on-line/documento17113696.html)). If a guarantee is not renewed or replaced by cash 30 days before it expires, the guarantee is enforced ([Law 47/2006, art 10(8)](https://www.consigliograndeegenerale.sm/on-line/documento17113696.html)). Services and personal-use rights are not contributable assets.

### Incorporation steps

1. **Owners and managers.** Check each owner, beneficial owner and director against the eligibility and certification rules; collect certificates and translations ([Law 47/2006, definitions and arts 16-17](https://www.consigliograndeegenerale.sm/on-line/documento17113696.html)).
2. **Seat, object and permissions.** The registered office must be in San Marino ([Law 47/2006, art 8](https://www.consigliograndeegenerale.sm/on-line/documento17113696.html)).
   Fix a lawful, possible and determined object ([Law 47/2006, art 9](https://www.consigliograndeegenerale.sm/on-line/documento17113696.html)). Some activities or types need special-law authorisation or a Congress of State clearance. Screen the proposed object against DD 43/2025 art 1 and special laws before signing; do not rely on the older collection's activity list ([Law 47/2006, art 16](https://www.consigliograndeegenerale.sm/on-line/documento17113696.html); [DD 43/2025 arts 1, 4-6](https://www.consigliograndeegenerale.sm/on-line/home/streaming-video-consiglio/documento17151276.html)).
3. **Deed.** A public deed under article 18 with the article 19 particulars: type and name, term, registered office, object, capital, owners and their contributions, governance and representation, first appointments and statutes ([Law 47/2006, arts 18-19](https://www.consigliograndeegenerale.sm/on-line/documento17113696.html)).
4. **Registration.** The notary prepares the OPEC case labelled “Prima iscrizione società” in the UAE service charter (the detailed service row also calls it “Iscrizione società”). Attach the public deed and statutes, required permissions, eligibility certificates/declarations, identity documents and proof of company-tax payment. File the deed within 15 days of the act or its registration; article 20 gives an ordinary three-working-day registration target. Retain the electronic registered communication confirming registration or refusal ([Law 47/2006, arts 6, 20](https://www.consigliograndeegenerale.sm/on-line/documento17113696.html); [UAE service charter, pp 17-18](https://www.gov.sm/pub1/GovSM/dam/jcr%3A4768689a-fa9a-4728-bfd6-e9490e41bb6e/Carta%20Servizi%20-%20luglio%202025.pdf)). The company acquires legal personality on registration; acts done before registration make the persons who did them jointly and without limit liable ([Law 47/2006, art 21](https://www.consigliograndeegenerale.sm/on-line/documento17113696.html)).
5. **After registration.** Obtain the authorisation/licence for the actual activity and complete tax and, when hiring, ISS registration before starting the relevant activity. The legal representative, or an expressly delegated professional, communicates the beneficial owner through RegTE within 30 days of company registration and within 30 days of a relevant change; annual confirmation applies in calendar years after the first or variation communication, under the rule effective from 1 January 2026 ([AIF FAQ, sections 1 and 3, Law 92/2008 art 23-quater](https://www.aif.sm/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/FAQ-RegTE-v.1.0_2.pdf)). This is a route and deadline summary, not a live portal walkthrough.

### Directors, disclosure and ongoing duties

- Directors and their representation powers follow articles 48-52 and the statutes ([Law 47/2006, arts 48-52](https://www.consigliograndeegenerale.sm/on-line/documento17113696.html)). Board meetings can be held by audio/video link under article 50(2) only where the statutes do not expressly prohibit it; where the chair is in San Marino, chair and secretary must be there and minutes and recording rules apply; where the chair is abroad, a San Marino public notary must act as secretary and fulfil the minutes/recording duties ([Law 47/2006, art 50](https://www.consigliograndeegenerale.sm/on-line/documento17113696.html)). The secretary must record the entire meeting in audio/video. When the chair is in San Marino, the chair must retain that recording for at least two years; when the chair is abroad, the San Marino notary acting as secretary bears that retention duty. The company-law text read sets no general rule that a director must be resident in San Marino.
- Owners, directors, powers and sole-member status are registered ([Law 47/2006, art 6](https://www.consigliograndeegenerale.sm/on-line/documento17113696.html)); article 17 specifically addresses fiduciary holdings and their beneficial owners. General beneficial-owner communication follows Law 92/2008 art 23-quater, as explained in the [2026 AIF FAQ](https://www.aif.sm/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/FAQ-RegTE-v.1.0_2.pdf).
- Books, accounts and financial statements follow articles 72 onward of [Law 47/2006](https://www.consigliograndeegenerale.sm/on-line/documento17113696.html), subject to later amendments. From tax period 2026, the journal, inventory book, depreciable-asset register and auxiliary accounting records required by IGR article 96(1) do not require stamping: [Law 141/2025 articles 27(1) and 54(1)](https://www.consigliograndeegenerale.sm/on-line/home/archivio-leggi-decreti-e-regolamenti/documento17155359.html) expressly derogate from article 72(6). This exception does not exempt all corporate share and minute books listed in article 72(4). Full accounting, financial-statement filing and audit requirements need their own check.
- **Tax residence** is a separate test: a company is resident if its registered office or effective management is in San Marino for most of the tax period ([IGR art 42(2)](https://www.consigliograndeegenerale.sm/on-line/documento17086436.html)).
- **New-company incentive:** registration alone does not give the 50% rate cut. The company must opt in and meet the shareholder and hiring conditions of IGR article 73 (9% instead of 18% for 2026). See `sm-corporate-income-tax`.

## Worked cases

### Example 1: two-owner ordinary SRL

Illustrative inputs, not statutory ownership percentages: capital 25,500, owners 60% and 40%. Subscriptions 15,300 and 10,200. Paying half within 120 days: 7,650 and 5,100, total 12,750; the remaining 12,750 within three years ([Law 47/2006 arts 10, 13](https://www.consigliograndeegenerale.sm/on-line/documento17113696.html)).

```oa-check
name: San Marino ordinary SRL capital split and first half
inputs:
  srl_capital: 25500
  first_owner_fraction: 0.60
  second_owner_fraction: 0.40
  initial_fraction: 0.50
steps:
  first_owner_subscription: srl_capital * first_owner_fraction
  second_owner_subscription: srl_capital * second_owner_fraction
  first_owner_initial: first_owner_subscription * initial_fraction
  second_owner_initial: second_owner_subscription * initial_fraction
  initial_total: first_owner_initial + second_owner_initial
  remaining_total: srl_capital - initial_total
expected:
  first_owner_subscription: 15300
  second_owner_subscription: 10200
  first_owner_initial: 7650
  second_owner_initial: 5100
  initial_total: 12750
  remaining_total: 12750
```

### Example 2: 9,000 SRL is paid in full (edge case)

An SRL using the 9,000 route must pay all 9,000 within 60 clear days. Paying only half (4,500), as on the ordinary route, leaves 4,500 missing. Compared with the ordinary 25,500 route, this route reduces subscribed capital by 16,500 but requires 9,000 in full sooner and restricts the cash deposit; the ordinary route initially requires 12,750. Choose from the funding and security facts, rather than treating half payment as available for both ([Law 47/2006 arts 10, 13](https://www.consigliograndeegenerale.sm/on-line/documento17113696.html)).

```oa-check
name: San Marino 9,000 SRL full payment requirement
inputs:
  alternative_srl_capital: 9000
  required_fraction: 1
steps:
  required_contribution: alternative_srl_capital * required_fraction
  shortfall_if_half_paid: required_contribution - alternative_srl_capital * 0.50
  ordinary_initial: 25500 * 0.50
  subscribed_capital_difference: 25500 - alternative_srl_capital
expected:
  required_contribution: 9000
  shortfall_if_half_paid: 4500
  ordinary_initial: 12750
  subscribed_capital_difference: 16500
```

## When to refuse or refer

Refer regulated, financial, professional, cooperative, fiduciary and technology-status formations to their special law. Do not promise an activity licence, immigration right, tax residence, incentive entitlement or remote-board validity from incorporation alone. Refer missing eligibility evidence, disputed beneficial ownership, in-kind valuation and capital-payment default to the notary or local adviser before proceeding.

## Completion checklist

Reconcile subscribed and paid capital by owner; retain deed, permissions, registration confirmation and contribution proof. Diary payment and RegTE obligations. Confirm the actual activity authorisation separately and hand tax, payroll and accounts matters to the relevant San Marino Guides.
