# Kyrgyzstan VAT (2026)

> **General reference only.** This skill is general tax/accounting reference material for AI-assisted workflows. It has not been reviewed for any specific person's facts, documents, elections, deadlines, residency, filing status, or local procedures. Do not rely on it to file, pay, amend, or take a tax position without review by a qualified professional in the relevant jurisdiction.

Ordinary VAT is 12%. Confirm regime, place of supply and exemption before computing output tax; general-regime VAT registration does not use the seed's turnover threshold.

## Who this applies to

Art253 covers general-regime organisations and individual entrepreneurs, taxable importers and the relevant foreign digital suppliers/recipients. Its express exclusions include specified agricultural producers/cooperatives, machinery-tractor stations and non-profits without taxable supplies. Special-regime treatment must be established separately. Do not infer a VAT obligation from turnover alone.

## The base

Separate taxable supplies, zero-rated supplies, exempt supplies, imports and foreign-service tax-agent obligations under arts251,253-254. Compute output VAT on the applicable statutory taxable value and deduct only art315 eligible input VAT. A supplier's invoice alone is insufficient: check supplier VAT status, electronic invoice, the required non-cash payment rules (and statutory barter exception), business use and payment/evidence conditions. Import credit requires paid customs VAT or the EAEU declaration and payment evidence; foreign-service agent credit requires paid tax and the self-issued invoice. Mixed taxable/exempt use requires allocation. Foreign B2C digital suppliers do not use an ordinary input-credit calculation.

Art263 places real-estate services at the property, movable-property work at the object subject to its exceptions, specified cultural/education/tourism/sport services at actual performance, and listed consultancy/legal/accounting/audit/engineering/advertising/design/marketing/information/software/IP/personnel and movable-leasing services at the qualifying recipient's location. Residual services use the supplier's location. Digital supplies have their own business-presence and consumer indicators. Establish the relevant category before a reverse-charge calculation.

Under art255, a newly registered general-regime taxpayer must register on tax registration; one moving from a special regime must register on entry into the general regime; a previously inactive taxpayer uses the first day of the month following commencement. Registration takes effect on the first day of the month after the application; failure/late application invokes deemed registration from the first day of the month after registration was required. Do not promise voluntary registration below an obsolete threshold: identify the lawful regime and effective date.

Reconcile sales, electronic invoices, taxable/exempt allocation, customs/EAEU documents and foreign services before entering the prescribed electronic VAT report under art100. Keep the filed report, submission evidence, invoice register, credit reconciliation and payment evidence. Current live portal boxes and refund/export-document submission screens are unconfirmed; do not invent field numbers. Art18 handles non-working last days. Failure to withhold leaves the VAT agent liable under art319(3).

## Penalties and interest

Art82 ordinary late-payment interest is 0.09% per calendar day from the day after the due date, capped at 100% of arrears. Review its statutory exceptions, including force majeure, qualifying deferral, bankruptcy and reliance on official explanations. Art163 separately covers non-filing, understatement and invalid-invoice VAT credit; this skill does not replace the offence-specific sanction analysis.

## Cross-border points

Keep domestic zero-rating distinct from an import exemption, and customs imports distinct from EAEU tax-service imports. HTP's foreign-service relief is the current art53(8) rule, not a blanket exemption from all import VAT. Exports require the actual statutory class and proof. Art302 separately zero-rates qualifying EAEU toll-processing work with onward export and prescribed conditions. Treaty and digital-consumer cases need their own source/location analysis.

## Two worked examples that recompute

Illustrative KGS inputs, excluding VAT. For ordinary domestic taxable sales of KGS 1000000, output VAT is KGS 120000. Eligible, documented input VAT of KGS 60000 leaves KGS 60000 payable. Assume all art315 conditions are met, with no exempt/private allocation or prior adjustments.

```oa-check
name: Ordinary taxable domestic sales and eligible input credit
inputs:
  sales_excluding_vat: 1000000
  eligible_input_vat: 60000
steps:
  - output_vat: sales_excluding_vat * 0.12
  - payable: max(0, output_vat - eligible_input_vat)
expected:
  output_vat: 120000
  payable: 60000
```

A qualifying ordinary business receives a KGS 200000 taxable consultancy service from a foreign supplier without a PE. Assume art263 places it in Kyrgyzstan, no art53 exclusion applies, the value is the statutory VAT base, and no contract gross-up issue exists. Agent VAT is KGS 24000. If it is paid and all self-invoice/taxable-use credit conditions are met, the later eligible credit is also KGS 24000. Payment and credit are separate obligations; do not net them before eligibility exists.

```oa-check
name: Taxable foreign consultancy and later eligible credit
inputs:
  foreign_service_base: 200000
steps:
  - agent_vat: foreign_service_base * 0.12
  - later_eligible_credit: agent_vat
  - eventual_net_vat: agent_vat - later_eligible_credit
expected:
  agent_vat: 24000
  later_eligible_credit: 24000
  eventual_net_vat: 0
```

## Ask the client first

- General/special regime, registration/application/commencement dates and tax-administration unit.
- Supply type, customer/supplier location, PE/digital registration and any HTP status.
- VAT-exclusive value, payment terms, currency and gross-up clauses.
- Electronic invoices, payment evidence, import documents and taxable/exempt use.
- Export destination, shipment/proof dates and transport class.
- Prior return, credit balance, payments and adjustments.
