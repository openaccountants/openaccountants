# Colombia: social contributions of the self-employed, 2026 v2.2

> **General reference only.** This skill is general tax/accounting reference material for AI-assisted workflows. It has not been reviewed for any specific person's facts, documents, elections, deadlines, residency, filing status, or local procedures. Do not rely on it to file, pay, amend, or take a tax position without review by a qualified professional in the relevant jurisdiction.

**Year.** Calendar 2026. The figures hang on the monthly minimum wage (SMMLV) of **COP 1,750,905** (Decreto 1469 of 29 December 2025, re-fixed in the same amount on a transitional basis by Decreto 0159 de 2026 after the Consejo de Estado suspended Decreto 1469).

**Which law.** Ley 2277 de 2022 art. 89 (the 40% base of independent workers, held constitutional in Sentencia C-019-24), Ley 100 de 1993 (pensions and health), Decreto 4982 de 2007 (pension rate), Ley 1562 de 2012 and Decreto 1072 de 2015 (ARL for contractors), and Decreto 780 de 2016 (DUR Salud: payment in arrears, the cost-presumption procedure, PILA dates and rounding). The pension reform, Ley 2381 de 2024, is not applied: the Senate text notes that its articles found constitutional take effect on 1 April 2027 (Sentencia C-264-26). Ley 1955 de 2019 art. 244, the earlier 40% rule, was declared unconstitutional with effect from 21 July 2022 and is replaced by Ley 2277 art. 89.

## Who this applies to

- **Independientes con contrato de prestación de servicios personales:** individuals paid under a contract for personal services (not a contrato de trabajo), with net monthly income of 1 SMMLV or more (Ley 2277 art. 89, third paragraph).
- **Independientes por cuenta propia** and **independientes con contratos diferentes a prestación de servicios personales** (who may subcontract, buy inputs or incur expenses), with net monthly income of 1 SMMLV or more (art. 89, first paragraph). Income tax filers whose main activity is rentista de capital fall in this second group (DUR 780 art. 3.2.7.2(b)).
- Health: independent workers with capacity to pay belong to the contributory regime (Ley 100 art. 157(A)(1)). Pension: contractors and independent workers are mandatory members (Ley 100 art. 15(1)).
- **Out of scope:** employees and employers (see [colombia-payroll](colombia-payroll.md)); independents below 1 SMMLV (subsidised regime and BEPS); special and exception regimes (teachers' fund, armed forces, Ecopetrol); voluntary caja de compensación contributions; posted workers and social security agreements.

## The base

**Step 1: classify the independent.** A contract for personal services (the person works personally, typically for one contracting entity) uses the contract value. Cuenta propia work, or contracts that involve subcontracting, buying inputs or expenses, use income less costs (Ley 2277 art. 89; DUR 780 art. 3.2.7.2).

**Step 2: monthly income.**

- *Contractor:* the contract value without IVA, spread over the months of the contract ("valor mensualizado"). No costs are deducted.
- *Cuenta propia and other contracts:* gross monthly income without IVA (accrued if the person must keep books, received if not; a person not obliged who keeps proper books may use either), minus costs that meet ET art. 107 and the support rules, not exceeding the costs in the income tax return for the year. The UGPP activity-based presumption is an alternative; actual costs differing from the presumption require statutory support. Resolución 532/2024 as amended by 566/2025 applies from 9 May 2026, one month after Decreto 379/2026 was published on 9 April 2026. Earlier 2026 periods require the preceding annex, whose coefficients are not supplied here. Under the current procedure the UGPP uses presumed costs for the activity (DUR 780 art. 3.2.7.5, as amended by Decreto 379 de 2026; Ley 2277 art. 89 par. 1). The UGPP may ask for the supports and, if there are none, applies its coefficient for the main activity reported in the income tax return (art. 3.2.7.5 par.).

**Step 3: the test.** If net monthly income is under 1 SMMLV, the art. 89 base does not apply. A person with income of 1 SMMLV or more is presumed obliged to join the contributory health regime (DUR 780 art. 3.2.7.4).

**Step 4: IBC.** At least 40% of step 2 income, with a full-month floor of 1 SMMLV and cap of 25 SMMLV, rounded up to the next peso. For cotizante 59, entry/exit contract months are prorated by the corresponding days, with the minimum prorated likewise. Cotizante 3 entry proration follows DUR 780 art. 2.1.3.1 paragraph 3. These are specific recorded novelties, not permission to reduce any quiet month (Res. 467/2025, retained by Circular 28/2025; current Res. 1529/2026). The 40% is a minimum: the independent may contribute on more.

**Step 5: contributions.** Health 12.5%, pension 16%, FSP 1% from 4 SMMLV plus the subsistence add-on from 16 SMMLV, and ARL at the class rate where ARL applies. Round each contribution up to the next COP 100.

**Several incomes at once.** Contributions are attributable to each income (Ley 2277 art. 89, fourth paragraph), which does not require a separate planilla or minimum floor per contract. Retained Res. 467/2025 rules require cotizantes 3/57 to report total monthly net income in one planilla; cotizante 59 with class I-III service contracts sums monthly fees, excludes IVA, applies at least 40% and pays in one planilla (repeated in current Res. 1529/2026). Do not extend this defined route to every mixed class IV/V arrangement. Bases are added together for all purposes of Ley 100, never above the 25 SMMLV cap, with health on the same base (Ley 100 art. 18 par. 1). Where a person is both an employee and an independent, the employer pays on the salary (see [colombia-payroll](colombia-payroll.md)) and the person pays separately on the independent income. A contractor with several contracts is covered by one ARL for all of them (Decreto 1072 art. 2.2.4.2.2.9); once the 25 SMMLV cap is reached, ARL is paid starting with the highest-risk contract (art. 2.2.4.2.2.12).

**Pensioners.** The obligation to contribute to pension ends when the person meets the requirements for the minimum old-age pension or is pensioned for invalidity or early retirement (Ley 100 art. 17). A survivor-pension recipient is not automatically exempt from pension contributions on independent work. Health is still due on the independent income (Ley 100 art. 157(A)(1)), and ARL where it applies.

**Income that changes month to month.** Payment is month in arrears. Ley 2277 art. 89 still requires accrued income for those obliged to keep books, received income for those not obliged, and permits the stated choice for proper voluntary books. Payment timing does not erase that distinction.

**AUDIT FLASH POINT.** Income tax returns and DIAN exogenous information can establish that an independent earns 1 SMMLV or more and must contribute (DUR 780 art. 3.2.7.4), and the UGPP may apply the cost presumption to audits of any tax year (Ley 2277 art. 89 par. 2). An independent who contributes on 1 SMMLV while the return shows much higher net income is the classic case. Penalties are in [colombia-payroll](colombia-payroll.md) (Ley 1607 art. 179).

**PILA routes and combined-source FSP.** Planilla I is the independent route: cotizante 3 for ordinary independent reporting and cotizante 59 for qualifying service contracts over one month. Planilla Y permits aportante 15 contracting parties to pay agreed health/pension contributions, and is the mandatory contracting-party ARL route for class IV/V; health/pension payment by that party remains optional. If it does not pay those, the contractor uses Planilla I cotizante 3 for the residual contributions (Res. 467/2025; Res. 1529/2026). When combined-source IBC reaches 4 SMMLV and FSP is not fully paid, Planilla I sends the IBC excess not already contributed to administrator code FSP001; the affiliate must report the combined IBC (Res. 0010/2026 art. 16, from February payments; current Res. 1529/2026). The latter's relevant cotizante 59/Planilla I updates apply to payments from 1 September 2026 for August pension periods.

The steps, in the order the independent meets them: affiliate to an EPS and a pension fund (and, for a contract over one month, give the contracting party your ARL, or let it affiliate you); each month, work out the previous month's income on the applicable accrual/receipt or monthly-contract basis, the IBC and each contribution; log in to a PILA operator, choose the applicable Planilla I route above, aggregate the defined categories, and enter the IBC and ARL class; pay by the business day set by your ID digits; retain the receipt; the contracting party's separate tax-deductibility/PILA verification duties are owned by [colombia-payroll](colombia-payroll.md). No blanket fee-payment precondition is established here. At year end, the contributions paid feed the [income tax return](colombia-income-tax.md).

## Penalties and interest

- **Late payment.** Interest at the ET late-payment rate under Ley 1066/2006 art. 3, which expressly covers parafiscal contribution taxpayers and responsible persons; Ley 100 art. 23 alone is framed for employers; see [colombia-payroll](colombia-payroll.md).
- **Health cover suspended.** Two consecutive unpaid periods can suspend affiliation/services where the EPS has not accepted the arrears, subject to payment agreements and exceptions (DUR 780 art. 2.1.9.3). Ongoing treatment can continue for up to four consecutive arrears periods under that article; art. 2.1.9.5 protects pregnant women and minors. Article 2.1.9.4 prevents new contribution/interest debt during suspension while preserving earlier unpaid contributions and interest. This is not unconditional cessation of all care.
- **UGPP.** Failure to file, under-declaration and failure to supply information carry the Ley 1607 art. 179 penalties; the scale is in [colombia-payroll](colombia-payroll.md).

## Cross-border points

- The art. 89 base reads the independent's income without distinguishing where the client is; a Colombian independent billing foreign clients computes the IBC the same way. Whether a person living abroad or a foreigner working in Colombia under a home-country system must contribute depends on residence rules and social security agreements, which are not covered here.
- Tax on fees paid to or by non-residents is in [colombia-cross-border](colombia-cross-border.md).

## Two worked examples that recompute

### Example 1: contractor with a six-month contract, ARL class I

Andrés signs a six-month contract for personal services with a Medellín company for COP 54,000,000 plus IVA, so COP 9,000,000 a month. Office work, ARL class I. He is not a pensioner and has no other income.

- IBC: 40% x 9,000,000 = **COP 3,600,000** (above 1 SMMLV of 1,750,905; below 4 SMMLV of 7,003,620, so no FSP).
- Health 12.5% = 450,000. Pension 16% = 576,000. ARL 0.522% = 18,792, rounded up to 18,800.
- **Total COP 1,044,800** a month, paid through PILA in the following month by the business day set by his ID digits. The contract lasts more than one month, so ARL is mandatory and, at class I, he pays it.

```oa-check
name: CO independent 2026, contractor COP 9,000,000 a month, ARL class I
inputs:
  smmlv: 1750905
  monthly_contract: 9000000
  arl_rate: 0.00522
steps:
  - ibc: clamp(ceil(monthly_contract * 0.40), smmlv, smmlv * 25)
  - salud: ceil(ibc * 0.125 / 100) * 100
  - pension: ceil(ibc * 0.16 / 100) * 100
  - arl: ceil(ibc * arl_rate / 100) * 100
  - total: salud + pension + arl
expected:
  ibc: 3600000
  salud: 450000
  pension: 576000
  arl: 18800
  total: 1044800
```

### Example 2: cuenta propia, actual or presumed costs (a decision)

Marta runs a design studio, CIIU 7410 in professional/scientific/technical section M. In June 2026, after the 9 May start of the current scheme, she has COP 30,000,000 income without IVA on the applicable art. 89 accounting basis, and COP 10,000,000 supported deductible costs included in her tax return. Assume a full contribution month, no mandatory ARL in these facts and no voluntary ARL election.

- **Actual costs:** net COP 20,000,000; IBC COP 8,000,000. Health COP 1,000,000; pension COP 1,280,000; FSP COP 80,000. Total **COP 2,360,000**.
- **UGPP presumption:** Res. 532/2024 art. 2(a), section M coefficient **62.04%**. Costs COP 18,612,000; net COP 11,388,000; IBC 40% = **COP 4,555,200**. Health COP 569,400; pension COP 728,832 rounded up to COP 728,900. Below 4 SMMLV, so no FSP. Total **COP 1,298,300**.
- **Decision:** the permitted presumption costs **COP 1,061,700 less** for these facts. Keeping actual-cost evidence does not inherently reduce contributions below the activity presumption. Classification, eligibility, date and support conditions matter; a gross-income comparison does not answer this choice.

```oa-check
name: CO June 2026 design studio, supported versus presumed costs
inputs:
  smmlv: 1750905
  gross: 30000000
  actual_costs: 10000000
  presumed_coefficient: 0.6204
steps:
  - ibc_actual: clamp(ceil((gross - actual_costs) * 0.40), smmlv, smmlv * 25)
  - total_actual: ceil(ibc_actual * 0.125 / 100) * 100 + ceil(ibc_actual * 0.16 / 100) * 100 + ceil(ibc_actual * 0.01 / 100) * 100
  - presumed_costs: gross * presumed_coefficient
  - net_presumed: gross - presumed_costs
  - ibc_presumed: clamp(ceil(net_presumed * 0.40), smmlv, smmlv * 25)
  - salud_presumed: ceil(ibc_presumed * 0.125 / 100) * 100
  - pension_presumed: ceil(ibc_presumed * 0.16 / 100) * 100
  - total_presumed: salud_presumed + pension_presumed
  - saving: total_actual - total_presumed
  - fsp_line: smmlv * 4
expected:
  ibc_actual: 8000000
  total_actual: 2360000
  presumed_costs: 18612000
  net_presumed: 11388000
  ibc_presumed: 4555200
  salud_presumed: 569400
  pension_presumed: 728900
  total_presumed: 1298300
  saving: 1061700
  fsp_line: 7003620
```

## Ask the client first

1. Is the income from a contract for personal services, own-account work, or a contract involving subcontracting or inputs? Contract value and duration, or monthly gross income and costs.
2. Does the person keep accounts, and are the costs supported by documents that meet ET art. 107 and match the income tax return?
3. Any other income at the same time: salary, other contracts, a pension? Is the person already a pensioner or entitled to the minimum pension?
4. For a contract over one month, the ARL risk class of the activity and of the contracting entity's workplace.
5. Health and pension fund (Colpensiones or a private AFP) and the last two digits of the identity document (PILA date).
