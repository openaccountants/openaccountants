# Turkmenistan payroll and social contributions, 2026 (version 1.1)

> **General reference only.** Source-cited draft, not reviewed by an accountant. Do not use it to file without professional review.

## Scope

Calculate ordinary employer pension contributions and identify employee payroll deductions in Turkmenistan. Mandatory employer contributions, additional occupational contributions and voluntary employee savings are distinct. This Guide covers ordinary covered employees; it also identifies the different treatment needed for foreign workers, individual entrepreneurs and other self-employed categories. Pension Insurance Law arts. 3, 6-7, 19-23: https://mejlis.gov.tm/single-law/218?lang=ru.

For personal income tax (PIT) rates, residence, income exclusions and deductions, use [personal income tax](tm-income-tax.md). Do not infer social-insurance coverage from PIT residence or nationality alone.

## Ask the client first

- Employer's legal form, registration and local establishment, and whether any presidential reduced-rate instrument applies.
- Worker citizenship and permanent-residence status; employment, civil contract or registered entrepreneurial activity; any treaty coverage facts.
- Each cash and non-cash payment, unused-leave pay, statutory compensation and excluded payment, with supporting records.
- Ordinary or designated harmful, heavy, aviation or other occupational pension category, and time worked in that category.
- Main workplace, joining date, year-to-date PIT and deductions; voluntary pension contract, election and contribution amount.
- Actual salary bank withdrawal/transfer dates, pension registration details and proof of any territorial levy already paid at the main workplace.

## The method, step by step

### 1. Determine who is insured and who pays

The employer categories include resident legal persons and locally registered foreign branches and representative offices, as well as registered entrepreneurs employing workers. Covered insured persons include Turkmen citizens and foreign citizens or stateless persons permanently resident in Turkmenistan, in the statutory employment and other listed categories. The diplomatic and household-employer rules are category-specific. A temporary foreign worker is not automatically treated as a permanently resident insured person. Read any applicable treaty and establish the legal category before calculating. Article 7(5) also ends compulsory participation for the specified entrepreneurial, household, tenant and self-employed categories when the listed pension or benefit is awarded; art. 7(5¹) separately addresses disability-organisation workers receiving a disability pension or benefit. Pension Insurance Law arts. 2, 6-7: https://mejlis.gov.tm/single-law/218?lang=ru.

### 2. Build the appropriate contribution base

For ordinary employees use wages and other cash or in-kind remuneration under employment and covered civil work/service contracts, including unused-leave compensation and specified continuing employment payments. Payments to registered individual entrepreneurs are outside that ordinary contract-payment limb. Value benefits in kind at the payment-date regulated price, or market price where no regulated price exists. Pension Insurance Law art. 19(1)(1), (5): https://mejlis.gov.tm/single-law/218?lang=ru.

Exclude payments only where art. 19(4) applies. These include state benefits, specified statutory compensation and travel/relocation allowances within the applicable norms, certain one-off assistance and prizes, and uniforms. Do not treat every bonus, reimbursement or severance payment as exempt merely from its label. Unused-leave compensation is expressly included in the remuneration base. Pension Insurance Law art. 19(1), (4): https://mejlis.gov.tm/single-law/218?lang=ru.

The ordinary employee remuneration limb does not state a general monetary ceiling. That is not a statement that all insured categories are uncapped: individual entrepreneurs, household workers, self-employed persons and agricultural tenants have separate minimum-wage or income bases, and the agricultural-tenant rule includes a ceiling. Obtain the relevant current base and classification before calculating those categories. Pension Insurance Law art. 19(1)(2)-(3): https://mejlis.gov.tm/single-law/218?lang=ru.

### 3. Apply the right rate and funding source

| Contribution and condition | Amount or rule for 2026 | Official URL |
|---|---|---|
| Ordinary covered employer contribution, statutory employer categories | 20% of the applicable remuneration base, art. 20(1)(1); employer's own funds under art. 23(5) | https://mejlis.gov.tm/single-law/218?lang=ru |
| Compulsory professional pension insurance, only designated qualifying work or service | Additional 3.5% of the art. 19(2) occupational base, arts. 6(2), 7(4), 20(2) | https://mejlis.gov.tm/single-law/218?lang=ru |
| Voluntary employee pension insurance under a contract | At least 2% of wages or income, art. 20(4); not a universal compulsory employee deduction | https://mejlis.gov.tm/single-law/218?lang=ru |
| Reduced mandatory contribution rate | Requires the applicable presidential instrument and financing of the difference, art. 20(3); do not assume one exists for the employer | https://mejlis.gov.tm/single-law/218?lang=ru |

Calculate each contribution as its own statutory base multiplied by its rate. Do not deduct the employer contribution from the employee's wages. Do not infer a mandatory employee share from the minimum voluntary rate. Compulsory professional insurance applies to the listed service and designated occupations, including qualifying harmful or heavy work and the specified civil-aviation personnel; it does not apply to every workplace with some risk. Pension Insurance Law arts. 6(2), 7(4), 19(2), 20, 22-23: https://mejlis.gov.tm/single-law/218?lang=ru.

### 4. Calculate payroll deductions separately

Calculate PIT through the [personal income tax Guide](tm-income-tax.md), including cumulative withholding, the main-workplace deduction restriction and the first incomplete month rule. Eligible voluntary pension contributions can be deductible under Tax Code art. 188(2), subject to the withholding restrictions in art. 195(3); a voluntary saving deduction and a tax deduction are different steps. https://mejlis.gov.tm/single-code/10?lang=ru.

Covered resident employees also pay the separate territorial-improvement levy. It is TMT 5 per month for individuals other than entrepreneurs, withheld by the agent under the PIT payment timetable. Do not withhold again where the worker presents the statutory main-workplace confirmation of payment for that month. This is a tax levy, not an employee pension contribution. Test residence and the art. 207 scope before using it. Tax Code arts. 207(1)(a), 208, 209(2): https://mejlis.gov.tm/single-code/10?lang=ru.

## Worked cases

### Case 1: ordinary employer, first incomplete month

Illustrative inputs: a resident Turkmen citizen, covered ordinary employment, TMT 5,000 cash salary for the first incomplete month, no tax exemption or reduction, no voluntary contract, no earlier withholding or levy payment, no other deductions and no employer reduced rate. Employer pension is TMT 1,000. The linked PIT method gives TMT 500 withholding; the resident employee levy is TMT 5. Pay after these specified deductions is TMT 4,495. Salary plus employer pension is TMT 6,000; this is not an estimate of every possible business expense. Pension Insurance Law arts. 19-20, 23 and Tax Code arts. 195(3), 207-209: https://mejlis.gov.tm/single-law/218?lang=ru and https://mejlis.gov.tm/single-code/10?lang=ru.

```oa-check
name: Ordinary employer pension and specified payroll deductions
inputs:
  salary: 5000
  pit_from_income_tax_method: 500
  resident_levy: 5
steps:
  employer_pension: salary * 0.20
  pay_after_specified_deductions: salary - pit_from_income_tax_method - resident_levy
  salary_plus_employer_pension: salary + employer_pension
expected:
  employer_pension: 1000
  pay_after_specified_deductions: 4495
  salary_plus_employer_pension: 6000
```

### Case 2: designated occupation and voluntary saving choice

Use the same illustrative TMT 5,000 salary and first incomplete month, but with confirmed compulsory professional-pension eligibility for the whole payment base. Ordinary employer pension is TMT 1,000, and the additional occupational contribution is TMT 175. If the employee elects the contractual minimum voluntary contribution, TMT 100 is also deducted from pay. At this first-incomplete-month withholding stage, the voluntary contribution does not create an art. 188 deduction; PIT remains TMT 500 and the levy TMT 5. Pay after specified deductions is TMT 4,395 with voluntary saving or TMT 4,495 without it. Employer salary-plus-pension cost is TMT 6,175 in either choice. Subsequent eligible annual PIT recalculation is separate. Pension Insurance Law arts. 19-20, 23 and Tax Code arts. 188(2), 195(3), 207-209: https://mejlis.gov.tm/single-law/218?lang=ru and https://mejlis.gov.tm/single-code/10?lang=ru.

```oa-check
name: Occupational pension and voluntary savings choice
inputs:
  salary: 5000
  pit_from_income_tax_method: 500
  resident_levy: 5
steps:
  employer_pension: salary * 0.20
  occupational_pension: salary * 0.035
  voluntary_saving: salary * 0.02
  pay_without_voluntary: salary - pit_from_income_tax_method - resident_levy
  pay_with_voluntary: pay_without_voluntary - voluntary_saving
  salary_plus_employer_pensions: salary + employer_pension + occupational_pension
expected:
  employer_pension: 1000
  occupational_pension: 175
  voluntary_saving: 100
  pay_without_voluntary: 4495
  pay_with_voluntary: 4395
  salary_plus_employer_pensions: 6175
```

## When to refuse or refer

Do not calculate pension contributions without coverage, payer and occupational status. Do not use the ordinary employee base for an entrepreneur's own contributions or assume a wage ceiling or a presidential concession. Hold the PIT allowance in manat until the current tax basic-unit instrument is confirmed through the linked Guide. Obtain the official occupational list, current forms and filing instructions for the actual case; do not invent a payroll portal or form field.

| Agent PIT remittance and annual information | Use the payment-date and annual-report rules in the linked income-tax Guide, Tax Code art. 195 | https://mejlis.gov.tm/single-code/10?lang=ru |
| Employee territorial levy | PIT remittance timetable; annual agent information by 20 January following the year, Tax Code art. 209(2) | https://mejlis.gov.tm/single-code/10?lang=ru |

The pension declaration form is approved by the responsible labour and finance ministries under art. 21. The current form edition, electronic filing route and field mapping are not confirmed here. Do not replace the statutory payment-date rule with the former approximate next-month dates. Pension Insurance Law arts. 21, 23: https://mejlis.gov.tm/single-law/218?lang=ru.

Pension late-payment interest is generally 0.03% per calendar day, including payment day. Article 34 excludes specified cases, including certain official calculation errors, budget financing, contractual individual insurance and tenants' own contributions, and excludes prescribed bank-processing time; it also limits the period for imposing interest. Resolve those facts and any separate sanctions before computing an arrears total. Pension Insurance Law art. 34: https://mejlis.gov.tm/single-law/218?lang=ru.

## Completion checklist

Confirm coverage and classification, reconcile pension and PIT bases separately, document exclusions and voluntary elections, keep employer-funded amounts out of wage deductions, check the territorial levy, and calendar the salary-date payment and next-month declaration separately. Obtain submission receipts, bank evidence and the current approved forms before treating filing as complete.
