# Maldives tax overview

## Scope

Identify the correct Maldives tax computation, registration and filing workflow for 2026. This overview routes each rate, band and filing calendar to the specialist Guide that owns it; it does not create a second copy of the same figures. MIRA, the Maldives Inland Revenue Authority, administers the tax system under the Tax Administration Act. This is tier 2 source-cited research without accountant approval.

The Maldives has income tax. Income Tax Act 25/2019 began taxation generally on 1 January 2020; remuneration taxation began on 1 April 2020 under section 78. The old broad no-income-tax description is historical and cannot answer a current question. The ordinary income-tax accounting year is the calendar year under Regulation section 58, with commencement, deregistration and other special-period rules.

## Ask the client first

1. Who is the taxpayer: individual, company, partnership, bank or another entity, and where is the relevant home, incorporation or management?
2. What year/period, residence/source facts, income activities and registration dates are involved?
3. Does the activity include tourism, property, overseas suppliers/customers, commercial imports or employment?
4. What are the actual tax obligations/TIN, prior returns, bank/invoice/accounts evidence, payroll records and payments?
5. Which currencies are earned and used for accounting, and is the request a calculation, registration, return, payment, refund or enforcement matter?

## The method, step by step

### 1. Choose the taxpayer and income base

Residents generally bring domestic and foreign income into the income-tax base; non-residents and the defined temporary residents use Maldives-source income. Establish the precise residence definition and source rules rather than equating residence with nationality or the location of the receiving bank. Companies can be resident by incorporation, head office, or control/management; an individual has different tests. [Income Tax Act sections 2, 10-11 and 79](https://www.mira.gov.mv/Legislations/View/Incometaxact).

An individual's tax-free slice and top marginal rate are in [individual income tax](mv-income-tax.md#the-method-step-by-step), together with all intermediate bands, deductions and combined-income conditions. A non-bank entity's band/rate, group allocation and the separate commercial-bank rate are in [corporate income tax](mv-corporate-income-tax.md#the-method-step-by-step). Do not apply the company table to a sole proprietor, or assume each member of a group receives a separate full band.

| Employee, self-employed or sole-proprietor annual tax | [Individual income tax](mv-income-tax.md) | Residence/source, income heads, deductions/losses, annual bands, EWT/foreign-credit reconciliation, filing exemption and annual calendar. |
| Company, partnership, bank or non-resident business payment | [Corporate income tax](mv-corporate-income-tax.md) | Entity/group category, accounts-to-tax, losses, withholding classes/finality, interims and entity return. Specialist bank/insurance branches remain qualified. |
| Sales/purchases, general GST or tourism GST | [Maldives GST](maldives-gst.md) | Correct sector/place/time of supply, rates and exemptions, registration, input attribution, period and return/payment. | Staff remuneration or pension | [Payroll and social contributions](mv-payroll-social.md) | MRPS eligibility/base versus EWT remuneration, contributions, pension deduction, multiple payers, monthly filing and remittance. |
| Incorporation and other legal vehicles | [Company formation and entity choice](mv-company-formation.md) | Registrar/name/constitution/ownership/foreign-investment preparation, actual service and tax/staff handoff. |

The annual personal computation and monthly payroll computation are related but different: employer withholding can become an annual credit, and a return exemption needs its actual conditions. Business receipts may include GST liabilities and platform fees; reconcile gross/net amounts before income tax. Paying the tax authority settles a liability and is not automatically a new expense.

### 3. Keep tourism levies separate

GST is the consumption-tax system, with general and tourism sectors; use the GST Guide for current rates, sector conditions and the return calendar. A resort customer does not itself decide the supplier's sector. Green Tax is a separate tourist-stay levy with establishment/guest conditions and its own monthly return/payment obligations; it is not a percentage of the same sales base. The MIRA page describes charges per day of stay, so do not infer a charge solely from an informal number-of-nights label. [MIRA Green Tax overview](https://www.mira.gov.mv/Pages/View/whatisgreentax).

For a Green Tax question, identify the operating licence holder, type/location/size of establishment or foreign-vessel/local-agent branch, guest eligibility and actual stays. Use the current official computation and return instructions or refer to a qualified Maldives practitioner; this pack does not contain a complete Green Tax return computation. Keep the levy and service charge separately identified when reconciling a guest bill to GST and income-tax revenue. An accounting receipt can contain several legal components.

### 4. Choose the accounting and filing currency

MVR is the rufiyaa currency used in the Act's stated bands; USD records are common in tourism. The legal functional, presentation and payment currencies are determined by the applicable tax's rules, not the bank account's label alone. Income-tax currency is governed by Regulation sections 105-108; GST payment currency and conversion are governed by GST Act section 62 and GST Regulation section 95. Load the respective Guide before converting or paying. Keep original-currency amounts and the permitted rate/date/source.

### 5. Establish the actual calendar and completion state

The individual/corporate Guides own annual income-tax final/interim dates and exemptions; payroll owns EWT and MRPS; GST owns its taxable period and return/payment date. Green Tax has its separate monthly requirement in the official source above. Do not transfer one tax's monthly calendar or form to another, or assume every registered person files every tax.

For each obligation, record taxpayer/TIN, registration/effective date, period, return/form, supporting schedules, legal due date and any specific holiday/extension notice, submission reference, amount payable, payment reference, actual receipt and tax-account reconciliation. MIRAconnect is the ordinary online tax route; the owning Guide states the published channels and any limits. A calculation, saved application or payment instruction is not a completed filing.

### Routing cases

**Employee with side business.** Obtain gross payroll/EWT/pension records and side-business accounts. Load the personal Guide for the combined annual result and return test; use payroll for monthly bases and credits, and GST if the business meets its own registration/supply rules. Do not decide that employer withholding settles all personal obligations.

**Tourism business with overseas booking receipts.** Establish the legal entity and contracting parties, gross accommodation receipts, commissions, deposits and levy components. Load the company or individual income-tax owner, the GST owner for tourism/place-of-supply and overseas-platform rules, payroll if staff, and the separate official Green Tax workflow. Do not classify every overseas platform deduction as the same tax.

## When to refuse or refer

Stop tax-base or rate selection where taxpayer identity, year, residence/source, sector or underlying transaction is unresolved. Refer bank/insurance/transport specialist bases, disputed foreign/treaty/PE matters, complex resort/lease/Green Tax/service-charge computations, enforcement and unresolved legal formation requirements. Preserve useful records and provisional reconciliations; state the specific missing fact or instrument rather than guessing.
