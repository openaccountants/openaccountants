# Zimbabwe company formation and entity choice v1.1

## Scope

Use for choosing and incorporating a Zimbabwe private limited company (Pvt Ltd) or private business corporation (PBC), then identifying tax registrations. This is partial formation guidance, not an investment licence, immigration opinion or promise of processing time. The Companies and Other Business Entities Act [Chapter 24:31] (COBE Act) commenced on 13 February 2020 (s.1 and the commencement annotation in the [government-hosted text](https://cgu.pfms.gov.zw/download/companies-and-other-business-entities-act-chapter-2431/?wpdmdl=21343)).

Quick answer: a company may have a corporate investor; a PBC normally admits natural persons acting in their own right. Both become bodies corporate on issue of the incorporation certificate, and membership alone does not make a member liable for entity debts, subject to the Act's exceptions (COBE Act ss.19, 20, 76, 253, [official text](https://cgu.pfms.gov.zw/download/companies-and-other-business-entities-act-chapter-2431/?wpdmdl=21343)). Tax on retained ordinary trade profit alone does not settle the choice.

## Ask the client first

1. Who will own the business: how many members, natural persons or corporate investors, and any legal disability?
2. Who will manage it, and which proposed directors are ordinarily resident in Zimbabwe?
3. What activity, sector and foreign investment or immigration permissions are involved?
4. What are expected taxable supplies, actual sales and receipt currencies over the relevant period?
5. Will it employ staff, and will profits be retained or distributed?
6. Is this a new incorporation, an existing entity needing re-registration, or a foreign branch?

## The method, step by step

### Choose the legal form

| Form or test | Rule and conditions | Authority | Official URL |
|---|---|---|---|
| Private company | One or more persons may form it; liability limited to the unpaid amount on shares. The articles restrict share transfers and public invitations and limit membership to fifty, excluding qualifying employees and former employees; joint holders count as a single member | COBE Act ss.76, 85 | https://cgu.pfms.gov.zw/download/companies-and-other-business-entities-act-chapter-2431/?wpdmdl=21343 |
| Private company directors | More than one and fewer than ten shareholders: two or more directors. Ten or more shareholders: at least three. At least one director ordinarily resident in Zimbabwe. Do not infer the minimum for a single-shareholder company from this wording | COBE Act s.195(1)-(2) | https://cgu.pfms.gov.zw/download/companies-and-other-business-entities-act-chapter-2431/?wpdmdl=21343 |
| PBC | One to twenty members; normally only individual natural persons acting in their own right. A deceased or legally disabled member's estate/representative is an express exception; minors and unrehabilitated insolvents have conditions | COBE Act ss.247, 252, 253 | https://cgu.pfms.gov.zw/download/companies-and-other-business-entities-act-chapter-2431/?wpdmdl=21343 |
| PBC accounting officer | Incorporation statement names an accounting officer and is signed by the members and a qualified accounting officer; annual statements go to a qualified officer for examination, review and report | COBE Act ss.248, 274, 275 | https://cgu.pfms.gov.zw/download/companies-and-other-business-entities-act-chapter-2431/?wpdmdl=21343 |

| Partnership or sole trader | Alternatives to incorporation; do not issue an incorporation certificate or describe them as a Pvt Ltd/PBC. A profit-making association or partnership of more than twenty persons must use a registered statutory form unless the designated-profession or other-law exception applies | COBE Act s.75 | https://cgu.pfms.gov.zw/download/companies-and-other-business-entities-act-chapter-2431/?wpdmdl=21343 |
| Foreign company branch | Where s.241 applies, requires ministerial authorisation, registration and a principal officer ordinarily resident in Zimbabwe or a Zimbabwe citizen. Section 241(16) excludes a foreign company with an investment licence, a bank or insurer licence, or operating in a statutory special economic zone; assess its separate regime | COBE Act s.241(1)-(3),(16) | https://cgu.pfms.gov.zw/download/companies-and-other-business-entities-act-chapter-2431/?wpdmdl=21343 |

A PBC is unsuitable for a proposed corporate member under s.253. The earlier claim that it must have a Zimbabwean member is not a condition stated in ss.247 or 253. Do not turn that correction into a general assurance that foreign investment is unrestricted: sector and investor permissions remain separate.

### Name, documents and registration

1. Apply to the Registrar for an acceptable name. A name can be reserved on application and payment of the prescribed fee for up to one month; it must pass the restrictions on misleading, conflicting and otherwise undesirable names (COBE Act s.25, [official text](https://cgu.pfms.gov.zw/download/companies-and-other-business-entities-act-chapter-2431/?wpdmdl=21343)).
2. For a company, prepare the memorandum and articles as applicable, including its name, limited liability, proposed number of shares, subscribers and signatures. Each subscriber takes at least one share. For a PBC, use the prescribed incorporation statement with identity details, member interests and contributions, physical and postal addresses, accounting officer and financial year end, signed as required (COBE Act ss.18, 77, 78, 248, [official text](https://cgu.pfms.gov.zw/download/companies-and-other-business-entities-act-chapter-2431/?wpdmdl=21343)). Current CR/PBC form identifiers need confirmation; do not assume the earlier PBC2 label remains current.
3. Lodge constitutive documents electronically or manually, as permitted, and pay the prescribed fee. The Registrar registers compliant documents, assigns a registered number and issues the incorporation certificate (COBE Act ss.18-19, [official text](https://cgu.pfms.gov.zw/download/companies-and-other-business-entities-act-chapter-2431/?wpdmdl=21343)). The statutory route is confirmed; the current registry portal screens and fee schedule are not.
4. Arrange ZIMRA/TaRMS registration and obtain the TIN before adding VAT. For VAT, follow Taxpayer Profile, Tax Type, New Tax Type, VAT and attach the supporting sales schedule, invoices or contracts, bank statement, public officer appointment and premises evidence ([ZIMRA VAT Registration, requirements and how to register](https://www.zimra.co.zw/domestic-taxes/vat/vat-registration)). Incorporation does not itself register every tax type.
5. If employing staff, route to [payroll and social contributions](zw-payroll-social.md) for PAYE, NSSA and other employer registration checks before operating payroll. Do not import that Guide's unresolved NSSA ceiling into an incorporation decision.

## Boundary and exception table

| Situation | Action |
|---|---|
| Corporate investor wants a PBC | Refer to the ordinary natural-person membership rule and estate exception in s.253; use a company analysis instead |
| Exactly one shareholder | Confirm the director requirement with the Registrar or local legal adviser; s.195(1)'s private-company numeric rules expressly address more than one shareholder |
| Only exempt supplies or no trade | Do not infer compulsory VAT registration from incorporation alone; apply the VAT scope test |
| Foreign branch, public company or co-operative | Use the distinct statutory process; check the foreign-company exclusions in s.241(16) before applying that section; this checklist does not complete it |
| Existing entity or regulated activity | Confirm current re-registration and licensing requirements separately |

## Worked cases

VAT after incorporation: keep the original inputs, expected taxable supplies of USD 30,000 in twelve months and a USD 25,000 threshold. The difference is USD 5,000. The expected-supplies test is met assuming these are taxable supplies; incorporation alone is not the trigger ([ZIMRA, compulsory registration](https://www.zimra.co.zw/domestic-taxes/vat/vat-registration)).

```oa-check
name: VAT registration test for a new company
inputs:
  expected_supplies: 30000
  threshold: 25000
steps:
  - excess: expected_supplies - threshold
expected:
  excess: 5000.00
```

Decision: company or sole trader on the original USD 100,000 taxable trade-income input. Assume both fall within the ordinary 25% rate, with no credits, special regime, owner salary or distributions. Each has USD 25,000 income tax and USD 750 AIDS levy, totalling USD 25,750; the income-tax-only difference is nil. This does not establish equal total tax after distributions or equal legal exposure ([ZIMRA, ss.14(2)(b)-(c) and levy](https://www.zimra.co.zw/domestic-taxes/corporate/tax-rates)).

```oa-check
name: Company against sole trader, income tax only
inputs:
  profit: 100000
steps:
  - company: profit * 0.25 * 1.03
  - individual: profit * 0.25 * 1.03
  - difference: company - individual
expected:
  company: 25750.00
  difference: 0.00
```

## When to refuse or refer

Do not quote a registration fee, turnaround guarantee, minimum USD capital amount or form code from the earlier indicative figures. Stop before advising a single-shareholder director minimum, foreign investment permission, immigration right or current re-registration deadline without the missing authority. Refer regulated, foreign-branch, public-company and co-operative formation to a Zimbabwe legal or company-registration professional.

## Filing and payment

For an ordinary company, file the annual return with the Registrar within twenty-one days of the incorporation, registration or re-registration anniversary, with the private-company certificate and signatures required by s.165. Section 82 licensed associations have statutory exemptions, so do not extend the rule indiscriminately. A PBC has its own financial statements and accounting-officer regime under ss.273-275; do not treat s.165 as a universal annual-return rule for every entity ([COBE Act](https://cgu.pfms.gov.zw/download/companies-and-other-business-entities-act-chapter-2431/?wpdmdl=21343)). For statutory records, a company or PBC must keep the original or certified constitutive documents and amendments at its registered office or accounting officer's physical address (s.21(2)); this is a narrow confirmed retention rule, not a complete statutory-register checklist. For tax, route ITF12C and provisional QPD returns to [corporate income tax](zw-corporate-income-tax.md); do not confuse them with the registry return.
