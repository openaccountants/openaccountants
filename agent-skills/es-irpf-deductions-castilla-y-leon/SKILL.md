# Spain: Castilla y León regional IRPF credits, 2026

**Sourced from the law.** This revision has not had an independent whole-pack review or accountant sign-off. Calculate regional credits after establishing tax residence and the taxpayer's facts. This Guide holds the Castilla y León catalogue of [es-irpf-deductions](es-irpf-deductions.md), moved here unchanged on 6 November 2026, so that each Guide stays within the size the site can deliver. Specific unresolved interactions are listed in Sources.

## Who this applies to

An ordinary IRPF resident whose community of residence for 2026 under [LIRPF art. 72](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2006-20764#a72) is Castilla y León. A regional credit reduces regional tax, not the income base. Before applying any row below, use [es-irpf-deductions](es-irpf-deductions.md) for the residence test, the deduction ledger, the state/regional order of credits, the national credits, deadlines, penalties and the questions to ask, and [es-income-tax](es-income-tax.md) for the bases, scales and minima. Where a row below refers to "the attributable-tax worksheet under The base", that worksheet is [es-irpf-deductions-attributable-tax](es-irpf-deductions-attributable-tax.md); national credits, the deduction ledger and any calculation "above" outside this catalogue are in [es-irpf-deductions](es-irpf-deductions.md#the-base). Basque and Navarre residents use [es-foral-income-tax](es-foral-income-tax.md); an IRNR taxpayer or an art. 93 incoming worker does not take these credits.

## Rates, bands and thresholds

All amounts are EUR. `BI` means the specified sum of general and savings **taxable income before base reductions** (base imponible); `BL` means base liquidable after the relevant reductions. Use the definition in each row: an income limit based on BI cannot be replaced with gross salary, BL or take-home pay. The table year is 2026 unless a transition is stated.

### Castilla y León: income tests, family and disability credits

**Check each credit's income test before calculating it.** The default test is `general BI + savings BI − the state personal/family minimum ≤ 18,900` on an individual return or `≤ 31,500` jointly. Equality qualifies. This test does **not** apply to family/birth/adoption credits in arts. 3–4, subsidised home rehabilitation in art. 7.2, rural rental rehabilitation in art. 7.3, entrepreneurship in art. 8, research gifts in art. 9.f or electric cars in art. 9.g. Do not replace this measure with salary or BL, or impose it on an exempted category ([DL 1/2013 art. 10.1–2](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOCL-h-2013-90254#a10)).

For arts. 3–5, prorate the statutory entitlement where more than one taxpayer is entitled. Large-family status requires cohabitation and the regional title under Ley 40/2003. Birth/adoption requires the descendant minimum. Count the new child and other natural/adopted children of either parent living with the taxpayer at accrual when fixing child order. For multiple events, births/adoptions within twelve months can qualify even if not simultaneous. The 901 is the additional event entitlement shared by entitled taxpayers, not an automatic 901 per child; count the two following years from the last birth/adoption in the qualifying event ([arts. 3–5 and 10](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOCL-h-2013-90254#a10); [AEAT multiple-event explanation](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/ayuda/manuales-videos-folletos/manuales-practicos/irpf-2025-deducciones-autonomicas/comunidad-autonoma-castilla-leon/partos-adopciones-multiples.html)). The disability credit excludes users of the community's public or publicly contracted residences. Retain competent-authority disability evidence.

Childcare requires a child under four at accrual who generates the descendant minimum, and qualifying work plus Social Security/Mutuality registration by the minimum-entitled parents. A domestic carer must be registered in the household-worker system. Nursery costs cover pre-enrolment, enrolment, ordinary/extended attendance and meals for complete months at a qualifying registered regional centre. If the claimant has entitlement to the national art. 81.2 maternity childcare increment, subtract that entitlement from both the regional nursery amount and its 1,320 ceiling, with no negative credit. Across both parents, regional childcare credits plus that national increment and relevant public childcare aid cannot exceed actual eligible expense; reduce regional claims proportionately if necessary ([art. 5.1](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOCL-h-2013-90254#a5)).

For attribution, AEAT directs equal allocation of joint marital-property expenditure; in separate-property or unmarried cases use the claimant's actual payments. Its same-wording guidance deducts nursery grants from the nursery expense base, but does not automatically deduct a domestic-carer grant from the wage base where credit plus grant remains within actual cost. Apply the statutory sharing rule and aggregate expense ceiling as well; do not give each parent an unshared duplicate claim ([AEAT childcare guidance, updated 29 September 2026](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/ayuda/manuales-videos-folletos/manuales-practicos/irpf-2025-deducciones-autonomicas/comunidad-autonoma-castilla-leon/cuidado-hijos-menores.html)).

Arts. 4–5 exclude analogous birth/adoption/childcare/conciliation aid requested and granted by the Junta. Classify the actual aid before denying a credit: AEAT expressly treats **Bono Concilia under Orden FAM/882/2021** and **working-time-reduction/leave aid under Orden FAM/1454/2023** as compatible with the childcare credit and excludes those amounts from its expense-ceiling calculation. By contrast, analogous Bono Nacimiento can bar birth/multiple-birth credits for the same triggering event. The guidance distinguishes simultaneous births from successive births and earlier-year benefits; a benefit for an earlier event does not automatically bar a later event's credit ([art. 10.3.c](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOCL-h-2013-90254#a10); [childcare](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/ayuda/manuales-videos-folletos/manuales-practicos/irpf-2025-deducciones-autonomicas/comunidad-autonoma-castilla-leon/cuidado-hijos-menores.html), [multiple births](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/ayuda/manuales-videos-folletos/manuales-practicos/irpf-2025-deducciones-autonomicas/comunidad-autonoma-castilla-leon/partos-adopciones-multiples.html)).

For the first-home and rural rental-rehabilitation routes, the municipality/minor local entity at acquisition or rehabilitation must have no more than 10,000 inhabitants generally, or no more than 3,000 if less than 30 km from the provincial capital; the property's acquisition-tax value must be **strictly below 150,000**. The young rural tenant uses the same population/distance limits but not that property-value condition. Use the relevant year's official population/list evidence, not a 2025 manual's list for a 2026 claim; final provision 7 uses the INE register in force at 1 January. First-home status excludes anyone who has ever held full ownership of at least 50% of another home. New construction means the first transfer within three years of the declaration of new construction, or qualifying direct construction costs. Apply the national habitual-home definition ([arts. 7 and 10.2; final provision 7](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOCL-h-2013-90254#dfseptima)).

The young first-home base includes qualifying acquisition expenses, loan principal, interest and financing costs, with mortgage-hedge receipts reducing interest. The net-wealth growth test excludes interest/financing costs from the investment comparison and excludes value movements in assets still held at year end. For art. 7.1/7.3 rehabilitation, apply the incorporated VAT definition: qualifying reconstruction work must represent more than 50% of project cost and total work must exceed 25% of the relevant acquisition/market value excluding land, using the statutory two-year acquisition lookback and connected-work rules ([art. 7.1/6](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOCL-h-2013-90254#a7); [LIVA art. 20.Uno.22.B](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-1992-28740#a20)).

Subsidised rehabilitation covers solar hot-water panels meeting at least 50% of the minimum technical contribution, thermal-system energy/renewable improvements, specified water-saving/sanitation works and disability access/sensory-communication adaptations for the taxpayer, spouse or an occupying relative through the third degree. It requires an approved state/regional housing programme; keep the managing body's bank-transfer proof of the grant. The statutory claim year is the grant-payment year. For rural rental rehabilitation, maintain qualifying letting for five following years to non-spouse/non-ascendant/non-descendant/non-third-degree relatives, lodge the legal deposit and satisfy the 300 monthly ceiling, including contractually recharged housing expenses. During vacancies, advertise through the property, media/Internet, estate agency or agent and keep the property unoccupied with utility evidence; AEAT identifies Orden EYH/668/2019 for these conditions ([arts. 7.2–3 and 10.4.e](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOCL-h-2013-90254#a7); [AEAT rural letting](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/ayuda/manuales-videos-folletos/manuales-practicos/irpf-2025-deducciones-autonomicas/comunidad-autonoma-castilla-leon/rehabilitacion-viviendas-medio-rural-destinadas-alquiler.html)). For a young tenant, the aid rule caps the **resulting credit** at net rent: `min(rate × rent paid, route cap, max(0, rent paid − public rent aid))`; it does not simply change the percentage base to net rent.

Entrepreneurship requires a financed investment project in Castilla y León and the qualifying company's regional registered/tax domicile described by AEAT. In the investment year or the next, increase employees measured as persons/year, economically dependent self-employed contracts and/or family-collaborator registrations against the prior-year comparison, and maintain the corresponding increase for three years. The maximum computable **project investment**, distinct from the taxpayer's 10,000 credit ceiling, is `100,000 × added annual employee units + 50,000 × additional qualifying dependent-contractor contracts + 50,000 × additional family-collaborator registrations`. Obtain the investee's certificate of destination, ownership percentage and employment conditions. A capital-only intermediary must put funds into a qualifying regional company within six months; apply the aggregate capital-percentage test, employment conditions and no reduction of the regional workforce ([art. 8](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOCL-h-2013-90254#a8); [AEAT investment conditions](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/ayuda/manuales-videos-folletos/manuales-practicos/irpf-2025-deducciones-autonomicas/comunidad-autonoma-castilla-leon/fomento-emprendimiento.html)).

Reconcile **all investors' qualifying subscriptions** to the certified project maximum once,not once per investor. If aggregate eligible financing stays within that maximum,each entitled taxpayer receives `min(20%×their own qualifying subscriptions,10,000)`,aggregating that taxpayer's investments before the personal cap;the same personal cap applies on joint filing. One added annual employee unit gives a100,000project maximum:subscriptions30,000/20,000yield6,000/4,000;60,000/40,000yield10,000/8,000. At90,000/30,000,total financing exceeds100,000,and the cited law/instructions do not prescribe a priority or proportionate removal of excess:retain that precise allocation question,rather than invent a split or reject every investor ([art8](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOCL-h-2013-90254#a8);[exactAEATcalculation](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/ayuda/manuales-videos-folletos/manuales-ayuda-presentacion/irpf-2025/10-cumplimentacion-irpf-deducciones-autonomicas/10_8-castilla-leon/10_8_10-deduccion-fomento-emprendimiento.html)).

The art. 9 gift/work routes are: **(a)** owner-paid authorised work on eligible protected regional heritage buildings meeting the incorporated heritage conditions; **(b)** authorised/favourably reported work by owners on regional Natura 2000 natural assets; **(c)** heritage-conservation gifts to eligible public, religious or Law 49/2002 heritage-purpose bodies; **(d)** regional Natura 2000 gifts to public bodies/institutions; **(e)** gifts to registered regional foundations with cultural, assistance or ecological purposes; **(f)** gifts to regional public universities or predominantly research/development/business-innovation institutions for qualifying regional projects. Obtain the required authorisations and donation certificates. The default BI-minus-minimum ceiling applies to a–e, but not f; the combined 10%-of-BL base cap applies to **all a–f** ([arts. 9–10](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOCL-h-2013-90254#a9)).

The vehicle must be a **new passenger car** that is pure electric, range-extended electric or plug-in hybrid with electric range **over 40 km under WLTP**. It cannot be used in anyone's business/professional activity; claim only in its registration year and retain it for four years. Subtract the claimant's actual national DA 58 entitlement from the regional cap before applying the cap, with no negative deduction; do not assume that a national headline maximum is the actual entitlement. Retain the invoice proving acquisition, date and price. Later loss of art. 7.1–3/8/9.g conditions requires regional recapture plus late-payment interest in the breach year ([arts. 9.g and 10.2.j/3.d/4](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOCL-h-2013-90254#a10)).

### Castilla y León: preserved housing and old family balances

DL 1/2013's repealing provision expressly preserves **DT 5 of DL 1/2008**, introduced by Ley 1/2012 art. 11. That separate first-new-home credit is **7.5%**, annual base **9,040**, for a regional habitual home whose new-construction project approval was obtained **1 September 2011–31 December 2012**. It runs only in the first-payment year and the next four years, not indefinitely for an old mortgage. Include qualifying acquisition/financing payments net of hedge receipts; apply the statutory previous-deduction/reinvestment exclusions. It covers acquisition/construction, not enlargement or rehabilitation. A 2026 claim requires evidence that 2026 falls inside the particular five-year window; old project approval alone is insufficient ([DL 1/2013 repealing provision, paragraph 3](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOCL-h-2013-90254#ddunica); [Ley 1/2012 art. 11](https://www.boe.es/buscar/doc.php?id=BOE-A-2012-4385); [AEAT preserved credit](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/ayuda/manuales-videos-folletos/manuales-practicos/irpf-2025-deducciones-autonomicas/comunidad-autonoma-castilla-leon/adquisicion-vivienda-nueva-construccion-residencia-habitual.html)).

**Preserved/current home calculations remain separate.** Ley1/2023's transition preserves earlier art7.1 rights under their entitlement-year requirements,with their annual base increased to10,000. Do not reject an otherwise valid pre2023continuing claim solely because the new-cohort headline starts2023. Calculate current/preserved art7.1 as15%of its eligible annual payments,base at most10,000,and independently survivingDT5as7.5%of its own eligible payments,base at most9,040,within its first-payment-year-plus-four window. The operative provisions give no cross-credit base offset or common home-investment cap. If the same documented home/payments independently satisfy both routes,add their separately capped credits,subject to available regional quota; this is statutory analysis,not a claimed official combined example. At12,000eligible payments,first payment2023and all independent conditions met,the credits are1,500+678=2,178;at6,000they are900+450=1,350. If only art7.1's income test fails,retain the independently eligibleDT5amount;its statutory requirements do not import that chapter's income ceiling ([Ley1/2023transition](https://www.boe.es/buscar/doc.php?id=BOE-A-2023-6456);[currentart7/repealingprovision](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOCL-h-2013-90254#a7);[preservedDT5](https://www.boe.es/buscar/doc.php?id=BOE-A-2012-4385);[AEATDT5calculation](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/ayuda/manuales-videos-folletos/manuales-ayuda-presentacion/irpf-2025/10-cumplimentacion-irpf-deducciones-autonomicas/10_8-castilla-leon/10_8_9-adquisicion-vivienda-nueva-construccion.html)).

Keep nationalDT18entitlement separate. Ordinary same-home first acquisition/construction payments before2013already exhaust DT5's five-year window before2026,so do not addDT5to every national legacy claim. An independently qualifying continuing ruralart7.1claim can still combine its10,000base with the national transition's own9,040base:at12,000independently eligible payments,1,500rural+1,356national total=2,856,before quota limits. A2021first payment endsDT5in2025;a2022first payment can endin2026. Do not grant a new refundable/carry-forward family credit for 2026 by copying the former additional provision: that provision was repealed from **1 January 2023**. The surviving transitional provision preserves specified **2019–2022** art. 3–5 balances for three following periods, then a regional payment-request route. A 2022 balance's last return-offset year was **2025**, not 2026. A payment request submitted in calendar 2026 concerns that old entitlement and is distinct from the 2026 return. The 2025 manual applies current-year credits before the old balance; it does not create a 2026 carry right ([DL 1/2013 transitional provision](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOCL-h-2013-90254#dt); [AEAT ordering](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/ayuda/manuales-videos-folletos/manuales-practicos/irpf-2025-deducciones-autonomicas/comunidad-autonoma-castilla-leon/orden-aplicacion-deducciones-sobre-cuota-autonomica.html)).

## Worked example

### Castilla y León young rural tenant: rate, cap and the rent-net-of-aid limit

Hypothetical 2026 facts: an individual resident aged **30** files individually with BI **22,000** and a state personal/family minimum of **5,550**: `22,000 − 5,550 = 16,450`, within the **18,900** default individual test. She rents a qualifying rural home meeting the population/distance limits in this Guide, pays **3,000** of rent and receives **2,600** of public rent aid. The rural route gives `25% × 3,000 = 750`, capped at **612**, but the credit cannot exceed rent minus all public rent aid: `3,000 − 2,600 = 400`. The credit is **400** ([DL 1/2013 arts. 7.4–5 and 10](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOCL-h-2013-90254#a7)).

```oa-check
name: Castilla y León young rural tenant, limited by rent net of aid
inputs:
  bi: 22000
  state_minimum: 5550
  rent: 3000
  public_rent_aid: 2600
steps:
  income_test: bi - state_minimum
  nominal: rent * 0.25
  credit: min(nominal, 612, rent - public_rent_aid)
expected:
  income_test: 16450.00
  nominal: 750.00
  credit: 400.00
```
