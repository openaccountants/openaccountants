# Argentina: company formation and entity choice 2026 v0.2

> **General reference only.** This skill is general tax/accounting reference material for AI-assisted workflows. It has not been reviewed for any specific person's facts, documents, elections, deadlines, residency, filing status, or local procedures. Do not rely on it to file, pay, amend, or take a tax position without review by a qualified professional in the relevant jurisdiction.

## Who this applies to

Ordinary companies formed under Ley General de Sociedades 19.550 (LGS) and the SAS provisions of Ley 27.349. This Guide is **Not yet approved**. National substantive rules and CABA IGJ procedures are distinguished; the registry of the company's actual domicile governs. IGJ is not the provincial registry for the whole country.

**Quick answer:** identify owners, activities, domicile, governance and funding before choosing SA/SAU, SRL or SAS. Ordinary SA has a fixed statutory peso minimum, SAS a current minimum-wage multiple, and SRL no fixed national peso minimum in its provisions. The cash-payment percentage is not the same for SAU. Obtain incorporation date, owners and foreign-company status, capital/source, intended objects, managers' actual domiciles and registry first.

Out of scope: public offers, regulated finance/insurance, public concessions and other SAS-ineligible activities, complex non-cash valuations, reorganisations, trust ownership, tax-neutral transfers and provincial permits. Limited shareholder liability does not eliminate unpaid-capital guarantees, management liability or liability before registration.

## The base

### Choose and document the entity

- **SA/SAU:** share capital and LGS governance. Under LGS art. 1 the ordinary LGS one-person company is SAU and cannot be constituted by another one-person company. SA requires a public instrument (arts. 165-166). Do not promise that incorporation itself limits every founder/management liability: art. 183 treats pre-registration acts separately.
- **SRL:** quotas and one or more managers, partners or outsiders (art. 157). Public or private constitutive instrument is permitted under arts. 4-5, with ratification/authentication conditions. Partners' limited quota liability remains subject to joint/unlimited contribution and valuation guarantees under art. 150. The old public-deed-only assertion is incorrect.
- **SAS:** special share-company form, one or several owners, public/private instrument with permitted signature certification or authorised digital instrument (arts. 34-35). A one-person SAS cannot constitute or participate in another one-person SAS. Under art. 39, no LGS art. 299(3)-(5) cases, no control by or participation above 30% in those entities. A triggered case has the statutory transformation/cure window of six months, with the prescribed knowledge rule where not caused by SAS and liability consequences after expiry. Do not substitute an ordinary capital threshold for these express exclusions.

The instrument must identify owners, company name/form and domicile/seat, objects, duration, subscribed capital and each contribution/payment plan, administration/representation/supervision, decisions, profit/loss distribution and dissolution/liquidation. SAS also identifies fiscal close and the required owner tax identifiers. Sources: LGS art. 11 and Ley 27.349 art. 36. Use a legally suitable model and actual facts, not a generic tax-rate comparison; corporate income-tax treatment is in [ar-corporate-income-tax](ar-corporate-income-tax.md).

### Governance domicile is entity-specific

LGS arts. 255-256 require the **absolute majority** of SA directors to have actual Argentine domicile and all to establish special Argentine notice addresses. Ordinary boards may have one or more directors; specified art. 299 companies generally need at least three, with the stated SAU exception. An SA with three directors does not satisfy the majority rule merely by having one local director.

SRL managers have directors' rights, obligations, prohibitions and incompatibilities through art. 157; apply relevant domicile rules to the actual management arrangement. SAS arts. 50-51 instead require at least one administrator with actual Argentine domicile, an alternate if there is no supervisory organ, and the prescribed CDI, representative and Argentine notice address for foreign members. Nationality alone is not actual domicile.

## Deadlines and filing

### National steps and timing

1. Select the registry of social domicile under LGS art. 5, with branch registration where needed. Check provincial procedure separately; CABA IGJ handles companies domiciled in CABA.
2. Execute the correct constitutive instrument and appointments, pay/validate contributions and prepare its legal publication. SA/SRL require the art. 10 notice for one day; SAS art. 37 has its own one-day content requirement. Non-cash assets need actual title/valuation/transfer evidence.
3. LGS art. 6 requires submission within 20 days of the constitutive act and gives 30 additional days to complete, with extension for normal procedure; late filing has opposition conditions. These are filing rules, not a promised 50-day service. Regular constitution occurs on registration (art. 7).
4. SAS art. 38 states registration within 24 hours counted from the next business day only with the approved model and all formal/regulatory requirements met. This does not prove universal actual processing time. The old general 30-60-day SA/SRL estimate remains unconfirmed.
5. Save filing receipt, observations and responses, registered instrument and registration details. Then complete CUIT and separate tax, local and employer registrations. Do not start all obligations on a guessed common date.

### Current CABA incorporation and delivery

[RG IGJ 11/2026](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/normativa/nacional/norma-430346/texto), effective 23 September 2026, supersedes older checklist conflicts. Under its new art. 74 present the constitutive instrument, any optional valid name reservation, separate seat instrument if needed, office acceptance if not in the instrument, required contribution proof, publication, PEP declaration and RePET statement. Ordinary incorporation no longer requires a compulsory professional prequalification opinion; voluntary use and expressly required cases remain, including the specified RePET inclusion case. Authenticity and substantive legal conditions remain.

Name reservation is optional, up to three proposed names for 30 days, first available without validating the name (new art. 59). New art. 61 allows plural sector/functional objects without mandatory connection; SAS can use the stated lawful-activity formula. New art. 62 accepts bank deposit/transfer, notarial evidence or authorised digital-system evidence; where the amount to pay is at most two SMVM, the prescribed signed declaration/receipt alternatives can apply. This is not permission to leave required capital unpaid.

For **SAS**, the current IGJ step guide/TAD inventory identifies **Trámites a Distancia (TAD) → Constitución de Sociedades por Acciones Simplificadas (SAS)** with Clave Fiscal. Select actual instrument/model/capital/signature route, supply owners/administrators, shares, general data and documents, then complete the published payment/submission route and retain the case reference. Apply RG 11 instead of older service-page statements requiring an opinion for every non-model instrument. Authenticated current field order has not been inspected.

For **SA/SRL**, IGJ's published service routes use appointment/Mesa de Entradas, the incorporation form and the specified **F.185** integrated-CUIT route. The urgent service's five-business-day target is expressly conditional on no observations; no ordinary elapsed-time promise is made. [RG 5/2026 and digitalisation implementation](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/justicia/igj/digitalizacion-registral) provide digital signed delivery through TAD from 10 August 2026; old paper-copy/physical-collection instructions are superseded. Digital delivery does not prove every intake is online. **SODA** is phased: its current enabled list covers accounts/balance submissions and moratorium, not incorporation. Do not send an incorporation there merely from its general platform description.

### Corporate CUIT and follow-up

ARCA's current corporate route is **Inscripción y Modificación de Personas Jurídicas**, representative/authorised person's Clave Fiscal level 3+, **Alta nueva persona jurídica → tipo de clave: CUIT**. Enter legal form, jurisdiction, name, constitution/registry dates, fiscal close, objects, addresses, contacts, ownership/capital and management; attach documents and establish electronic fiscal domicile and relationship administrator. Use a specific integrated regime if applicable instead of duplicating its application.

Where documents lack the stated digital certification, the ARCA page requires appointment and **F.420/E**, presenting within 30 calendar days. A company with registry inscription pending has 90 calendar days from CUIT to supply final registration; a justified one-time further 90 days may be requested. The prescribed subsequent notice gives 30 days before CUIT inactivation if missing documents remain. Do not confuse a pending CUIT with completed corporate registration. In **Consulta de trámites**, track the transaction number and notices; drafts may be edited until sent. Retain the assigned CUIT confirmation and then activate the appropriate national taxes.

### Ongoing compliance handoff

LGS arts. 62/67 impose form-dependent accounts and availability/submission duties; SA ordinary meeting for accounts/appointments is called within four months after close under art. 234. SAS art. 58 requires accounts and its minutes, share register, journal and inventory/balance records, electronically individualised. Check the current applicable registry accounts/SODA procedure rather than assuming every SRL files identical accounts.

Route annual corporate income tax/PUB, monthly IVA and staff F.931 to the specialist Argentina Guides. Provincial Ingresos Brutos and municipal licences need their actual jurisdiction. A general company “wealth-tax return” is not established here as universal; identify any applicable substitute-taxpayer/asset obligation separately.

## Penalties and interest

Current government/professional/publication fees and penalty amounts are not confirmed here. Registration defects, unintegrated capital, false declarations, pre-registration acts and missing tax filings have different legal effects; do not replace them with one flat fine. LGS art. 183 and SAS art. 39 identify liability situations; refer a defective/late or regulated case before issuing an incorporation-ready opinion.

## Cross-border points

LGS arts. 118/120 distinguish foreign-company isolated acts from habitual business/branch: establish lawful foreign existence, domestic domicile/publication/registration, decision of the competent corporate body and appointment of its representative; branch capital where special law requires it and separate local accounts. Art. 123 prior registration addresses foreign-company participation in local companies. An individual foreign shareholder is not thereby a foreign company. Art. 124 can treat an overseas company with Argentine seat/main object as local; do not use a foreign certificate as an automatic exclusion.

For CABA, RG IGJ 4/2026's amended art. 164 requires foreign existence/current certificate not older than six months, statutory documents, decisions/representative powers, acceptance/special address and current PEP/beneficial-owner declarations. Art. 118 has extra branch/permanent-presence, CABA seat, fiscal close, any assigned capital and publication documents. Its registration dispenses with a separate art. 123 file. Permitted joint foreign/local filing remains conditional on full foreign-file compliance.

[RG IGJ 13/2026](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/sites/default/files/2026/01/rg_igj_13-2026.pdf), effective 5 November including pending cases, allows constitutive instrument plus reforms or a consolidated current statute plus the specified identity/reorganisation/jurisdiction reforms unless contained in it. Apply its completeness declaration and prescribed certification/reform-evidence alternatives, not superseded reform-history requirements. International certification/translation must fit the destination/file; no universal untranslated-document acceptance is promised.

RG IGJ 12/2026's replacement AML rules take effect 30 calendar days after its 4 November publication, **4 December 2026**. They are not yet operative on the read date. This Guide does not present future AML screens as current; a filing from that date needs its own full implementation check. Income residence, PE, withholding and TP are separate tax analyses in [argentina-cross-border](argentina-cross-border.md) and [argentina-transfer-pricing](argentina-transfer-pricing.md).

## Two worked examples that recompute

### 1. Ordinary SA or SAU cash funding?

Assume all-cash subscribed capital at the current SA minimum, no special regime or fees. Ordinary SA: ARS 30,000,000 × 25% = 7,500,000 initially, remaining 22,500,000 within two years. SAU: all 30,000,000 initially. SAU requires 22,500,000 more initial funding. Capital remains the company's asset; it is not the incorporation fee.

```oa-check
name: Ordinary SA versus SAU initial cash
inputs:
  subscribed_capital: 30000000
steps:
  - ordinary_initial: subscribed_capital * 0.25
  - ordinary_balance: subscribed_capital - ordinary_initial
  - sau_initial: subscribed_capital
  - additional_initial_cash: sau_initial - ordinary_initial
expected:
  ordinary_initial: 7500000
  ordinary_balance: 22500000
  sau_initial: 30000000
  additional_initial_cash: 22500000
```

### 2. November SAS minimum cash versus full payment

Assume an eligible SAS incorporated in November 2026, all cash and minimum capital. SMVM 398,800 × 2 = 797,600 subscribed. Minimum initial 25% is 199,400, leaving 598,200 within two years. If owners elect full payment immediately, initial cash is 797,600, an additional 598,200. Do not backdate this November minimum to another month or apply SAU's full-payment rule automatically to a one-owner SAS.

```oa-check
name: November SAS capital payment decision
inputs:
  smvm: 398800
steps:
  - minimum_capital: smvm * 2
  - minimum_initial: minimum_capital * 0.25
  - remaining: minimum_capital - minimum_initial
  - full_initial: minimum_capital
expected:
  minimum_capital: 797600
  minimum_initial: 199400
  remaining: 598200
  full_initial: 797600
```

## Ask the client first

1. Exact activity and any licence/public offer/concession, owners/legal forms and intended domicile/province.
2. Ownership/control chart, foreign-company status/history, representatives and beneficial owners/PEP facts.
3. Incorporation date, subscribed capital, cash/non-cash assets, valuation/title and payment evidence.
4. Proposed instrument/objects/name, directors/managers/alternates and actual/special domiciles.
5. Registry/tax case numbers, CUIT status, existing registrations and observations.
6. Fiscal close, accounts/meeting plan, employees, IVA and provincial/municipal footprint.

Produce an entity-choice comparison, dated capital sheet, document checklist, registry/CUIT receipt tracker and tax handoff. Identify unresolved fees, screens and provincial requirements before submission.
