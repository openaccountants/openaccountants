### Scope and period

Subjects are the complete published principal ESTA sections 1–30, its Schedule and the 2018 tax-exemption notice, within the stated read bounds. This reference focuses on charging, separate period bands and selected collection provisions. It does not resolve every interaction within those texts or supply current Revenue Administration Act (RAA), prescribed forms, tax tables, penalties or operational procedure. It establishes no comprehensive NR coverage, social contribution, BPT, VAT, customs, property, formation or tax-absence conclusion. [S1]

ESTA section 2 distinguishes Gazette commencement from application to employment income and services fees paid on or after **1 October 2014**. The compilation's amendment table records original commencement on 26 September 2014 and includes the 2023 Schedule regulations and Constitution (Amendment) (No 2) Act 2026 No 21 section 5. These underlying original/2026 instruments and the exhaustive later chain are not qualified here. [S1, physical pp 5, 7]

Gazette No. 33 of **10 February 2023**, GN 179/2023, publishes SL 3/2023, Employment and Services Tax (Amendment to Schedule) Regulations 2023. Regulation 2 states that they are **'deemed to have commenced on 1 July 2022'**. Regulation 4 replaces Schedule paragraphs (1) and (3), the resident employment and services bands. It does not replace the entire Schedule or establish new non-resident rates. Notification, deemed commencement and the compilation's production date must remain separate. Actual period transitions and the complete applicable chain remain held. [S2]

## The method, step by step

### Person, income and charge conditions [S1](https://ronlaw.gov.nr/assets/docs/acts/2014/Employment%20and%20Services%20Tax%20Act%202014_serv6.pdf)

ESTA section 7 residence is a statutory test. It includes a citizen of Naoero except with a permanent home outside the Republic, the specified residing resettled-refugee/asylum-seeker limb and marriage to a paragraph (a) resident. A citizen who is a Government employee posted abroad is resident despite subsection (1). A non-resident individual is an individual who is not resident. No generic days-in-country or expatriate label replaces these tests. Section 3 also defines resident persons beyond individuals. [S1, physical pp 7–10]

Section 5 includes the stated remuneration and termination payments in employment income, with the Secretary's specified allowance exclusion and payment/associate rules. Section 8 defines services fees for independent services, including the listed professional, construction, know-how and management categories, and excludes employment income. Section 9 has both performance and payer/PE source rules, with its exceptions and leave provision. Payment outside the Republic does not by itself remove source. These conditions must be established before selecting a tax or band. [S1, sections 3–9]

The monthly, fortnightly and weekly figures are separate statutory figures. They are not interchangeable annual conversions or a general annual exemption. Exactly the stated boundary is in the zero-rate band. That zero rate does not itself establish an exempt payment, no withholding/reporting duty or no other tax. [S1, physical pp 25–26; S2]

Three source interactions remain specifically held:

1. **Notice versus zero rate.** The 2018 Notice states commencement on 1 July 2018 and repeals the 2016 Notice. Notice 4 uses **'less than $20,000'** and names the Republic. Employment limb 4(a) expressly says 'per annum'; services limb 4(b) does not repeat that wording. Notice 5 refers excess amounts to the Schedule. Schedule (2)(b)/(4)(b) instead includes exactly AUD 20,000 in the stated annual zero-rate band and names the Republic or an SOE. Do not turn that boundary into a Notice exemption, extend the Notice to SOEs, annualise its services wording or invent a reconciliation, withholding method or priority. [S1, physical pp 25–26, 31]
2. **Resident non-individual services.** Section 12 charges a person; the qualified Schedule supplies resident-individual and non-resident branches. It does not identify a rate for a resident company or other resident non-individual service provider. The outcome is unresolved, with no inferred zero rate, exemption or borrowed BPT rate. [S1, sections 3, 12 and Schedule]
3. **PE definition.** ESTA section 3 says the PE meaning is the same as BTA 2016, then retains an enumeration differing from BTA section 7. Do not silently choose, merge or substitute either test. Actual PE classification and its section 12 exclusion remain held. [S1, physical p 8; S3, pp 12–13]

### Recompute the retained hypothetical positions

The following blocks recompute 2 of the positions above using exactly their supplied premises. They are separate arithmetic exercises. The original classification, period, source, currency, precision and operational holds remain applicable. A nil or negative arithmetic component does not resolve an unknown liability or establish an exemption, refund or amount to pay.

```oa-check
name: NR employment position A assumed resident monthly component
inputs:
  gross: 10000
  monthly_band: 9240
steps:
  - excess: max(gross - monthly_band, 0)
  - component: excess * 0.20
expected:
  excess: 760
  component: 152
tolerance: 0.000001
```

name: NR employment separate fortnightly B and weekly boundary C

  fortnight_gross: 5000
