# Alaska corporate income tax, tax year 2026

> **Built from the law. Not yet approved.** Written by Codex. Not reviewed by an accountant. General reference for AI-assisted working papers. A qualified professional must review the established facts and filing result. Circular 230 section 10.37: consider relevant facts and circumstances; do not assume missing facts or rely on audit risk.

**Answer first.** Alaska taxes corporate income at graduated rates, even though it has no individual income tax. Ordinary multistate corporations generally use a water's-edge combined group and property, payroll and sales apportionment. The return deadline comes after the payment deadline. [Department overview](https://tax.alaska.gov/programs/programs/reports/Historical.aspx?60380).

## Scope

Ordinary C corporations and LLCs taxed as corporations, including identification of S corporation, partnership and exempt-organization filing duties. Federal corporate computations belong to `us-form-1120-c-corp`. Oil/gas, insurance, large corporate minimum tax, BEAT, personal-holding-company tax and specialized credits require a separate computation before finalizing any liability.

The Department's available instructions are **2025 Form 6000, revised January 1, 2026**, used here as a **2025-2026 filing-cycle reference**. The Department's current program overview corroborates the ordinary graduated regime and three-factor method; final 2026 forms and any later legislation must be checked before a 2026 filing. Proposed tax bills are not enacted law.

## Ask the client first

1. Legal entity and federal classification, Alaska activity, year end, account and prior filings.
2. Federal taxable income before NOL and dividends deductions, Alaska adjustment and basis schedules.
3. Unitary affiliates, ownership, federal consolidated members and foreign-company activities.
4. Property, payroll and sales by jurisdiction, nonbusiness income and partnership interests.
5. Alaska loss/credit carryovers, estimated payments, extensions and special-industry or minimum-tax exposure.

1. Establish nexus from activities, property, employees including telework, sales and Alaska-source income. Department registration alone does not establish a filing duty. Do not import a local sales-tax threshold into corporate nexus.
2. Determine the unitary water's-edge group. This is mandatory for ordinary corporations, not a separate-company election. The federal consolidated return and Alaska combined/consolidated group can differ. Prepare entity-by-entity reconciliations, eliminations and factor schedules; refer uncertain membership rather than dropping affiliates.
3. **2025-2026 filing-cycle mapping:** Schedule H line 1 begins with federal taxable income before federal NOL and dividends-received deductions. Reconcile group differences, state modifications, nonbusiness items and state deductions. Alaska adopts specified IRC provisions unless modified by Alaska law. Federal conformity is not permission to copy the federal taxable-income bottom line or assume all credits apply.
4. Separate allocable nonbusiness income from apportionable business income. Ordinary Schedule I averages Alaska/everywhere property, payroll and sales factors. Omit a factor whose numerator and denominator are both zero and average the remaining factors. Round factors as instructed. Special industry rules and alternative apportionment require review.
5. Track Alaska capital losses, charitable contributions, dividends deductions and NOL separately. Schedule A line 2 takes the supported Alaska NOL using Form 6385, not the federal NOL deduction. Apply the graduated schedule to final ordinary Alaska taxable income. Review Schedule E other taxes and required credit schedules before treating the ordinary result as final tax.
6. Reconcile payments and return identity, attach the required actual federal return and all state schedules, and verify the filed and paid status separately.

## Worked cases

**Case 1.** An invented standalone company has final Alaska taxable income USD 100, 000 after all adjustments and no other tax or credit. Ordinary tax under the cited schedule is 2, 230 +5% of 1, 000 =USD 2, 280.

```oa-check
name: Alaska ordinary corporate band calculation
inputs:
  ak_taxable: 100000
  lower_band: 99000
  base_tax: 2230
steps:
  - excess: max(ak_taxable - lower_band, 0)
  - ordinary_tax: base_tax + excess * 0.05
expected:
  excess: 1000
  ordinary_tax: 2280
```

**Case 2, entity boundary.** An invented standalone ordinary S corporation has USD 80, 000 pass-through income and no built-in gain, excess passive tax or other corporate-level tax. Its ordinary Alaska income tax is zero, but it still has the described page-1 filing duty. A C corporation with USD 80, 000 final Alaska taxable income instead has ordinary tax 1, 230 +4% of 6, 000 =USD 1, 470. Entity classification changes tax and does not eliminate every filing.

```oa-check
name: Alaska S corporation versus C corporation decision
inputs:
  ak_income: 80000
  c_band_floor: 74000
  c_base_tax: 1230
steps:
  - c_ordinary_tax: c_base_tax + max(ak_income - c_band_floor, 0) * 0.04
  - s_ordinary_passthrough_tax: ak_income * 0
expected:
  c_ordinary_tax: 1470
  s_ordinary_passthrough_tax: 0
```

## When to refuse or refer

Refer uncertain nexus/PL 86-272, unitary/foreign membership, complex state losses, special oil/gas or insurance rules, additional corporate taxes and conformity changes. Do not issue a filing-ready 2026 result from the ordinary bracket calculation while special taxes or final-year law remain unresolved.

## Filing and payment

**2025-2026 filing-cycle workflow:** register/activate Revenue Online, choose the return and tax period, complete Form 6000 or supported approved MeF submission, attach the actual federal return and state schedules, submit, retain acceptance, and separately confirm payment. Electronic filing is required unless DOR grants a waiver; Form 773 is requested at least 30 days before the due date and before filing. Software limitations alone do not justify a waiver. Payment thresholds above come from the detailed instructions, which include the threshold amounts themselves; the overview's wording says more than those amounts.

For an ordinary calendar-year C corporation, payment is normally the 15 th of the fourth month; filing is the 15 th of the fifth month; federal extension ordinarily moves filing to the 15 th of the eleventh month. Apply weekends/holidays and disaster relief. Extension never extends payment. June-year-end, S corporation, exempt organization and cooperative deadlines differ: consult the entity-specific table rather than copying the calendar C corporation date. Estimates follow the adopted corporate estimated-tax rules; use Form 6220 for the state underpayment computation and required annualized/seasonal attachment. Final 2026 estimate exceptions are not confirmed here.

The read instructions impose late filing at 5% of unpaid tax for each 30-day period or part, maximum 25%; late payment uses the same measure, but only late filing applies when both overlap. Unauthorized paper filing incurs the greater of USD 25 or 1% of tax before payments. Interest changes quarterly; obtain the rate for the period instead of assuming a fixed annual rate.

## Completion checklist

- Entity, nexus and actual combined/consolidated group established.
- Federal-to-state base, factors and state carryovers reconciled.
- Ordinary bracket tax and all additional taxes/credits reviewed.
- Correct entity-specific filing and earlier payment dates calendared.
- Final 2026 law/forms checked; e-file acceptance and payment posting retained.
