# Tonga Company Formation and Entity Choice v0.1

**General reference only.** This skill is general tax/accounting reference material for AI-assisted workflows. It has not been reviewed for any specific person's facts, documents, elections, deadlines, residency, filing status, or local procedures. Do not rely on it to file, pay, amend, or take a tax position without review by a qualified professional in the relevant jurisdiction.

## Scope

Use for Tonga company/entity-choice intake, online incorporation and a bounded registry-fee budget during 2026. Compare a local limited-liability company, sole trader/business name, partnership and an overseas-company branch with the actual liability, owners, activities, tax residence and licensing needs. The registry provides company, business-name, business-licence and foreign-investment services; these are distinct registrations. Partnership tax classification does not itself supply a partnership formation procedure.

The Companies Act makes a company a legal person separate from its shareholders. It does not make the cheapest registration route suitable for every business. The steps below cover the official registry's documented process; the cost examples cover specified company filings only.

## Ask the client first

Obtain the intended legal form and whether an overseas company already exists; proposed name and actual reservation notice; all shareholders, share classes and directors, citizenship and foreign ownership; activity and regulated-sector/foreign-investment permissions; physical registered office and address for service in Tonga; proposed constitution, capital, accounting balance date, start date and existing registry/TIN/licence records. For an existing company also obtain its allocated annual-return month, filing authority, current status, missed returns and any Registrar notice.

## The method, step by step

### Choose the legal route and reserve the name

Check the actual company and business-name registers. A company name must be reserved before incorporation. The Registrar refuses names contrary to law, identical/almost identical to another company or an available reserved name, or offensive. A reservation gives no proprietary right in the name. Apply the limited-liability suffix condition and the working-day validity above. Sources: [ss 24-27](https://ago.gov.to/cms/images/LEGISLATION/PRINCIPAL/1995/1995-0014/CompaniesAct_3.pdf); [reg 10](https://businessregistries.gov.to/Documentation/TO/CompaniesRegulations2025.pdf).

The company needs a name, constitution, shares, shareholder(s) and director(s). Under ss 16-16A the application identifies applicants, directors and their residential addresses, shareholders and allocations, registered office and service address. Attach the reserved-name notice and a constitution certified by at least 1 applicant (1 in words on the page) as the company’s constitution if not adopting the statutory standard constitution. Keep each director's signed consent at the registered office and produce it if requested by the Registrar. The application must be signed by each applicant under s 16(1)(b), using the accepted online signature/declaration procedure. The current online application is the prescribed data-field form; the old instruction to deliver paper Form 1 is not the current incorporation route. Companies Regulations 2025 reg 6 identifies a director-consent paper form for records, which must not be confused with an incorporation filing.

### Complete the current online procedure

At the [official Business Entities registry](https://businessregistries.gov.to/public/howto.aspx?cn=RegisterCompany&ctk=CompanyHowTo), create/use a client account, authenticate and select Business Entities. Reserve a Tonga Company Name or Overseas Company Name as appropriate; after approval choose Register a Tonga Company or Register an Overseas Company. Complete company name, directors, shares/classes, relevant addresses, constitution, declaration and payment. Save Draft if incomplete; use Checkout only after reviewing all categories. Accounts are free; payments are in Tongan pa'anga using the registry's approved credit-card or funded-account method. Front-desk assistance supports online use; the MTED notice states online-only applications, renewals and filings from 2 February 2026. The court-document service exception in reg 4(3) is not an ordinary paper incorporation option.

For an overseas company, provide home-jurisdiction details, directors, Tonga business address and evidence of incorporation/good standing. Companies Act s 345 requires that evidence to be no more than 30 days older than filing, the reserved-name notice and a person resident or incorporated in Tonga authorized to accept service. For an English/Tongan translation, reg 11 requires the translator to certify correctness before a person authorized to administer an oath: under the country-of-translation law when translated outside Tonga, or a Tonga oath-authorized person when translated in Tonga. The Registrar can permit different certification on conditions and request evidence of the translator’s ability. Under ss 341-343, first decide whether the overseas entity is carrying on business: merely maintaining a bank account, holding internal meetings, being party to proceedings, using an independent contractor for a property sale, soliciting an order accepted only abroad, creating debt/security, collecting debts/enforcing security, investing/holding property, or an isolated transaction completed within 31 days that is not one of repeated similar transactions does not alone qualify. A share-transfer/registration office or property administration as agent/personal representative/trustee can qualify. Reserve the name before carrying on business and apply for overseas registration within 10 working days after commencing to carry on business under s 343(1). Existing/deemed registrations need their actual transitional status checked. This is not permission to create a local company by submitting an overseas-company form.

The registry says a local company with any overseas shareholder must obtain foreign-investor certification; an overseas company must obtain it before commencing business. Obtain the actual applicable foreign-investment and activity permissions before trading. Business-name registration, foreign certification, business licensing and tax registration do not disappear because a company certificate has issued.

The Registrar reviews the application. Missing information produces pending status and a request for further material; upload corrections through the actual application. Under regs 4 and 12, an unacceptable or unaccepted document is deemed not submitted. Retain the payment receipt, accepted filing and emailed Certificate of Incorporation/Overseas Company Registration, then verify the public register/status. Do not treat a saved draft or debit as approval. No guaranteed processing time is supplied.

### Budget and calendar the continuing obligations

Select only the fee rows for actual filings, then add CT as shown in the schedule. The quoted company fees do not include professional services, foreign certification, business licence, translation, bank charges or sector permissions. The old USD/offline alternatives below are excluded. Obtain the actual checkout total and retain its receipt.

Maintain the registered office in Tonga and required company/accounting records (ss 195,198,203-204). For a local company under s 223, deliver the annual return each year during the month allocated by the Registrar, dated within that month and signed by a director or by a law practitioner/accountant authorized by the company. There is no return in the calendar year of incorporation; the approved holding-company/subsidiary month exception requires written approval. A changed month or default notice controls the actual follow-up. For an overseas company carrying on business, s 349 separately requires an annual return during its allocated month, dated within that month, confirming the overseas-register information. Apply any written month-change notice. The first calendar-year exemption under s 349(5) excludes deemed-registered overseas companies; do not import the local-company subsidiary-month exception. An overdue/default or restoration case requires the actual notice and full provisions. The corporate annual return is separate from the tax return and from a business-licence renewal.

Create the tax calendar using [the accepted entity facts](https://businessregistries.gov.to/public/howto.aspx?cn=RegisterCompany&ctk=CompanyHowTo) and the linked [corporate income-tax](to-corporate-income-tax.md), [individual income-tax](to-income-tax.md), [Consumption Tax](to-vat-gst.md) and [payroll/retirement](to-payroll-social.md) skills. Register applicable tax obligations, keep annual income-tax returns and periodic CT/PAYE filings as separate tasks, and verify live form/account access. Do not certify that every company has the same tax balance date or a universal audit exemption.

## Worked cases

These are hypothetical registry budgets for timely filings after 2 February 2026. CT is assumed charged as required by the schedule. They are not whole-business setup quotes or a recommendation to change legal form. All totals are derived from the linked fee schedule and its CT note.

### Local company: reserve and incorporate

The actual required filings are name reservation and local incorporation. TOP 80 + TOP 300 = TOP 380 before CT; TOP 57 CT gives TOP 437. A separate licence or foreign certificate is an additional open task and cost.

```oa-check
name: Local company reservation and incorporation fee budget
inputs:
  reservation: 80
  registration: 300
steps:
  fees: reservation + registration
  consumption_tax: fees * 0.15
  registry_budget: fees + consumption_tax
expected:
  fees: 380
  consumption_tax: 57
  registry_budget: 437
```

### Overseas route: do not use the local-company fee

An existing overseas company requires reservation and overseas registration. TOP 80 + TOP 800 = TOP 880; TOP 132 CT gives TOP 1,012. Applying the local fee would underbudget these same listed filings by TOP 575 including CT. The choice follows the actual legal entity, not this difference in cost.

```oa-check
name: Overseas-company versus incorrectly substituted local-company fee
inputs:
  reservation: 80
  overseas_registration: 800
  local_registration: 300
steps:
  overseas_fees: reservation + overseas_registration
  overseas_ct: overseas_fees * 0.15
  overseas_budget: overseas_fees + overseas_ct
  wrong_local_budget: (reservation + local_registration) * 1.15
  underbudget: overseas_budget - wrong_local_budget
expected:
  overseas_fees: 880
  overseas_ct: 132
  overseas_budget: 1012
  underbudget: 575
```

## When to refuse or refer

Refer bespoke constitutions, restricted foreign activity, uncertain beneficial ownership/capital/officer eligibility, legal disputes and restoration/defaults. Do not certify current legal compliance from a portal screen or a registration budget. Refer late-fee conflicts before calculating or paying a disputed charge. No accountant approval is claimed.

## Filing and payment

Use the actual online registry forms and approved payment channel above. For recurring changes/annual returns obtain authority to manage the entity; an account alone does not grant it. Keep submission, acceptance, receipt and certificate separately. Check that the register reflects the approved event and calendar its remaining tax/licensing/annual-return tasks.

## Completion checklist

Confirm form choice, reservation validity, owners/directors/addresses/constitution, signed consents, required foreign/activity permissions, correct TOP fee/CT, actual accepted registration, registry status and the allocated continuing-compliance calendar. Leave every unresolved legal or cost item open.
