# British Columbia speculation and vacancy tax v2.0

## Scope

Prepare an individual-owner BC Speculation and Vacancy Tax (SVT) workpaper and reviewer brief. The tax is annual, provincial and owner-specific, with property/location/classification, income/status and use tests. It is not the federal Underused Housing Tax (https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/U-0.5/FullText.html), the Vancouver Empty Homes Tax (https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/empty-homes-tax.aspx), a property-transfer tax, ordinary municipal property tax or the federal non-Canadian purchase prohibition. Keep each programme's year, legal person, property/base, exemptions and filing/payment evidence separate. No declaration or payment is submitted by this method. [Act sections 1,6,8,14](https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/18046).

This Guide uses **calendar 2026** current SVT law. The letters/declarations in 2026 concern **calendar 2025, the 2025-2026 filing cycle**, with the old rates. Do not apply the 2026increase to a2025declaration merely because it is filed or paid in 2026. Federal UHT law now removes tax and returns for 2025 onward, while historical 2022-2024 obligations remain. [Current provincial rate periods](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/tax-rates), [federal sections 1.1 and 6.1](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/U-0.5/FullText.html).

The supported ordinary computation is for an individual registered owner. Corporate, trust, partnership, beneficial/nominee, life-estate, agreement-for-sale and registered-lease ownership can be within the Act but require the actual attribution/owner path, not an automatic exemption or this simple individual calculation. Since 2024a registered leaseholder can be the relevant owner. Do not make a non-owner spouse file a separate owner declaration solely because they live in the residence. [Actual owner definition and separate-capacity rules](https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/18046), [declaration paths](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-to-declare).

## Ask the client first

1. What exact calendar year, property civic/legal address, PID/folio, residential classification/portion, current and relevant assessment roll, actual assessed amount and supplementary/revised assessment apply? Establish the legally applicable roll and date, not just the date printed on an old valuation.
2. Who is an owner at 31 December, with what registered capacity and interest? Obtain title, purchase/sale/lease/life-estate/beneficial documents and all co-owners. Joint tenants ordinarily have equal interests; tenants in common use actual instrument shares or the prescribed fallback.
3. Is the property wholly/partly in a specified area for that year, with any Indigenous/treaty/reserve, island, prescribed-resort, farm/improvement or excluded-property boundary? Use actual municipal/legal boundaries and statutory/regulatory map, not a postal code or the informal city name.
4. What Canadian citizenship/PR/provincial-nominee and actual BC/federal residence status applies at the required time? Obtain spouse/common-law and deemed-nonresident facts. A BC T1 alone does not establish every statutory condition.
5. What prior-calendar-year reported total income and net unreported income, inside or outside Canada, apply to the owner and spouse under the Act? Obtain assessments/returns, required-but-unfiled facts, excluded receipts and costs, not a gross 'foreign income percentage'. Separately obtain same-year and two preceding-year BC income balances for the other-owner credit.
6. Who lived in each residence, for which dates/months and under which agreement? Obtain principal residence/other homes, spouse/separation/work/medical facts, arm's-length relationship evidence, written rental terms, actual home use, non-arm's-length permission, tenant citizenship/residence/untaxed status and prescribed whole-property fair-market-rent computation where required.
7. What purchase/PTT, inherited/estate, death, disability, medical/care, hazardous damage, construction/renovation/heritage/new inventory, court order, strata-hotel/rental restriction, childcare, water-only access or bankruptcy facts support a specific exemption? Obtain permits/progress, dates, certifications, use and current programme/vintage conditions.
8. What original declarations/letters/codes, authorised representative documents, assessments, credits already used across properties, spouse income transfers, payments, revised CRA/assessment information and appeal dates exist? Do not expose SINs/codes or submit a live form while preparing the workpaper.

### Establish property, area, owner and value

Use Act definitions and regulation, not the old incomplete city lists. Current taxable areas include Abbotsford, Chilliwack, Courtenay, Duncan, Kamloops, Kelowna, Nanaimo, Parksville, Penticton, Salmon Arm, Vernon subject to the prescribed exclusion, West Kelowna, Coldstream, Lake Country, Lantzville, Mission, North Cowichan, Peachland, Squamish, Summerland, Comox, Ladysmith, Lake Cowichan, Qualicum Beach, Cumberland and Lions Bay, together with the actual Capital and Metro Vancouver municipal populations and UBC/University Endowment Lands. Check the actual mapped boundary for every property. The old Bowen Island expansion claim is wrong: usually air/water-only islands are excluded except Vancouver Island; Bowen is not automatically a designated municipality for this tax. [Current list and exclusions](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas), [Act specified area/regulation 2.1](https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/18046), [actual regulation](https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/275_2018).

The old 2024expansion list omitted Duncan, North Cowichan, Ladysmith and Lake Cowichan. Preserve all old named-city checks but use the current list and applicable commencement, not a remembered 'second wave' or fabricated OIC/section 132. Predator Ridge's specified mapped area in Vernon is excluded, with regulation 90/2025retroactive to 2024. Reserve/treaty/self-governing Indigenous lands and prescribed exclusions require the actual law, not an unsupported blanket about all First Nation owners or all neighbourhoods. Do not certify a disputed boundary without legal parcel/map evidence. [Current regulation, area 2.1 and Schedule 1](https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/275_2018), [current area guidance](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas).

Residential property includes specified class 1land/improvements and certain residential improvements on class 9land. The Act excludes the low-assessed-value population; regulation also excludes qualifying unstratified four-or-more rental apartments and specified associated land/care/group/bunk/cookhouse property. Mixed classification, hotels and prescribed uses require their actual rule; not every unit offered for short stays is an exempt strata accommodation property. Some specified exempt owners also have a declaration exception, so 'absolutely every owner in any listed area must declare' is too broad. [Definitions/section 62](https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/18046), [regulation 2](https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/275_2018), [individual exclusions and strata distinction](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/exemptions-speculation-and-vacancy-tax/individuals).

Establish 31 Decemberownership/capacity and section 8interest; do not use ownership days to invent a prorated rate. Joint tenants and tenants in common have different share rules. Contiguous-property treatment in section 9can affect the relevant residential property; physical subdivision or a mailing address does not decide it alone. Corporate/trust/partnership interest holders and separate capacities cannot be treated as ordinary individual co-ownership. [Actual sections 6-9](https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/18046).

For the base, section 14uses assessed value **determined on 1 July of the tax calendar year**. Section 6(4)points to the roll completed for the immediately following calendar year. The old '1 July of the prior year' shortcut can select the wrong assessment. Obtain the actual roll/classification, revised/supplementary notices and assessment-year linkage before computing. Do not confuse an assessment-roll year, its valuation date and the later declaration/payment year. A successful assessment revision has consequential SVT rules; calling the SVT office is not an assessment-value appeal. [Actual sections 1,6(4),14 and 70](https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/18046).

### Determine the rate and income populations

Apply sections 15-17using actual specified citizen/PR and BC-resident definitions, including untaxed-worldwide-earner exclusions, provincial nominees, deemed residence and year-end conditions. The lower route can apply to a qualifying Canadian residing outside BC; foreign ownership and a simple BC address are different issues. Do not assume tax filing in BC alone proves the lower rate or a principal-residence exemption. Unknown status is an unresolved calculation, not proof of the maximum tax. [Current status/rate rules](https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/18046), [rate-period guidance](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/tax-rates), [terms](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/terms-definitions).

An untaxed worldwide earner is tested using the owner's and relevant spouse's **immediately preceding-calendar-year** statutory unreported income versus reported total income. Unreported income can arise inside Canada too; reporting/taxation/foreign-source are not synonyms. Apply the actual income-after-costs and prescribed-exclusion rules, including federal-section 81excluded amounts through regulation 3. Reported income includes the actual statutory assessed/filed/not-required/no-return alternatives; required-but-unfiled returns cannot be replaced by a guessed reported amount. It is not a current-year simple 'more than half all worldwide gross receipts' calculation. [Act section 5](https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/18046), [regulation 3](https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/275_2018), [actual reporting-year explanation](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/terms-definitions).

For 2026use the new rates. The old overview/assessment pages still describe a2%maximum in examples addressing earlier declarations; that does not override the current Act's3%and explicit period table. The legislative-change table makes the 4%change effective 1 January 2027, so it is not a2026 rate merely because the amendment already exists. [Actual rates and period boundary](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/tax-rates), [law and commencement](https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/18046), [change table](https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/e4tlc18046).

### Test each exemption, not just whether the property is empty

Apply exemptions separately for each owner, using the precise person/property/year conditions. A household label or 'nothing owed' assumption does not remove an ordinary declaration duty. Principal residence means the place lived in for the longest period, but entitlement also requires the eligible status. Spouses normally share one principal-residence route; actual commuter/work/medical/separation provisions can allow a different result. Other principal-residence-related routes include a qualifying person with a disability, military absence, previous residence after moving out of province, residential care, medical absence and certain other absence. Do not automatically deny those because the owner was not resident on 31 Decemberwithout reading the applicable route. [Actual exemption catalogue and conditions](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/exemptions-speculation-and-vacancy-tax/individuals), [Act principal-residence provisions](https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/18046).

For the ordinary rental route, obtain genuine qualifying months, not the total of short-term bookings or occasional weekend visits. An arm's-length tenant needs an actual monthly-or-longer written tenancy and must make the home a residence. Non-arm's-length occupancy can qualify without rent or a written rental agreement if the actual permission/monthly-residence test is met. The owner category decides whether additional tenant residency/income rules apply; the old blanketBC-resident-and-three-times-rent test for **every** related tenant is wrong. A Canadian lower-rate owner can have a different result from the foreign co-owner on the same property. The tenancy requirements and Act sections 36-39govern. [Actual examples and scope](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/exemptions-speculation-and-vacancy-tax/individuals/tenancy-requirements), [controlling tenancy provisions](https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/18046).

For the higher-rate owner's related-tenant route, actual tenant residence/status and BC income thresholds apply; do not pool several tenants' incomes. Use prescribed annual fair-market rent for **all residences in the entire property**, determined under regulation 5using its CMHC class/bedroom/geography methodology, not a guessed advertised luxury-condo rent. Keep tenant income year and owner's prior-year untaxed-status test distinct. Owner/spouse/qualifying minor-child occupancy cannot simply be recast as a non-arm's-length tenant to defeat the exclusion. The 'niece' relationship alone is not a conclusive automatic related-person label; apply actual relationship/dealing facts. [Act sections 12 and 36-39](https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/18046), [regulation 5](https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/275_2018), [actual tenant conditions](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/exemptions-speculation-and-vacancy-tax/individuals/tenancy-requirements).

Preserve the old recent-purchase job, but a purchase is not an unconditional one-year buffer: actual section 49requires the PTT-paid/specified exemption route or the relevant registered-occupier rule. Inheritance and estate/death have distinct conditions. Death exemption can cover the relevant co-owners and estate in the death year and following year; a former power of attorney ends at death, and the actual executor/personal representative must handle estate declarations. Do not assume only the deceased's share benefits or use an expired POA. [Act sections 46-49](https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/18046), [actual exemption and estate procedures](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/exemptions-speculation-and-vacancy-tax/individuals), [special circumstances](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-to-declare/special-circumstances).

Construction/renovation, heritage conservation, phased development and vacant inventory need the actual stage, use, permit/progress, duration and claimant conditions, not merely a building permit. Preserve court-order, hazardous/uninhabitable, medical/care, marriage breakdown, strata/rental restriction and hardship investigations. A listing with a realtor is not by itself an exemption. The current individual catalogue separately explains disaster/damage duration, medical certifications including nurse practitioners, family-property separation without reconciliation, bankruptcy, minor testamentary trusts, charity trusts, strata hotels, childcare and water-only access. Resolve the matching actual statutory route; do not invent a current exemption called 'court order' or discretionary 'hardship under 87' (section 87addresses excess-refund interest). Specific court-order/hardship relief not established here remains a named hold. [Actual Act exemptions/interest](https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/18046), [current individual catalogue](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/exemptions-speculation-and-vacancy-tax/individuals).

The old preOctober 2018strata rental restriction does not create a continuing 2026exemption. The actual historical Act/administrative catalogue explains transitional years, rather than merely 'substantially narrowed after 2022'. Retain historic review where relevant, but do not use it for the current year. A strata hotel has its separate assessment/use route; a short-term rental unit is not automatically that class. [Historical/current distinction](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/exemptions-speculation-and-vacancy-tax/individuals), [actual section 51 and property provisions](https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/18046).

### Credits and supported final tax

Calculate gross per-owner tax from the supported rate, statutory assessed value and ownership interest after resolving exemption. Resident credit is non-refundable and share-limited; for 2026its total annual cap is 4,000across all eligible properties, not 4,000 per property. Allocation between properties is governed by section 54; the old 'cannot be applied across properties' claim was wrong. It is not a freely transferable cash credit between unrelated owners. Qualifying owner/status/age and no partnership/trust capacity conditions matter. The earlier 2,000cap remains the 2025route. [Current resident-credit explanation](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/tax-credits/bc-residents), [Act section 54](https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/18046).

Other-owner credits use actual current-year and two preceding-year BC income balances, deductions for prior credit use/transfers, property use and statutory tax cap. The prior-year household-income test for untaxed status is **not** the same population. Supported spouse transfer of BC income is possible with actual authorization; the old blanket 'credits never transferable between owners' obscured this permitted income transfer. Keep the individual's personal account distinct from trust/corporate income and partnership exclusions. Confirm actual credit application/assessment and do not reuse income already consumed in an earlier year. [Other-owner eligibility/balances/application](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/tax-credits/other-owners), [Act sections 52 and 55-61](https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/18046).

The cached other-owner page's2%/0.5%examples and 75%maximum describe the older rates. Current law sections 55-56use the actual applicable versus lowest rate, not a fixed 75%for 2026. With eligible use the cap can be the relevant tax; without that qualifying use the cap is gross tax×(applicable rate-lowest rate)/applicable rate, subject to lower-rate restrictions and the actual available income-credit balance. Current 3%versus 1%therefore has a different cap. Use law, preserve the historical worked route as historical and identify the actual year. [Controlling current credit formula](https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/18046), [older administrative examples](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/tax-credits/other-owners).

### Declaration, deadlines, assessment and records

The ordinary annual declaration date is 31 March of the following year, subject to actual statutory exceptions, demand and authorised extensions. Each relevant co-owner declares individually; one spouse's declaration does not satisfy the other owner's duty. Specified owners/prescribed classes have exceptions, and a demand may require a declaration even where an ordinary exemption would apply. Obtain actual role/property/year evidence rather than advise skipping because an exemption likely applies. [Act sections 62-64](https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/18046), [actual declaration guidance](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-to-declare).

Use the letter's Declaration Code and Letter ID, correct property list, SIN/date of birth and actual account/authorised role. Current guidance offers online or telephone declarations; a mandatory BC Services Card login was not established. Corporate/trust/partnership declarations request the actual underlying interest-holder and capacity information. Do not post private identity or declaration credentials in the workpaper. An authorised helper needs the accepted mandate; current special-circumstance page has inconsistent FIN 146/FIN 416labels, so verify the actual linked form rather than guessing the identifier. No sign-up, disclosure or submission is performed by this Guide. [Declaration requirements](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-to-declare), [actual representation/death procedure](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-to-declare/special-circumstances).

Retain the selected exemption/credit facts, declarations, confirmations, NOAs, payment receipts, title and CRA/BC Assessment changes. Keep the owner/tenant/household income years explicit. Contact the authority if a letter appears inconsistent with the actual boundary/title; do not invent an out-of-area exemption or ignore a formal demand. Correct legal ownership/mailing data with LTSA/BC Assessment as appropriate.

For 2025the official payment example is 2 July 2026. Under Act section 78the annual date is **2 July of the following year**, subject to applicable extension; a timely declarant ordinarily pays by the later of that annual date and 30days after the original NOA. Specified additional assessments have their own later-of rule. The old 'first business day inJuly, always' can miss the actual law and notice-specific timing. For 2026state the nominal 2 July 2027 annual date and actual applicable notice/extension check, not a guessed operational portal opening. Declaration is nominal 31 March 2027. [Act section 78](https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/18046), [actual period example](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/tax-rates).

If no declaration is filed, section 18applies the actual year's maximum rate; this is a tax assessment mechanism, not automatically an additional maximum-rate penalty on top of correctly assessed ordinary tax. Late/corrected declarations have actual acceptance/examination windows and discretion, not unlimited guaranteed reversal. General section 67normal reassessment is six years from the earlier original NOA/tax due date, with separate no-return, misrepresentation/fraud, waiver, late-declaration and consequential-assessment rules. The old section 78audit-window citation was wrong. Use original facts/dates before a limitation opinion. [Act sections 66-70 and 18](https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/18046), [declaration correction process](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-to-declare).

Late unpaid current tax can attract the statutory 10%penalty, separately from prescribed interest calculated daily and compounded monthly. Required-information failures under sections 81-82use greater 100 or 25 per day capped 2,500, not an invented 25,000 per day. Knowing/grossly negligent false statements under section 83use greater 100 or 100%additional tax on accurate information, not an unqualified penalty equal to the whole property's tax. Third-party and offence/prosecution rules have their own facts; do not label every mistake perjury or automatically criminal. GAAR is section 77, not 92(which addresses refunds). Waiver/cancellation, payment and case-specific interest rules need actual entitlement, not a presumed interest-free delay. Current prescribed rate is not confirmed here. [Actual enforcement provisions](https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/18046), [interest regulation 6](https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/275_2018).

Appeals, judicial review and classification/valuation disputes remain specialist work. A call to the office does not preserve a formal appeal. Current section 98restricts which assessments can be appealed, including certain self-declared/no-return assessments; it does not give every NOA an identical objection route. Do not assume an appeal stops payment or apply old generic Part 7references without the actual current provision, period and issue. [Current Act appeals](https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/18046), [assessment guidance](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/tax-assessments-appeals).

### Separate Vancouver and historic federal regimes

**Federal UHT:** current Act sections 1.1 and 6.1remove both payment and return requirements for 2025 and later. Do not file UHT-2900 or add 1%for 2025/2026because a foreign individual owns a vacant BC residence. Historical 2022-2024affected/excluded-owner, base/FMV election, qualifying occupancy, vacation-area, seasonal/new-building and exemption investigations remain relevant to those actual years; their changes must be checked against the period's legislation. The 2023owner-definition amendments do not replace the later cessation. Do not assume all Canadian trustees/partners were excluded in every historical year or every affected person owed tax. [Enacted law](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/U-0.5/FullText.html), [CRA exact effective years and retained duties](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/taxes/excise-taxes-duties-and-levies/underused-housing-tax/what-has-changed.html).

**Vancouver EHT:** applies to the City of Vancouver's actual qualifying residential property, not all Metro Vancouver or UBC/University Endowment Lands. The actual bylaw uses its own occupancy, principal-residence, arm's-length/term, exemption and deemed-vacancy conditions. More than six months unoccupied is not a universal 180-day literal test. EHT is one declaration per property, unlike SVT's individual co-owner process. The current rate is 3%from 2021 onward; the old '2024 onward' description was incomplete. [Bylaw sections 1-3](https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/11674c.PDF), [actual city FAQ](https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/empty-homes-tax-frequently-asked-questions.aspx).

Actual bylaw declaration is the **second business day in February**, not first Friday. Payment is the **tenth business day in April** for the normal notice, not first business day. The city states 2026reference-year declaration 2 February 2027 and payment 14 April 2027; its 2025FAQ states 3 February 2026. Supplementary notices/complaint/late evidence routes are separate. Obtain the actual taxable assessed value for the relevant municipal year; never assume the SVT base is automatically identical. ### Case 2:2026resident credit follows the owner's share and annual cap

## When to refuse or refer

Preserve the original ten referral jobs without treating every one as an automatic tax outcome:

- **R-BC-SVT-1:** corporate/partnership/trust holder and attributed underlying owners. Establish the actual owner path; not exempt by entity form.
- **R-BC-SVT-2:** mixed class 1/class 6,class 9residential portions, stratahotel and commercial/residential classification or taxable value dispute.
- **R-BC-SVT-3:** reserve/treaty/self-governing Indigenous land and owner exclusions under the actual law, not an assumed ordinary-tax override.
- **R-BC-SVT-4:** bare/alter-ego/joint-partner/testamentary trust, beneficial ownership and separate-capacity credits.
- **R-BC-SVT-5:** assessment appeal, objection/complaint, limitation, judicial review, fraud/penalty or formal demand. Preserve real deadlines with the appropriate professional.
- **R-BC-SVT-6:** historic strata rental restrictions, current stratahotel status and assessment evidence; no current transitional exemption by assumption.
- **R-BC-SVT-7:** short-term rentals, mixed occupancy or rental-business category; complete the real monthly-home/tenancy conditions, not booking-night aggregation.

- **R-BC-SVT-9:** outside-area letter, disputed boundary/title or missing code. Reconcile official legal data and contact the authority; do not file on a guess or ignore a demand.
- **R-BC-SVT-10:** federal purchase prohibition, foreign-buyer/transfer or other housing law. Different statute/definitions, not settled by SVT classification.
