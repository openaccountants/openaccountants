# Spain: regional tax attributable to selected income, for IRPF regional credits, 2026

**Sourced from the law.** This revision has not had an independent whole-pack review or accountant sign-off. This Guide holds the attributable-tax worksheet of [es-irpf-deductions](es-irpf-deductions.md), moved here unchanged on 6 November 2026 so that each Guide stays within the size the site can deliver. Specific unresolved interactions are listed in Sources.

## Who this applies to

A resident IRPF taxpayer claiming a common-territory regional credit whose amount or ceiling is the regional tax attributable to selected income: social-housing rent (Aragón), work moves (Castilla-La Mancha, Cantabria, Canarias) and public-aid credits (Asturias, Extremadura, Galicia, Illes Balears, Cataluña, Comunitat Valenciana). Each credit's entitlement, income gate and year stay in the regional Guide for that community, listed in [es-irpf-deductions](es-irpf-deductions.md#regional-credits-one-guide-per-community). Compute the bases, scales and minima with [es-income-tax](es-income-tax.md) first.

## The method, step by step

### Regional attributable tax: separate general and savings calculations

Use this worksheet for the social-housing, work-move and public-aid credits identified in the regional Guides listed in [es-irpf-deductions](es-irpf-deductions.md#regional-credits-one-guide-per-community). Keep each credit's entitlement, year, income ceiling, eligible claimant and settlement position already stated in its regional catalogue. The computation covers zero income, general-only income, savings-only income, mixed general/savings income, personal/family minimum spillover and ordinary base reductions. It does not turn an unestablished eligible-income numerator into zero.

**Calculate the quotas first.** Let `G` be general BI after its integration/compensation calculation, `B` the nonnegative general BL actually subject to the general scale, and `S` savings BL. Apply the joint-return reduction where due, then the other base reductions in their statutory order and permitted basket, before calculating tax. A pension reduction reduces the general base within its own limits; it does not automatically reduce savings income. The permitted remainder of the compensatory-pension reduction and joint-return reduction follows its own savings rule. Personal/family minima are a later **tax calculation**, not another subtraction from `G` or `B` ([LIRPF arts.48–50 and84](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2006-20764#a50)).

For each tax half `j`, determine its own legally applicable minimum `Mj`, general scale `Fj` and savings scale `H`. Its general minimum portion is `min(B,Mj)`; its savings minimum portion is `min(S,max(0,Mj−B))`. Thus `QGj=Fj(B)−Fj(min(B,Mj))` and `QSj=H(S)−H(min(S,max(0,Mj−B)))`, calculated in cents. Use `QGC+QSC` for total regional gross quota. State and regional minimum amounts can differ, so calculate their spillovers separately. If `B=0`, general quota is zero; if `0<B≤Mj`, that half's general quota is zero while savings quota can remain positive. Do not tax `B−Mj` at the bottom of the scale or subtract the minimum twice ([arts.56,63,66,74 and76](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2006-20764#a56)).

For a credit expressly using general average rates, calculate `rE=100×QGE/B` and `rC=100×QGC/B`, separately, with two decimal places. The official return implementation **discards excess rate decimals**; for example,8.8691…% is8.86%,not8.87%. If `B=0`, there is no positive general-tax amount to allocate and the general-income credit is zero; do not divide by zero or use the savings rate. The worked controls below use these expressed rates only for credits whose wording calls for them. A quota-attribution cap instead retains the quota ratio until the attributed tax is calculated ([arts.63.2/74.2](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2006-20764#a74); [official public RentaWEBOpen2025](https://www2.agenciatributaria.gob.es/wlpl/PARE-RW25/OPEN/index.zul?EJER=2025&TACCESO=COLAB), version1.19, checked27October2026).

**Establish the numerator before distributing base reductions.** `N` is the selected qualifying income actually included in general BI: reduced net rental income for social housing; reduced net employment/activity income of the entitled mover(s) for work moves; or the qualifying aid's included income amount. Deduct the income category's legally applicable expenses and reductions first. An activity grant is not automatically its gross cash amount: the Balearic emergency-aid input and Galician wildfire instructions require its attributable reduced net activity income. Keep the grant's income classification and any exemption separate from its credit. For an ordinary positive allocation with `0≤N≤G`, the selected amount in general BL is `A=round_to_cents(N×B/G)` if `G>0`; if no positive general base remains, its attributable general quota is zero. The minimum is already reflected in `QG`; do not subtract a second share of the minimum from `A` ([AEAT Balearic emergency-aid instructions](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/eu_es/ayuda/manuales-videos-folletos/manuales-ayuda-presentacion/irpf-2025/10-cumplimentacion-irpf-deducciones-autonomicas/10_4-illes-balears/10_4_19-determinadas-subvenciones-declaracion-zona-afectada.html); [Galician wildfire instructions](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/eu_es/ayuda/manuales-videos-folletos/manuales-ayuda-presentacion/irpf-2025/10-cumplimentacion-irpf-deducciones-autonomicas/10_11-galicia/10_11_22-subvenciones-incendios.html); controlled public-return calculations cited above).

For joint work-move claims, `G`, `B` and `QGC` belong to the joint return. Sum only the reduced net work/activity income of contributors actually entitled to that move credit in `N`; a non-entitled spouse's income still enters the joint bases and quotas. Apply the one combined attributed-tax ceiling to the sum of the eligible movers'500 or300 amounts. Do not give every mover the entire joint quota or remove the joint-return base reduction from the tax calculation. The actual credit is then subject to the existing regional settlement rule, including sufficient remaining regional tax under LIRPF77.2.

**Discriminating numerical controls.** With general BI21,000, general BL20,700, savings BL1,000 and selected net income1,000, current ordinary under65 minima/scales give regional general quota1,660.50 in CLM,1,480.25 in Cantabria and1,564.21 in Canarias. The work-move tax ceilings are respectively79.07,70.49 and74.49; savings tax is not included in those numerators. With Aragón general quota1,629.94, the attributed quota is77.62 and the social-housing credit23.29. These calculations preserve the300 base reduction, rather than treating general BI and BL as interchangeable. Each assumes the catalogue's separate entitlement and income gates pass.

For included aid1,000 in the same21,000/20,700 bases, `A=985.71`. Asturias general average rates8.02% state and7.83% regional give156.24; Extremadura's current2026 scale gives8.02%+6.62% and144.31; Galicia's8.02%+7.47% give152.69. An aid input1,000.64 gives Galicia `A=986.35` and152.79, distinguishing cent-rounding of the liquidable amount from prematurely rounding a rate or retaining an unrounded aid base. Valencia's current2026 regional general rate7.42% instead gives74.20 on aid1,000, not73.14. For Balearic emergency aid, general BI25,000/BL23,500, included aid1,000 and savings BL10,000 give `A=940`, rates8.85%+8.86% and166.47. Removing savings changes neither those general rates nor the aid credit when no other income condition changes.

If general BL3,500 is below the applicable minimum and savings BL10,000 remains, both general rates and the general-income attribution are zero even though savings tax is positive. If general BI and BL are both zero, no general-income quota can support these credits. Savings-only income cannot create qualifying employment, rental or aid income. A taxpayer whose numerator or eligibility is unknown is a different case; do not replace that missing fact with zero. The allocation and rounding methods were checked against fictional official2025 OPEN controls; the figures above are recomputed under current2026 law. Where2026 scales differ, the2025 programme amounts are not carried forward. No2026 return campaign was tested. The numerical allocation controls do not establish the separate programme or rental-entitlement facts.

**Distinct2026 Balearic war-aid base.** Art.3quinquies.1 uses the regional general rate and the included liquidable-base aid amount. Its separate paragraph2 expressly prescribes, for an activity grant, the grant's share of the activity's total gross receipts multiplied by that activity's reduced net income: `Nactivity=grant/gross activity receipts×reduced net activity income`; its stated credit is `Nactivity×rC/100`, rounded to cents. Do not replace that explicit paragraph2 base with the gross grant or add a second `B/G` factor to it. For gross receipts100,000 including grant20,000, positive reduced net activity income30,000 and regional general rate8.86%, the paragraph2 credit is531.60. This is derived from the current2026 enactment, rather than from an earlier inflation-aid campaign excluding business receipts ([art.3quinquies.1–2](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2014-6925#a3-15)).

**CLM individual signed-loss branch.** For an individual entitled mover, combine that person's reduced net employment and activity income in `N`; an activity loss is not omitted merely because the move was for employment. If a loss in another general-income category leaves positive `G` and `B` but makes that positive aggregate `N` exceed `G`, the CLM move attribution is limited to `QGC`: the credit before available-quota settlement is `min(500,QGC)`. It does not absorb savings quota. This is a legal application of the unchanged employment/activity-quota ceiling in art.12quater(1)(b), supported by actual fictional Renta2025 OPEN1.19 controls, rather than a tested2026 return campaign. For net wages20,000, net activity loss13,000 and savings1,000, `N=G=B=7,000` and `QGC=137.75`, so the credit is137.75. Replacing the activity loss with a general-capital loss13,000 gives `N=20,000>G=7,000`; the credit remains137.75, leaving95 savings quota. Adding a positive general gain1,000 makes `G=B=8,000` and `QGC=232.75`; the credit is232.75, still leaving95 savings quota. The under65 personal minimum is5,550. A further control retains `N=20,000`, `G=8,000` and savings1,000 and adds a valid individual pension contribution1,000: `B=7,000`, `QGC=137.75`, credit137.75 and regional liquid quota95. The first three controls have no global base reductions; the fourth confirms the separate pension-reduction step. No other credits apply. The current2026 scale and unchanged cap reproduce these amounts. Do not extend this individual CLM result to signed joint claims, other regions, an unestablished or nonpositive selected numerator, or shared wage/thalidomide expenses ([enacted cap, Law2/2021 DF9.Dosbis](https://www.boe.es/buscar/doc.php?id=BOE-A-2021-11513); [older BOE consolidation, operative art.12quater unchanged by the2026 amendment](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2014-1368#a1-6); [actual2026 amendment, art.18 and DF6](https://docm.jccm.es/portaldocm/verArchivoHtml.do?ruta=2026%2F03%2F30%2Fhtml%2F2026_2465.html&tipo=rutaDocm) ([official PDF, physical pp.15–19 and24 read](https://docm.jccm.es/portaldocm/descargarArchivo.do?ruta=2026/03/30/pdf/2026_2465.pdf&tipo=rutaDocm)); [pension reduction, LIRPF51–52](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2006-20764#a52); [AEAT cap and individual/joint income wording](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/ayuda/manuales-videos-folletos/manuales-ayuda-presentacion/irpf-2025/10-cumplimentacion-irpf-deducciones-autonomicas/10_7-castilla-mancha/10_7_16-traslado-vivienda-habitual.html)).

**CLM joint return with one entitled mover and positive selected income above general BI.** Article 12quater(1)(c) selects employment/activity income belonging to the members who generate the deduction; a non-entitled spouse’s activity loss is not added to that selected numerator. Joint taxation nevertheless accumulates both spouses’ income: current employment and activity results offset within general income under LIRPF 48(a), before adding a positive non-transfer general gain under 48(b). The married-joint reduction is 3,400, applied to general BI first; the basic under 65 personal minimum is 5,550 once for the joint family, rather than twice (LIRPF 84(1),84(2).2–3,84(5),57 and61). Retain each member’s income attribution under LIRPF 11. For exactly one entitled mover, where that member’s combined positive employment/activity `N` exceeds positive joint general `G` and general `B` is positive, the work-move attribution is limited to `QGC`; the credit before available-quota settlement is `min(500,QGC)`. It does not absorb savings quota.

Separate fictional signed joint control: both spouses are under 65 and resident in CLM; only the wage-earner qualifies for the move credit. That member has net wages 20,000; the other spouse has net professional activity −9,000. There is also a taxable general non-transfer gain 1,000 and savings income 1,000, with no other base reduction, extra minimum or credit. Joint `G=12,000`; after 3,400 joint reduction, `B=8,600`. The single 5,550 personal minimum gives `QGC=289.75`; savings quota is 95. The selected eligible-member `N` remains 20,000, so the credit is 289.75 and regional liquid quota 95. Combining the non-entitled spouse’s loss into `N=11,000` would instead give 265.60 by a directly rounded aggregate ratio and is not the observed one-entitled-mover result. These are current-law calculations using the unchanged CLM cap/scale, supported by an [actual fictional Renta2025 OPEN1.19 joint control](https://www2.agenciatributaria.gob.es/wlpl/PARE-RW25/OPEN/index.zul?EJER=2025&TACCESO=COLAB). They are not a tested 2026 return campaign. With both spouses entitled, art.12quater(c) provides 500 per mover and one attribution limit for the combined eligible employment/activity income; that is one joint attributable-tax ceiling, rather than a sum of separately capped member deductions. Exact signed aggregation and cent rounding in that both-entitled population remain unresolved: with both spouses entitled on the otherwise unchanged first case, selected combined `N=11,000` gives an actual 2025 credit 265.61 versus 265.60 from final-cent aggregate rounding. Raising only the general non-transfer gain to 2,000 gives `G=13,000`, `B=9,600`, `QGC=384.75` and actual credit 325.55 versus 325.56 from that ratio. Those results do not certify an intermediate or per-member rounding algorithm ([enacted joint-member cap, DF9.Dosbis](https://www.boe.es/buscar/doc.php?id=BOE-A-2021-11513); [AEAT entitled-member and joint combined-limit wording](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/ayuda/manuales-videos-folletos/manuales-ayuda-presentacion/irpf-2025/10-cumplimentacion-irpf-deducciones-autonomicas/10_7-castilla-mancha/10_7_16-traslado-vivienda-habitual.html); [LIRPF 11](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2006-20764#a11), [48](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2006-20764#a48), [57/61](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2006-20764#a57) and [84](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2006-20764#a84); current 2026 cap/scale source chain as in the individual branch above).

**Wages and thalidomide aid: establish employment expenses before the regional numerator.** Compute the employment category under LIRPF 19 before allocating an included aid amount to a regional credit. Deduct the applicable article 19(2)(a)–(e) expenses, then the ordinary 2,000 under 19(2)(f), limited to employment income left after those other expenses. That ordinary amount is annual, not an allowance for each payer or receipt; joint filing does not multiply it by the number of family members. The article 20 reduction tests employment income after 19(2)(a)–(e), before 19(2)(f), and cannot make the resulting net employment income negative. Its other-income gate and current bands still apply. These are category rules, not a direction to allocate the ordinary 2,000 or article 20 reduction between wages and aid ([LIRPF 19–20](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2006-20764#a19), [84(2)](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2006-20764#a84)).

The separate mobility increase is 2,000 in the residence-change year and the next: the claimant must be unemployed and registered with the employment office, then accept employment requiring an actual move to another municipality. The active-worker disability increase is 3,500, or 7,750 if the claimant needs third-person assistance, has reduced mobility or has disability of at least 65%; a disability of at least 33% and actual active employment must coexist. Receiving aid classified as employment income does not itself establish active employment. RIRPF 11(2) attributes each increase **exclusively to the employment generating it**, rather than to all employment receipts. For the applicable income ceiling, first apply any valid gross-income reductions, then deduct the expenses exclusive to that employment and the share of remaining expenses corresponding to the accepted contract's duration or the days of active employment with disability. This timing rule for the ceiling does not prescribe a gross-wage/aid allocation of the ordinary 2,000. The fixed increase is not itself prorated for a short eligible period, but eligible-period income limits its use. Recompute the mobility ceiling in each year: no income from the qualifying job in the second year gives no mobility expense that year. When both increases apply, their combined ceiling uses the union of the employment income generating one or both rights; do not count the same wages twice. Joint filing allows one mobility increase and one disability increase corresponding to the highest degree of disability among family members who qualify as active workers with disability; an inactive relative does not raise that amount. Each increase remains subject to its eligible members' income ceiling ([LIRPF 19(2)(f)](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2006-20764#a19), [RIRPF 11](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2007-6820#a11); [AEAT 2025 expense instructions, applying these unchanged provisions](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/ayuda/manuales-videos-folletos/manuales-practicos/irpf-2025/c03-rendimientos-trabajo/rendimiento-neto-trabajo-integrar-base-imponible/fase-2-determinacion-rendimiento-neto/particular-analisis-gastos-articulo-19_2_f-lirpf.html)).

A control with already established taxable aid 396,000, full-year eligible active employment 15,000, salary-only compulsory contributions 1,500, no gross-income reduction and no other article 19(2)(a)–(e) expenses gives employment income 409,500 before 19(2)(f). The ordinary 2,000 and eligible disability increase 3,500 leave 404,000. Article 20 does not apply because its pre-19(2)(f) measure is 409,500. The 3,500 belongs entirely to the qualifying employment, not proportionately to aid. This does **not** establish how much of the ordinary 2,000 reduces the aid numerator. A separate ceiling control follows AEAT’s published 2025 example under the unchanged provisions applicable in 2026. The claimant has a recognised 34% disability throughout the year. A January job ends, leaving 1,000 of eligible income; the claimant is then unemployed and registered with the employment office, receives 6,000 of unemployment benefit, and accepts a new December job requiring a residence move to another municipality. The December job supplies 800 of eligible income. Both eligible-income amounts are already net of the expenses used in the enhancement ceiling. Nominal mobility/disability increases of 2,000+3,500 are limited to 1,800, not 5,500 or 7,800. The 6,000 unemployment benefit cannot enlarge that ceiling. These are expense controls, not a computed regional thalidomide credit.

**Balearic thalidomide enactment and rate boundary.** Law 4/2026 DF 67(2) added article 3 sexties. DF 72 brought the law into force on 14 June 2026, the day after its BOIB publication, while article 3 sexties expressly reaches fiscal 2023 onward. The July 21 corrigendum changes an unrelated DF 2 law citation. The new provision says to apply a singular average tax rate to the aid amount included in liquidable base, following general-BI inclusion; neither that operative text nor ATIB's enactment notice specifies whether the rate is combined state/regional or regional-only. It supplies no fixed percentage. Retain that exact question and the shared ordinary-expense/reduction question before computing this new credit; do not substitute a 2025 campaign formula or infer a rate from another aid credit ([Law 4/2026 DF 67(2)/DF 72](https://www.boe.es/buscar/doc.php?id=BOE-A-2026-15579), [corrigendum](https://www.boe.es/buscar/doc.php?id=BOIB-i-2026-90195), [ATIB notice](https://atib.es/General/Novedad.aspx?idTexto=16987&idTipoTexto=1&lang=es)).
