# Sao Tome and Principe VAT v0.3

> **Sourced from the law, in part.** Tax year 2026. The VAT Code was read in the final text the Assembly published on 15 October 2019. Law 2/2023 and Decree-Law 12/2023 changed it and were not read, because the Finance Ministry download links returned 404 and the newer Ministry site refused access. Check every point against them. Held points sit on the `Not yet confirmed:` line. No accountant has signed this version.

## Who this applies to

Taxable persons are any person or entity that independently carries on economic activity, including production, trade, services, extraction, farming, forestry, livestock and fishing, and government bodies and other public bodies so far as they make taxable supplies ([VAT Code art. 3(1)](https://www2.camara.leg.br/saotomeeprincipe/diarios-da-an/xi-legislatura-22-11-2018/ii-serie-a/2.a-sessao/DAN30-IIS.pdf)). Employees are not taxable persons (art. 3(2)). The Code applies to the national territory (art. 2).

The 2026 Budget Bill calls the approving law Law 13/2019; the Assembly text read set entry into force on 1 March 2020 (approving law art. 6). The older skill said IVA has applied since 1 June 2023; the later start is consistent with the 2023 amendments but was not read, so it is held.

**Taxes replaced.** The approving law revoked the consumption tax of Decree-Law 20/76 and the consumption tax on services of Decree-Law 35/2000, with their amendments, and the stamp duty provisions on invoicing (approving law art. 2). The older skill also listed taxes on alcohol, spirits and cigarettes, telecoms and hotel tax, and restaurant tax; those were not in the text read and stay held.

## The base

- **Tax due** for a month is taxable operations times the rate, less deductible input tax under arts. 21 to 26 ([art. 28(3)](https://www2.camara.leg.br/saotomeeprincipe/diarios-da-an/xi-legislatura-22-11-2018/ii-serie-a/2.a-sessao/DAN30-IIS.pdf)).
- **Input tax.** The right to deduct arises when the input tax becomes chargeable (art. 21(1)). No deduction for pleasure boats, helicopters and aircraft, or tobacco (art. 22(1)); none for non-residents on the simplified registration (art. 22(2)); 50% for the items in the table (art. 22(3)).
- **Exemption regime.** A business on the exemption regime has no right to deduct (art. 44(4)). It must still file start and cessation declarations, pay the art. 48 tax, write "IVA - Regime especial de isenção" on its invoices and keep purchase and turnover records (art. 47).
- **Leaving and joining.** Opting out of the exemption is by electronic start or change declaration; the business must then stay in the normal regime for at least five years (art. 46(3)). A normal-regime business that falls below the limit and wants the exemption files a change declaration in January of the next year and adjusts input tax on remaining stock (art. 45). The Direccao dos Impostos can call in a business it believes has passed the limit, and tax is due from the month after the declaration (art. 49).
- **Internal exemptions** (art. 17): life insurance and reinsurance; financial intermediation (Annex II), except where a specific fee is charged; investment gold; sales of residential property other than the first sale; residential lettings.
- **Import exemptions** (art. 18): imports of goods whose home supply is exempt; gold, coins and notes imported by the Central Bank; imports under treaties and diplomatic relations. Special customs regimes are in art. 19.
- **Exports** (art. 20): goods sent abroad; goods for hydrocarbon operators in the economic zone; ship and aircraft supplies; international transport of people, goods and mail from abroad.
- **Reduced and zero rates.** The older skill said reduced and zero rates exist; the Code read has one rate plus the half-base rule, with art. 20 relief for exports.

## Penalties and interest

If a monthly return is missing, the Direccao dos Impostos assesses the tax from past returns or other information, including income tax data, after notice; the tax is due within 30 days of notice (arts. 28(4), 50(1) to (3)). Non-payment starts the procedure in art. 79 of the Tax Process Code and can lead to suspension of taxable-person status or a frozen bank account until paid (art. 50(3) and (4)). Filing the missing return and paying the tax with compensatory interest within that period cancels the assessment, without prejudice to any penalty ([VAT Code art. 50(5)](https://www2.camara.leg.br/saotomeeprincipe/diarios-da-an/xi-legislatura-22-11-2018/ii-serie-a/2.a-sessao/DAN30-IIS.pdf)). Omissions or errors in a return are corrected by additional assessment (art. 51). Compensatory interest runs at the Central Bank reference rate plus three percentage points a year ([General Tax Code art. 38](https://ministerio.financas.st/phocadownload/Imposto/legislacao/Lei_N6_2007_Lei_Que_Aprova_Codigo_Geral_Tributario.pdf)). Penalty amounts were not read.

## Cross-border points

- **Place of supply of services** (art. 10(1)): for a taxable buyer, where the buyer has its seat or the establishment receiving the service; for a non-taxable buyer, where the supplier is established. Services on property in STP, work on goods in STP, cultural, sporting and educational events in STP, hotel and restaurant services in STP and some transport are always in STP (art. 10(2)).
- **Reverse charge.** A taxable buyer accounts for the tax on services located in STP when the supplier is non-resident with no permanent establishment ([VAT Code art. 27(2)](https://www2.camara.leg.br/saotomeeprincipe/diarios-da-an/xi-legislatura-22-11-2018/ii-serie-a/2.a-sessao/DAN30-IIS.pdf)).
- **Non-resident suppliers** without a permanent establishment file electronically and appoint a resident taxable representative, jointly liable, unless they use the simplified registration set by the Direccao dos Impostos (art. 30).
- **Imports** are taxed on the importer (art. 27(3)).

## Two worked examples that recompute

**Example 1, normal month.** Standard sales of Db 100,000, Annex I food invoices of Db 20,000, deductible input tax of Db 8,000.

```oa-check
name: Monthly VAT
inputs:
  standard: 100000
  food: 20000
  input_vat: 8000
steps:
  - output: standard * 0.15 + food * 0.5 * 0.15
  - payable: output - input_vat
expected:
  output: 16500
  payable: 8500
```

**Example 2, decision: stay on the exemption regime or opt out.** A trader with sales of Db 800,000 pays 7% with no deduction. Compare with normal VAT on the margin, if customers can bear the 15% on top: with purchases carrying VAT of Db 500,000, normal VAT is lower by Db 11,000 a year. Opting out locks the trader into the normal regime for at least five years (art. 46(3)), with monthly returns. The art. 48 text is the 2019 version, so confirm the current art. 48 first.

```oa-check
name: Exemption regime against normal VAT
inputs:
  sales: 800000
  purchases: 500000
steps:
  - special: sales * 0.07
  - normal: (sales - purchases) * 0.15
  - saving: special - normal
expected:
  special: 56000
  normal: 45000
  saving: 11000
```

## Ask the client first

- Turnover in the last calendar year, and legal form
- Any sales of Annex I food
- Any services bought from non-residents
- Imports or exports, and any oil and gas customers
- Whether it opted out of the exemption regime, and when
- Whether it buys cocoa, coffee, vanilla or other crops from small sellers
