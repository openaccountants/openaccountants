# Ecuador Tax Overview (2026) v0.1

> **General reference only.** This skill is general tax/accounting reference material for AI-assisted workflows. It has not been reviewed for any specific person's facts, documents, elections, deadlines, residency, filing status, or local procedures. Do not rely on it to file, pay, amend, or take a tax position without review by a qualified professional in the relevant jurisdiction.

Ecuador uses the US dollar and taxes income, consumption and money leaving the country through one authority, the Servicio de Rentas Internas (SRI). Social security is collected by the Instituto Ecuatoriano de Seguridad Social (IESS). The figures below are for 2026, under the Ley de Régimen Tributario Interno (LRTI, SRI codification with last reform 1 April 2026) and its Reglamento (RLRTI, last reform 18 November 2025).

### Worked example: one salary, two taxes

An employee in Quito earns USD 1,500 a month in 2026 with no other income. Her IESS share is 1,500 × 9.45% = **USD 141.75** a month. Her projected taxable base is (1,500 − 141.75) × 12 = **USD 16,299.00**, which falls in the 10% band from USD 15,549 to 20,188: 167 + 10% × (16,299 − 15,549) = **USD 242.00** for the year before any gastos personales rebate (method and rebate in ecuador-payroll and ecuador-income-tax). Her employer adds 1,500 × 11.15% = **USD 167.25** a month to the IESS.

```oa-check

  pay: 1500
steps:
  - ee_iess: pay * 0.0945
  - er_iess: pay * 0.1115
  - base: (pay - ee_iess) * 12
  - tax: 167 + (base - 15549) * 0.10
expected:
  ee_iess: 141.75
  er_iess: 167.25
  base: 16299.00
  tax: 242.00
```
