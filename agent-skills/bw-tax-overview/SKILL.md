# Botswana Tax Overview v1.1

> **General reference only.** This Guide is general tax reference material. It has not been reviewed for any specific person's facts. Check figures against the official sources named here.

## Scope

A routing and headline reference for Botswana tax year 2026-27 (1 July 2026 to 30 June 2027). Load the relevant detailed Guide before computing or filing. The Income Tax Act, 2026 (No. 13), Tax Administration Act, 2026 (No. 14) and most of the Value Added Tax Act, 2026 (No. 15) operate from 1 July 2026; VAT has deferred inbound-tourism and gig/share-platform provisions. [SI 93 para 2](https://www.burs.org.bw/index.php/tax/tax-laws-2026?download=954:income-tax-act-amendment-2026), [SI 91 para 2](https://www.burs.org.bw/index.php/tax/tax-laws-2026?download=955:tax-administration-act-2026), [SI 95 paras 2–3](https://www.burs.org.bw/index.php/tax/tax-laws-2026?download=958:value-added-tax-act-2026).

## Ask the client first

- Which person/entity and job: forming, earning, employing, making supplies, paying abroad or transacting with associates?
- Tax year, company accounting-period start and end, and any approved substituted period?
- Residence, temporary-resident status, location of work/business, foreign income and permanent establishments?
- Type of income/payment or supply, taxable turnover, VAT registration/category and remote-supplier status?
- Existing BURS notices, approvals, TIN, withholding obligations and any unpaid tax?

## The method, step by step

1. **Fix the period and residence before choosing a rate.** The standard tax year is 1 July to 30 June (ITA s 2); “tax year” includes a company's accounting period (s 45(8)). For a continuing approved substituted period under the old Act, SI 92 reg 51(9)–(10) preserves the old Act and rate for periods commencing before 1 July 2026 and applies the new Act and rate to periods commencing after that date. Do not apply the headline rate to an earlier period without that check. [Official ITA bundle](https://www.burs.org.bw/index.php/tax/tax-laws-2026?download=954:income-tax-act-amendment-2026).
2. **Use the system summary with its conditions.** Currency notation below is Botswana Pula (BWP/P). The headlines do not replace full bands, exemptions or return exceptions.

4. **Apply the 2026 transition rather than an old website headline.** The ITA repeals Cap. 52:01 and VAT Act repeals Cap. 50:03, with savings (ITA ss 143–144; VAT ss 87–88). Earlier website figures of 22% company tax, 12% VAT and a P500,000 ordinary compulsory VAT threshold are not the enacted 2026 headlines. SI 90 reg 33 waives late-payment penalties for 12 months from commencement, through 30 June 2027; it does not waive late-payment interest. Interest remains simple, 1.5% for each month or part month, subject to TAA s 41, including its written-notification/payment exception. [TAA s 41 and SI 90 regs 21, 33](https://www.burs.org.bw/index.php/tax/tax-laws-2026?download=955:tax-administration-act-2026). Load the detailed tax Guide for other penalties and filing steps.

## Worked cases

A registered supplier makes a standard-rated domestic supply valued at P1,000 excluding VAT, with no exemption, zero rating or special rule. Under VAT s 7(2)–(3), VAT is P1,000 × 14% = P140 and the VAT-inclusive amount is P1,140. This illustrates the headline; it does not determine input-tax recovery or registration liability. [VAT Act s 7](https://www.burs.org.bw/index.php/tax/tax-laws-2026?download=958:value-added-tax-act-2026).

```oa-check
name: Botswana standard-rate headline
inputs:
  net_value: 1000
steps:
  - vat: net_value * 0.14
  - inclusive: net_value + vat
expected:
  vat: 140.00
  inclusive: 1140.00
```

## When to refuse or refer

Do not calculate from a marginal rate alone, decide treaty relief without an applicable instrument, or infer that company registration settles tax residence, VAT or employment obligations. For CIPA formation, current incorporation steps are held in the formation Guide. Capital transfer tax and detailed customs duties are outside this router's confirmed coverage.
