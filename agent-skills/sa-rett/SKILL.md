# Saudi Arabia real estate transaction tax: 2026

## Scope

Read before computing or declaring Saudi real estate transaction tax (RETT). The current law is **Royal Decree M/84 dated 19/3/1446H**, with the implementing regulation issued by decision **01-03-25**. Both took effect on **10 April 2025**. The old A/84 framework is relevant to earlier transactions, not the complete current law. [Law cover and arts. 1-2, 20](https://zatca.gov.sa/ar/RulesRegulations/Taxes/Documents/RETTrgulations.pdf); [ZATCA effective-date statement](https://zatca.gov.sa/ar/RulesRegulations/Taxes/Pages/RETTRegulation.aspx).

This **2026** method covers Saudi-situs property and qualifying direct/indirect transfers, exemptions, state support for an eligible first home and transaction registration/payment. It preserves the original March/July/September 2025 examples and the January 2024 inheritance with their dates. A calculation for an earlier event must use the law then in force; do not apply a new penalty or exemption retrospectively merely because a current skill is loaded.

**Quick reference:** identify the actual property/right/share disposition, date, consideration within fair-market-value bounds, transferor and evidence of an exemption or first-home certificate. RETT is a transaction tax on the relevant value, not a tax on gain. There is no ordinary annual RETT return. The current rates and conditions are below. Missing ownership, valuation or exemption facts require a hold, not a guessed higher charge or buyer withholding.

VAT treatment of rent, property supplies and input recovery remains a separate [VAT](saudi-arabia-vat.md) inquiry. Foreign ownership permission, holy-city restrictions, property title disputes, non-resident CIT/capital gains and treaty rights are separate legal jobs. A RETT payment does not establish permission to own property or settle another tax.

## Ask the client first

1. Transferor/transferee identities, authority to act, residence/legal form and any family, group, fund or financing relationship.
2. Saudi title/registration details, location, property/right description, current use, completion/off-plan status and all relevant contracts or usufruct/extension rights.
3. Actual transaction/documentation/possession/share-transfer dates, any unconditional agreement, agreed cash/non-cash consideration, financing term cost and payment evidence.
4. Market evidence and any qualified valuation, including property/other-asset allocations and related-party or non-cash terms.
5. Exemption claimed and its documents: inheritance/will, documented gift/relationship, waqf/charity/public status, corporate ownership/audited accounts, finance licence or other statutory conditions; obtain prior/future transfers for holding-period tests.
6. For first-home support: Saudi citizenship, actual dwelling eligibility, prior ownership and the valid housing tax-bearing certificate/application; for indirect transfers obtain full property/asset valuations, ownership histories and linked transactions.

Do not assume fair value equals a price merely because no appraiser was engaged. Equally, an appraisal is not an invented universal prerequisite for every arm's-length sale; evidence must support the statutory value and resolve a material conflict.

## The method, step by step

### 1. Identify the actual disposition and legal period

The law covers transfer of ownership or permanent benefit directly or indirectly, and a grant of benefit **for more than 50 years**. Property condition/use, an unfinished/off-plan state, partial or undivided interests and lack of notarisation do not by themselves remove a transfer from scope. Relevant inseparable property rights and permanently dedicated appurtenances follow the regulation. Review the actual rights and extension clauses; do not turn a 49-year or exactly 50-year arrangement into a longer right “defensively”. [Law arts. 1-2](https://zatca.gov.sa/ar/RulesRegulations/Taxes/Documents/RETTrgulations.pdf); [IR arts. 1-2](https://zatca.gov.sa/ar/RulesRegulations/Taxes/Documents/New%20Rett%20Regulation.pdf).

| Rate or threshold | 2026 rate or threshold and conditions | Official source |
|---|---|---|
| Ordinary in-scope disposition | 5% of the statutory value, subject to any established exemption | Law art. 2, [law](https://zatca.gov.sa/ar/RulesRegulations/Taxes/Documents/RETTrgulations.pdf) |
| Long benefit/usufruct right | **More than 50 years**, not 50 years or more; value uses the present-value rule below | Law art. 1; IR art. 2(f), [regulation](https://zatca.gov.sa/ar/RulesRegulations/Taxes/Documents/New%20Rett%20Regulation.pdf) |
| Real-estate-company asset test | Saudi property held to earn sale/rent income is at least 50% of total asset fair value at transfer or during the preceding 365 days | IR art. 2(h), [regulation](https://zatca.gov.sa/ar/RulesRegulations/Taxes/Documents/New%20Rett%20Regulation.pdf) |
| Indirect share-transfer trigger | Acting-together persons dispose of aggregate 30% or more through linked transactions within the defined 3-year period starting on/after reaching a 30% holding; apply the full rule, not a single percentage alone | IR art. 2(i), [regulation](https://zatca.gov.sa/ar/RulesRegulations/Taxes/Documents/New%20Rett%20Regulation.pdf) |
| Family gift | Documented gift to spouse or qualifying relative through degree 3, with the 3-year onward-gift condition | Law art. 3(a)(7); IR arts. 1, 3(a)(7), [regulation](https://zatca.gov.sa/ar/RulesRegulations/Taxes/Documents/New%20Rett%20Regulation.pdf) |
| Eligible citizen's first-home state support | Government bears RETT on up to SAR 1,000,000 of qualifying purchase price; this is payment support, not an exemption of that value from the taxable base | Current detailed guideline §1.1 and Royal Order A/84 explanation, [guideline](https://zatca.gov.sa/ar/HelpCenter/guidelines/Documents/Detailed-Guideline-for-RETT-In-accordance-with-provision-of-RETT-Law-and-its-Implementing-Regulations.PDF) |

A barter has separate property dispositions to analyse; do not net one property's value against the other to create a gains-only tax. For a legal disposition before the current law, identify the former instrument and actual event. The detailed guideline distinguishes the former **SAR 850,000 VAT-support purchase-price limit** from the **SAR 1,000,000 RETT-support price limit**; these are not successive exemptions from the RETT taxable base. [Detailed guideline §1.1](https://zatca.gov.sa/ar/HelpCenter/guidelines/Documents/Detailed-Guideline-for-RETT-In-accordance-with-provision-of-RETT-Law-and-its-Implementing-Regulations.PDF). Do not describe all property-related VAT as replaced: use the VAT skill for the actual supply and period.

### 2. Establish consideration and fair-market-value bounds

For an ordinary disposition, law art. 2(2) uses total agreed consideration **where it is within fair-market-value bounds at the transaction date**, excluding the qualifying financing term cost from a legally licensed financier. Include cash and non-cash value and inseparable rights. The old shorthand “always choose any appraiser's higher number” omits the statutory fair-value-range test. Keep the price, comparables and explanation; where declared value falls below fair-market bounds, obtain a supportable valuation/correction rather than simply accepting the price. ZATCA has the specific verification/assessment powers in law art. 8 and IR art. 8. [Law arts. 1-2, 8](https://zatca.gov.sa/ar/RulesRegulations/Taxes/Documents/RETTrgulations.pdf); [IR arts. 2(c)-(d), 8](https://zatca.gov.sa/ar/RulesRegulations/Taxes/Documents/New%20Rett%20Regulation.pdf).

For a taxable transfer of a real-estate-company interest, use the fair value of **all relevant directly/indirectly held Saudi property** at disposition multiplied by the interest transferred, or the higher agreed consideration allocated to the property as prescribed. Do not use only the share's book cost or the gain. For a benefit right beyond the term boundary, use the higher of the **present value of fair-market benefit** and the **present value of agreed consideration**, with the correction rule for later changes. It is not automatically undiscounted total rent. BOOT arrangements have their actual property-transfer/date rule. [IR art. 2(e)-(g)](https://zatca.gov.sa/ar/RulesRegulations/Taxes/Documents/New%20Rett%20Regulation.pdf).

### 3. Test property-rich entities and genuine financing separately

Read the company/fund asset test and the linked-transfer/acting-in-concert rules together. Related persons are presumed to act together unless the contrary is proved under the definition. Property-company share capital increases have specific non-disposition conditions: unchanged proportions for existing holders, or the prescribed retained old shares when new investors subscribe. A generic SPV share sale is not automatically a taxable transfer of the whole property's value. [IR arts. 1, 2(h)-(j)](https://zatca.gov.sa/ar/RulesRegulations/Taxes/Documents/New%20Rett%20Regulation.pdf).

The public-offering/listed-security and qualifying fund-unit provisions include their own exemptions/limits; in particular, unlisted investment-fund units have a specific aggregate-disposal test in IR art. 3(a)(9)(d). Do not substitute the ordinary company test or assume every fund disposal is exempt. Complex indirect/fund cases require the full ownership and transaction schedule before calculation. [Law art. 3(a)(9)-(10)](https://zatca.gov.sa/ar/RulesRegulations/Taxes/Documents/RETTrgulations.pdf); [IR art. 3(a)(9)-(10)](https://zatca.gov.sa/ar/RulesRegulations/Taxes/Documents/New%20Rett%20Regulation.pdf).

For Murabaha/lease-to-own/finance-lease arrangements, the once-only charge rule requires the prescribed unity of parties/property/value and contractual evidence: the initial disposition to the licensed financier is taxed, the linked later event is identified in the financing documents and the property description/value does not change as required. This is not a blanket exemption for the initial sale. Temporary security transfers and enforcement have separate treatment. [Law arts. 2(3), 3(a)(14)](https://zatca.gov.sa/ar/RulesRegulations/Taxes/Documents/RETTrgulations.pdf); [IR arts. 2(l), 3(a)(14)](https://zatca.gov.sa/ar/RulesRegulations/Taxes/Documents/New%20Rett%20Regulation.pdf).

Sale-and-leaseback, sukuk/SPV and group financing need analysis of the actual ownership/security rights, licensed parties and statutory route. Neither “all exempt if economically financing” nor “always taxable until a private ruling” is the law. A ruling may clarify the particular facts; it cannot create an exemption not authorised by the law. [Law arts. 3, 13](https://zatca.gov.sa/ar/RulesRegulations/Taxes/Documents/RETTrgulations.pdf); [IR arts. 2-3, 12](https://zatca.gov.sa/ar/RulesRegulations/Taxes/Documents/New%20Rett%20Regulation.pdf).

Spouse is independently named. Cousins or in-laws must not be inserted into the defined list merely by analogy. The old exclusion of all siblings/grandparents/grandchildren from gift relief was incorrect. An onward **sale** is not the same test as the stated disqualifying onward **gift**; analyse its own disposition and tax. [IR art. 1 and art. 3(a)(7)](https://zatca.gov.sa/ar/RulesRegulations/Taxes/Documents/New%20Rett%20Regulation.pdf).

**Restructuring correction:** the old generic 2-year post-restructuring hold is not the current rule. The route-specific table and IR art. 3(c) govern continuation, including specified public offerings, compulsory sales and qualifying later mergers/acquisitions with remaining holding periods preserved. Record the original exempt event and monitor the actual conditions; do not silently terminate the file after obtaining a zero-tax registration. [IR art. 3](https://zatca.gov.sa/ar/RulesRegulations/Taxes/Documents/New%20Rett%20Regulation.pdf).

### 5. Keep first-home state support separate from the taxable base

The current detailed guideline explains that the government bears RETT on up to **SAR 1,000,000** of a qualifying citizen's first-home purchase price under the relevant programme. Compute the full transaction tax first, then the amount validly borne under the actual certificate and any remaining payment. It is not a general zero-rated/exempt first SAR 1,000,000 in the RETT base. The earlier **SAR 850,000** figure relates to the prior VAT-bearing programme; do not describe it as a succession of RETT exemptions. [Current detailed guideline §1.1](https://zatca.gov.sa/ar/HelpCenter/guidelines/Documents/Detailed-Guideline-for-RETT-In-accordance-with-provision-of-RETT-Law-and-its-Implementing-Regulations.PDF).

Establish citizenship, qualifying dwelling/first-home history and the housing tax-bearing certificate through the [official service](https://sakani.sa/en/service-info/real-estate-tax-incurred-service). Raw land, commercial/mixed property and uncertain prior ownership require the actual programme eligibility decision. Do not presume support, deny it by default or manufacture a refund receivable while the facts are missing. The transferor's statutory responsibility remains distinct from any commercial agreement that the buyer bears the cost. [Law art. 7](https://zatca.gov.sa/ar/RulesRegulations/Taxes/Documents/RETTrgulations.pdf).

### 6. Establish the statutory date, payment and responsible person

The transferor is the primary liable person; representation does not change the actual person for whose benefit the disposition is made. The transferee is jointly liable only where ZATCA establishes it caused non-payment, with the prescribed notice/conditions, including avoidance arrangements or breach of conditions. A buyer who has not seen a receipt is not automatically a withholding agent, and a non-resident seller does not itself trigger buyer RETT withholding. [Law art. 7](https://zatca.gov.sa/ar/RulesRegulations/Taxes/Documents/RETTrgulations.pdf); [IR art. 7](https://zatca.gov.sa/ar/RulesRegulations/Taxes/Documents/New%20Rett%20Regulation.pdf).

| Event | Current date/payment treatment | Official source |
|---|---|---|
| Ordinary documented disposition | Tax due on transaction/documentation date; pay then or earlier, subject to the actual exceptions | Law arts. 4-5, [law](https://zatca.gov.sa/ar/RulesRegulations/Taxes/Documents/RETTrgulations.pdf) |
| Possession transferred for ownership without documentation | Actual possession-transfer date under the stated rule | IR art. 4(a), [regulation](https://zatca.gov.sa/ar/RulesRegulations/Taxes/Documents/New%20Rett%20Regulation.pdf) |
| Benefit right beyond term boundary | Date right granted, with the specified cancellation-within-30-days treatment | IR art. 4(b), [regulation](https://zatca.gov.sa/ar/RulesRegulations/Taxes/Documents/New%20Rett%20Regulation.pdf) |
| Property-company shares | Earlier share transfer/unconditional agreement; payment within 30 days | IR arts. 4(d), 5(a)(1), [regulation](https://zatca.gov.sa/ar/RulesRegulations/Taxes/Documents/New%20Rett%20Regulation.pdf) |
| Later breach of exemption | Statutory charge relates to the original disposition; pay within 30 days of breach as prescribed and correct the registration | IR arts. 4(f), 5(a)(2), 11(b), [regulation](https://zatca.gov.sa/ar/RulesRegulations/Taxes/Documents/New%20Rett%20Regulation.pdf) |

Law/IR anti-avoidance provisions can apply to sham/concealed transactions and arrangements aimed at postponing payment. Identify the real rights and actual event; a new document label does not move tax into another period. [Law art. 6; IR arts. 5(d), 6](https://zatca.gov.sa/ar/RulesRegulations/Taxes/Documents/RETTrgulations.pdf).

## Boundary and exception table

| Original boundary | Required treatment |
|---|---|
| Property outside Saudi Arabia | Outside this Saudi RETT scope. |
| Materially unsupported price or non-cash/related allocation | Resolve market evidence/valuation; do not invent the tax base. |
| Transaction under former law or earlier VAT regime | Retrieve the actual period's instrument and preserve dates; no current-law retroactive shortcut. |
| Sukuk, sale-leaseback or corporate restructuring | Apply the specific statutory route and conditions; refer complex documents, without imposing a universal private-ruling prerequisite. |
| Mixed-use/first-home support uncertain | Obtain the actual eligible dwelling/certificate/allocation; no assumed partial exemption. |
| Gift/inheritance documents incomplete | Hold relief until relationship/deed/certificate evidence establishes the claim. |
| Long right/optional extensions or beneficial-interest/SPV disposal | Establish actual legal term/ownership history and apply the precise thresholds, not an automatic tax on all shares. |
| Non-resident party, ownership permission, Makkah/Madinah or treaty/CIT issue | Obtain the separate current legal analysis; RETT liability does not prove ownership entitlement or authorise buyer withholding. |

## Worked cases

All monetary values are **illustrative inputs**; no example establishes a current market valuation, prior ownership or eligibility certificate. The original names and dates remain unchanged.

### A. Riyadh-based LLC, Olaya office tower: 12 March 2025

Preserve the sale on **12 March 2025**, price **SAR 45,000,000**, independent FMV **SAR 45,000,000**, unrelated buyer and buyer's contractual reimbursement of the seller's tax cost. This event precedes the new law. Under the former ordinary RETT treatment, the verified equal value gives **SAR 2,250,000**. The original **11 March 2025** declaration/payment plan is an early-compliance scenario, not a general legal requirement to file the day before every notary appointment. Use the former instrument for any actual correction or sanction; do not apply the new penalty table retroactively. [Historical official guide, ordinary rate/base/registration sections](https://zatca.gov.sa/en/HelpCenter/guidelines/Documents/Simplified%20guide%20to%20the%20real%20estate%20transaction%20tax.pdf).

```oa-check
name: Historical Olaya office sale equal supported consideration and value
inputs:
  agreed_price: 45000000
  independently_supported_value: 45000000
steps:
  - historical_base: max(agreed_price, independently_supported_value)
  - historical_rett: historical_base * 0.05
expected:
  historical_base: 45000000
  historical_rett: 2250000
```

### B. Jeddah second villa: 10 July 2025

Preserve the Saudi seller who already owned two prior properties, Saudi buyer who is already a homeowner, **10 July 2025** and price **SAR 3,200,000**. The seller's prior ownership is not the eligibility test for a different buyer's first-home support; here the stated buyer also fails the first-home fact. No independent appraisal was obtained in the original case. The **SAR 160,000** amount is conditional on evidence establishing that price within fair-market-value bounds and no other relief. Do not label SAR 3,200,000 an independently verified FMV merely because it equals the contract price. If supportable market evidence instead establishes the prescribed base at **SAR 3,500,000**, the sensitivity is **SAR 175,000**; the extra value is an explicit alternative assumption, not a fact about this villa. [Law arts. 2, 7-8](https://zatca.gov.sa/ar/RulesRegulations/Taxes/Documents/RETTrgulations.pdf).

```oa-check
name: Jeddah villa evidence-dependent fair-value base
inputs:
  price_within_supported_market_bounds: 3200000
  alternative_statutorily_established_base: 3500000
steps:
  - rett_if_price_supported: price_within_supported_market_bounds * 0.05
  - rett_if_alternative_base_established: alternative_statutorily_established_base * 0.05
  - difference: rett_if_alternative_base_established - rett_if_price_supported
expected:
  rett_if_price_supported: 160000
  rett_if_alternative_base_established: 175000
  difference: 15000
```

### C. Mr Al-Otaibi: inheritance in 2024, onward sale in September 2025

Preserve **Mr Al-Otaibi**, his father's Dammam plot, inheritance on **5 January 2024**, Sharia-court inheritance certificate and sale to an unrelated buyer on **20 September 2025** for **SAR 800,000**. The inheritance event follows the historical estate-distribution relief, with evidence/registration; it does not carry an exemption into the later sale. For the later sale, assuming that SAR 800,000 is a supported statutory value and no independent relief applies, RETT is **SAR 40,000**. No cost-basis uplift or inherited acquisition price is deducted from a transaction-tax base. [Historical guide, inheritance section](https://zatca.gov.sa/en/HelpCenter/guidelines/Documents/Simplified%20guide%20to%20the%20real%20estate%20transaction%20tax.pdf); [current law arts. 2-3](https://zatca.gov.sa/ar/RulesRegulations/Taxes/Documents/RETTrgulations.pdf).

```oa-check
name: Mr Al-Otaibi separately taxable onward sale
inputs:
  supported_onward_sale_base: 800000
steps:
  - onward_sale_rett: supported_onward_sale_base * 0.05
expected:
  onward_sale_rett: 40000
```

### D. Original first-home villa: distinguish tax from who pays it

For the original **SAR 1,800,000** residential villa, assume a qualifying Saudi citizen's first home, supported statutory value and valid programme certificate covering the applicable maximum. Full RETT is **SAR 90,000**. The state-borne portion is **SAR 50,000**, leaving **SAR 40,000** to settle through the actual transaction process. Without established support, show the **SAR 90,000** liability and an unresolved application, not an automatic exemption/refund. The original SAR 40,000 out-of-pocket arithmetic is retained, while its “first million exempt” explanation is corrected. Commercial allocation between buyer and seller does not change law art. 7. [Detailed guideline §1.1](https://zatca.gov.sa/ar/HelpCenter/guidelines/Documents/Detailed-Guideline-for-RETT-In-accordance-with-provision-of-RETT-Law-and-its-Implementing-Regulations.PDF); [law arts. 2, 7](https://zatca.gov.sa/ar/RulesRegulations/Taxes/Documents/RETTrgulations.pdf).

```oa-check
name: First-home state-bearing certificate versus no established support
inputs:
  statutory_property_value: 1800000
  eligible_certificate_price_limit: 1000000
steps:
  - full_rett: statutory_property_value * 0.05
  - state_borne_if_eligible: min(statutory_property_value, eligible_certificate_price_limit) * 0.05
  - balance_if_eligible: full_rett - state_borne_if_eligible
  - liability_if_support_not_established: full_rett
expected:
  full_rett: 90000
  state_borne_if_eligible: 50000
  balance_if_eligible: 40000
  liability_if_support_not_established: 90000
```

## When to refuse or refer

Do not finalise without the transaction identity/date, supported value and material exemption/support evidence. Preserve the original holds for unsupported valuation, foreign-situs property, historical regimes, sukuk/leaseback, group restructuring, mixed-use first-home claims, unknown ownership history and optional long-right extensions. Seek the actual documents and applicable specialist analysis rather than defaulting to tax or relief.

For gift/estate cases obtain the actual civil-status and inheritance/will documents. For finance/group cases obtain the contracts, entire ownership chain, audited statements, holding-period events and any ruling relied on. For indirect transactions obtain property/total-asset valuations, the look-back and linked transactions. An unavailable portal or imminent notary appointment is not permission to fabricate a clearance reference; establish the statutory payment exception or reschedule documentation through the proper process.

Refer live assessment/penalty objections, sham-transaction allegations, private rulings, non-resident tax/ownership rights and unresolved legal-term/classification questions. Do not advise a buyer to withhold RETT merely because the seller is non-resident, treat every share transfer as full property value or grant a family exemption without the defined relationship.

## Filing and payment

1. Use the ZATCA **RETT transaction-registration portal** as the transferor or authorised representative. Enter both parties, the property/title/contract reference, description, consideration and any exemption, with supporting documents. Registration is ordinarily **at or before the disposition date**, including exempt transactions; public-offering/listed-securities and the stated property-company-share registration rules have specific exceptions. [IR art. 11(a)](https://zatca.gov.sa/ar/RulesRegulations/Taxes/Documents/New%20Rett%20Regulation.pdf).
2. Determine the actual statutory due date from the table, pay to the authority's designated account/channel using the transaction reference and retain the acknowledgement. A taxable registration/payment produces its confirmation; an exempt registration has a registration confirmation rather than an invented paid amount. The law prevents documentation without the required tax settlement, subject to its specific deferred-payment exceptions. “Always the day before” is not the rule. [Law arts. 5, 19(2)](https://zatca.gov.sa/ar/RulesRegulations/Taxes/Documents/RETTrgulations.pdf); [IR arts. 5, 7, 11(a)](https://zatca.gov.sa/ar/RulesRegulations/Taxes/Documents/New%20Rett%20Regulation.pdf).
3. Request correction through the portal **within 30 days of learning the registered data is incorrect or a condition-breaching event occurs**, applying the proper additional-tax due date. A reduced amount requires the actual refund procedure, not a unilateral offset. [IR art. 11(b)](https://zatca.gov.sa/ar/RulesRegulations/Taxes/Documents/New%20Rett%20Regulation.pdf).
4. Keep deeds/agreements, payment records, valuation evidence, exemption/support documents and related books for **5 years from the disposition**, subject to longer requirements under another law or an unresolved matter. The records must be retained/accessible in the Kingdom as prescribed, with security controls. The old generic 6-year period is not the current RETT rule. [IR art. 11(f)](https://zatca.gov.sa/ar/RulesRegulations/Taxes/Documents/New%20Rett%20Regulation.pdf).

### Refunds, assessment and penalties under the current law

Overpaid/erroneous tax, an uncompleted transaction and a qualifying cancelled transaction have the specific law art. 9/IR art. 9 routes. For a cancellation, keep the full refund of consideration, unchanged description and documented return within the prescribed **90 days**. The ordinary claim limit is **12 months** from the relevant statutory payment date, with the specified **60-day** final-decision/settlement alternative. Read the actual decision, claim timing and disputed-amount conditions; a pending application is not proof that cash will be refunded. [Law arts. 3(a)(21), 9](https://zatca.gov.sa/ar/RulesRegulations/Taxes/Documents/RETTrgulations.pdf); [IR art. 9](https://zatca.gov.sa/ar/RulesRegulations/Taxes/Documents/New%20Rett%20Regulation.pdf).

ZATCA's valuation/assessment powers have their specified **3-year** periods from the transaction or discovery of an undocumented/undeclared disposition; exemption holding-period breaches are not extinguished by simply applying that ordinary period. [Law art. 8](https://zatca.gov.sa/ar/RulesRegulations/Taxes/Documents/RETTrgulations.pdf); [IR art. 8](https://zatca.gov.sa/ar/RulesRegulations/Taxes/Documents/New%20Rett%20Regulation.pdf).

| Current breach/procedure | Rule and conditions | Official source |
|---|---|---|
| Late payment | 2% of unpaid tax per month or part month, capped at 50% for this limb, starting after the statutory payment period | Law art. 15(2), [law](https://zatca.gov.sa/ar/RulesRegulations/Taxes/Documents/RETTrgulations.pdf) |
| Authority adjustment with unpaid tax | Additional 1% per month or part month, beginning after 30 days from adjustment notice; apply the actual statutory interaction, not an assumed single generic cap | Law art. 15(2), [law](https://zatca.gov.sa/ar/RulesRegulations/Taxes/Documents/RETTrgulations.pdf) |

| Other covered breach | Statutory maximum of the greater of tax due and SAR 50,000, subject to the article's exclusions and classification; not an automatic amount for every late form | Law art. 15(3), [law](https://zatca.gov.sa/ar/RulesRegulations/Taxes/Documents/RETTrgulations.pdf) |
| Complaint to ZATCA against its decision | 60 days from notification; authority decision period 90 days; subsequent competent-judicial challenge within the stated 30-day window | Law art. 17; IR art. 13, [law](https://zatca.gov.sa/ar/RulesRegulations/Taxes/Documents/RETTrgulations.pdf) |

The old blanket **5%-per-month** table is not the current-law penalty. Do not describe these sanctions as contractual interest or claim an automatic nullification of title for every registration fault. Actual sanctions require the relevant facts, classified breach and decision. Tax principal is not waived by the sanction. [Law arts. 14-17](https://zatca.gov.sa/ar/RulesRegulations/Taxes/Documents/RETTrgulations.pdf).

The **1 July-31 December 2026** penalty initiative is separately conditional on registration, required returns and principal settlement or an approved adhered-to plan, with its exclusion of returns due **after 30 June 2026**, evasion, previously paid fines and specified VAT fines. Obtain the actual eligibility/account treatment; do not cancel RETT principal or assume a new 2026 transaction's later default qualifies. [Initiative guideline pp. 3-9](https://zatca.gov.sa/en/HelpCenter/guidelines/Documents/Cancellation-of-Fines-Exemption%20of-Financial-Penalties.pdf).

## Completion checklist

- The actual event/date and applicable historical/current instrument are identified.
- Supported consideration/market bounds, present value or indirect-property basis is used; no gains-only netting or fabricated valuation.
- Exemption and first-home state-bearing are distinct, documented and monitored for continuing conditions.
- Transferor responsibility, any proven joint liability, registration/payment and notarial requirements are applied to the actual route.
- Corrected registrations, records, refunds, notices and current penalty provisions remain available for professional review.
