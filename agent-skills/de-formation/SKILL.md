# Delaware entity formation: LLC or corporation, tax year 2026

> **General reference only.** This skill is general tax/accounting reference material for AI-assisted workflows. It has not been reviewed for any specific person's facts, documents, elections, deadlines, residency, filing status, or local procedures. Do not rely on it to file, pay, amend, or take a tax position without review by a qualified professional in the relevant jurisdiction.

**Answer first.** Form the entity with the Delaware Division of Corporations, maintain a Delaware registered agent, then complete internal records, the EIN and the federal/operating-state handoffs. A Delaware registration does not settle tax or foreign qualification in the state where the business operates. The official formation schedule, revised **1 August 2026**, charges **$110** for a domestic LLC and starts at **$109** for a one-page corporation certificate, with the corporation amount varying by stock. [Division fee schedule, pp. 1 and 3](https://corpfiles.delaware.gov/Fee_Schedule/AugustFee2026.pdf).

Written by Codex. Not reviewed by an accountant. Sources below identify the sections actually read on 14 November 2026. Fees here apply to current filings from 1 August 2026; earlier transactions need the schedule then in force. No earlier fee schedule is represented as checked.

## Ask the client first

1. **Operations and purpose:** where will the business operate, employ staff and own property; will it seek equity investors, issue options, or pursue a public benefit?
2. **Owners and classification:** owners' tax residence and US tax status, entity owners, number of LLC members, and any existing federal election. Citizenship alone does not decide S eligibility.
3. **Stock:** each class's authorized shares, par value, issued shares, issue price, valuation, vesting and exact transfer date. Obtain expected assets for the annual-tax handoff.
4. **Agent and name:** proposed compliant name, Delaware agent's consent and physical street address, and any reservation already made.
5. **Structure and timing:** LLC, corporation, PBC, protected/registered series or trust; planned effective date, service speed and documents/certificates required by the bank.
6. **Existing obligations:** other Delaware entities, unpaid taxes, formation contributions/distributions, previous filings and proof of any founder-stock election.

## The method, step by step

### 1. Choose, check and prepare ([Division steps 1-4](https://corp.delaware.gov/howtoform/))

Record why the chosen legal form fits the facts. An investor's requirement for stock may justify a corporation; an LLC does not automatically reduce tax and Delaware does not remove operating-state costs. For a business operating only in another state, compare that state's formation and recurring charges with the additional Delaware charges (case 4).

Use the Division's **Check Entity Name Availability** service and appoint the agent before filing. Reservation is optional. If using a separate registered agent, inform that agent of the intended formation. If the business has a physical Delaware location it may act as its own agent, subject to the statutory agent requirements. A founder without a qualifying Delaware office needs another qualifying agent; the statute does not require that it be a commercial service. [Division, steps 2-3](https://corp.delaware.gov/howtoform/); [6 Del. C. §18-104](https://delcode.delaware.gov/title6/c018/sc01/index.html).

### 2. Complete the certificate fields ([LLC §18-201](https://delcode.delaware.gov/title6/c018/sc02/index.html); [corporation §102](https://delcode.delaware.gov/title8/c001/sc01/index.html))

**LLC (6 Del. C. ch. 18).** Its name must include "Limited Liability Company", "L.L.C." or "LLC" under §18-102.  The certificate of formation sets out: "(1) The name of the limited liability company; (2) The address of the registered office and the name and address of the registered agent for service of process ... ; and (3) Any other matters the members determine to include therein." The LLC exists from filing, or from a later date or time the certificate states. The LLC agreement "shall be entered into or otherwise existing either before, after or at the time of the filing". It is not filed with the state. [§ 18-201](https://delcode.delaware.gov/title6/c018/sc02/index.html); [§ 18-102 name](https://delcode.delaware.gov/title6/c018/sc01/index.html)

**Registered agent (LLC).** The agent may be the LLC itself, an individual resident in Delaware, or a domestic or qualified foreign entity. Its business office must be identical to the Delaware registered office. [§ 18-104](https://delcode.delaware.gov/title6/c018/sc01/index.html). The Division says every business entity must have a registered agent with "a physical street address in Delaware". It adds: "If the business is physically located in Delaware, then the business may act as its own registered agent." A founder without a qualifying Delaware office must appoint another qualifying agent. [How to form](https://corp.delaware.gov/howtoform/)

**Corporation (8 Del. C. ch. 1).** Any person may incorporate, "without regard to such person's or entity's residence, domicile or state of incorporation". The certificate of incorporation must state all of these:

- the name
- the registered office and agent
- the business purpose ("any lawful act or activity" is permitted)
- the authorized stock (classes, number of shares and par value)
- the incorporator's name and mailing address
- if the incorporator's powers terminate on filing, the initial directors' names and mailing addresses (§102(a)(6)); counsel must also supply class rights if multiple classes are authorized

The name must contain one of these words: "association," "company," "corporation," "club," "foundation," "fund," "incorporated," "institute," "society," "union," "syndicate," or "limited". Abbreviations of them are allowed. [§§ 101-102](https://delcode.delaware.gov/title8/c001/sc01/index.html)

**Public benefit corporation.** In its certificate of incorporation, a PBC must identify one or more specific public benefits within its purpose clause, and must "State within its heading that it is a public benefit corporation". It must give stockholders a statement on its promotion of those benefits "no less than biennially". A third-party standard or certification is required only if the certificate or bylaws say so. Formation fees are the same as for an ordinary corporation. [§§ 362, 366](https://delcode.delaware.gov/title8/c001/sc15/index.html)

#### Why a corporation's fee varies with stock ([8 Del. C. §391(a)(1)](https://delcode.delaware.gov/title8/c001/sc18/index.html))

The stock part of the corporation filing fee is set by share count. For par-value stock it is $0.02 per share for the first 20,000 shares, $0.01 per share up to 200,000 shares, and 2/5 of $0.01 per share above that. For par-value stock, "each $100 unit of the authorized capital stock shall be counted as 1 assessable share". No-par stock uses actual shares and a different schedule: $0.01 for each of the first 20,000 shares, $0.005 for each share above 20,000 through 2,000,000, then $0.004 for each share above 2,000,000. The stock fee is never less than $15. [§ 391](https://delcode.delaware.gov/title8/c001/sc18/index.html)

Take the common startup structure of 10,000,000 authorized shares at $0.0001 par. Authorized capital is $1,000, which counts as 10 assessable shares. The stock fee is therefore the $15 minimum, and the filing costs the scheduled $109. The same share count with **no par value** is charged per share and costs far more. Always run the Division's par or no-par filing-fee calculator before filing. [Fee calculators](https://corp.delaware.gov/fee/)

### 4. Submit and retain the result ([Division steps 4-7](https://corp.delaware.gov/howtoform/))

Use **Get Corporate Forms** from the Division's formation page. For the LLC Certificate of Formation, complete the name, agent and registered-office fields and have an authorized person execute it. For the stock Certificate of Incorporation, complete the name, Delaware office/agent, purpose, share classes/count/par and incorporator fields; include initial directors where required above. A PBC adds its statutory purpose and heading. These are named certificate fields, not tax-return line numbers.

Upload the executed certificate through the Division's **Document Filing and Certificate Request Service**, or mail it with the required cover sheet (name/entity name, return address and phone number). Pay the filing and chosen expedite fees on submission. If the bank needs good standing, request it in the comment section of the Document Filing Sheet and add its separate charge. Retain the submission/payment confirmation, stamped certificate and effective date in the formation file. The Division returns requests by regular mail unless a courier account is supplied; it does not return them by fax or email. The service is reached from [How to form, steps 4-7](https://corp.delaware.gov/howtoform/).

### 5. Organize and obtain the EIN ([§108-109](https://delcode.delaware.gov/title8/c001/sc01/index.html); [IRS EIN](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/apply-for-an-employer-identification-number-ein-online))

Complete the LLC agreement (not filed with the Division), or the corporation's incorporator/director actions, bylaws, officer appointments and stock issuance records with counsel. Section 108 permits unanimous written/electronic organizational consent unless restricted by the certificate. Do not confuse authorized shares with stock actually transferred to a founder.

Apply for the EIN **after** state formation, directly with the IRS free of charge. Online eligibility requires a US/territory principal place of business and the responsible party's SSN or ITIN. If the principal place of business is outside the US, use the IRS phone, fax or mail route. Non-US citizenship alone does not establish that online filing is unavailable. Print and retain the EIN confirmation. [IRS EIN, Who can use this tool and When to get an EIN](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/apply-for-an-employer-identification-number-ein-online).

For the founder-stock handoff, the election accelerates compensation income to transfer-date value less price instead of waiting until the stock is transferable or no longer subject to substantial forfeiture risk. An election on fully vested stock does not defer that income. The election is not automatically suitable: if stock later is forfeited, the compensation previously included is not deductible; the regulation separately permits a loss for unrecovered purchase price. The option/section 421 exceptions and the detailed computation belong in the federal equity skill. [26 U.S.C. §83(a),(b),(e)](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCODE-2024-title26/html/USCODE-2024-title26-subtitleA-chap1-subchapB-partII-sec83.htm); [Treas. Reg. §1.83-2(a), official 2025 codification](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CFR-2025-title26-vol2/pdf/CFR-2025-title26-vol2-sec1-83-2.pdf). The statutory election is generally irrevocable without IRS consent. Form 15620 explains the weekend/holiday postmark rule and permits a compliant statement instead of the form; the regulation permits filing before transfer. Do not improvise late relief.

### 7. Register operations and schedule recurring compliance ([Division](https://corp.delaware.gov/howtoform/))

Before commencing activities in another state, test that state's foreign-qualification rules and register when required; register for its payroll and sales taxes when applicable. The operating state sets the test, fees, annual reports and consequences. Do not infer a universal test from Delaware. If operating or hiring in Delaware, use the State's **One Stop Business Registration and Licensing System**, linked by the Division. Obtain any certificate the bank or operating state requires.

Load **`de-franchise-tax-and-llc`** and create the annual compliance calendar before closing the formation file. It is the single owner of the recurring rates and computations: annual corporation report/tax, both tax methods and their minimums/maximums, estimates, Large Corporate Filer rules, LLC/registered-series annual tax, no proration, late penalties/interest, cancellation and revival. Report both issued shares and gross assets for the assumed-par method; a high authorized share count can otherwise produce a large assessment. The annual report also asks for nature of business. [Division calculation instructions](https://corp.delaware.gov/frtaxcalc/); [annual-report instructions](https://corp.delaware.gov/paytaxes/).

Keep the prior-year boundary explicit: [85 Del. Laws c.273 §§23 and 45](https://delcode.delaware.gov/sessionlaws/ga153/chp273.shtml) raised the LLC and registered-series annual taxes effective **1 January 2026**. The Division's general formation page still carries the old LLC figure, while its dedicated tax page and current statute carry the new amount. Use the recurring-tax skill for the 2026 charge; do not treat the stale general page as controlling. The assessed amount for a delinquent **2025** balance remains unresolved here and in that skill: obtain the Division's entity statement before calculating a payoff. Fee-schedule changes took effect **1 August 2026**, separately from the annual-tax effective date. [Dedicated instructions](https://corp.delaware.gov/alt-entitytaxinstructions/); [LLC Act §18-1107](https://delcode.delaware.gov/title6/c018/sc11/index.html).

## Worked cases

The case facts are invented. Recurring-tax amounts below illustrate the rules owned by `de-franchise-tax-and-llc`; load it before computing an actual bill.

**Case 1: venture-track corporation, "startup standard" shares.** Assume the corporation was formed on 1 January 2026, operated throughout the year and had no capital amendments. Two founders authorize 10,000,000 authorized shares at $0.0001 par and issue 8,000,000 to themselves. At year-end, gross assets on Form 1120 Schedule L are $100,000. [How to calculate](https://corp.delaware.gov/frtaxcalc/)

- **Separate current filing illustration:** an identically capitalized new corporation filing on or after 1 August 2026 has the $15 minimum stock component and the scheduled $109 filing fee, plus $50 for 24-hour service, a total of $159. This is not the 1 January corporation's historical formation fee. [§ 391](https://delcode.delaware.gov/title8/c001/sc18/index.html); [Fee schedule](https://corpfiles.delaware.gov/Fee_Schedule/AugustFee2026.pdf)
- Authorized Shares method: $250 + 999 × $85 = $85,165. [How to calculate](https://corp.delaware.gov/frtaxcalc/)
- Assumed Par Value Capital method: assumed par $100,000 ÷ 8,000,000 = $0.0125. That is above the $0.0001 par, so capital = $0.0125 × 10,000,000 = $125,000. Tax = $125,000 ÷ $1,000,000 × $400 = $50, raised to the $400 minimum. [How to calculate](https://corp.delaware.gov/frtaxcalc/)
- Due by 1 March: the $400 tax plus the $50 annual-report fee = $450. Report the issued shares and gross assets so the lower method applies. [Division](https://corp.delaware.gov/paytaxes/)
- The founders pay a price equal to the stock's value at transfer, so the election brings nothing into income. Each founder should assess the election with the federal equity skill and meet its transfer-date deadline if electing. If the price paid were below value, the excess would be taxed at once and lost on forfeiture. Decide that case on its facts.

**Case 2: dormant LLC.** A client formed a Delaware LLC in 2024 and forgot its annual calendar. Assume all pre-2026 balances have been cleared; the following is the 2026 charge only, not a payoff for all years. The 2026-year tax of $400 is due 1 June 2027. If it is paid on 20 June 2027, the $200 penalty is added. Interest of 1.5% for the one month or part of a month is charged on the $600 of tax and penalty, which is $9. The total for that charge is $609; obtain the Division statement for the actual account payoff. If no payment is made for 3 years, the certificate is cancelled. Pay or formally cancel every forgotten LLC. Cancelling also requires the annual taxes to be paid. [§§ 18-1107, 18-1108](https://delcode.delaware.gov/title6/c018/sc11/index.html); [Division](https://corp.delaware.gov/alt-entitytaxinstructions/)

**Case 3: non-US founder, single-member Delaware LLC, no US office.** Assume no corporate tax election: default classification is disregarded. Assume its principal place of business is outside the US: the EIN cannot be obtained online, so apply by phone, fax or mail. Foreign ownership alone would not decide online eligibility. For each year in which it has a reportable transaction with its owner (the formation year, and any year with contributions, distributions or payments), the LLC files a pro forma Form 1120 with Form 5472 by the Form 1120 due date. It must fax or mail them; it cannot e-file. The penalty and any extension are handled in `us-form-5472-foreign-owned-us`. The Delaware tax of $400 is due each 1 June. The LLC is exempt from BOI because it was formed in the US. The founder's home-country tax is out of scope; refer it. [Form 5472 instructions](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i5472); [IRS EIN](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/apply-for-an-employer-identification-number-ein-online); [FinCEN BOI](https://www.fincen.gov/boi)

**Case 4: sole trader with no investors, operating in one other state, decision both ways.** A Delaware LLC adds the $400 Delaware tax and a Delaware registered agent. Assume its operations trigger home-state foreign qualification; it must register there and meet that state's charges. Unless there is a concrete reason for Delaware law (investors, a multi-state group, or a lender or counterparty requirement), compare this with forming in the home state, and record the reason for the choice. [Division](https://corp.delaware.gov/alt-entitytaxinstructions/)


For case 4, suppose that state's independently obtained formation quote is $100, and foreign qualification would cost $150. Assume the client has no Delaware office, a qualifying Delaware agent quotes $100 a year, and the home state's recurring cost is identical under either route. These are illustrative client quotes, not claimed state rates. Using the formation fee above and the annual charge owned by `de-franchise-tax-and-llc`, the Delaware route costs $110 + $150 + $100 + $400 = $760 through its first annual tax, versus $100 at home, a $660 difference. Choose home formation if the Delaware legal benefit is absent; choose Delaware only if the documented benefit is worth that additional cost and the legal form fits the facts. This compares costs, not a prediction of income-tax savings.

## When to refuse or refer

- The §83(b) 30-day window has passed, or the election cannot be proved to have been filed. Refer to a tax attorney before any return is filed. Do not re-date documents or file a "late" election as if it were timely.
- The client is converting an existing entity (for example LLC to corporation, or a "flip" of a foreign company). This can create taxable events. Refer for a transaction-specific tax analysis.
- Securities offerings, SAFEs, preferred stock terms, stock option plans or drafting of the LLC agreement or bylaws: refer to a corporate attorney.
- Non-US owners: home-country tax, treaty questions and US estate-tax exposure need a cross-border specialist.
- Statutory trusts, registered or protected series used as the liability shield for real estate, and insurance or banking entities: refer to a specialist. The series firewall depends on strict records and on how other states treat series.
- Any figure that must be exact for a large corporation's franchise tax (Large Corporate Filer status, amended share structures, prorated years): use `de-franchise-tax-and-llc` and the Division's calculator.
- A dispute between owners: refer to litigation counsel.
