# Free Zones SEZ Matrix v0.2

> **General reference only.** This skill is general tax/accounting reference material for AI-assisted workflows. It has not been reviewed for any specific person's facts, documents, elections, deadlines, residency, filing status, or local procedures. Do not rely on it to file, pay, amend, or take a tax position without review by a qualified professional in the relevant jurisdiction.

## What this Guide covers

Load `cross-border-workflow-base` first. This comparison and research method preserves the original shortlist of free zones, special economic zones, free trade zones and financial centres, using selected sources read **16 November 2026**. A zone, offshore company form or licence is not itself a tax exemption. QFC is in Qatar, not the UAE.

Use the dated rules and individual limitations below for 2026, including the UAE replacements for MD 139/2023/265/2023, Saudi’s current distinction between geographic and cloud SEZs, Hainan/Qianhai extensions through 2027, India’s 2026 IFSC changes, and the current domestic minimum-tax overlays. A headline below 15% does not by itself determine a Pillar Two liability. [uae-229](<https://tax.gov.ae/datafolder/Files/Pdf/2025/MD-No-229.pdf>); [sa-sez-current](<https://zatca.gov.sa/en/HelpCenter/guidelines/Documents/SEZ-Tax%26Customs-Procedures.pdf>); [hainan-extension](<https://policy.mofcom.gov.cn/claw/clawContent.shtml?id=103253>); [qianhai-extension](<https://www.tid.gov.hk/en/tradecircular/files/2026/ci1712026a.pdf>); [india-fa2026](<https://egazette.gov.in/WriteReadData/2026/271439.pdf>); [oecd-global](<https://www.oecd.org/en/topics/global-minimum-tax.html>).

**The reviewer is the customer of the output.** Record commercial fit, activity permissions, exit costs and every unresolved rule before an incorporation, restructuring or onshore-versus-zone election. Obtain credentialed local tax counsel’s review before execution; a source-cited research draft is not that sign-off.

## Scope

Cover CIT, withholding and indirect-tax headlines; qualifying activities; premises, employees and expenditure; de minimis limits; onshore/mainland and Indian DTA flows; OECD BEPS Action 5/FHTP status; and Pillar Two interaction. Track income classification separately from legal permission to trade.

Outside scope: detailed customs/free-circulation rates and procedures, employment and immigration, real-estate leasing economics, sector licences such as DFSA/ADGM FSRA/MAS, and designated-zone VAT mechanics. Route those to the relevant country Guides and actual regulator/customs instruments; this matrix gives only a customs/VAT summary.

## Ask the client first

- Exact entity, country/zone, licence and proposed commencement or tax period.
- Every revenue stream, counterparty type and actual use/destination of supplies.
- Premises, CIGA, personnel, outsourcing, expenditure and actual management.
- Accounting-period revenue, adjusted qualifying/non-qualifying split and domestic sales.
- Group ownership/consolidated revenue for the relevant prior years and minimum-tax footprint.
- Intended distributions, withholding/treaty route, IP ownership and exit/election constraints.

## The method, step by step

1. Identify the jurisdiction, exact zone and legal entity, licence, accounting period, revenue streams, counterparties and destination of goods/services. Confirm the enacted version and commencement before selecting any rate. Treat every **Not yet confirmed** item as an unusable source request, not an assumed favourable outcome.

2. Classify each stream against the actual qualifying/excluded list and apply substance, beneficial-recipient, permanent-establishment, property/IP and anti-abuse rules. Assess domestic sales separately from the tax de minimis calculation. Build a forecast with an explicit denominator and failure consequences.

3. Test withholding and treaty entitlement separately, then group revenue, covered/excluded entities, jurisdictional effective tax and any domestic/parent minimum-tax rules. Document remaining conditions and local reviewer questions rather than turning an authority overview into entity-specific clearance.

## UAE free zones

Corporate tax applies to tax periods beginning on or after **1 June 2023**; a calendar-year entity’s first such period may therefore be 2024. The law has taxable/exempt-person conditions; “all UAE entities” is too broad. A QFZP has **0% on Qualifying Income** and **9% on its taxable non-qualifying income** under Article 3(2), subject separately to applicable top-up tax. This is not a 0% rate automatically attached to any zone licence. [uae-2026](<https://mof.gov.ae/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Federal-Decree-Law-No.-47-of-2022-and-its-amendments-en-v13.1.26.pdf>).

### QFZP qualification and income

Article **18**, not Article 7, requires adequate substance, Qualifying Income, no Article 19 election into the ordinary regime, arm’s-length/transfer-pricing compliance and prescribed additional conditions. MD **229/2025** requires de minimis compliance and audited financial statements under its cited MD 84/2025. Article 34 governs arm’s-length conditions; Article 55’s master/local file duties depend on prescribed conditions, not every company automatically. [uae-2026](<https://mof.gov.ae/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Federal-Decree-Law-No.-47-of-2022-and-its-amendments-en-v13.1.26.pdf>); [uae-229](<https://tax.gov.ae/datafolder/Files/Pdf/2025/MD-No-229.pdf>).

Cabinet Decision **100/2023** replaced CD 55/2023; MD 229/2025 repeals MD 265/2023. Their specified application is from **1 June 2023**. Do not apply the old MD 139/2023 list as the current list. These Ministry-hosted English copies/consolidation are translation aids; the January 2026 law and MD 229 expressly warn that the English is not an official translation. The Arabic originals were not read. MD 336/2025 specifies VARA as a competent authority for the stated fund/wealth-investment-management purposes, not all virtual-asset businesses. [uae-100](<https://mof.gov.ae/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/Cabinet-Decision-No.-100-of-2023-on-Determining-Qualifying-Income-for-the-Qualifying-Free-Zone-Person.pdf>); [uae-229](<https://tax.gov.ae/datafolder/Files/Pdf/2025/MD-No-229.pdf>); [uae-336](<https://mof.gov.ae/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Ministerial-Decision-No.-336-of-2025-on-the-Specification-of-a-Competent-Authority-for-the-Purposes-of-Ministerial-Decision-No.-229-of-2025_-En.pdf>).

### Substance, de minimis and mainland sales

CD 100 Article 8 requires CIGA in the required **Free Zone or Designated Zone**, with adequate assets, qualified full-time employees and operating expenditure for each activity. Outsourcing normally stays in the required zone under adequate supervision; qualifying IP CIGA can instead be outsourced to a UAE person or an unrelated foreign person, with supervision. “Some premises anywhere in the UAE” is insufficient; CIGA means significant value-driving functions, not mainly support work. [uae-100](<https://mof.gov.ae/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/Cabinet-Decision-No.-100-of-2023-on-Determining-Qualifying-Income-for-the-Qualifying-Free-Zone-Person.pdf>).

The MD 229 Article 3 cap is the **lower of 5% of total revenue and AED 5,000,000**, applied to the tax period. CD 100 Article 4 defines non-qualifying revenue and removes the specified PE/property/non-qualifying-IP revenue from **both** numerator and denominator. Do not use gross accounting sales without those adjustments. [uae-100](<https://mof.gov.ae/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/Cabinet-Decision-No.-100-of-2023-on-Determining-Qualifying-Income-for-the-Qualifying-Free-Zone-Person.pdf>); [uae-229](<https://tax.gov.ae/datafolder/Files/Pdf/2025/MD-No-229.pdf>).

Tax classification does not establish legal permission for mainland trading. The former “only through a mainland distributor or within de minimis” rule was too broad: qualifying non-Free Zone activities can use Article 3(1)(b), while distribution has its own conditions. Obtain the licence/customs/sale-permission rule separately. A de minimis breach is not merely a 9% charge on the excess/relevant revenue: MD 229 Article 5 normally ends QFZP status from the beginning of the failing period and for **4 subsequent tax periods**, with Article 18’s ministerial conditions/circumstances still relevant. [uae-100](<https://mof.gov.ae/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/Cabinet-Decision-No.-100-of-2023-on-Determining-Qualifying-Income-for-the-Qualifying-Free-Zone-Person.pdf>); [uae-229](<https://tax.gov.ae/datafolder/Files/Pdf/2025/MD-No-229.pdf>); [uae-2026](<https://mof.gov.ae/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Federal-Decree-Law-No.-47-of-2022-and-its-amendments-en-v13.1.26.pdf>).

## QFC is in Qatar

The official English QFC Tax Regulations, **January 2026 version 7**, Article 9 charge **10%** on local-source taxable profits. Article 10 contains exceptions, including the new Article 10(1A) treatment for specified services for use outside Qatar by a QFC Entity that is not an Authorised Firm. Rule 1A requires **all** its conditions: audited accounts, at least **30%** income attributable to activities in Qatar, at least **3 full-time employees**, and the Tax Department’s anti-avoidance satisfaction. This is not an automatic foreign-income exemption or a 10% charge on all QFC income. [qfc-tax2026](<https://qfcra-en.thomsonreuters.com/sites/default/files/net_file_store/QFCRA_7103_VER7.pdf>); [qfc-rules2026](<https://qfcra-en.thomsonreuters.com/sites/default/files/net_file_store/QFCRA_8454_VER4.pdf>).

For **QFC is in Qatar**, resolve the individual source request under Sources before acting. [qfc-tax2026](<https://qfcra-en.thomsonreuters.com/sites/default/files/net_file_store/QFCRA_7103_VER7.pdf>).

## Pillar Two interaction

A below-**15%** headline is not a complete GloBE test. Establish the MNE group’s consolidated revenue, normally **EUR 750 million in at least 2 of the prior 4 fiscal years**, covered/excluded entities and domestic law; compute jurisdictional adjusted covered taxes/income with applicable exclusions, substance-based income exclusion, elections and safe harbours. Consider domestic minimum tax first and foreign IIR/UTPR in their legal order. A tax benefit is not necessarily “clawed back in full”. Route detailed computation to `pillar-two-globe-minimum-tax.md` and current national law. [uae-142](<https://tax.gov.ae/Datafolder/Files/Legislation/Cabinet-Decision-No-142-of-2024-on-Top-up-Tax-on-MNEs.pdf>); [oecd-global](<https://www.oecd.org/en/topics/global-minimum-tax.html>).

These are implementation/eligibility questions, not a universal exemption for a zone or for every group in a named country. Only the read introductory package paragraphs are used here; full safe-harbour/calculation clearance is not supplied. [oecd-sbs2026](<https://www.oecd.org/content/dam/oecd/en/topics/policy-sub-issues/global-minimum-tax/side-by-side-package.pdf>).

## Worked cases

**Case 1: UAE percentage limit binds.** Supplied full-period adjusted total revenue is **AED 70,000,000**, already including **AED 3,400,000** non-qualifying revenue. Assume the CD 100 exclusions are absent, all other QFZP conditions are met and no special ministerial treatment applies. **5% × 70,000,000 = 3,500,000**, below the AED 5,000,000 ceiling; headroom is **AED 100,000**. This only satisfies this revenue test, not every QFZP condition. [uae-100](<https://mof.gov.ae/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/Cabinet-Decision-No.-100-of-2023-on-Determining-Qualifying-Income-for-the-Qualifying-Free-Zone-Person.pdf>); [uae-229](<https://tax.gov.ae/datafolder/Files/Pdf/2025/MD-No-229.pdf>).

```oa-check
name: UAE percentage cap binds
inputs:
  adjusted_total: 70000000
  nonqualifying: 3400000
steps:
  - percentage_cap: adjusted_total * 0.05
  - applicable_cap: min(percentage_cap, 5000000)
  - headroom: applicable_cap - nonqualifying
expected:
  percentage_cap: 3500000
  applicable_cap: 3500000
  headroom: 100000
```

**Case 2: UAE fixed ceiling binds and is breached.** Supplied full-period adjusted total revenue is **AED 120,000,000**, including **AED 5,100,000** non-qualifying revenue, under the same scope assumptions. **5% × 120,000,000 = 6,000,000**, so the cap is **AED 5,000,000** and excess is **AED 100,000**. The ordinary failure consequence is QFZP loss from the period’s beginning and for 4 subsequent periods, not 9% on 100,000 alone. These are revenue comparisons, not corporate-tax calculations. [uae-100](<https://mof.gov.ae/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/Cabinet-Decision-No.-100-of-2023-on-Determining-Qualifying-Income-for-the-Qualifying-Free-Zone-Person.pdf>); [uae-229](<https://tax.gov.ae/datafolder/Files/Pdf/2025/MD-No-229.pdf>).

```oa-check
name: UAE fixed cap breach
inputs:
  adjusted_total: 120000000
  nonqualifying: 5100000
steps:
  - percentage_cap: adjusted_total * 0.05
  - applicable_cap: min(percentage_cap, 5000000)
  - excess: nonqualifying - applicable_cap
expected:
  percentage_cap: 6000000
  applicable_cap: 5000000
  excess: 100000
```

## Output specification

The reviewer brief must include:

1. **Zone selection rationale:** commercial fit, alternatives, operating and exit costs; identify the actual jurisdiction and entity.

2. **Qualifying activity confirmation:** classify every revenue stream against the published current list and exceptions; attach the legal source or an individual unresolved question.

3. **Substance plan:** premises, named personnel, headcount/qualifications, CIGA, OPEX, outsourcing and decision-making, with only legally established minimums.

4. **De minimis tracking:** adjusted numerator/denominator, forecast/headroom, monitoring and failure periods; do not confuse revenue with profit or transaction count.

5. **Onshore-mainland plan:** actual UAE sales/distributor/licence route or Indian DTA removal/customs mechanics, rather than a presumed prohibition/exemption.

6. **Withholding position:** inbound/outbound payment and recipient classification, domestic charge/exemption and treaty LOB/PPT/beneficial-ownership tests with actual sources.

7. **Pillar Two:** group revenue/year/entity scope, jurisdictional ETR, domestic charge, foreign IIR/UTPR, exclusions/elections/safe harbours and source gaps. Model only when all required current rules and inputs are established.

8. **Economic-substance compliance:** identify each applicable national statute/activity, reporting, records and consequence; no generic ESA checklist substitutes for the law.

9. **Reviewer questions:** label each [T2]/[T3] judgement or unconfirmed rate/box/procedure, missing source and decision blocked. No incorporation recommendation rests on an unresolved favourable number.

## Prohibitions

- **Do not** advise incorporation from an old FHTP result or an invented “OECD-approved zone” status; obtain the actual current regime outcome. [oecd-harmful](<https://www.oecd.org/en/topics/harmful-tax-practices.html>).

- **Do not** classify a non-qualifying activity by analogy to a related listed activity.

- **Do not** treat a tax de minimis cap as legal permission for mainland sales, a transaction-count limit or a profit threshold. [uae-100](<https://mof.gov.ae/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/Cabinet-Decision-No.-100-of-2023-on-Determining-Qualifying-Income-for-the-Qualifying-Free-Zone-Person.pdf>).
