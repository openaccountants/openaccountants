# E-commerce seller accounting v2.0

## Scope

For accountants reconciling marketplace, own-store, wholesale, fulfilment and cross-border goods businesses. Covers inventory, seller fees, returns, reimbursements and import costs, alongside the relevant country tax jobs.

This is a cross-country method for 2026. Establish the entity, country, tax year, reporting framework and permitted accounting basis before using it. Financial-reporting recognition is separate from income-tax deductions, capital allowances and VAT/GST. The IFRS principles below use the Australian adopted paragraph text as an official public reference; Australian-specific paragraphs and local simplified rules are not automatically rules in another country. Match the compilation and local adoption to the reporting period. Do not silently apply this method as US GAAP or a country's cash-basis tax computation.

## Ask the client first

- Which person/entity earns the income, owns the assets and signs the contracts? Obtain the legal structure, residence, business locations and reporting dates.
- Which reporting framework and tax accounting basis apply? What registrations, elections, prior balances and tax losses exist?
- Obtain contracts, invoices, credit notes, bank and platform statements, withholding certificates, refunds, chargebacks and opening/closing balances.
- What is sold, when and to whom? Identify customer location and status, performance obligations, intermediaries, private use and any money held for another person.
- Obtain cost invoices, asset ownership/use records, funding agreements, payroll/contractor documents, related-party dealings and the actual terms of insurance and licences.

## The method, step by step

1. Establish legal ownership and accounting/tax scope before coding transactions. Keep each business entity, owner and client-money account separate.
2. Map each contract to the actual promised goods/services and the consideration the business is entitled to. Determine recognition from performance and control. Billing, payout and cash dates are evidence, not universal recognition rules.
3. Reconcile each income source from transactions to returns/refunds, fees, withheld tax, reserves and bank settlement. Review principal/agent status before choosing gross or net reporting. Do not double-count a payout and the underlying sales.
4. Reconcile receivables, conditional contract assets, customer deposits, contract liabilities, deferred fees and client funds. Separate price concessions from credit losses. Obtain the applicable impairment standard before quantifying a loss allowance.
5. Establish business purpose, private proportion, capital versus revenue treatment and timing for every cost. Obtain the current country law before claiming a tax deduction; an accounting expense is not proof of tax deductibility.
6. Prepare an asset register with invoices, costs, ownership, available-for-use date, residual value, useful life, method and disposal proceeds. Maintain a separate tax-capital-allowance schedule. The prior purchase-price and useful-life ranges were unsupported planning estimates, not statutory values, and are held below.
7. Apply the country's VAT/GST/sales-tax rules by supply, customer, location and registration. Include reverse charges/imported services where relevant. Do not reclaim tax on exempt/private activity just because a supplier charged it.
8. Test payroll/contractor status, withholding, permanent establishment, cross-border reporting and elections using current country jobs. A platform tax form, incorporation or a merchant-of-record arrangement does not remove every obligation.
9. Deliver reconciled journals and schedules, source-linked tax adjustments, missing facts and a review checklist. Require the relevant professional review before a return, tax payment or material accounting judgment is relied on.

## Business model and records

Establish whether the entity resells owned stock, manufactures goods, dropships, acts as an agent, licenses products or supplies a marketplace service. Identify who owns goods in transit, at a warehouse/FBA location and with a customer, and who is importer of record. Amazon, eBay, Etsy, Shopify and WooCommerce describe channels, not one universal legal supply chain.

Obtain SKU-level inventory, purchase orders, supplier invoices, freight/customs documents, settlement transaction reports, warehouse movements, returns and stock counts. Monitor margins, stock age, sell-through, advertising return, stock cover and cash conversion using the client's data rather than the former unsourced industry ranges.

| Delivery, acceptance and shipping | Apply the contract/control test, including any substantive acceptance terms. Neither checkout nor dispatch is the universal recognition point | [AASB 15, paragraphs 31-38 and B83-B86](https://standards.aasb.gov.au/aasb-15-dec-2022) |
| Subscription box | Identify each promised delivery and allocate consideration; an annual prepayment is not automatically earned on receipt or necessarily evenly by calendar month | [AASB 15, paragraphs 22-45, 73-86 and 105-109](https://standards.aasb.gov.au/aasb-15-dec-2022) |
| Refund right | Review expected returns, revenue, refund liability and recovery asset for goods, rather than waiting for every refund to be paid | [AASB 15, paragraphs B20-B27](https://standards.aasb.gov.au/aasb-15-dec-2022) |
| Wholesale discounts and extended credit | Determine entitled consideration, discounts/variable amounts, financing where relevant and receivables; a named payment term is not the revenue event | [AASB 15, paragraphs 47-65 and 105-109](https://standards.aasb.gov.au/aasb-15-dec-2022) |

Reconcile product sales, shipping/gift-wrap charges, discounts, refunds, fees, fulfilment/storage, advertising, reimbursements, reserves, tax and actual bank payout separately. Do not treat a net marketplace deposit as all sales or apply a fixed settlement interval without the current contract.

A chargeback is not automatically both a revenue reversal and a stock write-off. Determine whether it is a valid refund/price adjustment, a credit loss, fraud, disputed consideration or another cost, and whether goods were recovered. Returned stock is measured from its actual condition and recoverable amount; it is not automatically restored at its original selling price. A refund paid before a return creates a timing issue to reconcile, not authority to invent stock.

## Inventory method

Under the stated IFRS model, use the lower of cost and net realisable value. Cost includes qualifying purchase, conversion and other costs of bringing goods to their present location and condition. Import duties and non-recoverable taxes can be included; recoverable tax is not inventory cost. Deduct trade discounts and rebates. Identify inbound freight, handling, inspection, insurance and product preparation by actual purpose. Storage is not automatically capitalised; selling costs and abnormal waste are excluded from inventory cost under the cited rules. [AASB 102, paragraphs 6-18](https://standards.aasb.gov.au/aasb-102-dec-2021).

Apply specific identification to appropriate non-interchangeable/project items; for other inventory apply FIFO or weighted average consistently for similar nature/use. There is no global “conservative FIFO” election that overrides the framework. LIFO is not an IFRS method; a US or other tax method requires its own current rules and elections. [AASB 102, paragraphs 23-27](https://standards.aasb.gov.au/aasb-102-dec-2021).

Net realisable value is expected selling price less completion and selling costs, supported by current evidence. Damaged, obsolete or slow-moving stock requires item-level or appropriately grouped analysis, not an unsupported flat reserve for an entire category. Keep photographs, disposal/count records, price evidence and approval. Reassess later: a reversal is limited to the original write-down and cannot take inventory above the lower of cost and revised NRV. [AASB 102, paragraphs 28-34](https://standards.aasb.gov.au/aasb-102-dec-2021).

Purchasing inventory is not automatically a full expense for financial reporting. Reconcile opening inventory, purchases/qualifying costs, transfers, goods sold, losses, write-downs and closing inventory; recognise the carrying amount sold with related revenue. Any permitted cash-basis tax exception must be identified separately in the country job.

### Worked case: lower of cost and recoverable selling amount

Fabricated currency units. For one homogeneous item lot, cost is 12,000. Expected sale proceeds are 11,000, with completion costs of 500 and necessary selling costs of 700. NRV is 9,800, so the write-down is 2,200. Later the same unsold lot's supported NRV rises to 12,500. Only the original 2,200 write-down can reverse, restoring cost of 12,000, not the higher selling amount. Assume no other cost change. [AASB 102, paragraphs 6, 9 and 28-34](https://standards.aasb.gov.au/aasb-102-dec-2021).

```oa-check
name: Inventory NRV write-down and capped recovery
inputs:
  cost: 12000
  estimated_selling_price: 11000
  completion_costs: 500
  selling_costs: 700
  later_nrv: 12500
steps:
  nrv: estimated_selling_price - completion_costs - selling_costs
  carrying_amount: min(cost, nrv)
  write_down: cost - carrying_amount
  later_carrying_amount: min(cost, later_nrv)
  reversal: later_carrying_amount - carrying_amount
expected:
  nrv: 9800
  carrying_amount: 9800
  write_down: 2200
  later_carrying_amount: 12000
  reversal: 2200
```

Boundary: a stock loss is different from an NRV estimate, and destroyed goods are not still on hand. A reimbursement from a fulfilment provider requires separate entitlement and accounting analysis. Do not restore inventory that was never recovered or assume every compensation is a reversal of cost of sales.

## Fees, fulfilment and product-development costs

Investigate these categories without assuming that every charge is a deduction, cost of sales or inventory:

- Referral, listing, insertion, transaction and processing charges; Amazon FBA pick/pack/ship, monthly and long-term storage; eBay, Etsy and Shopify service/app subscriptions. Obtain actual invoices and terms instead of the old standard fee percentages.
- Outbound shipping and insurance, return postage and packaging; Royal Mail, USPS, UPS, DHL and FedEx; ShipStation, Pirate Ship and Shippo. Distinguish inbound inventory costs, selling/distribution, future service prepayments and customer recharges.
- Amazon PPC/Sponsored Products, Meta/Facebook, Google and TikTok advertising; influencers, gifted goods, photography/video, SEO and Klaviyo/Mailchimp. Selling/advertising is not capitalised into stock merely to defer an expense. Product gifts require inventory removal and local VAT/GST/private-benefit treatment, not an automatic retail-value expense.
- Samples, prototypes, inspection, CE/FCC/safety testing, packaging/listing/A+ design, product certification, patent/trademark costs and liability insurance. Assess recognition and timing; a successful patent is not the sole test for capitalisation and an abandoned application does not erase every possible asset/cost consequence.
- Cin7, Linnworks, SellerBoard, RepricerExpress, Helium 10, Jungle Scout, Keepa, Xero, QuickBooks, A2X and listing tools. Establish what is acquired, business use, service period and any controlled rights.

For FBA/other warehouse losses, reconcile stock units, actual ownership, provider claim, cash or replacement goods, later recovery and any reversal obligation. Keep reimbursement income/receivables and inventory entries traceable without double recovery. A platform rule is not an accounting standard.

For goods withdrawn for private use, record quantities, ownership and supported values. Apply the country's tax/VAT deemed-supply or withdrawal rules; cost, market value and output tax are not interchangeable worldwide defaults.

## Brands, patents, licences and media

Distinguish internally generated brand value from separately acquired rights and qualifying development. Trademark filing or joining Brand Registry does not automatically justify an intangible at an assumed registration-period life. Patent, design and brand-defence costs require recognition analysis before expensing or capitalising.

For branded/character merchandise licences, identify royalty bases, minimum guarantees, territories, term and actual rights. Classify service or asset costs and allocate them to qualifying inventory or expense as appropriate. A flat fee is not automatically operating expense and a per-unit royalty is not always the only cost of sales.

Apply the actual framework to photography, video and user-generated-content rights. A multi-year commercial benefit alone is not the whole recognition test. Preserve the licence contract and any renewal or restriction evidence.
## Asset, lease and intellectual-property checks

Do not copy the former asset-price and useful-life ranges into an accounting or tax register. Use actual acquisition cost and the applicable framework; review private use, ownership, finance, impairment and disposal separately. For a lessee applying IFRS 16 there is no general exemption merely because the business is a sole practitioner or the lease is called operating. Specific short-term and low-value exemptions have conditions. [AASB 16, paragraphs 5-8 and 22](https://standards.aasb.gov.au/aasb-16-nov-2022).

Research costs and internally generated goodwill/brands are not automatically capitalisable. Development requires all recognition criteria, including technical feasibility, intent, ability to use/sell, probable benefits, resources and reliable cost measurement. Once the criteria are met, qualifying subsequent development costs are not excluded merely because the developer is self-employed. Previously expensed expenditure is not reinstated as an asset later. Finite-life intangibles are amortised from availability for use using a supported life and pattern; indefinite-life intangibles are not amortised and require impairment review. Purchased rights, service subscriptions and business-combination goodwill require separate classification. [AASB 138, paragraphs 48-71 and 97-110](https://standards.aasb.gov.au/aasb-138-dec-2022).

For an IFRS business combination, goodwill is not routinely amortised; it is carried subject to impairment, with annual testing. Separately identified finite-life customer lists or other intangibles can have amortisation. Other local GAAP and tax amortisation may differ. Do not post tax relief or a generic goodwill amortisation period without identifying the regime. [AASB 3, paragraph B63](https://standards.aasb.gov.au/aasb-3-dec-2022-0); [AASB 136, paragraphs 9-10 and 90](https://standards.aasb.gov.au/aasb-136-jan-2026-0).

## Platform, withholding and foreign-currency reporting

Identify the legal payer and contract separately for every platform or processor. Reconcile gross entitled consideration, platform fees, refunds, taxes withheld, rolling reserves, exchange conversion and settlements. A platform statement can include non-revenue transfers; a tax report can report gross payments that differ from accounting income. Check refunds, duplicated forms and the actual issuing entity. Do not assume every named platform issues the same tax form.

The former US Form 1099-K shorthand is not a taxability threshold. Current IRS guidance distinguishes payment-card transactions from third-party settlement organisations, notes that a form may be issued below the statutory reporting threshold, and explains that a form alone does not decide taxable income. Retain the reporting question in the current US job; this global method does not introduce a new US return computation. [IRS, Understanding Form 1099-K](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/understanding-your-form-1099-k).

For an international payment, establish the income's character, source, work location, beneficial recipient, withholding documentation and any treaty claim. A US payer does not make every payment a US-source royalty. A platform's withholding is not automatically the final liability, and a credit requires its own evidence. Record foreign-currency recognition at the applicable transaction date and review the framework's translation and settlement requirements; invoice date is not universal where performance occurred earlier. Exact exchange-rate methods, treaty rates and withholding forms remain country-specific items below.

## United Kingdom: expense boundary

For a UK sole trader or individual in a business partnership, use the business-expense rules and private-use allocation, with the correct cash or traditional basis. Do not apply the sole-trader overview as company tax law. The owner's drawings, loan principal and an invented charge for the proprietor's own unpaid time are not ordinary paid business costs. [HMRC self-employed expenses](https://www.gov.uk/expenses-if-youre-self-employed).

Do not use the former global half-deduction default for client meals: UK client entertaining is not generally allowable under the cited self-employed guidance. Business advertising, qualifying subscriptions, professional/legal costs and training have their own conditions. The UK training guidance can cover new skills supporting the existing business, not only maintaining old skills; training to start a new unrelated business is different. Accountancy fees for the business and the individual's own Self Assessment preparation/submission are also distinguished. Review the actual cost and its purpose. [HMRC marketing/entertainment](https://www.gov.uk/expenses-if-youre-self-employed/marketing-entertainment-subscriptions); [HMRC legal/financial costs](https://www.gov.uk/expenses-if-youre-self-employed/legal-financial); [HMRC training](https://www.gov.uk/expenses-if-youre-self-employed/training-courses).


## Cross-border and country handoffs

- **EU:** establish supplier/customer status, dispatch and destination, stock locations, import responsibility, marketplace deemed-supplier rules and the relevant OSS/IOSS scheme. The old single distance-selling threshold is not a universal registration exemption, particularly for a non-EU supplier or local warehouse stock. Customs and goods import rules remain separate from electronic services.
- **UK:** analyse domestic sales, imports, overseas sellers and platform responsibility using current UK law. The expense rules above do not establish customs valuation, duty relief, registration or output VAT.
- **Australia, Canada and other destinations:** obtain actual import/low-value goods, platform, VAT/GST and subnational obligations before charging tax or assuming a foreign warehouse always has the same registration consequence.
- **US:** retain current state nexus, marketplace facilitator, resale/exemption and federal reporting questions in the existing US jobs; no US-country rewrite or unsourced economic-nexus numbers are introduced here.

For Amazon, Shopify, Etsy and other platforms, match the actual reporting entity and tax forms to legal contracts. A “Shopify never issues a form” statement is not reliable across payment arrangements. Tie any gross-payment report to transactions, returns and sales taxes rather than expecting it to equal a net bank payout. Reconcile channels jointly for duplicated orders and inter-store transfers without netting away a legal seller's revenue.

## Insurance, growth and completion

Review product liability, cargo/transit and business-interruption policies, territorial coverage, product restrictions and excesses. A marketplace requirement and the country's legal requirement may differ. Professional memberships, accounting, customs and tax-compliance fees require business-purpose, capital and private-cost review; they are not all automatically deductible because the seller trades online.

Consider incorporation from liability, product risk, retained profits, investors, actual tax outcomes and compliance costs, not a common revenue threshold. Assess VAT/GST registration using the actual country, supply and period. Inventory systems, warehouses, staff and multi-channel accounting should follow SKU/transaction complexity, control failures and capacity, not the former unsupported SKU, order-count, revenue or profitability milestones.

Monitor gross margin after actual landed cost, contribution after platform/fulfilment/advertising, inventory age, cash conversion, return rates, stock cover and channel dependence. Keep accounting expense recognition separate from management metrics. Before hiring or contracting fulfilment support, assess actual worker status, payroll and agreements. Provide the country reviewer with reconciled stock, sales, customs, settlement and tax schedules.

## When to refuse or refer

Do not certify inventory without ownership/count evidence, treat a marketplace summary as a complete tax return, invent a compensation posting, expense all purchases while ignoring closing stock, or reclaim import VAT without entitlement documents. Refer disputed customs valuation, product compliance, foreign registration, principal/agent and materially uncertain inventory estimates with the actual records.
