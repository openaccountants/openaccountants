# Turkey Tax Overview

> **General reference only.** This skill is general tax/accounting reference material for AI-assisted workflows. It has not been reviewed for any specific person's facts, documents, elections, deadlines, residency, filing status, or local procedures. Do not rely on it to file, pay, amend, or take a tax position without review by a qualified professional in the relevant jurisdiction.

**Quick answer.** Turkey taxes people settled there on worldwide income at 15% to 40% (2026 tariff), companies at 25% (30% for financial firms) with a 10% domestic minimum tax, and supplies under VAT at 20%, 10% or 1%. The tax year is the calendar year; the currency is the Turkish lira (TRY). Every figure here is from the consolidated laws on mevzuat.gov.tr or a Revenue Administration (GİB) publication, read on 2026-11-14. Where a domain skill covers a point in depth, it is linked and that skill wins if the two differ.

## Who this applies to

This overview is for anyone asking how Turkey taxes an individual, a sole trader or a company in tax year 2026, before the domain skill is chosen.

- **Standard tax year.** Income is the net amount an individual earns in one calendar year (Income Tax Law No. 193, GVK, art. 1). Books follow the accounting period, normally the calendar year; the Ministry can set a 12-month special accounting period on request (Tax Procedure Law No. 213, VUK, art. 174).
- **Currency.** Turkish lira (TRY). Law No. 5083 made the (New) Turkish lira the currency, and the word "New" was dropped from 1 January 2009 (Law No. 5083, art. 1 and its note 1).
- **Tax authority.** The Revenue Administration (Gelir İdaresi Başkanlığı, GİB) of the Ministry of Treasury and Finance issues the communiqués and runs electronic filing. Social security premiums go to the Social Security Institution (SGK): see [tr-payroll-social](tr-payroll-social.md).
- **Individuals settled in Turkey.** Taxed on income from inside and outside Turkey (GVK art. 3). A person is settled if their domicile is in Turkey, or if they stay in Turkey continuously for more than six months in a calendar year; temporary absences do not break the stay (art. 4). Some foreigners who come for a set, temporary job, or for study, treatment, rest or travel, are not settled even after six months (art. 5).
- **Individuals not settled.** Taxed only on income earned in Turkey (art. 6).
- **New settlers from 2026.** A person treated as settled in Turkey, who had no domicile and no tax liability in Turkey in the last three calendar years before that, is exempt on income earned outside Turkey for twenty years (repeated art. 20/D, added by Law No. 7582, in force 4 June 2026 for people settled from 1 January 2026). Earlier Turkish rental, investment income or capital gain liability does not block the exemption. Detail: [turkey-pit](turkey-pit.md).
- **Companies.** A company whose legal seat or business centre (kanuni veya iş merkezi) is in Turkey is a full taxpayer on worldwide profits; a company with neither in Turkey is a limited taxpayer, taxed only on profits earned in Turkey (Corporate Tax Law No. 5520, KVK, art. 3(1), (2)). Detail: [tr-corporate-income-tax](tr-corporate-income-tax.md).
- **Out of scope here:** treaty rates, free zones, technology zones, investment incentives, the global minimum tax (Pillar Two) returns, and inheritance and property taxes. Use the domain skill or refer.

## The base

- **Individuals.** Seven income types make up income (GVK art. 2): business, agricultural, wage, self-employed, real estate, investment income and other gains. The annual tariff applies to the total after exemptions and deductions. Wage income alone, taxed through withholding, often needs no return. The base rules, exemptions and the simple-method traders are in [turkey-pit](turkey-pit.md).
- **Companies.** Corporate profit follows the income tax rules on business income (KVK art. 6), less the KVK art. 5 exemptions and art. 10 deductions. The 10% floor is worked on profit before those, with only the items in art. 32/C(2) still deducted, and then reduced for tax not collected because of the reduced rates in art. 32(6) to (8) (art. 32/C(3)). Detail: [tr-corporate-income-tax](tr-corporate-income-tax.md).
- **VAT.** Supplies of goods and services in Turkey in the course of business, imports and other listed transactions (VAT Law No. 3065, KDV, art. 1). The base, exemptions and input VAT are in [turkey-vat](turkey-vat.md).
- **Reduced corporate rates for 2026 profits.** The 12.5% production rate applies from 2027. For 2026 profits the earlier art. 32(8), added by Law No. 7351, applied 1 point below the standard rate to profits purely from production by industrial registry certificate holders. Such a manufacturer gets no second reduction on the part of those profits already reduced as exports. That earlier wording was read from a 6 October 2024 archive copy of the KVK, not reopened today: see "Not yet confirmed".

## Deadlines and filing

The laws set the statutory dates. GİB's filing calendar shows later dates for several returns. Where the two differ, the statutory date is the safe date and the later GİB date is shown next to it.

| Return | Statutory date | GİB calendar | Payment |
|---|---|---|---|
| Annual income tax return (2026 income) | 1 to 25 March 2027 (GVK art. 92) | By 23:59 on the last day of March (31 March 2027) | Two equal instalments, March and July (GVK art. 117); GİB shows end of March and end of July |
| Leaving Turkey for good / death | 15 days before leaving / within 4 months of death (GVK art. 92) | Same | Within the filing period |
| Annual corporate tax return (calendar-year company, 2026) | 1 to 25 April 2027 (KVK art. 14(5)) | By 23:59 on the last day of the fourth month (30 April 2027) | By the end of the month the return is filed (KVK art. 21(1)) |
| Provisional tax (companies, sole traders, self-employed) | Set by GİB | 17 May, 17 August, 17 November and 17 February; a fourth-quarter return is filed from tax year 2025 (Law No. 7566) | In the filing period |
| VAT return (monthly or quarterly) | 24th of the month after the period (KDV art. 41(1)); payment by the 26th (art. 46(1)) | By 23:59 on the 28th, citing VUK Circular No. 149 | In the filing period (GİB) |
| Monthly withholding and social security return (muhtasar ve prim hizmet) | Set by GİB | By 23:59 on the 26th of the following month | In the filing period |

The VAT Law sets quarterly periods and lets the Ministry put taxpayers on monthly periods by turnover (KDV art. 39(1)). Which period a client is on: [turkey-vat](turkey-vat.md).

## Penalties and interest

- **Late payment.** A tax not paid by its due date carries a late payment surcharge of 3.7% for each month, part months worked by day (Law No. 6183, art. 51, rate set by Presidential Decision No. 10556 from 13 November 2025). The President may cut it to a tenth or double it, so check the latest decision before computing.
- **Late interest on assessments.** Tax assessed after the due date carries late interest (gecikme faizi) at the surcharge rate, whole months only (VUK art. 112).
- **Tax loss.** Where a return is late, missing or understated and tax is lost, the penalty is one times the tax lost; three times where the loss comes from the fraud acts in VUK art. 359. A late return filed of the taxpayer's own accord, before an audit or a referral to the assessment commission, has the penalty halved (VUK art. 344).
- **Irregularity penalties** (usulsüzlük, VUK arts. 352 and 353) are fixed amounts re-set each year; they are not given here. See the domain skills.

## Cross-border points

- A non-settled individual or a limited-taxpayer company is taxed only on income earned in Turkey (GVK art. 6, KVK art. 3(2)). Withholding on payments abroad and the treaty network: [tr-cross-border](tr-cross-border.md).
- New settlers from 2026 can be exempt on foreign income for twenty years (GVK repeated art. 20/D). Check the three-year look-back on both domicile and tax liability before relying on it.
- Related-party prices, including disguised profit distribution through transfer pricing: [tr-transfer-pricing](tr-transfer-pricing.md).

## Two worked examples that recompute

**Example 1: a consultant and an employee, each TRY 1,200,000 taxable in 2026.** A self-employed consultant with taxable income of TRY 1,200,000 sits in the 35% slice: 232,500 on the first 1,000,000, plus 35% of 200,000 = TRY 302,500. An employee with the same taxable wage income stays in the wider 27% wage slice: 70,500 on the first 400,000, plus 27% of 800,000 = TRY 286,500. The wage tariff saves TRY 16,000 at this level. Payroll mechanics (cumulative withholding, exemptions) are in [tr-payroll-social](tr-payroll-social.md).

```oa-check
name: TR 2026 tariff, non-wage vs wage income of TRY 1,200,000
inputs:
  taxable: 1200000
steps:
  - tax_nonwage: 232500 + (taxable - 1000000) * 0.35
  - tax_wage: 70500 + (taxable - 400000) * 0.27
  - wage_saving: tax_nonwage - tax_wage
expected:
  tax_nonwage: 302500.00
  tax_wage: 286500.00
  wage_saving: 16000.00
```

**Example 2: does the 10% minimum tax bite? (a decision).** A company in its fifth year has corporate profit before exemptions and deductions of TRY 10,000,000 and exemptions of TRY 7,000,000 that are not on the art. 32/C(2) list. Normal tax: 25% of 3,000,000 = TRY 750,000. Floor: 10% of 10,000,000 = TRY 1,000,000. The floor is higher, so the company pays TRY 1,000,000, and the exemptions save less than they appear to. If the exemptions were on the 32/C(2) list (for example the R&D and design deductions under Law No. 4691), they would come off the floor base too. A new company in its first three periods pays only the TRY 750,000.

```oa-check
name: TR 2026 domestic minimum corporate tax test
inputs:
  profit_before_exemptions: 10000000
  exemptions: 7000000
steps:
  - normal_tax: (profit_before_exemptions - exemptions) * 0.25
  - minimum_tax: profit_before_exemptions * 0.10
  - tax_due: max(normal_tax, minimum_tax)
  - extra_from_floor: tax_due - normal_tax
expected:
  normal_tax: 750000.00
  minimum_tax: 1000000.00
  tax_due: 1000000.00
  extra_from_floor: 250000.00
```

## Ask the client first

1. Individual or company? If a company: financial sector, exporter, industrial registry holder, or in its first three accounting periods?
2. For an individual: where is the domicile, and how many continuous months in Turkey in 2026? Any domicile or tax liability in Turkey in the last three years?
3. Which income types (wages, business, self-employed, rent, investment)? Wage-only clients often need no annual return.
4. Turnover and VAT period (monthly or quarterly), and whether the supplies fall on VAT list I or II.
5. Is any tax late, and since when (for the 3.7% monthly surcharge)?
