# San Marino tax overview v0.7

> **General reference only.** This Guide is general tax/accounting reference material for AI-assisted workflows. It has not been reviewed for any specific person's facts, documents, elections, deadlines, residency, filing status, or local procedures. Do not rely on it to file, pay, amend, or take a tax position without review by a qualified professional in the relevant jurisdiction.

## Scope

A one-page map of San Marino tax for 2026, with the headline figures and which skill to load for the detail. It routes detailed computation and filing work to the specialist Guides. The worked control below demonstrates the conditional headline rate, without establishing incentive entitlement.

## Ask the client first

1. Is the client an individual, sole trader, professional or company?
2. Resident in San Marino in 2026, and where else they have ties?
3. Which income or transactions: wages, business, property, imports, payments abroad?
4. Any link with Italy (frontier work, Italian customers or suppliers)?

## Quick reference

| Item | 2026 position | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Tax year | Calendar year; statutory return date is 31 July of the following year, moved to the next office-opening day if a holiday or closure. For 2026 income, apply that rule to 31 July 2027, rather than promising the unadjusted date | [IGR art 93(1), (4)-(5)](https://www.consigliograndeegenerale.sm/on-line/documento17086436.html) |
| Currency | Euro | [Foreign Affairs, Monetary Agreement section](https://www.esteri.sm/pub2/EsteriSM/en/UE/Altri-accordi.html) |
| Tax authority | UO Ufficio Tributario for IGR collection, control and assessment | [IGR art 2(d)](https://www.consigliograndeegenerale.sm/on-line/documento17086436.html) |
| Social security | ISS (Istituto per la Sicurezza Sociale) and the FONDISS supplementary pension | [ISS circular 2187/UC/2026](https://www.iss.sm/on-line/home/menudestra/area-modulistica/modulistica-ufficio-contributi/documento49158490.html) |
| Residence | Individuals: registered residence or actual stay for most of the year, or centre of vital interests. Companies: registered office or effective management for most of the year | [IGR arts 10, 42](https://www.consigliograndeegenerale.sm/on-line/documento17086436.html) |
| Scope | Residents on worldwide income; non-residents on San Marino-source income | [IGR arts 1, 5](https://www.consigliograndeegenerale.sm/on-line/documento17086436.html) |
| Personal income tax (IGR) | Eight bands, 9% to 35% (top band over 80,000) | [IGR Annex C](https://www.consigliograndeegenerale.sm/on-line/documento17086436.html) |
| Business and professional income of individuals | 18% separate rate for 2026 to 2030 | [Law 141/2025, art 54(6)](https://www.consigliograndeegenerale.sm/on-line/home/archivio-leggi-decreti-e-regolamenti/documento17155359.html) |
| Corporate IGR | 18% for 2026 to 2030 (statutory 17%) | [Law 141/2025, art 54(6)](https://www.consigliograndeegenerale.sm/on-line/home/archivio-leggi-decreti-e-regolamenti/documento17155359.html); [IGR art 43](https://www.consigliograndeegenerale.sm/on-line/documento17086436.html) |
| New-activity incentive | 50% rate cut for the first five tax periods, with a permitted start deferral no later than the following tax period, for a qualifying new company, sole trader or professional that opts in (9% in 2026). Prior similar activity, ownership and company hiring conditions apply; see `sm-corporate-income-tax` | [IGR art 73](https://www.consigliograndeegenerale.sm/on-line/documento17086436.html) |
| VAT | The domestic indirect-tax system uses single-stage import tax (monofase), ordinary rate 17%, and a separate services tax (imposta complementare sui servizi) for listed final-customer services, with exclusions; use `sm-vat-gst` for classification and procedure | [DD 183/2011, art 1](https://www.consigliograndeegenerale.sm/on-line/home/documento17046854.html); [DD 50/2011, arts 2-3](https://www.consigliograndeegenerale.sm/on-line/home/archivio-leggi-decreti-e-regolamenti/documento17027521.html) |
| Employee social contributions | 7.80% ISS plus 2.50% FONDISS | [ISS circular 2187/UC/2026](https://www.iss.sm/on-line/home/menudestra/area-modulistica/modulistica-ufficio-contributi/documento49158490.html) |
| Employer pension and FONDISS | 17.60% plus 2.50% (other employer funds on top) | [ISS circular 2187/UC/2026](https://www.iss.sm/on-line/home/menudestra/area-modulistica/modulistica-ufficio-contributi/documento49158490.html) |
| Withholding on payments abroad | Domestic starting rules: 13% on covered capital income, subject to art 5 source exclusions and art 103 special rates; 5% on resident-company dividends paid to individuals; 20% on art 27 independent/assimilated services paid to non-residents, with art 102 exceptions; 10% on covered equipment-use payments to non-residents for equipment in San Marino. Classify royalties and directors' fees under their actual statutory income category; apply treaty relief only after checking it | [IGR arts 102-104](https://www.consigliograndeegenerale.sm/on-line/documento17086436.html) |
| Domestic e-invoicing | For covered domestic transactions between COE holders: optional from 1 October 2026; required from 1 January 2027 unless prior-calendar-year declared revenue is below 100,000. Exactly 100,000 is outside that annual exclusion. Continuing use after exceeding the threshold or opting is a distinct rule; route boundary and public-entity cases to `sm-vat-gst` | [DD 133/2026, arts 2, 11](https://www.bollettinoufficiale.sm/on-line/RicercaBU?operation=getDocBU&id=4d7f1ffcf645b166ffc567d328d888e43fcdd538) |

The 2026 reform: Law 141/2025 applies from tax year 2026 ([Law 141/2025, art 54(1)](https://www.consigliograndeegenerale.sm/on-line/home/archivio-leggi-decreti-e-regolamenti/documento17155359.html)); Delegated Decree 156/2026 of 12 October 2026 corrected it, also from 2026 ([DD 156/2026, art 19](https://www.bollettinoufficiale.sm/on-line/RicercaBU?operation=getDocBU&id=9c3a0dfeb72a7aeab52d1c5c0440879b4cf2ad33)).

## Worked cases

A hypothetical qualifying new company has EUR 100,000 taxable profit. Without the article 73 option its 2026 IGR at 18% is EUR 18,000; with all conditions met and a valid option, the 50% rate cut yields 9% and EUR 9,000. The EUR 9,000 difference tests the arithmetic only. Handoff to `sm-corporate-income-tax` for shareholder, hiring, timing and retention conditions ([Law 141/2025 art 54(6)](https://www.consigliograndeegenerale.sm/on-line/home/archivio-leggi-decreti-e-regolamenti/documento17155359.html); [IGR art 73](https://www.consigliograndeegenerale.sm/on-line/documento17086436.html)).

```oa-check
name: San Marino conditional 2026 new-activity rate comparison
inputs:
  hypothetical_taxable_profit: 100000
  ordinary_rate: 0.18
  conditional_rate_cut: 0.50
steps:
  ordinary_igr: hypothetical_taxable_profit * ordinary_rate
  conditional_rate: ordinary_rate * (1 - conditional_rate_cut)
  conditional_igr: hypothetical_taxable_profit * conditional_rate
  difference: ordinary_igr - conditional_igr
expected:
  ordinary_igr: 18000
  conditional_rate: 0.09
  conditional_igr: 9000
  difference: 9000
```
