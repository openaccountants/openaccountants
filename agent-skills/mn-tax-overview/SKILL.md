# Mongolia Tax Overview

## Who this applies to

A first-pass map of Mongolia's ordinary taxes for tax year 2026. It routes to the corporate, payroll/social, VAT and formation skills; it is not a complete personal-income return or treaty computation. The Mongolian Tax Administration (MTA / Tatvaryn Yeronkhii Gazar) administers the tax system. [General Tax Law art. 74](https://legalinfo.mn/mn/detail?lawId=14403).

**Quick reference:** identify the taxpayer, residence, income type, VAT status and period before quoting a headline rate. A resident company is generally taxed on worldwide income; foreign-company PE/source rules differ. Individuals have their own residence and source rules. Do not treat a future amendment already shown in the consolidation as the current rule.

## The base

For corporate residence, a Mongolian-incorporated entity is resident; a foreign entity may be resident through the statutory management test. Residents are taxed on worldwide income, PEs on connected Mongolian income, and other foreign entities on Mongolian-source income. Separate gross-basis categories from ordinary taxable profit. The corporate small-business taxable-income rule differs from the separately elected simplified gross-income regime. Use [mn-corporate-income-tax](mn-corporate-income-tax.md). [CIT arts. 5, 7, 18-20, 29](https://legalinfo.mn/mn/detail?lawId=14407).

For individuals, residence uses statutory presence/source criteria and special categories. Employment taxable income deducts paid employee social and health contributions under art. 14 and has the applicable resident credit; the top marginal rate does not apply to all gross salary. Non-resident gross taxation is different. Use [mn-payroll-social](mn-payroll-social.md) for employment only. A full individual business, investment, property or annual-return analysis remains outside this overview. [PIT arts. 5-7, 14, 20-23](https://legalinfo.mn/mn/detail?lawId=14410).

For VAT, check registration, the taxable supply, tax point, invoice/customs evidence, exemption and input restrictions. The bank statement is an intake source, not proof of every deduction. Use [mongolia-vat](mongolia-vat.md). For company forms, foreign investment, registration and financial statements use [mn-company-formation](mn-company-formation.md).

Take the taxpayer's actual registration, return type and schedule to the relevant filing skill. Retain approved returns, submission acknowledgments and payment reconciliation. An annual corporate tax deadline is not the accounting-statements deadline, and payroll/social dates are separate.

## Penalties and interest

General Tax Law art. 73 sets the interest framework and requires the year's rate to be determined separately. Obtain the actual notice, period and decision; no unsourced daily rate or generic penalty is provided. Future interest caps in the consolidation are not a 2026 rule. [General Tax Law](https://legalinfo.mn/mn/detail?lawId=14403).

## Cross-border points

Distinguish corporate PE, management residence and Mongolian source; individual tax residence; and treaty entitlement/social-insurance coverage. Domestic corporate withholding is generally 20% for the covered foreign non-PE income, with specific exceptions in the corporate skill. Treaty rates are not verified here. Refer foreign investors, regulated/mining operations, treaty claims and transfer pricing for the specific rules, rather than treating the headline table as a clearance. [CIT arts. 5, 7, 18, 20, 24](https://legalinfo.mn/mn/detail?lawId=14407); [PIT arts. 5-7, 20](https://legalinfo.mn/mn/detail?lawId=14410).

## Two worked examples that recompute

### Corporate profit above the ordinary threshold

Assume an ordinary company's established 2026 taxable profit is MNT 6,500,000,000 with no reduction, exemption or credit. MNT 600,000,000 + (MNT 500,000,000 × 25%) = **MNT 725,000,000**, derived. Use the corporate skill before applying a concession.

```oa-check
name: Ordinary corporate tax in the upper band
inputs:
  taxable: 6500000000
  threshold: 6000000000
  fixed_tax: 600000000
  excess_rate: 25
steps:
  - tax: fixed_tax + (taxable - threshold) * excess_rate / 100
expected:
  tax: 725000000
```

### VAT-inclusive ordinary domestic sale

Assume a registered business makes a documented ordinary taxable sale for MNT 11,000,000 including VAT. Net is MNT 10,000,000 and output VAT **MNT 1,000,000**, derived. Input credit requires its own evidence and is not assumed in this output calculation.

```oa-check
name: Extract VAT from VAT-inclusive domestic consideration
inputs:
  gross: 11000000
  vat_rate: 10
steps:
  - net: gross / (1 + vat_rate / 100)
  - vat: gross - net
expected:
  net: 10000000
  vat: 1000000
```

## Ask the client first

Obtain individual/company status, residence/management and source facts, income category and year, prior-year taxable income, activity/licences, related parties, VAT certificate and sales, payroll/social category, foreign counterparties/treaty evidence, current filing schedule and prior returns/payments. Route to the appropriate skill; do not infer a taxpayer's regime from the country alone.
