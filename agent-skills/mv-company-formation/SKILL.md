# Maldives company formation and entity choice

## Scope

Prepare a Maldives incorporation file, distinguish a private company from other available forms, and assemble the post-registration compliance handoff for 2026. Companies Act 7/2023 took effect on 1 January 2024; its first amendment and the private-company regulation must be read with it. The Registrar of Companies operates within the economic ministry. The Ministry's [current legislation catalogue](https://trade.gov.mv/en/laws-and-regulations/) identifies those instruments and the separate sole-proprietorship, partnership, cooperative and foreign-investment regimes.

This Guide supplies the documented workflow below, with explicit holds where an operative formation requirement could not be confirmed. It does not certify that an application is legally complete while a held eligibility/capital/governance issue remains unresolved. Tax rates belong to the [corporate](mv-corporate-income-tax.md), [individual](mv-income-tax.md), [GST](maldives-gst.md) and [payroll](mv-payroll-social.md) Guides. This is tier 2 material without accountant approval.

## Ask the client first

1. What activity, trading name and legal vehicle are intended, and is there a regulated activity, tourism operation or sector licence?
2. Who are the owners, ultimate controllers and proposed directors, where are they resident, and are any investors foreign? Obtain identity, ownership-chain and consent evidence.
3. What shares, rights, capital contributions, registered office, management and funding arrangements are proposed? Is there an existing company or a foreign company seeking re-registration?
4. Will shares or other securities be offered to the public, how many members are proposed, and will the Registrar's model articles or bespoke articles be used?
5. What are the intended start date, turnover/import activity, staff, accounting currency and initial tax/pension obligations? Obtain access/authority for the relevant filing accounts.
6. Does the file include an existing constitution, prior registrations, licence/foreign-investment approvals, annual filings, fees or overdue compliance requiring correction?

## The method, step by step

### 1. Select the vehicle and establish eligibility [https://trade.gov.mv/en/laws-and-regulations/](https://trade.gov.mv/en/laws-and-regulations/)

Do not incorporate merely to obtain a corporate tax band. Compare ownership, liability, administration, financing, licensing and distribution needs, then compute owner/entity tax under the relevant tax Guide. Private/public companies, government companies and local-authority companies are distinct forms under the Act; the [official ratification notice](https://presidency.gov.mv/Press/Article/29505) confirms these categories and the foreign-company re-registration branch. A sole proprietorship, partnership or cooperative follows its own statute, not the private-company incorporation route.

For a private company, the Ministry's explanation of Companies Act sections 43-44 requires articles consistent with the Act, prohibits public sale of registered securities and caps membership at 50. Its articles must cover meetings, board composition, powers, profit distribution, accounts, auditor procedures and winding up. A public-offer proposal therefore needs the public-company framework and its applicable governance/disclosure review rather than a private-company label on the same application. [Ministry articles guidance](https://trade.gov.mv/en/18801/).

The Registrar's English model articles expressly address a company with a single director in article 60. This supports a single-director governance mechanism; it is not proof that every applicant satisfies all statutory director eligibility, residence or capital conditions. The old draft's universal single-shareholder/single-director minima, unrestricted single-share registration and absence of minimum capital are held below pending direct operative confirmation. Do not invent an additional owner or director to fill an unknown requirement.

### 2. Reserve the name using the current published process [https://trade.gov.mv/en/17296/](https://trade.gov.mv/en/17296/)

Use the Ministry's company-name service route at [one.gov.mv](https://one.gov.mv). Choose the legal type from the drop-down: PVT LTD for a private company, PLC for a public company, and the different government/local-authority types where applicable. Do not type the type abbreviation into the ordinary name field instead of selecting it.

Check existing companies and other business names, reserved names, prohibited governmental/institutional affiliations, protected brands and other restrictions. A name appearing available to request is not yet an approved reservation: the Ministry requires an operator check. Keep the submitted name, chosen vehicle, application reference, decision, fee receipt and reservation expiry.

| Published name-service rule | Amount or condition | Period | Official source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Approved company-name reservation | MVR 100 fee; reservation valid 7 days | Published service guidance read for 2026 preparation | [Companies Act sections 66-76, Ministry reservation guide](https://trade.gov.mv/en/17296/) |
| Reservation extension | A further 7 days is possible; fee applies | Obtain the actual approved extension and invoice | [Ministry reservation guide](https://trade.gov.mv/en/17296/) |
| Private-company membership/public offering | Maximum 50 members; no public sale of registered securities | 2026 preparation; retain the constitutional conditions | [Companies Act section 44, Ministry explanation](https://trade.gov.mv/en/18801/) |
| New private-company registration | MVR 2,000 service fee; other services and conditions separately priced | Government guideline updated 24 August 2026 | [oneGov Company Registration and Changes](https://one.gov.mv/submissions/6481758f-b70d-b567-6719-90074d1037e1/new) |
| Constitution amendment notification | Within 30 days, with required documents and fee | Actual memorandum/articles amendment | [Companies Act section 50, Ministry explanation](https://trade.gov.mv/en/18801/) |

A name reservation does not incorporate the company or authorize it to trade. The Ministry also cautions against registering a domain on the strength of a reserved name before registration. Use the approved legal name on official documents, financial instruments and company locations as its guide requires.

### 3. Assemble the constitution, ownership and management file [https://trade.gov.mv/en/18801/](https://trade.gov.mv/en/18801/)

The [oneGov service guideline](https://one.gov.mv/submissions/6481758f-b70d-b567-6719-90074d1037e1/new), updated 24 August 2026 and opened through its Read Service Guideline control, requires the Managing Director's Declaration, tax-responsible-person information form, memorandum, articles and relevant identity copies. If a secretary is appointed, provide the secretary declaration and identity copy; additional documents apply where shareholders are companies or foreign persons/entities. Match spelling, rights, holdings, office and activity throughout the application. Use the actual linked forms and their current versions rather than manufacturing declaration text.

The same guideline requires a private company to appoint a managing director. For a managing-director change it states that the person must be a shareholder or a representative of a shareholding company/partnership. A company secretary is optional for a private company; if appointed, the secretary must be a Maldivian citizen. The director-change section states the Maldivian-citizenship rule and exceptions for foreign-investment companies, government companies and public companies with government holdings. These service conditions do not settle every statutory minimum or disqualification; retain the specific eligibility review.

Sections 43-53, as explained by the Ministry, allow a private company to adopt the model articles at registration or later by special resolution. Bespoke articles require consistency with the Act, a special resolution where applicable, the compliance statement and registration described by that guidance. A compliance statement is a lawyer's signed assurance; the model's availability does not authorize an AI to supply that signature. Keep the approved version and all resolutions.

Read the actual adopted articles, not only the PDF's title. The published model includes an annual general meeting rule, board minutes and a single-director written-resolution mechanism. Its text predates the September 2024 amendment; its older meeting notice language must be checked against the later statute and the company's actual constitution. Do not copy a model clause as proof that it overrides the amended Act.

The [official amendment notice](https://presidency.gov.mv/Press/Article/31628) records changes to Companies Act sections 100 and 126, including EGM notice and Registrar-directed director appointment time limits. The notice describes a seven-day EGM notice rule for private/public companies and ten-day Registrar-directed director/managing-director appointments. Read the precise trigger before calendaring; these are not a universal period for every ordinary incorporation action. No general four-board-meetings-per-year instruction is confirmed by the materials read here.

### 4. Clear the foreign-investment branch before committing to a structure [https://trade.gov.mv/wp-content/uploads/Annex-01-Foreign-Investment-Entry-Requirements.pdf](https://trade.gov.mv/wp-content/uploads/Annex-01-Foreign-Investment-Entry-Requirements.pdf)

Foreign ownership is not universally unrestricted. The Ministry's entry-requirements table distinguishes fully open activities, restricted ownership/joint-venture conditions and closed activities, with sector/activity-specific investment conditions. Match the actual activity to the current entry category and obtain the relevant approval and terms through the Ministry/Invest Maldives route. Do not substitute incorporation for foreign-investment permission, or apply one sector's investment threshold to another.

The current catalogue lists Foreign Investments Act 11/2024 and the Foreign Companies Re-registration Regulation 2024/R-100. A foreign company seeking to operate through a registered foreign presence needs that branch; a newly incorporated local subsidiary needs its own local-company and foreign-investment analysis. The detailed operative application/approval requirements not actually read remain held below.

### 5. Lodge and reconcile the registration result

Use [oneGov Company Registration and Changes](https://one.gov.mv/submissions/6481758f-b70d-b567-6719-90074d1037e1/new), reached through the Ministry's current [Business Services page](https://trade.gov.mv/en/business-services/). Sign in as the authorized managing director or delegated representative, select the registration service and follow Proceed to the Business Portal or the service application form. Complete the company particulars and attach the required documents. The public guideline gives a new-company service fee of MVR 2,000; reconcile the actual invoice to that service and any separate requested services. No login or real application was performed during this research.

The authorized representative submits and pays the actual invoice. The guideline says that email/SMS notifications follow submission, status is available in My Applications, and processed certificates, profile sheets or constitution documents are issued by email. Retain the acknowledgement/reference, monitor correction requests and respond against the same application. Once issued, match the certificate/register entry to the approved name, entity number, constitution, ownership and management. Resolve discrepancies before using the identity in tax, banking or contracts. A saved draft, fee payment or reserved name is not itself the certificate of registration.

The published guideline gives a service-processing target, not proof of actual acceptance or completion of a particular application. Do not turn it into an unconditional time guarantee. A complete setup budget also needs any separate services, licences, professional costs and confirmed capital requirements; those are not all included in the new-company service fee.

### 6. Complete the tax, payroll and annual-compliance handoff

Income Tax Regulation section 3(b) removes the separate MIRA 117 submission for a person applying to register its business through the Ministry; section 4 still imposes the relevant tax registration obligation. Confirm that the actual MIRA registration/TIN and business activity are correct rather than assuming certificate issuance completed every tax duty. GST registration has its own turnover/tourism/commercial-import triggers and effective dates; use the [GST Guide](maldives-gst.md), not the former shorthand of turnover or tourism alone.

For staff, confirm employer pension registration, employee enrollment and EWT obligations using the [payroll Guide](mv-payroll-social.md). Banking, statutory registrations and licences depend on the actual activity and owners; obtain current institution-specific requirements without inventing a universal account-opening list.

Set a compliance register for the constitution, members and share changes, directors, resolutions/minutes, financial accounts, auditor decisions, annual general meeting, Registrar annual return, applicable service fees, income-tax return/interims, GST and payroll filings. The model's article 39 requires an AGM in accordance with the Act; it does not supply every current AGM/annual-return deadline. Keep those statutory deadlines and any annual regulatory-service-fee amount as unresolved until the precise operative provisions are read. Do not carry forward the former annual-fee-replacement claim as a verified current payment instruction.

## Boundary and exception table

| Situation | Next decision |
|---|---|
| Proposed public securities sale or membership above private-company cap | Stop the private-company route and review the appropriate vehicle and approval process. |
| One owner/director or nominal single-share proposal | Verify statutory minimums/eligibility and capital requirements; the model's single-director mechanism alone is insufficient. |
| Foreign owner or an existing overseas company | Classify activity/entry conditions and local subsidiary versus foreign re-registration. |
| Existing company with pre-2024 articles | Check compliance with the new Act and amendments; do not treat the original transitional date as a fresh 2026 deadline. |
| Bespoke constitution, investor rights or regulated activity | Obtain qualified legal drafting and the necessary licence/approval before final submission. |
| Name approved but certificate not issued | Track reservation expiry, complete incorporation and do not represent the business as incorporated yet. |

## Worked cases

### Case 1: reservation versus incorporation budget

For an ordinary new private-company application requiring one approved name reservation, the published reservation fee is MVR 100 and the current registration service fee is MVR 2,000. Those two known fees total MVR 2,100. A MVR 1,000 budget is already short by MVR 1,100, before any other services, licences or professional costs. Do not describe the name reservation alone as incorporation. Complete the application within the reservation or obtain its approved extension. [Ministry name guide](https://trade.gov.mv/en/17296/); [current service guideline](https://one.gov.mv/submissions/6481758f-b70d-b567-6719-90074d1037e1/new).

### Case 2: private-company boundary

A proposed structure has 51 members and intends to sell registered securities to the public. The private-company route fails the Ministry's stated membership and public-sale conditions. Redesign the proposal or obtain advice on the public-company route before reserving the type and drafting articles; do not remove a member on paper while concealing the real beneficial arrangement. [Companies Act section 44, Ministry explanation](https://trade.gov.mv/en/18801/).

## When to refuse or refer

Refer unconfirmed minimum ownership/directorship/capital, disputed beneficial ownership, foreign-investment eligibility, public offerings, bespoke shareholder rights, regulated activities, winding up and statutory compliance defaults. Do not submit a legally complete incorporation file while its required conditions are still held. A source-cited preparation checklist cannot replace a lawyer's compliance statement or an authorized person's declaration.

## Filing and payment

Name reservation uses the published one.gov.mv service. Incorporation uses the named Company Registration and Changes service, its public guideline and the authorized signed-in application; required documents, steps and completion notifications are described above. No authenticated screen or successful filing was performed for this Guide. Confirm the live form and fee, submit the authorized application, retain its reference, obtain/correct the certificate and register entry, then complete the tax/pension/licensing handoff. Record each submission, payment and effective date separately.

## Completion checklist

- Vehicle, activity, owners/controllers, directors and foreign-investment branch resolved.
- Name approved with type, reservation expiry and receipt retained.
- Constitution and required compliance statement/consents complete and consistent with current law.
- Actual capital/fee requirements and required uploads confirmed before filing.
- Certificate/register identity reconciled; tax, staff and licensing registrations checked.
- Recurring compliance calendar completed from verified operative deadlines, with remaining holds identified.
