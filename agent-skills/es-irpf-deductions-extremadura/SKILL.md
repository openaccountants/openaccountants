# Spain: Extremadura regional IRPF credits, 2026

**Sourced from the law.** This revision has not had an independent whole-pack review or accountant sign-off. Calculate regional credits after establishing tax residence and the taxpayer's facts. This Guide holds the Extremadura catalogue of [es-irpf-deductions](es-irpf-deductions.md), moved here unchanged on 6 November 2026, so that each Guide stays within the size the site can deliver. Specific unresolved interactions are listed in Sources.

## Who this applies to

An ordinary IRPF resident whose community of residence for 2026 under [LIRPF art. 72](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2006-20764#a72) is Extremadura. A regional credit reduces regional tax, not the income base. Before applying any row below, use [es-irpf-deductions](es-irpf-deductions.md) for the residence test, the deduction ledger, the state/regional order of credits, the national credits, deadlines, penalties and the questions to ask, and [es-income-tax](es-income-tax.md) for the bases, scales and minima. Where a row below refers to "the attributable-tax worksheet under The base", that worksheet is [es-irpf-deductions-attributable-tax](es-irpf-deductions-attributable-tax.md); national credits, the deduction ledger and any calculation "above" outside this catalogue are in [es-irpf-deductions](es-irpf-deductions.md#the-base). Basque and Navarre residents use [es-foral-income-tax](es-foral-income-tax.md); an IRNR taxpayer or an art. 93 incoming worker does not take these credits.

## Rates, bands and thresholds

All amounts are EUR. `BI` means the specified sum of general and savings **taxable income before base reductions** (base imponible); `BL` means base liquidable after the relevant reductions. Use the definition in each row: an income limit based on BI cannot be replaced with gross salary, BL or take-home pay. The table year is 2026 unless a transition is stated.

### Extremadura: 2026 income gates, family and care

**Apply the 2026 income limits, then test the rural exception.** Ley 2/2026 final provisions 2 and 7 apply the amended IRPF rules from **1 January 2026**. For the credits identified below, the ordinary income ceiling is `general BI + savings BI ≤ 30,000 individual / 55,000 joint`. The 2025 manual's 19,000/24,000 and 28,000/45,000 limits are not the 2026 limits. Art. 12 bis waives the BI ceilings for **arts. 3, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, 11 ter and 11 quater only** if the taxpayer habitually resides in a municipality/minor local entity with **fewer than 3,000 inhabitants** and is either a legal large-family member or a legally separated/unmarried ascendant with two children, no maintenance entitlement and the whole descendant minimum. This is not a blanket waiver for every credit: new art. 3 bis birth/adoption and art. 11 nonies veterinary relief retain their own ceilings ([DL 1/2018 art. 12 bis](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2018-8159#a1-14); [Ley 2/2026](https://www.boe.es/buscar/doc.php?id=BOE-A-2026-17839)).

Birth/multiple-event relief requires cohabitation and entitlement to the national descendant minimum; divide equally when several cohabiting taxpayers qualify. Split a married couple's foster entitlement on individual returns, and split the qualifying registered-partner fostering entitlement. The additional provision preserves specified simple/permanent/pre-adoptive fostering continuing from the former legislation; do not silently substitute a new adoption credit for an old foster entitlement ([arts. 3–4 and additional provision](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2018-8159#da-5)).

Disabled-relative care requires disability at least 65% or the statutory judicial/representative-support status; uninterrupted cohabitation for at least half the tax period, certified by competent public services; and the relative's general/savings income, **including exempt income**, no more than twice annual IPREM, with no legal obligation to file Wealth Tax. At the same kinship degree, share equally; the closer degree takes priority. If only one cohabiting claimant meets the BI ceiling, that claimant takes the full credit. The 2025 AEAT guidance measures twice IPREM as `2 × 8,400 = 16,800`; the preserved state IPREM amounts and the 2026 budget extension support that continuing monetary benchmark, while the taxpayer's own BI ceiling is the separately amended 2026 figure ([art. 5](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2018-8159#a5); [AEAT disabled-relative guidance](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/ayuda/manuales-videos-folletos/manuales-practicos/irpf-2025-deducciones-autonomicas/comunidad-autonoma-extremadura/cuidado-familiares-discapacidad.html); [state IPREM provision](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2022-22128#da-90), [2026 budget extension](https://www.sepg.pap.hacienda.gob.es/sitios/sepg/es-ES/Presupuestos/PGE/PGE2025Prorroga/Paginas/PGE2025Prorroga.aspx)).

Childcare requires work and relevant Social Security/Mutuality registration by both parents and entitlement to the descendant minimum. Eligible care includes a registered household worker or authorised nursery, leisure centre, urban camp, sports centre, play centre or similar facility; retain invoices for centre costs. Share entitlement for the same descendants and respect the 400 **family-unit** cap. Widowhood relief excludes a taxpayer convicted by final judgment of gender violence against the deceased spouse ([arts. 6–7](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2018-8159#a6)).

For the fixed ALS credit, the spouse/registered partner must cohabit or be dependent on the claimant; an ascendant/descendant must generate the corresponding minimum. This is separate from the grant-neutralisation credit, whose mixed general/savings, minimum-spillover and base-reduction method is supplied in the attributable-tax worksheet under The base, with its precise signed-income boundary. Veterinary costs require statutory pet ownership or the qualifying assistance-dog user/legal-representative relationship, an authorised provider and an invoice in the taxpayer's name. Cover health-related consultations, vaccination, parasite treatment, diagnostic tests, surgery, medicines, hospitalisation, emergencies and specialist care; exclude merely aesthetic, recreational or accessory expenditure without a health need. The 200 ceiling is not added to a separate 100 ceiling ([arts. 11 septies–nonies](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2018-8159#a1-22)).

| Young first-home mortgage interest | 25% of eligible interest with a **base** cap 1,000, hence maximum ordinary credit 250; under 36 at accrual, first habitual home and ordinary BI ceiling/limited rural-family exception | 2026 | [Art. 11 quater](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2018-8159#a1-15) |

Art. 8 requires regional residence, first-home status, normally age under 36 at accrual, the statutory protected-home status outside the qualifying rural route and its BI test. A terrorism victim, or in statutory priority the qualifying spouse/partner or cohabiting children, uses the same relief without the age ceiling; this is not a second simultaneous art. 8 credit. Apply the acquisition/habitual-home/rehabilitation and wealth-growth rules incorporated as effective **31 December 2012**, but use the regional 6%/10% and its express interest exclusion. The historical BOE revision published 28 December 2012 already displays the repeal effective January 2013; the linked October revision preserves the relevant pre-repeal home text. Current national abolition does not abolish this regional incorporation ([arts. 8 and 13](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2018-8159#a8); [historical art. 70](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2006-20764&p=20121031&tn=1#a70)).

Rent requires one of: age under 36, legal large-family status, the specified two-child sole-ascendant situation, or disability at least 65%/qualifying judicial-support status. In a joint return, the age condition can be met by one spouse or the relevant parent. The property must be the taxpayer's actually occupied regional habitual home, with rental transfer tax paid or exemption justified; no home-investment deduction entitlement that year; and neither the taxpayer nor another family-unit member owning full title or a real use/enjoyment right in another home within **75 km**. Apply its BI ceiling/rural-family exception and share the deduction equally where several taxpayers qualify for the same property ([art. 9](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2018-8159#a9)).

The empty-home landlord must own/usufruct the regional property, have had it empty for at least a year before the contract, evidenced by art. 85 imputed property income, let it as the tenant's habitual home for at least three years, hold ownership/usufruct in **no more than three homes** counting any ownership percentage, and declare the letting as property income rather than business income. Garages/storerooms are excluded from that property count. Apply relief to the claimant's declared reduced net income, not gross rent or an assumed flat profit percentage. A tenant's death or other statutory habitual-home exception can preserve the three-year condition. Recalculate prior claims on breach; the law also excludes relief where a relevant tax-control procedure ends in regularisation ([art. 9 bis](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2018-8159#a9-8)).

Rural rental rehabilitation excludes a property-development or rental business within LIRPF art. 27. The property must be in an eligible municipality/minor local entity below 3,000 at **31 December**, or a qualifying differentiated settlement in the official regional census. Apply the VAT art. 20.Uno.22 rehabilitation definition and maintain habitual-home letting to non-spouse/non-ascendant/non-descendant/non-third-degree relatives for five following years. For non-owner-caused vacancies, retain evidence that it is unoccupied, available and advertised on property portals or through agencies; annual offered rent cannot exceed **30% of the municipality's latest INE mean household income at accrual**. Apply the statutory day proportion rather than assume uninterrupted full-year credit. Include qualifying rehabilitation financing costs, net of hedge receipts; exclude recognised public aid. AEAT directs aid reductions at both total and annual base levels and allocation across rehabilitation years for a one-payment grant. Allocate joint rehabilitation cost by ownership unless another allocation is proved. Breach triggers recapture ([art. 9 ter](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2018-8159#a9-9); [AEAT rural rental guidance](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/ayuda/manuales-videos-folletos/manuales-practicos/irpf-2025-deducciones-autonomicas/comunidad-autonoma-extremadura/inversiones-rehabilitacion-viviendas-zonas-rurales-alquiler.html)).

**Rural rental rehabilitation: grant ledger and completion-year day worksheet.** Establish the same claimant/property's eligible rehabilitation/finance costs and recognised public aid from invoices, financing/payment records and the award/certified-year schedule. Following [AEAT's exact base instructions](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/ayuda/manuales-videos-folletos/manuales-practicos/irpf-2025-deducciones-autonomicas/comunidad-autonoma-extremadura/inversiones-rehabilitacion-viviendas-zonas-rurales-alquiler.html), first subtract the full recognised grant from total qualifying project investment, then apply the180,000total ceiling; do not subtract the grant again from an already net total. Separately subtract the grant share attributable to the current rehabilitation year from that year's eligible payments, then apply the9,040annual base cap per return and the remaining project-total ceiling. A single grant payment must be allocated across the actual rehabilitation years; the cash receipt date does not put the whole grant against that year's costs. The published instruction does not specify universal equal-year or cost weights. Use the supported award/certified-year allocation and reconcile its shares to the single grant; do not invent a favourable split where those documents do not establish one.

**Documented two-year grant example.** A sole claimant has14,000of independently eligible paid rehabilitation costs in2025and14,000in2026. A recognised12,000grant is paid once in2026; the award and certified-year schedule establish6,000attributable to each year's eligible costs. These year shares are stated documentary facts of this fixture, not a universal rule that every two-year grant is divided equally. Total net project investment is28,000−12,000=**16,000**, below180,000. Current2026net payments are14,000−6,000=**8,000**, so the annual base is8,000, assuming the total ceiling is not previously consumed and no other property uses the return's annual cap. Without this annual grant subtraction,14,000would instead hit9,040. Do not deduct all12,000from2026payments, which would give2,000, or treat16,000as the current annual base. The2025year's net-cost allocation is not proof that its credit was claimable before completion or that an unused2025amount carries into2026.

**Actual day framework, explicitly bridged from2025implementation.** The [official completion instructions](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/ayuda/manuales-videos-folletos/manuales-ayuda-presentacion/irpf-2025/10-cumplimentacion-irpf-deducciones-autonomicas/10_10-extremadura/10_10_15-inversiones-rehabilitacion-zonas-rurales.html) ask separately for finish date, actual rented days and offered-but-vacant days after completion. In the public [RentaWEBOPEN2025, version1.19](https://www2.agenciatributaria.gob.es/wlpl/PARE-RW25/OPEN/index.zul?EJER=2025&TACCESO=COLAB), a fictional individual with10,000eligible annual investment and works finished31March2025received **986.18** for200rented+75offered days: `15% × min(10,000,9,040) × 200/275`. All275days rented instead gave1,356. With8,000annual net investment and the same200/75days, it gave **872.73**. A1January2025finish with300rented+64offered gave1,117.58; entering300+65was rejected because the day total must equal the days **after** completion. These actual separators support annual cap before the fraction, advertised vacancy outside the numerator, and the after-completion denominator rather than365. This is an inference for2026from the observed2025implementation of the unchanged9ter/agency instructions, not an observed2026return campaign or an expressly printed algebra ruling.

Apply that bounded framework to the2026grant fixture with works completed **31March2026**. Assume1April–17Octoberinclusive are **200**actual habitual-home letting days, and18October–31Decemberinclusive are **75**non-owner-caused vacant days, all unoccupied, available and advertised on property portals/agencies on qualifying terms. The tenant's early departure must preserve the statutory habitual-home status under an evidenced qualifying necessary-move exception; the landlord must continue the required five-year letting/advertised-vacancy undertaking and recapture on breach. For this example, assume the municipality's latest actual INE mean household income at accrual is an independently established30,000, so the annual advertised-rent ceiling is9,000; a700monthly offer annualises to8,400and passes. These are hypothetical verified-input assumptions, not a claimed income statistic for a named municipality. Preserve the real31Decemberpopulation/census evidence, dated INE release and offer terms, lease/nonrelationship/habitual-use evidence, dated finish certificate, rent/keys/advertisement records, proof the gap is not owner-caused, declared property income, grant allocation and sufficient regional quota. The credit is `round_to_cents(15% × 8,000 × 200/275)` = **872.73**. Counting advertised vacancy as actual rent would give1,200; using365would give657.53. Advertising preserves the qualified-gap treatment; it does not turn a vacant day into an actual rented day in this observed method.

**Timing boundary retained.** The same2025simulator rejected a31December2024completion with an explicit requirement that works finish in2025. Current9ter/agency text also speaks of annual rehabilitation/finance payments and the following five-year maintenance period, without an express completion-year-only entitlement sentence in the provisions read. The supplied positive example finishes in the claim year and avoids that conflict. Treatment of payments or financing in a different year from completion, pre-completion claims, zero post-completion days, and unearmarked/unequal grant-year weights remain precisely unconfirmed; no automatic carry, denial or blanket zero is substituted. The existing municipality, rehabilitation definition, five-year conditions and ownership/cost-allocation rules remain applicable.

The rural **own-home** credit is distinct: art. 11 bis requires acquisition/rehabilitation from **22 March 2022**, the commencement date of Ley 3/2022, and a municipality/minor local entity below 3,000 at 31 December. Include eligible acquisition costs, loan principal/interest and financing costs net of hedge receipts, with the cumulative and annual base caps. Reduce for prior relieved home investment and exempt reinvestment gain as the statute specifies; relief starts only after the required previous-investment/reinvestment threshold is exceeded. Former matrimonial-home payments can remain eligible where common children and the custodial parent continue using it as their habitual home. Apply the net-wealth increase test excluding finance costs and unrealised changes in still-held assets. Occupy permanently within twelve months and maintain habitual residence for three years, subject to the listed necessary-move/death exceptions. Construction must finish within four years of starting investment; enlargement must add permanently habitable area. Rehabilitation must principally reconstruct the structure/façade/roof or analogous elements and exceed 25% of relevant acquisition/market value excluding land, with the two-year lookback. This route does **not** acquire art. 8's first-home/age/BI conditions or art. 9 ter's differentiated-settlement extension merely because all three concern rural housing ([art. 11 bis](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2018-8159#a1-12); [Ley 3/2022 final provision 5](https://www.boe.es/buscar/doc.php?id=BOE-A-2022-5853)).

**Choose the own-home route before stacking claims:** arts. 8 and 11 quater cannot be combined with art. 11 bis. The tenant credit separately requires no home-investment deduction entitlement. Apply the overall regional tax floor of zero; these rules do not create a refund for unused ordinary regional credit ([art. 12](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2018-8159#a1-4)).

### Extremadura: investment, gifts and moving residence

| Credit | Amount or calculation | Tax year | Official source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Qualifying company subscription | 20% of eligible investment, annual credit cap 4,000 | 2026 | [Art. 11](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2018-8159#a1-3) |
| Money gifts for qualifying culture/art or sports sponsorship | 20%, **combined cap 500 per taxpayer** | 2026 | [Art. 11 quinquies](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2018-8159#a1-18) |
| Move habitual residence to Extremadura | 50% of regional gross tax in the move year and next two years; **75% instead** where the moving taxpayer is under 36 at accrual; maintain residence continuously at least three years | 2026 | [Art. 11 sexies](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2018-8159#a1-19) |

Subscriptions must be formation/capital-increase subscriptions in an SA, SL, labour SA/SL or cooperative with regional registered/tax domicile and genuine non-patrimonial economic activity. The investor plus spouse and relatives through the third degree cannot exceed **40% of capital or votes on any day of the calendar year**. Hold the investment for at least three years and perform no executive/management functions. A formation company must employ one full-time employee or at least two part-timers whose combined hours meet full time, registered and maintained at least 24 months. A capital-increase company must have been formed within the preceding three years; increase average employment during the following two tax years by at least one qualifying equivalent against the preceding twelve months, then maintain that increase for a further 24 months. Use a public deed identifying investors and amounts; breach requires repayment with interest in the breach-year return ([art. 11](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2018-8159#a1-3)).

Gifts must be **money**, pure, simple, irrevocable and documented in writing. Eligible cultural beneficiaries have regional tax domicile and carry out the statutory film/audiovisual/multimedia, performing, visual/design, publishing, heritage, traditional-cultural or applied-art activities in the region. Eligible sponsored sportspeople have regional tax domicile and the statutory sporting activity. Apply the exclusion for donations by qualifying participants and specified relatives where the individual/aggregated ownership threshold is at least **50%**; gifts to the donor's spouse/partner/ascendant/descendant sportsperson are excluded. Cultural recipients report donor identity, amount and eligible activity CNAE electronically in **January of the following year** ([art. 11 quinquies](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2018-8159#a1-18)).

The move must establish regional residence under national art. 72 and have the statutory family/personal/professional or non-business expenditure/investment basis. If family-unit members previously resided in different communities, the member with the greatest individually attributed BL must move to Extremadura for joint-return relief; the moving member must meet the under-36 test for 75%. Recapture if the conditions fail. Do not import a salary-only restriction or an income ceiling absent from art. 11 sexies, and calculate the percentage on regional gross tax before the final zero floor ([art. 11 sexies](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2018-8159#a1-19)).

## Worked example

### Extremadura tenant under 36: ordinary cap against the rural cap

Hypothetical 2026 facts: an individual Extremadura resident aged **30** files individually with general plus savings BI **24,000**, within the **30,000** individual ceiling. She pays **4,000** of rent for her habitual home and meets the other conditions this Guide states for art. 9. The credit is `30% × 4,000 = 1,200`. With an ordinary home the cap gives **1,000**; if the home is in a qualifying municipality below **3,000** inhabitants, the **1,500** cap applies and the credit is **1,200** ([DL 1/2018 art. 9](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2018-8159#a9)).

```oa-check
name: Extremadura rent, ordinary 1,000 cap against rural 1,500 cap
inputs:
  rent: 4000
steps:
  nominal: rent * 0.30
  credit_ordinary_home: min(nominal, 1000)
  credit_rural_home: min(nominal, 1500)
expected:
  nominal: 1200.00
  credit_ordinary_home: 1000.00
  credit_rural_home: 1200.00
```
