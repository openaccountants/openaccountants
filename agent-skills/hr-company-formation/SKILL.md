# Croatia Company Formation & Entity Choice v0.4

> **General reference only.** This skill is general tax/accounting reference material for AI-assisted workflows. It has not been reviewed for any specific person's facts, documents, elections, deadlines, residency, filing status, or local procedures. Do not rely on it to file, pay, amend, or take a tax position without review by a qualified professional in the relevant jurisdiction.

## Scope

- **Who this is for.** Founders choosing between a d.o.o., a j.d.o.o. and a d.d. in Croatia, and the steps from founding documents to court registration, the OIB, the first FINA filings and the first profit tax return, for 2026. Not yet approved: no accountant has signed this version.
- **Currency.** Croatia has used the euro since 1 January 2023. The Companies Act (Zakon o trgovačkim društvima, ZTD) states its capital figures in euro since the amendment in Narodne novine (NN) 114/22. All figures below are in euro.
- **Out of scope.** Profit tax computation ([hr-corporate-income-tax](hr-corporate-income-tax.md)), VAT ([croatia-vat-return](croatia-vat-return.md)), payroll ([croatia-payroll](croatia-payroll.md)), and the choice between an obrt and a company ([croatia-tax-optimization](croatia-tax-optimization.md)).

## Ask the client first

1. Which form: d.o.o., j.d.o.o. or d.d.? How many founders, and how many management board members (a j.d.o.o. allows at most five members and one board member)?
2. How much share capital, and will any share be paid in kind rather than in cash?
3. Will the founders sign in person at a notary, remotely with a notary, or remotely through the court register website without a notary?
4. Was the company registered before 1 January 2023 (euro alignment duty)?
5. Does the company use the calendar year as its business year (FINA and PD dates)?
6. For every founder/member and proposed director: relevant convictions, asset-freeze measures, occupational bans, public debts/refused debt certificates, unpaid-wage listing, qualifying related-company interests and recent insolvent-company roles? Obtain the declarations and official checks in Step 1 before treating formation as available.
7. If a sole founder leaves cash capital unpaid, what appropriate security and supporting document will cover the balance before application? Can the parties meet any required physical identity/capacity check on a remote route?

## The method, step by step

### Step 1. Choose the form and the capital

**Eligibility gate before proceeding.** Capital and fees do not establish a right to register. Check each founder/member and proposed director against ZTD arts. 62.a, 68(3), 160(2), 239(2), 386(2) and 423. Membership/founding is barred for the specified money-laundering or terrorist-financing conviction while its legal consequences continue, or an international asset-disposal restriction while it remains in force. Directors must be fully legally capable and meet the wider art. 239(2) criminal/occupational-ban and restriction tests. Use the current declarations and official register checks; an unresolved bar stops the proposed appointment or formation and goes to a Croatian notary/lawyer. Sources: [NN 18/23 arts. 2, 4, 7, 11, 15](https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2023_02_18_305.html), [ZTD baseline art. 239(2)](https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2011_12_152_3144.html).

Also check the founder/director and the related entities specified in art. 62.a: a d.o.o. interest of 25% or more, sole membership of a d.d., or personal-liability partnership membership. Public debts administered by the Tax Administration/refusal of a debt certificate and the published unpaid-wage list can bar formation; related debt can bar director appointment. The one-year look-back also bars specified owners or responsible persons of a company deleted in insolvency with unpaid public debts. These are substantive eligibility checks, separate from the court's certificate-related pause. The NN 136/24 carve-out is **public debt below EUR 30.00**, not at EUR 30.00 and not a clearance of unpaid wages, convictions, restrictions or every other bar. Resolve its precise application to the person and related entities before proceeding. Sources: [NN 130/23 arts. 4, 5, inserting ZTD 62.a and extending 68(3)](https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2023_11_130_1794.html), [NN 136/24 art. 2](https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2024_11_136_2248.html), [Court Register Act art. 43(6)-(8), NN 123/23](https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2023_10_123_1697.html).

- **Limited liability company (d.o.o.).** Društvo s ograničenom odgovornošću, the standard private limited company. It is founded on a company contract signed by all founders as a notarial act or a private document certified by a notary, which can be done electronically; a single founder gives a founding statement before a notary instead (ZTD art. 387(1), (2)).  _(Companies Act, NN 34/22, https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2022_03_34_398.html)_
- **Simplified limited liability company (j.d.o.o.).** Jednostavno društvo s ograničenom odgovornošću. A company with at most five members and one management board member can be founded this simplified way, using the notary's standard record forms annexed to the Act (ZTD art. 390.a(1)).  _(Companies Act, NN 40/19, https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2019_04_40_816.html)_
- **Joint-stock company (d.d.).** Dioničko društvo, used for larger or public ventures. Minimum share capital EUR 25,000 (ZTD art. 162). Lowest nominal amount of a share EUR 1 (art. 163(2)).  _(Companies Act, NN 114/22, https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2022_10_114_1712.html)_
- **Minimum share capital, d.o.o.** EUR 2,500 (ZTD art. 389(2)). Each business share is at least EUR 10 and a whole multiple of ten (art. 390(1)).  _(Companies Act, NN 114/22, https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2022_10_114_1712.html)_
- **Minimum share capital, j.d.o.o.** EUR 1, with a lowest share of EUR 1, in whole euro (ZTD art. 390.a(3)). Shares are paid only in cash, and the registration application is filed only after all shares are paid in full (art. 390.a(4)).  _(Companies Act, NN 114/22 and NN 111/12, https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2022_10_114_1712.html, https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2012_10_111_2392.html)_
- **Legal reserve, j.d.o.o.** The company must put one quarter of its annual profit, less the previous year's loss, into a legal reserve (ZTD art. 390.a(5)). The reserve may be used to raise capital or to cover losses. Only a formal capital increase to EUR 2,500 or more disapplies art. 390.a(3)-(6); reaching that amount in reserves or cash does not do so automatically. General company rules and the capital-increase decision/registration still apply. The company may keep its j.d.o.o. name instead of adopting a d.o.o. name (art. 390.a(7)).  _(Companies Act, NN 111/12, https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2012_10_111_2392.html)_

### Step 2. Pay in the capital

- **Paid-in capital at incorporation, d.o.o.** Before registration each founder must pay at least one quarter of each share taken in cash, and total cash payments must be at least one quarter of the share capital (ZTD art. 390(2)). On the EUR 2,500 minimum that is EUR 625. NN 34/22 deleted the old sentence that required the rest within one year. Shares paid in kind must be paid in full before registration, and if the assets are worth less than the share when the application is filed, the difference is paid in cash (art. 390(3), wording of NN 130/23 art. 45). Cash goes to the company's account at a Croatian credit institution, or to a notary's special deposit account, and the bank or notary confirms it (art. 390(6)).  _(Companies Act, NN 40/19, NN 34/22 and NN 130/23, https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2019_04_40_816.html, https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2022_03_34_398.html, https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2023_11_130_1794.html)_
- **Remote formation with a notary.** Shares are paid only in cash, to a temporary sub-account of the state budget or to the notary's special deposit account, and payment and proof of payment can be made remotely (ZTD art. 397.f(2)).  _(Companies Act, NN 34/22, https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2022_03_34_398.html)_
- **Sole founder with unpaid cash capital.** Before applying, give appropriate security that the unpaid part will be paid and attach its supporting document (ZTD art. 394(2), (5) point 4). The EUR 625 quarter-payment calculation does not remove this gate or the chosen route's requirements. NN 130/23 art. 50 changes terminology in art. 394 but preserves this obligation.  _(https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2011_12_152_3144.html; https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2023_11_130_1794.html)_

### Step 3. Sign the founding documents

- **Incorporation, notarisation.** A j.d.o.o. is founded on the notary's record form (ZTD art. 387(3)). Founders' proxies need a power of attorney certified by a notary (art. 387(5)). A d.o.o. or j.d.o.o. can also be founded remotely with a notary, who checks identity by video link (art. 397.f).  _(Companies Act, NN 34/22, https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2022_03_34_398.html)_
- **Incorporation, remote without a notary.** A d.o.o. or j.d.o.o. can be founded remotely through the court register website using the standard forms annexed to the Act (ZTD art. 387(4)). The application then goes only electronically, through the court register website or the START system (Court Register Act art. 52.a(1)). The standard forms annexed to the Act (annexes 1 to 6) cap the formation costs the company bears at EUR 250; on the j.d.o.o. forms (annexes 1 to 4) the cap is also no more than the share capital.  _(Companies Act NN 34/22, NN 114/22; Court Register Act NN 40/19, https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2019_04_40_817.html)_
- **Remote identity/capacity fallback.** Remote availability is conditional. A notary may require physical attendance where identity, capacity or authority is doubtful; the register court can require appearance before a notary for those checks (Court Register Act arts. 39(4), 52.d(2)). On the remote-without-notary route, ZTD art. 397.e(8) can require a notary-filed application within 15 days if the court suspects identity/capacity/authority, anti-money-laundering or other illegality; failure to comply means the proposal is treated as withdrawn. Sources: [Court Register NN 34/22](https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2022_03_34_397.html), [ZTD NN 34/22 arts. 19, 20](https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2022_03_34_398.html).

### Step 4. Register at the commercial court

- **Incorporation, registration.** The company is entered in the court register kept by the commercial courts (sudreg.pravosudje.hr). The company must register at least one e-mail address within three months of being entered (Court Register Act art. 38.b(1)).  _(Court Register Act, NN 40/19, https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2019_04_40_817.html)_
- **Incorporation timeline.** The court must decide within five working days of a complete and well-founded application with its attachments, unless there are justified reasons for longer (Court Register Act art. 53(1)). The period restarts when an incomplete application is corrected (art. 53(3)). It does not run while the court obtains the tax-debt certificates on persons with unpaid public debts (arts. 40.a, 53(4)). Notary and bank steps come on top.  _(Court Register Act, NN 40/19 and NN 123/23, https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2019_04_40_817.html, https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2023_10_123_1697.html)_
- **Court register fees (from 1 June 2026).** Set by the Decree on the Tariff of Court Fees, NN 50/26. An application filed electronically through e-Notar or START pays half the application fee (Tariff no. 25(3)), and the entry fee is halved when the court delivers its decision electronically through e-Notar or START (Tariff no. 26(6)).  _(Uredba o Tarifi sudskih pristojbi, NN 50/26, https://porezna-uprava.gov.hr/Propisi/Propis/uredba-o-tarifi-sudskih-pristojbi/5809)_

### Step 5. OIB and older companies

- **Tax identification number (OIB).** The Tax Administration (Porezna uprava) assigns an OIB by default. A legal person seated in Croatia gets it on the basis of its entry in the official register (Act on the Personal Identification Number art. 8(1), (3)). Croatian citizens and foreign persons with a reason to be tracked in Croatia also hold an OIB (art. 5).  _(Zakon o osobnom identifikacijskom broju, NN 60/08, https://porezna-uprava.gov.hr/Propisi/Propis/zakon-o-osobnom-identifikacijskom-broju/3300)_
- **Older companies and the euro.** A d.o.o. or j.d.o.o. registered before 1 January 2023 must align its capital and shares with the euro rules at its first change of founding act or contract, status change, or change of a share. The duty has no deadline and does not arise on a share transfer (NN 114/22 art. 21(3), as replaced by NN 130/23 art. 117). The court register makes the alignment entries free of charge (NN 114/22 art. 23).  _(https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2023_11_130_1794.html)_

## Worked cases

**Case 1. Three founders, d.o.o. with EUR 3,000 capital, all cash.** Shares of EUR 1,000 each (whole multiples of ten, at least EUR 10: ZTD art. 390(1)). Before registration each founder pays at least one quarter of his share, EUR 250, and the cash paid in total is at least one quarter of the capital, EUR 750 (art. 390(2)). The application goes through e-Notar, so the fees are half of EUR 30 and half of EUR 80 (Tar. nos. 25(3), 26(6)): EUR 55.

```oa-check
name: d.o.o. EUR 3,000, three equal cash founders, minimum paid in and e-Notar fees (2026)
inputs:
  capital: 3000
  founders: 3
  fee_application: 30
  fee_entry: 80
steps:
  - share_each: capital / founders
  - min_paid_each: share_each * 0.25
  - min_paid_total: max(min_paid_each * founders, capital * 0.25)
  - fees_electronic: fee_application / 2 + fee_entry / 2
expected:
  share_each: 1000.00
  min_paid_each: 250.00
  min_paid_total: 750.00
  fees_electronic: 55.00
```

**Case 2. Decision: j.d.o.o. or d.o.o. for a sole founder with EUR 500 to put in.** A d.o.o. needs EUR 2,500 capital with at least EUR 625 paid in (ZTD art. 389(2), 390(2)); a j.d.o.o. can be founded with the EUR 500 fully paid (art. 390.a(3), (4)). On paper the j.d.o.o. court fees are EUR 20 against EUR 110 (Tar. nos. 25, 26). The cost is the legal reserve: with a first-year profit of EUR 12,000 and no earlier loss, EUR 3,000 must be put into the reserve and cannot be paid out as dividend (art. 390.a(5)). Choose the j.d.o.o. when the founder cannot pay in EUR 625 now or wants the lower fees; choose the d.o.o. when profit will be paid out early and the reserve would lock cash in. Either way, profit tax and filing are the same (Profit Tax Act art. 28, 35).

**Registration condition for this comparison:** the founder/director eligibility gate in Step 1 applies to both options. For a sole-founder d.o.o. with only EUR 625 paid, the remaining EUR 1,875 (computed: EUR 2,500 - EUR 625) must be covered by appropriate security before application, with the supporting document under ZTD art. 394(2), (5) point 4. Cash, security and the chosen formation route must all be feasible before choosing the d.o.o.; the arithmetic below alone is not registration clearance. The original EUR 500, EUR 625, EUR 2,500 and EUR 12,000 inputs remain unchanged.

```oa-check
name: Sole founder, j.d.o.o. vs d.o.o., paper fees and first-year reserve (2026)
inputs:
  profit: 12000
  prior_loss: 0
  fees_doo: 110
  fees_jdoo: 20
steps:
  - reserve_jdoo: (profit - prior_loss) * 0.25
  - fee_saving_jdoo: fees_doo - fees_jdoo
  - min_cash_doo: 2500 * 0.25
expected:
  reserve_jdoo: 3000.00
  fee_saving_jdoo: 90.00
  min_cash_doo: 625.00
```

## When to refuse or refer

- Stop formation or appointment where a statutory founder/member/director bar applies or eligibility remains unresolved; refer to a Croatian notary/lawyer. Do not treat a small-debt exception, a certificate-related timing pause or available online forms as clearance of other bars.
- Notary fees, bank charges and the FINA public-disclosure fee are not in this skill (see Not yet confirmed); give no figure for them.
- Do not tell a foreign founder whether a court interpreter is needed at the notary: no official text was read.
- Branches of foreign companies, partnerships (j.t.d., k.d.), cooperatives and status changes (mergers, conversions) are out of scope: refer.
- A company that missed the euro alignment, or a d.d. with old kuna capital, needs a lawyer or notary for the alignment decision.

## Filing and payment

- **Annual financial statements filing.** Every company files with FINA (Financijska agencija). The balance sheet, income statement and additional data for statistics are due by 30 April for the previous calendar year (Accounting Act art. 50(1)). Statements for public disclosure, with the audit report where one is needed, are due within six months of the year end, so 30 June for a calendar year (art. 47(5)). A company whose business year is the calendar year meets both duties together only by filing the disclosure statements and the additional statistical data simultaneously by 30 April (art. 50(2)). A company that had no business transactions in the year and holds no assets or liabilities in its books files a statement of inactivity by 30 April instead (art. 47(9)).  _(Accounting Act, NN 85/24, 145/24, 151/25, 59/26, https://porezna-uprava.gov.hr/Propisi/Propis/zakon-o-racunovodstvu-redakcijski-procisceni-tekst/3223)_
- **Fine for not filing.** A company that misses the FINA public-disclosure or statistical filings is fined EUR 1,320 to EUR 13,270, and the responsible person EUR 660 to EUR 2,650 (Accounting Act art. 60(1) points 38 and 40, (2)).  _(Accounting Act, https://porezna-uprava.gov.hr/Propisi/Propis/zakon-o-racunovodstvu-redakcijski-procisceni-tekst/3223)_
- **Annual corporate tax return.** Profit tax is 10% where revenue in the period is up to EUR 1,000,000 and 18% above it (Profit Tax Act art. 28). The PD return is filed within four months of the period end, so by 30 April for a calendar year, and the tax shown is due on the last day of that period (art. 35(1), (2)). Every taxpayer files it electronically (art. 35(3)). Advances are paid monthly, by the end of the month for the month before, at the previous period's liability divided by its number of months (art. 34(1)). A newly started company pays no advances until its first return (art. 34(3)). See hr-corporate-income-tax.  _(Zakon o porezu na dobit, consolidated to NN 151/25, https://porezna-uprava.gov.hr/Propisi/Propis/zakon-o-porezu-na-dobit-redakcijski-procisceni-tekst/1641)_

## Completion checklist

- Founder/member and proposed director eligibility resolved under Step 1, with declarations/official checks and any narrow debt exception documented; no unresolved statutory bar.
- Form chosen and capital meets the minimum; shares are whole multiples of ten (d.o.o.) or whole euro (j.d.o.o.).
