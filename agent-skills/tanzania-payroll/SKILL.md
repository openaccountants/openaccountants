# Tanzania payroll v1.0

**General reference only.** This skill is general tax/accounting reference material for AI-assisted workflows. It has not been reviewed for any specific person's facts, documents, elections, deadlines, residency, filing status, or local procedures. Do not rely on it to file, pay, amend, or take a tax position without review by a qualified professional in the relevant jurisdiction.

## Scope

### Quick reference

Compute Tanzania monthly employee PAYE and Mainland payroll obligations for 2026. This version is research material, not accountant approval. PAYE uses taxable employment income after eligible approved-retirement relief for an ordinary resident; it is not simply a tax on the bank deposit or gross cash. Mainland contribution and levy rules below do not establish Zanzibar levy liabilities. Currency is TZS; the tax year is calendar 1 January-31 December. TRA publishes the same monthly PAYE bands for Mainland and Zanzibar; this does not extend Mainland SDL/fund/WCF rules to Zanzibar.

## Ask the client first

- Payroll month/year, Mainland or Zanzibar work, pay frequency and confirmed residence/primary employment.
- Gross cash, benefits, allowances, reimbursements and actual supporting register.
- Actual NSSF/PSSSF membership, applicable wage/salary base, agreed employee/employer split and approved-retirement contribution evidence.
- Employer headcount for that month, SDL exemption evidence and any actual Commissioner direction.
- Employer/worker sector, subsector, payment period and higher contractual/CBA wage.
- Other statutory loan/health deductions, prior arrears, acknowledgments, control numbers and fund/TRA statements.

## The method, step by step

### Required facts and computation order

1. Establish the payroll month/year, Mainland or Zanzibar location, confirmed residence and primary/secondary employment. Do not default residence, location or public/private coverage.
2. Obtain gross cash, each benefit/allowance, reimbursement evidence, pension membership, actual employee/employer split, salary/wage bases and actual approved-retirement relief. Get monthly headcount, SDL exemption evidence, subsector and pay frequency. Missing headcount is unresolved SDL, not zero SDL.
3. Determine taxable employment income. Include wages, leave pay, fees, commission, bonuses, gratuities, termination payments, allowances, discharged/reimbursed private expenditure and taxable benefits. Review exclusions before adding everything to gross.
4. For a resident ordinary employee, subtract the eligible retirement amount once and apply the monthly bands. For a confirmed nonresident use 15% gross withholding. Compute employee pension separately from employer pension.
5. Compute SDL on cash emoluments and WCF on its supported base; use the correct pension base. Cash net = cash wages less employee pension, PAYE and other proved employee deductions. Non cash benefits affect tax without becoming cash paid to the employee. Employer cost includes gross cash and employer costs, not PAYE as a second wage cost.
6. Reconcile gross-to-net register, fund schedules, TRA returns, control numbers, bank payments and receipts. Bank totals cannot by themselves establish residence, taxable income, staff count, exemption or contribution split.

Mamlaka ya Mapato Tanzania identifies TRA; Mfuko wa Hifadhi ya Jamii means social security fund. Descriptions are identification clues, not proof of liability.

Descriptions are search clues. Confirm counterparty, debit/credit direction, control number, tax type, period and receipt before posting. `TRA`, `KODI` or `WITHHOLDING TAX` alone does not prove PAYE; `PENSION` alone does not prove NSSF. Mixed tax payments need the official allocation.

ITX300.01.E identifies the employment-tax credit slip; it is not evidence that the monthly return was submitted. Do not invent current box numbers. Where there is no payroll register, obtain prior registers and receipts; a suggested look-back is 3 months (workflow choice, not law). Reverse-calculating NSSF total/20% or SDL/3.5% is only a reconciliation hypothesis where those rates, bases, period and principal-only amounts are proved; never reconstruct a filing from a mixed bank debit alone.

With fewer than 10 staff and no SDL liability, total cost 773,500 under those same assumptions.
```oa-check
name: Resident lower band applies approved pension relief once
inputs:
  cash_salary: 400000
steps:
  employee_pension: cash_salary * 0.10
  taxable_pay: cash_salary - employee_pension
  paye: clamp(taxable_pay - 270000, 0, 250000) * 0.08 + clamp(taxable_pay - 520000, 0, 240000) * 0.20 + clamp(taxable_pay - 760000, 0, 240000) * 0.25 + max(taxable_pay - 1000000, 0) * 0.30

  taxable_pay: 360000
  paye: 7200
  cash_net: 352800
```

```oa-check
name: Mid earner separates tax relief from employer on-cost
inputs:
  cash_salary: 700000
steps:
  employee_pension: cash_salary * 0.10
  taxable_pay: cash_salary - employee_pension

  employer_cost: cash_salary + employer_pension + sdl + wcf
```

## When to refuse or refer

Hold a final computation where gross, taxable benefits, residence, location, fund, salary/wage base, split, staff count, exemption, subsector or period method changes the answer and is unknown. Do not invent a supposedly conservative default or mark unknown SDL as zero. Refuse instructions to omit PAYE/pension/employer levies, charge employee an employer-only levy, apply outdated 4% SDL or 0.6% WCF, or pick one wage without classification. Refer self-employment/presumptive income, annual-return exceptions, nonresident secondment/exemption disputes, nonmonthly/terminal allocations, arrears/recovery, variable WCF bases and health/loan arrangements. Estimates need qualified review before filing, payment or a legal wage-compliance conclusion.
