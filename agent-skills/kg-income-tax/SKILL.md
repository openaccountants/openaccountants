# Kyrgyzstan personal income tax (2026)

> **General reference only.** This skill is general tax/accounting reference material for AI-assisted workflows. It has not been reviewed for any specific person's facts, documents, elections, deadlines, residency, filing status, or local procedures. Do not rely on it to file, pay, amend, or take a tax position without review by a qualified professional in the relevant jurisdiction.

Use this skill to calculate ordinary personal income tax (PIT), after classifying the individual and the income. The headline rate is not a universal rate for every worker or business.

## Who this applies to

Tax Code arts185-186 distinguish citizenship, residence, residence permits and income source. Kyrgyz citizens, and non-citizen residents with a Kyrgyz residence permit or kairylman status, fall within the worldwide-income rules. Non-citizen residents without that permit/status and non-citizens who are non-resident generally fall within the Kyrgyz-source rules. The foreign-branch employee exception in art186(1)(4) needs separate analysis. Payment abroad does not by itself remove Kyrgyz source.

Under art24, residence includes presence for at least 183 days in any consecutive 12-month period ending in the current tax period, or Kyrgyz government service abroad. The presence calculation includes specified treatment, business and study absences; diplomatic, consular and qualifying international-organisation personnel/family exclusions must be checked. A registered individual entrepreneur is outside this PIT charge except as tax agent, art185(3); do not apply this salary calculation to business profit.

There is no general progressive zero-rate band in art197. Relief operates through statutory exclusions and deductions, and salary floors can prevent deductions from reducing the total tax paid by the employer.

## The base

1. Identify the taxable income under arts185-187 and remove statutory non-taxable income before deductions. Use the calendar year under art188.
2. For ordinary salary, subtract documented standard deductions. At the employer, standard deductions generally require an employment relationship lasting at least 15 days in that month. Shorter employment and concurrent jobs permit only the social insurance contribution deduction, art200(7). Missing deduction/exemption documents mean withholding without that relief; art200(2)-(3) govern corrections.
3. Apply the published district/city average-wage floor under arts187 and200. Where it applies, the employee bears tax on the actual calculated base and the employer pays tax on the shortfall to the average wage from its own funds. Use cumulative actual months worked from the start of the year. Art187 exceptions include budget-funded pay, concurrent employment, designated junior support staff, specified housing/other cooperatives, disability groups and prison workers. Pro-rate the average for the specified incomplete-time cases. Cluster workers and agricultural cooperative/logistics employees have separate average-wage rules; refer rather than using the ordinary example.
4. Claim education and housing relief through the annual declaration with the required application and payment evidence. Housing relief excludes the specified non-close-relative free funding and interdependent-individual purchases; an heir may claim on death under art196(3). Arts195(5)/196(5) contain a separate asset-amnesty transition for pre-amnesty relationships; do not use an old transitional filing window for a current-year claim.
5. Multiply the resulting base by the applicable rate and reconcile tax already withheld. Do not subtract employer social contributions from the employee's salary tax base.

## Deadlines and filing

| Obligation | Due and condition | Official source |
|---|---|---|
| Ordinary mandatory individual annual declaration | Before 1 May of the following year, art106(6)(3); this includes specified income without an obligated tax agent. A salary-only taxpayer is not automatically required to file | https://cbd.minjust.gov.kg/112340/edition/latest/ru |
| Public officials covered by art107 | Before 1 April of the following year, art106(6)(2), rather than the ordinary individual date | https://cbd.minjust.gov.kg/112340/edition/latest/ru |
| Final self-assessed PIT | By the applicable unified annual declaration deadline, net of tax-agent amounts, art199 | https://cbd.minjust.gov.kg/112340/edition/latest/ru |
| Employer PIT remittance and report | Not later than 20 of the following month, arts202-203, for the relevant paid/received income | https://cbd.minjust.gov.kg/112340/edition/latest/ru |

Art106 lists mandatory declarants and special-regime exemptions; it also permits voluntary declarations, including education/housing claims. The old blanket individual April deadline is not used. Under art100, submit the prescribed unified annual declaration electronically through the tax authority's information system, stating TIN, addresses, period and tax obligation and signing as required. Retain the filed declaration, submission evidence, withholding reconciliation and payment evidence. Paper filing is limited to Cabinet-prescribed cases. Live portal screens and current field numbers have not been verified here; this is a computation reference, not a completed return.

Art18 moves a non-working last day to the next working day. An art102 filing extension does not extend payment.

## Penalties and interest

Tax Code art82 charges ordinary late-payment interest at 0.09% per calendar day from the day after the due date, capped at 100% of the arrears. Check its exceptions, including force majeure, qualifying deferral, bankruptcy and reliance on official explanations; do not compound interest on sanctions/interest. Art163 separately addresses late declarations/reports and understatements. Determine the taxpayer and filing type before assessing a sanction; this skill does not treat all late reports as the same offence.

## Cross-border points

Use citizenship/permit/source together, not a residence-only worldwide-income shortcut. Check the actual treaty under art3 before giving relief or a foreign-tax credit. Foreign share-disposal income and the art197(4) holding-period/holiday conditions are outside the salary examples. An agent can remain liable for failure to withhold under art53(5).

## Two worked examples that recompute

Illustrative KGS inputs, not official wage statistics. Both examples assume ordinary-rate employment for a full eligible month, no dependants, documented employee social contributions, no special-rate status, and an applicable average-wage floor below the calculated base. The indicator is KGS 100 under Resolution1115-III. Salary and the contribution amount are example inputs; verify the contribution under the payroll skill first.

```oa-check
name: Ordinary salary with personal and social deductions
inputs:
  salary: 50000
  employee_social: 5000
  indicator: 100
steps:
  - personal: 6.5 * indicator
  - base: salary - employee_social - personal
  - pit: base * 0.10
  - net_salary: salary - employee_social - pit
expected:
  base: 44350
  pit: 4435
  net_salary: 40565
```

For an eligible current-year education claim with fewer than 3 dependants, suppose the already reconciled annual base before education relief is KGS 600000 and qualifying tuition paid is KGS 100000. The cap gives a deduction of KGS 60000; there is no carry-forward of the excess. This is the annual liability before crediting withholding, not an automatic refund.

```oa-check
name: Education payment exceeds ordinary annual cap
inputs:
  pre_education_base: 600000
  tuition_paid: 100000
steps:
  - deduction: min(tuition_paid, pre_education_base * 0.10)
  - taxable_base: pre_education_base - deduction
  - pit: taxable_base * 0.10
  - tax_reduction: pre_education_base * 0.10 - pit
expected:
  deduction: 60000
  taxable_base: 540000
  pit: 54000
  tax_reduction: 6000
```

## Ask the client first

- Citizenship, days present, residence permit/kairylman status and any treaty residence claim.
- Income sources, employer/agent identity, business registration and tax already withheld.
- Employment dates, concurrent jobs, district/city, hours worked and any floor/rate exception.
- Dependant details, contribution records and deduction/exemption documents.
- Education or housing agreements, payment evidence, related parties and current versus pre-amnesty period.
- Mandatory declaration category, special-regime status and prior submissions/payments.
