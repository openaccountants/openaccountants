# Bhutan Goods and Services Tax (2026) | v2.1

GST is in force from 1 January 2026. Classify the supply, importer or overseas-services route, registration and credit entitlement before calculating net GST. The June amendment changes specific exempt goods, not the general rate. [ss.2-5,ScheduleI](https://www.drc.gov.bt/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Consolidated-GST-Act-of-Bhutan-2020-and-amendment-thereof.pdf); [ss.12-18;ScheduleI](https://www.drc.gov.bt/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Consolidated-GST-Act-of-Bhutan-2020-and-amendment-thereof.pdf); [notification and replacementScheduleIV-C](https://www.drc.gov.bt/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/GST_Public_Notification_2026.pdf); [whole addendum](https://www.drc.gov.bt/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Addendum_GST_Public_Notification-2026.pdf)

## Who this applies to

The Act distinguishes the taxable domestic supplier, importer of goods, recipient of imported B 2 B services, and supplier or electronic-distribution platform for B 2 C services. A consumer’s purchase does not by itself make the consumer a registered supplier. The person liable and the supply’s place, timing and exemption must be established before computing. [ss.12-18;ScheduleI](https://www.drc.gov.bt/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Consolidated-GST-Act-of-Bhutan-2020-and-amendment-thereof.pdf); [ss.17-24, 26-37](https://www.drc.gov.bt/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Consolidated-GST-Act-of-Bhutan-2020-and-amendment-thereof.pdf)

## The base

Registration turnover aggregates economic-activity supplies and imported B 2 B services, excluding capital-asset disposals and a sale of an activity as a going concern. Aggregate an individual’s businesses irrespective of location or nature; a jointly owned business is treated separately from the owners. An approved quarterly filer must apply to return to monthly accounting if turnover exceeds or is likely to exceed the threshold. [rr.5-9, 285(18)](https://www.drc.gov.bt/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/GST-Rule-2026.pdf); [rr.68-71](https://www.drc.gov.bt/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/GST-Rule-2026.pdf)

For domestic supplies and imported B 2 C services, apply the statutory tax fraction to consideration: at the standard rate GST is consideration × 5/105, derived. Imported B 2 B services use consideration × 5%, under s.18. Goods imports use the s.23 customs-value base including the specified duty, freight, insurance and fees without double counting. Related-party and reimport valuation have separate rules. [ss.12-18;ScheduleI](https://www.drc.gov.bt/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Consolidated-GST-Act-of-Bhutan-2020-and-amendment-thereof.pdf); [ss.17-24, 26-37](https://www.drc.gov.bt/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Consolidated-GST-Act-of-Bhutan-2020-and-amendment-thereof.pdf)

Exempt and zero-rated supplies are different. Section 27 denies credit to the extent inputs are used for exempt supplies; ss.28-29 allow the zero-rated route and its credit consequences. Where a supply is both exempt and zero-rated, s.31 treats it as zero-rated. Do not label all essentials or exports without checking the relevant schedule and conditions. [ss.17-24, 26-37](https://www.drc.gov.bt/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Consolidated-GST-Act-of-Bhutan-2020-and-amendment-thereof.pdf)

The 25 June notification replaces ScheduleIV-C with exact BTC codes/descriptions for fresh butter, specified rice and edible oils, salt, wheelchairs and sanitary products. Use that attached schedule, rather than a blanket exemption for all food, all cooking oil or every wheelchair. The addendum states legal commencement on 18 May 2026; the instruction to stop charging from midnight on 25 June is an operational collection instruction. GST already paid on qualifying imports/purchases from the legal commencement is addressed through the notified input-credit adjustment and passing the benefit to consumers. [notification and replacementScheduleIV-C](https://www.drc.gov.bt/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/GST_Public_Notification_2026.pdf); [whole addendum](https://www.drc.gov.bt/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Addendum_GST_Public_Notification-2026.pdf)

Section 32 permits qualifying GST paid or liable to be paid for business taxable supplies/imports, with the required supporting evidence. For taxable imported B 2 B services, recognize the output obligation and claim the corresponding input only if the credit conditions are satisfied. Blocked credits in s.34 include entertainment outside its ordinary-business exception, clubs, lotteries and passenger-vehicle/directly related items; s.35 has a taxable-employee-remuneration exception. Mixed use is apportioned under s.36. Do not subtract every purchase invoice. [ss.17-24, 26-37](https://www.drc.gov.bt/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Consolidated-GST-Act-of-Bhutan-2020-and-amendment-thereof.pdf)

Compute output GST minus allowable input credit, plus or minus statutory adjustments. A negative amount is not an automatic immediate cash refund: apply the Act and Rules 64-67 carry-forward and refund conditions. Refund-application eligibility normally follows 3 consecutive monthly periods or 1 quarterly period (quantities in words on the page), with specified cancellation, group/division and cessation exceptions. [ss.38-55, 337(105)](https://www.drc.gov.bt/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Consolidated-GST-Act-of-Bhutan-2020-and-amendment-thereof.pdf); [rr.64-67](https://www.drc.gov.bt/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/GST-Rule-2026.pdf)

Historical boundary: GST commencement supersedes the former Sales Tax framework; it was not already operating for 2025 transactions. The standalone Excise Tax Act 2025 commenced on 1 January 2026 and repeals GST Act PartII and its ScheduleVI Excise Equalization Tax rates. Excise and customs must still be classified separately; no excise rate is certified by this GST skill. [ss.2-5,ScheduleI](https://www.drc.gov.bt/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Consolidated-GST-Act-of-Bhutan-2020-and-amendment-thereof.pdf); [ss.1-7](https://www.drc.gov.bt/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/Excise-Tax-Act-of-Bhutan-2025-2.pdf)

## Deadlines and filing

Use DRC’s BITS GST service for registration and returns, with the registered taxpayer identity and approved period. Assemble sales/output GST, import and B 2 B-service records, allowable input credits, exemptions, credit notes and adjustments; reconcile to the ledger before submission. File the period return and pay a positive net amount by the statutory filing/payment time, retaining the submission and payment acknowledgements. The final live box numbers and payment-reference screens were not read; stop before representing this as a screen-by-screen filing guide. [ss.38-55, 337(105)](https://www.drc.gov.bt/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Consolidated-GST-Act-of-Bhutan-2020-and-amendment-thereof.pdf); [ss.17-24, 26-37](https://www.drc.gov.bt/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Consolidated-GST-Act-of-Bhutan-2020-and-amendment-thereof.pdf); [registration and filing/payment sections](https://www.drc.gov.bt/easy-guide-for-businesses-and-consumers/)

Goods-import GST is paid at customs clearance, including when goods ought to have been declared if omitted. The Act permits return amendments that increase liability within 5 years, or reduce it within 1 year (periods in words on the page); late payment and other consequences can still apply. Registration applications have their own deadline above. [ss.38-55, 337(105)](https://www.drc.gov.bt/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Consolidated-GST-Act-of-Bhutan-2020-and-amendment-thereof.pdf); [ss.56-62;ScheduleII](https://www.drc.gov.bt/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Consolidated-GST-Act-of-Bhutan-2020-and-amendment-thereof.pdf)

Issue serially numbered tax invoices and retain relevant invoices, adjustment notes and customs evidence. Keep records for 5 years from the end of the relevant period (in words on the page), and longer when DRC requires it. The later GST Rules correction deletes the old period-end foreign-currency provision; do not use an earlier uncorrected rule as current instructions. [ss.209-212](https://www.drc.gov.bt/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Consolidated-GST-Act-of-Bhutan-2020-and-amendment-thereof.pdf); [whole correction notice](https://www.drc.gov.bt/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Notification_GST-Rule-Change-1.pdf)

## Penalties and interest

Unpaid GST attracts daily-accruing late-payment penalty at 15% per annum under ss.261-263 and ScheduleVII-A. It is levied on outstanding tax, not on late-payment penalties, administrative penalties or offence penalties, and an instalment arrangement does not remove it. Registration, return, information and record failures have separate statutory penalties; identify the actual failure and applicable schedule/rule before stating an amount. [ss.261-264;ScheduleVII-A](https://www.drc.gov.bt/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Consolidated-GST-Act-of-Bhutan-2020-and-amendment-thereof.pdf)

## Cross-border points

Separate goods imports, imported B 2 B services, and B 2 C services/platform collection. The B 2 B recipient’s liability is not discharged merely because the supplier is offshore, and the output/input treatment depends on the actual credit entitlement. Check zero-rating conditions for exports rather than applying an income-tax or customs classification. The GST BITS registration is not the separate income-tax TPN required for a non-resident PE/SEP. [ss.12-18;ScheduleI](https://www.drc.gov.bt/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Consolidated-GST-Act-of-Bhutan-2020-and-amendment-thereof.pdf); [ss.17-24, 26-37](https://www.drc.gov.bt/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Consolidated-GST-Act-of-Bhutan-2020-and-amendment-thereof.pdf); [whole notification](https://www.drc.gov.bt/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/Notification-on-Prepayment-of-Tax-for-the-IY-2026.pdf)

## Two worked examples that recompute

A standard-rated domestic invoice has GST-inclusive consideration Nu.105,000 and Nu.2,000 fully allowable input credit. The derived output GST is Nu.5,000 and net GST Nu.3,000. There are no adjustments, exempt supplies or blocked purchases. [ss.12-18;ScheduleI](https://www.drc.gov.bt/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Consolidated-GST-Act-of-Bhutan-2020-and-amendment-thereof.pdf); [ss.17-24, 26-37](https://www.drc.gov.bt/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Consolidated-GST-Act-of-Bhutan-2020-and-amendment-thereof.pdf); [ss.38-55, 337(105)](https://www.drc.gov.bt/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Consolidated-GST-Act-of-Bhutan-2020-and-amendment-thereof.pdf)

```oa-check
name: GST-inclusive invoice and allowable credit
inputs:
  consideration: 105000
  allowable_credit: 2000
steps:
  output_gst: consideration * 5 / 105
  net_gst: output_gst - allowable_credit
expected:
  output_gst: 5000
  net_gst: 3000
```

A registered taxable person receives taxable imported B 2 B services for Nu.100,000. Output GST is Nu.5,000. If the services are wholly used for taxable business supplies with full evidence and no blocked credit, the corresponding input is Nu.5,000 and net impact nil. If the same taxable import is used wholly for exempt supplies, that credit is denied and net impact is Nu.5,000. Both branches are derived; classify the import and use before choosing a branch. [ss.12-18;ScheduleI](https://www.drc.gov.bt/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Consolidated-GST-Act-of-Bhutan-2020-and-amendment-thereof.pdf); [ss.17-24, 26-37](https://www.drc.gov.bt/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Consolidated-GST-Act-of-Bhutan-2020-and-amendment-thereof.pdf)

```oa-check
name: Imported B2B service credit depends on use
inputs:
  consideration: 100000
steps:
  output_gst: consideration * 0.05
  taxable_use_credit: output_gst
  taxable_use_net: output_gst - taxable_use_credit
  exempt_use_credit: 0
  exempt_use_net: output_gst - exempt_use_credit
expected:
  output_gst: 5000
  taxable_use_net: 0
  exempt_use_net: 5000
```

## Ask the client first

- Supply/import type, date, place and exact goods code or service description?
- Registration identity, all-business turnover and forward expectations?
- Monthly period or written quarterly approval?
- Tax-inclusive consideration, related parties and customs value?
- Taxable/exempt/private use, invoices, blocked-credit facts and adjustments?
- June-transition purchases, GST paid, acknowledgements and any DRC notice?
