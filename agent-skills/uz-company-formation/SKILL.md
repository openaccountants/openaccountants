# Uzbekistan Company Formation and Entity Choice v0.1

> **General reference only.** This skill produces a formation checklist and working papers for professional review. It is not an incorporation approval, licence, tax registration or accountant attestation. All amounts are UZS unless stated.

## Who this applies to

Use for founders considering an ordinary Uzbek limited liability company (LLC), including foreign founders, and its initial compliance calendar. Establish the proposed activity, owners, registration date, licence requirements, tax regime and foreign-investment status before selecting a form or capital amount.

The current LLC Law, ZRU-1137, initially took effect on **22 July 2026**; its current consolidation includes a later **25 July 2026** amendment. Do not confuse the consolidation date with commencement. Its article 68 repeals the former LLC Law. For a formation before commencement, retrieve the law effective on that formation date rather than backdating the new provisions. [LLC Law, arts. 68 and 71 and commencement metadata](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/8151376).

An LLC is a company with capital divided into participants' shares and legal personality from state registration. A joint-stock company (JSC), private enterprise, or a foreign company's branch/representative office is a different route with its own governing law and authorisations. A branch or representative office is not a separate LLC merely because it has local premises. The old skill's additional-liability-company option is historical: ZRU-1025 removes that form and directs conversion measures; do not offer it as a new formation. [LLC Law, arts. 3 and 6](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/8151376); [corporate reform, arts. 43 and 45](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/7367738).

## The base

Prepare the decision to form, company name and registered location, business activity, charter capital, participant commitments and proposed executive body. Founders approve the charter and non-cash valuation unanimously. A sole founder approves the charter alone; where there is more than 1 participant, the founding agreement is also required. Obtain the founders' actual approvals rather than treating this checklist as an executed decision. [LLC Law, arts. 11–16](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/8151376).

Capital is the sum of nominal contributions. For each participant record commitment, cash/non-cash form, valuation evidence, amount already contributed, balance and charter due date. Check the maximum contribution period separately from the registration-service duration. A contribution of rights to use property needs its own valuation and term; early termination may trigger compensation under article 16. Do not count borrowed working capital automatically as a founder's paid contribution.

**Foreign-investment boundary.** The Investment Law's statutory definition uses the foreign-participation percentage. The older decree contains additional capital, activity and territorial criteria. Identify the particular registration status or incentive sought and record the provision that requires its criteria; do not impose UZS 400,000,000 on every foreign-owned LLC or promise an incentive from the percentage alone. The old wording “more than 15% held by a foreign legal entity” was inaccurate: the statutory percentage is **at least** 15%, and the definition is not confined to foreign legal persons. Special-regime eligibility is not determined by this skill.

## Deadlines and filing

For tax filing, payment and notice deadlines expressed in days, count working days unless the provision expressly says calendar days. If the final date is a statutory nonworking day, apply the next-working-day rule. [Tax Code, art. 5](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/4674902).

1. Choose the appropriate legal form and check whether the activity needs a licence, permit or notification. Registration itself does not authorise a licensed activity. Keep the licence/capital decision with the formation file. [LLC Law, art. 3](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/8151376).
2. Prepare the Uzbek-language founding documents and any transaction-specific documents shown by the service. Use the Ministry of Justice's public-services centre or the business-registration service at [my.gov.uz](https://my.gov.uz/ru/service/57). Authenticate, select business registration, enter the actual founder/entity details, attach the required documents and pay the fee the service calculates under its statutory fee schedule. Do not invent a universal fee.
3. Save the submitted application, state registration result/certificate and taxpayer identifiers. The Ministry's investor guidance describes the electronic certificate and registry entry. Check them against the signed charter and ownership schedule. [Official registration guidance](https://invest.gov.uz/ru/guide/permitting-procedures).
4. Open the bank account and complete tax/employer and any VAT obligations for the actual regime. The bank-account application and other ancillary services may be selected with registration; their completion is a separate check. From June 2026 the ordinary turnover/general-regime boundary changed, so use [corporate income tax](uz-corporate-income-tax.md) and [VAT](uzbekistan-vat.md), rather than an old universal turnover threshold. [Official registration guidance](https://invest.gov.uz/ru/guide/permitting-procedures); [PF-100, para. 2](https://lex.uz/docs/-8236831).
5. Diary contribution dates and the members' annual meeting. Keep books and financial statements under the Accounting Law: national standards apply as prescribed; IFRS obligations depend on the entity and applicable transition rules. PP-282's public-interest-entity register and earlier statutory obligations must be checked. Its new register operates from 2026; it does not automatically make every LLC an IFRS reporter. [Accounting Law, arts. 6, 10 and 25](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/2931251); [PP-282, paras. 2 and 5](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/7728927).

Financial statements go to the statutory recipients, including owners and relevant authorities. Small enterprises/microfirms have the annual-only exception in Accounting Law article 25; exact form, current submission channel and due date must be set for the entity's reporting framework. A tax return is not the annual accounts.

## Penalties and interest

Do not invent a fixed incorporation fine. Unpaid capital, unlicensed activity, defective filings and tax arrears have different consequences. The participant-limit rule expressly allows court liquidation in the conditions stated above. Escalate an unpaid contribution or proposed distribution for review; LLC Law articles 26–27 restrict distributions, including before full capital payment and where insolvency/net-asset tests fail. Exact violation-specific fines are outside this formation calculation.

## Cross-border points

Foreign ownership, a foreign enterprise's branch and a representative office are separate legal questions. Retrieve the foreign investor's identity/status, ownership chain, document language/legalisation requirements and exact activity before filing. The Investment Law permits foreign-investment enterprises to maintain bank accounts in Uzbekistan and abroad, subject to applicable law; that does not establish a tax exemption. [Investment Law, arts. 3 and 51](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/4664142).

Do not choose an LLC just to assume treaty protection, visa rights, VAT exemption or an unreviewed foreign-investment incentive. Route PE, treaty, withholding and complex foreign-office matters to the appropriate specialist working papers.

## Two worked examples that recompute

### Example 1: contributions and outstanding capital

Assume an ordinary, unregulated LLC registered after the new law's commencement with charter capital UZS 100,000,000. Two participants commit 60% and 40%; they have paid UZS 30,000,000 and UZS 15,000,000. Their charter dates are within the statutory contribution limit. This checks the capital schedule, not a minimum-capital approval. [LLC Law, art. 15](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/8151376).

```oa-check
name: uz-formation-capital
inputs:
  capital: 100000000
  a_share: 0.6
  b_share: 0.4
  a_paid: 30000000
  b_paid: 15000000
steps:
  a_commitment: capital * a_share
  b_commitment: capital * b_share
  a_balance: a_commitment - a_paid
  b_balance: b_commitment - b_paid
  total_balance: a_balance + b_balance
expected:
  a_commitment: 60000000
  b_commitment: 40000000
  a_balance: 30000000
  b_balance: 25000000
  total_balance: 55000000
```

The commitments total UZS 100,000,000, paid capital UZS 45,000,000 and the outstanding balance UZS 55,000,000. Do not describe the whole charter capital as already paid.

### Example 2: the participant limit changes the route

An existing LLC has 48 participants. A proposed admission of 3 would give 51. The result exceeds the statutory maximum by 1, so do not approve the proposal as automatically compliant; obtain a membership reduction or transformation plan within the statutory period. Admitting only 2 gives 50. [LLC Law, art. 8](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/8151376).

```oa-check
name: uz-formation-members
inputs:
  existing: 48
  proposed: 3
  alternative: 2
  maximum: 50
steps:
  proposed_total: existing + proposed
  excess: proposed_total - maximum
  alternative_total: existing + alternative
expected:
  proposed_total: 51
  excess: 1
  alternative_total: 50
```

## Ask the client first

Confirm the actual formation/transaction date, legal form, activity and licensing needs; founders and ownership chain; registered address; charter capital and contributions; foreign-investment status/incentive being claimed; staff and payroll obligations; tax registration/regime and turnover history; annual accounts framework and any PIE register entry. Do not file from approximate capital or a generic registration-time promise.
