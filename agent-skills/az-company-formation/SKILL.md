# Azerbaijan Company Formation & Entity Choice v0.3

> **General reference only.** This Guide is general tax/accounting reference material for AI-assisted workflows. It has not been reviewed for any specific person's facts, documents, elections, deadlines, residency, filing status, or local procedures. Do not rely on it to file, pay, amend, or take a tax position without review by a qualified professional in the relevant jurisdiction.

## Scope

Register an Azerbaijani commercial entity for 2026 and choose the correct formation route. This Guide covers MMCs, open and closed joint-stock companies, foreign branches and representative offices, and the individual entrepreneur. Use the [company formation workflow base](../global/methods/company-formation-workflow-base.md) to assemble the formation working paper. Tax computations belong to the linked Azerbaijan tax Guides; sector licensing, practical legalisation times and unresolved accounting-framework points require referral.

## Ask the client first

- Who are the founders and beneficial owners, and is any founder a foreign legal entity?
- Which activities and regulated licences are planned, and where will the business operate?
- Is an MMC, joint-stock company, branch or individual business needed, and what capital is committed?
- Can the founders and representative use the eligible electronic route and required signatures?
- What turnover, customer mix, staff, premises and cash/POS receipts are expected?
- When does trading start, and which accounting/reporting framework applies?

### 1. Select the entity and fund it

- **Most common entity type**: limited liability company (MMC), set up by one or more individuals and/or legal entities, with charter capital divided into shares set by the charter. Members are not liable for the company's obligations and risk only the value of their contributions _(Civil Code art. 87.1)_ [Official source](https://frameworks.e-qanun.az/46/f_46944.html).

- **Other entity types**: closed and open joint-stock companies (qapalı / açıq səhmdar cəmiyyəti); branches and representative offices of foreign commercial entities, which are registered like commercial entities _(State Tax Service, fees and costs page; Registration Law art. 7-1)_; and the individual entrepreneur (fərdi sahibkar), an individual doing business on their own account without forming a legal entity, who registers with the tax authority on an individual registration application _(State Tax Service, "How to start a business")_ [Official source](https://www.taxes.gov.az/az/page/biznese-nece-baslamali).

- **Minimum charter capital, LLC**: no minimum requirement for LLCs and cooperatives _(State Tax Service, fees and costs page)_. The capital may not be less than the amount that protects creditors, and regulated financial-market firms follow their sector laws _(Civil Code art. 90.1)_ [Official source](https://www.taxes.gov.az/az/page/rusumlarvexercler).

- **Sector minimums**: closed joint-stock company AZN 2,000; open joint-stock company AZN 4,000; non-bank credit organisation set up as a commercial entity AZN 300,000; credit union AZN 4,000; investment fund manager AZN 125,000; joint-stock or unit investment fund AZN 500,000; credit bureau AZN 2 million; new bank AZN 50 million; new insurer AZN 5 million (non-life) or AZN 10 million (life), new reinsurer AZN 20 million _(State Tax Service, fees and costs page; these are authority-published sector illustrations, not a complete licensing-capital assessment)_ [Official source](https://www.taxes.gov.az/az/page/rusumlarvexercler).

- **Paying in LLC capital**: if the charter sets no payment period, the founders pay the charter capital in full before registration. If the charter sets a period, it may not exceed 3 months (venture capital funds set up as an LLC are excepted) _(Civil Code art. 90.2)_. Once the capital is fully paid, the registry must be told within 5 working days _(Registration Law art. 5.8)_ [Official source](https://frameworks.e-qanun.az/46/f_46944.html).

### 2. Prepare and submit the registration application

- **Registration authority**: the State Tax Service under the Ministry of Economy; the application "on state registration of a commercial entity" goes to the tax authority with the other registration documents _(State Tax Service, "How to start a business")_ [Official source](https://www.taxes.gov.az/az/page/biznese-nece-baslamali).

- **Registration timeline**: a local-investment LLC registered electronically is registered at once on the speedy track, or within 1 day on the ordinary electronic track. Other commercial entities, branches and representative offices of foreign commercial entities, and the electronic registration of a foreign-investment LLC are registered within 2 days _(Registration Law art. 7-1)_. Where a founder of a foreign-investment LLC is a foreign legal entity, its registration documents (trade-register extract, registration certificate) are attached to the electronic application, legalised where consular legalisation is required _(Registration Law art. 7-2.1)_ [Official source](https://frameworks.e-qanun.az/5/f_5403.html).

- **State registration duty**: no state duty for the electronic registration of an LLC, local or foreign investment. On paper: AZN 15 for most legal entities, AZN 5 for agricultural entities, and AZN 300 for banks, exchanges, insurers, insurance associations and brokers, and branches and representative offices of foreign legal entities _(State Duty Law arts. 20.1-20.3, 20-1.3; Registration Law art. 4.3)_. Re-registration costs 50% of those amounts, a duplicate registration certificate AZN 3, and a register extract AZN 2 per page; changes to the state register are made free of duty _(State Duty Law arts. 20.4-20.6, 20-1.1)_. Notarising the application costs AZN 3 per signature. An "Asan İmza" or e-signature costs AZN 18 _(State Tax Service, fees and costs page)_ [Official source](https://frameworks.e-qanun.az/2/f_2860.html).

- **Core incorporation steps**: choose the tax system and the activity types in the registration application; for an electronic LLC, the founder fills in the electronic form, enters the first employment contracts and the beneficial owner, and signs the generated charter with an enhanced electronic signature _(Registration Law arts. 5-2.1, 5-2.2; State Tax Service, "How to start a business")_. Then register each business site (shop, café, workshop, warehouse) with the local tax office, open a bank account and, for cash takings, install a cash register _(State Tax Service, "How to start a business")_ [Official source](https://frameworks.e-qanun.az/5/f_5403.html).

### 3. Complete employment, tax and accounting setup

- **Post-registration setup**: the tax authority passes the registration to the State Social Protection Fund, where the employer is registered automatically. Each employee needs an employment contract completed through the applicable electronic employment procedure; the STS startup page still describes a written contract and notification. Use the current procedure in [azerbaijan-payroll](azerbaijan-payroll.md). Changes to the registered activity types or tax system must be reported to the tax authority within 40 days _(State Tax Service, "How to start a business")_ [Official source](https://www.taxes.gov.az/az/page/biznese-nece-baslamali).

- **Tax system in year one**: a new taxpayer applies the method it chose in its registration application. An existing taxpayer elects the simplified tax, or opts out of it, by 20 April each year and cannot change the choice before the end of the calendar year _(Tax Code art. 218.2)_. The simplified tax is 2% of gross receipts for a non-VAT business with taxable operations of at most AZN 200,000 in any rolling 12 months after applicable POS weighting, subject to the exclusions in art. 218.5 _(Tax Code arts. 218.1.1, 220.1)_. Detail and eligibility tests: [az-corporate-income-tax](az-corporate-income-tax.md), [azerbaijan-tax-optimization](azerbaijan-tax-optimization.md) [Official source](https://frameworks.e-qanun.az/46/f_46948.html).

- **Annual compliance, profit tax**: annual return by 31 March of the following year, with tax settled by the same date; quarterly advances within 15 days after each quarter, normally one quarter of the previous year's tax. The alternative prior-year tax-to-revenue method and first-year/no-prior-profit method must be assessed separately under arts. 151.2-151.5 _(Tax Code arts. 149.2, 151.1, 152.1)_. A simplified-tax payer files and pays quarterly by the 20th of the month after the quarter _(Tax Code arts. 221.2, 221.3)_. Detail: [az-corporate-income-tax](az-corporate-income-tax.md) [Official source](https://frameworks.e-qanun.az/46/f_46948.html).

- **Ongoing compliance, VAT and payroll**: a VAT-registered company files a monthly VAT return by the 20th of the following month and pays within the same period _(Tax Code arts. 177.1.3, 177.2, 178)_; registration rules: [azerbaijan-vat](azerbaijan-vat.md). Income tax withheld from salaries is transferred by the 20th of the following month, and the withholding return is filed by the 20th of the month after each quarter _(Tax Code arts. 150.3.1, 150.3.3)_. Contribution rates and payment dates: [azerbaijan-payroll](azerbaijan-payroll.md), [azerbaijan-social-contributions](azerbaijan-social-contributions.md) [Official source](https://frameworks.e-qanun.az/46/f_46948.html).

- **Late returns**: failing without grounds to file a required return for a period with taxable or exempt operations costs a AZN 40 financial sanction _(Tax Code art. 57.1)_ [Official source](https://frameworks.e-qanun.az/46/f_46948.html).

- **Accounting**: keeping accounts under the accounting standards and rules set by the Law on Accounting is mandatory _(State Tax Service, "How to start a business")_. Which framework applies (national standards or IFRS) and the filing date are on the Not yet confirmed line. [Official source](https://www.taxes.gov.az/az/page/biznese-nece-baslamali).

## Worked cases

### Electronic or paper MMC: separate capital from fees

An ordinary local MMC has agreed capital of AZN 1,000, two application signatures, and no regulated activity. Assume it must acquire the STS-listed AZN 18 signature product for the electronic route. The electronic state duty is zero, giving AZN 18 in identified fees. On paper, duty AZN 15 plus two signatures at AZN 3 gives AZN 21. Adding company capital gives AZN 1,018 versus AZN 1,021 in funding, not a tax deduction or a complete professional-fee quote. Capital is fully paid before registration unless the charter validly provides the permitted payment period. Sources: State Duty Law arts. 20.3, 20-1.3; Civil Code art. 90.2; STS cost page above.

```oa-check
name: Ordinary local MMC electronic versus paper identified funding
inputs:
  capital: 1000
  electronic_signature: 18
  paper_duty: 15
  signatures: 2
  notary_per_signature: 3
steps:
  - electronic_fees: electronic_signature
  - paper_fees: paper_duty + signatures * notary_per_signature
  - electronic_funding: capital + electronic_fees
  - paper_funding: capital + paper_fees
expected:
  electronic_fees: 18
  paper_fees: 21
  electronic_funding: 1018
  paper_funding: 1021
```

### Foreign branch paper costs

A foreign branch's paper application has three signatures. State duty AZN 300 plus 3 × AZN 3 gives AZN 309. If a non-exempt applicant separately requests a four-page registry extract, 4 × AZN 2 adds AZN 8, giving AZN 317 in those identified charges. The founder-copy exemption in art. 20-1.2 means this extra AZN 8 must not be charged for an exempt founder copy. Translation, legalisation, bank and professional costs are outside this illustration. Sources: State Duty Law arts. 20.1, 20.6, 20-1.2; STS notary cost page above.

```oa-check
name: Foreign branch identified paper and nonexempt extract charges
inputs:
  duty: 300
  signatures: 3
  notary: 3
  pages: 4
  extract_per_page: 2
steps:
  - application_charges: duty + signatures * notary
  - extract_charges: pages * extract_per_page
  - total: application_charges + extract_charges
expected:
  application_charges: 309
  extract_charges: 8
  total: 317
```

## When to refuse or refer

Do not promise the statutory processing period covers legalisation, bank onboarding, licensing or professional preparation. Refer financial-sector capital, venture funds, tax regime eligibility and accounting-framework questions to the relevant specialist. Do not treat a name reservation or company seal as mandatory without current official support; preserve those old topics as unresolved below. Do not submit documents, hire employees or make a tax election from this Guide alone.

## Completion checklist

Keep founder/beneficial-owner evidence, entity decision, signed charter, capital payment terms, duty calculation, application receipt and registry extracts in the formation working paper. Check premises, banking, employment and tax-calendar handoffs against the linked Guides. Record each unresolved item before recommending registration.
