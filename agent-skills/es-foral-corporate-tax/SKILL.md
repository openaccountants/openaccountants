# Spain: foral corporate tax for2026 v0.7

## Who this applies to

For 2026, the agreements use the EUR 12 million preceding-period operations threshold. Annualise operations for a short period and use estimated first-year operations until actual figures are known. Operations means VAT-exclusive consideration for business supplies, excluding the equivalence surcharge; it is not automatically accounting turnover or assets. At or below EUR 12 million, the ordinary single company pays the administration of its fiscal domicile. Above it, collection follows the current year's territorial operations, with the percentage rounded to two decimals. Calculate one company's taxable income under the selected governing law, then allocate its liability. Do not apply each territory's headline rate separately to its sales. [CE arts.14–18; CN arts.18–23.](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2002-9969), [Navarra Economic Agreement](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-1990-31117)

For a Basque-domiciled company, Basque law governs unless preceding operations exceed EUR 12 million and at least 75% took place in common territory. A common-territory company above EUR 12 million uses Basque law if at least 75% of preceding operations took place in the Basque Country; a common-domiciled fiscal-group member needs 100% there. Select the province under that province's art.2: domicile normally controls; if all Basque operations occurred outside the domicile province, use the sole operating province or the province with the larger share of the other two; a common-territory company coming into Basque law uses the province with the largest Basque operations share. Record all three provincial percentages, the common-territory percentage and preceding/current years separately. [CE art.14; B/A/G art.2.](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2002-9969), [Bizkaia NF11/2013](https://www.bizkaia.eus/documents/880307/15187815/ca_11_2013.pdf/5a463c1d-a59b-7e48-6429-80fd22f30bc6?t=1768908575887), [Álava NF37/2013](https://web.araba.eus/documents/d/araba/norma-foral-del-impuesto-sobre-sociedades_cas-3-pdf), [Gipuzkoa NF2/2014](https://www.gipuzkoa.eus/documents/2456431/8.0761374E7/NF%202-2014%20%282026%29.pdf/f1231a01-1f83-ee15-e569-7b9b84d44d54)

For Navarra, exclusive Navarra collection entails Navarra law. A company paying both Navarra and the State uses domicile law, except a Navarra-domiciled company with at least 75% preceding operations in common territory uses common law; a common-domiciled company with at least 75% in Navarra uses Navarra law, with the art.18 fiscal-group exception. Keep a case involving both Navarra and one or more Basque provinces for a specific agreement analysis rather than treating Navarra as a fourth Basque province. [CN arts.18–19.](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-1990-31117)

Build the operations ledger from the statutory connecting factors: goods generally where made available or where dispatch starts; transformed goods at the final transformation; industrial installations use the preparation/manufacturing place if installation cost does not exceed 15%; electricity at generating plants; immovable supplies and related services where the property is; ordinary services where performed from. Agriculture/fishing products, transport and leasing transport equipment have domicile rules; allocate foreign operations proportionately to the remaining operations. Customer address alone does not supply this analysis. [CE art.16; CN arts.20–21.](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2002-9969), [Navarra Economic Agreement](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-1990-31117)

Advance payments use the last return's allocation, subject to the agreement's change-notification routes; deduct the instalment actually paid to each administration from its share. File with every administration entitled to collection. The governing law determines the instalment method even where another administration collects part of it. A State-law company allocating tax to a foral treasury must therefore retain State instalment calculations. [CE arts.17–18; CN arts.22–24.](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2002-9969), [Navarra Economic Agreement](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-1990-31117)

For the Basque ownership test, apply the shareholder's relevant quantitative size and workforce conditions under article 13.5, while testing the taxpayer company's own economic operation separately under article 13.1–4. An ordinary trading company that meets all its own micro conditions is not disqualified merely because a 25% corporate shareholder is passive, if that shareholder satisfies the relevant quantitative tests and no separate group rule changes the figures. For example, with the shareholder's assets 1.5 million, operations below 2 million and zero employees, its passive activity alone does not deny micro status to the otherwise qualifying company. A 25% shareholder with both assets and operations above 2 million, or average staff reaching 10, fails the relevant micro quantitative test. Do not remove the taxpayer company's own 85%-business-income or other statutory conditions. Sources: the current article 13.5 of each Basque law linked above.

For the Basque 85%-business-income condition, trace qualifying revenue and the local passive-income exclusions and look-through participation conditions. The property-business threshold differs: Bizkaia art.13.4 requires an average of at least three exclusive full-time unrelated employees; Álava art.13.4 requires five, with permitted related-entity staff aggregation; Gipuzkoa art.13.4 and DA 10 refer to its PIT art.24.3, which requires at least one exclusive full-time employee and excludes the specified related people. Do not import one province's employment threshold into another. [B/A/G art.13.4; G DA 10; Gipuzkoa PIT NF 3/2014 art.24.3.](https://www.bizkaia.eus/documents/880307/15187815/ca_11_2013.pdf/5a463c1d-a59b-7e48-6429-80fd22f30bc6?t=1768908575887), [Álava NF37/2013](https://web.araba.eus/documents/d/araba/norma-foral-del-impuesto-sobre-sociedades_cas-3-pdf), [Gipuzkoa NF2/2014](https://www.gipuzkoa.eus/documents/2456431/8.0761374E7/NF%202-2014%20%282026%29.pdf/f1231a01-1f83-ee15-e569-7b9b84d44d54)

Before granting trading-company methods, test patrimonial status under B/A/G art.14 and N art.8.2. The Basque rules include the asset mix, the 90-day period, the 75%-ownership composition, exceptions for qualifying participation/business reinvestment and the treatment of inactive companies. The Basque patrimonial computation uses art.32's restricted expenses and the art.56.2 progressive savings scale; it is not the ordinary 20% or 24% computation. Navarra patrimonial entities lose the art.51.1.b reduced rates. The dedicated patrimonial calculation and restructuring/group regimes remain separate branches.

### Taxable-base reconciliation

Start with the accounting result, add back local non-deductible expenses, reverse exempt income and timing differences, replace accounting depreciation with allowed tax depreciation, and apply local special deductions, reserves and eligible carried losses in their statutory order. Navarra art.42 puts the art.43 loss reduction before the art.44 investment-reserve reduction. Preserve a tax-basis register for each asset and a year-by-year register of deferred amounts. This sequence comes from B/A/G arts.15–55 and N arts.14–46; a State-law adjustment cannot be assumed to exist under a foral law.

### Basque domestic-dividend fallback

In Bizkaia, Álava and Gipuzkoa, failing the full article 33.1 participation-exemption conditions does not automatically make a dividend fully taxable. For dividends from a **Spanish-resident payer**, article 33.2 ordinarily integrates **50% of the dividend** into the recipient's tax base; Gipuzkoa expressly includes direct or indirect receipts. Dividends from the specifically named general-insurance mutuals, social-provision entities, mutual-guarantee companies or associations instead have zero integration under that paragraph. This domestic fallback is not a 50% rule for foreign dividends and is not Navarra's separate method. Sources: B/A/G article 33.2, in the current corporate laws linked above.

Apply article 33.3 before either the full exemption or the domestic fallback. Its exclusions include a distribution deductible to the payer or an equivalent benefit, payer nominal taxation below 10%, dividends from profits generated during the payer's patrimonial periods, a patrimonial recipient, and the specified deductible pass-through under a securities contract (with the actual recipient's own statutory conditions). Bizkaia/Álava also have article 33.3(f)'s three-year restriction after the specified tax-neutral contribution by an individual/non-PE non-resident who lacked dividend double-tax relief; test its stated reinvestment, onward-distribution or disposal exceptions. Do not import that extra paragraph into Gipuzkoa's article 33.3. Retain the distribution's profit/reserve origin and make the required article 33.6 return disclosure.

For example, an ordinary non-patrimonial company receives EUR 10,000 on a 1% holding in an unlisted Spanish trading company, taxed at a nominal rate of at least 10%. Assume no deductible distribution, pass-through or other article 33.3 exclusion. The 1% holding fails the ordinary 5% full-exemption threshold, but article 33.2 integrates only `10,000 × 50% = EUR 5,000`. If the full dividend is in accounting profit, the reconciliation deducts EUR 5,000. At the ordinary 24% rate the resulting incremental gross quota is EUR 1,200 before other adjustments, credits and minimum tax; integrating the full dividend would give EUR 2,400, and full exemption would give zero. If an article 33.3 exclusion applies, the 50% fallback does not cure it.

### Ordinary receivable impairment

For an ordinary nonfinancial company, start with a genuine receivable impairment booked in the accounts; overdue status alone does not create an additional expense. Test the selected territory's timing and exclusions before leaving that expense in the tax base. Sources: B/A/G article 22 and article 54.3/6; N article 20.1 and article 15.5/6, in the current corporate laws linked above.

| Territory | Ordinary individual-debtor test | Separate timing extension |
|---|---|---|
| Bizkaia | By the **last permissible date for formulating annual accounts**, either six months have elapsed from contractual maturity, the debtor is declared bankrupt, the debtor or its company administrator/representative is prosecuted for concealment of assets, or collection is subject to the specified judicial claim, litigation or arbitration | Article 22.1 uses the accounts-formulation deadline; do not replace it with tax-year end or import Álava's additional extension |
| Álava | The same four alternatives, tested at the **last permissible accounts-formulation date** | Bankruptcy or the specified judicial/litigation/arbitration circumstance also qualifies when it arises **before the return-filing window begins**; this extension does not move the six-month age test to that later date |
| Gipuzkoa | The same four alternatives, normally tested at **tax accrual** | Bankruptcy or the specified judicial/litigation/arbitration circumstance also qualifies when it arises **before the return-filing window begins**; the six-month age test remains at tax accrual |
| Navarra | The same four alternatives, tested at **tax accrual**, under article 20.1.1º | Do not import a Basque accounts-formulation or pre-return-window extension |

The Basque article 22.2 exclusions cover public-body debts/guarantees, bank or mutual-guarantee cover, real security/title-retention/retention rights, credit insurance and expressly renewed or extended debts, with the stated exception for arbitration or litigation over the debt's existence or amount and the stated lost/impaired-security exception. Related-party impairments require judicially declared insolvency. Navarra article 20.1.2º has its own exclusions: public-body debts, public/bank/mutual guarantees and credit insurance (to the covered amount), real security except lost/impaired security, and express renewal/extension; apply its specified judicial/arbitral exception rather than assuming that six months overrides a guarantee. Its related-party exception requires bankruptcy **and judicial opening of liquidation**, not merely a bankruptcy declaration. Retain evidence of maturity, court events, guarantees and relationships for each balance. Special regulated financial entities follow the separate statutory route.

Basque article 22.3 permits a separate global impairment for qualifying article 13 micro, small and medium companies, limited to **1% of eligible closing debtor balances**. Remove balances already individually impaired under article 22.1 and those whose impairments are excluded by article 22.2(a)/(b). The **closing allowance balance**, not a fresh annual addition on top of the old balance, cannot exceed that ceiling. On leaving the eligible size category, later impairments are non-deductible up to the existing global-allowance balance, as article 22.3 provides. Navarra article 20.1.2º(g) excludes global customer/debtor risk estimates; there is no ordinary Basque-style 1% election in that provision.

For example, a calendar-2026 company books an impairment of EUR 10,000 on an unrelated, unsecured receivable due 31 August 2026, with no bankruptcy, prosecution or collection proceedings. Assume its applicable latest accounts-formulation date is 31 March 2027. Six months have elapsed by that date, so the ordinary individual age test is satisfied in Bizkaia/Álava, subject to the other conditions. At 31 December 2026 only four months have elapsed: Gipuzkoa/Navarra require a EUR 10,000 add-back for 2026 on these facts. A later qualifying year requires a tracked reversal of that add-back, not a second book expense.

For an eligible Basque SME with EUR 500,000 closing debtors, EUR 100,000 already individually impaired and EUR 50,000 excluded secured/related balances, the global ceiling is `(500,000 - 100,000 - 50,000) × 1% = EUR 3,500`. If EUR 2,000 of global allowance is already outstanding, the available increase is EUR 1,500, subject to the booked amount; it is not another EUR 3,500. Keep a tax register of individually deducted amounts, disallowed balances and the separate global allowance. Tax-deducted impairment recoveries enter the reversal year's tax base under B/A/G article 54.6 or N article 15.6; reconcile previously disallowed amounts so that the same expense or recovery is not counted twice.

### Basque ordinary disposal monetary correction

**Basque disposal monetary correction (art.40.9).** For positive income on disposal of tangible fixed assets, intangible fixed assets or investment property, including those assets subsequently classified as non-current assets held for sale, calculate the monetary-depreciation deduction before integrating the disposal income into the tax base. Ordinary property held as trading inventory does not enter this asset population. Use the selected province's **CIT**, not PIT, coefficient table for tax periods beginning during2026 in [the separate2026 PE/CIT table](es-foral-non-resident-tax.md#separate2026-pecit-monetary-correction-coefficients). Start from the last authorised legal revaluation, or acquisition if later; the following ordinary calculation assumes no previous legal revaluation.

1. Calculate the disposal income under the ordinary accounting-to-tax reconciliation, retaining any separate tax-depreciation reversals or other fiscal adjustments. If that disposal income is zero or negative, art.40.9 gives no monetary-depreciation deduction.
2. Multiply acquisition/production cost by its year coefficient, each improvement by the coefficient for its own year, and each year's **accounted depreciation** by that year's coefficient. Let `indexed_net = indexed_costs − indexed_accumulated_depreciation`.
3. Calculate `monetary_depreciation = indexed_net − unindexed_carrying_value_of_the_disposed_asset`. The statute subtracts the asset's **carrying value** here. The indexed net amount is an intermediate calculation, not a new general tax basis replacing the depreciation/fiscal reconciliation.
4. Deduct the resulting positive monetary depreciation, capped at the positive disposal income. The correction cannot create or increase a disposal loss. Continue the existing corporate base, loss, rate, minimum-tax and credit sequence on the resulting income. Apply a special statutory vehicle limitation only where that vehicle provision actually governs.

Example, applicable to the identical2026 coefficients in all three provinces: an ordinary investment building acquired2023 for100,000, first brought into use2024, has accounted and tax-deducted depreciation3,000 in2024 and3,000 in2025; dispose at the opening of2026 for net proceeds150,000, with no2026 depreciation, prior revaluation or other fiscal adjustment. Carrying value is94,000 and pre-correction income56,000. Indexed cost is100,000×1.071=107,100; indexed depreciation is3,000×1.040+3,000×1.020=6,180; indexed net is100,920. Monetary correction is100,920−94,000=6,920; corrected taxable disposal income is49,080. If the otherwise identical transaction has positive disposal income only5,000, deduct5,000 and integrate zero. If it produces a loss, deduct zero under40.9.

Authorities: [Bizkaia NF11/2013 art.40.9](https://www.bizkaia.eus/documents/880307/15187815/ca_11_2013.pdf/5a463c1d-a59b-7e48-6429-80fd22f30bc6?t=1768908575887), [Álava NF37/2013 art.40.9](https://web.araba.eus/documents/d/araba/norma-foral-del-impuesto-sobre-sociedades_cas-3-pdf), [Gipuzkoa NF2/2014 art.40.9](https://www.gipuzkoa.eus/documents/2456431/8.0761374E7/NF%202-2014%20%282026%29.pdf/f1231a01-1f83-ee15-e569-7b9b84d44d54). Coefficient timing: [B DF115/2025 art.2](https://www.bizkaia.eus/lehendakaritza/Bao_bob/2025/12/15/I-1336_cas.pdf?hash=7bb6a27105d5d87568e6537aa6283dc3), [A DF41/2025 art.2](https://www.araba.eus/botha/Boletines/2025/146/2025_146_03735_C.pdf), [G DF27/2025 DA2](https://egoitza.gipuzkoa.eus/gao-bog/castell/bog/2025/12/30/c2508991.pdf).

### Passenger-vehicle expense computation

For an ordinary non-exclusively-business car, use the actual eligible expense first and then the statutory ceiling. In Bizkaia/Álava the usual lease/depreciation deduction is the least of 50% actual expense, EUR 3,000 and 50% of the taxpayer's depreciation rate times EUR 30,000 (the last comparator applies where depreciated); use EUR 4,000/EUR 40,000 for the named BEV/REEV/FCV/FCHV electric vehicles. Other running expenses are 50%, capped at EUR 3,600. Financing separately uses 50% of interest multiplied by min(1, EUR 30,000/acquisition price), or EUR 40,000 for the electric class. Proved exclusive business use or the valid benefit-in-kind election permits the 100% route: EUR 6,000/EUR 30,000 depreciation or lease comparator, EUR 7,200 other costs, and the corresponding full proportionate financing; electric comparators are EUR 8,000/EUR 40,000. Apply person/year and partial-year conditions. ### Álava: the ordinary amount-threshold investment case

**Source-derived interpretation, limited to the amount gate.** Álava article 59.4 tests whether an actual investment is capable of generating an article 61–65 credit, separately from calculating and using that credit. For an ordinary company taxed at the general 24% rate, an otherwise qualifying new business machine meets that investment limb where the **only** failed credit-generation condition is article 61.3(d)'s aggregate amount threshold. This is the identified interpretation of Álava's own potential-investment wording and article 61.1/3, not a B/G article 33 bis transplant or an express enacted Álava deeming rule. Its own article 53.2 and operative regulation article 32 bis expressly require actual-credit eligibility and all conditions for the separate productive reserve; that different test is not imported into article 59.4. Do not extend this amount-only method to a private, nonbusiness, used, land, unproved or otherwise unsuitable asset. [Own NF 37/2013 articles 53/59/61](https://web.araba.eus/documents/d/araba/norma-foral-del-impuesto-sobre-sociedades_cas-3-pdf), [own DF 41/2014 article 32 bis](https://web.araba.eus/documents/d/araba/indice-reglamento-del-impuesto-sobre-sociedades-cas-5-pdf).

Assume taxable base EUR 100,000, unadjusted liquid quota EUR 24,000, current indefinite staff 1 against prior 2, no staff increase, no complementary-tax exclusion, no surcharge or other quota adjustment, no minimum-exempt credit and no five-period block election. A new business machine costing EUR 5,000 meets the other article 61 asset conditions. Relevant prior net assets of EUR 100,000 and no other investment make it fail the strictly-over-10%-or-over-EUR-5m credit threshold. Generated article 61 credit is zero. Under the stated source-derived investment interpretation, the floor is EUR 17,000 instead of EUR 19,000. A separately entitled, unexpired EUR 6,000 ordinary-bucket credit fits the EUR 8,400 article 67 capacity and leaves EUR 18,000; without the investment limb only EUR 5,000 could be used and EUR 1,000 would carry. With a separately entitled EUR 8,000 credit, use EUR 7,000 and carry EUR 1,000. The machine itself creates no EUR 500 credit. With no separate credit, quota remains EUR 24,000: 17% is a credit-use floor, not the ordinary tax rate or a cash refund. Actual credit-origin, expiry, asset/workforce and retention evidence remain necessary. [Own articles 59/61/67](https://web.araba.eus/documents/d/araba/norma-foral-del-impuesto-sobre-sociedades_cas-3-pdf).

The current 19% default for 2026–2035 follows [NF 21/2025 article 3.Quinto](https://www.araba.eus/botha/Boletines/2025/147/2025_147_03861_C.pdf), despite its separate permanent 17% amendment; [NF 3/2025 article 12.Cuatro/DF1](https://www.araba.eus/botha/Boletines/2025/044/2025_044_01182_C.pdf) supplies article 59.4's 2026 effect. Álava's [July 2026 draft 45/2026](https://irekia.araba.eus/documents/2908004/21155021/26DF45.docx/f6429a06-3775-1778-d5f0-32866ad43181?t=1785328228887) proposes a broader article 33 bis clarification, but enactment has not been established. The amount-only method above does not rely on that draft having legal effect and does not settle other failed-credit-condition populations, excluded taxpayers or block elections.

### Navarra ordinary calculation and 2026 hard floors

Apply the correct rate to `BL`, the base after reductions/losses, to obtain gross quota. Subtract eligible bonuses and international double-tax credits under arts.54–57. Let `Z = BL − bonuses/rate`. Art.53's starting minimum is `18%*Z` for a general-rate company (including the conditional 25%), `16%*Z` for small/qualifying art.51.2 entities, or `13%*Z` for microcompanies. Let `F` be international double-tax credits actually used; let `B=film/financing credits actually used + u×R&D/innovation credits actually used`, where `u=0.5` for general and `u=1` for art51.1(b)/51.2. Set `p=18%/16%/13%` and `H=13%/11%/10%` for general/small-or-art51.2/micro respectively. If `B=0`, compute `minimum=max(0,p×Z−F)`: art53.2.4's restriction on reductions3(b)/(c) does not top up a foreign-credit-only case. If `B>0`, compute `minimum=max(0,H×Z,p×Z−F−B)`. Only credits actually claimed enter B, not unused generated balances. Candidate claims must leave actual effective quota at least that minimum and satisfy art67's separate order/bucket constraints.

For a general company with Z100,000 and foreign credit10,000 alone, the computed minimum is8,000, not13,000. With foreign6,000 and film5,000, the minimum is13,000 and effective quota is28,000−6,000−5,000=17,000. With foreign16,000, liquid quota12,000 is already below13,000: leave film/R&D credits unclaimed if no positive claim can satisfy the result floor, preserving the foreign credit. This is the direct conditional application of art53.2.3(a) versus3(b)/(c) and ordinal4, not an asserted official worked ruling or a universal top-up of foreign relief ([Navarra art53](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2017-2356#ar-53), [art67](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2017-2356#ar-67)).

Apply arts.58,64.B and 66 credits under their combined 25%-of-liquid-quota ceiling (art.58 before activity has its exception), prior carry before current year. Follow with the unlimited credits in art.67.5's statutory order, while respecting the art.53 minimum and the film/R&D amounts used in its computation. Keep a reconciliation of generated credit, claimed credit, expired credit and 15-year carry. Finally deduct withholdings and instalments; refund an advance-payment excess. Do not copy the Basque 35%/70%/50% buckets or 30-year carry. [N arts.52–53,67–70.](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2017-2356)
