# Timor-Leste Tax Overview

**General reference only.** This is a 2026 orientation to ordinary taxes, checked against the cited provisions on 2026-11-15. It is not accountant approval or a complete return-preparation instruction. Establish the taxpayer, income type, residence and any special regime before computing.

## Who this applies to

Individuals, employers and ordinary businesses needing the correct Timor-Leste tax workflow. The tax authority is Autoridade Tributária Timor-Leste (ATTL); INSS administers social contributions. Tax amounts are computed in USD. A resident's ordinary gross income includes domestic and foreign sources; a non-resident's includes Timor-Leste sources. Wages subject to Wage Income Tax (WIT) are excluded from the ordinary annual income-tax base, and final withholding needs separate classification. [Tax Act arts. 28, 61 and 65](https://www.mj.gov.tl/jornal/public/docs/2019/serie_1/SERIE_I_NO_33_B.pdf).

Use [income tax](tl-income-tax.md) for individual residence, wages and non-wage computations; [corporate income tax](tl-corporate-income-tax.md) for legal persons, deductions and withholding; [payroll/social](tl-payroll-social.md) for employer filings and contribution bases; [indirect taxes](tl-vat-gst.md) for designated services, imported goods and excise; and [company formation](tl-company-formation.md) for SERVE registration. Petroleum contracts, treaty-protected operations, incentives and other exceptional regimes need a separate scope decision; ordinary headline rates do not establish their treatment.

The ordinary domestic indirect-tax workflow uses sales, specified-services and excise taxes. No enacted ordinary 2026 VAT rate, threshold or commencement was established by the official sources checked. The government's September 2026 release still describes a draft; the later parliamentary budget report expressly calls 5% VAT from 2028 a planning assumption dependent on legislation not yet presented. Neither the earlier 2027 target nor the later assumption is an operative tax. Do not extend this ordinary-domestic conclusion to petroleum/treaty regimes. [Government draft status](https://timor-leste.gov.tl/?lang=pt&p=52081); [Parliament report, printed pp. 103 to 104](https://www.parlamento.tl/storage/iniciativas/iniciativa-422-relatorio-e-parecer-do-oge-2027-1793262060.pdf).

## The base

Separate wages, business/property income, finally withheld receipts and exempt income before applying a rate. Ordinary taxable income is gross taxable income less permitted deductions, not turnover multiplied by the income-tax rate. Preserve separate wage-tax and social-contribution bases; an exclusion from the social base need not be excluded from WIT. Compute and credit instalments only under the applicable income-tax rules. Tax Act arts. 26, 28, 29, 61 and 64 and the INSS contribution-base source above apply.

Residence is a legal fact. The individual test normally totals 183 days in any 12-month period beginning or ending in the year, with the permanent-abode exception and separate government-posting limb, rather than only days within the tax year. A legal person has its own residence definition. Use the personal/corporate skills and documents; do not elect residence merely to obtain a lower rate. Tax Act art. 1 supplies these definitions.

The fiscal chronology is the 2008 Act, the 2019 amendment/republication and the 2023 amendment, followed by the applicable 2026 budget rules. Law 17/2023 art. 8 replaced the permanent-establishment definition; do not present the old fixed-place or service-day summary as the current definition. The June 2026 budget amendment adds an essential-food/fuel import exemption conditional on an emergency declaration linked to the specified fuel-price circumstances; it is not a standing exemption for every shipment. [Law 17/2023](https://www.mj.gov.tl/jornal/public/docs/2023/serie_1/SERIE_I_NO_32_A.pdf_); [Law 8/2026 arts. 2 and 7](https://www.mj.gov.tl/jornal/public/docs/2026/serie_1/SERIE_I_NO_22_B.pdf).

## Deadlines and filing

Identify the actual tax and return before choosing the deadline. ATTL's [documents/forms](https://attl.gov.tl/taxation-documents/) route to the relevant domestic return; its public site also links e-tax. Use the income, payroll and indirect-tax skills for form/field evidence and the actual payment route. An old form's line numbers or bank instructions do not establish a 2026 live interface. Retain the submitted return, receipt/reference, tax calculation, withholding records and payment proof; reconcile payment to the right taxpayer, tax type and period.

Annual income tax and monthly tax deadlines are in the table. Social declarations/payment have a separate timetable in the payroll skill. Continuing nil-return obligations can survive a month with no tax; cessation or relief must follow the relevant legal rule. Do not assume no payable tax means no return. Current 2026 return fields and approved procedural exceptions remain held below where not confirmed.

## Penalties and interest

This overview does not establish current penalty or interest amounts. Preserve the assessment, notice/service date, tax period and payment history and use the applicable procedural source before computing charges or an appeal date. A headline income-tax percentage is not a penalty rate; neither filing nor payment should be deferred merely because this overview does not calculate the consequences.

## Cross-border points

Test residence, source, registration/PE and the actual payment type separately. The amended PE definition concerns a duly registered branch or other permanent local representation through which a non-resident demonstrably conducts business in Timor-Leste. Treaty and petroleum cases require their actual agreements and current special legislation; the old broad 30% petroleum headline is preserved as a held issue below, not applied to every oil/gas business. Ordinary wage and business rules do not replace those regimes.

## Two worked examples that recompute

**Ordinary company: taxable profit is different from turnover.** Assume USD 50,000 taxable receipts and USD 30,000 permitted expenses, with no exempt/finally withheld income, special regime or foreign credit. Taxable income is USD 20,000 and ordinary company tax USD 2,000. Properly evidenced current-year instalments of USD 500 reduce the balance to USD 1,500. The resident individual's annual band is not a company deduction. The inputs are hypothetical; deductibility and credit eligibility must be established first.

```oa-check
name: tl-overview-company-profit-and-credit
inputs:
  taxable_receipts: 50000
  permitted_expenses: 30000
  company_rate: 0.10
  eligible_instalments: 500
steps:
  taxable_profit: taxable_receipts - permitted_expenses
  gross_tax: taxable_profit * company_rate
  balance: gross_tax - eligible_instalments
expected:
  taxable_profit: 20000
  gross_tax: 2000
  balance: 1500
```

**Ordinary full-month payroll: worker and employer amounts differ.** Assume a resident employee's USD 1,000 cash salary is both the entire WIT wage base and entire contribution base, with no excluded payment or special regime, and the employer does not qualify for relief. WIT is USD 50; worker social contribution USD 40; net cash USD 910. Employer social contribution is a separate USD 60, making employer cost USD 1,060. Do not deduct the employer contribution from the worker's pay or reduce WIT wages by the worker contribution. This example does not apply to a reduced-rate employer or a part-month period.

```oa-check
name: tl-overview-payroll-separate-bases
inputs:
  wages: 1000
  resident_monthly_band: 500
  wit_rate: 0.10
  worker_rate: 0.04
  employer_rate: 0.06
steps:
  wit: max(wages - resident_monthly_band, 0) * wit_rate
  worker_contribution: wages * worker_rate
  employer_contribution: wages * employer_rate
  net_cash: wages - wit - worker_contribution
  employer_cost: wages + employer_contribution
expected:
  wit: 50
  worker_contribution: 40
  employer_contribution: 60
  net_cash: 910
  employer_cost: 1060
```

## Ask the client first

- Tax year/period, actual residence evidence and legal form.
- Income types, source countries, accounting profit and final-withholding certificates.
- Wages, employment dates, benefits and contribution-base components.
- Employer headcount, nationality mix, filing/payment compliance and relief eligibility.
- Imports or designated services, monthly/associate receipts and exemption evidence.
- Existing registrations, actual current forms/notices and any petroleum, treaty or incentive regime.
