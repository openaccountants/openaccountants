# Belize business tax (companies and businesses) v1.0

> **General reference only.** This skill is general tax/accounting reference material for AI-assisted workflows. It has not been reviewed for any specific person's facts, documents, elections, deadlines, residency, filing status, or local procedures. Do not rely on it to file, pay, amend, or take a tax position without review by a qualified professional in the relevant jurisdiction.

**Quick answer.** Belize has no general net-profit corporate income tax. Every company, partnership and self-employed person carrying on business in Belize pays **business tax on gross receipts**, at the rate the Ninth Schedule of the Income and Business Tax Act (Cap. 55) sets for the activity, with a monthly return and payment due by the **15th day of the following month**. Since basis year 2020, income tax is charged only on employed persons (Cap. 55 s.6(2), as replaced by Act No. 40 of 2024); see [bz-income-tax](bz-income-tax.md). Tax year: calendar year 2026. Currency: Belize dollar (BZD). Authority: Belize Tax Service (BTS), filing on IRIS Belize.

## Who this applies to

- **In:** any person practising a profession or carrying on business in Belize: sole proprietors, partnerships (assessed as one entity), companies, and local and foreign consultants. Registration is required whatever the amount of revenue ([BTS Business Tax Guide, FAQ 1 and 2](https://media.bts.gov.bz/uploads/Business_Tax_Guide_20250929_fc95296232.pdf)).
- **Companies with foreign receipts:** BTS states that a company's taxable receipts are not limited to activities in Belize and include gains on the realisation of foreign real estate, assets of a foreign permanent establishment and instruments issued by non-residents (Cap. 55 s.108A(2)(j)), taxed at 5% of annual net gains under Ninth Schedule paragraph (s) ([BTS notice to companies, 19 August 2026](https://media.bts.gov.bz/uploads/Notice_to_Companies_on_Foreign_Income_0baa126b8c.pdf)).
- **LLCs:** limited liability companies with tax residence in Belize are subject to all Belize tax laws under the International Limited Liability Companies (Amendment) Act No. 29 of 2023. A single-member LLC is treated as a sole proprietorship, two or more members as a partnership, or the LLC may opt to be treated as a company ([BTS notice to LLCs, 29 May 2024](https://media.bts.gov.bz/uploads/Notice_to_Limited_Liability_Companies_008_bd20925785.pdf)).
- **Out (not business tax):** employees on employment income, including consultants who work for one employer for periods adding up to 60 days or more in the year (they are taxed under PAYE); receipts of Designated Processing Area businesses; Government of Belize note and bond interest; rental receipts under BZD 800 a month where rent is the only source of livelihood; and non-trading receipts of credit unions, public charities, public educational institutions, friendly societies, local authorities, religious bodies and statutory boards ([BTS Business Tax Guide, FAQ 3](https://media.bts.gov.bz/uploads/Business_Tax_Guide_20250929_fc95296232.pdf)).
- **Out of this skill:** petroleum operations (income tax at 40% of chargeable income from those operations, payable in US dollars, Cap. 55 s.6(3)(a) as replaced in 2024) and approved Designated Processing Areas (3% of net chargeable income, s.6(3)(b)): refer to a specialist. General sales tax is in [bz-vat-gst](bz-vat-gst.md); employer duties in [bz-payroll-social](bz-payroll-social.md).

## The base

1. **Receipts, not profit.** Business tax is a tax on total revenues or receipts, in cash or in kind, received or accrued or credited, without any deduction ([BTS Business Tax Guide, FAQ 1](https://media.bts.gov.bz/uploads/Business_Tax_Guide_20250929_fc95296232.pdf)). There is no deduction for costs, depreciation or losses, except the annual net gain computation for foreign gains.
2. **Exclude amounts collected for others:** general sales tax collected (a BZD 5,400 fee plus BZD 432 GST is BZD 5,400 of receipts), revenue replacement duty on imported fuel, bulk fuel supplier-to-supplier sales, accommodation tax collected under the Hotels Act, dividends, principal and interest within PIC groups, funds received as agent, and excise duty (guide items 15 and 16).
3. **Apply the rate for each activity.** A business with several activities applies each Ninth Schedule rate to the receipts of that activity. Commissions are the only receipts with two rates: 5% under BZD 25,000 a year, 15% at or above; a person near the line (more than BZD 2,000 a month) is assessed at the higher rate first, with an annual review and refund on request.
4. **Partnerships** are one entity, assessed on total partnership receipts, with each partner's share listed on the return. Professionals who are not accredited partners are salaried employees under PAYE.
5. **Rental only source of livelihood:** a person whose only livelihood is rent may file and pay every six months instead of monthly.
6. **Credits against the monthly payment:** contract withholding suffered may be deducted from the monthly tax payable. Business tax paid counts as a credit toward any income tax on the same chargeable income, and any excess is carried forward as an expense (Cap. 55 s.21 as replaced in 2024).
7. **Foreign gains of companies:** net gain = net proceeds less asset cost, less deductible capital losses of a like kind (which cannot exceed like-kind realised gains), taxed at 5% (BTS notice, 19 August 2026).
8. **Records:** keep books and supporting documents in Belize for six years unless the Director General allows earlier destruction (guide item 14).

## Penalties and interest

- Late or non-filing: 10% of the tax due per month or part month, minimum BZD 10, up to 24 months. Late payment: 1.5% a month interest. BTS's own example: BZD 200 of December tax due 15 January, filed and paid on 5 February, adds BZD 40 penalty (10% x 2) and BZD 6 interest (1.5% x 2), BZD 46 in total ([BTS Business Tax Guide](https://media.bts.gov.bz/uploads/Business_Tax_Guide_20250929_fc95296232.pdf)).
- Failure to file a return: fine on summary conviction up to BZD 10,000, or up to two years' imprisonment in default. Failure to file a nil return: BZD 10 a day up to BZD 10,000.
- Failure to remit contract withholding: greater of BZD 100 or 10% of the tax.
- Real estate statement not filed: fine up to BZD 5,000, or up to one year's imprisonment, or both.
- A company not carrying on business in Belize may apply for a Certificate of Tax Exemption; failing to apply by the due date carries a penalty of up to BZD 1,500, and failing to file during an exempted period is an offence with a fine up to BZD 10,000 (Cap. 55 s.106(12) to (14), inserted in 2024).

## Cross-border points

- Payments to non-residents bear final withholding at the rates in the table (dividends 15%, interest 15%, management fees, technical services and equipment rental 25%, insurance premiums 25%); CARICOM treaty residents get the reduced CARICOM rates.
- Double taxation relief now applies to residents of any treaty country, not only Commonwealth countries (Cap. 55 s.47 as replaced in 2024). BTS publishes the United Kingdom 1947 agreement and the Austria convention among its resources ([BTS tax resources](https://bts.gov.bz/tax_resources)).
- Foreign consultants doing business in Belize pay business tax like residents ([BTS Business Tax Guide, FAQ 2](https://media.bts.gov.bz/uploads/Business_Tax_Guide_20250929_fc95296232.pdf)).
- Companies must include gains on the foreign assets in s.108A(2)(j) (BTS notice, 19 August 2026).
- Transfer pricing: the only arm's length rule found in Cap. 55 (Revised Edition 2011) is for petroleum operations. There is no general transfer pricing regime in the text read.

## Two worked examples that recompute

**Example 1: consulting firm, monthly return (simple).** A Belize consulting firm (a profession, 6%) invoices BZD 48,000 in March 2026, excluding GST. Business tax for March = 48,000 x 6% = BZD 2,880, filed and paid on IRIS Belize by 15 April 2026.

```oa-check
name: Belize consulting firm, March 2026 business tax at 6%
inputs:
  receipts_ex_gst: 48000
  profession_rate: 0.06
steps:
  - business_tax: receipts_ex_gst * profession_rate
expected:
  business_tax: 2880.00
```

**Example 2: building contractor and the payer's withholding (edge case).** A contractor wins a BZD 500,000 building contract. Its business tax is 6% on the first 40% of the price and 1.75% on the remaining 60%: 200,000 x 6% = 12,000 plus 300,000 x 1.75% = 5,250, total BZD 17,250. The contractor's regular rate on the first slice is 6%, so the payer withholds 3% of each payment, BZD 15,000 over the contract, which is credited against the contractor's tax. If a return carrying BZD 200 of tax is filed and paid two months late, the extra is BZD 46 (the BTS example).

```oa-check
name: Belize building contractor, 40/60 split, contract withholding and late charges
inputs:
  contract_price: 500000
  first_share: 0.40
  first_rate: 0.06
  rest_rate: 0.0175
  withholding_rate: 0.03
  late_tax: 200
  months_late: 2
steps:
  - tax_first: contract_price * first_share * first_rate
  - tax_rest: contract_price * (1 - first_share) * rest_rate
  - business_tax: tax_first + tax_rest
  - withheld_by_payer: contract_price * withholding_rate
  - late_penalty: late_tax * 0.10 * months_late
  - late_interest: late_tax * 0.015 * months_late
  - late_extra: late_penalty + late_interest
expected:
  tax_first: 12000.00
  tax_rest: 5250.00
  business_tax: 17250.00
  withheld_by_payer: 15000.00
  late_penalty: 40.00
  late_interest: 6.00
  late_extra: 46.00
```

## Ask the client first

1. What activities produce the receipts (trade, profession, rent, commission, real estate, financial services, construction)? Each has its own Ninth Schedule rate.
2. Entity type: sole proprietor, partnership, company, LLC (and its election), IBC or foreign company?
3. Gross receipts for the year so far and expected for the year (refund tests of BZD 75,000 and BZD 25,000; commission line of BZD 25,000).
4. Any receipts or gains from outside Belize, and any payments to non-residents or CARICOM residents?
5. Is the person working for one payer for 60 days or more in the year (then PAYE, not business tax)?
6. Contract withholding suffered, monthly returns filed, and any arrears or notices from BTS.
