# Macau Company Formation and Entity Choice v0.1

> **General reference only.** These instructions support working papers. Obtain qualified local review before filing, paying or adopting a position for particular facts.


## Who this applies to

For choosing an ordinary Macau private limited company by quotas (Sociedade por Quotas, Lda.), a single-member Lda., an S.A., sole proprietorship, partnership or a foreign-company branch. The Lda. is the common vehicle described by IPIM. A sole proprietor bears business debts personally; company and partnership liability depends on the chosen legal form. Regulated activities require their own approval; incorporation is separate from authority to work. [article 174](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/99/31/codcompt/codcom0001.asp?printer=1); [entity-type table and footnotes, last updated 20 September 2022](https://www.ipim.gov.mo/en/invest-here/industrytype); [Q2 business forms and liability](https://www.ipim.gov.mo/en/investment-promotion/business-information/q-a); [Q19 work authorization separate from ownership](https://www.ipim.gov.mo/en/investment-promotion/business-information/q-a).

Collect the proposed activity, legal form, owners and ultimate ownership, cash/in-kind capital and payment timing, administrators, local premises, permits and staff before selecting a route. Special-sector capital requirements are outside these ordinary-company minima.

## The base

For an Lda., distinguish subscribed capital from money immediately payable. At the minimum capital, the paid-value floor leaves no ordinary cash deferral. At a higher capital, cash deferral may be possible within both the quota-level half limit and the paid-value floor. Capital is funding of the company, not an incorporation expense. In-kind values require the statutory identification/valuation report; the general IPIM statement that no capital-assessment documents are needed does not replace that rule. [article 359](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/99/31/codcompt/codcom0001.asp?printer=1); [article 361](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/99/31/codcompt/codcom0001.asp?printer=1); [article 201](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/99/31/codcompt/codcom0001.asp?printer=1); [article 202](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/99/31/codcompt/codcom0001.asp?printer=1); [article 203](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/99/31/codcompt/codcom0001.asp?printer=1).

A natural or legal person may establish a single-member Lda.; another single-member Lda. cannot be its sole member. Apply the private-company rules with the statutory adaptations. Dealings with the sole member are outside this formation computation: article 391 requires a written instrument and a necessary, useful or convenient corporate purpose, on pain of nullity, plus a prior report by an unrelated auditor confirming protection of the company interests and normal market conditions/price before the dealing. Obtain that separate review before proceeding. The current Portuguese consolidation supersedes the older English translation that mentions only individuals. [article 390](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/99/31/codcompt/codcom0001.asp?printer=1); [article 391](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/99/31/codcompt/codcom0001.asp?printer=1).

Administrators must have the capacity required by article 234; a corporate administrator appoints an individual representative. Articles 234 and 383 do not themselves impose a general administrator-residence condition. This is not permission for a non-resident to work: use the work-permit process. A foreign company with permanent Macau activity has separate registration, allocated-capital and habitually resident representative requirements. [article 234](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/99/31/codcompt/codcom0001.asp?printer=1); [article 383](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/99/31/codcompt/codcom0001.asp?printer=1); [article 178](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/99/31/codcompt/codcom0001.asp?printer=1); [Q19 work authorization separate from ownership](https://www.ipim.gov.mo/en/investment-promotion/business-information/q-a).

A local, identified registered office is required. IPIM accepts commercial offices, industrial-building space and shops, but not a post-office box as the registered office. Shareholder nationality is not generally restricted under the IPIM ordinary-company guidance; specific licensed activities still require their own checks. [article 181](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/99/31/codcompt/codcom0001.asp?printer=1); [Q8-10 registered address, name and fees](https://www.ipim.gov.mo/en/investment-promotion/business-information/q-a); [Q11-16 capital, shareholder/director, nationality and foreign body](https://www.ipim.gov.mo/en/investment-promotion/business-information/q-a).


## Deadlines and filing

1. Obtain trade-name admissibility and settle the activity and premises. Prepare the articles and incorporation instrument in an official language, identify owners, administrators and capital payment terms, and execute the required signatures/authentication. The law also allows the prescribed electronic route with unanimous consent and adequate electronic identification; its conditions determine whether the lawyer declaration is dispensed with. [name/articles/registry/DSF commencement steps](https://www.ipim.gov.mo/en/investment-promotion/investors-one-stop-service/handle-company-registration-procedures); [article 179](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/99/31/codcompt/codcom0001.asp?printer=1).

2. Register the incorporation instrument with the Commercial and Movable Property Registry; legal personality follows registration. IPIM’s procedure calls for the instrument, owners/IDs, board appointment information and name certificate. Its published steps specify signing within 60 days of name approval and registry application within 15 days of signing. Use current registry instructions for the chosen paper/electronic channel. [name/articles/registry/DSF commencement steps](https://www.ipim.gov.mo/en/investment-promotion/investors-one-stop-service/handle-company-registration-procedures); [article 176](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/99/31/codcompt/codcom0001.asp?printer=1).

3. Declare commencement to DSF using the Business Tax M/1, with the required company/owner/administrator documents. A separate M/1 is submitted in duplicate for each establishment in the published paper instructions, in Chinese or Portuguese. Capture the expected commencement date, activity, capital, establishment and tax correspondence details; obtain the accepted receipt/registration number. Business registration and profits-tax returns both use an M/1 identifier, so identify the tax concerned. Do not treat the old bank-account/deposit step as a universal pre-registration requirement: capital payment evidence depends on the legal contribution and channel. [name/articles/registry/DSF commencement steps](https://www.ipim.gov.mo/en/investment-promotion/investors-one-stop-service/handle-company-registration-procedures); [business registration M1 instructions](https://www.dsf.gov.mo/uploads/dsf/20250106/E_instrucoes_DSF_Mod_223.pdf); [paperM1 instruction, official language](https://www.dsf.gov.mo/uploads/dsf/20250106/E_instrucoes_DSF_Mod_223.pdf); [paperM1 instruction, expected commencement](https://www.dsf.gov.mo/uploads/dsf/20250106/E_instrucoes_DSF_Mod_223.pdf); [paperM1 instruction, initial/current capital](https://www.dsf.gov.mo/uploads/dsf/20250106/E_instrucoes_DSF_Mod_223.pdf); [paperM1 instruction, correspondence address](https://www.dsf.gov.mo/uploads/dsf/20250106/E_instrucoes_DSF_Mod_223.pdf); [article 179](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/99/31/codcompt/codcom0001.asp?printer=1); [article 201](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/99/31/codcompt/codcom0001.asp?printer=1); [article 361](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/99/31/codcompt/codcom0001.asp?printer=1).

IPIM’s current FAQ describes generally about 15 working days after the required incorporation documents are signed and forwarded. This is a practical estimate, not a guaranteed statutory completion period. Its procedure page’s instruction to pay industrial tax must be read subject to the enacted collection waiver. [Q6 incorporation documents and practical time](https://www.ipim.gov.mo/en/investment-promotion/business-information/q-a); [name/articles/registry/DSF commencement steps](https://www.ipim.gov.mo/en/investment-promotion/investors-one-stop-service/handle-company-registration-procedures); [2026 Budget Law 13/2025 article 10](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/2025/52/lei13.asp?printer=1).

4. Set up accounts and determine the Group A/B assessment route in [corporate income tax](mo-corporate-income-tax.md). That skill owns annual return windows and payment rules; the old Group A June end-date is superseded. Group A uses properly organized accounts signed and verified by a qualified accountant/auditor; do not equate this alone with a universal statutory audit obligation. Group B still has record requirements. For staff, load [payroll/social](mo-payroll-social.md) for professional-tax and FSS registration, deductions and payment cycles. [article 4](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/78/36/lei21.asp?printer=1); [article 10](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/78/36/lei21.asp?printer=1); [article 18](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/78/36/lei21.asp?printer=1); [coverage, enrollment, rate splits and payment periods](https://www.fss.gov.mo/en/social/social-mandatory); [current group classification, calculation and employer obligations](https://www.dsf.gov.mo/en/tax/tax_introduction/salaries_tax).

Existing taxpaying entities must declare their tax domicile to DSF by 31 December 2026; use DSF Electronic Services, Macao Tax, the Business and Associations/Macao One Account platform or an accepted offline channel. A postal address for tax correspondence is distinct from the company registered-office condition. [2026 tax-domicile declaration and channels](https://nhp.dsf.gov.mo/topic/declare_tax_domicile/en/home); [Q8-10 registered address, name and fees](https://www.ipim.gov.mo/en/investment-promotion/business-information/q-a).


## Penalties and interest

Registration/declaration obligations remain despite the industrial-tax collection waiver. A foreign company cannot avoid responsibility for its local acts by failing to register; the statutory company, actor and administrator liabilities remain. Do not invent a fixed incorporation penalty or assume tax relief removes an administrative duty. [2026 Budget Law 13/2025 article 10](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/2025/52/lei13.asp?printer=1); [article 178](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/99/31/codcompt/codcom0001.asp?printer=1).


## Cross-border points

Foreign legal-person owners and a foreign branch are alternatives, not identical structures. Check corporate documents, representation, source of income, actual management and licensed-sector requirements. Registration as shareholder/director does not automatically confer labour-market status. Detailed immigration, treaty and foreign investment restrictions require separate clearance. [Q11-16 capital, shareholder/director, nationality and foreign body](https://www.ipim.gov.mo/en/investment-promotion/business-information/q-a); [Q19 work authorization separate from ownership](https://www.ipim.gov.mo/en/investment-promotion/business-information/q-a); [article 178](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/99/31/codcompt/codcom0001.asp?printer=1).


## Two worked examples that recompute

### Lda. cash capital at the floor versus a higher amount

Assume only cash quotas, equal owners, no special sector rule and valid deferral terms. With MOP 25,000 subscribed, the capital-floor condition requires all MOP 25,000 immediately and permits no deferral. With MOP 50,000 subscribed, MOP 25,000 may be deferred and MOP 25,000 paid. Each equal cash quota meets the half limit. These are derived funding amounts, not registration fees. [article 359](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/99/31/codcompt/codcom0001.asp?printer=1); [article 361](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/99/31/codcompt/codcom0001.asp?printer=1).

```oa-check
name: Lda. cash capital at the floor versus a higher amount
inputs:
  minimum_capital: 25000
  lower_capital: 25000
  higher_capital: 50000
steps:
  - lower_max_deferred: min(lower_capital / 2, max(0, lower_capital - minimum_capital))
  - higher_max_deferred: min(higher_capital / 2, max(0, higher_capital - minimum_capital))
  - lower_paid: lower_capital - lower_max_deferred
  - higher_paid: higher_capital - higher_max_deferred
expected:
  lower_max_deferred: 0
  higher_max_deferred: 25000
  lower_paid: 25000
  higher_paid: 25000
```

### The cash half-limit does not override the paid-value floor

Assume two equal MOP 20,000 cash quotas, valid deferral terms and no special sector rule. Deferring half of the MOP 40,000 total would leave only MOP 20,000 paid, below the floor. The maximum combined deferral is instead MOP 15,000, with MOP 25,000 paid; each shareholder defers MOP 7,500. Choose the funding plan after testing both constraints. All results are derived. [article 359](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/99/31/codcompt/codcom0001.asp?printer=1); [article 361](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/99/31/codcompt/codcom0001.asp?printer=1).

```oa-check
name: The cash half-limit does not override the paid-value floor
inputs:
  subscribed: 40000
  owners: 2
  minimum_capital: 25000
steps:
  - cash_half_limit: subscribed / 2
  - max_deferred: min(cash_half_limit, max(0, subscribed - minimum_capital))
  - paid: subscribed - max_deferred
  - deferred_per_owner: max_deferred / owners
expected:
  cash_half_limit: 20000
  max_deferred: 15000
  paid: 25000
  deferred_per_owner: 7500
```


## Ask the client first

- Which form, activity, owners and ultimate parent; are any owners single-member companies?
- What capital, cash/in-kind evidence and payment schedule; which administrator/signature powers?
- Which Macau address, permissions, intended start date, staff and immigration status?
- Which registry/DSF receipts, tax-domicile declaration and accounting classification?
