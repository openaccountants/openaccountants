# Timor-Leste VAT / GST

## Scope

For ordinary 2026 business compute **import sales tax and designated-services tax**, rather than an assumed general VAT/GST. No operative ordinary VAT commencement was established in the sources read. Petroleum legislation retains a separate VAT provision, so an unconditional countrywide “no VAT” claim is too broad. [Tax Act, arts. 5–18, 71, 90-C, republished by Law 5/2019](https://www.mj.gov.tl/jornal/public/docs/2019/serie_1/SERIE_I_NO_33_B.pdf).

The newer parliamentary 2027 Budget report treats **5% VAT from 2028 as a planning assumption dependent on legislation not yet presented**. September 2026's Government release described a draft and earlier 2027 budget target, not enactment. Neither forecast is a rate/threshold/commencement date for filing. [Parliament, physical pp. 105–106, printed pp. 103–104](https://www.parlamento.tl/storage/iniciativas/iniciativa-422-relatorio-e-parecer-do-oge-2027-1793262060.pdf); [Government, 23 September 2026](https://timor-leste.gov.tl/?lang=pt&p=52081).

## Ask the client first

- Transaction date, goods/services, domestic/import status and any petroleum contract/area.
- Monthly designated-service receipts, bundled supplies and relevant associated suppliers' receipts.
- Customs declaration/value, freight/insurance, classification, duty/excise assessments, quantities and alcohol strength.
- Exemption declaration/number, customs or incentive approval and qualifying public/donated-goods evidence.
- Taxpayer identification, filing history and current Monthly Taxes Form/customs declaration.
- Current-tax question versus a future VAT proposal.

### Compute designated-services tax [Tax Act, arts. 5–9 and Annex I](https://www.mj.gov.tl/jornal/public/docs/2019/serie_1/SERIE_I_NO_33_B.pdf)

Hotel services include temporary guest accommodation and related guest services. Restaurant/bar services include the defined on-premises provision and catering, excluding amounts already classified as hotel services. The telecommunications supplier operates/maintains the transmission system. The place-of-service rule uses services originating in Timor-Leste. Allocate a bundled payment between designated and other supplies on the reasonable basis contemplated by the Act. [Tax Act, arts. 1, 6–8](https://www.mj.gov.tl/jornal/public/docs/2019/serie_1/SERIE_I_NO_33_B.pdf).

Aggregate monthly gross designated-service receipts, including relevant associated suppliers' **same-type** receipts not already taxed under the chapter. Apply the applicable rate to the supplier's whole relevant consideration; costs do not reduce that gross base. Once required to file, continue returns in later nil-liability months unless written relief is granted on request. [Tax Act, art. 9 and Annex I(2)–(4)](https://www.mj.gov.tl/jornal/public/docs/2019/serie_1/SERIE_I_NO_33_B.pdf).

These receipts also enter the separate income-tax analysis. ATTL permits a deduction of services tax paid where the amount was included in receipts subject to income tax; that is not a VAT input credit. [ATTL services-tax guidance](https://attl.gov.tl/services-tax/).

### Compute border taxes in order [Tax Act, arts. 10–19 and Annexes II–IV](https://www.mj.gov.tl/jornal/public/docs/2019/serie_1/SERIE_I_NO_33_B.pdf)

Use customs transaction value including applicable cost, insurance and freight. Establish classification, quantity/value, origin and relief before accepting duty/excise assessments. Add applicable import duty and excise to customs value and then calculate sales tax. Do not use customs value alone or deduct another supplier's tax as a VAT credit. [Tax Act, arts. 1, 15–19 and Annex III](https://www.mj.gov.tl/jornal/public/docs/2019/serie_1/SERIE_I_NO_33_B.pdf).

Excise applies to specified imported goods and specified domestically produced goods under registered-producer rules. Alcohol, tobacco, petroleum products, vehicles and other scheduled goods need item-specific classification. The base schedule is overlaid by the enacted annual Budget; do not copy its historic rates unchanged. [Tax Act, arts. 10–14 and Annex II](https://www.mj.gov.tl/jornal/public/docs/2019/serie_1/SERIE_I_NO_33_B.pdf); [Law 8/2025, art. 3(4)](https://www.mj.gov.tl/jornal/public/docs/2025/serie_1/SERIE_I_NO_48_B.pdf).

## Worked cases

Hypothetical ordinary 2026 cases. The originals contained no historical cases.

### Below versus exactly at the services threshold

Restaurant monthly gross receipts USD 499, with no relevant associated receipts, produce no services tax. At exactly USD 500, tax is USD 25 on all receipts. At USD 800, tax is USD 40, not USD 15 calculated on an assumed excess. [Tax Act, Annex I; ATTL explanation](https://attl.gov.tl/services-tax/).

```oa-check
name: Services threshold and whole receipts
inputs:
  below_receipts: 499
  threshold_receipts: 500
  higher_receipts: 800
steps:
  below_tax: below_receipts * 0
  threshold_tax: threshold_receipts * 0.05
  higher_tax: higher_receipts * 0.05
  incorrect_excess_only_tax: (higher_receipts - threshold_receipts) * 0.05
expected:
  below_tax: 0
  threshold_tax: 25
  higher_tax: 40
  incorrect_excess_only_tax: 15
```

### Beer import: excise enters the sales-tax base

Assume a non-exempt import of 100 litres of low-strength malt beer, customs value USD 1,000 and accepted duty assessment USD 25. The duty is supplied assessment evidence, not a general duty-rate determination. Excise is USD 270; sales-tax base USD 1,295; exact unrounded sales tax USD 32.375; total border taxes USD 327.375. Use prescribed customs-assessment rounding for payment. No ordinary VAT credit arises. [Tax Act, art. 15 and Annex III](https://www.mj.gov.tl/jornal/public/docs/2019/serie_1/SERIE_I_NO_33_B.pdf); [current Budget art. 3(4)(b)](https://www.mj.gov.tl/jornal/public/docs/2026/serie_1/SERIE_I_NO_22_B.pdf).

```oa-check
name: Import base includes duty and current beer excise
inputs:
  customs_value: 1000
  assessed_duty: 25
  litres: 100
steps:
  excise: litres * 2.70
  sales_tax_base: customs_value + assessed_duty + excise
  sales_tax: sales_tax_base * 0.025
  total_border_taxes: assessed_duty + excise + sales_tax
expected:
  excise: 270
  sales_tax_base: 1295
  sales_tax: 32.375
  total_border_taxes: 327.375
```

## When to refuse or refer

Refuse draft-VAT rates presented as current law, input-credit/refund requests without an operative basis, or customs computations without classification/value/relief evidence. Refer petroleum/saved-area transactions, uncertain vehicle-excise interpretation and emergency-relief eligibility. Do not import a foreign VAT reverse-charge mechanism.

## Filing and payment

Reconcile monthly receipts and associated-supplier threshold adjustments, then complete the services-tax section of the **Consolidated Monthly Taxes Form**. ATTL's published instructions describe delivery/payment through BNU and its site links e-Tax. Confirm the current channel/form with the taxpayer's registration; authenticated 2026 boxes/channel are not certified here. Keep acknowledgement/payment evidence and reconcile the account. [ATTL services-tax instructions](https://attl.gov.tl/services-tax/).

Services-tax return/payment: **15th day after month-end**. Continue later nil-liability returns unless written relief is granted. Domestic monthly sales-tax filing does not defer import sales tax, which follows customs procedure. Keep exemption declarations and assessment evidence. [Tax Act, arts. 9, 17–18](https://www.mj.gov.tl/jornal/public/docs/2019/serie_1/SERIE_I_NO_33_B.pdf).

Assessment, collection and infringement regimes remain applicable to late or incorrect returns/payments. Do not invent a current numerical interest/penalty schedule; apply the actual provision/notice. [Tax Act, art. 93(4)–(5)](https://www.mj.gov.tl/jornal/public/docs/2019/serie_1/SERIE_I_NO_33_B.pdf).

## Completion checklist

- Separate sales tax, services tax, excise and duty; do not call them ordinary VAT.
- Test equality, whole receipts, same-type associated receipts and continuing nil returns.
- Trace customs value, classification, quantity, duty, current excise and relief before sales tax.
- Record petroleum/draft-VAT boundaries and current form/submission/payment evidence.
