# Samoa Corporate Income Tax v0.1

> **General reference only.** This skill supports AI-assisted working papers. It is not approval to file, pay or take a tax position for a particular person. Obtain qualified local review of the facts, applicable law and filing instructions.

## Who this applies to

Ordinary companies for the 2026 tax year. A company is resident if incorporated, registered or formed in Samoa, or centrally managed and controlled there. Residents generally include worldwide assessable income; non-residents include Samoa-source assessable income. Exemptions and gross final withholding can displace the ordinary net-income computation. [section 2, resident company](https://www.ag.gov.ws/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/Income-Tax-Act-2012.pdf); [section 14](https://www.ag.gov.ws/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/Income-Tax-Act-2012.pdf); [section 10](https://www.ag.gov.ws/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/Income-Tax-Act-2012.pdf).

## The base

Compute assessable income less allowed deductions, then ordinary tax and permitted credits. Revenue expenditure must relate to producing assessable income. Private, exempt-income, capital expenditure and distributions are not ordinary deductions; use statutory capital allowances rather than expensing an asset merely because cash was paid. Reconcile the tax computation to the accounts and retain receipts. [section 13](https://www.ag.gov.ws/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/Income-Tax-Act-2012.pdf); [section 18](https://www.ag.gov.ws/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/Income-Tax-Act-2012.pdf); [section 19](https://www.ag.gov.ws/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/Income-Tax-Act-2012.pdf); [section 81](https://www.ag.gov.ws/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/Income-Tax-Act-2012.pdf).

Final non-resident withholding is on gross income with no deduction, carried loss or tax credit against that final tax. A PE-attributable interest/royalty/management/service/natural-resource amount instead enters ordinary income tax; the statutory exclusion does not simply exempt it from all tax. Check classification before reducing a contract payment. [section 10](https://www.ag.gov.ws/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/Income-Tax-Act-2012.pdf); [section 12](https://www.ag.gov.ws/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/Income-Tax-Act-2012.pdf).

International companies are a separate regime under the International Companies Act 1988, not the draft’s 1987 title. Section 249(2) provides an exemption, not a general domestic 0% rate: it excludes licensed international banks and income from carrying on business in Samoa. The separate dividend/interest limb has ownership and trustee-company-distribution exceptions. A resident-owned domestic company cannot elect this treatment merely by earning foreign income. [section 249(2), exemptions and exclusions](https://sifa.ws/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/International-Companies-Act-1988.pdf); [section 6, shareholder restrictions](https://sifa.ws/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/International-Companies-Act-1988.pdf).

The enacted 2026 removal Act commences on 1 January 2028 and preserves the removed exemptions until that date. It amends the income/direct-tax wording, so do not assume every indirect-tax or stamp-duty exemption also disappears. Do not extend a 2026 exemption calculation into 2028 or infer future filing mechanics from the Act’s title. [sections 1-2 and 6, enacted deferred commencement](https://sifa.ws/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Miscellaneous-Removal-of-Tax-Exemption-for-International-Companies-Amendment-Act-2026-Eng.pdf).

## Deadlines and filing

The default year is calendar year. A company may use another 12-month period only with written Commissioner approval; the statutory transition-year rules apply. File the annual income tax return within 3 months of year end, including where there is a statutory loss, and pay on that due date. Thus a 31 December 2026 year end gives 31 March 2027, derived. IR4 is the company return; the listed 2025 form is the 2025-2026 filing-cycle form, not a final 2026 form. [section 2, tax year](https://www.ag.gov.ws/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/Income-Tax-Act-2012.pdf); [section 34](https://www.ag.gov.ws/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/Income-Tax-Act-2012.pdf); [section 80](https://www.ag.gov.ws/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/Income-Tax-Act-2012.pdf); [section 82](https://www.ag.gov.ws/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/Income-Tax-Act-2012.pdf); [Income Tax Forms, current listing](https://revenue.gov.ws/inland-revenue-services/).

Provisional instalments fall on 31 March, 31 July and 31 October. Start with the preceding-year assessed tax after relevant credits less the specified withholding-paid amount; normally pay 1/3 at each date (in words on the page). If the provisional amount is below WST 500, pay it on 31 March. File an estimate when new/unassessed, or a justified revision for significantly lower income; reconcile credits at final assessment. An estimate below 80% of actual statutory tax can attract 50% of the shortfall for fraud/wilful neglect or 10% otherwise, with the statutory reasonable-care/beyond-control protection for the latter. [section 85](https://www.ag.gov.ws/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/Income-Tax-Act-2012.pdf); [section 86](https://www.ag.gov.ws/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/Income-Tax-Act-2012.pdf); [section 88](https://www.ag.gov.ws/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/Income-Tax-Act-2012.pdf).

Withhold at the earlier of crediting the recipient’s account and actual payment. Pay withholding to the Commissioner within 15 days after month end; retain the certificate and reconcile any eligible credit. A withholding failure creates payer liability rather than permission to deduct the payment unchecked. [section 98](https://www.ag.gov.ws/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/Income-Tax-Act-2012.pdf); [section 99](https://www.ag.gov.ws/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/Income-Tax-Act-2012.pdf); [section 103](https://www.ag.gov.ws/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/Income-Tax-Act-2012.pdf).

## Cross-border points

Establish the payee’s residence, payment nature, Samoa source and PE connection before choosing net tax or final withholding. Treaties and entity-specific exemptions require the operative agreement, eligibility and evidence; this pack does not provide treaty rates. International-company restrictions are handled in [formation](ws-company-formation.md), separately from ordinary domestic incorporation. [section 7](https://www.ag.gov.ws/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/Income-Tax-Act-2012.pdf); [section 10](https://www.ag.gov.ws/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/Income-Tax-Act-2012.pdf); [section 17](https://www.ag.gov.ws/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/Income-Tax-Act-2012.pdf).

## Two worked examples that recompute

### Ordinary company with provisional credits

Assume WST 100,000 assessable income and WST 30,000 fully allowable revenue expenses, no special regime or other credits, and WST 15,000 provisional tax already paid for this year. Taxable profit is WST 70,000, tax WST 18,900 and balance WST 3,900, all derived. The provisional payment is a credit, not a deduction from profit.

```oa-check
name: Ordinary company with provisional credits
inputs:
  income: 100000
  deductions: 30000
  provisional_paid: 15000
steps:
  - profit: income - deductions
  - tax: profit * 0.27
  - balance: tax - provisional_paid
expected:
  profit: 70000
  tax: 18900
  balance: 3900
```

### Royalty with or without a Samoa PE connection

Assume a non-resident company receives a WST 20,000 Samoa-source royalty. Without PE attribution or exemption and before any treaty relief, final withholding is WST 3,000 on gross. If instead the royalty is attributable to its Samoa PE, with WST 8,000 proved deductible costs and no other items, ordinary taxable income is WST 12,000 and company tax WST 3,240. These derived alternatives show why a PE exception changes the computation rather than making the receipt tax-free.

```oa-check
name: Royalty with or without a Samoa PE connection
inputs:
  gross: 20000
  pe_costs: 8000
steps:
  - final_wht: gross * 0.15
  - pe_profit: gross - pe_costs
  - pe_tax: pe_profit * 0.27
expected:
  final_wht: 3000
  pe_profit: 12000
  pe_tax: 3240
```

## Ask the client first

- Which entity statute, residence, ownership, actual business activities and any exemption certificate?
- Which balance date and written approval, accounts, deductions and carried losses?
- What are the payment type, payee residence, treaty evidence and PE connection?
- What assessments, provisional payments, certificates and final-year return instructions exist?
