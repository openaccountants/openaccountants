# Armenia LLC formation and first compliance steps v0.3

## Scope

This guide covers an ordinary Armenian limited liability company (LLC) formed under the general company and registration laws, for registrations in 2026. Banks, insurers, investment businesses, crypto-asset service providers and other regulated activities have separate rules. A registration certificate does not establish that a regulated activity, work permit, residence status, tax regime, bank account or licence is available. Check the activity and founders before choosing the route. [Registration Law, Article 1](https://www.arlis.am/hy/acts/218933); [LLC Law, Article 1](https://www.arlis.am/hy/acts/207710/latest)

An LLC is a commercial legal person with separate property, liable for its obligations with that property. It may be established by one or more natural or legal persons, the Republic of Armenia (through the Government) or a community (through its head, with council consent). State and local self-government bodies cannot be founders or participants. [LLC Law, Articles 3 and 11(3)](https://www.arlis.am/hy/acts/207710/latest)

Out of scope: special-sector companies, joint-stock companies, foundations and other legal forms, regulated licences, immigration and employment permissions, and the company's later accounting and financial-statement rules.

## Ask the client first

1. Who are the founders: natural or legal persons, Armenian or foreign, and how many? (Changes the documents and the one-day or two-day registration period.)
2. What is the actual activity, and does it need a licence or a sector-specific minimum capital?
3. Will any founder contribute property, securities or rights instead of money, and at what nominal value? (The independent-appraiser test.)
4. Who is the executive head, and where will the permanently operating executive body sit? (Registered office.)
5. Does the company want turnover-tax or micro-business status from registration? (20-day statement deadline.)
6. Who are the beneficial owners under the Anti-Money Laundering Law? (40-day declaration.)

### Step 1. Decide founders, charter and capital

The founding decision or founders' meeting minutes must cover the founding, the participants, their share sizes, approval of the charter and formation of the executive body, and record that every decision was unanimous. A sole founder makes a written sole-founder decision and no founding agreement is signed. [LLC Law, Article 8](https://www.arlis.am/hy/acts/207710/latest)

The charter is approved unanimously and states the company name, registered office, charter capital and share sizes, and the participants' details (for natural persons citizenship, name, address and public-service number or the certificate of refusal, or for foreigners any identifying document number; for legal persons the state of incorporation and full name). It may add management bodies, decision rules, participant rights, exit and share-transfer rules. Charter changes bind third parties from their registration. [LLC Law, Article 10](https://www.arlis.am/hy/acts/207710/latest)

The LLC Law sets no general minimum charter capital; another law or legal act can set a sector-specific minimum. Capital and contributions are stated in dram. Contributions may be money, securities, other property or property rights, or other rights with a money valuation. Participants approve a non-cash valuation unanimously. An independent appraiser is required where the nominal value of the share paid in kind exceeds 500 times the minimum wage at the time the registration documents are submitted. The Minimum Wage Law (Article 3) bars other laws from using the AMD 75,000 monthly minimum wage as a calculation base and sets AMD 1,000 as that base, so the threshold is AMD 500,000. Laws that expressly point to the Article 1 amount (such as the pension contribution ceiling) are different. Each founder pays its contribution in full by the founding-agreement deadline, no later than one year after state registration, and cannot be released from it, including by set-off (a convertible-loan conversion is the stated exception). [LLC Law, Articles 28-30](https://www.arlis.am/hy/acts/207710/latest); [Minimum Wage Law, Articles 1 and 3](https://www.arlis.am/hy/acts/172160/latest)

The registered office is the location of one of the permanently operating executive bodies named in the charter, and registration is made there. The law provides for an executive body and allows its powers to be given to a commercial organisation or individual entrepreneur (a manager); it does not support a blanket claim here that every foreign-owned LLC can operate without a resident director or local work authorisation. Resolve the actual director, immigration and regulated-sector facts separately. [LLC Law, Articles 3(8) and 36(2)](https://www.arlis.am/hy/acts/207710/latest)

### Step 2. Assemble the registration file

Submit to the State Register Agency:

1. The application (applicant name, passport or ID details, address, the claim, attachment list, date and signature). Giving the Agency the information and signing the application it prepares also counts. [Registration Law, Article 17(1)-(2)](https://www.arlis.am/hy/acts/218933)
2. The founders' decision or founding-meeting minutes signed by all founders, or the sole founder's written decision. [Registration Law, Article 34(1)(2), (7)](https://www.arlis.am/hy/acts/218933)
3. The charter. [Article 34(1)(3)](https://www.arlis.am/hy/acts/218933)
4. Proof of state-duty payment. This guide does not state a fee amount or exemption; check the current State Duty Law rule and the filing route before budgeting. [Article 34(1)(4)](https://www.arlis.am/hy/acts/218933)
5. The executive head's (or acting head's) passport data, social card number or refusal certificate number, and email address. [Article 34(1)(5)](https://www.arlis.am/hy/acts/218933)
6. An Armenian legal-person founder adds its authorised management body's decision and its name and registration number. [Article 34(2)](https://www.arlis.am/hy/acts/218933)
7. A foreign-company founder adds a commercial-register extract or equivalent proof of legal status and its founding documents (or extracts), certified and translated into Armenian. [Article 34(3)](https://www.arlis.am/hy/acts/218933)
8. A foreign individual founder adds a certified copy of a passport or other identity document, translated into Armenian. [Article 34(4)](https://www.arlis.am/hy/acts/218933)

Copies are certified by the executive head's dated signature or by a notary. Special laws can require more documents. Documents that do not meet Article 17 are returned without review, except where Article 19 lets the applicant correct formal errors. [Registration Law, Articles 17(8), 17(12), 19 and 34(6)](https://www.arlis.am/hy/acts/218933)

### Step 3. File on paper or online and wait for the decision

Applications are submitted in person on paper or through the information system on the internet; the online identification and document procedure is set by the Minister. The ordinary decision period is no later than one working day after the required documents are submitted, and two working days if any founder is a legal person. A commercial applicant can ask for a longer period of up to 30 calendar days. Immediate registration applies only where the Agency's template documents are used under Article 17(9)-(10); it is not a general promise of same-day registration. For a commercial company the Agency checks the formation procedure, completeness and the charter's required contents, not the rest of the charter. [Registration Law, Articles 17(3), 35(1)-(5)](https://www.arlis.am/hy/acts/218933)

The company is created at state registration. On registration the information system automatically assigns the registration number, the taxpayer identification number and the social-payment personal account number. Registration is evidenced by a Register extract sent to the email address given. The Agency also emails a tax-authority notice on the tax regimes, the statement deadlines for choosing them and the related-party limits on special regimes. [Registration Law, Article 35(6)-(8), (12)](https://www.arlis.am/hy/acts/218933); [LLC Law, Article 8(4)](https://www.arlis.am/hy/acts/207710/latest)

Registration documents do not guarantee that a bank will open an account. [LLC Law, Article 3(5)](https://www.arlis.am/hy/acts/207710/latest)

### Step 4. First tax and beneficial-owner filings

Company registration is not the end of tax setup. A newly registered company that wants turnover-tax status files its statement by the 20th day after registration, inclusive; the micro-business statement has its own 20-day deadline and activity and eligibility conditions. Without a valid statement the company is a VAT payer from registration. Eligibility is not automatic. See [Armenia tax planning](armenia-tax-optimization.md) before selecting a regime. [Tax Code, Articles 59(1)(2), 254(1)(2) and 267(1)(2)](https://www.arlis.am/hy/acts/215611/latest)

An Armenian-registered legal person must identify and keep reliable information about its actual beneficial owners and the grounds for their status. It reviews this at least once a year and keeps the related records for at least five years. Persons it asks must answer within 20 days, and beneficial owners must tell the company of their status or changes within 20 days. The initial declaration is due within 40 days after registration. Changes must be declared immediately and no later than 40 days after the company learns of them. By 20 February each year the company must confirm that the latest declaration is current as at 31 December or submit updated data. Beneficial-owner identification follows the Anti-Money Laundering Law; the registration law does not itself define a universal ownership-percentage test. Central Bank-registered entities follow their own legislation. [Registration Law, Articles 60.1-60.3](https://www.arlis.am/hy/acts/218933)

### Step 5. Annual corporate calendar

The ordinary general meeting is held at least once a year unless the charter sets another frequency. Where the charter sets the timing of the meeting approving the annual results, it must be held not earlier than two months and not later than six months after the financial year ends. The general meeting alone approves the annual report and balance sheet and decides on profit distribution. This is a shareholder-meeting deadline, not a substitute for tax filings or any separate accounts-preparation requirement. [LLC Law, Articles 36-37](https://www.arlis.am/hy/acts/207710/latest)

## Worked cases

### 1. Non-cash contribution: is an appraiser needed?

Two founders form an LLC. Founder A pays AMD 400,000 in cash. Founder B contributes equipment for a share with a nominal value of AMD 600,000. The threshold is 500 x AMD 1,000 = AMD 500,000. Founder B's in-kind share exceeds it by AMD 100,000, so an independent appraiser must value the equipment, and both founders approve the valuation unanimously. Founder A's cash share needs no valuation. Charter capital is AMD 1,000,000; both contributions are due within the founding-agreement deadline and no later than one year after registration. If Founder B's in-kind share were AMD 500,000 exactly, it would not exceed the threshold and the unanimous participant valuation alone would apply. [LLC Law 29(2), 30(1)][llc]; [Minimum Wage Law 3][wage]

## When to refuse or refer

- Banks, credit and insurance businesses, investment firms, crypto-asset service providers, payment businesses and other licensed sectors: refer to the sector law and regulator.
- Any question on the State Duty Law fee, online identification steps, AML beneficial-owner classification, work permits or residence: this guide does not answer them.
- A company with more than 49 participants, or a redomiciliation: the specific reorganisation or Article 35(11) route applies.
- Do not say a filing has been accepted, a bank account will open or a regime has been granted.

Confirm the actual activity and licences; founder identity and document certification; registered office and director; state-duty amount; accepted online identification route; applicable tax-regime filing; beneficial-owner analysis under AML rules; accounting and financial-statement rules; and immigration or employment permissions.
