# Spain: Basque foral personal, family, housing and care credits, 2026

This Guide holds part of [es-foral-income-tax](es-foral-income-tax.md), moved here unchanged on 6 November 2026 so that each Guide stays within the size the site can deliver. Built from the law; not yet approved by an accountant.

## Who this applies to

An ordinary resident of Bizkaia, Álava or Gipuzkoa under the residence tests in [es-foral-income-tax](es-foral-income-tax.md#who-this-applies-to). Compute the bases, the general and savings quotas and the minoración in es-foral-income-tax before applying any credit below. Navarra residents use [es-foral-income-tax-navarra-credits](es-foral-income-tax-navarra-credits.md).

For descendants, age 30 qualifies but age>30 fails unless disability/dependency conditions bring the person within the exception. The descendant's non-exempt annual income must not exceed the SMI; the other-family-unit income test and the descendant's own filing/no-filing requirement also apply. Prorate equally between equally close entitled ancestors; the closest degree has priority, with the statutory low-income fallback. Support under a qualifying judicial arrangement can substitute for cohabitation under the precise territorial art 79.5. Apply legal guardianship/fostering/representative-curatorship assimilations. These are quota deductions, not taxable-base allowances. [Bizkaia NF13/2013, current2026 consolidation](https://www.bizkaia.eus/documents/880307/15187815/ca_13_2013.pdf/ce52e403-4fca-9ef7-bf85-2505ba29f921?t=1784098584068)[Álava NF33/2013, current2026 consolidation](https://web.araba.eus/documents/d/araba/indice_norma-foral_irpf_cas-4-pdf)[Gipuzkoa NF3/2014,2026 consolidation](https://www.gipuzkoa.eus/documents/2456431/8.0759126E7/NF%203-2014%20(2026-0).pdf/8ab5c95c-3f89-8fdf-5cb8-01950e3983cc) art 79.

Ascendant credit requires continuous permanent full-year cohabitation or the qualifying payment of residential-home costs, the SMI/other-family-unit tests, and the ascendant's own filing test. Álava excludes centres in the foral/equivalent social-service network from the relevant paid-residence assimilation. Apply nearest-degree/equal-sharing rules. Álava increases basic descendant/ascendant credits by 15% for qualifying residence in a municipality with fewer than 4,000 people where the family's principal personal/social/health/education centre is genuinely situated; this does not automatically increase every supplemental child credit. [Bizkaia NF13/2013, current2026 consolidation](https://www.bizkaia.eus/documents/880307/15187815/ca_13_2013.pdf/ce52e403-4fca-9ef7-bf85-2505ba29f921?t=1784098584068)[Álava NF33/2013, current2026 consolidation](https://web.araba.eus/documents/d/araba/indice_norma-foral_irpf_cas-4-pdf)[Gipuzkoa NF3/2014,2026 consolidation](https://www.gipuzkoa.eus/documents/2456431/8.0759126E7/NF%203-2014%20(2026-0).pdf/8ab5c95c-3f89-8fdf-5cb8-01950e3983cc) arts 79–81.

For art 82 relatives the ordinary income ceiling is twice SMI, excluding exempt income, with the statutory fourth-degree and residential-cost conditions. The disabled/dependent person's own deduction takes priority unless the permitted election transfers it; split the transferred credit equally between entitled household members. Do not claim it twice. [Bizkaia NF13/2013, current2026 consolidation](https://www.bizkaia.eus/documents/880307/15187815/ca_13_2013.pdf/ce52e403-4fca-9ef7-bf85-2505ba29f921?t=1784098584068)[Álava NF33/2013, current2026 consolidation](https://web.araba.eus/documents/d/araba/indice_norma-foral_irpf_cas-4-pdf)[Gipuzkoa NF3/2014,2026 consolidation](https://www.gipuzkoa.eus/documents/2456431/8.0759126E7/NF%203-2014%20(2026-0).pdf/8ab5c95c-3f89-8fdf-5cb8-01950e3983cc) art 82.

Age-credit base is the positive general base plus savings base, with negative general base treated aszero and the art 69 compensatory-pension adjustment. For 20,000<base<30,000 use `393−0.0393×(base−20,000)` or `714−0.0714×(base−20,000)` respectively; at/above 30,000 the credit is zero. Widow credit similarly is `200−0.02×(base−20,000)` in that taper band. Ordinary family status is determined at year-end, or at death of the person creating entitlement, without a general day-by-day proration; specific employment/care credits have their own duration rules. [Bizkaia NF13/2013, current2026 consolidation](https://www.bizkaia.eus/documents/880307/15187815/ca_13_2013.pdf/ce52e403-4fca-9ef7-bf85-2505ba29f921?t=1784098584068)[Álava NF33/2013, current2026 consolidation](https://web.araba.eus/documents/d/araba/indice_norma-foral_irpf_cas-4-pdf)[Gipuzkoa NF3/2014,2026 consolidation](https://www.gipuzkoa.eus/documents/2456431/8.0759126E7/NF%203-2014%20(2026-0).pdf/8ab5c95c-3f89-8fdf-5cb8-01950e3983cc) arts 82 bis/83/83 quinquies,84.

Judicial child-support credit is 15% of exclusive qualifying payments to descendants up to second degree, capped per descendant at 30% of the corresponding **basic** art 79.1 credit. Gipuzkoa art 80 also expressly covers the qualifying regulator agreement approved judicially, before the court officer or in a notarial public instrument. This is not the State separate-scale child-support mechanism. [Bizkaia NF13/2013, current2026 consolidation](https://www.bizkaia.eus/documents/880307/15187815/ca_13_2013.pdf/ce52e403-4fca-9ef7-bf85-2505ba29f921?t=1784098584068)[Álava NF33/2013, current2026 consolidation](https://web.araba.eus/documents/d/araba/indice_norma-foral_irpf_cas-4-pdf)[Gipuzkoa NF3/2014,2026 consolidation](https://www.gipuzkoa.eus/documents/2456431/8.0759126E7/NF%203-2014%20(2026-0).pdf/8ab5c95c-3f89-8fdf-5cb8-01950e3983cc) art 80.

Sources: [Bizkaia art. 91 quater](https://www.bizkaia.eus/documents/880307/15187815/ca_13_2013.pdf/ce52e403-4fca-9ef7-bf85-2505ba29f921?t=1784098584068), [Álava art. 87 ter](https://web.araba.eus/documents/d/araba/indice_norma-foral_irpf_cas-4-pdf), [Gipuzkoa art. 90 quater](https://www.gipuzkoa.eus/documents/2456431/8.0759126E7/NF%203-2014%20(2026-0).pdf/8ab5c95c-3f89-8fdf-5cb8-01950e3983cc), paragraphs 2/6/8.

Sources: [Bizkaia art. 91 quater](https://www.bizkaia.eus/documents/880307/15187815/ca_13_2013.pdf/ce52e403-4fca-9ef7-bf85-2505ba29f921?t=1784098584068), [Álava art. 87 ter](https://web.araba.eus/documents/d/araba/indice_norma-foral_irpf_cas-4-pdf), [Gipuzkoa art. 90 quater](https://www.gipuzkoa.eus/documents/2456431/8.0759126E7/NF%203-2014%20(2026-0).pdf/8ab5c95c-3f89-8fdf-5cb8-01950e3983cc), paragraphs 3/5/7.
