# Timor-Leste Corporate Income Tax

## Scope

Compute ordinary legal-person income tax for 2026 at **10% of taxable income**, separating exempt and final-taxed receipts before calculating the base and credits. Petroleum contractors, subcontractors and saved Bayu-Undan regimes need a separate regime determination. [Tax Act, arts. 26, 61, 69–72, 94 and Annex VI](https://www.mj.gov.tl/jornal/public/docs/2019/serie_1/SERIE_I_NO_33_B.pdf).

The sources read include the Portuguese Tax Act republication in Law 5/2019, its later permanent-establishment amendment in Law 17/2023, and the enacted 2026 Budget and June amendment. The original 2008 Act is not presented as a current consolidation. This skill produces computation working papers; authenticated current filing boxes remain a stated gap.

## Ask the client first

- Legal form, place of establishment, residence and any registered local branch/permanent representation.
- Accounting year, alternative-year approval, financial statements and tax reconciliation.
- Each income stream, source country, payer/recipient status, withholding receipts and written elections.
- Prior-year turnover, period receipts, paid instalments and foreign-tax evidence.
- Expenses, assets, losses, ownership/business changes and exemption approvals.
- Petroleum contract, area, project phase and contractor/subcontractor/Bayu-Undan status.

## The method, step by step

### Classify the taxpayer [Tax Act, arts. 1, 27–29, 47, 49–52](https://www.mj.gov.tl/jornal/public/docs/2019/serie_1/SERIE_I_NO_33_B.pdf)

A resident legal person is constituted, formed, organised or established in Timor-Leste. Residents include domestic and foreign-source income; non-residents include Timor-Leste-source income. Legal persons are taxed separately from their members. Apply the statutory source rules, not the location of the bank account. [Tax Act, arts. 1, 28(4), 47, 49](https://www.mj.gov.tl/jornal/public/docs/2019/serie_1/SERIE_I_NO_33_B.pdf).

The current permanent-establishment definition is a branch or other permanent local representation duly registered with Timor-Leste's commercial registry through which a non-resident demonstrably conducts business in national territory. The older fixed-place/services-duration definition was replaced. Analyse any relevant treaty separately. [Law 17/2023, art. 8 replacing Tax Act art. 1(u)](https://www.mj.gov.tl/jornal/public/docs/2023/serie_1/SERIE_I_NO_32_A.pdf_).

### Separate final tax from creditable withholding [Tax Act, arts. 53–61](https://www.mj.gov.tl/jornal/public/docs/2019/serie_1/SERIE_I_NO_33_B.pdf)

Correctly final-taxed income is excluded from the ordinary income pool; its related expenses/losses and depreciation are not deductible and the final withholding is not an ordinary credit/refund. The specified-service election out of finality is **irrevocable**, not a fresh invoice-by-invoice choice. The natural-person restriction concerns royalty/rent finality, not eligibility for the specified-service election. [Tax Act, art. 61](https://www.mj.gov.tl/jornal/public/docs/2019/serie_1/SERIE_I_NO_33_B.pdf).

For specified services the payer generally withholds; a natural-person payer or the UN/specialised agencies triggers recipient self-withholding, as does air/sea transport. Royalty self-withholding applies when the payer is a natural person; the rent rule additionally names the UN/specialised agencies. Issue the payment/withholding receipt and retain remittance evidence. [Tax Act, arts. 53–59](https://www.mj.gov.tl/jornal/public/docs/2019/serie_1/SERIE_I_NO_33_B.pdf).

### Reconcile the ordinary taxable base and credits [Tax Act, arts. 26–51](https://www.mj.gov.tl/jornal/public/docs/2019/serie_1/SERIE_I_NO_33_B.pdf)

Start from statutory financial-accounting net profit and reconcile to gross income less permitted deductions. Business expenses/losses must relate to taxable income. Exclude exempt dividends and qualifying capital contributions. Add back distributions, private/shareholder costs, excessive related-party remuneration, income tax, fines/penalties and bribes. **Interest expense is disallowed unless incurred by a financial institution**; this differs from taxable interest income. An otherwise deductible payment requiring withholding is deductible only after the withholding is remitted. [Tax Act, arts. 28–33](https://www.mj.gov.tl/jornal/public/docs/2019/serie_1/SERIE_I_NO_33_B.pdf).

Accrual is the ordinary method; gross annual turnover **below USD 100,000** permits cash or accrual, with transition adjustments preventing omissions/double counting. Ordinary non-contractor depreciation/amortisation is **100%**, subject to the asset, ownership/use and business-use conditions; land is not depreciable as a building. Do not use the petroleum contractor schedule for ordinary assets. [Tax Act, arts. 34–37 and Annex VII](https://www.mj.gov.tl/jornal/public/docs/2019/serie_1/SERIE_I_NO_33_B.pdf).

Eligible ordinary losses carry forward until used; asset-disposal losses have their separate restriction. A **50% or greater** underlying-ownership change restricts previous losses unless the same-business and anti-loss-trafficking conditions are met. Foreign-country losses remain ring-fenced to that country's income. [Tax Act, arts. 43, 48, 51](https://www.mj.gov.tl/jornal/public/docs/2019/serie_1/SERIE_I_NO_33_B.pdf).

Calculate gross tax and apply eligible credits in order: foreign tax, paid instalments, then creditable withholding. Foreign credit is computed by country and capped at Timor-Leste tax on that income; excess is neither deductible nor carried forward. Final withholding is not creditable. [Tax Act, arts. 26(4), 50, 60–64](https://www.mj.gov.tl/jornal/public/docs/2019/serie_1/SERIE_I_NO_33_B.pdf).

## Worked cases

Hypothetical ordinary resident-company cases for 2026. The originals contained no historical cases.

### Interest add-back

Receipts USD 300,000 less permitted operating costs USD 200,000 and borrowing interest USD 5,000 give accounting profit USD 95,000. This non-financial company adds back interest: taxable income USD 100,000, gross tax USD 10,000. Paid eligible instalments USD 1,500 leave USD 8,500. Assume no other adjustments/credits. [Tax Act, arts. 26, 31(k), 64 and Annex VI](https://www.mj.gov.tl/jornal/public/docs/2019/serie_1/SERIE_I_NO_33_B.pdf).

```oa-check
name: Ordinary company interest add-back
inputs:
  receipts: 300000
  operating_costs: 200000
  interest_expense: 5000
  instalments: 1500
steps:
  accounting_profit: receipts - operating_costs - interest_expense
  taxable_income: accounting_profit + interest_expense
  gross_tax: taxable_income * 0.10
  balance: gross_tax - instalments
expected:
  accounting_profit: 95000
  taxable_income: 100000
  gross_tax: 10000
  balance: 8500
```

### Construction: final tax versus irrevocable election

Receipts USD 100,000 produce final withholding USD 2,000 absent election. With qualifying costs USD 85,000, a valid election produces USD 15,000 profit and USD 1,500 gross tax; the credit produces USD 500 overpaid tax for statutory handling. With costs instead USD 60,000, elected tax is USD 4,000 and a further USD 2,000 is due. The derived cost break-even is USD 80,000. Consider future activities before an irrevocable election; final withholding is not refundable merely because profit is low. [Tax Act, arts. 26, 60–61 and Annexes VI/VIII](https://www.mj.gov.tl/jornal/public/docs/2019/serie_1/SERIE_I_NO_33_B.pdf).

```oa-check
name: Construction finality decision
inputs:
  receipts: 100000
  high_costs: 85000
  low_costs: 60000
steps:
  withheld: receipts * 0.02
  elected_high_cost_tax: (receipts - high_costs) * 0.10
  high_cost_overpayment: withheld - elected_high_cost_tax
  elected_low_cost_tax: (receipts - low_costs) * 0.10
  low_cost_balance: elected_low_cost_tax - withheld
  break_even_costs: receipts - withheld / 0.10
expected:
  withheld: 2000
  elected_high_cost_tax: 1500
  high_cost_overpayment: 500
  elected_low_cost_tax: 4000
  low_cost_balance: 2000
  break_even_costs: 80000
```

## When to refuse or refer

Do not finalise petroleum/saved-contract tax, treaty reductions, the non-resident service overlap, an incentive exemption or ownership-change loss claim without its operative regime and documents. Missing withholding is not proof of exemption. Refer disputed assessments and penalty notices with the applicable procedure.

## Filing and payment

The ordinary year is **1 January–31 December**, unless an alternative year is authorised. Annual return and final payment: **last day of the third month after year-end**, hence **31 March 2027** for calendar 2026 (derived calendar application). Filing extension does not extend payment. Business returns include the income statement, balance sheet and cash-flow statement; the filing rule includes loss-making taxpayers. [Tax Act, arts. 1, 42, 62–63](https://www.mj.gov.tl/jornal/public/docs/2019/serie_1/SERIE_I_NO_33_B.pdf).

Ordinary instalments and withholding/self-withholding are due by the **15th day after the relevant period ends**. Calendar quarterly instalments fall on **15 April, 15 July, 15 October and 15 January**. Instalment turnover excludes exempt income and income subject to withholding; paid instalments offset annual tax, with excess treated as overpaid tax. [Tax Act, arts. 58–59, 64](https://www.mj.gov.tl/jornal/public/docs/2019/serie_1/SERIE_I_NO_33_B.pdf); [ATTL instalments](https://attl.gov.tl/income-tax-instalment/).

Use ATTL's **Annual Income Tax Form** and periodic **Monthly Taxes Form**, with taxpayer identification, correct period, reconciled schedules, withholding notices and paid-credit evidence. The official site links [e-Tax](https://e-tax.mof.gov.tl/). Match current fields to the working papers; authenticated 2026/2027 boxes are not certified here. Retain submission acknowledgement/payment receipt and reconcile the account. [ATTL annual instructions](https://attl.gov.tl/annual-income-tax-return-guidelines/); [instalment form instructions](https://attl.gov.tl/income-tax-instalment/).

Failure to withhold makes the payer liable for the missing tax. Assessment/collection and infringement regimes remain applicable; do not invent a current penalty/interest tariff from the income-tax rate. [Tax Act, arts. 58(3), 60, 93(4)–(5)](https://www.mj.gov.tl/jornal/public/docs/2019/serie_1/SERIE_I_NO_33_B.pdf).

## Completion checklist

- Separate ordinary, exempt, final-taxed, foreign and petroleum receipts.
- Reconcile deductions, remitted withholding, assets, losses and foreign-credit limits.
- Trace credited withholding/instalments to receipts and the account; exclude final tax from credits.
- Record the year-end, filing/payment dates, current form and unresolved legal issues.
