# Spain: Cataluña regional IRPF credits, 2026

**Sourced from the law.** This revision has not had an independent whole-pack review or accountant sign-off. Calculate regional credits after establishing tax residence and the taxpayer's facts. This Guide holds the Cataluña catalogue of [es-irpf-deductions](es-irpf-deductions.md), moved here unchanged on 6 November 2026, so that each Guide stays within the size the site can deliver. Specific unresolved interactions are listed in Sources.

## Who this applies to

An ordinary IRPF resident whose community of residence for 2026 under [LIRPF art. 72](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2006-20764#a72) is Cataluña. A regional credit reduces regional tax, not the income base. Before applying any row below, use [es-irpf-deductions](es-irpf-deductions.md) for the residence test, the deduction ledger, the state/regional order of credits, the national credits, deadlines, penalties and the questions to ask, and [es-income-tax](es-income-tax.md) for the bases, scales and minima. Where a row below refers to "the attributable-tax worksheet under The base", that worksheet is [es-irpf-deductions-attributable-tax](es-irpf-deductions-attributable-tax.md); national credits, the deduction ledger and any calculation "above" outside this catalogue are in [es-irpf-deductions](es-irpf-deductions.md#the-base). Basque and Navarre residents use [es-foral-income-tax](es-foral-income-tax.md); an IRNR taxpayer or an art. 93 incoming worker does not take these credits.

## Rates, bands and thresholds

All amounts are EUR. `BI` means the specified sum of general and savings **taxable income before base reductions** (base imponible); `BL` means base liquidable after the relevant reductions. Use the definition in each row: an income limit based on BI cannot be replaced with gross salary, BL or take-home pay. The table year is 2026 unless a transition is stated.

For ordinary rent, qualify through age 35 or below at tax accrual, at least 183 days unemployed, disability at least 65%, or widowhood with age at least 65; the large/single-parent-family route instead uses its own condition. Do not require every tenant to satisfy all categories. Apply the art. 612-3.5 **per-home** cap and allocation where multiple taxpayers qualify: the statute divides the 10%-of-total-rent amount, capped at 1,000, between entitled declarants. Obtain the landlord NIF and supporting documents. Do not impose the old income limits or invent a percentage-of-income rent condition absent from the current article. The separate violence-victim credit requires the statutory proof of status; the labelled separate-grant worksheet immediately below supplies a same-rent calculation within its stated interpretation and claimant limits ([arts. 612-3 and 612-11](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2024-6951#a6-5)).

For language gifts, verify the named institute or recipient's inclusion in the language-policy census. For research gifts, use the specified Catalan universities, affiliated institutes or Generalitat-promoted/participated research centres. Retain receipts and reconcile the recipient's reporting; arts. 612-6 to 612-8 require the recipient's donor list within the first 20 days of the following year ([arts. 612-6 to 612-8](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2024-6951#a6-8)).

Angel investment requires eligible SA/SL or labour-company form, registered and tax offices in Cataluña, genuine economic activity, at least one full-time general-regime employee and annual turnover no more than 1 million. Combined ownership with spouse and relatives through the third degree cannot exceed 35%. Capital-increase companies must be formed within the preceding three years and not listed on the national or alternative market. The investor can be a board member but cannot exercise executive/management functions or be an employee. Formalise the subscription in a public deed identifying investors/amounts. Maintain the relevant conditions and contributions five years. The statute preserves relief for the specified reinvestment in another qualifying cooperative or statutory transfer. It does not state a numeric carry-forward expiry in art. 612-12; do not invent one ([art. 612-12](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2024-6951#a6-135)).

First establish [LIRPF DT18](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2006-20764#dtdecimoctava) entitlement using the existing national legacy-home procedure below. For the ordinary acquisition/construction/rehabilitation/extension base, add eligible amounts actually borne and paid in the year: qualifying acquisition/construction costs and taxes, principal repaid on traced acquisition finance, interest less hedge receipts, and qualifying financing costs. Exclude unrelated extra borrowing and returned mortgage-floor amounts excluded by LIRPF DA45. Apply the prior-home/reinvestment restrictions and net-wealth test already stated in that procedure. The annual base `B` is capped at **9,040 in total, including on a joint return**, with no carry-forward of excess payments. A qualifying couple paying 6,000 each has a joint base of 9,040; on separate returns each claimant's documented 6,000 stays below their own ceiling. These are deduction bases, not tax credits ([preserved arts.68.1/70/78/84](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2006-20764&p=20121031&tn=1#a68); [current AEAT expenditure/base method](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/ayuda/manuales-videos-folletos/manuales-practicos/irpf-2025/c16-deducciones-generales-cuota/deduccion-inversion-vivienda-habitual-regimen-transitorio/modalidades-deduccion/adquisicion-construc-rehabilitacion-ampliacion-vivienda-habitual/cantidades-satisfechas-derecho-deduccion-base-deducible.html)).

For acquisition, or construction payments, **before 30 July 2011**, use **9% regional** if the claimant is at most 32 at accrual, unemployed at least 183 days, disabled at least 65%, or a member of a family unit containing a child at accrual, and their **general BI + savings BI − personal/family minimum is at most 30,000**. Re-test the condition and income in the claim year. The 30,000 income gate is individual for each investing contributor even on a joint return; it is not a 60,000 household allowance. Rehabilitation/extension alone does not qualify for 9%. At `B=9,040`, ordinary regional relief is **678** and enhanced regional relief **813.60**; adding state relief gives **1,356** or **1,491.60**. A contributor failing the enhanced income test falls back to 7.5% if the underlying transition remains valid. Preserve the contributors' own expenditure/rate allocation within the single joint base, rather than granting 9% to the entire joint base because one contributor qualifies ([current ATC legacy-home explanation](https://atc.gencat.cat/es/tributs/irpf/deduccions-tram-irpf/), section13).

Keep disability adaptation in its **separate annual base capped at 12,080**, also one ceiling on a joint return. It carries **10% state + 15% Catalan regional**, giving maxima **1,208 + 1,812 = 3,020**, independently of the ordinary 9,040 base. Require the preserved certified necessity, qualifying disabled occupant/family relationship and ownership/tenancy/usufruct or qualifying common-element status. Pre-2013 payments and completion before 2017 are indispensable; a new adaptation in 2026 does not qualify merely because the house is old. The preserved regulation defines eligible accessibility, sensory-communication and common-element works ([preserved art.68.1.4](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2006-20764&p=20121031&tn=1#a68); [preserved RIRPF57](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2007-6820&p=20111220&tn=1#a57); DT18 and art.613-1 above).

Their scope includes legally classified rural municipalities, rural municipalities of special attention and the municipalities included in the regime by DA1.1. A PRE/415 entry labelled **DA1 ap1** is included under that provision and must not be rejected merely because its annex label is not MR or MRAE. Apply the actual legal designation rather than assigning the enhanced special-attention rate to every included municipality ([Ley8/2025 DA1.1](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2025-16833#da); [PRE/415 official annex](https://dogc.gencat.cat/es/document-del-dogc/index.html?documentId=1037694)). **Home-investment computation:** use each claimant's actual eligible payments and [art.60](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2025-16833#a6-2): attributable acquisition/rehabilitation expenses and taxes borne by the purchaser, eligible finance principal/interest/other financing costs, with hedge receipts reducing interest. Apply at most6,000 annually to the respective deduction. No provision doubles that base on a joint return: apply LIRPF84.2's unchanged quantitative ceiling, while testing the36,000 income condition individually as the rural articles expressly require. The acquisition/licence-start year plus **14 following years** is an expenditure-entitlement window, not a credit carry-forward or permission to deduct the same payment in successive years. DT1's separate new-event window ends four years after the law's approval; preserve its wording rather than substituting an unsupported31December2029 cutoff. A qualifying2026 home-investment vintage can have its15 claim years in2026–2040, subject to annual qualifying payments/conditions.

Rehabilitation means principally reconstruction through structural/facade/roof or analogous consolidation, with total qualifying rehabilitation cost **strictly exceeding25%** of the acquisition price if acquired within the preceding two years, otherwise market value at commencement; exclude the land value from that comparator. The licence year establishes start. Art.59.5 and ATC both say reduce the **deduction** by qualifying public aid received in the period; do not silently change that into a reduction of the expense base. For example,6,000 qualifying rural cost gives900 before aid;500 same-year qualifying aid leaves400 under that literal instruction, subject to available regional tax. Record the gross cost, aid and resulting credit separately.

Habitual occupation is within **12months of acquisition**, or **24months from the rehabilitation licence**, followed by **three continuous years**. Preserve art.60.4–5's death, necessary-move/disability and employment-provided-home exceptions, rather than treating every voluntary early move as harmless. One storage room and up to two qualifying parking spaces follow art.60.6. Prior deducted home investments and exempt reinvestment gains postpone/reduce new-home eligible investment under60.7–8.
