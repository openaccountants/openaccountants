# Isle of Man Personal Income Tax

## Personal income tax rates and allowances (2025/26)

- **Personal allowance (single)** — 14,750 IMP  _(Income Tax Act 1970 (https://www.gov.im/categories/tax-vat-and-your-money/income-tax-and-national-insurance/individuals/residents/rates-and-allowances/))_
- **Personal allowance (jointly assessed couple)** — 29,500 IMP  _(Income Tax Act 1970 (https://www.gov.im/categories/tax-vat-and-your-money/income-tax-and-national-insurance/individuals/residents/rates-and-allowances/))_
- **Personal allowance taper** — Reduced by IMP 1 for every IMP 2 of income above IMP 100,000 (single) / IMP 200,000 (couple)  _(Income Tax Act 1970 (https://www.gov.im/categories/tax-vat-and-your-money/income-tax-and-national-insurance/individuals/residents/rates-and-allowances/))_
- **Standard (lower) rate** — 10% %  _(Income Tax Act 1970 (https://www.gov.im/categories/tax-vat-and-your-money/income-tax-and-national-insurance/individuals/residents/rates-and-allowances/))_
- **Standard (10%) rate band (single)** — First IMP 6,500 of taxable income above the personal allowance IMP  _(Income Tax Act 1970 (https://www.gov.im/categories/tax-vat-and-your-money/income-tax-and-national-insurance/individuals/residents/rates-and-allowances/))_
- **Standard (10%) rate band (jointly assessed couple)** — First IMP 13,000 of taxable income above the personal allowance IMP  _(Income Tax Act 1970 (https://www.gov.im/categories/tax-vat-and-your-money/income-tax-and-national-insurance/individuals/residents/rates-and-allowances/))_
- **Upper rate** — 21% % (applies to taxable income above the standard-rate band)  _(Income Tax Act 1970 (https://www.gov.im/categories/tax-vat-and-your-money/income-tax-and-national-insurance/individuals/residents/rates-and-allowances/))_
- **Non-resident income tax rate** — 20% on Isle of Man-source income (no personal allowance) % ((approx — confirm))  _(Income Tax Act 1970 (https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/isle-of-man/individual/taxes-on-personal-income))_
- **Annual tax cap (election)** — IMP 220,000 individual / IMP 440,000 jointly assessed couple, fixed for a 5-year election period IMP  _(Income Tax Act 1970 (https://www.pwc.com/im/en/services/tax/isleofman-tax-rates.html))_
- **Deductible interest relief** — Tax relief available for qualifying loan/mortgage interest and certain other deductions, subject to caps ((approx — confirm cap))  _(Income Tax Act 1970 (https://www.gov.im/categories/tax-vat-and-your-money/income-tax-and-national-insurance/individuals/residents/rates-and-allowances/))_
- **Residence test** — Generally resident if present 183+ days in a tax year, or present on average 90+ days a year over four years ((approx — confirm))  _(Income Tax Act 1970 (https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/isle-of-man/individual/residence))_
- **Return filing deadline** — Before 6 October following the end of the tax year  _(Income Tax Act 1970 (https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/isle-of-man/individual/tax-administration))_
- **Late filing penalty** — IMP 100 automatic penalty; further IMP 200 if still outstanding by 5 April following IMP  _(Income Tax Act 1970 (https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/isle-of-man/individual/tax-administration))_
- **Balancing payment due date** — 6 January following the end of the year of assessment  _(Income Tax Act 1970 (https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/isle-of-man/individual/tax-administration))_

Resident individuals pay tax at just two rates on income above the personal allowance, and may elect an annual tax cap. Non-residents are taxed only on Isle of Man-source income, generally at the 20% non-resident rate.
