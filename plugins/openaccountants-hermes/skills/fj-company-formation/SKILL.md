# Fiji Company Formation & Entity Choice

## Entity types and formation

- **Company formation overview** — Companies in Fiji are formed under the Companies Act 2015 and registered with the Registrar of Companies. The most common vehicle for private business is a private company limited by shares.  _(Companies Act 2015)_
- **Private company limited by shares** — Most common entity; name ends in 'Pte Limited' / 'Pte Ltd'; membership capped at 50, share transfers restricted, no public share offers  _(Companies Act 2015 (https://laws.gov.fj/Acts/DisplayAct/3231))_
- **Public company** — May offer shares to the public; subject to heavier governance and disclosure requirements  _(Companies Act 2015)_
- **Other entity / structure types** — Sole trader, partnership, branch of a foreign company, and trusts are also used ((approx — partnerships/sole traders governed by separate law))  _(Companies Act 2015)_
- **Minimum directors / officeholders — private company** — At least 1 director who is ordinarily resident in Fiji  _(Companies Act 2015 (https://www.taxprofiji.com/services/how-to-register-a-private-limited-company-in-fiji))_
- **Minimum directors / officeholders — public company** — At least 3 officeholders, at least 2 ordinarily resident in Fiji, plus at least 1 company secretary  _(Companies Act 2015 (https://www.taxprofiji.com/services/how-to-register-a-private-limited-company-in-fiji))_
- **Company secretary — private company** — Not mandatory for a private company; if appointed, at least one secretary must be ordinarily resident in Fiji  _(Companies Act 2015)_
- **Minimum share capital** — No statutory minimum share capital for a private company ((approx — confirm; foreign investment registration may impose minimums))  _(Companies Act 2015)_
- **Small private company test** — Treated as 'small' where consolidated revenue is below FJ$5 million (lighter reporting) FJD  _(Companies Act 2015 (https://laws.gov.fj/Acts/DisplayAct/3231))_
- **Incorporation process** — Reserve name, lodge incorporation application with the Registrar of Companies (available online); a Certificate of Incorporation is issued  _(Companies Act 2015 (https://www.justice.gov.fj/company/))_
- **Foreign investment registration** — Foreign-owned businesses must obtain a Foreign Investment Registration Certificate before operating ((approx — confirm current foreign investment statute and reserved activities))  _(Investment Act 2021)_
- **Core annual compliance** — Annual return to the Registrar, maintenance of statutory registers, annual income tax return to FRCS, and (if registered) VAT returns  _(Companies Act 2015)_
- **Post-incorporation tax registration** — New companies must register with FRCS for a TIN and, where applicable, VAT, PAYE and FNPF  _(Tax Administration Act 2009)_
