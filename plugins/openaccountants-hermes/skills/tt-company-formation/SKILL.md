# Trinidad and Tobago Company Formation & Entity Choice

## Entity types and incorporation

- **Governing law** — Companies Act (Chap. 81:01)  _(Companies Act (Chap. 81:01) — https://legalaffairs.gov.tt/incorpcompany.php)_
- **Common entity types** — Private and public company limited by shares; company limited by guarantee (non-profit); unlimited company; external (branch of foreign) company; sole trader and partnership (registered business names)  _(Companies Act (Chap. 81:01) — https://legalaffairs.gov.tt/incorpcompany.php)_
- **Liability of members** — For a company limited by shares, members' liability is limited to amounts unpaid on their shares  _(Companies Act (Chap. 81:01) — https://legalaffairs.gov.tt/faqs.php)_
- **Minimum share capital** — No statutory minimum share capital; shares have no par value under the Companies Act ((approx — confirm))  _(Companies Act (Chap. 81:01) — https://legalaffairs.gov.tt/incorpcompany.php)_
- **Minimum directors** — At least one director (at least two for a public company); at least one director must be ordinarily resident in Trinidad and Tobago ((approx — confirm residency requirement))  _(Companies Act (Chap. 81:01) — https://legalaffairs.gov.tt/incorpcompany.php)_
- **Name reservation fee** — TTD 25, name held for 90 days TTD  _(Companies Act (Chap. 81:01) — https://legalaffairs.gov.tt/incorpcompany.php)_
- **Incorporation filing fee (profit company)** — TTD 520 TTD  _(Companies Act (Chap. 81:01) — https://legalaffairs.gov.tt/incorpcompany.php)_
- **Core incorporation steps** — Reserve company name, file Articles of Incorporation, Notice of Directors, Notice of Registered Office and Secretary, then obtain the Certificate of Incorporation  _(Companies Act (Chap. 81:01) — https://legalaffairs.gov.tt/incorpcompany.php)_
- **Typical incorporation timeline** — Approximately 3 to 6 weeks depending on documentation and approvals ((approx — confirm))  _(Companies Registry — https://www.usemultiplier.com/trinidad-and-tobago/company-registration)_
- **Tax registration after incorporation** — Obtain a BIR (Board of Inland Revenue) file number; register for PAYE, NIS, and VAT (where the threshold is met)  _(Income Tax Act (Chap. 75:01) — https://www.ird.gov.tt/)_
- **Annual return filing** — Every company must file an Annual Return with the Companies Registry each year  _(Companies Act (Chap. 81:01) — https://legalaffairs.gov.tt/faqs.php)_
- **Annual tax compliance** — File corporation tax return by 30 April, pay quarterly instalments, and account for Green Fund Levy and Business Levy as applicable  _(Corporation Tax Act (Chap. 75:02) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/trinidad-and-tobago/corporate/tax-administration)_
