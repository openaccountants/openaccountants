# Iraq Personal Income Tax

## Personal income tax rates and bands

- **Band 1 — up to IQD 250,000** — 3 percent (approx — confirm whether bands are annual or monthly)  _(Income Tax Law No. 113 of 1982 (https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/iraq/individual/taxes-on-personal-income))_
- **Band 2 — IQD 250,000 to 500,000** — 5 percent (approx — confirm)  _(Income Tax Law No. 113 of 1982 (https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/iraq/individual/taxes-on-personal-income))_
- **Band 3 — IQD 500,000 to 1,000,000** — 10 percent (approx — confirm)  _(Income Tax Law No. 113 of 1982 (https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/iraq/individual/taxes-on-personal-income))_
- **Band 4 — over IQD 1,000,000** — 15 percent (approx — confirm)  _(Income Tax Law No. 113 of 1982 (https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/iraq/individual/taxes-on-personal-income))_
- **Non-resident taxation** — Income derived from Iraq is taxed at the same progressive rates regardless of residence  _(Income Tax Law No. 113 of 1982 (https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/iraq/individual/taxes-on-personal-income))_
- **Annual personal allowance — single (bachelor)** — 2,500,000 IQD  _(Income Tax Law No. 113 of 1982 (https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/iraq/individual/deductions))_
- **Annual personal allowance — married** — 4,500,000 IQD  _(Income Tax Law No. 113 of 1982 (https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/iraq/individual/deductions))_
- **Age allowance (taxpayer over 63 years)** — 300,000 per year IQD  _(Income Tax Law No. 113 of 1982 (https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/iraq/individual/deductions))_
- **Child allowance** — 200,000 per child per year IQD (child must have no income, be under 18, and be in full-time study)  _(Income Tax Law No. 113 of 1982 (https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/iraq/individual/deductions))_
- **Risk allowance exemption** — Up to 30% of basic salary is tax-exempt percent  _(Income Tax Law No. 113 of 1982 (https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/iraq/individual/deductions))_
- **Social security contributions deductible** — Employee social security contributions are deductible from taxable income  _(Income Tax Law No. 113 of 1982 (https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/iraq/individual/deductions))_
- **Allowance documentation requirement** — Supporting documents must be filed or the taxpayer is treated as unmarried (single allowance only)  _(Income Tax Law No. 113 of 1982 (https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/iraq/individual/deductions))_
- **Individual return filing deadline** — 31 March of the assessment year  _(Income Tax Law No. 113 of 1982 (https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/iraq))_
- **Employer payroll withholding remittance** — Withheld tax is remitted within 15 days after the month in which salaries are paid (approx — confirm)  _(Income Tax Law No. 113 of 1982 (https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/iraq))_

Individuals are taxed at progressive rates from 3% to 15% under Income Tax Law No. 113 of 1982. Income derived from Iraq is taxable regardless of the individual's residence. Note: source figures for the bands vary; an Iraqi accountant should confirm the current thresholds and whether they are annual or monthly.
