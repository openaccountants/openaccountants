# Cape Verde Corporate Income Tax

## Corporate income tax (IRPC)

- **Corporate income tax (IRPC)** — Corporate income tax in Cape Verde is the Imposto sobre o Rendimento das Pessoas Colectivas (IRPC). Resident companies and permanent establishments are taxed on adjusted accounting profit; non-residents without a PE face withholding on Cape Verde-source income.
- **Standard corporate income tax rate** — 20% on taxable profit %  _(Codigo do IRPC (Corporate Income Tax Code) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/cabo-verde/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_
- **Municipal fire-brigade surcharge (Taxa de Incendio)** — 2% in Praia and Mindelo municipalities, giving an effective rate of about 20.40% %  _(Codigo do IRPC (Corporate Income Tax Code) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/cabo-verde/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_
- **Special single tax for micro and small companies (SST)** — 4% of gross annual sales, paid quarterly; replaces IRPC, fire surcharge, VAT and employer social security %  _(Regime Especial das Micro e Pequenas Empresas (REMPE) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/cabo-verde/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_
- **Tax base** — Accounting profit computed under local accounting rules, adjusted for tax purposes  _(Codigo do IRPC (Corporate Income Tax Code) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/cabo-verde/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_
- **Pillar Two global minimum tax** — 15% minimum tax for multinational/large domestic groups with annual revenue over EUR 750 million (approx — confirm; per 2026 legislation) %  _(Codigo do IRPC (Corporate Income Tax Code) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/cabo-verde/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_
- **Withholding tax on dividends (resident)** — Full relief in force on profit distributions  _(Codigo do IRPC (Corporate Income Tax Code) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/cabo-verde/corporate/withholding-taxes)_
- **Withholding tax on interest (resident)** — Generally 20%; bonds 10% (5% for listed stock-exchange bonds) %  _(Codigo do IRPC (Corporate Income Tax Code) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/cabo-verde/corporate/withholding-taxes)_
- **Withholding tax on royalties (resident)** — 20% %  _(Codigo do IRPC (Corporate Income Tax Code) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/cabo-verde/corporate/withholding-taxes)_
- **Withholding tax on dividends/interest/royalties (non-resident, non-treaty)** — 20% %  _(Codigo do IRPC (Corporate Income Tax Code) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/cabo-verde/corporate/withholding-taxes)_
- **Withholding tax on services (non-resident)** — 15% unless reduced by tax treaty %  _(Codigo do IRPC (Corporate Income Tax Code) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/cabo-verde/corporate/withholding-taxes)_
- **Annual return filing deadline** — 31 May of the year following the tax year  _(Codigo do IRPC (Corporate Income Tax Code) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/cabo-verde/corporate/tax-administration)_
- **Advance payments (standard regime)** — Three instalments due end of March, July and November at 30%, 30% and 20% of prior-year tax %  _(Codigo do IRPC (Corporate Income Tax Code) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/cabo-verde/corporate/tax-administration)_
