# San Marino Payroll & Social Contributions

## Social security (ISS) and payroll withholding

- **Total employer social-security contribution** — Approximately 27.4% of gross wages % (approx — confirm against current ISS schedule)  _([Istituto per la Sicurezza Sociale (ISS) contribution regulations](https://remotepeople.com/countries/san-marino/hire-employees/payroll-tax/))_
- **Employer — pension (first pillar)** — 16.6% % (approx — confirm)  _([Istituto per la Sicurezza Sociale (ISS) contribution regulations](https://remotepeople.com/countries/san-marino/hire-employees/payroll-tax/))_
- **Employer — Fondiss (supplementary pension)** — 2.0% % (approx — confirm)  _([Istituto per la Sicurezza Sociale (ISS) / Fondiss regulations](https://remotepeople.com/countries/san-marino/hire-employees/payroll-tax/))_
- **Employer — unemployment insurance** — 1.9% % (approx — confirm)  _([Istituto per la Sicurezza Sociale (ISS) contribution regulations](https://remotepeople.com/countries/san-marino/hire-employees/payroll-tax/))_
- **Employer — health and accident insurance** — 4.0% % (approx — confirm)  _([Istituto per la Sicurezza Sociale (ISS) contribution regulations](https://remotepeople.com/countries/san-marino/hire-employees/payroll-tax/))_
- **Employer — Social Services Fund** — 1.0% % (approx — confirm)  _([Istituto per la Sicurezza Sociale (ISS) contribution regulations](https://remotepeople.com/countries/san-marino/hire-employees/payroll-tax/))_
- **Total employee social-security contribution** — Approximately 8.3% of gross wages % (approx — confirm against current ISS schedule)  _([Istituto per la Sicurezza Sociale (ISS) contribution regulations](https://remotepeople.com/countries/san-marino/hire-employees/payroll-tax/))_
- **Employee — main pension pillar** — 5.9% % (approx — confirm)  _([Istituto per la Sicurezza Sociale (ISS) contribution regulations](https://remotepeople.com/countries/san-marino/hire-employees/payroll-tax/))_
- **Employee — Fondiss (supplementary pension)** — 2.0% % (approx — confirm)  _([Istituto per la Sicurezza Sociale (ISS) / Fondiss regulations](https://remotepeople.com/countries/san-marino/hire-employees/payroll-tax/))_
- **Employee — unemployment & family allowance** — 0.5% % (approx — confirm)  _([Istituto per la Sicurezza Sociale (ISS) contribution regulations](https://remotepeople.com/countries/san-marino/hire-employees/payroll-tax/))_
- **Payroll income-tax withholding (PAYE-equivalent)** — Employers withhold IGR from employees' salaries at source and remit to the Tax Office  _([Law no. 166 of 16 December 2013 (Imposta Generale sui Redditi)](https://remotepeople.com/countries/san-marino/hire-employees/payroll-tax/))_
- **Contribution / withholding remittance** — Employers remit ISS contributions and withheld IGR on a periodic (typically monthly) schedule (approx — confirm exact monthly due dates)  _([Istituto per la Sicurezza Sociale (ISS) contribution regulations](https://remotepeople.com/countries/san-marino/hire-employees/payroll-tax/))_
- **13th-month salary** — A mandatory 13th-month payment applies and is included in the contribution/withholding base  _([San Marino labour law / collective agreements](https://www.papayaglobal.com/countrypedia/country/san-marino/))_

Employers and employees contribute to the Istituto per la Sicurezza Sociale (ISS), funding pensions, healthcare, unemployment and family allowances. Employers also operate withholding of IGR on salaries. The component rates below are drawn from country payroll guides and should be confirmed against current ISS schedules.
