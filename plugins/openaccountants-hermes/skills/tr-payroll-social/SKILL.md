# Turkey Payroll & Social Contributions

## Social security & payroll (2025)

Employers withhold income tax and social security from wages and remit to the tax office and the Social Security Institution (Sosyal Güvenlik Kurumu, SGK). The 2025 figures below are set under Social Insurance Law No. 5510 and the annual minimum-wage/SSI determinations.

- **Employee social security contribution** — 14 % of gross wage (within SSI ceiling)  _(Social Insurance and General Health Insurance Law No. 5510 (https://www.cottgroup.com/en/legislation/item/statutory-deductions-on-payroll-for-2025))_
- **Employee unemployment insurance** — 1 % of gross wage (within SSI ceiling)  _(Unemployment Insurance Law No. 4447 (https://www.cottgroup.com/en/legislation/item/statutory-deductions-on-payroll-for-2025))_
- **Employer social security contribution** — 20.75% of gross wage (general); reduced by a 5-point incentive to 15.75% for qualifying employers/sectors % ((approx — confirm 2025 incentive eligibility/rate))  _(Social Insurance and General Health Insurance Law No. 5510 (https://www.cottgroup.com/en/legislation/item/statutory-deductions-on-payroll-for-2025))_
- **Employer unemployment insurance** — 2 % of gross wage (within SSI ceiling)  _(Unemployment Insurance Law No. 4447 (https://www.cottgroup.com/en/legislation/item/statutory-deductions-on-payroll-for-2025))_
- **State unemployment insurance contribution** — 1 % of gross wage (state share)  _(Unemployment Insurance Law No. 4447 (https://www.cottgroup.com/en/legislation/item/statutory-deductions-on-payroll-for-2025))_
- **SSI earnings floor (2025)** — 26,005.50 TRY/month (the minimum wage)  _(Social Insurance and General Health Insurance Law No. 5510 (https://turkishlaborlaw.com/2025-payroll-parameters/))_
- **SSI earnings ceiling (2025)** — 195,041.40 TRY/month (7.5x the floor)  _(Social Insurance and General Health Insurance Law No. 5510 (https://turkishlaborlaw.com/2025-payroll-parameters/))_
- **Minimum wage (2025)** — TRY 26,005.50 gross / TRY 22,104.67 net per month TRY/month  _(Labour Law No. 4857 / Minimum Wage Determination Commission (https://turkishlaborlaw.com/2025-payroll-parameters/))_
- **Wage income tax withholding (PAYE-equivalent)** — Employer withholds income tax on wages monthly using the progressive 15%–40% schedule; minimum-wage-equivalent portion is exempt  _(Income Tax Law No. 193 (https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/turkey/individual/other-taxes))_
- **Stamp tax on wages** — 0.759 % of gross salary (the minimum-wage portion is exempt)  _(Stamp Tax Law No. 488 (https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/turkey/individual/other-taxes))_
- **Payroll withholding remittance** — Income tax and stamp tax withheld on wages are declared and paid via the monthly withholding (muhtasar/concise) return; SGK premiums declared monthly to SGK ((confirm exact monthly remittance dates))  _(Income Tax Law No. 193; Social Insurance Law No. 5510 (https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/turkey/individual/tax-administration))_
- **Severance pay ceiling (2025)** — 46,655.43 TRY (per year of service, tax-exempt cap) ((approx — semi-annual ceiling; confirm period))  _(Labour Law No. 1475 (severance provisions) (https://turkishlaborlaw.com/2025-payroll-parameters/))_
