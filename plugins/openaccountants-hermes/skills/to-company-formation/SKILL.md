# Tonga Company Formation & Entity Choice

## Entity types and incorporation

- **Common entity types** — Limited liability company, sole trader/business name, partnership, and registered foreign company branch  _(Companies Act 1995 (https://www.businessregistries.gov.to/header-footer/how-to-register-a-company-2/))_
- **Company name requirement** — A company name must end with "Limited" or "Limiteti"  _(Companies Act 1995 (https://www.businessregistries.gov.to/header-footer/how-to-register-a-company-2/))_
- **Name reservation validity** — Reserved name valid for 20 days after approval; application must be filed within that window  _(Companies Act 1995 (https://www.businessregistries.gov.to/header-footer/how-to-register-a-company-2/))_
- **Incorporation application** — Form 1 delivered to the Registrar of Companies with fees and (if differing from the standard) the company constitution  _(Companies Act 1995 (https://www.businessregistries.gov.to/header-footer/how-to-register-a-company-2/))_
- **Company registration fee (online)** — USD 300 USD (per registry portal; confirm current TOP-denominated fee)  _(Companies Act 1995 (https://chandrawatpartners.com/llc-incorporation-in-tonga-step-by-step-process-key-insights/))_
- **Company registration fee (offline)** — USD 400 USD (per registry portal; confirm current TOP-denominated fee)  _(Companies Act 1995 (https://chandrawatpartners.com/llc-incorporation-in-tonga-step-by-step-process-key-insights/))_
- **Name reservation fee** — USD 60 online; USD 80 offline USD (per registry portal; confirm)  _(Companies Act 1995 (https://chandrawatpartners.com/llc-incorporation-in-tonga-step-by-step-process-key-insights/))_
- **Minimum share capital** — No statutory minimum share capital prescribed (approx — confirm against Companies Act/Regulations)  _(Companies Act 1995)_
- **Outcome of registration** — Certificate of Incorporation issued and the company listed on the Tongan Register  _(Companies Act 1995 (https://www.businessregistries.gov.to/header-footer/how-to-register-a-company-2/))_
- **Online registry system** — New online registration system for companies, business licenses, business names and foreign investments launched 11 December 2024  _(Companies Regulations 2025 (https://www.businessregistries.gov.to/))_
- **Core annual compliance** — File annual return with the Registrar, maintain a registered office and records, file annual income tax return, and file periodic Consumption Tax returns if registered (approx — confirm annual return due date)  _(Companies Act 1995)_
