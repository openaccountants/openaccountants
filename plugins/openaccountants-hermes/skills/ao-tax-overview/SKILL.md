# Angola Tax Overview

## Angola Tax Overview

## Angola tax system at a glance

- **Tax system structure** — Angola taxes through a set of separate schedular codes (personal income, corporate, investment income, VAT) administered by the AGT. The tax year is the calendar year and the currency is the Angolan kwanza (AOA).
- **Standard tax year** — Calendar year (1 January to 31 December)  _(Codigo do Imposto Industrial (Corporate Income Tax Code) - https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/angola/corporate/tax-administration)_
- **National currency** — Angolan kwanza (AOA)  _(Banco Nacional de Angola)_
- **National tax authority** — Administracao Geral Tributaria (AGT)  _(Administracao Geral Tributaria (AGT))_
- **Corporate tax basis** — Residents taxed on worldwide profits; non-residents with a PE taxed on profits attributable to the PE  _(Codigo do Imposto Industrial (Corporate Income Tax Code) - https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/angola/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_
- **Top personal income tax (IRT) rate** — 25% percent  _(Codigo do Imposto sobre os Rendimentos do Trabalho (IRT) - https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/angola/individual/taxes-on-personal-income)_
- **Standard corporate income tax rate** — 25% percent  _(Codigo do Imposto Industrial (Corporate Income Tax Code) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/angola/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_
- **Does Angola have VAT** — Yes — VAT (Imposto sobre o Valor Acrescentado, IVA) applies at a standard 14%  _(Codigo do Imposto sobre o Valor Acrescentado (VAT Code) - https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/angola/corporate/other-taxes)_
- **Standard VAT rate** — 14% percent  _(Codigo do Imposto sobre o Valor Acrescentado (VAT Code) - https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/angola/corporate/other-taxes)_
- **Corporate income tax annual return deadline (general regime)** — Last business day of May of the following year  _(Codigo do Imposto Industrial (Corporate Income Tax Code) - https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/angola/corporate/tax-administration)_
- **VAT return frequency** — Monthly  _(Codigo do Imposto sobre o Valor Acrescentado (VAT Code) - https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/angola/corporate/other-taxes)_
- **Personal income tax collection method** — PAYE — employer withholds IRT monthly; employees with only employment income generally do not file a return  _(Codigo do Imposto sobre os Rendimentos do Trabalho (IRT) - https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/angola/individual/taxes-on-personal-income)_
