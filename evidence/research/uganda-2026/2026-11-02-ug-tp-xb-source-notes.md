## URA, Compendium for various Domestic Tax Laws (as of July 2021), 1 060 pages
URL: https://ura.go.ug/en/download/compendium-for-various-domestic-tax-laws/  Read on: 2026-11-02
Old (pre-2024 revision) numbering, Income Tax Act Cap. 340. Current law to be confirmed on ULII (2024 revised
edition) and against the 2026 amendments.

- ITA s90 (Cap 340): in a transaction between associates or persons in an employment relationship, the Commissioner
  may distribute, apportion or allocate income, deductions or credits as necessary to reflect the chargeable income
  in an arm's length transaction; (2) adjust income from transfers/licences of intangibles between associates to be
  commensurate with the income attributable; (3) may determine source and character.
- s91: re-characterisation of tax avoidance schemes; disregard transactions without substantial economic effect.
- Income Tax (Transfer Pricing) Regulations 2011 (S.I. 2011 No. 30 per compendium index; made under ss90, 164; in
  force 1 July 2011):
  - r2: apply to a controlled transaction where one party is located in and subject to tax in Uganda and the other
    is located in or outside Uganda (i.e. domestic and cross-border).
  - r3: "controlled transaction" = transaction between associates ("associate" per ITA s3); "branch" = treaty PE
    or ITA s78 branch; five methods: CUP, resale price, cost plus, TNMM, transactional profit split.
  - r4: comparability factors (property/services, functions-assets-risks, contract terms, economic circumstances,
    business strategies).
  - r5: branch and headquarters deemed separate persons and associates.
  - r6: apply consistently with OECD MTC Art. 9 and the OECD TP Guidelines as updated; the Act prevails.
  - r7: determine income/expenditure consistent with the ALP; Commissioner may adjust; most appropriate method
    factors; Commissioner examines on the person's method if appropriate; other methods if none can apply;
    r7(6) offence: up to six months' imprisonment or fine up to 25 currency points, or both.
  - r8: written documentation sufficient to verify ALP, in place before the due date of the return for the year;
    Commissioner may specify items by notice; r8(4) same offence penalty.
  - r9: advance pricing agreements (request contents, Commissioner discretion, bilateral possible, cancellation).
  - r10: corresponding adjustments on request after a treaty partner's adjustment.
