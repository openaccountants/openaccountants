## VAT-D01: preserve the supplier's alternative for VAT charged in excess

RIVA 74bis correctly excludes these earlier-return requests from the ordinary rectificativa; it does not remove the alternative statutory adjustment.

The supplier can alternatively regularise under [LIVA89.Cinco(b)](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-1992-28740#a89) in the period when correction should occur or later returns within one year from that point, and must reimburse the recipient's excess VAT.
