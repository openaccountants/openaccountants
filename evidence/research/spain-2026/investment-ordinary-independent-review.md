I explicitly exported the selected element's full text and HTML for V0386-24 and V0835-25 under `independent/sources/`. The selected full texts are 42,236 and 27,792 bytes. The initial body-innerText-only captures are retained with `body-innertext-search-only` names for provenance and are not treated as authority.

**Fix for the parent:** preserve the original artifacts/hashes, identify the TXT as search-only, identify the HTML as containing the full selected ruling, and link the independent selected-element exports in the source map/receipt notes.
