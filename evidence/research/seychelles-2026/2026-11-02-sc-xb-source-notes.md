## Discrepancies noted

- The SRC page shows 5% on bearer bonds and 15% on Treasury Bills; S.I. 107 of 2024 replaced the non-resident interest table from 1 Jan 2025 (Government Securities 0%; bearer bonds no longer listed). The skill follows the regulation.
- The SRC page dates the economic substance change to 16 September 2021; S.I. 78 of 2021 fixes commencement of Act 2 of 2021 at 15 September 2021. The skill follows the notice.
- The SRC International Tax page says 28 DTCs; the Agreements page lists 29 DTAAs in force. The skill uses the Agreements list.
- BTA 2009 s 8(2)(b) originally cross-referred to s 60; Act 14 of 2012 s 2(b) changed that figure (OCR unclear). The skill states only that PE income is outside s 8.
