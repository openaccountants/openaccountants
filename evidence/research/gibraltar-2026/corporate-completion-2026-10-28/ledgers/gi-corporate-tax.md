## Assumptions and source limits

Synthetic pound/share/day inputs are examples, not sourced statutory amounts. The statutory rate, limit, ordering or valuation rule has its own row. Mixed-loss cap treatment remains the earlier reviewed statutory interpretation, without Commissioner acceptance. Gazette publication identity, literal capital-disposal/extended-period wording remain explicit. CT4 published postal instructions are now supplied; no particular submission receipt or reduction approval is inferred. Threshold-index rows repeat these same source-backed rules rather than adding a new regime. Intake and evidence lists are writer preparation controls, not prescribed extra filing obligations.
