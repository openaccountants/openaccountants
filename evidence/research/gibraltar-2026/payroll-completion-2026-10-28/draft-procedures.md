### Starter, leaver and missed-PAYE workflow

Keep separate employer registration, employee identity/contract, authority to receive the code, the actual code/system, contribution class and first payday. The current employer FAQ publishes **paye.enquiries@gibraltar.gov.gi** for company PAYE registration and **paye.registration@gibraltar.gov.gi** for employee registration. For a returning employee, the employee PAYE page also requests Part 1 of the previous P7A. These document routes do not replace the seven-day allowances declaration, the new-employer certificate procedure or the code-X fallback in regulations 4, 8 and 13A above. Retain the issued code and effective payroll period; a registration request is not an issued code. [Employer FAQ](https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/income-tax-office/employers-and-trusts/employers); [employee FAQ](https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/income-tax-office/individuals-and-employees/paye); [PAYE Regulations 4–8 and 13A](https://www.gibraltarlaws.gov.gi/uploads/legislations/income-tax/1989s114/1989s114(01-07-23).pdf).

On leaving an employment, keep the cessation date and P7A distribution/receipt evidence. The current employer page publishes **paye.p7a@gibraltar.gov.gi** for copies of P7A; its directions send Part 1 to ITO and Parts 2/3 to the employee. Regulation 13A additionally requires the new employer receiving Parts 2/3 to send Part 3 immediately after engagement. Do not confuse employer P7A delivery with an employee's final-assessment claim on leaving Gibraltar permanently/indefinitely, which uses the Claim for Final Assessment plus last-employer P7A Part 1 at **paye.leavinggib@gibraltar.gov.gi**. One does not establish completion of the other. [Employer leaver FAQ](https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/income-tax-office/employers-and-trusts/employers); [employee departure FAQ](https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/income-tax-office/individuals-and-employees/paye); [regulation 13A](https://www.gibraltarlaws.gov.gi/uploads/legislations/income-tax/1989s114/1989s114(01-07-23).pdf).

For PAYE previously not deducted/paid, regulation 4(6) permits the Commissioner, at discretion, to adjust the employee's coding for recovery; regulation 8(4) separately permits a directed deduction. Keep the actual revised code/direction, period and payment reconciliation. These powers do not permit the employer to invent its own tax code or prove a right to recover missed social-insurance contributions from later wages. Regulation 11(2)'s employer liability for a failure to deduct remains separate from recovery from the employee. [Regulations 4(6), 8(4) and 11(2)](https://www.gibraltarlaws.gov.gi/uploads/legislations/income-tax/1989s114/1989s114(01-07-23).pdf).

For a proposed redundancy exemption, the employer FAQ requests the Termination of Employment Payment Questionnaire, Department-of-Employment-approved termination form, Notice of Terms and Engagement, and compromise/settlement agreement if applicable, using **paye.enquiries@gibraltar.gov.gi**. It directs that payment be held pending confirmation. For retirement-gratuity processing it requests the questionnaire, Notice of Terms of Engagement and signed/stamped termination form clearly stating the gratuity figure, again holding payment for confirmation. The dated employer notes also require an ITO directive before cessation lump sums. [Current employer FAQ](https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/income-tax-office/employers-and-trusts/employers).

The exemption depends on the type of payment, not its label: Allowances rule 3(7) covers tribunal-awarded unfair-dismissal compensation; rule 3(8) covers an agreed amount the Commissioner considers would have been awarded; rule 3(9) covers the redundancy amount the Commissioner considers appropriate, including regard to service and pay. Rule 3(9A) separately concerns the stated construction-industry terminal gratuity, and rule 3(10) identifies its capital retiring/injury/death gratuity or compensation categories with the retirement-benefit-scheme exclusion. Keep the charging/exemption analysis, settlement, authority decision and PAYE directive separately. This method does not compute statutory employment entitlements or make all termination pay exempt. [Allowances Rules 3(7)–(10)](https://www.gibraltarlaws.gov.gi/uploads/legislations/income-tax/1992s016/1992s016(23-03-26).pdf).
