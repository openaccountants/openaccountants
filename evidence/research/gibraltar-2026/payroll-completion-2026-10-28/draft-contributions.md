### Published second-employment exemption application

ITO publishes a Social Insurance Exemption form addressed to the Commissioner for a second employment where the maximum contribution rate is paid through the primary employment. It asks for taxpayer reference, date of birth, both employments, signature/date and telephone/mobile; **the last three main-employment payslips and passport/ID must accompany it**. The form undertakes immediate notification of changed circumstances and reserves payslip review/approval fields for the office. It publishes **sicontributions@gibraltar.gov.gi**, corroborated by the current ITO contact page. Keep the application, actual approval/certificate, payroll class/covered periods and later changes separately. Submission alone is not exemption approval. [Official application](https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/uploads/Income%20Tax%20Office/docs/contributions/4%29%20SOCIAL%20INSURANCE%20EXEMPTION.pdf); [current contact page](https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/contacts/income-tax-office-76).

This supplies a published route for the maximum-in-primary-employment case, not a universal allocation formula for two jobs below the maximum, a retrospective refund decision or a reconciliation of every first-employer/weekly-cap issue identified below. Continue using the actual issued class/determination rather than ceasing contributions because an employee has a second job. The form's historic file location does not by itself show withdrawal; no application or enquiry has been sent in preparing this Guide.

### Court recovery is not an employee wage deduction

Social Insurance regulation 7 concerns the court-recovery circumstances and related sums payable to the Fund. Paragraph (6) expressly says an employer's payment under those provisions satisfies unpaid contributions and **no part is recoverable from the insured person**. That is a positive restriction for those sums, not a general answer authorising collection of an ordinary missed employee deduction from later wages. The Act's section 7 restrictions and the unresolved ordinary recovery mechanism remain as stated above. Keep the order, contribution period, employer payment and employee deductions distinct. [SI regulation 7(1)–(8), especially 7(6)](https://www.gibraltarlaws.gov.gi/uploads/legislations/social-security/1955.08.04-5/1955.08.04-5(01-07-15).pdf).
