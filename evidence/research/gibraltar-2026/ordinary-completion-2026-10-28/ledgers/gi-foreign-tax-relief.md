Exactly10%meets“atleast10%”;9.99%doesnot; these are boundary arithmetic, not proof of control. The31December2025→31December2031illustration is calendar arithmetic from the sourced six-year period.

The exit example assumes supported marketvalue1000000andtaxvalue600000, giving400000base. The retained-PEboundary assumes another asset300000/150000classifiedwithin18A(1)(c)exception; its150000difference is excluded from this event and does not prove later tax exemption. Scope, residence, classification and values are assumptions requiring case evidence. No taxrate, equal instalment, foreigntaxbasis, conversion or relief entitlement is invented. The two main blocks and one supplementary exit control are executable. Evidence lists are writer working-paper recommendations, not prescribed claim forms.
