## KRA, Pay As You Earn (PAYE)
URL: https://www.kra.go.ke/individual/filing-paying/types-of-taxes/paye
Read on: 2026-10-29

- Bands (stated as Finance Act 2023, effective 1 July 2023), monthly / annual:
  first 24 000 / 288 000 at 10%; next 8 333 / 100 000 at 25%; next 467 667 / 5 612 000 at 30%;
  next 300 000 / 3 600 000 at 32.5%; above 800 000 / 9 600 000 at 35%.
- Personal relief KShs 2 400 a month / 28 800 a year (resident individuals).
- Insurance relief 15% of premiums, max KShs 60 000 a year.
- Non-cash benefits taxable above KShs 5 000 a month / 60 000 a year; meals up to 5 000 a month exempt.
- Employer pension contribution exempt up to KShs 30 000 a month / 360 000 a year.
- Deductible: Affordable Housing Levy; post-retirement medical fund up to 15 000 a month; SHIF contributions;
  mortgage interest up to 360 000 a year; registered pension/provident up to 360 000 a year (30 000 a month).
- AHL: employee 1.5% and employer 1.5% of gross monthly salary; remit within 9 working days after month end.
- PAYE remit and return by the 9th day of the following month, via iTax; nil returns required.
- TO CHECK against the Income Tax Act (Cap. 470) Third Schedule and any Finance Act 2025/2026 changes.

## Income Tax Act, Cap. 470 (Kenya Law consolidated version dated 1 July 2026; latest version)
URL: https://new.kenyalaw.org/akn/ke/act/1973/16/eng@2026-07-01
Read on: 2026-10-29 (targeted reading of the provisions below only; Kenya Law terms prohibit bulk extraction)

- Third Schedule Head A para 1: personal relief KShs 28 800 (a year). Para 2: insurance relief 15% of premiums,
  max KShs 60 000 a year. Paras 3, 4 deleted (Act 12 of 2024).
- Third Schedule Head B para 1 (amended up to Act No. 19 of 2026 s21): individual rates: first 288 000 at 10%;
  next 100 000 at 25%; next 5 612 000 at 30%; next 3 600 000 at 32.5%; over 9 600 000 at 35%. MATCHES KRA PAYE page.
- s15(2)(ac): employee deduction for amount deducted under Affordable Housing Act 2024 s5(1)(a);
  (ad): post-retirement medical fund up to KShs 15 000 a month; (ae): SHIF contributions under Social Health
  Insurance Act 2023 s27(a)-(b). s15(2)(ga): employer AHL under AHA s5(b) deductible for a business.
- s22A(1): employee pension/provident deduction = lesser of contributions, 30% of pensionable income, KShs 360 000
  (KShs 30 000 per month of service for part years).
- s37(1): employer paying emoluments must deduct and account for tax as prescribed; (1A) must grant all applicable
  deductions, reliefs and exemptions; (2) failure to deduct or account: penalty 25% of tax involved or KShs 10 000,
  whichever is greater (or prosecution under s109(1)(j)); (4) deducted tax deemed paid by employee; (5) remittance
  time limit set by rules under s130 — late amounts treated as tax due with interest; (5A) objection within 30 days.
- s18(3): transfer pricing — where a non-resident carries on business with a related resident person (or through
  its PE) producing no or less than ordinary profits, profits are deemed to be those at arm's length.
  s18(5): PE profits without deduction for interest, royalties, management/professional fees paid to the non-resident
  (incl. head office), disregarding FX gains/losses on internal balances.
- s2 "related person": either person participates directly or indirectly in the management, control or capital of
  the other's business; for more than two persons, a third person or individual (incl. by marriage, consanguinity,
  affinity) so participating.
- s2 "resident" (body of persons): incorporated in Kenya; or management and control exercised in Kenya in the year;
  or declared resident by Gazette notice.
- s18C: resident MNE group member (not excluded) must notify the Commissioner by the last day of the reporting
  financial year whether it is the UPE / surrogate parent, or identify the UPE/SPE and its residence.
- s18D: CbC report by Kenyan-resident UPE (1), or by a resident constituent entity in the (1A) secondary cases;
  (1B) group consolidated turnover at least KShs 95 billion in the preceding financial year; (2) CbCR within
  12 months after year end; (3)-(4) master file and local file to be FILED within six months after the last day of
  the group's reporting financial year; (6) master file contents; (7) local file contents; (8) designation of one
  entity; (10) surrogate parent relief.
- s18E: failure to comply with s18C/18D is an offence, penalties under the Tax Procedures Act (Cap. 469B).

## National Social Security Fund Act 2013 (No. 45 of 2013; NSSF Act Cap. 258), Kenya Law version 31 Dec 2022
URL: https://new.kenyalaw.org/akn/ke/act/2013/45/eng@2022-12-31
Read on: 2026-10-29
- s20(1): employer pays employer's contribution 6% of monthly pensionable earnings and employee's 6% deducted from
  earnings; (1A) pay on the ninth day of each month (or later date the Board prescribes); (2) first five years per
  Third Schedule; (3) Tier I to Tier I credit, Tier II to Tier II credit (subject to s21 contracting out).
- s21: employer may contract out Tier II to a registered contracted-out scheme (conditions, Fourth Schedule).
- s2: "pensionable earnings" = lower of monthly wages and the UEL; LEL = gazetted average statutory minimum monthly
  wage; UEL = 4 x National Average Earnings (both subject to the Third Schedule).
- Third Schedule table: Year 4 LEL 9 000; UEL 3 x National Average Earnings; Year 5 LEL per para 2(a), UEL 4 x NAE.

## Social Health Insurance Act 2023 (No. 16 of 2023), Kenya Law version 24 Nov 2023
URL: https://new.kenyalaw.org/akn/ke/act/2023/16/eng@2023-11-24
Read on: 2026-10-29
- s27(1): liable to contribute: every Kenyan household; non-Kenyans ordinarily resident > 12 months; national and
  county governments; any other employer. s27(2)(a): salaried households by monthly statutory deduction from wages

## Tax Procedures Act 2015 (No. 29 of 2015, Cap. 469B), Kenya Law version 1 September 2026 (latest)
URL: https://new.kenyalaw.org/akn/ke/act/2015/29/eng@2026-09-01
Read on: 2026-10-29
- s38(1),(3),(4): late payment interest 1% per month or part month, simple, on the unpaid tax, in addition to penalties.
- s83(1)(a): late/non-submission of a return on account of employment income: 25% of the tax due or KShs 10 000,
  whichever is higher; (d)(i) other returns, non-individual: 5% of tax payable or KShs 20 000, higher; (d)(ii)
  individual: 5% or KShs 2 000; tax payable reduced by amounts paid and WHT credits. (2) failure to submit a
  document other than a return: KShs 1 000 per day, capped at KShs 50 000.
- s83A: late payment penalty 5% of the tax due.
- s84: tax shortfall penalty 75% of the shortfall where the false statement/omission was deliberate; +10 points on
  second, +25 points on third application; -10 points for voluntary disclosure before discovery/audit.
- No TP- or CbCR-specific penalty provision found by text search for "country-by-country", "master file",
  "transfer pricing" in this version. NOT CONFIRMED which TPA penalty attaches to ITA s18E.

## Income Tax (Transfer Pricing) Rules 2006 (LN 67 of 2006), Kenya Law version 31 Dec 2022
URL: https://new.kenyalaw.org/akn/ke/act/ln/2006/67/eng@2022-12-31
Read on: 2026-10-29
- r1: in force 1 July 2006. r2: "related enterprises" = one participates directly/indirectly in management, control
  or capital of the other, or a third person in both.
- r4: taxpayer chooses the method from r7. r5: scope — cross-border transactions between related enterprises of a
  multinational (one in Kenya), and PE–head office/branch dealings (PE treated as separate enterprise).
- r6: covered transactions: goods; tangible assets; intangibles; services; lending/borrowing; any other affecting
  profit/loss.
- r7 methods: CUP, resale price, cost plus, profit split, TNMM, other method prescribed by the Commissioner.
- r8(1)-(2): apply for s18(3); most appropriate method. (3) Commissioner may issue guidelines.
- r9: Commissioner may request information/documents: method selection and reasons; application and calculations;
  global organisation structure; transaction details; assumptions, strategies, policies; background. In English,
  prepared at the time the transfer price is arrived at.
- r10: a person claiming arm's length pricing must have a TP policy, determine the ALP per the Rules, and make
  documentation available on request.
- r11: Act provisions on fraud, failure to furnish returns and underpayment apply to TP. r12: TP tax unpaid deemed
  additional tax for ss94-95 (old references).
