Read on: 2026-10-29 (page last updated 28/10/2026)

  Same 27% for YAs ending 1 Apr 2025–31 Mar 2026 and 1 Apr 2024–31 Mar 2025 and 31 Mar 2023–31 Mar 2024.

  - 550 001+: 57 470 + 27% above 550 000
- SBC, YAs ending 1 Apr 2025–31 Mar 2026: 0% to 95 750; 7% above 95 750; 18 848 + 21% above 365 000;
  57 698 + 27% above 550 000.

## SARS, Unemployment Insurance Fund
URL: https://www.sars.gov.za/types-of-tax/unemployment-insurance-fund/
Read on: 2026-10-29

- Legislation: Unemployment Insurance Act 63 of 2001; Unemployment Insurance Contributions Act 4 of 2002 (in force 1 April 2002).
- Employee 1% of remuneration; employer 1%; employer pays total 2%.
- Ceiling: R17 712 per month / R212 544 a year with effect from 1 June 2021 (was R14 872 / R178 464 from 1 Oct 2012).
  Max employee deduction R177.12 a month.
- Excluded employees: under 24 hours a month with the employer; national/provincial government officers/employees;
  President, ministers, MPs, NCOP delegates, premiers, MECs, MPLs; municipal councillors, traditional leaders.
- Employers registered for employees' tax register with SARS for UIF too; others register with the UI Commissioner.
- Paid monthly on EMP201 within seven days after month end; if the last day is a weekend/public holiday, pay on the last
  business day before.
- Offences: Tax Administration Act ch. 17 applies to the UIC Act; fine or up to two years' imprisonment.
- TO CONFIRM against the Government Gazette: the R17 712 ceiling is set by ministerial notice; confirm no later notice.

## SARS, Skills Development Levy
URL: https://www.sars.gov.za/types-of-tax/skills-development-levy/
Read on: 2026-10-29

- Rate: 1% of total remuneration (wages, overtime, leave pay, bonuses, fees, commissions, lump sums).
- Liable when the employer expects total remuneration over the next 12 months to exceed R500 000; below that, exempt
  and not required to register.
- Also exempt: national/provincial public service employers; national/provincial public entities with 80%+ of
  expenditure from funds voted by Parliament; PBOs exempt under ITA s10(1)(cN) carrying on only certain activities,
  with a TEU exemption letter; municipalities with a Minister of Higher Education and Training certificate.
- Paid monthly on EMP201 within seven days after month end (same weekend/holiday rule as UIF). Distributed via SETAs.
- Statute: Skills Development Levies Act 9 of 1999 (cite the Act text for rate s3 and exemptions s4; TO READ).

  R254 each additional dependant (monthly).

- Official interest rate: repo rate + 100 bp (rand debt).
- Residential accommodation abatement: R99 000.
- Travel allowance: 80% of allowance subject to PAYE; 20% if employer satisfied >=80% business use.
  2027 vehicle cost ceiling R920 000; simplified rate R4.95/km.
- Bursaries: remuneration proxy R900 000; Gr R–12/NQF 1–4 R30 000 (R45 000 disability); NQF 5–10 R90 000 (R130 000).
- Company car fringe benefit: 3.5% per month of determined value (3.25% if maintenance plan included).
- Retirement fund deduction: lesser of R430 000 or 27.5% of the higher of remuneration / taxable income (with
  stated exclusions), limited to taxable income before s11F etc.
- Records: five years from submission of the return (longer if audit/objection).
- Payment of PAYE, SDL, UIF: within seven days after month end (Fourth Sch. paras 2(1), 5(1), 14(2); SDL Act s6;
  UIC Act ss7(4), 8; TAA ch. 10); if the seventh day is Sat/Sun/public holiday, last business day before.
  EMP201 with each payment. Employer personally liable for PAYE/UIF not deducted.

  10% penalty plus interest at the prescribed rate on late/unpaid PAYE, SDL, UIF; up to twice the amount for
  intent to evade.
- UIF ceiling table (UIC Act ss5, 6): from 1 June 2021 R17 712 a month (R212 544 a year). Minister determines and
  gazettes the threshold. IRP5 code 4141 (UIF), 4142 (SDL).
- SDL remuneration excludes: exempt para (c)/(d) "employee" amounts with para 2(5)(a) certificate; pensions/
  superannuation/retiring allowances; s1 "gross income" paras (a), (d), (e), (eA) amounts; payments to learners
  under SDL Act s18(3).
- Directors of private companies / CC members: are "employees" (Fourth Sch. para 1); s7B: variable remuneration
  accrues when paid (from 1 March 2017); company may recover PAYE on deemed remuneration from the director.
- Estimated assessments: TAA ch. 8; UIC Act s9A.

## Income Tax Act 58 of 1962 (SARS-provided consolidation via LexisNexis, updated to GG 55220 of 19 August 2026)
URL: https://sars.mylexisnexis.co.za/ (linked from https://www.sars.gov.za/legal-counsel/primary-legislation/)
Read on: 2026-10-29

- s12E(4)(a) small business corporation: CC, co-operative, private company or personal liability company whose
  shareholders/members are ALL natural persons at all times in the YA, and
  (i) gross income for the YA does not exceed R20 million (pro rata by months, part month = full month, if trading <12 months);
  (ii) no shareholder/member holds shares or equity in any other company, except listed shares, CIS portfolios,
       s10(1)(e)(i) bodies corporate etc., <5% of social/consumer co-ops / co-op burial societies / co-op banks,
       friendly societies, s12J VCCs, dormant companies (no trade ever and assets <= R5 000), companies in liquidation
       steps under s41(4);
  (iii) not more than 20% of all receipts and accruals (non-capital) plus capital gains is investment income plus
        income from rendering a personal service;
  (iv) not a personal service provider (Fourth Schedule).
- s12E(1): plant/machinery used directly in manufacturing by an SBC, first used on/after 1 April 2001: 100% deduction in
  year first used. s12E(1A): other s11(e) assets: election of 50/30/20 over three years (agreements on/after 1 April 2005).
  s12E(3B): no s12E deduction where s12BA granted.
- s20(1)(a)(i): companies (non-mining) set off balance of assessed loss brought forward only up to the HIGHER of R1 million
  and 80% of taxable income before s20. Limit lifted for companies that took s41(4) liquidation steps. Non-companies
  (iii): unlimited. Proviso (b): foreign trade losses cannot be set off against SA-source income. s20(2): assessed loss =
  s11 deductions exceeding income.
- s64E(1)(a): dividends tax 20% of dividends paid by any company other than a headquarter company (Minister may
  announce a different rate in the budget).
- Fourth Schedule para 23(1): companies pay provisional tax (a) within 6 months after the start of the YA, half the
  estimated normal tax; (b) by the last day of the YA, the full estimate less (a); both less employees' tax and
  s6quat foreign tax rebate. 23(2): (a) does not apply to YAs of 6 months or less.
- Para 20(1): underestimation penalty 20% (deemed TAA ch.15 percentage penalty) where taxable income > R1m and final
  estimate < 80% of actual: 20% x (tax on 80% of taxable income less employees' + provisional tax paid by YA end).
  Where <= R1m: 90% test and basic amount.
- Para 27(1): 10% penalty on provisional tax not paid in time (paras 21, 23, 25(1)).

## Skills Development Levies Act 9 of 1999 (SARS LexisNexis consolidation, updated to GG 51828 of 24 Dec 2024)
URL: https://sars.mylexisnexis.co.za/ (Acts administered by the Commissioner)
Read on: 2026-10-29

- s3(1)(a)(ii): 1% of the leviable amount from 1 April 2001 (Minister may announce another rate, s3(1)(b)).
- s3(3): leviable amount = total remuneration paid/payable/deemed in the month as determined under the Fourth Schedule
  for employees' tax, whether or not PAYE is actually deductible.
- s3(4) excludes: (a) exempt para (c)/(d) "employee" persons with para 2(5)(a) certificate; (b) pensions,
  superannuation, retiring allowances; (c) "gross income" paras (a), (d), (e), (eA), (eD) amounts.
- s4 exemptions: (a) national/provincial public service employers; (b) employer with reasonable grounds to believe
  remuneration over the following 12 months will not exceed R500 000; (c) s10(1)(cN) PBOs solely carrying on PBA in
  Ninth Sch. Part I paras 1, 2(a)-(d), 5 or solely funding such PBOs; (d) national/provincial public entities 80%+
  funded by Parliament; (e) municipalities with an exemption certificate.
- s6(1): pay not later than seven days after the end of the month; s6(1A) micro businesses under Sixth Schedule may
  pay on para 11(4A) dates; s6(2) return with payment.

## Unemployment Insurance Contributions Act 4 of 2002 (SARS LexisNexis consolidation, updated to GG 51828 of 24 Dec 2024)
Read on: 2026-10-29

- s4(1): applies to all employers and employees except (a) employed < 24 hours a month by that employer;
  (c) Public Service Act officers/employees in national and provincial government; (e) President, Deputy President,
  Ministers, Deputy Ministers, MPs, NCOP permanent delegates, Premiers, MECs, MPLs; (f) municipal councillors,
  traditional leaders and members of Houses/Council of Traditional Leaders.
- s5: employer and employee contribute monthly; employer pays to SARS (s8) or UI Commissioner (s9).
- s6(1)(a): employee 1% of remuneration paid/payable in the month; employer 1% for each employee. s6(2): no
  contribution on remuneration above the amount set by the Minister of Finance by Gazette notice (SARS guide G21 and
  SARS UIF page: R17 712 a month from 1 June 2021). The Gazette notice itself sits behind the subordinate-legislation
  login and was not read directly.
- s7(1)-(2): employer deducts employee's share monthly (or at the pay interval). s7(3)(c): may not recover arrear
  employee contributions after the end of the financial year. s7(4): employer liable if it fails to deduct.
  s7(4A): directors/shareholders controlling a non-listed company personally liable for deducted contributions not
  paid over.

## Tax Administration Act 28 of 2011 (SARS LexisNexis consolidation)
Read on: 2026-10-29
- s210: fixed-amount penalty for non-compliance listed in a Commissioner public notice (excludes late payment subject
  to a percentage penalty, understatement penalty cases, reportable-arrangement penalties).
- s211(1) table, by assessed loss / taxable income for the preceding year: assessed loss R250; R0–250 000 R250;
  R250 001–500 000 R500; R500 001–1 000 000 R1 000; R1 000 001–5 000 000 R2 000; R5 000 001–10 000 000 R4 000;
  R10 000 001–50 000 000 R8 000; above R50 000 000 R16 000. s211(2): increases by the same amount each month (or part)
  not remedied after one month, up to 35 months from assessment (47 months if SARS cannot deliver).
  s211(3): listed companies and companies above a gross-receipts level fall in item (vii) at least.
- s213(1): percentage-based penalty for tax not paid when due, at the percentage the tax Act prescribes, in addition
  to interest.
