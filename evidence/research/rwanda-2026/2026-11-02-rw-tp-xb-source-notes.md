## Text quirks

- TPL art 82: "twenty percent (10%)" in the middle band of late payment after timely declaration; the skills follow the figure 10%, as rw-corporate-income-tax already does.
- TPL art 80: "zero point five per cent (0.5)" without the % sign.
- ITL art 9 (2022 text): "FRW 2,000,0000" typo; superseded by L051 art 1.
- O003 art 19(2): "with the value below FRW 10,000,000 or with an aggregate value below FRW 100,000,000"; the "or" is ambiguous.
- O003 art 18(1): documentation "prepared before the deadline of income tax declaration and submitted to the tax administration"; art 18(2) limits routine submission to the structure document and schedule.
