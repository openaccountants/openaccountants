## Stamp tax

### Stamp Tax Law 111 of 1980
- URL: https://eta.gov.eg/sites/default/files/2026-05/law.no_.111.of_.1981.pdf (ETA "other related laws" page lists it as "181 لسنة 1981 قانون ضريبة الدمغة"; the file is the 1980 law).
- 40 scanned pages, no text layer. Page 1 read: Gazette 22 bis (a), 31 May 1980; issuing art. 1 (law applies), art. 2 (repeals Law 224/1951), art. 3 (Minister issues regulations), art. 4 (in force the day after publication); issued 26 May 1980.
- Schedule pages not relied on: amounts are 1980 amounts, replaced by later laws (Law 143/2006 and others) that are not on eta.gov.eg.

### Law 153 of 2026 (amends art. 83 bis)
- URL: https://eta.gov.eg/sites/default/files/2026-08/law.no_.153.of_.2026.pdf. Gazette 30 bis (a), 28 Jul 2026, pages 21 to 22. Text layer read.
- Art. 1, new art. 83 bis: tax on total sales of securities listed on the Egyptian Exchange except listed investment fund units, no costs deducted; borne by buyer and seller: 0.5 per thousand buyer (resident or non-resident) and 0.5 per thousand seller; 0.25 per thousand each for purchases and sales on the same day. Not applicable to companies licensed as approved market maker under Capital Market Law 95/1992. The body responsible for settlement withholds and remits to the competent tax office within five days from the start of the month after the trade, on the prepared form, jointly liable with seller and buyer for tax and delay charge.
- Art. 2: repeals art. 5 of Law 199/2020.
- Art. 3: in force the day after publication, so 29 Jul 2026. Issued 28 Jul 2026.

### Law 199 of 2020
- URL: https://eta.gov.eg/sites/default/files/2024-03/law_no.199-2020.pdf. Gazette 39 bis (z), 30 Sep 2020, pages 5 to 11. Text layer read.
- Art. 1, old art. 83 bis: tax on total sales of securities of all kinds, Egyptian or foreign, listed or not, no costs deducted: 1.25 per thousand non-resident seller, 1.25 per thousand non-resident buyer; 0.5 per thousand resident seller, 0.5 per thousand resident buyer; not on purchases and sales on the same day; settlement body withholds and remits within five days of the start of the next month, jointly liable.
- Art. 4: capital gains tax on listed securities for residents deferred to end 2021.
- Art. 5: residents not subject to the art. 83 bis stamp tax from the day after the art. 4 deferral ends.
- Art. 9: in force the day after publication.

### Law 76 of 2017
- URL: https://eta.gov.eg/sites/default/files/2024-03/law_no.76-2017.pdf. Gazette 24 bis (b), 19 Jun 2017. Text layer garbled; page images 3 and 4 read.
- Page 3 (Gazette p. 4), art. 83 bis (1): tax of 3 per thousand, no costs deducted, on each acquisition or exit in one deal where (1) the deal covers 33% or more of the shares or voting rights, by number or value, in a resident company; or (2) 33% or more of the assets or liabilities of a resident company in exchange for shares in the buying company. Seller bears 3 per thousand, buyer 3 per thousand. Purchases by one person in one company reaching the threshold within two years from the first purchase after the law are taxed, less tax already paid. The tax is not a deductible cost under the Income Tax Law. Misr for Clearing (شركة مصر للمقاصة) or any other settlement body withholds and remits to ETA per the Minister of Finance's procedure and dates, jointly liable.
- Page 4 (Gazette p. 5): art. 4 (repeal of conflicting provisions), art. 5 (in force the day after publication); issued 18 Jun 2017.

### Law 3 of 2022
- URL: https://eta.gov.eg/sites/default/files/2024-03/vat-law_no.3-2022.pdf. Gazette 3 bis (h), 26 Jan 2022. Text garbled; page images 10 and 11 read.
- Art. 5: advertising services subject to VAT at 14%; arts. 60 and 64 of the Stamp Tax Law repealed.
- Art. 7: in force the day after publication (27 Jan 2022).
- ETA news item 13 Mar 2022 (https://www.eta.gov.eg/ar/news/afa-alalanat-mn-drybt-aldmght-albalght-20-alty-kant-tkhd-lha-sabqana-wakhdaha-llsr-alam-ldrybt): advertising was subject to 20% stamp tax, now VAT 14% only.

### ETA news, 26 Jul 2026
- URL: https://eta.gov.eg/ar/news/tdylat-jwhryt-ly-qanwn-aldrybt-ly-aldkhl-wqanwn-aldrybt-ly-aldmght-llqda-ly-alazdwaj-aldryby. ### Investment Law 72 of 2017
- URL: https://eta.gov.eg/sites/default/files/2024-10/law-72-2017.pdf. Gazette 21 bis (c), 31 May 2017. Text garbled; page images 8, 11 and 12 read.
- Page 11 (Gazette p. 12), art. 9: all investment projects under the law enjoy the general incentives of the chapter except projects under the free zone system. Art. 10: exempt from stamp tax and notarisation and registration fees: incorporation contracts of companies and establishments and credit facility and mortgage contracts linked to their business, for five years from entry in the commercial register; also exempt from that tax and those fees: contracts registering the land needed to set up the companies and establishments.

## Built real estate tax and disposal tax

### Law 196 of 2008
- URL (ETA text copy, 12 pages, used): https://eta.gov.eg/sites/default/files/2024-10/law-196-2008.pdf. Text layer garbled; page images 2 to 12 read.
- URL (Gazette scan, 18 pages): https://eta.gov.eg/sites/default/files/2026-03/law.no_.196.of_.2008.pdf. Page 1 read: Gazette 25 bis (c), 23 Jun 2008.
- Issuing art. 3: replaces Law 91 arts. 37, 39, 45, 46. Art. 39: income from letting built property taxed on actual rent less 50% for all costs. Art. 45: built real estate tax paid deducted from income tax due on that property, not above that tax. Art. 46: art. 39 not for built property in the assets of an establishment or company.
- Issuing arts. 4 to 7: transitional (forgiveness of old arrears, disputes, 10% and 20% settlement bands); art. 8 executive regulations within six months; art. 9 start date.
- Law art. 1: definitions; "the Authority" is مصلحة الضرائب العقارية. Art. 2: taxpayer (replaced 2012). Art. 3: tax due 1 January each year. Art. 4: assessment every five years; reassessment begins at least one and at most three years before the end. Art. 5 (replaced 2012). Art. 6: tax debt payable at the real estate tax directorates. Art. 7: administrative courts. Art. 8: annual tax on all built property, any material, any use, permanent or not, let or not, occupied or empty. Art. 9: deemed built property (concession property, vacant land, roof and facade installations). Art. 10: built property on agricultural land. Art. 11: not subject (state public-purpose property, private state property until disposal, worship and religious teaching, expropriated property). Art. 12: 10% after 30% residential, 32% non-residential (replaced 2012). Arts. 13 to 15: survey and assessment committees, declarations (art. 14: (a) five-yearly declaration in the second half of the previous year; (b) annual declaration by end December for new property, additions, changes, end of exemption), publication and notice. Arts. 16 to 17: appeal within 60 days with EGP 50 deposit; appeal committee decides within 30 days, final. Art. 18: exemptions (associations and labour organisations; educational institutions, hospitals and shelters; parties and syndicates; residential unit under EGP 6,000 rental value in the original text; cattle yards and cemeteries; youth and sports centres; foreign government property on reciprocity; agricultural service buildings; social occasion houses); 60-day notice when exemption ends. Arts. 19 to 22: relief (exempt, demolished or ruined, land no longer independent), from month after request, EGP 50 deposit and last instalment paid; 30-day committee decisions; 60-day notice when relief ends. Art. 23: two equal instalments by end June and end December. Art. 24: tenants jointly liable up to rent. Arts. 25 to 26: collection and state privilege. Art. 27: delay charge from 1 January after the year due, Central Bank rate on 1 January before plus 2%. Art. 28: 25% of proceeds to governorates. Art. 29: Treasury may bear tax in social hardship. Art. 30: fine EGP 200 to 2,000 for not filing the art. 14 declaration or wrong data affecting up to 10% of the tax; doubled on repeat within three years. Art. 31: evasion fine EGP 1,000 to 5,000 plus compensation equal to the tax. Art. 32: prosecution only on the Minister's written request. Art. 33: settlement on paying tax, delay charge and compensation. Arts. 34, 35: judicial officers, staff.

### Law 103 of 2012 (decree-law amending Law 196)
- URL: https://eta.gov.eg/sites/default/files/2024-10/law-103-2012.pdf. Page images 1 to 6 read.
- Art. 1: tax due from 1 Jul 2013. Art. 2: old 1991/2000 assessment continues to 30 Jun 2013. Art. 3: replaces arts. 2 (taxpayer includes holder under unregistered deed), 5 (rise at reassessment capped at 30% residential, 45% non-residential over previous tax), 12 (10% after 30% residential, 32% non-residential), 13 (committees; industrial, tourist and petroleum facilities, airports, ports, mines valued by standards), 15 second para. (notice on prescribed form), 18 first para. (all exemptions in other laws repealed except issuing art. 4), 18 (b) (non-profit educational, health and charitable bodies), 18 (d) (residential unit under EGP 24,000 annual rental value, one unit per owner), 28 (25% to governorates, 25% to informal area development). Art. 4: "announcement" becomes "notice" in art. 16. Art. 5: deletes art. 18 (c). Art. 6: in force from 1 Jul 2013; issued 6 Dec 2012.
- Table 1 (residential tax burden): market value, capital value 60%, annual rental value 3% of capital value; e.g. market EGP 2,000,000, capital 1,200,000, rental 36,000, base 1,200, tax 120.

### Real Estate Tax Authority (rta.gov.eg)
- https://www.rta.gov.eg/news/amendments-and-facilities-real-estate-taxes, dated 2026-03-10, read in full: exemption for the private residence of the taxpayer and family (spouse, minor children) raised to EGP 100,000 (value of the exemption about EGP 8 million per unit); single declaration to any office; gradual move to e-declarations; unregistered units taxed from filing date; 25% residential and 10% non-residential incentive for declarations; 5% for prepayment before assessment; delay charge capped at the principal tax; delay charges waived for those who paid before the law or within six months; RTA appeal right removed, electronic appeals; settlement at 70% of disputed tax within six months, extendable once by six months; relief for demolition, damage, force majeure; write-off committees. ### Ministry of Finance explainer
- https://mof.gov.eg/ar/media/learn-more/news-2, dated 6 Oct 2026: 10% after 30% residential and 32% non-residential and the exemption; deduction EGP 100,000 private residence, EGP 1,200 non-residential; one unit for spouses and minor children exempt up to EGP 8 million market value; capital value 60% of market value, rental value 3% of capital value; five-yearly declaration for all properties, annual declaration for new or changed units; everyone files even if exempt, tenants do not; deadline end December 2026; 25% and 10% continuing discount, plus 5% for advance payment (30% and 15%); advance payments deducted from 2027 tax, refundable if exempt; due 1 January, two instalments; 60-day appeal; 70% settlement with delay charge waived.

### Law 151 of 2026 and correction notice
- Law 151/2026: https://eta.gov.eg/sites/default/files/2026-08/law.no_.151.of_.2026.pdf, Gazette 30 bis (a), 28 Jul 2026, text layer read. Disposal tax article (printed as art. 43): 2.5% without any reduction on the total value of disposals of real property or land for building, except villages; heirs included; not trading unless ETA proves it (art. 19 item 7), then credited; value per contract, ETA bears burden of proof; in-kind contribution to joint stock company out if shares kept five years; seller pays within 60 days, art. 110 delay charge from the next day; disposal includes sale, will, gift or donation other than to ascendants, spouses or descendants, usufruct or lease over 50 years; forced sales, expropriation and gifts to government out. Art. 6 repeals art. 18 (with transition to tax period 2027) and other articles. Art. 7: in force the day after publication.
- Correction notice: https://eta.gov.eg/sites/default/files/2026-08/law.no_.149.150.151.of_.2026.retractation.pdf, Gazette 32 bis, 8 Aug 2026, page 2 image read: the article is 42, not 43; "built" (المبنية) property, not العينية; art. 6 repeals art. 29 bis, not 39 bis.
