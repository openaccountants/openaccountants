## 6. Law 7 of 2025

URL: https://eta.gov.eg/sites/default/files/2025-02/law_no.7.of_.2025.pdf (2 pages, text layer read). Gazette 6 bis (f), 12 February 2025. ## 11. Changes against the old TP skill (v0.1, tax year 2025)

- Related-party definition: old skill cited Law 206/2020 art. 1; it is Law 91 art. 1 as replaced by Law 30/2023 (repeated in the ER of Law 206).
- Decree 221/2018: old skill said it amended Law 91 art. 30; it amended ER arts. 38 to 40.
- Threshold: old skill gave EGP 15m as the rule; the law says EGP 8m with a ministerial power to raise; later decisions unconfirmed.
- Thin cap: old skill cited art. 24 "2x CBE" as the thin-cap rule and gave no ratio; art. 24 item 4 is a rate cap; the ratio is art. 52 item 1 (3:1 for 2026 under Law 30/2023 art. 5; 2:1 permanent; 4:1 infrastructure).
- Late filing and payment: old skill gave art. 110 as 2% a month and art. 111 as 1.5% a month; art. 110 is the Central Bank rate plus 2% a year, capped at 100% (Law 7/2025).
- Assessment window: old skill said 5 years or 6 for fraud; Law 211/2020 replaced art. 44 with five years.
- Criminal exposure: old skill said Law 7/2025 introduced it; Law 7/2025 added settlement and the 100% cap; offences sit in Law 206 arts. 69 to 72.
- Penalty percentages (1%, 3%, 3%, 2%, 3% cap): confirmed from Law 211/2020.
- Local file two months after the return; CbC report within one year; EGP 3bn and EUR 750m thresholds: confirmed.
