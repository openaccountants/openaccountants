## Old files: what was wrong

- CIT: Law 5/2025 is a settlement law for unregistered persons and past periods; it sets no CIT rate, R&D or training deduction. No 10%/20% SME brackets, 20% listed company rate or 20% foreign bank branch rate exist in the laws read. Suez Canal Authority is 40%, not 40.55%. Thin cap is 3:1 for 2026 (2:1 in the statute), not 4:1, applies to all loans (not only related-party), and sits in art. 52, not art. 49. Participation exemption is 25% and two years, not 10% and one year. Advance payments are optional, 60% of last tax in three instalments, not four quarterly 25% instalments. Late filing "2% a month, max 20%" and late payment "1.5% a month" are not the law: delay charge is Central Bank rate + 2% (22.5% for 2026), capped at 100%, and filing failures carry Law 206 fines. Losses lapse only on all three conditions in art. 55, not on any ownership change over 50%. Depreciation rates were wrong (computers 50% pool, others 25% pool, buildings 5%, intangibles 10%). Article numbers (arts. 23 to 27, 40 bis, 18 bis) were wrong.
- WHT: form 41 is the quarterly form for art. 59 deductions from residents, not a monthly non-resident return; non-resident tax goes on form 11 within 15 days. Dividend participation relief for non-residents (10% and one year) does not exist in the 2026 text; 5% applies to listed shares. "No branch profits tax" is wrong: PE profits are deemed distributed and taxed at 10%. Capital gains "20% gross or 10% net" withheld by the buyer is not the law: the non-resident seller pays within 60 days.
