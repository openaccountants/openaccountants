## Why section58 cannot choose between the hypotheses

Use the question's explicit illustrative assumptions: a resident individual, identified company profitP=£100,000, company rate10%, attributable company taxC=£10,000, cash available before the distribution deductionA=P−C=£90,000, individual standard rate20%, and no foreign-tax relief, allowance, cap, notice variation or other special condition. The fact that all this cash is distributed is assumed, not a legal pool-allocation finding.

LetWbe the additional withholding. Then cashN=A−W; combined58/61amountK=C+W. The statutory gross-up gives:

`G = N + K = (A − W) + (C + W) = A + C = £100,000`.

The unknownWcancels. Section58's correct gross amount is therefore compatible with both candidates:
