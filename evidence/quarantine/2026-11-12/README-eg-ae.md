## Why this draft is held

Its Quick Reference says the new treaty was signed on 7 November 2021 and entered
into force on 19 April 2021. The signature date follows its own entry-into-force
date. The UAE Ministry of Finance's official agreement register instead records
Egypt (New) as signed on **14 November 2019**, entering into force on
**19 April 2021**. Its earlier register records the original agreement as signed
on **12 April 1994**, entering into force on **16 July 1995**; the draft instead
calls these 2002 and 2003. The full treaty text and each operative rate, condition
and article therefore need checking before this draft can return to guidance.

The Ministry also announced a supplementary protocol with Egypt on 12 February
2024. That announcement proves a protocol was signed; it does **not** establish
its entry into force or applicability. Those questions remain open for the
substantive review. No legal rule or rate in the preserved draft is corrected
or approved by this withdrawal.
