## ET-001 — Who this applies to

Use this Guide for the deceased's final income period and the separate income of an estate. The deceased's own liability ends at death. An estate taken into probate administration in the Faroes is a full taxpayer under Tax Act §1(1)(7); an overseas-administered estate requires the actual §2 source and limited-liability conditions. Inheritance duty on capital passing to an heir is a separate job in [the succession Guide](fo-inheritance-duty.md), rather than estate income tax. [Tax Act §§1–2, 11, 15–20][tax]

## ET-002 — Who this applies to

If a surviving spouse immediately retains the community estate undivided, §13 assesses the spouse on the couple's death-year income, with separate calculations for deceased and survivor; the survivor fully succeeds to the deceased's tax position. The same pre-death rule applies where the community estate remains undivided but the deceased's separate property is divided; that separate property still needs the §14 opening information. Do not apply the provisional-tax finality rule to that mixed case. [Tax Act §§13, 14(8), 39][tax]

## ET-003 — Who this applies to

In other cases, pre-death provisional tax due, or required withholding on income earned before death, is final **unless** the estate or TAKS validly requires final assessment. ## ET-017 — The base

7. **Keep securities distributions separate.** Capital Income Act §12 excludes the distribution gain/loss and gives the securities recipient the deceased's tax position only within its conditions. Those rules do not apply where the spouse/heir/legatee is not fully liable in the Faroes or is treaty-resident elsewhere; then the distribution's gain/loss is included in the estate's taxable income. A sale by the estate is not automatically an exempt distribution. Capital interest/dividends and capital-tax computation remain under that Act and the [individual-tax capital method](fo-individual-income-tax.md), rather than being folded into the ordinary estate rate without analysis. ## ET-026 — Cross-border points

For foreign-administered estates test the precise §2 source categories; Faroese property alone is not proof that every foreign receipt is taxable here.
