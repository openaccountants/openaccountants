## 1. DKK 25,000 allowance above the small-income exemption

**Exact question:** For an estate divided directly following death whose final inventory is made and submitted within 15 months, but whose ordinary taxable estate income exceeds DKK 25,000, is the national base reduced by DKK 25,000? If yes, which current binding provision/interpretation limits Assessment Act§6's incorporation of Tax Act§15(2) to the timing/division conditions while excluding that paragraph's income ceiling?

**Evidence checked:** Current Assessment Act§6 says 40% national tax and grants the deduction to estates meeting the conditions in Tax Act§15(2). Current§15(2) includes division directly following death, final inventory made and submitted within 15 months **and** taxable income no more than 25,000. The original parliamentary proposal retains that cross-reference in both Faroese and Danish; the explanatory note nevertheless says 40% after a 25,000 deduction (printedpp 64/66; PDFpp 89/91, visually read). That historical note supports legislative intent but does not itself identify a current carve-out from the income ceiling. The linked 2015 TAKS estate guidance states the exemption conditions and 40% for nonexempt estates, without explaining a general allowance. No unconditional allowance is added.

Sources: [current§6](https://logir.fo/Logtingslog/87-fra-01-09-1983-um-aseting-av-skatti-sum-seinast-broytt-vid-logtingslog-nr-170); [current§15(2)](https://www.logir.fo/Logtingslogarkunngerd/109-fra-19-09-2023-um-landsskatt-og-kommunuskatt); [original proposal/note](https://www.logting.fo/media/05llq3vx/1983t.pdf?ar=1983); [TAKS G.2.2](https://www.taks.fo/fo/log/vegleidingarskriv-vls-2015/g-deydsbugv). ## 2. National tax, municipal tax and post-death church liability

**Exact question:** For an ordinary fully liable estate, does TAKS add municipal tax calculated under Tax Act§§41(6),46–47 to the expressly national 40% in Assessment Act§6, then apply§54(2)'s proportional ceiling, or does a separate current rule allocate a municipal share out of the 40%? Supply the current calculation/authority for the base, municipal exempt amount and rate, estate credits, and allocation. For post-death estate income, does church liability follow the deceased's membership, an estate opt-in, or end with the individual, and under which clause?

**Evidence checked:**§41(6) expressly taxes the estate in the deceased's last municipality;§46 uses the national taxable-income base;§47 prescribes rate/rounding;§83(5) explicitly settles the municipality's estate-tax share;§54(2) supplies a combined ceiling. These are positive evidence of a municipal component, not support for a silent zero municipality or an all-in 40% conclusion. Current Church Finance Act§§11/13 charge members,§14 addresses nonmember notification to pay. Those charging clauses do not themselves give an estate succession/membership rule. No estate-specific administrative calculation or current bridge was established in the checked sources. A proposed numerical case would still choose the disputed base/municipal treatment, so no unverified working-paper result is inserted.

Sources: [Tax Act§§41,46–47,54,83](https://www.logir.fo/Logtingslogarkunngerd/109-fra-19-09-2023-um-landsskatt-og-kommunuskatt); [Assessment Act§6](https://logir.fo/Logtingslog/87-fra-01-09-1983-um-aseting-av-skatti-sum-seinast-broytt-vid-logtingslog-nr-170); [Church Finance Act§§11–14](https://logir.fo/Logtingslog/61-fra-07-06-2007-um-figgjarvidurskifti-folkakirkjunnar). ## 3. Estate-specific three-month demand versus annual instalments

**Exact question:** When precisely does an estate pay under Tax Act§80(5), three months after final assessment with the 20 th timely-payment date, and when under§81's October/November/December instalments? Is the distinction final closure/special period versus annual returns for an estate open beyond 15 months, or another classification? Which current instruction/rule implements that distinction, including an amended estate assessment under§81(2)?

**Evidence checked:**§80(5) expressly names estate tax as well as final-assessment arrears;§81 expressly includes estates by reference to§1(7–16). Neighbouring§40(3)–(4) distinguishes a single short estate period from annual returns;§24(3) uses the deceased's income year. A systematic reading suggests a possible final-period/annual distinction, but the quoted payment paragraphs do not expressly make that link. The current inheritance page and linked 2015 estate chapter do not explain their interaction. The earlier Tax Act consolidation's§80 amendment notes were inspected as a history lead; they do not themselves supply the missing classification. No universal payment rule is invented.

Sources: [Tax Act§§24,40,80–81](https://www.logir.fo/Logtingslogarkunngerd/109-fra-19-09-2023-um-landsskatt-og-kommunuskatt); [TAKS current inheritance](https://www.taks.fo/fo/borgari/skattur/skattur-og-arvur); [TAKS2015 estate chapter](https://www.taks.fo/fo/log/vegleidingarskriv-vls-2015/g-deydsbugv).
