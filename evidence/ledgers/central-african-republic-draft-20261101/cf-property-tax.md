## Examples

- Market-value floor changes the tax base: Assume a non-exempt individual building, market value XAF 20,000,000, actual assessed rent XAF 1,800,000, and the quoted baseline applies. The 12% floor is 2,400,000, so that rental value controls. After the 40% allowance the base is 1,440,000; at 15% the levy is 216,000, above the minimum. Underlying rule IDs: B01, B02, B03, B04, B05, B06, B07, B08.
- Individual or IS company rate on the same building: Assume identical non-exempt rental value XAF 2,400,000 and no currentness issue. The rounded base is 1,440,000. An individual rate gives 216,000; an IS company rate gives 432,000. The ownership decision therefore needs the taxpayer-category test and the other taxes, not just market value. Underlying rule IDs: B01, B02, B03, B04, B05, B06, B07, B08.
