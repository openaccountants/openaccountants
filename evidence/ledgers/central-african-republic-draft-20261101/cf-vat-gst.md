## Examples

- Output less eligible inputs: Assume an ordinary 19% domestic taxable supply of XAF 10,000,000 excluding VAT, eligible input VAT of XAF 760,000, and no other adjustments or withholding. Output is 1,900,000, leaving 1,140,000 payable. Inputs are supplied after eligibility checks, not guessed from all spending. Underlying rule IDs: V01, V02, V03, V04, V05, V06, V07, V08, V09, V10, V11, V12, V13, V14.
- Direct attribution or blanket prorata: Assume valid inputs: XAF 190,000 solely taxable, XAF 95,000 solely exempt and XAF 380,000 shared. With a supported 60% prorata, allowed input is 190,000 plus 228,000, or 418,000. Applying 60% to every input would give 399,000 and is wrong. Attribute first, apportion shared costs second. Underlying rule IDs: V01, V02, V03, V04, V05, V06, V07, V08, V09, V10, V11, V12, V13, V14.
