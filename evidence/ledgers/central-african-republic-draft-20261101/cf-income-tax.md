## Examples

- Annual salary bands on a supplied net base: Assume the source-currentness hold is cleared and annual net taxable salary is XAF 2,000,000 after verified deductions. The first slice is exempt; 1,302,000 at 8% gives 104,160 and the next 320,000 at 15% gives 48,000. Total: XAF 152,160. This starts from net taxable salary and does not guess social contributions. Underlying rule IDs: I01, I02, I03, I04, I05, I06, I07, I08, I09, I10, I11, I12.
- Business profit tax or minimum: For a non-agricultural individual business, assume XAF 120,000,000 covered turnover, XAF 4,000,000 taxable profit and no exemption. Profit tax is 1,200,000, turnover tax is 2,220,000 and exceeds both profit tax and the 1,850,000 floor. The minimum controls. This is a comparison of liabilities, not a choice of regime. Underlying rule IDs: I01, I02, I03, I04, I05, I06, I07, I08, I09, I10, I11, I12.
