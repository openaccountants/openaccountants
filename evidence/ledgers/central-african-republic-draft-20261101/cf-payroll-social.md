## Examples

- Monthly tax from a supplied annual liability: Assume a full-year fixed payroll after all salary rules are checked, with computed annual employee IRPP XAF 152,160. Bringing that liability to twelve identical months gives XAF 12,680 each. This arithmetic does not independently determine gross-to-net deductions or CNSS. Underlying rule IDs: W01, W02, W03, W04.
- Five or six workers changes remittance route: Assume each worker has XAF 12,680 properly computed monthly withholding, the quoted calendar still applies, and the workforce is unchanged for a full quarter. Five workers give quarterly remittance of 190,200 under the exception. Six workers give 76,080 per monthly remittance; withholding continues in both cases. Underlying rule IDs: W01, W02, W03, W04.
