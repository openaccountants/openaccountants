## Examples

- Foreign fee deduction cap: Assume article 126 bis 11 applies, all reality/normality conditions hold, no additional disallowance and taxable profit before covered fees is XAF 50,000,000. Covered fees are 12,000,000, the cap is 10,000,000 and 2,000,000 is rejected. Do not also add that 2,000,000 under another heading. Underlying rule IDs: T01, T02, T03, T04, T05, T06.
- Exactly half or more than half below: Assume comparable CAR ordinary tax would be XAF 3,000,000. Recipient tax of 1,500,000 is exactly half below, while 1,200,000 is more than half below. Only the latter meets this quantitative limb; other non-cooperative and transfer rules remain separate. Underlying rule IDs: T01, T02, T03, T04, T05, T06.
