## Examples

- General percentage sanction on a stated duty: Assume the applicable amended LPF paragraph has been established, no bad faith/fraud, XAF 4,000,000 relevant duty and a 25% sanction. The percentage penalty is 1,000,000. This does not establish that a separate flat or tax-specific fine must also be added. Underlying rule IDs: O01, O02, O03, O04, O05, O06, O07.
- Invoice visa versus a general declaration breach: For two established missing exemption-invoice visas, the specific 50,000-per-breach text gives 100,000. The general first late-declaration fine is 500,000, but it is not a substitute for classifying the actual offence. These are alternative classifications in this example and are not summed. Underlying rule IDs: O01, O02, O03, O04, O05, O06, O07.
