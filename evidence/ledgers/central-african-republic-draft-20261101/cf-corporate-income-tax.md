## Examples

- Profit tax exceeds turnover minimum: Assume the stated ordinary baseline applies, no exemption or credit, XAF 120,000,000 covered turnover and XAF 20,000,000 taxable profit. Profit tax is 6,000,000; turnover minimum is 2,220,000, above its floor. The liability is 6,000,000, not the sum. Underlying rule IDs: C01, C02, C03, C04, C05, C06, C07, C08, C09, C10, C11, C12, C13, C14.
- Agricultural loss still meets minimum floor: Assume an in-scope agricultural company, no exemption, XAF 60,000,000 turnover and a XAF 1,000,000 tax loss. Profit tax is zero; turnover percentage gives 180,000 but the agricultural floor is 300,000. Liability is 300,000. This corrects the old draft’s 3% agriculture minimum. Underlying rule IDs: C01, C02, C03, C04, C05, C06, C07, C08, C09, C10, C11, C12, C13, C14.
