## Examples

- Foreign service payment with domestic withholding: Assume a XAF 10,000,000 gross foreign service fee within the cited domestic provision, no treaty reduction and no gross-up clause. Withholding is 1,500,000 and net payment 8,500,000. VAT and deductibility are separate tests. Underlying rule IDs: X01, X02, X03, X04, X05, X06, X07, X08.
- Departure bank guarantee boundary: Assume the special permanent-departure certificate rule applies to a manager whose enterprise is under audit, with annual turnover XAF 200,000,000. A 15,000,000 guarantee is 5,000,000 below the statutory minimum, while 20,000,000 reaches it. This does not establish that either guarantee alone ensures clearance. Underlying rule IDs: X01, X02, X03, X04, X05, X06, X07, X08.
