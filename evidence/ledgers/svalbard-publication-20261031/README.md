# Svalbard partial-pack publication record

This publication preserves the exact ten reviewed Guide files and original
ledger/review records. All country gaps remain explicit. It does not claim
full Svalbard coverage, Jan Mayen coverage or accountant attestation.

Full newly captured website pages, their complete wrapper-derived readers
and browser source exports remain private in the original research worktree.
They are not included in this public branch or its new commit ancestry.
source-map.json identifies their original paths, byte hashes, sizes and
public source/provenance references. All original bytes and research commits
are preserved locally; nothing was deleted or replaced by a synthetic source.

Frozen writer/reviewer manifests describe the original research candidate,
which included those private artifacts. They remain historical evidence of
what was actually read and reviewed, rather than a promise that every capture
is distributed in this public checkout. Read the unchanged exact quotations,
article references, source URLs, read dates, source-access limits and review
outcomes alongside this map. A private-source path is not a missing public
filing form or a claim that an inaccessible source was read.

The three beneficial-owner statutory API XML documents and their generated
readers are the specifically licensed public-source exception. They retain
NLOD-2.0 under the narrow REUSE annotation and the recorded attribution,
transformation disclosure and non-endorsement conditions. This does not
relicense other website wrappers. Original OpenAccountants explanations
retain the repository's applicable terms; third-party quoted material retains
its underlying rights and does not become OpenAccountants-owned text.

The publication branch starts from the existing public PR head and normally
merges main. It does not merge the unpublished research branch. No force-push,
replacement PR, authority enquiry, filing or payment is part of this update.
