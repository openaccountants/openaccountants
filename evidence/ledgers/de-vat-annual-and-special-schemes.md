| 052 | The taxable base LF[94, 94] | **Input tax correction (§ 15a UStG).** If the use of an asset changes within five years of first use (ten years for land and buildings), the input tax deducted on it is corrected by one fifth (or one tenth) a year for each year of change (§ 15a(1) and (5)). For a still usable asset used more than once, a sale or deemed supply under § 3(1b) before the correction period ends is also a change if its VAT treatment differs from the use governing the original input-tax deduction (§ 15a(8)). A sale taxed on the same basis as the original fully taxable use does not trigger that disposal adjustment merely because the asset is sold.
