If the document is amended to a higher amount, pay tax on the increase. If the shares are not stated, split the amount equally (MOF/STA announcement 2022 No. 22 Art. 3(1)).<br>- **Share transfers.** The base is the stated price, excluding any stated part that relates to subscribed but unpaid capital (Art. 3(4)).
