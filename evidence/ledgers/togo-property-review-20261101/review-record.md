The principal rate, base, exemption duration, payment dates, relief periods and both example calculations agree with the read instruments. No rate or arithmetic correction was found. There are three Guide defects and a separate ledger evidence defect:

1. **CGI 261 complete exemption screen, P13.** All 11 categories are named, but several conditions are hidden behind "specified" or "qualifying". The screen needs the actual conditions so an AI can determine eligibility: public-tool concessions in the listed ports/navigation routes, operated under their concession specifications; water/energy works belonging to the State, prefecture or commune; actual exclusive occupation of the unique principal home by the owner, spouse or direct ascendants/descendants; foreign-state ownership and an accredited mission's official residence (including dependencies); premises exclusively serving an approved mutual, with ancillary activities inside the sector law. Keep the existing public-service/non-income, school/non-property-income, medical/social, rural livestock/crop and state-rail branches. This is a condition-completeness defect, not a finding that an omitted category was taxed or an existing numerical rule was wrong.
2. **LPF 376 claim admissibility, P36.** The Guide lists assessment, grounds, NIF/contact, signature and supporting evidence, but omits mandatory individual claims (with the statutory exceptions for collectively assessed taxpayers and members of partnerships contesting the partnership's tax), the addressee `Commissaire des impôts`, French language, the taxpayer's postal box, and provisional/final reassessment notices where the assessments arose from an audit. These are known statutory conditions, not unverified portal details. Add them beside the claim steps. Article 376 permits later regularisation by producing a listed supporting document and exempts claims from stamp duty; including these avoids implying every supporting-paper omission is permanently fatal. Do not imply all other defects can be regularised.
3. **Required template notice.** The new Guide goes directly from the H1 to `Who this applies to`. Insert the `General reference only` notice from the template below the title. The 15 mechanical shape checks do not enforce that notice.
4. **Quote anchors, separate evidence defect.** Almost every original ledger quote is the first words of the article rather than the sentence containing the figure/condition being asserted. They are useful locators and are not invented; their presence does not itself substantiate the complete grouped rule. P05 quotes article 2's title/opening but not its commencement clause; P06/P08 omit their tax rates; P07 omits 50%; P12 omits both duration branches; P20 omits the two deadlines; P23/P24/P30 omit the decisive time/consequence clauses; P39 repeats the opening of 262, and P40 quotes the 6% factor but not the asset qualification. Strengthen the numeric/condition anchors in both JSON and Markdown ledger forms. The per-row review records substantive correctness separately from this evidence defect.

Use "business/enterprise balance-sheet assets" as the translation of `à l'actif du bilan des entreprises` when clarifying the 6% condition. The enactment does not say only incorporated companies qualify. The current company example remains valid on its stated assumed facts; the generic rule should avoid an unnecessary legal-person restriction.

## Independent cases and distinctions

- CGI 270/275 with replacement CGI 271 and LPF 85: CFA 100,000,000 market value x 6% = CFA 6,000,000 gross rental value; x 50% = CFA 3,000,000 net cadastral income; x 7.5% = CFA 225,000 annual tax; /2 = CFA 112,500 per instalment. Land/incorporated assets are already inside the assumed market value; no separate unbuilt assessment is added for indispensable construction land.
- CGI 262/265-267: timely declarations and regular subdivision, completion 2023, no use change. First exemption year is 2024. Commercial/professional/industrial two-year window is 2024 and 2025; 2026 is taxable. Residential five-year window is 2024-2028; 2026 is exempt and 2026, 2027 and 2028 remain. CFA 2,000,000 x 50% x 7.5% = CFA 75,000 ordinary annual tax; outputs CFA 75,000 commercial and zero residential. A late declaration cannot retrospectively exempt the first year; it only accesses the still-running fraction from the following 1 January. No arithmetic correction needed.
- CGI 258 classifies specified commercial/industrial land in the built-property tax scope, and 268(4)/270 prevent ordinary double treatment of construction land as unbuilt. The Guide appropriately directs classification before rates. CGI 259 does not make every unused plot taxable urban land.
- LPF 100 assigns 3.75% of gross rent to property tax and 5% to income tax out of 8.75% rental withholding, on payment proof and after issue of assessment bulletins. LPF 101 provides the advance-payment branch for rental income without that withholding. The Guide deliberately does not calculate this whole separate job and correctly prevents deduction of the full 8.75% from the property assessment.
- CGI 278 needs involuntary vacancy/non-use, at least three months, and the entire property or independently rentable/usable part; the next-month start/end-of-return-month period is correct. CGI 279's destruction/demolition and 280's extraordinary disappearance have their separate following-month start rules. LPF 374's claim event must be selected, including the year minimum vacancy duration is met; 375 has its own reassessment period. No automatic payment stay is inferred.
