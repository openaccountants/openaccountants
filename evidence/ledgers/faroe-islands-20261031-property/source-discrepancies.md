# Source reconciliation, 31 October 2026

- Umhvørvisstovan’s registry overview attaches the fixed DKK1,000 fee to Act8 and the 1% duty/copy charge to Act76. The operative statutes show the reverse: Act8 §§1/3a charges the percentage duty; Act76 §§1/2 charges the fixed fee and §6 charges copies. The Guide follows the statutes and preserves both URLs.
- The agency overview describes older ministry appeal routes. Current Duty Act8 §5c directs duty appeals to Føroya Kærustovn. Regulation161/2021 §6 and Annex4.8 expressly include Fee Act76 decisions in land matters; Act147/2021 §§2/10/16 supplies the general clock, written route and completed jurisdiction transfer. The Guide includes the separate source bridge instead of inferring the fee route from the duty route.
- Duty Act8’s principal heading records amendment57/2025 but its repeated subtitle says60/2025. The operative amendment footnote and §5c are the actual 57/2025 appeal amendment. Regulation60/2025 is a different commencement instrument and is not that duty amendment.
- Fee Act76’s official footnote explains that the Faroese and Danish texts differ because later changes were only made in Faroese. This Guide and ledger use the current operative Faroese text, with its current amendment history, rather than the unupdated parallel Danish wording.
- Act128/2024 is not consolidated into the displayed base Acts for stated technical reasons. Its §5 permits different commencement dates for each register. Regulation60/2025 §1(3) confines its dates to the vehicle register. The verified land method is therefore kept separate from the vehicle digital/invoice rules; no land-book commencement is invented.

Only full private source captures, not published website copies, support these read receipts. The public evidence contains attributed operative legal excerpts and the short twenty-word registry submission instruction. The source-publication reuse assessment remains separate from substantive tax review.
