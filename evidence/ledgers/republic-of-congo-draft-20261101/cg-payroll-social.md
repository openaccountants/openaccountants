## Worked examples
- Salary quotient computation with supplied retirement amount: Assume a full-year salary XAF 3,000,000, no benefits/exemptions, an independently established deductible employee retirement contribution 120,000, and 1 part. Under I14–I17 taxable salary is(3,000,000−120,000)×80%=2,304,000. The first 615,000 gives 1,200; the next 885,000 gives 88,500; the remaining 804,000 gives 120,600. Annual ITS is 210,300. This does not compute the legally required CNSS contribution or a final monthly remittance.
- Decision: correct payroll family evidence: Using the same facts but a verified married employee with no children and 2 parts, quotient is 1,152,000; per-part tax 54,900 and annual ITS 109,800. A payroll setup incorrectly using 1 part would give 210,300, over-withholding 100,500. Correct the setup to the evidenced legal status; the employee does not elect whichever number is cheaper. Annual-to-monthly withholding implementation remains to verify.
