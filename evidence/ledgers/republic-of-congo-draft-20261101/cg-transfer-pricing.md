## Worked examples
- Supported cost adjustment: A hypothetical foreign related-party service charge is XAF 12,000,000; a fully supported functional/comparability study establishes arm's-length charge 10,000,000. Under T01 the 2,000,000 excess is restored. For a domestic ordinary company under C01, the incremental initial-law IS is 560,000, assuming minimum tax and all other rules do not change the result. The comparator is an example fact, not a statutory safe harbour.
- Decision: turnover below threshold but assets at equality: A Congo company with foreign related transactions has turnover 499,000,000. With gross assets 500,000,000, T02 applies; with assets 499,000,000, its numerical test is not met. The indicator below is 1 for the threshold being met. In either case T01's substantive arm's-length rule still applies. This decides whether to prepare the 81 documentation bundle, not whether to price correctly.

## Limits
