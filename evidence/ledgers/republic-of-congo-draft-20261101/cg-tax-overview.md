## Worked examples
- Compare business and company computations before distributions: Assume ordinary domestic company and ordinary IBA individual each have independently reconciled profit 10,000,000 and minimum receipts 100,000,000. Corporate C01/C08 gives 2,800,000 versus minimum 1,000,000. IBA I02 gives 3,000,000 versus minimum 1,500,000. Difference 200,000 is before distributions, social charges, other levies and compliance costs. It does not by itself decide incorporation.
- Decision: route centimes to VAT, not a blanket profit surtax: An ordinary VAT invoice with pretax base 1,000,000 needs VAT 180,000 and centimes 9,000 under V01. A system configured for VAT alone totals 1,180,000; correctly including centimes totals 1,189,000. Correct the invoice configuration by 9,000. This VAT rule does not substantiate the old blanket 5% surcharge on every profit-tax bill.
