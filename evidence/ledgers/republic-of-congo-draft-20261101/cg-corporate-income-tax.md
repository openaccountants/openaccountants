## Worked examples
- CIT above minimum: Assume an ordinary domestic company has reconciled taxable profit XAF 20,000,800 and minimum-tax receipts XAF 100,000,000. C01 rounds profit to 20,000,000; 28% is 5,600,000. C08 gives minimum 1,000,000. Ordinary IS governs, before verified credits or any separately established levy.
- Decision: deduction boundary changes ordinary tax: Assume pre-cost taxable profit XAF 10,000,000, an otherwise qualifying domestic cost of 200,000 and minimum receipts 20,000,000. Payment in cash fails C04 at equality, leaving IS 2,800,000. A qualifying traceable non-cash payment permits the cost, giving 2,744,000. The difference 56,000 is the ordinary IS consequence; all other deduction conditions still have to hold.

## Limits
Later 2026 rectifying law and implementation chain; the 86F four-month versus ACPCE 15May filing conflict; live electronic portal/forms/boxes; the old blanket 5% surtax and minimum floors; ordinary enforced late-return/late-payment sanctions; sector incentives and full depreciation tables. These gaps prevent operative or comprehensive 2026 coverage.
