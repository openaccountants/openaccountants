## Worked examples
- SARL share and deposit planning: Founders choose XAF 300,000 cash capital under F02, with shares at the minimum 5,000, giving 60shares. Assuming the ordinary 311-1 cash rule applies, at least 150,000 is paid at subscription and 150,000 remains within the statutory 2 year RCCM clock. F10's separate displayed creation fee is not part of that capital.
- Decision: distinguish entity fee from capital: For a proposed SARL using chosen capital 300,000, the displayed creation fee 300,000 gives 600,000 when comparing fee plus full intended capital. For an ordinary SA with minimum capital 10,000,000 and displayed fee 500,000, the comparable total is 10,500,000. Difference 9,900,000 is a funding/fee distinction, not a full cost estimate or recommendation. SA initial paid-in capital can differ from full subscribed capital; governance and fundraising requirements still decide form.

## Limits
Later company-law/national amendments; authenticated ACPCE fields, fee assessment/exemptions and final NIU/RCCM issuance sequence; processing time; full recurring governance/accounting/beneficial-owner filings and sanctions; in-kind valuation and regulated-sector capital; special branch dispensations. The corporate tax Guide retains the DSF/annual filing-date conflict.
