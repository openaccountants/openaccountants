| I02 | rates | IBA is 30% of profit rounded down to XAF 1,000, subject to a 1.5% minimum on total operating, financial and non-ordinary receipts. The minimum follows the IS minimum rules. Do not use the salary scale for an ordinary sole-trader profit. | CGI 95 | « Le taux de l’impôt sur les bénéfices d’affaires est fixé à 30%. ; inférieure à mille francs ; est fixé à 1,5% » | https://sgg.cg/JO/2026/congo-jo-2026-3-3.pdf | 2026-11-01 |
| I03 | base | IBA generally follows IS base rules, but depreciation is straight-line only, doubtful-debt provisions are not deductible, losses run only through the third following year, and the investment credit 3A is unavailable. | I04 | base | The forfait concerns taxpayers not exceeding the VAT threshold, but excludes regulated professions, bakers, works contractors, hardware operators, wholesalers and importers. VAT election immediately leads to ordinary IBA; decline below the threshold needs 2 successive years before returning to forfait. The forfait ceases after the year of excess. Exact current threshold and 5%/8% computation chain remain on hold. | CGI 96 | « sont exclus du régime du forfait ; cessent immédiatement ; deux exercices successifs » | https://sgg.cg/JO/2026/congo-jo-2026-3-3.pdf | 2026-11-01 |
| I05 | base | CGI 93B recognises habitual residence from an available owned/usufruct home, a rental lasting at least 1 continuous year, or principal stay, plus listed government/assignment/retained-interest cases. Absence continuously exceeding 24 months loses residence. Non-residents are taxed on Congo-source income. Foreign branch directors have a residence presumption; treaties and government-posting provisions affect the answer. This is not a universal 183-day test. | CGI 93B | « une absence continue d’une durée supérieure à vingt-quatre mois ; au moins une année ; les personnes physiques qui sont désignées » | https://sgg.cg/JO/2026/congo-jo-2026-3-3.pdf | 2026-11-01 |
| I06 | rates | IRCM is 15%, with 35% for hidden income. Distributed profits, interest, securities gains and deemed PE distributions have different bases and timing. Normal interest is not generally assumed tax-free merely because the recipient is resident. | CGI 103–110 | « Ce taux est fixé à 35% pour les revenus occultes. ; est fixé à 15% » | https://sgg.cg/JO/2026/congo-jo-2026-3-3.pdf | 2026-11-01 |
| I07 | base | IRCM is due where income is paid by an enterprise operated in Congo or the recipient is domiciled/resident in Congo. For dividends the tax event is the distribution decision, with exigibility at payment and no later than 3 months after that decision. The normal dividend base is gross dividends. | CGI 107–109 | « au plus tard trois (3) mois après la décision de leur distribution » | https://sgg.cg/JO/2026/congo-jo-2026-3-3.pdf | 2026-11-01 |
| I08 | rates | For property income outside an IS enterprise, IRF is 9% on gross rents and 10% on property gains. Congo property is within source scope regardless of recipient residence; a resident can also have foreign property in scope. The gain base permits documented acquisition expenses and qualifying construction/improvement costs. | CGI 111–113 | « 9% pour les revenus locatifs ; 10% pour les plus-values ; montant des loyers bruts » | https://sgg.cg/JO/2026/congo-jo-2026-3-3.pdf | 2026-11-01 |
| I09 | base | Exemptions cover free occupation by direct ancestors/descendants, disposal gains on a principal residence held at least 5 years, and State/local-authority property income. A domiciled recipient has employment income in scope even for work/employer abroad. | I14 | base | Start with gross cash and benefits. Benefits use actual value, or statutory defaults: housing 20% of salary capped for social security; domestic staff/security 7%, utilities 5%, telephone 2%, car 3%, food 20% of gross. The reference base deducts specified pension/mandatory-social amounts. Employer mutual premiums are not taxable if benefiting all employees. ITS then allows 20% deduction after the employee retirement contribution. Social contribution rates/ceilings are not established here. | CGI 115–116 | « une déduction de 20% est appliquée au montant net ; logement : 20% ; gardiennage : 7% ; gaz : 5% ; téléphone : 2% ; voiture : 3% ; nourriture : 20% » | https://sgg.cg/JO/2026/congo-jo-2026-3-3.pdf | 2026-11-01 |
| I15 | base | Round net taxable salary down to XAF 1,000 before dividing by parts; tax the quotient and multiply by parts. Basic parts: single/divorced/widowed without dependants 1; married without dependants 2; single/divorced with one child 2; married/widowed with one child 2.5; each additional child adds 0.5, maximum 6.5. A widow(er) supporting children not born of the marriage with the deceased spouse is treated as single with the same number of dependent children under 116B. Separately employed spouses are each treated as married, children assigned to the husband absent express exception. A childless widow(er) is treated as married for 2 years following death. Special 116C situations remain a referral. | CGI116A–116C | « jusqu’à un maximum de 6,5 parts ; 1 part ; 2 parts ; 2,5 parts ; une demi-part par enfant ; pendant les deux années ; non issus de son mariage avec le conjoint décédé » | https://sgg.cg/JO/2026/congo-jo-2026-3-3.pdf | 2026-11-01 |
| I16 | base | Dependants need no separately assessed income: legitimate/recognised/adopted children under 21, disabled children, students up to 25 with evidence, plus qualifying foster/protected children(the latter limited to 2). 116E requires the stated court decision for mothers claiming children. ## Worked examples
- IBA business profit: An ordinary IBA business outside forfait has tax-adjusted profit XAF 10,000,900 and total minimum-tax receipts 200,000,000. Round profit to 10,000,000.30% is 3,000,000 and 1.5% minimum isalso 3,000,000. No salary quotient applies.
- Decision: principal home versus investment disposal: An individual sells for XAF 30,000,000, with acquisition 20,000,000 and qualifying documented costs 2,000,000. If an ordinary investment property, the 8,000,000 gain yields 800,000 at 10%. If it is the principal residence held at least 5 years and I09 fully applies, that gain is exempt and tax 0. Other acquisition/registration taxes are not computed.
