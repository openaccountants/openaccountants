## Worked examples
- Nonresident royalty withholding: Assume a resident Congo company pays gross royalty XAF 10,000,000 to a nonresident within 86D, with no applicable treaty reduction and no gross-up clause. 20% withholding is 2,000,000 and net payment 8,000,000. VAT, foreign tax and other charges are outside this example.
- Decision: classify before using deemed margin: For gross Congo receipts 10,000,000, an enterprise genuinely qualifying under 92 with no allowable gross-base exclusions has deemed profit 2,200,000 and IS 726,000. Treating the same amount as an 86D payment instead gives 2,000,000 withholding. The difference 1,274,000 explains why classification and permits must be settled. These are alternative legal fact patterns, not freely elective rates and not taxes to stack.
