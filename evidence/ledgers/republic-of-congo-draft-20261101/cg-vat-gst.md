## Worked examples
- Ordinary VAT invoice: Assume a correctly classified ordinary-rated domestic supply with pretax base XAF 1,000,000. V01 produces VAT 180,000 and centimes 9,000, for total 1,189,000. The 5% centimes are on VAT, not on the pretax base.
- Decision: separate cash boundaries: Assume an otherwise fully eligible input expense at exactly XAF 200,000, including supported VAT 30,000. Under V05 the 2026 VAT cash exclusion alone disallows 0 because the amount does not exceed the boundary; at 200,001 it disallows the whole otherwise eligible 30,000. For IS the same 200,000 cash expense fails C04 at equality. A qualifying non-cash method avoids this particular cash restriction in either tax, but does not dispense with other evidence.
