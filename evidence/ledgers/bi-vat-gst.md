## Explicit limits

July transition for an entity already above the new annual threshold before 1 July; sector-specific exemptions and every tariff classification; actual current ordinary refund minimum if a later order changes 2021 art. 26; mixed-use prorata/capital adjustment execution; property gifts, mixed-use buildings and lease VAT; all EBMS offence stacking and interest; authenticated submission/payment/refund screens. These gaps are not permission to use blanket reduced rates or unlimited credit carryforward.
