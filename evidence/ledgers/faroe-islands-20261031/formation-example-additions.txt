## Cross-border points

The ordinary formation checklist does not establish a foreign branch's
registration, a foreign entity's licence eligibility or a cross-border
reorganisation method. The scope and source reservations above remain in
place. Do not substitute Danish or EU incorporation and audit rules for the
Faroese provisions. [Companies Act §§1–4][company]

## Two worked examples that recompute

### 1. Cash Sp/F versus cash P/F funding

Assume the ordinary cash-only facts and selected statutory reading stated
above. An Sp/F with DKK 200,000 subscribed capital and DKK 100,000 premium
pays DKK 50,000 capital and DKK 25,000 premium, total DKK 75,000. The 25%
capital fraction equals the absolute minimum. For a P/F, use its separate
minimum subscribed capital: with DKK 500,000 capital and the same fictional
DKK 100,000 premium, capital payment is DKK 125,000 and the entire premium
is paid, total DKK 225,000. The DKK 150,000 funding difference is one
consequence of the legal-form decision, not a conclusion that an Sp/F is
always preferable. Both figures exclude fees and other formation costs;
noncash contributions use full payment and do not use these fractions.
The Sp/F calculation retains the express §40/§33 wording issue and selected
specific incorporation reading above. [Companies Act §§4, 33, 40][company]

```oa-check
name: Faroese cash SpF versus PF capital and premium funding
inputs:
  spf_capital: 200000
  pf_capital: 500000
  premium: 100000
  fraction: 0.25
  absolute_minimum: 50000
steps:
  spf_capital_paid: max(spf_capital * fraction, absolute_minimum)
  spf_premium_paid: premium * spf_capital_paid / spf_capital
  spf_total: spf_capital_paid + spf_premium_paid
  pf_capital_paid: max(pf_capital * fraction, absolute_minimum)
  pf_total: pf_capital_paid + premium
  funding_difference: pf_total - spf_total
expected:
  spf_capital_paid: 50000
  spf_premium_paid: 25000
  spf_total: 75000
  pf_capital_paid: 125000
  pf_total: 225000
  funding_difference: 150000
```

### 2. Cumulative late-accounts charge per manager

Assume a new-law period, the demand's compliance period has expired,
the Registrar imposes §178 charges, exactly three responsible managers,
no relief or overlapping coercive-fine reduction, and the second commenced
month measured from the original filing deadline. The cumulative charge
is DKK 2,000 each, total DKK 6,000, rather than DKK 500 plus DKK 2,000 each.
At the third commenced month the cumulative figure becomes DKK 3,000 each,
total DKK 9,000. The three-month cap prevents adding another DKK 3,000
merely because a fourth month begins. This is a conditional charge
calculation, not an automatic dissolution or filing-extension entitlement.
[Annual Accounts Act §§177–179][accounts]

```oa-check
name: Faroese late accounts cumulative manager charges
inputs:
  managers: 3
  second_month_total_per_manager: 2000
  third_month_total_per_manager: 3000
steps:
  second_month_total: managers * second_month_total_per_manager
  third_month_total: managers * third_month_total_per_manager
  incremental_third_month: third_month_total - second_month_total
expected:
  second_month_total: 6000
  third_month_total: 9000
  incremental_third_month: 3000
```

## Ask the client first

- What legal form, cash or noncash contribution, subscribed capital, premium
  and per-share paid amounts are actually proposed or documented?
- What are the formation signature and effective dates, and who has signed,
  taken pre-registration obligations or been appointed to management/audit?
- Who are the legal owners and ultimate controllers, what rights or recent
  changes exist, and what identification evidence has been obtained?
- When does the financial year start/end, what are both years' size figures,
  and do group, holding, foundation or disqualification conditions apply?
- What audit election, Registrar/TAKS registrations, filing receipts and
  actual late-filing demands or court referrals exist?
- What accounting records and storage arrangements exist, and can they be
  accessed and reproduced in the Faroes throughout their retention period?
