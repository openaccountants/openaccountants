# Svalbard: 2026 tax pack

Svalbard has a separate Norwegian tax system, administered by Skatteetaten, using NOK and the calendar year. Gross salary withholding and separately assessed ordinary income are distinct. The nine Guides are **source-cited drafts**, not accountant-reviewed. The six previously reviewed Guides are preserved; the two additional levy Guides received [independent AI review on 31 October 2026](../../evidence/ledgers/svalbard-finish-20261031/independent-review/final-report.md), with their explicit limits retained. The new project-group payroll Guide and two added jurisdiction-boundary statements received [independent AI review on 31 October 2026](../../evidence/ledgers/svalbard-project-group-20261031/independent-review/final-report.md), with their declared gaps retained. The three explicit domain gaps above and the additional job limits below keep issue #424 open.

**Territory:** OWNERS.md and issue #424 identify this catalogue entry as Svalbard. The SJ metadata identifier does not establish Jan Mayen coverage. Svalbard Tax Act §1-1 sets this pack's boundary; Jan Mayen needs separate research and is not represented as complete by this work.
