# Statutory source data: NLOD 2.0

Contains data under the Norwegian Licence for Open Government Data (NLOD) 2.0,
distributed by Stiftelsen Lovdata. Source: https://api.lovdata.no/swagger .
Licence: https://data.norge.no/nlod/en/2.0 .

The three XML documents are exact members of the current official API datasets
downloaded on 31 October 2026. Their filenames, archive names, hashes and quoted
clauses are bound in rows.json. Reader TXT files were generated by OpenAccountants
for reading and exact quote lookup; whitespace and markup presentation differ.
Neither those transformations nor the English Guide are official translations.
No authority endorses the Guide.

These source XML/TXT files retain NLOD-2.0 terms and are explicitly excluded from
the repository's default AGPL/OA Guide licence mapping. The NLOD licence text is
in LICENSES/NLOD-2.0.txt. The English Guide and our original supporting records
remain under the repository's applicable licences; they do not relicense these
third-party source datasets.

Full Brreg/Altinn website wrappers are retained only in the private research
workspace pending the separate reuse check. Their official URLs, acquisition
receipts and exact relevant quotations are supplied, without including website
navigation, logos, layout or scripts as supposedly OA-owned material.
