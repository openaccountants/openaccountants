> Such type of companies consists of a number of persons, not less than two, who underwrite negotiable shares among themselves and without underwriting such shares in public The minimum share capital of company shall be fifty thousand BD (50,000 BD). > A Head office for the branch in Bahrain is required. The branch, agent or office shall deposit with the Ministry concerned with trade affairs a copy of the main office’s articles of incorporation and every amendment thereto. The branch is required to have a name identical to the original name of the parent company and followed by the statement “branch of a foreign company”. The branch is required to submit an audited annual budget to show the financial position of company. The representative office may not in any case practice any direct business with the clients of company in Bahrain and the branch business shall confine to the marketing and promotion of parent company without entering into direct transactions under its name. If the branch, agency, or office carries out its business before completing the branch registration procedures, the persons who conducted these businesses are personally and jointly liable for it. All documents submitted to the Ministry must be in Arabic or English. If it is written in other than these two languages, an Arabic translation certified by the competent authorites shall be attached to it.
