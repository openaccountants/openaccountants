Public Shareholding Company (BSC)
The number of founders may not be less than two founders save the companies that the government solely establishes or participate in their incorporation.
The share capital is required to be adequate to achieve the purposes of company with share capital not less than 1 Million BD (1,000,000 BD).
The company shall be managed by a board of directors. The company’s articles of association indicate the method of its formation and its term. The number of its members may not be less than five, and the term of membership in it may not exceed three years, subject to renewal, taking into account that the board includes a number of independent and non-executive members.
A Bahraini Shareholding company may be established with the participation of foreign capital or foreign expertise, and a decision by the Minister concerned with trade affairs may determine the proportions of participation of foreign capital or foreign expertise in certain sectors or activities.
GCC nationals and foreigners are allowed to own 100% of the company shares
Hold Four Board meetings
Perparing a Governance report & appoint a Governance officer

Closed Shareholding Company(BSC) (Closed)
Such type of companies consists of a number of persons, not less than two, who underwrite negotiable shares among themselves and without underwriting such shares in public
The minimum share capital of company shall be fifty thousand BD (50,000 BD).
The entire value of shares or at least 50% thereof shall be deposited at the approved banks provided that the remaining value of shares is fulfilled with a period not exceeding three years.
The company shall be managed by a board of directors. The company’s articles of association indicate the method of its formation and its term. The number of its members may not be less than three, and the term of membership in it may not exceed three years, subject to renewal, taking into account that the board includes a number of independent and non-executive members.

Limited Liability Company (W.L.L)
The capital of the limited liability company is divided into equal shares, and the share is indivisible, and there is no minimum capital or nominal value for each share.
The company shall not be incorporated unless all cash and in-kind shares are distributed between the partners as well as being fully paid and the in-kind shares are delivered to the company.
The partners may submit a solvency letter by the auditor as an alternative to the financial report unless the loss reaches 50% of the share capital.
The limited liability company shall not engage in businesses relating to insurance, banks or investment of others’ funds in general.
It may be owned by an individual or corporate a company
Holding a General assembly of Shareholder
Appointing an auditor
Prepare a financial Report
Preparing a Managment report
Assign Ultimate beneficiary owner (UBO)
Submission the requirements of Economic Substance Report.
Compliance with Anti-Money Laundering Requirements
Maintain a reserve Capital
Keeping Business books for 10 years

Non Profit Company (N.P.C) (W.L.L)
A non-profit company is a company that does not primarily aim to make a profit, and the profits are used to achieve the purposes for which it was established. The company may not distribute any profits directly or indirectly to its partners.

Partnership Company
The partners shall be jointly and personally liable in all their funds for the obligations of company.
The minimum number of partners is two.
There is no minimum capital or nominal value for each share.

Simple Commandite Company
The joint partners' liability is not limited, but the sleeping partners' liability is limited.
The minimum number of partners is two.
There is no minimum capital.
The sleeping partner may not interfere in managing the company even under power of attorney, otherwise they shall be jointly liable with the joint partners liable for the obligations arising from his/her management.
The name of the sleeping partner shall not be included in the name of the company. If it is included with his knowledge, he shall be liable as a joint partner towards third parties acting in good faith.

Commandite by Shares Company
The number of founders shall not be less than four founders.
The minimum share capital of company shall be twenty thousand BD (20,000 BD).

Foreign Branch/ Representative Office
A Head office for the branch in Bahrain is required.
The branch, agent or office shall deposit with the Ministry concerned with trade affairs a copy of the main office’s articles of incorporation and every amendment thereto.
The branch is required to have a name identical to the original name of the parent company and followed by the statement “branch of a foreign company”.
The branch is required to submit an audited annual budget to show the financial position of company.
The representative office may not in any case practice any direct business with the clients of company in Bahrain and the branch business shall confine to the marketing and promotion of parent company without entering into direct transactions under its name.
If the branch, agency, or office carries out its business before completing the branch registration procedures, the persons who conducted these businesses are personally and jointly liable for it.
All documents submitted to the Ministry must be in Arabic or English. If it is written in other than these two languages, an Arabic translation certified by the competent authorites shall be attached to it.

Individual Establishment (Traditional)
To be a Bahraini citizen or GCC citizens and be an individual resident in Kingdom of Bahrain or a citizen of one of the states associated with free trade agreements with the Kingdom of Bahrain and the applicant may not be of less than 18 years of age.
Employees in privet sector can issued an ICR -individual commercial registration- if they provide registration directorate with a letter of authorization from their companies.
Government employees cannot issue an ICR -individual commercial registration- unless they got it from transfer ownership by inheritance or it is the giveaway.
The owner of an individual commercial registration -ICR- cannot own more than one registration number (each personal ID can be issued for only one registration number of ICR, but it can be branched through the same number)
Obtaining the approvals of the competent authorities (for the activities requiring prior approvals).

Individual Establishment (Virtual)
Sijili is a commercial registration that grants an individual the ability to practice certain business activities without having to register an office address.
The applicant shall be a Bahraini national
The applicant shall not be an owner of an individual commercial registration/ICR or a sole partner in a with limited liability (W.L.L.) company. (The person who is a shareholder of a company is allowed, provided that he is not a manager or director thereof).
Holders of Sijili -virtual commercial registration-, shall not be entitled to permits to bring in foreign workers.
Approvals for licenses shall be obtained from the licensing entities (depending on the requirements of each commercial activity).
