## bj-property-tfu:03 | Who this applies to

## bj-property-tfu:21 | Penalties and interest

For 1 January–31 December 2026, original finance-law article2 disapplies the specified TFU late increases, interest, command costs and seizure costs if all TFU principal due is paid and the landowner has an IFU. Obtain evidence of both conditions; do not extend this TFU measure to rental IRF or assume principal is forgiven.
