## bj-rental-income-tax:15 | Deadlines and filing

IRF uses four instalments as for IBA, which article66 pays as IS: provisionally one quarter of preceding-year tax by 10 March, 10 June, 10 September and 10 December. Offset supported withholding without duplicating it.
