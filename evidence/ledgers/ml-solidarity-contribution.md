## Commencement, duration and current-year limit

The complete ordinance was read through article 20, not merely its rate clause. Its CSS chapter and final article contain no fixed term, sunset or delayed commencement; ratification is unconditional. No precise first liable day in 2025 is asserted. Different levies/funds and the former CGS are not this CSS.

The 2026 finance-law article 1 supplies collection authority but does not substitute for checking the enacted amendment chain. Article 46 refers to fiscal Annex U. One focused search across official DGI, SGG and ministry sources did not obtain enacted Annex U. The ministry source is expressly a proposed bill and is not used as enacted law. The missing annex blocks a current-year rate/conclusion: the Guide deliberately carries `tax_year: 2025` with explicit source-baseline notes and a prominent 2026 stop instruction. All rows above verify the enacted 2025 baseline unless their text specifically identifies a 2026 source limit or digital-service fact.

## Worked cases

Both examples use fictional inputs and the enacted ordinance baseline, not verified 2026 tax facts. Case 1 computes CSS of 100,000 despite a 5,000,000 business loss and the result after deduction of CSS paid. Case 2 selects the notice-dependent late-return branch, producing a penalty of 50,000 or 250,000 on CSS of 1,000,000; both exceed the 25,000 minimum. Neither example computes ordinary profit/minimum tax or unverified late-payment interest.
