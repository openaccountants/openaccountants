## Order 540/550/720/1145 of 27 February 2025

Official actual PDF: https://www.obr.bi/images/PDF/OM_PORTANT_ACTUALISATION_DES_TAUX_UTILISES_LORS_DE_LA_CESSION_IMMOBILIERE_OM_VALEURS_DE_CESSION_DES_PARCELLES_CENTRES_URBAINS_AU_29102026.pdf. SHA-256 `c81d4c2c780f8728a799183bd96d9336196ac98ba0ce85fc8e486969052057ed`. Actual cover and signature say 27 February 2025. The web filename's later date does not change the instrument's date.

Article 2 defines « taux utilisés lors de la cession immobilière » as « la valeur de référence par are ou par mètre carré de cession immobilière ».

Article 3: « la présente ordonnance détermine les valeurs de cession sur base desquelles tous les droits et taxes y relatifs doivent être calculés. » Its following paragraph preserves assessment on the real amount and says that when the agreed market value is greater than the calculated transfer value, « la base taxable est celle de la valeur marchande indiquée dans le contrat. »

Article 4 separates land location/surface, completed construction surface/material/equipment, and unfinished construction/pools/fences/gates surface/material. Annexes 1, 2 and 3 form an integral part. No location or construction tariff is certified as current 2026 by this review.

Article 6: « Toutes les dispositions antérieures contraires à la présente ordonnance sont abrogées. » This is a contrary-provisions rule; do not infer that every earlier clause is repealed.

Article 7 assigns implementation to the OBR Commissioner General and land-title/cadastre director and states that the order « entre en vigueur le jour de sa signature. » Actual signed date is 27 February 2025.

The complete operative clauses were visually read. Continuing 2026 effect, current replacement and a particular property's valuation remain held. This recovery repairs the factual basis of the hold; it does not certify a filing value or authorise changing the earlier accepted property Guide.
