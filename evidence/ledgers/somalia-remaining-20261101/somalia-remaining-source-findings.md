## Regulations 97, 105, 106, 107: implementation and collection

- Regulation 97-1, PDF 29: formula `(A-B)/C-D`. A is current estimated annual tax; B is prior instalments plus prior-period non-final withholding; C is remaining instalments including the current one; D is current-period non-final withholding. Read alongside Act 97(4),(5). Four identical instalments are not the universal method.
- Regulation 105-7, PDF 37: Act 87 and 88 (controlled foreign entities and underlying tax relief) do not apply in the first three assessment years of implementation.
- Regulation 105-9, PDF 37: qualifying presumptive individuals in the first three implementation years do not withhold rent/service fees under Act 91/92, but must report under 93(2).
- Regulation 105-10, PDF 37-38: a corporation/trust not previously liable to instalments and filing its first-year Act 98 estimate gets a 50% reduction on the final old-law return. Each of its first two instalments is the higher of 25% of the final old-law tax assessment and the ordinary Act 98 amount. Calendar mapping remains unresolved.
- Regulation 106-1, PDF 38: the Minister can postpone parts after Official Bulletin publication. Exact phrase: “shall specify, by public notice, the period”. An annual report does not replace that required notice.
- Regulation 107-1, PDF 38-39: temporary collection arrangements pending legislation clearly delineating government powers. Federal Member States continue the portions they currently collect in their territories, using the new Act and Revenue Administration Law. The Federal Government must identify those portions by formal circular. It collects the remainder and portions a State fails to collect. The allocation circular remains unlocated.

## Official implementation evidence and explicit uncertainties

Annual Report PDF 11 reform table says the law was signed in May 2025. Its next-step cell reads: “The law is implemented following in the fiscal year 2026.” PDF 18 lists delayed implementation and postponed enforcement of the new harmonised Act among revenue challenges. PDF 13 separately describes the payroll bank-evidence reform as pushed to January 2026 while awaiting the new income tax, with taxpayers communicated and reform postponed to FY2026. These are evidence of real implementation delays. They do not identify the Regulation 106-1 public notice, the provisions covered, or an employer-specific Regulation 105-8 notification.

Manual contradictions to preserve:

- PDF 7 corporation section labels $150 below $10,000 and 1.5% between $10,000 and $50,000 as corporate rates. PDF 11 of the same manual says presumptive taxation is for natural persons only. Regulation 9-1 confirms resident-individual scope. A legislative basis or amendment is needed before applying the website's corporate turnover method.
- PDF 9 capital-gains example uses 10% and $80,000 tax. PDF 6 table and PDF 9 narrative use 15%. The illustration is not a safe computational precedent.
- PDF 9 Faiza example sums the lower individual bands to $1,728. Parent independently visually checked that the Act's upper-band constant is printed $1,788.
- Manual PDF 3 states the Act took effect on 11 May 2025 and regulations were signed on 15 May 2025. The Annual Report records later implementation delays. Enactment and operational application must remain distinct.

Actual remaining source gaps: precise implementation public notice under Regulation 106-1; mandatory-payroll notice under 105-8; inflation circulars under 2-2; collection-allocation circular under 107-1; authoritative resolution of the annual upper-band constant; primary payroll tables reconciled with that constant; legislative basis for the corporate turnover regime on the website/manual. An empty search does not establish absence of amendments or notices.
