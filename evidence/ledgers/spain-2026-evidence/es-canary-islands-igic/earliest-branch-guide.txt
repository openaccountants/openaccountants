# Canary Islands: ordinary IGIC for 2026

## Who this applies to

IGIC applies in the Canary Islands under [Ley 20/1991 arts. 3–8](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-1991-14463#a3). A Canary establishment does not use the peninsula/Balearic IVA return for its local IGIC supplies. Determine the actual establishment, goods movement, customer status and transaction before choosing the territorial tax. Movements of goods from mainland Spain, other EU territory or another third territory into the islands are imports for IGIC; they are not ordinary mainland intra-EU acquisitions.

This Guide supplies the ordinary sole-trader/company computation and the small-business franchise gateway. The regional exemption/rate/regime provisions were consolidated in [Decreto Legislativo 1/2025](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOC-j-2025-90249); many older Ley 4/2012 article references were repealed and restated there. Ley 20/1991 still supplies the principal charge, place, time, base and deduction rules. Read both, including effective dates. The new 50,000 ordinary REPEP ceiling visible in the latest art. 89 starts in **2027**; the separate July 2026 election has different conditions.

Check exemption before rate. Current regional arts. 2–30 cover the actual social, health, education, financial, insurance, land, building and letting conditions. A genuine residential letting exemption is distinct from hotel-type accommodation. A tax-exempt output does not automatically preserve input recovery. Special modules, retail, agriculture, margin, travel, group, cash and gold regimes and product-specific AIEM assessments require their own conditions; the ordinary method below does not certify every specialist claim.

## The base

### 1. Establish charge, location, payer and accrual

For goods apply [art. 16](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-1991-14463#a16), including dispatch, installation and immovable-property rules. Ordinary B2B services follow the recipient establishment to which the service is supplied; ordinary B2C services follow the supplier's establishment, subject to art. 17's specified outside-EU recipient categories and special rules. Immovable-property services follow the property; transport, events, catering, vehicle hire, telecommunications and electronically supplied services need their actual art. 17 category. A foreign customer's address alone does not establish exemption.

Under [art. 19](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-1991-14463#a19), a Canary-established business recipient normally accounts for a taxable supply by a non-established supplier under reverse charge. The statutory exceptions include taxable immovable-property letting and intermediation in such letting. Other domestic reverse-charge categories include the defined construction/subcontract chains, specified property transfers, waste, metals and electronics. For the specified mobile/computer products, a non-reseller's same-operation invoice threshold is **over 10,000 excluding IGIC**; a qualifying reseller does not have that monetary exemption. Do not reverse-charge every foreign purchase without testing location, establishment and the exception.

[Art. 18](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-1991-14463#a18) ordinarily accrues goods when made available and services when performed. Continuous supplies accrue when each price becomes due; qualifying long intervals/no-due-date cases have the annual 31 December rule. A received advance accrues to the amount actually received before supply. Imports have their separate declaration/entry and special-regime release rule. An unpaid ordinary invoice does not defer output tax to collection unless an applicable special regime changes that timing.

### 2. Determine consideration and import value

For ordinary supplies, [arts. 22–23](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-1991-14463#a22) start with total consideration. Include charged commissions, transport, insurance, packaging, price-linked subsidies, assumed customer debts and the applicable other levies. Exclude IGIC itself, qualifying contemporaneous documented discounts, genuine non-consideration compensation and evidenced amounts paid in the customer's name and account under an express mandate. Qualifying inter-island transport/port/insurance costs have art. 22.3.d's distinct exclusion. A separately labelled reimbursement does not by itself satisfy it. Non-cash, related-party and composite transactions need the actual art. 23 valuation rule.

Imports follow [art. 25](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-1991-14463#a25): establish customs value and the prescribed duties/other charges and incidental expenses to the required destination, excluding IGIC and the incorporated AIEM exclusion. Keep the customs assessment and importer's identity. Calculate AIEM separately if the actual commodity code is within its charge; it is not an extra universal IGIC rate or an IGIC input credit.

For returns, cancellations, later discounts and bad debt, follow the applicable art. 22 reduction and art. 20 rectifying-invoice rules before adjusting output. Ordinary incorrigible-credit relief requires the statutory elapsed period, records, evidenced collection demand, recipient conditions, six-month modification window and communication. It excludes secured/insured portions and connected-party debts and has specific insolvency and public-debtor rules. Do not write off unpaid output tax merely because an invoice aged.

### 3. Recover eligible input, then allocate

Under [arts. 28–33](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-1991-14463#a28), recover only IGIC legally accrued, borne by the claimant, supported by the required invoice/import document, and used in deduction-entitling activity. Mainland IVA is not entered as IGIC input. Export/defined outside-territory supplies can preserve recovery; ordinary exempt health/financial/residential activity does not acquire it from a zero-output figure. Receive the document and satisfy accrual before claiming; retain the correct source year's definitive percentage if claiming later.

Private/non-business costs and blocked art. 30 entertainment, gifts, jewellery, food, tobacco and other specified costs are excluded, subject to their actual resale/industrial and business-travel exceptions. For mixed-use **investment goods**, art. 29.7 permits evidenced business-use allocation. Its passenger-car rule presumes **50%**, with the enumerated goods-transport, paid passenger transport, instruction, manufacturer demonstration, commercial-agent and security exceptions presumed **100%**. A return entry or accounting ledger alone does not prove use. These are IGIC provisions; do not import an IRPF motor-expense deduction from them.

A documented purchase from a qualifying exempt **retailer** can carry art. 29.3's recoverable implicit burden, provided the invoice states the retailer condition and the claimant otherwise has recovery entitlement. Use `price ×0.7×(ordinary applicable percentage/100)`; at a 7% underlying rate that is **4.9% of the qualifying consideration**. Do not also claim separately charged IGIC on that same exempt invoice. This is not a general credit on purchases from every exempt business.

Determine differentiated sectors under [art. 34](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-1991-14463#a34). Within a sector, remove blocked/private amounts first. For general prorrata compute `P = ceil(100×eligible turnover/included total turnover)/100` using art. 37's exclusions: outside-island establishments, IGIC itself, disposal of used investment goods, non-habitual property/financial transactions and specified non-subject operations. Property letting is deemed habitual for this purpose. Do not use all bank receipts as the denominator.

Calculate general recovery `G = P×otherwise eligible input`. Calculate special recovery `S = taxable-only input +P×shared input`; exempt-only input contributes zero. Special is compulsory when `G>0 and G≥1.10×S`, or a valid election applies. Apply the preceding year's percentage provisionally, then calculate the current definitive entitlement and post **definitive less provisional recovery** at the annual adjustment. A negative adjustment reduces input; it is not new taxable turnover ([arts. 35–39](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-1991-14463#a35)).

For investment goods retain the year, actual start of use, original input and definitive deduction. A subsequent annual percentage change strictly above **10 points** gives a tax adjustment `(original deducted amount − current-percentage entitlement)/5`, or `/10` for land/buildings; a positive amount is paid back, a negative amount is additional deduction. Entry in an input-adjustment box therefore uses the opposite sign. Sales during the adjustment period use art. 42's single remaining-period treatment and actual taxable/exempt outcome. Startup inputs instead use arts. 43/43 bis's provisional proposal and first-four-year reconciliation before remaining investment-good adjustments.

### 4. Small-business franchise and its 2026 transition

[Regional arts. 89–91](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOC-j-2025-90249#a8-11) ordinarily cover established individuals whose preceding-year total turnover does not exceed **30,000 in 2026**, after annualising a start year and applying the actual all-activity/worldwide turnover and succession rules. Companies do not qualify merely because they are small. The exemption covers qualifying outputs and denies related input recovery; imports and reverse-charge liabilities still require their separate treatment. Invoice wording is **“exención franquicia fiscal”**. Keep the census, records and information-return obligations. A valid renunciation operates across the taxpayer's activities for at least **three years**, with the prescribed tacit first-return route and revocation rules.

The exceptional [DA 7](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOC-j-2025-90249#da-9) allowed excluded/renouncing individuals with **2025 turnover no more than 50,000** to elect entry effective **1 July 2026**, by a modification census return **during July 2026**. A late election has no effect; 50,000 was not an automatic all-year 2026 ceiling. For electors, first-half inputs retain first-half entitlement and final prorrata uses **first-half transactions**; under special prorrata this reconciliation applies to the shared pool. Second-half inputs have no recovery, except the statutory investment-good regularisation rules. First-half investment inputs retain their first-half benchmark; second-half ones start at zero. For assets already used before 2026, the annual percentage includes the entire year and treats second-half franchise outputs as non-entitling. Record each acquisition/use period before adjusting. The ordinary 50,000 ceiling begins **1 January 2027**.

### 5. Reconcile the return

Sum tax by valid rate, recipient reverse charges and signed output corrections; subtract eligible current input, import input, signed annual/investment adjustments and available prior credits. Keep prior-credit opening, use and closing balances; do not deduct more prior credit than exists. A negative balance carries under art. 33, or is requested at the eligible year-end/monthly refund route under arts. 45–46. Exemption does not turn a disallowed input balance into a refund.

The ATC's linked [420 instructions](https://www3.gobiernodecanarias.org/tributos/atc/documents/65729/201004/Instrucciones_modelo_420.pdf/59fbc2b2-7aff-4a68-95ea-4984da563cfc?t=1742821063325) map output to **25**, eligible inputs/adjustments to **40**, their difference to **41**, the specified bad-debt recipient adjustment to **42**, prior credit to **43**, previous same-period result to **44**, and result `45=41+42−43−44`. Reverse-charge output is 19/20; ordinary/import current and investment inputs use 26–33; input corrections 35, compensation 36, investment 37, startup 38 and annual 39. Bases are reported before prorrata, quotas after recovery limitations. These published instructions retain old Ley 4/2012 references and do not by themselves prove the final 2026 program's rate-row capacity; use the actual 2026 form for its detailed fields. No live filing or program-parity run is claimed.

Use the competent ATC portal and approved program. The current 420 page states that submitting it through a generic electronic register is invalid. Electronic filing is compulsory apart from the specified individual-exclusive-property-lessor exception with prior-year turnover no more than 50,000. Preserve the submitted return, receipt, payment reference and census/election history. Mainland 036/303 obligations do not replace the Canary census and IGIC forms.

## Penalties and interest

Apply the actual conduct and tax outcome under [LGT arts. 26–28/188/191/198](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2003-23186#a27). Voluntary late payable returns without prior request attract **1% plus 1% per complete late month** within 12 months, then **15% plus interest after those 12 months**, with the conditional 25% surcharge reduction. Ordinary non-economic-loss late returns start at **200**, halved under the no-request rule. Classified underpayment offences carry 50%/50–100%/100–150%, with reductions only on their conditions. Collection surcharges are 5/10/20% by stage. Use the period's actual interest rate; these are separate from incorrectly recovered input, invoice and information-return offences. A paid tax balance does not by itself cure an omitted return.

## Cross-border points

Canary territory is outside EU VAT territory. Mainland-to-island goods need their actual export/import evidence; export treatment and importer input entitlement are different questions. Ordinary cross-border B2B services follow the correct establishment under art. 17 and art. 19's payer rules; islands-to-mainland services are not automatically local 7% invoices. Check special property/digital-service rules before treating a supply as outside territory. Foreign input taxes follow their own refund mechanism and are not claimed in 420. AIEM depends on the goods' tariff classification and domestic/import exemption and must be checked separately ([Ley 20/1991 arts. 8/11/16–19/25/67–73](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-1991-14463#a8)).

## Two worked examples that recompute

### Example 1: ordinary business, reverse charge and an implicit retail burden

Assume a fully deduction-entitling Canary ordinary business, no blocked expenses, valid documents, no franchise/cash regime, local sales 20,000 at 7%, a taxable non-established-supplier B2B service 2,000 at 7% reverse charge, local deductible input 350 and import IGIC 280. A passenger-car lease has IGIC 70 and a supported 50% business-use presumption. A qualifying exempt retailer invoice is 1,000 for goods whose underlying rate is 7%, giving implicit eligible burden 49. Output is 1,540; recoverable input is 854; net tax 686. Applying 200 existing credit gives 486 payable. These are hypothetical facts; the implicit burden is not separately charged invoice tax.

```oa-check
name: Canary ordinary IGIC with reverse charge vehicle allocation and retailer burden
inputs:
  sales: 20000
  foreign_service: 2000
  rate: 0.07
  local_input: 350
  import_input: 280
  vehicle_input: 70
  business_use: 0.5
  retailer_price: 1000
  implicit_multiplier: 0.7
  prior_credit: 200
steps:
  domestic_output: sales * rate
  recipient_output: foreign_service * rate
  output_total: domestic_output + recipient_output
  vehicle_recovery: vehicle_input * business_use
  implicit_retailer_burden: retailer_price * implicit_multiplier * rate
  input_total: local_input + import_input + recipient_output + vehicle_recovery + implicit_retailer_burden
  net_tax: output_total - input_total
  credit_used: min(prior_credit, max(0, net_tax))
  payable: max(0, net_tax - credit_used)
  credit_left: prior_credit - credit_used
expected:
  domestic_output: 1400
  recipient_output: 140
  output_total: 1540
  vehicle_recovery: 35
  implicit_retailer_burden: 49
  input_total: 854
  net_tax: 686
  credit_used: 200
  payable: 486
  credit_left: 0
```

### Example 2: compulsory special prorrata and a capital adjustment

One sector has annual entitling turnover 60,000 and ordinary exempt non-entitling turnover 40,000. Otherwise eligible input is 1,000 taxable-only,6,000 exempt-only and 3,000 shared. General recovery 6,000 exceeds 110% of special recovery 2,800, so special is compulsory. Against 3,500 provisional recovery the ordinary annual correction is−700. A separate qualifying investment good still in its five-year adjustment window had 7,000 input and 80% original recovery; current 60% is a 20-point change. Pay back 280, entered as **−280 additional input adjustment**. Total annual entitlement across these two input calculations is 2,520 and the combined correction against 3,500 provisional recovery is−980. This is an annual input reconciliation, not a whole-quarter output-tax calculation.

```oa-check
name: Canary compulsory special prorrata and annual investment recovery adjustment
inputs:
  entitled_turnover: 60000
  exempt_turnover: 40000
  taxable_only_input: 1000
  exempt_only_input: 6000
  shared_input: 3000
  provisional_recovery: 3500
  capital_input: 7000
  original_capital_percentage: 0.8
  adjustment_years: 5
steps:
  prorrata: entitled_turnover / (entitled_turnover + exempt_turnover)
  general_recovery: (taxable_only_input + exempt_only_input + shared_input) * prorrata
  special_recovery: taxable_only_input + shared_input * prorrata
  mandatory_threshold: special_recovery * 1.10
  ordinary_annual_adjustment: special_recovery - provisional_recovery
  capital_percentage_change_points: (original_capital_percentage - prorrata) * 100
  capital_tax_paid_back: capital_input * (original_capital_percentage - prorrata) / adjustment_years
  capital_input_adjustment: 0 - capital_tax_paid_back
  final_annual_input_entitlement: special_recovery + capital_input_adjustment
  combined_input_adjustment: final_annual_input_entitlement - provisional_recovery
expected:
  prorrata: 0.6
  general_recovery: 6000
  special_recovery: 2800
  mandatory_threshold: 3080
  ordinary_annual_adjustment: -700
  capital_percentage_change_points: 20
  capital_tax_paid_back: 280
  capital_input_adjustment: -280
  final_annual_input_entitlement: 2520
  combined_input_adjustment: -980
```

## Ask the client first

1. Which establishments, goods movements and customer business statuses locate each supply, import or service?
2. What actual products, tax points, invoice values and exemptions establish the rate and output period?
3. Which census regimes/elections applied, including prior turnover, renunciation and any timely July 2026 franchise entry?
4. What invoice/import documents, private use, exempt activities and differentiated sectors limit recovery?
5. What startup/investment dates, original recovery, provisional percentages and prior credits need reconciliation?
6. What filings, payments, requests, notices and actual portal status determine the correction and collection route?
