# Spain: ordinary IPSI in Ceuta and Melilla for2026

## Who this applies to

IPSI is a separate municipal indirect tax on the production/elaboration and importation of tangible movable goods, services, local immovable-property deliveries and electricity consumption. Each city administers its own tax. Ceuta and Melilla fall outside the Spanish VAT territory; selling goods into either city requires the export/import analysis, while services require the actual service-location analysis. A business address outside a city does not by itself exempt a service supplied there. The governing State statute is **Ley8/1991, BOE-A-1991-7645**. [Ley8/1991 arts1–3,6–9,12](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-1991-7645); [current VAT law art3](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-1992-28740#a3)

For a transaction file collect: city; legal entity and establishment; goods CN classification or service IAE heading; physical movement and customs documents; service description and customer business/private status; property location, building use and first/subsequent delivery; actual completion/delivery/payment dates; taxable consideration and ancillary charges; evidence of any exemption or special rate; and the business's direct/objective assessment status. The ordinary local retail resale of previously imported goods is not another production/import event solely because it is a resale: the statutory production, import, service or property trigger must exist. Usual handling to preserve goods or their commercial presentation does not constitute production. [Ley8/1991 art3](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-1991-7645)

Locate services using the VAT place-of-supply rules as applied to the particular city. That includes the ordinary B2B recipient rule and the specific property, transport, admission, electronic-consumer and other exceptions where applicable; do not infer location merely from where an invoice was issued. Ceuta art23 and Melilla art9 expressly adopt the VAT location method for their territory. A mixed or cross-border service must be classified before the local rate is selected. [Ceuta art23](https://www.ceuta.es/ceuta/component/jdownloads/finish/1583-enero/9410-bocce-extra2-30-01-2019?Itemid=534); [Melilla art9](https://bomemelilla.es/bome/descargar/BOME-A-2021-410.pdf)

The supplier normally accounts for IPSI on taxable local activity. For local property deliveries/services supplied by an entrepreneur not established in the city to a business/professional established there, the recipient is the taxpayer under the local reverse-charge provision. Ceuta's provision also covers electricity distribution and, from2024, the specified works within formally declared strategic projects using the art84.One.2(f) VAT framework. Melilla art26 expressly requires a self-invoice for its art13.2 reverse charge. A reverse charge does not create an input deduction where the IPSI deduction conditions fail. [Ceuta art30.2 amendment](https://www.ceuta.es/ceuta/component/jdownloads/finish/1931-marzo/22347-bocce-6391-15-03-2024?Itemid=534); [Melilla arts13,26](https://bomemelilla.es/bome/descargar/BOME-A-2021-410.pdf)

## The base

For each taxable transaction, establish the **base excluding IPSI**, select the actual local rate at the tax point, and calculate `tax=base×rate/100`. Add the quotas for the relevant return and subtract only separately justified deductible IPSI. Exempt or outside-scope transactions require their own legal basis and are not a zero-rate default. If the agreed price already includes IPSI, recover the base as `inclusive_price/(1+rate/100)` and the tax as the difference. Split supplies with different rates before calculating; do not apply an average rate to the turnover. [Ley8/1991 arts14–19](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-1991-7645); [Ceuta arts26–29,32–34](https://www.ceuta.es/ceuta/component/jdownloads/finish/1583-enero/9410-bocce-extra2-30-01-2019?Itemid=534); [Melilla arts11–16](https://bomemelilla.es/bome/descargar/BOME-A-2021-410.pdf)

For ordinary production, property and services, apply the VAT taxable-base rules adopted by Ley8/1991 art15: total consideration, the applicable ancillary charges, valid discounts and legally justified base corrections. Do not subtract operating expenses from service turnover merely because they are deductible for income tax. Ceuta art26 states the total consideration rule; Melilla art11 adopts the VAT method. Electricity consumption uses the total billed by the distributor. Production tax arises when the goods are made available to the customer; property/services use their VAT tax-point rules, including taxable advances where those rules require them; electricity arises on billing. Melilla art10.2 separately confirms advance-payment taxation on actual collections. [Ley8/1991 arts10,15](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-1991-7645); [Ceuta arts24–26](https://www.ceuta.es/ceuta/component/jdownloads/finish/1583-enero/9410-bocce-extra2-30-01-2019?Itemid=534); [Melilla arts10–12](https://bomemelilla.es/bome/descargar/BOME-A-2021-410.pdf)

For imports, first establish the customs value. Add import taxes/charges other than IPSI and the incidental costs required by the adopted VAT import-base rules, avoiding costs already in the customs value. Include complementary tobacco/fuel levies when applicable. Melilla art11 expressly includes commissions, packaging, freight, transport and insurance up to import clearance. Calculate the local quota using the goods' tariff, not the general service rate. Keep invoice, transport/insurance evidence, customs declaration, classification, tax assessment and proof of payment. [Ley8/1991 art16](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-1991-7645); [Ceuta arts28–29,31](https://www.ceuta.es/ceuta/component/jdownloads/finish/1583-enero/9410-bocce-extra2-30-01-2019?Itemid=534); [Melilla import arts8,11,15,24–29](https://bomemelilla.es/bome/descargar/BOME-AX-2022-6.pdf)

The import tax point is admission of the import declaration or, absent that, entry of the goods, subject to the relevant special regime. Ordinary import payment is before clearance/entry. It is separate from the subsequent quarterly return and is not postponed by the Ceuta2026 quarterly extension. [Ley8/1991 arts11,22](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-1991-7645); [Melilla import arts6,28,45](https://bomemelilla.es/bome/descargar/BOME-AX-2022-6.pdf)


### Selective input recovery and refunds


IPSI does **not** allow general VAT-style recovery on every business expense. Ley8/1991 art20 allows qualifying locally borne IPSI on goods acquired/imported for taxable production/elaboration, or qualifying definitive export. Goods used only to provide ordinary services do not acquire deduction entitlement just because the service business charges IPSI. Quotas relating to property deliveries, services, electricity consumption and complementary tobacco/fuel levies are excluded, subject to the specific statutory levy refund routes. Keep the goods link to production and the original tax invoice/import assessment and payment proof. The VAT deduction evidence, restrictions and limitations adopted by the statute still apply. [Ley8/1991 art20](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-1991-7645); [Ceuta arts58–61](https://www.ceuta.es/ceuta/component/jdownloads/finish/1583-enero/9410-bocce-extra2-30-01-2019?Itemid=534); [Melilla arts17–19](https://bomemelilla.es/bome/descargar/BOME-A-2021-410.pdf)

For direct-assessment producers, `return_balance=output_IPSI−eligible_goods_input_IPSI−properly_carried_credit`, with separately justified special adjustments. Do not include excluded service, electricity or property quotas as eligible goods input. In objective assessment, both cities expressly disallow deduction of input quotas for the affected activities. [Ceuta arts58–60,66.6](https://www.ceuta.es/ceuta/component/jdownloads/finish/1583-enero/9410-bocce-extra2-30-01-2019?Itemid=534); [Melilla arts17–19,23.2](https://bomemelilla.es/bome/descargar/BOME-A-2021-410.pdf)

A producer's excess input balance is not automatically a same-day refund: State law permits the31December annual balance refund; Melilla art19 follows that annual method. Ceuta art60 permits requests at each liquidation period as advance payments on the statutory annual refund. Follow the actual request and document route instead of treating a negative return as an automatic bank payment. [Ley8/1991 art20.2](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-1991-7645); [Ceuta art60](https://www.ceuta.es/ceuta/component/jdownloads/finish/1583-enero/9410-bocce-extra2-30-01-2019?Itemid=534); [Melilla art19](https://bomemelilla.es/bome/descargar/BOME-A-2021-410.pdf)

A qualifying definitive exporter can recover the relevant goods' previously paid IPSI through the city's export procedure, with the statutory exclusion for non-exempt exports. Ceuta requires prior exporter registration, proof of the original tax, the territorial-franco export certificate, a commercial expedition and the limitation-period condition under61; the2021 amendment to21 applies the Title10 route to qualifying vehicle/ship/aircraft exports. Melilla import38 requires prior notice using AnnexII, detailed identification linking entry invoice/DUA to exported goods, prior physical comparison, exit evidence and export within four years of import clearance. Its general route covers new commercial goods, with specified used business assets under38(E); the latter have monthly depreciation reductions, not full automatic reimbursement. The€30 inspection/review charge has the ordinance's same-supplier-return exception. Do not confuse export refunds with ordinary domestic input recovery. [Ceuta arts60–61](https://www.ceuta.es/ceuta/component/jdownloads/finish/1583-enero/9410-bocce-extra2-30-01-2019?Itemid=534); [Ceuta2021 art21 amendment](https://www.ceuta.es/ceuta/component/jdownloads/finish/1847-mayo/20648-bocce-6096-18-05-2021?Itemid=534); [Melilla import38](https://bomemelilla.es/bome/descargar/BOME-AX-2022-6.pdf)


### Objective-assessment gateways


The examples deliberately stipulate direct assessment. Ceuta63 applies objective assessment, absent waiver, to qualifying natural persons/IRPF attribution entities with a public establishment, listed activity and prior-year activity turnover no more than€600,000production,€100,000services or€300,000construction; its detailed exclusions include Annex3 services, rentals, mixed-rate activities, electricity, reverse charge, first property transfers, occasional operations and public works. Qualifying first-year activities also enter absent waiver. Its method uses the actual local Annex4 sector modules/instructions, a quarter of provisional annual base forQ1–Q3 and an annual-average reconciliation inQ4, proportional calendar days for commencement/cessation, with no input deduction. Do not substitute the Melilla income-tax module multiplier for Ceuta's local modules. A specific Ceuta module activity requires the complete current sector schedule before calculation. [Ceuta62–68](https://www.ceuta.es/ceuta/component/jdownloads/finish/1583-enero/9410-bocce-extra2-30-01-2019?Itemid=534)

Melilla21 sets a prior-year taxable-base ceiling of€450,000 and a first-year rule irrespective of turnover, subject to waiver. The covered activity must be included in the IRPF objective signs/indices/modules method and the taxpayer must not have expressly waived that method before AEAT; apply the actual local taxpayer/establishment conditions. Do not import Ceuta's expressly worded natural-person limitation into Melilla without resolving its distinct text. Reverse charge is excluded; the construction exclusion includes prior-year public contracting above€40,000. For a qualifying activity, calculate the2026 IRPF module net yield using the applicable annual inputs, multiply by the local coefficient and divide by4 for each ofQ1–Q3. Recalculate annual yield with actual average data forQ4, multiply by the coefficient and rate, then subtractQ1–Q3payments. Local coefficients:production1;general services2;hospitality/taxis1.5;specified residential/completed-building construction categories2. No input deduction is allowed. The annual IRPF module schedule and all eligibility/exclusion facts remain inputs to that method; do not use sales as a proxy for module yield. [Melilla20–23 and Annex2](https://bomemelilla.es/bome/descargar/BOME-A-2021-410.pdf)

## Penalties and interest

Apply the actual conduct and tax outcome under [LGT arts. 26–28/188/191/198](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2003-23186#a27). Voluntary late payable returns without prior request attract **1% plus 1% per complete late month** within 12 months, then **15% plus interest after those 12 months**, with the conditional 25% surcharge reduction. Ordinary non-economic-loss late returns start at **200**, halved under the no-request rule. Classified underpayment offences carry 50%/50–100%/100–150%, with reductions only on their conditions. Collection surcharges are 5/10/20% by stage. Use the period's actual interest rate; these are separate from incorrectly recovered input, invoice and information-return offences. A paid tax balance does not by itself cure an omitted return.

## Cross-border points

Ceuta and Melilla are outside the mainland/Balearic IVA territory. A mainland export and city import are separate events requiring actual transport/customs evidence; services use their actual place-of-supply rules and local recipient reverse-charge conditions. A local retail resale is not a second goods-production/import event solely because the seller has a local establishment. Keep foreign VAT refund procedures separate from IPSI input recovery ([Ley8/1991 arts3/8–12/20](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-1991-7645)).

## Two worked examples that recompute

**Ceuta: direct-assessment manufacturer and selective input recovery.** A Ceuta SL assembles computers for local sale inQ3 2026; it is in direct assessment. The stipulated correct classifications are output8471 and imported production components8473, with no special-use override. Output tax-exclusive base€30,000. Imported-component customs value€10,000 plus€600 of required incidental costs not already included gives base€10,600; all components are used in this taxable production, and the€371 import IPSI is paid and fully documented. A local consultant's€40 IPSI and electricity's€1 IPSI are excluded inputs. Output30,000×3.5%=€1,050; eligible goods input10600×3.5%=€371; interior return payable€679. Import€371 was paid separately, so own total import-plus-interior remittances are€1,050. This is not the company's total supplier tax/cost. TheQ3 filing extension is20April2027. [CN8471/8473 tariff](https://www.ceuta.es/ceuta/component/jdownloads/finish/1931-marzo/22325-bocce-6387-01-03-2024?Itemid=534); [Ceuta58–59](https://www.ceuta.es/ceuta/component/jdownloads/finish/1583-enero/9410-bocce-extra2-30-01-2019?Itemid=534); [Q3 2026 deadline](https://www.ceuta.es/ceuta/component/jdownloads/finish/1982-octubre/23296-bocce-extra48-19-10-2026?Itemid=0)

## Ask the client first

1. Which city, establishment, customer and transaction locate the charge and taxpayer?
2. What CN/IAE classification, tax point, consideration, customs value and rate evidence apply?
3. Is the activity in direct assessment or a valid objective scheme, and what elections exist?
4. Which goods inputs actually link to qualifying production/export, and what original tax/payment evidence supports them?
5. What special property, public-body, filing-extension or import authorisation applies?
