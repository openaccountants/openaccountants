21 # Spain: foral resident income tax for2026
22 
23 ## Who this applies to
24 
25 An ordinary Spanish resident whose habitual residence falls in Navarra uses Navarra IRPF. A resident whose habitual residence falls in Bizkaia, Álava or Gipuzkoa uses that historical territory's IRPF. These are separate complete income-tax systems, not an additional autonomous-community percentage on top of the State scale. Apply the residence tests in order: greatest number of days during the tax period, counting temporary absences and the rebuttable habitual-home presumption; then principal centre of interests using the statutory income exclusions; then last declared IRPF residence. A Spanish resident spending no more than 183 days in Spain uses the principal economic-interest test. Keep the spouse/minor-child residence presumption and artificial residence-change rules in the applicable agreement. Mixed-territory joint returns follow the member with the largest taxable base calculated under that member's applicable law. Sources: Basque Economic Agreement arts 6 and 43 [source]; Navarra Economic Agreement arts 8–9 [source].
26 
27 Payroll withholding jurisdiction is a separate decision from the employee's final annual IRPF residence. The Basque art 7 employment-service/assigned-work-centre rules expressly cover mixed working locations, telework, overseas work and ships; public pensions use the recipient's habitual residence; directors use the paying entity's fiscal domicile and the prescribed mixed-corporate allocation. Apply the public-State-employer exception rather than moving all State payroll to a province. Navarra arts 10–16 contain its separate withholding points of connection. A resident's final return credits the properly attributable withholding even where a different administration collected it. [source][source]

46 ### Foreign-assignment employment: local exemptions
47 
48 Select the competent territory before testing this ordinary resident-employee method. All four require actual foreign work for a non-Spanish-resident enterprise/entity or an overseas permanent establishment, and the comparable-income-tax/non-tax-haven condition in the **own** local law. A treaty with Spain containing an information-exchange clause satisfies the stated comparable-tax limb; without one, prove the analogous tax and the applicable local jurisdiction classification for that tax period. A foreign payer or work done from Spain does not by itself qualify. For linked recipients, the own local corporate rule must establish an intragroup service producing or capable of producing an advantage or utility for the recipient: Basque corporate art44.1, Navarra LF26/2016 art33, as incorporated by the local exemption/regulation and explained in the own manuals. Retain actual contracts, assignment calendar, foreign-beneficiary project/service evidence, remuneration and tax certificates. Do not transplant State7(p)'s administrative denominator correction to a local rule without its own source bridge.

57 **Ordinary full-year worksheet.** For a single employment relationship lasting all2026, segregate specific eligible foreign-service pay `S` from nonspecific salary `N`. With `D` evidenced qualifying foreign-assignment days and365 calendar days, calculate `eligible_foreign_pay = S + N ×D/365`; exempt the lesser of that amount and the territory's annual cap in the table. Specific pay is not prorated again. Subtract only that exemption from otherwise taxable gross salary, then apply the selected territory's employee expense/bonus rules; this is not a deduction of60,100/60,000 from every salary. The annual cap is one per taxpayer/year, not per trip or country. The own B/A/G regulations each expressly use total days of the year; the current own Navarra regulation permits the shorter accrual duration. A fully evidenced45-day assignment,36,500 nonspecific salary and2,000 specific pay gives6,500 exempt in each of the four systems and32,000 residual gross before local expenses. With80,000 wholly eligible foreign salary and no separate exempt reimbursement, B/A/G exemption is60,100 and residual19,900; Navarra exemption is60,000 and residual20,000. [source]; [source]; [source]; [source].

59 **Navarra shorter relationships and payers.** Currentreg5 expressly uses the year's days **or the shorter total remuneration-accrual days**. The own2025 manual expressly requires separate calculation for each payer, using the actual employment duration if less than a year, then one aggregate60,000 annual cap. For each qualifying relationship j, calculate `S_j + N_j ×D_j/T_j`, with `T_j` its actual in-year duration (365 for a full2026 relationship); sum the eligible results before the cap. Do not aggregate unrelated no-assignment payer earnings into that daily salary. A184-day relationship paying18,400 nonspecific and3,000 specific, with46 qualifying days, gives7,600 exempt and13,800 residual gross. With successive payers27,300/273days/39foreigndays and18,400/92days/36foreigndays, the nonspecific exempt amounts are3,900 and7,200, totaling11,100 before the one cap. These own Navarra sources support the method; the StateTEAC ruling is not its authority. [source]; [source] physical25–26.

117 **Basque resident ordinary non-business property disposals:** under local PIT44–45, compute net proceeds as the actual sale amount, subject to the statutory normal-market-value floor, less qualifying seller-paid disposal expenses and taxes. Acquisition value comprises purchase cost plus qualifying acquisition expenses/taxes and investment/improvement costs, excluding interest, less the depreciation required by the local rule, including its minimum depreciation. Apply [source] separately to acquisition/cost components by payment year and depreciation by corresponding year; preserve the31December1994 exception and any applicable historic relief. For a **resident**, place the resulting non-business disposal gain/loss in the local savings-disposal sub-basket, apply local66 netting/carryforward rules and then the resident savings scale in76 and applicable credits. The IRNR flat19% tax and nonresident purchaser-advance example do not determine the resident's liability. Business-asset disposals retain the business-income route in this Guide.

122 ### Ordinary home and small-disposal gain exemptions
123 
124 Calculate the gain under the selected territory's disposal rules before applying an exemption. In **Bizkaia, Álava and Gipuzkoa**, local article42(b)/(c) exempts an onerous disposal of the habitual home by a person in recognised severe or great dependency, or by a person **older than65**. The age-based route covers the **first400,000 of gain and one disposal only**. It is not a400,000 sale-proceeds ceiling. The dependency route is a separate paragraph without that age-based400,000 limit. For a qualifying first age-based disposal with gain450,000, exempt400,000 and retain50,000 for the applicable savings computation. A second disposal does not obtain a fresh age-based allowance. A person aged66 can meet the Basque age condition; that age alone does not meet Navarra's different condition. [source], [source], [source].
125 
126 In **Navarra**, article39.5(b) covers the **first onerous disposal** of the habitual home by a person **older than70**, or in recognised severe/great dependency. Its exempt gain is capped at **300,000**, with the excess taxable; the provision expressly denies a further claim to taxpayers who already used this exemption in an earlier tax period. A qualifying gain350,000 therefore leaves50,000 taxable, including under the Navarra dependency route. Article39.5(d) separately covers onerous disposals whose **aggregate proceeds for the calendar year do not exceed3,000**: exempt gain up to50% of those aggregate proceeds and tax the excess. With annual proceeds3,000 and gain1,800, exempt1,500 and retain300 taxable; with proceeds3,000 and gain1,200 the whole gain is exempt. Annual proceeds3,000.01 fail the proceeds condition, so this small-disposal paragraph gives no exemption. Aggregate all relevant disposals before testing the annual ceiling. [source].
127 
128 Habitual-home status is an evidence condition, not the address label on the sale deed. The ordinary Basque definition requires at least **three years' continuous residence** and effective permanent occupation within **twelve months** of acquisition/completion, subject to the stated necessary-move, death and employment-housing exceptions. For the age/dependency and reinvestment exemptions, a home qualifying at disposal or on any day within the previous **two years** remains within the specific former-home rule. B67.4 and G64.4 also preserve status after a required move to specified relatives' homes or residential care because of dependency/serious illness, provided there is no different use of the old home; do not insert that additional text into A61.4. B/A/G have their own annex/single-registered-property and family-main-home conditions. [source], [source], [source]. Navarra's own regulation40.1/6 supplies its three-year, twelve-month and previous-two-year conditions and necessary-move exceptions; its reinvestment definition includes a simultaneously acquired storage room and at most two garages, rather than the Basque single-registered-property test. [source].
129 
130 Basque42(e) also exempts qualifying habitual-home mortgage dation in payment and judicial/notarial foreclosure, including the stated debtor/guarantor cases. It requires the specified professional mortgage creditor and insufficient other assets/rights to pay the whole debt and avoid disposal. It is not an exemption for every distressed sale. A42 and G42.2 contain additional former-family-home provisions for judicial separation/partnership dissolution; apply their actual residence conditions. [source], [source], [source].

132 ### Replacement-home reinvestment method
133 
134 Use **B/A/G law49 with B regulation49, A46 or G45**, or **Navarra law45.1 with regulation40**. Establish both homes' local habitual-home conditions and qualifying acquisition/rehabilitation evidence. Álava and Gipuzkoa law49 expressly require full ownership of both homes, allowing shared full ownership with other co-owners; bare ownership/usufruct alone does not satisfy that sentence. B/A law49.3 separately admits the specified former family home left under a judicial separation/partnership-dissolution decision, subject to continuing habitual-home status for the former spouse/partner and, in B, the applicable descendants. Obtain that judicial/residence evidence before using the exception. For an ordinary positive-gain case, let `G` be the properly measured gain, `P` the tax transfer value and `L` outstanding principal of the loan used to acquire the sold home. The reinvestment target is `T = P − L`, **not the gain and not the original mortgage amount**. Let `R` be the eligible timely amount reinvested. If `R ≥ T`, the gain is fully exempt; if `0 ≤ R < T`, `exempt_gain = G × R / T` and `taxable_gain = G − exempt_gain`. These formulas assume a positive target and gain; a zero/negative-target case needs its own actual transaction treatment rather than division by zero. A sale with P300,000, outstanding acquisition principal100,000, gain100,000 and eligible reinvestment150,000 gives target200,000 and exempt gain75,000, leaving25,000 taxable. Dividing by gross300,000 would incorrectly leave50,000 taxable. [source], [source], [source], [source].

136 The ordinary window is **two years after disposal**; the local regulations also admit the stated qualifying new-home acquisition within the **two preceding years**. Keep the actual acquisition/payment dates and destination of proceeds. For an instalment-price disposal, apply the rule requiring each receipt to be devoted to the qualifying purpose within the tax period in which it is received. If reinvestment is deferred beyond the disposal year, state the intention to reinvest in that gain year's return. The Basque regulations assimilate qualifying rehabilitation under local law87.6. Navarra40.3 includes acquisition/rehabilitation payments and buyer-borne acquisition costs;40.8 requires the competent body's certificate for qualifying rehabilitation. Its40.7 construction route requires completion within four years from investment commencement, with a further four years for the stated developer-insolvency case and investment/insolvency documents attached to the initial-breach-year return. For other exceptional construction delays beyond the taxpayer’s control, request an extension within thirty days after the initial deadline is missed, with reasons and the necessary completion period (at most four additional years); silence for three months permits treating the request as refused. The twelve-month occupation clock in the insolvency case starts on delivery; do not treat an unfinished build as an unconditional completed acquisition. [source], [source], [source], [source].

It gives concrete family-size, recognised disability/dependency or medical-inadequacy cases; first/new work in a different municipality, changed work centre/job conditions; and marriage/partnership or separation/dissolution. Its economic-payment cases include job loss, work-preventing incapacity, insolvency, acquisition-mortgage commitments **greater than30%** of available income (sum of taxable bases less liquid quotas), or the home's share of planned community rehabilitation/improvements **greater than5%** of its cadastral value. Exactly30%,5%,20% or50% does not meet its respective strict numerical limb, although another proved statutory cause can still apply. 144 ### Disposal proceeds reinvested in an insured life annuity
145 
146 For a person **older than65**, B42(f)/regulation49bis, A42(f)/46bis, G42.1(f)/45bis and N45.3 provide their own asset-disposal-gain exemption where proceeds constitute an **insured life annuity in that taxpayer's favour**. 154

166 
167 For ordinary nonbusiness equity disposals, use **B/A/G article47.1(a)/(b) and47.2**, or **Navarra article43.1(a)/(b) and43.2**. Confirm the actual statutory market population before treating a security as listed. The following examples stipulate an already established tax acquisition value and zero expenses.
168 
169 Absent evidence that the actual price is what independent parties would agree in normal market conditions, the unlisted transfer-value floor is the greater of **the holding's net-asset value from the last closed financial year before tax accrual** and **its share of average results for the three closed financial years before tax accrual capitalised at20%**. Thus the second amount is `average_results /0.20`. Count distributed dividends and reserve allocations in those results, excluding the specified regularisation/revaluation reserves. Use the actual agreed amount if higher than the floor. Valid independent-price proof permits the actual-price route. All four current provisions use the **tax-accrual reference date**, not an assumed last balance before the disposal date. The calculated statutory transfer value is also relevant to the acquirer's acquisition value.

729 **Gipuzkoa, [source].** The professional exemption requires **at least 50% of the preceding calendar year's** receipts subject to withholding, while agriculture/livestock/forestry requires **at least 70%**, excluding current/capital grants and indemnities. For a new activity use the current payment period's ratio. The ordinary payment is `max(0, 5% × penultimate-year net activity income excluding business-asset gains and losses − 25% × penultimate-year activity withholding/payments on account)`. The exclusion removes both gains and losses; do not subtract gains while retaining an asset loss in the reference base. Seasonal/start/change-of-activity cases, no activity or nonpositive net income in the penultimate year, or current-quarter turnover below the corresponding penultimate-year quarter can use **1% of current-quarter turnover**, less that quarter's withholding/payments on account under arts. 108/117. The same 1% turnover rate applies to wholesalers and qualifying tobacco retailers. Higher voluntary percentages are permitted. With penultimate net income 40,000 including a business-asset gain 10,000, no asset losses and withholding 800, the ordinary payment is `1,500 − 200 = 1,300`. An eligible exceptional quarter with turnover 20,000 and relevant withholding 50 gives 150.
