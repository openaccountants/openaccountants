# Spain: foral nonresident income tax for2026

## Who this applies to

The Basque route is substantively different from the State route in2026. Navarra currently continues State substantive IRNR by express official instruction,while retaining its own collector and formal rules. Treat governing law,collector,withholding and return deadline as separate outputs. This Guide supplies ordinary non-PE receipts and trading PEs; its earlier supplied ordinary scope passed independent source and calculation review at the pinned earlier version. The added reasoned PE surcharge-reference interpretation is a separate source-cited method requiring its own exact implementation review. No licensed accountant has attested to it.

### Set residence, PE status, income type and governing territory

Confirm genuine nonresidence under the ordinary residence/treaty tests before using IRNR. Record the actual accrual date, recipient identity and residence, PE facts, payer identity, work/service/property location, and any evidenced treaty exemption or cap. A short Spanish stay alone does not exempt locally exercised employment paid by a local employer. CE 21 defines a PE through habitual premises or a habitually contracting dependent agent and includes construction/installation lasting more than six months; apply any controlling treaty definition to the actual facts. Ordinary rent is not automatically a PE.

For a **non-PE receipt**, use the connecting factors, then choose the substantive law:

| Receipt | Collector connecting factor |
|---|---|
| Direct property rent and direct property sale | Where the immovable property lies. Thus ordinary Bilbao rent/sale belongs to Bizkaia, Vitoria property to Álava, Donostia property to Gipuzkoa, Pamplona property to Navarra. |
| Work | Where the personal activity was actually performed; the specific public-remuneration rules still apply. |
| Pension | Employment from which the pension derives under CE 22/CN 29, rather than simply the pension payer's bank address. |
| Services | Actual performance/use; where those differ, the statutory use rule controls. |
| Public-entity dividends/interest and other capital receipts | The expressly identified public entity, payer or capital-use connection. |
| Private-entity/PE dividends, directors' remuneration, specified interest/royalties/securities gains and simultaneous-territory cases | The payer's corporate/PE territorial taxation proportions under CE 22.4/CN 29.3, rather than allocating every dividend solely to the payer's street address. |

In those **shared-payer cases**, governing substantive law follows the law applicable to the paying entity/PE in CIT/IRNR under CE 21.1 or CN 28.1. Collection follows the relevant proportions, with the representative-liquidation central-collection/refund exception below. Outside that exception, the competent province's actual IRNR law controls Basque-source receipts. Navarra's selected substantive route currently applies State IRNR under N-continuation. Basque provincial art. 13.4 supplies each province's share, rather than treating Navarra as a fourth Basque province. (CE 21–23; CN 28–30; B/A art. 1 and G art. 2; B/A/G art. 13.)

A non-PE receipt is calculated separately at each accrual. Do not offset losses from one taxable receipt against an unrelated positive receipt merely because both are reported together. Any permitted grouping is a filing rule with its own same-recipient/type/payer/rate/asset conditions. (B/A/G art. 15; State art. 15; local 210 orders.)

## The base

### Basque ordinary non-PE base, exemptions and rates

All three provinces removed **urban-property own-use/vacancy imputation** from 2026: art. 13.2(j) has no content, and the imputation base/accrual provisions are deleted. Therefore a nonresident individual's vacant or personally used Basque dwelling produces no 2026 imputed-income charge merely through ownership. Actual rent and sale gains remain taxable. A 2025 imputation filed during 2026 is a different accrual and still follows its old rule. CE's connecting factor does not itself create an imputation charge after the local charging rule is removed. (B/A/G arts. 13, 24, 27 and dated amendments; [Bizkaia FAQ 900007013](https://www.bizkaia.eus/ogasuna/faq/faq_detalle.asp?Idioma=CA&idregistro=900007013).)

For ordinary **work/rent/capital receipts**, start with gross income classified under local PIT and remove actual statutory exemptions. The general non-PE rule does not import the resident annual scale, personal/family credits, resident employment bonus or loss-pooling system. General rate is **24%**, or **19%** for qualifying EU/EEA residents with the required information-exchange condition. Dividends, interest and disposal gains use **19%** where taxable, regardless of the general-rate category. Apply an evidenced treaty exemption or lower applicable treaty cap; do not invent a treaty rate. (B/A/G arts. 14, 24–26.)

**EU/qualifying EEA expenses:** for an individual, deduct only the expenses actually permitted by the relevant local PIT provision and demonstrably directly and inseparably related to the Spanish receipt. For an entity use its corresponding local CIT expense provisions, with the same nexus proof. Ordinary nonqualifying third-country recipients use the enacted gross-income rule; the separate art. 24.2 non-PE activity provision permits its specified personnel/material/supply costs and is not a general landlord expense election. (B/A art. 24.6; G art. 24.5; ordinary third-country litigation or a specific ruling must not be silently turned into a universal statutory amendment.)

For **EU/qualifying EEA individual residential rent**, select the actual local PIT art. 32 category before calculating the base. The method below applies the incorporated provision; Bizkaia's current official 2026 landlord example independently confirms using the local bonus and limited expenses. The equivalent Álava/Gipuzkoa application is a direct statutory incorporation, not a separately published agency example:

- Ordinary LAU art. 2 residential letting: **30% of gross rent** bonus; Álava **35%** only for its qualifying DA 34 rural zone/nucleus.
- Specified Bizigune/ASAP/similar public programme: **70%** bonus and that category's financing-only expenses.
- Qualifying tense-market/reference-rent or voluntarily limited reference/public-intermediation-price letting: **70%**, requiring the actual statutory rent ceiling and supporting evidence. A tenant's nationality or an ordinary lease alone does not establish this category.
- Other dwelling category outside the above, without business activity in the dwelling: **20%** bonus and that category's financing-only expenses.
- For ordinary LAU/reference-price classes, allowable financing costs concern acquisition/rehabilitation/improvement, net of exempt financing grants; qualifying rent-default insurance is limited to **EUR 300**. Each property's bonus plus expenses cannot create a negative result. The public-programme/other-dwelling classes have their own expressly narrower expense lists.

For those classes set `base = max(0, gross_rent × (1−bonus_rate) − qualifying_finance_costs − allowed_default_insurance)`, omitting insurance where the selected class does not allow it. Do not additionally deduct IBI, community charges, repairs or State-style depreciation merely because State IRNR would allow such an expense. For genuinely **nonresidential premises**, use the separate art. 32 necessary-expense/depreciation limb: evidenced qualifying finance, property taxes/charges without punitive or lateness components, administration/services, lease/legal costs, repairs/conservation, property insurance, utilities and effective depreciation; improvements are capital cost. The total cannot create a negative result for the property. For an ordinary purchased building, effective annual depreciation is at most **3% of acquisition cost excluding land**, capped by remaining depreciable cost. Allocate land from the actual known amount; if unknown, Bizkaia/Gipuzkoa use each year's cadastral land/building proportions, while Álava uses the acquisition-year cadastral proportions. Do not use the State rule taking the greater of acquisition and cadastral construction value. For movables let with the property,use evidenced effective depreciation capped annually at the **minimum coefficient derived from the local CIT maximum depreciation period**,and by remaining depreciable cost; that coefficient is a maximum for this effective-depreciation test,not a compulsory deduction. Do not apply an automatic State furniture10%. A finite-duration acquired usufruct/use right uses cost divided by its years; a lifetime right uses3%, with the cumulative cost ceiling. Match any part-year allocation to the actual income-producing period. Example: full-yearEU owner, gross12,000, evidenced other deductible costs2,000,purchased building cost100,000 excluding land and adequate unamortised balance: depreciation3,000,base7,000,tax1,330. (B PIT regulation47/2014 arts.37–38; A40/2014 arts.36–37; G33/2014 arts.35–36.) This does not convert the residential rule into a universal property-expense rule. (Local PIT art. 32; B/A/G IRNR art. 24; [Bizkaia FAQ 900007012](https://www.bizkaia.eus/ogasuna/faq/faq_detalle.asp?Idioma=CA&idregistro=900007012).)

For **ordinary work**, qualifying EU/EEA employment expenses include evidenced directly connected mandatory social contributions under local PIT art. 22; the separate resident art. 23 employment bonus is not an expense deduction in this branch. The payer's withholding computation ignores the special EU/EEA expense paragraph, so final liability can be lower than gross withholding. A EUR 20,000 taxable wage, EUR 1,200 qualifying contributions and no treaty exemption gives final tax `(20,000−1,200)×19%=3,572`, versus gross withholding `3,800`, refund `228`. This is the statutory method at current rates; the official Bizkaia work example is historical 2020 and its obsolete State-PIT reference is not the authority for the 2026 expense list. (B/A/G arts. 24, 25, 31.2; local PIT art. 22.)

For **ordinary dividends of a nonresident individual**, current local IRNR art. 14.1(a) incorporates local PIT art. 9.24's **EUR 1,500 annual exemption** for the qualifying art. 34(a)/(b) dividend population. This is the derived application of the current incorporation; it is not State IRNR's abolished old exemption. Keep one annual consumed-cap ledger; do not give EUR 1,500 for every payment or collector. Exclude IIC distributions, cooperative-share interest and the statutory acquired-before/quickly-disposed homogeneous-share cases (two months for the specified quoted shares; one year for unquoted shares). An ordinary qualifying EUR 6,000 dividend with the annual cap unused gives `(6,000−1,500)×19%=855`, before eligible expenses/advances or a treaty cap. The statutory exemptions refer to individuals; do not give a corporate recipient this individual exemption. (B/A/G IRNR 14.1(a); local PIT 9.24 and 34.)

For **ordinary direct interest**, test the EU/qualifying EEA interest exemption, public-debt and registered nonresident-account exemptions before applying 19%. Preserve their statutory conditions and noncooperative-territory exclusions. Dividends are not automatically exempt merely because the recipient is EU-resident; the separate qualifying parent/subsidiary or institutional exemptions need their own facts. (B/A/G art. 14.1(b)/(c)/(e), art. 14.2.)

Where a pension is taxable, annual quota is `8%×min(P,12,000) + 30%×min(max(P−12,000,0),6,700) + 40%×max(P−18,700,0)`. Use the annual pension's average rate on the payment, rather than charging every payment independently through the annual bands. The statutory special diplomatic-work 8% and seasonal-worker 2% routes require their actual categories. (B/A/G art. 25.)

### Basque property sale computation

Use the local PIT gain provisions incorporated by art. 24, not the State capital-gain Guide's unindexed acquisition cost:

1. Establish actual acquisition cost plus evidenced acquisition taxes/costs (excluding interest), later qualifying improvements, ownership share and dates. Reduce for required minimum depreciation when applicable.
2. Apply the **sale year's approved local monetary-update coefficient separately to each cost/improvement component according to its payment year, and separately to depreciation by its year**. Do not multiply only the purchase price if the costs/improvements belong to other years.
3. Net sale proceeds are the actual consideration subject to the market-value floor, less seller-paid disposal costs/taxes. Gain is those net proceeds minus updated net cost, using the specific local exclusions and any proved relief. For an ordinary taxable disposal apply 19%; do not put the gain into the resident progressive savings scale or offset another separate non-PE disposal loss.
4. The buyer withholds **3% of agreed gross consideration**, using local Modelo 211 within **one month**. It is an advance, not 3% of the gain. The seller's Modelo 210 credits the actual advance against the final tax and settles the balance/refund; a loss does not dispense with the seller's return needed to recover the advance. The specific qualifying contribution-of-property-to-resident-company exception remains.

Example with **actual Bizkaia 2026/2019 coefficient 1.249**: sale400,000; seller cost1,500; acquisition200,000 plus same-year3,000 costs; no improvements/depreciation/relief. Updated basis253,547; net proceeds398,500; gain144,953; tax27,541.07; buyer advance12,000; seller balance15,541.07. [Bizkaia's current official sale example](https://www.bizkaia.eus/ogasuna/faq/faq_detalle.asp?Idioma=CA&idregistro=900007014) independently confirms the coefficient and arithmetic. (B/A/G IRNR arts. 24–26; local PIT arts. 44–46 and each year's coefficient decree.)

Historic pre-1994/2012 relief, former-main-home/reinvestment and specialist share disposals require their own local transitional/exemption provisions, rather than importing every State historic relief. They are bounded outside this ordinary example; this method does not erase those existing historic limitations.

### Navarra non-PE computation

Use **State substantive IRNR**, positively continued by N-continuation. The ordinary State method already supplied in the [common-territory IRNR Guide](es-non-resident-income-tax-irnr-and.md#the-base) therefore supplies Navarra's taxable rental base and eligible EU/EEA expenses, operative 2026 imputation rule, unindexed ordinary property gain, 19%/24% rates, pensions, statutory interest/dividend exemptions and 3% property-sale advance. Do not import the new Basque zero-imputation or individual1500 dividend exemption into Navarra. Ordinary own-use Navarra property therefore still needs the actual State imputation base and days/ownership proportion; e.g. a qualifying1.1% case with cadastral100,000,full-year/full-owner and19%rate gives209. The substantive handoff does **not** import State filing deadlines: local OF231/2013 controls those below.

### Complete2026 non-PE property-gain coefficients

For a disposal accruing in calendar 2026 under the Basque local non-PE IRNR method, use the following **PIT** coefficient table for the acquisition-cost/gain calculation incorporated by IRNR24. Apply each coefficient to the relevant acquisition/improvement/expense amount by its payment year and each depreciation amount by its corresponding year, then subtract updated depreciation from updated eligible cost. Use actual eligible/minimum depreciation under the selected local law. Do not apply one acquisition-year coefficient to later improvement costs or to the entire historical depreciation balance. Local PIT45.2 expressly supplies those separate timing rules. Seller expenses reduce proceeds under PIT45.3; they are not acquisition cost. Preserve applicable pre-1994 transitional relief as a separate subsequent legal test; this table alone does not resolve every historic acquisition.

## Two worked examples that recompute

### Example1: qualifying reference-price dwelling,not an ordinary ungated70% claim

An Italian-resident individual owns the whole Bizkaia dwelling,lets it throughout2026 under a proved qualifying reference-price rent restriction and receives12,000. There are no financing costs or qualifying default-insurance premiums. The70% local bonus leaves3,600;19% gives684. IBI500 and community charges1,200 do not reduce this residential base. No payment on account was made. This follows the current Bizkaia official example's substantive calculation with its missing reference-price condition made explicit; use the separate filing table for timing.

```oa-check
name: Bizkaia2026 EU residential reference-price rental
inputs:
  gross_rent: 12000
  bonus_rate: 0.70
  qualifying_finance: 0
  allowed_insurance: 0
  tax_rate: 0.19
  advances: 0
steps:
  bonus: gross_rent * bonus_rate
  base: max(0, gross_rent - bonus - qualifying_finance - allowed_insurance)
  tax: base * tax_rate
  balance: tax - advances
expected:
  bonus: 8400
  base: 3600
  tax: 684
  balance: 684
```

### Example2: Bizkaia indexed property gain and buyer advance

A nonresident sells an ordinary Bizkaia property in2026 for400,000 and pays1,500 disposal costs. The2019 purchase price was200,000 plus3,000 same-year acquisition costs. There is no depreciation,improvement,legacy exemption or treaty exemption. The actual2026coefficient for2019is1.249: updated basis253,547,gain144,953,tax27,541.07. The purchaser's3%advance is12,000; the seller's210balance is15,541.07. Keep cost/improvement/depreciation years separately where they differ; do not generalise1.249 to every purchase year or province.

```oa-check
name: Bizkaia2026 sale with2019 indexed basis and3percent advance
inputs:
  sale_price: 400000
  sale_cost: 1500
  acquisition_price: 200000
  acquisition_cost: 3000
  update_coefficient: 1.249
  gain_rate: 0.19
  buyer_rate: 0.03
steps:
  updated_basis: (acquisition_price + acquisition_cost) * update_coefficient
  net_proceeds: sale_price - sale_cost
  gain: net_proceeds - updated_basis
  tax: max(0, gain) * gain_rate
  buyer_advance: sale_price * buyer_rate
  seller_balance: tax - buyer_advance
expected:
  updated_basis: 253547
  net_proceeds: 398500
  gain: 144953
  tax: 27541.07
  buyer_advance: 12000
  seller_balance: 15541.07
```
