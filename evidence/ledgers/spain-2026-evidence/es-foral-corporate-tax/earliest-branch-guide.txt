# Spain: foral corporate tax for2026

## Who this applies to

For 2026, the agreements use the EUR 12 million preceding-period operations threshold. Annualise operations for a short period and use estimated first-year operations until actual figures are known. Operations means VAT-exclusive consideration for business supplies, excluding the equivalence surcharge; it is not automatically accounting turnover or assets. At or below EUR 12 million, the ordinary single company pays the administration of its fiscal domicile. Above it, collection follows the current year's territorial operations, with the percentage rounded to two decimals. Calculate one company's taxable income under the selected governing law, then allocate its liability. Do not apply each territory's headline rate separately to its sales. [CE arts.14–18; CN arts.18–23.](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2002-9969), [Navarra Economic Agreement](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-1990-31117)

For a Basque-domiciled company, Basque law governs unless preceding operations exceed EUR 12 million and at least 75% took place in common territory. A common-territory company above EUR 12 million uses Basque law if at least 75% of preceding operations took place in the Basque Country; a common-domiciled fiscal-group member needs 100% there. Select the province under that province's art.2: domicile normally controls; if all Basque operations occurred outside the domicile province, use the sole operating province or the province with the larger share of the other two; a common-territory company coming into Basque law uses the province with the largest Basque operations share. Record all three provincial percentages, the common-territory percentage and preceding/current years separately. [CE art.14; B/A/G art.2.](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2002-9969), [Bizkaia NF11/2013](https://www.bizkaia.eus/documents/880307/15187815/ca_11_2013.pdf/5a463c1d-a59b-7e48-6429-80fd22f30bc6?t=1768908575887), [Álava NF37/2013](https://web.araba.eus/documents/d/araba/norma-foral-del-impuesto-sobre-sociedades_cas-3-pdf), [Gipuzkoa NF2/2014](https://www.gipuzkoa.eus/documents/2456431/8.0761374E7/NF%202-2014%20%282026%29.pdf/f1231a01-1f83-ee15-e569-7b9b84d44d54)

For Navarra, exclusive Navarra collection entails Navarra law. A company paying both Navarra and the State uses domicile law, except a Navarra-domiciled company with at least 75% preceding operations in common territory uses common law; a common-domiciled company with at least 75% in Navarra uses Navarra law, with the art.18 fiscal-group exception. Keep a case involving both Navarra and one or more Basque provinces for a specific agreement analysis rather than treating Navarra as a fourth Basque province. [CN arts.18–19.](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-1990-31117)

Build the operations ledger from the statutory connecting factors: goods generally where made available or where dispatch starts; transformed goods at the final transformation; industrial installations use the preparation/manufacturing place if installation cost does not exceed 15%; electricity at generating plants; immovable supplies and related services where the property is; ordinary services where performed from. Agriculture/fishing products, transport and leasing transport equipment have domicile rules; allocate foreign operations proportionately to the remaining operations. Customer address alone does not supply this analysis. [CE art.16; CN arts.20–21.](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2002-9969), [Navarra Economic Agreement](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-1990-31117)

Advance payments use the last return's allocation, subject to the agreement's change-notification routes; deduct the instalment actually paid to each administration from its share. File with every administration entitled to collection. The governing law determines the instalment method even where another administration collects part of it. A State-law company allocating tax to a foral treasury must therefore retain State instalment calculations. [CE arts.17–18; CN arts.22–23.](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2002-9969), [Navarra Economic Agreement](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-1990-31117)

| Medium | Economic operation; assets at most EUR 43m **or** operations at most EUR 50m; average staff below 250 | No corresponding general medium rate band in art.51 |
| Ownership | Direct/indirect holding of 25% **or more** by an entity failing the quantitative size tests disqualifies, subject to the statutory venture-capital exception | A holding of **more than** 25% by entities failing the relevant turnover condition disqualifies, subject to public/venture-capital exceptions |

For the Basque 85%-business-income condition, trace qualifying revenue and the local passive-income exclusions and look-through participation conditions. The property-business threshold differs: Bizkaia art.13.4 requires an average of at least three exclusive full-time unrelated employees; Álava art.13.4 requires five, with permitted related-entity staff aggregation; Gipuzkoa art.13.4 and DA 10 refer to its PIT art.24.3, which requires at least one exclusive full-time employee and excludes the specified related people. Do not import one province's employment threshold into another. [B/A/G art.13.4; G DA 10; Gipuzkoa PIT NF 3/2014 art.24.3.](https://www.bizkaia.eus/documents/880307/15187815/ca_11_2013.pdf/5a463c1d-a59b-7e48-6429-80fd22f30bc6?t=1768908575887), [Álava NF37/2013](https://web.araba.eus/documents/d/araba/norma-foral-del-impuesto-sobre-sociedades_cas-3-pdf), [Gipuzkoa NF2/2014](https://www.gipuzkoa.eus/documents/2456431/8.0761374E7/NF%202-2014%20%282026%29.pdf/f1231a01-1f83-ee15-e569-7b9b84d44d54)

Before granting trading-company methods, test patrimonial status under B/A/G art.14 and N art.8.2. The Basque rules include the asset mix, the 90-day period, the 75%-ownership composition, exceptions for qualifying participation/business reinvestment and the treatment of inactive companies. The Basque patrimonial computation uses art.32's restricted expenses and the art.56.2 progressive savings scale; it is not the ordinary 20% or 24% computation. Navarra patrimonial entities lose the art.51.1.b reduced rates. The dedicated patrimonial calculation and restructuring/group regimes remain separate branches.


### Taxable-base reconciliation


Start with the accounting result, add back local non-deductible expenses, reverse exempt income and timing differences, replace accounting depreciation with allowed tax depreciation, and apply local special deductions, reserves and eligible carried losses in their statutory order. Navarra art.42 puts the art.43 loss reduction before the art.44 investment-reserve reduction. Preserve a tax-basis register for each asset and a year-by-year register of deferred amounts. This sequence comes from B/A/G arts.15–55 and N arts.14–46; a State-law adjustment cannot be assumed to exist under a foral law.

| Recurring item | Basque ordinary method | Navarra ordinary method |
|---|---|---|
| Corporate tax, penalties, distributions | Add back income-tax expense, fines/sanctions and the specified surcharges, distributions of own funds, gifts except statutory business-purpose exceptions, bribery and specified unlawful/cash-restricted expenses | Apply art.23's own exclusions and limits; interest on tax arrears is expressly included in deductible financing under art.24 subject to its ceiling |
| Client hospitality/travel | Deduct 50% of qualifying public-relations restaurant/hotel/travel costs, capped together at 5% of operations; business gifts up to EUR 300 per recipient/year with recipient evidence | Apply art.23.2's 50% qualifying expense deduction capped at 1% of net turnover, plus the EUR 300-per-recipient gift rule. This differs from the Basque 5%-operations ceiling |
| Ordinary passenger vehicle | Apply the local art.31.3 split and annual lease/depreciation, running-cost and financing caps; do not infer full business deduction from VAT treatment | Apply art.23.3–4, with vehicle/person and partial-year rules; retained source has the full method |
| Net financing costs | Generally limited to 30% of operating profit, with EUR 3 m annual allowance (prorated short period), own statutory adjustments to operating profit, indefinite expense carry and five-year unused capacity | 30% operating profit with EUR 1m annual allowance (prorated), 15-year disallowed-expense carry and five-year unused capacity |
| Dividend/participation income | Arts.33–35: determine exemption conditions locally; ordinary participation at least 5%, or 3% listed, one-year holding; subject/non-exempt payer, qualifying business-income condition and exclusions including nominal tax below 10%, deductible distributions and patrimonial payer/recipient | Arts.35–36 require their own 5%/one-year test and foreign-tax conditions. The 5% management-expense reduction in art.35must be considered; this is not the Basque full qualifying-income exclusion |

### Passenger-vehicle expense computation

For an ordinary non-exclusively-business car, use the actual eligible expense first and then the statutory ceiling. In Bizkaia/Álava the usual lease/depreciation deduction is the least of 50% actual expense, EUR 3,000 and 50% of the taxpayer's depreciation rate times EUR 30,000 (the last comparator applies where depreciated); use EUR 4,000/EUR 40,000 for the named BEV/REEV/FCV/FCHV electric vehicles. Other running expenses are 50%, capped at EUR 3,600. Financing separately uses 50% of interest multiplied by min(1, EUR 30,000/acquisition price), or EUR 40,000 for the electric class. Proved exclusive business use or the valid benefit-in-kind election permits the 100% route: EUR 6,000/EUR 30,000 depreciation or lease comparator, EUR 7,200 other costs, and the corresponding full proportionate financing; electric comparators are EUR 8,000/EUR 40,000. Apply person/year and partial-year conditions. [B/A art.31.3.a.](https://www.bizkaia.eus/documents/880307/15187815/ca_11_2013.pdf/5a463c1d-a59b-7e48-6429-80fd22f30bc6?t=1768908575887), [Álava NF37/2013](https://web.araba.eus/documents/d/araba/norma-foral-del-impuesto-sobre-sociedades_cas-3-pdf)

Gipuzkoa art.31.3.a/b has the same 50%/100%, EUR 3,000/6,000, EUR 30,000 and electric EUR 4,000/8,000/EUR 40,000 depreciation comparators and EUR 3,600/7,200 other-cost ceilings, but its own disposal-loss/monetary-correction method. Its statute does not separately reproduce the Bizkaia/Álava proportionate-financing formula in those clauses. Preserve that difference rather than inserting it by analogy. Special-purpose goods/passenger transport, manufacturer testing, driving instruction, surveillance and genuine rental vehicles have their expressly defined deduction exceptions. Sales representatives have a separate rule and a linked-person restriction; Gipuzkoa specifically retains the art.31.3.b.a' depreciation limit for those cars. [G art.31.3; B/A art.31.3.b/c.](https://www.gipuzkoa.eus/documents/2456431/8.0761374E7/NF%202-2014%20%282026%29.pdf/f1231a01-1f83-ee15-e569-7b9b84d44d54), [Bizkaia NF11/2013](https://www.bizkaia.eus/documents/880307/15187815/ca_11_2013.pdf/5a463c1d-a59b-7e48-6429-80fd22f30bc6?t=1768908575887), [Álava NF37/2013](https://web.araba.eus/documents/d/araba/norma-foral-del-impuesto-sobre-sociedades_cas-3-pdf)

Navarra art.23.3 uses 50% of eligible ordinary expenses with a depreciation/lease ceiling of 50% of the regulatory depreciation rate times EUR 25,000, and EUR 4,000 for other costs **including financing**. Its proved exclusive-use or benefit-in-kind route uses 100% with the depreciation-rate-times-EUR 25,000 ceiling and EUR 7,000 other-cost ceiling. The rate is the regulatory-table rate; prorate partial years and aggregate simultaneous vehicles by person. The art.23.3.e listed exclusive special activities have their own full-cost exception. The VAT deduction percentage does not decide this corporate expense. [N art.23.3–4.](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2017-2356)

### 2026 temporary depreciation and payroll-related overlay

The separately enacted emergency measures in B26 art.4, A26 art.4 and G26 art.4 permit 1.5 times the normal maximum depreciation for qualifying new tangible assets acquired in 2026, excluding buildings and the specified restricted transport assets. This ordinary-company measure is additional to the eligibility routes already present in art.21; it does not mean multiplying an already accelerated rate by another 1.5. Apply the new statutory coefficient from commissioning, with the local contract/construction and delivery-within-twelve-months provisions. Maintain that chosen tax-basis schedule through the asset's eligible life. The same separate instruments change 2026 hiring credits as set out below. January consolidations alone therefore omit a material 2026 overlay. [B26 arts.4–5; A26 arts.2,4; G26 arts.4–5.](https://www.bizkaia.eus/lehendakaritza/Bao_bob/2026/04/20/I-441_cas.pdf), [Álava DNUF2/2026](https://www.araba.eus/botha/Boletines/2026/045/2026_045_01089_C.pdf), [Gipuzkoa DF-Norma2/2026](https://www.gipuzkoa.eus/documents/2456431/8.813641E7/DF-Norma%202-2026%20medidas%20para%20mitigar%20Crisis%20Oriente%20Medio.pdf/925d814a-7f73-6e58-f249-a1437b1d76bf)

All three emergency instruments also remove the business-income Q 1 2026 instalment obligation, with valid voluntary payments expressly not refundable as undue payments. That relief concerns PIT, not corporate-tax instalments. [B26 art.3; A26 art.3; G26 art.3.](https://www.bizkaia.eus/lehendakaritza/Bao_bob/2026/04/20/I-441_cas.pdf), [Álava DNUF2/2026](https://www.araba.eus/botha/Boletines/2026/045/2026_045_01089_C.pdf), [Gipuzkoa DF-Norma2/2026](https://www.gipuzkoa.eus/documents/2456431/8.813641E7/DF-Norma%202-2026%20medidas%20para%20mitigar%20Crisis%20Oriente%20Medio.pdf/925d814a-7f73-6e58-f249-a1437b1d76bf)

### Micro deduction and reserves

The three Basque laws allow an eligible microcompany to deduct 15% of its positive pre-deduction ordinary base under art.32.4; remove the specified CFC/special imputations from its calculation and apply the exact local ordering, with Álava/Gipuzkoa expressly using the pre-loss base. This option is incompatible with the arts.51–53 result-application corrections. Thus an ordinary eligible EUR 100,000 pre-option base becomes EUR 85,000 before losses, rather than applying a blanket 15% tax discount after credits. [B/A/G art.32.4.](https://www.bizkaia.eus/documents/880307/15187815/ca_11_2013.pdf/5a463c1d-a59b-7e48-6429-80fd22f30bc6?t=1768908575887), [Álava NF37/2013](https://web.araba.eus/documents/d/araba/norma-foral-del-impuesto-sobre-sociedades_cas-3-pdf), [Gipuzkoa NF2/2014](https://www.gipuzkoa.eus/documents/2456431/8.0761374E7/NF%202-2014%20%282026%29.pdf/f1231a01-1f83-ee15-e569-7b9b84d44d54)

The alternative capitalisation correction is 15% of the increase in **fiscal** equity, increased to 20% for micro/small companies; maintain the fiscal equity and a reserve equal to the qualifying increase for five years, except the capitalised portion and loss exception. Each province's art.51.2 exclusions define fiscal equity differently, including contributions and exempt/foreign/nonbusiness assets. Do not use the raw balance-sheet increase. The correction cannot create/increase a tax loss; insufficient-base amounts carry with the local five-year maintenance commencement. ### Losses

Basque losses carry for 30 subsequent concluding periods. Outside the micro/small exception the normal utilisation ceiling is 50% of the positive pre-loss base. Current micro/small ordinary companies can use 100%; Álava excludes fiscal-consolidation cases from that exception. No State EUR 1m minimum is stated in these rules. Startups may begin the 30-year count at their first positive period. Apply debt-waiver/extinction and loss-trafficking rules, and the local patrimonial restrictions. Bizkaia additionally states the specified 90%-impairment-reversal exception. An elected Basque block of five consecutive periods allows use up to 100% in the first four, but cumulatively only 50% of positive bases over the block. In year five, restore any excess plus **15% of that excess** to taxable base. Elect in the first return, do not overlap blocks and align the block with an elected credit-limit block. These are documentation routes, not automatic substitution of an interest rate or salary for a proper arm's-length result. The detailed local declarations/evidence requirements must be met before claiming them. [B/A/G art.43.6–9 and corresponding regulations.](https://www.bizkaia.eus/documents/880307/15187815/ca_11_2013.pdf/5a463c1d-a59b-7e48-6429-80fd22f30bc6?t=1768908575887), [Álava NF37/2013](https://web.araba.eus/documents/d/araba/norma-foral-del-impuesto-sobre-sociedades_cas-3-pdf), [Gipuzkoa NF2/2014](https://www.gipuzkoa.eus/documents/2456431/8.0761374E7/NF%202-2014%20%282026%29.pdf/f1231a01-1f83-ee15-e569-7b9b84d44d54)

Navarra's separately fetched OF84/2018 art.2 requires model232 for same-counterparty related transactions over EUR250,000, specified operations over EUR100,000 per specified category, or same-nature/same-valuation-method transactions exceeding50% of turnover regardless of the counterparty total, subject to the statutory group/AIE/UTE/public-offer exceptions. Related-party intangible-income relief and the named noncooperative-country operations/holdings have separate reporting triggers regardless of amount. File electronically in the month after ten months have elapsed from period end; for a calendar-year period this is November of the following year. This information-return test is separate from preparing and retaining the valuation file. [Navarra OF84/2018 arts.1–6, https://www.lexnavarra.navarra.es/detalle.asp?r=50181.]

Álava's fetched 2026 official calendar confirms its local232 is also electronic in the month after ten months following period end; country-by-country231 is due within twelve months after period end. The calendar confirms deadlines and routes, not every art.2 filing trigger, which still needs the local order. [Álava 2026 filing calendar p.15, https://web.araba.eus/documents/d/araba/b_plazos-de-presentacion_2026_es-pdf.]

Keep the documentation available by the return-deadline endpoint prescribed by each local law/regulation, refresh only changed facts when a prior valid file remains applicable, and retain transaction/valuation evidence even after an exemption. The documentation exemption does not extinguish local information-return requirements. Check the applicable province's current related-party information model and timing separately; do not copy State model 232 by analogy. Country-by-country reporting has the local EUR 750m group-revenue threshold and parent/substitute/reporting-country conditions; the notification and return are separate from the ordinary file. [B/A/G art.43; N art.30; NR documentation chapter.](https://www.bizkaia.eus/documents/880307/15187815/ca_11_2013.pdf/5a463c1d-a59b-7e48-6429-80fd22f30bc6?t=1768908575887), [Álava NF37/2013](https://web.araba.eus/documents/d/araba/norma-foral-del-impuesto-sobre-sociedades_cas-3-pdf), [Gipuzkoa NF2/2014](https://www.gipuzkoa.eus/documents/2456431/8.0761374E7/NF%202-2014%20%282026%29.pdf/f1231a01-1f83-ee15-e569-7b9b84d44d54), [Navarra LF26/2016](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2017-2356), [Navarra DF114/2017](https://www.navarra.es/documents/48192/6699806/Reglamento%20IS.pdf/ea37edba-eb1b-22d7-a04b-9f9d8fbba4e6)

## Two worked examples that recompute

### Example 1: Basque microcompany, ordinary credit and default minimum

Assume a qualified ordinary trading microcompany, pre-option base100,000, no foreign credit, eligible6,000 ordinary-bucket investment credit and no staff-maintenance minimum reduction. Elect the15% micro-base deduction rather than incompatible reserves. The20% rate produces17,000. The ordinary35% bucket allows5,950, but the15%-of-base minimum limits this credit to4,250. Actual advance payments10,000 leave2,750 payable and1,750 credit carry. This example assumes no extraordinary-profit surcharge, which excludes this20% ordinary category.

```oa-check
name: Basque2026 micro base and minimum restricted credit
inputs:
  initial_base: 100000
  micro_deduction_rate: 0.15
  tax_rate: 0.20
  credit_generated: 6000
  credit_bucket_rate: 0.35
  minimum_rate: 0.15
  advances: 10000
steps:
  taxable_base: initial_base * (1 - micro_deduction_rate)
  gross_quota: taxable_base * tax_rate
  bucket_limit: gross_quota * credit_bucket_rate
  minimum_quota: taxable_base * minimum_rate
  credit_claimed: min(credit_generated, bucket_limit, max(0, gross_quota - minimum_quota))
  credit_carried: credit_generated - credit_claimed
  effective_tax: gross_quota - credit_claimed
  payable: effective_tax - advances
expected:
  taxable_base: 85000
  gross_quota: 17000
  bucket_limit: 5950
  minimum_quota: 12750
  credit_claimed: 4250
  credit_carried: 1750
  effective_tax: 12750
  payable: 2750
```

### Example 2: Navarra microcompany, ordinary credit and default October instalment

Assume all Navarra micro/activity/turnover tests are met, base100,000,19% rate, no bonuses/foreign/film/R&D credits, eligible8,000 ordinary credit within art.67's25% bucket and current-year advances10,000. The ordinary13% minimum is13,000; the25%-quota bucket4,750 binds before that floor. The separate default October instalment on a last eligible period's effective quota20,000 less2,000 withholding is30%×18,000=5,400. It is not an additional current effective-tax deduction until actually paid and attributable to that period.

```oa-check
name: Navarra2026 micro ordinary credit and reference period instalment
inputs:
  taxable_base: 100000
  tax_rate: 0.19
  credit_generated: 8000
  bucket_rate: 0.25
  minimum_rate: 0.13
  advances: 10000
  reference_effective_quota: 20000
  reference_withholding: 2000
steps:
  gross_quota: taxable_base * tax_rate
  bucket_limit: gross_quota * bucket_rate
  minimum_quota: taxable_base * minimum_rate
  credit_claimed: min(credit_generated, bucket_limit, max(0, gross_quota - minimum_quota))
  credit_carried: credit_generated - credit_claimed
  effective_tax: gross_quota - credit_claimed
  payable: effective_tax - advances
  october_instalment: (reference_effective_quota - reference_withholding) * 0.30
expected:
  gross_quota: 19000
  bucket_limit: 4750
  minimum_quota: 13000
  credit_claimed: 4750
  credit_carried: 3250
  effective_tax: 14250
  payable: 4250
  october_instalment: 5400
```

## Ask the client first

1. What fiscal domicile, prior/current operations and provincial shares select law and collectors?
2. What activity, assets, staff, ownership and group totals establish actual company size?
3. What accounts, tax-basis schedules, financing, losses and reserve elections reconcile the base?
4. What investment/project/worker evidence, grants and carry registers establish each credit and minimum?
5. What foreign taxes, related transactions, prior returns, advances and notices need separate treatment?
