This is a core payer computation for an identifiable ordinary nonresident group, not a special treaty table or an unusual tax challenge.

**Authority:** [Gipuzkoa NF16/2014 articles24.5,25 and31.2](https://www.gipuzkoa.eus/documents/2456431/4.3863475E7/NF%2016-2014%20(2026).pdf/c063bd52-a8c3-f949-c95a-692cfb8515d9). The retained current text places the expense rule in24.5 while31.2 excludes24.2/24.6. Consult its own DF11/2016 withholding regulation and the actual NF2/2025 amendment before selecting a method; B/A gross withholding is not Gipuzkoa authority.

**Minimum to close:** an own-current-law or own-administrative source bridge, or a clearly labelled independently supported own-law interpretation, selecting the prospective basis for a simple evidenced wage/rental expense case. Compute withholding, final tax and any refund consistently in one numerical control. Retain the mismatch and challenge boundary. A universally binding ruling is not required; silence about prospective withholding is insufficient.
