## F-A1-01: a dangling number is insufficient justification for selecting net withholding

The candidate tells the ordinary payer to apply the current expense paragraph24.5 prospectively because31.2 excludes24.2/24.6 rather than24.5. That is a possible textual argument. However, the proposal also establishes the precise contrary historical connection: the excluded EU-expense provision was24.6; the2025 replacement moves it to24.5 and removes the former intervening paragraph;31.2 retains its exclusion unchanged. The regulation's17.1 reference back to31.2 supplies no independent reason to prefer the net result. It returns to the very provision that needs interpreting.

The own interpretation rule matters. [Gipuzkoa NF2/2005 article11.1](https://www.gipuzkoa.eus/documents/2456431/8.6741042E7/NF%202-2005%20%282026%29.pdf/b8f36f3d-32d7-f2bf-c007-b7e250cb49e2) incorporates [Civil Code article3.1](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-1889-4763#a3), which requires words to be interpreted with context, history, legislative antecedents and purpose. This prevents an unexplained priority for current numbering alone. The proposal honestly mentions the competing historical-concordance reading, but does not positively explain why that identifiable referent should be discarded when calculating an ordinary payer's obligation. Preserving that objection in a disclaimer does not supply the missing interpretative justification.

The fresh own historical text confirms an actual connection, rather than a hypothetical analogy to Bizkaia, Álava or State practice. In [Gipuzkoa's official2020 consolidation](https://www.gipuzkoa.eus/documents/2456431/19242636/NF%2B16-2014%2B%282020-3%29.pdf/1f4447cb-30a6-fe43-38b1-b8368b0bca8d?t=1612341442000), article24.6 contains the EU/EEA expense rule and31.2 expressly excludes24.2/6. The [original NF2/2025 article2.Six](https://egoitza.gipuzkoa.eus/gao-bog/castell/bog/2025/11/28/c2508038.pdf) replaces article24 with the expense rule in5 and no6, while its listed IRNR amendments do not amend31.2. The historic text is evidence of the cross-reference's antecedent, not current authority for expense categories or rates.

The existing [current NF16/2014 articles24/25/31](https://www.gipuzkoa.eus/documents/2456431/4.3863475E7/NF%2016-2014%20(2026).pdf/c063bd52-a8c3-f949-c95a-692cfb8515d9) and [own DF11/2016 article17](https://www.gipuzkoa.eus/documents/2456431/null/DF%2011-2016%20(2026-0)/0988d0d9-65e5-f1f1-e6af-c452b324b425) establish the conflict and the arithmetic inputs. They do not expressly adopt the candidate's chosen net interpretation. In particular, the unchanged regulatory reference is not affirmative evidence of a new net-withholding policy.

This is materially different from A4: A4 applied the affirmative annual-day wording to a routine part-year case without an identified conflicting local shorter-day instruction. A1 selects one reading of an actually broken cross-reference whose former legal target is identifiable. The original scope review permitted reasoned own-law interpretations; it did not permit filling any omission by adding an arbitrarily preferred calculation and naming the objection.

**Minimum repair:** supply a substantive own-law interpretation that weighs the full current/history/purpose chain and explains the selected prospective treatment, or obtain a directly responsive own official instruction/ruling. A contextual historical-referent method can be proposed for independent review; it must stand on its own-source bridge, not on B/A analogy or assumed legislative intent. A binding ruling or legislative correction is **not** the only admissible route. This review has not pre-approved a future gross candidate.

Until then, the proposed net schedule can remain internal research of a competing position, but it cannot close the ordinary prospective A1 computation gap or be presented as the selected operational method. The final phrase calling the passage the “statutory method” must not obscure that unresolved legal selection.
