For a rental-only case with EUR 6,000 receipts, EUR 2,000 expenses and the whole EUR 500 available, taxable receipts are EUR 5,500; allowable expenses are `2,000 × 5,500 / 6,000`, approximately EUR 1,833.33, leaving approximately EUR 3,666.67 before the applicable filing-rounding steps.
