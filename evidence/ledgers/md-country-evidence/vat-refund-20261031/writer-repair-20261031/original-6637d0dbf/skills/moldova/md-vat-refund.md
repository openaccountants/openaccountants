# Moldova VAT refund and offset application, 2026

## Who this applies to

Use this Guide for the refund application following a reconciled [Moldova VAT return](moldova-vat.md). First identify the actual statutory entitlement. A credit carried forward is not itself an entitlement to cash: without a refund request, HG93 point 6 carries the excess into the next period. The statutory route, supply classification, actual payment evidence and SFS decision must all match. [Fiscal Code 101(2),(8),101¹–101⁸](https://www.legis.md/cautare/getResults?doc_id=157050&lang=ro); [HG93 points 2–6](https://www.legis.md/cautare/getResults?doc_id=152472&lang=ro).

The current Code repeals article 101⁴. HG93 still contains PPP references to that article; those references do not revive a repealed entitlement. Likewise its article 101¹ building wording predates the current Code's commissioned-and-used test. Apply the Code first. The renewable-storage route in Code 101⁸ and agricultural reimbursement programme require their own operative implementation; the gaps under Sources remain open.

## The base

Prepare a period-by-period workpaper. Reconcile the opening credit to the previous return, eligible current input, output, adjustments, prior refunds and closing credit. Separate paid and unpaid VAT, recording the date of actual payment required by the particular route. A deductible invoice can be unpaid even when its VAT is already in the return; deduction and refund payment conditions are different. [HG93 points 26–30,35¹,38¹,47³–47⁴](https://www.legis.md/cautare/getResults?doc_id=152472&lang=ro).

For the ordinary exemption-with-credit route, allocate output first against unpaid deductible input and then paid input. The refundable amount is the smallest of the remaining overall excess, remaining paid credit and supply-based cap. The official annex 2¹ example expressly demonstrates this order. Do not calculate the paid-input ceiling before allocating output and then use that higher figure after output has consumed paid credit. [HG93 points 35¹,38¹ and annex 2¹](https://www.legis.md/cautare/getResults?doc_id=152472&lang=ro).

For Code 101⁶, maintain separate chronological buckets: unpaid credits from January 2023 onward, paid credits from January 2023 onward, then December 2022 carry-forward. Determine eligible paid inputs at application; include the specified budget-paid VAT for cross-Nistru, insolvency and pledged/seized-property acquisitions where the conditions apply. Use the programme's own 70% ceiling and paid-excess calculation, not the export cap. Requested refunds reduce subsequent carry-forward under Code 101⁶(2). [HG93 point 47³; Code 101⁶](https://www.legis.md/cautare/getResults?doc_id=152472&lang=ro).

Do not include national-public-budget-funded investment VAT in the investment refund. For buildings retain commissioning and actual-use evidence. An unfinished, conserved or merely purchased building does not satisfy the current commissioned-and-used condition. For a supplementary claim for a previously refunded period, apply all three HG93 point 30 tests: minimum intervening credit, no intervening VAT-payable obligation, and the applicable payment requirement. Do not refund the same input again. [Code 101¹](https://www.legis.md/cautare/getResults?doc_id=157050&lang=ro); [HG93 point 30,42¹,44](https://www.legis.md/cautare/getResults?doc_id=152472&lang=ro).

## Deadlines and filing

1. **Identify the law and period.** Match the route above to the actual taxpayer and supply. Reconcile each requested month to submitted VAT12 returns. The ordinary request equals the return's requested refund; HG93 point 8 contains specific exceptions for nonregistered statutory routes and agricultural-machinery-input claims. Check the current statute before using any legacy exemption.
2. **Complete the actual approved annex 1.** Enter fiscal code, legal name, registered address/street/number and telephone. Enter the requested MDL amount and exact law/article. Enter start and end month/year as `MM/YYYY`; for one month repeat the same period in both fields. An application with no requested amount is not examined. These are named fields, not numbered boxes.
3. **Give settlement instructions.** State the taxpayer's own bank/payment-account IBAN or future-obligation allocation. The table has economic-classification code, locality code, principal/fines and late charges, plus total. SFS offsets arrears first; the bank instruction concerns the confirmed amount left after that offset. Date and have the head sign the application. Do not direct the taxpayer's refund to a creditor or assignee.
4. **Assemble route-specific proof.** Provide purchase and sales registers. E-Factura invoices need not be printed. For ordinary exported goods retain export contract, commercial/tax invoice, international transport proof except the postal exception, and customs export confirmation; postal exports also need the postal document identified in the customs declaration. For exported services retain foreign-customer confirmation, payment proof/account extracts and, where linked to exported physical goods, customs and outward transport proof. Translate customer confirmation through an authorised translator when required; Russian/English confirmation is excepted from that translation requirement. Electronic services have the specific foreign-recipient/payment-identification rule. Retain investment purchase/customs/payment proof; apply the particular point 32 subparagraph for transport, leasing, zones and other supplies.
5. **Submit the signed application to SFS.** Keep the dated receipt and exact submitted version. Retain proof that the corresponding VAT12 returns were submitted; ordinary requests are examined only after those returns. The application is distinct from filing VAT12. See the official annex 1 link under Sources. Do not invent a separate portal box or treat a saved application as received.
6. **Track the authority's procedure.** Ordinary HG93 point 12 route selection is within 3 working days; its listed investment/special-scheme exceptions matter. No-prior-thematic-audit treatment requires all point 13 conditions: at least 2 years' activity, at least 2 previous refunds, no more than 12 months since the last audited refund period for large taxpayers or 24 for others, exact agreement between claimed/confirmed amount at the last thematic audit, and last-audit extra liabilities no more than 3% of supplies in the audited periods. Otherwise SFS initiates thematic audit. Later audit still occurs at least every 12/24 months. Points 12 and 13 have different printed exclusion lists; do not assume the no-audit branch for a special scheme from point 12 alone.
7. **Reconcile decision to payment.** SFS leadership decides; reasons for a partial refund/refusal must appear in the audit act. Record confirmed amount, arrears offset, future-tax allocation, bank amount and remaining credit separately. Treasury execution follows SFS's payment documents. A decision is not evidence of cash received. Challenge an adverse decision under Title V; use the underlying act's notified appeal route and clock.

Sources: [HG93 points 7–19¹,22–32 and annex 1](https://www.legis.md/cautare/getResults?doc_id=152472&lang=ro); [Code 101(8),101¹(4),101³(2),101⁶(4)](https://www.legis.md/cautare/getResults?doc_id=157050&lang=ro).

## Penalties and interest

Retain the refund decision, submitted evidence and subsequent corrections. A refusal is not automatically a tax penalty. Incorrect VAT reporting engages the assessment/late-charge rules in the [VAT return Guide](moldova-vat.md#penalties-and-interest); do not calculate a late charge on a refused refund without an actual unpaid tax debt.

For a late authority refund, Code 176(3) provides taxpayer interest after the statutory refund period, using the previous November BNM base rate rounded up to the next whole percentage plus 5 points, divided by days in the year and rounded to 4 decimal places as a daily percentage. The statutory criminal-proceeding suspension excludes interest for its suspension period. The taxpayer submits the calculation for authority verification/approval under 176(4). Use the [pack's sourced 2026 benchmark calculation](moldova-vat.md#penalties-and-interest) and actual due/refund dates; do not assume interest is paid automatically. [Code 176(1),(3)–(4); HG93 point 51](https://www.legis.md/cautare/getResults?doc_id=157050&lang=ro).

## Cross-border points

Foreign currency receipts and an overseas address do not establish an export exemption. Obtain the place-of-supply conclusion and recipient evidence from the [VAT Guide](moldova-vat.md), then the route-specific HG93 point 32 proof above. Keep customs/import VAT payment evidence distinct from an overseas supplier payment. Zone/port/airport claims depend on actual resident status, the elected customs guarantee regime and the evidence in point 32(11). Cross-Nistru and treaty/project-specific implementing questions remain under Sources; a foreign transfer is not sufficient proof.

## Two worked examples that recompute

### 1. Official ordinary export cap and paid-credit order

Recompute HG93 annex 2¹: domestic output VAT 2,000; export value 6,000 with standard-rate cap 1,200; current input 1,000, of which 800 paid/200 unpaid; opening credit 2,500, of which 500 paid/2,000 unpaid. All eligibility/proof assumptions are those stated in the official annex, not a claim that a particular taxpayer qualifies. Allocate output against 2,200 unpaid first. The remaining paid credit is 1,300 and total excess 1,500; refund 1,200, leaving paid 100/unpaid 200. [Official annex 2¹](https://www.legis.md/UserFiles/Image/RO/2024/mo%20564-568%20ro/an_2_1_93_MD.docx).

```oa-check
name: Moldova official export refund paid-unpaid order
tolerance: 0.0001
inputs:
  output_vat: 2000
  export_value: 6000
  current_paid: 800
  current_unpaid: 200
  opening_paid: 500
  opening_unpaid: 2000
steps:
  total_paid: current_paid + opening_paid
  total_unpaid: current_unpaid + opening_unpaid
  output_against_unpaid: min(output_vat, total_unpaid)
  unpaid_left: total_unpaid - output_against_unpaid
  paid_left: total_paid - max(output_vat - total_unpaid, 0)
  excess: total_paid + total_unpaid - output_vat
  supply_cap: export_value * 0.20
  refund: min(excess, paid_left, supply_cap)
  paid_carry: paid_left - refund
  total_carry: excess - refund
expected:
  output_against_unpaid: 2000
  unpaid_left: 200
  paid_left: 1300
  excess: 1500
  supply_cap: 1200
  refund: 1200
  paid_carry: 100
  total_carry: 300
```

### 2. Qualifying e-Factura excess, arrears and bank balance

Hypothetical wholly eligible Code 101⁶ month after January 2023: qualifying system-documented output VAT 10,000; eligible input VAT actually paid at application 25,000; no unpaid or December 2022 credits, other adjustments or previous refunds. The paid excess is 15,000 but the 70% output cap is 7,000. Assume SFS confirms that amount, with 1,500 actual taxpayer arrears and no future-tax allocation. Offset 1,500 and bank 5,500; carry 8,000 remains. Bank receipt still requires Treasury evidence. [HG93 point 47³; Code 101⁶(2),(4); approved annex 1](https://www.legis.md/cautare/getResults?doc_id=152472&lang=ro).

```oa-check
name: Moldova e-Factura refund cap and settlement
tolerance: 0.0001
inputs:
  paid_input: 25000
  qualifying_output: 10000
  arrears: 1500
steps:
  paid_excess: paid_input - qualifying_output
  programme_cap: qualifying_output * 0.70
  confirmed_refund: min(paid_excess, programme_cap)
  offset: min(confirmed_refund, arrears)
  bank_instruction: confirmed_refund - offset
  carry_forward: paid_excess - confirmed_refund
expected:
  paid_excess: 15000
  programme_cap: 7000
  confirmed_refund: 7000
  offset: 1500
  bank_instruction: 5500
  carry_forward: 8000
```

## Ask the client first

- Which exact statutory route, tax months and supplies create the entitlement?
- Are all VAT12 returns submitted and opening/closing credit balances reconciled?
- Which input VAT was actually paid, when, by whom and through which account?
- Are export, customs, recipient, commissioning and actual-use documents complete for this route?
- Has any part been refunded, offset, assigned, disputed or included in another application?
- What arrears and future-tax instructions exist, and is the supplied IBAN the taxpayer's own?
- Are there previous audits, scheme-specific exclusions, a December 2022 balance or a criminal-proceeding suspension affecting this claim?
