## Interpretive points preserved

The company-law financial-statement filing clock is two months from adoption; the PRH eight-month general outer timetable does not postpone an earlier statutory deadline. A company is created on registration, not merely by assigning funding or saving a notification.

The ordinary audit-size omission rule in chapter 2 section 2 is positively framed: at most one exceedance in each of the concluded and immediately preceding periods. Some English summaries describe the two-year condition ambiguously. The Guide uses the statutory positive test, includes a one-year-two-trigger example and retains startup, articles, securities-holding, group and other-law checks. Arithmetic counts do not establish the legal outcome by themselves.
