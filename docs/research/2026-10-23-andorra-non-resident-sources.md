## Sources inspected

- https://www.portaljuridicandorra.ad/L2010094_F: identifies current fifth law consolidation, effective 17 April 2025, and amendment list. Entry into force and application can differ.
- https://www.portaljuridicandorra.ad/L2010094: current Law 94/2010 consolidation. Read source/PE provisions, exemptions, non-PE base, rates, accrual, declarations and withholding, plus boundaries to separate PE/property work.
- https://portaljuridicandorra.ad/R20231229B: current IRNR regulation consolidation, with amendments through Decree 412/2025. Articles 22–29 support quarterly filings, the final-month option, distinct monthly employment timetable, records and certificate communication.

Both consolidations expressly lack official legal status. They are supplied by the legal portal; the draft preserves that qualification rather than describing them as official consolidated enactments. Underlying enactment reconciliation and professional review remain distinct steps.
