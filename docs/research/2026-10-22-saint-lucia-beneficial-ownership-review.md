# Saint Lucia beneficial ownership: 22 October 2026

Read and captured revised sections 69A, 77, 177 and 551 and full enacted Act 4/2021. Initial notice 90 days subject to named exceptions; changes 15 days. Section 551 inclusive 25% limb distinct from ultimate effective control and transaction-on-behalf limb. Four owners at exactly 25% all qualify; below threshold does not exclude control. Register requires voting percentage but definition is wider.

Original 2021 section 7 confirms insertion of beneficial owners in director-change provision; revised text's director-consent wording remains awkward. Guide does not invent director appointment or claim to resolve the correct supporting form. Section 517A application-based extension maximum further 90 days, before or after expiry, not automatic or penalty waiver.

Later 2024 Acts already reviewed in company-reporting evidence do not change these provisions. Full currency, prescribed forms/fees, special regimes, identity documentation, privacy/access and enforcement remain open. No independent review or professional attestation. This is not completed country coverage.
