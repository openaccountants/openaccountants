## Examples

- Mixed services and separately invoiced equipment: Assume art. 132/133 applies without treaty relief, no PE and the baseline is operative. A contract separately invoices services MRU 100,000 and equipment MRU 400,000, with ancillary service costs MRU 10,000 paid for the provider. Service withholding base is 110,000 and tax 16,500. The separately invoiced equipment 400,000 is excluded from this withholding, not declared generally tax free.
- Decide whether equipment is truly separately invoiced: Assume an otherwise identical 500,000 contract has services 100,000 and equipment 400,000. With a qualifying distinct equipment invoice, service withholding is 15,000. If the art. 133(4) separate-invoice exclusion cannot be established and all 500,000 is within the taxable service consideration, the assumed alternative is 75,000. Obtain the documents: the difference of 60,000 is not permission to relabel services as goods.
