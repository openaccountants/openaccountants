## Examples

- Year-end routing before deadlines: Assume the baseline ordinary IS calendar is applicable and a company closes on 31 December with final tax MRU 250,000 and no credits. Allocate 100,000, 75,000 and 75,000 to the following March, June and September dates using C11 in the company Guide. Do not treat those dates as VAT or salary remittance dates.
- Choose payroll calendar by liability, not one universal date: For an assumed January payroll, the cited private CNAM decree uses day 10 of February, while the ITS code uses day 15 of February. The nominal calendar difference is five days. Reserve each liability separately: assumed CNAM 900 and ITS 2,100 require 3,000 total, not a single tax payment. This arithmetic does not establish weekends/extensions or the CNSS due date.
