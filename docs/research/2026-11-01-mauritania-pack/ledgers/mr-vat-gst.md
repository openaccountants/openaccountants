## Examples

- Ordinary VAT with allowed input: Assume the baseline ordinary rate applies to collected taxable consideration MRU 100,000 excluding VAT and all MRU 8,000 input VAT is eligible with required documents/certificate. Output VAT 16,000 minus input 8,000 gives 8,000 due. A missing certificate prevents treating the assumed input as allowed.
- Choose credit carryforward rather than assume refund: Assume ordinary taxable outputs MRU 40,000 excluding VAT and eligible input VAT 10,000. Output 6,400 gives a credit 3,600. Carry it forward; even if the activity were an eligible exporter, 3,600 is below the baseline 100,000 refund threshold and the other conditions still need checking.
