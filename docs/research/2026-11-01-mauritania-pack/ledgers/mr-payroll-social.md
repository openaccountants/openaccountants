## Examples

- Different social bases above the CNSS cap: Assume covered private indefinite employment, gross MRU 20,000 entirely within the relevant bases, and ministry CNSS rates apply. CNSS capped base is 15,000: employer 1,950, employee 150. CNAM uses gross 20,000: employer 1,000, employee 800. Employer contributions total 2,950; employee social deductions total 950. ITS and ONMT are not included.
- Apprenticeship relief: cap decides the deduction: Assume baseline annual remuneration MRU 1m, 100 eligible apprentices present on 1 January under regular contracts, no discretionary exemption. Levy is 6,000. Nominal relief 4,000 is capped at 3,000, giving 3,000 payable. With 20 eligible apprentices, relief 800 is below the cap and payable is 5,200.
