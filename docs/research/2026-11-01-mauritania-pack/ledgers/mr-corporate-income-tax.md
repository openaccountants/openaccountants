## Examples

- Normal IS baseline: profit beats products and minimum: Assume the ordinary normal baseline applies, profit MRU 1,200,000 and eligible products MRU 8,000,000 with no credits. Compare 300,000, 160,000 and 100,000: MRU 300,000. Baseline instalments are 120,000, 90,000 and 90,000.
- Choose the statutory limb, not the lower apparent rate: Assume the normal baseline applies: eligible products MRU 8m, taxable profit MRU 200,000. Profit limb is 50,000; products limb is 160,000, above the 100,000 floor. Choose 160,000. If profit were 800,000 with the same products, choose profit tax 200,000 instead.
