## Examples

- ITS baseline: exemption precedes formula: Assume cash-only monthly remuneration MRU 18,500, deductible compulsory contribution MRU 500 and no other deductions/benefits. Subtract the MRU 6,000 remuneration exemption, then round down to MRU 10: RI 12,000. Tax is 12,000 × 25% minus 900 = MRU 2,100. Gross-pay bands would give a wrong answer.
- Forfait or real election: compare both baseline amounts: Assume a non-importing business eligible for forfait with turnover MRU 2.4m and profit MRU 100,000. Forfait is 72,000. A valid voluntary intermediate-real election would compare profit tax 30,000, products minimum 60,000 and fixed floor 75,000, giving 75,000. This numerical comparison favours forfait by 3,000; it does not establish that an election was made or captures bookkeeping/VAT consequences.
