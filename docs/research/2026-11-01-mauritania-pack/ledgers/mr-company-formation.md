## Examples

- Document copies, not an invented minimum capital: APIM asks for one certified statutes copy plus three photocopies. For one company, prepare four copies, including the one certified copy. For two companies, prepare eight. This checks the dossier quantity; it does not assert a statutory capital amount.
- Capital proof document trigger versus minimum: Compare chosen capital MRU 8,000 and MRU 12,000 against APIM’s document trigger greater than 10,000. The first is 2,000 below, the second 2,000 above; prepare deposit proof/paid-capital declaration for the latter under the published checklist. The trigger does not decide whether the chosen legal form/capital complies with all company law.
