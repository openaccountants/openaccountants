## Examples

- Majority-linked documentation test: Assume the replacement art. 66 applies: a Mauritanian subsidiary has turnover MRU 12m and assets 20m but a parent owns 60% and has turnover 40m. The subsidiary falls within the majority-linked documentation test although its own figures are below 30m. The excess parent turnover over threshold is 10m; a 50% holding would not satisfy the more-than-half ownership limb, absent another control/eligibility fact.
- Documentation penalty: percentage or minimum: Assume the revised L.131(6) conditions are satisfied after an unanswered/incomplete formal notice, for one audited year. Missing supporting transactions of MRU 20m give 100,000 at 0.5%, so choose the 500,000 minimum. For affected transactions of 200m choose 1m, above that minimum. These are documentary penalties, not the tax on an arm’s-length adjustment.
