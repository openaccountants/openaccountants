# Guernsey UK pension and government-service allocation

Writer verification, 22 October 2026. Not independent review or accountant attestation.

Read Articles 17–19 of the official 2018 Agreement. Article 17 is subject to Article 18. Article 18 expressly includes pensions in its first ordinary-residence condition but excludes them from its service-only ordinary-residence alternative. No nationality test is supplied. Its paragraph 2 addresses profit-making government trade/business services. HMRC DT8602 corroborates the UK-resident government-pension distinction.

Examples test classification conditions rather than inventing a tax rate or pension exemption calculation. Article 19 applies only to the specified foreign-source support and presence purpose, not all student earnings. Ordinary-residence definitions, scheme facts, lump sums and operational claims remain open.
