# UK–Gibraltar employment and pensions: writer verification

22 October 2026; issue #398; Michael; same PR #514. Writer verification only, not independent review or accountant attestation.

Read agreement Articles 14–19, 21–22 and Protocol paragraph 3; reused verified residence/commencement/anti-abuse context. Cross-checked pensions, government service and arbitration in Gibraltar's official PDF printed pages 23, 30–31 and 40 against HMRC full agreement HTML.

Material finding: HMRC DT8152 summary table states other pensions taxable only in UK, government pensions under Article 18, and no arbitration. Published agreement Article 17 preserves source taxation, Article 18(1) excludes pensions, Article 24(5) provides requested arbitration subject to conditions. Both official full texts agree. No separate arrangement explaining the manual entries established. Draft exposes discrepancy rather than claiming an authoritative correction by HMRC. Existing investment Guide updated to flag arbitration conflict.

Ordinary employment: all three short-stay limbs, any relevant twelve months, exactly 183 boundary; no wage-only day count assumption. Government service has no nationality test; Regiment Protocol disapplies paragraph 1(b). Offshore thirty-day enterprise threshold is not an employee exemption. Student foreign-source maintenance is not blanket wage exemption. Gibraltar same-income credit includes residence-only exclusion and no assumed pension rate. Two oa-check examples recompute boundary and credit arithmetic; not proof of underlying eligibility.

Current payroll authorisation, domestic refund/return procedures, countable arrival/departure days, employer/recharge interpretation, pension source/classification and social coverage remain open. HMRC DT-Individual landing page inspected but not adopted as a pension exemption or employment procedure. No complete-country claim.
