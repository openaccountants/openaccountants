## Transfer pricing

Primary starting points:

- Fiscal Code, Title V, Chapter 11², official consolidated page (verify exact current consolidation before publication): https://www.legis.md/cautare/getResults?doc_id=142855&lang=ro
- Ministry of Finance Order 9/2024, amended by Orders 21/2025 and 8/2026: https://www.legis.md/cautare/downloadpdf/147520, https://www.legis.md/cautare/downloadpdf/152708
- Law 187/2025 amending Fiscal Code Article 226¹⁹: https://www.legis.md/cautare/downloadpdf/149593
- SFS transfer-pricing implementation page: https://www.sfs.md/ro/pagina/preturi-de-transfer
- SFS deadline notice for fiscal year 2025, 24 June 2026: https://www.sfs.md/ru/novosti/25-iunie-2026-termen-limita-de-prezentare-a-informatiei-privind-preturile-de-transfer-pentru-anul-2025
- SFS Generalized Fiscal Practice item 44.3.1: https://www.sfs.md/ro/ordinele-de-baze-de-date-de-generalizare/1200

SFS announced 25 June 2026 for the 2025 calendar-year information filing, covering aggregated affiliated-person transactions of at least MDL 20m excluding VAT. Law 187/2025 rewrote Article 226¹⁹: one MDL 20m threshold for the information filing; transfer-pricing file only on SFS request, within 120 days of the request and not earlier than the information deadline. There is no longer an automatic MDL 50m file-submission tier for 2025 periods. The 2024 transition had different documentation and deadline rules. Order 8/2026 further amended Order 9/2024, including APA procedure and implementation-rule provisions. The SFS page presents the current reporting process and 2025 master-file/local-file content. Follow the later law where older SFS FAQ 44.3.1 gives a superseded 60-day / MDL 50m summary.

Unresolved before any claim of comprehensive coverage: exact current statutory text and scope/exclusions; current Order 9 annexes and online form fields; off-calendar period deadline computation; full Article 260 offense and effective-date mapping; retention period trigger; APA mechanics and updated fees. A single SFS page gives the period-specific filing rule and controls over older SFS FAQ text that describes the earlier deadline.

## Cross-border

Official source base:

- Fiscal Code: https://www.legis.md/cautare/getResults?doc_id=142855&lang=ro

- SFS source characterization for services: https://www.sfs.md/ro/ordinele-de-baze-de-date-de-generalizare/1095
- SFS legal-entity treaty residence evidence, item 29.4.41: https://www.sfs.md/ro/ordinele-de-baze-de-generalizare/102
- SFS residence-certificate instructions: https://www.sfs.md/ro/pagina/certificate-de-rezidenta-persoane-fizice
- Ministry of Finance treaty list, reported as at 20 June 2022: https://mf.gov.md/ro/content/acorduriconven%C8%9Bii-pentru-evitarea-dublei-impuneri-la-situa%C8%9Bia-din-20062022

SFS practice says covered nonresident Article 71 income not connected to a Moldovan PE is generally withheld at source under Article 91, whose general rate is 12%, with statutory exceptions. SFS distinguishes specified service income that remains Moldovan-source even if performed abroad from residual services sourced by performance in Moldova. Treaty relief requires the relevant treaty analysis and residence proof before payment under the Code. Domestic PE examples include a fixed place and specified construction activity exceeding six months, subject to the full Code definition and treaty.

Unresolved: treaty-by-treaty current text/protocol and MLI status; complete source categories and current rate exceptions; payer forms/payment deadlines; PE profit computation and filings; individual residence tie-breakers and departure; foreign tax credits; employment and social-security coordination; property and capital gains; entity/CFC issues; interaction with VAT/customs. The Ministry list's stated 2022 status is stale for definitive treaty conclusions.

## Independent review and corrections

An independent pack review on 24 October 2026 found the initial TP draft incorrectly retained the pre-Law 187/2025 MDL 50m filing tier and 60-day request period. It also found the cross-border draft described a residence certificate as the only proof in all cases. Corrected the TP rules to the single MDL 20m information threshold and request-only file due within 120 days (not before the information deadline), added Law 187/2025 and Order 8/2026, and distinguished alternative proof for legal entities from the individual certificate requirement. Correction recheck confirmed the TP rules and legal-entity evidence change; it identified two leftover example/checklist references to a required certificate, now corrected. Final wording recheck is pending. No publication until the correction recheck and repository validation pass.
