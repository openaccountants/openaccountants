# Guernsey individual penalty and appeal procedure

22 October 2026. Expanded individual Guide using Revenue Service taxpenalties and its linked Form 690(b), revision 12/21. The webpage is historical as to its stated 2023-return deadline; that deadline is not reused. Formula example assumes 40 chargeable days independently confirmed, avoiding unsupported inclusive-date arithmetic. The 30-day grace is measured from initial penalty; late appeal clock from order issue. Accrual and discretionary written collection suspension are distinct. Low-income £50 restriction follows filing/assessment. Full statute, late-appeal jurisdiction, Tribunal steps and interest remain open.

This addition is operational guidance, not professional attestation or full compliance coverage.
