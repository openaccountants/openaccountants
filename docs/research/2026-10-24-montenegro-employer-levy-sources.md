## Primary evidence actually read

- [Actual 151/25 Chamber decision](https://www.sluzbenilist.me/propisi/389055/download): downloaded PDF, extracted entire one-page decision, rendered and visually inspected. Points 1–3 prescribe 0.27%, gross salaries reported in IOPPD versus entrepreneurs' independent-activity income in GPP-FL, and payment with the relevant submission. Points 4–5 cover the artisan-register exception and payment accounts. Point 6 applies from 1 January 2026. Local evidence: `/private/tmp/oa-me-chamber-2026.pdf`, `.txt` and `-p1.png`.
- [Municipal artisan registration notice](https://www.ul-gov.me/me/aktuelnosti/prijava-u-registar-zanatlija): actual notice read, 17 April 2026, describes register operation from 1 March and registration through 1 June. It does not establish an individual business's registration, retroactive refund or separate craft-Chamber dues. Local HTML retained.
- [Employment agency's disability-employment law through 55/16](https://www.zzzcg.me/wp-content/uploads/2018/01/Zakon-o-profesionalnoj-rehabilitaciji-i.pdf): downloaded/read targeted Articles 20–23 and surrounding status context. Article 21 has 20–50 / over-50 quota populations, paragraph-2-linked new-employer relief and old severe-disability counting. Article 22 has 20% per unfilled quota place, and a separate flat 5% employer charge for more than 10 and fewer than 20 employees with no qualifying worker. Article 22a exempts the stated disability organisations/associations; Article 23 assigns control to the tax authority. Local PDF/text retained.
- [Actual 122/25 amendment](https://www.sluzbenilist.me/propisi/385768/download): downloaded/read, especially Articles 9–10. New Articles 48b–48d distinguish pending procedures and continued rights; 48e repeals enumerated provisions including old Article 21(5) from application. Article 10 ties application to commencement of unified assessment. Local `/private/tmp/oa-me-disability-enacted-2025.pdf` and `.txt`.
- [Actual 92/26 deferral](https://www.sluzbenilist.me/propisi/395547/download): downloaded/read both operative articles. Article 1 substitutes 1 December 2026 for 1 July in unified-assessment Article 60(1); Article 2 takes effect on publication. Thus the October examples do not prematurely apply the December status/repeal gate. Local `/private/tmp/oa-me-disability-deferral-2026.pdf` and `.txt`. Recheck before December/later periods; no representation that future amendments have been cleared.
- [Agency explanation](https://www.zzzcg.me/programi/zaposljavanje-lica-sa-invaliditetom/): read as administrative context. Its loose 10–20 wording does not replace the statute's strict more-than-10 / fewer-than-20 population.

The law and agency text retrieved do not explicitly resolve gross versus net. No actual 2026 nominal disability levy is certified.

## Result, assumptions and limits

The Guide now distinguishes Chamber employee payroll versus entrepreneur annual income, an actual artisan-registration condition, employer funding versus employee deductions, headcount quota versus shortfall levy, and the December application gate. The last two use an expressly hypothetical established reference of 1,000; they are not the published national 2026 amounts. Existing six controls exclude these extra levies explicitly.
