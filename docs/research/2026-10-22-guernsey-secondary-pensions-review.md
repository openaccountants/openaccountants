# Guernsey secondary pensions: writer verification

Owner Michael rechecked on issue #397, 22 October 2026. Source URLs and hashes retained. This is not independent or professional review.

Read employer eligibility/phase table, contribution page, non-compulsory employer duties, opt-out guidance, overview quarterly reporting, and 2026 social-insurance limits. Contributions are separate from SI; 2026 minimum is 1% employer and 1.5% employee, with provider payments and Revenue Service reporting separated. Do not restart the phased rate on each employer's first year.

Eligibility wording is more than the annual lower limit, not an earnings deduction. Low earnings after enrolment has its own status/contract guidance, so the draft avoids an automatic stop/refund rule. Voluntary employees require the employee minimum but no statutory employer share. Employer can cover the combined minimum; cannot shift its share to employee.

Direct statutory access/reconciliation remains outstanding. The guidance's generic seven-day notice instruction sits alongside up-to-three-month deferral; the draft does not universalise seven days before detailed SP1/form review. Exact age boundaries, new employers, absence/status changes, specialist schemes and penalty schedules remain gaps. Two arithmetic examples cover ordinary and upper-capped monthly minimums. No completion or attestation claim.
