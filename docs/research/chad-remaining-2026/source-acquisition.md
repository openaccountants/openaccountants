## Explicit current-law holds and editorial defects

1. **Corporate below50m regime**: CGI37 excludes legal persons from IGL, while38RSI begins50m. Do not assign IGL to a company or claim a settled lowest-band regime without supplementary official clarification.
2. **50m–100m VAT**:229II’s100m threshold versus229III/IV RSI wording needs scope care. New229V self-liquidation is real2026 text; do not turn it into a universal rate/registration simplification.
3. **Quarterly versus monthly VAT**:38V says RSI quarterly;229III specifically says monthly without regime distinction and is reproduced in2026LF30. Proposed ordinary VAT workflow uses explicit specific229III/887 monthly rule and flags contradiction internally. This is an interpretation to review, not a claim of immaculate consolidation.
4. **Foreign-service WHT**:26XXIV says25%, immediately followed by substantively identical26XXV18%;85718% and85812.5% provide defined routes. Need underlying amendment or official application clarification before publishing a universal rate for those26fees. CEMAC26XXXIX7.5% and treaty exceptions add another route; no treaty entitlement inferred.
5. **Minimum-tax floor**:152IV references a151floor, but151 contains only2% and no amount. No floor figure established.
6. **Minimum payment timing**:151 monthly basis,152IV monthly wording,842I explicit monthly real/quarterlyRSI. Retain specific regime distinction and record conflict.
7. **Advances**:843 refers to an arrêtéart2 without identifying it; exact previous balance definition/reconciliation remains to acquire before describing anything beyond quoted instalment structure.
8. **IS penalties**:1041(1.5%),1044(2%),996(5%) coexist for different events;1060 statutory fine starts15May despite1008four-month return period. Do not improvise a universal combined formula or invented grace period.
9. **VAT cement**:238new provides range via Community texts, no actual percentage. An official implementing instrument is needed before quoting it.
10. **VAT exemptions/reduced rate overlap**:milk/meat appear in new23015 and2389% list. Different transactions/classification must be resolved. Old heading/product existence is not enough to assign rate.
11. **Asset depreciation maxima**:26requires ministerial order. Do not publish guessed20%/10% categories. Ordinary useful-life/start/pro-rata rules supported, maximum table unresolved.
12. **No claim of comprehensive2026amendment search**:this establishes official2025baseline plus read2026LFprovisions. It does not prove no later2026corrective/implementing law exists. Fresh reviewer should check relevant currently changed claims and the still-open gaps.
