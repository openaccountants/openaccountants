### Executable method and meaningful cases for the writer

1. Reconcile gross pay and genuine reimbursement evidence for each worker, each paid month.
2. For an ordinary full month, use `base = min(max(contributable_pay, 60000), 500000)` under CE. Partial months, multiple employers and any later SMIG changes require their own evidence; do not infer prorating.
3. Compute `employer = base * 0.165`; `employee = base * 0.035`; `remittance = employer + employee`. The old monthly maximum amounts follow arithmetically: CFA 82,500 employer and CFA 17,500 employee.
4. Sum workers for the applicable monthly/quarterly return determined by actual headcount. Pay by the 20th after period end. For a verified delay, `late_charge = remittance * days_late / 1000`.
5. At year end reconcile annual remuneration to the CFA 6m annual ceiling, subtract amounts already declared and preserve the reconciliation. CE does not provide partial-year/multiple-employer allocation detail, so those cases remain held.

Case A, floor versus ordinary pay: full-month contributable pay CFA 40,000 has base 60,000 and shares 9,900/2,100; contributable pay CFA 200,000 has shares 33,000/7,000. This is the floor decision, not a second flat-rate multiplication.

Case B, cap and delay: contributable pay CFA 800,000 has base 500,000 and shares 82,500/17,500; remittance 100,000. Ten days late adds 1,000, making 101,000. For nine employees Q1 is quarterly; for twenty employees January is monthly. These dates are period-selection examples, not invented current calendar concessions.

Recomputed directly: `60000*.165=9900`, `60000*.035=2100`, `200000*.165=33000`, `200000*.035=7000`, `500000*.165=82500`, `500000*.035=17500`, `100000*10/1000=1000`.

### Formation/reporting cases, recomputed

- CFA 1m cash-only SARL capital under art311-1: subscribe1m, pay at least500k initially, balance500k within2 years. A CFA10m cash-only SA under arts387–389 pays at least2.5m initially, balance7.5m within3 years. Keep capital commitments separate from ANIE fees and taxes.
- Audit boundary: SARL assets exactly125m, turnover260m, staff50 exceeds one threshold, so art376's two-threshold route does not apply. Assets126m, turnover260m, staff50 exceeds two and requires auditor. This does not exclude minority-requested court appointment or a special regulated-entity requirement.
- SARL net profit500k, prior losses100k, capital1m, opening legal reserve190k: eligible profit400k; ordinary10% allocation40k, remaining target10k; mandatory allocation10k reaches200k reserve. This is a mechanical minimum; statutes can reserve more and distribution must also satisfy arts143–146.
- December31 close: ordinary AGM no later thanJune30, subject valid court extension where available. An AGM onJune15 implies RCCM filing byJuly15 and an auditor delivery date at least45 days earlier. Do not substitute these corporate-law dates for tax-return deadlines.

## Finance-law cautions already conveyed to the writer

- LF art5 deletes the disability-salary exemption from CGI art3, but LF art8 adds it as CGI art45(18), referring to the Labour Code/OIT definition. It was **moved**, not abolished. Read both adjacent amendments together.
- LF art7's old CGI26 spans several pages before the new block. The new block changes bank provisions only and says the rest is unchanged. Distinguish unchanged baseline text from new language; old text also contains two apparently overlapping25%/18% foreign-service withholding paragraphs that need reconciliation before computation.
- LF art3/new CGI1 labour scale is complete in pp3–4; capital15% except rental IRPP10%. LF art15/new CGI119 gives rental prepayment10% natural persons/15% legal persons; equipment-rental wording remains15%. Do not conflate recipient categories or property rent with equipment rent.
- LF art32/new CGI238 gives standard VAT17.5%, reduced9% and0% categories. Check all neighbours and exemptions before applying a rate; don't import the old18% standard from a legacy secondary-source draft.
