## Primary evidence read

- [VAT Act 50/1988](https://www.althingi.is/lagas/nuna/1988050.html), current 1 October 2026 compilation: Articles 5B, 14, 34(4)–(5) and 35. The scheme is an optional simplified registration for specified foreign B2C supplies, distinct from the obligation to account for VAT and the purchaser-liability route. Article 14 includes qualifying electronic books at 11%; electronic delivery alone does not establish 24%.
- [Current Icelandic registration guidance](https://www.skatturinn.is/atvinnurekstur/virdisaukaskattur/skraning-a-vsk-skra), operative VOES application/restriction instructions, and [registration booklet section 4.3.6](https://www.skatturinn.is/media/baeklingar/VSK_Skraning_a_vsk_skra.pdf), fully read for that section. - [Regulation 1243/2019](https://files.reglugerd.is/pdf/1243-2019/current), all operative Articles 1–13, current reproduction effective 9 September 2022. Article 2 extends the separate refund route to Article 5B registrants and the specified nonregistrable-establishment case; Article 3 still requires all use/registration/vendor/input-entitlement conditions and excludes Iceland resale/final consumption, including its express foreign travel-operator example. ## Boundaries and source discrepancies

The current regulation and Icelandic guidance use the fifteenth of the following month for normal-cycle applications, late processing in the next cycle and an ultimate six years from the right arising. The English page instead says at least fifteen days after the period and six years after calendar-year end. Its broad no-Iceland-sales wording also omits the regulation's express simplified-registration extension. The addition states these discrepancies rather than silently harmonising them or certifying a contested operational result.

Article 8's 75,000/15,000 are December 2019 CPI 473.3 base amounts, indexed at each year's start. The English page's 101,000/20,200 are expressly 2025 amounts. The Icelandic page prints base values. The initial preparation at `e9424ac8` had not found a confirmed 2026 minimum. A follow-up search found [Skatturinn letter 1/2026](https://www.skatturinn.is/fagadilar/akvardandi-bref/obeinir-skattar/akvardandi-bref-1-2026), dated 20 January, effective 1 January: 105,500 for Article 8(1) two-month claims and 21,100 for Article 8(2) year/remainder claims. The current method and control now use those expressly published figures, closing the missing-current-minimum gap without inferring index rounding. No taxpayer refund entitlement follows from the numeric test alone.

The same letter expressly gives the Regulation 576/1989 Article 5(1) repair threshold as 3,457,000 for 2026. Reopened [the complete two-page regulation](https://files.reglugerd.is/pdf/0576-1989/current): Article 5 requires annual total cost/taxable value at least that indexed amount for the relevant Article 2/4 repair/maintenance branch; it is not an invoice allowance or VAT amount. The actual Article 6 market/calculated value remains separately supported. The authority letter's full operative table was read; its linked Word memorandum was unsupported by the web reader and was not used as read evidence.

The two new controls cover mixed-rate output without any VOES input offset, and disjoint exclusions from a separate refund register, now compared with the confirmed 2026 period minimums. Their inputs assume supported classification/use; they do not establish a taxpayer's registration, claim period, dates, FX, actual invoices or authority decision. These increase the country total from 64 to 66 controls. The firm Iceland source card was updated before drafting.
