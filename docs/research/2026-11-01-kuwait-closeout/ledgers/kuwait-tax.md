## Worked-case recomputation and non-legal instructions

The example inputs are stipulated facts, not legal thresholds. These exact cases are executed by `scripts/check-examples.py --require` and re-read by the fresh reviewer:

**Example 1.** A foreign branch outside DMTT with confirmed ordinary income-tax liability and no exemption or losses: KWD 100,000 at 15% gives KWD 15,000, filed by the 15th of the 4th month.

```oa-check
name: Foreign branch
inputs:
  income: 100000
steps:
  - tax: income * 0.15
expected:
  tax: 15000.00
```

**Example 2 (decision).** A payer within reg. art. 37 pays KWD 50,000 to a foreign contractor outside DMTT with an established ordinary-tax retention obligation. Without an MOF release, retain KWD 2,500 and pay KWD 47,500. With an actual MOF release of this payment, retention is zero and cash payment KWD 50,000. The release document decides the branch; failure to retain/remit can make the payer liable for the tax (arts. 38 and 39).

```oa-check
name: Retention
inputs:
  payment: 50000
steps:
  - held: payment * 0.05
  - paid: payment - held
  - held_with_mof_release: payment * 0
  - paid_with_mof_release: payment - held_with_mof_release
expected:
  held: 2500.00
  paid: 47500.00
  held_with_mof_release: 0.00
  paid_with_mof_release: 50000.00
```

Intake questions collect facts, not legal answers. ## Non-operative source holds

NON-OPERATIVE HOLDS. Historical no-VAT, no reverse charge, no input recovery, foreign VAT irrecoverability and no-VAT bank exclusions (see VAT Guide); customs 5% of CIF, tobacco exceptions; PIFSS 10.5% employee and 11.5% employer and all coverage details (see payroll Guide). Mixed ownership: how the 15% applies to a company part owned by Kuwaiti and part by foreign holders, and the Kuwaiti and Gulf exemption itself, including the earlier statements here that Gulf nationals are foreign and also exempt (the texts read define the taxpayer as any corporate body; Ministerial Decision 3 of 1989 on equal treatment of Gulf nationals is named in the NLST rule but the copy on the MOF site is a different document); KFAS 1% contribution; the 2.5% and 1% interaction with Free Trade Zone exemptions under Law 26 of 1995; the extension of the FY 2026/2027 DMTT retention carve-out and five-year transfer direction to private payers; DMTT Executive Regulations detail (Ministerial Resolution 55 of 2025, not read beyond the title page); any business profits tax draft, for which no enacted text was found; the old cap of 50% on the late filing fine, which no text read supports. Earlier worked cases that taxed the foreign share of a mixed company (70/30 and 60/40 at KWD 200,000) and test 2 (100% Kuwaiti, unlisted, no tax) are held here until that point is confirmed.
