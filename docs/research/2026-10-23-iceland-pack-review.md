## Findings for the writer

1. **P2: payroll personal-credit scope excludes eligible temporary workers.** `skills/international/iceland/iceland-payroll.md:43` says only people domiciled in Iceland are entitled. This contradicts its later temporary-worker discussion. [Skatturinn posted-worker guidance](https://www.skatturinn.is/english/individuals/posted-workers/) expressly provides day-based credit for temporary work; [limited-liability guidance](https://www.skatturinn.is/english/individuals/limited-tax-liability/) supplies the arrival-day rule. Remove the exclusive wording and cross-reference the applicable day/status determination. Do not imply every foreign income category qualifies.

2. **P2: the payroll referral uses six months as a definitive residence test.** `skills/international/iceland/iceland-payroll.md:325` says temporary work below six months creates limited liability. It omits domicile and uses a different boundary from the pack's individual-income Guide. [Income Tax Act Article 1](https://www.althingi.is/lagas/nuna/2003090.html) separately tests domicile and more than 183 days in a rolling twelve months, including normal holiday absences. Replace the parenthesis with a reference to the pack's residence decision. Being below the stay threshold alone does not displace the domicile test.

3. **P2: foreign-expert referral understates the statutory eligibility branches.** `skills/international/iceland/iceland-income-tax.md:375` requires an Iceland-based employer and no residence in the previous 60 months. [Article 30(A)(6)](https://www.althingi.is/lagas/nuna/2003090.html) also permits a qualifying legal entity with an Icelandic permanent establishment and excludes the first three months of Icelandic stay when applying the preceding continuous 60-month test. Preserve both qualifications in the referral; a reader should not reject a potentially eligible person using the shortened checklist.

4. **P2: mixed-input VAT needs an Article 10 scope gate.** `skills/international/iceland/iceland-vat.md:97` introduces the turnover-ratio method without excluding the income-tax-exempt bodies subject to [Regulation 192/1993 Article 10](https://files.reglugerd.is/pdf/0192-1993/current). That provision overrides the chapter's general method and generally permits only inputs exclusively connected to taxable sales; its public-service-enterprise exception has separate conditions. Add the entity-status screen before the ratio workflow and refer those cases to the applicable special method. Otherwise a registered exempt body could claim shared-cost VAT using the ordinary example.

5. **P2: simplified business-return eligibility omits the wage-payment condition.** In reviewed snapshot `355b3194`, `skills/international/iceland/iceland-income-tax.md:111` describes RSK 4.10 eligibility without checking wages paid to others. [Skatturinn startup guidance, RSK 4.10 conditions](https://www.skatturinn.is/atvinnurekstur/ad-hefja-rekstur/stofna-rekstur/) expressly includes no wages paid to others. Its [dedicated filing summary](https://www.skatturinn.is/atvinnurekstur/ad-hefja-rekstur/framtalsskil/) omits that condition rather than affirmatively allowing employees. Add the wage-payment check or explicitly hold those cases for confirmation using current instructions. The existing no-assets example does not test this branch. This is an additional finding after the writer began fixing findings 1–4.
