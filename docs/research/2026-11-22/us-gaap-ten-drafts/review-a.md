## Findings and precise repairs

1. **Wrong ASU 2025-12 issue locator in ASC 740 — corrected by coordinating agent during review.** The original body's exception boundary and Sources row said Issue 14. The original FASB PDF identifies the intraperiod-allocation repair as **Issue 12**, printed pp43–44, amending 740-10-55-38(b)(1). Issue 14 concerns equity-security references. The body and source-table locator were changed to Issue 12. This was a citation defect, not a wrong 2026 effective boundary. The writer ledger must use the corrected issue and final slug/hash too.
2. **Software cessation condition needed an explicit operational branch.** ASU 2025-06, 350-40-25-13 requires cessation if capitalization requirements cease to be met, and impairment assessment of existing balances. Readiness under -25-14 is a latest cessation point, not the only one. Precise addition supplied: “If the capitalization requirements later cease to be met, stop capitalizing further costs and assess existing balances under the impairment guidance; readiness is the latest cessation point, not the only one.” This adds a necessary adjacent condition without supplying the separately held full impairment method.
3. **Hosting amortization sentence compressed material term/start/method conditions.** ASU 2018-15, 350-40-35-13 through -35-17 supports straight-line unless another systematic/rational method better represents benefit; noncancellable term plus specified reasonably-certain customer option periods and vendor-controlled extension/nontermination periods; periodic term reassessment; readiness after substantial testing by module, including fully dependent modules. These must accompany the existing “over the hosting term” statement if it is to function as an executable draft instruction. The original presentation conclusions in -45-1 through -45-3 were correct.
4. **Induced conversion lookback/formula conditions needed specificity.** ASU 2024-04, 470-20-40-13A(a)–(c): use acceptance-date share fair value when either settlement formula uses future share prices; non-share-price indexation changes settlement form; a non-substantial exchange/modification within the preceding one year requires comparison against terms existing one year before acceptance. “Assess relevant prior-year modifications” alone was not a mechanical statement of that rule. Precise text was supplied to the coordinating agent. The limited offer/preserved consideration/substantive feature conditions and excess fair-value expense statement were already supported.
5. **Circular 230 wording should say written advice.** The original “any US federal tax advice” description was broader than §10.37's scope. Use “Any written advice on a US federal tax matter requires the fact-specific analysis in Circular 230 §10.37,” with its official URL/read scope if retained. This is a referral boundary, not a tax calculation in the Guide.
6. **Ledger locators required repair.** Convertible ledger R04 used the generic ASU boilerplate “An Accounting Standards Update is not authoritative” as its short fragment for adoption dates; that does not hold the dates. Replace with short exact effective-date fragments from the two transition summaries and appropriate paragraph/page locators. Software R05 included an arbitrary cross-reference fragment; use a short actual recognition/uncertainty fragment. Some ledgers still bore initial hashes during the review because the parent was making authorized narrow edits. Rebinding all final hashes and renamed paths is required; no full source re-review is needed for unchanged bodies.

The ASC 740 rename to `us-gaap-asc740-income-provision` is faithful to its accounting-provision scope. Its 25% is explicitly hypothetical, no operative jurisdiction tax rate/filing position is supplied, and actual tax law/measurement conclusions are held. This is financial reporting; the rename does not turn a tax-method claim into an accounting claim. Adding `last_updated: 2026-11-22` and replacing title separators do not alter the reviewed source conclusions or the writer's actual source-read dates.
