# Gibraltar non-resident pension relief: writer verification

Checked 22 October 2026 for the draft 2026/27 individual assessment Guide. This is writer verification, not independent review or professional attestation.

Source: [Allowances Rules, 23 March 2026 consolidation](https://www.gibraltarlaws.gov.gi/uploads/legislations/income-tax/1992s016/1992s016(23-03-26).pdf), printed pages 39–40, rules 24 and 29. Official PDF retrieved and relevant passages read again this turn.

- Rule 24(1): non-resident individual receiving Gibraltar-accruing pension; rules 6–21 relief as if resident throughout year, subject to other provisions. Not a universal non-resident allowance entitlement.
- Rule 24(2): lower limit is hypothetical worldwide-income tax multiplied by Gibraltar-chargeable income / total worldwide income. Guide defines inputs and does not infer hypothetical tax from a foreign assessment.
- Rule 24(3): leave foreign-tax relief out of both actual and hypothetical tax for this comparison. Separate credit analysis remains necessary.
- Rule 29: prescribed claim and supporting proof required; no assumed automatic award or invented filing channel.
- Arithmetic: 10,000 × 20,000 / 50,000 = 4,000. A 3,000 otherwise-relieved liability needs a 1,000 restriction; 5,000 needs zero. Inputs are illustrative assumptions, not a full assessment. Zero denominator expressly excluded.

Still open: individual relief qualification, hypothetical assessment under the relevant system, exemption/treaty interaction, exact claim form/channel and independent source/professional review. Does not establish comprehensive individual-tax coverage.
