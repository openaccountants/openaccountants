# Haiti first topic batch

Three source-cited drafts: payroll, personal income and business patente. This is not comprehensive country coverage. No independent review, accountant attestation or live-system publication.

## Evidence and corrections

The ministry's official enactment was downloaded in full and text-extracted. Pages 12, 13, 37, 45, 49 and 169 were rendered and read visually. The DGI document page independently identifies the replacement decree and its 19 October posting. Article 540 explicitly starts the new Code on 1 October 2026.

Older DGI pages conflict with the new enactment: TMS remains described as 2% on the webpage versus 3% in article 152; the patente page mixes old formulas and has different commune memberships. The Guides use the enactment. The new personal bands are not applied retrospectively to the 2025–2026 return. A news report of a DGI transition notice was found, but its primary notice was not retrieved and it is not used to establish an old-year schedule.

Article 152 specifies monthly filing/payment between days 1 and 15 without expressly stating the preceding-month linkage that article 100 supplies. This distinction is retained; implementation and first-period instructions need checking. Article 103 allows occupational circulars to revise the 15% supplemental-pay rate. No universal assurance that no circular exists is made.

Decimal assertions checked band boundaries 360,000 and 540,000, income examples 600,000 and 1,200,000, annual-to-monthly withholding, 3% employer tax, 15%/10% supplemental payments, patente base rounding plus fixed charge, and rent/donation examples.
