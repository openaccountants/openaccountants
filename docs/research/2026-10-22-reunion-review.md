# Réunion producer octroi research review

One initial Guide, not comprehensive country coverage. No accountant attestation or independent review.

The official July 2025 circular was downloaded and read, including its law annex. An older customs FAQ still uses €300,000 in a formalities paragraph, whereas the circular and retrieved 2026 article 2 use €550,000 inclusive. The Guide follows the latter. The circular explicitly distinguishes production turnover from total turnover and prorates the threshold for new businesses. Its wording “before 31 January” is preserved rather than silently changed to “by 31 January”.

The 2026 law changes the agricultural definition and regional-rate administrative framework. The current-date Légifrance open and direct HTML retrieval failed (403); search retrieval returned the amended article text. The PDF's annex is therefore not represented as a fully current consolidated law. VAT recodification, later amendments, tariffs and current administrative channels remain review gaps. No product rate or exemption is asserted.

Threshold arithmetic checked: €550,000 × 6/12 = €275,000; €280,000 exceeds it; exactly €550,000 qualifies; €300,000 production does not qualify solely because €400,000 separate services increases total revenue.

VAT and personal income drafts are not included: fetched source verification remains incomplete. No production data write or publication to the live service was performed.
