## Findings

- ABST: Act 13/2023 replaces the old 14%/15% section 8 rates with 17%. Act 1/2024 replaces the return deadline with 15 calendar days after period-end, from the period ending 31 March 2024. The older FAQ's rates and month-end wording are not used as current law. Payment rules and special reductions remain separate verification work.
- Social security: SI 60/2016 explicitly caps the staged increases at 15% public and 16% private from 2025. Read the amendment with SI 15/2013 rather than copying its superseded rates. The 2016 schedule sets EC$1,500 weekly/EC$6,500 monthly ceilings. Original scanned pages were visually inspected. The principal regulation establishes gross-earnings components, timing and fourteen-day remittance.
