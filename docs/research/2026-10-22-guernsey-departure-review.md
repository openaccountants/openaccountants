# Guernsey departure filing scope

22 October 2026. Source: Leaving Guernsey, Alderney or Herm, RS-FRM-LVG-348-v1 (6 pages), linked from the official penalties FAQ, id 101103. Added a filing-route section distinguishing permanent earnings/bank-interest cases from situations requiring the ordinary return as well. The form collects other income but page 1 explicitly requires the annual return in the stated exclusions; collecting a figure is not a dispensation. UK pension destination wording is not treated as a blanket filing exemption. Two classification examples cover a simple replacement route and a retained rental property. No departure-year tax, treaty result or statutory residence is inferred.

Statutory part-year computation, filing timing specific to departure and full residency/relief reconciliation remain open. No professional attestation or country completion claimed.
