## Findings that change the drafting approach

- Original Act 16/2023 section 1 commences on 2 August 2023. The retrieved Act contains no general two-year sunset. Do not infer expiry from introductory news or assume a later order must extend it.
- Act 21/2023 sections 3–5 change consideration, registered-person/recipient definitions, the goods base and payment scope. Its goods base is customs value under customs legislation, replacing the original CIF wording. Consideration excludes the levy, VAT, stamp duty and other duties/levies, allows discounts/rebates, and uses fair market value for related persons. Original text must not be copied without this amendment.
- Revised sections 8–10 cover resident-to-resident, nonresident-to-resident and overseas-business-to-local-branch services, subject to statutory definitions and exemptions. The 2.5% service rate applies to consideration, not automatically every invoice; section 2 defines services by Schedule 2. Section 10 includes registered recipients and requires levy before VAT. Section 12 imports VAT administration, but a specific return/payment deadline has not yet been verified.
- Sections 3–5 separately cover imported scheduled goods, payable to Customs. Section 15 refers exemptions to Schedule 3; section 16 permits rate orders.
- SI 95/2023 changes all three schedules. In particular, it removes sale of motor vehicles and two financial-service categories from Schedule 2. The original Schedule 2 alone is therefore unsafe.
- The AG revised-law index identifies SI 103/2023 as effective 14 August 2023. Its linked SI 100/2023 parliamentary resolution describes additional motor-repair, fuel and exemption changes. A resolution approving a draft is not a substitute for retrieving the operative order. SI 103 remains to retrieve and compare.
- SI 106/2023, revised-law section-2-01, gives services a historic zero rate from 2 August through 1 October 2023. Do not retroactively apply 2.5% to that whole launch interval. That HTML page was read but is not yet in the hash manifest.
- SI 108/2024 is a project-specific zero-rate order for supplies by consultants, contractors, subcontractors, suppliers and utility companies to Rodney Bay City Center Ltd and its contractors for the named approved project. It is not a general construction exemption. Full one-page PDF text read.
- SI 165/2025 amends timber-related tariff codes in Schedule 3 Part 1 item 2. It is not a duration extension. Full two-page PDF text read. SI 160/2025 is the preceding parliamentary-resolution document; captured, substantive comparison still pending.
