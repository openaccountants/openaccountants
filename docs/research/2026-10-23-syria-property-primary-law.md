# Syria property: primary-law recovery and reconciliation

Research checkpoint on 23 October 2026. No property Guide or accepted property coverage yet.

## Recovered original law

The historical presidency channel links [SANA's original Law 15/2021 text](https://www.sana.sy/?p=1347631), which resolves successfully to [the official archive](https://archive.sana.sy/?p=1347631). The entire law, articles 1–21, was retrieved and read. Do not confuse it with unrelated Law 15/2024 on consumption tax. Article 21 sets commencement at 3 May 2021; article 17 excludes property sales with an established date before commencement.

Drafting locators:

- Articles 1–4: ministry-adopted market value, financial classification and valuation committees; property-specific adjustment capped at 25% either way.
- Article 5: residential 1%; land inside approved planning boundary 2%; outside 1%; non-residential property 3%; residential rooftops 1%.
- Article 6: bare ownership/usufruct 40%/60%; commercial ownership/leasehold right (farough) 10%/90% of adopted value. Do not call these tax rates.
- Article 7: qualifying ascendant/descendant/spouse gifts use 15% **of the article 5 rate**, not a 15% property-value tax; other gifts treated as sales. Wills follow gift treatment except the obligatory bequest.
- Article 8: listed additions included, but local-administration addition excluded. A headline article 5 rate is not necessarily total payable.
- Article 9: written declaration within 30 days of sale/gift event; payment within 30 days of declaration. Non-declaration penalty 10% of tax; delayed payment cross-refers to Decree 146/1968, not an invented flat penalty.
- Article 10: enumerated exemptions and preserved statutory charity exemptions; articles 14–15 exclude economic public entities and licensed development-project company sales from this regime, leaving income-tax law applicable.
- Articles 11–12: objection within 30 days after notice of collection order; full prior tax/additions/supplements payment and 1% security; classification/value procedures and bank-account refund mechanics. Committee consideration period does not mean automatic acceptance.
- Article 13: financial clearance before registration; court payment document is expressly not final execution clearance. Court dismissal/striking-off has a separate reimbursement route.
- Article 16: historical residential/non-residential rental rules, minimums and filing. Do not publish as current until proposed/actual rental changes reconciled.
- Articles 19–21: repeals, executive-instruction power and commencement.

These are original-law findings, not proof of an unchanged 2026 consolidated regime.

## Later official evidence

[25 May 2025 cancellation decision reported by SANA](https://sana.sy/locals/2223654/): reported relief requires mutually abandoned sale, no completed property registration, and written declaration by seller/buyer. Paid tax is returned through the proper procedure. This is not a general refund for a completed transfer. Recover signed decision and implementation details.

[30 July 2025 ministry circular reported by SANA](https://sana.sy/economy/2256905/), updated 4 August: clearance resumes 3 August. Report identifies actual-price sale contract/stamp duty, removal of compulsory 50% bank deposit, a 30% reduction to previously adopted residential market values and contract-value fallback where adopted values do not exist. It also describes a three-month no-objection document replacing security approval. Do not automatically apply a residential reduction to a commercial property, or promise instantaneous processing from a historical announcement. Obtain Circular 135/2025 and follow-up clarifications before operational guidance.

[9 April 2026 ministry/authority meeting](https://sana.sy/economy/2446227/) discusses changes still to be issued, including property sales. It is not enactment evidence.

[21 May 2026 Justice Ministry restrictions in Suwayda](https://sana.sy/locals/2481883/) reports a temporary geographically scoped suspension. Full decision, scope and subsequent lifting/amendment must be checked before generalising national registration procedures to that governorate.
