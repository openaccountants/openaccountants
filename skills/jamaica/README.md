# Jamaica: tax pack

The year of assessment is the calendar year. Tax Administration Jamaica (TAJ) collects income tax, General Consumption Tax (GCT, the VAT) and the payroll contributions (NIS, NHT, Education Tax, HEART). Amounts are Jamaican dollars (J$). This map covers 2026.
