# Jamaica Corporate Income Tax

## Corporate income tax rates and base

- **Corporate tax classification and residence basis** — Jamaica applies different corporate rates depending on whether a company is 'regulated' (e.g. financial institutions) or 'unregulated'. Companies are taxed on worldwide income if resident.
- **Unregulated company rate** — 25% %  _(Income Tax Act (https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/jamaica/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income))_
- **Regulated company rate** — 33 1/3% %  _(Income Tax Act (https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/jamaica/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income))_
- **Building society rate** — 30% %  _(Income Tax Act (https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/jamaica/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income))_
- **Life assurance company rate** — 25% %  _(Income Tax Act (https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/jamaica/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income))_
- **Renewable energy IPP rate** — 25% for independent power producers with 75%+ renewable generation %  _(Income Tax Act (https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/jamaica/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income))_
- **Tax base** — Worldwide income for tax-resident companies; Jamaica-source income for non-residents  _(Income Tax Act (https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/jamaica/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income))_
- **WHT on dividends to non-residents** — 15% (reduced from prior 25% / 33 1/3%) %  _(Income Tax Act (https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/jamaica/corporate/withholding-taxes))_
- **WHT on dividends to residents** — 15% %  _(Income Tax Act (https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/jamaica/corporate/withholding-taxes))_
- **WHT on interest to non-residents** — 15% (non-treaty) %  _(Income Tax Act (https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/jamaica/corporate/withholding-taxes))_
- **WHT on royalties to non-residents** — 33 1/3% (non-treaty) %  _(Income Tax Act (https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/jamaica/corporate/withholding-taxes))_
- **WHT on management fees to non-residents** — 33 1/3% (non-treaty) %  _(Income Tax Act (https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/jamaica/corporate/withholding-taxes))_
- **WHT remittance deadline** — Within 14 days of the end of the month in which payment is made  _(Income Tax Act (https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/jamaica/corporate/withholding-taxes))_
- **Corporate income tax return deadline** — Annual return (Form IT02) due 15 March of the following year  _(Income Tax Act (https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/jamaica/corporate/tax-administration))_
- **Corporate estimated tax** — Quarterly instalments (Form IT07) due 15 March, 15 June, 15 September, 15 December  _(Income Tax Act (https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/jamaica/individual/tax-administration))_
- **Asset tax (financial institutions)** — 0.25% on taxable assets of deposit-taking institutions, securities dealers and insurers %  _(Assets Tax (Specified Bodies) Act (https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/jamaica/corporate/other-taxes))_
