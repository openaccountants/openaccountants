# Morocco cross-border tax v0.1

> **General reference only.** This skill is general tax/accounting reference material for AI-assisted workflows. It has not been reviewed for any specific person's facts, documents, elections, deadlines, residency, filing position or professional obligations. Verify figures against the cited primary sources or with a licensed professional before relying on them.

## Who this applies to

Moroccan businesses paying non-residents, non-resident companies with Moroccan income, and Moroccan companies or individuals with foreign tax to credit. Companies are taxed on income from property held, activity carried on and profit operations done in Morocco, even occasional, and on income a treaty gives Morocco to tax, wherever the seat is (Art. 5-I).

## The base

**Art. 15 list.** Withholding applies to amounts paid, made available or credited to non-residents for: copyright royalties, including film and television (I); patent, design, plan, formula, secret process and trademark licences (II); scientific, technical or other information and studies, in or outside Morocco (III); technical assistance and personnel provided to businesses in Morocco (IV); artistic or sporting activities (V); rental of any equipment (VI); interest and other fixed-income investments, except those in Art. 6-I-C-3 and Art. 45 (VII); road transport from Morocco abroad, for the part of the price for the trip in Morocco (VIII); commissions and fees (IX); and services of any kind used in Morocco or supplied by non-residents (X).

**Exempt from the list.** For non-resident companies, interest on loans to or guaranteed by the State, on foreign-currency or convertible dirham deposits, on foreign-currency loans of 10 years or more, and on EIB foreign-currency loans for government-approved projects (Art. 6-I-C-3); aircraft charter, rental and maintenance for international transport (Art. 6-I-C-4). Non-resident individuals have their own interest list in Art. 45.

**Who withholds.** Any taxpayer paying or involved in paying (Art. 160-I). If one non-resident is paid by another, the client in Morocco owes the tax (Art. 160-II).

**Branches.** Art. 5-II does not apply where a Moroccan branch does the work without the foreign head office intervening; that income falls in the branch result, taxed like a Moroccan company. Work the head office does for its own branch is caught by the withholding. Branch profit distributed is taxed under Art. 13-IV at the dividend rate above.

**Art. 16 flat tax.** The option is made with the Art. 148-III declaration or after each contract is signed. For a turnkey contract the base includes materials and equipment, whoever supplies them, however billed. The company pays within the month after each receipt (Art. 170-VI). Payment at 8% discharges the Art. 4 withholding, which includes the tax on distributed branch profit (Art. 19-III-A, 4-I, 13-IV).

**Non-resident capital gains.** Non-resident companies are exempt on gains on securities listed on the Casablanca exchange, except shares in real-estate-rich companies under Art. 61-II (Art. 6-I-A-10). A non-resident company with no Moroccan establishment files the Art. 20-III return for gains on real estate and securities realised in Morocco within 30 days after the month of sale, and pays with it (Art. 20-III, 170-VIII).

**Foreign tax credit.** Companies credit foreign tax on foreign income taxed in a treaty country, if paid and proved, up to the share of Moroccan tax on that income (Art. 19 bis). Individuals have the same limit under Art. 77. A treaty tax-sparing exemption counts as payment, with a certificate from the foreign authority. Without a treaty with the source country, Art. 19 bis and Art. 77 give no credit.

## Penalties and interest

Anyone who has not paid over the sums they are liable for on time owes them personally, whether or not withheld, plus the Art. 208 penalty and surcharges (Art. 198). The figures sit in [company tax](morocco-corporate-income-tax.md).

## Cross-border points

Treaties can reduce these rates, and treaty rates are not in this skill: read the treaty. VAT on services from non-residents is in [VAT](morocco-vat.md). Related party pricing is in [transfer pricing](ma-transfer-pricing.md).

## Two worked examples that recompute

**Example 1: royalty.** A Casablanca company pays a non-treaty licensor a trademark royalty of MAD 1,000,000 excl. VAT. Art. 15-II applies. Withhold MAD 100,000 and pay it in the following month; the licensor gets MAD 900,000.

```oa-check
name: Art. 15 royalty withholding
inputs:
  royalty: 1000000
steps:
  - tax: royalty * 0.10
  - net: royalty - tax
expected:
  tax: 100000.00
  net: 900000.00
```

**Example 2: should a foreign contractor opt for the flat tax?** The contract is MAD 10,000,000 excl. VAT. The flat tax is MAD 800,000 and discharges the withholding on distributed branch profit. If the profit stays in Morocco, normal tax at 20% costs the same on profit of MAD 4,000,000, a 40% margin. If all after-tax profit is distributed in 2026, the normal regime costs 20% plus 11.25% of the remaining 80%, or 29% of profit, and break-even falls to MAD 2,758,620.69, a 27.59% margin. Below the break-even the normal regime is cheaper; above it, the option is. Assume profit under MAD 100,000,000, no other Moroccan income and no treaty; a treaty rate on branch profit moves the second figure.

```oa-check
name: Art. 16 option break-even
inputs:
  contract: 10000000
steps:
  - flat: contract * 0.08
  - breakeven_profit: flat / 0.20
  - margin: breakeven_profit / contract
  - normal_rate_distributed: 0.20 + (1 - 0.20) * 0.1125
  - breakeven_distributed: flat / normal_rate_distributed
  - margin_distributed: breakeven_distributed / contract
expected:
  flat: 800000.00
  breakeven_profit: 4000000.00
  margin: 0.40
  normal_rate_distributed: 0.29
  breakeven_distributed: 2758620.69
  margin_distributed: 0.2759
```

## Ask the client first

1. Is the payer in Morocco, and is the payee resident, and where?
2. What exactly is paid for, so which Art. 15 line?
3. Is there a treaty, and has the payee shown residence?
4. Is there a Moroccan branch, and did the head office intervene?
5. For contractors: contract value, type, expected margin, and how much profit will leave Morocco.
6. Date of the payment or distribution.
