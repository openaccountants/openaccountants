# Burundi: domestic cross-border residence, PE and withholding for 2026 v1.1

> **General reference only.** This Guide is general tax/accounting reference material for AI-assisted workflows. It has not been reviewed for any specific person's facts, documents, elections, deadlines, residency, filing status, or local procedures. Do not rely on it to file, pay, amend, or take a tax position without review by a qualified professional in Burundi.

## Who this applies to

Use for a Burundi payer, a resident with foreign income or a nonresident with Burundi activity. An individual is resident if there is a permanent residence in Burundi or presence exceeds 183 days in a twelve-month period ending in the fiscal year; diplomatic/public-service residence cases require the remaining art. 3 condition, which is not fully legible in the captured scan and is excluded here. An entity is resident if constituted under Burundi law or effectively managed here at any time in the fiscal year. Residents include foreign-source income; nonresidents include Burundi-source income. [IR arts. 3–7, 20 and 100–102](https://www.obr.bi/images/LOI_N1_14_DU_24_DECEMBRE_2020_PORTANT_MODIFICATION_DE_LA_LOI_N1_02_DU_24_JANVIER_2013_RELATIVE_AUX_IMPOTS_SUR_LES_REVENUS_1.pdf).

Source includes employment/activity here, services physically or electronically supplied to a Burundi resident or Burundi PE, local immovables and specified investment/disposal income. Do not infer a foreign source from foreign invoicing or payment alone. [IR arts. 6–7](https://www.obr.bi/images/LOI_N1_14_DU_24_DECEMBRE_2020_PORTANT_MODIFICATION_DE_LA_LOI_N1_02_DU_24_JANVIER_2013_RELATIVE_AUX_IMPOTS_SUR_LES_REVENUS_1.pdf).

## The base

Apply the PE analysis before using final nonresident service withholding. A fixed business installation with permanence can be a PE; construction and service periods above are additional tests. Check the narrowly specified storage/display and preparatory/auxiliary exclusions, habitual contracting or stock-delivery dependent agents, independent agents acting in their ordinary business and the special insurance rule. Ownership/control alone does not create a PE. A PE requires separate books and profit attribution; the [corporate Guide](bi-corporate-income-tax.md) owns that computation. [IR arts. 8–13 and 108–109](https://www.obr.bi/images/LOI_N1_14_DU_24_DECEMBRE_2020_PORTANT_MODIFICATION_DE_LA_LOI_N1_02_DU_24_JANVIER_2013_RELATIVE_AUX_IMPOTS_SUR_LES_REVENUS_1.pdf).

For withholding, identify payer, recipient, payment category, gross payment and PE attribution. Both 2026 budgets replace art. 122 with the categories above; their identical core rates do not authorise withholding on every foreign payment. PE payers also apply the withholding rules. Keep a category schedule and certificates. A buyer's nonresident-asset withholding is not a substitute for the seller's gain-base computation. [Art. 148](https://www.obr.bi/images/PDF/Loi%20de%20Finances%202025-2026%20Modifi%C3%A9e.pdf); [Art. 156](https://www.obr.bi/images/LOI_DE_FINANCES_2026-2027_PROMULGUEE_compressed.pdf); [IR arts. 123–129](https://www.obr.bi/images/LOI_N1_14_DU_24_DECEMBRE_2020_PORTANT_MODIFICATION_DE_LA_LOI_N1_02_DU_24_JANVIER_2013_RELATIVE_AUX_IMPOTS_SUR_LES_REVENUS_1.pdf).

For foreign credit, prove country/tax-authority address, income-tax amount, reason, year and date/mode of payment using an assessment, withholding certificate or similar acceptable evidence, certified by the issuing foreign tax authority. Attach it to the return, or supply the proof within six months after Burundi tax becomes due; otherwise the deducted credit must be repaid with applicable interest/penalties. If not in Kirundi, English or French, provide a certified translation into one of those languages. This is a credit ceiling, not a guaranteed refund of all foreign tax. [IR arts. 14–15](https://www.obr.bi/images/LOI_N1_14_DU_24_DECEMBRE_2020_PORTANT_MODIFICATION_DE_LA_LOI_N1_02_DU_24_JANVIER_2013_RELATIVE_AUX_IMPOTS_SUR_LES_REVENUS_1.pdf).

Keep VAT on imported services separate from income withholding; use [VAT](bi-vat-gst.md) even if the recipient is not VAT-registered. Use [transfer pricing](bi-transfer-pricing.md) for group charges and financing, rather than treating withholding payment as proof of expense deductibility.

## Deadlines and filing

1. Obtain contracts/invoices, actual service location and personnel-day/project records, residence/entity facts, recipient identity and PE attribution. Document any asserted treaty before reducing a domestic amount.
2. In [monthly return section IV](https://www.obr.bi/images/DECLARATION_TVA-_IRE-_PF_ET_AUTRES_RETENUES_A_LA_SOURCE-2021-VF-juillet-2022-vf-1.pdf), map dividends, interest, royalties, nonresident services, specified group/technical fees and vehicle hire to lines 1–6. Enter payment date, taxable payment, rate and tax in columns 2–5. Nonresident asset acquisition belongs to line 9. The historic form's other sector lines do not establish current liability. July art. 156 separately sets 10% gaming charges on deposits, withdrawals, gross profit of gaming companies and promotional prizes; those charges are outside this Guide. Reconcile section IV total line 15 to the payment.
3. File the prescribed withholding return and remit within fifteen calendar days after the payment month. Retain payment-category and tax registers for the ten fiscal years following the withholding year; provide the recipient its evidence as required. The withholding agent is personally liable for a failure, and cannot shift its own penalties/interest to the recipient. [IR arts. 124–129](https://www.obr.bi/images/LOI_N1_14_DU_24_DECEMBRE_2020_PORTANT_MODIFICATION_DE_LA_LOI_N1_02_DU_24_JANVIER_2013_RELATIVE_AUX_IMPOTS_SUR_LES_REVENUS_1.pdf).
4. Use OBR's [category portal links](https://www.obr.bi/index.php/teledeclaration-telepaiement); keep declaration acknowledgment and quittance/bank reference matched to NIF, tax, period and total. Current authenticated completion screens are not established here. Claim a resident foreign credit through the annual income return's foreign-tax line with the certified evidence, respecting the domestic cap; use the [corporate](bi-corporate-income-tax.md) or [individual](bi-income-tax.md) filing route.

## Cross-border points

Treaty relief needs an applicable treaty, its actual entry into force and effective tax period, residence/beneficial-entitlement evidence and anti-abuse conditions. The OBR [treaty catalogue](https://www.obr.bi/index.php/conventions-fiscales) is a locator, not proof every listed agreement governs a 2026 payment.

Burundi's [Law 1/01 of 17 February 2026](https://www.obr.bi/images/LOI_RATIFICATIONMAROC.pdf) ratifies the Morocco convention signed in 2025. The actual convention's [art. 30, PDF p. 21](https://www.obr.bi/images/MAROC._DTA_Sign%C3%A9_par_les_Ministres_%C3%A0_ratifier.pdf) requires exchange of instruments and then applies withholding provisions to amounts paid/credited from 1 January of the following calendar year; other taxes also use following-year periods. The domestic ratification alone does not establish a 2026 reduced withholding rate. No date of exchange or treaty rate is asserted by this Guide. Treaty rates, residence tie-breakers, dependent-services exemptions and treaty-specific PE changes need their own completed check.

## Two worked examples that recompute

Assume a resident Burundi company pays BIF 10,000,000 gross for taxable nonresident services not attributable to a Burundi PE, with no established treaty relief and no contractual gross-up. Income withholding is BIF 1,500,000 and cash paid to the supplier BIF 8,500,000. Imported-services VAT is a separate calculation, not subtracted here.

```oa-check
name: Nonresident service domestic withholding
inputs:
  gross_fee: 10000000
steps:
  - withholding: gross_fee*0.15
  - supplier_cash: gross_fee-withholding
expected:
  withholding: 1500000
  supplier_cash: 8500000
```

Assume properly evidenced foreign income tax of BIF 4,000,000 versus BIF 7,000,000 in two otherwise identical cases, with BIF 20,000,000 foreign taxable business profit, 30% applicable Burundi tax and no other limitation or minimum-tax interaction. Burundi tax before credit is BIF 6,000,000. The lower foreign tax leaves BIF 2,000,000 Burundi tax; the higher case gives only BIF 6,000,000 credit, leaving BIF 1,000,000 foreign tax outside the credit. The larger foreign payment does not produce a Burundi refund.

```oa-check
name: Foreign tax credit ceiling decision
inputs:
  foreign_profit: 20000000
  foreign_tax_low: 4000000
  foreign_tax_high: 7000000
steps:
  - domestic_before_credit: foreign_profit*0.30
  - credit_low: min(foreign_tax_low,domestic_before_credit)
  - credit_high: min(foreign_tax_high,domestic_before_credit)
  - domestic_balance_low: domestic_before_credit-credit_low
  - domestic_balance_high: domestic_before_credit-credit_high
  - foreign_excess: foreign_tax_high-credit_high
expected:
  credit_low: 4000000
  credit_high: 6000000
  domestic_balance_low: 2000000
  domestic_balance_high: 0
  foreign_excess: 1000000
```

## Ask the client first

- What are permanent home, physical presence, incorporation and effective-management facts?
- What activity, project duration, personnel presence and agent powers exist in Burundi?
- What payment type, payer status, recipient status and PE attribution apply?
- Is a contract gross or net of tax, and what actual payment date/amount is involved?
- What treaty effectiveness and entitlement evidence supports any claimed reduction?
- What certified foreign assessments/withholding certificates and translation support a credit?
