**2026 research draft; operative 2026 law and procedures remain unproved.** This conditional workpaper reconstructs the older DGI online CGI/LPF text with amendment labels through 2017 in the provisions used. It does not establish unchanged 2025 or 2026 law. The enacted 2026 fiscal Annex U and complete later amendment chain have not been obtained. Do not use this draft to determine a client's current liability, deadline or filing without closing that source gate. The year metadata identifies the research job; it supplies neither a publication safeguard nor an accountant attestation.

## Who this applies to

Use this job for a Mali business paying a foreign provider, an enterprise operating in Mali without its headquarters there, or a contract with offshore and Mali components. The source taxes profits realised in Mali subject to duly ratified double-tax conventions; absence of a local office is not itself an exemption. LPF article 61 also addresses activity or services used/exploited in Mali without a fixed establishment, subject to article 440. These domestic terms do not prove a treaty PE, tax residence, exemption or current treaty entitlement. [CGI art. 44; LPF art. 61](https://www.dgi.gouv.ml/CGI/).

For payments, identify the actual beneficiary, activity, place of supply/use and whether the recipient has a permanent professional installation in Mali. Source article 94 covers the specified economic remuneration of persons without that installation, subject to applicable double-tax conventions. Article 95 includes natural persons, companies of any form and its specified visiting artists. The separate no-NIF withholding branch in LPF article 440 has its own service/payer conditions; NIF possession is not proof of an installation or treaty PE. [CGI arts. 94–95; LPF art. 440](https://www.dgi.gouv.ml/CGI/).

## The base

Build a payment register with contract, payer/payee identities, installation facts, NIF evidence, beneficiary country, gross amount, payment date, activity and use location. Keep royalties/rights, services and works/supplies distinguishable. Source article 94 enumerates professional fees, copyright, patents/marks/processes, equipment use, industrial/commercial/scientific know-how, services of any nature provided or used in Mali and public contracts. Payment to a foreign bank account is neither the complete scope test nor proof of relief. [CGI arts. 94–97](https://www.dgi.gouv.ml/CGI/).

After establishing the branch, use [corporate income tax](ml-corporate-income-tax.md) as the figure home for source articles 96–97. Apply the appropriate statutory base to gross receipts and then its withholding rate; do not interchange the service and works/supplies deduction. The source gives visiting performances/concerts a special prior reduction for taxes/duties on admissions. That branch needs its own supporting receipts and is excluded from the example. Record gross, statutory base, withholding and net cash separately. A contractual net-payment promise requires a separately agreed calculation; no automatic gross-up obligation is inferred here. [CGI arts. 95–97](https://www.dgi.gouv.ml/CGI/).

Apply VAT separately. Source article 196 addresses supply/use, specific immovable-property and event exceptions, and proof of a claimed location outside Mali. Article 197 requires the foreign taxable person's accredited Mali representative and, failing that, payment by the customer for that person. Use [VAT](ml-vat-gst.md) for the source method and figures; do not describe every foreign purchase as exempt or every transaction as the same reverse-charge procedure. [CGI arts. 98, 196–198](https://www.dgi.gouv.ml/CGI/).

For a foreign head-office allocation, establish whether article 80 applies, identify the head-office services/personnel/other parent costs incurred for subsidiaries or establishments, and reconcile the Mali general-expense base. The source ceiling is not a standard deemed deduction: actual business purpose, effective costs, sufficient justification and non-abnormal/non-excessive amounts still matter. Reconcile the allocation key, relevant cost pool, exclusions, invoices and any related-party pricing adjustment; apply the supported deduction and ceiling once. The example stipulates a legally established base because this draft does not resolve every denominator issue. [CGI arts. 49, 51(2), 80–81](https://www.dgi.gouv.ml/CGI/); [transfer pricing](ml-transfer-pricing.md).

Do not remove services from Mali taxation merely by splitting an offshore contract. LPF article 616 permits restoration of the true character of arrangements concealing profit/income transfers. It expressly addresses the listed tender studies/offers/negotiation/formalities, resource/finance arrangements, assistance and other offshore services used in Mali under a contract. It distinguishes the group member without a Mali fiscal establishment from the member established with a Mali NIF. Preserve the full contract and substance analysis; this is not a universal statutory definition of PE. [LPF arts. 615–616](https://www.dgi.gouv.ml/CGI/).

## Deadlines and filing

Separate the payer's withholding remittance from the foreign enterprise's own profit declaration and any VAT filing. Article 441 governs the source non-permanent-installation remittance branch; ordinary corporate filing remains at its [figure home](ml-corporate-income-tax.md). Source LPF article 57 calls for a global-profit declaration and Mali-activity declaration where an enterprise operates in Mali and elsewhere. Article 61 requires an enterprise operating in Mali without its headquarters there to annex a consolidated balance sheet and detailed general expenses to its declaration. These source requirements do not prove a current form or treaty allocation. [LPF arts. 57, 61, 441](https://www.dgi.gouv.ml/CGI/).

Prepare the contract classification, gross/base/withholding reconciliation, period payment register, recipient evidence, head-office schedule and separate VAT treatment. Then obtain the current prescribed forms, box instructions, permitted channel, payer registration, payment reference and receipt requirements. No authenticated withholding portal, current return model or confirmation sequence has been inspected. Retain the actual filed declaration, remittance evidence and recipient certificate if the operative process requires one; no current certificate format or relief application is invented.

For an invoked treaty, establish the actual instrument, entry into force, tax category, relevant period, amendments and any termination before applying it to proved residence and business facts. No treaty text or complete current treaty-status chain was read for this job. Consequently this draft supplies no country list, reduced rate, PE threshold, credit/exemption, mutual-agreement result or outbound dividend/interest result. Unproved treaty relief stays held; the domestic source reconstruction is not a final current-law fallback.

## Penalties and interest

Keep omission of withholding distinct from failure to pay sums withheld. Article 442's fine is additional to the omitted tax; article 443's precise wording addresses sums withheld but not declared. Article 444 refers to late-payment interest and the turnover-tax computation mechanism, with an article 450 cross-reference. That interaction, current interest and cumulative sanctions have not been reconciled; no automatic combined penalty or unsupported interest percentage is computed. LPF article 440's separate no-NIF branch refers to different wage-tax collection/sanction mechanics and must not be collapsed into article 441 merely because the recipient is abroad. [LPF arts. 440–444](https://www.dgi.gouv.ml/CGI/).

## Cross-border points

Treat business presence, source/use, withholding, VAT, related-party pricing and treaty entitlement as separate decisions with their own evidence. A Mali NIF is not proof of tax residence; no local office is not proof of no domestic tax; an offshore signature is not proof that the service is used abroad. The current residence tests, treaty PE and relief, exit/cessation tax, foreign exchange, customs and special mining/investment agreements remain outside the completed method. For wages, use [income tax](ml-income-tax.md); for entity establishment use [formation](ml-company-formation.md). [CGI arts. 4, 44, 94–98, 196–197; LPF arts. 61, 616](https://www.dgi.gouv.ml/CGI/).

## Two worked examples that recompute

Both cases test the identified older source only. Assume the source rules apply unchanged for the hypothetical exercise and no special regime or treaty relief applies. Neither settles an actual 2026 liability.

**Foreign service payment: gross, base and cash differ.** Assume a genuine service used in Mali, recipient with no permanent professional installation there, and XOF 20 million gross receipts. The corporate Guide's articles 96–97 service factors give taxable base XOF 10 million, withholding XOF 3 million and recipient cash XOF 17 million. A works/supplies classification would require its different figure-home base and cannot be selected merely to lower tax. VAT is separately unresolved for this case and is not netted against income-tax withholding. The formula repeats the established figure-home inputs solely to make this case executable; it creates no new rate home.

```oa-check
name: Mali older cross-border source, service withholding reconciliation
inputs:
  gross_service_receipts: 20000000
  source_service_expense_fraction: 0.50
  source_withholding_rate: 0.30
steps:
  taxable_receipts: gross_service_receipts * (1 - source_service_expense_fraction)
  withholding: taxable_receipts * source_withholding_rate
  recipient_cash: gross_service_receipts - withholding
  reconciled_gross: recipient_cash + withholding
expected:
  taxable_receipts: 10000000
  withholding: 3000000
  recipient_cash: 17000000
  reconciled_gross: 20000000
```

**Head-office limit is a ceiling, not an automatic allowance.** Assume article 80 applies and the legally established general-expense denominator is XOF 10 million. The booked allocation is XOF 3 million, of which only XOF 2.5 million meets all ordinary and related-party evidence conditions. The source ceiling is XOF 2 million, so XOF 2 million is deductible and XOF 1 million is added back, including unsupported and above-cap portions without double counting. If instead only XOF 1.5 million were supported, the deduction would be XOF 1.5 million and the add-back XOF 1.5 million. The cap cannot manufacture evidence for the difference.

```oa-check
name: Mali older cross-border source, allocation evidence and ceiling
inputs:
  established_general_expenses: 10000000
  booked_allocation: 3000000
  supported_a: 2500000
  supported_b: 1500000
steps:
  ceiling: established_general_expenses * 0.20
  deduction_a: min(booked_allocation, supported_a, ceiling)
  addback_a: booked_allocation - deduction_a
  deduction_b: min(booked_allocation, supported_b, ceiling)
  addback_b: booked_allocation - deduction_b
expected:
  ceiling: 2000000
  deduction_a: 2000000
  addback_a: 1000000
  deduction_b: 1500000
  addback_b: 1500000
```

## Ask the client first

- Who pays and receives each amount, for which activity and gross/net contractual promise?
- Where is the service delivered and actually used, and what proves those locations?
- What installation, registration, NIF and ownership/control facts apply to the recipient?
- Which period, treaty instrument and residence evidence are claimed, including any termination?
- What cost pool, allocation key, general-expense denominator and actual service evidence support head-office charges?
- Are offshore contract components, special regimes or existing assessments involved?
- Have the operative amendment chain, current forms and authorised filing/payment route been established?
