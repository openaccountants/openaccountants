## Scope and source conditions

The fictional branch is one ordinary locally incorporated trading corporation under the retained Corporations Act 1972, incorporated before 2025, with an independently established incorporation anniversary A in 2026. Holding corporations, foreign corporations, continuations, re-domiciliations, transitions, first-year questions, public enterprises, regional processing centre activity, regulated or special businesses and restructurings are outside this reference. The examples expressly stipulate that the selected source propositions govern their invented facts. They do not establish actual law or classification. ## Selected source propositions

L1 section 14 distinguishes the holding and trading branches: the selected trading branch requires 2 or more subscribers and compliance with registration requirements, and corporations under the Act are limited by shares. This does not establish that an actual proposed entity is eligible, has limited exposure in every circumstance or should be selected.

L1 section 133(1) requires the trading corporation's prescribed particulars and applicable accompanying documents/certificates. Subsection (3) requires the prescribed form, or as near as circumstances admit, and a made-up date no earlier than the twenty-eighth day before lodgement. Subsection (4) separately addresses branch-register entries. Subsection (5) requires the annual return signed by a director or secretary, lodgement with the Registrar and payment of the prescribed fee at least once in each calendar year, no later than 28 days **before** the incorporation anniversary. Do not turn this into 28 days after the anniversary, 28 days after a meeting, a financial-year-end rule or a certificate-renewal-only requirement. [L1 section 133(1), (3)-(5).]

The holding-corporation auditor and director certificates in section 133(6)-(7) are a different branch. Excluding them from this trading illustration does not establish an audit exemption: other accounts, audit, form and regulatory obligations remain unresolved. Section 134 separately requires the listed accounts and records to be retained for 7 years; this is not a universal retention period or authority to destroy records. Section 135's accounts obligations must not be replaced by the annual-return date calculation. [L1 sections 133(6)-(8), 134-135.]

L3 section 42 requires the legal entity's annual return to include a statement confirming compliance by the entity and nominated officer, submission of required beneficial-owner details and current/correct database information. The nominated officer countersigns agreement with that statement. These requirements are additional to other annual-return obligations. Section 43 requires corporation annual returns unless exempted in accordance with the Corporations Act. Neither an exemption nor truthful compliance can be assumed. Actual identifiers, birthdates, signatures and ownership records stay with the professional; no live collection or upload is performed here. [L3 sections 42-43.]

L1 section 15(4) separately makes the first incorporation certificate valid for 12 months and provides renewal for further 12-month periods from each anniversary on due annual-return lodgement and payment. The Registrar's stated 14-day issuance period runs after due lodgement and payment; it is not the annual-return deadline, an automatic renewal or permission to trade without a current certificate. Section 15(5) requires the certificate's expiry date on its face. Section 15(6) addresses personal liability of directors/officers permitting business after expiry and before a current certificate is issued, unless the Minister otherwise directs. Do not invent a grace period, dissolution date, direction or waiver. Section 15(4A) links incorporation/renewal to the business licensing, beneficial ownership and business-name statutes; their actual applicability must be resolved separately. [L1 section 15(4)-(6).]

L1's Forms and Fees Regulations 2018 expressly commence on 15 January 2019, repeal the 2017 fees regulations and distinguish Schedule 2 Form 1 incorporation, Form 2 renewal, Form 3 director consent and Schedule 3 Form 1 annual return. The retained renewal form asks for a copy of the filed annual return. The annual-return form includes meeting, office, ownership, capital and other declarations; it is not merely 3 dates. Their actual current versions, complete attachments and legally sufficient signatures remain held. Schedule 1 separately lists incorporation, renewal, annual-return and late-document fees. This guide quotes no payable amount, aggregates no charge and authorises no payment. [L1 regulations 2-9 and Schedules 1-3.]

## Fictional date mechanics

These are source-date illustrations only. Establish A (the expressly stipulated 2026 incorporation anniversary), D (the fictional lodgement date) and T (the date to which the return is made up) as complete, valid Gregorian dates. The invented mature corporation and governing propositions must be expressly established. Missing, malformed, conflicting or unsupported dates stay held. No date is inferred from a portal, meeting, financial year-end, certificate upload or another registration.

1. Compute the illustration's latest lodgement reference as A minus 28 calendar days. Compare D with that reference. Do not add 28 days or move the result to a business day. Actual counting law, holidays, time zones, relief and acceptance remain held. [L1 section 133(5).]
2. Compute the earliest fictional made-up date as D minus 28 calendar days. Require T on or after that reference and on or before D for this historical-information illustration. A future T is an unsupported premise; section 133(3)'s lower bound alone cannot validate it. Preserve D and T separately. [L1 section 133(3).]
3. Record separately whether this one fictional D occurs in calendar 2026. The source requires at least one occurrence each calendar year; meeting an anniversary reference with a December 2025 lodgement does not establish a 2026 occurrence. Do not invent a first-year exemption, additional filing instruction or reconciliation of January anniversaries. Actual calendar-year sufficiency requires the full history and legal interpretation. [L1 section 133(5).]
4. A row meeting all 3 arithmetic conditions establishes only those fictional comparisons. It does not establish timely legal filing, complete particulars, payment, signatures, beneficial ownership compliance, renewal, acceptance, complete outputs or professional sign-off. Failure of any condition holds the affected reference; it does not itself calculate a fine or decide actual breach.
