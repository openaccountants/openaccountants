### Scope, period and authority

The reference covers selected Business Tax Act 2016 (BTA) charging provisions, complete published Schedules 1–2 and supporting definitions, source, gross-revenue and currency provisions. It distinguishes annual business profits tax (BPT), quarterly small business tax (SBT), non-resident tax (NRT) and international transportation business tax. It supplies no general VAT, tax-absence, payroll, employment/services tax, social contribution, customs or comprehensive country conclusion. The existing NR corporate annual-return reference is a separate component. [S1]

BTA section 2 distinguishes Gazette commencement from application: subsection (2) states 1 July 2016, subject to subsections (3)–(4); section 35 requires the Secretary's Gazette notice and Schedule 3 clause 3 applies from 1 October 2014. The consolidation's amendment table records original commencement on 10 June 2016, the 2024 Schedule 1 amendment and Constitution (Amendment) (No 2) Act 2026 No 21 section 5. The original commencement notice, underlying 2026 amending Act and exhaustive later chain have not been qualified here. [S1, physical pp 5, 7]

Gazette No. 304 of 28 June 2024 publishes SL 22/2024, the Business Tax (Amendment of Schedule 1) Regulations 2024. Regulation 2 expressly states **1 July 2024**. Regulations 4–11 replace specified partnership, trust, company and NRT rows, change SBT from 2.5% to **3.5%** and add a Regional Processing Centre (RPC) definition. The September 2026 compilation reproduces these changes. No period apportionment or actual application is inferred. [S1, S2]

## The method, step by step

### Dated figures and position rules [S1](https://ronlaw.gov.nr/assets/docs/acts/2016/Business%20Tax%20Act%202016_serv6.pdf) [S2](https://naurufinance.info/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Nauru-Government-Gazette-No.-304-28-June-2024.pdf)

BTA section 45 requires amounts in **Australian dollars**. Foreign amounts use the RBA exchange rate applying on the date the amount is taken into account. No actual exchange rate, rounding convention or return precision is selected. 'Tax year' generally means 12 months ending 30 June; for a company it is its annual account-balance date, the substituted tax year. A 'quarter' is the 3 months ending 30 September, 31 December, 31 March or 30 June. [S1, section 3, physical pp 9, 11; section 45, p 41]

| Charge or figure | Dated rule | Required distinction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| BPT | Section 11 imposes annual tax on a person conducting business with taxable income. Section 16 defines taxable income as gross revenue less deductions allowed. | Assumed taxable income is not a completed deduction, credit or net-liability computation. Section 11(3) excludes a non-resident individual subject to SBT and the specified non-resident fishing-business agreement case. [S1](https://ronlaw.gov.nr/assets/docs/acts/2016/Business%20Tax%20Act%202016_serv6.pdf) |
| Resident individual BPT | Schedule 1(1)(a): taxable-income band AUD 0–250,000 at 0%, above AUD 250,000 at 20%. | Establish section 8 residence. This is not a universal exemption for every person or tax. Section 3's business definition excludes employment. [S1](https://ronlaw.gov.nr/assets/docs/acts/2016/Business%20Tax%20Act%202016_serv6.pdf) |
| Partnership/trust BPT | Solely Naoero resident partners/beneficiaries: 20% on taxable income remaining after AUD 250,000 for each such partner/beneficiary. Any other partnership/trust with the specified Naoero RPC business connection: 30%; any other partnership/trust: 20%. | Do not apply the concession to mixed-residence groups from the older wording. Residence, counts, allocation and RPC status remain to be established. [S1](https://ronlaw.gov.nr/assets/docs/acts/2016/Business%20Tax%20Act%202016_serv6.pdf) |
| Company BPT | Schedule 1(1)(d): A, resident annual gross revenue AUD 0–15,000,000, 20%; B, resident above AUD 15,000,000, 25%; C, resident controlled by a non-resident person associate, 25%; D, corresponding specified RPC-connected resident/control row, 30%; E, non-resident with Naoero PE, 25%; F, corresponding RPC-connected non-resident/PE row, 30%. | Gross revenue selects A/B; taxable income is the BPT base. Control, associates, residence, PE, RPC and category overlap need legal resolution. No category-priority rule is invented here. [S1](https://ronlaw.gov.nr/assets/docs/acts/2016/Business%20Tax%20Act%202016_serv6.pdf) |
| Other BPT persons | Schedule 1(1)(e): other non-resident person conducting business in Naoero in connection with RPC, 30%; (f) any other person, 20%. | Resolve the charge, definitions and other categories first. [S1](https://ronlaw.gov.nr/assets/docs/acts/2016/Business%20Tax%20Act%202016_serv6.pdf) |
| SBT | Section 12, Schedule 1(2) and Schedule 2: **3.5% of current-quarter total gross revenue** for an eligible non-resident individual. Prior-tax-year gross-revenue threshold **AUD 250,000**, inclusive under 'did not exceed'. | Establish all 3 section 12(1) conditions, associates and election/approval history. The threshold is not a quarterly allowance, deduction or universal business turnover limit. [S1](https://ronlaw.gov.nr/assets/docs/acts/2016/Business%20Tax%20Act%202016_serv6.pdf); [S2](https://naurufinance.info/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Nauru-Government-Gazette-No.-304-28-June-2024.pdf) |
| NRT | Section 13 charges a non-resident person on Naoero-source interest, royalty or insurance premium, using the gross amount. Schedule 1(3) shows specified RPC-connected rows at 30%, any other person at 20%. | Excludes exempt income, PE-attributable business amounts (instead section 11) and amounts subject to services tax. Do not impose NRT on a resident company from the rate row alone. The charge/rate-table interaction is held. [S1](https://ronlaw.gov.nr/assets/docs/acts/2016/Business%20Tax%20Act%202016_serv6.pdf) |
| International transportation | Section 14 and Schedule 1(4): 0% on the statutory gross base for a non-resident's outbound carriage, subject to exempt-income, transit-passenger and transhipment exclusions. | A statutory zero rate does not establish no records, filing, collection or other tax. No actual transport case is classified here. [S1](https://ronlaw.gov.nr/assets/docs/acts/2016/Business%20Tax%20Act%202016_serv6.pdf) |

Section 12(1) requires business solely in the Republic, no BPT in the previous tax year and prior-year total gross revenue not exceeding Schedule 2's threshold. Section 12(3) permits the Secretary to consider associates' gross revenue for that prior-year test. Subsections (4)–(10) govern applications, approval, proper records, election timing, the 3-year restriction on an elected BPT person's return application and written conditions. Eligibility does not follow from turnover alone. [S1, physical pp 15–16]

Section 17 separates gross revenue from accounting turnover through inclusions and exclusions. Subsection (2A) includes residents' amounts from sources both inside and outside Naoero and only Naoero-source amounts for non-residents. Do not treat all resident revenue as domestic. Section 15 makes taxes under sections 12–14 final on the relevant income, with no deduction for expenditure or loss in deriving it. BPT is distinct. [S1, sections 9, 15–18]

Section 11(2A)–(2B) offsets allowed credits in the stated order: section 25A foreign credit, then section 41 credit. Neither entitlement nor an actual credit is established here. BPT examples are before credits. Sections 13(4) and 14(3) refer to discharge through specified collection provisions; those and complete administration require qualification before withholding or payment instructions. [S1, sections 11, 13–14]

### Recompute the retained hypothetical positions

The following blocks recompute 2 of the positions above using exactly their supplied premises. They are separate arithmetic exercises. The original classification, period, source, currency, precision and operational holds remain applicable. A nil or negative arithmetic component does not resolve an unknown liability or establish an exemption, refund or amount to pay.

```oa-check
name: NR business position A assumed resident individual taxable income
inputs:
  taxable_income: 400000
  zero_band: 250000
steps:
  - component: max(taxable_income - zero_band, 0) * 0.20
expected:
  component: 30000
tolerance: 0.000001
```

```oa-check
name: NR business separate boundary B and SBT C components
inputs:
  boundary_income: 250000
  zero_band: 250000
  sbt_quarter_gross: 80000

  - boundary_component: max(boundary_income - zero_band, 0) * 0.20
  - sbt_component: sbt_quarter_gross * 0.035

  boundary_component: 0
```
