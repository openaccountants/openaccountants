# Nauru (NR): tax pack

**Built from the law. Not yet approved. Written by Ryan Duguid. Not reviewed by an accountant.** Nauru (Naoero) uses the Australian dollar. Its laws are published on RONLAW (ronlaw.gov.nr); taxes are administered by the Nauru Revenue Office (naurufinance.info) under the Revenue Administration Act 2014, the Business Tax Act 2016 and the Employment and Services Tax Act 2014. The tax year runs from 1 July to 30 June. Tax year 2026.

## What is not covered yet

- Consumption taxes, import duties and social contributions: no skill yet.
- Business tax: full taxable income computation, losses, credits and the regional processing centre branches.
- Corporations: holding and foreign corporations, business licences and business names.
