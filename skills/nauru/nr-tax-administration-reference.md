### TIN and records as dated rules [S1](https://ronlaw.gov.nr/assets/docs/acts/2014/Revenue%20Administration%20Act%202014_serv6.pdf) [S3](https://ronlaw.gov.nr/assets/docs/acts/2026/Constitution%20%28Amendment%29%20%28No.2%29%20Act%202026.pdf)

RAA section 9(1) requires the listed BTA resident-person category, another person becoming tax liable and a person requested by the Secretary to apply for a TIN unless already issued one. Application is in approved form, with prescribed identity evidence, within **7 days** of becoming liable to apply. Issue is by service of written notice after identity is established; refusal conditions and notice are specified. Section 10 requires the person's own TIN on documents under the tax law and prohibits another person's use. These are distinct from ESTA section 15's employer/payer registration. [S1, sections 9–10; S5]

Under RAA section 11, the person must notify cessation of tax liability in approved form within **7 days**. The temporary-cessation exception attaches to the Secretary's cancellation ground in subsection (3)(a); it is not an unexpressed exception to subsection (1)'s notification duty. Other false/duplicate-identity cancellation and replacement powers remain. ESTA section 15 instead specifies employer application within 7 days and payer application within 14 days, in each case or within such further time as the Secretary may allow, and a cancellation application within 14 days of ceasing. Its separate subsection (5) temporary-cessation exception likewise concerns the Secretary's cancellation duty. Do not merge those obligations. [S1, sections 9–11; S5, section 15]

Embedded RAA Regulations 2016 regulation 4 lists alternative individual identity evidence: the specified certified current passport or driver's-licence pages, birth certificate with a current photo or other evidence specified by the Secretary. Corporate, partnership, other-body and trust applications have distinct formation-document requirements. Regulation 5 says the Secretary **may require** biometrics for the specified identification/fraud purposes; it does not impose a universal biometric step. A form's passport field does not repeal the regulation's alternatives. The reviewer retains real identities and supplies any required documents personally; do not request, enter or submit them through this reference. [S1, regulations 4–5]

RAA section 14 requires records in English, including permitted electronic format, sufficient to ascertain the tax liability, normally retained **5 years from reporting-period end**, or the tax law's specified shorter period. Amended assessments can extend retention to section 21(6)'s period; proceedings begun before the 5-year period ends require retention until all proceedings finish. Section 21(6) uses the later of the specified 5-year original-return/assessment trigger and 1 year after service of the amended assessment; subsection (7) limits that latter amendment power. Do not turn either rule into a routine destruction date. Translation can be required by written notice. BTA section 38 separately requires adequate tax-computation records, SBT daily sales including credit sales, an employer's wage register and deduction/asset-cost evidence. [S1, sections 14 and 21; S4, section 38]

### Interest, penalties and relief boundaries [S1](https://ronlaw.gov.nr/assets/docs/acts/2014/Revenue%20Administration%20Act%202014_serv6.pdf) [S2](https://ronlaw.gov.nr/assets/docs/acts/2026/Revenue%20Administration%20%28Amendment%29%20Act%202026.pdf)

RAA section 23, as amended by original Act 26 section 6, permits a reasonable-cause extension and specified instalments at **2.5% with no stated period**. Do not supply 'annual', 'monthly' or 'one-off'. Written decision, default acceleration under (4), original-date interest under (5) and non-default 'further payment interest' condition under (6) must be read together. No blanket forgiveness, erasure of prior interest or actual instalment schedule is established. [S1–S2]

Section 28(1) literally prints **'[5% per annum]'**. Preserve the brackets rather than silently normalising the rate. Subsection (4) states daily simple interest but supplies no denominator, leap-year or rounding method in the qualified text. Subsection (9)'s non-accrual window requires the specified written notification and full payment within its time, including interest through notification; it is not automatic. Subsection (11) gives the telecommunications provision's rate priority, whose underlying authority is unqualified. No actual interest amount is computed. [S1, complete section 28]

RAA section 66A requires all 3 conditions: a BTA section 41(3B) taxpayer application, Secretary agreement and actual annual BPT exceeding **total instalments payable**, not instalments paid, by more than 20%. If it applies, the penalty is 10% of the resulting statutory shortfall. Do not replace those conditions with a generic underestimate, select a percentage denominator absent qualified authority or extend it to every first-year estimate. Section 67 separately requires the stated penalty assessment/notice and governs its due date. [S1; F5]

Original Act 26 section 7's replacement section 67(7) permits discretionary remission on a subsection (6) written application giving reasons or on the Secretary's own motion. Subsection (8) requires a public record of each remission and its reasons, reported quarterly to the Auditor General. Below AUD 5,000 is the Secretary's discretion; **AUD 5,000 or more requires Minister approval**. Both exclude section 64 tax-shortfall penalties. This is no promised remission, interest waiver or inference that section 66A and section 64 are the same penalty. Complete applicable recovery, objection, appeal, remission and amendment procedure is required for an actual case. [S1–S2]

### Scope, period and sources

The Revenue Administration Act 2014 (RAA) applies to its defined tax laws, including the BTA and ESTA listed in Schedule 2 and their regulations. Section 4 makes specified applications to customs, agency taxes and secondary liabilities; this is not a rule extending every RAA provision to every Government fee. BTA section 37 applies RAA subject to BTA Part 5; ESTA section 16 applies it subject to ESTA Part 4. Separate tax-specific rules control the selected obligations. No comprehensive NR coverage, tax absence, social, company-law filing, indirect-tax, property or general appeal conclusion is established. [S1, sections 3–4 and Schedule 2; S4; S5]

RAA section 2 specifies 1 October 2014 commencement except Part 3 Division 1, which commenced on 26 September 2014. Original Constitution (Amendment) (No.2) Act 2026 No 21 was certified on 12 May 2026 and commences on certification; section 5 substitutes 'Naoero' for 'Nauru' in written laws. It does not itself amend a tax rate or threshold. Original RAA (Amendment) Act 2026 No 26 was certified on 20 July 2026 and commences on certification. Its section 6 amends RAA section 23 and section 7 replaces section 67(7). Those particular original instruments are qualified; the exhaustive original enactment, amendment, commencement, later-law and electronic-copy authority chain remains open. Compilation production, enactment/certification, commencement, reporting period and retrieval must remain distinct. [S1–S3]
