# Tuvalu: ordinary monthly NPF contribution reference v0.2

> **General reference only.** This Tier 2 conditional draft illustrates selected ordinary contribution mechanics in dated legislation. It does not establish actual liability, a lawful deduction, current operational readiness or comprehensive jurisdiction acceptance.

Load workflow-base and social-contributions-workflow-base in full. Preserve all 4 universal outputs and 18 checks, all 5 social contributions outputs and 12 checks, all 13 universal and 12 social contributions mandatory country slots and every shared refusal. Missing applicable law, facts, tools, outputs or qualified review prevents delivery of an actual financial result. A worked component does not fill another mandatory slot by assumption.

The fictional branch is one established non-seafarer employed member, one established employer, one capacity and one complete monthly payment of ordinary money earnings in December 2022. Coverage, the applicable ordinary Schedule 1 rules, monthly allocation and absence of an exception, waiver, special agreement or abnormal-pay direction are stipulated. The employment is not within the short-employment exclusion. There are no arrears, advances, reversals, voluntary additions, foreign-currency payments or unresolved earnings components. These are illustrative premises, not findings about a real person or complete law for the month.

Multiple employers, separate capacities, split or more frequent payments and another period require their own allocation analysis. Regulation 5 expressly requires separate assessment for concurrent different employers or different capacities with separate payments. It does not itself establish a universal per-payday rule for all ordinary employment.

## Coverage and earnings intake

Establish the L1 section 3 definitions and section 12 branch, the linked employer, the complete applicable instruments and each L3 exception. A job title, citizenship, retirement age or existing registration alone is insufficient. The employed-person definition extends beyond a formally labelled contract of service. Do not infer that a service provider, director or office holder is excluded solely from the label.

L3 regulations 1-3 require separate findings about school-leaving age, domestic service within its private-dwelling definition, aid/volunteer schemes and the citizenship qualification, normal monthly earnings, foreign domicile and purpose of residence, age and prior benefit history. In particular, the foreign-person exclusion requires the stated conditions together; non-citizenship alone is insufficient. The source refers to age **65**, and a separate branch after **50** with **3 previous benefit entitlements**, rather than an automatic cessation at 55. [L3, regulations 1-3.] The affected low-income branch remains subject to the source hold above.

L2 regulation 12 separately excludes a period of employment whose total earnings are **less than $5**, not $5 or less. This is not the normal-monthly-earnings rule and is not an amount to subtract from every base. L1 section 16 hardship waiver is discretionary for the Minister or an authorised person; no self-applied waiver is assumed.

## Rates, precision and calculation

L2 regulation 2 applies the Schedule 1 rates to each member's monthly gross earnings. Its calculation is to the nearest **$0.10**, disregarding a residual **$0.05 or less**. Schedule 1 identifies two contributions: ordinary Group 1(i), **13%**, and Group 2, **10%**. **Selected source-bound interpretation:** calculate and round each share separately, then add the rounded shares. Regulation 2 refers to the plural rates and Schedule 1 separately defines both contributions; it does not expressly say to round each group before adding. This sequence is the interpretation used in the synthetic examples and requires qualified confirmation for actual work. Do not round a pooled 23% figure instead or round a combined total for multiple members or months. Preserve the exact percentage product until the ten-cent step; do not first round it to cents.

For these positive whole-cent AUD fixtures, let c be G in cents and r be 13 for Group 1(i), or 10 for Group 2. Use exact integer arithmetic:

~~~text
q = floor(r * c / 1000)
u = (r * c) mod 1000
share_cents = 10 * (q + (1 if u > 500 else 0))

R = rounded Group 1(i) share          scheduled employee share
E = rounded Group 2 share             scheduled employer share
T = R + E                            employer's initial Fund liability
~~~

The divisor converts a percentage of whole-cent earnings into ten-cent units. Exactly half a ten-cent unit rounds down. Gross earnings are not rounded to a whole dollar or to ten cents before multiplying. Other precision, zero, negative or corrected amounts are outside this fixture decoder and remain held; that limit is not a declaration that such statutory earnings cannot exist.

Under Schedule 1, the employer initially owes both ordinary contributions. R is not proof of an actual lawful deduction. Regulation 7(1) requires ordinary recovery no later than actual payment of the earnings. Later recovery under regulation 7(2) requires a good-faith error, notification before recovery and the stated limit tied to what is properly deductible from the same future payment. This reference does not calculate a catch-up deduction. Unknown or missed recovery leaves the actual deduction and employer cost held. E equals the employer's funding balance only where the entire R has actually been lawfully recovered; otherwise a different residual requires established evidence. Neither a missed deduction nor nonpayment creates an automatic contribution credit.

N4 has exact unrounded shares 130.0325 and 100.0250. They round to 130.00 and 100.00; T is 230.00. Rounding its pooled 23% product, 230.0575, would incorrectly give 230.10. N6 has raw shares 130.0663 and 100.0510, so both round up and T is 230.20; pooling would instead give 230.10.

N5's employer product is exactly 100.0500 and rounds down to 100.00; N6's is 100.0510 and rounds up to 100.10. First rounding N6's product to cents would destroy that distinction. N7-N9 straddle the employee midpoint: 130.6487, 130.6500 and 130.6513 become 130.60, 130.60 and 130.70. The half-down rule applies to the residual within each ten-cent interval, not only contributions below 10 cents.

Aggregation examples, with law and coverage independently stipulated for each unit:

- **A1, separate members:** 2 members each have monthly G = 1000.25. Each has R = 130.00, E = 100.00 and T = 230.00. Their sums are R = 260.00, E = 200.00 and T = 460.00. Combining G first would produce R = 260.10, E = 200.00 and T = 460.10, which is not the sum of their liabilities.
- **A2, separate months:** One member has G = 1000.25 in each of 2 separately established months under the same rule. The monthly liabilities sum to 460.00. Do not round the combined 2000.50 base or infer a carry of discarded fractions. The source-period stipulation for both months is part of this synthetic example.
- **A3, one complete payment with several earnings components:** Ordinary salary 900.00, overtime 50.39 and bonus 49.86 form one monthly G = 1000.25. R = 130.00, E = 100.00 and T = 230.00. Rounding the 3 components separately would instead give R = 130.10, E = 100.00 and T = 230.10. A payroll line is not itself a separate assessment unit.

## Procedure, outputs and verification

The following are dated source requirements, not a qualified current operating procedure:

- Under L4 regulations 3-8, the new-employer rule is registration within **7 days** of the first employee; a **14-day** period belongs to a Manager-request branch. A previously unregistered new employee requires submission forthwith. Failure to produce a card within **4 days** is prima facie evidence for that branch, not conclusive proof of non-registration. Obtain the applicable approved forms and evidence; no real identifiers are included here.
- L2 regulation 3 requires duly completed approved forms with payment. For post, receipt of the payment instrument controls. Foreign currency has a separate bank-rate-and-commission rule. The public forms page does not qualify a complete current employer return or payment channel.
- Under L2 regulation 8, ordinary payment is due by the **15th** of the month following the month in which earnings were actually paid. For the stipulated December 2022 payment, the source-date reference is **15 January 2023**, subject to applicable law and unresolved calendar/operational rules. The **16th** starts a **5%** surcharge on the unpaid amount for each month or part month. No surcharge, compounding or adjusted holiday deadline is calculated here.
- L2 regulations 9-11 require the specified records for at least **6 years from the last entry**, monthly returns and payment statements, immediate cessation treatment and immediate written notice of post-dispatch errors for Manager-directed adjustment. The final-pay certificate at year end or termination is in triplicate with distinct Fund, employee and employer copies. Do not confuse these rules with an arbitrary retention period from filing or a monthly-only cessation deadline.
- L2 regulation 13 requires satisfaction of erroneous payment, prior set-off and a residual exceeding **$1**, with the application period **6 years from financial-year end**, subject to a good-cause extension. The proper refund recipient depends on the original funding/deduction and stated consent rules. No automatic employer refund is supplied.
- L1 section 32 concerns Fund income, interest and withdrawals/payments, with express exceptions for specified fees, salaries and allowances. It does not establish deductibility of employee/employer contributions or a reduction of the income tax gross base. Other tax and programme interactions remain held.
