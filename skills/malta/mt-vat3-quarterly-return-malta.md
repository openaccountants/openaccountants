# Malta VAT returns: Article 10, Article 11 and Article 12

## Scope

This is the compatibility lookup for `malta-vat-return`, covering Malta-established businesses and 2026 VAT periods. “VAT3” does not establish the official form or registration article. Use the registration certificate and the actual period allocated by MTCA; do not assume calendar quarters. This Guide supplies the core preparation method. For authenticated return fields, submission screens and payment-reference mapping: Check this point with one of our partner accountants.

## Ask the client first

Obtain the registration certificate, allocated period, invoices and credit notes, previous return and credit balance, preceding-year and current-year turnover, and details of foreign purchases, exempt activities, private use, cars and capital assets. Confirm who files and that access works. The certificate states the registration article, number and effective date; Article 10 and 12 numbers have the MT prefix, while Article 11 numbers do not ([Act, arts 13 and 17](https://legislation.mt/getpdf/6ac352947b5e681a8405fbf4)).

## The method, step by step

### 1. Establish the registration and turnover

Article 10 is the ordinary return and qualifying input-credit registration. Article 11 is the domestic small-enterprise exemption, without ordinary input credit. An Article 11 person receiving customer-liable services must also register under Article 12 by the service-receipt date; there is no monetary service threshold. Article 12 does not confer ordinary input recovery ([Act, arts 11(10), 12(3), 22](https://legislation.mt/getpdf/6ac352947b5e681a8405fbf4)). Article 12 EU-goods acquisitions and outbound services have separate registration rules: Check this point with one of our partner accountants.

| Article 11 point | Treatment | Official source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Entry turnover | Preceding-calendar-year domestic turnover no more than €35,000; exactly €35,000 satisfies this threshold. Registration requires an application. | [Act, art. 11 and Sixth Schedule Part One item 1](https://legislation.mt/getpdf/6ac352947b5e681a8405fbf4) |
| New registration | If not registered under Article 10, effect starts at the later of commencement and the first day of the application-receipt month. An Article 10 switch starts the first day of the following month. | [Act, art. 11(2)](https://legislation.mt/getpdf/6ac352947b5e681a8405fbf4) |
| Article 10 switch restriction | First whole twelve calendar months ordinarily barred. Earlier conversion is at the Commissioner's discretion, requiring small-enterprise eligibility and no input credits claimed during that Article 10 registration. | [Act, Sixth Schedule Part One item 2](https://legislation.mt/getpdf/6ac352947b5e681a8405fbf4) |
| Continuing status | Qualification ends when current-calendar-year turnover exceeds €35,000. Apply to cancel within fifteen days. Under Article 11(5)(b)/(7), cancellation starts the first day of the month after qualification was lost; Article 10 starts then if required. Commissioner cancellation under Article 11(8) instead takes effect from the determined loss-of-qualification date. | [Act, arts 10(6)(c), 10(7a), 11(5), 11(7)-(8), Sixth Schedule Part One item 3](https://legislation.mt/getpdf/6ac352947b5e681a8405fbf4) |

For this threshold, turnover excludes VAT and capital-asset disposals. Include supplies that would be taxed under Article 10; the exempt-with-credit transactions in Fifth Schedule Part One items 1, 3, 5, 6, 7 and 8(1); and property, specified financial, insurance and reinsurance transactions unless ancillary. For non-individual Article 11 applicants or registrants, include proportionate turnover of a related owner/controller holding more than 10%, directly or indirectly; the Commissioner may prescribe relatedness ([Sixth Schedule definitions item 3 and Part One item 4](https://legislation.mt/getpdf/6ac352947b5e681a8405fbf4)).

**Article 11A boundary:** the preceding-year Union-turnover entry test is strictly below €100,000. Current-year exemption in other Member States ceases when Union turnover exceeds €100,000; equality alone does not end it. This does not establish host-State eligibility. For host-State conditions, Article 11A declarations/notifications and Article 11B approval or cessation: Check this point with one of our partner accountants. ([Sixth Schedule Part Two items 1 and 5](https://legislation.mt/getpdf/6ac352947b5e681a8405fbf4)).

### 2. Classify transactions before computing

Allocate transactions by VAT chargeability, rather than bank date. The default rate for a taxable supply is 18%, subject to the Eighth Schedule exceptions ([Act, arts 19 and 22; Fourth Schedule](https://legislation.mt/getpdf/6ac352947b5e681a8405fbf4)). For reduced rates, exemption or the chargeability of a particular transaction: Check this point with one of our partner accountants. The specialist topics below preserve the classifications that require that check.

For a taxable general-rule B2B service supplied in Malta by a supplier neither established in Malta nor registered under Article 10, the Malta recipient is liable under Article 20(2)(b). A Maltese fixed establishment which does not intervene in the supply does not prevent non-established treatment. Establish place of supply and taxable status first: a foreign hotel or exempt service is not automatically Malta reverse charge because no VAT appears. Incorrect foreign VAT does not remove an independently arising Maltese liability and is not Maltese input tax. For its correction, refund and treatment in the taxable consideration: Check this point with one of our partner accountants. ([Act, arts 20 and 22; Third Schedule Part Two item 2(a)](https://legislation.mt/getpdf/6ac352947b5e681a8405fbf4)).

### 3. Establish the deduction and balance

For an Article 10 credit, establish attributable deductible activity, business use and that no input block applies. Only the business-use proportion can qualify. Except where the Commissioner otherwise allows, keep the tax invoice or import document, properly accounted for, held and producible. Customer-liable and acquisition VAT must also be reported as due. Output tax and qualifying credit follow chargeability; an invoice indicating “Cash Accounting” instead links the deduction to payment ([Act, art. 22 and Tenth Schedule items 1-4](https://legislation.mt/getpdf/6ac352947b5e681a8405fbf4)). For blocked-item exceptions, partial exemption, outside-Malta deduction categories and capital-goods adjustments: Check this point with one of our partner accountants.

Net output tax against allowed deductions and only an unused prior credit that has neither been deducted nor refunded nor become refundable ([Act, art. 21(1)](https://legislation.mt/getpdf/6ac352947b5e681a8405fbf4)). Refund entitlement, timing, verification and interest: Check this point with one of our partner accountants.

### 4. File, pay and preserve the evidence

A sole proprietor can use their own e-ID, or authorise a registered practitioner with MTCA02 or a trusted non-practitioner with MTCA03 ([MTCA VAT return cycle](https://mtca.gov.mt/personal-tax/self-employed/vat-return-cycle)). For the current account-specific submission steps and reference: Check this point with one of our partner accountants. A return must be full and complete in all material aspects; Article 27 permits the designated electronic portal or prescribed form subject to applicable directions ([Act, art. 27(3)-(4)](https://legislation.mt/getpdf/6ac352947b5e681a8405fbf4)).

| Filing | Ordinary deadline / treatment | Official source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Article 10 return and tax | Fifteenth of the second month after the period-end month. Electronic filing within seven days after that date avoids the late-filing administrative penalty; interest relief also requires paying the tax with the return. | [Act, arts 21(1), 21(4A), 27(1), 42(1)(d)](https://legislation.mt/getpdf/6ac352947b5e681a8405fbf4) |
| Article 11 declaration | Normally a calendar year, due 15 February following; shorter elected or directed periods and first/final partial periods have a deadline on the fifteenth of the second month after their end month. The declaration is normally electronic. | [Act, Sixth Schedule Part One item 5](https://legislation.mt/getpdf/6ac352947b5e681a8405fbf4) |
| Article 12 customer-liable service and payment notice | Tax due by the fifteenth of the second month following the earlier invoice or receipt month. Determine actual receipt; an invoice alone does not establish the date. | [Act, arts 21(3) and 30A](https://legislation.mt/getpdf/6ac352947b5e681a8405fbf4) |
| Recapitulative statement | Default monthly reporting by the next month's fifteenth; quarterly eligibility and call-off stock require separate checks. Services exempt in the customer's Member State are excluded from Article 30(2)(c). | [Act, art. 30(2)(c)](https://legislation.mt/getpdf/6ac352947b5e681a8405fbf4); [S.L. 406.14 reg. 3](https://legislation.mt/eli/sl/406.14/eng/pdf) |

For Article 12 goods chargeability/payment notices, final returns, registration penalties, quarterly recaps and OSS: Check this point with one of our partner accountants. Apply each filing's own deadline. The ordinary domestic statutory weekend/public-holiday rule moves a date to the first following working day other than Saturday; OSS deadlines do not move for weekends or public holidays ([Act, art. 73(5)](https://legislation.mt/getpdf/6ac352947b5e681a8405fbf4); [Commission OSS guidance](https://vat-one-stop-shop.ec.europa.eu/one-stop-shop/declare-and-pay-oss_en)). For any year-specific administrative concession: Check this point with one of our partner accountants.

For ordinary unpaid tax, interest is 0.6% per month or part; balances payable before 1 September 2022 retain 0.33%. Appeal-delay suspension and the payment appropriation which stops interest require their own facts: Check this point with one of our partner accountants. ([Act, art. 21(4)](https://legislation.mt/getpdf/6ac352947b5e681a8405fbf4); [S.L. 406.19](https://legislation.mt/eli/sl/406.19/eng/pdf)).

Keep the VAT account, working papers and supporting records. The ordinary minimum is six years from year-end; for a late return or a formal Article 28(1) correction, it starts at filing or the Commissioner's receipt of the correction request. For capital-goods and OSS retention, detailed record categories and a records-production notice: Check this point with one of our partner accountants. ([Act, art. 48(4)](https://legislation.mt/getpdf/6ac352947b5e681a8405fbf4)).

## Worked cases

These preserve the original hypothetical inputs for 2026. They do not establish actual customer status, product classification or portal fields.

**Case 1 - March quarter.** For an Article 10 January–March 2026 period, return and payment are due 15 May 2026. Electronic filing by 22 May avoids the late-filing penalty; interest relief requires payment with that return ([Act, arts 21, 27 and 42](https://legislation.mt/getpdf/6ac352947b5e681a8405fbf4)).

**Case 2 - ordinary Article 10 return.** €10,000 sales at an established 18% rate produce €1,800 output VAT. €2,000 purchases at 18%, assumed wholly deductible with proper evidence, give €360 input credit: €1,440 payable. The separate car lease is excluded assuming no statutory vehicle exception applies; business use alone does not establish an exception. For the actual car classification: Check this point with one of our partner accountants. ([Act, arts 19, 22 and Tenth Schedule item 3](https://legislation.mt/getpdf/6ac352947b5e681a8405fbf4)).

**Case 3 - Article 11 consultant, Irish software.** The original invoice is dated 10 March 2026 for €1,000 with no VAT. Assuming the stated service is taxable in Malta at 18% and Article 20(2)(b) applies, Article 12 registration is required by receipt; €180 is payable with no ordinary input credit. Payment is 15 May only if receipt was not in an earlier month. The receipt month was not supplied. For the actual payment date: Check this point with one of our partner accountants. ([Act, arts 11(10), 12(3), 19, 20, 21(3), 22](https://legislation.mt/getpdf/6ac352947b5e681a8405fbf4)).

**Case 4 - late January–March return.** Original inputs: €5,000 net tax, due 15 May 2026, filed and paid 20 July 2026. Assuming €5,000 is also the excess before brought-forward credit, Article 38(1) gives max(1% × €5,000, €20) × three months/parts = €150, below the €250 cap. The cap below €250 tax payable is instead the greater of rounded tax payable and €50. Assuming no interest suspension or earlier payment, €5,000 × 0.6% × three months/parts = €90 interest. For an actual penalty assessment or relief: Check this point with one of our partner accountants. ([Act, arts 21(4), 38(1)](https://legislation.mt/getpdf/6ac352947b5e681a8405fbf4); [S.L. 406.19](https://legislation.mt/eli/sl/406.19/eng/pdf)).

**Case 5 - leaving Article 11.** A shop's qualifying 2025 turnover was €31,000. Its 2026 turnover exceeds €35,000 on 10 September: qualification ends then and the cancellation application is due 25 September. Applying under Article 11(5)(b), cancellation takes effect on 1 October with Article 10 from that date for the stated taxable activity. Commissioner cancellation under Article 11(8) instead takes effect on 10 September. No cancellation-penalty total can be calculated from these inputs. For that penalty: Check this point with one of our partner accountants. ([Act, arts 10(6)(c), 10(7a), 11(5), 11(7)-(8), Sixth Schedule Part One](https://legislation.mt/getpdf/6ac352947b5e681a8405fbf4)).

## When to refuse or refer

For each topic below: Check this point with one of our partner accountants.

- Reduced-rate classification: securities custody/management, non-grantor credit/guarantee management, pleasure-boat hire and prior same-kind hires, regulated body care and health studios; licensed accommodation, sporting facilities and accommodation bundles; electricity, listed CN consumables/confectionery/juices, medical accessories, printed/electronic books and audio books with exclusions, disabled-person goods, art imports, minor repairs, domestic care and cultural admission.
- Exemption: exports and transport evidence, intra-EU goods (including margin-scheme exclusions and recapitulative-statement shortcomings justified to the Commissioner), food versus catering, medicines, international passenger/scheduled transport, commercial vessels/aircraft, property transfers/lettings including short lets, habitation, company-to-business letting, garages, stores, cultural space and poker rooms; insurance, finance, medical-provider eligibility, education and postal services; exempt supplies under Articles 11, 11A and 11B.
- Deduction exceptions: tobacco/alcohol/art resale, vehicles/vessels/aircraft and their fuel, repair and hire, paid carriage, driver/self-drive hire, driving instruction, goods-designed vans and seating categories, commercial charter, client-meeting entertainment, restaurant inputs and employee transport; outside-Malta and financial/insurance deduction categories.
- Partial exemption: attribution, provisional/final ratios, annual adjustment, rounding, de minimis and exact equality. Capital goods: long-term fixed assets versus resale stock, laptop/furniture classification, price/open-market-value threshold and VAT inclusion, adjustment start and delayed first use, annual/one-off adjustment, disposal/private use/cessation, remaining-goods self-supply on Article 10 cancellation and extended retention.
- Credit notes and later price reductions; ordinary transaction and customs currency conversion versus OSS period-end conversion; excess-credit refund, verification extension, withheld returns/information and refund interest.
- Outbound services and customer-status evidence, EU goods acquisitions, quarterly/mixed recaps, call-off stock, distance-sale threshold/election, Union OSS eligibility/returns/nil periods and record retention, import schemes; Article 11A host-State tests, declarations and cessation notices, and Article 11B notification/approval/cessation.
- Corrections including the small-error and time-window rules, prescribed forms and assessed periods; late/incorrect-return, declaration, recap, registration, change-notice and cancellation penalties, reasonable excuse/remission, investigation settlements, assessments and appeals, interest suspension/payment appropriation, cancellation/final returns and missing invoice evidence.
- Margin schemes for second-hand goods/art/antiques and travel agents, VAT groups, non-established suppliers or representatives, development/property, investment gold, new means of transport, excise goods, business/traveller refunds, income-tax questions, historical pre-2025 turnover tests and late 2025 filings or declaration calendars.

## Completion checklist

Confirm article and allocated period; reconcile invoices, sales, output, allowed deductions and unused credit to the VAT account; resolve every relevant referred topic before filing; obtain the current return-field and payment-reference mapping; retain submission/payment evidence and apply each filing's own deadline.
