# Selling a company in Malta: share sale, duty and Form T, 2026

## Scope

This Guide gives the working order for the sale, by an individual or a company, of shares in a Maltese private limited company to a third party for money, in 2026. It covers the market value, the duty, the seller's provisional tax, the notification to the Commissioner and Form T at the Malta Business Registry (MBR).

The income tax on the gain (article 5 of the Income Tax Act, the participation exemption, the non-resident and listed-share exemptions) lives in [mt-capital-gains](mt-capital-gains.md) and [mt-holding-company-participation-exemption](mt-holding-company-participation-exemption.md); it is not repeated here.

Not for: an asset sale (business, goodwill, property); a reorganisation, merger or intra-group transfer claiming an exemption; a transfer causa mortis or a donation; listed shares; earn-outs, deferred consideration or vendor loans that cannot be valued today. Send those to a specialist. Stop before computing anything if the company, directly or through subsidiaries, owns Malta immovable property and there is no current Perit's valuation: duty, market value and the gain all depend on it.

**Not yet confirmed:** the prescribed provisional tax for Income Tax Act property-company shares, client-specific late-notification/default consequences, BO filing and exceptional valuations. Check this point with one of our partner accountants. These are non-usable holds; do not execute a package that depends on them. The ordinary duty payment and Form T clocks below are separately established.

Built from the law: not yet approved by an accountant.

## Ask the client first

1. Who is selling, and are they resident and domiciled in Malta (individual or company)?
2. When and how were the shares acquired, and at what cost (deed, Form T, causa mortis declaration)?
3. Does the company, or any subsidiary, own or hold rights over immovable property in Malta? Has a Perit valued it?
4. What is the agreed price, and is any of it deferred, contingent or in kind? Is it a controlling interest?
5. Is the buyer related to the seller?
6. Will beneficial ownership change (form BO2)?

## The method, step by step

1. **The seller:** individual or company, resident or not, domiciled or not; how and when the shares were acquired.
2. **What the company owns:** Malta immovable property or rights over it, directly or through subsidiaries. Apply the **duty** article 42(2) test below and separately the Income Tax Act article 2 property-company definition. They are different tests. Property alone does not establish 5% duty; the income-tax property-company test is not the duty 75% test.
3. **Supported share value.** MTCA's FAQ says an accountant works it out under the Capital Gains Rules; the operative rules require specific auditing credentials. S.L. 123.27 rule 5(10) requires the transferor's certified public auditor statement of whether it is controlling, with grounds; normally Schedule C/computation if controlling, Schedule D/valuation otherwise, subject to rule 5(14) Schedule E exceptions. S.L. 364.06 rule 11 requires the transferee's statement by a CPA holding a practising certificate in auditing and real-value computation, except fixed-return-only shares under 11(2). Under the current rules, as amended by LN 156 and LN 158 published in Gazette 21,650 on 22 May 2026, where the share value takes account of the market value of immovable property, the Perit's valuation must come "together with a site plan issued by the Land Registry, signed and stamped by a Perit" (L.N. 156 of 2026, rules 5(10) and 5A(5); L.N. 158 of 2026 for the duty rules S.L. 364.06).
4. **Duty** under Cap. 364 art. 42 on the higher of consideration and **real value**, using €2 per €100 **or part**, and the conditional €3 addition below. Preserve the ceiling calculation instead of treating every fractional €100 as a simple 2% multiplication. Duty and income tax use distinct statutory bases/definitions.
5. **Seller's income tax:** compute the gain or confirm the exemption in writing, using [mt-capital-gains](mt-capital-gains.md).
6. **Provisional tax** ordinarily within 15 calendar days under ITMA art. 43, with its controlling-interest, property-company, exemption and special-date qualifications below. A form is not automatically enough to displace payment; the Commissioner may require supporting documents and the specified article 33 exception matters.
7. **Notify the Commissioner** (MTCA Property Tax Directorate), which vets the workings and receipts the notification. If the company owns property, an internal board decides whether to accept the declared values, and may send the department's architect (MTCA Share Transfers).
8. **Form T**: company notice within 14 days after company registration of the transfer, with applicable notification evidence. Article 120(2) requires a relevant-duty-paid, otherwise valid transfer; do not wait for the Form T clock before dealing with duty/tax. **Not yet confirmed:** the client's current due-diligence attachments and BO2 duty under S.L. 386.19, including register-of-members and other exceptions. Check this point with one of our partner accountants.
9. **Seller's return:** report a taxable gain in the return for the year after the sale.

Keep duty and the seller's gain separate: duty normally uses higher consideration/real value, while income-tax higher-market-value treatment depends on its rules. Obtain a written transfer document: Cap. 364 art. 43(1) requires writing for an inter vivos transfer executed in Malta, under pain of nullity.

### Traps

- **Conflating the property tests.** For duty, the direct test excludes current assets except immovables. For indirect property companies, article 42(2)(b) additionally requires aggregate proportional property values plus the transferring company's property to reach 75% of the specified combined non-current assets, disregarding book share values and including current immovables. The €3 increase is not charged twice where (a) already applies. Article 42(2)(c) also restricts deductible liabilities, with limited proved bank/acquisition-debt exceptions. **Not yet confirmed:** a client's complex group or liability valuation. Check this point with one of our partner accountants.
- **Duty on the price only.** It is on the higher of price and real value.
- **No site plan.** The current provisions amended by L.N. 156 and L.N. 158 of 2026 require the applicable Perit's property valuation to include a Land Registry site plan signed and stamped by a Perit.
- **Form T before notifying the Commissioner.** "No person or authority shall ... register any transfer, reduction, or allotment of company shares ... before ascertaining that such transfer ... has been notified to the Commissioner" (Cap. 364 art. 64(1), as the MBR quotes it). The full statutory gate applies where subject to duty or the specified article 42B exempt transfer of value; it has qualifications. A receipt stamp is usual evidence, not proof of an exemption or of all tax compliance.
- **Missing the 14 days.** Late filing brings penalties on company officers.
- **Forgetting provisional tax** or the seller's return.

## Worked cases

### Case 1: non-property company, real value above price

Price EUR 100,000; accepted real value EUR 120,000. Base EUR 120,000; at 2%, duty EUR 2,400. If the seller is a resident individual who bought for EUR 40,000 and this is not a controlling interest under the 18-month rights/related-transfer rules, no exemption/special-rate/property-company rule applies, and ordinary timely payment is assumed, provisional tax is 7% of the consideration, EUR 7,000, within 15 days.

```oa-check
name: Non-property share sale, real value above consideration, duty and provisional tax
inputs:
  consideration: 100000
  real_value: 120000
steps:
  - duty_base: max(consideration, real_value)
  - duty: ceil(duty_base / 100) * 2
  - provisional: pct(consideration, 7)
expected:
  duty_base: 120000
  duty: 2400
  provisional: 7000
```

### Case 2: property company

Price EUR 200,000; accepted real value EUR 180,000; the art. 42(2) property-company test is met. Duty is EUR 2 plus EUR 3 per EUR 100 on the higher EUR 200,000: EUR 4,000 + EUR 6,000 = EUR 10,000. This case assumes no exemption/reduced duty and that article 42(2) supports the increase after all group/liability conditions. The Income Tax Act property-company test must be assessed separately. **Not yet confirmed:** any prescribed property-company provisional tax and actual tax treatment for this case. Check this point with one of our partner accountants. The duty block does not compute that tax.

## Filing and payment

1. **MTCA:** submit the required auditing-credential valuation statement and supporting transfer/property documents. S.L. 364.06 rule 11(4) requires the statement in triplicate **with duty payment**, and gives a stamped acknowledged copy; notice/delivery is not valid without that payment. For ordinary documents/transfers executed in Malta, rule 2(1) requires duty within **15 working days after completion**. Cap. 364 art. 5(3)(a) treats a multiparty document as complete at its last required signature. Documents executed abroad and liable by use in Malta have a before-use rule. Keep the duty receipt annexed to the transfer document (rule 2(6)). The early notification gate is article 64; the separate PT and Form T clocks do not extend the duty deadline.
2. **Provisional tax:** apply ITMA article 43(1)(b) and (2)–(4), normally 7% consideration within 15 calendar days, or 7% higher value/consideration for controlling interests; the ordinary payment is credited against final tax for the relevant YA. The Commissioner may authorise a lower rate on proof of a gain below the statutory 20% comparison, including the higher-value comparator for controlling transfers. Do not assume this authorisation. A non-taxable transfer needs the prescribed form and requested documents; the article 33 proviso can still require tax. S.L. 123.27 rule 5(11) treats delivery/notice as invalid unless required PT accompanies it.
3. **MBR:** deliver the article 120(3) company notice within 14 days after registration with the company; transmissions have a separate one-month rule, and listed public companies have a qualified 90-day rule. This Guide's private-company cash sale uses 14 days. The MBR notice limits BAROS single-point processing to valid article 47 determination transfers **requiring Schedule E**, or transactions exempt from filing a capital-gains schedule. Duty/capital-gains-payment transactions stay on existing procedures. **Not yet confirmed:** current Section 7 portal steps, attachment fields and BO filing for this case. Check this point with one of our partner accountants.
4. **Seller's return:** for an ordinary individual outside article 11(2), the statutory return is 30 June of the YA (S.L. 372.16 rule 2(c)(ii)); for a taxable 2026 sale this is 30 June 2027. Settlement normally follows that date, subject to rule 5 exceptions; company sellers have the later ninth-month/31 March rule. An extension must be checked for the actual year. ITMA article 44(2A)/S.L. 372.28 sets 0.6% per month/part on tax due 31 August 2022 onward, capped at tax; actual start and overlapping PT/additional tax depend on the relevant provisions. **Not yet confirmed:** a client's filing exceptions, notices, extensions and default computation. Check this point with one of our partner accountants.
5. **Working paper:** the market value working, the duty computation, the property valuation and site plan, the gain computation or the exemption relied on, the Commissioner's receipt, the provisional tax receipt or exemption form, the filed Form T and BO2, the return reference. Before signing, reconcile the number, class and nominal value of shares on Form T to the agreement and the register of members, and the duty value to the market value working.

### Controlling interest and valuation boundary

S.L. 123.27 rule 5(2) tests the transferor's rights at **any time in the preceding 18 months**: at least 25% nominal capital, voting rights or ordinary-profit rights, **or** a director appointment/nomination/veto right. Rule 5(3) aggregates same/related-person transfers within 18 months; special deemed-transfer provisions also matter. It is not simply a sale of more than 50%. Higher-market-value income-tax treatment of controlling transfers therefore needs its own assessment. **Not yet confirmed:** actual rights, related-party aggregation and detailed rule 5(5)–(7) valuation/provisos, specialist alternative valuation, fixed-return-only shares or Schedule E exemption. Check this point with one of our partner accountants. Accepted values in the two original examples are assumptions, not proof of a statutory valuation.

### Late or incomplete filing

Cap. 386 art. 120(4) makes defaulting company officers liable to a penalty and continuing daily penalty. Cap. 364 art. 64(2) makes a person registering contrary to its notification gate liable for the relative duty. **Not yet confirmed:** actual late-notification/duty/default amounts and overlapping liabilities, including duty interest and remission. Check this point with one of our partner accountants. This Guide does not substitute income-tax interest for duty-law interest.
