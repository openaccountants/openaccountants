# Malta: tax pack

Malta taxes individuals on a calendar basis year, assessed in the following year of assessment (basis year 2026 is year of assessment 2027). The authority is the Malta Tax and Customs Administration (MTCA); companies register with the Malta Business Registry (MBR). Amounts are in euro. Companies pay 35% with shareholder refunds; property sales carry a final tax on the transfer value under article 5A; rent may be taxed at a 15% final rate. Where an MTCA page and the Act differ, the skills follow the Act and say so.
