# Malta: tax pack

Calendar tax year (basis year), assessed the following year. Authority: Malta Tax and Customs Administration (MTCA). Euro. Worldwide taxation for residents who are domiciled; remittance basis for resident non-domiciled individuals with a EUR 5,000 minimum tax. Corporate tax at 35% with a full imputation system and shareholder refunds. VAT at 18%, EU rules apply.
