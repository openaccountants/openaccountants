# Angola Company Formation & Entity Choice v0.1

## Scope

Use this Guide to choose an Angolan company form and assemble its formation file, capital arrangements, signed constitutive document, commercial registration and tax registration. The Guiché Único da Empresa (GUE) and Commercial Registry are the formation channels identified in the original Guide; the statute permits special immediate and online procedures, subject to implementing regulations. This is partial formation coverage: the present GUE application, current fees, payment reference and acceptance sequence were not accessible for an actual read. **Do not use this Guide alone to submit or certify a completed incorporation.** [Law 11/15, arts 12-15](https://www.cmc.ao/sites/default/files/2024-08/Lei%20n.%C2%BA%2011-15%2C%20de%2017%20de%20Junho%20-%20da%20Simplifica%C3%A7%C3%A3o%20do%20Processo%20de%20Constitui%C3%A7%C3%A3o%20de%20Sociedades%20Comercias.pdf).

The company-law rules below are the enacted texts actually read, including the 2015 simplification amendments. A private firm's description of a popular vehicle is not proof that it is the most common, that a particular form suits a larger business, or that a filing will finish on time. Those old rankings and timing claims remain held.

## Ask the client first

- Who will own the company: one or several persons, individuals or bodies, resident or non-resident, and does an intended sole owner already own a single-member company?
- What activity and licences are involved, and is any special capital or authorisation regime applicable?
- What name, actual Angolan address, management arrangements and representative authority are proposed?
- What total capital, individual quotas, cash or property contributions, payments already made and deferred amounts are agreed?
- When will the first economic exercise end, and what are the trading, VAT and staff forecasts?
- Which identity, corporate-authorisation, NIF and foreign-document validation records already exist?

## The method, step by step

### 1. Choose the entity and check the owner restrictions

| Form or question | Statutory rule and conditions | Official source |
|---|---|---|
| Sociedade por Quotas, usually ending in Lda. | Ordinary pluripersonal company requires at least two parties, unless a special rule applies. The old claim of no statutory maximum is not confirmed by this scoped read | [Law 1/04, arts 8(2)-(3), 217 and 220](https://www.cmc.ao/sites/default/files/2024-08/Lei%201-04%20-Lei%20das%20Sociedados%20Comerciais.pdf) |
| Capital of an ordinary quota company | Set freely in the contract as the sum of subscribed quotas; each nominal quota must be at least Kz 1. This does not apply to a company governed by special legislation or requiring special authorisation | [Law 11/15, arts 6 and 9; amended Law 1/04 art 221](https://www.cmc.ao/sites/default/files/2024-08/Lei%20n.%C2%BA%2011-15%2C%20de%2017%20de%20Junho%20-%20da%20Simplifica%C3%A7%C3%A3o%20do%20Processo%20de%20Constitui%C3%A7%C3%A3o%20de%20Sociedades%20Comercias.pdf) |
| Sociedade Unipessoal por Quotas | One individual or body owns all capital and subscribes the constitutive act. The single indivisible quota is set freely, with nominal value at least Kz 1; the former USD 1,000 minimum in the 2012 text was replaced | [Law 19/12, art 7](https://www.cmc.ao/sites/default/files/2024-08/Lei_19_12_Sociedades%20Unipessoais.pdf); [Law 11/15, art 20, replacing art 16(1)](https://www.cmc.ao/sites/default/files/2024-08/Lei%20n.%C2%BA%2011-15%2C%20de%2017%20de%20Junho%20-%20da%20Simplifica%C3%A7%C3%A3o%20do%20Processo%20de%20Constitui%C3%A7%C3%A3o%20de%20Sociedades%20Comercias.pdf) |
| Restrictions on a single-member route | An individual may be sole member of only one single-member company. A single-member company cannot establish another such company or participate in other commercial or civil companies; apply the restrictions before choosing a corporate sole owner | [Law 19/12, art 20(1)-(4)](https://www.cmc.ao/sites/default/files/2024-08/Lei_19_12_Sociedades%20Unipessoais.pdf) |
| Sociedade Anónima, S.A. | Capital is divided into shares. Ordinary formation requires at least five shareholders; it can be two where the State, public enterprises or legally equivalent entities hold a majority. Single-member S.A. is a distinct statutory route, not an exception invented for an ordinary S.A. | [Law 1/04, arts 301 and 304](https://www.cmc.ao/sites/default/files/2024-08/Lei%201-04%20-Lei%20das%20Sociedados%20Comerciais.pdf); [Law 19/12, arts 2 and 7](https://www.cmc.ao/sites/default/files/2024-08/Lei_19_12_Sociedades%20Unipessoais.pdf) |
| S.A. capital and governance | Ordinary S.A. capital is expressed in national currency and indexed to at least USD 20,000. Its contract must specify the form of administration and supervision. The quota-company free-capital rule does not apply; no exchange-rate conversion or full S.A. offering/board filing package is supplied here | [Law 1/04, arts 302(h) and 305(3)](https://www.cmc.ao/sites/default/files/2024-08/Lei%201-04%20-Lei%20das%20Sociedados%20Comerciais.pdf); [Law 11/15, art 6(2)](https://www.cmc.ao/sites/default/files/2024-08/Lei%20n.%C2%BA%2011-15%2C%20de%2017%20de%20Junho%20-%20da%20Simplifica%C3%A7%C3%A3o%20do%20Processo%20de%20Constitui%C3%A7%C3%A3o%20de%20Sociedades%20Comercias.pdf) |

A single-member company is not a universal personal-liability shield. Law 19/12 arts 3-4 preserve direct owner-liability provisions; art 23 makes the sole owner and any manager jointly and unlimitedly liable for transactions before registration. Do not trade under an assumed completed-company status. Ordinary pre-registration liability also needs consideration under Law 1/04 art 42 as amended by Law 11/15. [Law 19/12](https://www.cmc.ao/sites/default/files/2024-08/Lei_19_12_Sociedades%20Unipessoais.pdf); [2015 amendment](https://www.cmc.ao/sites/default/files/2024-08/Lei%20n.%C2%BA%2011-15%2C%20de%2017%20de%20Junho%20-%20da%20Simplifica%C3%A7%C3%A3o%20do%20Processo%20de%20Constitui%C3%A7%C3%A3o%20de%20Sociedades%20Comercias.pdf).

### 2. Set the capital and document when it will be paid

For an ordinary quota company, the contract must state the amounts each member pays at formation and those payable by the end of the first economic exercise, counted from definitive registration. At formation, produce deposit evidence or another proof of payment, or make the responsible declaration committing to pay the shortfall by that deadline. At the first annual general meeting after the deadline, members who deferred payment must declare that they have delivered it. If cash payment is not deferred, amended art 223 requires the company account and deposit evidence at registration. [Law 11/15, arts 7-8 and 19, amending Law 1/04 arts 222-223](https://www.cmc.ao/sites/default/files/2024-08/Lei%20n.%C2%BA%2011-15%2C%20de%2017%20de%20Junho%20-%20da%20Simplifica%C3%A7%C3%A3o%20do%20Processo%20de%20Constitui%C3%A7%C3%A3o%20de%20Sociedades%20Comercias.pdf).

The first economic exercise is not automatically the next few weeks or the incorporation anniversary: Law 1/04 art 72 sets a six-to-eighteen-month duration, without prejudice to fiscal obligations. Record the legally applicable first closing date before fixing the capital deadline. Do not use this cash-deferral workflow for property contributions or an S.A. without checking their separate rules. [Law 1/04, arts 70-73](https://www.cmc.ao/sites/default/files/2024-08/Lei%201-04%20-Lei%20das%20Sociedados%20Comerciais.pdf).

### 3. Prepare the name and constitutive document

The contract must identify the members, legal form, name, object, seat, capital, each member's quota and contribution type, payments made, and the description/value of non-cash contributions; record any special rights. The seat must be a concrete location, and the name must meet the applicable language, distinctiveness and non-misleading-name conditions. Use the approved constitutive model appropriate to the selected route; an arbitrary private template does not establish acceptance. [Law 1/04, arts 10-14](https://www.cmc.ao/sites/default/files/2024-08/Lei%201-04%20-Lei%20das%20Sociedados%20Comerciais.pdf); [Law 11/15, art 19, amending art 8](https://www.cmc.ao/sites/default/files/2024-08/Lei%20n.%C2%BA%2011-15%2C%20de%2017%20de%20Junho%20-%20da%20Simplifica%C3%A7%C3%A3o%20do%20Processo%20de%20Constitui%C3%A7%C3%A3o%20de%20Sociedades%20Comercias.pdf).

For a single-member company, use the sole member's written, articulated act with the owner's signature recognised by a notary or commercial registrar. A corporate member must provide proof of its status/identity and lawful representation. Its name includes Sociedade Unipessoal, Unipessoal or (SU) before Lda. or S.A.; its seat and decision centre are in Angola. [Law 19/12, arts 12-14](https://www.cmc.ao/sites/default/files/2024-08/Lei_19_12_Sociedades%20Unipessoais.pdf), with [Law 11/15 art 20 replacing art 12(1)](https://www.cmc.ao/sites/default/files/2024-08/Lei%20n.%C2%BA%2011-15%2C%20de%2017%20de%20Junho%20-%20da%20Simplifica%C3%A7%C3%A3o%20do%20Processo%20de%20Constitui%C3%A7%C3%A3o%20de%20Sociedades%20Comercias.pdf).

### 4. Apply the correct signature and registration route

**A public deed is generally optional, not universally compulsory.** Law 11/15 art 3 allows an approved written private instrument with signatures recognised in person. It preserves the more solemn form required for relevant property transactions and transformations between company types. A signature recognised before a registrar is not the same thing as dispensing with signature recognition. [Law 11/15, arts 3-5 and 22](https://www.cmc.ao/sites/default/files/2024-08/Lei%20n.%C2%BA%2011-15%2C%20de%2017%20de%20Junho%20-%20da%20Simplifica%C3%A7%C3%A3o%20do%20Processo%20de%20Constitui%C3%A7%C3%A3o%20de%20Sociedades%20Comercias.pdf).

After the required signatures are recognised, register the contract with the Commercial Registry. For a single-member company, art 19 separately requires registration of the constitutive act. A signed contract or a NIF is not a substitute for the registration record. Obtain the actual commercial registration certificate and check whether registration is definitive; a single-member company's provisional personality under art 10 is lost if definitive registration is not obtained within 180 days. [Law 11/15 art 19, amending Law 1/04 art 20](https://www.cmc.ao/sites/default/files/2024-08/Lei%20n.%C2%BA%2011-15%2C%20de%2017%20de%20Junho%20-%20da%20Simplifica%C3%A7%C3%A3o%20do%20Processo%20de%20Constitui%C3%A7%C3%A3o%20de%20Sociedades%20Comercias.pdf); [Law 19/12, arts 10 and 19](https://www.cmc.ao/sites/default/files/2024-08/Lei_19_12_Sociedades%20Unipessoais.pdf).

The legal framework replaces Gazette/newspaper publication with public internet publication promoted by the registry, but Law 11/15 art 24 contains a transition until the publication site is implemented. This read did not clear the implementing regulations or current publication output. Retain the actual required publication evidence; do not promise that a specific button, form or fee completes it. [Law 11/15, arts 14-15 and 24](https://www.cmc.ao/sites/default/files/2024-08/Lei%20n.%C2%BA%2011-15%2C%20de%2017%20de%20Junho%20-%20da%20Simplifica%C3%A7%C3%A3o%20do%20Processo%20de%20Constitui%C3%A7%C3%A3o%20de%20Sociedades%20Comercias.pdf).

### 5. Complete the company's and owners' NIF registration

Registration and a NIF are mandatory for individuals and bodies, with one registration and one NIF per person/entity. For an Angolan citizen the NIF is the identity-card number, **but that coincidence does not dispense with taxpayer registration**. Foreign individuals and bodies receive sequential numbers assigned by AGT. The taxpayer or an authorised representative may request registration; AGT confirms identity evidence and issues the NIF at the end of the procedure. [Decree 245/21, arts 4-8](https://www.ucm.minfin.gov.ao/cs/groups/public/documents/document/aw4x/nzm3/~edisp/minfin1737929.pdf).

For a company, art 10 lists its name, legal nature, officers' identity documents, incorporation date, seat/effective-management/permanent-establishment location, object, branches and locations where relevant, legal representative's NIF where required, phone and email. AGT may also request a public deed **or another creation instrument**, so its wording does not reinstate a universal deed requirement. For individuals use art 9, including the applicable representative details; non-residents have the separate art 11 identification, fiscal-representative and residence-evidence rules. Foreign documents and foreign signature recognitions require validation by the competent Angolan authorities under art 12. [Decree 245/21, arts 9-12](https://www.ucm.minfin.gov.ao/cs/groups/public/documents/document/aw4x/nzm3/~edisp/minfin1737929.pdf).

Capture the actual issued NIF and registration details in the file. The authenticated AGT/GUE screen sequence and a new-company accountant-certification requirement were not verified here; they remain submission holds. The 2025 amendment to the NIF regime changes suspension provisions, not a substitute for the initial registration process. [Decree 87/25, arts 1-4](https://www.ucm.minfin.gov.ao/cs/groups/public/documents/document/aw40/nzu5/~edisp/minfin4759073.pdf).

### 6. Open the first compliance file

Prepare annual management/accounts records and a minute book. Law 1/04 art 70 requires annual accounts and management reporting, ordinarily presented and considered in the first three months of each civil year, unless another legal rule applies. Law 11/15 arts 10-11 requires accounting under the General Accounting Plan and a legalised minute book; its amendment of Commercial Code art 40 requires commercial records/correspondence to be kept for ten years. These company-law duties are separate from tax returns. [Law 1/04, arts 70-73](https://www.cmc.ao/sites/default/files/2024-08/Lei%201-04%20-Lei%20das%20Sociedados%20Comerciais.pdf); [Law 11/15, arts 10-11 and 18](https://www.cmc.ao/sites/default/files/2024-08/Lei%20n.%C2%BA%2011-15%2C%20de%2017%20de%20Junho%20-%20da%20Simplifica%C3%A7%C3%A3o%20do%20Processo%20de%20Constitui%C3%A7%C3%A3o%20de%20Sociedades%20Comercias.pdf).

For Imposto Industrial general-regime companies, the recovered official AGT page gives annual Modelo 1 and final assessment/payment by the last working day of May, with the listed accounts signed by the responsible accountant. Treat that as the general rule, not a confirmed exact 2026 calendar date. The 2026 Budget art 22(1) requires electronic returns in both general and simplified regimes, with AGT assistance for those without technological capacity. [AGT, declarative obligations and payment paragraphs](https://portaldocontribuinte.minfin.gov.ao/impostos-e-taxas/imposto-industrial); [Law 14/25, arts 22(1) and 43](https://www.ucm.minfin.gov.ao/cs/groups/public/documents/document/aw41/mje2/~edisp/minfin5216784.pdf).

VAT, IRT payroll and INSS registration/returns remain part of the original annual-compliance topic. Assess each duty from the company's actual activity, VAT regime and staff; do not infer that all three monthly returns apply to every newly incorporated company. Use [VAT](ao-vat-gst.md), [payroll/social](ao-payroll-social.md) and [corporate tax](ao-corporate-income-tax.md) for the detailed jobs, including their existing unverified items. This Guide does not clear monthly social-security deadlines, an audit exemption or a live tax filing.

## Worked cases

### Case 1: one owner or two, with the same Kz 1,000 capital

An individual proposes an ordinary, unregulated cash-funded business and has not already formed a single-member company. With one owner, consider a Sociedade Unipessoal por Quotas with one Kz 1,000 quota. With two genuine members sharing capital equally, each quota is Kz 500, above the Kz 1 nominal floor, and the ordinary two-party condition is met. Capital arithmetic does not overcome the ownership restrictions or establish that either application has been accepted. Kz 1,000 is a chosen input, **not a statutory capital minimum**. [Law 11/15, arts 6 and 9 and amended single-member art 16](https://www.cmc.ao/sites/default/files/2024-08/Lei%20n.%C2%BA%2011-15%2C%20de%2017%20de%20Junho%20-%20da%20Simplifica%C3%A7%C3%A3o%20do%20Processo%20de%20Constitui%C3%A7%C3%A3o%20de%20Sociedades%20Comercias.pdf); [Law 1/04, art 8(2)](https://www.cmc.ao/sites/default/files/2024-08/Lei%201-04%20-Lei%20das%20Sociedados%20Comerciais.pdf).

```oa-check
name: One owner or two with chosen quota capital
inputs:
  capital: 1000
  two_owners: 2
  nominal_floor: 1
steps:
  - ordinary_quota: capital / two_owners
  - ordinary_margin: ordinary_quota - nominal_floor
  - single_quota: capital
  - single_margin: single_quota - nominal_floor
expected:
  ordinary_quota: 500
  ordinary_margin: 499
  single_quota: 1000
  single_margin: 999
```

### Case 2: immediate cash payment or agreed deferral

An ordinary quota company subscribes Kz 1,000. Paying all at formation leaves no unpaid capital. Alternatively, an agreement records Kz 250 paid and Kz 750 to be delivered by the legally determined end of its first economic exercise, with the required declaration and later confirmation. The calculation is a capital-funding comparison, not an incorporation fee or tax deduction. Neither option dispenses with identity, signatures or registration. [Law 11/15, arts 7-8 and amended arts 222-223](https://www.cmc.ao/sites/default/files/2024-08/Lei%20n.%C2%BA%2011-15%2C%20de%2017%20de%20Junho%20-%20da%20Simplifica%C3%A7%C3%A3o%20do%20Processo%20de%20Constitui%C3%A7%C3%A3o%20de%20Sociedades%20Comercias.pdf).

```oa-check
name: Immediate or deferred cash contribution
inputs:
  subscribed: 1000
  paid_at_formation: 250
steps:
  - immediate_payment: subscribed
  - deferred_balance: subscribed - paid_at_formation
  - funding_now_difference: immediate_payment - paid_at_formation
expected:
  immediate_payment: 1000
  deferred_balance: 750
  funding_now_difference: 750
```

## When to refuse or refer

Do not complete a formation file without real owners, identity evidence, representative authority, agreed capital and a lawful address/activity. Refer special-sector capital, public offerings, full S.A. governance, property contributions, conversions, non-resident investment/exchange-control requirements and single-member restrictions to an Angolan professional. Do not certify registration, publication, tax activation or permission to trade from this Guide's preparation steps. The source-access holds below prevent a complete submission instruction.

## Filing and payment

Use the GUE/Commercial Registry route appropriate to the chosen company and obtain its current accepted constitutive form and documentary requirements before signature/submission. This is an unresolved procedure checkpoint, not a direction to guess portal boxes or use an old private template. Keep capital payments separate from registry/notarial charges and tax payments. No current charge, reference, service standard or bank-account opening time is confirmed here. AGT registration is a distinct legal obligation and its NIF is issued only after the registration procedure.

## Completion checklist

- Record the chosen form and the genuine owners, including single-member and sector restrictions.
- Reconcile total capital to individual quotas, payment evidence/declarations and the first-exercise deadline.
- Retain the applicable constitutive instrument, recognised signatures and representative/foreign-document evidence.
- Retain actual commercial registration, required publication and NIF records; resolve provisional status and current GUE requirements before treating the file as complete.
- Establish accounting/minutes and the company's own annual/tax/payroll compliance calendar; keep all unresolved branches visible.

### Not yet confirmed: non-usable holds

The old claims that Lda. is the most common SME vehicle, that S.A. is for larger ventures, that there is no maximum quota-holder count, and the approximate Kz 1,000 capital practice are not verified as rankings or legal rules. The old turnaround of 3-5 business days and full bank-inclusive process of 4-6 weeks is not usable. Current GUE forms/fields, detailed name-clearance/publication implementation, the Formulário Único and its current accepted attachments, registry/notarial charges, payment references, any new accountant-certification condition and complete S.A./special-sector/foreign-investment procedures remain held. GUE pages/manual/leaflet could not be actually opened; search excerpts were not promoted to operative proof. The exact 2026 fiscal-calendar dates, monthly VAT/IRT/INSS applicability/deadlines and social-security rules are not cleared by this formation Guide. No bank, tax, licence or incorporation acceptance is implied.
