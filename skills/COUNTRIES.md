# Countries

Owners drive a region's packs: see [OWNERS.md](../docs/OWNERS.md). "On 2026" counts skills on the current tax year. State is the next job a pack needs: Needs writing (a required domain is missing), Needs updating (a required skill is on an old or missing tax year), Needs signature (current, not all Accountant-reviewed), Complete.
