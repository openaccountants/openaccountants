# Jordan Personal Income Tax

## Who this applies to

Any individual with income arising in Jordan, whatever the place of payment (art. 3(a)). A natural person is resident if actually present for at least 183 days in the tax period, continuous or not, or is a Jordanian employee of government or public bodies working any period of it, inside or outside Jordan (art. 2). The bands apply to every natural person (art. 11(a)). The article 9 exemptions go to the resident natural person (art. 9(a)). A resident is taxed on net foreign income only if it arose from funds or deposits from Jordan, at a flat 10% with no deductions (art. 3(c)).

Company tax is in [jo-corporate-income-tax](jo-corporate-income-tax.md). Social security is in [jo-payroll-social](jo-payroll-social.md).

## The base

Business income, interest, royalties, rents, employment income and other income listed in art. 3, less allowable expenses, then the exemptions above. Income in kind is valued at market price on the date it falls due (art. 3(b)). E-commerce income is taxable (art. 3(d)). Exemptions, including certain dividends, are in art. 4.

A non-resident Jordanian may use the dependants exemption for dependants resident in Jordan whom they support (art. 9(d)). A woman who supports dependants may claim the dependants exemption; if several supporters claim, it is split by share of support with evidence, otherwise equally (art. 9(e)). Either spouse may use the unused part of the exemption, within JOD 23,000 (art. 9(c)).

How to file: log in to ISTD e-services with the tax number, open the individual return, enter income and exemptions, submit, then pay any balance by 30 April. Screen names and box numbers are not confirmed. Some groups are excused from filing under a regulation (art. 17(e)); check it before telling anyone they need not file.

## Penalties and interest

Late payment: 0.4% (four per thousand) of the balance for each week or part of a week; penalties may not exceed the tax (art. 36). If the return was on time and a later difference is up to JOD 5,000, the charge runs from the audit notice (art. 36(b)). The audit window is four years from filing (art. 34).

## Cross-border points

A non-resident's service income is taxable where the work is done in Jordan or its output is used there (art. 3(a)). Payers withhold 10% on non-exempt income paid to a non-resident on the due or payment date, whichever is earlier, and give a statement (art. 12(b)). Treaty relief is not covered here.

## Two worked examples that recompute

Simple: single resident, no dependants, income JOD 20,000, with JOD 1,000 of qualifying rent. Exemptions JOD 10,000, taxable JOD 10,000, tax JOD 750.

```oa-check
name: Single resident, income JOD 20,000
inputs:
  income: 20000
steps:
  - taxable: income - 9000 - 1000
  - b1: min(taxable, 5000) * 0.05
  - b2: (min(taxable, 10000) - 5000) * 0.10
  - tax: b1 + b2
expected:
  tax: 750.00
```

Edge: resident at the family cap with JOD 300,000. Taxable JOD 277,000. The first four bands cost JOD 2,500, the rest JOD 64,250, and the 1% contribution on JOD 77,000 adds JOD 770.

```oa-check
name: Family at cap, income JOD 300,000
inputs:
  income: 300000
steps:
  - taxable: income - 23000
  - bands: 250 + 500 + 750 + 1000
  - top: (taxable - 20000) * 0.25
  - nc: (taxable - 200000) * 0.01
  - total: bands + top + nc
expected:
  total: 67520.00
```

## Ask the client first

- Days in Jordan in 2026, and nationality if working for a Jordanian public body.
- Spouse income, and whether a joint return is agreed.
- Dependants and who supports them.
- Income types: salary, business, rent, foreign funds.
- Qualifying expense receipts and tax already withheld.
