# Ethiopia Tax Overview v0.3

## Who this applies to

Initial scoping of Ethiopian income tax and VAT in 2026. This is a federal overview, not a complete return or a regional-tax/customs computation. Amounts are Ethiopian Birr (ETB). The Ministry of Revenues handles federal tax; customs questions go to the Ethiopian Customs Commission. Use the relevant detailed Guide after establishing the income type and taxpayer's period.

Required inputs: legal form, residence and income source, accounting-year dates, income type and amount, taxable supplies and VAT status, and any foreign activity or special regime.

## The base

Resident persons are within income tax on income from Ethiopia and elsewhere; non-residents on Ethiopian-source income (979/2016 art. 7). The source and residence tests must be established before scoping the return. [Income Tax Proclamation arts 5 to 8](https://justice.gov.et/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/%E1%8A%A0%E1%8B%8B%E1%8C%85-%E1%89%81%E1%8C%A5%E1%88%AD-979-2008.pdf).

The former blanket fiscal-year statement needs a distinction: for an individual the default is Hamle 1 to Sene 30, unless the Authority permits a different accounting year in writing; for a body the tax year is its accounting year. Article 28 also covers accounting and transitional years (979 art. 2(21), art. 28, renumbered art. 30). Record Ethiopian-calendar dates and convert the relevant year's dates accurately. Do not apply an assumed 8 July to 7 July year to every company. [979/2016](https://justice.gov.et/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/%E1%8A%A0%E1%8B%8B%E1%8C%85-%E1%89%81%E1%8C%A5%E1%88%AD-979-2008.pdf); [1395/2025 renumbering](https://www.hopr.gov.et/admin/Files/2026/4/8/5fc57d68-d0f6-4bf1-91b4-5b23abd0df72%E1%8C%88%E1%89%A2%20%E1%8C%8D%E1%89%A5%E1%88%AD%20%201395.pdf).

The 2025 amendment's ordinary changes apply to income derived from 8 July 2025; it has separate commencement clauses for alternative minimum tax and Schedule D/transaction withholding. The employment example is within the ordinary commencement limb (1395 art. 4(3)). [Gazetted amendment](https://www.hopr.gov.et/admin/Files/2026/4/8/5fc57d68-d0f6-4bf1-91b4-5b23abd0df72%E1%8C%88%E1%89%A2%20%E1%8C%8D%E1%89%A5%E1%88%AD%20%201395.pdf).

## Deadlines and filing

- Category A annual filing follows the actual taxpayer year-end and the four-month period above. Keep the profit-and-loss account and balance sheet required by art. 83(5)(a), now art. 87. The old draft's automatic 7 November Gregorian due date is not a verified conversion for every year/company. [979 arts 83(4), (5)](https://justice.gov.et/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/%E1%8A%A0%E1%8B%8B%E1%8C%85-%E1%89%81%E1%8C%A5%E1%88%AD-979-2008.pdf); [1395 renumbering](https://www.hopr.gov.et/admin/Files/2026/4/8/5fc57d68-d0f6-4bf1-91b4-5b23abd0df72%E1%8C%88%E1%89%A2%20%E1%8C%8D%E1%89%A5%E1%88%AD%20%201395.pdf).
- VAT returns are monthly, even with no net VAT due. Payment is due when the return is required (1341 art. 59(1)); preserve the Ethiopian accounting-month definition. [VAT Proclamation arts 2, 58, 59](https://www.hopr.gov.et/admin/Files/2026/1/21/237b0abc-d9d2-4749-96a8-7cf2ae7dbcf91341-2016%20%E1%8B%A8%E1%89%B0%E1%8C%A8%E1%88%9B%E1%88%AA%20%E1%8A%A5%E1%88%B4%E1%89%B5%20%E1%89%B3%E1%8A%AD%E1%88%B5%20%E1%8A%A0%E1%8B%8B%E1%8C%85.pdf).
- Use the [Ministry of Revenues](https://www.mor.gov.et/domestic-law-proclamation) to locate the current filing service and forms. Authenticated submission, box numbers and payment-reference generation were not read/tested here, so this Guide does not authorise filing from a guessed screen.

Refer the case with the return, amount, due date, payment date and notices.

## Cross-border points

Residence and source change the income-tax scope; identify all foreign income and any permanent establishment before computing. Do not use the headline rates as treaty withholding rates. Treaty entitlement, foreign credits, non-resident withholding and customs duties require their applicable authority and are outside this initial overview. The old customs-authority routing is retained; no customs rate is asserted.

## Two worked examples that recompute

**Preserved simple case.** A VAT-registered business makes an ordinary taxable sale with net value ETB 100,000. At 15% the output VAT is ETB 15,000. This assumes the supply is not zero-rated or exempt and does not determine net return liability. [1341 art. 8(2), (3)](https://www.hopr.gov.et/admin/Files/2026/1/21/237b0abc-d9d2-4749-96a8-7cf2ae7dbcf91341-2016%20%E1%8B%A8%E1%89%B0%E1%8C%A8%E1%88%9B%E1%88%AA%20%E1%8A%A5%E1%88%B4%E1%89%B5%20%E1%89%B3%E1%8A%AD%E1%88%B5%20%E1%8A%A0%E1%8B%8B%E1%8C%85.pdf).

```oa-check
name: Standard VAT
inputs:
  v: 100000
steps:
  - vat: v * 0.15
expected:
  vat: 15000.00
```

**Preserved edge case.** Monthly taxable employment income ETB 30,000, with no exemption or taxable-base adjustment assumed. The lower taxable slices contribute 300 + 600 + 750 + 1,200 = ETB 2,850; the excess ETB 16,000 contributes ETB 5,600, total employment tax ETB 8,450. This is income tax, not net pay; pension is not computed here. [1395 art. 2(6), replacing art. 11; commencement art. 4(3)](https://www.hopr.gov.et/admin/Files/2026/4/8/5fc57d68-d0f6-4bf1-91b4-5b23abd0df72%E1%8C%88%E1%89%A2%20%E1%8C%8D%E1%89%A5%E1%88%AD%20%201395.pdf).

```oa-check
name: Top band employment tax
inputs:
  s: 30000
steps:
  - lower: 300 + 600 + 750 + 1200
  - top: (s - 14000) * 0.35
  - tax: lower + top
expected:
  lower: 2850.00
  top: 5600.00
  tax: 8450.00
```

## Ask the client first

- Individual, company, LLP, collective fund or another legal form?
- Residence and all Ethiopian/foreign income sources?
- Actual accounting year and Ethiopian-calendar year-end dates?
- Employment, business, rental or investment income, and any special regime?
- VAT registration, taxable supplies and the nature of each supply?
- Any employees, foreign activity, overdue filing, tax notice or claimed treaty relief?
