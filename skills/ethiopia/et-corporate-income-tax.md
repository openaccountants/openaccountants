# Ethiopia Corporate Income Tax

## Corporate income tax rates and base

Resident companies pay corporate income tax at a flat 30% on net business profit; non-resident companies are taxed on Ethiopian-source income. The 2025 amendment introduced a minimum alternative tax, a tax on undistributed profits, and higher dividend/interest withholding rates.

- **Standard corporate income tax rate** — 30 %  _([Income Tax Proclamation No. 979/2016](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/ethiopia/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income))_
- **Tax base** — Net business profit (gross income less deductible expenses) per the financial accounts as adjusted for tax  _(Income Tax Proclamation No. 979/2016)_
- **Mining / petroleum sector rate** — Mining 25%; petroleum operations subject to separate sector rules % ((approx — confirm current sector rate))  _([Mining Income Tax Proclamation](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/ethiopia/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income))_
- **Minimum Alternative Tax (MAT)** — 2.5% of annual turnover where the declared tax liability falls below that amount; creditable against future tax %  _([Income Tax (Amendment) Proclamation No. 1395/2025](https://www.afriwise.com/blog/update-on-ethiopias-new-income-tax-amendment-proclamation))_
- **Tax on undistributed profits** — 15% on undistributed profits of corporate bodies unless reinvested (used to increase capital/shareholders' equity) within 12 months of year-end %  _([Income Tax (Amendment) Proclamation No. 1395/2025](https://chambers.com/articles/what-s-changed-under-ethiopia-s-new-income-tax-amendment-proclamation-no-1395-2025))_
- **Withholding tax on dividends** — 15% (increased from 10%) %  _([Income Tax (Amendment) Proclamation No. 1395/2025](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/ethiopia/corporate/withholding-taxes))_
- **Withholding tax on interest** — 10% (increased from 5%); final tax %  _([Income Tax (Amendment) Proclamation No. 1395/2025](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/ethiopia/corporate/withholding-taxes))_
- **Withholding tax on royalties** — 5% to 10% depending on residency/context % ((approx — confirm split between resident/non-resident))  _([Income Tax (Amendment) Proclamation No. 1395/2025](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/ethiopia/corporate/withholding-taxes))_
- **Withholding on domestic supplies of goods/services** — 2% withholding on payments for goods/services above a de minimis threshold % ((approx — confirm threshold))  _([Income Tax Proclamation No. 979/2016](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/ethiopia/corporate/withholding-taxes))_
- **Non-resident taxable-presence (PE) time threshold** — Reduced from 183 days to 91 days for services and construction/installation projects  _([Income Tax (Amendment) Proclamation No. 1395/2025](https://www.afriwise.com/blog/update-on-ethiopias-new-income-tax-amendment-proclamation))_
- **Annual return filing/payment deadline** — Within 4 months of the end of the tax year ((approx — confirm))  _([Tax Administration Proclamation No. 983/2016](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/ethiopia/individual/tax-administration))_
- **Quarterly advance income tax (large taxpayers)** — Category A large taxpayers make quarterly advance payments, settled within 30 days after each 3-month cycle  _([Ministry of Finance advance income tax directive (2025)](https://ethiodiasporahub.com/ethiopia-clarifies-advance-tax-payment-schedule/))_
