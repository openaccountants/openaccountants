# Philippines Company Formation & Entity Choice

2026 rules checked on 2026-11-16; version 1.1. Not yet approved. Obtain Philippine legal advice for restricted activities and a Philippine CPA's review of the tax registrations.

## Scope

Choose the legal vehicle before submitting its name, ownership and capital. This Guide covers ordinary formation and continuing-registration decisions. Foreign-company branches and representative offices remain separate SEC licensing routes; the domestic incorporation checklist does not establish their eligibility or permit a representative office to trade. [RA 11232, sections 10, 116 and 140-143](https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/2/86463).

| Vehicle | Registration route and decision |
|---|---|
| Domestic stock corporation | SEC; determine ownership, subscribers, directors, capital and permitted purpose. [RA 11232, sections 10-14](https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/2/86463) |
| One Person Corporation (OPC) | SEC; a single eligible stockholder, with nominee arrangements and separate corporate records. [RA 11232, sections 116-130](https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/2/86463) |
| Partnership | SEC; agree the partners' terms and confirm professional-practice restrictions. [FIA IRR, sections 19 and 21](https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/5/96433) |
| Sole proprietorship | DTI business-name registration if trading under a name other than the proprietor's true name; BIR and local permits are separate. [DTI FAQs](https://bnrs.dti.gov.ph/faq) |
| Foreign-company branch or representative office | SEC foreign-company licensing; obtain a specific activity, remittance and tax review. [RA 11232, sections 140-143](https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/2/86463) |

## Ask the client first

- What will the business actually do, and in which municipality will it operate? Identify any regulated activity or professional practice before choosing a corporation.
- Who are the legal and beneficial owners, their nationalities, and their voting rights? Is a foreign parent setting up a subsidiary, branch or representative office?
- Is this a new incorporation or an increase in an existing corporation's authorised capital? State authorised capital, subscriptions, paid amounts and any non-cash contributions separately.
- Will it serve the Philippine domestic market or export? For a claimed foreign-capital exception, obtain the agency certificate and employee undertaking.
- Will it employ staff, and when will business commence? Establish the BIR, LGU and employer-registration sequence before that date.
- What are its year-end, total assets, total liabilities and gross annual sales, earnings, receipts or output? These change the annual filing and audit requirements.

## The method, step by step

### 1. Match owners and activities to a permitted vehicle

An ordinary corporation uses the SEC route; DTI registration alone does not incorporate it. Natural-person incorporators must be of legal age and each stock-corporation incorporator must hold or subscribe to a share. The Code permits no more than **15 incorporators**; a corporation with a single stockholder follows the OPC chapter. The ordinary multi-owner route therefore has **2 to 15 incorporators**. The OPC's single stockholder must be a natural person, trust or estate; the Code excludes specified financial/public entities and generally prevents an OPC being used to practise a licensed profession unless a special law permits it. [RA 11232, sections 10 and 116](https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/2/86463).

For an OPC, identify the nominee and alternate nominee and their written consent in the articles. No bylaws are required. Put **OPC below or at the end of the corporate name**, as section 120 permits; it is not limited to the end. The single stockholder is director and president, cannot also be corporate secretary, and must keep the corporation's funds and records separate. No minimum capital is not immunity from liability for an inadequately financed or commingled OPC. [RA 11232, sections 118-124 and 127-130](https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/2/86463).

### 2. Apply the correct capital test

| Situation | Capital rule and conditions | Official authority |
|---|---|---|
| Ordinary stock corporation on original incorporation | No general statutory minimum capital stock; special-law requirements still apply. Do not import the later-increase subscription tests into original incorporation. | [RA 11232, sections 12-14](https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/2/86463) |
| OPC | No general minimum authorised capital stock; special laws, nationality restrictions and adequate financing still matter. | [RA 11232, sections 117 and 130](https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/2/86463) |
| Later increase in a stock corporation's capital | At least **25% of the increase subscribed**, and **25% of the subscribed increase paid** in cash or transferred property; SEC approval and the treasurer's sworn statement are required. This is a different event from original incorporation. | [RA 11232, section 37](https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/2/86463) |
| Non-Philippine national's micro/small domestic-market enterprise | The general reservation to Philippine nationals applies below **USD 200,000 paid-in equity**, subject to the statutory exception below and separate retail/other special laws. A foreign shareholding alone does not settle Philippine-national status. | [FIA IRR, sections 1(ii) and 39(c)](https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/5/96433) |
| Qualifying reduced foreign-capital route | **USD 100,000 minimum paid-in equity** if any one route is satisfied: DOST-determined advanced technology; DTI/DICT/DOST startup or startup-enabler endorsement; or a notarised undertaking that a majority of direct employees are Filipino and at least **15 direct employees are Filipino**. Obtain the respective agency certificate. | [FIA IRR, sections 19(c) and 39(c)](https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/5/96433) |

It does not establish every field or assessment currently imposed by the SEC portal. The separate practical-capital claim remains under Sources, not a statutory floor.

The IRR requires DOLE validation within **6 months from commercial operations** for the employment undertaking; failure can require the higher investment. Meeting a capital test does not override a nationality restriction in the Foreign Investment Negative List or a special law. [FIA IRR, sections 18-20 and 39](https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/5/96433).

### 3. Assemble and submit the correct registration

For a domestic corporation, document the proposed name, purposes, Philippine principal office, incorporators, directors, authorised shares, subscriptions and payments in the articles. Add the OPC nominee particulars where relevant. Use the [SEC eSPARC entry point](https://esparc.sec.gov.ph/) to select the available processing route, retain the submitted articles, payment assessment and certificate, and reconcile them to the agreed ownership and capital. The statute permits electronic filing; the live eSPARC/OneSEC screens were blocked during this review, so this Guide does not invent their current button names or promise OneSEC eligibility. [RA 11232, sections 13-14 and 118](https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/2/86463).

For a sole proprietor, use [DTI BNRS](https://bnrs.dti.gov.ph/faq) when a business name is required. A DTI business-name certificate is not the business/Mayor's permit. For partnerships and foreign-company offices, use the applicable SEC application rather than filing domestic-corporation articles. [DTI FAQs](https://bnrs.dti.gov.ph/faq); [FIA IRR, sections 19 and 21-22](https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/5/96433).

Before paying SEC fees, obtain its actual payment assessment and check the capital basis, legal research component and any separate charges. The former fee formula and Jonathan's credit are preserved as unresolved evidence under Sources; no total registration quote is produced from an unread schedule.

### 4. Complete tax, local and employer registrations

Register with the BIR **on or before commencement of business**, obtaining or confirming the TIN, Certificate of Registration, applicable tax types, books and invoicing arrangements. Use the RDO or authorised electronic registration route appropriate to the entity and retain the confirmation; do not confuse the SEC certificate with BIR registration. [NIRC section 236(A)-(B), as amended by RA 11976 section 34](https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/2/96948); [BIR RMC 97-2021, section 6(A)-(B)](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/local/pdf/RMC%20No.%2097-2021%20%281%29.pdf).

Obtain the LGU business/Mayor's permit and the clearances the particular location and activity require. Add employer registrations with **SSS, PhilHealth and Pag-IBIG if staff are engaged**, and establish payroll/remittance records; the contribution computations are addressed in [Philippine social contributions](ph-sss-philhealth.md). Do not treat an owner's self-employed registration as the employer account. [DTI permit FAQ](https://bnrs.dti.gov.ph/faq); [SSS compulsory coverage](https://www.sss.gov.ph/compulsory-coverage/); [RA 11223, sections 4(f) and 10](https://www.philhealth.gov.ph/about_us/RA11223_UHC.pdf); [Pag-IBIG Circular 460, C.1.1-C.1.2](https://naro.law.upd.edu.ph/storage/01JQWKTTA92H57M9EWV31W8F1D.pdf).

The BIR annual registration fee was abolished from **22 January 2024** for new and existing business taxpayers. An existing COR showing that fee remains valid. Do not renew a BIR registration annually merely to pay the abolished fee, and do not relabel it documentary stamp tax. Any documentary stamp tax actually arising on a separate instrument requires its own legal basis and calculation. [BIR RMC 14-2024, first page](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/local/pdf/RMC%20No.%2014-2024.pdf).

## Worked cases

### A. Foreign-capital route: an undertaking changes the minimum

Assume a permitted domestic-market activity, an investor that is not a Philippine national, no retail/special-law override, and **20 direct employees, 15 Filipino**. The Filipino employees form a majority and meet the numerical floor. With the required DOLE certificate and genuine undertaking, the reduced capital route permits **USD 100,000** rather than the general **USD 200,000** boundary, a **USD 100,000** difference. If only 10 of those 20 employees were Filipino, the majority and floor would both fail; without another qualifying route, the lower-capital plan cannot proceed. These are eligibility alternatives, not permission to adjust declared headcount. [FIA IRR, sections 19(c) and 39(c)](https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/5/96433).

```oa-check
name: Foreign domestic-market capital and staffing decision
inputs:
  direct_employees: 20
  filipino_employees: 15
  alternative_filipino_employees: 10
  general_capital: 200000
  qualifying_capital: 100000
steps:
  filipino_share: filipino_employees / direct_employees
  alternative_share: alternative_filipino_employees / direct_employees
  capital_difference: general_capital - qualifying_capital
expected:
  filipino_share: 0.75
  alternative_share: 0.50
  capital_difference: 100000
```

### B. Existing corporation: the increase has its own subscription test

An existing ordinary stock corporation proposes a **PHP 4,000,000 increase** in authorised capital. On the minimum section 37 subscription/payment assumptions, **PHP 1,000,000 must be subscribed** and **PHP 250,000 paid** in actual cash or qualifying transferred property. The **PHP 750,000** unpaid balance of that subscription remains a subscription obligation. Board/stockholder approvals, the sworn treasurer's statement and SEC approval are still necessary. This does not create a minimum paid-up figure for a new corporation. [RA 11232, section 37](https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/2/86463).

```oa-check
name: Capital increase subscriptions and initial payment
inputs:
  capital_increase: 4000000
steps:
  minimum_subscription: capital_increase * 0.25
  minimum_payment: minimum_subscription * 0.25
  unpaid_subscription: minimum_subscription - minimum_payment
expected:
  minimum_subscription: 1000000
  minimum_payment: 250000
  unpaid_subscription: 750000
```

## When to refuse or refer

- Do not clear foreign ownership or a reduced-capital route without the activity classification, beneficial-ownership analysis and applicable certificate/undertaking. Refer restricted activities and professional practice to Philippine counsel.
- Do not equate an incorporation certificate or DTI business-name certificate with authority to operate, BIR registration, or employer compliance.
- Do not quote the unread SEC fee schedule, the former practical capital floor or an overall completion time as confirmed current requirements.
- Do not waive an SEC audit because turnover falls below the BIR threshold, or attach an audit indiscriminately without checking both agencies' tests and applicable SEC issuance.
- Do not apply the earlier 2026 AFS filing date to accounts ending in December 2026. Leave the later filing deadline pending its governing issuance.
- Do not certify a company, its officers or its filings on the strength of this Guide marked Not yet approved. Obtain the responsible Philippine professional's review before submission.
