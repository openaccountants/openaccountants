# Philippines Tax Overview

2026 rules checked on 2026-11-16; version 1.1. Not yet approved, pending Philippine professional review. Monetary amounts below are Philippine pesos (PHP).

## Scope

The National Internal Revenue Code (https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/2/3896) governs national income tax and VAT, administered by the **Bureau of Internal Revenue (BIR)** under the Department of Finance. Read the Code with its amendments, including TRAIN, CREATE, CREATE MORE, EOPT and the more recent transaction-specific changes. A historical version of a section is not a current rate card. [NIRC section 2, BIR powers](https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/2/3896); [RR 8-2018 implementing TRAIN](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/local/pdf/RR%20No.%208-2018.pdf); [BIR RR 7-2025 implementing CREATE MORE](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/RR%20No.%207-2025.pdf).

This overview establishes the tax year, geographical income scope, ordinary rates and annual filing route. It does not calculate a final return without the income classification, deductions, exemptions, withholding and other credits. VAT is a separate transaction tax; an income-tax exemption or rate does not by itself determine VAT liability. [NIRC sections 23, 31 and 43](https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/2/3896); [NIRC 108, amended by EOPT section 17](https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/2/96948).

## Ask the client first

- Is the taxpayer an individual, corporation, partnership or another entity? Obtain incorporation/registration documents; a general professional partnership is not automatically taxed as an ordinary corporation.
- For an individual, what are citizenship, actual residence, dates abroad and Philippine/foreign income sources? For a company, where was it incorporated and does it do business in the Philippines?
- What is the taxable period and approved year-end? Separate 2026 income from an earlier year's return filed during 2026.
- What are ordinary taxable income, gross sales, passive receipts, capital transactions, expenses and withholding certificates? Establish amounts and source before applying a headline rate.
- Is the business VAT registered, and is the particular transaction standard-rated, zero-rated, exempt or outside Philippine VAT? Is an agreed price VAT-inclusive or exclusive?
- Does a special corporate regime, registered-project incentive, treaty, substituted filing or other exemption apply, and what documents establish its conditions?

## The method, step by step

### 1. Set the taxpayer, currency and taxable period

Individuals use the **calendar year ending 31 December**. Corporations may use a calendar or fiscal year; changing the accounting period requires the Commissioner's approval. Record the actual year-end instead of assuming all annual corporate returns are calendar-year returns. The PHP figures below are pesos, not foreign currency or amounts in thousands. [NIRC sections 22(P)-(Q), 43, 46 and 52(B)](https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/2/3896).

The corporate definition includes many partnerships and associations, but excludes qualifying general professional partnerships and specified joint ventures. Resolve that classification before loading a company rate merely because an entity has registered with SEC. [NIRC section 22(B)](https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/2/3896).

### 2. Establish geographical income scope before selecting a rate

| Taxpayer | Ordinary geographical scope and condition | Official authority |
|---|---|---|
| Philippine citizen residing in the Philippines | **Worldwide income**, subject to specific exclusions, exemptions and separately taxed categories. | [NIRC 23(A)](https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/2/3896) |
| Nonresident Philippine citizen | **Philippine-source income only**; establish nonresident-citizen status and any year-of-arrival/departure distinction, not merely an overseas address. | [NIRC 22(E), 23(B)-(C)](https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/2/3896) |
| Resident alien | **Philippine-source income only**. A foreign citizen living in the Philippines is not thereby a Philippine citizen taxed on worldwide income. | [NIRC 22(F), 23(D)](https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/2/3896) |
| Nonresident alien | **Philippine-source income only**; the engaged/not-engaged classification affects the rate and filing method. Do not automatically apply the graduated table below. | [NIRC 23(D), 25; CMEPA's current nonresident rules](https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/2/99213) |
| Domestic corporation | **Worldwide income**, with the appropriate statutory exceptions and separately taxed receipts. Domestic means incorporated under Philippine law. | [NIRC 22(C), 23(E)](https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/2/3896) |
| Foreign corporation, resident or nonresident | **Philippine-source income only**; whether it does business here affects ordinary net-versus-gross taxation and special rules. | [NIRC 22(D), (H)-(I), 23(F)](https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/2/3896) |

“Paid from abroad” and “foreign-source” are not interchangeable. Classify the income under the applicable source rule before excluding it. For a disputed cross-border or treaty position, obtain a specific review; this overview does not invent a treaty exemption. [NIRC section 42](https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/2/3896).

### 3. Apply ordinary income-tax rates to taxable income

The graduated individual schedule below applies to the relevant individual ordinary taxable income under section 24(A), effective **1 January 2023 onwards**. The first **PHP 250,000 of taxable income** falls in the zero band; it is not a universal deduction against every gross receipt or final-taxed payment. [RR 8-2018 section 3, NIRC 24(A)(2)(a)](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/local/pdf/RR%20No.%208-2018.pdf).

| Annual ordinary taxable income | 2026 tax calculation | Official authority |
|---|---|---|
| Not over **PHP 250,000** | **0%** | [RR 8-2018 section 3, schedule from 2023 onwards](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/local/pdf/RR%20No.%208-2018.pdf) |
| Over **PHP 250,000**, not over **PHP 400,000** | **15%** of excess over **PHP 250,000** | [RR 8-2018 section 3, NIRC 24(A)](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/local/pdf/RR%20No.%208-2018.pdf) |
| Over **PHP 400,000**, not over **PHP 800,000** | **PHP 22,500 + 20%** of excess over **PHP 400,000** | [RR 8-2018 section 3, NIRC 24(A)](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/local/pdf/RR%20No.%208-2018.pdf) |
| Over **PHP 800,000**, not over **PHP 2,000,000** | **PHP 102,500 + 25%** of excess over **PHP 800,000** | [RR 8-2018 section 3, NIRC 24(A)](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/local/pdf/RR%20No.%208-2018.pdf) |
| Over **PHP 2,000,000**, not over **PHP 8,000,000** | **PHP 402,500 + 30%** of excess over **PHP 2,000,000** | [RR 8-2018 section 3, NIRC 24(A)](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/local/pdf/RR%20No.%208-2018.pdf) |
| Over **PHP 8,000,000** | **PHP 2,202,500 + 35%** of excess over **PHP 8,000,000** | [RR 8-2018 section 3, NIRC 24(A)](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/local/pdf/RR%20No.%208-2018.pdf) |

A business's gross receipts are not the same as its taxable income. Establish the lawful deduction/election and separate final-taxed receipts first. Qualifying alternative regimes need their own conditions; neither the zero band nor a headline maximum rate substitutes for that classification. [NIRC sections 31 and 34](https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/2/3896); [RR 8-2018 sections 3 and 8](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/local/pdf/RR%20No.%208-2018.pdf).

| Corporate rate | Conditions beside the figure | Official authority |
|---|---|---|
| **25%** | Ordinary domestic corporate income-tax rate on taxable income, subject to qualifying reduced/special regimes. | [BIR RR 7-2025, section 2(A)](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/RR%20No.%207-2025.pdf) |
| **20%** | Smaller domestic company only if taxable income is **not over PHP 5,000,000 AND relevant total assets not over PHP 100,000,000**, excluding the specified office/plant/equipment land. | [BIR RR 7-2025, section 2(A)](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/RR%20No.%207-2025.pdf) |
| **25%** | Ordinary resident foreign corporation rate on **Philippine-source taxable income**; do not extend the smaller-domestic-company test to it. | [BIR RR 7-2025, section 2(B)](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/RR%20No.%207-2025.pdf) |

Registered enterprises under the enhanced deductions regime have a separate reduced rate for registered activities. MCIT, OSD, dividends, interest and branch remittances also need separate calculations. Use [corporate income tax](ph-corporate-income-tax.md) for those jobs; the ordinary corporate rates above are not a tax on all bank receipts. [RR 7-2025, sections 2-3](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/RR%20No.%207-2025.pdf).

### 4. Treat VAT as a separate computation

The standard VAT rate is **12%**; for an ordinary taxable service, section 108(A) imposes it on gross sales. First determine registration and whether the particular supply is taxable, zero-rated, exempt or outside the scope. Do not charge VAT solely because a company pays corporate income tax, or apply the standard rate to a transaction with a specific different treatment. [NIRC 108(A), EOPT section 17](https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/2/96948).

For a confirmed standard-rated transaction, distinguish the net sale from its VAT-inclusive invoice total. Output VAT is not automatically the net VAT payable for a period: eligible input tax, withholding and other statutory adjustments require a separate supported return calculation. [NIRC 110, EOPT section 19 VAT credit method](https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/2/96948). This overview gives a transaction example below, not a complete VAT return.

### 5. Choose the actual annual return and preserve filing/payment evidence

| Annual return | Ordinary statutory deadline and filing condition | Official authority |
|---|---|---|
| Individual income tax return | **15 April of the following year**, where an individual return is required. The statutory zero-income band does not automatically waive a business/professional's filing obligation. Confirm any substituted filing or other actual exemption. | [NIRC 51(C)](https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/2/3896); [RR 4-2024 section 5, current filing-exemption boundary](https://naro.law.upd.edu.ph/storage/01JT27H00Q1JABYK2R53AB4D14.pdf) |
| Corporate final adjustment return | **15 April** following a calendar year, or the **15th day of the fourth month after fiscal year-end**. Choose the proper return for the company's actual tax position and pay at filing. | [NIRC 77(B)-(C), amended by EOPT section 11](https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/2/96948) |

An individual may need **1700, 1701 or 1701A**, depending on the income and applicable regime; a corporation may need **1702-RT, 1702-MX or 1702-EX**. Obtain the current version and the exact filing instructions rather than choosing from the amount payable alone. The BIR's 2026 annual circular concerns **2025 income filed in 2026**. Its eFPS/eBIRForms/provider routes and evidence instructions help identify the process, but do not announce a new deadline for accounts ending in 2026. [BIR RMC 20-2026 digest](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/RMC%20No.%2020-2026%20Digest.pdf).

At handover, retain the tax classification, taxable-income reconciliation, source analysis, supporting withholding certificates, the filed return, attachments and separate payment confirmation. Where a zero return or filing exemption is claimed, record its legal conditions. Do not treat an annual return as proof that every quarterly or withholding obligation has been completed.

## Worked cases

### A. The same income amounts have a different geographical base

Assume **PHP 1,000,000 Philippine-source** and **PHP 500,000 foreign-source ordinary taxable income**, after the relevant deductions, for a whole calendar year. No final-taxed items, treaty relief or foreign-tax credits apply. A resident Philippine citizen includes both, giving **PHP 1,500,000** and **PHP 277,500** ordinary tax. A resident alien includes only the Philippine-source **PHP 1,000,000**, giving **PHP 152,500** ordinary tax. The difference is **PHP 125,000** before any payment credits. Citizenship/residence and true source decide which computation applies; the taxpayer cannot choose the favourable category. [NIRC 23(A), (D)](https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/2/3896); [RR 8-2018 section 3, 2023-onwards schedule](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/local/pdf/RR%20No.%208-2018.pdf).

```oa-check
name: Resident citizen versus resident alien income scope
inputs:
  philippine_taxable_income: 1000000
  foreign_taxable_income: 500000
steps:
  citizen_base: philippine_taxable_income + foreign_taxable_income
  alien_base: philippine_taxable_income
  citizen_tax: 102500 + (citizen_base - 800000) * 0.25
  alien_tax: 102500 + (alien_base - 800000) * 0.25
  tax_difference: citizen_tax - alien_tax
expected:
  citizen_base: 1500000
  alien_base: 1000000
  citizen_tax: 277500
  alien_tax: 152500
  tax_difference: 125000
```

### B. A VAT-inclusive quote is not the net sale

Assume a VAT-registered supplier provides an ordinary **12% standard-rated service**, with an agreed **PHP 112,000 VAT-inclusive price**. The net sale is **PHP 100,000** and output VAT **PHP 12,000**. If the agreement instead says **PHP 112,000 exclusive of VAT**, output VAT is **PHP 13,440** and the invoice total **PHP 125,440**. Identify the contract basis before issuing the invoice. Neither case calculates the supplier's net quarterly VAT payable or income tax. [NIRC 108(A), EOPT section 17](https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/2/96948).

```oa-check
name: VAT-inclusive versus exclusive contract price
inputs:
  quoted_price: 112000
  vat_rate: 0.12
steps:
  inclusive_net: quoted_price / (1 + vat_rate)
  inclusive_vat: quoted_price - inclusive_net
  exclusive_vat: quoted_price * vat_rate
  exclusive_total: quoted_price + exclusive_vat
expected:
  inclusive_net: 100000
  inclusive_vat: 12000
  exclusive_vat: 13440
  exclusive_total: 125440
```

## When to refuse or refer

- Do not select a geographical base or rate from nationality, a bank location, an overseas customer or an entity's name alone. Obtain the legal classification and income-source facts.
- Do not apply graduated personal rates to all nonresidents, final-taxed passive receipts or specially taxed capital transactions.
- Do not grant the reduced corporate rate without both domestic-company tests, or extend a registered-project incentive to unrelated income.
- Do not turn the VAT example into a registration decision, exempt-supply determination or net return without the relevant evidence.
- Do not say “no return” simply because taxable income is within the zero band or no balance is payable. Check the particular filing duty and exemption.
- Refer unsupported treaty positions, disputed sources, special regimes and incomplete records to the appropriate Philippine professional before filing.
