# Cyprus VAT return: rates, registration, the return and penalties (2026)

Use this Guide to prepare a 2026 Cyprus periodic VAT return. Read the source for the transaction date and establish the supply, customer status, tax point and deduction entitlement before calculating VAT. The examples below state fictional assumptions, not findings about an actual supplier or client.

## Who this applies to

For a business established in Cyprus, the ordinary taxable-turnover registration threshold is **EUR 15,600**: test the preceding 12 months at each month end, and separately whether there are reasonable grounds to expect taxable supplies above that amount in the next 30 days. [Tax Department registration FAQ](https://www.gov.cy/mof-tax/documents/eggrafi-akyrosi-f-p-a/eggrafes-mitrooy-f-p-a/sychnes-erotiseis-gia-tin-eggrafi-f-p-a/).

For registration turnover exclusions, notice/effective dates, relevant EU B2B services, EU acquisitions, voluntary registration, non-established businesses, citrus growers, deregistration and cessation: Check this point with one of our partner accountants. Do not treat the ordinary turnover threshold as settling these separate questions.

## Rates, bands and thresholds

The standard rate is **19%** under VAT Law article 17. The reduced rates apply only to transactions within the applicable schedule: **9%** under article 18A/Twelfth Schedule, **5%** under article 18/Fifth Schedule and **3%** under article 18B/Fifteenth Schedule. [Operative rate provisions](https://www.cylaw.org/nomoi/enop/non-ind/2000_1_95/sub-division-sd6b01e0c7-6d3e-82e6-8a4e-7082b5cd0977.html).

For ordinary food supplies without supporting services, Fifth Schedule Table A paragraph 7 provides the 5% category, excluding alcoholic drinks, beer, wine and soft drinks. Apply the temporary zero-rate exclusions below first. A restaurant/catering supply with supporting services needs a separate classification. [Fifth Schedule](https://www.cylaw.org/nomoi/enop/ind/2000_1_95/appendix-apb4813117-691a-764b-68b2-21cbcb409949.html).

For temporary zero-rating, the precise goods/CN classification and transaction date must match the order; a broad grocery label is insufficient:

- Listed baby milk, children's/adult nappies, feminine hygiene products and specified fresh fruit/vegetables: 0% from 1 January to 31 December 2026 under [Κ.Δ.Π. 337/2025](https://www.cylaw.org/KDP/data/2025_1_337.pdf), paragraphs 3–4. For the separately enacted 2027 continuation: Check this point with one of our partner accountants.
- Specified meat/offal and fish, cuttlefish, squid and octopus: 0% from 6 April to 30 September 2026 under [Κ.Δ.Π. 168/2026](https://www.cylaw.org/KDP/data/2026_1_168.pdf), then from 1 October 2026 to 31 May 2027 under [Κ.Δ.Π. 354/2026](https://www.cylaw.org/KDP/data/2026_1_354.pdf). Fish must match CN 0302, 0303 or 0304; the cephalopod entry is ex 0307, not all seafood.
- Bread, milk, coffee, sugar and baby food: 0% from 12 October 2026 to 31 May 2027 under Κ.Δ.Π. 354/2026, paragraphs 3(b) and 4(b). For qualifying bread/milk before 12 October, the ordinary 5% food category applies.

For transport, accommodation/catering, care homes, water/gas, medicines, agricultural supplies, contraceptives, disability equipment, child seats, publications, waste/sewage, performance admissions, household electricity (including boundary readings), home solar, exports, vessels/aircraft and international passenger transport: Check this point with one of our partner accountants.

## The taxable base

Input recovery is subject to VAT Law articles 19–21, the permitted use and supporting evidence; an invoice showing VAT alone does not establish entitlement. Use only deductible input VAT for the period in Box 4. For exemptions (postal, medical, education, insurance, finance, lotteries/betting), payment-processing/debt-collection classifications, mixed taxable/exempt use, partial exemption, private use, entertainment, cars, gifts, asset disposals, commissions, self-billing and recovered bad debts: Check this point with one of our partner accountants. [Articles 19–21](https://www.cylaw.org/nomoi/enop/non-ind/2000_1_95/division-dd223a782b-f611-4b00-bdd0-4db73d7c58f8.html).

### The return boxes

These are destinations after the legal treatment has been established. All Box 4 entries remain subject to article 21 deduction entitlement. The Tax Department's [return-completion guide](https://www.gov.cy/media/sites/167/2026/01/%CE%9F%CE%B4%CE%B7%CE%B3%CF%8C%CF%82-%CE%B3%CE%B9%CE%B1-%CE%A3%CF%85%CE%BC%CF%80%CE%BB%CE%AE%CF%81%CF%89%CF%83%CE%B7-%CE%94%CE%AE%CE%BB%CF%89%CF%83%CE%B7%CF%82-%CE%A6%CE%A0%CE%91-06082024.pdf) supplies the box definitions; its older refund/payment passages do not establish current TFA permissions.

| Box | Destination |
|---|---|
| 1 | Output VAT, including qualifying foreign-service reverse charges |
| 2 | VAT on acquisitions of goods and linked services from other EU member states |
| 3 | Box 1 + Box 2 |
| 4 | Input VAT deductible for the period, including qualifying acquisition, import and reverse-charge VAT |
| 5 | Box 3 − Box 4; a negative result is a credit |
| 6 | Outputs without VAT, including the amounts in 8A, 8B, 9, 10 and 11B |
| 7 | Business inputs without VAT, including 11A and 11B |
| 8A | Qualifying intra-EU goods supplies and linked services; also included in 6 |
| 8B | Relevant services to taxable persons in other member states where the recipient owes VAT; also included in 6 |
| 9 | Zero-rated supplies, including exports, excluding 8A/8B; also included in 6 |
| 10 | Out-of-scope sales carrying input deduction rights, excluding 8B; also included in 6 |
| 11A | EU goods acquisitions and linked services; also included in 7 |
| 11B | Services received from taxable persons in other member states; also included in 6 and 7 |

For a service taxable in Cyprus for which the Cyprus recipient must reverse-charge, put output VAT in 1 and eligible input VAT in 4; put the value in 6 and 7, and also in 11B when received from an EU taxable supplier. A non-EU service does not go in 11B. For qualifying EU goods acquisitions, use 2, eligible input VAT in 4, and values in 7/11A. Import VAT is eligible for 4 only where the importer, business use, evidence and deduction conditions are met; the goods value goes in 7.

Boxes 1–5 use two decimal places with a comma decimal sign; Boxes 6–11B use values without VAT rounded to the nearest euro. Use no thousands separators and enter 0 for an empty amount. Use a minus sign for a negative amount. Check 3 = 1 + 2 and 5 = 3 − 4; check 6 is at least 8A + 8B + 9 + 10 + 11B, and 7 at least 11A + 11B.

For credit-note adjustments, exempt transactions, triangular transactions, acquisition tax points, VIES obligations and eligibility for export/intra-EU zero-rating or overseas deduction: Check this point with one of our partner accountants. For credit carry-forward, offsets and refund applications: Check this point with one of our partner accountants. Do not substitute OSS for the older guide's MOSS references without checking the particular scheme.

### Classifying a bank statement

Use the statement to reconcile payments to invoices and receipts. Record the actual contracting/invoicing entity, country, supply, date, business use and VAT evidence. A bank/payee name, logo, IBAN or payment processor does not establish VAT treatment. Keep unresolved items out of a filing calculation until resolved; do not invent a default country, customer status, rate or input entitlement.

For bank/processor fees, interest, loan principal, dividends, own transfers, salaries, Social Insurance, taxes/Registrar charges, utilities, telecoms, professional services, insurance, post/couriers, travel, supermarkets/restaurants, SaaS, rent, fuel/cars and cash-funded purchases: Check this point with one of our partner accountants. Inspect the actual export's delimiter and date format and reconcile cash withdrawals to underlying receipts.

## Deadlines and filing

Use the actual period and dates notified on the VAT registration certificate/TFA. The ordinary deadline is the 10th day after the end of the month following the period end. The return is submitted electronically through **Tax For All (TFA)**. The [Tax Department return guide](https://www.gov.cy/media/sites/167/2026/01/%CE%9F%CE%B4%CE%B7%CE%B3%CF%8C%CF%82-%CE%B3%CE%B9%CE%B1-%CE%A3%CF%85%CE%BC%CF%80%CE%BB%CE%AE%CF%81%CF%89%CF%83%CE%B7-%CE%94%CE%AE%CE%BB%CF%89%CF%83%CE%B7%CF%82-%CE%A6%CE%A0%CE%91-06082024.pdf), opening instructions, states both points.

The period ending **31 March 2026** had its return and payment deadline extended to **20 May 2026** by the [Tax Department notice of 4 May 2026](https://www.gov.cy/mof-tfa/mi-katigoriopoiimeno/prosorini-diakopi-sti-leitoyrgia-toy-taxforall-tfa-kai-paratasi-prothesmion-ypovolis-pliromis-f-p-a-kai-vies/). Under the ordinary rule, periods ending 30 June, 31 July and 31 October 2026 fall due on 10 August, 10 September and 10 December respectively; check for a specific extension before filing.

For non-standard periods, weekend/holiday adjustments, return amendments, error-correction limits, VIES deadlines/corrections, records and invoice requirements: Check this point with one of our partner accountants.

## Penalties and interest

VAT Law article 45(2) imposes **EUR 100** where a required VAT return is not received by the deadline. Article 45(3) imposes **10% additional tax** on unpaid return VAT or VAT assessed by the Commissioner. Article 45(4) charges interest from when the VAT became due. [Article 45](https://www.cylaw.org/nomoi/enop/ind/2000_1_95/section-sced833a56-3884-5b76-c351-cb00b306de64.html).

For ordinary VAT arrears in **2026**, apply **3.50% a year**: Uniform Public Default Interest Law 167(I)/2006 article 3 substitutes the prescribed public default rate for a rate printed in another law, and [Κ.Δ.Π. 376/2025](https://www.cylaw.org/KDP/data/2025_1_376.pdf) fixes 3.50% from 1 January 2026. Apply each year's prescribed rate to that year's arrears; do not carry the 2026 rate into 2027. [Substitution and annual-rate provisions](https://www.cylaw.org/nomoi/enop/non-ind/2006_1_167/full.html).

For late registration, VIES penalties, consumer-receipt failures, other charges and estimated assessments: Check this point with one of our partner accountants.

## Worked examples

### Example 1: a Limassol consultancy's January to March 2026 return

Assume a registered consultancy in Limassol sells EUR 40,000 of consulting to Cyprus clients in January to March 2026, all at 19%. It buys Google Ads from Google Ireland Ltd for EUR 2,000 (an EU service, so the business reverse-charges it) and has Cyprus purchase invoices showing EUR 3,800 of input VAT, all for its taxable business and fully deductible under article 21. For this example, assume the actual Google Ireland Ltd invoice is for a standard-rated service with its place of supply in Cyprus, the Cyprus recipient is liable to reverse-charge it, and no supplier establishment in Cyprus intervenes in the supply. The brand alone does not establish these facts.

Output VAT on sales is 40,000 x 19% = EUR 7,600. The Google service adds 2,000 x 19% = EUR 380 to Box 1 and the same EUR 380 to Box 4. Box 1 is EUR 7,980. Box 4 is 3,800 + 380 = EUR 4,180. Box 5 is EUR 3,800. Box 6 includes the EUR 2,000 service as well as the EUR 40,000 of sales, and Boxes 7 and 11B include the EUR 2,000. The return and payment are due on 20 May 2026 under the Tax Department's 4 May 2026 extension.

```oa-check
name: Limassol consultancy, Q1 2026, 19% sales plus an EU reverse-charge service
inputs:
  sales: 40000
  google_ads: 2000
  domestic_input_vat: 3800
steps:
  - vat_sales: sales * 19 / 100
  - vat_reverse_charge: google_ads * 19 / 100
  - box_1: vat_sales + vat_reverse_charge
  - box_4: domestic_input_vat + vat_reverse_charge
  - box_5: box_1 - box_4
expected:
  vat_sales: 7600
  vat_reverse_charge: 380
  box_1: 7980
  box_4: 4180
  box_5: 3800
```

### Example 2: a grocer's August to October 2026 quarter, filed and paid late

A Nicosia grocer has a quarter running August to October 2026. Its sales, without VAT, are: fresh fish EUR 30,000 in August and September and EUR 10,000 in October; listed fresh fruit and vegetables EUR 20,000 across the quarter; bread and milk EUR 12,000 from 1 August to 11 October and EUR 4,000 from 12 to 31 October; other food EUR 50,000; soft drinks EUR 8,000. Input VAT on purchase invoices is EUR 3,000, assumed fully deductible. Assume each fish and fruit/vegetable product matches the CN classifications in the cited orders, the bread and milk meet the listed categories, the other food qualifies under Fifth Schedule Table A paragraph 7 and is outside every temporary zero-rate list, and the soft drinks are ordinary standard-rated supplies.

Fish is at 0% for the whole quarter: to 30 September under Κ.Δ.Π. 168/2026 and from 1 October under Κ.Δ.Π. 354/2026. The fruit and vegetables are at 0% (Κ.Δ.Π. 337/2025 runs to 31 December 2026). Bread and milk are at the 5% food rate up to 11 October, 12,000 x 5% = EUR 600, and at 0% from 12 October (Κ.Δ.Π. 354/2026). Other food is at 5%: EUR 2,500. Soft drinks are excluded from the food rate, so 19%: EUR 1,520. Output VAT is 600 + 2,500 + 1,520 = EUR 4,620 and Box 5 is 4,620 - 3,000 = EUR 1,620.

Under the ordinary deadline rule, the return and payment fall due on 10 December 2026; assume no later extension applies to this dated case. The grocer files and pays in January 2027. The late return costs EUR 100 (art. 45(2)) and the late payment adds 10% of the VAT due, EUR 162 (art. 45(3)): EUR 262 before interest. Apply the prescribed public default interest rate for each arrears period: 3.50% for the 2026 portion under Κ.Δ.Π. 376/2025. For the January 2027 interest rate: Check this point with one of our partner accountants. The 2026 order does not establish the 2027 rate.

```oa-check
name: Grocer, Aug-Oct 2026, basic goods split by date, late return and payment
inputs:
  fish_aug_sep: 30000
  fish_oct: 10000
  fruit_veg: 20000
  bread_milk_to_11_oct: 12000
  bread_milk_from_12_oct: 4000
  other_food: 50000
  soft_drinks: 8000
  input_vat: 3000
  late_return_charge: 100
steps:
  - vat_fish: (fish_aug_sep + fish_oct) * 0 / 100
  - vat_fruit_veg: fruit_veg * 0 / 100
  - vat_bread_milk_5: bread_milk_to_11_oct * 5 / 100
  - vat_bread_milk_0: bread_milk_from_12_oct * 0 / 100
  - vat_food: other_food * 5 / 100
  - vat_drinks: soft_drinks * 19 / 100
  - output_vat: vat_fish + vat_fruit_veg + vat_bread_milk_5 + vat_bread_milk_0 + vat_food + vat_drinks
  - vat_payable: output_vat - input_vat
  - additional_tax: vat_payable * 10 / 100
  - charges_before_interest: late_return_charge + additional_tax
expected:
  vat_fish: 0
  vat_bread_milk_5: 600
  vat_food: 2500
  vat_drinks: 1520
  output_vat: 4620
  vat_payable: 1620
  additional_tax: 162
  charges_before_interest: 262
```
