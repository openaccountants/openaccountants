# Liberia Transfer Pricing v0.1

> **General reference only.** This skill is general tax/accounting reference material for AI-assisted workflows. It has not been reviewed for any specific person's facts, documents, elections, deadlines, residency, filing status, or local procedures. Do not rely on it to file, pay, amend, or take a tax position without review by a qualified professional in the relevant jurisdiction.

Tax year: 2026. Read on: 2026-11-01. Source-cited country draft; currentness gaps are listed below.

## Who this applies to

Relatedness includes practical directions/control, relatives, partners, PE/head office and direct/indirect 50% rights to income/capital/votes with related-person aggregation. Arm’s length allocation applies to related transactions, including domestic ones. [ss 208,211](https://www.lra.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/REVENUE-CODE-LIBERIA-REVENUE-CODE-AMENDEMENT-2020-min.pdf) [T01]

Controlled dealings cover goods/services, tangible/intangible assets, loans, manufacture/distribution and PE/head-office dealings. Compare product/service, functions/assets/risks, contracts, economy/markets, strategy; adjust material differences. Risk control/mitigation and financial capacity matter as well as contracts. [regs 3.5,4.1](https://www.lra.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2022/01/Adminsitrative-Regulation-on-transfer-pricing-2016.pdf) [T02]

## The base

Select the most appropriate CUP, resale-price, cost-plus, TNMM or profit-split method using nature, comparability, reliable data and strengths/weaknesses. Another method needs written CG approval and proof ordinary methods cannot reasonably apply. No universal cost-plus margin or automatic method hierarchy is supplied. [reg 4.2(a)–(g)](https://www.lra.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2022/01/Adminsitrative-Regulation-on-transfer-pricing-2016.pdf) [T03]

Closely linked same counterparty transactions can be combined when not reliably separable. Related services must actually be rendered, nonduplicative, commercially beneficial and independently payable/performable; exclude incidental group membership/shareholder costs. Joint-service allocations need reasonable measurable benefit-based criteria; the literal uncontrolled-transaction wording of 5.3(e)(ii) is not silently rewritten. [regs 5.2,5.3](https://www.lra.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2022/01/Adminsitrative-Regulation-on-transfer-pricing-2016.pdf) [T05]

The 2024 amendment expressly permits recharacterising related arrangements, including debt as equity, income/source/type and allocating expenditure. [amended s211 adjustment procedure](https://www.lra.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/TAX-AMENDMENT-ACT-OF-DECEMBER-2024-AMENDING-THE-LIBERIA-CONSOLIDATED-REVENUE-CODE-AS-AMENDED.pdf) [T08]

Prepare: ownership/control map, transaction ledger, contracts and actual-conduct evidence, functions/assets/risks, comparability search, selected method with reasons, tested financial indicator and adjustments, arm’s length conclusion and tax reconciliation. Do not attach a generic markup without the comparability work.

## Deadlines and filing

Related-party income-return filers file a TP return with the annual income return. Keep sufficient pricing information in place before the income-return due date. Small taxpayers have a simplified documentation package, not a waiver of arm’s length pricing. [reg 6.1(a)–(d)](https://www.lra.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2022/01/Adminsitrative-Regulation-on-transfer-pricing-2016.pdf) [T06]

The regulation provides for CG notice requiring master/local files: group structure, business/value chain, services, intangibles, finance, accounts/rulings; local management, transactions/agreements, FAR, methods/comparables, assumptions, adjustments, financial tie-outs and relevant APAs. Keep the full prescribed categories; a narrative contract alone is insufficient. [reg 6.1(e)(i)–(xxxv)](https://www.lra.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2022/01/Adminsitrative-Regulation-on-transfer-pricing-2016.pdf) [T07]

The small taxpayer package includes accounts, each controlled transaction category and counterparties, payment/receipt amounts, method/arm’s length explanation, and agreements. TP return filing is additional to retention and production on request; current return fields remain unverified. [Practice Note 10.1,11](https://www.lra.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2022/01/Transfer_Pricing_Practice_Note.pdf) [T09]

The annual filing deadline is linked from [Corporate income tax](lr-corporate-income-tax.md#deadlines-and-filing). Records stay in English in Liberia and are retained under the overview’s seven-year rule. A request to produce documents is separate from having them prepared. No unverified thirty-day response deadline is supplied. [s 55(a)–(b)](https://www.lra.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/REVENUE-CODE-LIBERIA-REVENUE-CODE-AMENDEMENT-2020-min.pdf) [O06]

Use the official [LRA Forms and Schedules](https://www.lra.gov.lr/forms-and-schedules/) catalogue and [LITAS](https://elitas.lra.gov.lr/) link published by LRA. Select the tax kind, year and TIN; reconcile the return with the working paper and withholding credits; submit the applicable return and schedules, pay against the resulting reference and preserve submission/payment confirmation. The public catalogue was read; live portal screens, box numbers, payment references and a successful filing have not been verified. Stop before submission if the live workflow differs. [amended s50(b),(g),(h)](https://www.lra.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/TAX-AMENDMENT-ACT-OF-DECEMBER-2024-AMENDING-THE-LIBERIA-CONSOLIDATED-REVENUE-CODE-AS-AMENDED.pdf) [O07]

## Penalties and interest

Apply the resulting-underpayment recordkeeping fine and general filing/payment provisions described in [Tax overview](lr-tax-overview.md#penalties-and-interest). Regulation 6.2 expressly points to Code sanctions; a missing TP return does not create an invented fixed USD penalty. [amended s55(e)](https://www.lra.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/TAX-AMENDMENT-ACT-OF-DECEMBER-2024-AMENDING-THE-LIBERIA-CONSOLIDATED-REVENUE-CODE-AS-AMENDED.pdf) [O11] [reg 6.1(a)–(d)](https://www.lra.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2022/01/Adminsitrative-Regulation-on-transfer-pricing-2016.pdf) [T06]

## Cross-border points

A foreign adjustment permits a request for a corresponding Liberian adjustment where a treaty exists and the same profits are also taxable in Liberia; CG must accept consistency with arm’s length. It is not automatic relief from any foreign assessment. [reg 6.2(c)](https://www.lra.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2022/01/Adminsitrative-Regulation-on-transfer-pricing-2016.pdf) [T10]

For source, PE and gross withholding use [Cross-border](lr-cross-border.md). An arm’s length service price does not itself eliminate withholding or establish the interest deduction.

## Two worked examples that recompute

### Equally comparable narrow range and median

Assume a documented analysis has established an applicable arm’s length range of 100–120 and median 110. This case tests only the outside-range adjustment, not which conflicting range-selection clause controls. Tested price 90 is outside; median 110 for 100 units increases taxable revenue by 2,000. The low-to-high spread is 20%; no unqualified narrow-range safe harbour is inferred. [reg 5.1(b)–(d)](https://www.lra.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2022/01/Adminsitrative-Regulation-on-transfer-pricing-2016.pdf) [T04]

```oa-check
name: Equally comparable narrow range and median
inputs:
  low: 100
  middle: 110
  high: 120
  tested: 90
  units: 100
steps:
  - spread_percent: (high - low) / low * 100
  - median: middle
  - adjustment: (median - tested) * units
expected:
  spread_percent: 20
  median: 110
  adjustment: 2000
```

### Service benefit test before pricing

Assume a related-party invoice 50,000 includes 20,000 solely for parent shareholder meetings and 30,000 of genuinely rendered, nonduplicative services with verified arm’s length price. Exclude 20,000 shareholder cost before considering 30,000 as potentially deductible. Retaining the whole invoice would overstate the service deduction by 20,000; other deductibility and withholding tests still apply. [regs 5.2,5.3](https://www.lra.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2022/01/Adminsitrative-Regulation-on-transfer-pricing-2016.pdf) [T05]

```oa-check
name: Service benefit test before pricing
inputs:
  invoice: 50000
  shareholder_only: 20000
steps:
  - potentially_eligible: invoice - shareholder_only
  - disallowed_service: invoice - potentially_eligible
expected:
  potentially_eligible: 30000
  disallowed_service: 20000
```

## Ask the client first

- Control, relatives, owners, PE/head-office dealings and all related counterparties.
- Goods, services, loans, licences and actual transaction conduct.
- Functions, assets, risk control, comparable evidence and selected method.
- Turnover classification, accounts, agreements, previous filings and foreign adjustments.
