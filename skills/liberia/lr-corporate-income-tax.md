# Liberia Corporate Income Tax v0.2

> **General reference only.** This skill is general tax/accounting reference material for AI-assisted workflows. It has not been reviewed for any specific person's facts, documents, elections, deadlines, residency, filing status, or local procedures. Do not rely on it to file, pay, amend, or take a tax position without review by a qualified professional in the relevant jurisdiction.

Tax year: 2026. Read on: 2026-11-01. Source-cited country draft; currentness gaps are listed below.

## Who this applies to

Corporate residence is not incorporation alone: Liberian formation plus management/control or majority operations qualifies; some local business plus majority resident ownership, and resident partners/trustees, are other limbs. Vessel and nonresident-domestic-entity exceptions apply. Maintaining a bank account/agent/meetings alone is insufficient; a nonresident PE is resident only for attributable income. [s 801(a)–(e)](https://www.lra.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/REVENUE-CODE-LIBERIA-REVENUE-CODE-AMENDEMENT-2020-min.pdf) [C11]

A business natural/legal person below LRD 3 m annual turnover falls in 4% presumptive tax unless electing ordinary taxation; petty-trader rules apply to natural persons below LRD 200,000. Do not apply 25% automatically to every registered business. [s 200(c)](https://www.lra.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/REVENUE-CODE-LIBERIA-REVENUE-CODE-AMENDEMENT-2020-min.pdf) [C03]

A legal person deriving more than 30% of business from life insurance has 4% gross presumptive income tax instead of ordinary income tax; full insurance computations are outside this Guide. [s 200(b)(2)(B)](https://www.lra.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/REVENUE-CODE-LIBERIA-REVENUE-CODE-AMENDEMENT-2020-min.pdf) [C04]

## The base

A resident company’s gross income includes worldwide economic benefits, regardless of source; a nonresident’s gross income includes only Liberia-source economic benefits and is taxed under Chapter8. A nonresident’s permanent establishment is treated as a resident legal person only with respect to income attributable to that establishment, with the related filing and advance-payment requirements. [Revenue Code ss201(c),801(d)](https://www.lra.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/REVENUE-CODE-LIBERIA-REVENUE-CODE-AMENDEMENT-2020-min.pdf) [C12]

Deduct ordinary income-production expenses with adequate records. Capital expenditure follows depreciation; personal and excluded-income costs, Liberian or foreign income tax, interest relating to any Liberian tax, fines and bribes are disallowed. Business bad debts need prior inclusion and charge-off; investment losses only offset investment gains with carryforward; ordinary business losses carry no later than the fifth succeeding year. [ss 203(a)–(c),(e),206](https://www.lra.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/REVENUE-CODE-LIBERIA-REVENUE-CODE-AMENDEMENT-2020-min.pdf) [C05]

Interest to persons other than resident banks is capped at interest income plus 20% of EBITDA excluding interest income, under amended s203(d), replacing the old 50%-of-income formula. Deduct only interest attributable to the year and properly paid/accrued. [amended s203(d)](https://www.lra.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/TAX-AMENDMENT-ACT-OF-DECEMBER-2024-AMENDING-THE-LIBERIA-CONSOLIDATED-REVENUE-CODE-AS-AMENDED.pdf) [C06]

Pool heavy movable machinery at 30% and light machinery at 40%; fixed and intangible property use fifteen-year straight-line. Land/inventory are not depreciable. Pool additions enter when placed in service, disposals reduce the pool; excess proceeds create income and a residual below LRD 10,000 can be deducted. Mixed use is apportioned. [s 204(b)–(c)](https://www.lra.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/REVENUE-CODE-LIBERIA-REVENUE-CODE-AMENDEMENT-2020-min.pdf) [C07]

A legal person other than a trust/partnership uses accrual accounting. Recognition requires entitlement for income and liability, reliable measurement and economic performance for expense. Method changes generally require prior written permission. [ss 214–216](https://www.lra.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/REVENUE-CODE-LIBERIA-REVENUE-CODE-AMENDEMENT-2020-min.pdf) [C08]

Prepare an accounting-to-tax bridge, apply the interest/depreciation/loss restrictions, compute regular tax and minimum tax separately, compare them, then apply only permitted current credits. The old vague specialised-sector range is replaced by the named statutory regimes. Do not treat an investment certificate or concession as automatically exempting the whole entity.

## Deadlines and filing

Income return and final payment are due by the last day of the third month after the tax year, March 31 for a calendar year. [s 902](https://www.lra.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/REVENUE-CODE-LIBERIA-REVENUE-CODE-AMENDEMENT-2020-min.pdf) [I10]

Ordinary income-return filers make 2% gross-income quarterly advances, with qualifying resident withholding credits if a TIN was supplied. Due 15 days after quarter end, with the approved statement. Prior minimum-tax carryforward cannot reduce these advances; final annual comparison is separate. [s 904(a),(d),(e)](https://www.lra.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/REVENUE-CODE-LIBERIA-REVENUE-CODE-AMENDEMENT-2020-min.pdf) [C10]

The catalogue names Annual Income Tax Return Corporation v 7.1, Partnership return and Quarterly Income Tax Return. Match the entity and attach reconciliations; current electronic boxes remain unverified. Use the official [LRA Forms and Schedules](https://www.lra.gov.lr/forms-and-schedules/) catalogue and [LITAS](https://elitas.lra.gov.lr/) link published by LRA. Select the tax kind, year and TIN; reconcile the return with the working paper and withholding credits; submit the applicable return and schedules, pay against the resulting reference and preserve submission/payment confirmation. The public catalogue was read; live portal screens, box numbers, payment references and a successful filing have not been verified. Stop before submission if the live workflow differs. [amended s50(b),(g),(h)](https://www.lra.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/TAX-AMENDMENT-ACT-OF-DECEMBER-2024-AMENDING-THE-LIBERIA-CONSOLIDATED-REVENUE-CODE-AS-AMENDED.pdf) [O07]

## Two worked examples that recompute

### Ordinary tax versus minimum tax

Assume ordinary resident company, LRD 10 m gross income and LRD 400,000 taxable profit, no sector relief. Regular tax is 100,000; minimum tax 200,000 controls. Excess minimum tax 100,000 enters the conditional future-credit ledger, not a current advance-payment refund. [s 200(b)(2)(C)](https://www.lra.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/REVENUE-CODE-LIBERIA-REVENUE-CODE-AMENDEMENT-2020-min.pdf) [C01] [s 200(b)(2)(C)(i)–(v)](https://www.lra.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/REVENUE-CODE-LIBERIA-REVENUE-CODE-AMENDEMENT-2020-min.pdf) [C02]

```oa-check
name: Ordinary tax versus minimum tax
inputs:
  gross: 10000000
  profit: 400000
steps:
  - regular: profit * 0.25
  - minimum: gross * 0.02
  - liability: max(regular, minimum)
  - minimum_credit: liability - regular
expected:
  regular: 100000
  minimum: 200000
  liability: 200000
  minimum_credit: 100000
```

### Interest restriction changes the tax base

Assume interest is payable to a nonresident lender, business interest 350,000, interest income 20,000 and EBITDA excluding interest income 1 m. The amended cap is 220,000, so 130,000 is disallowed. It is not the old 50% formula. [amended s203(d)](https://www.lra.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/TAX-AMENDMENT-ACT-OF-DECEMBER-2024-AMENDING-THE-LIBERIA-CONSOLIDATED-REVENUE-CODE-AS-AMENDED.pdf) [C06]

```oa-check
name: Interest restriction changes the tax base
inputs:
  expense: 350000
  interest_income: 20000
  ebitda_ex_interest: 1000000
steps:
  - cap: interest_income + ebitda_ex_interest * 0.20
  - deduction: min(expense, cap)
  - disallowed: expense - deduction
expected:
  cap: 220000
  deduction: 220000
  disallowed: 130000
```

## Ask the client first

- Entity legal form, operations, ownership, management and special tax regime.
- Gross income, tax profit bridge, losses and minimum-tax credit vintages.
- Lender residence/bank status, interest income and EBITDA calculation.
- Asset use/disposals, payments subject to withholding and tax-year approval.
