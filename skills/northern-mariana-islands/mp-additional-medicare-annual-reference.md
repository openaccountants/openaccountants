# Northern Mariana Islands 2026 annual Additional Medicare reference

> **General reference only.** This Tier 2 draft illustrates the selected annual employee wages-only reference on independently stipulated statutory category and complete covered wages. It establishes neither actual filing classification nor accepted tax, withholding, credits, refunds, complete Medicare/FICA, territorial income tax or financial outputs. Guide presence and component checks do not establish comprehensive jurisdiction acceptance.

## Scope and source gates

Load all declared bases in full. Preserve the 4 universal outputs and 18 checks, the 5 social-contributions outputs and 12 checks, every mandatory country slot, shared refusals and the exact topical refusals below. This reference leaves the social-contributions country contract incomplete. Actual payroll also requires payroll-workflow-base and a complete Northern Mariana Islands payroll companion; no output or country acceptance transfers by implication.

The fictional annual branch concerns covered ordinary private employment and wages received from 1 January to 31 December 2026. Both the legally applicable statutory category and all covered wages are expressly established independently for every numeric position. A joint branch includes both spouses' complete wages. The employee is not self-employed and has no RRTA compensation, international agreement, special service, disputed component, mixed covered/excluded service, correction, predecessor/successor or common-paymaster history. P16 expressly stipulates 2 independently covered employers for one person. These are fictional premises, never defaults for missing facts; actual classification and complete applicable law remain open.

Federal FICA's territorial reach has a separate Covenant chain. IRC section 3121(e) names Guam, not the Northern Mariana Islands expressly. [48 USC section 1801, Covenant section 601(c)](https://uscode.house.gov/us/usc/119-124/t48/s1801/) deems Internal Revenue Code references to Guam also to refer to the Northern Mariana Islands where not otherwise distinctly expressed or manifestly incompatible with the intent of the Code or Covenant. Section 606(b) applies the specified US social-security tax and benefit laws as they apply to Guam, from the first following calendar year after trusteeship termination or an agreed earlier date. The same official page reproduces Proclamation 4534's commencement of sections 601-605 on 9 January 1978 and Proclamation 5564's determination that trusteeship ended for the Northern Mariana Islands on 3 November 1986. The selected chain and [SSA POMS RS 03001.100](https://secure.ssa.gov/poms.nsf/lnx/0303001100) support the 1 January 1987 transition; this reference computes only the stipulated 2026 profile. The selected original Covenant, amendment and proclamation passages were also compared as described below; this is not a complete later-law or original-instrument audit. Federal FICA coverage is not inferred from territorial income-tax mirroring, residence or citizenship alone.

Government cases are outside the fictional private-employment branch. Section 3121(b)(7) contains distinct State, territorial and retirement-system branches. Resolve the Covenant and the complete Northern Mariana Islands government-coverage chain separately; do not assume that another territory's government treatment transfers.

The Covenant bridge also matters to exceptions. [IRS Announcement 2012-43](https://www.irs.gov/irb/2012-51_IRB) explains the section 3121(b)(18) exception for Philippine residents temporarily in Guam in H-2 status and its Covenant-linked Northern Mariana Islands application. Its separate administrative relief for services before 1 January 2015 is historical and is not a 2026 general exemption. All immigration/status cases are held here; do not treat another visa, including CW status, as H-2 or infer an exception from residence alone. The announcement's historical immigration discussion is not an audit of current immigration law.

The [IRS possession FICA summary](https://www.irs.gov/individuals/international-taxpayers/persons-employed-in-a-us-possession-fica) provides administrative corroboration and examples of exceptions. Its summary is not complete statutory intake.

Government hospital-insurance coverage also requires the separate IRC section 3121(u) analysis. An OASDI exemption or State/territorial government label alone does not establish Medicare coverage or exclusion. No government result is computed here.

## Intake and conditional mechanics

Reuse relevant redacted records before grouped questions. Establish actual service and locations, each person and employer, full annual remuneration and exclusions, marital/filing history, spousal wages where relevant, timing, agreements, special employment, corrections, withholding and reviewer readiness. Keep identifiers, banking information and signatures with the reviewer. Present and confirm the profile before actual classification; confirmation cannot establish missing law.

1. **Establish the category independently.** K is the applicable statutory annual employee-liability category, expressly established for the fictional calendar tax year 1 January to 31 December 2026: JOINT, MFS or OTHER. These are notation, not alternative legal definitions. JOINT means the legally applicable joint-return branch; MFS means the legally applicable married-separate branch; OTHER means the statutory other-case branch. Residence, marriage alone, a territorial joint return or an income-tax election does not establish K. Actual classification, death, separation, nonresidence, part-year status and elections remain held until the complete applicable current law is independently resolved.
2. **Establish complete covered wages independently.** A is the expressly stipulated non-negative total of all Additional Medicare covered wages received during that tax year, after all relevant coverage and inclusion/exclusion questions have been independently resolved. For JOINT, A = A1 + A2 includes both spouses' complete covered wages from every employer. For MFS or OTHER, A is that individual's complete covered wages; do not combine separate taxpayers. No OASDI W/capped C, ordinary Medicare M, territorial taxable income, accounting gross expense or net cash may be copied into A without independent reconciliation. Missing wages or a missing spouse never establish zero.
3. **Apply only the selected annual reference.** H = 250000 for JOINT, H = 125000 for MFS and H = 200000 for OTHER. X = max(0, A - H) and L = 0.009 × X. H applies once to the legally established annual unit, not once per spouse, employer, payment or component. The max function applies to the excess of a valid non-negative A, not to invalid inputs or negative corrections. No OASDI ceiling or additional upper cap applies to this selected formula. L is the exact fictional annual employee reference before unresolved operational precision, adjustments or credits, not an accepted actual liability.
4. **Separate employer collection.** Section 3102(f)'s employer collection threshold of 200000 and its calendar-year employer wage test are separate from H and the employee's complete annual tax base. P14-P16 illustrate why below-threshold employer payments do not establish nil annual employee tax and crossing one employer's threshold does not establish positive annual employee tax. This guide supplies no actual employer withholding amount. Do not offset presumed withholding against L or infer a refund, balance payable, deposit or employer compliance from a comparison. There is no employer-matched 0.9% component; ordinary employee/employer Medicare, OASDI, complete FICA and other programmes remain separate.
5. **Preserve exact illustration precision.** Keep A, A1/A2 where relevant, H, X and L separately at their stated precision. A tiny positive X is not rounded away below. Fractional L is not lawful per-payment withholding, annual filing or remittance. The retained employee collection fraction-of-a-cent rule does not complete annual allocation, precision, reconciliation or adjustment authority. No invented pay-period allocation, rounding, truncation or credit is authorised.
6. **Hold actual outputs and coordination.** The fictional branch excludes RRTA compensation, self-employment income, international agreements, mixed covered/excluded service, disputed components, corrections, predecessor/successor or common-paymaster histories and special employment. It does not decide their legal treatment. Complete current classification, coordination, withholding, credits, refunds, deposits, forms, deadlines, delivery, professional review and all prescribed outputs remain separate gates. No remittance or credit is accepted without evidence.
