The ordinary branch expressly stipulates one independently covered private employee, one identified employer with stable independently resolved assignment, ordinary cash wages, explicit calendar 2026, independently established actual/constructive payment timing and complete covered wage history. It excludes disputed coverage, employer-assignment changes, special services and deemed timing, tips, noncash remuneration, deferred compensation, gross-ups, agreements, RRTA, self-employment, corrections and prior-year repayments. These cases require separate sources and facts; exclusion from this worksheet does not establish legal exemption.

Federal FICA's territorial reach has a separate Covenant chain. IRC section 3121(e) names Guam, not the Northern Mariana Islands expressly. [48 USC section 1801, Covenant section 601(c)](https://uscode.house.gov/us/usc/119-124/t48/s1801/) deems Internal Revenue Code references to Guam also to refer to the Northern Mariana Islands where not otherwise distinctly expressed or manifestly incompatible with the intent of the Code or Covenant. Section 606(b) applies the specified US social-security tax and benefit laws as they apply to Guam, from the first following calendar year after trusteeship termination or an agreed earlier date. The same official page reproduces Proclamation 4534's commencement of sections 601-605 on 9 January 1978 and Proclamation 5564's determination that trusteeship ended for the Northern Mariana Islands on 3 November 1986. The selected chain and [SSA POMS RS 03001.100](https://secure.ssa.gov/poms.nsf/lnx/0303001100) support the 1 January 1987 transition; this reference computes only the stipulated 2026 profile. The selected original Covenant, amendment and proclamation passages were also compared as described below; this is not a complete later-law or original-instrument audit. Federal FICA coverage is not inferred from territorial income-tax mirroring, residence or citizenship alone.

Government cases are outside the fictional private-employment branch. Section 3121(b)(7) contains distinct State, territorial and retirement-system branches. Resolve the Covenant and the complete Northern Mariana Islands government-coverage chain separately; do not assume that another territory's government treatment transfers.

The Covenant bridge also matters to exceptions. [IRS Announcement 2012-43](https://www.irs.gov/irb/2012-51_IRB) explains the section 3121(b)(18) exception for Philippine residents temporarily in Guam in H-2 status and its Covenant-linked Northern Mariana Islands application. Its separate administrative relief for services before 1 January 2015 is historical and is not a 2026 general exemption. All immigration/status cases are held here; do not treat another visa, including CW status, as H-2 or infer an exception from residence alone. The announcement's historical immigration discussion is not an audit of current immigration law.

The [IRS possession FICA summary](https://www.irs.gov/individuals/international-taxpayers/persons-employed-in-a-us-possession-fica) provides administrative corroboration and examples of exceptions. Its summary is not complete statutory intake.

Government hospital-insurance coverage also requires the separate IRC section 3121(u) analysis. An OASDI exemption or State/territorial government label alone does not establish Medicare coverage or exclusion. No government result is computed here.

1. **Establish the paid-wage unit independently.** C is the complete non-negative amount of Additional Medicare covered wages this employer legally paid to this employee earlier in the same explicit calendar year 2026. P is the positive independently established covered wages in the identified current payment. Both are fictional source-scale dollar amounts. C and P exclude each other and include covered deducted remuneration once. Missing records never establish C = 0. Wages are not automatically salary, net cash, territorial taxable income, accounting expense, ordinary Medicare notation or an OASDI-capped base.
2. **Establish timing and assignment before arithmetic.** Actual or constructive payment matters, with constructive receipt requiring unrestricted availability under the employee's control. Neither pay-period end nor bank receipt alone proves the legally paid date. Complete C includes earlier covered payments legally made in this calendar year even if booked or banked differently. Special deemed timing and employer-assignment cases remain held. A changed EIN, payroll account, business name or physical payer does not itself establish a new employer threshold.
3. **Apply the selected employer threshold once.** H = 200000 for this employer/employee/calendar unit. Before = max(0, C - H); after = max(0, C + P - H); X = after - before; raw Q = 0.009 × X. On the crossing payment X is its excess above H; once C is at least H, X = P. The max functions apply to valid stipulated inputs, not invalid or negative correction amounts. There is no OASDI wage ceiling or extra upper cap in this selected formula. Do not annualise P, divide H by periods or restart H per payment.
4. **Keep separate people, employers and annual liability.** Disregard spouse wages, other-employer wages and RRTA compensation for this ordinary employer threshold. Do not aggregate employees. Personal filing status and annual thresholds do not replace H. Employer collection below/above H does not establish the annual employee result, a credit or refund. A spouse-based stop request does not alter the statutory employer test. There is no employer-matched 0.9% component. Ordinary Medicare, OASDI, other contributions, income tax and full employer cost remain separate.
5. **Preserve raw precision and the statutory collection rule.** Keep C, P, H, before, after, X and raw Q separately at their established precision. Section 31.3102-1(d) expressly disregards employee-tax fractions below half a cent and increases half a cent or more to one cent. This guide preserves raw Q and does not approve a rounded isolated deduction, cumulative rounding shortcut, combined Medicare/FICA, cash net pay or quarter reconciliation. Publication 15's per-employee rounding and fractions-of-cents adjustments require their complete context and actual records. Unknown integration rules require Needs Input, not a currency-minor-unit default.
6. **Hold errors and all operational outputs.** Earlier missed collection does not authorise deducting all prior uncollected tax in the present payment. Employer and employee liability, relief based on proof of employee payment, penalties, adjustments, reimbursement, prior-year repayments, deposits, current forms and deadlines need their separate complete chains.
