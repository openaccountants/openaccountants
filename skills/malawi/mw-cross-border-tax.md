# Malawi cross-border tax working papers v0.1

> **General reference only.** This Guide is general tax/accounting reference material for AI-assisted workflows. It has not been reviewed for any specific person’s facts, documents, elections, deadlines, residency, filing status, or local procedures. Do not rely on it to file, pay, amend, or take a tax position without review by a qualified professional in Malawi.

## Who this applies to

**Current-year source hold.** This is a reconstruction of the cited texts for 2026 research intake, not confirmed operative 2026 coverage. The appointed commencement notices for the Tax Administration Act and the 2021 administrative cutover remain unread; period allocation and the complete operative chain are unresolved. [Act 3/2026](https://media.malawilii.org/media/legislation/15519/source_file/2e96ab6e73cdf961/mw-act-2026-3-publication-document.pdf) has been read, but that does not close this hold. Do not use this draft to determine an actual 2026 tax position, file, pay or complete a live registration. The examples apply the stated source-text assumptions only.

Use for Malawi-source payments to persons abroad and relief on income taxed in Malawi and another country. Begin with the actual taxpayer, source, period, income character, residence and permanent-establishment attribution. Overseas payment or foreign incorporation alone does not settle these questions. The historical principal text is reconstructed only to the extent stated below; it is not an approved consolidated 2026 Act. [Taxation Act 2, 11, 27, 76A, 123–124](https://elibrary.rat.gov.mw/server/api/core/bitstreams/06b35bf5-faa2-4fa1-81f7-af650cce4dff/content).

## The base

1. **Identify residence and PE separately.** The principal Act’s inclusive residence definition includes Malawi-incorporated companies and Malawi-law trusts, estates and partnerships, as well as the stated individual presence test. A PE includes an office or other fixed place through which business activity is carried on. Do not turn this inclusive statutory text into an exhaustive treaty PE/residence rule or infer a treaty day-count threshold. [Taxation Act 2](https://elibrary.rat.gov.mw/server/api/core/bitstreams/06b35bf5-faa2-4fa1-81f7-af650cce4dff/content).
2. **Determine the source, rather than the bank location.** Section 11 includes cash/noncash amounts and capital gains from actual or deemed Malawi sources, excluding exempt income from assessable income. Section 27(1)(a) covers services/work in carrying on trade in Malawi irrespective of payer residence or payment place. Section 27(1)(d) covers remuneration for services/work wherever done when the payer may claim, or otherwise would claim, a deduction connected with a Malawi PE. Section 27(5) separately deems amounts incurred, claimed or claimable in connection with a Malawi PE to have a Malawi source regardless of recipient residence/payment place. [Taxation Act 11, 27(1)(a),(d),(5)](https://elibrary.rat.gov.mw/server/api/core/bitstreams/06b35bf5-faa2-4fa1-81f7-af650cce4dff/content).
3. **Keep other source categories distinct.** Dividends attributable to taxable income of a Malawi-incorporated company are deemed Malawi-source under 27(4); PE/Malawi-held foreign-currency gains/losses under 27(6); Malawi tangible-property gains/losses and interests in Malawi-incorporated companies under 27(7). Pensions have the wholly-outside-service exclusion and proportionate-service rule in 27(1)(b); government/local-authority/statutory-corporation service abroad has the sole-purpose residence condition in 27(1)(c). Do not route every foreign receipt to a universal worldwide-income tax. [Taxation Act 27](https://elibrary.rat.gov.mw/server/api/core/bitstreams/06b35bf5-faa2-4fa1-81f7-af650cce4dff/content).
4. **Apply the nonresident gate before multiplying.** Section 76A(1) requires nonresidence, Malawi source and no attribution to the recipient’s Malawi PE. Its stated exclusions cover First Schedule exempt income/amounts and pension or annuity payments. The pension exclusion is from this tax, not proof that every pension is exempt from all income tax. For PE-attributable company income, use the separate [corporate tax Guide](mw-corporate-income-tax.md), including its currentness hold. [Taxation Act 76A](https://elibrary.rat.gov.mw/server/api/core/bitstreams/06b35bf5-faa2-4fa1-81f7-af650cce4dff/content).
5. **Do not revive the foreign-company surcharge.** Act 14/2025 s 3 deletes the additional 5% provisos for companies not incorporated in Malawi and specified nonresident taxpayers from Eleventh Schedule (c)/(d). That deletion is distinct from the later large-company additional tax, MAT and sector rules. Read the owning corporate job; no universal 35% foreign-company rate is inferred here. [Act 14/2025 s 3](https://elibrary.rat.gov.mw/server/api/core/bitstreams/fb807c0f-1335-442a-a1ce-9d6644186525/content); [Act 36/2025 ss 3–6](https://elibrary.rat.gov.mw/server/api/core/bitstreams/f9c5b41b-5dce-4764-b7c2-923c808bda48/content); [Act 3/2026 s 12](https://media.malawilii.org/media/legislation/15519/source_file/2e96ab6e73cdf961/mw-act-2026-3-publication-document.pdf).
6. **Calculate credits only after eligibility and inclusion.** For an applicable agreement allowing foreign tax as credit, 123 reduces domestic tax by the permitted credit, capped by its formula and total-tax ceiling. Foreign tax later adjusted can require a corresponding relief adjustment. For no-agreement relief, 124 requires the specified domestic-taxpayer/deemed-source connection, tax on the same income in the source country, proof to the Commissioner and a request; it applies the 123(3) limits. Do not give a credit merely because tax was withheld overseas. [Taxation Act 123–124](https://elibrary.rat.gov.mw/server/api/core/bitstreams/06b35bf5-faa2-4fa1-81f7-af650cce4dff/content).

## Deadlines and filing

Under the cited 76A(2) text, deduction is triggered by accrual, payment to the person or account inside/outside Malawi, remittance or crediting the amount/value in the person’s favour; the payer is responsible to deduct and remit **forthwith**. Do not substitute a convenient month-end deadline or wait for cash when an earlier statutory trigger applies. The applicable operative administrative rule must be settled before live remittance. [Taxation Act 76A(2)](https://elibrary.rat.gov.mw/server/api/core/bitstreams/06b35bf5-faa2-4fa1-81f7-af650cce4dff/content).

Build a payment file containing the contract, invoice, accrual/credit date, source and PE attribution reasoning, gross/net covenant, residence evidence, actual treaty/order if claimed, withholding proof and recipient statement. A claim for treaty relief needs the actual agreement, entry-into-force/application dates, Minister’s gazetted order and the taxpayer’s satisfaction of its conditions. TAA 4 gives agreement priority only on the stated order condition; 129 preserves pre-commencement agreements. This Guide has not authenticated any treaty-specific claim form. [TAA 4, 129(1)](https://elibrary.rat.gov.mw/server/api/core/bitstreams/42eb322b-f2a9-48b9-baaf-01c2ade040f5/content).

## Penalties and interest

TAA 73(5) makes a person who fails to withhold/deduct as required personally liable for the amount not withheld. That is not itself a new penalty percentage. Manager liability and its care defence are separate under 73(1)–(4). No current penalty amount, interest rate or currency-point conversion is approved here; use the actual law/order and taxpayer period after closing commencement. [TAA 73](https://elibrary.rat.gov.mw/server/api/core/bitstreams/42eb322b-f2a9-48b9-baaf-01c2ade040f5/content).

## Cross-border points

The 2021 miscellaneous-amendment Schedule deletes old Taxation 122, substitutes the TAA agreement reference in 123(1)/124, and deletes former representative-taxpayer Part VIII. Its appointed commencement remains unresolved in this pack. Preserve that fork in the research file rather than claiming old 122 or old Part VIII is automatically the present procedure. The credit computation in 123(3) must not be confused with the administrative route by which an agreement takes effect. [Statute Law (Miscellaneous Amendments) Act 15/2021 s 1 and Schedule](https://elibrary.rat.gov.mw/server/api/core/bitstreams/e43a8644-e1e1-46d8-82d7-7e80afd5fd50/content); [TAA 4, 129](https://elibrary.rat.gov.mw/server/api/core/bitstreams/42eb322b-f2a9-48b9-baaf-01c2ade040f5/content).

TAA 4(6) requires a separate look through the specified underlying ownership; merely producing an incorporation certificate from a treaty country does not meet it. TAA 28(2)’s representative for a registrable nonresident without a fixed place of business is also distinct from company-law foreign-registration agents. Use [formation](mw-company-formation.md) and [TP](mw-transfer-pricing.md) for those bounded jobs. [TAA 4(6)–(7), 28(2)](https://elibrary.rat.gov.mw/server/api/core/bitstreams/42eb322b-f2a9-48b9-baaf-01c2ade040f5/content).

## Two worked examples that recompute

**Decision: gross covenant versus net-of-tax covenant.** Assume operative 76A text at 15%, all nonresident/source/no-PE gates satisfied, no exclusion or treaty relief, and a gross K1,000,000 fee. Deduct K150,000 and pay K850,000. If the actual agreement instead promises K1,000,000 after this tax, the gross is K1,176,470.59 and tax K176,470.59. Adding 15% to net is wrong: it leaves the recipient K977,500. These are alternative contract facts, not an automatic gross-up election. [Taxation Act 76A](https://elibrary.rat.gov.mw/server/api/core/bitstreams/06b35bf5-faa2-4fa1-81f7-af650cce4dff/content).

```oa-check
name: Gross fee versus contractual net amount
inputs:
  amount: 1000000
  rate: 0.15
steps:
  tax_on_gross_fee: amount * rate
  net_on_gross_fee: amount - tax_on_gross_fee
  gross_for_net_promise: amount / (1 - rate)
  tax_for_net_promise: gross_for_net_promise * rate
  wrong_gross: amount * (1 + rate)
  wrong_net: wrong_gross * (1 - rate)
expected:
  tax_on_gross_fee: 150000
  net_on_gross_fee: 850000
  gross_for_net_promise: 1176470.59
  tax_for_net_promise: 176470.59
  wrong_gross: 1150000
  wrong_net: 977500
```

**Decision: limit foreign-tax relief.** Assume eligible same-income credit, included foreign taxable income K2,000,000, total taxable income K10,000,000, Malawi tax before relief K3,000,000 and proven credit-eligible foreign tax K800,000. The limit is K600,000; domestic tax after credit is K2,400,000. Deducting all K800,000 understates it by K200,000. These inputs do not establish eligibility, carryforward or refund of the excess. [Taxation Act 123(3), 124](https://elibrary.rat.gov.mw/server/api/core/bitstreams/06b35bf5-faa2-4fa1-81f7-af650cce4dff/content).

```oa-check
name: Eligible foreign tax capped by domestic attributable tax
inputs:
  foreign_taxable_income: 2000000
  total_taxable_income: 10000000
  domestic_tax_before_credit: 3000000
  eligible_foreign_tax: 800000
steps:
  attributable_limit: foreign_taxable_income * domestic_tax_before_credit / total_taxable_income
  allowed_credit: min(eligible_foreign_tax, attributable_limit, domestic_tax_before_credit)
  domestic_after_credit: domestic_tax_before_credit - allowed_credit
  excess_not_allowed_here: eligible_foreign_tax - allowed_credit
expected:
  attributable_limit: 600000
  allowed_credit: 600000
  domestic_after_credit: 2400000
  excess_not_allowed_here: 200000
```

## Ask the client first

- Actual period, legal taxpayer, residence evidence, days in Malawi and complete ownership chain.
- Contract, income character, work/activity locations and Malawi PE attribution or deduction connection.
- Accrual, credit, payment and remittance dates, gross/net terms and amounts.
- Exact treaty and gazetted implementation order, if relief is sought.
- Foreign income included in Malawi taxable income, foreign tax proof/adjustments and domestic tax before relief.
- Existing registrations, notices, representatives and prior withholding/credit submissions.
