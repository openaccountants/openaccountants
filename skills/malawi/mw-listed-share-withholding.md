# Malawi listed-share disposal withholding v0.1

> **General reference only.** This Guide is general tax/accounting reference material for AI-assisted workflows. It has not been reviewed for any specific person's facts, documents, elections, deadlines, residency, filing status, or local procedures. Do not rely on it to file, pay, amend, or take a tax position without review by a qualified professional in Malawi.

## Who this applies to

**Current-year source hold.** This reconstructs the cited texts for 2026 research. F4 remains open: the appointed Tax Administration Act and 2021 administrative-cutover notices, including the identified GN 21/23 chain, remain unread. The complete operative amendment chain, general-law commencement rule and transaction-period allocation are unresolved. Reading Act 3/2026 does not close those holds. Do not use this draft to determine an actual tax position, withhold, pay or file. The examples assume that the quoted provisions apply to the hypothetical transaction. No accountant attestation or publication/current-law acceptance is claimed.

The distinct job is a **payment by a broker or dealer to a seller for disposal of shares listed on the stock exchange**, under Fourteenth Schedule paragraph (q) and note 11, inserted by [Taxation (Amendment) Act 3/2026 s 13(c),(e), printed pp 5–6](https://media.malawilii.org/media/legislation/15519/source_file/2e96ab6e73cdf961/mw-act-2026-3-publication-document.pdf). It is not a general disposal-tax calculator. The text says “seller” and “person”, without an individual-only or company-only restriction. It does not justify treating every private-share transfer or every payment by a purchaser as paragraph (q). Note 11(4) imports “broker” and “dealer” from the Securities Act; their actual definitions have not been read here. Obtain legal classification before applying this job.

## Rates, bands and thresholds

| Cited-text condition | Measure and limit | Official source |
|---|---|---|
| Broker/dealer payment to a seller for disposal of listed shares, with no established exemption | 2% of the amount realized from disposal; not 2% of the gain | [Act 3/2026 s 13(c),(e), Schedule (q), note 11(1), pp 5–6](https://media.malawilii.org/media/legislation/15519/source_file/2e96ab6e73cdf961/mw-act-2026-3-publication-document.pdf) |
| Tax withheld under note 11 | Final tax; disposer is not required to include the income received in taxable income | [Act 3/2026 s 13(e), note 11(2), p 6](https://media.malawilii.org/media/legislation/15519/source_file/2e96ab6e73cdf961/mw-act-2026-3-publication-document.pdf) |
| Listed shares disposed of by a person exempt from income tax | No withholding deduction under note 11(3)(i); establish the person's exemption, not merely an exempt receipt elsewhere | [Act 3/2026 s 13(e), note 11(3)(i), p 6](https://media.malawilii.org/media/legislation/15519/source_file/2e96ab6e73cdf961/mw-act-2026-3-publication-document.pdf) |
| Disposer immediately uses disposal proceeds to invest in other listed shares | No withholding deduction under note 11(3)(ii); no numerical grace period or partial-reinvestment formula is supplied | [Act 3/2026 s 13(e), note 11(3)(ii), p 6](https://media.malawilii.org/media/legislation/15519/source_file/2e96ab6e73cdf961/mw-act-2026-3-publication-document.pdf) |

## The base

1. **Identify the payment and parties.** Record the actual seller, payer, share listing, disposal and payment dates, amount realized and any withholding already deducted. Paragraph (q) identifies the broker/dealer payment, while Taxation 102A(1) requires withholding before making a scheduled payment. Do not substitute the seller's account balance or an assumed purchaser obligation. [Taxation 102A, printed p 70](https://elibrary.rat.gov.mw/server/api/core/bitstreams/06b35bf5-faa2-4fa1-81f7-af650cce4dff/content); [Act 3/2026 s 13](https://media.malawilii.org/media/legislation/15519/source_file/2e96ab6e73cdf961/mw-act-2026-3-publication-document.pdf).
2. **Test exemptions before multiplying.** Establish the seller's income-tax exemption or the actual immediate reinvestment in other listed shares. An intention to reinvest later is not the fact described in note 11(3)(ii). The text gives neither a day-count safe harbour nor a proportional exemption. Partial, delayed, disputed or fee-reduced reinvestment needs further source resolution; do not invent relief. Note 11 says no withholding, not that every exempted payment is also excluded from the seller's underlying taxable income. [Act 3/2026 note 11(2)–(3)](https://media.malawilii.org/media/legislation/15519/source_file/2e96ab6e73cdf961/mw-act-2026-3-publication-document.pdf).
3. **Use the amount realized.** Apply the paragraph (q) rate to the confirmed disposal amount under note 11(1). Do not deduct acquisition cost or substitute the gain. Historical Taxation 2 defines a capital gain as the excess over adjusted basis or basis, and 11 includes capital gains in income. Those definitions explain the different bases; they do not replace note 11's express withholding base. Fees, noncash consideration, foreign currency, loss relief and indexed basis are not resolved by this Guide. [Taxation 2,11, printed pp 9–11,19](https://elibrary.rat.gov.mw/server/api/core/bitstreams/06b35bf5-faa2-4fa1-81f7-af650cce4dff/content); [Act 3/2026 note 11(1)](https://media.malawilii.org/media/legislation/15519/source_file/2e96ab6e73cdf961/mw-act-2026-3-publication-document.pdf).
4. **Record finality separately.** For tax so withheld, note 11(2) makes it final and removes the income received from the disposer’s taxable-income inclusion. Do not automatically add ordinary income tax on the same receipt and treat this as an ordinary provisional credit. Conversely, that clause does not settle the underlying-tax outcome where no tax is withheld because of an exemption, a payment outside (q), or noncompliance. The general credit wording in historical 104A must be read with this specific finality rule and the administrative cutover. [Act 3/2026 note 11(2)](https://media.malawilii.org/media/legislation/15519/source_file/2e96ab6e73cdf961/mw-act-2026-3-publication-document.pdf); [Taxation 104A, printed p 71](https://elibrary.rat.gov.mw/server/api/core/bitstreams/06b35bf5-faa2-4fa1-81f7-af650cce4dff/content).
5. **Route other computations to their owners.** For final-tax expense restrictions and the company MAT turnover bridge, use [company tax](mw-corporate-income-tax.md). Ordinary individual rate tables remain in [individual tax](mw-income-tax.md). Their historical source holds remain in force; this addition neither duplicates those figures nor rewrites their gain or deduction statements. No comprehensive underlying capital-gain computation is supplied.

## Deadlines and filing

Taxation 102A(1)'s cited trigger is **before payment**. The historical withholding regulations 2–7 address scheduled payments, certificates, remittance summaries and exemption certificates. Their old deadline and penalty are not adopted as operative 2026 rules here. Act 15/2021's Schedule changes 102A's regulation-making reference to the TAA and deletes its paragraph (2)(c); the 2023 Act separately amends the certificate restriction. The actual cutover and current regulations must therefore be established before using an old certificate or remittance timetable. [Principal 102A and withholding regulations, printed pp 70,124–126](https://elibrary.rat.gov.mw/server/api/core/bitstreams/06b35bf5-faa2-4fa1-81f7-af650cce4dff/content); [Act 15/2021 s 1 and Schedule, pp 1–4](https://elibrary.rat.gov.mw/server/api/core/bitstreams/e43a8644-e1e1-46d8-82d7-7e80afd5fd50/content); [Act 17/2023 s 7, p 4](https://elibrary.rat.gov.mw/server/api/core/bitstreams/72477a6a-7348-4dd8-8a05-a7ea265a5e00/content).

Prepare a research file with the contract/trade confirmation, listing and payer classification, seller identity/exemption evidence, amount-realized reconciliation, reinvestment transactions and timestamps, withholding calculation and statement. Before live processing, obtain the current official return, share-payment category, remittance deadline, prescribed certificate and submission/payment channel. These operational details have not been authenticated. No portal field, form number, monthly deadline or universal certificate waiver is invented here.

## Penalties and interest

Under the conditional TAA text, 73(5) makes a person who fails to withhold or deduct tax as required personally liable for the amount not withheld. This is principal liability, not itself an additional penalty percentage. Use [cross-border tax](mw-cross-border-tax.md) for the separate manager-liability boundary and [company tax](mw-corporate-income-tax.md) for the held late-payment framework. No share-specific penalty, current interest input or currency-point conversion is approved. [TAA 73, printed p 52](https://elibrary.rat.gov.mw/server/api/core/bitstreams/42eb322b-f2a9-48b9-baaf-01c2ade040f5/content).

## Cross-border points

Paragraph (q) and note 11 contain no express residence split. This observation does not establish that every foreign-listed share is within Malawi's taxing jurisdiction or resolve interaction with nonresident tax, source rules or a treaty. Route those questions to [cross-border tax](mw-cross-border-tax.md), including its currentness hold. Do not stack its nonresident computation on this final tax or promise treaty reduction without establishing the operative interaction. [Act 3/2026 s 13, pp 5–6](https://media.malawilii.org/media/legislation/15519/source_file/2e96ab6e73cdf961/mw-act-2026-3-publication-document.pdf).

## Two worked examples that recompute

**Case 1: disposal proceeds versus a supplied gain.** Assume the quoted 2026 rule applies, a legally classified broker pays an ordinary non-exempt seller K5,000,000 for listed shares, no reinvestment exemption applies, and there are no fees/noncash/foreign-currency issues. The seller supplies a separately established gain of K800,000. Withholding is K100,000 and the payment after this withholding is K4,900,000. Multiplying the supplied gain gives K16,000 and understates withholding by K84,000. Note 11(2)'s finality applies to the tax so withheld. The supplied gain is not a validated basis computation. [Act 3/2026 Schedule (q), note 11(1)–(2)](https://media.malawilii.org/media/legislation/15519/source_file/2e96ab6e73cdf961/mw-act-2026-3-publication-document.pdf).

```oa-check
name: Listed-share proceeds are the withholding base
inputs:
  amount_realized: 5000000
  independently_supplied_gain: 800000
  rate: 0.02
steps:
  withholding: amount_realized * rate
  payment_after_this_withholding: amount_realized - withholding
  wrong_gain_withholding: independently_supplied_gain * rate
  shortfall_if_gain_used: withholding - wrong_gain_withholding
expected:
  withholding: 100000
  payment_after_this_withholding: 4900000
  wrong_gain_withholding: 16000
  shortfall_if_gain_used: 84000
```

**Case 2: category decision, ordinary seller versus income-tax-exempt seller.** Assume the same operative rule and qualifying broker payment of K3,000,000 with no fees or other deductions. If the seller is not income-tax exempt and no reinvestment exception applies, withhold K60,000 and pay K2,940,000. If the seller instead has an independently established income-tax exemption, note 11(3)(i) requires no deduction, leaving K3,000,000 before other charges. The K60,000 difference depends on actual exemption status, not an election to retain the money. Immediate reinvestment into other listed shares is a separate no-deduction branch under note 11(3)(ii); this case does not decide its unresolved implementation or underlying-gain treatment. [Act 3/2026 note 11(3)](https://media.malawilii.org/media/legislation/15519/source_file/2e96ab6e73cdf961/mw-act-2026-3-publication-document.pdf).

```oa-check
name: Income-tax-exempt seller changes the deduction
inputs:
  amount_realized: 3000000
  rate: 0.02
steps:
  ordinary_withholding: amount_realized * rate
  ordinary_payment_after_withholding: amount_realized - ordinary_withholding
  exempt_withholding: 0
  exempt_payment_before_other_charges: amount_realized - exempt_withholding
  payment_difference: exempt_payment_before_other_charges - ordinary_payment_after_withholding
expected:
  ordinary_withholding: 60000
  ordinary_payment_after_withholding: 2940000
  exempt_withholding: 0
  exempt_payment_before_other_charges: 3000000
  payment_difference: 60000
```

## Ask the client first

- Actual disposal/payment dates, settlement documents and the assessment period concerned.
- Seller's legal identity, residence and evidence of any income-tax exemption.
- Payer's legal broker/dealer status and the exchange/listing evidence.
- Amount realized, charges, noncash/foreign-currency elements and existing deductions.
- Actual reinvestment transactions, other listed shares acquired and exact timing.
- Existing tax registrations, certificates, returns, MRA directions and withholding statements.
