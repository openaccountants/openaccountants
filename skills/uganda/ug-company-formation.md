# Uganda Company Formation and Entity Choice v0.3

> **General reference only.** This skill is general tax/accounting reference material for AI-assisted workflows. It has not been reviewed for any specific person's facts, documents, elections, deadlines, residency, filing status, or local procedures. Do not rely on it to file, pay, amend, or take a tax position without review by a qualified professional in the relevant jurisdiction.

## Scope

Use this Guide to prepare a Uganda company-registration and tax-onboarding file. It is partial company-law coverage, not a complete incorporation or investment opinion. The tax calendar is illustrated for the ordinary 1 July 2026 to 30 June 2027 year; obtain the entity's actual accounting period before setting dates. The provisional-payment example separately labels the latest statutory text actually read.

URSB registers public and private companies, foreign companies and legal documents. Start from its [Business Registration page](https://ursb.go.ug/services/business-registration/) and official online registration link. Incorporation, a TIN application, VAT registration and EFRIS approval are separate steps.

## Ask the client first

Obtain the proposed name and activity; whether this is a new Uganda company or an existing foreign company establishing a Uganda place of business; private/public and shares/guarantee/unlimited/single-member choice; member and proposed officer identities; registered-office details; nominal share capital or no-capital status; incorporation and other existing registration documents; actual accounting year; taxable supplies excluding VAT for the past and next three consecutive calendar months and twelve months; expected annual taxable profit and tax withheld; staff and sector-specific licences. Do not invent missing identity, ownership, authorisation, capital or forecast data.

## The method, step by step

1. **Choose the legal route before filling forms.** Companies Regulations 2023 regulations 2 and 4 distinguish private/public, limited by shares/guarantee/unlimited, and single-member companies. A single member in these regulations is a natural person. A business-name or sole-trader registration is a different route; this Guide does not establish its full requirements. Nor does foreign-company registration incorporate a new Uganda subsidiary. The complete minimum-capital and director rules, including the separate single-member regulations, remain held below. Source: [SI 74/2023, regulations 2-6](https://ursb.go.ug/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/The-Companies-Regulations-2023.pdf).
2. **Prepare the URSB application.** Use the prescribed registration form specified in the Second Schedule to the Companies Act, with the fee and memorandum/articles, if any. Regulation 3 permits hard copy or electronic submission as the registrar determines; use URSB's current official start link, not an assumed screen sequence. Name reservation is an application for approval: regulation 21 addresses misleading government connections, unlawful or confusing names. Retain the certificate issued after registration; do not promise approval or a completion time. Source: [SI 74/2023, regulations 3-6 and 21](https://ursb.go.ug/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/The-Companies-Regulations-2023.pdf).
3. **Use the correct current company-law document labels.** The 2023 regulations prescribe Form 21 for the registered-office notice where not provided at incorporation and subsequent changes (regulation 28); Form 23 under regulation 29 for director/secretary appointment/change notices; regulation 29(3) says the notice provision may apply when particulars were supplied at incorporation. Regulation 46 separately prescribes Form 40 for the section 228(5) change notice. Without the operative Act provision reconciled, this Guide does not choose between those routes or infer an incorporation-notice or fee exemption. The old file's Form 18/20 shorthand is not a verified current office/officer mapping. These numbers also conflict with URA's older TIN checklist in step 5. Source: [SI 74/2023, regulations 28-29 and 46](https://ursb.go.ug/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/The-Companies-Regulations-2023.pdf).
4. **For an existing foreign company, prepare the separate registration file.** Regulation 34 requires the section 252 documents within 30 days after establishing a Uganda place of business. It prescribes Form 28 for directors/secretary, Form 29 for Uganda-resident persons authorised to accept service, and Form 30 for office particulars; regulation 35 prescribes the registration certificate in Form 31. This identifies the route and forms; the complete underlying Act document and exemption conditions have not been reconciled here. Refer that file before submitting. Source: [SI 74/2023, regulations 34-37](https://ursb.go.ug/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/The-Companies-Regulations-2023.pdf).
5. **Prepare the non-individual TIN application without concealing the form discrepancy.** URA instructs companies/entities to complete and upload its Excel template, subject to staff verification and approval. Its page lists a private company's incorporation certificate and Company Form 20 as mandatory; for a foreign company it lists the registration certificate and Forms 19 and 21. It also requires signed registration forms and identity-document copies before issue. These are the checklist's actual labels, not a statement that the 2023 Form 20 is an officer return. Obtain the current accepted URSB extract/document mapping from URA for the entity before a live upload; that acceptance is unverified here. For public companies, use the entity-specific checklist and confirm its unusual government-memorandum requirement rather than borrowing the private-company list. Source: [URA non-individual TIN procedure and document table](https://ura.go.ug/en/domestic-taxes/tin-application/tin-registration-non-individual/).
6. **Test VAT using taxable supplies, not share capital or every receipt.** URA's current page gives the UGX 300 million annual and UGX 75 million three-consecutive-calendar-month tests, exclusive of VAT. If the past three months exceed UGX 75 million, apply within 20 days after the period in which the obligation arose; if the next three months are reasonably expected to exceed it, apply at the beginning of that period. A public authority/body carrying on or intending business must apply regardless of turnover. Below the threshold, voluntary registration requires a fixed place of business, proper records and fit-and-proper status, with Commissioner General approval. Do not treat incorporation or a TIN as VAT approval. Source: [URA VAT, who must register and mandatory/voluntary registration](https://ura.go.ug/en/value-added-tax-vat/?pfstyle=wp).
7. **Register for EFRIS when required.** URA's October 2026 guidance requires VAT-registered businesses and gazetted EFRIS sectors to register. Its registration instructions use portal credentials and a one-time password sent to the taxpayer's registered phone/email, then first-time registration, choice of e-invoicing/EFDs and business locations, submission and URA approval before use. An agent must have the taxpayer's authority; never invent access or approval. Sources: [URA CG41](https://ura.go.ug/en/ask-ura-commissioner-general-41/) and [EFRIS registration steps](https://ura.go.ug/en/efris/efris-registration/).
8. **Set the actual filing calendar and retain records.** Apply the non-individual provisional and final-return dates above to the company's actual year. For a 30 June 2027 year-end, the six-month final-return rule gives 31 December 2027, by calendar arithmetic. Download the applicable URA return template, complete/validate it, upload through the taxpayer's account and retain the acknowledgement; a filing extension does not itself extend payment. Keep registry and tax duties distinct: regulation 45 requires company records, including members, accounting records, agreements and minutes/resolutions, with secure electronic or paper storage, for seven years from generation. Do not assume every company has the same annual-registry-return deadline or audit exemption. Sources: [URA returns procedure](https://ura.go.ug/en/domestic-taxes/returns/), [CG41](https://ura.go.ug/en/ask-ura-commissioner-general-41/) and [SI 74/2023, regulation 45](https://ursb.go.ug/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/The-Companies-Regulations-2023.pdf).

## Worked cases

### Example 1: VAT registration on the forward three-month test

A new private company reasonably expects taxable supplies excluding VAT of UGX 90,000,000 in its first three consecutive calendar months. This preserves the original case inputs. The test is UGX 300,000,000 / 4 = UGX 75,000,000, exceeded by UGX 15,000,000. Apply at the beginning of that period under [URA's forward test](https://ura.go.ug/en/value-added-tax-vat/?pfstyle=wp); do not wait for an arbitrary fiscal-quarter end. This computation tests registration, not VAT payable or permission to issue VAT invoices before registration.

```oa-check
name: Formation, VAT quarterly test
inputs:
  threshold: 300000000
  quarter: 90000000
steps:
  - test: threshold / 4
  - excess: quarter - test
expected:
  test: 75000000.00
  excess: 15000000.00
```

### Example 2: first provisional instalment, historical statutory illustration only

**Not usable to pay or file for 2026-27 without current-law reconciliation.** The original decision case estimates annual tax of UGX 10,000,000 with UGX 0 withheld. The July 2021 statutory compilation, Income Tax Act section 111(3), gives (50% × estimated annual tax) minus relevant tax withheld before the instalment due date, with estimates governed by section 112 and possible Commissioner variation under section 111(6). Under those assumptions the first instalment is UGX 5,000,000. URA's October 2026 guidance confirms the two instalment dates, but does not reproduce that amount formula. This Guide has not completed the intervening amendment/current-credit reconciliation. Preserve this as arithmetic and a cash-planning question for the adviser, not a current payment instruction. Source: [URA July 2021 domestic-tax compilation, sections 111-112](https://ura.go.ug/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/10580_DT_LAWS_JULY_2021.pdf).

```oa-check
name: Formation, first instalment
inputs:
  estimate: 10000000
  withheld: 0
steps:
  - instalment: estimate * 0.5 - withheld
expected:
  instalment: 5000000.00
```

## When to refuse or refer

Do not submit registration, TIN, VAT or EFRIS applications without the entity's authority and complete identity/ownership documents. Refer disputed control, nominee arrangements, single-member director conditions, foreign-company underlying documents/exemptions, regulated activity or investment licensing, capital requirements, stamp duty, audit exemptions and mismatched live TIN forms to a qualified Uganda professional. Missing facts and the holds below prevent a complete filing instruction. Do not substitute a business name, foreign branch or guarantee company merely to avoid an unverified requirement.
