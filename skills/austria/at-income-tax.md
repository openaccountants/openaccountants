# Austrian income tax for individuals: E 1, L 1 and prepayments (2026)

> **General reference only.** Prepare a working paper for professional review before filing or paying. This Guide does not establish eligibility from bank descriptions alone.

Original contributor: Open Accountants Community.

## Who this applies to

A natural person with an Austrian home or habitual abode has unlimited liability on domestic and foreign income; without either, liability is limited to § 98 income. Each individual is the taxpayer. This Guide computes the ordinary resident assessment for calendar 2026, assessed after the year ends. [EStG § 1](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/NormDokument.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=10004570&Paragraf=1&FassungVom=2026-11-27); [EStG § 2](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/NormDokument.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=10004570&Paragraf=2&FassungVom=2026-11-27).

Identify all seven income types: farming/forestry, self-employment, trade, employment, capital, letting and other statutory income. The first three use profit; the others use receipts less Werbungskosten. Do not treat every bank credit as income or every receipt as trade turnover. Section 22 includes supervisory-board activity and remuneration from a company to a person holding more than 25% whose work otherwise has all employment characteristics; director status alone is insufficient. [EStG § 22](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/NormDokument.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=10004570&Paragraf=22&FassungVom=2026-11-27). [EStG § 2](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/NormDokument.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=10004570&Paragraf=2&FassungVom=2026-11-27).

Use [at-svs-contributions](at-svs-contributions.md) for contribution amounts, [at-payroll-and-social-insurance](at-payroll-and-social-insurance.md) for payroll and special salary payments, [at-vat-return](at-vat-return.md) for VAT, [at-corporate-income-tax](at-corporate-income-tax.md) for companies, [at-cross-border](at-cross-border.md) for residence changes and treaties, and [at-tax-overview](at-tax-overview.md) for routing.

## The taxable base

1. Compute each income category separately. Capital income subject to § 27a remains outside ordinary tariff income even without Austrian withholding: 25% for qualifying bank deposits/non-securitised bank claims and 27.5% otherwise within that regime. The exclusions in § 27a(2), including ordinary private loans without a bank transaction, prevent a blanket rate for all interest. A tariff election covers all income in the special-rate regime. [EStG § 27a](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/NormDokument.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=10004570&Paragraf=27a&FassungVom=2026-11-27).
2. Private property-sale income subject to § 30a has a separate 30% rate even if ImmoESt was not collected; a tariff election covers all such special-rate income. A missing withholding payment can require a return, rather than automatically making the income ordinary tariff income. Exemptions and exceptional property transactions need separate analysis. [EStG § 30a](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/NormDokument.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=10004570&Paragraf=30a&FassungVom=2026-11-27); [EStG § 42](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/NormDokument.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=10004570&Paragraf=42&FassungVom=2026-11-27).
3. For cash-basis business income, reconcile receipts and business expenses, including qualifying compulsory SVS contributions, before the Gewinnfreibetrag. Use actual expenses or an eligible flat rate; do not deduct expenses twice. In the net VAT method, remove output VAT and deductible input VAT; the gross method includes them. Normally recognise cash-basis receipts/payments in the year received/paid, subject to the regularly recurring year-end exception and other § 19 rules. A later SVS adjustment is not automatically allocated back to the contribution year. [EStG § 4](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/NormDokument.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=10004570&Paragraf=4&FassungVom=2026-11-27); [§ 19](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/NormDokument.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=10004570&Paragraf=19&FassungVom=2026-11-27).
4. For employment, reconcile Lohnzettel data and deductions already allowed. The EUR 132 annual Werbungskosten allowance is applied in the tax computation; do not assume it has already been subtracted from a Lohnzettel taxable-pay figure. Ordinary actual expenses replace that allowance when higher. Compulsory contributions, Pendlerpauschale and § 16(1) Z 7a deductions are among the deductions allowed without using up the EUR 132. No employee allowance applies to pension income qualifying for the pensioner credit. [EStG § 16](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/NormDokument.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=10004570&Paragraf=16&FassungVom=2026-11-27).
5. Combine ordinary income, apply permitted loss offsets, then Sonderausgaben and extraordinary expenses. Apply the tariff, then Familienbonus Plus only down to zero, then the other § 33(4)-(6) credits. Refunds of negative tax require the statutory refund conditions below. Round assessed income tax to full euros, below EUR 0.50 down and from EUR 0.50 up; credit assessed prepayments and relevant withheld tax. [EStG § 2](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/NormDokument.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=10004570&Paragraf=2&FassungVom=2026-11-27); [EStG § 33](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/NormDokument.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=10004570&Paragraf=33&FassungVom=2026-11-27); [EStG § 39](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/NormDokument.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=10004570&Paragraf=39&FassungVom=2026-11-27); [EStG § 46](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/NormDokument.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=10004570&Paragraf=46&FassungVom=2026-11-27).

### Business reliefs

**Basispauschalierung eligibility.** For § 22/23 cash-basis income, no bookkeeping duty and no voluntary profit-and-balance-sheet books; prior business-year turnover must not exceed EUR 420,000. Check earlier years too: for ordinary commercial sole traders, UGB § 189 generally creates a duty after exceeding EUR 700,000 in two successive years, from the second following year, or after exceeding the threshold by at least EUR 300,000, from the following year. Free professions and other § 189(4) exceptions differ. BAO § 125 separately governs farming and economic business operations. A low most-recent turnover alone does not prove eligibility. [EStG § 17, 2026 operative wording](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/NormDokument.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=10004570&Paragraf=17&FassungVom=2026-06-30); [UGB § 189](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/NormDokument.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=10001702&Paragraf=189&FassungVom=2026-11-27); [BAO § 125](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/NormDokument.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=10003940&Paragraf=125&FassungVom=2026-11-27).

**2026 flat expenses.** 6% of turnover, capped at EUR 25,200, for commercial/technical consulting, § 22 Z 2 activities, writing, lecturing, science, teaching and education; otherwise 15%, capped at EUR 63,000. Additional actual deductions are restricted to eligible goods/materials, wages/payroll and directly productive outside labour, qualifying compulsory social/vorsorge contributions, the workplace allowance, the statutory 50% business-used non-transferable public-transport pass deduction, and equally reimbursed travel (also removed from the flat-rate turnover base). Only the basic GFB survives; no investment GFB or IFB. Returning after switching to actual expenses/books requires five business years to elapse. [§ 17](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/NormDokument.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=10004570&Paragraf=17&FassungVom=2026-06-30); [EStG § 10](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/NormDokument.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=10004570&Paragraf=10&FassungVom=2026-11-27); [EStG § 11](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/NormDokument.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=10004570&Paragraf=11&FassungVom=2026-11-27). Compare only costs covered by the flat rate, after confirming eligibility and lockout history.

**Historical 2025 flat expenses.** The general rate is 13.5%, capped at EUR 43,200; the 6% cap is EUR 19,200 and the prior-year turnover limit EUR 320,000. § 124b Z 474 overrides the older numbers still visible in the 2025 § 17 text. The old 12% examples below are corrected without changing their input amounts. [EStG § 124b](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/NormDokument.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=10004570&Paragraf=124b&FassungVom=2026-11-27).

**Gewinnfreibetrag (GFB).** Exclude § 24 sale gains and the specified special-rate capital income from the base. The schedule is 15% of the first EUR 33,000, 13% of the next EUR 145,000, 7% of the next EUR 175,000 and 4.5% of the next EUR 230,000, maximum EUR 46,400. The first portion, maximum EUR 4,950, needs no investment and is available once per taxpayer, not once per business. Allocate multiple businesses under § 10(1) Z 7; partners claim their corresponding shares. [EStG § 10](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/NormDokument.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=10004570&Paragraf=10&FassungVom=2026-11-27).

Above EUR 33,000, the GFB is capped by acquisition/production costs of qualifying investments in that year. Qualifying tangible assets must be new depreciable fixed assets with at least four years' useful life, attributed to an Austrian income-producing business/PE; predominant paid hire outside the EU/EEA fails that attribution test. Exclusions include cars except driving-school/at-least-80%-passenger-transport cars, aircraft, immediately expensed GWG, used assets, assets from a controlled business and research-premium assets. [EStG § 10](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/NormDokument.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=10004570&Paragraf=10&FassungVom=2026-11-27).

For business years beginning before 2027, qualifying § 14(7) Z 4 securities dedicated to an Austrian business/PE for at least four years remain eligible through § 124b Z 252 and 496, applying the pre-2014 § 10(3) Z 2 wording. Do not infer eligibility from an investment account's value. [Historical § 10(3)](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/NormDokument.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=10004570&Paragraf=10&FassungVom=2013-12-31); [EStG § 124b](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/NormDokument.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=10004570&Paragraf=124b&FassungVom=2026-11-27). Keep the asset/GFB register. Early disposal or foreign transfer normally recaptures the relevant investment GFB; statutory replacement rules and force majeure/official intervention exceptions require checking the specific transaction. [EStG § 10](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/NormDokument.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=10004570&Paragraf=10&FassungVom=2026-11-27).

**Investitionsfreibetrag (IFB).** An additional deduction alongside AfA for qualifying depreciable fixed assets with at least four years' life attributed to an Austrian business/PE; no flat-rate profit method. It cannot cover the same costs as investment GFB. Exclusions in § 11(3) include used assets, immediately expensed GWG, excluded intangibles and fossil-fuel assets; § 8 special-depreciation assets are excluded except the expressly listed zero-emission vehicles and heating/network equipment. Claim in the purchase/completion year, or permitted capitalised portions over a longer project, record in the asset register, and apply four-year recapture rules. For costs demonstrably attributable to 1 November 2025–31 December 2026, rates are 20%, or 22% for qualifying ecological investments; otherwise the statutory rates are 10%/15%. The cost ceiling is EUR 1,000,000 per twelve-month business year, proportionate for short years and periods outside the enhanced window under § 124b Z 480. [EStG § 11](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/NormDokument.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=10004570&Paragraf=11&FassungVom=2026-11-27); [EStG § 124b](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/NormDokument.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=10004570&Paragraf=124b&FassungVom=2026-11-27). Special asset classification or replacement/recapture exceptions: Check this point with one of our partner accountants.

### Expenses and depreciation

**AfA.** Use evidenced useful life for straight-line depreciation; do not hard-code ten years for all furniture or five years for every printer. More than six months' use gives full annual AfA; otherwise half. Declining balance up to 30% of remaining book value is an option only for eligible assets: § 8 special forms (except zero-emission cars), excluded intangibles, used assets and specified fossil-fuel assets are excluded. A switch from declining to straight line is permitted at a business-year start; the reverse is not. Keep the asset register. [EStG § 7](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/NormDokument.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=10004570&Paragraf=7&FassungVom=2026-11-27).

Without proving a different useful life, business buildings use up to 2.5%, housing-use buildings up to 1.5%; accelerated building AfA has separate conditions. Trade/farm goodwill is spread over fifteen years. Ordinary new cars require at least eight years, with special vehicle exceptions. Private letting buildings ordinarily use 1.5% under § 16(1) Z 8; separate land from depreciable buildings. [EStG § 8](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/NormDokument.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=10004570&Paragraf=8&FassungVom=2026-11-27); [EStG § 16](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/NormDokument.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=10004570&Paragraf=16&FassungVom=2026-11-27). For accelerated/special building relief: Check this point with one of our partner accountants.

**Cars.** Ordinary car acquisition costs including VAT and NoVA are limited to EUR 40,000; value-dependent running costs are cut proportionately. Apply the evidenced private-use exclusion as well. A full-year eight-year write-off is therefore at most EUR 5,000 before the business share, not an automatic deduction for a leasing payment. Determine asset ownership, cost, lease terms, use and first-use date. [PKW-Angemessenheitsverordnung § 1](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/NormDokument.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=20003797&Paragraf=1&FassungVom=2026-11-27); [EStG § 8](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/NormDokument.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=10004570&Paragraf=8&FassungVom=2026-11-27). Actual business vehicle costs are apportioned; the statement that no running costs can ever be deducted at 50% or less business use is removed. For a private car outside business assets, kilometre relief is an alternative, not an extra deduction on top of the covered actual costs. [EStG § 4](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/NormDokument.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=10004570&Paragraf=4&FassungVom=2026-11-27); [KmGV §§ 3–4](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/NormDokument.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=20012716&Paragraf=4&FassungVom=2026-11-27).

**GWG.** An eligible independently usable depreciable asset costing at most EUR 1,000 may be immediately expensed; economically integrated components count as a unit and assets intended for paid hire are excluded. On the cash basis, the deduction is in the payment year. Determine VAT recoverability in the cost instead of assuming every bank debit is a net price. [EStG § 13](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/NormDokument.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=10004570&Paragraf=13&FassungVom=2026-11-27).

**Domestic business travel.** Deduct actual business journey costs. For meal per diems, a tax-law journey is needed: official BMF/USP guidance requires at least 25 km from the normal activity centre, and no per diem after a new activity centre is created (in its stated examples, more than five continuous days or more than fifteen visits annually). A local journey can still have deductible transport costs. Meal allowance: at most EUR 30 per 24 hours; after three hours, one twelfth per started hour, capped at the daily amount. Overnight allowance including breakfast: EUR 17 without proof of higher actual costs. Do not claim a per diem for costs borne by someone else. [EStG § 4](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/NormDokument.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=10004570&Paragraf=4&FassungVom=2026-11-27); [EStG § 26](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/NormDokument.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=10004570&Paragraf=26&FassungVom=2026-11-27); [BMF/USP travel guidance](https://www.usp.gv.at/themen/steuern-finanzen/steuerliche-gewinnermittlung/weitere-informationen-zur-steuerlichen-gewinnermittlung/betriebseinnahmen-und-ausgaben/reisekosten.html).

**Private-car kilometres.** EUR 0.50 per business km for an ordinary car, maximum 30,000 km per business year, only for a vehicle outside business assets and with evidence of business journeys. This covers depreciation, fuel, servicing, taxes/fees, insurance and financing; no double claim. [KmGV §§ 1, 3–4](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/NormDokument.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=20012716&Paragraf=4&FassungVom=2026-11-27); [RGV § 10](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/NormDokument.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=10008156&Paragraf=10&FassungVom=2026-11-27). Official travel guidance describes a private car used less than 50% for business; determine ownership/classification rather than guessing use from fuel payments.

**Home workspace, 2026.** The self-employed workplace allowance requires no other room available for the business and no simultaneous deduction for an Arbeitszimmer: EUR 1,200 if no other active income exceeding EUR 13,539 has a workplace outside the home; otherwise EUR 300, with the separate ergonomic-furniture rules up to EUR 300. Prorate for short years/months without eligibility; allocate once across businesses by receipts. The amended statutory wording that omits the allowance applies only to business years beginning after 2026. [§ 4(4) Z 8, operative text](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/NormDokument.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=10004570&Paragraf=4&FassungVom=2026-06-30); [EStG § 124b](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/NormDokument.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=10004570&Paragraf=124b&FassungVom=2026-11-27). An actual home-office deduction instead needs a room that is (almost) exclusively professional and the centre of the entire business/professional activity. [EStG § 20](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/NormDokument.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=10004570&Paragraf=20&FassungVom=2026-11-27); [BMF guidance](https://www.bmf.gv.at/themen/steuern/arbeitnehmerveranlagung/was-kann-ich-geltend-machen/werbungskosten/abc-der-werbungskosten.html).

**Excluded costs and meals.** Exclude private living costs, income tax, fines and penalties. Business-friend meals are half deductible only when advertising purpose is proved and business/professional purpose far outweighs the private element; a restaurant label or a general business conversation is insufficient. Costs directly tied to exempt income or the statutory special-rate regimes have the § 20(2) restrictions. [EStG § 20](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/NormDokument.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=10004570&Paragraf=20&FassungVom=2026-11-27).

**Employees and telework.** Actual work tools, typical work clothing, qualifying training, travel and justified double-household costs require evidence of professional purpose and allocation; ordinary private clothing is not work clothing. For telework, up to EUR 3 per exclusive telework day, maximum 100 days, can be paid tax free if days and payment are recorded on the Lohnzettel. An underpayment can give a deduction for the difference to EUR 3, within that regime; adjust digital-work-equipment costs for the allowance/difference. Ergonomic furniture has its own EUR 300 ceiling and at least 26 telework-day condition. [EStG § 16](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/NormDokument.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=10004570&Paragraf=16&FassungVom=2026-11-27); [EStG § 26](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/NormDokument.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=10004570&Paragraf=26&FassungVom=2026-11-27).

**Computer topic restored.** BMF guidance for an employee's home computer requires evidence or credible professional use; absent special proof it assumes a 40% private share where substantial professional use is established, with at least three years' useful life. Do not turn these employee assumptions into fixed lives for all business equipment; apply the telework coordination above. [BMF, Computer](https://www.bmf.gv.at/themen/steuern/arbeitnehmerveranlagung/was-kann-ich-geltend-machen/werbungskosten/abc-der-werbungskosten.html).

**Sonderausgaben.** Where not already business expenses/Werbungskosten, compulsory contributions to legally recognised Austrian churches/religious bodies or their equivalent EU/EEA bodies are capped at EUR 600 per year; fees to professionally authorised tax advisers and statutory voluntary continued pension insurance/buy-back payments have their own § 18 rules. Contractual annuities/permanent burdens require the § 18(1) Z 1 conditions, not automatic deduction of any payment called a pension. Domestic church/donation/pension recipients normally transmit qualifying payments when supplied with the payer's name/date of birth; correct missing/erroneous transmissions rather than duplicating deductions. [EStG § 18](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/NormDokument.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=10004570&Paragraf=18&FassungVom=2026-11-27).

Private eligible donations share a ceiling with business donations of 10% of current total income after loss offset, with the statutory adjustments; it is not 10% of prior-year income. Business donations to eligible § 4a recipients have a separate 10% profit ceiling before the specified deductions/GFB, with eligible excess potentially under § 18. Verify the recipient and reporting requirements. [EStG § 18](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/NormDokument.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=10004570&Paragraf=18&FassungVom=2026-11-27); [§ 4a](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/NormDokument.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=10004570&Paragraf=4a&FassungVom=2026-11-27).

**Losses.** Apply statutory current-year restrictions, including § 2(2a) models. A loss carry-forward under § 18(6) requires a business loss calculated under §§ 4–14 from proper books or proper cash-basis records, and must not already have been used. This provision does not carry forward ordinary private letting losses. [EStG § 2](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/NormDokument.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=10004570&Paragraf=2&FassungVom=2026-11-27); [EStG § 18](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/NormDokument.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=10004570&Paragraf=18&FassungVom=2026-11-27). Net private property-sale losses subject to the special-rate regime are reduced to 60%, spread over the loss year and fourteen following years, against § 28(1) Z 1/4 letting income only; alternatively elect use of the reduced loss in the loss year against that income. [EStG § 30](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/NormDokument.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=10004570&Paragraf=30&FassungVom=2026-11-27). Hobby/Liebhaberei and restricted-loss classifications: Check this point with one of our partner accountants.

**Extraordinary expenses.** Costs must be exceptional, unavoidable and materially affect ability to pay, with no duplicate business/special deduction. The ordinary own-cost thresholds are 6% up to income EUR 7,300, 8% over that to EUR 14,600, 10% to EUR 36,400 and 12% above; compute the base under § 34(4)–(5), including relevant special salary payments. Reduce by a percentage point for the qualifying sole-earner/single-parent or qualifying spouse condition and by a point for each qualifying child. Disability and catastrophe deductions have specified exceptions to the own-cost threshold; not every medical bill is exempt. [EStG § 34](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/NormDokument.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=10004570&Paragraf=34&FassungVom=2026-11-27).

### Credits and family relief, 2026

**Familienbonus Plus.** Austrian family-allowance entitlement and the child's permanent EU/EEA/Swiss residence are required; test monthly. Up to the month of the eighteenth birthday: EUR 166.68 monthly (computed EUR 2,000.16 for twelve months); thereafter EUR 58.34 (computed EUR 700.08) while entitlement continues. The eligible allowance recipient/partner can take all or half each; where support credit is due, the eligible support payer replaces the partner. No support-credit month means no bonus for that support payer. With unchanged circumstances, choose the split uniformly for the year; excessive combined claims become half each. It cannot refund tariff tax below zero. Claim again in the return even if payroll used E 30, giving each child’s social-insurance number or European Health Insurance Card personal identifier; L 1k-bF covers special monthly circumstances. BMF also recognises qualifying differential-benefit entitlement even if the Austrian difference payment is zero, so do not reject that case merely for lack of a cash receipt. [EStG § 33](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/NormDokument.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=10004570&Paragraf=33&FassungVom=2026-11-27); [BMF family-bonus guidance](https://www.bmf.gv.at/themen/steuern/arbeitnehmerveranlagung/steuertarif-steuerabsetzbetraege/familienbonus-plus.html).

**Sole-earner/single-parent credit.** EUR 612 for one child, EUR 828 for two, plus EUR 273 per further child. A qualifying child under § 106(1) requires child-credit entitlement for more than six months. Sole-earner status requires the statutory more-than-six-month marriage/partnership/cohabitation conditions, no permanent separation where applicable, and partner income no more than EUR 7,411, including the specific exempt income counted by § 33(4) Z 1. Only one partner claims it. A single parent must live without a partner for more than six months with a qualifying child. The statutory EU/EEA/Swiss child-residence condition also applies. [EStG § 33](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/NormDokument.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=10004570&Paragraf=33&FassungVom=2026-11-27); [§ 106](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/NormDokument.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=10004570&Paragraf=106&FassungVom=2026-11-27).

**Maintenance credit.** For statutory support paid for an eligible child outside the payer's household, with neither the payer nor their non-separated partner receiving the family allowance: EUR 38 monthly for the first child, EUR 56 for the second, EUR 75 for each further child. With incomplete support, count only months for which full support is arithmetically satisfied; support arrears are recognised only in the payment year. Apply the child-residence condition and no duplicate credit for the same child. [EStG § 33](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/NormDokument.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=10004570&Paragraf=33&FassungVom=2026-11-27).

**Employee credit and commuting.** The Verkehrsabsetzbetrag is EUR 496 for income from an existing employment. With Pendlerpauschale entitlement it increases to EUR 853 up to income EUR 15,069, tapering to EUR 496 at EUR 16,056. The separate Zuschlag is EUR 804 up to EUR 19,761, tapering linearly to zero at EUR 30,259. [EStG § 33](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/NormDokument.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=10004570&Paragraf=33&FassungVom=2026-11-27).

Annual Pendlerpauschale with reasonable public transport: EUR 696 for 20–40 km, EUR 1,356 over 40–60 km, EUR 2,016 over 60 km. Where public transport is unreasonable for at least half the route: EUR 372 for 2–20 km, EUR 1,476 over 20–40, EUR 2,568 over 40–60, EUR 3,672 over 60. Full monthly allowance needs at least eleven commute days; eight to ten gives two thirds, four to seven one third. Apply company-car, employer-ticket/transport and multiple-home restrictions; use the official Pendlerrechner. Pendlereuro is EUR 6 annually per one-way kilometre from 2026, following the same proportional rules. [EStG § 16](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/NormDokument.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=10004570&Paragraf=16&FassungVom=2026-11-27); [EStG § 33](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/NormDokument.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=10004570&Paragraf=33&FassungVom=2026-11-27); § 124b Z 476.

**Pensioner credit.** Where the employee credit is unavailable and qualifying pension/former-employment income is received, the ordinary credit is EUR 1,020, tapering to zero between taxable current pension income EUR 21,614 and EUR 31,494. The increased credit is EUR 1,502 up to EUR 24,616, tapering to zero at EUR 31,494; it requires more than six months married/registered and not permanently separated, spouse income at most EUR 2,720 under the statutory income definition, and no sole-earner-credit entitlement. [EStG § 33](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/NormDokument.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=10004570&Paragraf=33&FassungVom=2026-11-27).

**Negative tax/SV refund.** Refund the sole-earner/single-parent credit only to the extent of negative tax. For qualifying employees, 55% of specified compulsory contributions (excluding works-council levies) is refundable, maximum EUR 496 or EUR 750 with Pendlerpauschale, increased by up to the EUR 804 Zuschlag/SV bonus. Pensioners: 80% of specified contributions, maximum EUR 723, reduced by relevant exempt allowances. All are limited by the negative tax; treaty-exempt income is treated as taxable for these calculations. [EStG § 33](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/NormDokument.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=10004570&Paragraf=33&FassungVom=2026-11-27).

**Kindermehrbetrag.** Requires at least thirty days with taxable active income of categories 1–4, or exclusively the listed childcare/maternity/care-leave benefits throughout the year, qualifying § 106(1) children and tariff tax below EUR 700 per qualifying child. The amount is EUR 700 times the qualifying child count less tariff tax, never below zero. BMF gives EUR 17,038 income for one child in 2026 (tariff tax below EUR 700); the threshold rises with the child count. The sole-earner/single-parent credit must be due, or the partner must also have active category 1–4 income and tariff tax below the corresponding child-count threshold; in that latter case only the family-allowance recipient qualifies. Treaty-exempt income counts as taxable in this test. The increased threshold for multiple children follows the statutory additional-child amount, as explicitly tabulated in the BMF 2026 guidance. [BMF Kindermehrbetrag](https://www.bmf.gv.at/themen/steuern/arbeitnehmerveranlagung/steuertarif-steuerabsetzbetraege/familienbonus-plus.html). [EStG § 33](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/NormDokument.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=10004570&Paragraf=33&FassungVom=2026-11-27). The Kinderabsetzbetrag is separately paid with family allowance, EUR 70.90 per month for 2026 under the 2025 amount and 2026–2028 indexation suspension; it is not a further deduction from this tax calculation. [EStG § 33](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/NormDokument.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=10004570&Paragraf=33&FassungVom=2026-11-27); § 124b Z 475.

## Deadlines and filing

**Return duty.** File when requested by the tax office, or where business profit was required to be or was determined by balance-sheet comparison. Without wage-taxed income the 2026 income threshold is EUR 13,539. With wages, § 42 applies the EUR 14,769 threshold to its listed § 41(1) Z 1, 2, 4, 5, 9 and 12 cases, including other income exceeding EUR 730, concurrent separately taxed wages/pensions and specified incorrectly applied employer reliefs. Other § 41 grounds can produce assessment and must not be collapsed into that threshold. Special-rate capital income without KESt (unless a tariff election would produce no liability), or private property-sale income without the required ImmoESt/final settlement, independently require filing. [EStG § 41](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/NormDokument.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=10004570&Paragraf=41&FassungVom=2026-11-27); [EStG § 42](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/NormDokument.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=10004570&Paragraf=42&FassungVom=2026-11-27). With wages, the ordinary assessment allowance for non-wage income is up to EUR 730, tapered euro-for-euro above EUR 730 and excluding § 27a(1) income. [EStG § 39](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/NormDokument.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=10004570&Paragraf=39&FassungVom=2026-11-27).

**Forms.** E 1 with E 1a for business, E 1b for letting, E 1kv for relevant capital income, L 1i for foreign/non-withheld wages, L 1k/L 1k-bF for children, and L 1ab for extraordinary expenses. L 1 serves the employee assessment. Reconcile electronically transmitted Lohnzettel and domestic special-expense data. [Official return guidance](https://www.usp.gv.at/themen/steuern-finanzen/einkommensteuer-ueberblick/weitere-informationen-est/einkommensteuererklaerung.html).

Electronic filing is the statutory default; technical unreasonableness permits paper. For a self-filer, the regulation requires internet access and a VAT pre-return obligation because the turnover limit is exceeded. The relevant prior-year VAT turnover boundary is EUR 55,000, with the VAT regulation's excluded turnover and timely-payment/no-prepayment conditions. Do not generalise a representative's or foreign filer's position from that self-filer test. [EStG § 42](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/NormDokument.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=10004570&Paragraf=42&FassungVom=2026-11-27); [FinanzOnline-Erklärungsverordnung § 2](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/NormDokument.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=20005197&Paragraf=2&FassungVom=2026-11-27); [VAT pre-return regulation § 1](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/NormDokument.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=10005119&Paragraf=1&FassungVom=2026-11-27).

For 2026, ordinary deadlines are 30 April 2027 on paper and 30 June 2027 electronically; a justified extension can be requested. The statutory representative quota system, with valid mandate and enrolled return, can run to 31 March 2028, or to 30 June 2028 if the office extends it, subject to quota rules/earlier recall. It excludes returns declaring only wage-taxed employment income. [BAO §§ 134–134a](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/NormDokument.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=10003940&Paragraf=134a&FassungVom=2026-11-27).

A voluntary 2026 employee assessment can be requested until 31 December 2031. The automatic assessment after June requires the statutory file-based wage-only/refund conditions (including at least EUR 5 and no expected higher credit); it does not cancel a filing duty. A later application within five years replaces it. [EStG § 41](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/NormDokument.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=10004570&Paragraf=41&FassungVom=2026-11-27).

**Prepayments.** Four quarters due 15 February, 15 May, 15 August and 15 November. Use last assessed liability less the specified withheld tax/property amounts, uplift 4% for the immediately following year plus 5% for each further year, and round down to euros; annual amounts at most EUR 300 are set to zero. The tax office can adapt to estimated current liability, including first-year estimates. Wage-taxed persons receive prepayments only in § 41(1) Z 1/2 cases. Apply the 30 September change/application cutoff, its appeal exception and the 31 October catastrophe application exception; notices after 15 October increasing/first setting payments generally allow one month. [EStG § 45](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/NormDokument.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=10004570&Paragraf=45&FassungVom=2026-11-27).

**Balance and payment grace.** Generally due one month after notice. A due date on a Saturday, Sunday, statutory holiday, Good Friday or 24 December moves to the next eligible day. Bank-transfer payment is credited when received; three-day late-credit grace excludes those same non-working days. [BAO § 210](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/NormDokument.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=10003940&Paragraf=210&FassungVom=2026-11-27); [§ 211](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/NormDokument.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=10003940&Paragraf=211&FassungVom=2026-11-27).

For 2025 returns, ordinary deadlines were 30 April/30 June 2026; check extensions/quota coverage (normally 31 March 2027) rather than assuming every represented return is extended. Voluntary 2025 L 1 claims can run to 31 December 2030. [BAO § 134](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/NormDokument.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=10003940&Paragraf=134&FassungVom=2026-11-27); [§ 134a](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/NormDokument.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=10003940&Paragraf=134a&FassungVom=2026-11-27); [EStG § 41](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/NormDokument.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=10004570&Paragraf=41&FassungVom=2026-11-27).

## Penalties and interest

**Anspruchszinsen:** 2 percentage points above the applicable base rate, for 2026 from 1 October 2027 until notice, maximum forty-eight months; amounts below EUR 50 are not set. Apply the base rate in force for each period, not a permanent rate copied from a past example. Properly notified and paid income-tax advance deposits reduce the charge base; refund interest also requires the statutory payment conditions. [BAO § 205](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/NormDokument.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=10003940&Paragraf=205&FassungVom=2026-11-27); [OeNB rate series](https://www.oenb.at/isawebstat/stabfrage/createReport?report=2.1&lang=DE).

**Late payment:** first surcharge 2%; second 1% after three months from enforceability; third 1% three months after the second liability arises, subject to statutory suspensions/interruption. Amounts below EUR 50 are not set. The first surcharge does not arise for a delay of at most five eligible days where the same tax account's liabilities were all timely paid in the preceding six months; exclude non-working days and begin after § 211(2) grace where applicable. On application, reduce/omit to the extent no gross fault exists. [BAO § 217](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/NormDokument.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=10003940&Paragraf=217&FassungVom=2026-11-27).

**Late filing:** discretionary surcharge up to 10% of assessed tax where the delay is inexcusable; amounts below EUR 50 are not set. [BAO § 135](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/NormDokument.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=10003940&Paragraf=135&FassungVom=2026-11-27). The ordinary assessment appeal period is one month, subject to the missing-reasons and extension rules. [BAO § 245](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/NormDokument.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=10003940&Paragraf=245&FassungVom=2026-11-27). For litigation, audit and voluntary-disclosure strategy: Check this point with one of our partner accountants.

## Cross-border points

A qualifying non-resident EU/EEA national with Austrian § 98 income may elect resident treatment if at least 90% of income is Austrian-taxable or other income is no more than EUR 13,539 in 2026, with the foreign authority's income certificate; treaty-limited Austrian income has the statutory special treatment in this test. [EStG § 1](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/NormDokument.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=10004570&Paragraf=1&FassungVom=2026-11-27). Otherwise the 2026 limited-liability tariff adds EUR 11,077, and the return threshold for assessable domestic income is EUR 2,463. These are routing figures, not a substitute for the § 102 assessment and deduction conditions. [EStG § 102](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/NormDokument.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=10004570&Paragraf=102&FassungVom=2026-11-27); [EStG § 42](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/NormDokument.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=10004570&Paragraf=42&FassungVom=2026-11-27).

For resident foreign income, determine the applicable treaty before using exemption/progression or credit. Where progression applies, compute the average rate before family/other credits, then deduct the permitted credits from the resulting Austrian tax. [EStG § 33](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/NormDokument.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=10004570&Paragraf=33&FassungVom=2026-11-27). Do not automatically exempt every foreign receipt; use [at-cross-border](at-cross-border.md).

## Worked examples

All cases below retain their original numerical inputs and years. Eligibility assumptions added to make a computation usable are stated explicitly. The original cases are not evidence of those missing facts.

### Example 1: Freelancer on the Basispauschalierung, 2026

A graphic designer has EUR 60,000 turnover, EUR 8,000 actual covered expenses and EUR 9,000 paid qualifying SVS; no other income, a rented studio, no special/extraordinary deductions and EUR 1,400 quarterly prepayments (EUR 5,600). Assume § 22/23 classification qualifies for the 15% rate, prior turnover below EUR 420,000, no book duty/voluntary books, no return lockout, no other extra deductions and no credit entitlement.

Flat expenses EUR 9,000 exceed covered actual expenses EUR 8,000. Profit EUR 42,000 less basic GFB EUR 4,950 gives EUR 37,050 taxable income. Tax EUR 1,690.60 + EUR 4,339.80 + EUR 236.80 = EUR 6,267.20, rounded EUR 6,267; balance EUR 667. An appropriately notified and paid deposit before 1 October 2027 removes this balance from the interest base. See business reliefs, rounding and payment rules above.

```oa-check
name: Austria freelancer 2026
inputs:
  turnover: 60000
  svs: 9000
  prepayments: 5600
steps:
  - flat_rate: min(turnover * 0.15, 63000)
  - profit: turnover - flat_rate - svs
  - grundfreibetrag: min(profit, 33000) * 0.15
  - taxable: profit - grundfreibetrag
  - tax: (21992 - 13539) * 0.20 + (36458 - 21992) * 0.30 + (taxable - 36458) * 0.40
  - assessed_tax: round(tax)
  - balance_due: assessed_tax - prepayments
expected:
  flat_rate: 9000
  profit: 42000
  grundfreibetrag: 4950
  taxable: 37050
  tax: 6267.20
  assessed_tax: 6267
  balance_due: 667
```

### Example 2: Employee and unused family bonus, 2026

Original inputs: Einkommen EUR 19,000 after social insurance and the ordinary allowance, two children under eighteen all year, family allowance received, partner income EUR 40,000, qualifying compulsory contributions/chamber levy EUR 3,900, wage tax EUR 300. Assume full-year qualifying family/partner/child residence, full bonus elected by this employee and none by the partner, no commuter or sole-parent relief, no excluded works-council levy and no treaty-exempt income.

Tariff tax EUR 1,092.20 uses only that much of the EUR 4,000.32 bonus, leaving EUR 2,908.12 unused. Employee credit EUR 496 plus Zuschlag EUR 804 produces negative tax EUR 1,300. The contribution refund is the lowest of EUR 2,145, EUR 1,300 cap and EUR 1,300 negative tax; total refund including withholding is EUR 1,600. No Kindermehrbetrag under these assumptions. Electing the entire bonus for the qualifying partner could use the whole amount if their tariff tax permits. Half each gives the partner only EUR 2,000.16: EUR 907.96 remains unused by the lower earner, so half-sharing does not transfer the whole EUR 2,908.12.

```oa-check
name: Austria employee family bonus and SV refund 2026
inputs:
  income: 19000
  children_under_18: 2
  sv_contributions: 3900
  wage_tax_withheld: 300
steps:
  - tariff_tax: (income - 13539) * 0.20
  - fbp_full: children_under_18 * 166.68 * 12
  - fbp_used: min(fbp_full, tariff_tax)
  - fbp_lost: fbp_full - fbp_used
  - tax_after_credits: tariff_tax - fbp_used - 496 - 804
  - sv_refund: min(sv_contributions * 0.55, 496 + 804, -tax_after_credits)
  - total_refund: wage_tax_withheld + sv_refund
  - half_share_unused: fbp_full / 2 - tariff_tax
expected:
  tariff_tax: 1092.20
  fbp_full: 4000.32
  fbp_lost: 2908.12
  tax_after_credits: -1300
  sv_refund: 1300
  total_refund: 1600
  half_share_unused: 907.96
```

## Ask the client first

Obtain residence and all income records, business activity/method and prior turnover/book-duty history, VAT method, prior flat-rate exit, SVS payments, asset cost/use dates and registers, family/partner income and monthly child entitlement, donations/medical records, prior notices and filing representation. Record missing eligibility inputs explicitly; do not select a flat rate or claim an allowance by default.

Bank descriptions such as HONORAR/STRIPE/PAYPAL, GEHALT/LOHN/PENSION, MIETE, ZINSEN/DIVIDENDE, SVS/SVA, BÜROMIETE, business insurance/adviser/legal fees, office supplies/software/marketing/training/chamber/bank fees, FLUG/HOTEL/ÖBB/TAXI, fuel, KIRCHENBEITRAG/SPENDE, KREDITZINSEN, USt/ESt, own transfers/drawings and loan principal are reconciliation prompts. Match invoices, Lohnzettel, VAT records and the rules above; do not treat payout amounts as gross sales or every tax refund as exempt income.

Partnership returns, farming/sector/small-business flat rates, business disposal/closure, unusual real-estate sales, startup shares, hobby/restricted losses, unusual gift/inheritance/lottery-receipt classifications and complex audit/appeal/disclosure outcomes need specialist work. No usable outcome is supplied for those matters here. Check this point with one of our partner accountants.
