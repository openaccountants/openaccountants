# Bahamas: tax pack

The Bahamas has no income tax: none on individuals, none on company profits, none on capital gains, and no inheritance or gift tax. What exists is VAT (10% standard, 5% on listed medical goods, unprepared food from food stores exempt from 1 April 2026), the business licence tax on turnover, National Insurance (NIB) contributions, real property tax, VAT on property conveyances, stamp duty and customs duties, and the Domestic Minimum Top-Up Tax for large multinational groups. The tax year is the calendar year; the Department of Inland Revenue (DIR) runs VAT, business licence, real property tax and DMTT; the National Insurance Board runs NIB. Amounts are BSD, at par with USD. This map covers 2026.
