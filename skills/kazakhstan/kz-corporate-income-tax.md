# Kazakhstan Corporate Income Tax

## Corporate income tax rates and base (2025)

- **Standard CIT rate** — 20% %  _(Tax Code of the Republic of Kazakhstan (https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/kazakhstan/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income))_
- **Reduced CIT rate for qualifying agricultural producers** — 10% on income from production of agricultural products % (approx — confirm)  _(Tax Code of the Republic of Kazakhstan (https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/kazakhstan/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income))_
- **Tax base** — Taxable income = aggregate annual income less allowable deductions and carried-forward losses  _(Tax Code of the Republic of Kazakhstan (https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/kazakhstan/corporate/income-determination))_
- **Loss carryforward** — Tax losses may be carried forward for up to 10 years  _(Tax Code of the Republic of Kazakhstan (https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/kazakhstan/corporate/deductions))_
- **Branch profits tax on non-resident permanent establishments** — 15% on after-CIT net income of a non-resident's permanent establishment (subject to treaty reduction) % (approx — confirm)  _(Tax Code of the Republic of Kazakhstan (https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/kazakhstan/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income))_
- **Withholding tax on dividends to non-residents** — 15% standard; 5% may apply where qualifying holding/holding-period conditions are met %  _(Tax Code of the Republic of Kazakhstan (https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/kazakhstan/corporate/withholding-taxes))_
- **Withholding tax on interest to non-residents** — 15% (unless reduced by an applicable double tax treaty) %  _(Tax Code of the Republic of Kazakhstan (https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/kazakhstan/corporate/withholding-taxes))_
- **Withholding tax on royalties to non-residents** — 15% (unless reduced by an applicable double tax treaty) %  _(Tax Code of the Republic of Kazakhstan (https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/kazakhstan/corporate/withholding-taxes))_
- **Withholding tax on payments to low-tax (tax-haven) jurisdictions** — 20%, regardless of income type, where the recipient is in a listed preferential-tax jurisdiction %  _(Tax Code of the Republic of Kazakhstan (https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/kazakhstan/corporate/withholding-taxes))_
- **Advance CIT payments** — Large taxpayers above a turnover threshold pay monthly CIT advances; smaller taxpayers are generally exempt from advance payments (approx — confirm)  _(Tax Code of the Republic of Kazakhstan (https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/kazakhstan/corporate/tax-administration))_
- **CIT return filing deadline** — 31 March of the year following the reporting year  _(Tax Code of the Republic of Kazakhstan (https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/kazakhstan/corporate/tax-administration))_
- **Final CIT payment deadline** — Within 10 calendar days after the filing deadline (i.e. by 10 April) (approx — confirm)  _(Tax Code of the Republic of Kazakhstan (https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/kazakhstan/corporate/tax-administration))_
