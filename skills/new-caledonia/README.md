# New Caledonia (NC): tax pack

**Built from the law. Not yet approved. Written by Ryan Duguid. Not reviewed by an accountant.** New Caledonia is a French sui generis collectivity with its own tax code (Code des impôts de la Nouvelle-Calédonie, on juridoc.gouv.nc) and tax authority, the Direction des services fiscaux (DSF, dsf.gouv.nc). Currency is the CFP franc (XPF). Personal income tax (IRPP) is assessed on the household with family parts. Tax year 2026.
