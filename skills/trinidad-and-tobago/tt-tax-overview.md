# Trinidad and Tobago Tax Overview

## Trinidad and Tobago tax system at a glance

- **Tax year (year of income)** — Calendar year, 1 January to 31 December  _(Income Tax Act (Chap. 75:01) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/trinidad-and-tobago/individual/tax-administration)_
- **Currency** — Trinidad and Tobago dollar (TTD / TT$)  _(Central Bank of Trinidad and Tobago)_
- **Tax authority** — Inland Revenue Division (IRD), Ministry of Finance  _(Inland Revenue Division — https://www.ird.gov.tt/)_
- **Individual residence basis** — Residents are taxed on worldwide income; non-residents are taxed only on Trinidad and Tobago-source income  _(Income Tax Act (Chap. 75:01) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/trinidad-and-tobago/individual/taxes-on-personal-income)_
- **Corporate residence basis** — Resident companies are taxed on worldwide income; non-resident companies are taxed only on income accruing in or derived from Trinidad and Tobago  _(Corporation Tax Act (Chap. 75:02) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/trinidad-and-tobago/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_
- **Headline personal income tax rates** — 25% on chargeable income up to TTD 1,000,000; 30% on the excess percent  _(Income Tax Act (Chap. 75:01) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/trinidad-and-tobago/individual/taxes-on-personal-income)_
- **Headline corporation tax rate** — 30% standard rate (higher rates for banks, petrochemical and petroleum companies) percent  _(Corporation Tax Act (Chap. 75:02) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/trinidad-and-tobago/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_
- **Value Added Tax** — Yes — VAT applies at a standard rate of 12.5% percent  _(Value Added Tax Act (Chap. 75:06) — https://www.ird.gov.tt/VAT)_
- **Annual individual income tax return deadline** — 30 April of the year following the year of income  _(Income Tax Act (Chap. 75:01) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/trinidad-and-tobago/individual/tax-administration)_
- **Annual corporation tax return deadline** — 30 April following the end of the income year  _(Corporation Tax Act (Chap. 75:02) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/trinidad-and-tobago/corporate/tax-administration)_
- **Green Fund Levy** — 0.3% of gross income/receipts, payable by companies and partnerships percent  _(Miscellaneous Taxes Act (Chap. 77:01) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/trinidad-and-tobago/corporate/other-taxes)_
- **Business Levy** — 0.6% of gross revenue/receipts, payable where it exceeds the corporation/income tax liability percent  _(Corporation Tax Act (Chap. 75:02) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/trinidad-and-tobago/corporate/other-taxes)_
