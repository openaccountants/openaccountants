# Greenland payroll reporting and employer AMA

**Built from law. Not yet approved.** Prepare a bounded employer working paper and filing handoff. This is not complete Greenland payroll/net-pay coverage, professional attestation or authority to file.

## Scope

For a liable employer's established 2026 AMA base, the base charge is **1.9%**. The private maternity-fund supplement is **0.0% for 2026**, so the combined rate remains **1.9%**, not the 2025 combined 2.1%. Keep employee A-tax, employer AMA, monthly reporting and actual payment separate. [AMA Act 42, ss 1–2 a](https://nalunaarutit.gl/groenlandsk-lovgivning/2025/selvstyrets-lovbekendtgoerelse-nr-42-af-05_05_2025?sc_lang=da); [enacted 2026 Finance Act 85, s 2](https://nalunaarutit.gl/groenlandsk-lovgivning/2025/inatsisartutlov-nr-85-af-8_12_2025?sc_lang=da); [Annex 2, account 24.11.22, p 40](https://nalunaarutit.gl/-/media/lovfiler/2025/forarbejder/ina_85/ina_85_2025_bilag2_da.pdf).

## Ask the client first

Obtain employer registration/CVR, payroll period, employee identifiers and tax municipality, employment contracts, tax cards or authority data, A-income and benefits, foreign pension data, pension-scheme classifications, documented AMA base and exemptions, previous returns, any shortened-deadline notice, payroll register, tax invoice and bank evidence. Ask about public-sector status, mineral-licence terms, Danish/cross-border withholding arrangements, holiday-pay timing and any cessation. Stop if the actual tax-card or contribution-base computation is unresolved.

## The method, step by step

### 1. Register and establish withholding

Register in the Tax Authority's employer register/Sulinal when there are employees, including short engagements. Obtain the applicable tax card/secondary or exemption card, or authority-supplied card data. Withhold A-tax at each A-income payment, subject to the statutory special rules, and provide written pay/withholding information. The amount comes from the employee's actual position, not a generic flat percentage. Holiday-pay timing, missing cards, foreign labour and combined Danish withholding require their own statutory analysis before net pay is derived. [Employer guidance](https://aka.gl/da/erhverv/arbejdsgiverregistret); [income law, ss 75–82](https://nalunaarutit.gl/groenlandsk-lovgivning/2025/selvstyrets-lovbekendtgoerelse-nr-62-af-24_09_2025?sc_lang=da).

### 2. Establish AMA liability and base separately

AMA liability follows the employer's A-tax withholding obligation under income-law section 76, cf 78. The state and its institutions are exempt; a mineral-permit holder's exemption depends on the licence terms. Section 2 covers employment remuneration including wages, holiday pay, fees, commission and specified board/committee remuneration to Greenland-taxable persons. Maintain accounts supporting the charge. [AMA ss 1–2, 8](https://nalunaarutit.gl/groenlandsk-lovgivning/2025/selvstyrets-lovbekendtgoerelse-nr-42-af-05_05_2025?sc_lang=da).

Section 2(4) excludes employer payments qualifying under income-law sections 39, 40 and 47 b(6). Establish the actual arrangement and all relevant conditions before exclusion: the life-pension/rate-insurance provisions and the separate pension-bank-deposit rule are different tests; section 47b(6) also requires the employee's full Greenland tax liability. Eligibility for the mandatory-pension minimum does not automatically establish AMA exclusion. In particular, do not deduct every Danish pension payment merely because the mandatory-pension law can recognise a Danish scheme. Hold an unclassified payment and refer it before setting the base. [AMA s 2(4)](https://nalunaarutit.gl/groenlandsk-lovgivning/2025/selvstyrets-lovbekendtgoerelse-nr-42-af-05_05_2025?sc_lang=da); [income-law ss 39–41, 47 b](https://nalunaarutit.gl/groenlandsk-lovgivning/2025/selvstyrets-lovbekendtgoerelse-nr-62-af-24_09_2025?sc_lang=da); [mandatory-pension scope](gl-mandatory-pension.md).

### 3. Apply the 2026 charge

Multiply the verified AMA base by 1.9%. For private employers the 2026 Finance Act's enacted Annex 2 account 24.11.22 fixes the section 2a supplement at 0.0%. The Act gives the notes legal effect; this attachment is not used merely because its URL contains a legislative-document folder. The Sulinal notice says digital reporting changes automatically and manual declarations must use the changed rate. Municipalities and Greenland Self-Government are outside the section 2a supplement under that provision. [AMA ss 2–2 a](https://nalunaarutit.gl/groenlandsk-lovgivning/2025/selvstyrets-lovbekendtgoerelse-nr-42-af-05_05_2025?sc_lang=da); [Finance Act 85, s 2](https://nalunaarutit.gl/groenlandsk-lovgivning/2025/inatsisartutlov-nr-85-af-8_12_2025?sc_lang=da); [Annex 2, p 40](https://nalunaarutit.gl/-/media/lovfiler/2025/forarbejder/ina_85/ina_85_2025_bilag2_da.pdf); [Sulinal notice](https://sulinal.nanoq.gl/DocumentsForDownload/Bilingual/PRIVATE_arbejdsgivere-AMA-satser_%C3%A6ndres_fra_2026.pdf).

Keep contractual pension payments and employee A-tax separate. Do not deduct employer AMA from employees' cash pay as if it were their income tax. The former manual's July 2025 example reportedly used 2.1%; that is retained only as a historical warning, not proof of any 2026 amount. The enacted 2026 authorities above govern this working paper.

### 4. Prepare the monthly return

The Tax Authority's public monthly instructions require salary, tax, pension and benefits data. Prepare employee CPR/name, tax municipality, employer CVR/name/address, period, A-income, A-tax, foreign-pension payments and tax, benefits and pension data. Use Sulinal's per-employee entry or its current CSV route; do not invent private upload fields. Employers with 20 or more employees must report digitally; smaller employers may use the public A 1 paper route. Give employees the monthly pay/pension/tax information. No authenticated account, private schema, submission or payment has been tested here. [Monthly instructions](https://aka.gl/da/erhverv/arbejdsgiverregistret/maanedsredegoerelse).

Report AMA by the 10th of the next month under sections 3–4. Income-law section 81(3) sets the corresponding A-tax monthly reporting date and domestic preparation/storage requirements, with its bilateral-agreement exception. The public monthly page confirms the 10th and weekend/holiday roll. Retain the submitted return, its acknowledgement and any rejected-field corrections. [AMA ss 3–4](https://nalunaarutit.gl/groenlandsk-lovgivning/2025/selvstyrets-lovbekendtgoerelse-nr-42-af-05_05_2025?sc_lang=da); [income-law s 81](https://nalunaarutit.gl/groenlandsk-lovgivning/2025/selvstyrets-lovbekendtgoerelse-nr-62-af-24_09_2025?sc_lang=da); [monthly instructions](https://aka.gl/da/erhverv/arbejdsgiverregistret/maanedsredegoerelse).

### 5. Schedule and prove payment

AMA is due on the first of the following month, with last timely payment on the first day of the month after the due month. Saturdays, Sundays and public holidays move the last timely date to the following working day. AMA section 6 permits shorter periods/payment deadlines for late payers; income-law section 81(2) permits special A-tax payment rules for disorder/neglect. A-tax section 81(1) gives the same ordinary due/last-payment sequence. Check the authority's actual notice and holiday calendar before using a calendar date. [AMA ss 5–6](https://nalunaarutit.gl/groenlandsk-lovgivning/2025/selvstyrets-lovbekendtgoerelse-nr-42-af-05_05_2025?sc_lang=da); [income-law s 81](https://nalunaarutit.gl/groenlandsk-lovgivning/2025/selvstyrets-lovbekendtgoerelse-nr-62-af-24_09_2025?sc_lang=da).

Compare payroll register, Sulinal return, tax invoice and bank payment. The public guidance separates reporting from payment: a submission receipt is not proof of remittance. Preserve separate components even if paid together. [Monthly instructions](https://aka.gl/da/erhverv/arbejdsgiverregistret/maanedsredegoerelse); [annual instructions](https://aka.gl/da/erhverv/arbejdsgiverregistret/yearly-tax-report).

### 6. Close the annual reporting and employee copies

Prepare A 11 information for the authority and each employee. The public annual instructions distinguish per-employee A 11 and aggregate A 10 paper information; reconcile the aggregate to employee totals and include pension/benefit/foreign-tax fields as applicable. The authority's annual window is January after the income year; cessation has a separate deadline. Confirm the applicable route and current form before submitting. [Annual instructions](https://aka.gl/da/erhverv/arbejdsgiverregistret/yearly-tax-report); [Tax Administration Act, ss 30–32](https://nalunaarutit.gl/groenlandsk-lovgivning/2021/l-bkg-11-2021?sc_lang=da).

## Worked cases

### Case 1: original monthly reconciliation

Assume the original DKK 100,000 is the **already verified 2026 AMA base** and employee A-tax totals DKK 32,000 after individual card computations. Employer AMA is DKK 1,900; the combined reconciliation total is DKK 33,900. The DKK 32,000 is an assumed payroll result, not a prescribed 32% withholding rate; this case does not calculate net pay or prove that a payment was made.

```oa-check
name: Original monthly AMA and A-tax reconciliation
inputs:
  verified_ama_base: 100000
  ama_rate: 0.019
  verified_employee_a_tax: 32000
steps:
  - employer_ama: verified_ama_base * ama_rate
  - combined_reconciliation: employer_ama + verified_employee_a_tax
expected:
  employer_ama: 1900
  combined_reconciliation: 33900
```

### Case 2: original September 2026 calendar illustration, conditional

For September 2026 the unadjusted report date is 10 October and ordinary last timely payment date is 1 November. They fall on Saturday and Sunday respectively, so the **weekend-only** results are 12 October and 2 November. These remain conditional illustrations: the Greenland holiday calendar and any accelerated notice have not been established for a client's facts. Do not present them as confirmed filing/payment dates until both checks are complete. [Underlying deadline rules](https://nalunaarutit.gl/groenlandsk-lovgivning/2025/selvstyrets-lovbekendtgoerelse-nr-42-af-05_05_2025?sc_lang=da); [A-tax s 81](https://nalunaarutit.gl/groenlandsk-lovgivning/2025/selvstyrets-lovbekendtgoerelse-nr-62-af-24_09_2025?sc_lang=da).

## When to refuse or refer

Do not compute employee residence, card allowances, net pay, pension contributions, benefits in kind, holiday-pay timing, gross-tax-scheme eligibility, combined Danish withholding or late-payment interest from this Guide. Obtain separate evidence and qualified review for each. Refer unclassified AMA exemptions or pension payments; no automatic pension deduction is allowed before the cross-referenced conditions are confirmed.

**Unusable pending evidence:** the September 2026 manual is publicly announced by the Tax Authority, but its PDF could not be read in the current recovery. Its asserted nil-monthly-report duty while registered is not established here. Do not infer either a permission to skip a return or a specific nil-filing rule from that inaccessible manual. The public monthly page says an employer with no current employees can inform Sulinal; obtain the actual authority instruction for a nil/cessation period before treating it as complete. The A-tax deadline sequence is independently supported by section 81, rather than by this unread manual. [Publication notice](https://aka.gl/da/nyheder/skattetraek-i-groenland-er-opdateret-september-2026); [monthly page](https://aka.gl/da/erhverv/arbejdsgiverregistret/maanedsredegoerelse); [manual target, unread](https://multisite.digitalimik.gl/media/ag1ls2s5/skattetraek-i-groenland-dk-sept26.pdf).

## Completion checklist

Retain registration and cards, classified remuneration and pensions, separate A-tax/AMA reconciliations, rate source, return/form version, reporting and payment calendars, holiday/notice checks, acknowledgement, tax invoice, bank proof, annual/employee copies and all held issues. A qualified local reviewer must check the actual facts and current law before filing or payment. No professional attestation is claimed.
