# Saint Kitts and Nevis tax system reference v0.1

Use this reference to select the relevant tax person, territorial branch, period, currency rule and authority. Load workflow-base in full. Establish the requested date, legal form, residence, income source, activity, island and representative capacity before selecting a calculation or return workflow.

The source periods are **31 December 2017** for the specified revised statutes, **31 December 2002** for the Inland Revenue Act and the separate **26 March 2021** company amendment. They do not form a complete consolidation of all law at 2021 or 2026. The [country reference](references.md) and [income tax, payroll and Social Security guide](kn-social-security-reconciliation.md) retain separately dated rate, filing, VAT, property and contribution material and their unresolved conditions.

## Territorial reach

Income Tax Act, Cap. 20.22, s 3 starts with specified income of any person accruing in, derived from or received in Saint Christopher and Nevis or elsewhere, whether received locally or not. Its income heads and qualifications must be read with **s 3(3)**: income accruing on or after **1 May 1980** to a person other than a company is excluded from the general charge. Historical personal rates, allowances and representative machinery do not undo that exclusion. Separate business taxes and withholding provisions require their own scope analysis. [Sections 2–3, physical pp 7–11](https://lawcommission.gov.kn/wp-content/documents/Revised-Acts-of-St-Kitts-and-Nevis/Revised-Acts-of-St-Kitts-and-Nevis-2017/Ch-20_22-Income-Tax-Act.pdf#page=7)

The retained s 3 proviso charges the locally received amount of foreign **earned income arising to a person not ordinarily resident or not domiciled** in the Federation. Preserve that combined wording. A separate mixed local/foreign trade provision treats the whole profit as locally arising for a resident individual whose home is in the Federation. Another proviso concerns foreign income of a person present only temporarily, without an intention to establish residence, who has not actually resided locally for an aggregate 6 months in the basic year. These historical provisos remain subject to s 3(3); do not convert them into a current ordinary personal income charge. [Section 3, physical pp 10–11](https://lawcommission.gov.kn/wp-content/documents/Revised-Acts-of-St-Kitts-and-Nevis/Revised-Acts-of-St-Kitts-and-Nevis-2017/Ch-20_22-Income-Tax-Act.pdf#page=10)

Act 12 of 2021 inserted s 3A. A resident company's assessable income includes its assessable income wherever arising. A non-resident company carrying on local trade or business through a local permanent establishment is assessed on income attributable to that establishment. This is not a charge on every foreign company's worldwide income. The amendment records assent and Gazette publication on 26 March 2021 and contains no postponed or appointed-day clause. Its operation from that date is a **source-based inference** from those records and Constitution s 42(3)–(4), which requires Gazette publication for operation whilst permitting postponement or retrospective legislation. [Amendment, physical pp 1–4](https://lawcommission.gov.kn/wp-content/documents/Annual-Laws/2021/ACTs/Act-12-of-2021-Income-Tax-Amendment-Act-2021.pdf#page=1) · [Constitution, physical p 36](https://lawcommission.gov.kn/wp-content/documents/Annual-Laws/The-Constitution-of-St-Christopher-and-Nevis.pdf#page=36)

The inserted permanent-establishment definition includes a fixed place of business, specified construction or related supervisory activity lasting **more than 6 months** and specified services exceeding **183 days** in its stated 12-month/fiscal-year frame. Its preparatory or auxiliary exceptions, anti-fragmentation conditions, dependent-agent branches and ordinary-course independent-agent exception must be applied together. The independent-agent exception is restricted where the agent acts exclusively or almost exclusively for one or more enterprises to which the agent is a related person. Control of one company by another does not alone establish a permanent establishment. Preserve the amendment's actual wording and awkward cross-references; obtain an interpretation where they affect a result. The services duration is not an individual residence test. [Inserted s 3A, physical pp 2–4](https://lawcommission.gov.kn/wp-content/documents/Annual-Laws/2021/ACTs/Act-12-of-2021-Income-Tax-Amendment-Act-2021.pdf#page=2)

Unincorporated Business Tax Act, Cap. 18.46, ss 2–4 separately applies to persons carrying on business in the Federation, subject to its exclusions. Its business definition excludes employment. Companies **registered under the Companies Act, Cap. 21.03**, and partnerships treated as companies and liable under the Income Tax Act are excluded. Do not expand those words into an exemption for every entity called a company or into an ordinary wage charge. The separate payment-withholding branches in Income Tax Act ss 36–37 require their own conditions. Detailed rates, relief, withholding conflicts and later instruments remain in the linked references. [UBT ss 2–4, physical pp 5–6](https://lawcommission.gov.kn/wp-content/documents/Act17TOC/Ch-18_46-Unincorporated-Business-Tax-Act.pdf#page=5) · [Income Tax Act ss 36–37, physical pp 35–39](https://lawcommission.gov.kn/wp-content/documents/Revised-Acts-of-St-Kitts-and-Nevis/Revised-Acts-of-St-Kitts-and-Nevis-2017/Ch-20_22-Income-Tax-Act.pdf#page=35)

## Tax residence

Inserted s 3A(4) defines a resident company by **management and control from within Saint Christopher and Nevis, regardless of its registration location**. Management and control is where the company's central mind, management and control is ordinarily situated. Incorporation, a registered office or an agent address alone does not establish that factual test. [Act 12 of 2021, physical p 2](https://lawcommission.gov.kn/wp-content/documents/Annual-Laws/2021/ACTs/Act-12-of-2021-Income-Tax-Amendment-Act-2021.pdf#page=2)

The inspected Cap. 20.22 s 2 definitions do not supply a general individual residence day count. Ordinary personal income is subject to s 3(3)'s exclusion; residence can still matter for a particular withholding, exemption, relief or representative provision. Section 36(5) defines non-residence for its withholding section by a person not residing in the Federation as determined under the Act. The management/control wording in the shipping exemption and the residence rule confined to Commonwealth double-tax relief serve their own provisions. Do not substitute either rule, or the permanent-establishment services test, for a universal individual residence test. [Definitions, physical pp 7–9](https://lawcommission.gov.kn/wp-content/documents/Revised-Acts-of-St-Kitts-and-Nevis/Revised-Acts-of-St-Kitts-and-Nevis-2017/Ch-20_22-Income-Tax-Act.pdf#page=7) · [Shipping exemption, physical p 15](https://lawcommission.gov.kn/wp-content/documents/Revised-Acts-of-St-Kitts-and-Nevis/Revised-Acts-of-St-Kitts-and-Nevis-2017/Ch-20_22-Income-Tax-Act.pdf#page=15) · [Section 36(5), physical p 37](https://lawcommission.gov.kn/wp-content/documents/Revised-Acts-of-St-Kitts-and-Nevis/Revised-Acts-of-St-Kitts-and-Nevis-2017/Ch-20_22-Income-Tax-Act.pdf#page=37) · [Section 89(6), physical p 58](https://lawcommission.gov.kn/wp-content/documents/Revised-Acts-of-St-Kitts-and-Nevis/Revised-Acts-of-St-Kitts-and-Nevis-2017/Ch-20_22-Income-Tax-Act.pdf#page=58)

## Taxpayer types and representatives

Cap. 20.22 s 2 includes a body of persons within 'person' and distinguishes 'individual'. Its body definition includes specified incorporated and unincorporated bodies; its company definition includes locally and foreign incorporated or registered companies. Apply those tax definitions rather than assuming that a company-law label fixes every tax result. [Section 2, physical pp 7–9](https://lawcommission.gov.kn/wp-content/documents/Revised-Acts-of-St-Kitts-and-Nevis/Revised-Acts-of-St-Kitts-and-Nevis-2017/Ch-20_22-Income-Tax-Act.pdf#page=7)

Part V, ss 19–29, retains spouse, deceased-person, partnership, trustee, non-resident representative and attribution machinery. Its partnership and representative rules address the person assessable, required information and income attribution under their conditions. Read them with s 3(3) before inferring a substantive charge. A representative's filing or payment duties do not automatically classify the underlying estate, trust or partnership as a company. [Part V, physical pp 27–30](https://lawcommission.gov.kn/wp-content/documents/Revised-Acts-of-St-Kitts-and-Nevis/Revised-Acts-of-St-Kitts-and-Nevis-2017/Ch-20_22-Income-Tax-Act.pdf#page=27)

Cap. 18.46 s 3 includes a partnership and a firm within 'person'. Section 5(5)–(8) permits a one-time election for Income Tax Act computation, continuing thereafter, and deems the resulting tax corporate tax. Section 6 makes the partners jointly and severally liable and addresses the designated responsible partner. Check the s 2 company/qualifying-partnership exclusions before selecting that route. Keep legal form, substantive charge, income attribution, election and responsible filer as separate questions. [UBT ss 2–6, physical pp 5–7](https://lawcommission.gov.kn/wp-content/documents/Act17TOC/Ch-18_46-Unincorporated-Business-Tax-Act.pdf#page=5)

Tax Administration and Procedures Act, Cap. 20.52, s 2 separately includes specified withholding payers and exempt persons required to submit returns in 'taxpayer'. An information or withholding obligation can therefore arise without proving that every receipt of that person is charged to ordinary income tax. [Administration ss 2–3, physical pp 7–8](https://lawcommission.gov.kn/wp-content/documents/Act17TOC/Ch-20_52-Tax-Administration-and-Procedures-Act.pdf#page=7)

## Currency

Cap. 20.52 s 20 makes tax within that Act's scope payable in **Eastern Caribbean dollars**. Sections 2–3 define its scope by covered tax laws and other taxes administered by Inland Revenue; 'tax law' excludes laws imposing customs or excise duty. Read the scope before applying its payment rule. Section 21 identifies statutory payment places or authorised recipients, and s 10(5) permits the specified electronic authorisation. Those provisions do not establish which bank, portal or institution is currently approved or accepts a payment. [Scope, physical pp 7–8](https://lawcommission.gov.kn/wp-content/documents/Act17TOC/Ch-20_52-Tax-Administration-and-Procedures-Act.pdf#page=7) · [Electronic arrangements, physical p 10](https://lawcommission.gov.kn/wp-content/documents/Act17TOC/Ch-20_52-Tax-Administration-and-Procedures-Act.pdf#page=10) · [Payment, physical p 12](https://lawcommission.gov.kn/wp-content/documents/Act17TOC/Ch-20_52-Tax-Administration-and-Procedures-Act.pdf#page=12)

Customs Act, Cap. 20.04, s 262(b) separately permits its specified customs duties, rates, charges and drawback to be paid in any currency that is legal tender in the Federation. Do not apply Cap. 20.52 s 20 indiscriminately to Customs or every Social Security remittance. [Customs s 262, physical p 137](https://lawcommission.gov.kn/wp-content/documents/Revised-Acts-of-St-Kitts-and-Nevis/Revised-Acts-of-St-Kitts-and-Nevis-2017/Ch-20_04-Customs-Act.pdf#page=137)

ECCB Act, Cap. 21.06, Schedule Article 17(1) names the monetary unit as the Eastern Caribbean Dollar divided into 100 cents. Articles 18–20 separately address issue, legal tender, coin limits, altered notes or coins and withdrawal. Section 4 requires a Ministerial Gazette-appointed date for the Articles' local force. The complete appointment chain has **not** been established here; the Act's own recorded in-force date does not independently prove that separate appointment. Describe the scheduled denomination with that qualification. Interpretation Act s 2's British West Indian Currency definition alone supplies no general modern 'dollar' definition. No live exchange rate, conversion method or accepted payment channel is established. [ECCB ss 2–4, physical p 5](https://lawcommission.gov.kn/wp-content/documents/Revised-Acts-of-St-Kitts-and-Nevis/Revised-Acts-of-St-Kitts-and-Nevis-2017/Ch-21_06-Eastern-Caribbean-Central-Bank-Act.pdf#page=5) · [Articles 17–20, physical pp 21–22](https://lawcommission.gov.kn/wp-content/documents/Revised-Acts-of-St-Kitts-and-Nevis/Revised-Acts-of-St-Kitts-and-Nevis-2017/Ch-21_06-Eastern-Caribbean-Central-Bank-Act.pdf#page=21) · [Interpretation s 2, physical pp 5–10](https://lawcommission.gov.kn/wp-content/documents/Revised-Acts-of-St-Kitts-and-Nevis/Revised-Acts-of-St-Kitts-and-Nevis-2017/Ch-01_02-Interpretation-Act.pdf#page=5)

## Assessment periods

Cap. 20.22 s 2 defines the assessment year as the 12 months beginning on 1 January. Section 4 ordinarily assesses the preceding basic year ending on 31 December, subject to ss 68–69. The Commissioners may permit another trade accounting date; the corresponding basis continues for later assessments and a change can require reasonable adjustments. Income of the basic year can remain assessable even if no income arises in the assessment year. Preserve the stated permission and adjustment conditions. [Definitions and s 4, physical pp 9–12](https://lawcommission.gov.kn/wp-content/documents/Revised-Acts-of-St-Kitts-and-Nevis/Revised-Acts-of-St-Kitts-and-Nevis-2017/Ch-20_22-Income-Tax-Act.pdf#page=9)

The assessment year, income/basic year, permitted accounting date, instalment cycle and return deadline are distinct. Cap. 18.46 s 5 uses monthly goods and services bases; s 7 supplies a separate quarterly return and payment cycle. The linked guide preserves the conflict between statutory monthly floors and quarterly agency allowances. A filing example or a quarterly allowance does not redefine the income-tax basic year or settle that computation conflict. [UBT ss 5–7, physical pp 6–7](https://lawcommission.gov.kn/wp-content/documents/Act17TOC/Ch-18_46-Unincorporated-Business-Tax-Act.pdf#page=6) · [Detailed UBT reconciliation](kn-social-security-reconciliation.md#unincorporated-business-tax)

## Responsible authorities and island allocation

The 31 December 2002 Inland Revenue Act, Cap. 20.23, ss 2–4 establishes Inland Revenue under ministerial supervision and provides for the Comptroller and officers. Its revenue definition excludes collection assigned to Customs. Its old Schedule is not an exhaustive list of taxes introduced later. Cap. 20.52 ss 46–47 separately provides for the Comptroller and the assessment, collection, accounting and administration functions within that Act. Income Tax Act s 38 addresses appointments under that Act, including assessment-appeal Commissioners. Use the relevant instrument's actor and function. [Inland Revenue ss 2–4, physical p 5](https://lawcommission.gov.kn/wp-content/documents/Act02and09TOC/Ch-20_23-Inland-Revenue-Act.pdf#page=5) · [Administration ss 46–47, physical pp 20–21](https://lawcommission.gov.kn/wp-content/documents/Act17TOC/Ch-20_52-Tax-Administration-and-Procedures-Act.pdf#page=20) · [Income s 38, physical p 39](https://lawcommission.gov.kn/wp-content/documents/Revised-Acts-of-St-Kitts-and-Nevis/Revised-Acts-of-St-Kitts-and-Nevis-2017/Ch-20_22-Income-Tax-Act.pdf#page=39)

Customs Act ss 2 and 4–6 separately defines its department and Comptroller, assigns administration and addresses authorisation and delegation. That mandate does not make Inland Revenue the collector of every customs duty. Social Security Act, Cap. 22.10, s 3 establishes the Social Security Board; s 14 requires the Board to appoint its Director as chief executive officer. Keep those contribution authorities distinct from Inland Revenue and Customs. [Customs, physical pp 12 and 17–18](https://lawcommission.gov.kn/wp-content/documents/Revised-Acts-of-St-Kitts-and-Nevis/Revised-Acts-of-St-Kitts-and-Nevis-2017/Ch-20_04-Customs-Act.pdf#page=12) · [Social Security Board, physical p 10](https://lawcommission.gov.kn/wp-content/documents/Act17TOC/Ch-22_10-Social-Security-Act.pdf#page=10) · [Director, physical p 12](https://lawcommission.gov.kn/wp-content/documents/Act17TOC/Ch-22_10-Social-Security-Act.pdf#page=12)

Income Tax Act ss 83–84 contains Federal Government/Nevis Island Administration corporate-tax allocation provisions tied to business activities on the relevant island and the stated attribution conditions. They do not allocate solely by registered-office location. Their text refers to their coming into force; the underlying commencement instruments have not been independently established here. Do not infer a live revenue allocation or an island exemption from this orientation. Detailed property, entity and island regimes require their specific instruments. [Sections 83–84, physical pp 55–56](https://lawcommission.gov.kn/wp-content/documents/Revised-Acts-of-St-Kitts-and-Nevis/Revised-Acts-of-St-Kitts-and-Nevis-2017/Ch-20_22-Income-Tax-Act.pdf#page=55)

## Classification checks

These are manual source-selection checks, not executed tax returns or arithmetic fixtures.

| Fictional case | Required classification |
|---|---|
| Ordinary employee receives wages after 1 May 1980 | Apply s 3(3) before historical personal tax or PAYE machinery; check separate payroll levies and Social Security. |
| Foreign-incorporated company is managed and controlled from within the Federation | Test inserted s 3A(4)'s actual central mind, management and control facts; registration abroad alone does not establish non-residence. |
| A person provides services for 184 days | Identify whether the stated corporate permanent-establishment services branch and its full period/activity conditions apply; do not infer individual tax residence from this number alone. |
| An unincorporated partnership proposes an income-tax computation election | Check UBT s 2 exclusions, the one-time continuing election and resulting corporate-tax treatment, separately from partner liability and the responsible filer. |
| A covered Inland Revenue payment and a Customs payment are proposed in foreign currency | Distinguish Cap. 20.52 s 20 from Customs s 262; establish the actual authority, legal tender, conversion and accepted channel before payment. |
| A company selects a non-December accounting date | Obtain the relevant Commissioners' permission and apply the continuing basis and adjustment rules; distinguish the tax assessment year from its accounting period and filing deadline. |
| A Nevis registered office is offered as the only allocation fact | Establish actual island business activity and the ss 83–84 conditions and commencement; do not substitute the address for attribution evidence. |

## Source and review limits

The 2017 revisions, older Inland Revenue Act and separate 2021 amendment support this dated orientation. Selected complete definitions, charging, company residence, permanent-establishment, persons-assessable, period, currency and authority provisions were reconciled. The Constitution is used for the identified commencement inference. The retained Social Security Act supplies Board and Director provisions; it does not resolve the contribution-rate conflict in the separate guide.

The framework covers the 6 orientation components without certifying all later legislation, relief, exemptions, detailed tax calculations or operational procedures. The ECCB appointment and income-tax island-allocation commencement chains remain qualified. Later corporate rates, ordinary withholding conflicts, UBT computation differences, Social Security charging bridges, incentives and actual filing or payment conditions remain in their respective topics.
