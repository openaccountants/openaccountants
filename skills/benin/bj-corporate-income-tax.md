# Benin corporate IS working paper v0.1

> **General reference only.** This skill is general tax/accounting reference material for AI-assisted workflows. It has not been reviewed for any specific person's facts, documents, elections, deadlines, residency, filing status, or local procedures. Do not rely on it to file, pay, amend, or take a tax position without review by a qualified professional in the relevant jurisdiction.

## Who this applies to

Prepare the ordinary Benin IS calculation and instalment reconciliation in 2026 using the law applicable to the particular accounting period. SARL, SA and SAS fall within the form-based charge; exemptions, incentives and sector conventions must be checked before this ordinary computation is used. [CGI 2026, art. 3](https://apisiteweb.impots.bj/media/6adcbd46ab83b_B%C3%A9nin-Code%20G%C3%A9n%C3%A9ral%20des%20Imp%C3%B4ts%202026.pdf#page=6) [CGI 2026, art. 4](https://apisiteweb.impots.bj/media/6adcbd46ab83b_B%C3%A9nin-Code%20G%C3%A9n%C3%A9ral%20des%20Imp%C3%B4ts%202026.pdf#page=7) [CGI 2026, art. 145](https://apisiteweb.impots.bj/media/6adcbd46ab83b_B%C3%A9nin-Code%20G%C3%A9n%C3%A9ral%20des%20Imp%C3%B4ts%202026.pdf#page=71) [CGI 2026, art. 146](https://apisiteweb.impots.bj/media/6adcbd46ab83b_B%C3%A9nin-Code%20G%C3%A9n%C3%A9ral%20des%20Imp%C3%B4ts%202026.pdf#page=71)

The territorial rule covers enterprises operated in Benin and profits allocated to Benin under a double-tax agreement. Resident entities and non-residents with a permanent establishment are addressed by articles 5–6. Do not assume a foreign branch is automatically outside the computation without checking those conditions. This Guide produces a reviewed calculation schedule, not a completed electronic return. [CGI 2026, art. 5](https://apisiteweb.impots.bj/media/6adcbd46ab83b_B%C3%A9nin-Code%20G%C3%A9n%C3%A9ral%20des%20Imp%C3%B4ts%202026.pdf#page=9) [CGI 2026, art. 6](https://apisiteweb.impots.bj/media/6adcbd46ab83b_B%C3%A9nin-Code%20G%C3%A9n%C3%A9ral%20des%20Imp%C3%B4ts%202026.pdf#page=10)

## The base

1. Start from the accounts and identify the correct taxpayer, period, territorial scope and any exempt income. The ordinary year closes on 31 December; teaching establishments and qualifying first periods have article 8 exceptions. [CGI 2026, art. 8](https://apisiteweb.impots.bj/media/6adcbd46ab83b_B%C3%A9nin-Code%20G%C3%A9n%C3%A9ral%20des%20Imp%C3%B4ts%202026.pdf#page=13)
2. Reconcile accounting profit to taxable profit. The net-profit rule is article 9. Deduct only effective business charges of the correct period, sufficiently supported, producing non-exempt income, reducing net assets and, where required, backed by paid withholding. Apply specific expense restrictions separately; this Guide does not certify every adjustment. Fines and penalties within article 36 are non-deductible. [CGI 2026, art. 9](https://apisiteweb.impots.bj/media/6adcbd46ab83b_B%C3%A9nin-Code%20G%C3%A9n%C3%A9ral%20des%20Imp%C3%B4ts%202026.pdf#page=13) [CGI 2026, art. 20](https://apisiteweb.impots.bj/media/6adcbd46ab83b_B%C3%A9nin-Code%20G%C3%A9n%C3%A9ral%20des%20Imp%C3%B4ts%202026.pdf#page=18) [CGI 2026, art. 36](https://apisiteweb.impots.bj/media/6adcbd46ab83b_B%C3%A9nin-Code%20G%C3%A9n%C3%A9ral%20des%20Imp%C3%B4ts%202026.pdf#page=24)
3. Reconcile produits encaissables separately from turnover and taxable profit: include amounts giving or capable of giving a financial flow; exclude capitalised production, inventory production, transfers of charges, and reversals of provisions/depreciation. [CGI 2026, art. 47](https://apisiteweb.impots.bj/media/6adcbd46ab83b_B%C3%A9nin-Code%20G%C3%A9n%C3%A9ral%20des%20Imp%C3%B4ts%202026.pdf#page=29)
4. Compare profit-based IS, the applicable sector minimum and the absolute floor. Keep the ORTB levy separate. Route newly created, restructured, art-trading and approved-incentive cases to their specific assessment before finalising. [CGI 2026, art. 46](https://apisiteweb.impots.bj/media/6adcbd46ab83b_B%C3%A9nin-Code%20G%C3%A9n%C3%A9ral%20des%20Imp%C3%B4ts%202026.pdf#page=28) [CGI 2026, art. 47](https://apisiteweb.impots.bj/media/6adcbd46ab83b_B%C3%A9nin-Code%20G%C3%A9n%C3%A9ral%20des%20Imp%C3%B4ts%202026.pdf#page=29) [CGI 2026, art. 145](https://apisiteweb.impots.bj/media/6adcbd46ab83b_B%C3%A9nin-Code%20G%C3%A9n%C3%A9ral%20des%20Imp%C3%B4ts%202026.pdf#page=71) [CGI 2026, art. 146](https://apisiteweb.impots.bj/media/6adcbd46ab83b_B%C3%A9nin-Code%20G%C3%A9n%C3%A9ral%20des%20Imp%C3%B4ts%202026.pdf#page=71)
5. Deduct documented instalments and allowable credits once. Record any excess as a credit under article 51 rather than assuming an immediate refund. [CGI 2026, art. 51](https://apisiteweb.impots.bj/media/6adcbd46ab83b_B%C3%A9nin-Code%20G%C3%A9n%C3%A9ral%20des%20Imp%C3%B4ts%202026.pdf#page=32)

## Deadlines and filing

The annual result declaration is due by 30 April for the preceding exercise. Attach the article 50 accounts, expense/depreciation/provision schedules and required supporting disclosures. Ordinary accounts use the applicable OHADA presentation; regulated sectors have their own frameworks. [CGI 2026, art. 49](https://apisiteweb.impots.bj/media/6adcbd46ab83b_B%C3%A9nin-Code%20G%C3%A9n%C3%A9ral%20des%20Imp%C3%B4ts%202026.pdf#page=30) [CGI 2026, art. 50](https://apisiteweb.impots.bj/media/6adcbd46ab83b_B%C3%A9nin-Code%20G%C3%A9n%C3%A9ral%20des%20Imp%C3%B4ts%202026.pdf#page=30)

Four instalments, each normally one quarter of the preceding year's IS, fall due by 10 March, 10 June, 10 September and 10 December. The first instalment can use the penultimate year's tax provisionally if the latest return is not yet filed; regularise at the second instalment. Pay the balance with the annual declaration. A reduction of instalments requires the article 52 process, not an informal decision. [CGI 2026, art. 51](https://apisiteweb.impots.bj/media/6adcbd46ab83b_B%C3%A9nin-Code%20G%C3%A9n%C3%A9ral%20des%20Imp%C3%B4ts%202026.pdf#page=32) [CGI 2026, art. 52](https://apisiteweb.impots.bj/media/6adcbd46ab83b_B%C3%A9nin-Code%20G%C3%A9n%C3%A9ral%20des%20Imp%C3%B4ts%202026.pdf#page=32)

Prepare the calculation and attachment list for the authorised filer on [DGI e-services](https://e-services.impots.bj/). The authenticated form, field mapping, payment reference and acknowledgement were not accessed. Stop before filing until those are confirmed.

## Two worked examples that recompute

**Ordinary company, profit tax wins.** Assume all tax adjustments have been checked, no incentive or special-sector rule applies, and taxable profit is FCFA 10 million. Produits encaissables are FCFA 100 million. Profit IS is 3 million and the ordinary minimum is 1 million. IS is 3 million; the separate annual ORTB levy is 4,000. The example does not calculate credits or payment timing.

```oa-check
name: Ordinary IS versus minimum
inputs:
  taxable_profit: 10000000
  produits_encaissables: 100000000
steps:
  profit_is: taxable_profit * 0.30
  percentage_minimum: produits_encaissables * 0.01
  annual_is: max(profit_is, percentage_minimum, 250000)
  is_plus_separate_levy: annual_is + 4000
expected:
  profit_is: 3000000
  percentage_minimum: 1000000
  annual_is: 3000000
  is_plus_separate_levy: 3004000
```

**Low profit, the minimum changes the result.** With the same ordinary scope, taxable profit is FCFA 100,000 and produits encaissables FCFA 20 million. Profit IS is 30,000 and the percentage minimum is 200,000; the absolute floor makes IS 250,000. Add the levy separately.

```oa-check
name: Absolute IS floor
inputs:
  taxable_profit: 100000
  produits_encaissables: 20000000
steps:
  profit_is: taxable_profit * 0.30
  percentage_minimum: produits_encaissables * 0.01
  annual_is: max(profit_is, percentage_minimum, 250000)
  is_plus_separate_levy: annual_is + 4000
expected:
  profit_is: 30000
  percentage_minimum: 200000
  annual_is: 250000
  is_plus_separate_levy: 254000
```

## Ask the client first

- What are the legal form, activity, accounting period and start date?
- Are there exemptions, incentives, restructurings or a mining/petroleum convention?
- Which Benin and foreign operations, branches or permanent establishments produced the profit?
- What are taxable profit, each adjustment, produits encaissables and any sector-specific minimum base?
- Which losses, instalments, AIB or other credits are supported and unused?
- Are there distributions, retained earnings, foreign payments or related-party transactions?
