# Benin SARL and SA formation working paper v0.1

> **General reference only.** This skill is general tax/accounting reference material for AI-assisted workflows. It has not been reviewed for any specific person's facts, documents, elections, deadlines, residency, filing status, or local procedures. Do not rely on it to file, pay, amend, or take a tax position without review by a qualified professional in the relevant jurisdiction.

## Who this applies to

Prepare the registration and capital checklist for an ordinary Benin SARL/SUARL or SA in 2026, with other forms referred to their own legal and APIEx requirements. APIEx's portal lists SARL, SA, SAS, SUARL, SNC, SCS and GIE; a sole-trader establishment and branch are separate choices, not interchangeable company forms. This Guide prepares the packet and capital/fee comparison. It does not submit an authenticated registration or determine a regulated-activity licence. [APIEx](https://monentreprise.bj/creer/societe) [AUSCGIE, art. 309](https://biblio.ohada.org/doc_num.php?explnum_id=2032)

The signed national SARL decree is dated **26 March 2014**, despite the portal link label saying 28 March. Its article 6 permits freely determined capital; OHADA article 311 expressly allows contrary national provisions. Use the signed text, not the obsolete regional default as a mandatory Benin SARL minimum. [AUSCGIE, art. 311](https://biblio.ohada.org/doc_num.php?explnum_id=2032) [Decree 2014-220, arts. 6 and 11](https://monentreprise.bj/Decret_N_2014_220_du_28_mars_2014_portant_modalites_de_creation_des_Societes_a_Responsabilite_Limitee_a73a58faf6.pdf#page=3)

| Published APIEx SA/SAS fee | FCFA 37,000; notary/professional charges not included | [APIEx](https://monentreprise.bj/informer/couts-delais); [APIEx](https://monentreprise.bj/creer/societe) |

## The base

1. Choose the legal form from the ownership, liability, governance, financing and regulated-activity facts. A SARL can have one or several associates with liability limited to contributions. Do not treat a branch or sole trader as a company with identical capital/liability. [AUSCGIE, art. 309](https://biblio.ohada.org/doc_num.php?explnum_id=2032) [APIEx](https://monentreprise.bj/creer/societe)
2. Separate subscribed capital, paid capital, the remaining payment obligation, the government registration fee and professional charges. SARL cash funds are deposited in an authorised bank/credit/microfinance institution with a receipt in the formation company's name; record the payment/deposit in the statutes. [AUSCGIE, art. 311-1](https://biblio.ohada.org/doc_num.php?explnum_id=2032) [Decree arts. 7–8](https://monentreprise.bj/Decret_N_2014_220_du_28_mars_2014_portant_modalites_de_creation_des_Societes_a_Responsabilite_Limitee_a73a58faf6.pdf#page=3)
3. Prepare statutes signed by all associates, identity documents for associates/managers, the manager's criminal-record extract, head-office domiciliation evidence, SA/SAS subscription/payment declaration and cash-deposit certificate where applicable. APIEx requires a notary for SA/SAS; SARL statutes can be private or notarised. Confirm the specific document list for foreigners and in-kind contributions before submission. [APIEx](https://monentreprise.bj/creer/societe) [Decree art. 2](https://monentreprise.bj/Decret_N_2014_220_du_28_mars_2014_portant_modalites_de_creation_des_Societes_a_Responsabilite_Limitee_a73a58faf6.pdf#page=2)
4. Test statutory-auditor requirements against the law's two-of-three SARL test and the separate SA rule. Do not use the portal comparison's capital-based/any-one test in place of OHADA article 376. Review appointment/release procedures separately for a live case. [AUSCGIE, art. 376](https://biblio.ohada.org/doc_num.php?explnum_id=2032) [AUSCGIE, art. 694](https://biblio.ohada.org/doc_num.php?explnum_id=2032)

## Deadlines and filing

Start at [APIEx MonEntreprise](https://monentreprise.bj/creer/societe), select the actual form and prepare the stated documents. The public process describes an online form, online payment, APIEx processing with the tribunal/DGI/CNSS and downloadable certificates. The authenticated fields, checkout, final validation and receipts were not accessed; this working paper stops before submission. [APIEx](https://monentreprise.bj/informer/processus)

Reconcile the received RCCM extract, IFU tax identifier, CNSS employer notification, labour declaration and legal announcement against the application. Check their authenticity using the portal's certificate instructions. IFU is the DGI-assigned identifier; do not substitute a guessed tax number. [APIEx](https://monentreprise.bj/informer/certificats)

For continuing ordinary-company compliance, maintain the applicable OHADA accounts and the annual IS attachment pack, use the [corporate Guide](bj-corporate-income-tax.md) for tax filing dates, and the [VAT](bj-vat-gst.md) and [payroll](bj-payroll-social.md) Guides when those obligations apply. The annual accounts meeting is required within six months of exercise close under article 140; account filing and any extension need their actual procedure. [CGI 2026, art. 50](https://apisiteweb.impots.bj/media/6adcbd46ab83b_B%C3%A9nin-Code%20G%C3%A9n%C3%A9ral%20des%20Imp%C3%B4ts%202026.pdf#page=30) [AUSCGIE, art. 140](https://biblio.ohada.org/doc_num.php?explnum_id=2032)

## Cross-border points

Foreign associates, a foreign manager and a branch require the actual identity, residence and parent-entity documents. Benin registration does not decide the foreign parent's PE or treaty outcome, nor automatically licence a regulated activity. Tax PE and withholding must be tested separately. [APIEx](https://monentreprise.bj/informer/pieces-requises) [CGI 2026, art. 5](https://apisiteweb.impots.bj/media/6adcbd46ab83b_B%C3%A9nin-Code%20G%C3%A9n%C3%A9ral%20des%20Imp%C3%B4ts%202026.pdf#page=9) [CGI 2026, art. 6](https://apisiteweb.impots.bj/media/6adcbd46ab83b_B%C3%A9nin-Code%20G%C3%A9n%C3%A9ral%20des%20Imp%C3%B4ts%202026.pdf#page=10)

## Two worked examples that recompute

**SARL paid capital and registration fee are different amounts.** Assume the associates choose FCFA 2 million in cash capital and use the minimum half-payment at subscription. Paid capital is 1 million, unpaid capital is 1 million and the published APIEx fee is 22,000. Initial identified funding is 1,022,000 before professional costs, not 2,022,000 and not a claim that the whole capital is a fee.

```oa-check
name: SARL capital versus fee
inputs:
  subscribed_cash_capital: 2000000
  official_fee: 22000
steps:
  cash_paid_now: subscribed_cash_capital*0.50
  cash_unpaid: subscribed_cash_capital-cash_paid_now
  identified_initial_funding: cash_paid_now+official_fee
expected:
  cash_paid_now: 1000000
  cash_unpaid: 1000000
  identified_initial_funding: 1022000
```

**SA capital decision.** Assume an ordinary SA at the statutory minimum with all contributions in cash. At least one quarter is paid at subscription, so identified paid capital is 2.5 million and the balance is 7.5 million. Add the published 37,000 fee separately; notary/professional costs remain outside the amount. This uses the signed law's quarter rule, not the inconsistent half-capital portal comparison.

```oa-check
name: SA quarter capital payment
inputs:
  subscribed_cash_capital: 10000000
  official_fee: 37000
steps:
  minimum_paid_now: subscribed_cash_capital*0.25
  remaining_cash_capital: subscribed_cash_capital-minimum_paid_now
  identified_initial_funding: minimum_paid_now+official_fee
expected:
  minimum_paid_now: 2500000
  remaining_cash_capital: 7500000
  identified_initial_funding: 2537000
```

## Ask the client first

- Which form, owners, liability/governance needs and regulated activity are proposed?
- Are there foreign owners/managers, a branch or a non-Benin parent?
- What capital is subscribed, in cash/in kind, paid and still due?
- Which signed statutes, identity, criminal-record, address and deposit documents are ready?
- What are the balance sheet, turnover and permanent staff for the auditor test?
- What registration identifiers, certificates, tax regimes and ongoing filing obligations already exist?
