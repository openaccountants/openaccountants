# Benin cross-border withholding working paper v0.1

> **General reference only.** This skill is general tax/accounting reference material for AI-assisted workflows. It has not been reviewed for any specific person's facts, documents, elections, deadlines, residency, filing status, or local procedures. Do not rely on it to file, pay, amend, or take a tax position without review by a qualified professional in the relevant jurisdiction.

## Who this applies to

Prepare the domestic-law withholding classification for Benin distributions, ordinary interest and payments for foreign services/royalties in 2026. The recipient's status, actual payment/expense event and treaty must be established first. A foreign service provider with a Benin permanent establishment is not put through the article 141 non-established-provider rule. Residence and permanent-establishment tests are in articles 5–6; a foreign shareholder alone does not settle them. [CGI 2026, art. 5](https://apisiteweb.impots.bj/media/6adcbd46ab83b_B%C3%A9nin-Code%20G%C3%A9n%C3%A9ral%20des%20Imp%C3%B4ts%202026.pdf#page=9) [CGI 2026, art. 6](https://apisiteweb.impots.bj/media/6adcbd46ab83b_B%C3%A9nin-Code%20G%C3%A9n%C3%A9ral%20des%20Imp%C3%B4ts%202026.pdf#page=10) [CGI 2026, art. 141](https://apisiteweb.impots.bj/media/6adcbd46ab83b_B%C3%A9nin-Code%20G%C3%A9n%C3%A9ral%20des%20Imp%C3%B4ts%202026.pdf#page=69)

This Guide incorporates the June rectifying-law gates relevant to distributions, capital gains and rental platforms. It does not compute their unresolved special bases, certify treaty entitlement, gross up a net-of-tax contract or complete an electronic declaration.

## The base

1. Classify the payment: dividend, ordinary interest, bond/security product, royalty/service, rental-platform receipt or a different item. Record the gross contractual amount, accessory costs, currency evidence, expense recognition and payment/meeting dates. [CGI 2026, art. 85](https://apisiteweb.impots.bj/media/6adcbd46ab83b_B%C3%A9nin-Code%20G%C3%A9n%C3%A9ral%20des%20Imp%C3%B4ts%202026.pdf#page=48) [CGI 2026, art. 86](https://apisiteweb.impots.bj/media/6adcbd46ab83b_B%C3%A9nin-Code%20G%C3%A9n%C3%A9ral%20des%20Imp%C3%B4ts%202026.pdf#page=48) [CGI 2026, art. 142](https://apisiteweb.impots.bj/media/6adcbd46ab83b_B%C3%A9nin-Code%20G%C3%A9n%C3%A9ral%20des%20Imp%C3%B4ts%202026.pdf#page=69)
2. Establish recipient residence, legal form, beneficial ownership and PE facts. For a claimed service/royalty treaty relief, obtain fiscal-residence evidence, beneficial-owner evidence and evidence of liability to corporate/equivalent tax without exemption. Communicate the article 142 information to the administration in the month the charge is recognised. No named-country treaty relief is supplied here without the actual convention. [CGI 2026, art. 142](https://apisiteweb.impots.bj/media/6adcbd46ab83b_B%C3%A9nin-Code%20G%C3%A9n%C3%A9ral%20des%20Imp%C3%B4ts%202026.pdf#page=69)
3. Reconcile goods and services. The goods exclusion needs separate invoicing under article 142(4); merely describing a mixed contract as 'equipment' is insufficient. Calculate the withholding on the confirmed base and keep any VAT assessment separate. [CGI 2026, art. 142](https://apisiteweb.impots.bj/media/6adcbd46ab83b_B%C3%A9nin-Code%20G%C3%A9n%C3%A9ral%20des%20Imp%C3%B4ts%202026.pdf#page=69)
4. For ordinary dividends and interest, apply their own category, not the foreign-service percentage. Check the approved-exchange condition before a listed-company rate and the specific treaty conditions before any reduction. [CGI 2026, art. 86](https://apisiteweb.impots.bj/media/6adcbd46ab83b_B%C3%A9nin-Code%20G%C3%A9n%C3%A9ral%20des%20Imp%C3%B4ts%202026.pdf#page=48) [CGI 2026, art. 88](https://apisiteweb.impots.bj/media/6adcbd46ab83b_B%C3%A9nin-Code%20G%C3%A9n%C3%A9ral%20des%20Imp%C3%B4ts%202026.pdf#page=49) [CGI 2026, art. 89](https://apisiteweb.impots.bj/media/6adcbd46ab83b_B%C3%A9nin-Code%20G%C3%A9n%C3%A9ral%20des%20Imp%C3%B4ts%202026.pdf#page=49)
5. **Hold special distribution cases for a separate calculation.** LFR articles 3–7 add and define the retained-profit rule, associated tax base/deadline and transitional regularisation. A declared dividend is not proof that undistributed-reserve or prior-tax-credit issues have been settled. Article 1 gives an income-period rule tied to the exercise closed 31 December 2025 unless otherwise stated. Do not infer the applicable income period merely from the signature date. [LFR 2026, art. 1](https://apisiteweb.impots.bj/media/6adcb8249c4ab_B%C3%A9nin-Loi%20de%20finances%20rectificative%202026.pdf#page=1) [LFR 2026, art. 3](https://apisiteweb.impots.bj/media/6adcb8249c4ab_B%C3%A9nin-Loi%20de%20finances%20rectificative%202026.pdf#page=2) [LFR 2026, art. 5](https://apisiteweb.impots.bj/media/6adcb8249c4ab_B%C3%A9nin-Loi%20de%20finances%20rectificative%202026.pdf#page=2) [LFR 2026, art. 6](https://apisiteweb.impots.bj/media/6adcb8249c4ab_B%C3%A9nin-Loi%20de%20finances%20rectificative%202026.pdf#page=3) [LFR 2026, art. 7](https://apisiteweb.impots.bj/media/6adcb8249c4ab_B%C3%A9nin-Loi%20de%20finances%20rectificative%202026.pdf#page=3)
6. LFR article 12 also addresses gains on securities of Benin-established enterprises regardless of seller/buyer residence, subject to treaties. LFR article 13 places the specified Benin-property rental-platform withholding on the platform owner, excluding operations for hotel establishments. Route these to their actual gain/rent calculation before payment; do not apply a dividend or service rule to them by analogy. [LFR 2026, art. 12](https://apisiteweb.impots.bj/media/6adcb8249c4ab_B%C3%A9nin-Loi%20de%20finances%20rectificative%202026.pdf#page=4) [LFR 2026, art. 13](https://apisiteweb.impots.bj/media/6adcb8249c4ab_B%C3%A9nin-Loi%20de%20finances%20rectificative%202026.pdf#page=4)

## Deadlines and filing

For ordinary distributions/interest, the tax is normally due by the 10th of the next month after making the products available; an approved dividend distribution uses the month of the approving meeting. LFR article 6 retains those limbs while adding the separate retained-profit deadline, so a cash payment date alone is insufficient for that special case. The accompanying monthly statement shows gross sums and tax. [CGI 2026, art. 90](https://apisiteweb.impots.bj/media/6adcbd46ab83b_B%C3%A9nin-Code%20G%C3%A9n%C3%A9ral%20des%20Imp%C3%B4ts%202026.pdf#page=49) [CGI 2026, art. 91](https://apisiteweb.impots.bj/media/6adcbd46ab83b_B%C3%A9nin-Code%20G%C3%A9n%C3%A9ral%20des%20Imp%C3%B4ts%202026.pdf#page=50) [LFR 2026, art. 6](https://apisiteweb.impots.bj/media/6adcb8249c4ab_B%C3%A9nin-Loi%20de%20finances%20rectificative%202026.pdf#page=3)

Article 143 foreign-service withholding is remitted by the 10th of the month after the expense is recognised, not automatically the later cash-payment month. The debtor, relevant billing/collection person and provider have the article 143 joint-responsibility rule. [CGI 2026, art. 143](https://apisiteweb.impots.bj/media/6adcbd46ab83b_B%C3%A9nin-Code%20G%C3%A9n%C3%A9ral%20des%20Imp%C3%B4ts%202026.pdf#page=70)

Prepare a payment-by-payment withholding schedule and treaty evidence pack for the authorised [DGI e-services](https://e-services.impots.bj/) filer. Authenticated fields, submission and payment acknowledgement were not accessed. Do not treat a working-paper amount as filed.

## Penalties and interest

General sanctions are owned by the [overview](bj-tax-overview.md#penalties-and-interest). Foreign-service failure to withhold/remit also falls under articles 143 and 492(4): article 492(3) specifies a 100% fine on the unwithheld/unremitted amount, halved for a first failure, together with the underlying remittance. Assess the exact failure before applying it. [CGI 2026, art. 143](https://apisiteweb.impots.bj/media/6adcbd46ab83b_B%C3%A9nin-Code%20G%C3%A9n%C3%A9ral%20des%20Imp%C3%B4ts%202026.pdf#page=70) [CGI 2026, art. 492](https://apisiteweb.impots.bj/media/6adcbd46ab83b_B%C3%A9nin-Code%20G%C3%A9n%C3%A9ral%20des%20Imp%C3%B4ts%202026.pdf#page=218)

## Cross-border points

Tax treaties take priority under CGI article 1, but eligibility, income classification, PE, residence and beneficial ownership must be tested on the convention actually applicable. The legacy reference to the UEMOA convention is not a substitute for reading that instrument. Related-party pricing and deductibility may still require the [TP screen](bj-transfer-pricing.md); VAT liability/place is a separate [VAT](bj-vat-gst.md) decision. [CGI 2026, art. 1](https://apisiteweb.impots.bj/media/6adcbd46ab83b_B%C3%A9nin-Code%20G%C3%A9n%C3%A9ral%20des%20Imp%C3%B4ts%202026.pdf#page=6) [CGI 2026, art. 30](https://apisiteweb.impots.bj/media/6adcbd46ab83b_B%C3%A9nin-Code%20G%C3%A9n%C3%A9ral%20des%20Imp%C3%B4ts%202026.pdf#page=22) [CGI 2026, art. 45](https://apisiteweb.impots.bj/media/6adcbd46ab83b_B%C3%A9nin-Code%20G%C3%A9n%C3%A9ral%20des%20Imp%C3%B4ts%202026.pdf#page=28) [CGI 2026, art. 142](https://apisiteweb.impots.bj/media/6adcbd46ab83b_B%C3%A9nin-Code%20G%C3%A9n%C3%A9ral%20des%20Imp%C3%B4ts%202026.pdf#page=69)

## Two worked examples that recompute

**Residence changes the ordinary dividend rate.** Assume an ordinary declared cash dividend of FCFA 1 million, no special reserve/retained-profit issue, no more favourable applicable treaty and no approved-listed-company condition. The non-resident-associate rate gives 50,000; the other-dividend rate gives 100,000. Establish the recipient condition before choosing the 50,000 result.

```oa-check
name: Ordinary dividend recipient decision
inputs:
  gross_dividend: 1000000
steps:
  nonresident_associate_tax: gross_dividend*0.05
  other_dividend_tax: gross_dividend*0.10
  difference: other_dividend_tax-nonresident_associate_tax
expected:
  nonresident_associate_tax: 50000
  other_dividend_tax: 100000
  difference: 50000
```

**Separate invoicing changes the service base.** Assume no treaty relief or PE, and a foreign contract supplies goods of FCFA 4 million and Benin-used services of FCFA 1 million, with no other accessory costs. Separate goods invoicing gives service withholding of 200,000. Without separate invoicing the 5 million contract gives 1 million. The difference is 800,000. No net-contract gross-up or VAT is calculated.

```oa-check
name: Mixed contract separate invoicing decision
inputs:
  goods: 4000000
  services: 1000000
steps:
  with_separate_goods_invoice: services*0.20
  without_separate_invoice: (goods+services)*0.20
  difference: without_separate_invoice-with_separate_goods_invoice
expected:
  with_separate_goods_invoice: 200000
  without_separate_invoice: 1000000
  difference: 800000
```

## Ask the client first

- What is the exact payment type, gross amount, accessory cost and contract/invoicing split?
- What are recipient residence, beneficial ownership, tax status and Benin PE facts?
- Which actual treaty and entitlement evidence support a claimed reduction?
- When were the charge, payment and any dividend-approval meeting recorded?
- Are there undistributed reserves, capital gains, past tax, rental platforms or hotel operations?
- What prior withholding, filing receipts and debtor/provider allocation need reconciliation?
