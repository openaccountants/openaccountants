# Benin annual property TFU working paper v0.1

> **General reference only.** This skill is general tax/accounting reference material for AI-assisted workflows. It has not been reviewed for any specific person's facts, documents, elections, deadlines, residency, filing status, or local procedures. Do not rely on it to file, pay, amend, or take a tax position without review by a qualified professional in the relevant jurisdiction.

## Who this applies to

Prepare a separate annual TFU ownership-tax working paper for property situated in Benin in 2026. A rental IRF computation is a different job, even when both appear on one assessment notice. [CGI arts151,104(3)](https://apisiteweb.impots.bj/media/6adcbd46ab83b_B%C3%A9nin-Code%20G%C3%A9n%C3%A9ral%20des%20Imp%C3%B4ts%202026.pdf#page=74)

Classify permanently fixed constructions as built property. Uncultivated land used commercially or industrially is assimilated to built property. Light structures without masonry foundations are normally non-built, but income-producing or commercial light structures use the built regime. [CGI art152](https://apisiteweb.impots.bj/media/6adcbd46ab83b_B%C3%A9nin-Code%20G%C3%A9n%C3%A9ral%20des%20Imp%C3%B4ts%202026.pdf#page=74)

Start with the owner on 1 January, liable for the whole year. For usufruct use the usufructuary; for an emphyteutic lease use the lessee. For a building lease, the owner owes through the completion year and the tenant from the following year; keep the owner’s name after the liable person on the notice. [CGI art156(1)–(2)](https://apisiteweb.impots.bj/media/6adcbd46ab83b_B%C3%A9nin-Code%20G%C3%A9n%C3%A9ral%20des%20Imp%C3%B4ts%202026.pdf#page=76)

If the owner cannot be reached, the possessor, agent, tenant, legatee or other successor pays on the owner’s behalf. If none is known, the administration can assess using the unique parcel number or another national/communal cadastral identifier; absence of the article598(1) particulars cannot by itself annul that notice. [CGI art156(3)–(4); LF2026 art15](https://apisiteweb.impots.bj/media/6adcbd46ab83b_B%C3%A9nin-Code%20G%C3%A9n%C3%A9ral%20des%20Imp%C3%B4ts%202026.pdf#page=76)

## The base

For built property, obtain the supported rental value under the ministerial methodology. Do not substitute purchase price, actual rent or a public-land sales-price schedule without the applicable valuation evidence. [CGI art157](https://apisiteweb.impots.bj/media/6adcbd46ab83b_B%C3%A9nin-Code%20G%C3%A9n%C3%A9ral%20des%20Imp%C3%B4ts%202026.pdf#page=76)

For non-built property, use the administrative assessment at 1 January. Municipal councils determine assessments from market values, revisable every five years. They notify DGI by 30 November before the end of each five-year period for values effective the following 1 January. If no values had been adopted when this provision commenced, a ministerial order sets the bases. [CGI art158](https://apisiteweb.impots.bj/media/6adcbd46ab83b_B%C3%A9nin-Code%20G%C3%A9n%C3%A9ral%20des%20Imp%C3%B4ts%202026.pdf#page=76)

Check the actual communal rate decision for the year, delivered to DGI by 30 November for the following 1 January. Where that notification is absent, article159(2) retains the preceding year’s rates. For valuation-period notification failure, article158(3) literally refers to the preceding quinquennium’s “taux”; do not invent a revised assessment from that wording. [CGI arts158(3),159(2)](https://apisiteweb.impots.bj/media/6adcbd46ab83b_B%C3%A9nin-Code%20G%C3%A9n%C3%A9ral%20des%20Imp%C3%B4ts%202026.pdf#page=77)

Screen the article153 exemption classes: income-unproductive public property; diplomatic/international-organism property with a headquarters agreement; agricultural land and the specified agricultural uses; qualifying school/university/sport land; school/university buildings owned by the establishment; medical/social-assistance property; and worship property owned by the religious community. Record which exact class and factual conditions are met. [CGI art153](https://apisiteweb.impots.bj/media/6adcbd46ab83b_B%C3%A9nin-Code%20G%C3%A9n%C3%A9ral%20des%20Imp%C3%B4ts%202026.pdf#page=74)

Unsubdivided land sown, planted or replanted with trees of any species has the separate article155 exemption. Do not treat every vacant plot as exempt. [CGI art155](https://apisiteweb.impots.bj/media/6adcbd46ab83b_B%C3%A9nin-Code%20G%C3%A9n%C3%A9ral%20des%20Imp%C3%B4ts%202026.pdf#page=76)

New construction, reconstruction or extensions exclusively for the owner and family’s dwelling enter TFU in the fourth year following completion or first use. The exemption is only for the principal residence and only once for the same person. A later different use ends the remaining exemption from the year of conversion. [CGI art154(1),(3)](https://apisiteweb.impots.bj/media/6adcbd46ab83b_B%C3%A9nin-Code%20G%C3%A9n%C3%A9ral%20des%20Imp%C3%B4ts%202026.pdf#page=75)

To claim that temporary exemption, send the tax service a letter with a building-permit copy and title proving ownership in the completion/first-use year, before the following 1 January. Existing-property TFU must be current. Without timely declaration, taxation starts permanently on 1 January of discovery. Do not grant relief from the construction date alone. [CGI art154(2)](https://apisiteweb.impots.bj/media/6adcbd46ab83b_B%C3%A9nin-Code%20G%C3%A9n%C3%A9ral%20des%20Imp%C3%B4ts%202026.pdf#page=75)

After the liable person, property category, exemptions, lawful value and applicable local rate are supported, multiply the taxable value by that rate. Keep IRF and its ORTB levy outside this TFU multiplication. [CGI arts157–159;104(3)](https://apisiteweb.impots.bj/media/6adcbd46ab83b_B%C3%A9nin-Code%20G%C3%A9n%C3%A9ral%20des%20Imp%C3%B4ts%202026.pdf#page=55)

## Deadlines and filing

Declare acquisition or completed construction to the tax service by simple letter within 30 days. Identify the parcel and full address, including the “Rue entrée de la parcelle” number; article161(2) requires a sign on non-built property or an inscription at the building entrance. Retain the letter and evidence of delivery. [CGI art161(1)–(2)](https://apisiteweb.impots.bj/media/6adcbd46ab83b_B%C3%A9nin-Code%20G%C3%A9n%C3%A9ral%20des%20Imp%C3%B4ts%202026.pdf#page=77)

By 10 December each year, the owner or principal tenant, or property manager in their place, supplies a written occupancy statement: each tenant and let space, principal rent and charges; free occupants and space; owner-occupied space; vacant space. Inaccurate or missing information invokes article485. [CGI art161(3)](https://apisiteweb.impots.bj/media/6adcbd46ab83b_B%C3%A9nin-Code%20G%C3%A9n%C3%A9ral%20des%20Imp%C3%B4ts%202026.pdf#page=78)

Prepare the TFU instalment schedule from the preceding year’s total: 50% by 10 February and the remaining 50% by 30 April. Reconcile the tax notice before settling any assessed balance; this Guide does not invent a first-year provisional amount without a prior assessment. [CGI art162](https://apisiteweb.impots.bj/media/6adcbd46ab83b_B%C3%A9nin-Code%20G%C3%A9n%C3%A9ral%20des%20Imp%C3%B4ts%202026.pdf#page=78)

Tenants/farm lessees pay TFU for the leased property on the owner’s behalf; the owner must accept the TFU receipt against the rent. Keep the receipt-to-rent reconciliation separate from IRF withholding to prevent two deductions for the same payment. [CGI art163](https://apisiteweb.impots.bj/media/6adcbd46ab83b_B%C3%A9nin-Code%20G%C3%A9n%C3%A9ral%20des%20Imp%C3%B4ts%202026.pdf#page=78)

Arrange mutation of the tax record on transfer. Until mutation occurs, the former owner remains assessed and can be pursued, with recourse against the new owner. Article165(2) requires the tax-certified TFU-current statement on acts granting/transferring ownership or use and the listed permits/titles. Tax receipts create the statutory presumption in article165(1), not a substitute for resolving a disputed title. [CGI arts164–165](https://apisiteweb.impots.bj/media/6adcbd46ab83b_B%C3%A9nin-Code%20G%C3%A9n%C3%A9ral%20des%20Imp%C3%B4ts%202026.pdf#page=78)

Prepare a dated packet with title/rights evidence, parcel identifier, property use, exemption evidence, the valuation instrument, local rate decision, previous assessment, occupancy letter and payment receipts. The statutory letter route is supported; authenticated DGI screens, field IDs, bank/payment-reference generation and acknowledgement were not accessed. Do not label the packet as a submitted return.

## Penalties and interest

For 1 January–31 December 2026, original finance-law article2 disapplies the specified TFU late increases, interest, command costs and seizure costs if all TFU principal due is paid and the landowner has an IFU. Obtain evidence of both conditions; do not extend this TFU measure to rental IRF or assume principal is forgiven. [LF2026 art2](https://budgetbenin.bj/storage/2025/12/Loi-des-finances-2026.pdf#page=2)

General sanctions are routed to the [overview](bj-tax-overview.md#penalties-and-interest). Determine the charge and relief conditions before paying; the specific TFU relief does not prove that every declaration penalty is waived.

## Cross-border points

TFU follows the Benin location of the property and the article156 rights-holder rules. A foreign address does not substitute for identifying the liable person. This working paper does not grant treaty relief or credit in another country. [CGI arts151,104(3)](https://apisiteweb.impots.bj/media/6adcbd46ab83b_B%C3%A9nin-Code%20G%C3%A9n%C3%A9ral%20des%20Imp%C3%B4ts%202026.pdf#page=74) [CGI art156(1)–(2)](https://apisiteweb.impots.bj/media/6adcbd46ab83b_B%C3%A9nin-Code%20G%C3%A9n%C3%A9ral%20des%20Imp%C3%B4ts%202026.pdf#page=76)

## Two worked examples that recompute

**Two categories, two bases.** Assume the commune’s actual 2026 decisions and valuation evidence have been supplied: a built building has assessed rental value FCFA1,200,000 and a 6% rate; non-built land has administrative value FCFA8,000,000 and a 5% rate. Neither is exempt. The rates are example facts within article159, not claimed national defaults. TFU is 72,000 and 400,000, total 472,000. No IRF or ORTB is included. [CGI arts157–159](https://apisiteweb.impots.bj/media/6adcbd46ab83b_B%C3%A9nin-Code%20G%C3%A9n%C3%A9ral%20des%20Imp%C3%B4ts%202026.pdf#page=76)

```oa-check
name: Separate built and nonbuilt TFU bases
inputs:
  built_rental_value: 1200000
  supported_built_rate: 6
  nonbuilt_assessed_value: 8000000
  supported_nonbuilt_rate: 5
steps:
  built_tfu: built_rental_value*supported_built_rate/100
  nonbuilt_tfu: nonbuilt_assessed_value*supported_nonbuilt_rate/100
  tfu_total: built_tfu+nonbuilt_tfu
expected:
  built_tfu: 72000
  nonbuilt_tfu: 400000
  tfu_total: 472000
```

**Principal-residence use changes the decision.** Assume a principal residence completed and first used in 2023, a timely valid exemption letter and all other article154 conditions. The fourth following year is 2027, so exclusive owner/family use would still be exempt in 2026. Instead the whole building is converted to a commercial letting in 2026: article154 ends the remaining exemption from that conversion year. With supported rental value FCFA900,000 and actual local rate 6%, the conditional 2026 assessment is 54,000 rather than zero. This is a use-change decision, not a month-by-month proration. [CGI art154](https://apisiteweb.impots.bj/media/6adcbd46ab83b_B%C3%A9nin-Code%20G%C3%A9n%C3%A9ral%20des%20Imp%C3%B4ts%202026.pdf#page=75)

```oa-check
name: Temporary residence exemption ends on changed use
inputs:
  completion_year: 2023
  rental_value: 900000
  supported_rate: 6
  qualifies_exclusive_residence: 0
steps:
  fourth_following_year: completion_year+4
  tfu_if_taxable: rental_value*supported_rate/100
  tfu_2026_after_conversion: tfu_if_taxable*(1-qualifies_exclusive_residence)
expected:
  fourth_following_year: 2027
  tfu_if_taxable: 54000
  tfu_2026_after_conversion: 54000
```

## Ask the client first

- What is the parcel, commune, address, title and rights-holder position on 1 January?
- Is the property built, non-built or an assimilated commercial/light structure?
- Which current valuation and communal rate decisions support the actual base and rate?
- What exemption facts, first-use/completion dates, use changes, permit, title and timely letter exist?
- What prior assessment, IFU, occupancy statement, rent offsets and payment receipts exist?
- Has ownership changed without the tax-record mutation being completed?

The original finance law expressly commences on 1 January 2026 and its article15 inserts the cadastral-identifier rule in CGI156(4). The June rectifying fiscal amendments read do not replace CGI151–165 or original article2. This is a bounded source-chain check, not a certification of all later instruments.
