# Tunisia company formation and entity choice v1.1

## Scope

Choose a SARL, SUARL or SA, prepare its formation dossier and identify the next registrations and annual obligations. Coverage: registry procedures read in November 2026, including an APII checklist updated 31 August 2026; company-law rules from the official French Commercial Companies Code (CSC) edition updated 10 February 2022, cross-checked against the CMF-hosted Arabic text through the 2019 amendments. These are the latest confirmed statutory texts read, not a 2026 consolidation. Only the Arabic text published in JORT is legally authoritative ([CMF notice](https://www.cmf.tn/?q=textes-de-r%C3%A9f%C3%A9rence)).

The legal form, investment declaration and permission to conduct the activity are separate decisions. This Guide covers formation of the three ordinary company forms; a public securities offer, regulated activity or foreign-investment restriction needs its own analysis. It does not establish a tax-saving result between forms.

## Ask the client first

1. How many shareholders, natural persons or legal entities, and does a proposed SUARL owner already have a SUARL or itself constitute a SUARL?
2. What activity, sector, premises and proposed company name? Is an authorisation, professional-order approval or cahier des charges required?
3. What cash and in-kind capital, ownership allocation and evidence of payment are proposed? Will the SA call on public savings?
4. Who will manage the business? For an SA, which governance structure and proposed officers?
5. Any foreign owners, foreign-currency contributions, overseas activity or special investment status?
6. What year end, gross assets, products excluding tax and average staffing are expected? Is an auditor required by the statutes or requested by shareholders?
7. What dates have the statutes and, for an SA, the subscription-and-payment declaration actually been signed? Has a fiscal card already been issued?

## The method, step by step

### Compare the forms

| Option or condition | Rule | Authority and official URL |
|---|---|---|
| SARL membership and liability | Two to fifty members; members bear losses only up to their contributions. Above fifty, reduce membership or convert to a share company within one year; otherwise judicial dissolution can be requested, subject to a court extension | CSC arts.90,93: https://www.cmf.tn/sites/default/files/pdfs/reglementation/textes-reference/code_societes_fr.pdf |

On the directors route, three to twelve directors; initial officers and auditors are addressed in constitutive minutes | CSC arts.181,188,189: https://www.cmf.tn/sites/default/files/pdfs/reglementation/textes-reference/code_societes_fr.pdf |

The old suggestion of three shareholders for a non-public SA is not supported by article 160. Do not confuse the board's three-member minimum with the shareholder minimum. The APII FAQ's statement that a legal person cannot form a SUARL predates and conflicts with the express 2019 amendment; use article 149 above.

### Prepare and register

1. **Reserve the name and establish the activity route.** APII's current formation page starts with RNE name reservation. Its project-declaration step is inapplicable to commerce companies and optional for industry and service companies. Its industry/services checklist accepts the relevant APII, APIA, ONA, ONTT or CEPEX declaration where applicable; this is not a universal requirement to declare every formation to every agency. Preserve the agriculture/APIA and tourism/ONTT routes when the activity calls for them. Use APII's online dossier-admissibility and appointment service for its one-stop-shop assistance ([APII formation workflow](https://www.tunisieindustrie.nat.tn/fr/doc.asp?mcat=16&mrub=186&msrub=269), [31 August 2026 SARL/SUARL checklist](https://www.tunisieindustrie.nat.tn/fr/download/cfga/SARL-SUARL_SERVICE_INDUSTRIE_AUTRES_plus.pdf)).
2. **Prepare the premises and constitutive documents.** For an SARL, the signed act identifies members, activity, duration, capital and interests, cash/in-kind contributions, managers where applicable, payment arrangements and year end (CSC art.96). Obtain managers' identity documents, reservation certificate and evidence of registered lease, permitted domiciliation or the other premises route specified by the applicable APII checklist. Obtain activity authorisations or validated cahier des charges and any professional-order approval. In an SARL, a contribution auditor values in-kind contributions and annexes the report; a majority may waive appointment where each in-kind contribution is at most TND 3,000, with members jointly liable to third parties for the assigned values. Do not apply that waiver automatically to a SUARL: article 151 requires the sole member to appoint the auditor and provides personal liability if none is appointed (CSC arts.100,151). A land contribution requires the special drafting formalities. Sources: [CSC arts.3,96-100,151](https://www.cmf.tn/sites/default/files/pdfs/reglementation/textes-reference/code_societes_fr.pdf); [APII SARL/SUARL dossier](https://www.tunisieindustrie.nat.tn/fr/download/cfga/SARL-SUARL_SERVICE_INDUSTRIE_AUTRES_plus.pdf).
3. **Complete tax identification.** Prepare the déclaration d'existence using APII's linked DIM form and obtain the carte d'identification fiscale. The APII checklists expressly place online beneficial-owner declaration and RNE registration after the fiscal card. For an SA, also prepare subscription bulletins, subscriber/payment statement, bank evidence, constitutive general-meeting minutes, first board/supervisory-board minutes, and the subscription-and-payment declaration received by the tax receiver. Use the applicable full checklist for originals and registration formalities ([APII SARL/SUARL](https://www.tunisieindustrie.nat.tn/fr/download/cfga/SARL-SUARL_SERVICE_INDUSTRIE_AUTRES_plus.pdf), [APII SA](https://www.tunisieindustrie.nat.tn/fr/download/cfga/SA_plus.pdf)).
4. **Submit the appropriate RNE dossier and beneficial-owner declaration online at [RNE](https://www.registre-entreprises.tn).** For SARL/SUARL, C012 requests the fiscal-card copy, original registered signed statutes, directors/managers list and IDs, beneficial-owner declaration, and contribution-auditor report, separate manager/auditor appointment minutes and auditor professional card where applicable. Acquisition, lease or gérance libre of a business adds the registered contract and its JORT publication. For an SA, C004 additionally specifies constitutive and first board/supervisory-board minutes, certified subscription-and-payment declaration, founders' identities, contribution report where relevant and statutory-auditor card; a public-call SA has a preliminary draft-statutes deposit. Sources: [RNE C012, complete checklist](https://www.registre-entreprises.tn/rne-public/assets/pdfs/check-liste-creation/RNEC012_creation_personne_morale_SARL_SUARL_FR.pdf), [RNE C004, both pages](https://home.registre-entreprises.tn/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/orders/fr/RNE%20C%20004_Fr.pdf).
5. **Diary the legal deadline and cost separately from processing time.** C012: file within thirty days of signing the statutes. C004: thirty days from the subscription-and-payment declaration. Each principal registration costs TND 50, including beneficial-owner declaration and Centre publication; this is not the total formation cost. Late filing attracts half the operation's fee per month or part month. RNE publication is electronic, twenty-four hours after acceptance, without further publication procedure or fee. Retain the accepted registration evidence and publication. This does not establish a five-to-ten-day end-to-end incorporation time ([RNE C012](https://www.registre-entreprises.tn/rne-public/assets/pdfs/check-liste-creation/RNEC012_creation_personne_morale_SARL_SUARL_FR.pdf), [C004](https://home.registre-entreprises.tn/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/orders/fr/RNE%20C%20004_Fr.pdf), [live formalities table](https://home.registre-entreprises.tn/formalites/)).
6. **Control capital use and operating registrations.** An SARL/SUARL manager cannot use paid-in funds until formation formalities and RNE registration are completed (CSC arts.98,148; [Law 2019-47 art.4](https://igppp.tn/fr/node/534)). Keep formation, fiscal identification and activity permissions as distinct completion items. Identify VAT and employer obligations from the actual activity and staffing; use the unchanged Tunisia [VAT](tunisia-vat.md), [payroll and social contributions](tn-payroll-social.md) and [corporate-income-tax](tn-corporate-income-tax.md) Guides for their own methods and unresolved items.

### Accounts, audit and annual filings

- Prepare annual accounts, inventory and management documentation rather than treating incorporation as the end of compliance. CSC art.128 identifies the SARL accounts package; art.153 requires the SUARL sole member's approval within three months of closing; arts.201 and 275 require SA financial statements under the enterprise accounting system and an ordinary meeting within six months of closing. The conflicting Arabic/French SARL meeting deadline is held below; do not substitute the SA deadline ([CSC French](https://www.cmf.tn/sites/default/files/pdfs/reglementation/textes-reference/code_societes_fr.pdf), [CSC Arabic](https://www.cmf.tn/sites/default/files/pdfs/reglementation/textes-reference/Code%20des%20soci%C3%A9t%C3%A9s%20commerciales%20arabe.pdf)). SUARL resolutions must be signed and kept in the special court-initialled register (art.154; [2019 amendment](https://igppp.tn/fr/node/534)).
- **Statutory audit:** share companies, including SAs, are not within the ordinary exemption. Other commercial companies are exempt for their first accounting period, or where they do not meet two of the three limits: gross balance-sheet total TND 100,000; products excluding tax TND 300,000; average employees ten. The cessation test during an existing auditor's mandate looks at its last two accounting periods. Articles and shareholder requests can override the size exemption: in an SARL a request by holders of at least one fifth of capital makes appointment mandatory through the court. Higher qualification thresholds require an OECT-registered expert when two limits are met: assets TND 1,500,000, products excluding tax TND 2,000,000, average employees thirty. For these tests, decree art.7 uses gross assets before depreciation/provisions plus leased assets at contractual value excluding finance/margin; products exclude inventory variation; staff is the opening/closing average including occasional staff in person-years ([CSC arts.13,123-125 and Decree 2006-1546 arts.1,2,7, official compilation](https://www.cmf.tn/sites/default/files/pdfs/reglementation/textes-reference/code_societes_fr.pdf)).
- **RNE accounts deposit:** live formality M004.09 lists financial-statement filing by the end of the seventh month following year end, fee TND 50; M004.37 lists approval-minutes filing within thirty days, fee TND 20. Keep RNE deposits separate from tax-return attachments ([RNE formalities](https://home.registre-entreprises.tn/formalites/)).
- **Annual IS and advance instalments:** retain these as separate tax-compliance tasks and load [Tunisia corporate income tax](tn-corporate-income-tax.md). Its annual-return and advance-payment method must be applied to the year end and audit facts. The old shorthand “25 June for audited companies” must not be used as a blanket extension of the original annual IS filing deadline; that topic needs the provisional-return/amendment conditions in the tax Guide. This formation Guide does not independently restate or confirm those tax deadlines.

## Worked cases

### Case 1: seven shareholders, with and without a public call

Assume an ordinary unregulated activity, seven eligible shareholders, no in-kind capital and TND 10,000 fully subscribed and paid. Without a public call, capital exceeds the TND 5,000 floor by TND 5,000 and the shareholder minimum is met. If the same project calls on public savings, its TND 50,000 minimum leaves a TND 40,000 capital shortfall. It cannot proceed on the same capital merely because the shareholder count is sufficient (CSC arts.160-166 above). Governance, audit and the public-offer rules still need completion.

```oa-check
name: SA capital decision with and without a public call
inputs:
  capital: 10000
  private_minimum: 5000
  public_minimum: 50000
steps:
  - private_excess: capital - private_minimum
  - public_shortfall: public_minimum - capital
expected:
  private_excess: 5000
  public_shortfall: 40000
```

### Case 2: one eligible individual becomes two members

Assume a natural person eligible under article 149, an unregulated activity and an agreed TND 1,000 cash capital fully paid. One member points to a SUARL; after a second member subscribes under the correct formation documents, use SARL rather than SUARL. With two equal contributions, TND 1,000 / 2 = TND 500 each. The TND 1,000 is a client-chosen capital, not a statutory minimum. An SA remains unavailable with two shareholders, regardless of paid-in capital (CSC arts.90,92,97,148-149,160 above).

```oa-check
name: Two equal SARL contributions at chosen capital
inputs:
  chosen_capital: 1000
  members: 2
steps:
  - equal_contribution: chosen_capital / members
expected:
  equal_contribution: 500
```

## When to refuse or refer

Refuse a completed-formation assurance where shareholder eligibility, required activity permission, capital payment, beneficial owners or accepted registration evidence is missing. Refer a public securities offer and regulated or foreign-investment case for the applicable specialist rules. For the disputed SARL approval deadline, obtain the operative Arabic JORT text before setting the meeting date. Do not invent portal fields, a total formation-cost quote or an end-to-end completion date from the checklist.
