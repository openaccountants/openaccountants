# Georgia Tax Overview

## Georgia tax system at a glance (2026)

Georgia (the country in the South Caucasus, not the US state) runs a low, mostly flat tax system under one Tax Code, collected by the Revenue Service. Personal income is taxed at a flat 20%. Company profit is taxed only when it is distributed. VAT has a single standard rate. Figures below are from the consolidated Tax Code on matsne.gov.ge, publication of 15 October 2026 (Law No 1888, published 16 October 2026), read on 31 October 2026.

- **National and local taxes**: the national taxes are income tax, profit tax, VAT, excise and import duty. Property tax is a local tax _(Tax Code art. 6(5), (6))_
- **Tax year**: the calendar year for most persons. Companies on the distributed-profit model account by calendar month _(Tax Code art. 135(1), (2))_
- **Currency**: Georgian lari (GEL); Tax Code amounts are stated in GEL _(Tax Code, e.g. art. 165(1))_
- **Tax authority**: the Revenue Service, a legal entity under public law within the Ministry of Finance _(Tax Code art. 48(1), (2))_
- **Residence, individuals**: resident for the whole tax year if present in Georgia for 183 days or more in any continuous 12-month period ending in that year. High net worth individuals can be granted residence by the Minister of Finance _(Tax Code art. 34(2), (6))_. Detail: [georgia-income-tax](georgia-income-tax.md).
- **Residence, companies**: a Georgian enterprise has its place of business or place of management in Georgia _(Tax Code art. 22(1))_. Detail: [ge-corporate-income-tax](ge-corporate-income-tax.md).
- **Taxation basis, individuals**: income (including gains) of a resident individual that is not Georgian-source income is exempt. In effect, residents are taxed on Georgian-source income only _(Tax Code art. 82(1)(u))_. Non-residents are taxed on Georgian-source income _(Tax Code art. 80(2), (3))_
- **Headline personal income tax rate**: 20% flat _(Tax Code art. 81(1))_. Rent of residential space for living, with no deductions claimed: 5% _(art. 81(2))_. Detail: [georgia-income-tax](georgia-income-tax.md).
- **Small business status**: an individual entrepreneur pays 1% of taxable income, which is Georgian-source income other than salary and the excluded income types _(Tax Code art. 90(1), (3))_. Once gross income for the year passes GEL 500,000 (GEL 700,000 for wine and agro tourism), the rate is 3% from the start of the month the limit is passed to the end of the year _(art. 90(2))_. A monthly return and payment are due by the 15th _(art. 93(1^1))_. Detail: [georgia-tax-optimization](georgia-tax-optimization.md).
- **Micro business status**: an individual with no employees and gross income up to GEL 30,000 a year pays no income tax _(Tax Code arts. 84(1), 86)_
- **Profit tax (companies)**: 15% _(Tax Code art. 98(1))_. It is charged on distributed profit, costs unrelated to the business, free supplies and entertainment over the cap, each divided by 0.85 _(art. 97(1), (10))_. Profit kept in the company is not taxed. Banks, credit unions, microfinance organisations and loan providers pay 20% on annual profit _(arts. 97(12), 98(4))_. Detail: [ge-corporate-income-tax](ge-corporate-income-tax.md).
- **Withholding on dividends and interest**: 5% _(Tax Code arts. 130(1), 131(1))_. Most other Georgian-source payments to non-residents: 10%; to a person in a preferential tax country: 15% _(art. 134(1)(e), (1^1))_
- **VAT**: standard rate 18% _(Tax Code art. 166)_. Registration is required within 2 business days of exceeding GEL 100,000 of taxable supplies in any 12 consecutive months _(art. 165(1))_. Detail: [georgia-vat](georgia-vat.md).
- **VAT filing**: the tax period is a calendar month; the return and payment are due by the 15th of the following month _(Tax Code arts. 167, 168(1))_
- **Profit tax filing**: a monthly return by the 15th of the following month _(Tax Code art. 153(10))_. The tax is paid by the same date _(art. 62(2))_
- **Payroll**: employers withhold income tax and file a monthly return on salaries paid and tax withheld by the 15th of the following month _(Tax Code art. 153(5))_. Withheld tax goes to the budget when the salary is paid _(art. 154(3)(a))_. Detail: [georgia-payroll](georgia-payroll.md); funded pension contributions: [georgia-social-contributions](georgia-social-contributions.md).
- **Annual income tax return**: residents whose income was not taxed at source, and others outside the monthly profit tax regime, file before 1 April of the following year (Georgian text: "1 აპრილამდე", so by 31 March) _(Tax Code art. 153(1))_
- **Current (advance) income and profit tax payments**: for taxpayers on a calendar-year basis, 25% of last year's tax each by 15 May, 15 July, 15 September and 15 December _(Tax Code art. 155(1))_
- **Property tax (companies)**: up to 1% a year of the average net book value of taxable property, set locally within that cap _(Tax Code arts. 6(6), 202(1))_
- **Late payment**: penalty interest of 0.05% of the unpaid tax for each day late _(Tax Code art. 272(4))_
