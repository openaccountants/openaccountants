# Namibia VAT / GST

## VAT rates, registration and filing

- **VAT overview** — Namibia levies Value-Added Tax (VAT) on taxable supplies of goods and services and on imports, administered by NamRA under the Value-Added Tax Act. The standard rate is 15%, with certain zero-rated and exempt categories.
- **Standard VAT rate** — 15 percent  _(Value-Added Tax Act (https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/republic-of-namibia/corporate/other-taxes))_
- **Zero-rated supplies** — 0 percent (includes exports, certain basic foodstuffs (e.g. mahangu, maize meal, bread, sugar), and fuel (approx — confirm specific list))  _(Value-Added Tax Act (https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/republic-of-namibia/corporate/other-taxes))_
- **Exempt supplies** — Includes certain financial services, residential accommodation, and educational/medical services (approx — confirm)  _(Value-Added Tax Act (https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/republic-of-namibia/corporate/other-taxes))_
- **Compulsory VAT registration threshold** — 1,000,000 NAD (Taxable supplies exceeding N$1,000,000 in any 12-month period)  _(Value-Added Tax Act (https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/republic-of-namibia/corporate/other-taxes))_
- **Voluntary VAT registration threshold** — 200,000 NAD (Taxable supplies that exceed (or will exceed) N$200,000 in any 12-month period)  _(Value-Added Tax Act (https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/republic-of-namibia/corporate/other-taxes))_
- **VAT return filing frequency** — Generally every 2 months (bi-monthly tax periods)  _(Value-Added Tax Act (https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/republic-of-namibia/corporate/other-taxes))_
- **VAT return and payment due date** — By the 25th day of the month following the end of the tax period (approx — confirm)  _(Value-Added Tax Act (https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/republic-of-namibia/corporate/other-taxes))_
- **Import VAT** — 15 percent (levied on the value of imported goods at the point of entry)  _(Value-Added Tax Act (https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/republic-of-namibia/corporate/other-taxes))_
- **Imported services (reverse charge basics)** — VAT on imported services is accounted for by the recipient where the services are used for non-taxable purposes (approx — confirm)  _(Value-Added Tax Act (https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/republic-of-namibia/corporate/other-taxes))_
