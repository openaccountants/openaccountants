# Norfolk Island: employment pay, leave and payslips

Establish the employer, employment relationship, work location, instrument,
classification, service, hours and pay period before computing gross pay.
Use `nf-payg-withholding` for selected withholding/reporting and
`nf-super-guarantee` for separate employer super. Tax and contribution bases
do not establish lawful wages.

Load `workflow-base` and `payroll-workflow-base`. Preserve all source,
period, profile-confirmation and refusal gates, universal outputs and
applicable payroll outputs and checks. This reference fills selected
employment parameters, not the complete payroll country contract. Missing
mandatory facts remain Needs Input and unresolved rules remain held. No
general missing-fact default, payment, submission or personal-record
collection is authorised here.

## Coverage and ongoing Norfolk modifications

The Fair Work Act's geographical definition of Australia includes Norfolk
Island. Sections 13/14 define national-system employee/employer, including
the territorial activity/employment connection. Check section 31 exclusions
and section 32A's Norfolk modifications; an NF address alone does not
establish identical coverage for everyone. [ACT1]

The Norfolk Rule modifies section 14 for specified NSW/Queensland
public-sector employment. Those exceptions do not remove status arising
on another statutory basis. The Rule expressly preserves the Norfolk
Island Regional Council and specified locally established bodies from
those particular exceptions. Establish the actual employer, applied-law
and alternative coverage conditions; do not exclude all public workers.
[NI, Schedule 1 items 2 and 6][NI]

Section 15AA ordinarily requires the real substance, practical reality and
true nature of the whole relationship, including practical performance
and contract terms. It has exclusions; sections 15AB to 15AD permit a
qualifying high-income opt-out and revocation. A contract label, invoice or
ABN does not decide employee/contractor or casual status. Disputed status,
opt-outs, overseas connections and special categories require their full
applicable rules. [ACT1]

The FWO describes the transition ending on **1 July 2018**. Its reform page
is marked historical guidance. The current Rule still contains ongoing
service, preserved-leave and possible take-home-pay protections. Do not
discard those because the transition ended. [FWO]; [NI]

## Select the applicable wage instrument

Establish the actual modern award/agreement, version, coverage,
classification, ordinary hours, allowances, penalties and overtime.
Award/agreement-free means neither applies, not that none has been found.
Section 61 protects NES minima. Section 206 separately preserves the
applicable award or national-minimum base-rate floor under an enterprise
agreement. No universal award, overtime multiplier, salary set-off or
piecework arrangement is selected here. [ACT1, sections 12, 61 and 206][ACT1]

Order PR799279 operates from **1 July 2026**, taking effect for an employee
from their **first full pay period starting on or after** that day. Merely
including 1 July in a pay period is insufficient. It replaces the 2025
Order and continues until the next order operates. [ORDER, clause 2][ORDER]

For the ordinary award/agreement-free adult branch, excluding juniors,
training arrangements and employees with disability, the prescribed
minimum is **A$1,004.90 per week on a 38-ordinary-hour basis, or A$26.44
per hour**. Retain both published amounts and verify the lawful pay basis;
the rounded hourly amount multiplied by 38 does not reproduce the weekly
figure. Do not silently replace one with the other. [ORDER, clause 4][ORDER];
[FWC]

The award/agreement-free casual loading is **25% of the employee's base
rate**, which may exceed the national minimum. Special minima address
disability without reduced productivity, qualifying supported wages,
juniors, apprentices and trainees. They do not permit an automatic wage
reduction. Establish eligibility, supported assessment, the applicable
Miscellaneous Award provisions and rounding before actual special pay.
Those computations remain held. [ORDER, clauses 5 to 10 and Schedule A][ORDER]

## Hours, pay bases and selected leave

Section 62 ordinarily limits requested/required weekly hours to **38 for
full-time employees**, or the lesser of 38 and ordinary weekly hours for
others, unless additional hours are reasonable. Employees may refuse
unreasonable additional hours. Consider all statutory factors, including
health/safety, personal circumstances, workplace needs, compensation,
notice and industry/role factors. Authorised leave/absence counts for the
test. Sections 63/64 permit specified averaging; an award-free written
arrangement has a **26-week maximum**. These provisions alone do not price
overtime or make extra hours unpaid. [ACT1]

Section 16's **base rate** ordinarily excludes bonuses, loadings, monetary
allowances, overtime/penalties and separately identifiable amounts.
Section 18's **full rate** ordinarily includes them. Pieceworkers have
separate rules. Select each entitlement's statutory rate; neither label
automatically determines taxable wages or qualifying earnings. [ACT1]

| Selected NES entitlement | Conditions and pay basis |
|---|---|
| Annual leave, section 87 | Four weeks per service year excluding casual employment; five for a qualifying NES shiftworker. Accrues progressively by ordinary hours and accumulates. Establish the actual shiftworker conditions. |
| Annual leave taken, sections 88 to 90 | Agreed taking, with unreasonable refusal prohibited. Statutory payment uses base rate for ordinary hours; public holidays and specified other leave are separate. Preserve more favourable applicable terms. |
| Annual leave on termination, section 90(2) | Amount that would have been payable had the untaken leave been taken. Do not discard an applicable loading by assuming base-only final pay. Transfers and other conditions remain material. |
| Paid personal/carer's leave, sections 96 to 99 | Ten days per service year excluding casual employment; accrues progressively by ordinary hours and accumulates. Qualifying illness/injury/caring purposes and notice/evidence apply. Payment uses base rate for ordinary hours. Public holidays/specified other leave are separate. |
| Paid family and domestic violence leave, sections 106A to 106C | Ten days each employment 12-month period, available in full at its start, including part-time/casual employees, without accumulation. Qualifying purpose and inability to deal with it outside work hours apply. Full-rate pay applies; casual payment uses rostered/accepted hours. Taking leave outside rostered hours does not itself require payment for those hours. |
| Public-holiday absence, sections 114 to 116 | Base rate for ordinary hours falling on the holiday, subject to entitlement. Requests to work/refusals have reasonableness tests. No ordinary hours means no section 116 payment. Work on a holiday needs the applicable instrument's rates. |

These are selected rules, not every NES entitlement or condition. Cashing
out, transfers, evidence, parental/community/compassionate leave, long
service leave, flexible work and special cases need further analysis.
Check actual Norfolk holiday law, location, dates and substitutions; do
not automatically use a mainland or ATO business-day calendar. [ACT1]; [FWO]

### Historical service and take-home pay

The Rule's inserted section 131B counts Norfolk service on or after
**1 July 2016** for specified annual/personal accrual and notice
entitlements. Sections 131C to 131F separately preserve qualifying
pre-2016 accrued/started leave and prior steps. Old leave is not simply
zeroed. Verify deemed accrual, prior payment and historical records before
computing a balance. [NI]

Section 131A generally counts earlier Norfolk service for non-accruing
NES entitlements, with listed exceptions and no double entitlement.
Redundancy ordinarily uses post-1 July 2016 Norfolk service under
131B(3). The historical terms exception in 131A(3)/131B(4) can include
earlier service where pre-reform terms provided redundancy entitlement.
Preserve transfer and notice-timing conditions in 131G to 131J. One
service-start date does not settle every entitlement. [NI]

Inserted sections 168M to 168S address specified take-home-pay reductions
caused by the 2018 transition, with former-instrument, position,
hours/quantity and causation conditions. A relevant FWC order may continue
while the award covers the employee, including when an agreement begins
to apply. Obtain the actual order/instrument and history before reducing
pay or asserting a protected amount. [NI]

## Notice and redundancy components

For terminations within section 117, written notice identifies the
termination day. Ordinary notice uses eligible continuous service when
notice is given, excluding casual service and subject to Norfolk service
modifications and section 123 exclusions. [ACT1]; [NI]

| Eligible service | Ordinary notice |
|---|---|
| No more than one year | One week |
| More than one, no more than three years | Two weeks |
| More than three, no more than five years | Three weeks |
| More than five years | Four weeks |

Add one week where the employee is **over 45** and has **at least two
years** of eligible continuous service. Payment in lieu uses the **full
rate** for hours the employee would have worked during that period, not
automatically base wages. Resignation notice is a separate instrument
question under section 118. [ACT1, section 117][ACT1]

Section 119's ordinary redundancy component uses **base rate for ordinary
hours**, subject to all entitlement conditions. The ten-year decline is
in the table; do not smooth it away. [ACT1]

| Eligible service at termination | Redundancy period |
|---|---|
| At least one, below two years | Four weeks |
| At least two, below three years | Six weeks |
| At least three, below four years | Seven weeks |
| At least four, below five years | Eight weeks |
| At least five, below six years | Ten weeks |
| At least six, below seven years | Eleven weeks |
| At least seven, below eight years | Thirteen weeks |
| At least eight, below nine years | Fourteen weeks |
| At least nine, below ten years | Sixteen weeks |
| At least ten years | Twelve weeks |

Check termination circumstances, sections 120 to 123, actual FWC reductions,
transfers and applicable industry schemes. Small-business treatment is not
a blanket exemption: section 121(4) covers specified insolvency-related
staff reductions. Section 23 ordinarily uses **fewer than 15 employees**,
counting associated entities together, qualifying regular casuals and
specified terminating employees at the relevant time. The Rule's
interpretation modifications remain material. [ACT1]; [NI]

Casuals, specified period/task/season and other listed employees have
particular section 123 exclusions, with an anti-avoidance qualification.
Notice and redundancy exclusions differ. Awards/agreements can preserve
additional obligations. These tables do not settle all final pay, tax,
dismissal legality or payment deadlines.

## Payment, deductions, payslips and records

Section 323 requires work-related amounts in full, in money by a permitted
method, **at least monthly**, subject to permitted deductions. Applicable
awards/agreements can prescribe method and require more frequent payment.
A frequency convention does not permit delaying lawful pay. [ACT2]

Section 324 permits specified written employee-benefit authorisations,
agreement/award/FWC-order authority or statutory/court authority. Preserve
variable-amount, authorisation-content, withdrawal and change rules.
Sections 325/326 limit unreasonable employer-benefit arrangements and
terms involving under-18 employees without required parent/guardian
agreement. Obtain actual authority and relevant regulations before a
deduction; consent alone does not settle every restriction. [ACT2]

Section 536 requires a payslip **within one working day of payment**.
Regulation 3.45 permits electronic/hard copy. Regulation 3.46 requires:

- Employer/employee names, period, payment date, gross/net amounts,
  separately identifiable bonuses/loadings/allowances/penalties and other
  entitlements, and employer ABN **if any**.
- Each deduction amount and the prescribed fund/account identification.
- For hourly pay, ordinary rate, hours at that rate and amount at that
  rate; for annual pay, the rate at the latest relevant date.
- Specified super contributions made during the period **or** intended for
  it, with prescribed fund information. The fund-name exception requires a
  payslip due within the initial **14-day** period from first pay, and, when
  issued, no chosen fund and no ATO notification of whether a stapled fund
  exists or its details. It concerns fund identification, not the amount,
  payment deadline or all payslip duties. Defined-benefit contributions have
  a separate exclusion from that definition. [ACT2]; [REG]

Keep identities, account details and confidential evidence with the reviewer
outside the agent. Stable references suit review drafts; a redacted draft
does not satisfy actual legal identity fields until the reviewer completes
and checks the lawful payslip. Do not collect or submit identifiers here.

Family/domestic-violence leave has specific confidentiality requirements.
Regulations 3.47/3.48 prohibit its payment description, period and balance
on a payslip. Report the payment as ordinary hours or another permitted
work-related payment. Only the employee's specified request permits the
alternative label for another leave type, never the protected leave's name.
Do not expose its reason in a review bundle or generic leave-balance field.
Actual confidential records remain subject to statutory duties. [ACT1,
section 106C][ACT1]; [ACT2, section 536][ACT2]; [REG]

Section 535 requires prescribed employee records for **seven years**.
Regulations 3.31 to 3.41 cover English/legible/accessible records, identity,
employment dates/types, pay, relevant hours/overtime, leave, super,
agreements, termination and transfers. Regulation 3.44 requires prompt
correction with an error notation; do not silently overwrite history or
use known false records. Actual access/confidentiality and other retention
conditions need their applicable rules. [ACT2]; [REG]

Section 125 requires the Fair Work Information Statement before or as soon
as practicable after starting, with its repeat-employment rule. Section
125B separately requires the casual statement at commencement and specified
six/twelve-month and later intervals. Small-business exceptions remove the
six-month and subsequent annual requirements, not commencement and the
first twelve-month requirement. Check current statements and actual timing;
fixed-term and other statement duties require separate rules. [ACT1]

## Fictional components and checks

These cases stipulate the applicable branch and otherwise satisfied
conditions. Amounts are AUD. They illustrate components, not complete
payslips, actual redundancy entitlement or payment instructions.

| Stipulated case | Component or classification |
|---|---|
| Period 29 June to 12 July 2026; next starts 13 July | The first full period starting on/after 1 July begins 13 July; merely including 1 July is insufficient. |
| Prescribed ordinary hourly minimum, ten hours | A$26.44 × 10 is A$264.40. Separately, 38 × A$26.44 is A$1,004.72, not the A$1,004.90 weekly figure. Confirm lawful pay basis. |
| Award-free adult casual, A$30 base and ten eligible ordinary hours | Base A$300 plus loading A$75 gives A$375; do not use A$26.44 for the loading instead. |
| Four-week annual entitlement, 19 ordinary hours weekly | Full eligible year's component is 76 hours, not 152. Progressive accrual, service and used leave still need reconciliation. |
| Six eligible years, over age 45, full weekly rate A$1,200 | Four weeks plus one gives five; full-rate component A$6,000. Base-only A$1,100 would understate this stipulated component by A$500. |
| Same service but exactly age 45 | The over-45 condition fails; no extra week under that condition. |
| Eight-and-a-half eligible years, section 119 conditions met, ordinary weekly base A$1,100 | Fourteen-week component is A$15,400. |
| Eligible service changes from nine-and-a-half to exactly ten years | Table period changes from sixteen to twelve weeks; entitlement and Norfolk historical-service gates still apply. |
| Part-time/casual starts a fresh eligible FDV leave year | Ten days are available in full, not pro-rated or accumulated; taking, payment and confidentiality conditions still apply. |
| Generic payslip field displays every leave balance | Exclude protected FDV information and follow its specific lawful reporting route. |

Check coverage, instrument, classification, period start, pay basis, rate
definition, hours, historical service, accrual/used balances, leave purposes,
notice/redundancy endpoints, deduction authority, identity completion,
protected information and records. Gross, deductions, net cash, employer
cost and journal checks still need every payroll source and input.
