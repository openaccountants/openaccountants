# Norfolk Island: annual income-tax returns and filing controls

Establish the person or entity, capacity, residence history, income year,
accounting period, activities, losses, withholding, instalments, child-support
status, notices and agent arrangements. Record reviewer-held evidence
readiness without collecting private identifiers, bank details or signing
credentials. An NF address does not establish residence or a filing exemption.

Load workflow-base, nf-tax-system and the applicable income-tax or corporate
workflow base and country references. Preserve the source and period gates,
confirmed profile, refusals, four universal outputs and every applicable
domain output and check. A filing register is supporting work, not a complete
return or substitute for those outputs. Mandatory unknown facts remain
Needs Input; unresolved law remains held. The reviewer completes any
authentication, declaration, submission and payment personally.

## Period and current authority

The **2026 year** in the annual instruments ordinarily means the year ended
**30 June 2026**, with a separate approved substituted-accounting-period
branch. A guide checked in October 2026 does not turn those instruments into
rules for the year ended June 2027. Keep 2025-26 return preparation separate
from the ordinary 2026-27 income-tax components in nf-income-tax and
nf-company-tax. Verify the correct year's rates, forms, labels and schedules
before an actual return. [RETURN, section 5][RETURN]; [CHILD, section 4][CHILD]

The current Register records identify **F2026L00618** and **F2026L00619** as
in force. The 1936 Assessment Act source is **C193, effective 1 October
2026**. The latest published Taxation Administration Act compilation is
**C226, 27 August-30 September 2026**, and the 1997 Act is **C267** for the
same interval. Their live versions pages identify the unincorporated October
Acts 71 and 86. The selected form, deferral, interpretation and definition
provisions were checked against those amendment targets; neither old
compilation alone is represented as all-law current. [RETURN-RECORD];
[CHILD-RECORD]; [ASSESS-RECORD]; [ADMIN-RECORD]; [ADMIN-VERSIONS];
[DICT-RECORD]; [DICT-VERSIONS]; [REFORM2]; [OCTOBER]

The ATO Norfolk page, updated **5 May 2026**, describes annual return
lodgment and a usual 31 October date. Apply the actual annual instrument
and adjustments below rather than treating that summary as a universal
deadline or exemption. [ATO-NORFOLK]

## Determine whether a return is required

Section 161 requires a return when the Commissioner's legislative instrument
requires one. Sections **162 and 163** separately permit further, fuller or
special returns and information within the required time, including where an
ordinary annual exemption would otherwise apply. Check an actual notice
before concluding that no return is needed. Returns must use the approved
form under section 161A. [ASSESS, sections 161-163][ASSESS]

Screen **all** applicable section 6 triggers, then the precisely applicable
section 7 exception. This table highlights selected branches; it does not
replace the full instruments or establish an exemption from other reporting.
[RETURN]

| Selected 2025-26 branch | Filing control |
|---|---|
| Ordinary full-year resident individual outside full self-assessment | Taxable income **more than A$18,200** triggers section 6(3)(a)(i). Income equal to the threshold does not trigger that paragraph, but another trigger may apply. [RETURN] |
| Part-year resident | Test the lesser of **A$18,200** and **A$13,464 plus A$395 for each resident month**, including a month in which residence begins or ends. Do not apply the full-year threshold automatically. [RETURN, section 6(3)(a)(ii)][RETURN] |
| Withholding or PAYG instalments | Section 6(2)(a) has specified withholding exclusions; section 6(2)(k) separately concerns an instalment amount paid. Low income alone does not remove these triggers. [RETURN] |
| Business or losses | Carrying on business, an incurred/deductible tax loss, a net capital loss or entitlement to apply an earlier net capital loss can trigger filing independently of positive taxable income. Companies/trusts also have the specified **more than A$1,000** undeducted/unapplied prior-loss branch. [RETURN, section 6(2)(b)-(f)][RETURN] |
| Foreign resident | Test taxable Australian income and the particular final-withholding exclusions; use the separate foreign-resident study-loan/worldwide-income branch where relevant. Do not infer non-lodgment from a foreign address. [RETURN, sections 6(3)(b), 6(10)][RETURN] |
| Resident company or other full self-assessment taxpayer | Section 6(4) tests income including capital gains, with separate fund, trustee and consolidated-group provisions. A nil tax payable figure alone does not establish no return. [RETURN, sections 5-7][RETURN] |
| Partnership | Determine the return and responsible partner/agent under section 6(12). Section 7(5)(b)'s specified nonbusiness joint rent, interest or dividend exception does not remove each partner's own reporting. Do not apply it to a business merely because ownership is joint. [RETURN] |
| Under-18, pension/benefit, SAPTO, super, exempt entity, fund or other special status | Apply the complete relevant section 6 trigger and section 7 exception, including their limited cross-references. An exception to named triggers does not necessarily remove another trigger. Special fund, NFP and charity returns require separate screening. [RETURN, sections 6-17][RETURN] |

A tax-free threshold is not a universal filing threshold. Section 6 also
contains primary-production, exempt foreign-service plus other income,
reportable-benefit/super and other triggers. Do not omit them because a
summary table mentions low income. Preserve the main instrument's specific
withholding exclusions rather than applying the ATO's broad non-lodgment
summary to every foreign-resident payment. [RETURN]; [ATO-NONLODGE]

### Child support is a separate test

For **at least one day** in the 2026 year, being a liable parent or carer
entitled to child support, other than a non-parent carer, triggers the
separate child-support instrument unless its section 5(2) exception applies.
That exception requires **both**:

- A total **less than A$31,047** of the six specified components: taxable
  income excluding assessable FHSS released amounts, tax-free pensions or
  benefits, target foreign income, reportable fringe benefits total, total
  net investment loss and reportable superannuation contributions.
- Receipt of one or more of the **listed** government pensions, allowances
  or payments for the **whole** year.

Use the statutory component definitions and full list of qualifying payments.
Taxable income alone, any government payment, part-year receipt or equality
with the threshold does not establish this exception. An exception under
this instrument does not cancel a separate main-instrument or notice
requirement. [CHILD, section 5][CHILD]

### Non-lodgment advice and other returns

The ATO's **31 August 2026** page explains non-lodgment advice after a
person establishes that a return is unnecessary. Its online records may
prevent that advice where an ABN, withholding or instalment registration is
active; it directs an active-ABN taxpayer to a return, potentially nil.
Establish the facts and required administrative route rather than treating
an ABN as either proof of business activity or automatic statutory exemption.
A non-lodgment advice is not a device to override a return requirement.
[ATO-NONLODGE]; [RETURN]

Family-assistance income confirmation to Services Australia, child-support
income details, franking-credit refund applications and annual tax returns
are separate processes. The main instrument separately addresses NFP
self-review, franking, venture-capital deficit, fund and community-charity
returns. Do not copy the ordinary income-tax due date onto them. October
Act 86 Schedule 7 also changes ancillary-fund terminology to giving funds
and preserves specified instrument references; the old form wording does
not itself establish that obligations ended. Actual special-entity returns
and their calendars require their own current sources. [ATO-NONLODGE];
[RETURN, sections 6(11), 7(4), 9-17][RETURN]; [OCTOBER, Schedule 7][OCTOBER]

## Establish the actual filing deadline

| Selected 2026-return route | Ordinary instrument deadline before adjustments |
|---|---|
| Ordinary year ended 30 June 2026, excluding section 6(7)/(8) persons | **31 October 2026**. [RETURN, section 8(1)][RETURN] |
| Approved substituted period, full self-assessment taxpayer, excluding those persons | **15th day of the seventh month** after period end. [RETURN, section 8(2)(a)][RETURN] |
| Approved substituted period, other taxpayer, excluding those persons | **Last day of the fourth month** after period end. [RETURN, section 8(2)(b)][RETURN] |
| Section 6(7)/(8) shipping/nonresident-money or insurer-agent route | **First day of the sixth month of the following income year**; determine the actual applicable year. [RETURN, section 8(3)][RETURN] |
| Child-support instrument | **31 October 2026**, or **last day of the fourth month** after the approved substituted period. [CHILD, section 6][CHILD] |

Under Schedule 1 section **388-52**, an approved form due on a nonbusiness
day may be given on the first business day afterwards. The section 995-1
business-day definition excludes weekends and a public holiday for the
whole of **any State, ACT or NT**. It is not just the taxpayer's local
workplace calendar. [ADMIN1, section 3AA][ADMIN1]; [FORMS]; [DICT]

**31 October 2026 is a Saturday**. Applying that rule and the checked 2026
holiday list gives **Monday 2 November 2026** for the ordinary unextended
31 October filing route. Tasmania's Recreation Day on 2 November is a
regional holiday, rather than a whole-state holiday. This date is a derived
application of the statute and official calendar, not a separately verified
client notice or a payment deadline. Check later announcements and the
actual notice or confirmed agent arrangement before use. [FORMS]; [DICT];
[HOLIDAYS]; [ATO-PREPARE]

Most registered agents have a special program, but the date depends on the
client and when the agent is engaged. The ATO advises first-time or changing
clients to contact the agent **before 31 October**. Do not assume a universal
May date, move that contact advice to 2 November automatically, or treat
hiring an agent as a granted deferral. Record the actual confirmed return
and payment dates and their authority. [ATO-AGENT]

Section **388-55** permits a lodgment deferral but expressly says it **does
not defer payment**. A payment deferral uses its separate authority. A
request alone is not an approved extension. Payment, instalments, BAS,
withholding, FBT and any special-return dates remain separate calendar
entries; use their own notice and provisions. No 2027 annual-return date,
current interest rate or dollar penalty is supplied by this reference.
[FORMS]; [RETURN, section 4][RETURN]

## Approved forms, declarations and preparation sequence

Section **388-50** requires the approved form, required signed declaration,
required information and prescribed delivery method; its limited subsection
(1A) alternative depends on the Commissioner's written information
requirement. A saved worksheet or incomplete online draft is not itself a
lodged approved form. Sections **388-60-388-75** distinguish entity, agent,
paper, electronic and telephone declarations. [FORMS]

Where an agent gives the document, section **388-65** requires the entity's
written, signed authority and true-and-correct declaration **before** the
agent gives it. The entity must retain it or a copy for **5 years after it
is made**, unless a permitted shorter period applies, and produce it when
required within that period. This declaration-specific period is not the
retention start for every tax record. [FORMS]

1. Confirm scope, person/capacity, residence, ordinary or approved substituted
   period and correct annual instrument. Identify every applicable workflow.
2. Build a complete trigger and exception register with clause references,
   evidence readiness and the separate child-support/notice tests. Do not
   default unknown facts to non-lodgment.
3. Record each return/report, unadjusted date, statutory day adjustment,
   actual agent program or granted deferral, authority and reviewer action.
   Keep filing and payment columns separate.
4. Reconcile income, deductions, losses, offsets, withholding and required
   schedules using the correct year's substantive references and approved
   form instructions. Preserve the applicable domain outputs and refusals.
5. Record declaration and authorisation readiness, reviewer approval and
   personal submission action. Do not sign, lodge, pay or access a private
   account through this guide. The reviewer retains sensitive information.
6. After reviewer lodgment, reconcile the acknowledgement and assessment
   against the reviewed work through redacted evidence. Processing or an
   assessment alone does not verify every position. Sections 166-169A allow
   assessment, deemed/default assessment and reliance on supplied statements;
   an omitted return can lead to default assessment. [ASSESS]

Amendments, objections, penalty liability/remission, debt and interest have
their own grounds, clocks and evidence. Use the selected routes already
identified in nf-property-transfers and nf-foreign-income where applicable;
this annual-filing reference does not supply a general dispute or penalty
calculation. An inability to pay does not by itself remove filing.

## Fictional boundary examples and self-checks

These stipulated components illustrate the selected rules. They are not
actual tax returns, all-trigger assessments or child-support calculations.

| Stipulated component | Result to record |
|---|---|
| Full-year resident individual's taxable income is A$18,200 | Section 6(3)(a)(i) alone is not triggered. Screen every other trigger, exception, child-support rule and notice before non-lodgment. |
| Same individual has A$18,201 | That paragraph is triggered, subject to the applicable section 7 exception and other requirements. |
| Three resident months, with no other residence month | Part-year component: A$13,464 + 3 x A$395 = **A$14,649**, below the A$18,200 cap. |
| A business incurs a loss and has no positive taxable income | Business/loss triggers still need filing assessment; zero tax is not a non-lodgment conclusion. |
| A parent satisfies the year-long listed-payment condition; six statutory components total A$31,046 | The child-support instrument's monetary condition is met; check the other condition and separate main-instrument/notice obligations. |
| Same total is A$31,047 | Strictly-less-than condition fails. |
| Taxable income is A$20,000; other stipulated components total A$12,000 | Total is **A$32,000**; taxable income below A$31,047 does not establish the exception. |
| Monetary condition is met but qualifying benefits cover only part of the year | Whole-year condition is not established; do not assume the exception. |
| Approved substituted period ends 31 December 2025, full self-assessment taxpayer outside special-agent branches | Unadjusted instrument date is **15 July 2026**; then check day adjustments and actual deferrals. |
| Same period, ordinary non-full-self-assessment taxpayer | Unadjusted date is **30 April 2026**; then apply the applicable checks. |
| An ordinary 2026 return has no special notice or confirmed extension | Instrument date 31 October; the checked business-day adjustment permits **2 November 2026**. Payment remains separate. |
| Lodgment is deferred but no payment deferral is evidenced | Update only the filing entry; retain the independently established payment date. |

Check strict inequalities, part-year months and caps, every trigger and
limited exception, child-support components and whole-year receipt, notice
requirements, taxpayer capacity, approved accounting period, business-day
definition, actual agent date, declaration timing and payment separation.
Never turn a missing statutory fact or confirmation into an assumed exemption.
