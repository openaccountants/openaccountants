actual due date, approved form and receipt, statement and correct tax

## Sources and separate liabilities

The latest published TAA compilation is **C226, 27 August-30 September
2026**. Its versions page identifies unincorporated October Acts **71 and
86**; their amendment targets do not replace the selected Divisions
**284, 286 or 298**. Read the selected provisions with that amendment check,
not C226 alone as all-law current. [ADMIN-RECORD]; [ADMIN-VERSIONS];
[REFORM2]; [OCTOBER]

Late lodgement, a false statement, a position that is not reasonably
arguable, a default assessment, interest and a criminal offence are
separate questions. Correcting or paying the underlying tax does not by
itself establish that every penalty disappears. This guide selects ordinary
administrative penalties below. It does not compute scheme/transfer-pricing,
minimum-tax, super-guarantee, excise or criminal penalties, or a local
Norfolk charge. Territorial application and each special regime retain
their own sources and facts. [ADMIN, sections 284-75 and 286-75][ADMIN]

## Failure to lodge in the approved form

Section **286-75(1)** requires a taxation-law obligation to give the
Commissioner a specified document in the approved form by a particular day,
and failure to do so. Establish that obligation first. A late document
with no tax payable or a refund is not a statutory blanket exemption.
Subsection **(2)** excludes documents under its listed superannuation Acts;
they require their own penalty rules. Other recipient/reporting branches
in section 286-75 also have separate triggers. [ADMIN]

The section **286-75(1A)-(1B)** registered-agent safe harbour requires:

- Engagement of a registered tax agent or BAS agent.
- All relevant taxation information supplied so the agent could give the
  approved-form document by its due date; the taxpayer bears the evidential
  burden for this condition.
- The agent's failure to give it by that date, without the failure resulting
  from the agent's intentional disregard or recklessness.

Using an agent alone does not prove safe harbour. Distinguish information
supplied too late from information enabling timely lodgement. Preserve
dated instructions, information delivery and agent correspondence through
redacted evidence references. [ADMIN]

### Count periods, then value and classify

Section **286-80(2)** supplies **1 penalty unit for each 28-day period or
part**, capped at **5 base units**. The period starts on the due date and
ends when the document is given. PS LA 2011/19 paragraph **16** explains
its calculation from the due date to the **date before receipt**. For an
established late document with N calendar days between those dates,
count the ceiling of N divided by 28, capped at 5. Thus **28 days late**
is one base unit and **29 days late** is two. First verify the effective
due date and valid receipt, rather than a draft completion date. [ADMIN];
[PS-FTL, paragraphs 15-16][PS-FTL]

Under **286-80(5)**, value each unit at the amount applying at the **start
of its own 28-day period**. The current Crimes Act record is **C167,
effective 27 August 2026**. The published 2026 instrument states a unit
of **A$364 from 1 July 2026**. Its June registration date is not that
operative date. Do not apply A$364 indiscriminately to older periods, or
use one current rate for every block spanning a unit change. This guide
uses the published amount, not a reconstruction of the CPI indexation
formulas. [ADMIN]; [CRIMES]; [CRIMES-RECORD]; [UNIT]

Select the section **286-80** increase separately:

| Branch | Conditions relevant to the ordinary calculation |
|---|---|
| Subsection (3): multiply base amount by **2** | Medium withholder for the due month, **or** assessable income for the income year in which due **more than A$1 million but less than A$20 million**, **or** current GST turnover worked out at a time in that due month in the same interval. |
| Subsection (4): multiply base amount by **5** | Large withholder for the due month, **or** that assessable income **A$20 million or more**, **or** that current GST turnover **A$20 million or more**. |
| Subsections (4A)-(4B): specified SGE branch, **500** | Requires the particular filing failure, prior assessment/determination/reporting or group condition and latest-period SGE test; subsection (4B) can retrospectively disapply it. The ordinary 2/5 branches do not apply when (4A) does. |
| Subsection (4C): specified minimum-tax group branch, **500** | Has its own IIR/UTPR or DMT liability and group-entity conditions; ordinary 2/5 branches do not apply when it does. Actual minimum-tax outcomes remain held. |

All rows cite **286-80(3)-(4C)**. Do not stack the ordinary 2 and 5
multipliers, or replace a special branch with an ordinary size label.
Confirm every applicable test, including legal withholder status; exact
A$1 million fails the strict income/turnover limb in subsection (3), but
another limb may still increase the penalty. Current GST turnover is a
statutory measure, not simply one month's sales. Actual turnover and
special-group classifications need their underlying rules. [ADMIN];
[PS-FTL, paragraphs 17-22][PS-FTL]

Withholder status under **16-95 to 16-115** has historical financial-year,
group and Commissioner-determination rules. The ordinary statutory monetary
limbs say amounts withheld **exceeded A$1 million** for large and
**exceeded A$25,000** for medium, with different period conditions and
other routes. Medium also requires not large; small requires some amount
withheld in the month and neither large nor medium. Do not assign a class
from today's payroll total alone or turn those strict tests into inclusive
ones. Check effective determination notices. [WITHHOLDING]

A granted lodgement deferral and a suspension of enforcement differ:
PS LA 2011/19 paragraphs **29-30** use the deferred date for the former
but the original due date for the latter. Verify the actual authority and
terms; a payment arrangement is not a filing extension. A notice can issue
before the document is lodged, and section **286-80(6)** permits a later
increase. Reconcile any later notice to the same obligation and cap.
[ADMIN]; [PS-FTL]

## False statements, shortfalls and default assessments

Section **284-20** includes oral, written and electronic statements;
**284-25** treats an agent's statement as the taxpayer's. Under
**284-75(1)/(4)**, identify a materially false or misleading statement,
including relevant omissions, and the actual recipient/statutory branch.
A statement need not produce a tax shortfall to attract a penalty. The
reasonable-care exception in **(5)** requires both taxpayer and relevant
agent to have taken reasonable care. The registered-agent exception in
**(6)-(7)** separately requires all relevant tax information and no agent
recklessness/intentional disregard, with the taxpayer's evidential burden
for supplying that information. [ADMIN]

Section **284-80** measures the shortfall by the difference in the relevant
tax liability, payment or credit, not simply an omitted income amount.
Verify the correct substantive tax result first. Trust, partnership,
consolidation and other special attribution rules need their own checks;
this guide's examples stipulate an ordinary single-taxpayer shortfall.
[ADMIN, sections 284-30, 284-35 and 284-80][ADMIN]

| Ordinary section 284-90 base penalty item | Base amount before applicable adjustments/remission |
|---|---|
| Shortfall caused by intentional disregard: item 1 | **75% of shortfall** |
| Shortfall caused by recklessness: item 2 | **50% of shortfall** |
| Shortfall caused by failure to take reasonable care: item 3 | **25% of shortfall** |
| False statement without shortfall: items 3A/3B/3C | **60 / 40 / 20 penalty units**, respectively, for those behaviours |
| Qualifying not-reasonably-arguable position: ordinary item 4 | **25% of shortfall**, subject to its threshold and conditions |
| Required document absent and Commissioner determines liability: item 7 | **75% of the tax-related liability**, not 75% of omitted income |

The table cites **284-75 and 284-90**. If multiple base items apply,
**284-90(2)** selects the highest BPA; do not add them. Behaviour needs
actual evidence, not an automatic assumption that any error is careless.
SGE doubling under **284-90(1A)-(1B)** has its specified prior-status and
trigger-day conditions and exception; minimum-tax **(1C)** is separate.
Actual specialist cases remain held. No-shortfall unit amounts require
the applicable event and unit value, not automatic use of today's rate.
[ADMIN]; [ATO-STATEMENTS]

The section **284-75(2)** not-reasonably-arguable branch is distinct from
reasonable care. Section **284-15** asks whether, having regard to relevant
authorities, the argued position is about as likely correct as incorrect,
or more likely correct; an assumed discretion has a further condition.
For ordinary item 4, the shortfall must be **more than** the greater of
**A$10,000 or 1%** of the relevant return-based income-tax/PRRT liability.
Exact equality does not meet that limb. The specified trust/partnership
items use their own shortfall/net-income conditions and threshold of the
greater of **A$20,000 or 2% of return-based net income**. Minimum-tax item
6A and its threshold are separate. Do not apply a single ordinary threshold
to every taxpayer or tax. [ADMIN, sections 284-15, 284-75 and 284-90][ADMIN]

The **284-75(3)** default-assessment branch requires the missing document
to be necessary to determine the tax liability accurately and a determination
without it. A merely late return is not automatically a 75% default
penalty. The statute expressly notes that FTL liability can coexist;
check each cause separately. [ADMIN]

## Adjustment order and voluntary disclosure

First work out the correct BPA, including **284-224** to the extent the
position agreed with Commissioner advice, general administrative practice
or an approved publication. Preserve the actual advice and causal link.
This is separate from a voluntary-disclosure percentage. [ADMIN];
[PS-STATEMENTS, paragraphs 106-111][PS-STATEMENTS]

Section **284-220** increases the BPA by **20%** for specified obstruction,
awareness without timely disclosure or the specified prior-penalty item
groups. PS LA 2012/5 paragraph **113** confirms one 20% increase even if
multiple criteria apply. A prior remitted penalty can still matter; actual
history and the relevant item group must be established. [ADMIN];
[PS-STATEMENTS, paragraphs 112-123][PS-STATEMENTS]

Under **284-225(1)**, disclosure after being told of an examination can
reduce the BPA by **20%** if voluntary, in the approved form and reasonably
estimated to save significant examination time or resources. Under
**(2)-(4A)**, qualifying voluntary approved-form disclosure **before the
day** of examination notification, or the specified earlier public-statement
deadline, gives **80%** for a shortfall/part of **A$1,000 or more**, or
reduces its BPA to **nil** below A$1,000. A qualifying no-shortfall false
statement also has the nil branch. Scheme shortfalls have a separate 80%
branch. Subsection **(5)** permits, but does not guarantee, treating a later
disclosure as before notification. Do not split disclosures artificially
or assume a casual admission meets every condition. Actual disclosures,
aggregation, mixed components and discretion decisions require the full
applicable rules and evidence. [ADMIN]

For percentage adjustments, section **284-85(2)** uses:

**Penalty = BPA + BPA × (increase percentage − reduction percentage).**

The official HTML's accessible formula text and PS LA 2012/5 paragraph
**111** agree. Both percentages act on the BPA; do not apply the reduction
sequentially to an already increased total. Work separately where different
parts attract different behaviour or adjustments. Apply any actual remission
after establishing the statutory amount. [ADMIN]; [PS-STATEMENTS]

## Notice, remission and review

Section **298-10** requires written notice of liability and reasons;
reasons are not required where the penalty is fully remitted. Under
**298-15**, the notified payment date must be **at least 14 days after**
the notice is given. Section **298-20** permits remission of all or part;
a refusal/partial remission requires written decision and reasons.
Identify the actual notice and decision, not only an account balance.
[ADMIN]

The **2023 remedial determination** modifies section 298-20(1) for FTL
under **286-75**, allowing remission for an entity or relevant class,
including existing or future liabilities. Its classes cover specified
serious external adversity, minor-amount considerations and ongoing
compliance history. This is a power, not an automatic entitlement or a
universal modification of every penalty. Do not turn the ATO's usual
request-after-lodgement process into an absolute statutory inability to
remit a future qualifying FTL liability. [REMEDIAL]

The ATO says it generally does not apply FTL to an isolated late lodgement
or a return producing nil tax/refund, with stated exceptions. Its remission
practice considers delay, cause, compliance history and relevant external
circumstances; safe harbour and remission are separate questions.
PS LA 2011/19 is an internal staff instruction and is marked as under
review. Administrative practice is not a statutory exemption or guaranteed
outcome. Preserve evidence of the cause, affected period, reasonable steps,
any deferral request and subsequent compliance. [ATO-FTL]; [PS-FTL]

Under **298-20(3)**, a remission decision can be objected to under Part IVC
where the remaining penalty is **more than 2 penalty units**. Exactly two
is not more than two. Under **298-30**, a Division 284 penalty assessment
has its separate objection right; do not treat that provision as a direct
assessment objection right for every FTL penalty. Establish the enabling
right, decision, giving/service, time limit and grounds using
nf-tax-amendments-objections and the applicable law. Other review avenues
and actual tribunal/court procedures require their own verification.
[ADMIN]

An objection or remission request is not a filing extension or automatic
payment/recovery stay. Section **298-25** can add GIC to an unpaid penalty;
actual interest rates, periods, deferrals, remission and recovery action
need their own current sources. Keep penalty principal, underlying tax,
interest, payment arrangements and disputed amounts separate. No payment
or filing action is taken through this reference. [ADMIN]

## Fictional components and reviewer checks

For the FTL monetary examples only, stipulate an ordinary multiplier,
valid due/receipt dates, all relevant period starts on/after **1 July 2026**,
A$364 per unit and no safe harbour or remission. For statement examples,
stipulate the correct shortfall, proved behaviour, no special group regime
and only the specified adjustments. These are components, not real notices.

| Component | Result |
|---|---|
| Valid approved form given on the confirmed due date | No section 286-75(1) failure on that timing fact. |
| FTL at 1 or 28 days late, ordinary multiplier 1 | One unit: **A$364**. |
| FTL at 29 or 31 days late, multiplier 1 | Two units: **A$728**. |
| FTL at 112 days late, multiplier 1 | Four units: **A$1,456**. |
| FTL at 113 or 200 days late, multiplier 1 | Five-unit cap: **A$1,820**. |
| FTL two base units, proved multiplier 2, then proved multiplier 5 | Respectively **A$1,456** and **A$3,640**; do not combine multipliers. |
| Assessable income exactly A$1 million; all other increase branches proved absent | The income limb of subsection (3) does not increase the base amount. |
| Assessable income exactly A$20 million; no special branch | The subsection (4) income limb selects multiplier **5**. |
| A required GST-turnover or withholder classification is unknown | Needs Input; do not assume the smallest multiplier. |
| Ordinary A$10,000 tax shortfall; carelessness, recklessness or intentional disregard | Respective BPAs **A$2,500**, **A$5,000**, **A$7,500**. |
| BPA A$2,500, 20% increase and 80% disclosure reduction | **A$1,000** before remission, using the common BPA. |
| Same BPA with only an 80% reduction, then only a 20% reduction | Respectively **A$500** and **A$2,000**. |
| Ordinary A$1,000 shortfall, 25% BPA and qualifying pre-notification disclosure, no increase | BPA A$250; 80% reduction leaves **A$50**. |
| Same conditions with A$999 shortfall and no increase | The below-A$1,000 nil branch applies; do not substitute an 80% reduction. |
| Ordinary return-based income-tax liability A$2 million | RA threshold **A$20,000**; a shortfall exactly A$20,000 does not exceed it. Other penalty branches can still apply. |
| Material false statement without tax shortfall, lack of reasonable care, no exception | Item 3C BPA **20 penalty units**, not zero merely because tax is unchanged. |
| Registered agent engaged but timely information supply unproved | Hold safe harbour; engagement alone does not satisfy it. |
| Remaining penalty after remission exactly 2 units, then 3 units | Section 298-20(3)'s more-than-two-unit condition fails, then succeeds; verify the actual decision and clock. |

Check source/application periods, statutory obligation and approved form,
effective due date versus enforcement suspension, receipt evidence,
28-day boundaries and cap, each block's unit, every multiplier branch,
reasonable care and safe harbour separately, correct tax shortfall,
behaviour and prior history, threshold equality, common-BPA adjustments,
approved-form disclosure, notice/reasons, actual remission and review
route. Reconcile all amounts to the reviewer-held notices and required
workflow outputs before any professional action.
