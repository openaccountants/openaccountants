# Norfolk Island: company registration, records and reporting

Establish the entity, proposed activity, owners, funding, officers,
location, registration history and relevant event/financial year. This
reference covers selected Corporations Act company obligations. It does
not choose a company over a sole trader, partnership, trust, association
or another body, or establish their complete separate rules.

Load `workflow-base`, `company-formation-workflow-base` and `nf-tax-system`.
Retain the confirmed profile, source and period gates, refusals, universal
outputs, all five formation outputs, ten domain checks and mandatory
country slots. This reference fills selected parameters, not a complete
formation companion. Missing facts remain Needs Input and unresolved law
remains held. A source reference is not permission to register a company,
make payments or submit identity, tax or banking information.

## Territorial application and the transferred-company branch

Corporations Act 2001 section 9 expressly includes **Norfolk Island** in
the definition of Territory. Section 5 applies the Act in this jurisdiction,
which includes those Territories; provisions also operate according to
their tenor. ASIC has general administration under section 5B. Do not use
an old exclusion of Norfolk or assume every island body is a company
registered under the Act. Registration and tax residence are distinct
questions. Use `nf-company-tax` for selected tax rules. [ACT1]

The relevant corporate reform commenced on **2 August 2021**, under
Territories Legislation Amendment Act 2020 section 2, table item 4. The
Act's assent in December 2020 is not that commencement date. Current
Corporations Act Part 10.51 retains the transition. [REFORM]; [ACT6]

Section 1678's eligible corporations exclude foreign companies registered
under Part 25 of the former Norfolk Companies Act and require the other
listed conditions: no Chapter 5 body corporate and no outstanding specified
winding-up or compromise/arrangement application. Section 1678A requires
ASIC registration of an eligible corporation on commencement, with
corresponding type and characteristics, subject to continued eligibility.
Do not treat every historical island corporation as automatically
transferred. [ACT6]

Section 1678B provides the ACN/certificate, Norfolk registration status,
name rules, inherited memorandum/articles and director conditions. Its
constitution rule is read with the Part 5B.1 modification requirement;
the note identifies a three-month post-registration adjustment. Confirm
the actual corporate record and historical compliance. Section 1678E
preserves specified rules for particular corporations. A new company
application and a transferred company's historical evidence require
different checklists. [ACT6]

## Company type, officers and the registration record

Section 112 permits proprietary companies limited by shares or unlimited
with share capital, and specified public-company and investment-vehicle
types. A no-liability company has mining-purpose conditions. Section 114
requires at least **one member**. A proprietary company ordinarily has a
limit of **50 counted non-employee shareholders** under section 113: joint
holdings, employee/former-employee holdings and qualifying crowd-sourced
funding (CSF) holdings have particular counting rules. A raw headcount of
every holder does not establish breach or eligibility. Fundraising also
has separate restrictions. [ACT1]

| Company branch | Selected officer conditions |
|---|---|
| Ordinary proprietary company | At least **one director**, ordinarily resident in Australia; a secretary is optional, but if secretaries are appointed at least one must ordinarily reside in Australia. |
| Proprietary company with CSF shareholders | At least **two directors**; if exactly two, at least one ordinarily resident in Australia; otherwise a majority ordinarily resident in Australia. Making a CSF offer has its own director condition. |
| Public company | At least **three directors**, excluding alternates, with at least two ordinarily resident in Australia; at least one secretary, with at least one ordinarily resident in Australia. |

These are sections 201A and 204A conditions. Sections 201B and 204B require
an individual aged at least **18** and address disqualification, including
the relevant permission or leave. Sections 201D and 204C require signed
consent before appointment and retention of that consent. Establish the
actual residence and eligibility; an address entered on a form does not
prove ordinary residence. [ACT1]

Section 117 requires an ASIC application in the prescribed form. It
identifies the company type/name, consenting members and officers,
registered office and principal business address, proposed State/Territory
of registration, share classes/numbers/paid and unpaid amounts, beneficial
holding status, ultimate holding company and applicable guarantee or
non-cash particulars. The applicant must hold the required consents and
agreements at lodgement and give them to the registered company to keep.
Public-company constitutions have an additional lodgement requirement.
[ACT1]

Under sections 118 and 119, ASIC registration and the certificate establish
the company's registration and existence; merely submitting the application
does not. Section 119A distinguishes jurisdiction of incorporation from
the recorded State/Territory of registration. Do not substitute an ABN,
business-name registration or a tax registration for this company record.
Capital, liability, constitution choices, name availability, non-cash
valuation and any regulated activity need their full applicable rules.
[ACT1]

Director ID is a separate gate. Sections 1272B–1272E address eligible
officers, the requirement to have an ID, pending applications, directions
and extensions. Section 1678F contains a specific Norfolk transfer
transition and disapplies the stated default transition. Obtain the
current prescribed periods, determinations and any relevant extension
before deciding an individual's deadline. Do not apply a universal
28-day grace period or treat company registration as completing this
obligation. Actual identity applications stay with the authorised person;
record readiness without collecting identifiers or identity documents.
[ACT6]

## Registered office, owners and change notifications

Section 142 requires a registered office in this jurisdiction. Where the
company does not occupy it, retain the occupier's consent under sections
117/143. A registered-office address change must be notified within
**28 days after the change**; section 142(3) separately makes the notice
effective from the later of the seventh day after lodgement or the later
specified day. Keep the notice deadline and effective date separate.
Section 146 gives the principal-business-address change its own 28-day
notice rule. [ACT1]

Section 205B generally requires appointment, personal-detail changes and
cessation notices within **28 days**, with stated exceptions. For example,
becoming an officer on registration under section 120 is distinguished
from a subsequent appointment. Public registered-office opening hours
and changes have additional sections 144/145 conditions. No actual form
or personal address is prepared here. [ACT1]

Sections 168/169 require the internal member register and applicable
option/debenture registers. The member register records membership and
share particulars, specified non-beneficial holdings and applicable CSF
data. Former-member information is retained for the previous **seven
years**. A company with more than 50 members needs a usable index unless
the register itself functions as one. These are company records, not an
invitation to upload real ownership information to an agent. [ACT1]

Sections 178A/178B require specified proprietary-company changes to be
notified to ASIC, with a **top-20-per-class** limitation where there are
more than 20 members. That limitation does not reduce the internal
register to 20 members. Sections 178C/178D connect some member/share
notifications to court corrections, class changes, share issues or capital
reductions and their specific notice periods. The residual branch is
**28 days after adding or altering the register particular**. It is not
a universal deadline for all share transactions. [ACT1]

Record the statutory non-beneficial-holding flag and obtain the underlying
notice rules when relevant. This reference does not establish that all
beneficial-ownership, tracing, foreign-investment or activity disclosures
are absent, or that ASIC's member information replaces them.

## Company size and financial records

For **2019–20 and later financial years**, current Regulation 1.0.02B,
with application provision 10.30.01, prescribes the section 45A amounts:

| Criterion for company and controlled entities | Small-company limb | Large-company limb |
|---|---|---|
| Consolidated annual revenue | Less than **A$50 million** | **A$50 million or more** |
| Consolidated gross assets at year end | Less than **A$25 million** | **A$25 million or more** |
| Employees at year end | Fewer than **100** | **100 or more** |

At least **two of the three** limbs must be satisfied for the relevant
classification. Do not use the Act's lower fallback amounts where the
Regulations prescribe replacements. Control, revenue/assets and employee
counts require section 45A's accounting-standards and proportionate
part-time/full-time-equivalent rules, including controlled entities.
Separate legal entities do not automatically receive separate size tests.
[ACT1]; [REG1]; [REG4]

Section 286 requires written financial records explaining transactions,
position and performance and enabling true and fair statements to be
prepared and audited. This includes trustee transactions. Retain them for
**seven years after the covered transactions are completed**, rather than
assuming seven years from the invoice date. Sections 287/288 address
English translation on request and conversion of electronic records to
hard copy. Section 289 imposes local-information/notification conditions
where financial records are kept outside this jurisdiction. [ACT2]

Section 323D generally starts a company's first financial year on
registration/incorporation. It lasts 12 months or the directors' determined
period of no more than **18 months**. Subsequent years, changes and group
synchronisation have further conditions. Do not make the financial year,
income-tax year and annual review date identical by default. [ACT2]

## Financial reports, audits and delivery

Section 292 generally requires annual financial/directors' reports from
public and large proprietary companies and the other specified entities.
For a small proprietary company, test shareholder/ASIC directions,
foreign control with the specified lack of consolidation in ASIC-lodged
statements, and CSF shareholders. Small size alone is not a complete
exemption. Section 293 permits shareholders with **at least 5% of votes**
to direct reporting no later than **12 months after year end**; it can vary the
specified requirements and request audit. Section 294 gives ASIC its own
direction power, with a reasonable compliance date and six-year limit on
making the direction. [ACT2]

Section 301 generally requires audit and an auditor's report where it
applies. Its small-company exceptions depend on why the report is
required, CSF proceeds and whether a direction requires audit. The
relevant current CSF audit threshold and any relief instrument must be
checked; this reference does not select a fixed threshold from the Act's
fallback definition. Guarantee companies and special entities have
separate branches. An accountant preparing accounts is not automatically
the statutory auditor. [ACT1]; [ACT2]

Keep preparation, audit, delivery to members and ASIC lodgement separate:

| Selected obligation | Statutory timing/condition |
|---|---|
| Member reporting for public companies and non-scheme disclosing entities | Section 315(1): earlier of **21 days before the next AGM** and **four months after year end**. |
| Small proprietary shareholder direction given after year end | Section 315(2): later of **two months after the direction** and **four months after year end**. |
| Other proprietary member reporting within section 315(4) | **Four months after year end**. |
| ASIC lodgement under section 319 | **Three months** for specified disclosing entities/schemes/superannuation entities/passport funds; **four months** for others to which it applies. |

Section 319(2) has exceptions for specified small companies preparing
direction-based reports, including a condition concerning CSF shareholders.
Do not convert a member-direction report into an automatic ASIC filing,
or ignore an ASIC direction's particular requirements. Establish the
actual reporting category, financial year, direction, relief and operative
deadline rules before preparing a calendar. [ACT2]

Sustainability reporting is a separate section 292A gate. It requires
financial reporting plus its size, NGER or specified-entity conditions,
subject to consolidated reporting and Part 10.77's phased application.
The ordinary section 45A size test alone does not decide a 2026 climate
reporting duty. Obtain the applicable phase, accounting/sustainability
standards, assurance and relief rules before an actual report. Complete
financial-statement contents, accounting policies and audit engagement
requirements also remain to be established. [ACT2]; [ACT6]

## Annual company review and selected current fees

Section 345A ordinarily uses the registration anniversary, with approved
changes and other statutory branches. A would-be 29 February review date
becomes **28 February**. Use the actual ASIC review date and company
record. Section 346A requires an extract of particulars with its issue
date; it may be made available electronically. [ACT2]

Where a response is required because particulars are wrong or information
is requested, section 346C requires it within **28 days after the issue
date**, in the prescribed signed/authenticated form. Do not substitute
the review date or receipt date. A compliant response can satisfy another
notification requirement but does not erase earlier late fees or offences.
Keep event-driven notifications current throughout the year. [ACT2]

Under section 347A, directors must pass a solvency resolution within
**two months after the review date**, except where the specified Chapter
2M financial report was lodged during the preceding 12 months. A negative
resolution triggers a notice within **seven days after it is passed**.
No resolution by the end of the two-month period triggers a different
seven-day notice clock. [ACT2, sections 347A/347B][ACT2]

Section 347C can deem a solvency representation where the fee is paid
and its other conditions are met. The Act expressly says that this does
not mean the directors have passed the required resolution. Paying the
fee and making the decision are separate acts. Retain the directors'
resolution and supporting evidence; actual solvency is not decided here.
[ACT2]; [ANNUAL]

ASIC INFO 30's table **from 1 July 2026**, checked on 18 October, gives
these selected authority fees in Australian dollars and states the listed
fees are not subject to GST:

| Selected service/category | Fee |
|---|---|
| Reserve a company name, Form 410 | **A$65** |
| Part 2A.1 company registration with share capital, Form 201 | **A$636** |
| Part 2A.1 registration without share capital, Form 201 | **A$524** |
| Annual review: ordinary proprietary company | **A$342** |
| Annual review: ordinary public company | **A$1,591** |
| Annual review: qualifying special-purpose proprietary/public company | **A$70 / A$65**, respectively, subject to the defined category. |

Annual-review amounts exclude special-purpose and other exempt/different
categories as stated in the source. The authority table generally requires
annual review payment within **two months after review date**. Its selected
late lodgement, review and payment fee bands are **A$102** within one
month after the applicable due time and **A$428** more than one month
after it. Different fees can accumulate. A calendar month is not a
universal 30-day grace period. Confirm the actual service, event, due date,
exemption and current indexed fee before quoting a liability. [FEES]

Do not make name reservation mandatory in every formation or add an annual
review fee as though it were due on registration day. Professional,
registered-office, bank, licence, activity and other costs require their
own evidence; an unknown cost is not zero. No total formation cost or
processing-time promise is supplied here. [FEES]

## Fictional component cases and self-checks

| Stipulated facts | Component result |
|---|---|
| Consolidated revenue A$49,999,999, assets A$24,999,999, employees 100 | Two small limbs; small proprietary size classification. Reporting still needs section 292 screening. |
| Revenue exactly A$50 million, assets exactly A$25 million, employees 99 | Two large limbs; large proprietary classification despite fewer than 100 employees. |
| Assets and employee count unknown | Needs Input; one known revenue limb cannot decide the two-of-three test. |
| Ordinary proprietary company has three directors and CSF shareholders, but only one director ordinarily resides in Australia | The majority-residence requirement is not met on these stipulated facts. |
| Company has 30 members; a change concerns a holder outside every relevant top 20 | The notice limitation must be assessed; the internal register still records that holder. |
| New share-capital company, with an optional name reservation actually used | Selected formation fee components: A$636 + A$65 = **A$701**. This excludes other costs and future review fees. |
| Ordinary proprietary review fee A$342 with applicable A$102 late-payment fee, no other charge stipulated | Selected components total **A$444**. Confirm the actual period/category and other liabilities first. |
| Annual extract issued after the review date | Keep the 28-day response clock tied to issue; keep solvency/payment clocks tied to review. |
| A shareholder directs a small company's report, with no CSF shareholders | Check section 319(2)'s exception and the direction; member reporting does not by itself create ordinary ASIC lodgement. |

Check territory and entity type; transferred/new status; actual officers,
consents and director-ID source branch; internal versus ASIC registers;
all size limbs with consolidation/FTE; report/audit/delivery/filing
conditions; financial versus review years; and each fee/deadline trigger.
Hold missing mandatory facts or source conditions before producing any
engagement outputs.
