# Norfolk Island: land fees and business transaction levies

Identify the transfer, parties, asset or rights, title, consideration,
unencumbered value, execution and ownership-change dates, location and
claimed relief. This reference supplies selected statutory parameters,
not conveyancing advice, an actual assessment or a complete property pack.

Load `workflow-base` and `nf-tax-system`, plus the applicable domain bases
for any wider engagement. Retain the confirmed profile, source and period
gates, refusals, four universal outputs and applicable domain outputs and
checks. Required facts remain Needs Input and unresolved law remains held.
Do not register a transfer, submit private ownership or identity records,
or make a payment on the strength of this reference.

## Classify the charge before choosing a percentage

The **Land Administration Fees Act 1996** charges for registration of
instruments evidencing a transfer of fee simple, a transfer of a Crown
lease, or a change/purported change in shareholding referred to in Land
Titles Act section 55C. The company branch has a separate unresolved scope
gate below. The **Business Transactions (Levy Imposition) Act 2006** charges
specified instruments under its Schedule 1, read with the Administration
Act. Its section 6(2) excludes levy on the part of consideration or value
to which the Land Administration Fees Act applies. Do not apply both
ad valorem charges to the same part. [LAND, sections 4 and 6][LAND];
[LEVY, sections 3, 4 and 6][LEVY]

The business levy is not confined to land or the sale of a whole business.
Administration Act section 3 includes goodwill of a Norfolk-connected
business, specified business-name/IP/information rights, licences, options,
unquoted marketable securities and conditional composite chattels.
Conveyance includes agreements, assignments, settlements, partnership
acquisitions, trust declarations and statements under section 30; the
definition excludes the grant of a lease or patent. These are classification
rules, not a conclusion that every item in a sale has the same treatment.
Confirm the full definitions and exemptions for the actual property.
[ADMIN, section 3][ADMIN]

A mortgage, trustee liability or partnership debt does not simply reduce
the business levy base: section 3 defines unencumbered value without those
encumbrances or liabilities. Sections 4 and 5 address value-reduction
arrangements and levy avoidance. Obtain evidence rather than using a
nominal price or an artificial split to select relief. [ADMIN]

## Land registration fee and valuation

For a transaction within section 4(1), section 4(2) sets the fee at the
**greater of 33 fee units or 4% of the value of the property interest or
shares transferred**, subject to exemptions. [LAND]

Interpretation Act section 12B and the current Continued Laws Ordinance's
replacement of that section specify **A$28.40 per fee unit**, with two
rounding branches:

- Fewer than **2 units**: multiply by A$28.40 and round to the nearest whole
  dollar, rounding 50 cents up.
- At least **2 units**: multiply by A$28.40 and round to the nearest whole
  multiple of A$5, rounding odd multiples of A$2.50 up.

Thus 33 units converts from A$937.20 to **A$935**. Half a unit converts from
A$14.20 to **A$14**; two units converts from A$56.80 to **A$55**. These are
derived statutory conversions as checked in October 2026, not a general
inflation index or the amount of every council charge. Do not apply the
fee-unit rounding rule to the separate 4% monetary branch, or import the
business levy's 5-cent floor into the land fee without authority.
[INTERP, sections 5, 7, 12 and 12B][INTERP];
[CONTINUED, Schedule 1 item 167][CONTINUED]

For a transferred **property interest**, section 4(2A) uses these separate
valuation branches:

| Statutory facts | Value used |
|---|---|
| Sale between related persons for bona fide consideration below unencumbered market value | That consideration. |
| Other sale where it appears to the Registrar that consideration is not below unencumbered market value | That consideration. |
| Any other case | Unencumbered market value. |

Here property interest means fee simple or a Crown lease; related persons
means a person and the person's **spouse, parent or grandparent**. Preserve
the bona fide sale and Registrar conditions. Do not replace these branches
with the business levy's universal higher-of test or assume every relative
falls within this definition. A gift generally reaches the other-case
market-value branch unless a separate exemption applies. [LAND, section 4]
[LAND]

The Registrar can require information, the contract and a competent
valuer's opinion; determine value; or obtain a valuation if evidence is
absent or insufficient. Some or all valuation expenses can be charged to
the liable person. Section 4(6B) permits disregard of value-reducing
interests, agreements or arrangements made on or after **15 July 1998**
unless the liable person satisfies the Registrar of a purpose other than
reducing property value. The apparent price is not conclusive. [LAND]

## Land fee exemptions and other registration fees

Section 6 removes the section 4 fee for specified transfers to the
Administration, qualifying charitable/community organisations or their
trustees, qualifying share transfers, Part 8 title registration and
prescribed transactions. The section 3 organisation definitions matter;
a label such as community business does not itself qualify. [LAND]

Land Administration Fees Regulations regulation 3A prescribes, with its
conditions:

- Estate transfers by or to a personal representative or estate trustee
  under a will/intestacy, including the specified in-specie alternative,
  **excluding transfers pursuant to a sale**.
- Transfers for the purposes of, or consequential on, the specified
  registered maintenance agreement or order under **Part VIII** of the
  Commonwealth Family Law Act.
- Trustee retirement/appointment transfers where the Registrar is satisfied
  that the transfer is not part of the specified scheme conferring a
  benefit to the detriment of another beneficial/potential interest.

Do not expand these into every estate sale, separation agreement, de facto
property settlement or trustee change. Establish the operative instrument,
beneficial interests and current family-law connection before selecting
an actual exemption. [LANDREG, regulation 3A][LANDREG]

Section 7 separately lets a person apply in the approved form to the Chief
Executive Officer for full/partial relief where no consideration passed
and the officer considers charging unjust. A gift is **not automatically
exempt**. Section 5 separately permits full/partial exemption for qualifying
share changes that do not substantially affect the nature of company
ownership. Record the actual decision; neither is a self-elected relief.
[LAND]

Section 6B provides approved registration fees for instruments other than
those in section 4(1) and section 6(a)–(e). Do not automatically add a flat
registration fee to every section 4 transfer. The note routes approved fees
through Part 10 of Chapter 15 of the applied Local Government Act.
The current dollar amount, classification, adoption and amendments require
their own evidence. A prescribed section 6(f) transaction is not separately
named in the section 6B exclusions: check whether its instrument falls
within the other exclusions before deciding any approved fee. [LAND];
[CONTINUED, Schedule 1 items 190H–190RB][CONTINUED]

Section 8 permits review of a section 4(2) fee-calculation decision by the
local Administrative Review Tribunal. That is distinct from the federal
regulations' listed review of a **section 5** decision. Do not substitute
the section 5 discretionary share-fee exemption for a section 8 calculation
review. Establish the exact decision, review route and applicable period;
neither a common tribunal name nor the business levy objection clock
settles those questions. [LAND]; [FEDREG, section 27 item 18][FEDREG]

## Company landholding and share-transfer hold

Land Titles Act sections 55A–55F have special company ownership,
registration and beneficial-interest rules. Section 55A's property-owning
company definition and section 55B refer to incorporation/foreign
registration under the **Companies Act 1985**; section 55B(3) also provides
for a ministerial exemption instrument. Section 55C specifies **14 days
after a qualifying shareholding change**, or a period allowed by the
Registrar, for an instrument and prescribed fee. It includes voidness and
extension/cure consequences. This is not an ordinary ASIC notice rule.
[TITLES]

The **2 August 2021** corporate transition and current Corporations Act
section 1678A(6) affect references to companies registered under a Territory
or Norfolk law. This reference has not resolved those provisions, any
applicable exemption/saving instruments and sections 55A–55C for a current
company. Keep actual company land acquisition, share-change liability and
filing instructions held until that interaction is verified; do not apply
the old definition to every ASIC company or infer a blanket exemption.
Use `nf-company-administration` for the separate corporate framework.
[CORPORATIONS, Part 10.51][CORPORATIONS];
[REFORM, commencement table item 4][REFORM]

Where the land-fee share branch is legally established, section 4(3) gives
the value as **ST / T × L**: shares transferred divided by issued shares,
multiplied by unencumbered market value of the company's **fee-simple**
property interest at transfer. That formula does not authorise substituting
a Crown-lease value for L or establish that an actual company is within the
branch. No actual company share-fee example is selected here. [LAND]

## Business levy: amount, rounding and relief

Schedule 1's ordinary conveyance branch, excluding Land Administration
Fees Act property and its specific branches, uses **4% of the higher of
consideration and unencumbered value**. The retained-joint-tenant branch
uses that rate. The separate subsequent conveyance conforming to an
already ad valorem stamped agreement branch specifies **0.5 fee unit**;
the agreement, conveyance and conformity conditions must be established.
It is not another 4% assessment on the same already-charged agreement.
[LEVY, Schedule 1][LEVY]; [ADMIN, section 22][ADMIN]

Section 7 reduces a levy amount that is not a multiple of **5 cents** to
the next lower multiple. This is a floor, not rounding to the nearest
5 cents. Section 8 allows the Officer to waive a levy/additional penalty
**below A$5**. The waiver is discretionary; exactly A$5 is not below A$5.
[LEVY]

Schedule 2 contains specific government, statutory vesting, winding-up,
trustee, beneficial-interest, spouse-home, bankruptcy, licence and other
reliefs. Each has conditions. For example, item 7 requires the Officer to
be satisfied of the spouses' or de facto partners' principal residence,
a transfer from one to both with equal ownership, no consideration and
no other person obtaining an interest or benefit. Neither marriage,
unchanged beneficial ownership
nor a charitable description alone selects a general nil levy. Schedule 2
item 12's temporary no-beneficial-change relief ended **30 June 2017**;
it cannot support a 2026 transfer. [LEVY];
[CONTINUED, Schedule 1 item 28A][CONTINUED]

Section 10's matrimonial refund has its own paid-levy, court-order and
**12-month** conditions and excludes property subject to the land fee.
Do not substitute that refund for the land regulations' exemption, or
apply it before checking the actual current family-law instrument.
[LEVY]

## Business levy lodgement, payment and objections

Unless another express provision applies, Administration Act section 10(2)
requires the liable person to lodge a chargeable instrument for assessment
within **60 days after first execution**, wherever signed and regardless
of when received in Norfolk. First execution generally means first
signature, with the written acceptance rule for an offer. Completion,
receipt and all-party signature dates are not interchangeable. Sections
12 and 16 preserve offshore instruments and early lodgement rules.
[ADMIN, sections 3, 10, 12 and 16][ADMIN]

For an instrument required to be assessed, section 10(1)(b) generally makes
payment due at the end of its lodgement period, or on a later assessment
notice date if specified. A return has its own due-furnishing date; another
instrument is payable when required to be stamped. An Officer's actual
extension or instalment decision can change payment timing. Default on an
instalment makes the outstanding levy payable; do not invent an automatic
extension from waiting for assessment. [ADMIN, section 10][ADMIN]

Part 3 Division 3 addresses specified transactions not effected or evidenced
by a chargeable instrument. Section 30 requires the liable party's approved
statement within **60 days after the change in beneficial ownership or
acquisition/creation event**, with the higher-of value base and payment on
lodgement. Its conditions, section 29 exceptions and subsequent-instrument
interaction must be checked. Section 33's **six-month** offence threshold
does not extend section 30's 60-day filing period. Some internal references
in this Division require legal resolution; do not use them to design an
alternative filing or avoidance route. [ADMIN]

Sections 20–22 address distinct matters, connected instruments, foreign
currency conversion at the instrument date and stamped counterparts.
Sections 13–14 address Norfolk apportionment; partnership acquisitions have
their own Division 2. This guide does not compute a mixed-property,
partnership, franchise, company-share or multi-jurisdiction transaction.
An unwritten arrangement, offshore signing or debt assumption is not an
automatic exemption. [ADMIN]

The Officer can request evidence/valuations and make default or amended
assessments. Unstamped instruments face registration/evidentiary constraints;
late filing, late payment and false statements can have separate penalties.
The text contains internal cross-reference issues, including sections 31,
46 and 57. Preserve the source wording and obtain a resolved legal basis
before any affected calculation. This guide supplies no penalty calculator
or prescribed refund-interest rate. [ADMIN, sections 11, 31, 36–47,
54–58 and 69–72][ADMIN]

For an assessment objection under section 50, lodge/post written detailed
grounds to the Officer within **30 days after being informed of the
assessment**. A reasonable-excuse extension is discretionary. Section 51
allows an objector dissatisfied with the decision to appeal to the local
Administrative Review Tribunal within **30 days after service of notice
of that decision**, or further time allowed by the Officer. Appeal grounds
are limited to the objection grounds and the objector bears the burden of
showing an excessive assessment. Under section 52, a pending appeal does
**not** suspend liability or recovery; later adjustment is separate.
Confirm current procedure, service and any specific extension. [ADMIN]

## Local and federal tribunal routes

The local **Administrative Review Tribunal Act 1996 (NI)** remains in
force. Its sections 14–16 govern reviewable decisions and affected
interests; section 14(7) permits another enactment to add to, exclude or
modify its procedure. Section 22 contains application/time rules, including
written reasons, deemed decisions, Gazette notices and extensions. Do not
replace the specific Administration Act section 51 route above with a
universal 28-day local rule. [LOCALART]

There is also a **Commonwealth** Administrative Review Tribunal under the
2024 Act. Section 297(2) authorises regulations for Norfolk enactment
decisions. Current Regulations section 27 lists, among other decisions:

| Item | Decision covered by the federal list |
|---|---|
| 10 | A decision under the Business Transactions (Administration) Act 2006. |
| 11 | A decision under subsection 9(2) of the Business Transactions (Levy Imposition) Act 2006. |
| 18 | A decision under section 5 of the Land Administration Fees Act 1996. |
| 19 | The specified Land Titles Act decisions in paragraph 138(1)(a), (b) or (c), or paragraph 146(2)(f). |
| 20 | A decision under subsection 79(1) of the Land Valuation Act 2012. |

The federal list is not a statement that every Norfolk decision, or every
land-fee provision, has the same review route. Regulations section 28
modifies references to instruments for listed Norfolk decisions; it does
not say that the local tribunal has been abolished or that every reference
to it becomes a reference to the federal tribunal. Preserve the decision
and power, decision-maker, standing and any internal-review/specific
procedure requirements. [FEDACT, sections 12, 17 and 297][FEDACT]; [FEDREG]

For the federal route, section 18 refers to the period prescribed by the
Rules; its note recognises that the decision's legislation may specify a
different period. Rule 5's general written-notice branch ends **28 days
after the applicant is given notice**, with separate statements-of-reasons,
refusal and inadequate-reasons branches. Rule 6 separately addresses deemed
decisions and a subsequent written decision. Section 19 permits the
Tribunal to extend a period in the stated circumstances, including after
expiry; an extension is not automatic. Do not use the decision date,
notice date, reasons date or section 51's service trigger interchangeably.
These are selected rules, not a choice of the applicable clock for an
actual application. [FEDACT]; [FEDRULES, sections 5 and 6][FEDRULES]

An application itself does not stay operation or implementation under
federal section 32 or local section 33. An actual stay order has its own
conditions and duration. Record the precise review route, legal basis,
trigger and last day, service/reasons evidence and any actual extension or
stay. The interaction of routes, actual forum/period, fees and complete
procedure remain held for the particular case; do not let a longer period
on one route stand in for another route's deadline. [FEDACT]; [LOCALART]

## Fictional computation and boundary checks

These October 2026 examples stipulate classification, sufficient valuation
evidence, no other chargeable component and no relief unless stated. They
are not quotes for an actual transaction. Land examples apply sections
4(2)/(2A); levy examples apply the ordinary Schedule 1 branch and section
7. Fee-unit conversion uses section 12B. [LAND]; [LEVY]; [INTERP]

| Stipulated facts | Computation or result |
|---|---|
| Land-fee value A$20,000 | 4% gives A$800; the A$935 minimum governs. |
| Land-fee value A$23,375 | 4% gives A$935, equal to the minimum. |
| Land-fee value A$25,000 | 4% gives A$1,000, exceeding the minimum. |
| Bona fide sale to a parent for A$80,000, market value A$125,000; section 4(2A)(a) stipulated satisfied | Base A$80,000; fee A$3,200. Do not automatically use the higher value. |
| Unrelated sale for A$80,000, market value A$125,000; other-case branch established | Base A$125,000; fee A$5,000. |
| Gift with market value A$50,000 and no exemption granted/applicable | Base A$50,000; fee A$2,000. No consideration is not nil value. |
| Ordinary leviable goodwill: consideration A$120,000, unencumbered value A$123,456 | Base A$123,456; raw levy A$4,938.24; section 7 reduces it to A$4,938.20. |
| Ordinary levy base A$125,000 | Levy A$5,000 is already a multiple of 5 cents; no reduction. |
| Ordinary levy base A$100 | Levy A$4; record payable unless the Officer actually waives it. |
| Ordinary levy base A$125 | Levy A$5; section 8's below-A$5 condition is not met. |
| Subsequent conveyance meets the stamped-agreement 0.5-unit branch | Derived levy A$14, subject to all branch conditions. |
| 2026 transfer has no beneficial-ownership change but no established relief | Do not use the Schedule 2 item 12 relief that ended in 2017. |
| No instrument, and six months have not elapsed | Test section 30's 60-day event-based statement duty now; do not wait for the offence threshold. |
| An actual ASIC company's shares change while it holds Norfolk land | Hold company/title-law interaction; do not issue a blanket 14-day instruction or nil-fee result. |
