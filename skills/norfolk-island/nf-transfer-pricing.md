## Income-tax geography and the substantive test

For the relevant income-tax use, ITAA 1997 section 960-505 expressly
includes **Norfolk Island** in geographical Australia. The former Norfolk
income-tax exemption was repealed under the 2015 reforms; use nf-tax-system
and nf-foreign-income for the separate residence, historical and income
conditions. NF is a catalogue identifier, not evidence that a dealing is
international for income tax. GST territory and a treaty's geographical
scope require their own rules. [ITAA10, 960-505][ITAA10]; [REFORM]

Subdivision 815-B requires actual conditions differing from arm's length
conditions, the section 815-120 cross-border test and the specified benefit
in taxable income, loss, offset or interest/royalty withholding. Its
cross-border table distinguishes Australian residents, resident trusts,
partnerships with only resident partners and other entities by the relevant
PE conditions, with special international tax-sharing and treaty
residence rules. Apply the whole test. A foreign counterparty, group
relationship or transaction value alone is insufficient, and an NF to
mainland address difference alone is not a cross-border test. [ITAA9,
815-105 to 815-120][ITAA9]

Select the most appropriate and reliable method or combination under
815-125 from actual functions, assets, risks, contracts, the property or
service, economic circumstances and strategies. Apply 815-130's actual
relations, substance and conditional reconstruction rules. A business
label cannot supply a margin or a method. Section 815-135 identifies the
OECD guidance last amended **20 January 2022** and any prescribed documents;
check the applicable regulations and period. [ITAA9]

CUP, resale price, cost plus, TNMM and profit split require evidence for
the actual transaction. Traditional methods over profit methods, or CUP
over another method, have the OECD preference only where equally reliable.
Missing comparable data is not a reason to choose TNMM automatically.
Select a tested party by reliable application and comparables, usually
rather than invariably the less complex party. [OECD, 2.2-2.5, 3.18-3.19][OECD]

Subdivision 815-C treats PE attribution separately from dealings between
entities. It allocates actual income/expenditure under its statutory
separate-entity conditions, excludes the specified foreign-bank branch
case and prioritises the pre-**22 July 2010** Article 7/commentary in
815-235. Subdivision 815-D modifies trust and partnership application.
Thin capitalisation interacts under 815-140; CFC attribution, debt deduction
creation, anti-avoidance, treaties, customs and indirect taxes retain their
own source and fact gates. A TP result alone does not establish a deduction
or every other tax. [ITAA9, 815-140, 815-205 to 815-310][ITAA9]

## Evidence, benchmarking and currency

Document each dealing, actual conduct, financial reconciliation, functional
analysis, method alternatives, search date/source, independence/geographic
filters, rejections and reliable adjustments. Obtain actual comparable
data. Foreign comparables are not automatically unsuitable; their economic
and other comparability must be evaluated. A database name does not prove
acceptability. Neither an industry label nor CbC aggregates supply an
arm's length benchmark. [ITAA9, 815-125][ITAA9]; [OECD, 3.34-3.38, 5.25][OECD]

A supported point or full reliable range can be appropriate. IQR narrowing
is conditional on residual comparability defects and enough observations;
a median is not automatic. Review documentation annually. OECD permission
for a tax administration to allow a **3-year** unchanged-conditions search
cycle, with annual financial updates, is not an automatic Australian
entitlement. Hold missing search or comparability evidence. [OECD,
3.55-3.62, 5.37-5.38][OECD]

For selected ordinary tax amounts, use AUD and translate underlying
amounts before netting, subject to the applicable exceptions. ITAA 1997
960-50 table item 6 selects receipt where ordinary income is received at
or before derivation, otherwise derivation. Item 8 selects payment where
a non-Division-40 deduction is paid at or before becoming deductible,
otherwise when deductible. Apply 960-55's commencement/transitional rules.
Functional currency, other table items, forex gains and form-specific
translation need their own rules. [ITAA10]

The ATO FX overview directs daily rates to the RBA, supplies monthly/annual
data and permits a reasonable external rate for an unlisted currency.
Retain the actual date, source, currency pair, quote direction and legally
selected basis. Verify conditions before averaging; year-end rates are
not universal defaults. A hypothetical rate below is not current rate
verification. [FX]

## Keep documentation and filing obligations separate

General business and other record duties remain; use nf-tax-records for
selected retention rules. TAA Schedule 1 **284-250 and 284-255** do not
create a universal mandatory TP-document filing requirement. Their special
RAP treatment requires records prepared **before actual return lodgement**,
in English or readily accessible and convertible into English, explaining
815-B/C treatment and guidance consistency and making the required
methods/conditions/consequences readily ascertainable. Records are
necessary but do not establish RAP by themselves. [TAA2]

CBC status differs from SGE status. A non-individual CBC parent's annual
global income must be at least **AUD 1 billion**, with the statutory control,
accounting-group and exception conditions. Section 815-355 uses status in
a period overlapping the **previous income year**, a specified current-year
residence/trust/partnership/PE connection and exemption/regulation gates.
Foreign parent filing alone cannot clear a Norfolk entity's obligations.
[ITAA9, 815-355, 815-370 to 815-380][ITAA9]; [ITAA10, 960-555 to 960-575][ITAA10]

Where required, Master File, Australian Local File and CbC statements are
due within **12 months after the relevant reporting period**. Written
replacement-period, entity/statement exemption, class exemption and
deferral rules are distinct. Establish the current approved schema, short
form eligibility, actual reporting entity, filing route and any permitted
exchange arrangement. An internal workflow Local File is not evidence
of a statutory filing. [ITAA9, 815-355 to 815-365][ITAA9]

The **2026 IDS**, concerning 2025-26, forms part of the relevant entity's
return. The instructions cover companies, trusts, partnerships, AMITs and
attribution CCIV sub-funds. Entity-specific triggers include overseas
interest/royalty amounts, specified foreign interests or branches,
financing-rule questions and related-party transactions exceeding
**AUD 2 million**, including property and loan balances. Do not replace
the whole list with that one amount. Own branches are not separate
international related parties for this schedule. [IDS-GET]; [IDS-DEFS]

Section A question 1b's specified-question relief depends on lodging Part A
of the Local File **at the same time as the return**. Question 2b requires
a small business entity that is not an SGE, dealings **no more than
AUD 5 million** and **no more than 50% of aggregated turnover**. These
conditions do not establish a blanket IDS exemption, no substantive TP
liability or PCG eligibility. Use current instructions for every actual
field and the actual return due date. Public CBC and RTP schedules are
separate. [IDS-A, questions 1-2][IDS-A]; [IDS-NEW]

## Compliance options, adjustments and penalties

PCG 2017/2 concerns ATO compliance resources. Eligibility needs all the
chosen option's conditions, contemporaneous evidence, self-assessed
815-B/C/D compliance and required disclosure. It does not itself satisfy
284-E or create a statutory safe harbour. For example, small-taxpayer
turnover **below AUD 50 million** alone is insufficient; loss/restructure,
royalty/R&D, service, distributor and transaction exclusions also matter.
Use the full relevant PCG option. [PCG, 1-23, 76-103][PCG]

Low-value service conditions include qualifying activities, aggregate
charges of **no more than AUD 2 million**, or, above that, the separate
**15%** group expense/revenue tests; expenses **no more than 25%** of
pre-service profit; received mark-up **no more than 5%** and provided
mark-up **at least 5%**; and the other loss/restructure/compliance
conditions. Relevant direct and indirect costs exclude pass-through
costs from the mark-up base. These are cumulative conditions, not an
unconditional OECD 5% price. Other PCG options and annual loan rates need
their own selected rules. [PCG, 24-43, 76-103][PCG]; [OECD, 7.43-7.65][OECD]

A consequential adjustment under 815-145 is a separate decision.
Sections 815-150 and 815-240 have special **7-year** assessment-amendment
provisions and a consequential-adjustment exception. Use those with
nf-tax-amendments-objections; an ordinary clock does not override them.
[ITAA9]

For the selected TP penalty, establish the **284-145(2B)** Commissioner
amendment/withholding-notice gateway and additional tax. The 284-150(4)
scheme shortfall is additional income/withholding tax, not the price or
profit adjustment. Under 284-165, compare that shortfall with the taxpayer's
reasonably arguable threshold. The ordinary threshold is the greater of
**AUD 10,000 and 1% of return-basis tax payable**; the trust/partnership
threshold is the greater of **AUD 20,000 and 2% of return-basis net income**.
Apply separate statutory branches and the additional trust/partnership
net-income/loss-adjustment exceptions, including equality and nil rules.
[TAA2, 284-90(3), 284-145 to 284-165][TAA2]

Once liability remains, 284-160(3) applies **50% non-RAP / 25% RAP** to the
relevant portions where the sole/dominant TP-benefit purpose branch
applies, otherwise **25% non-RAP / 10% RAP**. Split reasonably arguable
matters rather than applying one rate to every matter. Apply 284-224,
conduct/disclosure adjustments under 284-220/225 and the conditional
284-155(3) SGE doubling separately. Remission is a separate discretionary
298-20 decision. Use nf-tax-penalties-remission and nf-tax-interest for
selected other penalties/interest; verify every applicable source and fact
before a calculation. RAP, a report or an NF address cannot guarantee nil.
[TAA2]

## APA availability and remaining country requirements

The ATO programme and PS LA 2015/4, revised **29 October 2025**, provide
unilateral, bilateral and multilateral APAs. The usual **3-5 years** is the
arrangement term. The process comprises early engagement, invited formal
application and compliance monitoring, with agreed plans, critical
assumptions and annual reporting. This selected evidence does not establish
a universal fee or processing deadline. Actual treaty access, application,
rollback, renewal and cancellation require their complete procedure and
facts. [APA-PS, 1A-3C, 9A-10E][APA-PS]; [APA]

## Fictional positions and self-checks

All amounts are invented AUD except the stated foreign-currency example.
These cases test branch selection and arithmetic, not real comparables or
facts, and do not establish an actual taxpayer's liability.

| Stipulated position | Working and bounded conclusion |
|---|---|
| Two Australian resident companies, one in Norfolk and one on the mainland, have wholly Australian dealings with no overseas PE or other cross-border condition | Both locations are geographically Australia for the selected income-tax use. Different addresses alone do not satisfy 815-120. Establish every actual condition; do not label the dealing international merely from NF. |
| Norfolk company has AUD 1.8 million international related-party dealings and an overseas-interest IDS trigger in its 2026 company return | 1.8 million is below 2 million, but the separate interest trigger still requires the IDS. The amount supplies no substantive TP exemption. |
| Prior-year CBC status and exemptions unknown; parent current revenue AUD 1.2 billion | 1.2 billion exceeds 1 billion, but does not establish the statutory prior-year group/status and exemption conditions. Hold each statement conclusion. |
| Ordinary company return-basis tax AUD 600,000, TP scheme tax shortfall AUD 10,000 | max(10,000, 1% × 600,000) = **10,000**. Equality meets 284-165(1); no penalty under 284-145(2B), without cancelling tax or interest. |
| Same company, AUD 100,000 tax shortfall; liability gateway met, AUD 60,000 non-RAP/AUD 40,000 RAP, no purpose branch, no other adjustments or doubling stipulated | 25% × 60,000 + 10% × 40,000 = **19,000** BPA. Do not use 10% for the entire shortfall or assume remission. |
| Qualifying service cost AUD 100,000 plus AUD 20,000 pass-through, 5% provided mark-up and every other PCG condition stipulated | 5% × 100,000 = **5,000** mark-up; total charge = **125,000**. The arithmetic does not prove an unstipulated eligibility or deduction condition. |
| Ordinary income FC 1,000 received before derivation; applicable receipt quote FC 2 per AUD 1 stipulated | Receipt is selected under item 6; 1,000 ÷ 2 = **AUD 500**. The invented quote is not a real observation or permission to use an annual average. |
| Comparable data absent for a Norfolk distributor | Hold the method-dependent result and list the missing data. No automatic TNMM, IQR, median or 3% margin fills the gap. |

Check income-tax territory separately from treaty/GST geography; all
815-120 conditions; actual relations and method reliability; every data
and conversion gate; independent documentation/IDS/CBC duties; every PCG
condition; tax versus profit shortfall; separate trust/partnership
exceptions; matter-level RAP and adjustments; and consistency across all
outputs. All 10 TP completion checks remain required.
