# Norfolk Island (NF): tax pack

**Built from the law. Not yet approved. Written by Ryan Duguid; imported and conformed by Claude. Not reviewed by an accountant.** Norfolk Island is an Australian external territory. Since 1 July 2016 Australian federal tax law (income tax, Medicare levy, PAYG withholding, FBT, super guarantee) applies there, administered by the ATO, with Norfolk modifications: the zone tax offset, a phased super guarantee rate (11% for 2026-27), a pre-23 October 2015 CGT cutoff, and GST only on supplies connected with the mainland. Local charges (Council rates, land registration fees, the business transaction levy) are set by Norfolk law and the Norfolk Island Regional Council. Currency is the Australian dollar. The income year runs 1 July 2026 to 30 June 2027; the FBT year 1 April 2026 to 31 March 2027.

For the mainland figures these skills share, the Australian pack is in [`skills/australia/`](../australia/), starting with [`au-rates-2026-27`](../australia/au-rates-2026-27.md).
