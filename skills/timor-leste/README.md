# Timor-Leste: tax pack

Tax year 2026. Amounts are computed in USD. Ordinary annual income tax, monthly wage withholding, designated-services tax, import sales tax and social contributions have separate bases and procedures. Future VAT planning is not an enacted ordinary tax; petroleum/treaty and investment-incentive regimes require separate analysis. These skills remain tier 2 and are not accountant-approved.
