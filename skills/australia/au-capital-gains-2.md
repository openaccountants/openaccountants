# Australian capital gains tax

## Scope

CGT includes a net capital gain in assessable income; it is not a separate flat tax. This Guide covers ordinary Australian resident investment disposals before 1 July 2027. [ITAA 1997, s 102-5](https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2004A05138/2026-06-27/2026-06-27/text/original/pdf/3).

**Later transactions:** the Treasury Laws Amendment (Tax Reform No. 1) Act 2026 changes CGT arrangements from 1 July 2027, with transitional rules for existing holdings. Its Schedule 1 item 82 applies the amended Division 102/152 method to assessments for the income year including 1 July 2027 and later years. Do not use the pre-2027 calculation below for those later assessments or deemed-sale transitions. [Schedule 1, including items 12–16 and 82](https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2026A00049/asmade/2026-06-26/text/1/epub/OEBPS/document_1/document_1.html).

## Ask the client first

Record legal and beneficial ownership, residence history, asset/use, acquisition history, disposal contract/settlement, proceeds, invoices, deductions available or claimed, recoupments, capital works, capital losses and proposed concessions. For a home, obtain occupation, absences, rental/business use and land area. For business relief, obtain group turnover, connected/affiliate net assets and active-use history. These are required workpaper inputs, not assumed eligibility.

## The method, step by step

### Identify the asset and CGT event

- **Disposal:** A1 requires a change of ownership; ceasing legal ownership while retaining beneficial ownership is not a disposal. For an ordinary sale the event date is the disposal contract date, not settlement; without a contract it is the ownership-change date. A contract that falls through without completion does not itself produce A1. [Section 104-10](https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2004A05138/2026-10-01/2026-10-01/text/original/epub/OEBPS/document_3/document_3.html).
- **Other events:** gifts, cancellations, trusts, options and cessation of residency need their own event analysis; a gift can still be A1. Where event-specific treatment is unestablished, hold the outcome. Check this point with one of our partner accountants.
- **Capital versus revenue:** establish commercial purpose and activities; a business or profit-making undertaking can produce ordinary income. Holding for more than a year does not settle classification. Apply anti-overlap rules where CGT also applies; s 118-20 has exceptions, including specified buy-back dividends and franked distributions. [ATO property guidance](https://www.ato.gov.au/businesses-and-organisations/assets-and-property/property/tax-consequences-on-sales-of-property); [s 118-20](https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2004A05138/2026-10-01/2026-10-01/text/original/epub/OEBPS/document_3/document_3.html).
- **Pre-CGT:** A1 gains/losses on assets acquired before 20 September 1985 are generally disregarded. Check exceptions and separate later assets; pre-CGT shares/trust interests can involve K6. [Section 104-10(5), Subdivision 108-D](https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2004A05138/2026-10-01/2026-10-01/text/original/epub/OEBPS/document_3/document_3.html).
- **Main residence:** the basic exemption is for an individual's dwelling that was their main residence throughout their ownership period, excluding the deceased-estate route. Check income-producing use, absences, adjacent land and residency restrictions separately. Moving into an investment home later does not erase the earlier taxable period. Apply ss 118-110, 118-120, 118-145, 118-185 and 118-190 before calculating an exemption. [Subdivision 118-B](https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2004A05138/2026-10-01/2026-10-01/text/original/epub/OEBPS/document_3/document_3.html).

### Establish proceeds and cost base

Proceeds include money and market-value property received **or entitled to be received**, valued at the event. Exclude net GST on a taxable supply. Gifts and non-arm's-length transactions may require market-value substitution; apply s 116-30 and its exceptions. Reconcile gross proceeds to the contract and count selling costs once. [Sections 116-20 and 116-30](https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2004A05138/2026-10-01/2026-10-01/text/original/epub/OEBPS/document_3/document_3.html).

The five cost-base elements are: (1) acquisition money and market-value property given/required; (2) eligible incidental acquisition/event costs; (3) eligible ownership costs only for assets acquired after 20 August 1991; (4) qualifying capital expenditure to increase/preserve value or install/move the asset, excluding goodwill; and (5) capital expenditure to establish, preserve or defend title/rights. Ownership costs do not form the third element for collectables or personal-use assets. Professional tax advice qualifies as an incidental cost only if provided by a recognised tax adviser. [Sections 110-25, 110-35, 108-17 and 108-30](https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2004A05138/2026-10-01/2026-10-01/text/original/epub/OEBPS/document_3/document_3.html).

**Adjust the cost base before using it:**

- Deducted **or deductible** second/third-element expenditure is excluded under ss 110-40(2) and 110-45(1B), subject to the older-asset Division 243 exception in s 110-40(4). For assets acquired after 7.30 pm ACT time on 13 May 1997, other deductible expenditure is also excluded, subject to s 110-45(2)'s deduction-reversal, Division 243 and balancing-charge-relief exceptions. Section 110-45 also covers post-30 June 1999 fourth-element land/building expenditure on older assets. [Sections 110-40, 110-45](https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2004A05138/2026-10-01/2026-10-01/text/original/epub/OEBPS/document_3/document_3.html).
- Exclude recouped expenditure to the extent the recoupment is not assessable. Under s 110-45(4), reduce for deductible capital expenditure of a previous owner, subject to the stated reversal/relief exception; capital works may reduce cost base even if not claimed. Apply s 110-53's historical exceptions; partnership interests use ss 110-43/110-50. [Sections 110-40(3), 110-43, 110-45(3)–(4), 110-50 and 110-53](https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2004A05138/2026-10-01/2026-10-01/text/original/epub/OEBPS/document_3/document_3.html).
- Apply the remaining exclusions in s 110-38 and ss 110-45(5)–(6), 110-54, and reduce for net input tax credits. A denied deduction does not automatically become cost base. Hold any unresolved historical offset, complex capital-works or partnership adjustment out of the calculation. Check this point with one of our partner accountants. [Sections 110-25 note 2, 110-38–110-54](https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2004A05138/2026-10-01/2026-10-01/text/original/epub/OEBPS/document_3/document_3.html).

**Gain or loss:** A1 gives a gain when proceeds exceed adjusted cost base, or a loss when proceeds are below **reduced cost base**. Reduced cost base has no indexation; its third element concerns specified balancing adjustments, not general ownership costs. Apply its separate deductible/hypothetically deductible and recoupment exclusions. Proceeds between reduced cost base and cost base can produce neither gain nor loss. [Sections 104-10(4), 110-55](https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2004A05138/2026-10-01/2026-10-01/text/original/epub/OEBPS/document_3/document_3.html).

### Apply losses and concessions in order

1. Disregard exempt gains/losses first, including a gain qualifying for the small business 15-year exemption. Do not consume losses on an already disregarded gain.
2. Reduce taxable gains by current-year capital losses, then available unapplied earlier net capital losses, oldest first. Apply collectable losses only against collectable gains. A net capital loss cannot reduce salary or other ordinary assessable income. Check availability, including bankruptcy restrictions.
3. Apply the eligible discount to remaining discount gains, then any available Division 152 active-asset reduction, retirement exemption or rollover under that concession's own rules. Sum the remaining gains to obtain the net capital gain.

[Pre-2027 s 102-5, ss 102-10/102-15](https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2004A05138/2026-06-27/2026-06-27/text/original/pdf/3); [s 108-10 and Division 115](https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2004A05138/2026-10-01/2026-10-01/text/original/epub/OEBPS/document_3/document_3.html); [Division 152](https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2004A05138/2026-10-01/2026-10-01/text/original/epub/OEBPS/document_4/document_4.html); [application item 82](https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2026A00049/asmade/2026-06-26/text/1/epub/OEBPS/document_1/document_1.html).

**Discount:** for eligible events before 1 July 2027, the ordinary discount is 50% for individuals and trusts and 33⅓% for complying super funds. Ordinary companies cannot use it; the statute separately recognises life insurers' complying-super assets. Generally hold the asset at least 12 months; exclude acquisition and event days when counting. Check special acquisition rules and excluded events/gains. Residence on sale alone does not establish a full discount: foreign/temporary-resident periods after 8 May 2012 may restrict it. Affordable-housing cases need their specific rules; do not assume the ordinary percentage settles them. [Sections 115-5–115-50, 115-100–115-125](https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2004A05138/2026-10-01/2026-10-01/text/original/epub/OEBPS/document_3/document_3.html); [ATO discount guidance](https://www.ato.gov.au/individuals-and-families/investments-and-assets/capital-gains-tax/calculating-your-cgt/cgt-discount).

**Older assets:** qualifying acquisitions at or before 11.45 am ACT time on 21 September 1999 can permit indexation frozen at September 1999 instead of the discount; satisfy Division 114 and compare eligible methods. Do not combine indexation and discount or index a capital loss. [Sections 110-36, 110-55, 115-20](https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2004A05138/2026-10-01/2026-10-01/text/original/epub/OEBPS/document_3/document_3.html); [ATO indexation guidance](https://www.ato.gov.au/individuals-and-families/investments-and-assets/capital-gains-tax/calculating-your-cgt/indexing-the-cost-base).

**Trust distributions:** a trust's percentage does not establish its beneficiary's final taxable gain; Subdivision 115-C requires a separate beneficiary calculation. This Guide does not determine gross-up amounts or any beneficiary credits/offsets. Check this point with one of our partner accountants. [Subdivision 115-C](https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2004A05138/2026-10-01/2026-10-01/text/original/epub/OEBPS/document_3/document_3.html).

### Test small business concessions separately

Division 152 provides the 15-year exemption, 50% active asset reduction, retirement exemption and rollover. A threshold alone does not establish entitlement: meet the basic conditions, active-asset test and each concession's additional conditions. For this Guide's period, principal gateways are a CGT small business entity using the **below AUD 2 million** aggregated-turnover tests of s 328-110 (with the s 152-10 substitution), or net CGT assets **not exceeding AUD 6 million just before the event**. Carry on a business (or meet the statutory winding-up rule), and apply the previous-year, likely-current-year or actual-current-year turnover test. Assess a likely-current-year forecast at the start of the year or business commencement; that path is unavailable if both previous business years had aggregated turnover at or above AUD 2 million. Aggregate relevant connected entities/affiliates and apply intra-group exclusions. [Sections 152-10, 152-15–152-20](https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2004A05138/2026-10-01/2026-10-01/text/original/epub/OEBPS/document_4/document_4.html); [ss 328-110–328-115](https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2004A05138/2026-10-01/2026-10-01/text/original/epub/OEBPS/document_7/document_7.html); [application item 82](https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2026A00049/asmade/2026-06-26/text/1/epub/OEBPS/document_1/document_1.html).

Partnership/passively held assets have separate s 152-10 pathways; shares/trust interests and certain partnership rights have extra conditions. Do not treat an investment rental as automatically active: s 152-40 excludes assets mainly used to derive rent, subject to its exceptions. For assets owned **15 years or less**, active use must total at least half the statutory period; for **more than 15 years**, at least **7½ years**. The period begins at acquisition and ends at the earlier of the event or business cessation within the preceding 12 months (or longer allowed by the Commissioner). Apply s 152-40's definition/exclusions and any rollover continuity rules. [Sections 152-10, 152-35–152-45](https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2004A05138/2026-10-01/2026-10-01/text/original/epub/OEBPS/document_4/document_4.html).

## Worked cases

Illustrations in AUD for ordinary resident individuals, disposal contracts in June 2026, acquisitions in June 2023, no other adjustments, concessions or residency changes.

**A — losses before discount.** Proceeds 100,000; acquisition cost 60,000; eligible incidental costs 2,000; current-year capital losses 8,000; available earlier net losses 10,000. Gain = 38,000; after losses = 20,000; 50% discount leaves 10,000 assessable net capital gain. [Sections 104-10, 110-25, 115-100](https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2004A05138/2026-10-01/2026-10-01/text/original/epub/OEBPS/document_3/document_3.html); [pre-2027 s 102-5](https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2004A05138/2026-06-27/2026-06-27/text/original/pdf/3).

```oa-check
name: A - June 2026 losses before discount
inputs:
  proceeds: 100000
  acquisition: 60000
  incidentals: 2000
  current_losses: 8000
  earlier_losses: 10000
steps:
  - gain: proceeds - acquisition - incidentals
  - after_losses: gain - current_losses - earlier_losses
  - net_capital_gain: after_losses * 0.5
expected:
  gain: 38000
  after_losses: 20000
  net_capital_gain: 10000
```

**B — reduced cost base.** Proceeds 45,000; acquisition cost 50,000; eligible incidental costs 2,000; otherwise eligible non-deductible ownership costs 3,000. Ordinary cost base = 55,000, reduced cost base = 52,000, capital loss = 7,000. Ownership costs cannot inflate the capital loss to 10,000. No balancing adjustment, deduction entitlement, recoupment or GST credit affects these stipulated amounts. [Sections 104-10(4), 110-25, 110-55](https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2004A05138/2026-10-01/2026-10-01/text/original/epub/OEBPS/document_3/document_3.html).

```oa-check
name: B - June 2026 reduced cost base
inputs:
  proceeds: 45000
  acquisition: 50000
  incidentals: 2000
  ownership: 3000
steps:
  - cost_base: acquisition + incidentals + ownership
  - reduced_cost_base: acquisition + incidentals
  - capital_loss: reduced_cost_base - proceeds
expected:
  cost_base: 55000
  reduced_cost_base: 52000
  capital_loss: 7000
```

## When to refuse or refer

Do not issue an ordinary resident investment calculation for unestablished event treatment, foreign/temporary-resident or post-1 July 2027 outcomes. Trust gross-ups/credits and unresolved historical cost-base adjustments remain non-usable holds as specified above. Check this point with one of our partner accountants.

Retain a separate eligibility record for each concession, event dates, proceeds, adjusted bases, losses, valuations, discount and choice/rollover documentation with the return workpapers.
