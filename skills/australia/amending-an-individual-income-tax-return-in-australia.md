# Amending an individual income tax return in Australia

Correct an error by requesting an amendment to the assessment for the affected income year. Wait for notification that the original return or any previous amendment has been processed, establish the applicable time limit, and reconcile every proposed change to supporting records. This guide covers individuals and sole traders. The original source check was dated 8 September 2026; the procedural rules and source passages listed below were checked on 27 November 2026. Use the rules for the affected assessment and income year, not the year in which you discover the error.

## Establish the amendment period

Most individuals have a two-year amendment period. Sole traders generally had two years for 2023–24 and earlier income years. For 2024–25 and later years, sole traders can generally request amendments within four years. Record the income year and the date the ATO gave notice of the original assessment. The period starts on the day after notice of the original assessment is given; it does not run from the end of the income year or the day the return was lodged. [ATO: time limits on amendments](https://www.ato.gov.au/individuals-and-families/your-tax-return/amend-your-tax-return/time-limits-on-amendments).

The four-year extension for small and medium businesses is a taxpayer-requested amendment mechanism. Item 3A of subsection 170(1) requires small or medium business entity status for that income year, eligibility for the ordinary two-year period under item 1, 2 or 3, an application in the approved form before four years have ended, and expiry of that ordinary two-year period. The Commissioner may amend to give effect to the decision on that application. It applies to assessments issued after 1 January 2025 for income years starting on or after 1 July 2024. It does not give the ATO an unrestricted four-year review period for every sole trader. [ITAA 1936, s 170(1), item 3A](https://www.legislation.gov.au/C1936A00027/2026-10-01/2026-10-01/text/original/epub/OEBPS/document_3/document_3.html); [2024 amending Act, Schedule 3, item 6 and commencement table](https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2024A00135/asmade/2024-12-10/text/original/epub/OEBPS/document_1/document_1.html).

Some individuals fall outside the ordinary two-year category, including through business, partnership, trust or avoidance circumstances. Where item 1, 2 or 3 does not apply, item 4 ordinarily allows four years. Do not infer eligibility from residence or sole-trader status alone. Fraud or evasion and review or appeal decisions have separate rules. An amended assessment does not restart the amendment period for the whole return: sections 170(2)–(4) restrict further amendments and allow particular matters to be revisited in specified circumstances. For an assessment near its deadline, establish the statutory period before lodging. Section 170(5) permits a later amendment giving effect to a decision on an approved-form application made before the limited amendment period ended; private-ruling, court-order and written-consent extensions have separate conditions in subsections (6)–(7). For a disputed deadline, unusual exception or particular-specific refreshed period, do not use this general guide to decide the outcome. Check this point with one of our partner accountants. [ITAA 1936, section 170](https://www.legislation.gov.au/C1936A00027/2026-10-01/2026-10-01/text/original/epub/OEBPS/document_3/document_3.html).

## Prepare the correction

Keep the original return, original notice of assessment, every amendment and ATO decision, and the records supporting the correction. Prepare one reconciliation for each affected label:

| Item | Record |
| --- | --- |
| Existing amount | Amount in the latest processed assessment or return data |
| Correct amount | Recalculated total for the same income year |
| Difference | Increase or decrease, with the sign stated |
| Evidence | Invoice, statement, calculation or other supporting record |
| Explanation | What was wrong and why the replacement amount is correct |

Recalculate related items, such as taxable income, offsets, capital losses, Medicare levy and study loan repayment income where affected. Use the affected year's rates and the person's actual residence and other eligibility facts; this is a reconciliation instruction, not an entitlement to any deduction or offset. A deduction added to a return does not necessarily produce a refund equal to the deduction multiplied by one marginal rate. Other thresholds, offsets and liabilities can change.

Separate a favourable correction from a tax shortfall. An understated liability or overstated entitlement may create a shortfall; a correction that reduces tax is not automatically a shortfall. Do not invent missing expenses, dates or valuations. Mark incomplete items for resolution before submission.

## Lodge through an available channel

The ATO accepts individual amendment requests through ATO online services, a registered tax agent, the paper **Request for amendment of income tax return for individuals**, or a letter containing the required information. In myGov linked to the ATO, use **Manage tax returns**, then **Amend** beside the relevant income year. Enter the correct totals for the affected labels, rather than replacing a total with only the adjustment.

Retain the submitted request and acknowledgement. If you complete the paper form yourself, you must sign it. An agent uses the applicable tax-professional process and declaration requirements. Follow the ATO's current instructions for required identifiers and supporting documents, and provide them through the ATO or authorised agent's secure channel. The ATO does not charge a fee for requesting an amendment. Processing times are estimates, not a guarantee that a request will be completed before a deadline. [ATO: how to request an amendment](https://www.ato.gov.au/individuals-and-families/your-tax-return/amend-your-tax-return/how-to-request-an-amendment-to-your-tax-return).

If the amendment period has expired, consider an objection and, where necessary, an extension of time to object. An amendment request and an objection are different processes. Use the objection process where disputing an ATO decision requires it; do not assume another amendment request preserves review rights. [ATO: amendment time limits and objections](https://www.ato.gov.au/individuals-and-families/your-tax-return/amend-your-tax-return/time-limits-on-amendments). An out-of-time objection needs a written extension request explaining the delay, and acceptance is not automatic. The four-year self-amendment route does not itself extend the ordinary two-year objection period for item 3A assessments. [TAA 1953, ss 14ZW(1)(aa), (2)–(3) and 14ZX](https://www.legislation.gov.au/C1953A00001/2026-08-27/2026-08-27/text/original/epub/OEBPS/document_1/document_1.html).

## Check interest, penalties and the result

Additional income tax from an amended assessment can carry shortfall interest charge (SIC). This is separate from any statement penalty. Check the amended assessment and applicable interest provisions; this guide supplies no interest rate or interest deduction. [TAA 1953, Schedule 1, s 280-100](https://www.legislation.gov.au/C1953A00001/2026-08-27/2026-08-27/text/original/epub/OEBPS/document_2/document_2.html); [ATO: shortfall interest charge](https://www.ato.gov.au/individuals-and-families/paying-the-ato/interest-and-penalties/interest-we-charge/shortfall-interest-charge). A false or misleading statement that produces a shortfall may also attract a penalty. For a false or misleading statement with a shortfall attributable to the conduct, the ordinary base penalties are:

| Conduct by taxpayer or agent | Base penalty on the attributable shortfall or part | Official source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Failure to take reasonable care | 25% | [TAA 1953, Schedule 1, s 284-90(1), item 3](https://www.legislation.gov.au/C1953A00001/2026-08-27/2026-08-27/text/original/epub/OEBPS/document_2/document_2.html) |
| Recklessness | 50% | [TAA 1953, Schedule 1, s 284-90(1), item 2](https://www.legislation.gov.au/C1953A00001/2026-08-27/2026-08-27/text/original/epub/OEBPS/document_2/document_2.html) |
| Intentional disregard | 75% | [TAA 1953, Schedule 1, s 284-90(1), item 1](https://www.legislation.gov.au/C1953A00001/2026-08-27/2026-08-27/text/original/epub/OEBPS/document_2/document_2.html) |

 These are starting points, subject to the applicable provisions, increases, reductions and remission. An honest error is not proof of recklessness, and using an agent does not by itself establish reasonable care. Section 284-75(5) excludes this statement penalty if both taxpayer and agent (if relevant) took reasonable care. Under subsection (6), relief can apply where a registered agent made the statement, the taxpayer supplied all relevant taxation information, and the error did not result from the agent's recklessness or intentional disregard; the taxpayer bears the evidential burden for supplying that information. These exceptions do not exclude every other penalty regime. For significant-global-entity multipliers, statements without a shortfall, scheme penalties or a position that is not reasonably arguable, do not use the ordinary table to calculate a final penalty. Check this point with one of our partner accountants.

Voluntary disclosure can reduce penalties, but does not guarantee that tax, interest or penalties disappear. Disclose in the approved form and check the timing and content requirements under s 284-225; notification of an examination and any earlier public-disclosure deadline affect relief. Obtain legal advice where fraud, evasion or privilege is in issue. Do not infer a safe outcome from this guide. Check this point with one of our partner accountants. [ATO: false or misleading statement penalties](https://www.ato.gov.au/individuals-and-families/paying-the-ato/interest-and-penalties/penalties/penalties-for-making-false-or-misleading-statements).

When the amended assessment arrives, compare it with the submitted reconciliation. Check the tax difference, interest, penalties, credits, payment date and any effect on later returns. Retain the assessment and the evidence supporting each corrected figure.

## Worked reconciliation

Illustrative label arithmetic only: a supported corrected total of AUD 900 replaces an existing total of AUD 1,200. The signed change is AUD −300; enter AUD 900 in the label, not AUD −300. This example establishes no deduction entitlement, tax saving or refund, and does not substitute a new tax year for the affected year.

```oa-check
name: Correct total and signed label adjustment
inputs:
  existing_total: 1200
  correct_total: 900
steps:
  - adjustment: correct_total - existing_total
  - reconstructed_total: existing_total + adjustment
expected:
  adjustment: -300
  reconstructed_total: 900
```
