**SBITO eligibility and income.** The offset is for an individual carrying on an eligible small business or receiving qualifying income from an unincorporated small-business partnership/trust; it does not apply to the individual acting as trustee. The entity must carry on a business in the current year and meet the prior-year or current-year turnover route. Assess likely turnover at the first day of the year, or business commencement; that route is unavailable if both preceding business years were at or above $5m. The actual-current-year route uses year-end turnover. A qualifying winding-up business can retain status. Aggregate connected entities and affiliates, excluding the statutory intra-group dealings and non-connected periods. [ITAA 1997 ss 328-110, 328-115, 328-355 and 328-357](https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2004A05138/2026-10-01/2026-10-01/text/original/epub/OEBPS/document_7/document_7.html).

Use assessable business income less attributable deductions, excluding net capital gains and PSI not produced from a personal services business. Do not subtract tax-related expenses, gifts/contributions or personal super deductions in this calculation. Floor a loss at zero. Reduce qualifying partnership/trust amounts by the individual's relevant attributable deductions before including them in the numerator. The $1,000 offset cap applies across all eligible businesses. [ITAA 1997 ss 328-360 to 328-370](https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2004A05138/2026-10-01/2026-10-01/text/original/epub/OEBPS/document_7/document_7.html).

**Standard work-related deduction conditions.** Assessable labour income means the withholding categories in section 25-130(4): employee, director, office-holder and religious-practitioner payments, return-to-work, retirement/termination and parental leave pay. The top-up reduces for the specified labour-related general, car, workplace-travel, repair, depreciation/balancing and COVID-test deductions. In the general-deduction reduction, disregard income-protection, personal sickness and accident premiums and trade/business/professional association membership. [Tax Reform No. 1 Act 2026 Schedule 4, s 25-130 and item 17](https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2026A00049/asmade/2026-06-26/text/original/epub/OEBPS/document_1/document_1.html).

### HELP/study loans (2025-26 onwards: marginal system)

- **Repayment income definition.** Start with taxable income, excluding any assessable FHSS released amount. Add total net investment losses, reportable fringe benefits, exempt foreign income as defined in the Act and reportable super contributions.  _([Higher Education Support Act 2003, s 154-5](https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2004A01234/2026-10-17/2026-10-17/text/original/epub/OEBPS/document_1/document_1.html))_

Liability requires repayment income above the minimum and accumulated HELP debt on 1 June immediately before assessment. There is no liability under section 154-1 where section 8 of the Medicare Levy Act makes the person's levy nil or reduces it. For a liable person, repayment is the least of the marginal amount, 10% of repayment income and repayable debt. [HESA ss 154-1, 154-15 and 154-20](https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2004A01234/2026-10-17/2026-10-17/text/original/epub/OEBPS/document_1/document_1.html).

## Worked examples

For 2026-27, assume repayment income of $70,000 and a repayable HELP debt of $50 under section 154-1, with no section-8 Medicare family-relief exemption. The first marginal band gives $70.80 and 10% of repayment income is $7,000. The repayable debt caps the compulsory repayment at $50. [Higher Education Support Act 2003, ss 154-1 and 154-20](https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2004A01234/2026-10-17/2026-10-17/text/original/epub/OEBPS/document_1/document_1.html) and [2026-27 indexation notice](https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2026G00249/asmade/2026-03-27/text/original/epub/OEBPS/document_1/document_1.html).

```oa-check
name: HELP repayment capped by repayable debt (2026-27)
inputs:
  repayment_income: 70000
  minimum: 69528
  repayable_debt: 50
steps:
  - marginal_amount: pct(max(repayment_income - minimum, 0), 15)
  - income_cap: pct(repayment_income, 10)
  - repayment: min(marginal_amount, income_cap, repayable_debt)
expected:
  marginal_amount: 70.80
  repayment: 50
```

For 2026-27, a member's $3.1 million closing total superannuation balance includes a $200,000 limited recourse borrowing amount added under paragraph 307-230(1)(d). The Division 296 balance is $2.9 million and the member is below the $3 million threshold. The 2026-27 first-year rule uses the closing balance. This resolves only the balance threshold, not the full tax computation. [ITAA 1997 ss 296-30 and 296-260](https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2026A00008/asmade/2026-03-13/text/original/epub/OEBPS/document_1/document_1.html).

```oa-check
  lrba_add_back: 200000
  threshold: 3000000
steps:
  - division_296_balance: ordinary_closing_balance - lrba_add_back
  - excess: max(division_296_balance - threshold, 0)
expected:
  division_296_balance: 2900000
  excess: 0
```
