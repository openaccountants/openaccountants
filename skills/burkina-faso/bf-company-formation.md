# Burkina Faso Company Formation & Entity Choice

## Entity types and incorporation

- **Governing company law** — OHADA Uniform Act on Commercial Companies and Economic Interest Groups (AUSCGIE)  _([OHADA Uniform Act on Commercial Companies (AUSCGIE)](https://www.expanship.com/bf/blog/incorporation-requirements-in-burkina-faso))_
- **SARL (Société à Responsabilité Limitée)** — Private limited liability company; most common form for SMEs  _([OHADA Uniform Act on Commercial Companies (AUSCGIE)](https://www.expanship.com/bf/blog/incorporation-requirements-in-burkina-faso))_
- **SA (Société Anonyme)** — Public limited company / joint-stock company, used for larger ventures  _([OHADA Uniform Act on Commercial Companies (AUSCGIE)](https://www.expanship.com/bf/blog/incorporation-requirements-in-burkina-faso))_
- **SAS / SASU and sole proprietorship (entreprise individuelle)** — Simplified joint-stock company (SAS) and sole proprietorship are also available  _(OHADA Uniform Act on Commercial Companies (AUSCGIE))_
- **SARL minimum share capital** — Freely set by the partners but at least the par value of one share (5,000 FCFA); in practice often around 1,000,000 FCFA FCFA FCFA  _([OHADA Uniform Act on Commercial Companies (AUSCGIE)](https://www.usemultiplier.com/burkina-faso/company-registration))_
- **SA minimum share capital** — 10,000,000 FCFA FCFA FCFA  _([OHADA Uniform Act on Commercial Companies (AUSCGIE)](https://www.usemultiplier.com/burkina-faso/company-registration))_
- **Minimum nominal value of a share** — 5,000 FCFA FCFA FCFA  _([OHADA Uniform Act on Commercial Companies (AUSCGIE)](https://www.usemultiplier.com/burkina-faso/company-registration))_
- **Registration one-stop-shop** — CEFORE (Centre de Formalités des Entreprises), Maison de l'Entreprise du Burkina Faso  _([CEFORE business registration procedures](https://www.usemultiplier.com/burkina-faso/company-registration))_
- **Beneficial ownership disclosure** — Founders must identify and declare ultimate beneficial owner (UBO) information at the point of CEFORE registration  _([CEFORE business registration procedures](https://www.expanship.com/bf/blog/incorporation-requirements-in-burkina-faso))_
- **Incorporation timeline** — Registration through CEFORE typically completed within a few days to a couple of weeks ((approx — confirm))  _([CEFORE business registration procedures](https://www.usemultiplier.com/burkina-faso/company-registration))_
- **Incorporation cost** — Government administrative registration fees under ~50 EUR; total professional/notary budget commonly 1,000–1,500 EUR (excluding share capital) ((approx — confirm))  _([CEFORE business registration procedures](https://www.expanship.com/bf/blog/incorporation-requirements-in-burkina-faso))_
- **Core annual compliance** — Annual financial statements (SYSCOHADA), annual IS return, monthly TVA returns, IUTS and CNSS declarations, and renewal of business registrations  _(Code Général des Impôts (CGI); SYSCOHADA accounting framework)_
