# Tanzania Mainland VAT v3.0

**General reference only.** This skill is general tax/accounting reference material for AI-assisted workflows. It has not been reviewed for any specific person's facts, documents, elections, deadlines, residency, filing status, or local procedures. Do not rely on it to file, pay, amend, or take a tax position without review by a qualified professional in the relevant jurisdiction.

## Scope

### Quick reference

Prepare and reconcile a 2026 Tanzania MAINLAND VAT working paper from actual supply/purchase/customs/withholding documents. Standard 18%; ordinary monthly return 20th; withholding agent 10 day deadline from July 2026. Conditional 16% requires actual eligible-person notice/payment proof; Zanzibar requires its own law. EAC customs rules do not create a common VAT return. This version is research material, not accountant approval.

## Ask the client first

- Mainland/Zanzibar place of supply, period, entity, supplier/customer registration and actual withholding agent notice.
- Nature/time/value of supply, net or tax-inclusive price, and current exemption/zero-rating item.
- Valid fiscal invoices/receipts, credit notes, customs declaration/paid VAT, export proof and withholding certificates.
- Purchases' taxable/exempt/private use, credit entitlement, acquisition/invoice dates and registration date.
- Prior returns, carryforward/refund claims, payments/control numbers and actual capital/mining/project relief approvals.
- Actual reduced-rate public notice and electronic payment proof; unknown facts stay unresolved.

## The method, step by step

1. Establish Mainland or Zanzibar, supply period, supplier and customer VAT status, place/nature/time of supply, and whether customer is an actual withholding agent. Obtain invoices/fiscal receipts, credit notes, customs declarations/export proof, valid withholding certificates and payment proof. A bank statement alone is not sufficient.
2. Separate taxable 18%, statutory conditional 16%, proved zero-rated, proved exempt and outside-scope transactions. Hold 16% without eligible-person notice/payment evidence. Separate cash from net taxable value and VAT; avoid double tax on tax-inclusive total.
3. Build output VAT from actual supply records, including appropriate timing/adjustments. Withholding does not reduce 18% gross output VAT: subtract valid withheld output tax separately in return calculation. Identify assisted-government advance VAT only with actual certificate.
4. Test each input under 72-74, including paid import VAT, valid invoice/fiscal receipt, timing, blocked/private/exempt use and apportionment. For actual taxable imported services record output and permitted input in SAME return; do not assume net zero. At 90% or more potential credit the imported-service charge predicate itself fails under 2.
5. Reconcile output plus applicable advance VAT less valid withheld output tax less allowed input and statutory increasing/decreasing adjustments/carryforward. Match documents to live TRA IDRAS return and acknowledgment. Exact old`ITX222.01.E` / `A1-A7` / `B1-B7` / `C1-C3`mapping was not recovered; do not invent boxes.
6. File ordinary monthly return/pay by 20th; withholding agent 10 day payment/statement from July 2026. Keep certificate/receipt evidence and nil returns. Older weekend extension was deleted 2025; no automatic next working day shift. Identify any actual statutory extension separately.

### Import/export, mixed use and special activity

For goods imports, VAT base under 9 includes customs value, whether duty payable or not, plus applicable duty/taxes; do not equate invoice price with the assessed taxable base. Obtain customs VAT payment and importer identity/registration evidence. Transfers from Zanzibar need the applicable evidence/credit rules, not an assumed EAC common VAT return. Goods exports require actual export documents; foreign payment or foreign buyer alone does not zero-rate a Mainland hotel stay. International transport/exported services have specific rules; Section 61 covers international transport;62 covers services physically performed/used outside Tanzania;64 is the general exported-service provision. Section 64 requires the customer outside the United Republic at supply and effective use/enjoyment outside, excludes local land/goods cases, and excludes a right to later local supply or a nonresident agreement rendered to an unregistered person locally. These provisions were read; complete amendment/classification and actual qualifying evidence for a particular export claim remain unconfirmed, so hold that application. [VAT Act ss61-64](https://www.tra.go.tz/images/uploads/acts/The_Value_Added_Tax_Act.pdf).

The old selected exemptions are retained as a classification checklist, NOT blanket exemptions: agricultural implements/inputs and basic foods; livestock; medicines/pharmaceuticals; healthcare; education/materials; financial services; specified insurance (health, life, aircraft, workers compensation, crop/livestock); residential rent/vacant land; unbottled water; specified petroleum; gaming; solar; passenger transport excluding relevant taxi/hire/charter categories. Obtain current Schedule item, HS/conditions and 2024-2026 amendment chain for each actual supply before classification. Do not equate agriculture, diplomatic customer or EPZ/SEZ status with zero-rating. Mining/licence/framework agreement and government-project relief require actual instruments/approval; Finance 2026 s 92 new construction-phase mining relief has ownership/agreement/sole-use conditions and ceases at production. Refer mining-specific positions rather than infer from a bank description.

At partial input ratios exactly 10% or 90%, use apportionment rather than <10/>90 simplification. Annual true-up under 74 goes into sixth period following year or actually prescribed earlier period; do not use the old unproved 5% exempt de-minimis. Preregistration credit under 83 is for qualifying goods held for resale, not a blanket six-month credit for all purchases. Refunds require actual statutory eligibility/minimum, returns and support; bank receipt of refund is not a new supply. Capital goods deferment under 11 remains approval-based, with recapture/revocation on transfer/noncompliance and actual Gazette conditions; no tax exemption from merely labelling a purchase capital.

## Worked cases

All inputs hypothetical TZS; results derived, conditional on stated taxable classification/documents.

### Standard goods sale to an actual withholding agent after 1 July 2026

Net sale 10,000,000; output VAT 1,800,000. Agent withholds 300,000 (3% of net, NOT 54,000 which is 3% of VAT), pays supplier 11,500,000, and remits 300,000 with statement within 10 days after period end. Supplier retains valid certificate and may credit withheld VAT; full output VAT is still 1,800,000. With no other input/adjustment, the supplier's remaining output liability is 1,500,000.

```oa-check
name: Goods withholding uses net supply value and preserves gross output VAT
inputs:
  net_sale: 10000000
steps:
  output_vat: net_sale * 0.18
  invoice_total: net_sale + output_vat
  withheld: net_sale * 0.03
  supplier_cash: invoice_total - withheld
  wrong_withholding: output_vat * 0.03
  remaining_supplier_tax: output_vat - withheld
expected:
  output_vat: 1800000
  withheld: 300000
  supplier_cash: 11500000
  wrong_withholding: 54000
  remaining_supplier_tax: 1500000
```

### EAC goods import and ordinary purchase credit

Kenya-origin goods have PROVED assessed import VAT base 20,000,000, standard 18% and paid VAT 3,600,000. This is customs VAT, not an EU intra-community self assessment. If valid importer documents, taxable use and timing prove full credit, credit 3,600,000; otherwise hold/apportion. Uganda origin is not a VAT exemption by itself. Separately, qualifying business furniture 5,000,000+VAT 900,000 supports 900,000 input only with valid invoice/paid import as applicable, no block and timing.

```oa-check
name: Assessed import base and eligible input reconciled against domestic output
inputs:
  assessed_import_base: 20000000
  taxable_domestic_sales: 30000000
steps:
  paid_import_vat: assessed_import_base * 0.18
  output_vat: taxable_domestic_sales * 0.18
  allowable_import_credit: paid_import_vat
  net_payable: output_vat - allowable_import_credit
expected:
  paid_import_vat: 3600000
  output_vat: 5400000
  net_payable: 1800000
```

### SaaS and imported service: credit-entitlement gate

An otherwise 18% taxable service from a nonresident has net price 5,000,000 and a registered purchaser entitled to only 80% credit. Assuming the imported-services definition/place conditions are met, output VAT 900,000, input 720,000 and net 180,000. It is NOT automatically net zero. For another purchaser who would be entitled to 90% or more credit, the 2 definition excludes that imported-service charge; do not invent an output 900,000/input 900,000 pair just to make the old net zero result work. Actual supplier registration and local-supply rules must also be checked.

```oa-check
name: Mixed-use taxable imported service is not automatically net zero
inputs:
  service_value: 5000000
  permitted_input_ratio: 0.80
steps:
  output_vat: service_value * 0.18
  input_credit: output_vat * permitted_input_ratio
  net_vat: output_vat - input_credit
expected:
  output_vat: 900000
  input_credit: 720000
  net_vat: 180000
```

Retained regressions: proved coffee export 100,000,000 has 0 output under 55 and eligible inputs separately tested; entertainment input blocked unless actual statutory exception; exempt financial supply 50,000,000 has no output and associated input not automatically creditable; credit note adjustment requires actual note/supply/timing, not unproved box A 6; mining referred; cessation needs 84 prior input/valuation; hotel paid foreign currency does not establish zero-rating; bad debt 12 months is wrong under 78's more-than 18 months and write off conditions. Old gift 500,000→90,000 and gift threshold 100,000 are unconfirmed and not usable instructions; classification/valuation must first be established. Out-of-scope CIT/PAYE/SDL/pension rates are handled by those skills; old SDL 4% is not carried into VAT or payroll.

## When to refuse or refer

Do not finalise from bank statement alone, unknown place/registration, supplier pattern, unconfirmed exemption or missing receipt. Hold 16% without actual notice/proof, nonresident digital procedure without governing rules, exported services without conditions, unproved gift threshold, unrecovered Zanzibar figures, capital/mining/project relief without approval, and penalty/refund claims without records. Refuse automatic EAC zero tax, input on blocked/private/exempt costs, withholding 3%/6% of the VAT amount, automatic SaaS net zero, old 20 day withholding from July 2026, or capital deferment sunset assertion without 2026 amendment. Mining and disputed classifications need qualified local review; calculations remain estimates until fact/form review.

This VAT working paper template does not submit a return or establish eligibility for relief. Check actual records, current official forms and approvals, and obtain qualified local review before filing, paying or claiming credit/refund.
