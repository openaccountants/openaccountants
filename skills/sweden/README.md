# Sweden: tax pack

Calendar tax year for individuals; companies are taxed per financial year (räkenskapsår), which may differ from the calendar year. Authority: Skatteverket. Swedish kronor (SEK). Individuals pay municipal income tax plus state income tax above a threshold, and 30% on capital income. Companies pay a single state corporate tax of 20.6% with no municipal layer. VAT (moms) follows EU rules. Employers pay arbetsgivaravgifter; the self-employed pay egenavgifter.
