# Armenia tax overview v0.4

## Scope

This is the Armenian country map for tax year 2026 (the calendar year). It routes a question to the detailed Guide that holds the method. It does not compute a liability, classify a taxpayer or settle residence, source, treaty or regime eligibility. The State Revenue Committee (SRC) administers tax filings. The Armenian dram (AMD) is the currency used in the domestic tax rules. [Tax Code, Article 13](https://www.arlis.am/hy/acts/215611/latest)

## Ask the client first

1. Is the taxpayer an individual, a registered individual entrepreneur, a resident commercial organisation, a non-resident with Armenian-source income, or a non-resident with a permanent establishment?
2. What is the receipt or activity (salary, dividend, interest, rent, business sales, services, property)?
3. Is the person resident in Armenia, and is a treaty in point?
4. Is the business on the general system, turnover tax or micro-business, and what was last year's turnover across all activities?
5. Is the business paying individuals (salary, fees) so that it acts as a tax agent?
6. Which tax year is the question about? (2025 and 2026 personal declarations have different windows.)

## The method, step by step

1. **Identify the taxpayer.** Individual, individual entrepreneur, resident commercial organisation, non-resident with Armenian-source income or a permanent establishment. [Tax Code, Articles 103 and 140](https://www.arlis.am/hy/acts/215611/latest)
2. **Classify the receipt and activity, then check source and residence.** Residence, source, treaty relief, permanent establishment, tax-agent duties and the correct tax base depend on the actual facts. This overview does not determine those facts. [Tax Code, Articles 11 and 20](https://www.arlis.am/hy/acts/215611/latest)
3. **Test any special regime before applying a headline rate.** The individual entrepreneur's business income does not automatically use the ordinary personal 20% method: on the general system it bears 23% profit tax, and on turnover tax a fixed AMD 5,000 a month. [Tax Code, Article 125(3), (3.1)](https://www.arlis.am/hy/acts/215611/latest)
4. **Route to the detailed Guide** using the table below.

### Deadlines to route correctly

Ordinary corporate profit tax returns and payment are due by 20 April after the tax year. The annual individual declaration has different dates for the transition year: the 2025-income declaration window is 2 March through 1 November 2026; the general 2026-income window is 2 March through 1 July 2027. The latter date applies to the declaration population and rules in the Code (from tax year 2025 onwards, resident Armenian citizens, other than minors at 31 December, under Article 156(4.1)), not as a universal filing obligation for every person. Check the individual's filing status and any amended deadline before using it. [Tax Code, Articles 134, 136, 156 and 158](https://www.arlis.am/hy/acts/215611/latest); [HO-76-N](https://www.arlis.am/hy/acts/206337)

## Worked cases

### 1. Resident company profit and a dividend to a resident individual

A resident LLC on the general system has a taxable profit of AMD 10,000,000 for 2026 and no special rate. Profit tax at 18% is AMD 1,800,000, due with the return by 20 April 2027. If it distributes the remaining AMD 8,200,000 to a resident individual shareholder, the company withholds 5% income tax on the dividend, AMD 410,000. Total tax on the two layers is AMD 2,210,000. Route the company layer to [corporate income tax](am-corporate-income-tax.md) and the dividend layer to [individual income tax](armenia-income-tax.md); the regime comparison is in [tax planning](armenia-tax-optimization.md). This ignores VAT, advances, loss relief and any treaty. [Tax Code 125(1), 150(8)][code]

```oa-check

  taxable_profit: 10000000
  profit_tax_rate: 0.18
  dividend_rate: 0.05

  - profit_tax: taxable_profit * profit_tax_rate
  - distributable: taxable_profit - profit_tax
  - dividend_tax: distributable * dividend_rate
  - total_tax: profit_tax + dividend_tax

  profit_tax: 1800000
  distributable: 8200000
  dividend_tax: 410000
  total_tax: 2210000
```

## When to refuse or refer

The headline rates above are signposts, not a computation. This map does not settle the taxpayer's residence, source, classification, VAT status, special regime, treaty protection, deductions or filing obligation. It does not catalogue every tax, duty, municipal charge, customs rule, exemption, refund, licence or sector-specific obligation. The domain Guides state their remaining gaps and must be read with their source dates. All seven ordinary-domain methods are supplied. All are checked against the law, with independent source review recorded in the country research and named professional attestation pending. Exceptional late-export/health-error recovery and specialist populations retain the explicit boundaries in the detailed Guides. Refer anything outside the detailed Guides to an Armenian adviser.
