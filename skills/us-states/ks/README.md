# Kansas (US-KS): tax pack

Calendar tax year for individuals; corporations follow their federal year. Authority: the Kansas Department of Revenue (KDOR), ksrevenue.gov. Currency: US dollars. Kansas taxes individuals at 5.20% and 5.58% (K.S.A. 79-32,110), corporations at a 4% normal tax plus a 3% surtax on Kansas taxable income over $50,000 (K.S.A. 79-32,110(c); no rate reduction for 2026 per KDOR Notice 25-06), and retail sales at 6.5% state plus local taxes (K.S.A. 79-3603), with a 0% state rate on food and food ingredients from 1 January 2025 (K.S.A. 79-3603d). The Kansas franchise tax was repealed for tax year 2011 and after. A US state: federal income tax, FICA and FUTA sit on top of every domain here.
