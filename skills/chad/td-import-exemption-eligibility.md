# Chad import-exemption eligibility v0.2

> **General reference only.** This skill is general tax/accounting reference material for AI-assisted workflows. It has not been reviewed for any specific person's facts, documents, elections, deadlines, residency, filing status, or local procedures. Do not rely on it to file, pay, amend, or take a tax position without review by a qualified professional in the relevant jurisdiction.

## Who this applies to

Use this Guide to prepare a line-by-line eligibility working paper for goods imported into Chad from **9 September 2026**, where the goods and their intended use fit the signed order. Article 3 takes effect from signature; the stamped signature date is 9 September 2026. Do not apply it to an earlier import merely because the document is filed under the 2026 finance-law programme. [Arrêté 135, arts. 1–3](https://ecofinances.gouv.td/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Arrete-N135-Exoneration-des-droits-et-taxes-sur-les-produits09092026164852.pdf#page=2)

**Wheat is excluded from this Guide’s supported scope.** Finance-law article 65 lists CEMAC-origin produits du cru, including wheat under heading 10 01, at a 5% customs duty for three years from 1 January 2026. The September order also names wheat, but this draft does not resolve the interaction or the authority for overriding that statutory rule. Hold every wheat line for customs/legal reconciliation, including where its origin is unknown. Do not conclude either exemption or a final 5% liability from this draft. [Finance law 2026, art. 65](https://ecofinances.gouv.td/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Loi-n008-AN-SENAT-2025-Portant-loi-de-Finances-2026-LF-2026.pdf#page=78); [Arrêté 135, arts. 2–3](https://ecofinances.gouv.td/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Arrete-N135-Exoneration-des-droits-et-taxes-sur-les-produits09092026164852.pdf#page=2).

The result identifies a supported exemption basis for the listed goods. It does not provide a tariff code, customs valuation, declaration code or release approval. A missing product description or unresolved use means the line remains on hold. This hold is an instruction for using this Guide, not a finding that the goods are taxable.

The table describes an **exemption**, not a general zero-rated domestic VAT regime. The order's words concern import duties and taxes. Do not extend them to a later domestic sale, transport invoice, brokerage fee or other charge without a separate source. Articles 1–3 supply no turnover threshold, HS tariff codes or numerical duty rates. [Arrêté 135, arts. 1–3](https://ecofinances.gouv.td/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Arrete-N135-Exoneration-des-droits-et-taxes-sur-les-produits09092026164852.pdf#page=2)

## The base

1. Take the commercial invoice and packing list. Give each product its own line, description, quantity, invoice value and supporting specification. Invoice values in this working paper reconcile the shipment; they are not asserted to be customs values.
2. First hold all wheat lines and record their origin evidence for reconciliation. For the other goods, match each line to one of the four groups in the table. For livestock-feed products, water equipment and forage-production equipment, document the relevant use. Record the contract, technical sheet or other actual evidence supplied by the importer. These are evidence requests for the working paper, not an invented statutory certificate list. [Arrêté 135, arts. 1–3](https://ecofinances.gouv.td/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Arrete-N135-Exoneration-des-droits-et-taxes-sur-les-produits09092026164852.pdf#page=2)
3. Mark a non-wheat line **supported** only when its identity and any required use match the cited wording and there is no unresolved contrary applicable instrument. Mark uncertain descriptions, mixed-use items and goods not established within that wording **hold for customs review**. Do not presume that wheat flour qualifies merely because wheat grain does, or that all farm machinery is forage-production machinery. [Arrêté 135, arts. 1–3](https://ecofinances.gouv.td/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Arrete-N135-Exoneration-des-droits-et-taxes-sur-les-produits09092026164852.pdf#page=2)
4. Total the supported and held invoice lines separately. Reconcile their sum to the invoice. This is a line-classification reconciliation, not a percentage exemption for an entire mixed shipment.
5. Produce an importer review schedule: line ID, product, intended use where relevant, article, evidence, supported/hold decision, invoice value and unresolved question. It does not replace the customs declaration or authorise release.

## Deadlines and filing

Article 3 places implementation with the **Directeur Général des Douanes et Droits Indirects** and makes the order effective from signature. It also repeals contrary earlier provisions. It gives no separate application deadline or submission form. [Arrêté 135, arts. 1–3](https://ecofinances.gouv.td/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Arrete-N135-Exoneration-des-droits-et-taxes-sur-les-produits09092026164852.pdf#page=2)

Prepare the schedule and the actual supporting documents for the importer or its customs representative. Before making an exemption claim, confirm origin and any interacting customs instrument, then obtain the current declaration procedure, relevant tariff classification and exemption codes from customs, then match them to the actual goods and applicable date. Retain the classification decision and the resulting declaration/release evidence with the schedule. The codes, form boxes, portal screens and authenticated release process were not accessed; this Guide stops at the eligibility working paper and cannot instruct a completed filing.

## Penalties and interest

The three-article order does not state a penalty, late-payment interest rate or assessment/appeal timetable. No such figure is supplied here. A disputed classification, an earlier import, an assessment or a request for retrospective relief needs the applicable customs procedures checked separately. Do not import a penalty from another country's code or from the legacy Chad Guides. [Arrêté 135, arts. 1–3](https://ecofinances.gouv.td/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Arrete-N135-Exoneration-des-droits-et-taxes-sur-les-produits09092026164852.pdf#page=2)

## Cross-border points

The scope is the listed **imports into Chad**. The order gives product and use descriptions rather than a general exemption for every food, agricultural or water-sector business. It does not itself establish export relief, a domestic VAT input credit, a treaty benefit or an origin rule. Preserve a separate assessment for any other tax or customs issue that changes the claim. [Arrêté 135, arts. 1–3](https://ecofinances.gouv.td/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Arrete-N135-Exoneration-des-droits-et-taxes-sur-les-produits09092026164852.pdf#page=2)

Do not treat a line held for review as proof of tax liability, or a supported line as proof that a shipment can clear without customs formalities. Neither conclusion is made by the order's three articles.

## Two worked examples that recompute

**Listed cereals, with product identity established.** A hypothetical post-signature shipment contains rice invoiced at XAF 3 million and maize at XAF 1 million, each confirmed as the named goods. Article 2 supports both product descriptions. The eligible invoice-line subtotal is XAF 4 million and the held subtotal is zero. The example does not calculate customs value or incidental charges.

```oa-check
name: Reconcile named cereal invoice lines
inputs:
  rice_invoice_value: 3000000
  maize_invoice_value: 1000000
steps:
  supported_invoice_subtotal: rice_invoice_value + maize_invoice_value
  held_invoice_subtotal: 0
  shipment_invoice_total: supported_invoice_subtotal + held_invoice_subtotal
expected:
  supported_invoice_subtotal: 4000000
  held_invoice_subtotal: 0
  shipment_invoice_total: 4000000
```

**Do not extend the forage condition to every tractor.** Assume an importer supplies adequate specifications and use evidence for water-pumping equipment invoiced at XAF 2 million and equipment dedicated to forage-crop production invoiced at XAF 4 million. A further tractor line of XAF 6 million says only “general farm use”, with its actual intended use unresolved. The first two lines match article 1; the tractor remains held. Supported and held subtotals are XAF 6 million each, reconciling to XAF 12 million. The held amount is not a computed taxable customs base and does not prove the tractor is ineligible under any other law.

```oa-check
name: Keep uncertain agricultural use separate
inputs:
  water_pump_invoice_value: 2000000
  forage_equipment_invoice_value: 4000000
  unresolved_tractor_invoice_value: 6000000
steps:
  supported_invoice_subtotal: water_pump_invoice_value + forage_equipment_invoice_value
  held_invoice_subtotal: unresolved_tractor_invoice_value
  shipment_invoice_total: supported_invoice_subtotal + held_invoice_subtotal
expected:
  supported_invoice_subtotal: 6000000
  held_invoice_subtotal: 6000000
  shipment_invoice_total: 12000000
```

## Ask the client first

- What are the import date, origin evidence and customs procedure proposed for the goods?
- Is any line wheat, requiring the mandatory hold until the finance-law/order interaction is reconciled?
- What exact goods, quantities and individual invoice values appear on the invoice and packing list?
- For feed, water or agricultural equipment, what actual intended use and specifications support the relevant wording?
- Are any goods mixtures, wheat flour, general-purpose farm machinery or described too broadly to classify under this order?
- What tariff/exemption classification or written direction has customs already given, and is any liability or earlier import under dispute?
