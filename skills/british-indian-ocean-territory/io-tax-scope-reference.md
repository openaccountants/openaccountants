## Selected dated rules

| Rule | Wording and required distinction |
|---|---|
| **Taxation s3** | The restriction addresses law having effect through Constitution Order s3(2)(a): such law may not require or permit the imposition within the Territory of income tax, corporation tax, capital gains tax or a substantially similar tax upon income, profits, gains and capital, whether arising inside or outside it. This describes the named preserved-law restriction. It is not an opinion that every possible local, foreign, payroll or transaction tax is absent. |
| **Constitution ss3 and10; Courts s3** | Establish the applicable preservation, law-making and received-law paths separately. Do not infer that the Taxation Ordinance prevents every later ordinance, or that all English law automatically applies unchanged. Requested-period hierarchy and suitability remain required. |
| **Companies s3** | No body corporate or entity treated as a body corporate for tax purposes in another territory is regarded as resident in the Territory under the captured wording. It does not determine foreign residence or liability, or supply an incorporation/registration procedure. |
| **Employment ss2–3** | Unless context otherwise requires, employee includes express/implied and oral/written contracts of service or personal work, and employer means a person employing **10 or more employees**. Section 3 excludes the Crown, persons in its service/employment, any country's government and persons in its service/employment. Establish the actual employer and relationship; a contractor is not proved to be a government employee by its customer. |
| **Employment s24** | Within the Ordinance's scope, the employer must keep each employee insured **at all times while employed in the Territory**, against death or personal injury **arising out of and in the course of employment**. The Commissioner must be satisfied that amount and manner are reasonable in that employee's circumstances, including the relevant laws/practices of the employer's principal-business country and the employee's prior-residence country. Personal injury includes disease and physical/mental impairment under s2. Subsection(2) permits the Commissioner to exempt an employer where satisfied that foreign law applying to that employer for employees in the Territory requires compensation of amounts satisfactory under subsection(1). Foreign insurance or compensation is not an automatic exemption. |
| **Employment ss25–27** | The employer must provide, or ensure availability **in the Territory** of, adequate medical-care/attention facilities for personal injury **however arising**. On termination of employment in the Territory, repatriation is to the country from which the employee came for employment, or another country that will admit the employee **and to which the employee is willing to go**. Section27 connects non-satisfaction of ss24–25 to entry-permit action and non-compliance with s26 to an offence. No penalty amount or operational permit advice is supplied. These duties are separate from tax and contribution questions. |

The 2020 source statements also retain open dependencies on later UK Acts/Orders/extensions, Courts s4 declarations, Employment regulations and exemption instruments, incorporated criminal/immigration provisions and current Constitution/treaty machinery. The 1966/1976 US notes, imports regime and service-charge legislation have not been independently qualified for this reference.

The only numerical applicability condition used here is the captured **10-employee** definition. This guide supplies no tax rate, contribution rate, return threshold, monetary penalty, filing deadline, currency-conversion or rounding rule. Source silence does not establish that an obligation is absent.

## Fictional scope positions

These invented cases resolve only the stated dated-text question. Each retains the period/amendment gate and produces no liability or complete employment opinion.

| Case | Stated facts, rule and result |
|---|---|
| **1. Preserved income-tax law** | Assume a proposed local charge is income tax imposed through law preserved by Order s3(2)(a). S1 s3 supplies a restriction on that path. Hold the actual charge and current-law determination under R-IO-PERIOD and R-IO-CALC; no universal zero-tax conclusion follows. |
| **2. Different-country corporate status** | An invented entity is documented as treated as corporate for tax in another territory. S2 s3 addresses BIOT residence under its captured wording. Foreign residence, incorporation and tax remain separate held questions. |
| **3. 9 workers** | A private person employs 9 persons, and no context-based departure from the definition is established. The simple s2 numeric definition is not met. That result does not exempt it from all laws, prove worker classification or resolve later amendments. |
| **4. 10 workers** | A private person employs 10 documented employees and no s3 exclusion is established. The numeric limb is met. Review ss24–27 and all other applicable duties; tax/social amounts remain held. |
| **5. Government customer** | A private contractor's sole customer is a foreign government. The contract alone does not establish that its staff are persons in that government's service/employment. R-IO-FACTS fires until the actual relationship and exclusion are established. |
| **6. Foreign compensation** | A covered employer produces evidence of a foreign compensation duty but no Commissioner exemption. S3 s24(2) makes the exemption a separate decision. Do not substitute the evidence for that decision; R-IO-FACTS holds any asserted exemption. |
| **7. Consumption-tax claim** | A request treats S1 s3 as proof that a particular import or sale has no tax. The named provision does not settle that obligation. R-IO-CALC fires pending its complete authority and facts. |

## Intake, defaults and reviewer policy

Obtain the requested period and obligation, exact island/location, taxpayer type, citizenship and residence evidence, payer/employer identity and legal status, place and dates of work, contract and income source, transaction direction and supporting documents. For employment, obtain the employee count and legal relationship, the Crown/government facts, actual insurance and compensation evidence and any Commissioner decision. A tax-scope request also needs the specific charging instrument and legal path.

No default is authorised for unknown law, commencement, residence, employer status, source, exemptions or territorial reach. Keep the affected decision as Needs Input. Missing evidence is not a zero liability, an exemption or a negative registration requirement. Ask the base's structured questions for missing facts and put missing legal sources in the action list.

Reviewer policy, not a statutory cash threshold: flag every territorial, employer and commencement decision regardless of amount. An unresolved source condition stops the affected classification; an attention flag does not resolve it.
