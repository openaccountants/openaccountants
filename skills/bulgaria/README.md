# Bulgaria: tax pack

Calendar tax year for individuals and companies. Authority: the National Revenue Agency (НАП, nra.bg); tax laws are drafted by the Ministry of Finance (minfin.bg) and published in the State Gazette (dv.parliament.bg). Bulgaria adopted the euro on 1 January 2026 at BGN 1.95583 = EUR 1; amounts still printed in leva in the tax laws are read as euro at that rate. Corporate income tax and personal income tax are flat 10%. Transfer pricing rules apply to domestic as well as cross-border related-party deals.
