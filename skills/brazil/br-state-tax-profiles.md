# Brazil state tax profiles (2026)

## Who this applies to

Use the destination and transaction facts before selecting a state percentage. This Guide owns state parameters and local operational differences; [br-indirect-tax](br-indirect-tax.md) remains the ordinary national ICMS method and classification owner. It also owns the common interstate-rate selection and detailed credit calculation. [brazil-vat](brazil-vat.md) owns the consumption-reform transition; these current ICMS cards do not settle a CBS/IBS question. [br-simples-nacional](br-simples-nacional.md) owns the small-business regime and sublimit test.

Required inputs: actual origin/destination establishments; tax-event date; product description/NCM and CEST where relevant; taxpayer status and final-use versus resale; tax-included price and freight/other charges; regime, registrations, relief and special terms. Identify the fund separately before computing a total. A residual rate is used only after excluding the state's specific-rate, exempt, reduced-base, ST, anticipation and monophase branches.

Classify taxpayer status from actual activity and the special import/acquisition rules; an IE number alone is not the whole test. For interstate final consumers, the recipient is responsible for the difference when a taxpayer; the sender/service provider when the recipient is not a taxpayer. [LC 87/1996 art.4 caput,§§1 to 2](https://www2.camara.leg.br/legin/fed/leicom/1996/leicomplementar-87-13-setembro-1996-370965-normaatualizada-pl.html).

**Not yet approved.** Research is dated to the portions actually read below. A verified residual clause does not establish every product rate or make a partial local procedure filing-ready.

### Poverty-fund classification and collection

FCP, FECOEP, FECOP, FUNCEP and AMPARA are not interchangeable national charges. Match the state article, exact product and event, relief/exclusion, time period, base and collector. A blank or inaccessible fund field does not establish zero.

- AL: residual FECOEP 1% applies to goods/services outside art.2 I, including internal-rate/ST cases. Exclusions include food supply, specified passenger/water transport, low-consumption electricity, executive-listed basic basket/school material and human medicines. Selective 2% goods include the remaining art.2 I luxury categories, rather than all goods. Article 3§2 bars its additional amount from calculating any fiscal benefit or incentive; art.2-A§1 applies that restriction to the residual 1% too. The scanned official legislative attachment was visually read at pp 3 to 8; it is a 2023 reproduction, supplemented by the subsequent amendments. [Lei 6558/2004 art.2-A and art.3; Lei 7767/2015 art.1 II](https://sapl.al.al.leg.br/media/sapl/public/normajuridica/2016/1186/1186_texto_integral.pdf).

## The base

Compute the national transaction base using the linked owner, then apply the state classification. Record separately: own output tax; lawful input credit/reversal; destination DIFAL; ST or anticipation; and the applicable fund. Do not label the sum of output components as net tax payable before credits and other adjustments.

ST requires the state-law responsibility and product/event match; interstate ST requires the agreement between the interested states. A product code in a general catalog does not replace those tests. Keep own output, destination differential and substituted tax in distinct calculations. [LC 87/1996 arts.6 to 9](https://www2.camara.leg.br/legin/fed/leicom/1996/leicomplementar-87-13-setembro-1996-370965-normaatualizada-pl.html).

Begin with the legally applicable transaction value, including the tax itself and the specified charges. The tax-included invoice value must not be grossed up a second time. Taxpayer final-use DIFAL has origin/destination bases; noncontributor DIFAL has the common transaction-value branch. Apply the actual destination technical rule before choosing a shortcut. [LC 87/1996 art.13 caput,§§1 to 3,6 to 7](https://www2.camara.leg.br/legin/fed/leicom/1996/leicomplementar-87-13-setembro-1996-370965-normaatualizada-pl.html).

The national owner supplies the detailed credit algorithm, CIAP timing and documentary restrictions. For each state computation, reconcile eligible documented inputs with output tax and identify reversals for exempt/non-taxed outputs, unrelated use, loss or deterioration. Export exceptions must be preserved. Do not treat tax stated on any purchase invoice as automatically recoverable. [LC 87/1996 arts.19 to 21,23,33](https://www2.camara.leg.br/legin/fed/leicom/1996/leicomplementar-87-13-setembro-1996-370965-normaatualizada-pl.html).

In the noncontributor final-consumer branch, prior input credit is deducted only from origin-state output, not automatically from the destination DIFAL or a separately protected fund charge. State-specific fund credit exclusions still apply. [LC 87/1996 art.20-A; SP RICMS 56-C§4](https://www2.camara.leg.br/legin/fed/leicom/1996/leicomplementar-87-13-setembro-1996-370965-normaatualizada-pl.html).

The Mato Grosso technical note distinguishes a taxpayer buying for final use/fixed assets from a noncontributor final consumer. In the former branch, exclude origin ICMS from the origin value and include destination ICMS in the destination base. In the latter branch, use the tax-included single transaction base and apply the difference of the applicable rates. This state-specific application is not authority to impose a double base on every interstate sale. The note also preserves applicable exemption/reduction and product-specific fund conditions. [Nota Técnica 058/2026, items 1 to 2](https://app1.sefaz.mt.gov.br/04256E4C004D9CE4/A846E70F044285D7042572F9004EEAA2/FDD91D19DD25DD9B03258E6E004E95A9).

## Cross-border points

An interstate sale is not an international import or export. ## Two worked examples that recompute

### Example 1: Alagoas changes during the year

Fictional ordinary sale with a BRL 10,000 tax-included base, no ST, no exemption/reduction and a product actually within the residual FECOEP branch, outside every selective category and exclusion. The same output calculation for January to March is BRL 1,900 own ICMS plus BRL 100 FECOEP, or BRL 2,000. From April the own component is BRL 2,050 and the same fund component BRL 100, or BRL 2,150. The event-date difference is BRL 150. These are output components before lawful input credits, not a net return/payment amount. [Lei 8779 arts.1,4](https://sapl.al.al.leg.br/media/sapl/public/normajuridica/2022/2419/lei_no_8.779_de_20_de_dezembro_de_2022.pdf); [Lei 9776 arts.1,3](https://sapl.al.al.leg.br/media/sapl/public/normajuridica/2025/3699/lei_no_9.776_de_22_de_dezembro_de_2025.pdf); [Lei 6558 art.2-A/Lei 7767](https://sapl.al.al.leg.br/media/sapl/public/normajuridica/2016/1186/1186_texto_integral.pdf).

```oa-check
name: Alagoas ordinary output before and after April 2026
inputs:
  base: 10000
steps:
  own_before: base * 0.19
  own_after: base * 0.205
  fund: base * 0.01
  total_before: own_before + fund
  total_after: own_after + fund
  difference: total_after - total_before
expected:
  own_before: 1900
  own_after: 2050
  fund: 100
  total_before: 2000
  total_after: 2150
  difference: 150
```

### Example 2: Mato Grosso buyer classification changes the destination result

The official technical-note example supplies a BRL 1,000 already tax-included origin transaction, applicable origin rate and ordinary destination classification, no exemption/reduction and no product-specific fund. If the buyer is a taxpayer acquiring for final use, origin tax is BRL 70, destination base is BRL 1,120.48, destination output BRL 190.48 and the difference BRL 120.48. If the otherwise comparable buyer is a noncontributor final consumer, the single-base branch gives BRL 100. The BRL 20.48 difference is decided by the legal recipient/use classification, not by choosing the cheaper calculation. Do not reuse the example's origin percentage without the national origin/destination/imported-content test. [Nota Técnica 058/2026 items 1 to 2 and both examples](https://app1.sefaz.mt.gov.br/04256E4C004D9CE4/A846E70F044285D7042572F9004EEAA2/FDD91D19DD25DD9B03258E6E004E95A9).

```oa-check
name: Mato Grosso final-use taxpayer versus noncontributor
inputs:
  inclusive_value: 1000
  origin_rate: 0.07
  destination_rate: 0.17
steps:
  origin_tax: inclusive_value * origin_rate
  destination_base: round((inclusive_value - origin_tax) / (1 - destination_rate), 2)
  destination_tax: round(destination_base * destination_rate, 2)
  taxpayer_difal: destination_tax - origin_tax
  noncontributor_difal: inclusive_value * (destination_rate - origin_rate)
  difference: taxpayer_difal - noncontributor_difal
expected:
  origin_tax: 70
  destination_base: 1120.48
  destination_tax: 190.48
  taxpayer_difal: 120.48
  noncontributor_difal: 100
  difference: 20.48
```

## Ask the client first

1. What is the tax-event date, origin, actual destination and each establishment's registration/regime?
2. What exact goods/service, NCM/description, use and any CEST, fuel or essential-goods classification are involved?
3. Is the recipient an ICMS taxpayer, and is this resale/industrial input or final use/consumption/fixed assets?
4. Is the amount tax-included; what freight, insurance, conditional discount, import charge and benefit or special term changes the base?
5. What documents support input credit, reversal, ST, DIFAL and fund classification, and what local return/dispensation and payment receipts exist?
6. Is the question actually ITCMD/IPVA, an incentive/free-area regime or consumption reform requiring the separate facts and owner?
