# Leaving India for the UAE or Singapore: the NRI tax map v 1.1

## Scope

For Indian citizens and persons of Indian origin moving to the UAE or Singapore, including employees, founders and families, and their accountants. Produce a documented residence, income, treaty and bank-account working paper. **Primary period: Tax Year 2026-27, 1 April 2026 to 31 March 2027**, under the Income-tax Act, 2025. Income through 31 March 2026 belongs to the separate **2025-2026 filing cycle**, FY 2025-26 / AY 2026-27, under the 1961 Act; do not put new form numbers on that earlier period. [Act ss.1, 3,536](https://egazette.gov.in/WriteReadData/2025/265620.pdf).

Indian domestic tax residence, treaty residence and FEMA exchange-control residence are separate determinations. A foreign address, visa, TRC or low India day count alone does not settle all of them. The Indian side is shared across the destinations; the treaty differences must be checked separately. Companies, trusts, partnerships, PE/business structures and contested residence require specialist review. This is not a company migration, estate, investment-dealing or final return-computation skill. Currency is INR unless AED, SGD or USD is stated.

The old introduction's claims of the world's largest migration flows, a generous corridor, no exit tax/deemed disposal/inheritance tax, and day counts being 80% of the task are not established by the provisions read here. They are preserved as **unconfirmed claims**, not reasons to execute a departure, disposal or succession plan. RNOR is a conditional income-scope rule, not a fixed tax holiday.

## Ask the client first

- Citizenship/PIO evidence; why and when leaving or visiting; employment contract, visa and crew records where relevant; proposed return and family locations.
- Actual India travel records for the current year and preceding 10 years; earlier residence classifications. Resolve uncertain arrival/departure days before any threshold result. Keep boarding passes; a midnight-only convention is not confirmed here.
- Total income excluding **income from foreign sources as defined in s.6(14)**, not a casual India-sourced receipts total; liability to tax in every other relevant country or territory by domicile, residence or similar criteria.
- Every income stream, actual work location, first receipt location, ownership, foreign business controlled in India/profession set up in India, home/family/economic ties and foreign taxes.
- Asset class, acquisition/transfer dates and cost, STT evidence, reinvestment proposals, tenancy/loan documents, buyer/tenant residence and proposed withholding/certificates.
- Resident/NRE/NRO/FCNR accounts, FEMA departure/return facts, source of each balance, current income versus capital, remittances under the annual facility, demat/fund KYC, PPF/NSC and EPF records, nominees and mandates.
- The particular treaty claim, beneficial ownership, TRC period, TIN/Form 41 documents, possible PE/fixed base/anti-abuse issues, return year, PAN status and any applicable Aadhaar linkage exception.

## The method, step by step

### Part 1: The three Indian statuses and day counts

#### 1.1 Resident or non-resident

Test each Indian tax year; count history across the Act transition. Figures expressed in words in s.6 are shown as numerals below.

| Test | Complete trigger/exception | Exact short excerpt and source |
|---|---|---|
| Ordinary current-year test | At least 182 India days; equality is resident (in words on the page) | s.6(2)(a), "one hundred and eighty-two days or more"; https://egazette.gov.in/WriteReadData/2025/265620.pdf |
| Ordinary combined test | At least 60 current-year days AND at least 365 across the preceding 4 tax years (in words on the page) | s.6(2)(b), "four years preceding such tax year"; https://egazette.gov.in/WriteReadData/2025/265620.pdf |
| Citizen employment/Indian-ship crew departure | s.6(2)(b) does not apply to the qualifying citizen leaving for employment outside India or as crew of an Indian ship; 182-day test and separate deemed-residence test remain | s.6(3), "shall not apply"; https://egazette.gov.in/WriteReadData/2025/265620.pdf |
| Citizen/PIO living outside India who comes on a visit | Combined test normally disapplied; if total income excluding statutory foreign sources exceeds INR 1,500,000, use 120 current days AND 365 preceding-4-year days instead; 182-day test remains. Income threshold and day counts in words on the page | ss.6(4)-(5), "exceeding fifteen lakh rupees"; https://egazette.gov.in/WriteReadData/2025/265620.pdf |
| Foreign-bound ship crew day counting | Citizenship, voyage and prescribed conditions matter; do not invent a midnight rule | s.6(6); https://egazette.gov.in/WriteReadData/2025/265620.pdf |

Do not give every high earner a universal under-120 visit instruction or add an under-60 requirement to a qualifying visitor. The 365-day look-back still matters for the 120-day combined test. A person who is not a qualifying visitor may face the ordinary 60+365 test. A citizen employment leaver should plan fewer than 182 days for the current-year test, but that alone does not defeat deemed residence. A visit budget is a planning output from the full tests, not a substitute for them. Family weddings and winter stays count in the documented travel history.

#### 1.2 Deemed residence: not just a Dubai rule

Under ss.6(7)-(8), an Indian citizen with total income **exceeding INR 1,500,000** (in words on the page), excluding statutory foreign sources, is deemed resident if not liable to tax in **any other country or territory** due to domicile, residence or similar criteria. It does not apply if already resident under ss.6(2)-(6). Deemed residents are RNOR under s.6(13)(c). Merely living in a zero-salary-tax country does not itself establish every limb, and a UAE TRC does not automatically defeat this domestic test. Singapore residence must also be established from facts; it is not a destination-name exemption. [Full s.6](https://egazette.gov.in/WriteReadData/2025/265620.pdf).

Treaty entitlement is the next determination, not a domestic factual shortcut. For dual treaty residence, test permanent home, centre of vital interests, habitual abode, nationality and competent-authority resolution in the order prescribed. The UAE synthesized text's Article 4(3)(c) prints an apparent "either"/"neither" wording problem; a case dependent on that limb is referred, not silently repaired. [UAE Article 4](https://www.incometaxindia.gov.in/documents/20117/6715095/Synthesized-text-India-UAE-DTAA.pdf/25244922-8e6a-1569-81f3-04f9fcc29015?download=true&t=1762943463017&version=1.0); [Singapore Article 4](https://www.iras.gov.sg/media/docs/default-source/dtas/protocol-amending-singapore-india-dta-%28ratified%29%28mli%29%281-oct-2019%29.pdf).

#### 1.3 RNOR on departure or return

| RNOR route, after establishing resident status | Condition | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Residence-history route | Non-resident in 9 of preceding 10 tax years OR no more than 729 India days in preceding 7 tax years (in words on the page) | s.6(13)(a); https://egazette.gov.in/WriteReadData/2025/265620.pdf |
| High-income citizen/PIO visitor route | Statutory non-foreign income exceeds INR 1,500,000 and 120 or more but fewer than 182 India days; apply together with residence/visit conditions, not to every 120-day visitor (in words on the page) | s.6(13)(b) and (5); https://egazette.gov.in/WriteReadData/2025/265620.pdf |
| Deemed-resident citizen | Resident under s.6(7) | s.6(13)(c); https://egazette.gov.in/WriteReadData/2025/265620.pdf |

RNOR does **not** mean Indian income only. Both NR and RNOR include income received/deemed received in India and income accruing/arising/deemed accruing in India. RNOR also includes foreign-accruing income from a business controlled in India or profession set up in India. ROR ordinarily includes worldwide income. Income already included on accrual is not included again on receipt; do not treat every transfer of accumulated savings as new income. [s.5(1)-(4)](https://egazette.gov.in/WriteReadData/2025/265620.pdf).

A mistimed departure may yield ROR or RNOR; RNOR is not automatic. Returning citizens must compute each year's status and look-backs before timing foreign gains, deferred payouts or restructuring. The old typical **2-3 RNOR years** claim is an unconfirmed generalization; no fixed window is promised. First receipt, source, Indian control/profession, treaty and anti-abuse consequences can remain even during RNOR.

Apply domestic source/receipt rules, then the more beneficial applicable treaty, with documentation and anti-abuse review. UAE/Singapore tax treatment does not by itself extinguish India's taxing right. [ss.5, 9,159](https://egazette.gov.in/WriteReadData/2025/265620.pdf).

Detailed return assembly is routed to `in-return-assembly` and `india-income-tax`; residency to `in-tax-residency`.

### Part 3: FEMA, NRE/NRO/FCNR and remittances

FEMA residence is a separate test. RBI reproduces FEMA 2(v): preceding-year presence over 182 days (in words on the page) has exclusions for a person going/staying outside for employment, business/vocation or circumstances showing indefinite stay; corresponding inward-purpose rules also apply. Thus departure purpose can matter immediately; do not describe every foreign trip as an instant change. Record purpose/date/evidence and tell the authorised-dealer bank. [RBI resident-account FAQ Q 1](https://www.rbi.org.in/Commonperson/english/Scripts/FAQs.aspx?Id=357).

| Account | Permitted purpose/status and tax boundary | Repatriation/return condition | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| NRE | Eligible NRI/PIO INR account; overseas remittances and specified other credits including qualifying Indian current income, not foreign salary only. Tax exemption requires Schedule IV 1 conditions above | Principal/interest repatriable; on return for employment or status change, redesignate resident or eligible RFC immediately | RBI Q 1-3 table; https://www.rbi.org.in/commonperson/Upload/English/FAQs/PDFs/Accountresidents16012025.pdf ; https://egazette.gov.in/WriteReadData/2025/265620.pdf |
| NRO | Nonresident bona-fide rupee transactions, including Indian rent/dividends/sale proceeds. Interest not automatically exempt | Eligible current income can be remitted separately. Other balances for eligible NRI/PIO : USD 1,000,000 (in words on the page) per FY April-March, aggregate with other eligible assets, including NRO-to-NRE transfers within facility; conditions/taxes apply | RBI Q 3 table; https://www.rbi.org.in/commonperson/Upload/English/FAQs/PDFs/Accountresidents16012025.pdf |
| FCNR(B) | Eligible NRI/PIO foreign-currency term deposit; tax exemption eligibility is held pending the full incorporated-condition check above | Repatriable; may continue to contracted maturity after return, then convert to resident deposit or eligible RFC. Continued deposit permission does not decide tax status | RBI Q 3 table; https://www.rbi.org.in/commonperson/Upload/English/FAQs/PDFs/Accountresidents16012025.pdf ; https://egazette.gov.in/WriteReadData/2025/265620.pdf ; https://wm.incometaxindia.gov.in/w/tax-free-incomes |

Redesignate existing resident accounts to NRO when FEMA status changes. Update demat, fund folios, KYC, mandates and nominees; obtain the authorised dealer/depository's applicable permitted investment route. **Exact demat conversion/PIS requirements by instrument are not yet confirmed here**; do not require PIS universally merely because the original mentioned it.

The USD 1,000,000 (in words on the page) facility is not a universal cap on all rent, dividends and interest. Nor does a large property sale automatically fit it: verify asset eligibility, other remittances, source/tax evidence and bank requirements. Sequencing across Indian financial years may be considered only after legal eligibility and taxes are settled; never split to conceal transactions or misstate source. NRE/FCNR repatriability is not a waiver of documentation or tax.

**PPF/NSC:** General Rules 3(g) defines NRI by non-residence under the Income-tax Act, 1961; this is separate from the FEMA residence label used for bank accounts. General Rules 4 limits new accounts to eligible resident citizens, and 4(3) allows an account whose holder subsequently becomes NRI to continue to maturity on a non-repatriation basis, with no extension/continuation beyond maturity even if otherwise permissible. Rule 9(4) preserves interest only to maturity for that case. PPF 2019 paragraph 16 and NSC 2019 paragraph 10 apply General Rules where their schemes make no provision. This does **not** justify the old blanket instruction to stop all contributions to a valid predeparture PPF account. Notify the accounts office; distinguish continuation within lawful maturity from a new account or prohibited extension; irregular/legacy extensions and any later special directions are referred before further deposits. [General Rules 3(g), 4, 9](https://www.nsiindia.gov.in/InternalPage.aspx?Id_Pk=149); [PPF paragraphs 2-6, 11-12, 16](https://www.nsiindia.gov.in/InternalPage.aspx?Id_Pk=169); [NSC paragraphs 2-3, 10](https://www.nsiindia.gov.in/InternalPage.aspx?Id_Pk=167).

### Part 4: Destination differences

#### 4.1 UAE

Natural-person wages, qualifying personal-investment income and qualifying unlicensed real-estate-investment income are outside UAE corporate tax. This is narrower than a universal statement that all investments/business are tax-free. A natural person conducting business can enter corporate tax where calendar-year business turnover exceeds AED 1,000,000; that is a **turnover gateway**, separate from taxable-income rates. Qualifying wages/personal/real-estate investment income do not enter that turnover gateway. [FTA natural-person bulletin Q 1-6](https://www.tax.gov.ae/Datafolder/Files/Pdf/2024/CT%20Bulletin/Basic%20Tax%20Information%20bulletin-%20Natural%20Person-English.pdf).

Article 13 allows India tax on Indian immovable-property gains, qualifying PE/fixed-base movable property, property-rich shares and other shares of an Indian-resident company, but residual other property falls under the residence-only provision. It does **not** say every asset connected with India is always India-taxable. Classify the asset before asserting a treaty exemption. Article 29 as modified by MLI has a principal-purpose test. A TRC is necessary documentation for an Indian nonresident treaty claim, not guaranteed entitlement or a universal deemed-residence defence. [Articles 4, 13, 29](https://www.incometaxindia.gov.in/documents/20117/6715095/Synthesized-text-India-UAE-DTAA.pdf/25244922-8e6a-1569-81f3-04f9fcc29015?download=true&t=1762943463017&version=1.0); [Act 159](https://egazette.gov.in/WriteReadData/2025/265620.pdf).

The Third Protocol was signed 30 December 2016, entered force 27 February 2017, and its share-gains changes apply 1 April 2017. Article 13(4A) preserves a residence-only route for shares acquired **before 1 April 2017**, subject Article 24A limitation of benefits and MLI PPT; Article 13(4B) permits company-residence-state tax on shares acquired **on or after 1 April 2017**. For shares in an Indian resident company, India may tax; residence in Singapore does not itself exempt new acquisitions. The reduced transition rate for gains in 1 April 2017-31 March 2019 is historical, not a 2026 concession. Other Article 13 property categories must still be tested. Do not declare every old treaty structure invalid without classifying its acquisitions and substance. [Main Article 13 as replaced by Annex D (2005 Protocol) Article 1 and subsequently amended by Annex B (Third Protocol) Articles 2-4; MLI Annex A](https://www.iras.gov.sg/media/docs/default-source/dtas/protocol-amending-singapore-india-dta-%28ratified%29%28mli%29%281-oct-2019%29.pdf).

**Indian EPF retained as a held topic:** the original suggested withdrawal after a qualifying non-employment period or keeping the balance, and automatic taxability of post-employment interest. Those specific current conditions are **not yet confirmed** here. The EPFO scheme PDF was blocked in direct fetch and an ordinary browser on 15 November 2026. CPF exemption does not decide Indian EPF .

#### 4.3 What neither destination automatically changes

Indian source-income, lawful withholding, filing and FEMA duties remain subject to their own tests. Singapore estate duty was removed for deaths **on or after 15 February 2008**; estate income and succession administration remain separate. [IRAS estate-duty page](https://www.iras.gov.sg/taxes/other-taxes/estate-duty/estate-duty). The original broad claims of no Indian/UAE inheritance tax and no estate-tax layer anywhere are **not yet confirmed from the sources read here**. ## Worked cases

### Case 1: compare treaty dividend caps for an individual

Hypothetical INR 1,000,000 ordinary Indian-company dividend; separately assume a qualifying UAE resident or Singapore resident individual beneficial owner, no connected PE/fixed base, full TRC/Form 41, anti-abuse clearance, and treaty entitlement. Compare **treaty maximum source-tax amounts**, not a final return, domestic cess/surcharge computation or an instruction to pay. UAE Article 10 cap 10%; Singapore Article 10 individual cap 15%. UAE Article 10 states 10% without the express gross-amount phrase used in Singapore Article 10. This illustration applies the UAE cap to the stated dividend amount as that reading; it does not resolve a disputed tax base. The original both 10% statement understates the latter cap by INR 50,000. Inputs hypothetical; outputs derived. [UAE ](https://www.incometaxindia.gov.in/documents/20117/6715095/Synthesized-text-India-UAE-DTAA.pdf/25244922-8e6a-1569-81f3-04f9fcc29015?download=true&t=1762943463017&version=1.0); [Singapore](https://www.iras.gov.sg/media/docs/default-source/dtas/protocol-amending-singapore-india-dta-%28ratified%29%28mli%29%281-oct-2019%29.pdf).

```oa-check
name: India UAE and Singapore individual dividend caps
inputs:
  dividend: 1000000
steps:
  uae_cap: dividend * 0.10
  singapore_individual_cap: dividend * 0.15
  difference: singapore_individual_cap - uae_cap
expected:
  uae_cap: 100000
  singapore_individual_cap: 150000
  difference: 50000
```

### Case 2: distinguish current income from the annual balance facility

Hypothetical eligible NRI/PIO has USD 1,400,000 documented NRO funds: USD 200,000 eligible current income and USD 1,200,000 other eligible balances/assets. Taxes and bank conditions assumed satisfied; no other facility use. Current income uses its separate route, and only USD 1,000,000 of other balances fits the annual facility: USD 200,000 of other balances remains for a future lawful route/year. Total illustrated current-year remittance USD 1,200,000 does not mean USD 1,400,000 can all leave or all NRO fundsare capped at USD 1,000,000. This is a classification arithmetic check, not bank authorization. Inputs hypothetical; outputs derived. ## Filing and payment

### Part 5: sequenced checklist retained

**Before departure**

1. Compute departure-year status from actual days and citizen employment/crew conditions; fewer than 182 days helps only the current day test. Preserve offer letter/visa/travel proof and separately test deemed residence/RNOR.
2. Notify bank of FEMA change; resident to NRO and eligible NRE/FCNR setup; update KYC/demat/fund folios/nominee mandates. Confirm instrument route and valid existing PPF continuation rather than blanket stop.
3. Prospective NRI landlord/property seller: brief payer on 393(2), obtain 395 review/certificate or appropriate taxable proportion where appropriate before paying/signing; final assessment remains separate.
4. If defined non-foreign income exceeds INR 1,500,000, document liability elsewhere and actual UAE/Singapore residence; obtain treaty TRC but do not call it an automatic deemed residence defence.

**First years abroad**

5. Set visit budget from all applicable tests and lookbacks; keep actual presence log, including weddings/winter stays; no invented midnight-only count.
6. Determine Indian filing obligation/refund and form; reconcile TDS and credits; claim treaty only with documentation and entitlement. For current Act nonresident treaty relief, 159(8) requires TRC and rule 75 Form 41 with support; old 10F is earlier period, not current form. [Act 159](https://egazette.gov.in/WriteReadData/2025/265620.pdf); [rule 75](https://www.incometax.gov.in/iec/foportal/sites/default/files/2026-03/En-Notified-IT-Rules-2026-20-03-2026.pdf).
7. Before property/fund sale, settle classification, withholding/certificates and reinvestment review; separate current income from eligible annual balance facility and aggregate prior remittances. Banks must verify lawful eligibility; sequencing is not permission to split false information.

**On return**

8. Compute each year's RNOR/ROR status before foreign gain/payout/restructuring plans; test section 5 receipt/source/control conditions and destination tax separately. Notify bank promptly under actual status change rules; NRE resident/eligible RFC redesignation, FCNR maturity transition and the held FCNR exemption require separate checks. No assumed 2-3-year window.

### Current remittance information forms: not every remittance needs a CA certificate

Rule 220 prescribes **Form 145/146**, not blanket old 15CA/15CB for TY 2026-27. For chargeable sums, Form 145 Part A where payment/year aggregate at most INR 500,000; Part B above INR 500,000 with AO 395(1)/(2) certificate/order; Part C above threshold with accountant Form 146 when Part B not furnished. Nonchargeable sums use Part D unless 220(3) exception applies, including qualifying individual remittances not requiring prior RBI approval and listed purposes. Form 145 must be submitted in prescribed electronic/signed manner and given to the authorised dealer before remitting under 220(4). This is an information-form classification, not an assertion every NRO transfer is chargeable or requires Form 146. Current portal box/utility steps must be confirmed before execution; 15CA/15CB earlier period conditions are not projected here. [Full rule 220](https://www.incometax.gov.in/iec/foportal/sites/default/files/2026-03/En-Notified-IT-Rules-2026-20-03-2026.pdf).

For the Indian return, prepare the residence/income/treaty working paper, choose prescribed form under 164, gather supported TDS/tax payments and documents, then follow the current return assembly skill; check actual utility and verification/acknowledgement before payment or filing. No 2027 box numbers or automatic filing deadline extension is assumed. Retain Form 41/TRC/withholding certificates/bank remittance references and then reconcile the processed return and refund rather than promise a timing.
