# Sao Tome and Principe VAT v0.2

> **Sourced from the law, in part.** Tax year 2026. The Finance Ministry site refused access on 1 November 2026, so the tax codes themselves were not read. Rows marked unverified carry older figures that no official page has confirmed. No accountant has signed this version and it has had no independent review yet.

## Who this applies to

Businesses, professionals, farmers, fishers and public bodies acting independently are taxable persons under [VAT Code art. 3](https://www2.camara.leg.br/saotomeeprincipe/diarios-da-an/xi-legislatura-22-11-2018/ii-serie-a/2.a-sessao/DAN30-IIS.pdf). Employees are not. Law 13/2019 approved the code. Law 2/2023 and Decree-Law 12/2023 changed it; those two texts were not read, so check any point below against them.

The tax replaced earlier consumption taxes. The older skill named the general consumption tax, services tax, taxes on alcohol, spirits and cigarettes, telecoms and hotel tax, and restaurant tax; that list is unverified. The code read shows art. 2 revocations, not checked line by line.

## The base

Tax due is taxable operations times the rate, less deductible input tax under arts. 21 to 26 ([art. 28(3)](https://www2.camara.leg.br/saotomeeprincipe/diarios-da-an/xi-legislatura-22-11-2018/ii-serie-a/2.a-sessao/DAN30-IIS.pdf)). Tax on some items is deductible at 50% only (art. 22(3)). A business on the exemption regime has no right to deduct (art. 44(4)). Exports and related transport are covered by art. 20; internal exemptions by art. 17; import exemptions by art. 18. The older skill said reduced and zero rates exist; the code read has one rate plus the half-base rule, with art. 20 relief for exports.

## Deadlines and filing

Start of activity: declare 15 days before it begins (art. 31). Changes: within 30 days (art. 32). Cessation: within 30 days (art. 33). Invoice: within 8 days (art. 34).

The periodic return is monthly and goes in electronically by the last day of the following month, with payment at the same time ([arts. 29(1)(c), 28(1), 37](https://www2.camara.leg.br/saotomeeprincipe/diarios-da-an/xi-legislatura-22-11-2018/ii-serie-a/2.a-sessao/DAN30-IIS.pdf)). A nil return is still required (art. 29(3)). Import VAT is paid by the importer (art. 28(5)).

How-to, portal, form boxes: unverified. The Tax Directorate page for this task could not be opened.

## Penalties and interest

If a return is missing, the Directorate assesses from past returns or other data, and tax is due within 30 days of notice. Non-payment starts the procedure in art. 79 of the Tax Process Code and can lead to suspension of taxpayer status or a frozen bank account. Filing the return and paying with compensatory interest within that period cancels the assessment ([VAT Code art. 50](https://www2.camara.leg.br/saotomeeprincipe/diarios-da-an/xi-legislatura-22-11-2018/ii-serie-a/2.a-sessao/DAN30-IIS.pdf)). Penalty amounts: unverified.

## Cross-border points

A taxable buyer accounts for the tax on services located in STP when the supplier is non-resident with no permanent establishment ([VAT Code art. 27(2)](https://www2.camara.leg.br/saotomeeprincipe/diarios-da-an/xi-legislatura-22-11-2018/ii-serie-a/2.a-sessao/DAN30-IIS.pdf)). Place of supply is in art. 10: for a taxable buyer, where the buyer has its seat or establishment. Non-resident taxpayers have their own duties in art. 30.

## Two worked examples that recompute

**Example 1, normal month.** Standard sales of Db 100,000, listed food invoices of Db 20,000, deductible input tax of Db 8,000.

```oa-check
name: Monthly VAT
inputs:
  standard: 100000
  food: 20000
  input_vat: 8000
steps:
  - output: standard * 0.15 + food * 0.5 * 0.15
  - payable: output - input_vat
expected:
  output: 16500
  payable: 8500
```

**Example 2, decision: stay on the exemption regime or opt out.** A trader with sales of Db 800,000 pays 7% with no deduction. Compare with normal VAT on margin if customers can bear it; the art. 48 text is the 2019 version, so confirm first. If purchases carrying VAT are Db 500,000, normal VAT is lower.

```oa-check
name: Exemption regime against normal VAT
inputs:
  sales: 800000
  purchases: 500000
steps:
  - special: sales * 0.07
  - normal: (sales - purchases) * 0.15
  - saving: special - normal
expected:
  special: 56000
  normal: 45000
  saving: 11000
```

## Ask the client first

- Turnover in the last calendar year, and legal form
- Any sales of Annex I food
- Any services bought from non-residents
- Imports or exports
- Whether it opted out of the exemption regime
