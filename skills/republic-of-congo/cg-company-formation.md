# Republic of Congo Company Formation and Entity Choice v1.1

> **General reference only.** This skill is general tax/accounting reference material for AI-assisted workflows. It has not been reviewed for any specific person's facts, documents, elections, deadlines, residency, filing status, or local procedures. Do not rely on it to file, pay, amend, or take a tax position without review by a qualified professional in the relevant jurisdiction.

**Draft scope:** Persons forming or maintaining a Congo company/branch in 2026. Company-law sources are the fetched 2014 uniform act and 2017 national decree, with the public 2026 ACPCE route. Later national amendments and live submission are not fully verified. Independent pack review is pending. Initial 2026 law is not represented as a fully consolidated or operational 2026 position.

## Who this applies to

Persons forming or maintaining a Congo company/branch in 2026. Company-law sources are the fetched 2014 uniform act and 2017 national decree, with the public 2026 ACPCE route. Later national amendments and live submission are not fully verified.

## Rates, bands and thresholds

| Item | Rule and conditions | Authority |
|---|---|---|
| Congo SARL capital | National decree 2017-41 art 4 lets members set SARL capital freely in the articles; art 5 requires equal shares with nominal value at least XAF 5,000. The OHADA default XAF 1,000,000 expressly yields to national rules and is not Congo's required minimum under this decree. | [Decree2017-41 arts4–5](https://acpce.cg/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/congo-decret_2017-41_sarl.pdf); ledger F02 |

| SA and SAS capital | Ordinary SA minimum is XAF 10,000,000; cash subscription at least one-quarter paid, remainder within 3 years after RCCM. Do not apply SA minimum mechanically to SAS. | [OHADA387–389,824,853-4–5](https://acpce.cg/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/ACTE-UNIFORME-REVISE-RELATIF-AU-DROIT-DES-SOCIETES-COMMERCIALES-ET-DU-GROUPEMENT-DINTERET-ECONOMIQUE.pdf); ledger F04 |

These are displayed creation taxes/fees, not paid-in capital or a quote for professional work. Applicable exemptions, amendments and ancillary costs require confirmation before payment. | [ACPCE public portal, Taxe unique de Création](https://monentreprise.acpce.cg/); ledger F10 |

## The base

Select the legal form using F01. Prepare articles and appointments; verify the actual capital subscription, valuation and deposit rules for that form. Assemble F06–F08 documents and separate creation fees from capital. The national SARL rule supersedes the old default-minimum assertion. Plan accounting/audit under F13/F14 and the linked tax Guides.

| Item | Rule and conditions | Authority |
|---|---|---|
| Entity choice | OHADA 309 permits a one-or-more-member SARL with liability limited to contributions; 385 permits an SA with a single shareholder; 853-1 permits one-or-more-member SAS with articles setting governance subject to mandatory rules. A branch lacks separate legal personality under 117. Choose governance, liability and regulated-activity requirements before comparing tax. | [OHADA309,385,853-1,117](https://acpce.cg/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/ACTE-UNIFORME-REVISE-RELATIF-AU-DROIT-DES-SOCIETES-COMMERCIALES-ET-DU-GROUPEMENT-DINTERET-ECONOMIQUE.pdf); ledger F01 |
| SARL form of articles | Congo decree 2 permits private or notarised SARL articles; deposit of privately signed articles in notarial minutes is not compulsory. 3 lists required company, capital, shareholder, contribution and governance particulars. The simplification is not a blanket exemption from preparing valid articles or capital evidence. | [Decree2017-41 arts2–3](https://acpce.cg/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/congo-decret_2017-41_sarl.pdf); ledger F05 |
| Registered office | OHADA 25 requires a sufficiently precise address/geographic location, not only a post-office box. ACPCE accepts ownership, lease, utility/internet or other office evidence under its public checklist; do not invent a mandatory separate commercial-office lease. | [OHADA25;ACPCE creation checklist](https://acpce.cg/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/ACTE-UNIFORME-REVISE-RELATIF-AU-DROIT-DES-SOCIETES-COMMERCIALES-ET-DU-GROUPEMENT-DINTERET-ECONOMIQUE.pdf); ledger F06 |
| SARL audit thresholds | OHADA 376 requires at least 1 statutory auditor if 2of3conditions at year-end hold: balance sheet over XAF 125,000,000, annual turnover over XAF 250,000,000, permanent employees over 50. Falling outside 2criteria for the 2 years before mandate expiry releases the obligation; a qualifying minority can seek appointment in court even below thresholds. | [OHADA376](https://acpce.cg/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/ACTE-UNIFORME-REVISE-RELATIF-AU-DROIT-DES-SOCIETES-COMMERCIALES-ET-DU-GROUPEMENT-DINTERET-ECONOMIQUE.pdf); ledger F13 |
| SA audit and annual compliance | OHADA 694 requires SA audit by one or more statutory auditors. Tax DSF/accounts requirements are separate under CGI 130–131 and the corporate Guide. Keep governance, accounting, tax and beneficial-ownership filings as separate checklists; complete current filing obligations are not all verified here. | [OHADA694;CGI130–131](https://acpce.cg/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/ACTE-UNIFORME-REVISE-RELATIF-AU-DROIT-DES-SOCIETES-COMMERCIALES-ET-DU-GROUPEMENT-DINTERET-ECONOMIQUE.pdf); ledger F14 |

## Cross-border points

A foreign branch is not a separate legal person, and its OHADA conversion obligation does not decide PE or withholding tax. Use [cross-border](cg-cross-border.md) and [corporate tax](cg-corporate-income-tax.md). Foreign-founder identity/residency and regulated-activity approvals remain separate facts.

## Two worked examples that recompute

### 1. SARL share and deposit planning

Founders choose XAF 300,000 cash capital under F02, with shares at the minimum 5,000, giving 60shares. Assuming the ordinary 311-1 cash rule applies, at least 150,000 is paid at subscription and 150,000 remains within the statutory 2 year RCCM clock. F10's separate displayed creation fee is not part of that capital.

```oa-check
name: SARL share and deposit planning
inputs:
  chosen_capital: 300000
  share_nominal: 5000
steps:
  - shares: chosen_capital / share_nominal
  - initial_cash_minimum: chosen_capital * 0.50
  - remaining_cash: chosen_capital - initial_cash_minimum
expected:
  shares: 60
  initial_cash_minimum: 150000
  remaining_cash: 150000
```

### 2. Decision: distinguish entity fee from capital

For a proposed SARL using chosen capital 300,000, the displayed creation fee 300,000 gives 600,000 when comparing fee plus full intended capital. For an ordinary SA with minimum capital 10,000,000 and displayed fee 500,000, the comparable total is 10,500,000. Difference 9,900,000 is a funding/fee distinction, not a full cost estimate or recommendation. SA initial paid-in capital can differ from full subscribed capital; governance and fundraising requirements still decide form.

```oa-check
name: Decision: distinguish entity fee from capital
inputs:
  sarl_capital: 300000
  sarl_fee: 300000
  sa_capital: 10000000
  sa_fee: 500000
steps:
  - sarl_fee_plus_full_capital: sarl_capital + sarl_fee
  - sa_fee_plus_full_capital: sa_capital + sa_fee
  - difference: sa_fee_plus_full_capital - sarl_fee_plus_full_capital
expected:
  sarl_fee_plus_full_capital: 600000
  sa_fee_plus_full_capital: 10500000
  difference: 9900000
```

## Ask the client first

- Proposed form, members, governance and fundraising?
- Activities and required licences?
- Foreign participants/branch, residence and identity documents?
- Cash/in-kind contributions, deposit and office proof?
- Balance sheet, turnover, staff and auditor?
