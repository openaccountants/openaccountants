# Republic of Congo Cross-Border and Nonresident Tax v1.0

> **General reference only.** This skill is general tax/accounting reference material for AI-assisted workflows. It has not been reviewed for any specific person's facts, documents, elections, deadlines, residency, filing status, or local procedures. Do not rely on it to file, pay, amend, or take a tax position without review by a qualified professional in the relevant jurisdiction.

**Draft scope:** Congo enterprises, foreign companies, branches, service suppliers and investors. This Guide does not certify a treaty network, current tax-residence certificate process or complete departure clearance. Independent pack review is pending. Initial 2026 law is not represented as a fully consolidated or operational 2026 position.

## Who this applies to

Congo enterprises, foreign companies, branches, service suppliers and investors. This Guide does not certify a treaty network, current tax-residence certificate process or complete departure clearance.

## Rates, bands and thresholds

| Item | Rule and conditions | Authority |
|---|---|---|
| Project and service PE tests | 4A lists fixed places and deems a construction/assembly/installation/supervision project a PE when lasting more than 3 months. Services through personnel for the same/connected project create a PE when exceeding 183 days in any 12 months. Equality alone does not satisfy these specific duration limbs, but a fixed place or agency can independently create a PE. | [CGI4A(1)–(2)](https://sgg.cg/JO/2026/congo-jo-2026-3-3.pdf); ledger X02 |
| Nonresident services/royalties | Subject to applicable treaties,86D imposes 20% IS withholding on payments by a resident company or for a nonresident company's PE for services rendered by nonresidents or royalties paid to them. Royalties include rights, equipment, know-how and software acquisition/rental as stated. Classify actual contracts first; do not apply the old miscellaneous reduced-rate list by default. | [CGI86D](https://sgg.cg/JO/2026/congo-jo-2026-3-3.pdf); ledger X05 |

## The base

Identify the legal person, effective management and actual operations. Test X01–X04 before selecting an ordinary PE or special intermittent regime. Classify each payment by nature and recipient, locate performance and source, then test any actually applicable treaty. Keep contracts, duration logs, personnel schedules, beneficial-ownership evidence and foreign tax documents.

| Item | Rule and conditions | Authority |
|---|---|---|
| Corporate territory | Enterprises registered in Congo or effectively managed there are resident enterprises; non-residents with a Congo PE are operated in Congo. Nonresident PE profit attribution includes the PE, same/similar goods sales and same/similar industrial/commercial activities in Congo under 7. Do not assume worldwide exemption or only invoiced branch profits. | [CGI4,7](https://sgg.cg/JO/2026/congo-jo-2026-3-3.pdf); ledger X01 |
| Preparatory exceptions and fragmentation | Storage/exhibition, processing by another enterprise, purchasing/information and other listed activities need the relevant preparatory/auxiliary limitation. Anti-fragmentation denies the exception for linked complementary business functions, an existing PE or a combined non-auxiliary activity. Do not split one coherent business across sites to claim a warehouse exemption. | [CGI4A(3)–(4)](https://sgg.cg/JO/2026/congo-jo-2026-3-3.pdf); ledger X03 |
| Agency, insurance and control | Habitual contract conclusion/principal negotiating role or habitual stock delivery can create an agency PE. Nonresident insurers have the stated premium/risk rule except reinsurance. An ordinary independent agent can fall outside these limbs; near-exclusive activity for closely linked enterprises prevents independence. Control alone does not create a PE. 4A(8)–(9) define close links, including more-than 50% tests, but other actual control is relevant. | [CGI4A(5)–(9)](https://sgg.cg/JO/2026/congo-jo-2026-3-3.pdf); ledger X04 |
| Foreign receipts and double tax | 11 brings foreign PE results belonging to a CEMAC company and foreign dividends, interest, royalties and service fees of an enterprise operated in Congo into the stated tax base. Double tax can be relieved by foreign-tax deduction or a credit limited to Congo tax on the same income, subject to actual entitlement. Foreign PE losses are restricted to later PE profits within 5 years. Do not use both relief routes for the same amount. | [CGI11](https://sgg.cg/JO/2026/congo-jo-2026-3-3.pdf); ledger X06 |
| Special intermittent foreign enterprise | 92 concerns foreign legal entities whose intermittent/precarious activities do not permit the ordinary 86C–F regime. 92A fixes 22% of qualifying tax-exclusive Congo turnover as deemed profit and 92C points to 86A, whose foreign-person rate is 33%. Thus 7.26% is the derived IS alone, not a universal service withholding rate or all-in burden. Mobilisation, reimbursed supplies and chain subcontract exclusions have cumulative conditions not certified in this short computation. | [CGI92–92C](https://sgg.cg/JO/2026/congo-jo-2026-3-3.pdf); ledger X07 |
| Nonresident securities gains | 107 taxes nonresident direct/indirect gains on shares of a Congo-registered company where the person alone/with linked companies held at least 10% of capital in the preceding 365 days. This test does not eliminate other property-rich gain rules. Use the income Guide for the IRCM base/rate; 106 fusion relief has its own conditions. | [CGI107](https://sgg.cg/JO/2026/congo-jo-2026-3-3.pdf); ledger X10 |

## Deadlines and filing

X08 states only the special-regime statutory declarations and departure trigger. The actual forms and clearance procedure, withholding payment timing for 86D, and ordinary annual deadline conflict remain open. A contract's payment date does not by itself establish the PE test.

| Item | Rule and conditions | Authority |
|---|---|---|
| Special-regime declarations and departure | 92 requires existence declaration, temporary operating authority and amendments; service declaration/payment by 15th of the second month following service execution, and liquidation by cessation before mobilised people/equipment depart. 92D requires a supplier-by-supplier statement naming contract, address, tax and billing month/amount. This is distinct from ordinary annual IS. | [CGI92(2),92D](https://sgg.cg/JO/2026/congo-jo-2026-3-3.pdf); ledger X08 |

## Penalties and interest

X09 is special-regime text, not the general penalty for an ordinary cross-border payment. Do not choose whichever statutory paragraph gives the smallest amount without establishing scope and hierarchy.

| Item | Rule and conditions | Authority |
|---|---|---|
| Special-regime defaults | 92E provides XAF 100,000 per missing/incomplete invoice element; non/partial payment after 8-day demand can incur 100% of evaded principal; late payment before demand has 1% per working day capped 50%. The within-current-month deferral rule instead has XAF 500,000 without that interest. Classification/overlap with 374ter must be settled before using a penalty calculation. | [CGI92E](https://sgg.cg/JO/2026/congo-jo-2026-3-3.pdf); ledger X09 |

## Two worked examples that recompute

### 1. Nonresident royalty withholding

Assume a resident Congo company pays gross royalty XAF 10,000,000 to a nonresident within 86D, with no applicable treaty reduction and no gross-up clause. 20% withholding is 2,000,000 and net payment 8,000,000. VAT, foreign tax and other charges are outside this example.

```oa-check
name: Nonresident royalty withholding
inputs:
  gross: 10000000
steps:
  - withholding: gross * 0.20
  - net_payment: gross - withholding
expected:
  withholding: 2000000
  net_payment: 8000000
```

### 2. Decision: classify before using deemed margin

For gross Congo receipts 10,000,000, an enterprise genuinely qualifying under 92 with no allowable gross-base exclusions has deemed profit 2,200,000 and IS 726,000. Treating the same amount as an 86D payment instead gives 2,000,000 withholding. The difference 1,274,000 explains why classification and permits must be settled. These are alternative legal fact patterns, not freely elective rates and not taxes to stack.

```oa-check
name: Decision: classify before using deemed margin
inputs:
  receipts: 10000000
steps:
  - deemed_profit: receipts * 0.22
  - special_is: deemed_profit * 0.33
  - ordinary_withholding: receipts * 0.20
  - difference: ordinary_withholding - special_is
expected:
  deemed_profit: 2200000
  special_is: 726000
  ordinary_withholding: 2000000
  difference: 1274000
```

## Ask the client first

- Legal entity, registration and effective management?
- Fixed places, contracts, stock, agents and project/day counts?
- Service/royalty/goods/payment facts and permits?
- Actual treaty, recipient residence and beneficial entitlement?
- Foreign income/tax proof and departure or sale facts?
