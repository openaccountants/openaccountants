# Forming a company in Germany (GmbH, UG, AG and partnerships)

## Scope

German formation in 2026: compare the legal forms, then follow the cash GmbH/UG process below. The matrix retains AG, sole trader, GbR, OHG and KG choices; their specialist formation work is held for advice. Currency: EUR. A referral below is a non-usable hold: do not infer an outcome or a fee from it.

## Ask the client first

- Who will own the business, and is a founder itself a GbR? Which legal form and activity are proposed?
- What capital and cash can each founder pay before filing? Any assets contributed, purchases from founders, repayments or earlier contracts?
- Who will manage it? Any capacity restrictions, professional bans or criminal convictions, including abroad?
- Where will the seat, business address and actual management be? Which identity documents and electronic identification are available?
- When did formation, preparatory work and trading begin? What turnover, employees and cross-border transactions are expected?

## The method, step by step

### 1. Choose the form and fund it

Use the boundary matrix below. For a GmbH, [§ 5 GmbHG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/gmbhg/__5.html) sets minimum share capital at twenty-five thousand euros. Every share is a whole-euro nominal amount and the shares must total the capital. Under [§ 7(2) GmbHG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/gmbhg/__7.html), a cash formation must satisfy **both** tests before the register application: at least one quarter of each cash share and at least half the statutory minimum in total (computed EUR 12,500). Unpaid capital remains owed ([§ 19(2) GmbHG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/gmbhg/__19.html)); this is not a reduction in the stated capital.

A UG is a GmbH formed below that minimum. Whole-euro shares make one euro the lowest possible capital (derived from § 5(2) and [§ 5a GmbHG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/gmbhg/__5a.html)). Its name must contain “Unternehmergesellschaft (haftungsbeschränkt)” or “UG (haftungsbeschränkt)”. Before application, pay the entire capital in cash; contributions in kind are excluded. Do not use the minimum as a recommendation for the cash the business needs.

Under § 5a(3), reserve one quarter of positive annual net profit after deducting the previous year's loss carried forward; no positive remainder means no allocation. The reserve may only finance a capital increase from company funds, cover an annual loss not covered by profit brought forward, or cover a loss brought forward not covered by annual profit. The duty does not end because cash or reserves reach the GmbH minimum: § 5a(5) requires an increase of registered capital to that minimum or above. The UG name may then be retained. The mechanics and audit of that increase are held below.

### 2. Set the articles and appoint the director

[§ 3 GmbHG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/gmbhg/__3.html) requires the name, seat, business object, share capital, and the number and nominal amounts of the shares each shareholder takes. A fixed duration or additional shareholder obligations must also appear in the articles when applicable. The GmbH name includes “Gesellschaft mit beschränkter Haftung” or a generally understood abbreviation ([§ 4](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/gmbhg/__4.html)); the contractual seat is in Germany ([§ 4a](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/gmbhg/__4a.html)).

For the simplified formation under [§ 2(1a) GmbHG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/gmbhg/__2.html), there may be at most three shareholders and one managing director. Use Annex 1 without provisions departing from the statute; it also serves as the shareholder list. It provides cash contributions, with payment in full or 50% immediately and the balance when called; a UG must strike the second alternative. Custom articles, vesting and investor rights: Check this point with one of our partner accountants.

| Annex 2 video protocol | EUR 600, also capped by share capital; excess borne by the shareholder(s) | [Annex 2, clause 5](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/gmbhg/anlage_2.html) |

[§ 6 GmbHG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/gmbhg/__6.html) requires one or more managing directors, each a natural person with full legal capacity. Screen the complete exclusions in subsection 2: a relevant consent reservation, a matching professional/trade ban, and the listed intentional convictions. The listed fraud-related offences require imprisonment of at least one year; the other listed conviction categories do not share that sentencing threshold. The five-year conviction exclusion runs from final judgment **excluding time detained in an institution by official order**. Comparable EU/EEA bans and comparable foreign convictions count. Do not treat nationality or residence alone as a substitute for that screening. Director eligibility, immigration or regulated-profession uncertainty: Check this point with one of our partner accountants.

### 3. Notarise and arrange payment

Under [§ 2 GmbHG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/gmbhg/__2.html), the articles require notarial form and all shareholders sign. A representative needs a notarially executed or certified power of attorney; execution of that power may itself be by video. Video notarisation of the articles is allowed unless other form rules prevent it; remote shareholders sign with qualified electronic signatures. Annex 2 video protocols are a separate option under § 2(3); do not assume every video formation is the simplified Annex 1 procedure.

The notary uses the Federal Chamber of Notaries' system and should refuse video if duties, identification or capacity cannot reliably be established ([§ 16a BeurkG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/beurkg/__16a.html)). [§ 16c](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/beurkg/__16c.html) requires an accepted electronic identity at notified assurance level “high”: a listed German eID, or a recognised cross-border EU/EEA eID. A chip-read photograph from an accepted identity document is also required unless the notary knows the participant. Foreign location alone does not supply the required identification.

Arrange the bank/payment process with the notary and provider. Bank acceptance, “i.G.” account naming, document lists, routine bank confirmations and opening times: Check this point with one of our partner accountants. There is no verified bank-processing estimate here.

### 4. Apply to the Handelsregister and wait for entry

File at the court for the company's seat ([§ 7(1) GmbHG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/gmbhg/__7.html)); the commercial-register application is electronic and publicly certified, with video certification permitted ([§ 12 HGB](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/hgb/__12.html)). Arrange the certified filing through the notary.

Under [§ 8 GmbHG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/gmbhg/__8.html), attach the articles and any representative powers, evidence of director appointments if not in the articles, and the signed or qualified-electronically-signed shareholder list. The application states a domestic business address and the directors' representation powers. Directors assure that the required contributions have been made and are finally at their free disposal, and that none of the exclusions specified in § 8(3) applies and they have received the required instruction. That instruction can be written or given by the listed domestic/foreign professionals or consular officer. Only serious doubts entitle the court to demand contribution evidence such as payment receipts from an EU-established financial institution or payment provider. Non-cash formation, disguised contributions and money flowing back to founders: Check this point with one of our partner accountants.

Before entry, a GmbH/UG does not exist **as such**. Persons acting in its name before entry are personally and jointly liable ([§ 11 GmbHG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/gmbhg/__11.html)); this is not limited to the managing director. Pre-formation contracts and allocation of those obligations: Check this point with one of our partner accountants. Check a purported court bill against the actual court notice before paying.

**Tax comparison for an ordinary taxable GmbH/UG/AG in 2026.** Corporate income tax is 15% of taxable income ([§ 23 KStG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/kstg_1977/__23.html)); the solidarity surcharge is 5.5% of the corporate-tax assessment base, not of profit ([§§ 3–4 SolzG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/solzg_1995/__3.html)). Corporate activity counts as a trade ([§ 2(2) GewStG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/gewstg/__2.html)). On the final trade-income base, round down to full hundreds and apply the 3.5% assessment rate, then the municipality's Hebesatz ([§ 11](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/gewstg/__11.html), [§ 16](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/gewstg/__16.html)). The EUR 24,500 natural-person/partnership allowance does not apply to these corporations. There is no single national combined rate. Do not use accounting profit as the final trade-income base without adjustments. Entity-specific exemptions, partnership/owner income tax, elections and tax structuring: Check this point with one of our partner accountants.

## Worked cases

Original 2026 case inputs are retained; amounts below are arithmetic under the cited capital or trade-tax rules, not new statutory figures.

**Case 1: two founders, cash GmbH, share capital EUR 25,000.** A takes EUR 15,000 and B EUR 10,000. Their quarter minima are EUR 3,750 and EUR 2,500, totalling EUR 6,250. The simultaneous total test is EUR 12,500, so another EUR 6,250 must be paid. One valid allocation is A EUR 7,500 and B EUR 5,000. [§§ 5, 7 GmbHG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/gmbhg/__7.html).

```oa-check
name: DE GmbH formation, Case 1 cash to pay in before the register application
inputs:
  share_a: 15000
  share_b: 10000
  min_capital: 25000
steps:
  - quarter_rule: pct(share_a, 25) + pct(share_b, 25)
  - half_rule: min_capital / 2
  - required: max(quarter_rule, half_rule)
  - a_pro_rata: required * share_a / (share_a + share_b)
expected:
  quarter_rule: 6250.00
  required: 12500.00
  a_pro_rata: 7500.00
```

**Case 2: a UG instead.** For stated capital below EUR 25,000, application requires all of it paid in cash. Allocate the quarter reserve on positive annual profit after prior-year losses; reaching a reserve balance alone does not end the duty. No invented UG capital or profit input is supplied. [§ 5a GmbHG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/gmbhg/__5a.html).

The original separate minimum-payment check remains:

```oa-check
name: DE GmbH 2026, least paid in before the register application
inputs:
  minimum_capital: 25000
steps:
  - paid_in: minimum_capital * 0.5
expected:
  paid_in: 12500.00
```

**Original trade-tax check.** A GmbH has final trade income EUR 50,000 after all adjustments/loss relief. The hypothetical municipality's multiplier is 400%, not a national rate. No corporate allowance applies. EUR 50,000 × 3.5% × 4 = EUR 7,000. [§§ 11, 16 GewStG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/gewstg/__11.html).

```oa-check
name: DE GmbH 2026, trade tax, no allowance, multiplier 400%
inputs:
  trade_income: 50000
  hebesatz: 4.0
steps:
  - base: trade_income * 0.035
  - trade_tax: base * hebesatz
expected:
  trade_tax: 7000.00
```

## Completion checklist

- Both cash-payment tests (or full UG payment) satisfied; directors, articles, identity and representation checked with the notary.
- Certified application, attachments and contribution assurance complete; register entry verified before treating the company as existing as such.
- Trade, tax-office/municipal, questionnaire, transparency and accident-insurer notices tracked separately with evidence of submission.
- Standing duties calendared; a named professional has resolved any relevant holds before an answer or filing relies on them.
