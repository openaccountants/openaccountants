The ordinary gross quota uses **base imponible in Bizkaia/Álava** and **base liquidable in Gipuzkoa**. The surcharge's repeated charging clauses nevertheless refer to the quota portion corresponding to a taxable-base increase derived from the profit excess. They give four marginal percentages but do not expressly prescribe a single adjusted-profit-derived rate on the entire quota. The B/G implementing provisions define the adjusted profit and exclusions but do not specify allocation of depreciation/timing differences, nondeductible expenses, result-application corrections or usable losses across those portions. The special disapplication of arts.59.3–5/67.1 and treatment of a fiscal group as taxpayer are real rules, but they do not supply that allocation.

Ordinary general-rate companies with depreciation or routine tax/accounting adjustments remain affected. This is an ordinary computation gap, not a specialist exclusion. Medium companies are expressly excluded by the charging provisions, and ordinary micro/small 20% companies do not fall within the listed rate categories. The live corporate final reservation's words `Ordinary medium/general-company surcharge coverage` are therefore imprecise as a population description: the unresolved positive method concerns potentially chargeable general-rate companies, not an invented medium-company charge. This phase makes no public edit to that wording.
