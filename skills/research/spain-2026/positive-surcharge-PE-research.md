# Proposal to parent: retain separate PE applicability reservation

Read 27 October 2026. Evidence only; `es-foral-non-resident-tax.md` was not edited.

Recommendation: retain the existing distinction. Do not add a positive extraordinary-profit charge to all ordinary PEs simply because a corporate method is eventually confirmed. Do not state a universal exemption either on the evidence obtained here.

The three fresh actual IRNR article 19 bodies have the same material structure:

- Article 19.1 names the applicable **CIT article 56.1** rate and separately the **56.6 hydrocarbon** rate for that specified activity.
- Article 19.4(a) incorporates **Title V Chapters II and III deductions**, with the **article 59** minimum rules. Article 19.4(b) separately incorporates the article 68 payments rules.
- The extraordinary-profit charges sit in **Bizkaia 57bis / Álava 56.7 / Gipuzkoa 56.7**, in the preceding gross-quota/rate chapter. Those provisions are not expressly named by article 19.1 or brought in merely by its Chapter II/III deductions reference.
- The charges refer to taxpayers of the respective corporate tax. IRNR article 3 requires interpretation in concordance with PIT/CIT, but that wording is not an explicit numerical incorporation of the separate positive charge. Corporate article 11's ordinary taxpayer population concerns entities resident in Spain; a foreign entity with a PE must be analysed through IRNR's own charging provisions.

A source sufficient to close the remaining question would be a provincial IRNR amendment expressly incorporating that surcharge, a binding interpretation establishing incorporation through the existing provisions, or another express applicable legal route. No such authority was found by this research. This is a precise applicability issue, not an assertion that every corporate gap automatically blocks every ordinary PE computation.

If the parent wants a clearer source sentence in a later independent change, a source-supported proposal is:

> IRNR19.1 expressly names CIT56.1 and, for its specified hydrocarbon activity,56.6. Its19.4 deduction reference concerns TitleV ChaptersII/III and59 minimum rules. These references do not expressly name the separate B57bis/A56.7/G56.7 positive surcharge. Its application to a particular PE therefore remains an independently unresolved incorporation question; the existing corporate amount and Álava implementation reservations also remain where application is established.

This proposed sentence preserves the current Guide's reservation. It does not supply or certify the missing incorporation and should not be used to claim that no PE ever owes the charge.
