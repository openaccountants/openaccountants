## 1. Non-intra-EU reverse charge: replacement text

> For a general-rule B2B service received in the Spanish VAT territory from a supplier established outside the EU, declare its full taxable base and output VAT in12/13. If the input is an ordinary service or current good, put its unprorated base in28 and its legally deductible VAT in29; an actual art108 tangible investment good uses30/31. These are domestic-located acquisitions for this classification, not customs imports merely because the supplier is overseas. Customs import input remains32–35, and qualifying intra-EU input36–39. Apply private-use, blocked-input and prorrata limits to the input quota; do not reduce output13 by the prorrata. A US service of1,000 at21%, shared within a60% deduction-entitling business, gives12=1,000,13=210,28=1,000,29=126 and84 net VAT before other transactions/credits. A service recorded as an accounting intangible does not become an art108 tangible investment good solely through capitalisation.

Primary chain: [LIVA69/84/92/108](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-1992-28740#a69), [AEAT international services](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/iva/iva-operaciones-comercio-exterior/prestaciones-servicios.html), [current2026 instructions,12/13 and28–39](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/todas-gestiones/impuestos-tasas/iva/modelo-303-iva-autoliquidacion_/instrucciones-2026/instrucciones-02-12-2t-4t-2026.html), [HAC27/2026 approved303](https://www.boe.es/buscar/doc.php?id=BOE-A-2026-1761). The current instructions expressly require unprorated bases and deductible quotas after prorrata. The28/29 versus30/31 mapping is an application of the official domestic/current-versus-investment form categories to the statutory service location. I did not find an AEAT page explicitly phrased as a US-provider worked example and do not claim one exists. Foreign supplier nationality does not create a new input-row category.

## 2. Investment acquisition value: confirmed ordinary boundary and cost assembly

> For art108, exclude an item whose acquisition value is **below3,005.06**; equality is not excluded by that monetary limb. Apply this to the individual qualifying asset, not a yearly basket. For an ordinary acquisition with recoverable VAT, use acquisition value **excluding that recoverable VAT**, including directly attributable costs required to bring the asset into working condition. Thus3,000+630 recoverable VAT remains below the threshold despite a3,630 invoice total. A qualifying machine costing2,900 plus150 directly attributable installation has acquisition value3,050 and crosses the monetary limb; unrelated repairs/replacement parts do not become investment goods merely because expensive. Check the tangible/work-instrument/>one-year definition and the other exclusions separately.

Primary support: [LIVA108](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-1992-28740#a108) excludes **inferior**, not≤. [Official Bizkaia/Batuz worked exampleKA-01732](https://dfb.microsoftcrmportals.com/es-ES/Articulo/?Code=KA-01732) expressly treats3,000+21%VAT as below3,005.06 and saysIVAexcluded. Its official-government ownership is visible in the page header/footer and links to the actual government LROE application; it is administrative guidance, not a binding State DGT ruling. [DGT V0055-19,4January2019](https://petete.tributos.hacienda.gob.es/consultas/?num_consulta=V0055-19) was read in full in the ordinary browser:50k machinery plus2k/2.5k separate-provider setup costs. It expressly applies PGCNRV2 to the acquisition-value assembly for art108 and includes necessary setup costs in the machine's value. [PGCNRV2, initial valuation/price](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2007-19884) excludes directly recoverable indirect tax and includes necessary directly attributable pre-operation costs.

**Do not expand that sentence into a blanket assertion that every nonrecoverable indirect tax is excluded.** PGCNRV2 expressly includes indirect taxes not directly recoverable in acquisition/production cost. V0055-19 applies the accounting acquisition-value rule but does not expressly decide a partially recoverable-VAT threshold case. The ordinary fully recoverable case is closed; if an exact mixed/nondeductible-VAT boundary is decisive, distinguish that narrower issue and do not silently infer its answer from the3k fully recoverable example. This is a bounded source/application question, not a missing2027 form.

## 3. Ordinary corrective autoliquidación: replacement text

> For a2026 Modelo303, correct the same period using an autoliquidación rectificativa, retaining all unchanged data as well as corrected/new data and identifying the earlier return. This covers an increased amount payable and a reduced amount payable/refund/credit, subject to prescription and the rules for already assessed elements. **Improper VAT charged to another taxpayer** follows the rectification-request procedure under RGAT129, rather than the ordinary rectificativa route. OSS-regime corrections follow their own regime. If the sole ground is a reasoned claim that the applied rule breaches a superior legal, constitutional, EU or treaty norm, choose the art120.3 rectification-request procedure or a rectificativa; when other grounds also exist, those other grounds require a rectificativa. A disagreement with an administrative interpretation is a separate form reason: box108 is exceptional and can be used only with that reason where the adjustment cannot be entered in the normal boxes. Do not confuse correcting an earlier return with a later art80 transaction/base change whose own adjustment period is prescribed by law.
>
> For ordinary303 result reconciliation use the period's approved formula, including70 for the earlier amount payable and109 for refunds already agreed. For the ordinary case without the specialised fuel adjustment, `71=69−70+109`. Recalculating an original1,000 payable as800 gives−200 when70=1,000 and109=0. If a300 refund was already agreed and the corrected entitlement is500,69=−500,70=0,109=300 gives71=−200, avoiding a second500 refund. This arithmetic does not override the legal refund route or proof of an amount already agreed.

Primary: [RIVA74bis](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-1992-28925#a7-2), [RGAT126–129](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2007-15984#a129), [current2026 instructions, result/rectificativa reasons](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/todas-gestiones/impuestos-tasas/iva/modelo-303-iva-autoliquidacion_/instrucciones-2026/instrucciones-02-12-2t-4t-2026.html), [AEAT explanatory rectificativa page](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/ayuda/manuales-videos-folletos/manuales-practicos/manual-iva-2024/capitulo-01-novedades-destacar-2024/autoliquidacion-rectificativa.html). ## 4. Simultaneous change of business use and prorrata: bounded result

The sources establish three distinct points:

1. Art95.Tres.3 requires use-based correction by the investment-good deduction/regularisation procedure, substituting actual business use for the operation-based percentage. Art107 supplies the4/9 subsequent-year periods and strictly-over10point gate;109 compares the original deduction with the hypothetical current-year deduction and divides by5/10. Current-period use×prorrata allocation is already supplied in the Guide.
2. An **initial mistake or later proof of original use** is not automatically a later-year use change. I read [DGT V1330-17,30May2017](https://petete.tributos.hacienda.gob.es/consultas/?num_consulta=V1330-17) in full in the browser. Its vehicle was exclusively industrial from acquisition but only50% had been deducted. The DGT permits recovering the previously unexercised deduction within the four-year expiry, subject to proof/documents, while preserving later capital-adjustment obligations. It does not say every later claim of100% is a1/5 adjustment, and it does not decide simultaneous changingU/P.
3. The searched AEAT vehicle note's indexed excerpt describes regularisation on the **business-affected input pool** with a prorrata change. Its directPDF and news index no longer yield the full note here: thePDF404s and the news body has no note link. The excerpt is not treated as a fetched complete ruling. Targeted ordinary-browser DGT searches for questionprorrata+factsafectación returned the software caseV1519-09; questionprorrata+factsvehículo returned five cases about holdings, financial leasing, insurance/disposal and restructuring, not a two-variable private-use/prorrata boundary. This is a finite targeted search, not an assertion that the whole DGT database lacks such authority.

**Ready replacement clarification:**

> Keep evidence of the original use allocation, original definitive deduction, subsequent actual business-use fraction and the sector's current definitive deduction percentage separately. Determine whether new evidence corrects the original entitlement or proves a genuine later change; V1330-17's initial-error recovery must not be automatically divided by5. Apply the existing fixed-use prorrata adjustment to the affected input pool and the statutory use-change procedure to genuine later use changes. Do not add two complete original-to-current adjustments for the same input or introduce a universal10point test on the productU×P.

**Still not verified:** a general two-variable algorithm covering one/both independently material changes, especially where each moves≤10points but the product moves>10, or only one statutory gate is crossed. The literal provisions do not explicitly say to collapse the two gates into one product gate. I cannot certify that collapse. A direct109 full-entitlement diagnostic when both changes are material is mathematically `(T×Uy×Py−T×U0×P0)/n`; it is saved as an **unapproved diagnostic**, not proposed as a universal legal formula. A licensed interpretation or a directly relevant primary case must settle the exact interaction before that universal branch is represented as supplied. Ordinary fixed-use mixed input, annual prorrata, sector/startup and one-variable controls are not missing because this narrow combined interaction remains bounded.
