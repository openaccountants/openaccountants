Current Armenian texts control. The current English Tax Code translation was used only as a supporting translation and checked against the Armenian Code for the provisions used below.

## Formation claims retained

- The registration application includes the founding decision or minutes, charter, proof of state-duty payment and executive-head details. Foreign individual founder identity documents and foreign legal-founder evidence have certification and Armenian-translation requirements. Special entity laws can add documents. Registration Law Article 34.
- Paper and online filing are provided for. Ordinary registration is due no later than one working day after complete documents; two working days applies if a founder is a legal person. The applicant can request up to 30 calendar days. Immediate registration is limited to the distinct Article 17(9)–(10) filing route. Registration Law Articles 17 and 35.
- Registration assigns a registration number, taxpayer account number and social-payment account number. The entity exists from registration. Registration Law Article 35.
- The LLC Law sets no general minimum capital but allows special laws to set sector-specific minimums. It allows non-cash contributions, requires unanimous participant valuation and an independent appraiser over the statutory 500-fold threshold. Contributions are due by the founders' agreement deadline, no later than one year from registration. The AMD value of the appraisal threshold was deliberately not stated; the statutory salary basis was not reconciled to a current AMD value for this Guide. LLC Law Articles 28–30.
- An LLC may have no more than 49 participants under the general rule. LLC Law Article 11.
- The annual results meeting is held once yearly, no earlier than two months and no later than six months after year-end. LLC Law Article 37. It approves the annual report and balance sheet under Article 36.
- Registered legal persons must identify and maintain beneficial-owner information, conduct periodic due diligence at least annually, file an initial declaration within 40 days of registration, promptly report changes (within 40 days after the company learns of them at the latest), and confirm or update data by 20 February annually. Registration Law Articles 60.1–60.3. The underlying beneficial-owner criteria are in the AML Law, which was not read for this record; no percentage test is asserted.

## Tax-planning claims retained

- Ordinary resident-company profit tax is generally 18%; the Code has special taxpayer and tax-base rules. Article 125.
- Article 253 distinguishes resident commercial organisations from individual entrepreneurs: the turnover-tax regime substitutes for profit tax and/or VAT for a qualifying organisation; for an individual entrepreneur it substitutes for VAT. Article 125(3) separately sets AMD 5,000 monthly profit tax for the specified turnover-regime entrepreneur case. This does not make either regime universally available.
- The turnover-tax threshold is prior-year sales not exceeding AMD 115 million; the micro-business threshold is prior-year sales not exceeding AMD 24 million. Existing annual and new-registration statements use different dates, including the 20th day after registration. Articles 254 and 267.
- The turnover-tax exclusion includes specified regulated activity and activity classes 69, 70, 78.2 and 78.3. The micro-business exclusions are separately enumerated and broader. Articles 254(3)–(3.1) and 267(3)–(5). Full aggregation, classification and exit rules were not exhaustively reconstructed.
- Article 258 rates used in the Guide: 10% ordinary trade; 5% listed secondary raw-material trade; 1.5% newspaper editorial sales; 7% production; 10% rent, interest and royalties; 12% catering; 20% specified other catering-taxpayer income; 1% government-listed high-technology activity; 10% other assets including immovable property; and 10% other activity. The high-technology row is effective through 31 December 2031. The current Government activity list was not read, so no activity is represented as qualifying for that rate.
- For the specified trade, production, catering and other-activity branches, Article 258 permits expense reductions at 9.5%, 5%, 9% and 6%, with respective tax-base floors of 1%, 3%, 3.5% and 4.5%. It restricts deductible documentation and expense categories, permits qualifying unused reductions to carry forward, and Article 260 requires separate records or proportional allocation for specified shared administrative and sales expenses.
- Articles 261–262 set quarterly turnover-tax return and payment dates by the 20th of the next month. Article 270 sets a 1 February annual turnover report for covered micro-business taxpayers and retains separate withholding/exception reporting.

## Deliberately unresolved

The Guide does not state an incorporation-fee amount or fee waiver. The state-duty amount for the selected formation route was not verified. The end-to-end online identification route, statutory-minimum-salary base for the LLC appraisal threshold, current AML beneficial-owner test, full Article 254 aggregation and regime-exit rules, complete Article 267/Annex 3 activity classification, high-technology list, current forms and portal, and sector-specific formation rules remain open. No accountant or lawyer has reviewed or attested to the Guides.
