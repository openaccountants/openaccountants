# Monthly Prehľad sanction: bounded primary-source investigation

The ordinary fine-band reservation can be resolved on a source-supported basis: a late required monthly **Prehľad** falls under TC §154(1)(j), with the **EUR 100–10,000** range in current 2026 §155(1)(e), subject to the special ITA §39(11) trigger and §155(4) first-breach non-imposition. The annual **Hlásenie** is separately deemed a tax return; the monthly Prehľad is not made one by that sentence.

## Exact ordinary trigger

1. Establish whether a Prehľad was required for the month under ITA §39(9)(a), subject to §39(12). The current official FS FAQ confirms that paying taxable wages can require a filing even where no wage advance is payable. If no employment income was paid in the relevant period, do not invent a mandatory nil Prehľad.
2. Establish the actual §49(2) filing deadline: end of the following calendar month, applying the general non-working-day rule where relevant.
3. Under §39(11), the special sanction route is engaged only if the required Prehľad was not filed within five days after that deadline **and** that month either carried an obligation to remit wage-tax advances or a request to pay out the tax bonus, interest-paid bonus or employment premium specified there.
4. Apply §154(1)(j)/§155(1)(e), with §155(4) first-breach treatment and an evidence-based amount within the range. Do not present EUR 100 as a universal fixed fine. §155(16) also permits an equal repeat fine where the fine failed to produce a remedy and the continuing non-compliance remains.

If the financial/request condition in step3 is absent, do not assert that this special §39(11) trigger is met. That does not erase the separate filing duty or settle an independently different breach.

The official FS example is directly reproducible: for July2026 the normal deadline is 31August2026; the fifth day is Saturday5September, so filing by Monday7September is without the filing fine. A filing on8September is outside this tolerance, subject to the other statutory facts and first-breach rule. `controls.json` records that calculation and conditional variants.

## First breach and payment interest are separate

For a 2026 breach, §155(4) can suppress the range fine even after the five-day tolerance has expired. Retain offence-history evidence, including earlier §154(1)(j) non-monetary-duty breaches and any first-breach notice. The FS second-chance page says offence categories are assessed separately by the tax and customs offices. It does not grant a fresh allowance per monthly Prehľad. The 2025 decision supplies a concrete example of an imposed fine after a prior first-breach notice; its old range is not the current rate.

Interest under §156 requires a payment failure: ITA §35(6) ordinarily requires remittance within five days after payment/remittance/crediting of taxable wages, unless the tax office has granted a different date. Determine that due date separately from the monthly filing deadline. For the collected wage advance, §156 uses four times the ECB rate at the statutory reference date (the next day after it should have been remitted), at least15% a year, counting from the next day after due date through payment inclusive. Apply §156(5)'s wage-advance endpoint: payment, or the annual reconciliation day where unpaid then, and in any event its statutory annual-return-deadline limit. Do not apply only the generic four-year maximum while overlooking that earlier specific cutoff. Section156(6)(b) excludes interest of EUR5 or less per relevant case.

Consequently, a late Prehľad with advances already paid on time produces no late-payment interest merely from the late form. A form filed within the tolerance can coexist with interest on late/short advances. First-breach relief for the filing fine does not waive such interest. A bonus-payout request can satisfy the filing-fine gateway while leaving no unpaid tax balance on which payment interest runs.
