# Wisconsin (US-WI): 2026 tax pack

Wisconsin has individual income tax, corporate franchise or income tax, sales/use tax, employer withholding and unemployment insurance. DOR administers the revenue taxes; DWD administers UI. The former README's statement that Wisconsin has no franchise tax was incorrect. The sales job includes county and city-of-Milwaukee tax, so a universal state-plus-county maximum is also incorrect. See the owner skills and their official sources below.

The 2026 estimate instructions publish actual 2026 individual brackets/deductions and corporate installment rules. Final-return procedures expressly labelled **2025-2026 filing cycle** retain verified 2025 forms. A tax_year 2026 snapshot does not relabel those older form fields as published 2026 instructions. All four owner skills are tier 2 and await independent review; none has accountant approval.
