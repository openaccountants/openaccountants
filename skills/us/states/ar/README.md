# Arkansas: 2026 state tax pack

**Built from the law. Not yet approved.** Arkansas taxes individual wages/business income, corporate income, retail sales/use and employer payroll. The skills below are partial where final 2026 tables/forms or specific rule confirmation remain open. A 2026 tag does not mean complete coverage. Amounts expressly retained for the **2025-2026 filing cycle** remain dated to that cycle.

| Core job | One owner | Coverage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Individual wage/business income tax | `ar-income-tax` | 2026 statutory top rate 3.7%; final indexed return tables pending |
| Sales and use tax | `ar-sales-tax` | General state rate plus local tax; qualifying groceries state-exempt from January 1 2026, local taxes continue |
| Corporate income and annual franchise tax | `ar-corporate-tax` | Corporate top rate 4.3% in 2026; 4.1% starts 2027; franchise filing remains partial |
| Payroll withholding and unemployment insurance | `ar-payroll` | Ordinary state withholding and contributing-employer UI, with special cases flagged |

Sole proprietors do not file a corporate income-tax return merely because they operate a business. A corporation/LLC's separate franchise obligation is not disproved by that fact. Federal tax amounts and returns belong to the linked federal owners.

Property tax is administered locally and is outside this pack. Estate/inheritance, fuel/tobacco/alcohol excises, severance and special industry taxes are not supplied as skills here; no blanket absence of a tax is implied. The old README's unsupported no-estate-tax assertion is held for confirmation, not repeated as law.
