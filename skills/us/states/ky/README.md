# Kentucky: 2026 tax skills

Kentucky has individual income tax, sales/use tax, corporate income tax, LLET and employer withholding/UI. There is no local sales tax; separate local occupational taxes still matter. USD; state agencies are DOR and OUI. Federal computation is owned by the federal skills.
