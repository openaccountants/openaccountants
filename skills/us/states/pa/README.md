# Pennsylvania (US-PA): tax pack

Calendar tax year for individuals; corporations follow their federal year. State authority: the Pennsylvania Department of Revenue (pa.gov/agencies/revenue); unemployment compensation: the Department of Labor and Industry; local income tax: municipalities and school districts through county tax collection districts, overseen by DCED; Philadelphia: its own Department of Revenue. Currency: US dollars. For 2026 Pennsylvania taxes individuals at a flat 3.07% on eight classes of income (no netting between classes), corporations at 7.49% corporate net income tax (tax years beginning in 2026, falling 0.5 points a year to 4.99% in 2031), and retail sales at 6% state plus 1% in Allegheny County or 2% in Philadelphia (destination-sourced from 2026 under Act 21 of 2026). Most municipalities and school districts add a local earned income tax, and Philadelphia levies its own wage and net profits taxes. The capital stock and franchise tax was phased out for tax years beginning on or after 1 January 2016. A US state: federal income tax, FICA and FUTA sit on top of every domain here.
