## Worked rate addition

The existing 2026 Brevard rate adds the ordinary 6% state rate and 1% surtax to 7%. The expiry correction does not change that addition. Transaction-specific taxability and the surtax base still require their own conditions.

```oa-check
name: Existing Brevard ordinary combined rate
inputs:
  state_percent: 6
  county_percent: 1
steps:
  combined_percent: state_percent + county_percent
expected:
  combined_percent: 7
```
