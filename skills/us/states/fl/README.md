# Florida (US-FL): tax pack

Calendar tax year for individuals; corporations follow their federal year. Authority: the Florida Department of Revenue, floridarevenue.com. Currency: US dollars. Florida has no personal income tax, so there is no individual return and no state income tax withholding. It taxes corporations (Florida corporate income tax), retail sales (6% state sales and use tax plus county discretionary sales surtax), transient rentals, and employers through reemployment tax (state unemployment insurance) on the first USD 7,000 of each employee's wages. A US state: federal income tax, FICA and FUTA sit on top of every domain here.

## What Florida does not have, or this pack does not cover

- Individual income tax: none (Florida Constitution, Article VII, section 5).
- Estate and inheritance tax: none for deaths after 31 December 2004 (see `fl-moving-to-florida-domicile`).
- Not covered: property tax rates and millage (set by each county), documentary stamp tax, communications services tax, insurance premium tax.
