## Rate arithmetic only

For an assumed taxable base of $1,000, Highlands' 5% gives $50. Current North Walton 3% gives $30. These are rate multiplications only; they do not establish a parcel's district, historical rate, exemption, taxable base, state/surtax or final liability.

```oa-check
name: Highlands stipulated base only
inputs:
  assumed_taxable_base: 1000
steps:
  local_tax: assumed_taxable_base * 0.05
expected:
  local_tax: 50
```

```oa-check
name: Current North Walton stipulated base only; commencement held
inputs:
  assumed_taxable_base: 1000
steps:
  local_tax: assumed_taxable_base * 0.03
expected:
  local_tax: 30
```
