# Utah corporate franchise and income tax, tax year 2026

> **General reference only.** Written by Codex. Not reviewed by an accountant. Obtain qualified professional review before filing, paying or taking a tax position.

> **Circular 230 section 10.37:** Apply relevant law to the taxpayer's facts, evaluate reasonable assumptions and relevant circumstances, and obtain professional review of federal tax implications. Federal figures and computations belong to the linked federal owner skills.

**Answer first.** An ordinary taxable corporation computes Utah franchise/income tax at **4.45%** of Utah taxable income, with a **USD 100 minimum**. The franchise and income provisions cover different bases/circumstances within the same chapter; do not charge both on the same income. Use enacted 2026 law for the rate and labeled final 2025 instructions for the verified filing-cycle return structure. Source: [SB 60 sections 1,2 and 5](https://le.utah.gov/~2026/bills/sbillenr/SB0060.pdf).

## Scope

Ordinary C corporation with Utah franchise/income-tax liability and a supported Utah taxable-income base. The worked method is for a single corporation with all business income in Utah. Multistate apportionment, unitary groups, financial institutions, insurers, REITs/nonprofits and pass-through entity tax need specialist work. TC-20 is ordinary C corporation return; TC-20S and TC-20MC are separate forms, not automatic substitutions. Federal corporate work belongs to `us-1120-corporation-return-schedule-ordinary-business`; owner individuals to `ut-income-tax`; sales/use to `ut-sales-tax`; employer WH/SUI to `ut-payroll`. Source: [File the Right Corporate Forms and general instructions,2025-2026 filing cycle](https://files.tax.utah.gov/tax/forms/current/tc-20inst.pdf).

## Ask the client first

1. Federal tax classification, Utah registration/doing-business/source facts, tax year and prior returns.
2. Federal corporate return and taxable-income workpapers, including federal NOL/contributions treatment.
3. Related entities, unitary group, states of activity, receipts and nonbusiness allocations.
4. State additions/subtractions, Utah loss carryforwards, credits and certification evidence.
5. Prior/current Utah liability, quarterly payments, extension, withholding and refundable credits.
6. Untaxed purchases/use tax, amendments, audits and final-return/closure facts.

## The method, step by step

1. Determine franchise/income-tax nexus and exemptions before assuming no liability. SB 60 section 1 covers domestic/foreign corporations exercising corporate franchise/doing business; section 2 reaches Utah-source income not already in section 1's base, subject to statutory exemptions. Federal P.L. 86-272 or other protection is not established by a remote seller merely having no Utah office; refer the actual activity facts. Source: [59-7-104 and 201 as amended](https://le.utah.gov/~2026/bills/sbillenr/SB0060.pdf).
2. The verified **2025-2026 filing cycle** TC-20 Schedule A starts at federal Form 1120 line 28, not line 30 after federal NOL/special deductions. Add Schedule B additions and subtract Schedule C subtractions. Remove Utah/non-Utah net nonbusiness income to form apportionable base, apply Utah contributions deduction Schedule D, then supported apportionment Schedule J. Add Utah nonbusiness income back and deduct allowable Utah loss to reach net Utah taxable income. For all-Utah ordinary business with no allocation adjustments, apportionment fraction is 1. Source: [Schedule A lines 1-17](https://files.tax.utah.gov/tax/forms/current/tc-20inst.pdf).
3. Prepare Schedule B/C by actual state adjustment codes; do not use federal taxable income without reconciling state taxes, interest, loss/contribution deductions and conformity differences. The current instructions are final 2025: **2026 conformity changes, full addback/subtraction rules and final schedules are Not yet confirmed** here. Nonbusiness allocations and special industry apportionment are not solved by the ordinary 1.0 case. Source: [Schedules A, B, C, H and J,2025-2026 filing cycle](https://files.tax.utah.gov/tax/forms/current/tc-20inst.pdf).
4. Utah loss is a state schedule, not simply federal NOL. Verified 2025-2026 filing-cycle Schedule A line 16 limits loss deduction to 80% of line 15 income and requires support. Final 2026 loss/conformity exceptions are Not yet confirmed. Compute 2026 tax=max(positive net Utah taxable income x 4.45%, minimum). For group returns, obtain Schedule M minimum for included corporations before using USD 100. Sources: [2026 rate/minimum](https://le.utah.gov/~2026/bills/sbillenr/SB0060.pdf); [Schedule A lines 16-18](https://files.tax.utah.gov/tax/forms/current/tc-20inst.pdf).
5. Supported nonrefundable credits cannot reduce corporate net tax below the minimum in the verified 2025-2026 filing cycle. Reconcile recapture/installment-interest/foreign-income installment items separately where applicable. Refundable credits/payments reduce balance due; do not confuse the statutory minimum with a minimum cash payment after prepayments. Specialist credit amounts and 2026 changes are Not yet confirmed. Source: [Schedule A lines 19-25 and 28 and TC-20 return instructions](https://files.tax.utah.gov/tax/forms/current/tc-20inst.pdf).
6. Report use tax through sales-tax account when held; otherwise the verified 2025-2026 filing cycle places it on TC-20 Schedule A line 26. Its location/category rate and eligible other-state tax credit are owned by `ut-sales-tax`; do not multiply purchases by the corporate income rate. Source: [Schedule A line 26](https://files.tax.utah.gov/tax/forms/current/tc-20inst.pdf).

## Worked cases

**Case 1, ordinary 2026 all-Utah corporation.** Invented federal starting income USD 220,000, documented state additions USD 10,000, subtractions USD 30,000, no separate contribution/nonbusiness/loss/credit adjustments and 1.0 apportionment produces USD 200,000 Utah taxable income. At 4.45%, tax USD 8,900; USD 8,000 prepayments leaves USD 900. Actual return line numbers must be checked against final 2026 instructions.

```oa-check
name: Utah2026 corporate bridge and tax
inputs:
  federal_start: 220000
  additions: 10000
  subtractions: 30000
  payments: 8000
steps:
  - utah_taxable: federal_start + additions - subtractions
  - corporate_tax: max(max(utah_taxable, 0) * 0.0445, 100)
  - balance_due: max(corporate_tax - payments, 0)
expected:
  utah_taxable: 200000
  corporate_tax: 8900
  balance_due: 900
```

**Case 2, minimum-tax boundary.** Two invented ordinary taxable corporations each have no credits or other tax items. One has USD 1,000 Utah taxable income and calculated USD 44.50, so minimum USD 100 applies; another has USD 3,000 income and tax USD 133.50 above minimum. A loss ordinarily still leaves USD 100 minimum, unless an actual statutory exemption applies.

```oa-check
name: Utah corporate100minimum decision
inputs:
  low_income: 1000
  higher_income: 3000
steps:
  - low_computed: low_income * 0.0445
  - low_tax: max(low_computed, 100)
  - higher_computed: higher_income * 0.0445
  - higher_tax: max(higher_computed, 100)
expected:
  low_computed: 44.5
  low_tax: 100
  higher_computed: 133.5
  higher_tax: 133.5
```

## When to refuse or refer

Refer multistate nexus/protection, unitary combined reporting, water's-edge questions, allocation/apportionment disputes, financial/special entities, pass-through elections, unverified state conformity, loss limitations and certified credits. Do not assume minimum-tax exemption merely because federal tax is zero.

## Filing and payment

Verified **2025-2026 filing cycle**: TC-20 ordinarily due 15th day of fourth month after year end or federal return due date, whichever later; weekend/legal holiday next business day. Automatic up to six-month filing extension needs no extension form but does not extend payment. Extension prepayment:  90% current liability(minimum USD 100 if greater) or 100% prior liability under instructions. Pay through https://tap.tax.utah.gov or TC-559 as applicable; retain accepted return and payment confirmation separately. **Final 2026 form/calendar and current e-file mandate thresholds are Not yet confirmed.** Source: [Due Date, Filing Extension and Prepayment Requirements](https://files.tax.utah.gov/tax/forms/current/tc-20inst.pdf).

Corporations with USD 3,000 or more liability current or prior year generally owe quarterly estimates; combined groups aggregate, first-year Utah exception requires minimum paid by original due date. Due 15th day of 4th, 6th, 9th and 12th month of tax year, subject to official federal date changes. Instructions permit prior-year safe-harbor installments and current-year computations subject to their rules; document method before paying. Do not import individual Utah no-estimates rule into corporate tax. Late filing/payment creates penalties/interest; current dated Pub 58 calculations and 2026 relief remain Not yet confirmed. Source: [Quarterly Payments and Penalties/Interest,2025-2026 filing cycle](https://files.tax.utah.gov/tax/forms/current/tc-20inst.pdf).
