# Washington (US-WA): tax pack

Authority for business taxes: the Washington Department of Revenue (DOR), dor.wa.gov; for unemployment insurance and Paid Family and Medical Leave: the Employment Security Department (ESD), esd.wa.gov; for workers' compensation: Labor and Industries (L&I), lni.wa.gov. Currency: US dollars. Washington has no individual or corporate income tax for 2026. Businesses pay the business and occupation (B&O) tax on gross receipts instead, and collect retail sales tax at 6.5% state plus local rates (7.6% to 10.7% combined in Q4 2026). Both go on one combined excise tax return in My DOR. A US state: federal income tax, FICA and FUTA sit on top of every domain here.

## Capital gains excise tax (no skill yet)

Individuals pay a capital gains excise tax of 7% on Washington long-term capital gains, plus 2.9% on the portion over $1,000,000 from 1 January 2025 ([RCW 82.87.040](https://app.leg.wa.gov/RCW/default.aspx?cite=82.87.040), read 2026-11-13). The standard deduction was $270,000 for 2024 and $278,000 for 2025 and is inflation-adjusted each year; DOR had not published the 2026 figure on its page when read ([DOR capital gains tax](https://dor.wa.gov/taxes-rates/other-taxes/capital-gains-tax), read 2026-11-13). Returns and payment are due 15 April, filed electronically in My DOR with a copy of the federal return; from tax year 2026 the tax may be prepaid up to six months before the due date. There is no skill for this tax in the pack yet: refer.

## Out of scope for this pack

City B&O taxes (each city runs its own), property tax, real estate excise tax, estate tax, public utility tax, insurance premiums tax, cannabis, liquor, tobacco and lodging taxes, and workers' compensation rates (L&I). Washington's 2028 income tax gets a skill once DOR publishes forms.
