# Wyoming formation and entity maintenance: 2026

## Scope

Form a domestic Wyoming LLC or profit corporation, maintain its registered agent and annual report, and identify when a series, trust, interstate operation or foreign owner needs a separate specialist. Domestic articles cost $100; the annual report/license tax is the greater of $60 or 0.0002 of assets located and employed in Wyoming. These are separate obligations. [SOS fee schedule, effective 1 July 2026](https://sos.wyo.gov/Business/Docs/BusinessFees.pdf).

General reference, not a legal opinion or authority to file. Have the filing and proposed structure reviewed before submission. No accountant has approved this version. Wyoming formation does not remove another jurisdiction's registration or tax obligations. Federal classification, income tax, EIN, elections, beneficial-ownership reporting and international reporting belong to their federal skills. Close LLCs, DAOs, private trust companies, captive insurers, regulated custody and creditor litigation require specialist advice.

## Ask the client first

1. What is the objective, proposed entity type, ownership chain and actual place of management, work, property, inventory and employees? Which states and countries are involved?
2. Who are the real owners, managers, organizer/incorporator and authorized signer; what information may be public, and what disclosures must the agent, bank or government receive?
3. What name, Wyoming registered agent with written consent, principal/mailing addresses and monitored email will be used? Is the name eligible for online filing?
4. What assets are located and employed in Wyoming, what evidence supports their valuation/allocation, and what is the formation anniversary month or existing entity status?
5. Are there existing creditors, claims, guarantees, regulated activities, lender restrictions or transfers requiring legal advice? What costs and compliance capacity can the structure support?
6. Are series, trusts, non-US owners or outside investors involved? Obtain the operating agreement, series records, proposed trustee/administration and federal reporting analysis before recommending a structure.

## The method, step by step

### 1. Establish the job and jurisdiction before selling Wyoming

Separate a holding company from each operating or property company and identify every place of activity. Wyoming's lack of a general individual/corporate income tax is not an exemption from the owner's residence-state tax or another state's business tax. Load the Wyoming pack and `us-state-formation-matrix` for those questions. Article 15 section 18 of the Wyoming Constitution requires credits if an income tax is imposed; it does **not** say an income tax requires a constitutional amendment. Do not repeat that old claim. [Constitution, article 15 section 18](https://www.wyoleg.gov/statutes/compress/title97.pdf).

Compare the actual proposed structures, including all entities, agents, accounts, licenses and destination-state registrations. The old six-state price ranking, "cheapest" claim and automatic Delaware recommendation for venture funding are not current fee advice. Use `us-state-formation-matrix` and counsel for Delaware/Nevada/California/Texas/New York/Massachusetts alternatives; this skill owns only Wyoming formation and maintenance figures. Investors' requirements and governing-law preferences need actual facts.

For a non-Wyoming entity entering Wyoming, W.S. 17-29-114 applies the corporate foreign-qualification provisions to foreign LLCs. W.S. 17-16-1501 lists exclusions including bank accounts, internal affairs, owning property without more, specified debt activity and interstate commerce; subsection (d) addresses acting as organizer, manager or member of a company. These are **Wyoming** rules. Do not export them to another state or conclude that every holding company qualifies for an exemption there. [Title 17, sections 17-29-114 and 17-16-1501](https://wyoleg.gov/statutes/compress/title17.pdf).

### 2. Prepare the right articles and private governance

Check name availability and the applicable suffix. W.S. 17-29-108 and the LLC form allow Limited Liability Company and listed abbreviations including LLC, L.L.C., Limited Company, LC and L.C.; name availability does not establish trademark rights. The current online instructions send names beginning with the letter "A" to paper/manual review. Do not force an ineligible filing through the online workflow. [LLC form and instructions](https://sos.wyo.gov/forms/business/llc/llc-articlesorganization.pdf); [registration instructions](https://wyobiz.wyo.gov/Business/RegistrationInstr.aspx).

For an ordinary LLC, prepare name, any deliberate special election, registered agent name and Wyoming physical street address, mailing address, principal office address, required email/service certification and organizer signature/contact details. Obtain the registered agent's written consent; attach it to the paper filing, or retain it when certifying consent online. Use the separate series form for a series election. Do not inadvertently elect close-LLC treatment. [LLC form](https://sos.wyo.gov/forms/business/llc/llc-articlesorganization.pdf); [agent instructions](https://sos.wyo.gov/Business/Docs/HowToFindOrBecomeARegisteredAgent.pdf).

For a profit corporation, use articles of incorporation: name, any close-corporation election, agent/address, mailing/principal office, number of authorized shares, incorporator name/address, required email certification and signature. W.S. 17-16-202 also permits specified optional provisions, including initial directors. LLC operating agreements are not a substitute for corporate organizational actions, bylaws, share issuance and records. Have counsel settle those documents. [Corporate form](https://sos.wyo.gov/Forms/Business/PROF/P-ArticlesIncorporation.pdf); [W.S. 17-16-202](https://wyoleg.gov/statutes/compress/title17.pdf).

Prepare the LLC operating agreement privately; it is not an attachment required by the ordinary articles form. Address members and contributions, economic/tax allocations, distribution decisions and restrictions, management authority, transfers, buy-sell/death/exit events, records, dispute forum and governing law. W.S. 17-29-110 limits what an agreement can vary. Counsel must review creditor-sensitive or nominee arrangements; naming a manager creates legal duties and authority questions, not guaranteed anonymity. Separate funds, contracts, asset titles and books for each entity; obtain lender/insurer approval before transferring encumbered property. [W.S. 17-29-110 and 17-29-211](https://wyoleg.gov/statutes/compress/title17.pdf).

### 3. Submit, pay and retain proof

Start at [Wyobiz](https://wyobiz.wyo.gov/Business/RegistrationInstr.aspx), choose LLC or profit corporation, check the name, complete the entity/agent/address/signature fields, review the preview and processing charge, then pay and save the filed articles, certificate and receipt. The SOS June 2026 guide says an online company is active when the process completes. Confirm its actual status in the entity search before trading; a payment attempt alone is insufficient. [How to create a Wyoming company](https://sos.wyo.gov/Business/Docs/HowToCreateAWyomingCompany.pdf).

For paper filings, use the current signed form and agent consent with payment and the address/instructions printed on the form. The current guide allows up to 15 business days after receipt and sends evidence by email. Do not promise the old 3-day or 30-minute search timings. [June 2026 creation guide](https://sos.wyo.gov/Business/Docs/HowToCreateAWyomingCompany.pdf).

The older ordinary articles instructions say there is no expedite option. The June 2026 expedited-service instructions supersede that blanket statement: eligible paper documents can receive paid priority review, but filings available online, including initial formations and annual reports, are excluded. Use the signed expedite cover and permitted submission/payment method specified there; expedited review does not guarantee approval and its fee is nonrefundable if a rejected filing is not corrected. [Current expedite instructions](https://sos.wyo.gov/Business/Docs/HowToRequestAnExpeditedFiling.pdf).

Then complete federal EIN/classification/election work through the appropriate federal owner, open accounts subject to the bank's actual requirements, register required sales/payroll accounts through `wy-sales-tax` and `wy-payroll`, and obtain activity/local licenses. SOS formation is not a business license or federal tax election. Do not promise a bank account, a particular vendor's acceptance, an EIN turnaround, S status or a tax saving from the certificate alone. [Creation guide, other agencies](https://sos.wyo.gov/Business/Docs/HowToCreateAWyomingCompany.pdf).

### 4. Calendar the report and keep the agent valid

The annual report is due on the first day of the anniversary month. A company formed 14 March 2025 first reports on 1 March 2026; do not wait until 14 March. Use the prior-fiscal-year financial information and Wyoming-located/employed asset basis required by W.S. 17-29-209, with current information for the other report fields. Retain the underlying records for the statutory three years. Section 209(b) contains special intrastate-asset treatment for public interstate carriers and a mine/mineral valuation rule; obtain specialist review where either applies. Do not assume an out-of-state bank balance, intangible or crypto wallet is a Wyoming asset merely because the entity is formed here. Escalate uncertain situs/valuation. [W.S. 17-29-209](https://wyoleg.gov/statutes/compress/title17.pdf); [annual-report FAQ](https://sos.wyo.gov/FAQS.aspx?root=BUS).

Search the entity and start its Annual Report Wizard, verify identity/status and report fields, enter the asset schedule, review the calculated license tax, sign and pay, and retain report/payment confirmation. The FAQ says reports with a fee greater than $500 cannot be e-filed; use the printed report/payment process and current instructions for that entity. A $5 million Wyoming-asset LLC owes $1,000 and therefore takes that paper route. Do not apply the formation card-processing percentage to every annual payment without checking that payment screen. [FAQ](https://sos.wyo.gov/FAQS.aspx?root=BUS); [license-tax schedule](https://sos.wyo.gov/Business/Docs/BusinessFees.pdf).

The agent must maintain an actual Wyoming physical address; a PO box, drop box or mail-forwarding address alone is insufficient. An individual agent must be at least 18 and reside in Wyoming; an entity agent must qualify under the statute. Keep written consent and monitored communications. Agents retain key individuals' and natural-person communications-contact information; provide changes as required by W.S. 17-28-107. This is different from a public member list. [Agent instructions](https://sos.wyo.gov/Business/Docs/HowToFindOrBecomeARegisteredAgent.pdf); [W.S. 17-28-101, 103 and 107](https://wyoleg.gov/statutes/compress/title17.pdf).

A resigning agent gives the entity advance notice under W.S. 17-28-103; the entity's statement-of-change deadline is 30 days after receipt of the resignation statement. The old 31-day claim is incorrect. Annual nonpayment or loss of agent can lead to administrative dissolution; section 17-29-705 provides a 60-day compliance period from the specified notice for the agent/fee defaults, a two-year reinstatement window, and conditional relation-back for the fee-default reinstatement in subsection (b). Do not treat dissolution as cancellation of debt, or promise that all rights vanish/revive in every litigation. Resolve the actual notice, all arrears and agent deficiency using the SOS procedure. The old flat $50 late/reinstatement assertions are not current law or fees. [W.S. 17-28-103 and 17-29-705](https://wyoleg.gov/statutes/compress/title17.pdf); [reinstatement FAQ](https://sos.wyo.gov/FAQS.aspx?root=BUS); [fees](https://sos.wyo.gov/Business/Docs/BusinessFees.pdf).

### 5. Bound the protection and privacy advice

W.S. 17-29-503(a) permits a judgment creditor's charging order against the debtor's transferable interest and distributions otherwise payable. Subsection **(g)** makes that the exclusive remedy described there and expressly includes a sole member, dissociated member or transferee; subsections (b) and (c) are reserved. The old subsection-(c) citation is wrong. The text bars foreclosure and specified creditor interference; it is not a promise that every asset, transfer, federal claim, personal guarantee or business tort is protected. [W.S. 17-29-503, read with 502](https://wyoleg.gov/statutes/compress/title17.pdf).

Preserve separation and document contributions, management/distribution decisions and transfers, but do not present discretionary distributions or a nominee manager as a litigation strategy guaranteed to defeat creditors. Fraudulent transfers, alter ego/reverse veil claims, pre-existing debt, personal wrongdoing, bankruptcy, federal liens and judgment enforcement need counsel. The old United States v. Craft citation remains a federal-law referral, not a Wyoming enforcement opinion. This skill does not reproduce the old unverified Rev. Rul. 77-137 "phantom income" settlement tactic, unnamed reverse-piercing case or out-of-state charging-order rankings.

The ordinary LLC form does not demand a public member/manager roster. It does demand organizer and address/contact information; filed documents may contain more, and agents must know key individuals. Corporate articles identify incorporators, may identify initial directors, and the corporate annual report must give officers and directors under W.S. 17-16-1630; corporations have a different disclosure pattern. "No owner names ever appear" is too broad. Review the actual filing preview and each annual report, use accurate permitted addresses, and explain that privacy from a casual search does not mean secrecy from the IRS, banks, agent, subpoena or discovery. The old generic John Smith public-record illustration is only an illustration, not a promise of concealment. [LLC/corporate forms](https://sos.wyo.gov/forms/business/llc/llc-articlesorganization.pdf); [corporate form](https://sos.wyo.gov/Forms/Business/PROF/P-ArticlesIncorporation.pdf); [W.S. 17-28-107 and 108](https://wyoleg.gov/statutes/compress/title17.pdf).

A US-formed company with a foreign owner must not be confused with a foreign-formed company registered in the US. Load the current federal beneficial-ownership discussion in `us-state-formation-matrix` before giving a BOI conclusion. Wyoming's FinCEN webpage still describes a March 2025 suspension/proposed-rule stage; it is not a reliable current federal filing instruction. Federal EIN responsible-party, bank CDD, Forms 5472/1120 and other disclosures require the federal analysis even where no public member roster is required. No federal threshold, penalty or filing deadline is owned here. [Dated Wyoming FinCEN notice](https://sos.wyo.gov/FinCEN/).

### 6. Series and long-duration trusts require a separate legal decision

A series is not just a bookkeeping label. W.S. 17-29-211(c) requires separately maintained, objectively identifiable asset records, operating-agreement liability limitations and notice in the articles for the statutory separation to apply. Agreements can assume broader liabilities under subsection (d). The statute gives series powers to contract, hold title and sue/be sued; a series can terminate separately, with statutory winding-up/distribution rules. Do not promise another state's courts, a lender or bankruptcy proceedings will recognize the desired separation. [W.S. 17-29-211](https://wyoleg.gov/statutes/compress/title17.pdf).

Elect series status on the appropriate articles, provide the liability notice and identify established series. Disclose creation and termination through the required filings within 30 days under Chapter 5; follow either permitted name pattern with sequential numeric designations, and do not reuse a terminated number. A later designation/name change requires the corresponding amendment and restated series names. All series share the master LLC's agent, who must retain series and key-person/contact records. [Series articles](https://sos.wyo.gov/Forms/Business/LLC/SeriesLLC-ArticlesofOrganization.pdf); [Chapter 5 sections 2-4](https://sos.wyo.gov/Forms/WyoBiz/Series_LLC_Rules.pdf).

The old 12-property illustration remains a legal design question: a master with 12 established series has $100 + 12 × $10 = $220 initial state filing fees before payment processing, while 12 ordinary LLC formations cost $1,200. The $980 initial difference does not settle lender approval, titles, record segregation, recognition elsewhere, annual asset reporting or federal tax/EIN treatment. Do not promise one EIN or one federal return for all series. Current annual reporting/asset allocation for the specific proposed master/series structure needs SOS/counsel confirmation; the old unconditional "one $60 report" is held. [Series fees](https://sos.wyo.gov/Business/Docs/BusinessFees.pdf); [W.S. 17-29-211](https://wyoleg.gov/statutes/compress/title17.pdf).

For dynasty-trust questions, W.S. 34-1-139 is conditional: its post-1-July-2003 exception permits a non-real-property trust duration up to 1,000 years with Wyoming governing law, the specified Wyoming trustee/administration connection, and termination/vesting within the permitted period. Real property is treated separately, including in mixed trusts. This does not itself exempt assets from federal estate/GST tax or establish that every beneficiary's interest is outside an estate. Route grantor/non-grantor, Form 1041, estate/GST, trustee powers and other-state nexus to trust counsel and the federal owners; the old 2025 federal exemption/sunset figures are not reproduced. [W.S. 34-1-139, including (b) and (e)](https://wyoleg.gov/statutes/compress/title34.pdf).

## Worked cases

### Annual tax and formation payment: recompute before filing

With $0 or $300,000 of qualifying Wyoming assets, the annual minimum is $60. With $5 million it is $1,000, and the FAQ's over-$500 restriction directs paper filing. An ordinary $100 online formation adds $2.40 processing. Formation plus a later minimum annual report totals $162.40 in those two state charges, before any annual-payment processing or outside costs; they are not both payable as one formation fee. [Fees](https://sos.wyo.gov/Business/Docs/BusinessFees.pdf); [registration instructions](https://wyobiz.wyo.gov/Business/RegistrationInstr.aspx); [FAQ](https://sos.wyo.gov/FAQS.aspx?root=BUS).

```oa-check
name: Wyoming formation and annual license tax
inputs:
  wy_assets: 5000000
  articles_fee: 100
steps:
  - annual_tax: max(60, wy_assets * 0.0002)
  - boundary_tax: max(60, 300000 * 0.0002)
  - online_charge: max(1, articles_fee * 0.024)
  - online_formation: articles_fee + online_charge
  - formation_plus_later_minimum: online_formation + 60
expected:
  annual_tax: 1000
  boundary_tax: 60
  online_charge: 2.40
  online_formation: 102.40
  formation_plus_later_minimum: 162.40
```

### Jamal: four rental properties and a separate holding company

Retained facts: Texas resident; two Texas properties, one Arizona and one Tennessee property; $58,000 annual net rental income and $1.65 million property value. Proposed Wyoming parent owns four property LLCs. Determine the actual state of each child, lender permission, deeds, insurance, accounts and foreign qualification before implementation. If only the parent is formed in Wyoming, its articles are $100 and its annual tax is at least $60, with actual Wyoming assets determining the final amount. An agent's fee requires an actual quote. The property value and rental income are not automatically its Wyoming license-tax base. [Wyoming fees](https://sos.wyo.gov/Business/Docs/BusinessFees.pdf).

The old $460 multi-state annual total is withdrawn: the displayed state figures did not even sum to that amount without the assumed $100 agent charge, and the Tennessee label mixed a fee with franchise tax. Do not replace that total with another guessed one. Load the destination-state skills and `us-state-formation-matrix` for Texas/Arizona/Tennessee costs, and the federal rental/entity owners for Schedule E, disregarded entities, partnership returns and EIN requirements. A parent does not erase a guarantee, a property-level tort or recognition issues.

### Priya: Colorado home office, Wyoming software company

Retained facts: Colorado resident and US software engineer; SaaS business with $180,000 net income, managed and operated from the Colorado home office. Assess Colorado foreign qualification, state tax, payroll and local rules rather than treating Wyoming formation as the operating footprint. Wyoming's state component is $100 articles plus at least $60 each annual report; apply the asset formula. The old Colorado $100/$10 figures and combined $70 annual total are not current advice and are routed to the Colorado owner and formation matrix. The $180,000 profit is not the Wyoming annual-license-tax base. [Wyoming fees](https://sos.wyo.gov/Business/Docs/BusinessFees.pdf).

Compare a Colorado-only entity against the extra Wyoming structure using current destination fees and real agent/administration costs. Preserve lawful public-record privacy objectives without promising that an out-of-state filing hides every name. Federal Schedule C, self-employment tax and possible S election belong to federal skills; no saving is assumed here.

### Mateo: Buenos Aires services and a foreign-owned Wyoming LLC

Retained facts: Argentine citizen living in Buenos Aires, remote US clients, no US office, employees, physical presence or US immigration status. A Wyoming single-member LLC may be proposed for contracting and banking, but it does not itself determine source of income, US trade/business, withholding, treaty consequences, Argentina tax or bank acceptance. Wyoming articles cost $100 and the annual tax is at least $60 subject to actual Wyoming assets. [Fees](https://sos.wyo.gov/Business/Docs/BusinessFees.pdf).

Load `us-form-5472-foreign-owned-us` and `us-form-1040-nr-nonresident-return` where their facts fit, plus the relevant federal entity/international owners. Analyze reportable transactions and the applicable federal return, rather than asserting that every foreign-owned LLC owes the same annual filing or that foreign services always produce no US tax. US formation with foreign ownership is distinct from foreign formation for BOI; use the current federal owner. Old federal dates/penalties and bank-vendor promises are withheld from this state skill.

### Riley: $3.4 million crypto portfolio, one entity or four

Retained facts: US resident, $3.4 million portfolio across hardware wallets, exchanges and staking; proposed parent plus Long-Term Hold, Active Trading and Staking subsidiaries. Four Wyoming LLC formations cost $400 before payment processing. If each has no more than $300,000 in assets located and employed in Wyoming, their combined annual minimum is $240. The old unconditional $240 statement needed that asset condition. Agent fees, books, contracts and custody operations are additional. [Fees](https://sos.wyo.gov/Business/Docs/BusinessFees.pdf).

Test both asset assumptions rather than infer situs from a wallet or the incorporation certificate. With no Wyoming assets, one entity costs $60 annually and four cost $240, an extra $180. If, solely for arithmetic, the entire $3.4 million is qualifying Wyoming assets held directly in one entity, annual tax is $680. If instead four entities each hold $850,000 of separate qualifying assets with no additional parent asset base, four × $170 is also $680. That hypothetical allocation is not a conclusion that a real holding parent has no reportable assets; parent interests and double-count/allocation questions require the actual asset schedule and advice. Cost alone does not decide liability separation or regulatory obligations. [W.S. 17-29-209](https://wyoleg.gov/statutes/compress/title17.pdf); [fees](https://sos.wyo.gov/Business/Docs/BusinessFees.pdf).

## When to refuse or refer

Withhold a final filing calculation without the actual entity/status, anniversary, asset schedule or current payment instructions. Refer creditor disputes, estate/GST plans, series recognition, nominee arrangements, regulated activity, uncertain asset situs, complex reinstatement and cross-border consequences to the appropriate professional. Do not advise a transfer to frustrate an existing creditor. No constitutional tax immunity, absolute anonymity, one-EIN-for-all-series, automatic multi-state exemption or guaranteed foreign-owner tax/bank result may be inferred from this skill.

## Completion checklist

- Deliver a concise structure diagram naming every entity, owner, purpose, operating/property location and alternative considered.
- Give initial and recurring costs separately: Wyoming figures, actual agent/legal quotes and destination-state costs with their owners. Reconcile the asset schedule to the annual report; exclude profits as a substitute base.
- Record name check, correct form/elections, consent, public-field preview, signer authority, payment, filed articles, certificate and active status.
- Complete private governance, contributions, titles, separate accounts/books, lender/insurance consent and required licenses before relying on separation.
- Calendar the anniversary-month first day, report/payment route, agent contact, notices and record retention; verify report and receipt after submission.
- Record privacy limits, current federal disclosure/referral outcome, home/destination-state qualification and taxes, federal entity/EIN/election/reporting work and any open item.
- State precisely what charging-order or series statute applies, and what counsel has not confirmed. Do not present a long-duration trust as a federal estate-tax exemption.
- Recheck the original failure points: formation is not tax residence; no ignored foreign qualification, report lapse, commingling, guaranteed protection, missed foreign-owner reporting, wrong public-record promise, blind series election or unconfirmed bank/regulatory access.
