# California single-member LLC: Form 568 (2026)

A disregarded SMLLC doing business in California, organized here or registered with the Secretary of State (SOS) files Form 568; its income still belongs on the owner's return. For calendar 2026, the ordinary return deadline is **15 April 2027**, except an S corporation, partnership or partnership-classified LLC owner gives **15 March 2027**. The return includes the owner's consent to California tax jurisdiction. These duties follow [R&TC §18633.5(i)](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=RTC&sectionNum=18633.5.).

**2026 form-output hold.** The 2026 Form 568 booklet URL returned 404 on 27 November 2026. The 2025 layout, schedule tests and Schedule T numerical instructions below are usable **only for 2025 returns**. Do not file a 2026 return using an assumed side, line, schedule threshold or Schedule T rate carried from 2025. This hold leaves the proved statutory filing, consent, annual-tax, fee, deadline and cancellation rules available. Check this point with one of our partner accountants.

## Scope and who this is for

One owner, with disregarded status actually established. Under [26 CFR §301.7701-3(a), (b)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-26/chapter-I/subchapter-F/part-301/subpart-ECFR5ffaf3310af6b61/section-301.7701-3), a domestic eligible entity with one owner defaults to disregarded status unless it elects otherwise; a foreign eligible entity has different default rules. Do not assume every foreign single-owner entity is disregarded. Federal “domestic” includes organization under any US state law, so a Delaware LLC is not federally foreign just because California calls it a foreign LLC ([26 CFR §301.7701-5(a)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-26/section-301.7701-5)). California generally follows federal classification, subject to the pre-1997 exception in [R&TC §23038(b)(2)](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=RTC&sectionNum=23038.).

A corporation-classified LLC uses the corporation return regime rather than this disregarded-LLC method ([§18633.5(h)](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=RTC&sectionNum=18633.5.)). An LLC exempt under §23701h or §23701x is excluded from the annual-tax/fee regime by §17941(d). Multi-member returns, the owner's income-tax computation and estimated personal tax are outside this method. The companion [ca-llc-fee-and-tax](ca-llc-fee-and-tax.md) owns the detailed annual-tax, fee and nexus computation. Form 568 does not replace the owner's return.

## Ask the client first

- Obtain organization/registration dates, state or country of formation, SOS status, classification elections and any ownership change.
- Identify the direct and ultimate owner, owner type, residence and taxable year; obtain the consent decision.
- Obtain receipts, cost of goods sold (COGS), other income and California assignment evidence; separate fee income from net income.
- For an unregistered out-of-state entity, obtain California and total sales, property and payroll, agents' activities and pass-through shares.
- Obtain prior-year fee, current payments and dates, returns already filed, any notice and demand, suspension or disaster relief.
- If closing or claiming an exception, obtain business cessation/cancellation dates, short-year activity and deployment evidence.

## The method, step by step

### Filing trigger and taxable year ([R&TC §§17941, 18633.5](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=RTC&sectionNum=18633.5.))

1. Confirm disregarded status and a filing trigger: **doing business, California organization or SOS registration**. Registration alone can trigger annual tax even without activity; tax continues for each year or part until qualifying termination. A final-return box alone does not terminate it ([§17941](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=RTC&sectionNum=17941.)).
2. For annual tax and fee, substitute the **owner's taxable year**, as §18633.5(i)(4) requires. For the initial domestic LLC period, use the SOS articles date; existing foreign LLCs use their home-state organization date for first-year timing, and pay immediately on California entry/registration if the fourth-month date has already passed ([2026 FTB 3522](https://www.ftb.ca.gov/forms/2026/2026-3522.pdf)).
3. An unregistered foreign SMLLC that is disregarded and **not doing business** need not file 565 or 568 merely because it has California-source income; owner reporting remains separate ([2025 booklet, General Information D](https://www.ftb.ca.gov/forms/2025/2025-568-booklet.html)).

“Doing business” includes active transactions for financial gain in California, organization/commercial domicile here, or any separate sales/property/payroll test under [§23101](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=RTC&sectionNum=23101.). Each amount must **exceed the lesser** of the indexed dollar threshold and 25% of its corresponding total. Sales include agents/independent contractors; include statutory pro rata/distributive shares of partnerships and S corporations. Equality is insufficient for that test but does not defeat another trigger. The [FTB table](https://www.ftb.ca.gov/file/business/doing-business-in-california.html) gives **2025 only**: sales $757,070, property $75,707 and payroll $75,707. The old $735,019 figure was a 2024 sales figure, not a property/payroll figure. The reused source read contains no 2026 indexed row: any 2026 conclusion depending on that unpublished dollar amount is non-usable. Check this point with one of our partner accountants.

A first year beginning in 2026 does not become a first year in 2027 ([§17941(g)](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=RTC&sectionNum=17941.)). Future relief for a foreign LLC first organized earlier, or after an exempt short year, remains non-usable here. Check this point with one of our partner accountants.

### Consent, Schedule T and owner's return ([R&TC §18633.5(e)–(i)](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=RTC&sectionNum=18633.5.))

The SMLLC return gives its sole owner's name and taxpayer identification number and includes the owner's consent to California tax jurisdiction. **The statute does not limit this consent duty to nonresident owners.** If consent is missing, §18633.5(i)(2) requires payment on the owner's behalf consistent with the nonresident-member payment in (e). Do not treat residency as permission to omit the consent without considering that duty.

The statutory payment is the specified highest marginal rate times the owner's California-source income, reduced by tax the LLC previously withheld **and paid** for that owner. The 2025 Schedule T instructions floor the result at zero. Payment is due by the original return deadline and is credited to the owner; it does not satisfy the owner's filing obligation. Late-payment penalty/interest treatment follows §18633.5(e)(2); its (e)(3) exception applies if the owner timely files and pays all California tax on the LLC income ([§18633.5](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=RTC&sectionNum=18633.5.), [2025 Schedule T instructions](https://www.ftb.ca.gov/forms/2025/2025-568-booklet.html)).

| Schedule T owner type | Numerical rate, **2025 only** | Official source |
|---|---|---|
| Individual, estate or trust; the booklet also lists partnerships/LLCs, whose tiered-owner application is held below | 12.3% under the 2025 booklet; this is the Schedule T payment, not the owner's final income-tax computation | [2025 Schedule T](https://www.ftb.ca.gov/forms/2025/2025-568-booklet.html) |
| C corporation | 8.84% under the 2025 booklet | [2025 Schedule T](https://www.ftb.ca.gov/forms/2025/2025-568-booklet.html), [§23151](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=RTC&sectionNum=23151.) |
| S corporation | 1.5% under the 2025 booklet | [2025 Schedule T](https://www.ftb.ca.gov/forms/2025/2025-568-booklet.html), [§23802](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=RTC&sectionNum=23802.) |

The owner includes the SMLLC's income, deductions and credits on its own return. Resident individuals use Form 540; nonresidents/part-year residents use Form 540NR as applicable; corporate owners use Form 100 or 100S. Do not force every owner's activity onto Schedule C: rentals, investments and other activities use their applicable schedules ([2025 booklet S](https://www.ftb.ca.gov/forms/2025/2025-568-booklet.html), [2025 Schedule CA (540)](https://www.ftb.ca.gov/forms/2025/2025-540-ca-instructions.html), [2025 Form 100, Question CC](https://www.ftb.ca.gov/forms/2025/2025-100-booklet.html)). For **2025**, Schedule CA (540NR) column E includes California-source nonresident business profit/loss; multistate owner-income apportionment is a separate computation ([2025 Schedule CA (540NR)](https://www.ftb.ca.gov/forms/2025/2025-540nr-ca-instructions.html)).

**Credit limit.** Credits and carryovers generated by the disregarded entity have an additional cap: regular tax with that entity's income minus regular tax without it; no credit if including that income gives less regular tax. Carryovers remain subject to the governing credit rules. This cap is not permission to use a credit contrary to other limits. The 2025 booklet requires the applicable Schedule P and limits use to the available credit, this SMLLC-income cap and the net-tax balance available at that stage ([§17039(g)](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=RTC&sectionNum=17039.), [§23036(i)](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=RTC&sectionNum=23036.), [2025 booklet S](https://www.ftb.ca.gov/forms/2025/2025-568-booklet.html)). Do not carry a 2025 Schedule P line into 2026.

### Form preparation: issued 2025 layout only ([2025 booklet S, Schedule IW and specific lines](https://www.ftb.ca.gov/forms/2025/2025-568-booklet.html))

- Complete Sides **1, 2, 3 and 7 (Schedule IW)**. Side 3 contains the sole-owner information and consent, not a balance sheet. A nonconsenting nonresident owner requires Schedule T on Side 4; the statute's broader missing-owner-consent duty above also applies.
- Add **Schedules B and K** if either test is met: Schedule B line 1 or any of lines 3–11 income/loss amount is **$3,000,000 or more**, or Schedule K line 21a is **at least $3,000,000 or at most minus $3,000,000**. Do not rewrite the Schedule B test as an absolute-value test. Schedule B is income/deductions, not a balance sheet. Use the owner's corresponding federal amounts attributable to the disregarded entity for Schedule K.
- An SMLLC does not prepare Schedule K-1 (568) or FTB 3832. Its own Form 568 consent supplies the consent mechanism.
- Below those B/K tests, prepare IW from the California amounts attributable to the entity in the owner's federal Schedules B, C, D, E, F or other applicable schedules. For 2025, section 1231 gains from the relevant schedule go on IW line 14; IW line 17 goes to Form 568 line 1 and cannot be negative. This is a fee-income computation, not owner net profit.
- Keep the LLC's identification separate from the owner's identification. The 2026 payment vouchers request the LLC's SOS number/FEIN; no blanket instruction to invent an EIN or replace every missing field with an SSN is given ([2026 FTB 3522](https://www.ftb.ca.gov/forms/2026/2026-3522.pdf)).

For 2026, produce the statutory obligations, owner-year deadlines and fee workpaper. **The final 2026 form/sides/lines, B/K/IW transfer instructions, Schedule T numerical output, military zero-line entries and owner-return line mapping remain non-usable pending the issued instructions.** Check this point with one of our partner accountants.

### Penalties and interest ([R&TC §§19131–19135](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=RTC&sectionNum=19131.))

- **Late filing:** unless reasonable cause and no willful neglect, 5% of tax remaining after timely payments/credits per month or part, maximum 25%. If the extended filing deadline is missed, measure from the original due date ([§19131](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=RTC&sectionNum=19131.)).
- **Late payment:** unless reasonable cause and no willful neglect, 5% of total unpaid tax plus 0.5% of remaining tax per month/part, up to 40 months and an overall 25% cap for that penalty. Section 19132(b) reduces this penalty by the overlapping §§19131/19133 penalties; do not assert a universal combined cap covering every penalty ([§19132](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=RTC&sectionNum=19132.)). Annual tax has its earlier 3522 deadline; fee/consent tax has the original return deadline.
- **Notice-and-demand penalty:** $2,000 per taxable year for a doing-business unqualified/forfeited foreign LLC or suspended domestic LLC that fails to file within **60 days after** FTB notice and demand, absent reasonable cause and no willful neglect. It is additional, not an automatic charge merely for failing SOS registration ([§19135](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=RTC&sectionNum=19135.)).
- **Interest:** runs from the original payment due date; an extension does not stop it. No current rate or dated interest amount is supplied here ([2025 booklet G](https://www.ftb.ca.gov/forms/2025/2025-568-booklet.html)).
- **Per-member penalty hold:** §19172's partnership wording does not establish a disregarded SMLLC's liability. The old $18 per month/member, 12-month/$216 maximum, Case E $144 and historical Test 568-7 $72 are **not usable SMLLC penalty assessments**. Check this point with one of our partner accountants.

### Cancellation ([R&TC §17947](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=RTC&sectionNum=17947.))

For ordinary closure, prepare the final Form 568 and the SOS dissolution/cancellation documents (LLC-3/LLC-4/7 as applicable). Merely stopping trade or checking final does not stop annual tax. Relief for the following year requires **all** of: a timely final preceding-year return including extensions; no California business after that final year; and the prescribed SOS termination filing within 12 months starting when the final return was filed. A qualifying final annual return must not require a subsequent return to reflect tax under this chapter ([§17947](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=RTC&sectionNum=17947.), [2025 booklet H](https://www.ftb.ca.gov/forms/2025/2025-568-booklet.html)).

**Short-form cancellation (LLC-4/8):** the domestic LLC must never have conducted business; file within 12 months of its articles. The certificate states its name/SOS number, no debts/liabilities except the final tax-return provision, final franchise/annual return has been or will be filed, remaining assets distributed after paying/adequately providing for known liabilities (or no assets acquired), no business since articles, the statutory **50% or more voting-interest** approval, and investor payments returned. Execution follows the member/manager/organizer hierarchy in [Corporations Code §17707.02](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=CORP&sectionNum=17707.02.); do not replace it with a loose “majority” test. A qualifying filed certificate invokes first-year annual-tax relief through [§17941(e)/§23153(f)(1)](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=RTC&sectionNum=17941.), **without reimbursement of annual taxes or fees already paid**. It does not establish a general LLC-fee waiver.

## Worked cases

### Case A: resident consultant, 2026

Original facts retained: California SMLLC formed in **2023**, resident individual/calendar owner, **$180,000** consulting fees from California clients and no other income. Assuming the clients receive the service benefit in California, fee income is $180,000: fee zero, annual tax **$800 due 15 April 2026**, entity charges $800; Form 568 due **15 April 2027**. Owner reports the activity on Form 540. The 2026 final form layout is held above ([§17942](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=RTC&sectionNum=17942.), [§18633.5](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=RTC&sectionNum=18633.5.)).

### Case B: reseller, 2026

Original facts: individual/calendar owner; goods delivered only to California buyers; receipts **$620,000**, COGS **$400,000**, 2025 fee **$900** and timely 2026 estimate **$900 on 15 June**. Assuming ordinary non-US-government purchasers, gross income $220,000 plus COGS gives **$620,000** fee income, fee **$2,500**. Prior-year protection avoids the 10% penalty; balance **$1,600 due 15 April 2027**. Annual tax plus fee is **$3,300**. The old net-income approach would incorrectly produce a nil fee ([§17942](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=RTC&sectionNum=17942.)).

```oa-check
name: CA SMLLC 2026 reseller fee base and balance
inputs:
  gross_receipts: 620000
  cogs: 400000
  fee: 2500
  estimate_paid: 900
  annual_tax: 800
steps:
  - gross_income: gross_receipts - cogs
  - ca_fee_income: gross_income + cogs
  - balance_with_return: fee - estimate_paid
  - entity_total: annual_tax + fee
expected:
  gross_income: 220000
  ca_fee_income: 620000
  balance_with_return: 1600
  entity_total: 3300
```

### Case C: first year beginning 10 September 2026

Original individual/calendar owner files domestic articles **10 September 2026** and trades immediately. September is month one: **$800 due 15 December 2026**. The year ends **31 December 2026** before the sixth-month date in February 2027, so no 2026 estimated fee; any fee is due **15 April 2027**. The second-year annual tax is **$800 due 15 April 2027**, not the first-year $400 relief ([§17941](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=RTC&sectionNum=17941.), [2026 FTB 3536](https://www.ftb.ca.gov/forms/2026/2026-3536.pdf)).

### Case D: Nevada-resident owner, 2025 return

Original calendar facts: California total income **$300,000**, California-source net income **$120,000**, annual tax **$800 paid 15 April 2025**. Fee **$900**, estimate deadline corrected to **16 June 2025**; balance and consent tax due **15 April 2026**, even if the return is extended to **15 October 2026**. Signed consent leaves the owner to report on Form 540NR. Without consent, **2025** Schedule T is 12.3% × $120,000 = **$14,760**, less qualifying withholding already paid, floored at zero; credited to the owner, who still has a filing obligation. Consent is not a way to avoid that obligation. The owner's final tax is not computed at this flat rate ([2025 Schedule T](https://www.ftb.ca.gov/forms/2025/2025-568-booklet.html), [§18633.5](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=RTC&sectionNum=18633.5.)).

```oa-check
inputs:
  ca_source_income: 120000
  schedule_t_rate: 0.123
  withholding_already_paid: 0
steps:
  - schedule_t_tax: max(0, ca_source_income * schedule_t_rate - withholding_already_paid)
expected:
  schedule_t_tax: 14760
```

### Case E: 2025 return filed 20 November 2026

Original resident individual/calendar owner; 2025 fee **$900**, 2024 fee **nil**, annual **$800 timely paid**, no fee paid; return **20 November 2026** after **15 October** extension. Nil prior fee protects against the estimate penalty. Absent reasonable cause/relief, eight months or parts from **15 April 2026** gives late-filing penalty **$225** (25% cap). If the $900 remains unpaid through that filing date, the ordinary late-payment computation is $81 (5% + eight × 0.5%); §19132(b) reduces it to zero against the $225 filing penalty. It is not a universal cap on all penalties. Interest depends on actual payment dates/rates. The old **$144** SMLLC per-member assessment remains non-usable. Check this point with one of our partner accountants.
