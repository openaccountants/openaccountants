# California (US-CA): tax pack

Calendar tax year for individuals. Income tax authority: the Franchise Tax Board (FTB), ftb.ca.gov; sales and use tax: the California Department of Tax and Fee Administration (CDTFA); payroll: the Employment Development Department (EDD). Currency: US dollars. California taxes residents on all income at nine rates from 1% to 12.3% (2026 FTB tax rate schedules), plus a 1% Behavioral Health Services Tax on taxable income over $1,000,000 (R&TC 17043). It follows the Internal Revenue Code as of January 1, 2025 (R&TC 17024.5) and so does not follow the One Big Beautiful Bill Act. An LLC doing business in California pays an $800 annual tax. A US state: federal income tax, FICA and FUTA sit on top of every domain here.
