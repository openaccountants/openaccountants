# California State Historic Rehabilitation Tax Credit, 2026 and the AB 1265 credit from 2027 v0.1

> **General reference only.** This skill is general tax/accounting reference material for AI-assisted workflows. It has not been reviewed for any specific person's facts, documents, elections, deadlines, residency, filing status, or local procedures. Do not rely on it to file, pay, amend, or take a tax position without review by a qualified professional in the relevant jurisdiction.

> **Source-cited draft.** Built from the Franchise Tax Board's final analysis of AB 1265 (readable PDF); the chaptered text of AB 1265 (Stats. 2026, Ch. 515) and the codified R&TC 17053.91, 17053.92, 23691 and 23692 in the official California Legislature bulk data archive; 26 U.S.C. 47 and 50 on govinfo.gov; the Office of Historic Preservation's State Historic Rehabilitation Tax Credit page; and the FTB 2025 Form 540 booklet and form FTB 3835. All were read on 2026-11-22 (California, Pacific time). No accountant has reviewed it.

**Answer first.**

- **Two credits, two periods.**
  - **Taxable years beginning in 2021 to 2026:** the existing credit (R&TC 17053.91 for personal income tax, 23691 for corporation tax). It is 20% of qualified rehabilitation expenditures (QREs), or 25% for listed project types, plus a USD 5,000 to 25,000 credit for an owner-occupied qualified residence. The credit is USD 0 "unless otherwise specified in any bill providing for appropriations related to the Budget Act". OHP says funding for 2025 to 2026 "has been exhausted" ([OHP, State Historic Rehabilitation Tax Credit](https://ohp.parks.ca.gov/?page_id=27495)).
  - **Taxable years beginning on or after 1 January 2027 and before 1 January 2032:** the new AB 1265 credit (R&TC 17053.92 and 23692). It is 20% of QREs for a certified historic structure, and the credit "shall not exceed five million dollars ($5,000,000) per taxpayer" ([FTB final analysis of AB 1265](https://www.ftb.ca.gov/tax-pros/law/legislation/2025-2026/AB1265-FINAL.pdf), p. 1; R&TC 17053.92(a), 23692(a)). The new sections have no 25% rate and no qualified residence credit, unlike R&TC 17053.91(a)(2) and (a)(3).
- **Allocation first.** No allocation, no credit. The taxpayer applies to the State Office of Historic Preservation (OHP) with the California Tax Credit Allocation Committee (CTCAC). Under the new credit:
  - The pool is "the amount authorized by the Legislature in the annual Budget Act or another measure, plus any carryover of unallocated credits from prior years". **No 2027 amount was found** in the sources read.
  - Applicants are ranked by a score of QREs plus USD 600,000 per housing unit created, preserved or rehabilitated.
  - Rounds: applications 1 to 31 August (awards by 15 October) and 1 to 28 February (awards by 15 April), starting 1 August 2027.
  - The final credit is allocated on CTCAC's receipt of a cost certification, issued by a licensed CPA when QREs exceed USD 250,000.
- **Claiming.** Claim the whole credit "in the first taxable year in which the structure is placed in service". Carry any excess over the tax to "the following year, and the seven succeeding years". Reduce basis by the credit, and take no deduction for the expenses credited.

**Tax year.** Tax year label 2026-27. AB 1265 took effect immediately as a tax levy (SEC. 4); the codified sections read "Effective September 27, 2026". The existing credit applies to taxable years beginning on or after 1 January 2021 and before 1 January 2027; R&TC 17053.91 is repealed on 1 December 2028 and R&TC 23691 on 1 December 2027. The new credit applies to taxable years beginning on or after 1 January 2027 and before 1 January 2032, and both new sections are repealed on 1 December 2032. Both periods are shown below. Because credits are claimed in the placed-in-service year, a new-credit allocation produces a claim no earlier than the 2027 taxable year; FTB's revenue estimate assumes the revenue loss begins in the 2028 taxable year, "because the allocated credits cannot be used until the building is placed in service" (FTB final analysis, p. 7).

**Circular 230 disclosure.** This material is general information, not written advice on a specific transaction under 31 CFR 10.37. It is not intended to be used, and cannot be used, to avoid penalties.

## Scope and who this is for

In scope:

- **Individuals, estates, trusts and partners** (Personal Income Tax Law: R&TC 17053.91, 17053.92).
- **C corporations** (Corporation Tax Law: R&TC 23691, 23692).
- **Partnerships** applying for the credit and allocating it to partners.

The job: decide which credit period applies, whether the building and the spending qualify, what credit an allocation can produce, and how to claim, carry over and protect it.

Out of scope, and where it goes:

| Topic | Where |
| --- | --- |
| The federal rehabilitation credit (IRC 47), including its five-year ratable claim | refer; federal |
| S corporations and Form 100S | refer to the separate Form 100S guidance |
| National Register nominations, Part 1 to 3 certification and the Secretary of the Interior's Standards | refer to OHP and the National Park Service |
| The OHP and CTCAC regulations, application forms and fees (not yet adopted for the new credit) | refer to OHP |
| Low-income housing credits and other credits on the same project | refer |
| Property tax (Mills Act contracts) | refer (local) |

## Ask the client first

- When does the taxable year in which the building will be placed in service begin? Before 2027 (existing credit) or 2027 to 2031 (new credit)?
- Has the taxpayer received a tax credit allocation from OHP and CTCAC? Under which program, for how much, and with what confirmation number?
- Is the building listed on the California Register of Historical Resources, and is it a "certified historic structure" under IRC 47(c)(3) (National Register listed, or certified as contributing to a registered historic district)?
- Is the building depreciable (rental, commercial, or other business use), and will the QREs in the 24-month measuring period exceed the greater of its adjusted basis or USD 5,000?
- What are the QREs, excluding acquisition cost and enlargements? How many housing units will the project create, preserve or rehabilitate? In which county is it?
- Is the applicant a partnership? If so, how does the partnership agreement allocate the credit?
- What is the taxpayer's expected California tax for the placed-in-service year and the following years?
- Is any sale of the building, or of a partnership interest, planned within five years of placing it in service?

### Steps

1. **Pick the period.** Use the taxable year in which the structure is placed in service, because the credit is claimed in that year. A year beginning before 1 January 2027 uses the existing credit; a year beginning in 2027 to 2031 uses the new credit.
2. **Check the building.**
   - It must be a "certified historic structure" under IRC 47(c)(3), meaning listed in the National Register or located in a registered historic district and certified as significant to it ([26 U.S.C. 47(c)(3)](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCODE-2024-title26/html/USCODE-2024-title26-subtitleA-chap1-subchapA-partIV-subpartE-sec47.htm)), **and** "a structure in this state" that "is listed on the California Register of Historical Resources" (R&TC 17053.92(b)(1)).
   - The IRC 47 tests still apply "except as otherwise provided". The building must be "substantially rehabilitated" (QREs during the 24-month period selected by the taxpayer must "exceed the greater of" the building's adjusted basis or USD 5,000), and depreciation must be allowable (IRC 47(c)(1)). Refer close cases.
3. **Measure QREs.** QREs follow IRC 47(c)(2): amounts chargeable to capital account for depreciable nonresidential or residential rental property, excluding acquisition cost and enlargements. California adds that a building's tax-exempt use does not disqualify QREs, and the 2017 federal amendment to IRC 47(c)(2)(B)(iv) (Pub. L. 115-97, sec. 13402(b)(1)(B)) does not apply; FTB reads this as removing the requirement that the expenditure be attributable to "a certified rehabilitation identified by the Secretary of the Interior" (FTB final analysis, p. 2). The pre-2017 text of IRC 47(c)(2)(B)(iv), shown in govinfo's amendment notes, also refers to a certified rehabilitation, so the practical effect is not settled; refer. OHP must still ensure the project meets the Secretary of the Interior's Standards (R&TC 17053.92(g)(3)).
4. **Apply for an allocation (new credit).** In an August or February round from 1 August 2027, send OHP and CTCAC the location and site control, California Register listing, projected housing units, enumerated QREs, evidence of a financing gap, evidence of a start within 180 days, and an attestation that the project is not already complete (R&TC 17053.92(c)(2)). Rank is by score = QREs + housing units x USD 600,000.
5. **Compute the credit.** Credit = 20% x QREs, capped at USD 5,000,000 per taxpayer (new credit). The allocation, then CTCAC's certification after the cost certification, fixes the amount.
6. **Claim it.** In the placed-in-service year, attach the credit form. For the existing credit, FTB's 2025 Form 540 booklet lists "State Historic Rehabilitation Tax – FTB 3835", credit code 243 ([FTB 2025 Form 540 booklet](https://www.ftb.ca.gov/forms/2025/2025-540-booklet.html); [form FTB 3835](https://www.ftb.ca.gov/forms/2025/2025-3835.pdf)). The form and code for the new credit were not published by 22 November 2026.
7. **Carry over, adjust basis, watch recapture.** Use any excess in the following year and the seven succeeding years. Reduce the property's basis by the credit allowed and deduct none of the credited expenses. A disposition within five full years triggers IRC 50(a) recapture at the table percentage.
8. **Partnerships.** Allocate the credit to partners under the partnership agreement, whatever the federal credit allocation or substantial economic effect. If the allocation lacks substantial economic effect, a loss on selling the partnership interest before the recapture period ends is deferred to the year after it ends (R&TC 17053.92(i); FTB final analysis, p. 2).

**AUDIT FLASH POINT** (the USD 5,000,000 "per taxpayer" cap). The statute caps the credit at USD 5,000,000 "per taxpayer" without saying whether that is per year, per project or for the life of the program. FTB's revenue estimate applies "an annual cap of $5 million per taxpayer" (FTB final analysis, p. 7), but that is an estimating assumption, not a ruling. Nothing read says whether a partnership or each partner is the "taxpayer" for an allocation made to a partnership. Refer, and do not split a project across entities to multiply the cap without review.

**AUDIT FLASH POINT** (timing at the edges). A structure placed in service in a taxable year beginning in 2032 or later falls outside the new credit's operative period, and the sections are repealed on 1 December 2032, inside the eight-year carryover window. Whether allocations made before 2032 can still be claimed, and how carryovers survive the repeal, is not addressed in the sections read. Refer.

## Boundary and exception table

| Situation | Result | Basis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Building placed in service in a taxable year beginning 2026 | Existing credit (17053.91 or 23691), if allocated and funded | R&TC 17053.91 |
| Building placed in service in a taxable year beginning 2028 | New credit (17053.92 or 23692), if allocated | R&TC 17053.92 |
| National Register building not listed on the California Register | Not a certified historic structure for California | R&TC 17053.92(b)(1) |
| Owner-occupied historic home, 2027 onward | No credit: the residence credit is gone, and IRC 47 requires depreciable property | R&TC 17053.92; IRC 47(c)(1)(A)(iv) |
| Affordable housing project, 2027 onward | 20%, not 25%; housing units raise the ranking score | R&TC 17053.92(a)(1), (h)(2)(A)(ii) |
| QREs USD 30,000,000 | 20% would be USD 6,000,000; capped at USD 5,000,000 | R&TC 17053.92(a)(2) |
| QREs USD 2,000,000 | Eligible for the 20% small-project reserve | R&TC 17053.92(h)(2)(E) |
| QREs USD 250,000 or less | Cost certification needed, but not necessarily from a CPA | R&TC 17053.92(h)(2)(C) |
| Works not started within the OHP regulation period | Allocation forfeited | R&TC 17053.92(c)(4) |
| Building sold after two full years but within three full years of being placed in service | IRC 50(a) recapture at 60% (100% within one full year, then 80, 60, 40, 20) | 26 U.S.C. 50(a)(1)(B) |
| S corporation applicant | Out of scope; refer to the Form 100S guidance | Scope |

## Worked cases

Both cases are made up for illustration. Each assumes an allocation was received and certified, and that the Budget Act funded the pool, which was not confirmed for 2027.

**Case 1: large project, C corporation, the cap and the ranking score.**

- **Facts:** a calendar-year C corporation rehabilitates a building in Oakland (Alameda County, so northern California) that is on the National Register and the California Register. QREs are USD 30,000,000, and the project rehabilitates 40 housing units. It applies in the August 2027 round and places the building in service in 2029.
- **Ranking score:** USD 30,000,000 + 40 x USD 600,000 = USD 54,000,000. This only ranks the application; it is not a credit amount.
- **Credit:** 20% x USD 30,000,000 = USD 6,000,000, capped at **USD 5,000,000**.
- **Claim:** the whole USD 5,000,000 on the 2029 return, the first taxable year the building is placed in service, with a CPA's cost certification (QREs exceed USD 250,000). QREs of USD 2,500,000 or more mean the project competes outside the 20% small-project reserve.
- **Basis:** reduced by the USD 5,000,000 credit allowed.

```oa-check
name: Large historic rehabilitation, 2027 credit at 20 percent capped at 5 million, ranking score
inputs:
  qre: 30000000
  housing_units: 40
  per_unit_score: 600000
  rate: 0.20
  cap: 5000000
steps:
  - ranking_score: qre + housing_units * per_unit_score
  - credit_before_cap: qre * rate
  - credit: min(credit_before_cap, cap)
  - amount_over_cap: credit_before_cap - credit
  - basis_reduction: credit
expected:
  ranking_score: 54000000
  credit_before_cap: 6000000
  credit: 5000000
  amount_over_cap: 1000000
  basis_reduction: 5000000
```

**Case 2: small project, C corporation, carryover.**

- **Facts:** a calendar-year C corporation rehabilitates a listed commercial building in Fresno (central California) with QREs of USD 2,000,000 (inside the small-project reserve) and places it in service in 2028.
- **Credit:** 20% x USD 2,000,000 = **USD 400,000**, below the cap.
- **Use:** its California "tax" available for credits is assumed to be USD 150,000 in each of 2028, 2029 and 2030.
  - 2028: uses USD 150,000; USD 250,000 carries over.
  - 2029: uses USD 150,000; USD 100,000 carries over.
  - 2030: uses USD 100,000; nothing remains.
- **Carryover window:** the following year and the seven succeeding years, 2029 to 2036.
- **Basis:** reduced by USD 400,000, so USD 1,600,000 of the QREs remains in depreciable basis.
- **Recapture risk:** if the building were sold within one full year of being placed in service, the IRC 50(a) recapture percentage would be 100%, falling by 20 points a year. How the percentage applies to credit carried over but not yet used was not confirmed.

```oa-check
name: Small historic rehabilitation, 2027 credit, three-year use and basis reduction
inputs:
  qre: 2000000
  rate: 0.20
  cap: 5000000
  tax_2028: 150000
  tax_2029: 150000
  tax_2030: 150000
  placed_in_service_year: 2028
steps:
  - credit: min(qre * rate, cap)
  - used_2028: min(credit, tax_2028)
  - carry_after_2028: credit - used_2028
  - used_2029: min(carry_after_2028, tax_2029)
  - carry_after_2029: carry_after_2028 - used_2029
  - used_2030: min(carry_after_2029, tax_2030)
  - carry_after_2030: carry_after_2029 - used_2030
  - last_carryover_year: placed_in_service_year + 1 + 7
  - basis_after_credit: qre - credit
expected:
  credit: 400000
  used_2028: 150000
  carry_after_2028: 250000
  used_2029: 150000
  carry_after_2029: 100000
  used_2030: 100000
  carry_after_2030: 0

  basis_after_credit: 1600000
```

## When to refuse or refer

- **No allocation.** Do not compute a claimable credit without an OHP and CTCAC allocation and certification.
- **2027 funding and regulations.** Until the Budget Act amount and OHP's regulations and forms for the new credit exist, give only the statutory framework.
- **The USD 5,000,000 cap and partnerships.** Any structuring across entities, and any partnership allocation question.
- **Edge timing.** Placed-in-service years beginning in 2032 or later, carryovers after 1 December 2032, and buildings straddling the two credits.
- **Federal credit interaction, Mills Act contracts and low-income housing credits.**
- **S corporations.** Refer to the separate Form 100S guidance.
- **Recapture computations** on early sales, especially with unused carryover.

## Filing and payment

- **Allocation.** Apply to OHP in the August or February round (new credit, from 1 August 2027). Keep the allocation confirmation number, the CTCAC certification and the cost certification.
- **Return.** Claim on the return for the placed-in-service year. For the existing credit, use form FTB 3835 (line 1 is the amount allocated by CTCAC, with the Tax Credit Allocation Confirmation Number) and credit code 243. For the new credit, use the FTB form and code once published. On the 2025 form FTB 3835, carryover from prior years goes on line 3 and carryover available for future years on line 6.
- **Recapture.** Report any IRC 50(a) recapture as additional tax in the year of disposition. The 2025 form FTB 3835 has Part II, Credit Recapture (line 7), and the 2025 Form 540 booklet lists FTB 3835 among the forms whose recapture is included on line 63.
- **Records.** Keep QRE invoices, the 24-month substantial-rehabilitation computation, California Register and National Register evidence, the partnership agreement, and basis records for at least the carryover and recapture periods.

## Completion checklist

- [ ] Placed-in-service taxable year identified; existing or new credit chosen.
- [ ] National Register (or district) status and California Register listing confirmed.
- [ ] Depreciable use and the substantial-rehabilitation test checked.
- [ ] QREs measured under IRC 47(c)(2), excluding acquisition and enlargements.
- [ ] Allocation, CTCAC certification and cost certification (CPA if over USD 250,000) in hand.
- [ ] Credit = 20% of QREs, capped at USD 5,000,000 (new credit).
- [ ] Carryover schedule for the following year and seven succeeding years.
- [ ] Basis reduced by the credit; no deduction for credited expenses.
- [ ] Recapture period (five years) noted; S corporations referred.
