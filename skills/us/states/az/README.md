# Arizona (US-AZ): tax pack

Calendar tax year for individuals; corporations follow their federal year. Authorities: the Arizona Department of Revenue (ADOR, azdor.gov) for income, corporate, withholding and transaction privilege tax; the Arizona Department of Economic Security (DES, des.az.gov) for unemployment insurance. Currency: US dollars. Arizona taxes individuals at a flat 2.5%, corporations at 4.9% with a $50 minimum (A.R.S. 43-1111), and business activity through the transaction privilege tax (TPT), a tax on the seller at 5.6% state for most classifications plus county and city rates. Arizona has no corporate franchise tax, no gross receipts income tax and no estate or inheritance tax. A US state: federal income tax, FICA and FUTA sit on top of every domain here.
