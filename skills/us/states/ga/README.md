# Georgia (US-GA): tax pack

Georgia taxes individuals and corporations at the same flat rate, 4.99% for taxable years beginning in 2026 (5.19% for 2025), and charges corporations a small net worth tax. Sales and use tax is 4% state plus local taxes set by delivery jurisdiction, changing quarterly. The Department of Revenue (dor.georgia.gov, filing on the Georgia Tax Center) administers income, net worth, withholding and sales tax; unemployment insurance is run by the Department of Labor (dol.georgia.gov); entities are formed with the Secretary of State on eCorp. Georgia has no estate or inheritance tax. Pack rewritten to tax year 2026 on 2026-11-13 (tier 2, not reviewed by an accountant), apart from the three approved files below.
