# Vermont: 2026 state tax skills

Vermont taxes wages and individual business income, corporate net income with an annual minimum, retail sales/use and separate meals/rooms categories. Employers have income-tax withholding, unemployment insurance and the Child Care Contribution. Federal taxes remain separate. This pack does not describe an ordinary corporate gross-receipts tax: Vermont gross receipts instead determine the corporate minimum.
