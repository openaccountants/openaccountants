# Delaware 2026 state tax skills

Delaware has personal and corporate income taxes, seller gross receipts tax, corporate franchise/alternative-entity annual taxes and employer withholding/UI. It has no state or local general sales/use tax; that does not exempt seller receipts from GRT. [Division of Revenue](https://revenue.delaware.gov/business-tax-forms/doing-business-in-delaware/step-4-gross-receipts-taxes/). Written by Codex. Not reviewed by an accountant.
