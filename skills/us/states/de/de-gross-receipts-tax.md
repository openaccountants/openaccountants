# Delaware gross receipts tax, 2026

## Scope

Delaware imposes no state or local general sales/use tax. Its gross receipts tax is a seller tax, separate from personal/corporate income tax and entity annual tax. This 2026 skill covers common activities, exclusions, source receipts and monthly/quarterly filing. It preserves the older sole-proprietor workflow and extends the same activity rules to entities. Petroleum, insurance and specialist regimes are referral points. [State source](https://revenue.delaware.gov/business-tax-forms/doing-business-in-delaware/step-4-gross-receipts-taxes/).

**Circular 230 §10.37 disclosure:** This skill supplies general state-tax working papers from the sources below. It is not a professional approval. A qualified tax professional must review the facts, assumptions and outputs before filing.

## Ask the client first

1. Each business activity, Delaware operations/property/staff, business licence and business-group code?
2. Gross receipts by activity, period and location, including delivery records, returns and excluded items?
3. Common ownership/control and branches sharing one exclusion; any mixed retail/service/leasing operation?
4. Assigned filing frequency, 2026 lookback taxable receipts and new-business start date?
5. Government customers, out-of-state shipments, wholesale resale/delivery certificates or statutory exemption evidence?
6. Filed periods, payments, notices, changes of legal entity and closing date?

## The method, step by step

1. **Register and classify first.** Obtain the DOR business licence through One Stop before conducting the licensed business. Different activities require separate licences/returns; do not apply the service rate to all income because the entity is a sole proprietor. Register the legal entity correctly: an account-number/entity-type change needs a new application. [State source](https://revenue.delaware.gov/frequently-asked-questions/gross-receipts-tax-faqs/); [State source](https://onestop.delaware.gov/).
2. **Build gross receipts by activity and source.** No deduction for goods sold, materials, labour, interest, paid discounts, delivery costs, taxes or other expenses. Apply only a named statutory exclusion. Commercial real-property rentals belong to §2301; tangible-property leasing has separate lessor/lessee taxes under Chapter 43. A residential landlord is not automatically a commercial lessor. [State source](https://revenue.delaware.gov/frequently-asked-questions/gross-receipts-tax-faqs/); [State source](https://delcode.delaware.gov/title30/c023/index.html); [State source](https://delcode.delaware.gov/title30/c043/index.html).
3. **Resolve delivery/exemption facts.** Seller-shipped merchandise directly to a customer outside Delaware is excluded with interstate-shipment evidence. Retail pickup/delivery in Delaware remains taxable even if the buyer later takes it away. Wholesale pickup for later out-of-state delivery/consumption requires Form 373 at or before sale, certificate list and invoice notation. Government sales are treated as wholesale; they are not universally exempt. Retail §2901 has specific agricultural-producer, nonprofit and certain government-contract exclusions; casual sale, insurance-premium and other claimed exemptions must be matched to their own statute, not to the old blanket list. [State source](https://revenue.delaware.gov/frequently-asked-questions/gross-receipts-tax-faqs/); [State source](https://delcode.delaware.gov/title30/c029/index.html).
4. **Subtract the correct period exclusion once.** Common ownership/direction/control shares the activity's exclusion across the enterprise. Ordinary quarterly exclusions are three times the monthly figure, as the relevant statute provides. No unused exclusion is carried to another period. For mixed activities retain separate computations and confirm any shared-base issue with DOR. The Chapter 43 $300,000 is expressly one quarterly deduction first against nonmotor leases, remainder against motor leases. [State source](https://delcode.delaware.gov/title30/c023/index.html); [State source](https://delcode.delaware.gov/title30/c029/index.html); [State source](https://delcode.delaware.gov/title30/c043/index.html).
5. **Compute positive taxable receipts times the activity rate.** Floor the base at zero, use the assigned monthly/quarterly period and the return's rounding. State tax tips round the tax to the nearest whole dollar. The old $199.15 monthly service example below remains the exact arithmetic before return rounding. GRT is imposed on the seller; pricing can recover the cost but does not convert it into collected general sales tax. The commercial-lease statute specifically preserves contractual reimbursement rights. [State source](https://revenuefiles.delaware.gov/TaxTips/genretailers_2018.pdf); [State source](https://delcode.delaware.gov/title30/c023/index.html).

### Determine the filing period

The old annual/quarterly/monthly test based on tax dollars was incorrect. GRT uses the statutory taxable-gross-receipts lookback, not the withholding lookback threshold. For Chapter 23, the lookback is July 1 of the second preceding calendar year through June 30 immediately before the calendar year; thus 2026 uses July 1, 2024-June 30, 2025. Taxable receipts not exceeding the activity's indexed threshold qualify for quarterly filing. New businesses are initially quarterly. Keep the DOR assigned frequency; do not switch silently. Chapter 43 lessor returns are quarterly. [State source](https://delcode.delaware.gov/title30/c023/index.html); [State source](https://revenuefiles.delaware.gov/2025/Annual_Threshold_Adjustment_Factor_for_CY26.pdf); [State source](https://revenue.delaware.gov/frequently-asked-questions/gross-receipts-tax-faqs/).

## Worked cases

**Case 1:** Monthly general-service receipts $150,000, one $100,000 exclusion: $50,000 x 0.003983=$199.15, rounded $199 on the tax-tip whole-dollar convention. No expense deduction. Hypothetical figures; real §2301 rate.

```oa-check
name: DE monthly services retained example
inputs:
  receipts: 150000
  exclusion: 100000
steps:
- base: max(receipts - exclusion, 0)
- exact_tax: base * 0.003983
- return_tax: round(exact_tax)
expected:
  base: 50000
  exact_tax: 199.15
  return_tax: 199
```

**Case 2, sourcing decision:** $200,000 retail receipts include $40,000 shipped directly out of state by the seller, with documents. Delaware base after the $100,000 exclusion is $60,000; tax $448.08, rounded $448. If those same buyers picked up in Delaware, no shipment exclusion applies: base $100,000 and tax $746.80, rounded $747. Buyer destination alone does not decide.

```oa-check
name: DE retail sourcing both ways
inputs:
  receipts: 200000
  seller_shipped: 40000
  exclusion: 100000
steps:
- shipped_base: max(receipts - seller_shipped - exclusion, 0)
- pickup_base: max(receipts - exclusion, 0)
- shipped_tax: round(shipped_base * 0.007468, 2)
- pickup_tax: round(pickup_base * 0.007468, 2)
expected:
  shipped_base: 60000
  pickup_base: 100000
  shipped_tax: 448.08
  pickup_tax: 746.8
```

## When to refuse or refer

Refer petroleum composites, insurance, agricultural or casual-sale exclusions without an exact statutory match, disputed remote-business nexus, unallocated mixed businesses, acquisitions and audits/reclassification. No unsupported economic-nexus dollar threshold is supplied. Local Wilmington wage/net-profits obligations and federal deduction treatment belong to their own rules; federal ScheduleC is `us-schedule-c-and-se-computation`.

## Filing and payment

File/pay online at tax.delaware.gov; online GRT filing is mandatory subject to TIM 2020-2 exceptions. Monthly returns are due the 20 th of the following month; quarterly returns by the last day of the month after quarter-end. The 2026 date table gives actual weekend/holiday adjustments. File required zero returns until DOR closes the account, even when below the exclusion. Amend one period using the applicable amended GRT return; multiple periods/earlier years use Claim for Revision. Notify DOR when ceasing business. [State source](https://revenue.delaware.gov/frequently-asked-questions/gross-receipts-tax-faqs/); [State source](https://revenuefiles.delaware.gov/2025/2026_GR_DueDateTable.pdf).

For bookkeeping, the old patterns `DE DIV REVENUE GRT`, `STATE OF DE BUS TAX`, `DELAWARE GRT PMT` are candidate payment labels only. Reconcile to the account/period and split licence fees, tax, penalty and interest before posting. A bank label proves neither the tax base nor deductibility. Federal treatment stays in the federal skill.

## Completion checklist

- Activity and licences match actual receipts; no ordinary sales-tax collection substituted for GRT.
- Source/delivery and any government/wholesale exclusions documented.
- Enterprise exclusion, assigned frequency and 2026 threshold checked.
- Gross receipts reconciled to books; tax rounded as the return directs; zero/closing/amended returns resolved.
- Filing confirmation and payment matched by period.
