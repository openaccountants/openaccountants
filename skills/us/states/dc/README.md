# District of Columbia: tax skills (2026)

DC taxes resident individual income, corporate and unincorporated business franchises, sales/use, resident wage withholding and employer unemployment. D-20/D-30 duties are not eliminated by nonresident personal-income immunity. Currency USD. The latest annual income/franchise form mechanics are labeled 2025-2026 filing cycle; 2026 estimated-tax and payroll-return instructions and UI announcements are separately identified. This is not a claim of complete filing-ready coverage where partials remain.
