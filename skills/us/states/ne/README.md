# Nebraska state skills, 2026

> **Built from the law. Not yet approved.** Written by Codex; this version has not been reviewed by an accountant. General reference only. Under Circular 230 section 10.37, apply the cited authorities to the actual facts and obtain qualified professional review before filing or acting.

Nebraska has resident individual and corporate income tax, state/local sales/use, employer withholding/UI and a specialized financial-institution franchise tax. Ordinary 2026 corporate rate is 4.55%; individual bands use 2.46%, 3.51% and 4.55%. [final 2026 individual schedule](https://revenue.nebraska.gov/sites/default/files/doc/tax-forms/2026/2026_tax_calculation_schedule.pdf); [corporate rate law](https://nebraskalegislature.gov/laws/statutes.php?statute=77-2734.02); [financial-institution franchise law](https://nebraskalegislature.gov/laws/statutes.php?statute=77-3802).
