# Rhode Island employer withholding and unemployment, tax year 2026

> **General reference only.** Written by Codex. Not reviewed by an accountant. Apply the official sources to the taxpayer's facts and obtain qualified professional review before filing, paying or taking a tax position.

> **Circular 230 section 10.37:** Consider relevant facts, reasonable assumptions and applicable law. Evaluate federal tax implications using the federal owner skills and obtain professional review where needed.

**Answer first.** Employers must separately compute RI wage withholding, employer Employment Security/UI plus Job Development Fund, and employee TDI. For 2026, ordinary UI/JDF wage base is USD 30,800; highest-rated employers use USD 32,300. A new employer's 1% UI rate is **before** the additional 0.21% JDF. Employee TDI is 1.1% on the first USD 100,000. Sources: [final 2026 quick reference](https://dlt.ri.gov/sites/g/files/xkgbur571/files/2025-12/FINAL%202026%20Quick%20Reference.pdf); [2026 TX-17 instructions](https://dlt.ri.gov/sites/g/files/xkgbur571/files/2026-02/TX-17%202026.pdf).

## Scope

Ordinary RI private employers with covered employees, including RI withholding on RI services and applicable resident wages. Public/reimbursing, religious, household, agricultural, family and interstate cases need separate coverage research. Federal withholding/FICA/FUTA figures belong to `us-form-941-940-payroll`; RI individual liability to `ri-income-tax`. This skill owns both state withholding and SUI, with TDI/JDF needed for the same payroll workflow.

The publicly posted 2026 withholding booklet bears **DRAFT 11/07/2025** throughout, despite its explicit wages-on-or-after-1-January-2026 tables. Its percentage example is reproduced with that limitation; a final replacement is **Not yet confirmed**. The UI quick reference and TX-17 are final/current 2026 evidence.

## Ask the client first

1. Employer entity/FEIN, RI accounts, first payroll date and employee work/residence locations.
2. Employee RI W-4 allowances/additional withholding and exemption documentation.
3. Pay dates/frequency, taxable wages, benefits and bonus/supplemental payment treatment.
4. Each employee's year-to-date UI/JDF/TDI wages and employer's actual 2026 rate notice.
5. Prior monthly withholding, combined state tax liability/gross income and assigned payment frequency.
6. Quarter payroll totals, headcounts/weeks/hours, prior deposits and annual wage statements.

## The method, step by step

### Rates, bands and thresholds

| Highest-rated employer UI/JDF base | USD 32,300 if Employment Security rate is 9.19% or higher | [2026 instructions, items 3 and 6](https://dlt.ri.gov/sites/g/files/xkgbur571/files/2026-02/TX-17%202026.pdf) |
| Schedule F UI component | 0.69% to 9.19%, assigned rate notice controls | [Employment Security rates](https://dlt.ri.gov/sites/g/files/xkgbur571/files/2025-12/FINAL%202026%20Quick%20Reference.pdf) |

| Combined experienced UI/JDF | 0.90% to 9.40% | [Schedule F range](https://dlt.ri.gov/sites/g/files/xkgbur571/files/2025-12/FINAL%202026%20Quick%20Reference.pdf) |
| New employer | 1.00% UI plus 0.21% JDF = 1.21% combined, ordinary base | [new employer and JDF rows](https://dlt.ri.gov/sites/g/files/xkgbur571/files/2025-12/FINAL%202026%20Quick%20Reference.pdf) |
| TDI employee deduction | 1.1% of first USD 100,000 per employee | [TDI rate/base](https://dlt.ri.gov/sites/g/files/xkgbur571/files/2025-12/FINAL%202026%20Quick%20Reference.pdf) |
| Posted weekly withholding allowance | USD 19.23 each; high-wage allowance restriction applies | [percentage method, posted draft](https://tax.ri.gov/sites/g/files/xkgbur541/files/2025-11/2026%20Withholding%20Tax%20Booklet_d.pdf) |
| Posted weekly adjusted wage through USD 1,578 | 3.75% | [weekly percentage table, posted draft](https://tax.ri.gov/sites/g/files/xkgbur541/files/2025-11/2026%20Withholding%20Tax%20Booklet_d.pdf) |
| Over USD 1,578 through USD 3,586 | USD 59.18 +4.75% of excess over USD 1,578 | [weekly percentage table, posted draft](https://tax.ri.gov/sites/g/files/xkgbur541/files/2025-11/2026%20Withholding%20Tax%20Booklet_d.pdf) |
| Over USD 3,586 | USD 154.56 +5.99% of excess over USD 3,586 | [weekly percentage table, posted draft](https://tax.ri.gov/sites/g/files/xkgbur541/files/2025-11/2026%20Withholding%20Tax%20Booklet_d.pdf) |

1. Register using BAR/online business registration, identify payroll start, locations, officers/owners and responsible contacts, and obtain both withholding and employer UI registration. BAR section E asks expected monthly withholding and employees/first wage date. Its industry description includes out-of-state businesses with RI home-working employees. Do not assume one account covers federal or another state's payroll. Source: [BAR payroll and industry sections](https://dlt.ri.gov/sites/g/files/xkgbur571/files/2024-08/BAR-%20Business%20Application%20and%20Registration%20Form.pdf).
2. Determine RI taxable wages, work/residence sourcing and employee RI W-4 allowances. The posted booklet covers an RI employer required to withhold federally on services in RI, RI-source wages of nonresidents and specified resident/out-of-state arrangements. Refer remote/interstate exceptions before treating all wages as RI wages. Source: [coverage and wage instructions](https://tax.ri.gov/sites/g/files/xkgbur541/files/2025-11/2026%20Withholding%20Tax%20Booklet_d.pdf).
3. Use the table for the actual pay frequency. In the posted weekly percentage method, subtract USD 19.23 per allowance, then apply the table above and add requested additional withholding. Its high-wage restriction disallows allowances where annual wages exceed USD 290,800; apply the relevant frequency rule from the booklet. Do not substitute the personal-return USD 5,250 exemption or USD 11,200 standard deduction for the payroll allowance. Supplemental wages have a 5.99% option under the booklet's stated method/conditions; do not apply it to every regular paycheck. Source: [percentage method, allowance restriction and supplemental wage section](https://tax.ri.gov/sites/g/files/xkgbur541/files/2025-11/2026%20Withholding%20Tax%20Booklet_d.pdf).
4. Compute UI on each employee's remaining annual UI base at the actual assigned Employment Security rate. Compute JDF separately at 0.21% on the same applicable base; do not add JDF again if a rate has already been quoted as combined UI/JDF. UI/JDF are employer liabilities, not employee deductions. Source: [items 3 to 8](https://dlt.ri.gov/sites/g/files/xkgbur571/files/2026-02/TX-17%202026.pdf).
5. Compute employee TDI at 1.1% through USD 100,000 annual wages. Keep its separate, larger wage-base accumulator. TX-17 item 11 requires computed TDI or the amount actually withheld, whichever is greater. Reconcile excess deductions/refund issues separately. Source: [items 9 to 11](https://dlt.ri.gov/sites/g/files/xkgbur571/files/2026-02/TX-17%202026.pdf).
6. Reconcile RI withholding deposits and quarterly RI-941 separately from TX-17 UI/JDF/TDI and wage detail. TX-17 includes employee SSN/name, total wages, paid weeks/hours and headcount for payroll including each month's 12th. Report total wages even when an employee exceeds a contribution base; cap only taxable wages for the appropriate fund. Source: [items 1 to 20 and wage-record requirements](https://dlt.ri.gov/sites/g/files/xkgbur571/files/2026-02/TX-17%202026.pdf).

## Boundary and exception table

| Situation | Action |
|---|---|
| YTD wages reach UI/JDF base | Only remaining base is taxable, while TDI can continue to USD 100,000 |
| Employer rate 9.19% or higher | Use USD 32,300 UI/JDF base; do not use ordinary USD 30,800 |
| New employer | Add 0.21% JDF to 1% UI, not to an already combined rate |
| Family/owner, exempt or multi-state worker | Test coverage and wages; TX-17 identifies family exceptions and multi-state election forms |
| High wages or supplemental bonus | Follow allowance restriction and correct supplemental method; posted draft limitation remains |
| Prior-quarter corrections | Report current quarter separately and submit correction with explanation, not netted into current wages |

## Worked cases

**Case 1, ordinary weekly withholding.** The posted booklet's invented weekly example has wages USD 2,195 and one allowance. Adjusted wages USD 2,175.77 give USD 59.18 + (2,175.77 -1,578) x4.75% = USD 87.574075, or **USD 87.57** to cents. This demonstrates the posted 2026 percentage method; confirm final booklet before implementing payroll tables.

```oa-check
name: Rhode Island posted 2026 weekly withholding method
inputs:
  wages: 2195
  allowances: 1
steps:
  - adjusted_wages: wages - allowances * 19.23
  - withholding: 59.18 + (adjusted_wages - 1578) * 0.0475
expected:
  adjusted_wages: 2175.77
  withholding: 87.574075
```

**Case 2, wage-base decision.** Invented new employer has one employee with prior 2026 wages USD 30,000 and a current USD 2,000 payroll. Only USD 800 remains under the ordinary USD 30,800 UI/JDF base. UI is USD 8, JDF USD 1.68, employer total USD 9.68; employee TDI continues on the full USD 2,000, USD 22. Using the TDI base for UI would overstate employer contributions.

```oa-check
name: Rhode Island new employer UI JDF cap and continuing TDI
inputs:
  prior_wages: 30000
  current_wages: 2000
steps:
  - ui_taxable: min(current_wages, max(30800 - prior_wages, 0))
  - ui: ui_taxable * 0.01
  - jdf: ui_taxable * 0.0021
  - employer_total: ui + jdf
  - tdi: current_wages * 0.011
expected:
  ui_taxable: 800
  ui: 8
  jdf: 1.68
  employer_total: 9.68
  tdi: 22
```

## When to refuse or refer

Refer remote/interstate employees, exempt/family coverage, reimbursing entities, successor rates, rate appeals, TDI overdeductions and final withholding-table discrepancies. Do not equate payroll withholding with final personal income-tax liability.

## Filing and payment

**Withholding:** use https://taxportal.ri.gov and the withholding account. The posted booklet/BAR use expected monthly withholding: USD 600 or more means weekly remittance, USD 50 to under USD 600 monthly, below USD 50 quarterly. Weekly is due next banking day after the Sunday-to-Saturday week and must be electronic. Monthly non-quarter-ending months use WTM/RI-941M due 20th following month; quarter-ending months and quarterly filers use RI-941 due last day of following month. **RI-941 is quarterly for every employer**, regardless of deposit frequency. Reconcile the quarter, submit and keep acceptance/payment evidence. Monthly withholding at least USD 200, or larger-business criteria of combined annual RI tax at least USD 5,000 or gross income over USD 100,000, trigger electronic requirements described by the booklet. RI-W3/wage statements follow the January 31 annual reconciliation route, with calendar relief/termination rules checked. Sources: [payment frequency, quarterly/annual reconciliation and calendar](https://tax.ri.gov/sites/g/files/xkgbur541/files/2025-11/2026%20Withholding%20Tax%20Booklet_d.pdf); [payroll section and RI-941 note](https://dlt.ri.gov/sites/g/files/xkgbur571/files/2024-08/BAR-%20Business%20Application%20and%20Registration%20Form.pdf).

**UI/JDF/TDI:** use DLT's employer tax reporting route linked from [Employer Tax Unit](https://dlt.ri.gov/employers/employer-tax-unit?language=en) and submit **TX-17** for the quarter/year. Reconcile all wages, capped taxable wages, assigned rate, JDF and TDI, employee wage detail, totals and responsible-party certification; pay the combined fund amount and retain confirmation. Employers with at least 25 employees must submit wage data electronically under TX-17. The fetched blank form has an unpopulated due-date field; DLT's undated unit-page notice says third-quarter taxes are due 31 October; it does not establish 2026 holiday/weekend relief. The complete current due-date calendar, including the 2026 adjusted Q3 date, is **Not yet confirmed** here; do not assume a blank field grants an extension. Source: [2026 TX-17 form and instructions](https://dlt.ri.gov/sites/g/files/xkgbur571/files/2026-02/TX-17%202026.pdf).

TX-17 states late contribution interest 1.5% per month, USD 25 report failure penalty and 10% failure to contribute to each fund; wage-information failure adds USD 25 per failure and another USD 25 each delinquent month, maximum USD 200 per report. Apply actual assessment rules and period, rather than treating these as a payroll deduction. Source: [interest and penalties section](https://dlt.ri.gov/sites/g/files/xkgbur571/files/2026-02/TX-17%202026.pdf).

## Completion checklist

- WH and UI registrations, actual rate notice, employee declarations and work locations documented.
- Correct-frequency withholding and remaining UI/JDF/TDI bases recomputed per employee.
- Employer liabilities separated from employee deductions; RI-941 and TX-17 reconciled independently.
- Final withholding draft replacement/due-date gaps resolved; return and payment confirmations retained.
