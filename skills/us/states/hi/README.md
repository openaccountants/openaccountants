# Hawaii: 2026 state tax pack

Hawaii (US-HI) taxes individual and corporate net income and imposes a broad General Excise Tax (GET) on business gross receipts. GET fills the sales-tax job but is a tax on the seller, not conventional customer sales tax. Complementary use tax applies to qualifying imports. All four counties currently impose a 0.5% surcharge on general 4% activities. Employers have state withholding and unemployment obligations. USD applies. Authority: Hawaii DOTAX and DLIR.

| Job | covered_by | Coverage |

| Individual wage/business income tax | `hi-income-tax` | 2026 deductions and all status schedules; final form/ancillary gaps listed |
| Sales/use tax job through GET and use tax | `hi-sales-tax` | Gross receipts, all-county surcharge, pass-on, import classification, returns; exemptions gap listed |
| Corporate income tax | `hi-corporate-tax` | Ordinary C-corporation tax and alternatives; special entity and multistate gaps listed |
| Payroll withholding and state unemployment | `hi-payroll` | 2026 withholding appendix, UI base/rates, E&T, both registrations and return schedules |

Each skill is tier 2 and has not been approved by an accountant. 2025 publications retained for the 2025-2026 filing cycle are explicitly identified. Final-year unconfirmed items are not guessed.

Separate jobs outside this pack: transient accommodations, conveyance and estate tax; detailed partnership/S-corporation returns; financial institutions and special entity regimes; detailed nonresident allocation; disability, health coverage and workers compensation computations. Their exclusion from these four skills is not a claim that the obligation does not exist.
