# Kansas (US-KS): tax skills

**Built from the law. Not yet approved. Written by Codex. Not reviewed by an accountant.** Ordinary individual income, sales/use, corporate income, payroll withholding and employer unemployment jobs each have one owner below. Calendar year applies to individuals/payroll; corporations follow their actual federal tax year. KDOR administers revenue taxes; KDOL administers unemployment contributions. Currency is US dollars.

The existing researched `ks-income-tax` is retained. It supplies individual wage/business-income computation rather than a new overlapping wage skill. Sales, corporate and withholding repairs are preserved and completed where evidence supports them; `ks-payroll` and `ks-social-contributions` are distinct withholding/UI jobs, not duplicates. Separate cross-border and related-party jobs remain partial. No files or contributor names were deleted.

Corporate rate reconciliation: base 79-32,110 must be read with 74-50,321 and **Notice 23-10**, which reduced the normal rate from 4% to **3.5%** effective tax years beginning 2024, with 3% surtax above 50,000 unchanged. Notice 25-06 says no **additional revenue-triggered 2026 reduction**; it does not undo the earlier APEX cut. The corporate skill corrects the old examples and distinguishes 79-3221 return timing from 79-3225 fourth-month payment timing. [Notice 23-10](https://www.ksrevenue.gov/taxnotices/notice23-10.pdf), [Notice 25-06](https://www.ksrevenue.gov/taxnotices/notice25-06.pdf), read 2026-11-13.

The general franchise tax is repealed for tax year 2011 onward. This does not establish absence of every special gross-receipts/premium levy. Bank/thrift/trust privilege tax, insurance premium tax, property taxes, full K-120S/pass-through return detail and amended individual returns remain separate/uncompleted jobs. The old USD 55 Secretary of State fee is not verified as current 2026. [KDOR franchise page](https://www.ksrevenue.gov/bustaxtypesfranchise.html), prior read 2026-10-28 retained.

Federal computations are owned by `us-federal-income-tax-return-1040`, `us-schedule-c-and-se-computation`, `us-form-1120-c-corp`, `us-pl-86-272-income-tax-nexus` and `us-form-941-940-payroll`. Do not restate federal rates/bases here.
