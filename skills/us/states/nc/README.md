# North Carolina (US-NC): tax pack

Calendar tax year for individuals; corporations follow their own income year. Authority: the North Carolina Department of Revenue (NCDOR), ncdor.gov; unemployment insurance is run by the Division of Employment Security (DES), des.nc.gov. Currency: US dollars. For 2026 North Carolina taxes individuals at a flat 3.99% (G.S. 105-153.7), C corporations at 2% (G.S. 105-130.3, falling to 0% after 2029), every corporation's apportioned net worth through the franchise tax (G.S. 105-122), and retail sales at 4.75% state plus local and transit taxes, 6.75% to 8.25% combined from July 1, 2026 (G.S. 105-164.4; NCDOR rate table). There is no local income tax and no estate or inheritance tax. North Carolina follows the Internal Revenue Code as of July 5, 2025. A US state: federal income tax, FICA and FUTA sit on top of every domain here.
