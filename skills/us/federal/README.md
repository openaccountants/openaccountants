# United States: federal tax pack

This is the US **federal** pack for tax year 2026, administered principally by the IRS, with Social Security rules from SSA. Amounts are US dollars. Individuals generally use the calendar year; corporations may have a permitted fiscal year. State, DC and local tax packs are separate and are not certified complete by this map.
