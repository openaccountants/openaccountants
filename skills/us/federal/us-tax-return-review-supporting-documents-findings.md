# IRS examination of a filed return: supporting documents, findings and deadlines, 2026

> **Built from the law. Not yet approved.** General reference for federal examinations handled in 2026; an audited return may concern an earlier year. Apply that year's substantive tax rules. Have a qualified professional review the facts and outputs before action. For written advice under [Circular 230 §10.37](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/pcir230.pdf), establish the relevant facts, assumptions and applicable law; this Guide alone does not resolve a taxpayer's case.

**Answer first.** Start with the actual IRS letter and its response date. The IRS initiates an audit by mail. Send copies of requested supporting records, organized by year and issue. If you dispute the findings, distinguish an open examination, an assessed liability and a notice of deficiency: each has a different response route. An IRS document response or Appeals request does not replace a timely Tax Court petition. The thresholds below have conditions; do not infer that an old return is closed simply from its age.

## Scope and who this is for

Individual and business taxpayers notified that a filed federal income tax return is being examined. This covers correspondence, office and field audits, records, findings and the initial dispute routes. It does not decide whether an underlying deduction or credit is allowable.

## Ask the client first

Read the available notice, return, report and correspondence before requesting missing facts. Record:

- Notice type, mailing date, response date, delivery evidence, all later letters and any printed petition deadline; whether the notice was addressed to a person outside the United States.
- Each return year, taxpayer/entity type, actual filing evidence, original due date, extensions, assessment history and any statute-extension consent or special relief.
- Items under examination, documents requested, original or reconstructed evidence, and the precise adjustments disputed on Form 4549 or the examiner's report.
- Proposed tax and penalty for every period, any claimed refund, whether assessed tax is still unpaid or credits were denied, new evidence, prior agreements and court decisions.

## The method, step by step

### Contact, documents and response time

1. **Verify and classify the letter.** The IRS notifies audit selection by mail and does not initiate an audit by telephone. Audit interviews can be at an IRS office, the taxpayer's home or business, or the representative's office. For questionable contact, verify through independently obtained IRS contact details before disclosing information. [IRS audits](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/irs-audits).
2. **Follow the request's channel and date.** A mailed audit request identifies the mailing address; for an in-person audit, bring the requested records. Provide the documents supporting the income, deductions or credits at issue: receipts, bills, canceled checks, legal papers, employment documents and Schedules K-1 as relevant. Organize them by year and income/expense type with a transaction summary and an explanation connecting each document to the item. Send copies, never original records. Keep your response and delivery confirmation. [IRS records request](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/audits-records-request), [delivery confirmation](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/irs-audits).
3. **Request time; do not assume it.** Use the procedure below. If records are lost, identify what is missing and seek bank/payer copies or other reconstruction; do not invent evidence or assume reconstruction proves deductibility. Check this point with one of our partner accountants.

| Situation | Response rule | Official source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mail audit needing more time | Fax a written request to the number on the letter; if unable to fax, mail it to the letter's address. IRS can ordinarily grant a one-time automatic 30-day extension and contacts you if unable to grant it. | [IRS audits](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/irs-audits) |
| In-person audit needing more time | Contact the assigned auditor, or the manager if necessary; the mail-audit extension is not a blanket entitlement for interviews. | [IRS audits](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/irs-audits) |
| Notice of deficiency received by certified mail | IRS says it cannot grant more time to submit supporting documents; continued discussions do not extend the petition deadline below. | [IRS audits](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/irs-audits) |
| No response by the notice's date | IRS completes the audit and sends its report with proposed changes; do not substitute an assumed timetable for the actual letters. | [IRS audits](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/irs-audits) |

### How old a return can be: assessment is a separate test

IRS generally audits returns filed within the last 3 years; substantial errors may lead to extra years, usually no more than 6. These are selection practices, **not statutory maximums**. [IRS audits](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/irs-audits).

| Income-tax assessment situation | Basic period and operative conditions | Official source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Valid filed return, no exception | Generally 3 years after filing. A return filed before the unextended statutory due date is deemed filed on that date. Filing during an extension period starts the clock on the legally determined filing date, not automatically the extended deadline. | [26 U.S.C. 6501(a), (b)(1)](https://uscode.house.gov/us/usc/t26/s6501/), [IRS IRM 25.6.1.6.15](https://www.irs.gov/irm/part25/irm_25-006-001r) |
| Gross-income omission exceeding 25% of gross income stated in the return | 6 years after filing, subject to the statutory definition and disclosure exclusion below. It is not a test that the taxpayer reported 25% or less of actual income. | [26 U.S.C. 6501(e)(1)(A)-(B)](https://uscode.house.gov/us/usc/t26/s6501/) |
| Omission attributable to a foreign financial asset described in section 6038D | 6 years if omitted income exceeds USD 5,000; the statutory asset-reporting test disregards the reporting dollar threshold and section 6038D(h)(1) exceptions. Other international reporting rules require separate review. | [26 U.S.C. 6501(e)(1)(A)(ii)](https://uscode.house.gov/us/usc/t26/s6501/) |
| False or fraudulent return with intent to evade tax; failure to file a return | Assessment at any time under section 6501(c)(1) or (3). An IRS substitute return does not itself start the period. Suspected fraud goes to counsel. | [26 U.S.C. 6501(b)(3), (c)(1), (c)(3)](https://uscode.house.gov/us/usc/t26/s6501/) |
| Written assessment extension | Taxpayer and IRS may consent in writing before expiry; the taxpayer can refuse or limit it. This is separate from extra time to answer a letter. Obtain advice before signing. | [26 U.S.C. 6501(c)(4)](https://uscode.house.gov/us/usc/t26/s6501/) |

For the omission test, trade/business gross income means receipts from goods/services before cost of sales/services. An overstatement of basis can be an omission. An item adequately disclosed on the return or an attached statement is excluded from the omitted amount, except for the basis-overstatement rule. [26 U.S.C. 6501(e)(1)(B)](https://uscode.house.gov/us/usc/t26/s6501/).

Do not certify a final expiration date without checking filing evidence, applicable exceptions, consents, deficiency-related suspension, international reporting, postponements and weekend/holiday rules. A final date involving these adjustments is a **non-usable hold** here: Check this point with one of our partner accountants. The IRS summary page's “including extensions” and “reported 25% or less” shortcuts are not used; section 6501 and the IRS filing-date instructions above control.

### Findings, Appeals and Tax Court

4. **Read every adjustment.** If you agree, IRS asks you to sign the examination report or similar audit form. Form 4549 is the income-tax examination changes report. Do not sign until the adjustments are understood. For payment or collection use `us-irs-collections-and-controversy`. [IRS audits](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/irs-audits), [Form 4549 in reconsideration instructions](https://www.irs.gov/credits-deductions/audit-reconsideration-process-for-correspondence-examination-audits-by-mail).
5. **Dispute an open examination.** A manager conference is available; an administrative appeal depends on enough assessment time remaining. Follow the letter offering Appeals rights. Send the protest to that IRS office, **not directly to Appeals**. [IRS audits](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/irs-audits), [requesting Appeals](https://www.irs.gov/appeals/preparing-a-request-for-appeals).

| Dispute route | Threshold, deadline and action | Official source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Formal written protest | Send within the time in the appeal-rights letter, generally 30 days from its date. Required if any period exceeds USD 25,000; then use it for all periods. Count proposed increases/decreases in tax and penalties or claimed refund as instructed. | [IRS Appeals](https://www.irs.gov/appeals/preparing-a-request-for-appeals), [Publication 5, pp. 3-4](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/p5.pdf) |
| Small Case Request for an examination | Entire additional tax and penalty proposed for **each** period must be USD 25,000 or less; S corporations, partnerships, employee plans and exempt organizations are ineligible regardless of amount. Follow the letter; use Form 12203, the specified form, or a brief written statement listing disputed items and reasons. This is distinct from the Tax Court small-case election. | [IRS Appeals](https://www.irs.gov/appeals/preparing-a-request-for-appeals), [Publication 5, p. 3](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/p5.pdf) |
| Tax Court after a notice of deficiency | Ordinarily 90 days **after mailing**, or 150 days if addressed to a person outside the United States. A Saturday, Sunday or DC legal holiday is not counted as the last day. A petition filed by the IRS-specified last date in the notice is timely under section 6213(a). IRS discussions/reconsideration do not substitute for filing with the Court. Special postponements or disputed notice/deadline facts: urgent specialist hold; Check this point with one of our partner accountants. | [26 U.S.C. 6213(a)](https://uscode.house.gov/us/usc/t26/s6213/), [IRS audits](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/irs-audits) |
| Income-tax small tax case | Neither deficiency in dispute nor claimed overpayment may exceed USD 50,000 for any taxable year; disputed deficiency includes applicable additions, additional amounts and penalties under section 7463(e); taxpayer elects and Tax Court must concur before hearing. Its decision cannot be reviewed by another court and is not precedent. Do not elect solely on size. | [26 U.S.C. 7463(a)-(b)](https://uscode.house.gov/us/usc/t26/s7463/) |

A formal protest includes the request to appeal; name, address and daytime phone; each disputed issue, period, proposed change and reason; supporting facts and legal authority, if any; and the appropriate signed penalties-of-perjury statement. Publication 5 gives distinct declarations for the taxpayer and representatives with/without personal knowledge: the actual signer must choose and make their own declaration. If an authorized representative will discuss the appeal without you, provide completed Form 2848. [Publication 5, p. 4](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/p5.pdf), [IRS Appeals](https://www.irs.gov/appeals/preparing-a-request-for-appeals).

### Audit reconsideration after assessment

6. **Check eligibility before requesting reconsideration.** For an unpaid disputed audit assessment or denied credits, identify new information not previously considered, a later taxpayer-filed return after an IRS substitute, or a processing/computational error. It is not automatic merely because you disagree. A fully paid individual liability uses a formal refund claim, generally Form 1040-X. Reconsideration is unavailable after specified binding agreements (Form 906, compromise, Appeals Form 870-AD), final TEFRA partnership adjustments, or a final court determination. [Publication 3598, p. 1](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/p3598.pdf).
7. **Prepare and submit.** Review Form 4549; explain every disputed adjustment in a letter or Form 12661; attach copies of new supporting documents and Form 4549 if available. For correspondence examinations use the IRS Document Upload Tool at [irs.gov/examreply](https://www.irs.gov/examreply), or mail the request to the office that handled the audit, as identified in the audit letter. Keep copies and delivery evidence. IRS estimates a response in 30 days but says it may take months; this is no guaranteed resolution date. [IRS reconsideration instructions](https://www.irs.gov/credits-deductions/audit-reconsideration-process-for-correspondence-examination-audits-by-mail).

### Keep the evidence after the response

Retain copies of filed returns. The following are IRS **record-retention guidance**, not a complete refund-deadline or document-destruction decision. Keep records while material to an open/extended case and check other legal or contractual retention duties. [IRS retention guidance](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/how-long-should-i-keep-records), [Topic 305](https://www.irs.gov/taxtopics/tc305).

| Record situation | IRS retention guidance | Official source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ordinary income-tax records | 3 years after filing, treating early returns as filed on the due date; use longer applicable periods below, not an unconditional 3-year disposal rule. | [IRS retention guidance](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/how-long-should-i-keep-records) |
| Claim for credit/refund after filing | Later of 3 years from original return filing or 2 years from payment. This does not independently establish refundable amounts or all claim deadlines. | [IRS retention guidance](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/how-long-should-i-keep-records) |
| Claim for worthless-securities loss/bad-debt deduction | 7 years under the retention guidance. Claim filing periods are a separate calculation. | [IRS retention guidance](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/how-long-should-i-keep-records) |
| More than 25% gross-income omission | 6 years; check the assessment conditions above before treating any period as closed. | [IRS retention guidance](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/how-long-should-i-keep-records) |
| No return, or fraudulent return | Indefinitely. | [IRS retention guidance](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/how-long-should-i-keep-records) |
| Employment tax | At least 4 years after tax becomes due or is paid, whichever is later. | [IRS Topic 305](https://www.irs.gov/taxtopics/tc305) |
| Property, including exchanged-property basis records where relevant | Until the limitations period expires for the disposal year's return; preserve the records needed for basis, depreciation, amortization, depletion and gain/loss. | [IRS retention guidance](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/how-long-should-i-keep-records) |

## Boundary and exception table

| Situation | Treatment |
| --- | --- |
| Missing letter date, unclear notice type or missing petition deadline | Obtain all letters immediately; no invented response date and no assumption that every notice starts a 90-day period. |
| Deficiency notice already issued, including one more than 60 days old | Urgent tax-counsel deadline review; do not wait for Appeals, documents or reconsideration. Age of the notice is an urgency flag, not a new statutory deadline. |

## Worked cases

**Case 1, is the 6-year period open?** Preserve the original facts: a sole proprietor's **2022** return filed on time on **18 April 2023**, gross income stated **USD 200,000**, omitted receipts **USD 60,000**, alternative omission **USD 45,000**. For this illustration assume a valid return legally filed on that date, trade/business gross receipts correctly measured, an omission not adequately disclosed, no basis dispute, and no other exception. USD 200,000 × 25% = **USD 50,000**. USD 60,000 exceeds it, so the basic period is **6 years**; USD 45,000 does not, leaving the ordinary **3-year** period on these assumptions. The case's historical filing date is consistent with the [IRS 2023 filing announcement](https://www.irs.gov/newsroom/irs-kicks-off-2023-tax-filing-season-with-returns-due-april-18); the tests are in [section 6501](https://uscode.house.gov/us/usc/t26/s6501/).

The base anniversaries are **18 April 2029** and **18 April 2026**. The latter is a Saturday: the previous assertion that assessment necessarily closed on that date is withdrawn. Section 7503 provides a next-business-day rule, but applicable holidays, consents and suspensions must also be checked. **Neither date is a certified final expiration date or permission to ignore an audit.** Final-date conclusion is a non-usable hold: Check this point with one of our partner accountants. [26 U.S.C. 7503](https://uscode.house.gov/us/usc/t26/s7503/).

```oa-check
name: Six-year assessment period test
inputs:
  gross_income_stated: 200000
  omitted: 60000
  omitted_smaller: 45000
steps:
  - threshold: gross_income_stated * 0.25
  - six_year_applies: min(floor(omitted / (threshold + 0.01)), 1)
  - years_open: 3 + 3 * six_year_applies
  - six_year_if_smaller: min(floor(omitted_smaller / (threshold + 0.01)), 1)
  - years_if_smaller: 3 + 3 * six_year_if_smaller
expected:
  threshold: 50000
  six_year_applies: 1
  years_open: 6
  six_year_if_smaller: 0
  years_if_smaller: 3
```

**Case 2, a decision: Small Case Request or formal protest?** The original report proposes **USD 18,000** tax and **USD 3,600** penalty for **2023**, and **USD 22,000** tax plus **USD 4,400** penalty for **2024**. The supplied penalty figures are inputs, not a determination that a penalty applies. Totals are **USD 21,600** and **USD 26,400**. One period exceeds **USD 25,000** by **USD 1,400**, so use a formal written protest for **all periods**, even if the other period would satisfy the small-request amount test. Send it within the actual letter's deadline; assume the original case's letter specifies **30 days**, rather than treating that as universal. If both periods were within the cap and the taxpayer were eligible, the Small Case Request would be available. [Publication 5, pp. 3-4](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/p5.pdf).

```oa-check
name: Small Case Request test per period
inputs:
  tax_2023: 18000
  penalty_2023: 3600
  tax_2024: 22000
  penalty_2024: 4400
  limit: 25000
steps:
  - total_2023: tax_2023 + penalty_2023
  - total_2024: tax_2024 + penalty_2024
  - largest_period: max(total_2023, total_2024)
  - over_limit_by: max(largest_period - limit, 0)
expected:
  total_2023: 21600
  total_2024: 26400
  largest_period: 26400
  over_limit_by: 1400
```

## When to refuse or refer

A criminal investigation or fraud concern goes to tax counsel; a petitioned case to its representative/counsel. State, exempt-organization, employee-plan and special partnership procedures, missing evidence, disputed notice validity, special deadline relief and final assessment-expiry calculations require specialist review. These are non-usable outcome holds: Check this point with one of our partner accountants. Collection and voluntary amendments retain the companion destinations in Scope.

## Filing and payment

Send an audit response through the notice's instructed channel with an itemized cover letter and copies; keep the submission receipt. Send an administrative protest to the originating office identified in the Appeals-rights letter. A Tax Court petition goes to the **Tax Court**, not to the examiner or Appeals. Use the reconsideration channels above only for an eligible reconsideration. Before a representative acts alone, obtain the applicable authority; payment arrangements belong to the collections Guide. Sources: [records request](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/audits-records-request), [Appeals](https://www.irs.gov/appeals/preparing-a-request-for-appeals), [section 6213(a)](https://uscode.house.gov/us/usc/t26/s6213/).

## Completion checklist

Confirm notice and case stage; record actual deadlines and owners; map each requested item to supporting copies; retain proof of response/extension; review each adjustment and choose agreement, protest, eligible Small Case Request, reconsideration or urgent court handoff; preserve records for the applicable period. A missing fact remains an explicit hold, not a default financial or deadline conclusion.
