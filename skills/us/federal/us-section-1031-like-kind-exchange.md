# US federal like-kind exchanges of real property: 2026

Section 1031 can defer gain when business or investment real property is exchanged for like-kind real property. Both properties must meet the use test. In a deferred exchange, identify within **45 days** and receive by the earlier of **180 days** or the transfer-year return due date including extensions. Cash, other property and net debt relief can produce taxable gain; recapture can exceed that boot-driven amount. [Section 1031(a)–(d)](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCODE-2024-title26/html/USCODE-2024-title26-subtitleA-chap1-subchapO-partIII-sec1031.htm); [section 1245(b)(4)](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCODE-2024-title26/html/USCODE-2024-title26-subtitleA-chap1-subchapP-partIV-sec1245.htm).

> **Source-cited draft.** Federal tax year 2026; no professional approval is claimed. This written reference depends on the facts and assumptions below and does not establish a filing position for an undocumented transaction. Obtain qualified tax advice on actual facts before filing (Circular 230 §10.37).

## Scope and who this is for

Individuals, sole proprietors, disregarded LLC owners, corporations, partnerships, trusts and estates exchanging real property held for business or investment. Simultaneous and deferred exchanges are covered; reverse and improvement arrangements have additional conditions below. Dealer inventory and solely personal residences do not qualify. The use test applies to each property; there is no general one-year or two-year holding safe harbor. Short holds, development, recent conversions and prearranged resale need factual review. [Section 1031(a)](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCODE-2024-title26/html/USCODE-2024-title26-subtitleA-chap1-subchapO-partIII-sec1031.htm); [Rev. Proc. 2005-14 §2.05](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-drop/rp-05-14.pdf).

## Ask the client first

Collect legal owner/taxpayer and entity classification; addresses, country and legal interests; business, rental, resale and personal-use history; transfer, identification and receipt dates; tax year and extension; QI/EAT agreements, agency relationships, related entities and access to proceeds; values, mortgages, cash and other property on both sides; closing statements; adjusted basis, land allocation and depreciation/component schedules; home-use dates; construction progress; disaster facts; and relevant states. Obtain fair-rental and personal-use logs for each vacation-home testing period.

## The method, step by step

### Property and like-kind tests

Real property includes land, improvements, unsevered natural products, and water/air space above land. Test distinct assets and permanency under Reg. 1.1031(a)-3; local-law real-property status at transfer is another route, subject to its intangible exclusions. Equipment, vehicles, aircraft, boats, livestock, collectibles, supplies, severed products and business goodwill are not made eligible merely by being transferred with a building. Depreciation classification is separate: section 1245 property can still be real property for section 1031. [Reg. 1.1031(a)-3(a)](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CFR-2025-title26-vol13/pdf/CFR-2025-title26-vol13-sec1-1031a-3.pdf).

Listed real-property intangibles include fee ownership, co-ownership, leaseholds, options, easements, cooperative housing stock and land-development rights. Similar rights must derive value from real property and be inseparable from it. Mutual ditch/reservoir/irrigation shares require the stated section 501(c)(12)(A) and state-law conditions. An operating-business permit, other stocks/bonds/notes/securities, trust/beneficial interests and choses in action are excluded despite local law. Partnership interests are excluded except a valid section 761(a) election out of all subchapter K: then test underlying assets. [Reg. 1.1031(a)-3(a)(5)](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CFR-2025-title26-vol13/pdf/CFR-2025-title26-vol13-sec1-1031a-3.pdf); [section 1031(e)](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCODE-2024-title26/html/USCODE-2024-title26-subtitleA-chap1-subchapO-partIII-sec1031.htm).

Like kind concerns nature or character, not grade or quality. The ordinary pairs below assume qualifying US real property on each side, held for business/investment, and all other conditions met.

### Qualified intermediary and control of funds

A QI is a safe harbor, not a statutory requirement for every exchange. The QI must be neither taxpayer nor disqualified, and must enter a written exchange agreement to acquire/transfer both properties. Direct conveyances can qualify through the regulatory contract/assignment rules; notify all relevant contract parties in writing of an assignment on or before the relevant transfer. Qualified escrow/trust arrangements are separate safe harbors. [Reg. 1.1031(k)-1(g)(3)–(4)](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CFR-2025-title26-vol13/pdf/CFR-2025-title26-vol13-sec1-1031k-1.pdf).

Disqualified persons include an actual agent, and an employee, attorney, accountant, investment banker/broker or real-estate agent/broker acting within the 2 years ending on the first relinquished transfer. Prior section 1031 exchange services and the listed routine financial/title/escrow/trust services are excepted. Apply sections 267(b)/707(b) relationships with **10% substituted for 50%** to the taxpayer and, separately, the agent-related-person test; the latter bank/bank-affiliate exception applies only where disqualification would arise solely from membership in the same section 267(f) controlled group (using 10%) as an investment banker/broker that served the taxpayer in the two-year period. A qualifying bank affiliate must principally facilitate section 1031 exchanges and be wholly owned by a bank/bank holding company. [Reg. 1.1031(k)-1(k)](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CFR-2025-title26-vol13/pdf/CFR-2025-title26-vol13-sec1-1031k-1.pdf).

The agreement must restrict receiving, pledging, borrowing or otherwise benefiting from funds. Constructive receipt includes funds credited, set aside or freely available; an agent's receipt generally counts, except the qualifying safe harbors. Receipt of **all** consideration before replacement makes a sale, even if replacement is later bought. Partial cash boot does not automatically destroy an otherwise qualifying exchange; direct payment by another party can coexist with the QI safe harbor. [Reg. 1.1031(k)-1(f), (g)(4)(vii)](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CFR-2025-title26-vol13/pdf/CFR-2025-title26-vol13-sec1-1031k-1.pdf).

The agreement may permit release after the exchange period, or: after identification-period end if nothing was identified; after receipt of all replacement property to which the agreement entitles the taxpayer; or, after identification-period end, on a written material/substantial exchange contingency beyond the taxpayer's and disqualified person's control (except the person obliged to transfer replacement). A generic default clause does not establish this exception. [Reg. 1.1031(k)-1(g)(6)](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CFR-2025-title26-vol13/pdf/CFR-2025-title26-vol13-sec1-1031k-1.pdf).

Incidental personal property can be disregarded for the (g)(6) restrictions if typically transferred together AND no more than **15% of aggregate replacement-real-property FMV**; it is still non-like-kind for gain purposes. Interest/growth factors are interest, with separate restrictions to prevent early receipt. Do not treat retained QI earnings or an imputed-interest question as resolved: **HOLD. Check this point with one of our partner accountants.** [Reg. 1.1031(k)-1(g)(5), (g)(7), (h)](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CFR-2025-title26-vol13/pdf/CFR-2025-title26-vol13-sec1-1031k-1.pdf).

### Reverse and improvement exchanges

For the QEAA safe harbor, an EAT must hold qualified indicia of ownership continuously until the prescribed transfer. It cannot be taxpayer or disqualified, and must be subject to federal income tax; a partnership/S corporation EAT instead needs **more than 90%** of interests/stock owned by taxable partners/shareholders. Qualified indicia include title, commercial-law beneficial ownership such as contract for deed, or ownership of a disregarded entity holding those interests. Taxpayer must have bona fide exchange intent when ownership moves to EAT. [Rev. Proc. 2000-37 §4.02(1)–(2), bulletin pp. 308–310](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-irbs/irb00-40.pdf).

| QEAA requirement | Limit and condition | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Written agreement | Within 5 business days of EAT's acquisition; state facilitation purpose, EAT beneficial ownership for all federal income tax purposes and consistent reporting by both parties | [Rev. Proc. 2000-37 §4.02(3)](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-irbs/irb00-40.pdf) |
| Identification | Within 45 days of EAT acquiring replacement, identify relinquished property consistently with deferred identification principles | [Rev. Proc. 2000-37 §4.02(4)](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-irbs/irb00-40.pdf) |
| Transfer and combined parking | Within 180 days transfer parked property to taxpayer as replacement, or to someone neither taxpayer nor disqualified as relinquished; combined QEAA holding of both properties ≤ 180 days | [Rev. Proc. 2000-37 §4.02(5)–(6)](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-irbs/irb00-40.pdf) |
| Prior ownership bar | Safe harbor does not cover replacement owned by taxpayer within the 180-day period ending with transfer to EAT | [Rev. Proc. 2004-51 §4.03](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-drop/rp-04-51.pdf) |

The safe harbor permits taxpayer loans, guarantees, leasing/management and construction supervision under §4.03; these do not waive the other requirements. It also accommodates parking relinquished property (exchange first). Outside its terms, no inference of eligibility follows. [Rev. Proc. 2000-37 §§3–4](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-irbs/irb00-40.pdf). An exchange for improvements on land already owned by the same taxpayer does not meet section 1031. [Rev. Proc. 2004-51 §2.05](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-drop/rp-04-51.pdf).

Property to be produced can be identified by land legal description and as much construction detail as practicable; estimate its FMV at expected receipt for the identification value tests. Usual production changes are permitted, substantial changes are not. For unfinished real property, count only qualifying real property actually received, assuming completed property would be substantially the same; subsequent construction services are not like-kind receipts. There is no extra construction extension. [Reg. 1.1031(k)-1(e)](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CFR-2025-title26-vol13/pdf/CFR-2025-title26-vol13-sec1-1031k-1.pdf). Unusual construction, own-land/leasehold or arrangements outside the safe harbor: **HOLD. Check this point with one of our partner accountants.**

### Compute boot, recapture and basis in that order

1. Separate non-like-kind property given up: Form 8824 lines 12/12a identify FMV/description; line 13 is adjusted basis; line 14 reports its gain/loss as a sale, subject to the applicable tax rules. Test actual real-property status independently of section 1245 labels. [2026 instructions, lines 12–14](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/i8824.pdf).
2. Line 15 = cash received + FMV other property received + positive excess of liabilities assumed by the other party over (liabilities taxpayer assumes + cash taxpayer pays + FMV other property taxpayer gives up), less exchange expenses, never below zero. Do **not** offset cash received by excess replacement debt. In the original debt-only illustration, USD 400,000 relieved less USD 300,000 assumed gives USD 100,000 net debt relief before any other offsets. Describe other receipts on line 15a. Recourse debt counts where recipient agrees and is expected to satisfy it; nonrecourse debt generally follows the property, with the section 357(d) shared-collateral exception. [2026 instructions, lines 15–15a](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/i8824.pdf); [Reg. 1.1031(d)-2](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CFR-2025-title26-vol13/pdf/CFR-2025-title26-vol13-sec1-1031d-2.pdf).
3. Line 16 = FMV like-kind property received; line 17 = lines 15 + 16. Line 18 = relinquished adjusted basis + expenses not already used on line 15 + positive excess of (liabilities assumed + cash paid + FMV other property given) over liabilities assumed by the other party. Line 19 = line 17 − line 18. Line 20 generally = max(0, min(line 15, line 19)); see home exception below. The like-kind loss is deferred, not currently deducted. [Section 1031(b)–(c)](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCODE-2024-title26/html/USCODE-2024-title26-subtitleA-chap1-subchapO-partIII-sec1031.htm); [2026 instructions, lines 15–20](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/i8824.pdf).
4. **Section 1245:** first compute section 1245(a)(1) ordinary gain for the component from recomputed basis, adjusted basis and allocated amount realized. In an otherwise qualifying exchange, section 1245(b)(4) caps it at other recognized gain plus FMV of acquired non-section-1245 property not already counted. It can exceed line 20, including with no cash boot. Truly non-real-property components do not gain deferral from section 1031. [Section 1245(a), (b)(4)](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCODE-2024-title26/html/USCODE-2024-title26-subtitleA-chap1-subchapP-partIV-sec1245.htm); [2026 instructions, line 21](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/i8824.pdf).
5. **Section 1250:** for ordinary income from additional depreciation, line 21 is the smaller of sale recapture or the larger of line 20 and (sale recapture − FMV section 1250 property received). This is distinct from unrecaptured section 1250 capital gain; never assume all depreciation carries over untaxed or tax all of it at a flat rate. Untriggered additional-depreciation potential carries under section 1250(d)(4)(E); the linked IRS allocation example carries USD 5,000 of potential recapture to the received building. [Section 1250(d)(4)](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCODE-2024-title26/html/USCODE-2024-title26-subtitleA-chap1-subchapP-partIV-sec1250.htm); [2026 instructions, line 21](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/i8824.pdf).
6. Line 22 = max(line 20 − line 21, 0); line 23 = lines 21 + 22. Line 24 = line 19 − line 23 (or deferred loss). Line 25 = line 18 + line 23 − line 15, modified by section 121 below. Allocate replacement basis on lines 25a–25c under the 2026 instructions; other non-like-kind property received takes FMV basis and ordinary recapture can require special allocation. Do not blindly allocate all mixed assets pro rata. [Section 1031(d)](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCODE-2024-title26/html/USCODE-2024-title26-subtitleA-chap1-subchapO-partIII-sec1031.htm); [2026 Form 8824](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/f8824.pdf).
7. Holding-period tacking under section 1223(1) requires basis determined wholly/partly by relinquished basis and relinquished property that was a capital asset or section 1231 property. It does not itself prove held-for-investment intent. [Section 1223(1)](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCODE-2024-title26/html/USCODE-2024-title26-subtitleA-chap1-subchapP-partIII-sec1223.htm).

Exchange expenses include qualifying brokerage, attorney, deed-preparation and acquisition closing costs. Property taxes, rent prorations, security deposits and repairs on the statement are not exchange expenses. Distinguish financing costs, and never deduct the same expense on both lines 15 and 18. [Pub. 544, Exchange expenses](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p544); [2026 instructions, line 18](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/i8824.pdf).

### Replacement depreciation

For MACRS property within Reg. 1.168(i)-6 (excluding its tax-exempt-use exception), exchanged basis is the lesser of replacement basis and relinquished adjusted depreciable basis after disposition-year depreciation; excess basis is the remainder. With unchanged recovery period/method, continue the old schedule. A longer replacement period is applied as though replacement was placed in service when relinquished property was; a shorter period retains the old period. A less accelerated replacement method applies; a more accelerated one does not accelerate exchanged basis. Apply the required convention, including mid-month if either property is mid-month property. Account for disposition/replacement-year allocations and any intervening-year gap before claiming a deduction. [Reg. 1.168(i)-6(b)–(c)](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CFR-2025-title26-vol3/pdf/CFR-2025-title26-vol3-sec1-168i-6.pdf).

Depreciable excess basis is generally newly placed in service at replacement and follows the applicable new-property period, method and convention. Eligibility for section 179/bonus is a separate test. Election out under (i)(1) treats the combined basis as newly placed in service **for depreciation only**, not as a taxable disposal for section 1031/recapture. Make it separately for each exchange, by the owner (partnership/S corporation, not owners separately; common parent for consolidated members), on Form 4562 as instructed by the replacement-year return due date including extensions. Revocation requires IRS consent in extraordinary circumstances. [Reg. 1.168(i)-6(d), (g)–(j)](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CFR-2025-title26-vol3/pdf/CFR-2025-title26-vol3-sec1-168i-6.pdf).

## Worked cases

Cases 2–4 retain the IRS figures. Cases 1 and 5–7 are illustrations. Historical examples below keep their original years and inputs.

**Case 1: return due date.** Calendar-year individual, rental transferred **15 November 2026**: day 45 = **30 December 2026**; day 180 = **14 May 2027**. Assuming ordinary domestic individual filing dates and no special relief, unextended return due **15 April 2027** cuts the period short. Timely extension to **15 October 2027** lets day 180 control; payment is still due in April. The older original **15 November 2025** case likewise gives **30 December 2025**, **14 May 2026**, and unextended/extended return dates **15 April / 15 October 2026**. Use the actual transferor's deadline, not an individual's deadline for every entity. The old "after 17 October" shortcut works for these non-leap-year date pairs, not every year; the still older "after July 4" shortcut is withdrawn. [Section 1031(a)(3)](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCODE-2024-title26/html/USCODE-2024-title26-subtitleA-chap1-subchapO-partIII-sec1031.htm); [IRS extension guidance](https://www.irs.gov/filing/get-an-extension-to-file-your-tax-return); [section 6072(a)](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCODE-2024-title26/html/USCODE-2024-title26-subtitleF-chap61-subchapA-partV-sec6072.htm).

**Case 2: cash and debt boot.** Investment real estate FMV **USD 110,000**, basis **USD 80,000**, received property **USD 100,000** plus **USD 10,000** cash, exchange expenses **USD 5,000**: amount realized **USD 105,000**, gain **USD 25,000**, boot/recognized gain **USD 5,000**, deferred gain **USD 20,000**, replacement basis **USD 80,000**. This assumes no separate recapture. Variant: the other party assumes **USD 30,000** old debt, no offsetting debt assumed: realized gain **USD 55,000**, recognized **USD 35,000**. This is the IRS's consideration-based example, not an assertion that an unchanged USD 110,000 gross sale would itself produce extra consideration. [Pub. 544, Partially Nontaxable Exchanges](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p544).

```oa-check
name: Section 1031 boot, cash and debt relief (Pub. 544 example)
inputs:
  fmv_given: 110000
  basis_given: 80000
  cash_received: 10000
  expenses: 5000
  debt_relief_variant: 30000
steps:
  - realized: fmv_given - expenses - basis_given
  - boot: max(cash_received - expenses, 0)
  - recognized: min(realized, boot)
  - deferred: realized - recognized
  - new_basis: basis_given + recognized - boot
  - realized_variant: fmv_given + debt_relief_variant - expenses - basis_given
  - recognized_variant: min(realized_variant, cash_received + debt_relief_variant - expenses)
expected:
  realized: 25000.00
  recognized: 5000.00
  deferred: 20000.00
  new_basis: 80000.00
  realized_variant: 55000.00
  recognized_variant: 35000.00
```

**Case 3: recapture and form lines.** Taylor's building: FMV **USD 220,000**, adjusted basis **USD 100,000**, mortgage **USD 80,000**. Taylor receives Finley's building, FMV **USD 250,000**, mortgage assumed **USD 150,000**, plus **USD 40,000 cash**. Line 15 = **USD 40,000** (new debt does not erase cash); line 16 **USD 250,000**; line 17 **USD 290,000**; line 18 **USD 170,000** (100,000 + 150,000 − 80,000); line 19 **USD 120,000**; line 20 **USD 40,000**. Preserve the IRS's component conditions: relinquished section 1245 real property has FMV **USD 55,000**, original basis/depreciation **USD 35,000**, adjusted basis **zero**, remaining section 1250 FMV **USD 165,000**; all property received is section 1250. Line 21 is **USD 35,000**; line 22 **USD 5,000**; line 23 **USD 40,000**; deferred line 24 **USD 80,000**; basis line 25 **USD 170,000**. This example's recapture is within boot; the statute permits recapture above boot in other component mixes. [2026 instructions, lines 15–25 examples](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/i8824.pdf).

**Case 4: related party, original 2024/2025 years.** December **2024** business property FMV **USD 200,000**, basis **USD 65,000**, plus cash **USD 15,000**, exchanged for sister's property FMV **USD 215,000**, basis **USD 70,000**. Taxpayer realized **USD 135,000**, recognized **zero**, basis **USD 80,000**; sister realized **USD 145,000**, recognized **USD 15,000**, basis **USD 70,000**. Taxpayer sells in **2025** for **USD 220,000**, within two years, no exception. Recognize the previously deferred **USD 135,000** in 2025, increase taxpayer basis by it to **USD 215,000**, then sale gain **USD 5,000** (ignoring sale costs and intervening depreciation). Sister recognizes remaining **USD 130,000** and increases basis to **USD 200,000**. Do not double tax deferred gain by using the unadjusted USD 80,000 basis for that sale. [Section 1031(f)](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCODE-2024-title26/html/USCODE-2024-title26-subtitleA-chap1-subchapO-partIII-sec1031.htm); [Pub. 544 related-person example](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p544).

**Case 5: identification decision.** Relinquished FMV **USD 600,000**; four whole candidates **USD 300,000 each**, total **USD 1,200,000**, exactly 200%: valid identification. Fifth raises total to **USD 1,500,000**, exceeding both alternatives. Assuming these values remain applicable on the rule's valuation dates, 95% is **USD 1,425,000**, so receipt of all five whole properties is needed for that rescue; partial interests or changed values need a fresh calculation. Without rescue only property actually received within the identification period survives. Identify four rather than five on these facts. The historical portfolio illustration **USD 2,000,000** relinquished / **8 × USD 500,000** candidates also equals 200%. [Reg. 1.1031(k)-1(c)(4)](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CFR-2025-title26-vol13/pdf/CFR-2025-title26-vol13-sec1-1031k-1.pdf).

**Case 6: vacation rental.** First replacement 12-month period: **200 fair-rental days**, **25 personal days**. Limit = max(14, 10% × 200) = **20 days**, so safe harbor fails. That alone does not establish taxable treatment: assess investment intent, and amend **if necessary** if a return relied on expected compliance. Alternative **14 personal days** meets that period's numerical test; the second 12-month period, 24-month ownership and all other exchange conditions must also be met. [Rev. Proc. 2008-16 §4](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-drop/rp-08-16.pdf).

**Case 7: reverse timing.** EAT acquires replacement **2 March 2026**: agreement by **9 March 2026** (five business days), relinquished identification by **16 April 2026**, transfer by **29 August 2026** (180 days), with combined parking also within 180 days. Transfer recipient for relinquished property must be neither taxpayer nor a disqualified person, not merely an "unrelated buyer." Prior taxpayer ownership in the 180-day period ending on the EAT transfer defeats this safe harbor. Other QEAA and exchange requirements still apply; do not assume a weekend extension of 29 August. [Rev. Proc. 2000-37 §4.02](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-irbs/irb00-40.pdf); [Rev. Proc. 2004-51 §4.03](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-drop/rp-04-51.pdf).

## Filing and payment

File Form 8824 with the return for the relinquished-transfer year, including zero-recognized-gain exchanges; also file for the next **2 years** after a related-party exchange. Multiple exchanges may use a summary plus a complete statement per exchange. Parts I/II record property, acquisition/transfer, identification (line 5), receipt (line 6) and related-party facts (line 7; exceptions 11a–11c); later-year related-party filings follow the Part II instructions. [2026 instructions, When To File and lines 5–11](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/i8824.pdf).

For business/noncapital gain on line 22, use **Form 4797 line 5 or 17**, as applicable; for capital assets follow Schedule D instructions with the exchange date. Route installment treatment to Form 6252. For section 121, enter the exclusion beside line 19 without reducing that line, use max(0, min(line 15 − exclusion, line 19)) on line 20, and add exclusion to the normal line 25 formula. Use 25a–25c for the required asset splits, including 1252/1254/1255 on 25b. Part IV is the separate section 1043 regime. [2026 instructions](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/i8824.pdf); [2026 form](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/f8824.pdf). Use the actual year's form for a historical return; this Guide's 2026 form is not a substitute for 2025 instructions.
