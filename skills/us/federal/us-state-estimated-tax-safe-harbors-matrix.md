# State estimated tax safe harbors for individuals: 50-state matrix

> **Built from the law · Not yet approved.** General reference for tax year **2026**, with payments in 2026–2027. Separately labelled 2025 rules and historical case inputs remain below. This is not a filing or payment instruction for a particular taxpayer.

## Scope and who this is for

This matrix covers individual state estimates. Keep federal, corporate, PTE elective, city, school district, sales/property and withholding-deposit obligations separate; the historical corporate/PTE topics are expressly held below. Use `us-quarterly-estimated-tax`, `us-pte-state-matrix` and `ny-corporate-franchise-article-9a` for those separate jobs.

**H means HOLD - non-usable outcome.** Do not calculate or advise a payment, exemption or penalty from that held point. **Check this point with one of our partner accountants.** A hold applies only to the point named, not the other proven fields in the row.

## Ask the client first

Ask for each residence/source state, filing status, 2025 return and AGI, length of prior tax year, 2026 tax projection, withholding/credits, payment dates, gross farming/fishing income and uneven income. Identify each state's own tax and permitted credits before comparing alternatives. A federal payment is not a state payment. Use each state's worksheet: subtract withholding/credits once, not again from a net figure. A prior-year option, annualization, no-liability exception and early-return exception are different tests.

## The method, step by step

For ordinary full-year calendar filers, **S** denotes four equal installments on **15 April, 15 June, 15 September 2026 and 15 January 2027**. Other schedules appear in the row. Dates stated as nominal still require that state's weekend/holiday adjustment; fiscal-year dates, disaster relief and late-start annualization are H unless established from the applicable instructions. Do not infer that a later catch-up payment erases an earlier shortfall. Each row cites its own source; source reads for the retained rules: **27 November 2026**.

A 100% cell does not establish blanket federal conformity.
