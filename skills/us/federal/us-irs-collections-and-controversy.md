# IRS notices, audits, collections and appeals, tax year 2026

Read the actual notice and calendar its response or petition date immediately. A proposal, an assessment, a lien notice and a levy notice have different effects; do not start every clock from receipt or assume a reminder restarts it.

## Scope and who this is for

Federal notice response and collection procedure for individuals and small businesses. Establish the underlying tax with the relevant return Guide. Use `us-form-1040-individual-return`, `us-quarterly-estimated-tax`, `us-fbar-and-fatca-8938`, `us-form-941-and-940-payroll` or the relevant state procedure for their respective subjects. Business extensions and mailing rules belong with `us-corporate-extension-form-7004-and-the`.

## Ask the client first

Collect every notice and envelope; notice, mailing and receipt dates; all affected years and forms; account, return and wage-and-income transcripts; filing and payment history; prior hearing opportunities; all pending or rejected collection applications; and income, assets, debts and essential expenses. Obtain the chronology of bankruptcy, absence abroad, military service and spouse claims. Do not invent a missing assessment date, compliance history or financial figure.

Form 2848 authorizes an eligible representative; Form 8821 authorizes receipt or inspection of information without representation. [Publication 1660](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/p1660.pdf)

## The method, step by step

1. Identify and calendar the notice's actual deadline, preserving the notice and proof of submission. Escalate an imminent deadline immediately.
2. Reconcile the proposed or assessed tax to the return, transcripts and source records. Separate a dispute about liability from an inability to pay.
3. Check missing returns and current payments, then compare full payment, payment plans, hardship and an offer against the verified collection period and the client's finances.
4. Confirm posting and follow-up dates. Treat a request as a request until the IRS confirms its disposition.

### Collection Due Process and CAP

At CDP, raise relevant collection alternatives and spousal defenses. Challenge the underlying liability only within section 6330(c)(2)(B); previously decided issues in which the taxpayer participated meaningfully may be barred. A timely levy CDP request suspends the levy action at issue and the collection period while the hearing and appeals are pending, subject to section 6330(e)(2)'s court-authorized levy exception. At least 90 days remain after final determination. Petition for review within 30 days of the determination; a late petition needs urgent legal review, not an assumption that the deadline can be ignored. [26 USC 6330(c)-(e)](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCODE-2024-title26/html/USCODE-2024-title26-subtitleF-chap64-subchapD-partI-sec6330.htm)

For a late CDP request, ask for an equivalent hearing by the end of one year after the levy notice, or one year plus 5 business days after lien filing. It does not suspend the CSED or provide Tax Court review of the equivalent-hearing decision. CAP, on Form 9423, covers specified lien, levy, seizure and installment-agreement actions; a manager conference may be required. CAP decisions have no court review through CAP. Follow Publication 1660's action-specific steps immediately. [Publication 1660](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/p1660.pdf), [IRM 5.1.19.3.3](https://www.irs.gov/irm/part5/irm_05-001-019)

### The collection clock

The ordinary period is 10 years after each assessment, subject to suspensions and written extensions. A timely court collection proceeding can extend levy collection until the tax liability or judgment is satisfied or becomes unenforceable; a transcript's administrative 20-year entry is not a universal legal expiry rule. [26 USC 6502(a)](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCODE-2024-title26/html/USCODE-2024-title26-subtitleF-chap66-subchapA-sec6502.htm)

| Event | Collection-period effect | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Offer accepted for processing | Suspension while pending, 30 days after rejection, and during a timely rejection appeal. On joint debt, an offer by only one spouse suspends only that spouse's period. | [26 USC 6331(k)](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCODE-2024-title26/html/USCODE-2024-title26-subtitleF-chap64-subchapD-partII-sec6331.htm), [IRM 5.1.19.3.4](https://www.irs.gov/irm/part5/irm_05-001-019) |
| Installment request | Suspension while pending, 30 days after rejection or termination, and during timely appeals. An installment agreement in effect does not itself suspend the CSED. | [26 USC 6331(k)](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCODE-2024-title26/html/USCODE-2024-title26-subtitleF-chap64-subchapD-partII-sec6331.htm), [IRM 5.1.19.3.5](https://www.irs.gov/irm/part5/irm_05-001-019) |

### Paying over time and hardship

All amounts below include tax, penalties and interest. Individuals may apply online for a long-term plan at USD 50,000 or less with all required returns filed; for a short-term plan, the balance must be less than USD 100,000 and payable in 180 days or less. These are application conditions, not guaranteed approval. Sole proprietors apply as individuals. Otherwise contact the IRS or submit Form 9465 with financial information as required. [IRS payment plans](https://www.irs.gov/payments/payment-plans-installment-agreements)

| Plan setup | Online fee | Phone, mail or in-person fee | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Short-term | USD 0 | USD 0 | [IRS payment plans](https://www.irs.gov/payments/payment-plans-installment-agreements) |
| Long-term direct debit | USD 29 | USD 107 | [IRS payment plans](https://www.irs.gov/payments/payment-plans-installment-agreements) |
| Long-term other payment method | USD 69 | USD 178 | [IRS payment plans](https://www.irs.gov/payments/payment-plans-installment-agreements) |

Low-income individuals (latest available AGI at or below 250% of the applicable federal poverty level) can have the direct-debit setup fee waived. Otherwise the setup fee is USD 43; reimbursement depends on inability to use direct debit and completion of the agreement. Revision/reinstatement fees and their reimbursement need the current IRS fee table applied to the actual method; do not use a universal zero-fee rule. [IRS payment plans](https://www.irs.gov/payments/payment-plans-installment-agreements)

Simple Payment Plans require current filing and payment compliance. Individuals: USD 50,000 or less; businesses without trust fund taxes: USD 50,000 or less; businesses with trust fund taxes: USD 25,000 or less, or USD 50,000 or less for an out-of-business sole proprietorship. They do not require a collection information statement or lien determination; most taxpayers have up to 10 years, subject to their actual collection period. [IRS Simple Payment Plans](https://www.irs.gov/payments/simple-payment-plans-for-individuals-and-businesses)

A partial-payment installment agreement may be possible if full payment by the CSED is unaffordable. Supply a financial statement and supporting evidence; an approved agreement is reviewed every two years and the payment can change. [Publication 594](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/p594.pdf)

CNC can temporarily delay most collection for financial hardship. The debt remains; applicable penalties and interest continue. The IRS may request Forms 433-F, 433-A or 433-B and evidence, file a lien, offset refunds, review finances and resume collection if ability to pay improves. [IRS hardship collection](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/temporarily-delay-the-collection-process)

### Offer in compromise

An offer is not an automatic balance reduction. File all required returns, make required estimated payments and, for employers, deposits for the current and last two quarters; an open bankruptcy makes the applicant ineligible. Form 656-L addresses doubt as to liability; Form 656 addresses inability to pay or exceptional hardship/equity grounds. Use the current Form 656-B and its financial statements to evaluate the appropriate ground. [Publication 594](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/p594.pdf), [IRS offers](https://www.irs.gov/payments/offer-in-compromise)

The usual fee is USD 205. For a lump-sum offer, send 20% with the application and, if accepted, pay the remainder in five or fewer payments within five months. For a periodic offer, send the first payment and continue periodic payments during consideration. Qualifying low-income individuals send no fee, initial payment or periodic payments while the IRS reviews the offer. [IRS offers](https://www.irs.gov/payments/offer-in-compromise), [Form 656-B, payment options](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/f656b.pdf)

Most collection is suspended while a processable offer is considered; the collection period is extended and a lien may be filed. Distinguish a rejected offer from an ineligible application returned with its application fee: payments on a returned application are applied to the debt. [IRS offers](https://www.irs.gov/payments/offer-in-compromise)

The accepted offer requires timely filing and payment for five years from acceptance, as well as payment of the offer itself. Do not promise acceptance, a fixed forgiven amount, refund retention treatment or automatic two-year acceptance without checking the actual offer terms and operative exceptions. For asset discounts, reasonable collection potential, periodic-offer duration, refunds, deemed acceptance, rejection appeal and default consequences: Check this point with one of our partner accountants. [Form 656-B, section 7(l)](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/f656b.pdf)

### Liens, levies and passports

A lien secures the government's claim; a levy takes property. Paying the tax debt in full results in lien release within 30 days. Discharge removes the lien from specified property, subordination permits another creditor priority, and withdrawal removes the public notice without cancelling the debt. Fresh Start withdrawal eligibility must be established separately; three payments alone do not qualify. [IRS liens](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/understanding-a-federal-tax-lien)

A bank surrenders levied deposits only after 21 days after service of the levy. Contact the IRS immediately with evidence for a release or ownership objection. [26 USC 6332(c)](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCODE-2024-title26/html/USCODE-2024-title26-subtitleF-chap64-subchapD-partII-sec6332.htm)

For calendar 2026, the section 6334(a)(2) household-property exemption is limited to USD 11,980, and the section 6334(a)(3) books/tools necessary for the trade, business or profession exemption to USD 5,990. Do not treat all property or all benefit deposits as exempt. Wage exemptions, continuous levies, federal-payment levies, retirement assets and principal-residence seizures need separate review. Check this point with one of our partner accountants. [Rev. Proc. 2025-32, section 4.49](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-drop/rp-25-32.pdf)

Passport certification is a risk for assessed, legally enforceable individual federal tax debt exceeding USD 66,000 in 2026, with a filed lien whose administrative remedies have lapsed or been exhausted, or a levy. Exceptions include timely paid agreements/offers and collection suspended for levy CDP or spouse relief. The IRS also excludes hardship CNC, pending plan/offer requests, identity theft, bankruptcy, designated disasters and accepted adjustments fully satisfying the debt; combat-zone certification is postponed. A balance alone does not establish certification. Check the actual CP508C and exclusions before advising on travel. [26 USC 7345](https://uscode.house.gov/us/usc/t26/s7345/), [IRS passports](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/revocation-or-denial-of-passport-in-cases-of-certain-unpaid-taxes), [Rev. Proc. 2025-32, section 4.60](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-drop/rp-25-32.pdf)

### Penalty relief

AEP begins with eligible 2025 tax-year returns and 2026 quarterly returns, applying when an original return completes processing. Eligible series are Forms 1040, 1065, 1120, 940, 941, 943, 944, 945 and CT-1. The same return type must have timely compliance for the prior three years or 12 quarters; no assessed penalty other than estimated tax, unless later abated for reasonable cause or IRS error. Business conditions also exclude four or more prior failure-to-deposit waivers and EFTPS avoidance. Event-based returns, daily delinquency penalties and information reporting dependent on another filing are excluded. Relief covers eligible filing, payment and deposit penalties, not all penalties or tax interest. The IRS notifies the taxpayer; contact it if an assessed penalty should have qualified. [IRS administrative relief](https://www.irs.gov/payments/administrative-penalty-relief)

FTA remains request-based for earlier periods and eligible 2025/2026 returns not considered for AEP: call the notice number or send a statement or Form 843. Failure-to-pay may continue until full payment under FTA. If neither applies, evaluate reasonable cause using documented facts; do not assume it follows from inability to pay or reliance on a preparer. Interest related to a penalty is reduced if that penalty is reduced; this is not the sole statutory route to interest relief. For erroneous written IRS advice, interest-abatement claims and special reasonable-cause rules: Check this point with one of our partner accountants. [IRS administrative relief](https://www.irs.gov/payments/administrative-penalty-relief)

## Worked cases

**Case 1: CP2000, income already reported.** A proposed 1099-NEC adjustment may duplicate Schedule C receipts. Reconcile deposits to the ledger and return; if already included, disagree with a signed statement and supporting records by the response deadline. Do not promise the IRS will close the case. [Topic 652](https://www.irs.gov/taxtopics/tc652)

**Case 2: payment plan.** With all required returns filed, USD 38,000 meets the online long-term balance test. Usual direct-debit setup fees: USD 29 online or USD 107 by mail. At USD 62,000, paying USD 12,000 reduces the balance to USD 50,000 before new accruals; otherwise use the non-online route. Verify the current balance and approval terms. [IRS plans](https://www.irs.gov/payments/payment-plans-installment-agreements)

**Case 3: late 2025 return.** Original inputs: due 15 April 2026, no extension, filed 10 July 2026; USD 10,000 tax, none paid; a 2023 failure-to-file penalty. Three months or fractions have elapsed and filing is more than 60 days late. Before relief, assuming no disaster postponement, credits, payments or increased-rate trigger: FTP = 10,000 × 0.005 × 3 = USD 150; concurrent FTF = 10,000 × 0.045 × 3 = USD 1,350; floor = min(525, 10,000) = USD 525; total = USD 1,500 plus interest. The 2023 assessment alone does not rule out relief: check whether it was later abated for reasonable cause or IRS error. [26 USC 6651](https://uscode.house.gov/us/usc/t26/s6651/), [IRS minimum](https://www.irs.gov/payments/failure-to-file-penalty), [IRS relief](https://www.irs.gov/payments/administrative-penalty-relief)

```oa-check
name: Late 2025 return before relief, filed July 2026
inputs:
  tax: 10000
  months: 3
  ftf_minimum: 525
steps:
  - ftp: tax * 0.005 * months
  - ftf_computed: tax * 0.045 * months
  - ftf_floor: min(ftf_minimum, tax)
  - ftf: max(ftf_computed, ftf_floor)
  - total: ftp + ftf
expected:
  ftp: 150.00
  ftf_computed: 1350.00
  ftf_floor: 525.00
  ftf: 1350.00
  total: 1500.00
```

**Case 4: CDP.** LT11 dated 5 October 2026: ordinary 30-day date is 4 November 2026. **Case 5: deficiency petition.** Notice mailed 1 September 2026 to Ohio: ordinary 90-day date is 30 November 2026; addressed to Portugal: ordinary 150-day date is 29 January 2027. Both are weekdays. Use a later IRS-specified petition date if present and check exceptional postponements. [26 USC 6213](https://uscode.house.gov/us/usc/t26/s6213/)

**Case 6: lump-sum offer.** Debt USD 62,000, offer USD 20,000: usual initial payment USD 4,000 plus fee USD 205 = USD 4,205; remaining offer USD 16,000 in five or fewer payments within five months after acceptance. Qualifying low-income individuals omit the initial payment and fee. USD 42,000 is only debt minus offer arithmetic, not an established forgiven amount or recommendation. Eligibility, collection potential and acceptance remain to be determined. Check this point with one of our partner accountants. [IRS offers](https://www.irs.gov/payments/offer-in-compromise), [Form 656-B](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/f656b.pdf)
