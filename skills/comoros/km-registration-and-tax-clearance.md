# Comoros: tax registration and clearance documents

## Who this applies to

This skill covers the tax administration of the Union of the Comoros, not Mayotte's tax regime. Tax year 2026.

- **Registration (LPF L.1, as replaced by Finance Law 2026).** Every natural or legal person, entity and legal arrangement carrying on an income-generating industrial, artisanal or commercial activity and liable to a tax, duty, levy or advance payment under the General Tax Code, **including operation of an electronic platform**, must request registration with the territorially competent tax service. Article 14 of [Law 25-020/AU, Finance Law 2026, promulgated by decree 26-003/PR of 16 January 2026](https://finances.gouv.km/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Decret-portant-promulgation-du-loi-des-finances-2026.pdf) replaces L.1 as earlier amended by article 13 of the Finance Law 2024 (page 12).
- **Declaration before starting (CGI art. 42 bis, Amending Finance Law 2024).** Anyone taking up a profession that makes them liable to the profit tax in that chapter of the code. Legal persons of every form also file a beneficial-owner declaration.
- **Clearance documents (L.4 bis and L.4 ter, inserted by Finance Law 2026).** The quitus fiscal is issued to "entreprises citoyennes" and persons with compliant tax obligations for at least one fiscal year. The attestation de regularite fiscale covers an enterprise, and also an individual acting in the management of private assets.
- **Foreign taxpayers without a seat in Comoros** that carry on economic activity there have the same registration duties and must appoint an accredited tax representative (2023 code, L.2).

Out of scope: company incorporation and commercial-register formalities, tax rates and exemptions, and Mayotte. This tax-registration requirement does not itself prove company incorporation, commercial-register registration, licensing or completion of every tax enrolment. This is not comprehensive country or formation coverage, and no independent accountant review has occurred.

## The base

### What the registration request contains (2023 code, L.2 and L.3)

For a company: name, registered office, amount and split of share capital, identity of the managers, activities, and the physical and postal address of each establishment. Attach the articles, the minutes of the constituent meeting, the trade and personal property credit register (RCCM) number and the trader's card. For a sole trader: name and address, activities and establishment addresses, with the RCCM number and trader's card. [2023 code, L.2](https://justice.gouv.km/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/comores-cgi-2023.pdf).

On those documents the DGI issues an attestation d'immatriculation and a tax identification number (NIF). Only one NIF is issued per person; it is strictly personal. A business quotes it on returns and tax payments, imports and exports, public tenders and credit applications, and no tax exemption is granted to a person who has not given a NIF with the request. [2023 code, L.3](https://justice.gouv.km/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/comores-cgi-2023.pdf).

The 2023 code version of L.1 also named embassies, consulates, international organisations, associations and occasional importers. The 2026 text replaces L.1 and does not repeat that list; L.3 still requires the NIF for import operations. Whether an occasional importer outside the new L.1 wording must still register is not confirmed.

### The customs withholding base (L.4 ter)

The base is the customs value of the goods ("valeur en douane"). The 15% is an advance on the taxpayer's tax debts. It is not described there as a final income-tax charge or as a replacement for customs duties. The provision does not set a refund or crediting process. [L.4 ter, page 13](https://finances.gouv.km/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Decret-portant-promulgation-du-loi-des-finances-2026.pdf).

## Deadlines and filing

### Step by step for a new business

1. **Before starting operations**, declare the new profession to the Direction generale des Impots on the administrative form designed for it (CGI art. 42 bis(1)). A legal person attaches a declaration of its beneficial owners within the meaning of Law 12-008/AU of 28 June 2012 (anti money laundering). A company attaches a copy of its articles; a societe anonyme also attaches the subscription and payment declaration. [Amending Finance Law 2024, pages 15 to 16](https://finances.gouv.km/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/LOI-DE-FINANCES-RECTIFICATIVES-2024.pdf).
2. **Within fifteen days after activity starts**, file the registration request with the territorially competent tax service (L.1). The trigger is commencement of activity, not the date of the first tax assessment. [2026 law, article 14, amended L.1, page 12](https://finances.gouv.km/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Decret-portant-promulgation-du-loi-des-finances-2026.pdf).
3. Keep the start date and evidence that the registration request was received. Those are practical controls for the statutory deadline.
4. Receive the attestation d'immatriculation and the NIF (L.3), and quote the NIF on every invoice and return.
5. **Changes:** report a change of manager, a sale, a closure or a move of the place of business within fifteen working days (L.3).

Article 42 bis sits in the code and L.1 in the procedure book, and the 2026 text does not repeal 42 bis. Work to the stricter date: declare before starting, and in every case inside the fifteen days of L.1. Current supporting-document requirements and forms still need to be reconciled with implementing material.

### Sale or closure (CGI art. 42 bis(3) to (8))

On a sale or closure of all or part of a business, profit tax on profits not yet taxed is assessed immediately. Within ten days the taxpayer notifies the tax office of the sale or closure and its effective date (and the buyer's name and address, where there is one), and files the actual-profit return with its supporting documents. The ten days run from:

- publication of the sale in a legal-notices journal, for a fonds de commerce;
- the day the buyer actually took over management, for other businesses;
- final closure of the establishments, for a closure.

If the information is not given, or supporting evidence is not supplied within ten days of a request, the base is fixed by the administration (d'office). Assessments under this article are payable in full at once. [Amending Finance Law 2024, pages 15 to 16](https://finances.gouv.km/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/LOI-DE-FINANCES-RECTIFICATIVES-2024.pdf).

### Two documents with different conditions

| Document | Provision | Stated condition and validity |
|---|---|---|
| Quitus fiscal | L.4 bis | Tax compliance for at least one fiscal year; validity **one month** |
| Attestation de regularite fiscale | L.4 ter | Certifies the holder is in order with tax obligations; validity **three calendar months**, without extending beyond the fiscal year |

The quitus provision covers qualifying enterprises and persons; the attestation provision also expressly addresses individuals acting in management of their private assets. For each document only the original is valid, and each gives access to administrative facilities determined by the Director General of Taxes. Do not translate those facilities into an unverified tax exemption, refund or guaranteed processing time. [2026 law, article 14, L.4 bis and L.4 ter, page 13](https://finances.gouv.km/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Decret-portant-promulgation-du-loi-des-finances-2026.pdf).

Three calendar months is not necessarily ninety days. Obtain the stated expiry date and check the fiscal-year limit before relying on an attestation. The one-month quitus and three-month attestation periods must not be silently exchanged.

### When an attestation is requested (L.4 ter)

The list is introduced with "notamment", so it is not closed. The attestation must be asked for by:

- contracting authorities of public procurement, whoever the bidders are;
- banks and financial institutions, for fund transfers or any financing sought by a professional;
- public services issuing approvals, visas or residence cards;
- bodies registering members of professional orders;
- emigration services, before issuing boarding permission to a person fiscally domiciled in Comoros who is leaving permanently;
- customs, when goods are removed, without prejudice to the Comorian Customs Code;
- candidates for elective office.

For departure, the listed case concerns a person fiscally domiciled in Comoros leaving permanently; do not generalise that wording into a new rule for every tourist journey. The 2023 code (art. 125) separately says emigration services must not allow departure without a quitus fiscal; see [km-personal-tax-domicile](km-personal-tax-domicile.md). [L.4 ter, page 13](https://finances.gouv.km/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Decret-portant-promulgation-du-loi-des-finances-2026.pdf).

### Substitution: unresolved

The final sentence on page 13 reads "Dans tous les cas où l'attestation de régularité fiscale est exigée, le quitus fiscal en tient" and stops there; the next page starts a new provision. The words "le quitus fiscal" are partly under the presidential stamp but legible on the scan, viewed on 16 November 2026. The natural reading is "en tient lieu", meaning a quitus stands in for the attestation, but the last word is missing from the enacted scan. Treat substitution as unconfirmed until the tax office confirms it, and resolve it before deciding a particular importer lacks sufficient evidence.

## Penalties and interest

- **No declaration before starting:** fixed fine KMF 500,000 (CGI art. 42 bis(2)).
- **Late or wrong registration request:** KMF 20,000 for not filing a registration request or a change request within the legal time, or for a registration request with obviously wrong details (2023 code, L.101).
- **No NIF:** minimum collection of 5% of turnover; no import or export licence, no public tender and no bank credit; 10% of the invoice amount where an invoice lacks the NIF. If a formal notice to register is still unanswered fifteen days after its receipt, the Minister of Finance may ban the profession for up to two years (2023 code, L.102).
- **Breach of L.1, L.4 or L.9:** may lead to loss of the right to trade, exclusion from public tenders, a ban on importing, and publication of the offender's name (2023 code, L.114).
- **Sale or closure:** base fixed by the administration and immediate payment of the whole assessment (CGI art. 42 bis(6) and (8)).
- **Customs withholding:** 15% of customs value where an importer does not present the attestation. It is an advance, not a fine.

The 2023 code rows are the consolidated official text. The Amending Finance Law 2024 article 10, the Finance Law 2025 tax section and the Finance Law 2026 tax section do not amend L.2, L.3, L.101, L.102 or L.114 (read 16 November 2026). Late-payment interest is in [km-electronic-filing-and-tax-procedure](km-electronic-filing-and-tax-procedure.md). [2023 code, L.101, L.102, L.114](https://justice.gouv.km/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/comores-cgi-2023.pdf).

## Cross-border points

- A foreign taxpayer carrying on economic activity in Comoros without a seat there has the same registration duties and must appoint a tax representative accredited with the tax administration. The ministerial order on accreditation was not found (2023 code, L.2).
- Operators of electronic platforms are expressly inside the 2026 L.1 registration duty.
- Importers: the 15% customs withholding applies when the attestation is not presented at removal of goods (L.4 ter). Banks ask for the attestation on fund transfers for professionals.
- Individuals leaving Comoros permanently: see [km-personal-tax-domicile](km-personal-tax-domicile.md).
- Transfer pricing declarations for linked companies sit in [km-transfer-pricing](km-transfer-pricing.md).

## Two worked examples that recompute

An importer with no attestation clears goods with a customs value of KMF 2,000,000. Withholding at 15% is KMF 300,000, an advance on its tax debts. That illustration does not calculate other duties, fees or the eventual tax-account settlement. Keep the customs valuation and payment receipt so the advance can be reconciled.

```oa-check
name: Withholding on KMF 2,000,000
inputs:
  customs_value: 2000000
steps:
  - withheld: customs_value * 0.15
expected:
  withheld: 300000
```

The same on KMF 10,000,000 gives KMF 1,500,000.

```oa-check
name: Withholding on KMF 10,000,000
inputs:
  customs_value: 10000000
steps:
  - withheld: customs_value * 0.15
expected:
  withheld: 1500000
```

A decision, worked both ways: a trader with turnover of KMF 40,000,000 has not registered and has no NIF. Under L.102 the minimum collection is 5% of turnover, KMF 2,000,000, and it cannot import, bid or borrow. Registering costs at most the KMF 20,000 L.101 fine for the late request (plus the KMF 500,000 art. 42 bis fine if no declaration was made before starting). The difference is KMF 1,980,000 against the L.101 fine alone, before any income tax on actual profit. On these facts registering is far cheaper, and it also lifts the import, tender and credit bans.

```oa-check
name: No NIF against late registration
inputs:
  turnover: 40000000
  late_registration_fine: 20000
steps:
  - minimum_collection: turnover * 0.05
  - difference: minimum_collection - late_registration_fine
expected:
  minimum_collection: 2000000
  difference: 1980000
```

## Ask the client first

- On what date did (or will) the activity start, and was the DGI declaration made before that date?
- Is the business a company, a sole trader, a foreign business without a seat in Comoros, or an electronic platform?
- Does it import goods, and does it hold an original attestation de regularite fiscale or quitus fiscal? What expiry date does it show?
- What is the document needed for: a tender, a bank transfer, a visa or residence card, customs, a permanent departure, an election?
- Is a sale, closure or change of manager or premises planned, and on what date?
