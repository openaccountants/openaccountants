# Rwanda VAT v2.1

Tax year 2026. Law 049/2023 as amended by Law 009/2025.

## Who this applies to

Anyone making taxable supplies in Rwanda, importers, public bodies that withhold VAT (art 5), buyers of foreign services (art 14), and from 2026 non-resident online suppliers (Order 004). Registration: over FRW 20,000,000 in 12 months or FRW 5,000,000 in three consecutive months, within 7 days; voluntary registration is open (https://www.rra.gov.rw/en/domestic-tax-services/value-added-tax/default-title-1).

## The base

**Taxable.** All goods supplied in Rwanda except exempt; services where both parties are resident, or the recipient is resident and benefits in Rwanda, or the provider is resident and the recipient benefits in Rwanda (art 6). Tax point: earliest of invoice, payment, removal or delivery of goods, delivery of service, or deregistration application (art 12).

**Zero-rated (art 7).** Exports of goods and services, minerals sold domestically, international transport of goods into Rwanda and transit, commission on all-inclusive tour booking, duty-free shops, security organ shops, diplomats, agreement organisations and funded projects, locally assembled electric and hybrid vehicles, batteries and chargers.

**Exempt (art 8 as replaced by Law 009/2025).** Non-profit water supply and environmental services, except sewage pump-out; health services, disability equipment, listed pharmaceuticals, sanitary pads; listed educational items, books, newspapers, journals; passenger land transport in vehicles of 14 persons or more, air passenger transport, boat transport, household waste transport; sale or lease of land; sale of residential buildings; residential renting over 90 days; lease of movables by licensed financial institutions; financial and insurance services listed by Order 001/25; funeral items; listed energy equipment; unprocessed farm and livestock products, plus processed maize, rice and milk other than powder and milk derivatives; agricultural insurance; listed farm inputs; gaming; returning nationals' effects; zone user imports for special economic zones; defence goods; aircraft on the list; business transfers between related residents; securitisation.

Since Law 009/2025, fuel and road transport of goods are taxable. Fee-based financial services outside the Order are taxable.

**Input tax.** Credit needs a VAT invoice or customs proof (art 19) and the output tax having been paid (art 17). Personal use is denied. Mixed use is apportioned (art 17). Denied (art 20): passenger vehicles with parts and repairs unless the business sells or rents them or is a driving school; entertainment unless it is the business; accommodation unless the business or staff away from home; 40% of the VAT on inseparable costs. New registrants claim on stock in the first return (art 18).

**Foreign services (art 14).** Output tax is always due on the return for the period of delivery. Input is allowed only where the service is not available in Rwanda and the Minister authorised it. So it is not always net zero, as our earlier file said. The Handbook describes the tab as applying to services unavailable locally; the law's art 14(1) has no such limit and wins.

**Online supplies (Order 004, 2026).** Non-residents supplying customers in Rwanda register or appoint a representative. If unregistered, the bank facilitating payment withholds the VAT and files by the 15th of the next month. Registered suppliers file by the 15th after the period. Art 3 lists software, ride-hailing and marketplace platforms, online gaming, search, data sales, journals, streaming, hosting and more.

**Invoices (Order 006, in force September 2026).** The electronic invoicing system (EIS) replaces the 2013 EBM order. Rules: system at the sales point, notice with TIN and SDC serial, "do not pay if an invoice is not issued", buyer-initiated invoices must be approved, ERPs must integrate. Those not filing annual income tax need not issue on each sale but must approve buyer-initiated invoices (art 8). Adjustment and cancellation: Order 004 arts 10-22, via the RRA portal; cancel before the end of the tax period (art 19); adjust once.

**Deferral (July 2026).** After the exemption ended, manufacturing, mining, mineral exploration and gas extraction taxpayers may defer import VAT on Chapter 84 and 85 machinery, excluding passenger vehicles, office items, consumables and spares unless approved. Test: 90% of turnover taxable, or 80% exports, or new investor conditions, 12 months compliance, no arrears. Apply to the Commissioner for Customs Services before arrival. Declare in the monthly return following release.

## Deadlines and filing

| Period | Due |
|---|---|
| Monthly | 15 days after month end |
| Quarterly | 15 April, 15 July, 15 October, and the fourth quarter date in https://www.rra.gov.rw/fileadmin/user_upload/13.1.23_OG_n___Special_of_11.01.2023_Kumenyekanisha_no_kwishyura_umusoro_ku_bihembo_n_umusoro_ku_nyongeragaciro_ku_gihembwe.pdf |

File even with no sales or a nil result (art 28). Pay by the same date (art 29). Import VAT is due at the customs point.

### How to file

1. Connect the EBM or EIS to the internet so back office data syncs with E-Tax (https://www.rra.gov.rw/en/domestic-tax-services/value-added-tax/penalties-and-interest).
2. Log in at https://etax.rra.gov.rw.
3. Annexure Downloads, VAT. Enable content in VatAnnexure. Tabs: Sales, Purchases, VAT Importation, Deductible VAT Reverse, VAT Retained.
4. Sales: buyer TIN, name, nature, invoice number, date, total ex VAT, exempt, zero-rated, exports, taxable, VAT. Exports go in the Exports column, not zero-rated.
5. VAT Importation: customs station, DD COM number, date, nature, origin, CIF, VAT paid from V02 on the customs declaration.
6. Deductible VAT Reverse: foreign supplier, invoice, amount, VAT at 18%. VAT Retained: public body TIN and amount withheld.
7. Validate and save each tab, then fill the declaration. Totals must equal the annexures.
8. Tick the certifying box, submit, and pay against the RRA Reference Number on the receipt.

An invoice not recognised on purchase upload is saved for the next period. Steps from https://www.rra.gov.rw/fileadmin/Folder_for_2025/RRA_Tax_Handbook_2025_Final.pdf.

## Cross-border points

Exports are zero-rated with documents. There is no EAC intra-community mechanism in the law: imports from EAC countries pay VAT at the customs point. Foreign services and online supplies: see The base.

## Two worked examples that recompute

**Example 1. Quarter.** Taxable sales FRW 10,000,000; deductible purchases FRW 6,000,000.

```oa-check
name: Net VAT for a period
inputs:
  sales: 10000000
  purchases: 6000000
steps:
  - output: sales * 0.18
  - input: purchases * 0.18
  - net: output - input
expected:
  net: 720000.00
```

**Example 2. Decision: buy a service abroad or locally.** Service of FRW 3,000,000 from abroad, with similar local supply available. Output FRW 540,000 is due and no input is allowed, so it costs 540,000. The same service from a local VAT-registered supplier costs nothing net. With Minister authorisation for an unavailable service, net cost is zero.

```oa-check
name: Foreign service, local alternative
inputs:
  price: 3000000
  input_allowed: 0
steps:
  - output: price * 0.18
  - input: output * input_allowed
  - cost: output - input
expected:
  cost: 540000.00
```

## Ask the client first

1. Turnover over the last 12 months and last 3 months?
2. Monthly or quarterly?
3. Exempt supplies too? If so, apportion.
4. Foreign services? Available locally? Authorised?
5. Imports of machinery after 30 June 2026?
6. EIS or ERP in use?
