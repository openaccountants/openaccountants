# Rwanda Corporate Income Tax

## Corporate income tax rates, base and deadlines

The standard corporate rate was reduced to 28% under the 2025 reform package, with reduced regimes for small businesses, micro-enterprises and registered investors. Companies make quarterly advance payments and file an annual return within three months of year-end.

- **Standard corporate income tax rate** — 28% percent (Reduced from 30% effective 2025; medium-term target of 20%)  _([Income Tax Law (Law No. 027/2022) as amended by 2025 reforms](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/rwanda/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income))_
- **Small business turnover tax (turnover RWF 12m – 20m)** — 3% of turnover (lump-sum regime) percent  _([Income Tax Law (Law No. 027/2022)](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/rwanda/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income))_
- **Micro-enterprise flat tax, turnover RWF 2m – 4m** — RWF 60,000 per year RWF  _([Income Tax Law (Law No. 027/2022)](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/rwanda/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income))_
- **Micro-enterprise flat tax, turnover RWF 4m – 7m** — RWF 120,000 per year RWF  _([Income Tax Law (Law No. 027/2022)](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/rwanda/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income))_
- **Micro-enterprise flat tax, turnover RWF 7m – 10m** — RWF 210,000 per year RWF  _([Income Tax Law (Law No. 027/2022)](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/rwanda/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income))_
- **Micro-enterprise flat tax, turnover RWF 10m – 12m** — RWF 300,000 per year RWF  _([Income Tax Law (Law No. 027/2022)](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/rwanda/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income))_
- **Reduced rate for company listing 30%+ of shares to the public** — 25% for 5 years percent  _([Income Tax Law (Law No. 027/2022)](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/rwanda/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income))_
- **Reduced rate for company listing 40%+ of shares to the public** — 20% for 5 years percent  _([Income Tax Law (Law No. 027/2022)](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/rwanda/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income))_
- **Corporate tax base** — Worldwide business profit for resident companies (accounting profit adjusted for tax); Rwandan-source profit for non-residents/permanent establishments  _([Income Tax Law (Law No. 027/2022)](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/rwanda/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income))_
- **Domestic withholding tax on dividends, interest, royalties and service fees** — 15% percent (Standard non-treaty rate; dividends between resident companies excepted)  _([Income Tax Law (Law No. 027/2022)](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/rwanda/corporate/withholding-taxes))_
- **Reduced WHT on dividends/interest from listed securities** — 5% percent (for residents or East African Community beneficiaries; also 3-year+ treasury bonds)  _([Income Tax Law (Law No. 027/2022)](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/rwanda/corporate/withholding-taxes))_
- **WHT on public tender / public procurement payments** — 3% (15% if the supplier is not tax-registered) percent  _([Income Tax Law (Law No. 027/2022)](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/rwanda/corporate/withholding-taxes))_
- **Quarterly advance CIT instalments** — Due by the 6th, 9th and 12th month of the tax period (30 June, 30 Sept, 31 Dec for calendar-year taxpayers)  _([Tax Procedures Law / Income Tax Law (Law No. 027/2022)](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/rwanda/corporate/tax-administration))_
- **Annual CIT return and final payment deadline** — Within three months after year-end (31 March for a 31 December year-end)  _([Tax Procedures Law / Income Tax Law (Law No. 027/2022)](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/rwanda/corporate/tax-administration))_
