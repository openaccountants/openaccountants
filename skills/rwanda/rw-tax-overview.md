# Rwanda Tax Overview

## Rwanda tax system at a glance

Rwanda's taxes are administered by the Rwanda Revenue Authority (RRA) and largely codified in Law No. 027/2022 of 20/10/2022 establishing taxes on income (the Income Tax Law). A multi-year reform package (2025-2030) is phasing the corporate rate down and pension contributions up.

- **National tax authority** — Rwanda Revenue Authority (RRA)  _([Law establishing the Rwanda Revenue Authority](https://www.rra.gov.rw))_
- **Currency** — Rwandan franc (RWF)  _([RRA general guidance](https://www.rra.gov.rw))_
- **Standard tax period (fiscal year)** — Calendar year, 1 January to 31 December (a different 12-month accounting period may be authorised)  _([Income Tax Law (Law No. 027/2022)](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/rwanda/corporate/tax-administration))_
- **Basis of taxation** — Residents are taxed on worldwide income; non-residents are taxed only on Rwandan-source income  _([Income Tax Law (Law No. 027/2022)](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/rwanda/individual/taxes-on-personal-income))_
- **Top personal income tax rate** — 30 percent  _([Income Tax Law (Law No. 027/2022)](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/rwanda/individual/taxes-on-personal-income))_
- **Standard corporate income tax rate** — 28 percent (Reduced from 30% under the 2025 reforms; medium-term target of 20%)  _([Income Tax Law (Law No. 027/2022) as amended by the 2025 tax reforms](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/rwanda/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income))_
- **Does Rwanda have VAT?** — 18 percent (Yes — standard VAT rate)  _([Law on Value Added Tax](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/rwanda/corporate/other-taxes))_
- **Annual income tax return deadline** — Within three months after the end of the tax period (31 March for a calendar-year taxpayer)  _([Tax Procedures Law / Income Tax Law (Law No. 027/2022)](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/rwanda/corporate/tax-administration))_
- **Monthly PAYE and VAT remittance deadline** — By the 15th day of the month following the period  _([Tax Procedures Law](https://www.rra.gov.rw))_
- **Digital services tax (DST)** — 1.5 percent (1.5% on income of foreign digital platforms with significant presence in Rwanda; Introduced under the 2025 reforms (approx — confirm scope))  _([2025 tax reforms (digital services tax)](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/rwanda/corporate/significant-developments))_
