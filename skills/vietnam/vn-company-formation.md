# Vietnam Company Formation and Entity Choice v2.1

> **General reference only.** This skill is general tax/accounting reference material for AI-assisted workflows. It has not been reviewed for any specific person's facts, documents, elections, deadlines, residency, filing status, or local procedures. Do not rely on it to file, pay, amend, or take a tax position without review by a qualified professional in the relevant jurisdiction.

## Who this applies to

Founders, local or foreign, setting up a business entity in Vietnam in 2026 under the Enterprise Law 59/2020/QH14 as amended by Law 03/2022/QH15 and Law 76/2025/QH15 (in force 1 July 2025). From 1 March 2026, foreign investors can register an entity before applying for its investment registration certificate, subject to market-access conditions (Investment Law 143/2025/QH15 arts 8, 19 and 51). Before 1 March, Investment Law 61/2020/QH14 art 22(1)(c) generally required the investment procedure first. The detailed investment application is outside this skill. Tax after formation: [vn-corporate-income-tax](vn-corporate-income-tax.md), [vietnam-vat](vietnam-vat.md), [vn-payroll-social](vn-payroll-social.md).

| Entity | Owners | Liability | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Single-member LLC (công ty TNHH một thành viên) | One organisation or individual | Limited to charter capital | Enterprise Law Art. 74 |
| Multi-member LLC (công ty TNHH hai thành viên trở lên) | 2 to 50 members | Limited to capital contributed | Art. 46 |
| Joint-stock company (công ty cổ phần) | At least 3 shareholders, no maximum; capital divided into shares; may issue securities | Limited | Art. 111 |
| Private enterprise (doanh nghiệp tư nhân) | One individual | Unlimited | Enterprise Law |

## The base

The steps, in the order a founder meets them:

1. **Choose the entity** from the table above. One founder with limited liability: single-member LLC. Two to 50 partners: multi-member LLC. Plans to raise outside equity or list: joint-stock company.
2. **Foreign investors:** choose whether to register the enterprise or obtain the investment registration certificate first. If the enterprise is registered first, include the market-access commitment and complete the investment certificate procedure within 12 months of establishment. Complete investment registration before starting the project or adding other business lines (Decree 96/2026 art 72(1)–(4)).
3. **Prepare the file:** registration request, charter, list of members or founding shareholders, the **list of beneficial owners** (Art. 3(35) defines them as the individuals who in fact own charter capital or control the enterprise; Law 76/2025 added the duty to collect, update and keep this information and to provide it on request), and identity documents.
4. **File** with the provincial business registration office, online through the national business registration portal (dangkykinhdoanh.gov.vn) or in person. Within 3 working days of receiving the file, the office checks validity, issues the certificate for a valid file, or gives written corrections or reasons for refusal (Art. 26(5)). The enterprise code is also the tax code.
5. **Contribute capital** in full and in the promised assets within 90 days of the certificate (Art. 47(2), Art. 75). A member who has contributed nothing stops being a member; a partial contributor keeps rights pro rata; the unpaid part may be offered for sale (Art. 47(3)).
6. **If capital is short,** register the reduced charter capital and ownership ratios within 30 days after the applicable contribution deadline (Art. 47(4)). Defaulting members are liable in proportion to the capital they promised for financial obligations incurred before the change is registered.
7. **Tax start-up:** open a bank account, register for electronic invoices, choose the CIT period (calendar or notified financial year), register employees for social insurance, and check the CIT exemptions (new SME three-year exemption; the VND 1 billion revenue exemption in [vn-corporate-income-tax](vn-corporate-income-tax.md)). No licence fee return is due for 2026 onward. Fees already owed for 2025 or earlier remain payable.

## Deadlines and filing

| Step | Deadline |
| --- | --- |
| Registration decision | 3 working days from receipt of the file (Art. 26(5)) |
| Capital contribution | 90 days from the certificate (Art. 47(2)) |
| Charter capital adjustment after shortfall | 30 days after the contribution deadline (Art. 47(4)) |
| First CIT provisional payment | Last day of the first month after each quarter; none if revenue is expected to be VND 1 billion or less (Decree 141/2026 Art. 2) |

A foreign-invested enterprise must have its annual financial statements audited before filing them. The standard filing deadline is 90 days after the accounting year ends (Independent Audit Law art 37(1)(a); Accounting Law arts 29(3) and 33(1)).

## Penalties and interest

Members who fail to contribute remain liable in proportion to the capital they promised for financial obligations incurred before registration of the capital change (Art. 47(4)). From 21 July 2026, a change registration that is 1 to 10 days late attracts a warning. Longer delays or failure to register can attract fines (consolidated text 60/2026/VBHN-BTC art 44). Late tax payment interest is 0.03% per day (Law 108/2025/QH15 Art. 16(2)(a)).

## Cross-border points

- Foreign ownership caps and conditions apply in conditional sectors (for example banking, insurance, some services); check the market access list before choosing the structure.
- For foreign investment in cash, use the investment account required for the transaction under Circular 38/2026/TT-NHNN, in force from 18 August 2026. A company established before its investment certificate may open a limited-use investment account at an authorised bank under art 7(3). Confirm the appropriate account with the bank and distinguish cash contributions from contributions of assets.
- Treaty and withholding questions on dividends and service fees: [vn-corporate-income-tax](vn-corporate-income-tax.md).

## Worked examples

### Two-member LLC, charter capital VND 2 billion, only 1.2 billion contributed by day 90

If the contributions still total VND 1.2 billion, register that amount within 30 days after the contribution deadline. The members’ council may offer the unpaid VND 800 million for sale. With the certificate on day 0, no excluded asset-transfer days and both deadlines falling on working days, the change is due on day 120. Recalculate if an excluded period or non-working-day rule applies (Enterprise Law art 47(2), (4); Civil Code arts 147–148).

## Ask the client first

1. Number and type of owners (individuals or companies), and whether any is foreign.
2. Business lines, and whether any is a conditional sector.
3. Charter capital and the assets to be contributed, with dates.
4. Who the beneficial owners are, through every layer.
5. Expected first-year revenue for the VND 1 billion CIT exemption and whether the company sells directly to consumers for e-invoice registration.
