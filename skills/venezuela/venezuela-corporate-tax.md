# Venezuela: ordinary corporate income tax (2026) v0.1

> **General reference only.** This skill is general tax/accounting reference material for AI-assisted workflows. It has not been reviewed for any specific person's facts, documents, elections, deadlines, residency, filing status, or local procedures. Do not rely on it to file, pay, amend, or take a tax position without review by a qualified professional in the relevant jurisdiction.

**Answer layer.** For an ordinary company within LISLR art. 9, apply Tarifa 2 to annual global net taxable enrichment expressed in tax units (U.T.): 15% on the first 2,000 U.T., 22% on the next 1,000 U.T., and 34% on the excess over 3,000 U.T. These are marginal bands. Classify the entity and activities first: a partnership taxed through its members, a financial business subject to proportional tax, or an extractive-sector business is not automatically an ordinary Tarifa 2 company. Domestic and foreign losses remain separate; qualifying losses carry forward only within the next three tax periods, with a 25% current-enrichment cap. [LISLR arts. 4, 7-12, 52, 55](https://contraloriadebarquisimeto.gob.ve/images/baselegal/LOAFSP.pdf#page=6).

The primary texts read on 11 November 2026 are the consolidated **Ley de Impuesto sobre la Renta** (LISLR), Gaceta Oficial 6.210 Extraordinario, 30 December 2015, and the **Código Orgánico Tributario** (COT), Gaceta Oficial 6.507 Extraordinario, 29 January 2020. These are official gazette facsimiles hosted by Venezuelan public bodies. Their filenames name other laws contained in the gazettes; the actual LISLR and COT articles cited here were read. This new Guide is tier 2 and has no accountant approval. Accessible primary material did not establish the current UT currency value, all subsequent administrative measures or the current return's fields/calendar; the gaps are recorded below.

## Who this applies to

Use the ordinary computation for companies limited by shares and their assimilated taxpayers, including limited-liability companies. LISLR art. 7, first paragraph, assimilates the specified corporate forms; art. 9 applies the company regime to foreign corporations regardless of form and to the other legal/economic entities in art. 7(e). Foundations and nonprofit associations are directed by art. 9 to art. 50, subject to an actual applicable exemption. The public ownership of a commercial enterprise does not itself exempt it: art. 13 subjects national, state and municipal enterprises to this law. [LISLR arts. 7, 9, 13](https://contraloriadebarquisimeto.gob.ve/images/baselegal/LOAFSP.pdf#page=6).

A resident/domiciled company generally reports worldwide income. A non-domiciled company without a PE/fixed base is subject on Venezuelan-source income; a foreign company with a PE/fixed base is subject on domestic and foreign income attributable to it. COT art. 30 treats legal persons incorporated in Venezuela or legally domiciled there as domiciled. A bank account or invoice currency does not decide source. Establish Venezuelan-source causes under LISLR art. 6 and document foreign income separately. [LISLR arts. 1, 6-7](https://contraloriadebarquisimeto.gob.ve/images/baselegal/LOAFSP.pdf#page=4); [COT art. 30](https://www.cmchacao.gob.ve/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/Decreto-con-Rango-Valor-y-Fuerza-de-Ley-que-Reforma-la-Ley-que-Establece-el-Impuesto-al-Valor-Agregado.pdf#page=5).

## The base

### The method, step by step

1. **Fix the legal/tax perimeter.** Identify entity form, incorporation and domicile, fiscal year, activities, PE/fixed base, special-taxpayer notification and any exemption. Separate income taxed under a proportional/special regime. Do not simply apply 34% to accounting profit. [LISLR arts. 1, 7-14, 52](https://contraloriadebarquisimeto.gob.ve/images/baselegal/LOAFSP.pdf#page=4).
2. **Reconcile gross receipts and timing.** Gross income includes sales/services, rents and regular or incidental receipts. Foreign-source gross income uses the fiscal-year average exchange rate under the BCV methodology. Income is generally available when the producing transaction occurs; credit income of the enumerated entities and rents use accrual. The art. 5 exceptions for recoverable credit assignments/discount operations and employment/fortuitous gains require separate treatment. [LISLR arts. 5, 16](https://contraloriadebarquisimeto.gob.ve/images/baselegal/LOAFSP.pdf#page=5).
3. **Compute territorial and foreign gross profit separately.** Deduct the permitted cost of goods sold/services from corresponding gross income. Support acquisition cost by the direct seller's invoice, with prices no higher than normal market prices, and the required RIF, retaining the foreign-purchase invoice exception; an unsupported affiliate debit note is not cost proof. Keep capital expenditure out of recurring expenses. Common territorial/foreign costs and deductions are allocated in proportion to the respective income. Taxable/exempt common costs are also allocated proportionately. [LISLR arts. 15, 21, 23](https://contraloriadebarquisimeto.gob.ve/images/baselegal/LOAFSP.pdf#page=8).
4. **Deduct only permitted expenses, at the statutory time.** Art. 27's general rule requires non-cost, normal and necessary expenditure incurred domestically to produce enrichment, subject to its express exceptions. Apply the detailed expense gates below. For income available on realization, deductions generally follow accrual, subject to art. 32's specific exceptions; for income available on payment, they follow actual payment, without losing the permitted depreciation and casualty-loss deductions. [LISLR arts. 27, 32](https://contraloriadebarquisimeto.gob.ve/images/baselegal/LOAFSP.pdf#page=9).
5. **Apply related-party and cross-border adjustments.** Apply art. 109's general arm's-length requirement to related-party transactions and the art. 116 interest limitation; identify the import/export and interposed foreign-party provisions separately; do not assume a transfer-pricing report alone makes interest deductible. Identify foreign-source and PE/head-office expenses and any withholding consequences. [LISLR arts. 27, 109-117](https://contraloriadebarquisimeto.gob.ve/images/baselegal/LOAFSP.pdf#page=20).
6. **Determine the inflation-adjustment result where legally applicable.** First exclude financial/insurance businesses and expressly designated special taxpayers under art. 171. For an included taxpayer, complete the initial and regular schedules described below and reconcile the resulting taxable adjustment to historical accounts; do not import the accounting inflation result. Uncompensated net inflation losses cannot be carried forward. [LISLR arts. 171-190, 193](https://contraloriadebarquisimeto.gob.ve/images/baselegal/LOAFSP.pdf#page=26).
7. **Apply eligible same-source operating losses.** Maintain separate territorial and foreign vintage schedules. For each source, limit the current deduction to eligible unexpired balances and 25% of that source's current enrichment. Sum the resulting ordinary territorial and foreign net enrichment; foreign losses cannot reduce the territorial base. [LISLR arts. 4, 55](https://contraloriadebarquisimeto.gob.ve/images/baselegal/LOAFSP.pdf#page=5).
8. **Apply the correct rate and credits.** Express the ordinary annual base in U.T. using the verified fiscal-close value; compute each marginal band, then convert tax using the same applicable unit. Keep separately taxed items separate. Apply any allowable foreign-tax credit within art. 2's cap, then reconcile documented withholding/advance payments against annual tax. Calculate a payable balance or documented excess-credit/refund request, not an unexplained negative tax. [LISLR arts. 2, 52, 56, 83](https://contraloriadebarquisimeto.gob.ve/images/baselegal/LOAFSP.pdf#page=4); [COT art. 3](https://www.cmchacao.gob.ve/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/Decreto-con-Rango-Valor-y-Fuerza-de-Ley-que-Reforma-la-Ley-que-Establece-el-Impuesto-al-Valor-Agregado.pdf#page=2).
9. **Complete the return workpapers and current filing instructions.** Reconcile trial balance, permanent/temporary adjustments, source schedules, losses, units and credits to the annual return. Obtain current SENIAT instructions, fields, deadline and payment route before filing. The law sets the duty; this Guide does not supply unconfirmed portal details. [LISLR arts. 77, 81-85, 88-89](https://contraloriadebarquisimeto.gob.ve/images/baselegal/LOAFSP.pdf#page=16).

For a nonnegative ordinary base `B` in U.T., the direct band formula is:

`tax_UT = 0.15 × min(B, 2000) + 0.22 × min(max(B - 2000, 0), 1000) + 0.34 × max(B - 3000, 0)`.

This is arithmetic derived from art. 52, not a separate published deduction/sustraendo table. For B above 3,000 U.T., it equals `0.34 × B - 500 U.T.`; the 500 is the algebraic difference between marginal bands and a flat top rate, not an additional relief. [LISLR art. 52](https://contraloriadebarquisimeto.gob.ve/images/baselegal/LOAFSP.pdf#page=13).

### Expense gates and the accounting-to-tax reconciliation

**Payroll and proprietors.** Art. 27(1) allows the listed remuneration and professional-service expenditure only with the employer's legal obligations fulfilled. The first paragraph blocks specified remuneration to the taxpayer, spouse/minor descendants and specified partners/participating managers. For limited partners, corporate administrators/assimilated taxpayers and their listed relatives, apply the second paragraph's 15%-of-global-gross-income ceiling to the covered remuneration, not automatically to the whole payroll. When gross income is absent, use its preceding-year or comparable-enterprise references. SENIAT may reduce remuneration resembling a dividend and reject specified excess foreign-payroll expense under the third paragraph. [LISLR art. 27(1), first-third paragraphs](https://contraloriadebarquisimeto.gob.ve/images/baselegal/LOAFSP.pdf#page=9).

**Interest.** Ordinary loan interest must relate to capital invested in producing income. For direct/indirect related-party debt, art. 116 additionally requires the statutory debt/equity and market-terms tests. Compute average equity as the mean of beginning/end equity before current inflation adjustment and without current profit/loss; compute average debts from month-end balances over the number of months, excluding that month's accrued interest. The deductible-interest formula in its third paragraph subtracts average independent-party debt from average equity and divides a positive balance by related-party debt: if the ratio is at least one, all otherwise eligible related-party interest passes this quantitative limit; if below one, only that fraction passes. No positive headroom means no positive deduction under that formula. Keep the statutory debt-to-equity recharacterization and independent-market debt conditions in the analysis even where an amount passes the ratio. [LISLR arts. 27(2), 116](https://contraloriadebarquisimeto.gob.ve/images/baselegal/LOAFSP.pdf#page=20).

**Depreciation, capital items and repairs.** Art. 27(5) permits a reasonable depreciation/amortization amount for permanent assets/other invested elements located in Venezuela, without a duplicate cost deduction; similar-life assets may be grouped. The regulation may prescribe tables, but no current table was established here. Art. 27's ninth paragraph restricts building depreciation to permanent income-producing assets or housing rented to workers. Ordinary repairs may qualify under item 17; acquiring or improving a capital asset does not become an immediate repair deduction merely because it is paid. Self-revaluation does not establish deductible depreciation/amortization except the law's applicable inflation rules (art. 28); a fully depreciated asset's accounting revaluation has no fiscal effect (art. 187). Retain cost, location, use, start dates, reasonable useful life and any actual regulatory table relied on. [LISLR arts. 27(5), (17), ninth paragraph; 28, 174, 187](https://contraloriadebarquisimeto.gob.ve/images/baselegal/LOAFSP.pdf#page=9).

**Bad debts and destruction losses.** A bad-debt loss must come from business operations, have been included in declared gross income (subject to the financial-loan/employee-loan exceptions), and be written off in the tax year because of debtor/guarantor insolvency or uneconomic collection. A generic expected-credit-loss reserve does not establish those tests. Fixed-asset losses in item 6 require casualty/force majeure, income-producing fixed assets, no insurance/other compensation, and no cost duplication. The nineteenth paragraph rejects destruction-loss deductions for inventory/goods for sale and rejects fixed-asset destruction claims not meeting item 6. [LISLR art. 27(6), (8), nineteenth paragraph](https://contraloriadebarquisimeto.gob.ve/images/baselegal/LOAFSP.pdf#page=9).

**Taxes, rent, administration and other expenses.** Business/property-related paid taxes may qualify under item 3, but ISLR itself is excluded; non-transferable, non-refundable consumption tax is a cost item under its conditions. Rent of income-producing property, business transport, ordinary insurance of the identified business/personnel risks, publicity, actual paid R&D and documented representation reimbursements must pass their respective art. 27 items. For rented real estate, item 11 requires actual payment and return control information for administration/conservation; the fourth paragraph caps actually paid administration at 10% of gross rents. Do not apply that administration ceiling indiscriminately to every maintenance cost. For aircraft outside the principal business, apply the tenth paragraph's 50% ceiling as well as normal/necessary/domestic conditions. [LISLR art. 27(3), (11)-(13), (18)-(22), fourth, fifth and tenth paragraphs](https://contraloriadebarquisimeto.gob.ve/images/baselegal/LOAFSP.pdf#page=9).

**Donations.** Qualifying voluntary expenditure must serve the collective/social-responsibility purposes listed in the twelfth paragraph, or be a donation to the Nation, states, municipalities or autonomous institutes, with a domiciled beneficiary. The ordinary ceiling is 10% of net income before donation deduction through 10,000 U.T., plus 8% of the excess. The art. 7(d) activities instead have the separate 1% ceiling and are outside the ordinary worked computation. A loss in the immediately preceding year bars these donation deductions. Keep purpose, recipient, domicile, payment evidence, pre-deduction income and preceding-year result together; do not treat the ceiling as an automatic deduction. [LISLR art. 27, twelfth, thirteenth and fifteenth paragraphs](https://contraloriadebarquisimeto.gob.ve/images/baselegal/LOAFSP.pdf#page=10).

### Inventory, construction and property events

For ordinary goods/services, start from corresponding gross receipts less permitted costs under art. 21, with invoices and cost evidence under art. 23. Capital cost for real estate includes the acquisition amount, improvements and registration duties, subject to applicable inflation rules. Shares received as stock dividends from the specified liquid/collected earnings or asset revaluations have no cost attributed under art. 23, fourth paragraph; do not invent an acquisition payment. A merger passes the merged entities' tax benefits and responsibilities to the resulting company under art. 16, fifth paragraph; it does not by itself prove a general tax-free reorganization. [LISLR arts. 16, 21, 23](https://contraloriadebarquisimeto.gob.ve/images/baselegal/LOAFSP.pdf#page=8).

For construction over one year, art. 19 recognizes gross receipts in proportion to construction, using the ratio of current applicable cost to total work cost. Art. 23, fifth paragraph, assigns cost to the constructed portion. Where a project is begun/completed within no more than one year but spans two tax years, art. 19 permits total-income recognition in the completion year or proportionate recognition; the corresponding art. 23 cost option must be handled consistently. Apply prospective adjustments for the statutory changes in income/cost estimates. Keep contract value, total/current cost, work progress, chosen treatment and future-balance adjustments. [LISLR arts. 19, 23, fifth paragraph](https://contraloriadebarquisimeto.gob.ve/images/baselegal/LOAFSP.pdf#page=8).

An art. 86 real-estate disposal/contribution/distribution advance is 0.5% of price, even for a credit sale, unless an exception applies. The seller/contributor self-assesses/pays before execution and provides proof to the relevant judge/notary/registrar; the principal-home exception requires its registration evidence. For a credit sale, impute the paid advance to each year's tax in proportion to actual receipts. If the sale never takes place, the statute permits the refund procedure with registrar certification. Include the disposal income in the ordinary global computation as required; the advance does not replace tax on net enrichment. [LISLR art. 86](https://contraloriadebarquisimeto.gob.ve/images/baselegal/LOAFSP.pdf#page=17).

The separate exchange-sale tax under arts. 74-76 requires both the stipulated public-offer authorization and sale through a domestic exchange. Tax is 1% of gross proceeds; the exchange withholds and remits within three working days following settlement/withholding. Associated losses cannot offset other enrichment, and the separately taxed income is excluded from the ordinary global net base. An off-exchange share sale does not qualify merely because the issuer is Venezuelan. [LISLR arts. 74-76](https://contraloriadebarquisimeto.gob.ve/images/baselegal/LOAFSP.pdf#page=16).

### Inflation adjustment: decide eligibility before using an index

Art. 171 requires the statutory initial adjustment at the first fiscal-year close for the enumerated commercial/industrial/extractive taxpayers keeping accounts and commencing operations from 1 January 1993, with its additional rule for included taxpayers whose fiscal year closes after 31 December 1992. It excludes taxpayers conducting banking, financial, insurance/reinsurance activities and taxpayers designated special by SENIAT. Do not infer inclusion merely from inflation in the economy. A qualifying noncommercial business keeping books may elect in under the first paragraph and cannot subsequently opt out merely by changing activities. Obtain the actual special-taxpayer designation history. [LISLR art. 171](https://contraloriadebarquisimeto.gob.ve/images/baselegal/LOAFSP.pdf#page=26).

For an included taxpayer:

1. **Classify the balance sheet.** Establish the statutory nonmonetary assets/liabilities. Foreign-currency or index-linked credits/debts and prepaid/received interest treated as deferred charges/credits are monetary for art. 171. Exclude unauthorized revaluations, the specified exempt/presumed/non-taxable assets/liabilities and related-party receivables as required. Track historical fiscal equity exclusions separately. Art. 185 separately values negotiable investments quoted or sold through Venezuelan exchanges and held at fiscal close by the relevant exchange quotation at disposal or close; classify them as monetary in the updated fiscal balance while unsold. Keep arts. 74-75's disposal tax treatment separate. [LISLR art. 171, second-fourth paragraphs](https://contraloriadebarquisimeto.gob.ve/images/baselegal/LOAFSP.pdf#page=26); [art. 185](https://contraloriadebarquisimeto.gob.ve/images/baselegal/LOAFSP.pdf#page=28).
2. **Prepare the initial adjustment.** Use the competent authority's INPC change from the month preceding acquisition (or January 1950 for earlier assets) to first-year close under art. 173. Record the resulting updated fiscal balance/equity and the statutory asset registry. Art. 172's 3% levy concerns the adjustment increase of depreciable fixed assets, with up to three equal consecutive annual portions; a preoperative business determines/pays after its preoperative period ends with its first invoice. Remaining depreciation uses the originally reasonably estimated life under art. 174. [LISLR arts. 171-174](https://contraloriadebarquisimeto.gob.ve/images/baselegal/LOAFSP.pdf#page=26).
3. **Recompute regular non-inventory adjustments.** Art. 177 updates net nonmonetary amounts by annual INPC for opening items or acquisition-month-to-close changes for additions, and updates cost and accumulated depreciation/amortization. Use the revised amount for a subsequent disposal's fiscal cost under art. 178. Preserve the asset-by-asset register in art. 179. [LISLR arts. 176-179](https://contraloriadebarquisimeto.gob.ve/images/baselegal/LOAFSP.pdf#page=26).
4. **Compute inventory separately.** Under art. 180, update prior closing inventory by annual INPC, compare current and prior historical totals, and treat closing historical inventory up to opening inventory as carried from opening. Historical excess over opening is not adjusted. Compare the resulting updated closing amount with historical closing inventory, then compare its accumulated adjustment with the prior accumulated adjustment; book the increase/decrease through inventory and fiscal inflation adjustment. Handle accessories/spares according to whether they enter traditional cost of sales or manufacturing expense; the specific-identification alternative needs SENIAT approval and actual acquisition dates. [LISLR art. 180](https://contraloriadebarquisimeto.gob.ve/images/baselegal/LOAFSP.pdf#page=27).
5. **Adjust equity and movements.** Art. 182 updates opening fiscal net equity, subject to its exclusions; the increase/decrease operates as a decrease/increase of taxable income. Under its second paragraph, movements during the year in Exclusiones Fiscales Históricas al Patrimonio are treated as equity increases/decreases under arts. 183-184. Art. 183 updates qualifying actually paid capital increases from their month to close as decreases of taxable income; pending shareholder capital contributions must be capitalized in the following tax year or are monetary liabilities. Current-year profits are not capital increases. Art. 184 updates distributions and other qualifying equity reductions as increases of taxable income. [LISLR arts. 182-184](https://contraloriadebarquisimeto.gob.ve/images/baselegal/LOAFSP.pdf#page=28).
6. **Close and reconcile.** Under art. 188, include the increment/decrement from the regular net-equity inflation adjustment in equity from the fiscal year's last day and transfer it to Actualización del Patrimonio. Keep the additional fiscal book, opening/closing fiscal balances, item calculations, equity exclusions and historical-result-to-taxable-income reconciliation under art. 190. Art. 176 controls preoperative timing; art. 189 excludes presumed enrichment and the associated net equity. Art. 181 prohibits carrying uncompensated net inflation losses to later years. [LISLR arts. 176, 181, 188-190](https://contraloriadebarquisimeto.gob.ve/images/baselegal/LOAFSP.pdf#page=26).

**Foreign-currency/indexed adjustment limit.** The printed art. 186 refers to gains/losses from adjusting foreign-currency or exchange-indexed assets/liabilities in the year in which they are due, collected or paid, whichever happens first. Its gazette sentence is grammatically incomplete and supplies no complete operative verb. The full recognition treatment is therefore **not yet confirmed** here. Refer that specific question for clean authoritative corroboration and professional interpretation before including an amount; do not complete the missing wording or infer a gain/loss recognition rule from memory. [LISLR art. 186, printed text](https://contraloriadebarquisimeto.gob.ve/images/baselegal/LOAFSP.pdf#page=28).

An excluded taxpayer does not apply this included-taxpayer schedule. Art. 193 requires SENIAT to regulate accounting adjustments caused by suppression of the system and directs subsequent estimated returns to prior-year global net enrichment excluding inflation effects. The currently applicable implementation providencia and actual INPC series are not confirmed here, so this Guide cannot complete a numeric included-taxpayer inflation computation without them. Do not replace the tax system with an accounting inflation adjustment or with a guessed annual percentage. [LISLR arts. 171, 193](https://contraloriadebarquisimeto.gob.ve/images/baselegal/LOAFSP.pdf#page=26).

### Losses, credits and the final balance

Art. 55's operating-loss schedule is source-specific, time-limited and capped. Identify each loss's tax year and use only the next three periods, after checking amounts already used. Use no more than 25% of the matching-source current enrichment. A foreign loss cannot offset territorial enrichment, and an inflation loss does not become an ordinary three-year operating loss. Preserve unused qualifying balances until expiry; do not assume carryback or indefinite use. [LISLR arts. 4, 55, 181](https://contraloriadebarquisimeto.gob.ve/images/baselegal/LOAFSP.pdf#page=5).

Foreign income tax actually paid may be credited under art. 2, only on foreign income included in Venezuela and no more than Venezuelan global tax multiplied by foreign net enrichment divided by global net enrichment. Tax on a separately proportional item has its own Venezuelan-item cap. Translate the foreign tax at its actual payment-date exchange rate under art. 2, distinct from art. 16's fiscal-year-average rule for foreign gross income. Require foreign assessment/payment proof, inclusion and source reconciliation. [LISLR arts. 2, 16](https://contraloriadebarquisimeto.gob.ve/images/baselegal/LOAFSP.pdf#page=4).

Documented withholding and art. 85 advance payments are on account of annual tax under art. 83. An excess under art. 56 can be requested in later declarations against subsequent tax until exhausted, without prejudice to the refund right; retain the actual request and credit trail. Do not treat a withheld amount as an expense and as a credit, nor treat an unsupported opening balance as cash available for payment. [LISLR arts. 56, 83-85](https://contraloriadebarquisimeto.gob.ve/images/baselegal/LOAFSP.pdf#page=14).

## Deadlines and filing

### Required workpapers and current instructions

LISLR art. 77 requires annual income/loss declarations for companies/assimilated entities, partnerships, communities and the other identified entities regardless of amount. No-profit or inactive accounting does not itself eliminate that duty. Art. 81 taxes annual available net enrichment and leaves exceptional short periods to the regulation. Arts. 82 and 85 govern self-assessment/payment under prescribed procedures. The law does not itself provide all current return fields or an automatically applicable filing date for every entity. [LISLR arts. 77, 81-85](https://contraloriadebarquisimeto.gob.ve/images/baselegal/LOAFSP.pdf#page=16).

Prepare the following sequence before submission:

1. Confirm RIF, fiscal year, legal representative, domicile and special-taxpayer status against the entity's records. Art. 96 establishes the RIF duty. Obtain the **current official SENIAT calendar/providencia and return instructions** for that taxpayer/year; identify annual/short period, any extension and separate estimate/advance duties. [LISLR arts. 77, 80-85, 96](https://contraloriadebarquisimeto.gob.ve/images/baselegal/LOAFSP.pdf#page=16).
2. Complete source-separated trial-balance reconciliation, documented deductions, inflation eligibility/result, loss-vintage schedule and ordinary versus proportional bases. Attach the calculation using the confirmed fiscal-close UT. [LISLR arts. 4, 27, 52, 55, 88, 171-190](https://contraloriadebarquisimeto.gob.ve/images/baselegal/LOAFSP.pdf#page=5); [COT art. 3](https://www.cmchacao.gob.ve/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/Decreto-con-Rango-Valor-y-Fuerza-de-Ley-que-Reforma-la-Ley-que-Establece-el-Impuesto-al-Valor-Agregado.pdf#page=2).
3. Match return inputs to the **actual current fields** for gross income, costs, deductions, fiscal adjustments, losses, taxable income, rate, credits and balance. Current labels/numbers and any electronic attachments have not been confirmed; do not manufacture a form number or menu path. Read the live return/instructions before entering them. [LISLR arts. 77, 82, 85](https://contraloriadebarquisimeto.gob.ve/images/baselegal/LOAFSP.pdf#page=16).
4. Reconcile receipt/payment or available credit evidence, submit through the authorized medium and preserve the actual acknowledgement, return copy, payment evidence and all workpapers. Art. 88 requires ordered books supported by documents; art. 89 requires compliant local invoices with issuer RIF as expense proof. A draft computation is not evidence of successful filing/payment. [LISLR arts. 85, 88-89](https://contraloriadebarquisimeto.gob.ve/images/baselegal/LOAFSP.pdf#page=17).

**Portal status.** Direct access to https://seniat.gob.ve and https://declaraciones.seniat.gob.ve was unavailable on 11 November 2026; earlier ordinary-browser visits also failed and no usable dated archive instructions were obtained. No portal fields or 2026 administrative deadline are represented as confirmed. This Guide supplies a statutory computation, not a completed filing how-to.

**Estimates and advances.** Art. 80 permits the executive to require specified categories with prior-year net enrichment over 1,500 U.T. to file estimates; it also permits prescribed use of prior definitive returns and special estimates for relevant extraordinary income. Art. 85 permits specific advance-payment arrangements, including up to twelve portions within a year and a possible 75% requirement. These are enabling limits, not proof that every company currently owes the same estimate, percentage or installments. Current implementing rules and any special-taxpayer advances must be established separately. [LISLR arts. 80, 85, 193](https://contraloriadebarquisimeto.gob.ve/images/baselegal/LOAFSP.pdf#page=17).

**Changes of address/seat.** LISLR art. 97 requires notice within 20 days after the identified residence/domicile or corporate seat/principal-establishment changes. COT art. 35 separately sets a maximum one month for specified changes, including fiscal domicile. Record and satisfy each applicable duty. COT art. 10 counts day periods as working days unless expressly continuous; month/year periods are continuous and nonworking expiry extends under its rules. Do not silently substitute one notice period for the other. [LISLR art. 97](https://contraloriadebarquisimeto.gob.ve/images/baselegal/LOAFSP.pdf#page=18); [COT arts. 10, 35](https://www.cmchacao.gob.ve/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/Decreto-con-Rango-Valor-y-Fuerza-de-Ley-que-Reforma-la-Ley-que-Establece-el-Impuesto-al-Valor-Agregado.pdf#page=3).

## Penalties and interest

A missed declaration and a late payment are different infringements. COT art. 103(1) covers nonfiling or delay over one year: where premises exist, ten continuous days' closure and a fine of 150 times the official exchange rate of the highest-valued currency published by BCV. Art. 103(3) covers an incomplete return or delay up to and including one year, with a 100-times fine. The statutory closure applies to all establishments/branches. The ten-day declaration sanction is in art. 103; do not replace it with the five-day registration sanction in art. 100. COT art. 108 increases pecuniary formal penalties by 200% for designated special taxpayers. Calculate monetary penalties only with the correct statutory exchange-rate date and actual BCV evidence under arts. 91-92; no current conversion is supplied. Other fraud, underassessment, withholding and collection sanctions require their own facts and articles. [COT arts. 91-92, 100, 103, 108](https://www.cmchacao.gob.ve/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/Decreto-con-Rango-Valor-y-Fuerza-de-Ley-que-Reforma-la-Ley-que-Establece-el-Impuesto-al-Valor-Agregado.pdf#page=11).

COT art. 66 causes late-payment interest automatically from the statutory payment deadline to full extinguishment at **1.2 times the applicable active bank rate** for the respective periods. The reference is the specified BCV-computed prior-month average for the six largest commercial/universal banks by deposits, excluding preferential-interest portfolios. SENIAT publishes the rate within the first ten continuous days; absent timely publication the last published active rate applies. Interest continues despite administrative/judicial suspension. A current published rate and date-by-date principal/payment schedule are needed for an amount; do not use 1.2% as the rate. [COT art. 66](https://www.cmchacao.gob.ve/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/Decreto-con-Rango-Valor-y-Fuerza-de-Ley-que-Reforma-la-Ley-que-Establece-el-Impuesto-al-Valor-Agregado.pdf#page=8).

## Cross-border points

A corporate computation keeps territorial and foreign schedules separate even when final ordinary positive net enrichment is combined. Common source costs are proportionate under art. 23, seventh paragraph. Foreign-source expenses must meet art. 27, sixteenth paragraph: normal/necessary operation expenditure, foreign legally compliant documents identifying provider/vendor and domicile, transaction nature, date and amount, and Spanish translation. A PE may deduct demonstrated transaction-purpose expenses including demonstrated management/general-administration expenses incurred locally or abroad, but art. 27, seventeenth paragraph, blocks the specified head-office/group royalties, fees, assistance and commission payments except actual expense reimbursements; interest is referred to the TP chapter. [LISLR arts. 1, 4, 23, 27](https://contraloriadebarquisimeto.gob.ve/images/baselegal/LOAFSP.pdf#page=4).

Foreign technical-assistance/technological-service expense cannot be deducted or costed under art. 27, fourteenth paragraph, where the services are or could be performed domestically at accrual; evidence of attempts to contract domestically is required. Outbound payments can trigger withholding on payment or book credit, whichever occurs first, under art. 84. Art. 196 continues Decree 1.808 pending the relevant providencias; this Guide does not state an unconfirmed withholding table or percentage. [LISLR arts. 27, 84, 196](https://contraloriadebarquisimeto.gob.ve/images/baselegal/LOAFSP.pdf#page=10).

Treaty relief requires an actual in-force treaty and residence proof meeting art. 3's translation/legalization conditions; no treaty-specific reduction is assumed. Foreign dividends, distributions and a branch deemed dividend involve separate arts. 64-73 and must be scheduled independently; do not assume corporation tax exhausts every shareholder/branch charge. Controlled low-tax-jurisdiction investments require the actual designated-jurisdiction and control tests of arts. 98-108/192. Art. 109's general arm's-length rule applies to related-party transactions; art. 110's import/export methodology and art. 115's interposed foreign-related arrangements are specific cross-border provisions. This does not establish a current domestic-transaction administrative filing requirement. These specialist computations are outside this ordinary-company Guide. [LISLR arts. 3, 64-73, 98-168, 192](https://contraloriadebarquisimeto.gob.ve/images/baselegal/LOAFSP.pdf#page=5).

## Two worked examples that recompute

### Example 1: ordinary bands and documented advances

Assume an ordinary domiciled company, outside the financial/extractive proportional regimes, with final ordinary taxable enrichment of 5,000 U.T. after all valid adjustments and losses. The actual fiscal-close UT conversion has already been performed outside this hypothetical. There is no foreign-tax credit or separate proportional item; validated withholding/advances equal 200 U.T. The first 2,000 U.T. generates 300 U.T., the next 1,000 generates 220 U.T., and the remaining 2,000 generates 680 U.T. Annual tax is 1,200 U.T.; the remaining amount after advances is 1,000 U.T. A flat 34% of the whole base would overstate tax by 500 U.T. [LISLR arts. 52, 83](https://contraloriadebarquisimeto.gob.ve/images/baselegal/LOAFSP.pdf#page=14).

```oa-check
name: Ordinary company marginal bands and advances in tax units
inputs:
  taxable_base_ut: 5000
  validated_advances_ut: 200
steps:
  - first_band: min(taxable_base_ut, 2000) * 0.15
  - second_band: min(max(taxable_base_ut - 2000, 0), 1000) * 0.22
  - top_band: max(taxable_base_ut - 3000, 0) * 0.34
  - annual_tax: first_band + second_band + top_band
  - remaining_payable: annual_tax - validated_advances_ut
  - flat_rate_overstatement: taxable_base_ut * 0.34 - annual_tax
expected:
  first_band: 300
  second_band: 220
  top_band: 680
  annual_tax: 1200
  remaining_payable: 1000
  flat_rate_overstatement: 500
```

### Example 2: same-source operating-loss cap

Assume the ordinary company has Venezuelan-source enrichment of 8,000 U.T. before loss carryforward, no foreign ordinary income, and a documented unused Venezuelan operating loss of 3,000 U.T. arising in the immediately preceding tax period. There is no uncompensated inflation loss in that balance, no additional deductions and no advances/credits. The 25% current-enrichment limit permits 2,000 U.T., leaving taxable enrichment of 6,000 U.T. and 1,000 U.T. of the loss still subject to its remaining statutory time limit. Tarifa 2 tax is 1,540 U.T. A separate foreign loss could not replace this domestic-source loss. [LISLR arts. 4, 52, 55, 181](https://contraloriadebarquisimeto.gob.ve/images/baselegal/LOAFSP.pdf#page=14).

```oa-check
name: Venezuelan operating loss limited to a quarter of current enrichment
inputs:
  current_enrichment_ut: 8000
  eligible_domestic_loss_ut: 3000
steps:
  - current_loss_limit: current_enrichment_ut * 0.25
  - loss_used: min(eligible_domestic_loss_ut, current_loss_limit)
  - taxable_base: current_enrichment_ut - loss_used
  - loss_remaining: eligible_domestic_loss_ut - loss_used
  - annual_tax: min(taxable_base, 2000) * 0.15 + min(max(taxable_base - 2000, 0), 1000) * 0.22 + max(taxable_base - 3000, 0) * 0.34
expected:
  current_loss_limit: 2000
  loss_used: 2000
  taxable_base: 6000
  loss_remaining: 1000
  annual_tax: 1540
```

## Ask the client first

1. What is the legal form, RIF, incorporation/domicile, representative and actual fiscal year? Is this a company, partnership/consortium or other entity; has SENIAT designated it special, and from when?
2. Which activities and receipts exist? Identify financial/insurance/extractive activities, domestic/foreign source, PE/fixed base, exempt income and proportional-tax items; provide the actual exemption/decree and conditions if claimed.
3. Provide the complete trial balance, financial statements, sales/cost records, invoices, payroll/legal-compliance evidence, fixed-asset/depreciation register, donation evidence, related-party agreements and interest/debt/equity month-end schedules.
4. Provide each source's tax-loss vintages, amounts used, prior-year return and donation/loss result, inflation-system eligibility/history, fiscal books and current official INPC/implementation material where applicable.
5. Which official UT value was effective at fiscal close? Provide the current calendar, return instructions and special-taxpayer/advance rules, foreign tax assessments/receipts, exchange-rate evidence, withholding certificates and payment/credit history.
6. Have there been property/share disposals, mergers, distributions, foreign investments, address/seat changes, nonfiling or late payments? Supply contracts, dates, notifications and prior submissions, so separate regimes and deadlines can be identified.

### When to refuse or refer

Do not finalize a currency computation without the applicable UT evidence, a filing/payment deadline without the current official calendar, or an expense/credit without its evidence. Refer mixed or disputed entity classification, financial/extractive regimes, sector exonerations, complex reorganizations, contested debt-equity treatment, specialist treaty/low-tax/branch-dividend work and audit/penalty appeals for the specific primary law and professional review. This Guide does not replace the missing evidence with a historical default.
