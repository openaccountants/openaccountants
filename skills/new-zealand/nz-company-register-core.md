# New Zealand company incorporation and register annual return v0.1

## Scope and source period

Prepare selected register working papers for an ordinary domestic company limited by shares under the Companies Act 1993, after the entity choice has been confirmed. 'Private company' here describes the engagement's ordinary ownership and activities; it is not an assertion that every company with private owners has the same reporting, tax or regulatory status. Overseas-company registration, public offers, financial markets participants, co-operatives, unlimited companies, restructurings and special regimes need separate analysis.

Load workflow-base and company-formation-workflow-base. Retain their profile and entity-choice confirmations, source gates, refusals, required outputs and qualified-review controls. This guide contributes selected company-register facts. It does not supply a complete entity catalogue or all mandatory country slots. Hold an entity recommendation or complete formation package until the missing slots below have current evidence. An annual-return engagement likewise needs every applicable statutory declaration established before its return can be marked complete.

The Companies Act consolidation reviewed is dated 1 July 2025; the regulations reviewed are dated 1 July 2023. The live versions warning lists and selected unincorporated changes were checked for the provisions used here. This is a scoped review, not assurance over the whole Act. Deposit Takers Act 2023 and Employment Leave Act 2026 changes screened here do not replace the selected incorporation and annual-return provisions. The Companies (Address Information) Amendment Act 2025 is not yet in force at the check date. Its section 2 provides for commencement by order or, at the latest, 18 November 2026; the address-information regulations expressly commence on 18 November 2026. Revalidate address disclosure and protection before work on or after that date. Do not use this version to approve a new alternative-address application. [Act][S1], [regulations][S8], [address Act][S9], [address regulations][S10], [Deposit Takers changes][S16], [Employment Leave changes][S17].

The Companies Office procedural pages were checked on 13 November 2026. Their fee table states an effective date of 1 July 2022. Recheck the actual service, channel, fee and notices for the proposed transaction. No processing estimate is a guaranteed incorporation date. Do not collect identity documents, tax identifiers, bank details, passwords or signing credentials; the authorised professional retains them and performs any submission or payment.

## Establish the engagement and missing country slots

Confirm the intended activities, domestic or overseas entity, owners, funding, liability objectives, employees, cross-border features, intended date and actual register status. For an existing company obtain a redacted register summary, incorporation year, assigned annual-return month, last return, current notices and prescribed return information, with separate reporting-status work where relevant. For a proposed company obtain a redacted reservation notice, proposed share allocations, director eligibility review, address arrangements, constitution decision, ultimate holding company assessment and consent status. Do not infer facts from a name or an absence of trading.

## Incorporation prerequisites and sequence

1. **Confirm the selected entity and prerequisites.** Section 10 requires a name, one or more shares, one or more shareholders and one or more directors. At least one director must live in New Zealand, or live in an enforcement country and be a director of a body corporate incorporated there under equivalent law. Regulation 12 prescribes Australia. An Australian address alone is insufficient: establish that Australian directorship and the prescribed information; Companies Office asks for the Australian company number. Citizenship does not establish where a director lives. [Section 10][S1], [section 12][S24], [regulation 12][S8], [director guidance][S6].
2. **Check every director.** Section 151 requires a natural person who is not disqualified. Check age of at least 18, undischarged bankruptcy, applicable insolvency prohibitions, company/limited-partnership prohibitions, Financial Markets Conduct Act or Takeovers Act prohibitions, employer-officer prohibitions where the company is an employer, prescribed overseas company/limited-partnership prohibitions, relevant property orders and any constitution qualifications. The exact subsection and underlying order matter. Regulation 10 prescribes Australia for section 151(2)(eb); do not treat that as a complete interpretation of every overseas prohibition, including paragraph (ec). A declaration alone cannot cure an actual disqualification. Missing, conflicting or unreviewed eligibility facts stop the appointment. [Section 151][S5], [regulation 10][S30].
3. **Reserve an acceptable name.** Section 20 requires reservation before registration. Under section 22, the Registrar must refuse a name that contravenes an enactment, is identical or almost identical to another company or a still-reserved name, or is considered offensive. The notice states the date from which the name is available for 20 working days, unless sooner revoked. Retain the actual notice and its expiry. A search result alone is not a reservation. [Sections 20/22][S2].
4. **Establish company and contact addresses.** The registered office must be in New Zealand. Its description must include the required location particulars, including the host firm/person and location within a building where applicable. The address for service must also be in New Zealand, may be the registered office or another place and must not be at a postal centre or document exchange. Companies Office distinguishes physical office/service addresses from correspondence and director/shareholder residential information. A PO Box does not satisfy the physical office/service requirements. Obtain the appropriate professional arrangements without putting private addresses in these working papers. [Section 186][S7], [section 192][S29], [company addresses][S11].
5. **Prepare the application and evidence.** Section 12 requires the prescribed application signed by each applicant; each director's signed consent and non-disqualification certificate; each shareholder's signed consent to the specified class and number of shares; any written authority where an agent signs that shareholder consent; the reservation notice; and a constitution certified by at least one applicant if a constitution is chosen. The application includes applicant, director, shareholder, share-allocation, ultimate holding company, office and service information specified by section 12. Directors' full names, birth details and residential addresses remain statutory inputs. Keep those private values with the professional. Do not sign for a director or infer shareholder-agent authority. A shareholder with more than one share allocation may need multiple consent forms and must sign and return each form received; do not cap the required forms at the number of shareholders. [Section 12][S24], [consent guidance][S4].
6. **Resolve constitution and ownership status.** An ordinary company may have a constitution but is not required by section 26 to have one. With a constitution, rights and duties remain those of the Act except for modifications permitted by the Act; without one, the Act supplies them. This does not establish the desired share rights or waive a special regime. Establish whether there is an ultimate holding company; Companies Office describes a controlling body corporate that is not itself a subsidiary. Provide its required particulars if present and obtain advice if the chain is unresolved. This assessment does not resolve individual beneficial ownership. [Sections 26–28][S3], [ultimate holding companies][S12].
7. **Keep the two timing conditions distinct.** Companies Office says to complete incorporation, including consents, within the name-reservation period. Its separate consent page requires signed forms returned and accepted before 5 pm on the last working day of the 20-working-day period after submitting the online incorporation application. A rejected replacement remains within the original consent period. Starting the application later does not extend the name reservation. Track both actual deadlines and resolve any conflict with the Registrar; do not assume a fresh consent period renews the name. Late missing forms can cause deletion of application information and require a new process and fees. [Incorporation][S13], [consents][S4], [section 22][S2].
8. **Obtain the result before treating the company as incorporated.** Under sections 13–14 the Registrar registers a properly completed application and issues the certificate. Incorporation takes effect from the date stated in that certificate. Section 15 establishes separate legal personality until removal from the register; it is not a promise of immunity from every personal liability. The professional must check the actual certificate and register outcome. [Section 13][S25], [section 14][S26], [section 15][S27].

Use official working-day rules and the actual notice for calendar calculations. This guide does not supply a complete holiday or non-working-day engine. Hold a derived deadline if its calendar assumptions are unverified.

## Separate Companies Office annual return

An annual return confirms register information; it does not replace the company income-tax return, statutory financial statements or an audit. Section 214 requires the prescribed information drawn from Schedule 4. Regulation 4 prescribes the Schedule 1 forms and their particulars; Form 12 is the annual-return form for section 214(1). Its published fields do not include the Schedule 4 financial-reporting, audit or resolution questions. Do not add those catalogue items as mandatory Form 12 answers merely because they appear in the Schedule. Keep each separate reporting obligation and its completion status open until independently resolved. [Section 214][S15], [Schedule 4][S23], [regulation 4][S31], [Form 12][S32], [Companies Office guidance][S20].

Section 214 requires the board to ensure delivery during the company’s allocated month each year, in the prescribed or specifically approved form and containing as much of the Schedule 4 information as is prescribed. Date the return as at a day within that filing month and compile the information as at that date. It must be signed by a director, or an authorised solicitor or qualified statutory accountant within the statutory definition. No annual return is required in the calendar year of registration; the first is due the following calendar year, not automatically 12 months after incorporation. A subsidiary’s use of its holding company’s filing month needs the Registrar’s written approval. Do not derive the due month from the accounting balance date or assume an income-tax agent extension changes it. [Section 214][S15], [completion guidance][S20].

Form 12 in the 1 July 2023 regulations consolidation is publicly available; its history note records replacement from 1 May 2015. Section 214(7)–(9) also permits Gazette prescriptions and specifically approved forms. This review did not locate a separate Gazette prescription or approval instrument, and did not inspect an authenticated conditional portal flow. Verify the applicable current form and instructions; additional or different questions require their authority and the facts needed to answer them. Absence from Form 12 is not proof a different approved form or conditional portal flow cannot request them. [Section 214][S15], [regulation 4][S31], [Form 12][S32].

Reconcile the prescribed Form 12 information below against the records as at the return date. Missing required information holds the affected return. Keep private values with the professional. [Form 12][S32].

| Published Form 12 item | Required reconciliation |
| --- | --- |
| Company and contact details | Company name and number, registered office, service and communication details; establish the email contacts. For an online return, regulation 7A requires an email for communication with the filer and an email for the company. The same rule applies to an online incorporation application; it does not require these to be different addresses |
| Every director | Full legal first names and surname, and full residential address. Form 12 itself does not show birth details or Australian-directorship fields; do not infer a broader portal prohibition or omit information required by another applicable process |
| Ultimate holding company | Required name, country and registration identifier; registered office if required in its country, otherwise the applicable service-address particulars. Establish UHC status and the form’s address conditions rather than silently leaving unresolved particulars blank |
| Shares and shareholders | Total shares issued, share parcels and the named largest holders with required identity/address particulars. Where there are more than 10 share parcels, an unlisted company must provide the other shareholders’ names, addresses and holdings on additional sheets in the prescribed manner. Do not discard those holders |
| Certification and completer | Correct particulars certified by the authorised person, with date, full legal name, title and signature; completer contact details. Reconcile the section 214(3) signer requirement; account access alone does not establish authority |

Regulation 7A(2)–(3) defines the online annual-return email requirement. Companies Office separately requires a RealMe login, online account and confirmed authority to complete the return. The professional handles authentication and payment. [Regulation 7A][S33], [completion guidance][S20].

Schedule 4 also lists reporting-status, code-company, financial-product-offer and sections 207I/207J/207K resolution/notice information. Its wider catalogue is not the current Form 12 questionnaire. If applicable law or an established different form requires one of those declarations, verify it before answering. This guide does not determine the underlying reporting/audit thresholds or exemptions and gives no completion credit for those separate obligations. [Schedule 4][S23].

Record the actual assigned month and year, registration-year exception or approved extension, current due/overdue state and the professional’s action. Companies Office allows a filing-month change only whilst the current return is neither due nor overdue; December and January cannot be selected. An extension can be requested during the filing month. If granted, its guidance allows 20 working days from grant, with the new deadline confirmed by email. A request alone is not approval. Missing the return may lead to removal and section 214(10) separately creates a directors’ offence for non-compliance with subsections (1) or (2), with the penalty in section 374(2). No penalty amount is adopted here. Removal is not automatic at month end; refer actual notices promptly for professional action. An inactive business is not, by itself, evidence that the register return is excused. [Section 214][S15], [annual-return guidance][S18], [month changes][S21], [extensions][S22].

## Tax and other compliance handoffs

Companies Office permits a combined application for an IRD number, GST and employer registration. The convenience of that route does not itself establish tax residence, ordinary company tax treatment, GST liability, an accepted non-active declaration or employment obligations. Route the applicable facts to nz-company-income-tax-ir4, nz-gst and nz-employer-payroll with their own source periods and limits. Do not submit identifiers or bank details through this workflow. [Tax registration][S19].

Preserve separately outstanding capital/share-rights work, industry permissions, ownership disclosures, event-driven register changes, record keeping, financial statements, audit and tax obligations. Obtain applicable primary authority before marking a stream inapplicable. No complete company-formation or reporting topic is claimed by this selected core.

## Worked controls

All cases are synthetic. Eligibility, applicable law, channel and calendar facts are independently established inputs unless the case expressly leaves them unresolved. Numeric checks test the stated arithmetic only; they do not establish legal eligibility or exercise a live portal.

### Case 1: selected ordinary online register costs

A proposed company has an accepted ordinary online reservation and incorporation fee basis. Add the published GST-inclusive amounts once. The register-only formation subtotal is $148.05. If one ordinary online annual return is later required at the same published fee basis, those 3 register services total $205.25. Professional, office and other costs remain separate. [Fee schedule][S14].

```oa-check
name: nz-register-selected-online-costs
inputs:
  reservation: 11.50
  incorporation: 136.55
  annual_return: 57.20
steps:
  formation_register_subtotal: reservation + incorporation
  register_services_total: formation_register_subtotal + annual_return
expected:
  formation_register_subtotal: 148.05
  register_services_total: 205.25
tolerance: 0
```

### Case 2: reservation expires before the consent period

The professional has independently checked the notices and calendar: 5 working days remain on the reservation and 20 remain on the consent period. The earlier current constraint is 5 working days. Record both deadlines, arrange acceptance in time and obtain Registrar confirmation if the reservation needs a different treatment. Do not promise 20 more days for incorporation. This diagnostic does not calculate either statutory clock from raw dates. [Section 22][S2], [incorporation][S13], [consents][S4].

```oa-check
name: nz-register-two-existing-deadlines
inputs:
  reservation_days_remaining: 5
  consent_days_remaining: 20
steps:
  earlier_constraint: min(reservation_days_remaining, consent_days_remaining)
  excess_consent_window: consent_days_remaining - earlier_constraint
expected:
  earlier_constraint: 5
  excess_consent_window: 15
tolerance: 0
```

### Case 3: one missing director consent

An otherwise prepared application names 2 directors and 2 shareholders. Each shareholder has one verified share allocation; both required shareholder consents and one director consent have been accepted. One director consent remains missing. Hold completion and retain the original deadlines; shareholder consents do not replace the missing director's personal consent/certificate. A later rejected replacement also needs acceptance within the original period. [Section 12][S1], [section 152][S5], [consents][S4].

```oa-check
name: nz-register-missing-director-consent
inputs:
  directors_named: 2
  director_consents_accepted: 1
steps:
  missing_director_consents: directors_named - director_consents_accepted
expected:
  missing_director_consents: 1
tolerance: 0
```

### Case 4: Australian residence without the required directorship

The sole proposed director lives in Australia; no NZ-resident director is proposed and the required Australian body-corporate directorship is unverified. Needs Input: obtain the equivalent-law directorship and prescribed company information or a compliant alternative structure. Do not infer compliance from Australian citizenship, residence or a willingness to sign. [Section 10][S1], [section 12][S24], [regulation 12][S8].

### Case 5: age or other disqualification

A proposed director is 17. Section 151 disqualifies the person; stop that appointment. For a different adult director with an unresolved bankruptcy, prohibition, property-order or constitution question, hold eligibility pending evidence. Being an adult and signing a consent do not establish all remaining conditions. [Section 151][S5], [section 152][S28].

### Case 6: address and transition controls

A company offers only a PO Box as its registered office and address for service. Obtain compliant NZ physical arrangements and location particulars. A prospective director separately asks to substitute an alternative address under the forthcoming safety process on 18 November 2026. Hold that request for the operative amended law, prescribed form/process and qualified review; this pre-transition guide does not approve it or waive residential information supplied to the Registrar. [Section 186][S7], [section 192][S29], [company addresses][S11], [address Act][S9], [address regulations][S10].

### Case 7: register return and tax return have different statuses

An existing company has filed its IR4 but its assigned Companies Office return is outstanding. Keep the register obligation open and check the actual due/overdue position. Alternatively, register filing does not establish that IR4, financial statements or any audit are complete. [Section 214][S15], [Schedule 4][S23], [annual-return guidance][S18].

### Case 8: calendar-year exception and conditional extension

A company registered in November 2026 has no section 214 annual-return requirement in calendar year 2026; check the assigned month for its first 2027 return. For an older company with an overdue return, changing the filing month is unavailable under Companies Office guidance. A pending extension request is not a granted extension. Keep the actual notice and deadline; do not borrow the IR4 extension or calculate 20 working days without an established grant date and calendar. [Section 214][S15], [completion guidance][S20], [month changes][S21], [extensions][S22].

### Case 9: prescribed fields and separate reporting work

The prescribed Form 12 fields reconcile, but the company’s separate financial-reporting assessment is unresolved. Do not invent a reporting-status answer or add a Schedule 4 catalogue item as an extra Form 12 requirement. Keep that separate assessment open. If an established applicable alternative form or conditional instruction requires a reporting declaration, obtain its authority and supporting facts before answering it. A register return never proves full company reporting compliance. [Section 214][S15], [Schedule 4][S23], [regulation 4][S31], [Form 12][S32].

### Case 10: retain the other shareholders

An unlisted company has 12 distinct shareholders, each with one share parcel. The professional has verified the holdings and ranking. Listing the 10 largest alone would leave 2 shareholders unreported. Provide their required particulars on the additional sheet and reconcile all 12. This count check neither ranks holdings nor treats parcels and people as interchangeable in other cases. [Form 12][S32].

```oa-check
name: nz-register-other-single-parcel-shareholders
inputs:
  distinct_single_parcel_shareholders: 12
  largest_holders_listed: 10
steps:
  other_shareholders: distinct_single_parcel_shareholders - largest_holders_listed
  all_shareholders_reconciled: largest_holders_listed + other_shareholders
expected:
  other_shareholders: 2
  all_shareholders_reconciled: 12
tolerance: 0
```

### Case 11: one shareholder with 2 required allocation consents

One shareholder has 2 verified share allocations, A and B, and the Companies Office has issued a required consent form for each. Both correctly signed forms have been accepted, so no required allocation form is missing. If B’s form is absent, one required form remains missing and completion stays on hold. A duplicate of A’s form cannot satisfy B’s requirement. Do not reject the complete set merely because 2 forms exceed one shareholder. This reconciliation does not establish the remaining formation conditions. [Consent guidance][S4].

```oa-check
name: nz-register-multiple-share-allocation-consents
inputs:
  required_allocation_forms: 2
  complete_set_verified_forms: 2
  incomplete_set_verified_forms: 1
steps:
  complete_set_missing: required_allocation_forms - complete_set_verified_forms
  incomplete_set_missing: required_allocation_forms - incomplete_set_verified_forms
expected:
  complete_set_missing: 0
  incomplete_set_missing: 1
tolerance: 0
```

## Outputs and self-checks

Retain the workflow-base working paper, reviewer brief, checklist and unresolved-item record. For a formation engagement, preserve the formation base's 5-output requirement and entity-choice checkpoint: this guide alone cannot discharge its missing country slots. Give the professional an evidence-linked register checklist, separate register/tax/reporting calendar rows, selected fee subtotal, both actual incorporation clocks and all unresolved items. Mark preparatory records as incomplete wherever a required condition remains open.

Before delivery check: correct domestic entity and date; current sources and amendments; all directors' actual eligibility and residence/directorship conditions; every required consent and written shareholder-agent authority; reserved name and both deadlines; valid office/service arrangements and address transition; actual certificate; correct fee/channel; established annual-return year/month/exception, every prescribed field and any evidenced additional applicable response; separate tax/accounts/audit statuses; all mandatory formation slots; consistent outputs and qualified review. Reject malformed counts. Reconcile director consents and certificates by unique named director; a distinct-director consent count cannot exceed the named directors. Reconcile shareholder consents to every required, verified share allocation: one shareholder may require multiple forms, and a duplicate form cannot replace another required allocation’s form. No executable arithmetic block proves a filing, identity check or practitioner sign-off.
