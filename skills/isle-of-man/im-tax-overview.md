# Isle of Man Tax Overview v0.2

> **General reference only.** This is a routing Guide. Use the relevant topic Guide and professional review before filing or taking a tax position.

## Ask the client first

- Is this an individual, company, LLC or other person, and what is its tax residence?
- Which tax year or company accounting period, income sources and activity countries apply?
- For an individual, what assessment category, allowance-taper income and existing tax-cap election apply?
- Are there wages, self-employment, Manx property, banking, retail or petroleum activities?
- What taxable supplies, registrations, group size, foreign tax and treaty facts are involved?
- Which Registry anniversary, return notices, tax payments and employer records are already outstanding?

## The method, step by step

The Isle of Man is a self-governing Crown Dependency with its own direct taxes, set by Acts of Tynwald. Income tax and National Insurance are run by the Income Tax Division of the Treasury. VAT, customs and excise are run by the Customs and Immigration Division of the Treasury (its pages still use the Customs and Excise name in places). Under the Customs and Excise Agreement with the United Kingdom the Island is treated as part of the UK for customs, excise and VAT. The figures below are for 2026/27; the linked domain skills carry the detail and win if anything here differs.

## Worked cases

### Ordinary resident rate screen

Assume a full-year single resident with GBP 40,000 income, the full GBP 17,000 allowance, no other relief or special income and no tax-cap election. Taxable income is GBP 23,000. Applying 10% to GBP 6,500 and 21% to the GBP 16,500 balance gives GBP 4,115 before credits. This arithmetic does not establish residence, the taxable base or allowance eligibility. Use im-income-tax for the complete workpaper. [2026/27 rates and allowances](https://www.gov.im/categories/tax-vat-and-your-money/income-tax-and-national-insurance/individuals/residents/rates-and-allowances/).

```oa-check
name: Isle of Man ordinary resident headline rate screen
inputs:
  assumed_income: 40000
  single_allowance: 17000
  lower_band: 6500
  lower_rate: 0.10
  higher_rate: 0.21
steps:
  taxable_income: max(0, assumed_income - single_allowance)
  lower_band_tax: min(taxable_income, lower_band) * lower_rate
  higher_band_tax: max(0, taxable_income - lower_band) * higher_rate
  tax_before_credits: lower_band_tax + higher_band_tax
expected:
  taxable_income: 23000
  lower_band_tax: 650
  higher_band_tax: 3465
  tax_before_credits: 4115
```

## When to refuse or refer

Load im-income-tax for residents, im-non-resident-income-tax for individuals moving or receiving Manx income, im-corporate-income-tax for entities, im-payroll-social for employment/self-employment contributions, isle-of-man-vat for supplies, im-related-party-uk-treaty for the UK corridor, and im-company-formation for Registry compliance. Do not infer eligibility for the personal cap, a zero company rate, treaty relief or a special VAT rate from this table. Refer unresolved residence, special regimes, large-group top-up tax, foreign entities and filing-calendar conflicts.
