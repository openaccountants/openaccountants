# Serbia: tax pack

Calendar tax year for individuals and, by default, for companies. Authority: the Tax Administration (Poreska uprava, purs.gov.rs); laws are drafted by the Ministry of Finance (mfin.gov.rs) and published in the Službeni glasnik RS. Currency: Serbian dinar (RSD). Corporate income tax is a flat 15%; VAT (PDV) has a standard and a reduced rate; social contributions are split between employer and employee. Serbia is an EU candidate: several 2026 tax amendments apply only from the day it joins the EU.
