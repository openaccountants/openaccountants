# Lithuania Tax Overview

## Scope

This is a 2026 headline tax card and route into the Lithuanian pack, not a complete liability calculation. Classify the person, source, income category and applicable period before using a figure. Particular exemptions, allowances, relief, PE status and filing procedures remain in the linked domain Guides. The cited statutory paragraphs and authority guidance were read on 28 November 2026. Use the 2026 rules; the historical 2025 rates and unconfirmed future amendments are not interchangeable with them.

## Ask the client first

Obtain the legal form, residence/citizenship and day counts, treaty-residence evidence, income type and source, annual amounts, company period and related owners, VAT supplies/acquisitions, employee contract and accident group, second-pillar status and cumulative contributions. Missing facts mean no final rate, exemption, deadline or net-pay conclusion.

### Lithuanian tax system at a glance

The figures below are in EUR and cover tax year 2026. VMI supplies the tax-law texts and guidance cited here; Sodra supplies the contribution rates and annual ceiling.

This page is the summary. Each line points to the Guide for the full rules:

**Average wage (VDU) used for the 2026 bands.** Several 2026 thresholds are multiples of VDU. Sodra gives 60 VDU for 2026 as EUR 138,729.00, so 1 VDU is EUR 2,312.15, 36 VDU is EUR 83,237.40 and 12 VDU is EUR 27,745.80.

- **Tax year:** PIT uses the calendar year (GPMĮ art. 7(1)). CIT normally does too; the tax administrator may approve a different 12-month period on request, considering the activity. Changes to that period require objective reasons and permission (PMĮ art. 6(1), (2)). [PIT text](https://www.vmi.lt/evmi/documents/20142/391224/GPM%2Baktualus%2Bkomentaras%2B%28aktuali%2Bredakcija%2B2026-11-04%29%2B.pdf/541c2138-7e9c-3e99-33bf-fc547c5cdc28#page=81); [CIT text](https://www.vmi.lt/evmi/documents/20142/751579/Pelno%2Bmokes%C4%8Dio%2Bkomentaras%2B%28aktualus%2B2026-03-25%29.pdf/c9688bd6-8557-df3a-c0a3-e4032cfff2b6?t=1774447164536#page=143).
- **Who is resident (individuals):** domestic criteria include a permanent home in Lithuania, personal/social/economic interests more in Lithuania than abroad, at least 183 days in a tax year, or at least 280 days over two consecutive tax years with at least 90 days in one (GPMĮ art. 4(1)). These are screening tests: apply the statutory day-count rules, other residence cases and exclusions, departure provisions and any treaty tie-breaker before concluding residence. See [lt-cross-border](lt-cross-border.md). [Statutory text](https://www.vmi.lt/evmi/documents/20142/391224/GPM%2Baktualus%2Bkomentaras%2B%28aktuali%2Bredakcija%2B2026-11-04%29%2B.pdf/541c2138-7e9c-3e99-33bf-fc547c5cdc28#page=52).
- **Scope:** residents generally include Lithuanian and foreign income. Under GPMĮ art. 5(3), a person resident under art. 4(1)(3)/(4) or art. 4(3), who is not a Lithuanian citizen and is also resident for the same period in a treaty country, includes only Lithuanian-source income when that country’s competent authority informs VMI of the fact. Non-residents include income attributable to a Lithuanian fixed base (including connected foreign income), plus the specifically enumerated Lithuanian-source items in art. 5(4), such as dividends and qualifying interest. A Lithuanian company generally includes domestic and foreign income, but business income through an EEA/treaty-country PE is outside its Lithuanian base if taxed there by corporate or equivalent tax (PMĮ art. 4(1)). See [lt-cross-border](lt-cross-border.md). [PIT text](https://www.vmi.lt/evmi/documents/20142/391224/GPM%2Baktualus%2Bkomentaras%2B%28aktuali%2Bredakcija%2B2026-11-04%29%2B.pdf/541c2138-7e9c-3e99-33bf-fc547c5cdc28#page=58); [CIT text](https://www.vmi.lt/evmi/documents/20142/751579/Pelno%2Bmokes%C4%8Dio%2Bkomentaras%2B%28aktualus%2B2026-03-25%29.pdf/c9688bd6-8557-df3a-c0a3-e4032cfff2b6?t=1774447164536#page=82).
- **Personal income tax rates (2026):** the ordinary annual taxable-income bands are 20% through 36 VDU (EUR 83,237.40), 25% on the slice above 36 through 60 VDU (EUR 138,729), and 32% on the slice above 60 VDU (GPMĮ art. 6(1)). Aggregate the relevant annual categories; do not give each payer or income type a fresh set of bands. Statutory exemptions, allowances and special rates still require classification. [Statutory text](https://www.vmi.lt/evmi/documents/20142/391224/GPM%2Baktualus%2Bkomentaras%2B%28aktuali%2Bredakcija%2B2026-11-04%29%2B.pdf/541c2138-7e9c-3e99-33bf-fc547c5cdc28#page=68).
- **Other personal rates (2026):** eligible non-employment taxable income, including interest, rent and gains where not exempt or specially classified, is taxed at 15% through 12 VDU (EUR 27,745.80), with the ordinary bands on the excess (art. 6(2)). That 15% tranche still counts when locating the ordinary bands. Individual activity, board fees, employer copyright payments, non-member small-partnership manager fees, waste disposals and the art. 6(6) categories have separate rules; do not treat all non-employment income alike. Dividends and gains on shares acquired outside an investment account more than 5 years before disposal are taxed at 15% and excluded from ordinary-band aggregation (art. 6(6), (10)). Ordinary individual-activity taxable income up to EUR 42,500 is taxed at 20%, reduced by the art. 18-2 credit; business-licence and agricultural categories are excluded. Above EUR 42,500, the whole ordinary individual-activity amount uses the ordinary bands without that credit. All ordinary individual-activity taxable income counts in the annual aggregation. See [lithuania-pit](lithuania-pit.md). [Statutory text and commentary](https://www.vmi.lt/evmi/documents/20142/391224/GPM%2Baktualus%2Bkomentaras%2B%28aktuali%2Bredakcija%2B2026-11-04%29%2B.pdf/541c2138-7e9c-3e99-33bf-fc547c5cdc28#page=69); [VMI rate and aggregation table](https://www.vmi.lt/evmi/tarifai-6-str.-1).
- **Corporate income tax rate (2026):** 17% of taxable profit for periods beginning in 2026 (PMĮ art. 5(1)(1)). The art. 5(2) small-entity route requires period income no greater than EUR 300,000 and no art. 5(3) exclusion. Its 0% first- and second-period rate additionally requires all owners to be individuals and, over three consecutive periods including those first two, no suspension, liquidation, reorganisation or transfer to new owners. The 7% route remains available when those additional 0% conditions fail but the income and association conditions are met. Under art. 5(3), combine the income of the specified associated units: its individual-enterprise/family cases and entities where the same owner or owners together hold more than 50% on the period’s last day. If that combined income exceeds EUR 300,000, neither small-entity rate applies. See [lt-corporate-income-tax](lt-corporate-income-tax.md). [2026 statutory text and conditions](https://www.vmi.lt/evmi/documents/20142/751579/Pelno%2Bmokes%C4%8Dio%2Bkomentaras%2B%28aktualus%2B2026-03-25%29.pdf/c9688bd6-8557-df3a-c0a3-e4032cfff2b6?t=1774447164536#page=96).
- **VAT:** the standard rate is 21%. Selected reduced-rate cases are 12% for accommodation supplied in accordance with tourism law and passenger/baggage transport on regular routes established by the ministry, its authorised body or a municipality; 5% for qualifying wholly/partly reimbursed medicines, medical aids and special medical food, and non-reimbursed prescription medicines (subject to art. 19(5)). Printed/electronic books and non-periodical information publications can qualify for 5%; calendars, notebooks and similar publications are excluded, as are publications with advertising above four-fifths or wholly/mainly music or video content. These are examples, not the complete category lists. [PVMĮ arts. 2, 19](https://www.vmi.lt/evmi/documents/20142/390965/PVM%C4%AE%2Bkomentaras%2B%28aktuali%2Bredakcija%2B2026-11-25%29.pdf/f6d2b91a-2a6b-19fe-8606-8a163daddb36?t=1795614042457#page=320).
- **VAT registration screen:** under art. 71(2), a Lithuanian taxable person may use the domestic small-business scheme if relevant domestic consideration was no more than EUR 45,000 in the preceding calendar year and is not expected to exceed it this year (new businesses use the current-year expectation). The exception excludes new means of transport supplied to other Member States. VAT starts in the month of crossing and applies to all supplies that cause the threshold to be exceeded. The EUR 45,000 test alone does not settle cross-border acquisition/service or controlled-entity registration rules; assess those in the VAT Guide before concluding no registration or no VAT. [Statutory text](https://www.vmi.lt/evmi/documents/20142/390965/PVM%C4%AE%2Bkomentaras%2B%28aktuali%2Bredakcija%2B2026-11-25%29.pdf/f6d2b91a-2a6b-19fe-8606-8a163daddb36?t=1795614042457#page=782).
- **Social insurance (2026, employees):** the employee headline is 19.5%: pension 8.72%, sickness 1.99%, maternity 1.81%, health 6.98%; add 3% for a participating second-pillar saver. The employer’s unemployment rate is 1.31% for open-ended contracts or 2.03% for fixed-term contracts. Accident groups I–IV are 0.14%, 0.49%, 0.7% and 1.4%; use the employer’s actual Sodra group. The Guarantee Fund is 0.16% of accrued pay; the Long-term Employment Benefit Fund is 0.16% on a base no lower than the minimum monthly wage. Both exempt the Bank of Lithuania and budget institutions; the Guarantee Fund also exempts political parties, trade unions and religious communities/associations. Thus 1.77% is only the combined headline for an open-ended/group-I employer liable to both funds, subject to the applicable bases, minimum-base rules and ceilings. [Sodra 2026 rates and bases](https://sodra.lt/imokos/idarbinimas-pranesimai-imokos-ir-tarifai).
- **VSD ceiling:** for 2026 the insured person’s and employer’s VSD contributions under art. 10(9) use the 60 VDU annual ceiling (EUR 138,729), with a 0% rate above it; track each social-insurance type separately. PSD is excluded. The older Sodra employer-unlimited note does not override the enacted text. The separate Guarantee Fund and Long-term Fund ceiling question remains on hold below. See [lt-payroll-social](lt-payroll-social.md). [2026 statutory text](https://e-seimas.lrs.lt/rs/actualedition/TAIS.1327/kxecHVoOzo/format/ISO_PDF/); [Sodra amount](https://sodra.lt/imokos/idarbinimas-pranesimai-imokos-ir-tarifai).
- **Personal income tax return:** where the ordinary annual filing obligation applies, art. 27(1), (5) set filing and payment of the annual balance by 1 May of the following year. For 2026 income the unadjusted anchor is 1 May 2027, a Saturday; MAĮ art. 162(6) moves it to the next working day, **3 May 2027**. VMI’s issued commentary corroborates that date. Filing exemptions and special arrival/departure cases require the detailed PIT/cross-border Guide. [PIT text](https://www.vmi.lt/evmi/documents/20142/391224/GPM%2Baktualus%2Bkomentaras%2B%28aktuali%2Bredakcija%2B2026-11-04%29%2B.pdf/541c2138-7e9c-3e99-33bf-fc547c5cdc28#page=382); [working-day rule](https://www.vmi.lt/evmi/documents/20142/1866649/MA%C4%AE%2Bkomentaras%2B2026-11-24.pdf/516e3e39-a70e-f3b4-4d28-de774f135258?t=1795587939069#page=397); [VMI issued commentary](https://www.vmi.lt/evmi/documents/20142/737112/R-3122.pdf/63b4f82d-9d01-b90d-1cb6-cd49f7d84701?t=1795412060645).
- **Corporate income tax return:** the ordinary annual return is due by the 15th day of the next tax period’s sixth month: 15 June 2027 for calendar 2026. The final period on cessation has a separate rule; do not reuse the ordinary deadline (PMĮ art. 51(2)). [Statutory text](https://www.vmi.lt/evmi/documents/20142/751579/Pelno%2Bmokes%C4%8Dio%2Bkomentaras%2B%28aktualus%2B2026-03-25%29.pdf/c9688bd6-8557-df3a-c0a3-e4032cfff2b6?t=1774447164536#page=611).
- **VAT return:** the general period is a calendar month, subject to art. 84 exceptions. An individual VAT payer normally has a half-year period unless a month is requested; confirm the assigned period. Monthly returns are due by the 25th of the next month; quarterly returns by the 25th of the next quarter’s first month; half-year returns by the 25th of the next half-year’s first month. Non-working final days move to the next working day. Eligibility and procedure remain in [lithuania-vat-return](lithuania-vat-return.md). [PVMĮ arts. 84, 85](https://www.vmi.lt/evmi/documents/20142/390965/PVM%C4%AE%2Bkomentaras%2B%28aktuali%2Bredakcija%2B2026-11-25%29.pdf/f6d2b91a-2a6b-19fe-8606-8a163daddb36?t=1795614042457#page=901).

## Worked cases

### Derive VDU-based screening amounts

Assume Sodra's published 2026 ceiling of EUR 138,729 for 60 VDU. Division gives EUR 2,312.15 per VDU, and multiplication gives EUR 83,237.40 for 36 VDU and EUR 27,745.80 for 12 VDU. These derived thresholds help route annual-income questions. They do not classify a payment, apply personal allowances, aggregate special-rate income or calculate a taxpayer's liability.

```oa-check
name: Lithuania 2026 VDU screening amounts
inputs:
  ceiling: 138729
  ceiling_units: 60
  band_units: 36
  other_units: 12
steps:
  - one_vdu: ceiling / ceiling_units
  - ordinary_band: one_vdu * band_units
  - other_income_band: one_vdu * other_units
expected:
  one_vdu: 2312.15
  ordinary_band: 83237.40
  other_income_band: 27745.80
```

## When to refuse or refer

Do not calculate total tax or net pay from this card alone. Obtain the detailed Guide’s inputs; unresolved residence, classification, ownership or insurance facts prevent a final answer.

The Guarantee Fund/Long-term Fund ceiling outcome, VAT categories or factual classifications not established above, and the previously mentioned future corporate amendment (Law XV-488/2027) are non-usable here.
