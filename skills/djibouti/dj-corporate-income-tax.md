# Djibouti Corporate Income Tax

## Who this applies to

Individuals and companies with commercial, industrial, craft or professional activity in Djibouti (arts. 19 to 21, 25). Exports are inside the tax since 2023. Investment-code and free-zone firms: see art. 62 and their own codes.

## Rates, bands and thresholds

## The base

Accounting profit adjusted for tax. Tax year is the calendar year (art. 27). A business starting in the last six months may close its first accounts at the end of the next year (art. 28). Payments over 50,000 by VAT-registered firms must be by crossed cheque (art. 90, LF 2016 art. 16). A litigation provision needs a court act (LF 2022 art. 12). Simplified-regime firms keep books with an approved centre or accepted accountant (LF 2022 art. 13).

| Item | Figure | Source |

## Cross-border points

Non-resident rate and exemptions above. Tax yields to double tax conventions signed by Djibouti (art. 25). DGI may exchange information with treaty states (art. 244 bis, 2024 rectified law). No transfer pricing rule was found in the texts read.

## Two worked examples that recompute

Example 1: profit 10,000,000, turnover 200,000,000. IBP 2,500,000 beats IMF 2,000,000, so IBP is due.

```oa-check
name: IBP against IMF
inputs:
  profit: 10000000
  turnover: 200000000
steps:
  - ibp: profit*0.25
  - imf: max(turnover*0.01,120000)
  - due: max(ibp,imf)
expected:
  due: 2500000.00
```

Example 2, the decision: loss year, turnover 8,000,000. 1% is 80,000, so the 120,000 floor applies, unless the patente is lower (art. 62).

```oa-check
name: Loss year floor
inputs:
  turnover: 8000000
steps:
  - imf: turnover*0.01
  - due: max(imf,120000)
expected:
  due: 120000.00
```

## Ask the client first

- Turnover and profit last year.
- Goods or services (regime limit).
- Investment-code or free-zone status.
- Payments to non-residents.
- Dividends planned.
- Foreign shareholders.
