# Portugal VAT (IVA): rates, thresholds, returns and penalties, tax year 2026 v2.6

> **Built from the law.** General reference for preparing a working paper; obtain a qualified Portuguese professional's review before filing. This version does not record a new human approval.

**What this Guide does.** It sets out the law layer of Portuguese VAT for 2026: who is in, the rates on the mainland and in the autonomous regions, the small-business exemption, return frequency, deadlines, refunds and penalties. Figures come from the VAT Code (Código do IVA, "CIVA"), the General Tax Law ("LGT") and the General Regime for Tax Offences ("RGIT"), read on the Portal das Finanças, from AT circulars and from the Diário da República.

**Sibling Guides.** [portugal-einvoice](portugal-einvoice.md) covers certified invoicing software, ATCUD and SAF-T. [portugal-bookkeeping](portugal-bookkeeping.md), [pt-income-tax](pt-income-tax.md) and [pt-corporate-tax](pt-corporate-tax.md) cover the rest. Load `vat-workflow-base` and `eu-vat-directive` alongside.

## Who this applies to

- **Taxable persons.** Independent producers, traders and service providers, including liberal professions, and the isolated operations within CIVA art. 2(1)(a), are covered. This includes individuals and companies; confirm the regime before preparing a return. File the start-of-activity declaration before starting, except the isolated-operation dispensation in art. 31(3). Sources: https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/codigos_tributarios/civa_rep/Pages/iva2.aspx, https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/codigos_tributarios/civa_rep/Pages/iva31.aspx.
- **Small-business exemption (regime especial de isenção, art. 53).** A taxable person with its seat or domicile in Portugal is exempt if it does not carry out exports or related activities and its annual turnover in Portugal in the previous calendar year did not exceed EUR 15,000. A business starting up uses its estimate for the current year (art. 53(1) and (5)). An exempt business charges no VAT and cannot deduct input VAT (art. 53(3)). The scheme does not apply to an isolated taxable operation or intra-EU supplies of new means of transport (art. 53(6)). Turnover is net of VAT: include the operations listed in art. 52-B, including non-accessory financial/insurance/property operations, and exclude disposals of investment goods. Organised accounting and imports no longer exclude a business from the scheme. Restart restrictions in art. 56 still apply: no exemption during the 12 months after a normal-regime cessation, or in the following year where cessation would otherwise avoid normal-regime entry. Sources: https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/codigos_tributarios/civa_rep/Pages/iva52a.aspx, https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/codigos_tributarios/civa_rep/Pages/iva52b.aspx, https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/codigos_tributarios/civa_rep/Pages/iva56.aspx.
- **Losing the exemption (art. 58).** The exemption ends: from 1 January of the next year if turnover in the year exceeded EUR 15,000; at once if turnover in the current year exceeds EUR 15,000 by more than 25% (that is, above EUR 18,750); or when another condition stops being met (art. 58(2) and (4)). The business then files a change declaration (art. 32) within 15 working days of year-end, crossing the margin, or losing another condition, respectively (art. 58(5)). VAT is already due on the invoice that causes turnover to exceed EUR 18,750, not just later invoices (AT circular 25062/2025, point 20(b)). Source: https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/legislacao/instrucoes_administrativas/Documents/Oficio_circulado_25062_2025.pdf.
- **Moving into the exemption.** A business in the normal regime that meets the conditions files a change declaration (art. 32) to apply it (art. 54(1)). The declaration can only be filed in January and takes effect from 1 January of that year (art. 54(2)). The business must then adjust the VAT it deducted on capital goods and on stock left at year-end, in its last-period return (art. 54(4)). A voluntary election out of exemption under art. 55 takes effect on filing and normally binds for at least five years; return to exemption is by a January change declaration after that period. An essential change in business conditions permits an earlier request, effective only as notified on approval. Sources: https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/codigos_tributarios/civa_rep/Pages/iva54.aspx, https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/codigos_tributarios/civa_rep/Pages/iva55.aspx.
- **EU small-business scheme.** A business established in another EU state must meet the Portuguese conditions above as well as the EU-wide EUR 100,000 ceiling, home-state prior notification and an EX identification number (art. 53(2)); exemption starts only when that number is communicated/confirmed. The EU cap must not have been exceeded in the preceding year and ends exemption immediately if exceeded in the current year (art. 58(3)-(4)); notify changes to the home state. Portuguese businesses using exemption abroad also need the host state's local threshold, EU cap, prior AT notification and EX confirmation (art. 58-A). Sources: https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/codigos_tributarios/civa_rep/Pages/artigo-53-o-do-civa.aspx, https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/codigos_tributarios/civa_rep/Pages/iva58.aspx, https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/codigos_tributarios/civa_rep/Pages/iva58a.aspx.
- **Special regimes.** The small-retailer, second-hand/margin, travel-agent, farmers' flat-rate and VAT-group computations, fiscal-representative cases, triangulation and tourist refunds are NON-USABLE here. Check this point with one of our partner accountants. Ordinary import VAT and deduction are covered below; elective import settlement through the return needs the conditions in art. 27(8).

## The base

Classify the actual supply and legal invoice, not the bank payee, brand, IBAN or cash movement. The former supplier-pattern library is not a rate or deduction ruling: energy bills can contain different rates; a bank or payment processor can sell taxable services; public-body charges and customs payments need their underlying documents. Resolve unknown customer status, location, rate, supplier establishment and business use before finalising the return. Reconcile invoices/credit notes, customs documents, SAF-T data and the prior return to the bank; retain a transaction schedule and the correction questions for the professional reviewer.

- **Scope and consideration.** Goods/services supplied for consideration in Portugal, imports and intra-EU acquisitions within their respective rules are taxable. For an ordinary domestic supply, start from the consideration, add associated charges and taxes other than VAT, and exclude the items in art. 16(6), such as qualifying discounts and properly recorded disbursements. Foreign-currency amounts use the last ECB rate or a selling rate of a bank established in Portugal; art. 16(9) allows the chargeability-date or first-working-day-of-month option. Sources: https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/codigos_tributarios/civa_rep/Pages/iva1.aspx, https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/codigos_tributarios/civa_rep/Pages/iva16.aspx.
- **Regional location.** Determine location under art. 6(16), applying the preceding place-of-supply tests with regional adaptations; transport services have the establishment exception in art. 6(17). Ordinary goods dispatched from the mainland to an Azores/Madeira customer use the mainland departure location, not an automatic destination rate. B2B general-rule services use the receiving establishment; B2C general-rule services use the supplier establishment, subject to the article's specific exceptions. Sources: https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/codigos_tributarios/civa_rep/Pages/iva6.aspx, https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/codigos_tributarios/civa_rep/Pages/iva18.aspx.
- **Exemptions.** Art. 9 contains conditional exemptions for medical/education, insurance, specified financial/postal operations and property letting. Do not treat every fee charged by a bank, payment processor, insurer or public body as exempt. Letting does not include hotel-type accommodation, parking, certain fixed equipment/business exploitation, safes or exhibition/advertising space. An ordinary exempt business cannot deduct related VAT merely because the expenditure is business-related. Sources: https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/codigos_tributarios/civa_rep/Pages/iva9.aspx, https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/codigos_tributarios/civa_rep/Pages/iva20.aspx.
- **Foreign general-rule services.** The Portuguese taxable-person customer self-assesses services supplied to its Portuguese establishment when the supplier lacks a Portuguese establishment from which they are supplied (art. 2(1)(e), 6(6)(a)); deduction remains subject to arts. 19–21. A foreign VAT charge does not itself cancel that liability: seek correction if VAT was wrongly charged. Accommodation/restaurant consumption abroad follows its specific location rule, not the general B2B rule; foreign VAT is not Portuguese input VAT, and any foreign recovery is a separate, NON-USABLE outcome here. Check this point with one of our partner accountants. Sources: https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/codigos_tributarios/civa_rep/Pages/iva2.aspx, https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/codigos_tributarios/civa_rep/Pages/iva6.aspx, https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/codigos_tributarios/civa_rep/Pages/iva19.aspx.
- **Construction reverse charge in 2026.** Contracts/subcontracts for construction, remodelling, repair, maintenance, conservation or demolition of immovable property: Portuguese-established taxable-person customers with wholly or partly deductible operations self-assess, regardless of whether their own business is construction. From 1 July 2026 the rule also covers customers making only non-deductible operations when buying qualifying item 2.42 works; supplier and customer may jointly elect the relevant DL 97/2026 treatment from 1 January 2026. The wider removal of the deduction condition by Law 65/2026 applies only when tax becomes chargeable from 1 January 2027: its art. 12(1) must be read with Rectification 39-B/2026/1. Private consumers are not brought into the rule. Deduction is a separate test. Sources: https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/legislacao/diplomas_legislativos/Documents/decreto-lei-97-2026.pdf, arts. 4 and 18; https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/legislacao/diplomas_legislativos/Documents/lei-65-2026.pdf, arts. 2, 8 and 12; https://files.diariodarepublica.pt/1s/2026/11/21501/0000200002.pdf.
- **Other domestic reverse charges.** Annex E scrap/waste requires a taxable-person supplier and a buyer with total/partial deduction rights (art. 2(1)(i)). Emission rights require a Portuguese-established buyer with total/partial deduction rights (l). Cork, wood, pine cones and pine nuts in shell require the same buyer conditions (m). Investment gold and renewable-energy-certificate outcomes are NON-USABLE here; the old article references did not establish those results. Check this point with one of our partner accountants. Source: https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/codigos_tributarios/civa_rep/Pages/iva2.aspx.
- **Input evidence and use.** Hold a legal invoice in the taxpayer's name and possession, or the qualifying customs document; exclude simulated operations and the knowledge-based fraud restriction in art. 19. Self-assessed VAT is deductible only when actually assessed and otherwise eligible. Use must serve taxed, non-exempt operations or a specifically qualifying art. 20(1)(b) operation; no automatic full recovery on every export-related expense. Ordinary domestic office supplies of EUR 200 + EUR 46 VAT (EUR 246 gross) give EUR 46 deductible only under these conditions. Sources: https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/codigos_tributarios/civa_rep/Pages/iva19.aspx, https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/codigos_tributarios/civa_rep/Pages/iva20.aspx.
- **Passenger vehicles.** Art. 21(1)(a) blocks acquisition, leasing, use, conversion and repair of tourism vehicles, pleasure boats, aircraft, motorcycles and similar listed assets. The statutory vehicle definition, including the seating/use test, controls; business use alone does not remove the block. A EUR 25,000 ordinary passenger car plus EUR 5,750 VAT remains blocked if no exception applies; the original facts did not establish propulsion, so the electric/plug-in/GPL branches below must first be excluded. Source: https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/codigos_tributarios/civa_rep/Pages/iva21.aspx.
- **Fuel.** For an ordinary tourism vehicle, diesel, LPG, natural gas and biofuel VAT is 50% deductible; ordinary petrol is blocked. Full fuel deduction is limited to the listed heavy passenger vehicles, licensed public transport excluding rent-a-car, qualifying machines, predominantly agricultural tractors and goods vehicles over 3,500 kg, subject to eligible use/evidence. A generic commercial-vehicle description is insufficient. EUR 80 diesel + EUR 18.40 VAT gives EUR 9.20 deductible under the ordinary-car rule. Source: https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/codigos_tributarios/civa_rep/Pages/iva21.aspx, art. 21(1)(b).
- **Electric/plug-in and gas vehicles.** Acquisition/manufacture/import, lease and qualifying conversion of tourism cars is excepted from the block for electric vehicles costing at most EUR 62,500, or plug-in hybrids at most EUR 50,000; GPL/GNV at most EUR 37,500 permits 50% recovery. Limits exclude VAT but include the whole related acquisition cost, including separately invoiced accessories/legal costs. Above the limit there is no partial recovery under this exception. It does not cover ordinary use/maintenance/repair; electricity for electric/plug-in vehicles has its own exception, so EUR 30 + EUR 6.90 VAT yields EUR 6.90 recoverable for wholly eligible business use. Private use after recovery requires a separate review, not an assumption of unrestricted deduction. Sources: https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/codigos_tributarios/civa_rep/Pages/iva21.aspx, art. 21(2)(f)–(h); Portaria 467/2010 art. 1(4), as amended by Law 82-D/2014 art. 24, https://files.diariodarepublica.pt/1s/2014/12/25202/0032000338.pdf; AT circular https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/legislacao/instrucoes_administrativas/Documents/Oficio_circulado_25088_2025.pdf, points 7–15.
- **Travel, meals and hospitality.** Business travel/tolls, accommodation, food/drink/tobacco, receptions, entertainment and luxury expenditure are blocked by art. 21(1)(c)–(e), unless a specific exception applies. There is no general 50% representation-meal recovery or business-trip exception. Direct participant expenses for organising congresses/fairs/exhibitions/seminars/conferences and similar events (excluding tobacco) permit 50% when directly contracted with the provider or a legally authorised intermediary and demonstrably serving taxable operations. Attending such events permits 25% on the specified travel/accommodation/food/drink when contracted directly with the organiser and demonstrably serving taxable operations. Source: https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/codigos_tributarios/civa_rep/Pages/iva21.aspx, art. 21(2)(d)–(e).
- **Other art. 21(2) exceptions.** Listed vehicles/assets whose sale or exploitation is the taxpayer's business; own provision of staff canteens/dormitories and similar; or art. 21(1)(a)–(d) expenditure incurred in one's own name for a third party and recharged to obtain reimbursement. These are specific tests, not automatic relief for every taxi/driving school or recharged cost. Source: https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/codigos_tributarios/civa_rep/Pages/iva21.aspx, art. 21(2)(a)–(c).
- **Gifts and samples.** A free transfer of business goods after total/partial VAT deduction is ordinarily treated as a supply. The exception concerns qualifying promotional samples and gifts in accordance with commercial practice of unit value at most EUR 50 and annual total at most five per thousand of prior-year turnover. Startup estimates are corrected on the final return if actual turnover is lower. Detailed sample/offer qualification under the implementing ordinance is NON-USABLE here. Check this point with one of our partner accountants. Source: https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/codigos_tributarios/civa_rep/Pages/iva3.aspx, art. 3(3)(f), (7)–(8).
- **Mixed use.** Directly allocate eligible purchases where possible. Common taxable/exempt economic costs follow art. 23's annual turnover fraction, or permitted objective actual-use allocation; non-economic/private use requires actual-use criteria. The fraction excludes investment disposals and accessory property/financial transactions; include the relevant non-taxed subsidies, round the ratio upwards to the next hundredth and true up in the last period. Do not infer an apportionment percentage from a bank description. EUR 1,500 + EUR 345 laptop VAT at documented 60% eligible business use/40% personal gives EUR 207 deduction; classification/evidence still need review. Source: https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/codigos_tributarios/civa_rep/Pages/iva23.aspx.
- **Capital goods.** Pro-rata adjustments compare the definitive percentage in the first-use year and following four years for movables, or first-occupation year and following nineteen years for property, with the acquisition/construction percentage; a difference of at least five percentage points triggers one-fifth/one-twentieth adjustment. Actual-use allocations instead use art. 24(4)'s EUR 250 VAT-change test. The annual adjustments in (3)–(4) exclude goods of unit value below EUR 2,500 or prescribed useful life under five years; this does not generally exempt disposal or change-of-use adjustments. A disposal during the period requires a one-off remaining-period adjustment under (5); property changes in (6) also need review. Include adjustments in the last-period return. Sources: https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/codigos_tributarios/civa_rep/Pages/iva24.aspx, https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/codigos_tributarios/civa_rep/Pages/iva54.aspx.
- **Property waiver.** Urban buildings/units let to taxable persons using them wholly/mainly in deduction-entitled activities, and eligible sales of such property/building land, can fall within art. 12(4)–(6). A commercial lease alone does not establish a valid waiver: special-legislation eligibility/certification and the transaction outcome are NON-USABLE here. Check this point with one of our partner accountants. Source: https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/codigos_tributarios/civa_rep/Pages/iva12.aspx.

**Housing hold — item 2.42.** NON-USABLE eligibility/rate conclusion: the original 6% mainland construction/rehabilitation candidate covers own permanent-home sales or exclusive housing lets within DL 97/2026 price/rent limits (2.42.1), and investment-contract housing lets/sublets (2.42.2). The original procedural window 25 September 2025–31 December 2029, VAT chargeability from 1 January 2026, July 2026 commencement/joint January option and 31 December 2032 sunset are retained for review, not as a sufficient eligibility test. The sale/letting conditions, apportionment, later corrections and different commencement of the investment-contract regime must be resolved before applying relief. Check this point with one of our partner accountants.

## Deadlines and filing

- **Frequency.** Monthly if turnover in the previous calendar year was EUR 650,000 or more; quarterly if below (art. 41(1)). A quarterly business may opt for monthly returns: on registration (effective on filing), or by a January change declaration effective from 1 January; renunciation is also by January declaration, effective for that year (art. 41(2)-(4)). A quarterly business that reached EUR 650,000 files a change declaration in January and moves to monthly from 1 January of that year (art. 41(5)). A part-year turnover is annualised (art. 41(7)), and a new business uses its estimate for the current year (art. 41(8)). Quarterly returns are not available to the monthly-refund register or elective import-VAT settlement users (arts. 41(6), 22(15)). The fuel-supplier exclusion added by Law 65/2026 must not be applied to 2026 fuel transactions prematurely: read its rectified commencement and art. 8(2) January 2027 transition. A fuel-chain filing transition is NON-USABLE here. Check this point with one of our partner accountants. ## Two worked examples that recompute

**Example 1: quarterly return, Q3 2026, mainland.** A Lisbon Lda. with 2025 turnover of EUR 300,000 files quarterly. In July to September 2026 it invoices EUR 40,000 of services at 23%. It buys EUR 12,000 of goods and services at 23%, EUR 1,000 net of diesel at 23%, a EUR 300 net client lunch at 13% and EUR 600 net of passenger-car leasing at 23%. Output VAT is 9,200. Input VAT on general purchases is 2,760. The diesel VAT is 230, of which 50% is deductible: 115. The lunch VAT (39) and the car leasing VAT (138) are not deductible, assuming an ordinary tourism car outside the electric/plug-in/GPL/GNV and business-exploitation exceptions, and no event/recharge exception for the lunch. All other purchases are fully eligible and supported by legal invoices. VAT payable is 6,325. The return is due by 20 November 2026 and the payment by 25 November 2026. If it pays late, the ordinary LGT art. 44 late-payment rule starts after 25 November; the 2026 rate is 7.221%. Determine any RGIT art. 114 fine separately with fault, entity limits and relief; do not infer a final fine or interest bill from this example.

```oa-check
name: Portugal quarterly periodic return Q3 2026 with blocked and 50% input VAT
inputs:
  sales_net: 40000
  purchases_net: 12000
  diesel_net: 1000
  lunch_net: 300
  car_lease_net: 600
steps:
  - output_vat: sales_net * 0.23
  - input_general: purchases_net * 0.23
  - diesel_deductible: diesel_net * 0.23 * 0.50
  - lunch_deductible: lunch_net * 0.13 * 0
  - car_lease_deductible: car_lease_net * 0.23 * 0
  - deductible: input_general + diesel_deductible + lunch_deductible + car_lease_deductible
  - payable: output_vat - deductible
expected:
  output_vat: 9200
  deductible: 2875
  payable: 6325
```

**Example 2: a freelancer crosses the 25% margin.** A designer in Porto is under the art. 53 exemption for 2026, having billed EUR 14,000 in 2025. By 31 October 2026 she has billed EUR 18,000. That is above EUR 15,000 but within the 25% margin (EUR 18,750), so she stays exempt for now. In November she bills EUR 1,500, taking the year to EUR 19,500, 30% over the limit. The exemption ends at that moment (art. 58(4)(b)). Assuming ordinary mainland design services with these amounts net of VAT, the crossing November invoice itself bears EUR 345 (1,500 × 23%). She files a change declaration within 15 working days. On EUR 2,000 of December invoices she charges EUR 460. Even had she stayed within the margin, her 2026 turnover above EUR 15,000 would have ended the exemption from 1 January 2027 (art. 58(4)(a)).

```oa-check
name: Portugal art. 53 exemption, crossing the 25% margin in 2026
inputs:
  billed_to_october: 18000
  november_invoice: 1500
  limit: 15000
  december_net: 2000
steps:
  - margin_ceiling: limit * 1.25
  - year_to_november: billed_to_october + november_invoice
  - excess_pct: (year_to_november - limit) / limit * 100
  - over_ceiling: year_to_november - margin_ceiling
  - vat_crossing_invoice: november_invoice * 0.23
  - vat_december: december_net * 0.23
expected:
  margin_ceiling: 18750
  year_to_november: 19500
  excess_pct: 30
  over_ceiling: 750
  vat_crossing_invoice: 345
  vat_december: 460
```

## Ask the client first

- What was your turnover in Portugal in 2025, and what will it be in 2026? Do you export? (Decides the EUR 15,000 exemption and the 25% margin.)
- Was your 2025 turnover EUR 650,000 or more, and have you opted for monthly returns? (Decides monthly or quarterly.)
- Where are your operations treated as made: mainland, Azores or Madeira? (Decides the rate.)
- Do you make exempt supplies such as letting, health, education or financial services? (Decides apportionment.)
- Do you have passenger cars, fuel, travel, meals or hospitality costs? (Decides blocked and 50% input VAT.)
- Do you buy construction work, services from abroad or scrap? (Decides self-assessment.)
