# Portugal: corporate income tax (IRC), tax year 2026 v0.4

Fix the tax-period start date first: mainland general IRC is **19% for periods starting in 2026**, with **15% on the first EUR 50,000** for an eligible SME or Small Mid Cap. Surcharges use taxable profit before losses; IRC uses taxable income after losses. Never turn an unknown municipal rate, vehicle cost or relief condition into a final filing number.

## Who this applies to

Resident companies with their head office or effective management in Portugal and a commercial, industrial or agricultural main activity, including business services, are within this computation; residents are taxed on worldwide income. Non-residents' Portuguese permanent establishments are included only after attributable profit has been established (CIRC arts. 3 and 4). [Base](https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/codigos_tributarios/CIRC_2R/Pages/irc3.aspx); [territorial scope](https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/codigos_tributarios/CIRC_2R/Pages/irc4.aspx).

The tax period normally matches the calendar year. An eligible company may adopt a different annual accounting period, normally maintained for at least five subsequent periods (art. 8(2), subject to its group-accounting exception). A period from 1 July 2025 to 30 June 2026 uses the 2025 rate, not the 2026 rate. [Period](https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/codigos_tributarios/CIRC_2R/Pages/irc8.aspx).

Required inputs: approved accounts and tax adjustments; period dates; documented SME/Small Mid Cap and de minimis eligibility; municipality and establishments; previous-period turnover, IRC and withholding; losses by year and ownership history; financing schedule; vehicle acquisition costs, fuel/emissions and annual charges; credits, withholding certificates and payments already made. Look in the taxpayer's records before asking for missing facts. If records are insufficient, stop the affected calculation.

Banks, insurers, supervised finance businesses, funds, non-business/non-profit entities, simplified taxation, oil/gas/extractive regimes, reorganisations and active inspections/disputes are outside this computation. RETGS, minimum-tax groups, Madeira/Azores and CINM cases have explicit holds below. Check this point with one of our partner accountants.

**Municipal computation.** Confirm the actual period's rate, exemptions and any reduced rate in the municipality's decision/AT table. AT's Annex A instructions say “não ultrapasse” for the reduced-rate turnover boundary, which includes equality, but do not substitute that for checking the actual relief adopted. Annex A is required when taxable income exceeds EUR 50,000 AND there are permanent establishments/local representations in more than one municipality. Under its general criterion, allocate using municipal payroll divided by total payroll (six decimal places), multiply taxable profit by that ratio and the municipal rate, and carry the total to Modelo 22 Q10 field 364. Special power-generation/mining criteria are held. [Annex A](https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/apoio_contribuinte/modelos_formularios/irc/Documents/M22ANA.PDF).

**SME qualification.** Do not infer eligibility from turnover or employee count alone: establish the DL 372/2007 classification including partner/linked enterprises and de minimis availability. The restructuring transition in Lei 24-D/2022 art. 229 preserves the reduced rate for the two exercises after qualifying mergers, divisions, asset contributions or share exchanges between 1 January 2023 and 31 December 2026, where all parties qualified and the recipient loses size qualification because of the operation. Specialist reorganisation computation remains held. [Art. 87 and transition](https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/codigos_tributarios/CIRC_2R/Pages/irc87.aspx).

## The taxable base

1. **Reconcile accounts to taxable profit (lucro tributável).** Include net accounting result, relevant positive/negative equity changes not in that result, and CIRC adjustments. Retain a reconciliation of each addition and deduction. Taxable income (matéria coletável) then deducts available losses and applicable base deductions. [Arts. 17](https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/codigos_tributarios/CIRC_2R/Pages/irc17.aspx) and [15](https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/codigos_tributarios/CIRC_2R/Pages/irc15.aspx).
2. **Dividends received, participation exemption (art. 51).** All conditions matter: direct or direct-and-indirect holding of at least 10% under art. 69(6); uninterrupted year before distribution or continued holding to complete it; recipient outside fiscal transparency; payer subject and not exempt to an eligible IRC/Directive/similar tax, with statutory rate at least 60% of the art. 87(1) reference rate; no listed preferential domicile. Art. 51(2) waives the tax condition where art. 66(7)'s income-composition exclusion is met. Distributions deductible for the payer are excluded; art. 51(10)(b) also excludes distributions from untaxed/exempt payers outside qualifying EU/EEA cooperation unless the underlying sub-subsidiary income was taxed and not exempt. Non-genuine tax-advantage arrangements fail. Foreign tax credit may instead be available under arts. 91/91-A, but is not computed here. The 2026 reference-rate interaction for a payer passing only against the printed future rate is non-usable: Check this point with one of our partner accountants. [Art. 51](https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/codigos_tributarios/CIRC_2R/Pages/irc51.aspx); [art. 66(7)](https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/codigos_tributarios/CIRC_2R/Pages/irc66.aspx); [rate transition](https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/codigos_tributarios/CIRC_2R/Pages/irc87.aspx).
3. **Share gains/losses (art. 51-C).** Shares held uninterruptedly at least one year: exclude both gains and losses if art. 51(1)(a), (c), (e), and (d) or art. 51(2) are satisfied at sale, regardless of the percentage sold. Associated equity instruments, including supplementary contributions, are included. The exclusion fails where Portuguese immovables/rights exceed 50% of assets directly or indirectly, except property used in agricultural/industrial/commercial business other than buying/selling property. Prior deductible impairments/value adjustments covered by art. 51-C(6) are added back on an exempt disposal. Apply the same reference-rate hold. [Art. 51-C](https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/codigos_tributarios/CIRC_2R/Pages/irc51c.aspx).
4. **Financing (art. 67).** Net financing expense is tax-relevant financing cost less taxable, non-exempt interest/similar income, up to that cost. Tax EBITDA is taxable profit/loss subject and not exempt, plus net financing expenses and tax-deductible depreciation/amortisation. Deduct up to the higher ceiling in the table, prorating the fixed ceiling by months for a short period. Excess cost carries five periods, after current-period costs; unused 30%-EBITDA capacity carries to the fifth following period. Use oldest carry-forwards first. A change of more than 50% of capital or majority of votes can extinguish carry-forwards, except art. 52(9) changes or where tax evasion was not a main purpose (valid economic reasons may establish this). Supervised finance/insurance businesses are excluded. Group elections are held. [Art. 67](https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/codigos_tributarios/CIRC_2R/Pages/irc67.aspx).
5. **CFC (art. 66).** A resident's direct/indirect holding of at least 25% of capital, votes or rights to income/assets, including nominees and holdings of art. 63(4)(a)-(e) related entities, may trigger attribution. The foreign entity must be in a listed territory OR pay profit tax below 50% of CIRC-equivalent tax. The income-composition exclusion applies if the sum of royalties/IP, dividends/share disposals, finance leasing, related-party banking/insurance/finance, low-value related invoicing, and interest/other investment income is no more than 25% of total income. EU/qualifying EEA entities are excluded where valid economic reasons and real activity using staff, equipment, assets and premises are demonstrated. Do not apply a historical alternative 10% control test. Attribution, foreign-tax deductions, previously attributed distributions, disposal adjustments and CFC loss schedules require a separate calculation: Check this point with one of our partner accountants. [Art. 66](https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/codigos_tributarios/CIRC_2R/Pages/irc66.aspx).
6. **Losses (art. 52).** No fixed carry-forward period for post-2022 deductions, including earlier losses whose window remained open at commencement of Lei 24-D/2022; the pre-2023 REAID exception keeps its former deduction period. Deduct up to 65% of current taxable profit, increased by ten points only for the extra deduction of 2020/2021 losses. Unused losses remain available; there is no carry-back. No deduction in an indirect-method assessment year, without destroying later availability. Losses of exempt/reduced activities cannot offset other activities, and transparent entities' losses remain with those entities. [Art. 52 including art. 228 transition](https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/codigos_tributarios/CIRC_2R/Pages/irc52.aspx); [Lei 27-A/2020 art. 11(2)](https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/legislacao/diplomas_legislativos/Documents/Lei_27_A_2020.pdf).
7. **Loss ownership test.** At the end of the deduction period, compare ownership with the loss period. A change of more than 50% of capital OR majority of votes ends deduction unless tax evasion was not a main purpose. Art. 52(9) excludes direct/indirect switches and common-majority transfers under art. 69(6), qualifying reorganisations, death succession, acquisitions by an uninterrupted holder of more than 20% of capital or majority voting rights since the loss period began, and acquisitions by an employee/board member since then. Do not assume an AT permission procedure. Mixed-vintage allocation, expired old losses and REAID evidence must be resolved before filing. [Art. 52](https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/codigos_tributarios/CIRC_2R/Pages/irc52.aspx).
8. **Compute IRC and surcharges.** Apply the eligible SME band then the general rate to taxable income. Compute both derramas on taxable profit before losses. For state derrama, the first charged slice is EUR 6,000,000 at 3%, the next EUR 27,500,000 at 5%, then profit above EUR 35,000,000 at 9%. Under RETGS each member's own taxable profit is used, including the parent. [Art. 87](https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/codigos_tributarios/CIRC_2R/Pages/irc87.aspx); [87-A](https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/codigos_tributarios/CIRC_2R/Pages/irc87a.aspx); [Annex A](https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/apoio_contribuinte/modelos_formularios/irc/Documents/M22ANA.PDF).
9. **Autonomous taxes.** Charge the expense base independently of taxable income. Vehicle bands use acquisition cost, but tax annual depreciation/rent, insurance, maintenance, fuel and ownership/use taxes. Passenger cars, the ISV art. 7(1)(b) light-goods class and motorcycles are covered for non-exempt business taxpayers. Public-transport/business-rental vehicles and cars under the employee-tax agreement are excluded. Hybrids need grid charging, at least 50 km electric range, and official emissions below 50 gCO2/km (below 80 if Euro 6e-bis approved); GNV passenger cars also qualify. Do not guess missing acquisition cost or emissions. No deductions against the autonomous-tax total; expenses attributable to a foreign permanent establishment's business are excluded. [Art. 88(3)-(6), (17), (18), (23)](https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/codigos_tributarios/CIRC_2R/Pages/irc88.aspx).
10. **Managers and loss-year uplift.** Art. 88(13)(a) termination charges include non-productivity compensation and the excess over remaining contractual remuneration on early termination. The bonus escape needs at least 50% deferred at least three years AND payment conditional on positive company performance throughout; later failure adds the missed tax in that failure period. All art. 88 rates rise ten points for a tax-loss year relating to non-exempt business activity, except the start year and next. For 2026, no uplift if either there was taxable profit in one of the previous three periods AND both prior periods' Modelo 22/IES obligations were met as prescribed, OR 2026 is the start period or either of the next two. RETGS uses the group loss. [Art. 88(13)-(15), (21)-(22), OE 2026 art. 95(5)](https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/codigos_tributarios/CIRC_2R/Pages/irc88.aspx).
11. **SIFIDE II.** For qualifying direct R&D expenses in periods starting in 2014-2026, apply the table's base/incremental calculation, capped by IRC collection. Exclude non-repayable State-funded amounts; current art. 38(10) also bars relevant R&D financed directly/indirectly by SIFIDE investment funds or other national/international public support. No indirect-method profits; tax/social-security debts must be absent OR payment duly secured. Keep the certifying declaration OR proof of its request and the calculation/debt-status documentation. Application: end of the fifth month of the following year; earlier-year applications are not accepted. Unused credit carries to the twelfth following period. The young-SME base uplift, fund subscriptions, eco-design expenses, group calculations and project-specific expense eligibility are non-usable here until their full conditions are established: Check this point with one of our partner accountants. [CFI 35](https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/codigos_tributarios/cfi/Pages/cfi-35.aspx), [38](https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/codigos_tributarios/cfi/Pages/cfi-38.aspx), [39](https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/codigos_tributarios/cfi/Pages/cfi-39.aspx), [40](https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/codigos_tributarios/cfi/Pages/cfi-40.aspx). Do not apply the 2026 funding amendment retrospectively to the 2025 case below.
12. **Balance.** Apply permitted foreign-tax credits, tax benefits and creditable withholding in the statutory order, then payments on account against the balance. Autonomous tax cannot be reduced by those credits. Keep IRC, derramas and autonomous tax separately reconciled. Foreign-credit eligibility and RFAI are held below. [Art. 90](https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/codigos_tributarios/CIRC_2R/Pages/irc90.aspx); [104](https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/codigos_tributarios/CIRC_2R/Pages/irc104.aspx); [88(23)](https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/codigos_tributarios/CIRC_2R/Pages/irc88.aspx).

**Payments calculation.** Use the immediately prior period's art. 90(1) IRC after creditable withholding, not the total including surcharges/autonomous taxes. At prior turnover exactly EUR 500,000 the 80% share applies; above it use 95%. Divide by three and round each instalment up to a whole euro. Reference-period IRC below EUR 200 gives the exemption; equality does not. Where art. 8(4)(d)'s transition applies, annualise the reference tax. RETGS entry/exit calculations are held. [Art. 105](https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/codigos_tributarios/CIRC_2R/Pages/irc105.aspx); [104](https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/codigos_tributarios/CIRC_2R/Pages/irc104.aspx).

**Third-payment limitation.** Omit it if payments already made cover the expected tax, or limit it to the expected balance. If suspension/limitation leaves unpaid more than 20% of the normal third payment, compensatory interest runs from its due date to the return deadline or earlier self-assessment payment. Late payments on account start compensatory interest immediately, under art. 104(5). [Art. 107](https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/codigos_tributarios/CIRC_2R/Pages/irc107.aspx); [104](https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/codigos_tributarios/CIRC_2R/Pages/irc104.aspx).

**PEC.** Article 106 was repealed for periods starting on or after 1 January 2022. No 2026 PEC applies to large companies either; do not revive the old minimum, turnover formula or March/October instalments. [Art. 106](https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/codigos_tributarios/CIRC_2R/Pages/irc106.aspx).

File through the Portal das Finanças using the actual period's Modelo 22/IES instructions and retain acceptance and payment evidence. Annex A's general municipal amount goes to Q10 field 364. The old Guide's other Q/annex mappings, withholding remittance procedures and record-retention period are held below. Under RETGS the parent files the group return and each member also its individual return; group eligibility and computations remain held. [Art. 120(6)](https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/codigos_tributarios/CIRC_2R/Pages/irc120.aspx); [Annex A](https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/apoio_contribuinte/modelos_formularios/irc/Documents/M22ANA.PDF).
