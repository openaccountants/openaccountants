## The method, step by step

### Selected registration and non-assujetti rules [S2](https://www.wallis-et-futuna.gouv.fr/content/download/5383/37094/file/N%25C2%25B0%2520486%2520-%252031%2520DECEMBRE%25202018.pdf) [S5](https://www.wallis-et-futuna.gouv.fr/content/download/7300/47983/file/JOWF%20N%C2%B0%20511%20du%2015%20DECEMBRE%202019.pdf)

S2 article 1 replaces principal article 1. It requires inscription on the patente roll for a natural or legal person undertaking a profession, including temporarily, in the Territory, for their own account and for profit. 'Profession' refers to an activity under a specific code in the current INSEE nomenclature. Undeclared activities are also recognised as professions and must be entered on the roll. This does not establish the actual classification code or make registration and tax liability identical.

S2's replacement article 7 listed 'independent artists'. S5 article 1 later replaces article 7 with these 5 categories, effective from **1 January 2020**:

1. Régie Locale des Tabacs.
2. Institut d'Emission d'Outre-Mer.
3. Persons holding a professional artist card valid in the Territory.
4. Sales associated with manufacturing traditional craft objects made mainly from locally sourced raw materials, expressly including sculpture and sales of objects sculpted from local wood.
5. The first year for young business creators in the Territory who were **under 30 at constitution**.

Preserve 'not subject to the contribution' as the selected article 7 treatment. Do not turn this into an automatic registration waiver, zero return, nil assessment, perpetual relief, relief for every artisan or an exemption for every person describing themselves as independent. Failing one listed condition does not prove liability where another category or unqualified provision could apply. S5 article 2 leaves the other principal provisions unchanged by that amendment; it is not evidence that the entire regime remained unchanged thereafter.

### Artist-card conditions and limits [S2](https://www.wallis-et-futuna.gouv.fr/content/download/5383/37094/file/N%25C2%25B0%2520486%2520-%252031%2520DECEMBRE%25202018.pdf) [S4](https://www.wallis-et-futuna.gouv.fr/content/download/6975/46017/file/JOWF%20N%C2%B0%20506%20du%2015%20OCTOBRE%202019.pdf) [S5](https://www.wallis-et-futuna.gouv.fr/content/download/7300/47983/file/JOWF%20N%C2%B0%20511%20du%2015%20DECEMBRE%202019.pdf)

S4 article 1 defines an artist as a natural person who professionally creates or interprets art, as a principal or secondary activity, and contributes significantly to art and culture. A separate Prefect order on the Assembly culture commission's proposal is to specify covered artistic activities. Article 2 recognises professional status through a card and generally reserves the deliberation's support schemes to cardholders unless otherwise provided.

Article 3 requires significant artistic activity and income from it for at least **3 years**, fulfilment at application of the patente-related declaration to the contributions service referred to in article 9 and WF residence for at least **6 months**. It also requires the culture service engaging a cardholder for a school performance or before minors to request bulletin n°3 of the criminal record. That latter situation is not converted into a universal initial-card condition or an instruction for an agent to collect identity documents.

Article 4 requires a written initial or renewal request to the territorial cultural affairs service with article 3 evidence. The service issues the card following the specified commission's proposal and criteria. The complete initial application is instructed within **3 months** of deposit; further information can be sought. A favourable decision makes the card available; an adverse decision must state reasons and applicable remedies and time limits. A later Assembly deliberation is to determine card form, mentions and commission operation. Those implementing instruments, the activities order, commission establishment and current form have not been qualified.

Article 5 gives a **5-year** card validity only whilst the original conditions remain valid. Renewal is by letter **3 months before expiry**, with article 3 evidence. Article 6 preserves the contradictory-process safeguard, reasoned notification, suspension up to **3 months** when conditions cease and the stated subsequent withdrawal condition. Articles 7–8 concern the artist register and budget-dependent aid; future aid allocation and use rules are not supplied here.

Article 9 says qualifying cardholders **may** be exempt from patente subject to the principal's exemption conditions. Article 10 makes applications receivable from establishment of the article 4 commission. Article 11 ties application to entry into force of the approval order. Read these conditions alongside S5's later replacement article 7. Neither provision proves that a particular person has a card, an operative exemption or fulfilled declaration duties.

### Administration from 1 January 2025 [S6](https://www.wallis-et-futuna.gouv.fr/contenu/telechargement/14335/117826/file/JOWF%20N%C2%B0%20703%20du%2031%20d%C3%A9cembre%202024.pdf)

S6 délibération 81/AT/2024 article 1 replaces article 2 point 2 of 128/AT/2022. The Cellule Gestion des entreprises of the economic affairs, development and tourism service receives management of the patente contribution and the *centime additionnel*, amongst its listed functions. Article 2 expressly takes effect **1 January 2025**.

Délibération 82/AT/2024 creates the customs-attached bureau des contributions diverses. Its article 2 lists 'essential activities' for the tax on companies without activity, tobacco revenue, vehicle registration, insurance-contract tax and financial-operations tax. Article 3 repeals 23/CP/97 of 14 February 1997; article 4 expressly takes effect **1 January 2025**. Its recital also describes the transfer of patente management to economic affairs. An essential-activities list is not an exhaustive exclusion of every other customs function.

These selected management provisions do not prove the amount of any charge or identify an accepted filing/payment channel. Do not equate the *centime additionnel* label with a proved 30% charging provision. S7 still directs patente declarations and register entry to the contributions service. Preserve that administrative-page wording and the S6 transfer as a routing issue requiring current confirmation. Do not select a filing destination or bank/payment instruction from the old contact list.

