# Wallis and Futuna (WF): tax pack

**Built from the law. Not yet approved. Written by Ryan Duguid. Not reviewed by an accountant.** Wallis and Futuna is a French overseas collectivity. Its taxes are set by deliberations of the Territorial Assembly, approved by arrêté and published in the Journal officiel de Wallis et Futuna (JOWF) on the prefecture's site, wallis-et-futuna.gouv.fr. Currency is the CFP franc (XPF). The main business tax covered so far is the patente. Tax year 2026.
