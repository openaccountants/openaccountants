# Niger VAT (TVA) v0.2

> **General reference only.** This skill is general tax/accounting reference material for AI-assisted workflows. It has not been reviewed for any specific person's facts, documents, elections, deadlines, residency, filing status, or local procedures. Do not rely on it to file, pay, amend, or take a tax position without review by a qualified professional in the relevant jurisdiction.

**Tax year 2026.** Niger's VAT sits in Book 2, Title 1 of the Code Général des Impôts (CGI). The 2026 finance ordinance (Ordonnance 2025-44) replaced the exemption article (art. 322) and the deduction-exclusion article (art. 339) "A compter du 1er janvier 2026". Its first rectification (Ordonnance 2026-45, signed 21 August 2026) replaced art. 322 again "A compter de la signature". Use the list for the date of the operation.

## Who this applies to

Businesses making operations within the scope of Niger VAT, and their advisers, who need to screen exempt operations and the listed input-VAT exclusions. Passing an exclusion screen does not establish deduction entitlement, timing or the deductible amount. The CGI ties VAT to the UEMOA framework: Ordonnance 2025-44 refers to "la directive communautaire portant harmonisation des législations des Etats membres en matière de taxe sur la valeur ajoutée" [CGI art. 23(3), PDF page 5](https://finances.gouv.ne/index.php/lois-de-finances/file/1303-ordonnance-n-2025-44). Who must register, the turnover threshold and the rate were not in the texts read and are held.

## The base

### Exempt operations from 1 January 2026 (CGI art. 322 as replaced by Ordonnance 2025-44 art. 13, PDF pages 11 to 14)

1. Operations of farmers, livestock breeders and fishermen in the normal course of their activity.
2. Imports and sales of the products in the art. 322 tariff table (NTS codes), including: milk and cream not concentrated or sweetened; seed potatoes and potatoes; roots and tubers of heading 07 14; cereals (maize, millet, sorghum, fonio, wheat, rice other than luxury rice); cassava flour, gari and semolina; infant food preparations for retail sale; ethyl alcohol for medical or pharmaceutical use; salt; coal gas; lamp kerosene; butane; provitamins and vitamins; quinine; antibiotics; pharmaceutical products of chapter 30; X-ray plates and film; specified fungicides; rubber hygiene and pharmacy articles; surgical gloves; school and scientific books; newspapers and periodicals; unused postage and revenue stamps; parts of hand pumps, other pumps and liquid elevators; medical and laboratory sterilisers; specified sprayer parts; single-axle tractors; wheelchairs and their parts; microscopes; medical, surgical and mechanotherapy apparatus; breathing apparatus; orthopaedic articles; medical X-ray and radiation apparatus; medical and veterinary furniture. Check the exact NTS line in the table before relying on it.
3. Domestic sales and resales of butcher's meat, poultry offal, fruit and vegetables.

5. Receipts of school, university, technical and vocational education establishments.

7. Direct exports of goods and services, and re-exports following a suspensive customs regime.

### Changes from 21 August 2026 (CGI art. 322 as replaced by Ordonnance 2026-45 art. 7, scan pages 5 to 8)

- Item 27: operations of insurance brokers approved by the Minister of Finance, in the normal course of their activity.
- Item 28: portfolio management commissions received by general insurance agents approved by the Minister of Finance.
- Item 29: domestic sales and resales of raw gold intended for export by approved gold-trading legal persons.
- Item 30: imports of raw gold intended for re-export.

### Input VAT that may not be deducted from 1 January 2026 (CGI art. 339, PDF pages 14 to 15)

VAT on these items is excluded from deduction:

a. Goods or services used by third parties, managers or staff, such as housing, lodging, reception, restaurant and entertainment costs, and any expense linked directly or indirectly to travel or residence. This does not cover work or protective clothing, or free housing of staff specially responsible for guarding the workplace.
b. Vehicles and machines designed or fitted to carry people or for mixed use that are fixed assets, or otherwise are not for resale new, with their components, spare parts and accessories.
c. Goods given or services rendered free or below cost, unless low-value advertising items with a unit price of XOF 20,000 or less excluding VAT.
d. Movable items other than commercial equipment or office equipment.
e. Exports of goods and services that are not taxable domestically.
f. Operations of any kind on goods or services excluded from deduction.
g. Purchases of XOF 2,000,000 or more not settled by bank.

The general right to deduct, its timing and the pro-rata rule for mixed taxable and exempt businesses are in CGI articles not read: they are held.

## Cross-border points

- Direct exports of goods and services, and re-exports after a suspensive customs regime, are in the art. 322 exemption list (item 7). Art. 339(e) excludes from deduction only exports of goods and services **not taxable domestically**. How an exporter recovers input VAT and claims refunds was not read and is held.
- Aircraft provisioning, air transport and aircraft work for mainly international airlines are exempt (items 8 to 10).
- The CGI applies subject to ratified international conventions [CGI preamble art. 1(2), PDF page 3](https://finances.gouv.ne/index.php/lois-de-finances/file/1303-ordonnance-n-2025-44).
- VAT on imported services (reverse charge) was not read and is held.

## Two worked examples that recompute

**1. Input VAT screen.** In October 2026 a business holds four purchase invoices, each with the VAT amount shown on it (amounts taken as documented; no rate is assumed): office equipment, XOF 3,000,000 excluding VAT, paid by bank transfer, VAT XOF 540,000; goods for resale, XOF 2,500,000, paid in cash, VAT XOF 450,000; a passenger car used as a fixed asset, VAT XOF 1,800,000; staff restaurant meals, VAT XOF 90,000. The cash purchase is excluded by art. 339(g), the car by 339(b) and the meals by 339(a): excluded VAT is XOF 2,340,000. The office-equipment VAT of XOF 540,000 survives these specified exclusion screens on the stated facts. It is a potential amount only. General entitlement, timing and any pro-rata remain unconfirmed, so no right to deduct or deductible total is established.

```oa-check
name: Niger 2026 input VAT exclusions under CGI art. 339
inputs:
  vat_office_equipment_bank: 540000
  vat_cash_purchase_over_limit: 450000
  vat_passenger_car: 1800000
  vat_staff_meals: 90000
steps:
  potential_after_listed_exclusions: vat_office_equipment_bank
  excluded: vat_cash_purchase_over_limit + vat_passenger_car + vat_staff_meals
  total_documented: potential_after_listed_exclusions + excluded
expected:
  potential_after_listed_exclusions: 540000
  excluded: 2340000
  total_documented: 2880000
```

**2. Advertising gifts at the XOF 20,000 boundary.** A business gives away 100 branded items at XOF 20,000 each excluding VAT (documented VAT XOF 3,600 each) and 50 items at XOF 20,500 each (documented VAT XOF 3,690 each). The first lot falls within the low-value advertising exception to art. 339(c); the dearer items do not. VAT surviving only this gift-exclusion screen is XOF 360,000; VAT excluded by the gift rule is XOF 184,500. The first lot totals XOF 2,000,000 excluding VAT and XOF 2,360,000 including documented VAT (both totals derived). Its payment method was not supplied: the bank-payment condition in art. 339(g) remains unresolved under either threshold basis. Decision: the unit-price limit answers only the gift-exclusion question. General entitlement, timing, pro-rata and the payment condition remain unconfirmed, so no right to deduct or deductible total is established.

```oa-check
name: Niger 2026 advertising gifts, unit price limit XOF 20,000
inputs:
  qty_at_limit: 100
  vat_each_at_limit: 3600
  qty_over_limit: 50
  vat_each_over_limit: 3690
steps:
  potential_after_gift_exclusion: qty_at_limit * vat_each_at_limit
  excluded: qty_over_limit * vat_each_over_limit
expected:
  potential_after_gift_exclusion: 360000
  excluded: 184500
```

## Ask the client first

- What exactly is supplied (with the NTS tariff line for goods), to whom, and on what date (before or from 21 August 2026)?
- Is the supply an export, a re-export under a suspensive regime, or aviation-related?
- For each purchase of XOF 2,000,000 or more: was it paid by bank?
- Is the purchase used by staff or managers, a passenger or mixed-use vehicle, a gift, or furniture that is not office or commercial equipment?
- Is the business registered, and since when did it start activity?
- Does it make both taxable and exempt supplies?
