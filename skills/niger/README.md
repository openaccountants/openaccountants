## Additional source-backed drafts

- [Cash-payment tax working paper](ne-cash-payment-tax.md): ordinary buyer-chosen cash payments involving a real-regime enterprise from **21 August 2026**. The signed rectification changes the bearer/invoicing rule. Supplier-requested cash withholding is identified but its calculation/base interaction and invoicing mechanics remain outside the completed computation. Current form, portal workflow, VAT sanctions and earlier periods are unresolved.
- [Money-transfer tax repeal review](ne-money-transfer-tax-repeal.md): signed Ordonnance 2026-45 article 9 repeals CGI 394 bis–nonies from **21 August 2026**. The old 0.5%/5% figures are historical only, never current rates. The job separates former tax from operator fees and prepares charge-review evidence. Initial commencement, straddling transactions, historic filing periods, continuing liabilities and refund/complaint procedure remain unresolved.

The ministry's original 2026 law and signed first rectification were downloaded through their public forms on the valid non-www HTTPS host. The DGI CGI2025 host had an expired certificate and the www ministry host a certificate-name mismatch; neither warning was bypassed. No rule is attributed to the unread CGI. The two new jobs are confined to the tax provisions actually read; this is not a complete 2026 code consolidation or verification of the legacy Guides.
