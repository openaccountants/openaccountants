# North Macedonia: 2026 tax pack

North Macedonia uses the calendar year and Macedonian denar (MKD). The Public Revenue Office (UJP) administers the taxes below. All seven computational skills are **Checked against the law** (tier 2): written from primary sources on 24 October 2026 and independently reviewed on 30 October 2026. No named licensed accountant has signed off. The cross-border domain retains the specific exit gap above; its presence is not complete coverage. Self-employed and standalone director groups have different fund coverage. Use the individual skills, including their dated rate tables and limitations, for calculation.

## Scope and follow-ups

The seven skills above are the tax computation routes. `mk-tax-overview` is a
supporting router, while `mk-company-formation` and
`north-macedonia-tax-optimization` support ordinary entity choice and regime
comparison. All three are refreshed for 2026 and use primary sources. They do
not override the category computations or settle the core gaps above. Existing
platform metadata and complete CTA tails remain unchanged; inherited CTA wording
is not a professional-review claim.

The July lump-sum administration gap is resolved by UJP's 22 September 2026
implementation notice: own-proprietor MPIN is UJP-generated from July periods,
with a rule preventing duplicate payment of July/August calculations already
filed and paid by the person. Employee MPIN remains the proprietor's obligation.


Business-loss treatment follows the signed negative DLD-DB field 22 into the
current DLD-GDP annual reconciliation; it does not change salary withholding or
authorise prior-year PIT carry relief. Ordinary employee floors and ceilings use
UJP's hours method with 2026 money parameters. First-calendar-year professionals
use the specific half-reference starter route, corroborated by current UJP MPIN
code 060, before the established-professional minimum applies. Each computation
and its exact primary sources are in the corresponding skill.

Special regulated-sector/state-aid regimes, minimum global top-up tax, gambling/unexplained-wealth assessments, specialist non-working insurance categories, VAT special schemes and product-level rate decisions, comprehensive treaty-rate tables and treaty-specific MAP limits are outside this ordinary pack. They do not establish an exemption or zero liability. The explicit cross-border gap is distinct from these specialist exclusions. Departure cases require further primary-law resolution before a final exit/deemed-disposal calculation; no nil charge is asserted.
