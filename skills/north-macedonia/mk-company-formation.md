# North Macedonia entity choice and tax handover v0.2

## Scope

For 2026, establish the actual entity and activity before using a tax rate.
[Invest North Macedonia's registration guidance](https://investnorthmacedonia.gov.mk/registering-a-company/) identifies ordinary LLC,
joint-stock, partnership and sole-trader routes through the
Central Registry. A sole trader's business income follows PIT Law arts. 19–30;
a resident legal person's profits follow Profit Tax Law arts. 4–19. Registration
is not proof that all sector permissions or a tax exemption exist.

## Ask the client first

- What exact activity, regulated sector and operating location are proposed?
- Who are the legal/beneficial owners, managers and actual income earners?
- What cash/in-kind capital and ownership arrangements will be registered?
- Will there be employees, an uninsured director, foreign contracts or related parties?
- Are a simplified form, incentive regime, branch or cross-border reorganisation
  being considered, requiring separate statutory conditions?

### Ordinary LLC capital and evidence

The ordinary LLC (**DOO**, or **DOOEL** for a single member) has 1-50
members under [Trade Companies Law art. 167](https://www.sec.gov.mk/Files/Attachments/%D0%97%D0%B0%D0%BA%D0%BE%D0%BD%20%D0%B7%D0%B0%20%D1%82%D1%80%D0%B3%D0%BE%D0%B2%D1%81%D0%BA%D0%B8%20%D0%B4%D1%80%D1%83%D1%88%D1%82%D0%B2%D0%B0%20-%20%D0%BD%D0%B5%D0%BE%D1%84%D0%B8%D1%86%D0%B8%D1%98%D0%B0%D0%BB%D0%B5%D0%BD%20%D0%BF%D1%80%D0%B5%D1%87%D0%B8%D1%81%D1%82%D0%B5%D0%BD%20%D1%82%D0%B5%D0%BA%D1%81%D1%82%202021.pdf). Art. 172 prescribes at least
**EUR 5,000** capital in MKD equivalent at the National Bank middle rate on payment day, unless the founders agree to use the day the company agreement/founding declaration is signed. The MKD capital must be an integer divisible by 100 (art. 172(2)). This is ordinary LLC capital, not a registration fee. The
[current investment authority guidance](https://investnorthmacedonia.gov.mk/registering-a-company/) corroborates that ordinary route.
Do not apply it as the capital requirement for every regulated or simplified
company form. A joint-stock company (**AD**) needs at least **EUR 25,000** capital
when founded simultaneously, or **EUR 50,000** when founded successively through
a public subscription, unless another law sets a different minimum; each share has a nominal value of at least **EUR 1** (Trade Companies Law art. 273). For AD capital and share values, use the National Bank middle rate published on the day before the statute is adopted, or before the capital-change decision. Do not import the LLC exchange-date rule.

Prepare the constitution and founding member statement/agreement, owner identity
or corporate-status evidence, local registered seat, activity, managers and
representation, and the capital/contribution evidence appropriate to the actual
form. Trade Companies Law arts. 170–175 address formation, constitutional content
and contributions. Keep capital promised, capital paid and ownership interests
separate in the records. This skill makes no blanket deferred-capital payment
promise; establish the operative requirement for the actual chosen route.

### Registration and operating handover

The official registration guidance describes the Central Registry's one-stop
system and electronic registration. Retain the registry decision, tax identifier,
constitution, current ownership/manager data and any activity-specific permission.
The guidance identifies regulated sectors needing separate permits. Do not treat
a quoted service estimate as a statutory registration deadline or a guarantee.
This skill does not state a fee or universal incorporation turnaround.

Before the first transaction or engagement, hand over:

- The actual taxpayer and form to [corporate profit tax](mk-corporate-income-tax.md)
  or [individual business income tax](north-macedonia-income-tax.md), including
  books, first-year advances and annual balances.
- Expected supplies, imports and foreign services to
  [VAT](north-macedonia-vat.md); incorporation itself is not the VAT-registration test.
- Employees, self-employed principals and directors to
  [social contributions](north-macedonia-social-contributions.md) and
  [payroll](north-macedonia-payroll.md), with their actual insured status.
- Related parties and foreign contracts to
  [transfer pricing](north-macedonia-transfer-pricing.md) and
  [cross-border tax](north-macedonia-cross-border.md), including WHT and treaty paperwork.

Record each regime's registration/form/deadline independently. The current
computational skills govern the numbers; an incorporation summary does not
replace them or settle their expressly unconfirmed cases.

## Worked cases

**Ordinary one-member choice.** The owner requests an ordinary DOOEL and proposes EUR 4,000 subscribed capital. Art. 172 requires EUR 5,000 equivalent, so the proposal needs EUR 1,000 more commitment. This is a subscribed-capital test, not a statement that every ordinary contribution must already have been paid. Convert at the actual statutory exchange date and check denomination before application; no illustrative market exchange rate is assumed.

```oa-check
name: MK ordinary DOOEL capital commitment gap
inputs:
  proposed_capital_eur: 4000
steps:
  - minimum_eur: 5000
  - commitment_gap_eur: max(0, minimum_eur - proposed_capital_eur)
expected:
  minimum_eur: 5000
  commitment_gap_eur: 1000
```

**AD choice with a public-subscription boundary.** Proposed nominal capital is EUR 30,000. It exceeds the ordinary simultaneous minimum by EUR 5,000, but a successive public-subscription foundation needs another EUR 20,000. The actual fundraising method decides the applicable route; passing the private simultaneous capital test does not authorise a public offer. Other legal/permit conditions still apply.

```oa-check
name: MK AD simultaneous versus successive capital
inputs:
  proposed_capital_eur: 30000
steps:
  - simultaneous_buffer_eur: proposed_capital_eur - 25000
  - successive_gap_eur: max(0, 50000 - proposed_capital_eur)
expected:
  simultaneous_buffer_eur: 5000
  successive_gap_eur: 20000
```

## When to refuse or refer

Do not select a form from its headline tax rate, guarantee an incorporation date or invent a registry fee. Refer a simplified or regulated form, state-owned governance, in-kind valuation dispute, capital conversion or cross-border reorganisation for its actual operative conditions. Missing owner/manager identity, contribution evidence, permissions or a defensible tax/insurance classification prevents a completed handover.

## Filing and payment

Use the Central Registry one-stop/e-registration route and retain the decision, constitution, current owners/managers and tax identifier. The investment authority describes an electronic-certificate, documentation and application sequence; its service estimate is not a statutory deadline. Establish current fees and signing/upload requirements for the actual application. Hand over each tax registration, form and payment date separately to the linked computational skills. A capital contribution is not a registration fee or a tax payment. The same official guidance states that annual accounts go to the Central Register by the end of February of the following year, or by 15 March if submitted electronically, and that medium and large entities file electronically; the [corporate skill](mk-corporate-income-tax.md) ties the profit-tax return to that deadline.

## Completion checklist

- Actual legal form, activity, owners and manager match the proposed filing.
- Ordinary/special capital, exchange date, subscribed versus paid amounts and ownership reconcile.
- Registry and activity permissions are retained; no promised turnaround or unsupported fee appears.
- PIT or company/PE tax, VAT, payroll/insurance, TP and cross-border handovers are separately assigned.
- Every unresolved specialist requirement is identified before the recommendation is acted on.
