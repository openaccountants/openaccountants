# North Macedonia tax planning and regime comparison v0.4

## Scope

This is 2026 supporting planning material, not a replacement for the seven
computational tax skills. Compare the taxpayer's eligible routes using the same
receipts, costs, insurance, distributions and compliance period. State the
actual UJP decision/election and the assumptions that change the result. Do not
recommend a route merely from the ordinary 10% headline rate.

## Ask the client first

- What activity, staffing, investment by others and prior-year net income determine eligibility?
- Which actual expenses, insurance status and UJP decisions support each route?
- How much cash will remain in the business or be paid as genuine salary/dividend, and what years produced the retained earnings?
- Which assets will be bought, used and retained, and are disposal plans already known?
- Are there losses, a first-year professional activity or reduced hours changing
  the computation, or a foreign move requiring the exit gap to be settled?

### Individual business: actual costs or assessed lump sum

[PIT arts. 19–29](https://www.ujp.gov.mk/files/attachment/0000/1466/ZAKON_ZA_DANOKOT_NA_LICEN_DOHOD_-_Azuriran_so_Sl.vesnik____274___od_20.12.2022.pdf) separate actual business income from assessed lump-sum
net income. Under art. 29, the applicant must be unable to maintain books in the
specified circumstances, or books must significantly hinder the activity. UJP
sets the base by decision, considering premises, equipment, market conditions,
capacity, comparable income and other statutory facts. It is not a self-selected
deduction percentage.

Art. 29(4) excludes trading, catering and commission activity except the specified
organised green-market sales; a business employing more than 1 person besides
the proprietor; investment by other persons; and prior-year **net income** above
2 annual average gross wages under the statutory reference. Do not replace
that net-income test with a guessed sales threshold or a salary-insurance
January reference. An eligible applicant files annually by the preceding year's
end. The scanned art. 29(3) contains the words "во рок од 15 дена или од денот на регистрација". UJP's [DLD-P instructions, page 2](https://www.ujp.gov.mk/files/attachment/0000/1166/89-19-DLD-P_07.05.2019.pdf#page=2) clarify the filing route as within 15 days from registration in the relevant register or before starting the activity. Keep the alternative filing routes; do not replace them with a requirement to meet both deadlines. A person carrying on a temporary activity applies before it starts, using the form's temporary-activity field. Until UJP
sets the assessed income, the taxpayer pays advances at the level of the prior-year
decision, and UJP decides by 31 January after the application year or by the end of
the month after a new activity starts ([PIT arts. 95, 99(3)](https://www.ujp.gov.mk/files/attachment/0000/1466/ZAKON_ZA_DANOKOT_NA_LICEN_DOHOD_-_Azuriran_so_Sl.vesnik____274___od_20.12.2022.pdf#page=30)). If UJP finds
the conditions have ceased, it requires books from the next year (art. 29(5)).
Obtain the current reference and UJP decision before selecting this route.

Actual-income business uses accounting receipts/costs and the art. 21–28 tax
reconciliation. The [individual skill](north-macedonia-income-tax.md) supplies
expense limits, depreciation, own compulsory contributions, investment relief
and the actual business-loss limitation. Lump-sum assessment does not eliminate
insurance: [Contributions Law arts. 13–16](https://www.ujp.gov.mk/files/attachment/0000/1616/Precisten_tekst_na_ZPZSO_07_2026______________.pdf) still apply, including the
July administrator change and the applicable first-year or established-professional rule.

### Company regime and distributions

[Profit Tax Law arts. 31–35](https://www.ujp.gov.mk/files/attachment/0000/0965/Zakon_za_danok_na_dobivka_199_2023.pdf) has its own eligible small-company regime:
the annual-revenue exemption/election tests, excluded activities, 1% turnover
election and required period are in the [corporate skill](mk-corporate-income-tax.md).
They are not the individual's art. 29 lump-sum rules. Compare total-revenue tax
with ordinary profit tax only after verifying eligibility and the election.

Company profit tax and a shareholder's receipt are separate charges. Individual
dividends use [PIT arts. 55–57 and 11](https://www.ujp.gov.mk/files/attachment/0000/1466/ZAKON_ZA_DANOKOT_NA_LICEN_DOHOD_-_Azuriran_so_Sl.vesnik____274___od_20.12.2022.pdf); salary uses actual employment,
benefits, insurance and the salary-only reduction under arts. 10 and 14–17.
The 2026 published salary reduction is MKD 10,932 monthly under the
[UJP publication](https://www.ujp.gov.mk/e/plakjanje/licno_osloboduvanje).
It is not a sole-trader, rent or dividend deduction. Determine whether a director
is employed or belongs to the separate uninsured-director insurance class before
comparing extraction. No universal salary/dividend split is recommended.

For the company route, arts. 31-36 exclude banking, finance, insurance and gambling/amusement activities; use total revenue from all sources and retain the election. Exceeding eligibility or abusive related-party arrangements can remove the company from the turnover register, with ordinary tax recomputed and turnover tax paid credited. The 3-year consistency rule applies while revenue remains within the election band. Exempt small-company annual revenue does not remove the filing duty. [Arts. 31-36](https://www.ujp.gov.mk/files/attachment/0000/0965/Zakon_za_danok_na_dobivka_199_2023.pdf#page=11).

### Investment relief and retention

For individual expansion investment, [PIT art. 28](https://www.ujp.gov.mk/files/attachment/0000/1466/ZAKON_ZA_DANOKOT_NA_LICEN_DOHOD_-_Azuriran_so_Sl.vesnik____274___od_20.12.2022.pdf) gives 30% of qualifying
investment, capped at 50% of that period's base; specified activity vehicles have
their separate rule. Retain assets for 5 years starting with the investment year and keep eligible asset/use
and acquisition evidence; disposal or failure of the statutory purpose triggers
recapture and interest. Art. 28 states no carry-forward for the ordinary
relief, so do not carry it to a later year merely because the cap restricts current use. Fiscal-device credit has a separate
approval/carry rule and must not be confused with investment-base relief.

Company reinvested-profit relief instead follows [Profit Tax Law art. 28](https://www.ujp.gov.mk/files/attachment/0000/0965/Zakon_za_danok_na_dobivka_199_2023.pdf),
including its own eligibility, retention and recapture conditions, described in
the corporate skill. Disposal within 5 years of the investment (in words on the page), or ending a
finance lease, makes the forgone tax due on form DD-DO within 30 days; failing to
prove the qualifying investment, or to determine and pay the recapture tax under art. 39(2), makes it due
at 5 times the forgone tax (in words on the page) (arts. 28(3)-(6), 39(2)). Do not transfer an individual deduction into a company
calculation or assume that buying a private-use asset creates relief.

### Identify the retained-profit vintage

Before comparing a company distribution with salary or independent-business
income, identify the year the retained profit was earned. [Profit Tax Law art. 48](https://www.ujp.gov.mk/files/attachment/0000/0965/Zakon_za_danok_na_dobivka_199_2023.pdf) separately charges distributions of accumulated **2009-2013**
profits through **DD-ID**. Keep that transitional company charge separate from
the dividend recipient's PIT/withholding; do not assume every retained-profit
balance has already borne the current profit-tax system.

## Boundary and exception table

| Fact | Treatment |
|---|---|
| UJP has not assessed the individual's lump-sum base | Do not select a guessed deduction or sales percentage; obtain the decision and current statutory wage reference. |
| Specialist services, employees or outside investment affect eligibility | Apply all art. 29 conditions; do not replace prior net income with turnover or the January insurance reference. |
| Loss, reduced hours, first calendar year or uninsured director | Use the dedicated income/social/payroll route; the tax comparison does not create a contribution exemption or a loss carryforward. |
| Investment is private/administrative, an ordinary passenger car, or will be disposed of | Check exact qualifying use and the separate activity-vehicle exception; compute recapture before presenting a saving. |
| Ordinary individual investment cap prevents full use | No carry of unused ordinary investment-base relief is stated; fiscal-device tax credit has a separate approval/carry rule. |
| Company profit versus shareholder receipt | Separate company tax, genuine salary/insurance and dividend PIT; identify 2009-2013 earnings and DD-ID before assuming prior corporate tax was paid. |
| Foreign move, incentive zone or related-party payment | Preserve the exit gap and obtain specific treaty/incentive law; apply genuine substance and TP rather than choosing a label. |

## Worked cases

**Decision: eligible company turnover election versus ordinary profit.** Total revenue is MKD 5,000,000 and ordinary reconciled taxable profit MKD 800,000. Assume the actual company satisfies all arts. 31-33 gates and can validly elect for the required period, with no additional credits/losses/reliefs. Whole-revenue 1% tax is MKD 50,000; ordinary 10% profit tax is MKD 80,000. The MKD 30,000 difference is a company-tax-only comparison (derived), not a recommendation: retain election history and separately include shareholder tax, insurance and compliance before advising. A company already bound to a different election history cannot use this as permission to switch.

```oa-check
name: MK eligible company turnover versus ordinary decision
inputs:
  total_revenue: 5000000
  ordinary_taxable_profit: 800000
steps:
  - turnover_tax: total_revenue * 0.01
  - ordinary_profit_tax: ordinary_taxable_profit * 0.10
  - company_tax_difference: ordinary_profit_tax - turnover_tax
expected:
  turnover_tax: 50000
  ordinary_profit_tax: 80000
  company_tax_difference: 30000
```

**Edge: individual expansion relief hits the cap.** Actual-business adjusted base before relief is MKD 800,000, already including admissible own insurance and all required add-backs. Qualifying ordinary expansion investment is MKD 2,000,000 and the retention/use conditions are satisfied. Potential 30% relief is MKD 600,000, but the 50% base cap permits MKD 400,000. Taxable base is MKD 400,000 and PIT MKD 40,000, rather than MKD 80,000 without relief. The unused MKD 200,000 potential relief is not carried as an ordinary investment deduction; this example is not the separate vehicle or fiscal-device route.

```oa-check
name: MK individual ordinary investment cap decision
inputs:
  adjusted_base: 800000
  qualifying_investment: 2000000
steps:
  - potential_relief: qualifying_investment * 0.30
  - relief: min(potential_relief, adjusted_base * 0.50)
  - taxable_base: adjusted_base - relief
  - tax_with_relief: taxable_base * 0.10
  - tax_without_relief: adjusted_base * 0.10
  - unused_potential: potential_relief - relief
expected:
  potential_relief: 600000
  relief: 400000
  taxable_base: 400000
  tax_with_relief: 40000
  tax_without_relief: 80000
  unused_potential: 200000
```

## When to refuse or refer

Do not promise a saving, sole-trader insurance exemption, loss relief or treaty outcome from a headline rate. Refuse an election/assessment comparison without eligibility, actual costs, ownership, insured status and the decision/election evidence. Refer a specialist vehicle/incentive, private-use or disputed-retention case to its operative conditions before treating relief as available. No universal salary/dividend split is recommended.

## Filing and payment

Keep the individual's annual lump-sum application/decision separate from company DB-VP and ordinary DB. A turnover-regime company files DB-VP in the annual-account period and pays within 30 days after that filing deadline, with no monthly advances ([Profit Tax Law arts. 39(3), 40(4)](https://www.ujp.gov.mk/files/attachment/0000/0965/Zakon_za_danok_na_dobivka_199_2023.pdf#page=12)). Link the chosen route's actual return, instalment, withholding, contribution and payment calendar. UJP's [22 September 2026 notice](https://ujp.gov.mk/mk/javnost/soopstenija/pogledni/1284?print=1) confirms that, from July periods, UJP calculates the lump-sum proprietor's own MPIN, the proprietor still pays, and employee MPIN remains the proprietor's duty. Reconcile already filed/paid July-August calculations before paying a generated duplicate; a change in administrator is not exemption from insurance. The notice states that the contribution base is the monthly advance of the assessed net income, so the PIT and contribution bases are identical, and that a higher base may be requested from UJP. Investment disposal, failed purpose and retained-profit distribution need their own tax forms/payments under the linked computational skill; the comparison itself is not a return.

## Completion checklist

- Match the activity, legal/beneficial owners, current registration and actual
  employment/insured status to the chosen law.
- Retain the election or UJP decision and use the correct prior-year measure.
- Reconcile all taxes and insurance, not just the first income-tax percentage.
- Check distributions, actual business costs, asset use and retention; do not
  invent a deduction or loss offset to make a route appear cheaper.
- Price related-party transactions and genuine remuneration under the
  [TP skill](north-macedonia-transfer-pricing.md). A payment label is not evidence
  of business substance or deductibility.
