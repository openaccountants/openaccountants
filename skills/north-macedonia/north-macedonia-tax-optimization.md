# North Macedonia tax planning and regime comparison v0.2

## Scope and required output

This is 2026 supporting planning material, not a replacement for the seven
computational tax skills. Compare the taxpayer's eligible routes using the same
receipts, costs, insurance, distributions and compliance period. State the
actual UJP decision/election and the assumptions that change the result. Do not
recommend a route merely from the ordinary 10% headline rate.

## Individual business: actual costs or assessed lump sum

[PIT arts. 19–29](https://www.ujp.gov.mk/files/attachment/0000/1466/ZAKON_ZA_DANOKOT_NA_LICEN_DOHOD_-_Azuriran_so_Sl.vesnik____274___od_20.12.2022.pdf) separate actual business income from assessed lump-sum
net income. Under art. 29, the applicant must be unable to maintain books in the
specified circumstances, or books must significantly hinder the activity. UJP
sets the base by decision, considering premises, equipment, market conditions,
capacity, comparable income and other statutory facts. It is not a self-selected
deduction percentage.

Art. 29(4) excludes trading, catering and commission activity except the specified
organised green-market sales; a business employing more than one person besides
the proprietor; investment by other persons; and prior-year **net income** above
two annual average gross wages under the statutory reference. Do not replace
that net-income test with a guessed sales threshold or a salary-insurance
January reference. An eligible applicant files annually by the preceding year's
end, or within the statutory 15-day registration/start route: art. 29(3).
Obtain the current reference and UJP decision before selecting this route.

Actual-income business uses accounting receipts/costs and the art. 21–28 tax
reconciliation. The [individual skill](north-macedonia-income-tax.md) supplies
expense limits, depreciation, own compulsory contributions, investment relief
and the actual business-loss limitation. Lump-sum assessment does not eliminate
insurance: [Contributions Law arts. 13–16](https://www.ujp.gov.mk/files/attachment/0000/1616/Precisten_tekst_na_ZPZSO_07_2026______________.pdf) still apply, including the
July administrator change and the applicable first-year or established-professional rule.

## Company regime and distributions

[Profit Tax Law arts. 31–35](https://www.ujp.gov.mk/files/attachment/0000/0965/Zakon_za_danok_na_dobivka_199_2023.pdf) has its own eligible small-company regime:
the annual-revenue exemption/election tests, excluded activities, 1% turnover
election and required period are in the [corporate skill](mk-corporate-income-tax.md).
They are not the individual's art. 29 lump-sum rules. Compare total-revenue tax
with ordinary profit tax only after verifying eligibility and the election.

Company profit tax and a shareholder's receipt are separate charges. Individual
dividends use [PIT arts. 55–57 and 11](https://www.ujp.gov.mk/files/attachment/0000/1466/ZAKON_ZA_DANOKOT_NA_LICEN_DOHOD_-_Azuriran_so_Sl.vesnik____274___od_20.12.2022.pdf); salary uses actual employment,
benefits, insurance and the salary-only reduction under arts. 10 and 14–17.
The 2026 published salary reduction is MKD 10,932 monthly under the
[UJP publication](https://www.ujp.gov.mk/e/plakjanje/licno_osloboduvanje).
It is not a sole-trader, rent or dividend deduction. Determine whether a director
is employed or belongs to the separate uninsured-director insurance class before
comparing extraction. No universal salary/dividend split is recommended.

## Investment relief and retention

For individual expansion investment, [PIT art. 28](https://www.ujp.gov.mk/files/attachment/0000/1466/ZAKON_ZA_DANOKOT_NA_LICEN_DOHOD_-_Azuriran_so_Sl.vesnik____274___od_20.12.2022.pdf) gives 30% of qualifying
investment, capped at 50% of that period's base; specified activity vehicles have
their separate rule. Retain assets for five years and keep eligible asset/use
and acquisition evidence; disposal or failure of the statutory purpose triggers
recapture and interest. The ordinary relief cannot be carried to a later year
merely because the cap restricts current use. Fiscal-device credit has a separate
approval/carry rule and must not be confused with investment-base relief.

Company reinvested-profit relief instead follows [Profit Tax Law art. 28](https://www.ujp.gov.mk/files/attachment/0000/0965/Zakon_za_danok_na_dobivka_199_2023.pdf),
including its own eligibility, retention and recapture conditions, described in
the corporate skill. Do not transfer an individual deduction into a company
calculation or assume that buying a private-use asset creates relief.

## Checks before presenting a comparison

- Match the activity, legal/beneficial owners, current registration and actual
  employment/insured status to the chosen law.
- Retain the election or UJP decision and use the correct prior-year measure.
- Reconcile all taxes and insurance, not just the first income-tax percentage.
- Check distributions, actual business costs, asset use and retention; do not
  invent a deduction or loss offset to make a route appear cheaper.
- Price related-party transactions and genuine remuneration under the
  [TP skill](north-macedonia-transfer-pricing.md). A payment label is not evidence
  of business substance or deductibility.

## Ask the client first

- What activity, staffing, investment by others and prior-year net income determine eligibility?
- Which actual expenses, insurance status and UJP decisions support each route?
- How much cash will remain in the business or be paid as genuine salary/dividend, and what years produced the retained earnings?
- Which assets will be bought, used and retained, and are disposal plans already known?
- Are there losses, a first-year professional activity or reduced hours changing
  the computation, or a foreign move requiring the exit gap to be settled?

Before comparing a company distribution with salary or independent-business
income, identify the year the retained profit was earned. [Profit Tax Law art. 48](https://www.ujp.gov.mk/files/attachment/0000/0965/Zakon_za_danok_na_dobivka_199_2023.pdf) separately charges distributions of accumulated **2009-2013**
profits through **DD-ID**. Keep that transitional company charge separate from
the dividend recipient's PIT/withholding; do not assume every retained-profit
balance has already borne the current profit-tax system.
