# Niue private company annual reporting reference

> **General reference only.** This is a bounded Tier 2 draft for professional review. It does not authorise formation, a financial statement, tax result, fee payment or filing. Guide presence, source preparation and component checks do not establish comprehensive jurisdiction acceptance.

## Scope, rules and source gate

See the [Niue source register](references.md) for dated company registration, business licensing, overseas return and separate tax ownership notification sources.

Load both mandatory bases in full. Retain all 4 universal outputs and 18 checks, all 5 formation outputs and 10 checks, all mandatory country slots and every loaded refusal. This is a selected ongoing-compliance companion, not a complete formation or financial reporting system. A conditional reference example does not waive an engagement's source, profile, factual, reviewer or execution gates.

The selected branch is an already registered ordinary domestic private share company whose actual registered rules and alterations establish that unchanged Schedule 2 model rules apply to the matters discussed. Verify private status throughout the relevant period, the complete governing rules and any applicable Registrar approval or notice. Schedule 3, custom or unknown rules, public companies, overseas companies, groups, regulated entities, legacy IBC transitions, insolvency and ownership structures needing separate analysis are held. Private status under s 9 requires the stated rules, including the public-offer prohibition and shareholder limit, and no more than 100 actual shareholders. Registration alone does not prove every premise.

Before an actual calculation or document, establish the relevant accounting period, incorporation and balance dates, requested obligation, complete operative Act/amendment/commencement/regulation chain and current prescribed forms, fees, notices and calendar. The 2019 reprint and tables through 31 December 2024 do not establish the absence of later direct or indirect amendments. A current registry page corroborates its published procedure, not the completeness of all governing law. Unknown law is a source hold. No ordinary business, tax or beneficial ownership exemption follows from this guide.

### Source and scope conflicts

- **Allocated month.** Section 124 uses the month allocated to the company and permits a written alteration. General June guidance does not establish a particular company's current allocation.
- **Audit dispensation.** Schedule 2 cl 71(6) permits unanimous written agreement to dispense with an audit/remove the auditor. Section 131 imposes audit requirements on specified triggers and s 16 subordinates inconsistent rules to the Act. This reference does not resolve their interaction or implement a waiver. Hold a request relying on that dispensation; do not cancel a triggered or prior-period audit by assumption.
- **Share capital guidance.** The registry's [before-you-set-up page](https://www.companies.gov.nu/help-centre/registering-a-company/before-you-set-up-a-company/) describes nominal values, whilst Act s 21 states that shares must not have a nominal/par value. Its 500-share illustration allocates only 450. Do not reuse those explanations, infer capital or recommend formation from this reference.
- **Different model rules.** Schedule 3 has different financial statement delivery wording. Do not apply Schedule 2's 20-working-day delivery allowance to a Schedule 3 company. Unknown rules remain held.
- **Currency and calendar.** The published dollar sign does not establish actual ISO currency, conversion or rounding. Statutory month/working-day wording is retained; no actual due date, holiday adjustment, late charge or payment amount is cleared here.

## Intake and obligation map

Reuse redacted records before grouped questions. Establish the company, registration/classification history, complete registered rules and changes, incorporation date, balance-date history, allocated return month and notices, directors and authority, addresses/record locations, share register and rights by class, shareholder demands and their delivery dates, existing/unfinished audits, accounting records, prior returns/reports/statements, actual forms, authorised recipient and qualified reviewer. Keep names, identifiers, signatures and personal addresses with the reviewer. Do not upload identity documents or bank details.

1. **Separate the obligations.** A company annual return to the Registrar is not an income tax return, financial statements, an annual report to shareholders or an annual meeting. One does not discharge another. Section 126 also preserves separate event notices for rules, addresses, shares, directors and record locations; do not wait for the annual return to make a required event filing.
2. **Annual return, s 124.** Deliver during the current allocated month with prescribed/approved form, information and fee. Date and compile the information as at a day within that month. Signature must be a director's or an authorised solicitor's/public accountant's. The incorporation calendar year is excepted; it is not a general 12-month filing holiday. Verify any company-specific form approval or month alteration. Do not extend a domestic rule to an overseas company.
3. **Form 10.** Verify current prescribed status and actual acceptance route. Public fields include classification, communication/registered/postal addresses, directors, total issued shares, the persons holding the 10 largest numbers of shares, their addresses and holdings, applicable auditor details, return date and authorised signature. The table separately labels share parcels and flags more than 10 parcels. Keep that distinction, the complete share register and the exact prescribed holder criterion. Multiple parcels per holder, ties or unusual rights require established selection evidence; do not silently rank parcels or invent an aggregation rule. Registered ownership and trustee fields do not settle beneficial ownership obligations.
4. **Records, ss 117–119.** Retain rules, current directors and the share register. Section 40 requires 7-year historical shareholder names/addresses, shares held by class and specified issue, repurchase, redemption and transfer entries, not just current holders. Minutes/resolutions and relevant shareholder communications have a 7-year basis. Accounting records cover the current accounting period and last 7 completed periods; financial statements cover the last 7 completed periods. The written Registrar reduction in s 117(2) applies to its specified minutes/communications and accounting/statement periods; it does not itself reduce s 40 share-register history. Other tax, dispute or legal holds may require longer retention; this is no deletion authority. Records must be accessible/convertible to writing and protected against falsification.
5. **Location, s 119.** An undivided share register remains at the registered office; s 119(1) permits another Niue location for s 117(1)(a)–(g), not the share register in (h). Division into multiple registers must be expressly permitted by the rules. If permitted, keep the principal register in Niue, a copy of every register with it, corresponding entries in the local copy within 10 working days of an entry elsewhere and location/division/change notices within the applicable 10 working days. Do not treat notification alone as permission to move an undivided register. Overseas accounting records require qualifying accounts/returns sent to and kept in Niue that disclose the financial position at intervals not exceeding 6 months and enable compliant statements, plus location notices. A cloud service alone does not prove these conditions. Registered office and postal address in Niue remain necessary under ss 17–18.
6. **Accounting and statements, ss 129–130.** Preserve the statutory daily money/purpose, assets/liabilities, goods/invoice/stocktake and service records. The ordinary cash-retail daily-total concession is specific, not permission to discard all invoices. Complete compliant statements within 4 months after balance date; an established private-company special resolution can extend completion to at most 7 months. Send to every shareholder within 20 working days of the required completion date, even if completed earlier. The first balance period and intervals between balance dates must not exceed 15 months. Statements need the true and fair view, applicable regulations, date and 2 director signatures or the sole director's signature.
7. **Annual report, s 56 and Schedule 2 cl 41.** Under the selected unchanged rules, a shareholder's written demand before period end triggers the annual report. If no report is required, send each shareholder the notice to that effect by the same statutory deadline. Financial statements remain independently mandatory. The report includes compliant statements, required audit report, current/departed director information, prescribed/rule content, date and required director signatures. The deadline is 20 working days after required statement completion, not 20 days after actual early completion.
8. **Meeting, Schedule 2 cls 33–34.** Subject to the first-meeting and written-resolution provisions, all applicable limits operate: once each calendar year, within 5 months after balance date and within 15 months of the previous annual meeting. Where statement completion is validly extended, the balance-date limb becomes 20 working days after required completion; the other limbs do not disappear. First meeting: incorporation-year exception but within 18 months of incorporation. A written-resolution substitute requires everything required at the meeting to be done through the compliant resolution, not merely an accounts approval.
9. **Written resolutions, s 52.** The ordinary selected threshold is not less than 75% of entitled votes. Any higher majority or additional requirements must also be satisfied. Send every nonsigning shareholder a copy within 5 working days. Voting rights, headcount, shares and distribution rights are distinct; establish the actual denominator. This is not a formation or audit-waiver authorisation.
10. **Audit, ss 131–132.** Check public registration at any time in the period, actual rules and a written pre-period-end demand by holders of more than 20% of distribution rights. Exactly 20% is not that demand threshold; it does not by itself clear the other triggers. Appointment is within 30 working days of the trigger/vacancy as applicable. Schedule 2 cl 71(4) allows appointment by ordinary resolution or, for a vacant office, by the directors. If directors appoint to a vacant office, they must give all shareholders notice within 10 working days. That conditional notice limb is not automatically imposed on an ordinary-resolution appointment; other applicable duties remain. A prior required-period audit must still be completed after a later trigger ceases. Existing auditor cessation, waiver, qualification, independence, report content and filings require their own complete evidence; do not certify an audit exemption from this component.

### Statement content and accounting limits

Regulations 11–12 and Schedule 3 prescribe statement information and policies, including accrual accounting, receivables at estimated net realisable value, inventories at lower of cost and net realisable value, the specified depreciation/asset treatments and taxes-payable accounting. Preserve comparatives, remuneration/expense/equity disclosures, accounting policies and changes, valuation bases, material securities/guarantees/contingencies and audit status. The immaterial-item exception requires both current and comparative immateriality. Regulation 13's international-standard provision concerns public companies; do not declare that every private company must use New Zealand IFRS.

These references do not supply every recognition, valuation, disclosure, group, tax or audit rule. Actual statements, review sign-off and filing-ready packages remain held until all applicable content and execution contracts are established. Do not invent a prescribed filing of all private-company statements merely because they must be sent to shareholders.
