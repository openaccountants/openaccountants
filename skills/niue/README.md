# Niue (NU): tax pack

**Built from the law. Not yet approved. Written by Ryan Duguid. Not reviewed by an accountant.** Niue is a self-governing state in free association with New Zealand and uses the New Zealand dollar. Laws are published by the Government of Niue (gov.nu); companies are registered with the Niue Companies Office (companies.gov.nu) under the Companies Act 2006; taxes are run by the Niue Tax Office (mof.gov.nu/niuetax). The Tax Administration Act 2025 applies from 1 July 2026. Tax year 2026.
