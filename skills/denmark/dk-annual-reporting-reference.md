## Scope statement

The starting branch is an ordinary ongoing domestic ApS or A/S with an established reporting history, no group or influential participations, no financial-sector reporting regime, no listing or state-company status, no insolvency or reconstruction, and no special exemption. These are facts to establish, not defaults. First periods, changed years, other forms, funds, foundations, groups, holding companies, listed or regulated entities and uncertain status require the applicable specialist rules before a conclusion. Section 3 identifies the ordinary company forms, subject to its §§ 4–6 exceptions; registration alone does not prove an exemption or every reporting premise. [S1, §§ 1–3 and 7](https://www.retsinformation.dk/eli/lta/2026/402)

Retain all 26 universal intake fields. Obtain the following through reviewer-held, non-sensitive records; keep credentials, personal identifiers and bank details outside agent inputs:

- Actual legal form, current and prior reporting class, status changes and applicable law/framework choices.
- Registered financial-year dates, approval and proposed receipt dates, whether first or changed periods, and enough prior history for the actual class-transition test.
- Each relevant year's total assets, net turnover, period length and average full-time employees, with the underlying measurement and employee-count authority.
- Financial and qualifying investment income, their relationship to net turnover, and source evidence for their classification.
- Ownership, subsidiaries, influence, group totals and any need for consolidation or a statutory exemption.
- Principal CVR industry at the actual balance date, current classification order and both years' measured turnover.
- Existing auditor, any prospective shareholder decision and articles/registration requirements, special disqualifications and any authority decision.
- Complete approved annual report, required signatures, management statement/report, notes, assurance report and actual submission route/version.
- Bookkeeping system/provider and applicable backup disclosures, checked by the reviewer outside agent inputs where identifiers are needed.
- Any demand letter, its date and receipt history, actual filing receipt, dissolution/enforcement stage, relief application and decision.

Missing facts remain **Needs Input**. Do not assume zero financial income, no group, no risk classification, no assurance duty, a valid opt-out or a timely filing. For an actual position, load the complete applicable source chain and professional decision first.

## Filing requirements

Ordinary class B and C reports must be submitted without undue delay after approval and received by Erhvervsstyrelsen within 6 months after financial-year end. Class D has 4 months. Section 138 contains exceptions through §§ 140–141; reconstruction, bankruptcy, liquidation and other special situations require their full rules. No general discretionary extension is established. [S1, §§ 8 and 138](https://www.retsinformation.dk/eli/lta/2026/402)

Apply § 153 to the deadline, not the tax-return rule. For a period stated in months, use the corresponding date; if the event was at month end or the corresponding date does not exist, use month end. A deadline ending on a weekend, public holiday, Constitution Day, Christmas Eve or New Year's Eve moves to the first following working day. For the stipulated ordinary financial year ending **31 December 2026**, the unadjusted 6-month receipt date is **30 June 2027**, subject to the actual class, year, § 153 calendar and any applicable statutory exception. Requalify the law and calendar before actual use. No 9-am tax-return convention is imported. [S1, § 153](https://www.retsinformation.dk/eli/lta/2026/402)

Under current BEK 859/2025, management remains responsible even when an adviser submits. The permitted reporting channels are mandatory. An automatic receipt confirms receipt, not substantive compliance; the authority says the filing duty is satisfied only when a proper report has been received. Do not treat a draft, upload, email, reset or correction request as accepted filing. Keep the final approved version, required evidence and receipt under reviewer control. [S4, §§ 2–3 and 9–10](https://www.retsinformation.dk/eli/lta/2025/859) [S6, receipt and filing sections](https://erhvervsstyrelsen.dk/vejledning-aarsrapporter)

Section 138 A separately requires the stated bookkeeping-system/provider and backup disclosures alongside annual filing. Its identifiers are for the reviewer to verify through the actual reporting process. It does not establish that the system satisfies the complete bookkeeping law. Resolve that companion before preparing an affected filing. [S1, § 138 A](https://www.retsinformation.dk/eli/lta/2026/402)

## Computation rules

1. **Establish the regime and source chain.** Verify legal form, existing class/history, actual financial year, sector, listing, group and special status. Resolve applicable amendments, orders and the complete required companion before an actual position. The ordinary method cannot substitute for class C/D, group, financial-sector or sustainability rules. [S1, §§ 1–3 and 7](https://www.retsinformation.dk/eli/lta/2026/402)
2. **Measure each year separately.** Assets are the sum of asset items. Annualise net turnover proportionately where a financial year differs from 12 months; do not annualise the closing balance sheet by the same multiplier. Apply the actual full-time-employee method. Section 7(4) requires the specified financial and investment income to be added when its condition is met; it is not automatically part of the revenue caption in the financial statements. Do not omit it from the threshold screen or invent eligibility. The authority explains this distinction in its audit-size guidance. [S1, § 7(3)–(4) and § 135(6)](https://www.retsinformation.dk/eli/lta/2026/402) [S6, § 7.1.1](https://erhvervsstyrelsen.dk/vejledning-aarsrapporter)
3. **Apply the separate class and micro screens.** A small company does not exceed 2 of the 3 § 7 size limits in the actual consecutive-year test. A current-year number alone does not settle class transitions or first-year treatment. Section 22 A has its own smaller limits and excludes the specified influential participations, investment vehicles/connected vehicles and derivative rights or obligations. Record a positively established result and each exception; do not infer eligibility from the word 'micro'. [S1, §§ 7 and 22 A](https://www.retsinformation.dk/eli/lta/2026/402)
4. **Determine report contents and approval evidence.** Class B requires the stated management statement, balance sheet, income statement, notes and management report, with the actual § 9 A and § 22 B exceptions. Include any issued auditor/assurance report. Under § 9 all responsible management members sign and date, subject to the actual digital/signature and single-member rules. Section 10 does not permit a dissenting member simply to omit a signature. A sole-member § 9 A management-statement exception is not a blanket exemption from approval, reporting or the required declarations. Section 76 A requires the selected management-report discussion of principal activities and material changes. The authority guidance also lists a principal-activity note alternative, but the selected current statutory text does not itself establish its conditions. This discrepancy remains unresolved: do not omit the management report or treat the note as sufficient without the complete applicable authority and professional decision. Complete statement formats, notes, measurement and lawful approval mechanics remain held here. [S1, §§ 8–10 A, 22 and 76 A](https://www.retsinformation.dk/eli/lta/2026/402) [S6, class-B contents](https://erhvervsstyrelsen.dk/vejledning-aarsrapporter)
5. **Screen audit and assistance independently.** Section 135 starts with audit and permits its stated class-B alternatives only on their full conditions. Apply the 4-million/8-million/12-person screen separately from class B. Check the 50-million-assets override, ineligible entity types, influential holdings and § 135 A disqualifications, including specified convictions/fines, management bankruptcy disqualification and authority control findings. The prospective shareholder decision, articles/registration and first-year mechanics require their complete company-law sources before a real opt-out. A financial size pass alone clears none of these. Section 135 B restricts the relevant statutory and voluntary reports to auditors approved under Revisorloven; a generic adviser or accountant credential does not establish that approval. [S1, §§ 135–135 B](https://www.retsinformation.dk/eli/lta/2026/402)
6. **Apply the current risk-industry screen.** BEK 1488/2025 lists 15 exact codes. Verify the principal CVR code at the current balance date and measured turnover above 5 million in both relevant years. The authority's example does not require the same risk code in the earlier year. The order starts on 1 January 2026, applies to the stated financial years beginning 1 January 2025 or later and has a 2-financial-year classification period. Do not extend it indefinitely or reuse the repealed older list. A company may satisfy the ordinary audit size screen yet still need an independent accountant's assistance or assurance report. [S5](https://www.retsinformation.dk/eli/lta/2025/1488) [S7](https://erhvervsstyrelsen.dk/klassificerede-risikobrancher-og-krav-om-erklaering-fra-revisor)
7. **Select the actual permitted submission route and format.** Current BEK 859 §§ 13–14 permit Basis or Special for the stated class-B cases, with exceptions for class-C elements, IFRS, multiple languages and split accounting policies. Its system-to-system option also has explicit exclusions. Section 17 distinguishes Basis-generated readable/machine data from Special's specified XBRL/Inline XBRL versions and taxonomy, and requires readable and structured information to correspond. Obtain the actual current taxonomy, validation and portal instructions before output preparation. The old detailed guidance's BEK 771/2020 numbering does not establish current procedure. [S4](https://www.retsinformation.dk/eli/lta/2025/859) [S8](https://erhvervsstyrelsen.dk/vejledning-til-bekendtgorelse-om-indberetning-til-og-offentliggorelse-af-arsrapporter-mv-i)
8. **Reconcile deadline, evidence and any late measures.** Calculate the correct § 138/153 receipt deadline and obtain the actual receipt. Section 150 distinguishes the demand letter's 8-working-day period from its 4-week possible dissolution/referral route. If its fee conditions apply, § 151 counts from the original statutory filing deadline, uses cumulative totals per relevant management member and includes its adjustment for stated coercive fines. Section 152 relief is exceptional and needs evidence and a decision; staff/adviser error alone is insufficient. Do not treat the demand period as an ordinary extension or add 500 to the second-month total of 2,000. Actual notices, calendar, referral stage, relief and payable result remain held. [S1, §§ 150–153](https://www.retsinformation.dk/eli/lta/2026/402)

### Case 1: threshold turnover differs from the revenue caption

For a fictional 12-month period, establish net turnover of DKK 4 million, eligible financial income of DKK 5 million and eligible investment income of DKK 2 million. The § 7(4) condition is stipulated as satisfied. The threshold measure is **DKK 11 million**, not DKK 4 million. This does not require presentation of all 11 million as revenue in the accounts and does not decide class or audit eligibility without the other tests. Actual income classification remains held. [S1, § 7(4)](https://www.retsinformation.dk/eli/lta/2026/402) [S6, § 7.1.1](https://erhvervsstyrelsen.dk/vejledning-aarsrapporter)

```oa-check
name: Case 1; separate threshold turnover from statement revenue
inputs:
  net_turnover: 4000000
  eligible_financial_income: 5000000
  eligible_investment_income: 2000000
steps:
  - other_income: eligible_financial_income + eligible_investment_income
  - threshold_turnover: net_turnover + other_income
expected:
  other_income: 7000000
  threshold_turnover: 11000000
```

### Case 2: annualise turnover, not every size measure

For a fictional properly established 6-month period, net turnover is DKK 60 million and there is positively established zero other income under the threshold rule. The annualised turnover measure is 60 million × 12/6 = **DKK 120 million**, DKK 9 million above the small-company turnover limit. This is one size measure, not a final class or first-year conclusion. Do not double the balance-sheet assets merely because turnover is annualised. Actual changed/first-period authority and history remain required. [S1, § 7(3)](https://www.retsinformation.dk/eli/lta/2026/402)

```oa-check
name: Case 2; turnover normalisation for a stipulated six-month period
inputs:
  actual_turnover: 60000000
  period_months: 6
  standard_months: 12
  small_turnover_limit: 111000000
steps:
  - annualised_turnover: actual_turnover * standard_months / period_months
  - amount_above_turnover_limit: annualised_turnover - small_turnover_limit
expected:
  annualised_turnover: 120000000
  amount_above_turnover_limit: 9000000
```

### Case 3: the second-month late fee is a cumulative total

Stipulate an established ordinary receipt deadline of 30 June 2026 and actual electronic receipt on 1 August 2026. The properly dated demand letter's 8-working-day period has expired; 3 relevant management members are established, no dissolution/referral has displaced the fee route, and no reduction or relief applies. These are fictional positive facts, not defaults. The authority's corresponding date example places this receipt in the second commenced month. The selected fee is **DKK 2,000 per member**, **DKK 6,000 total**, not DKK 2,500 per member or DKK 7,500 total. The separate 4-week dissolution/referral route and actual notice must never be ignored in a real case. This is a calendar-year 2026 filing illustration, not the filing of the separate 2026 financial year. [S1, §§ 150–153](https://www.retsinformation.dk/eli/lta/2026/402) [S6, late-fee example](https://erhvervsstyrelsen.dk/vejledning-aarsrapporter)

```oa-check
name: Case 3; cumulative second-month annual-report fees per member
inputs:
  members: 3
  cumulative_second_month_fee: 2000
steps:
  - illustrative_total: members * cumulative_second_month_fee
expected:
  illustrative_total: 6000
```
