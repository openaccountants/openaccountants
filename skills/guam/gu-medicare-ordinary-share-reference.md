# Guam 2026 ordinary Medicare share reference

> **General reference only.** This Tier 2 draft illustrates ordinary federal Medicare hospital-insurance shares on independently stipulated covered remuneration. It establishes neither actual covered wages, Additional Medicare, complete FICA, territorial income tax, lawful withholding, a remittance nor accepted financial outputs. Guide presence and component checks do not establish comprehensive jurisdiction acceptance.

## Scope and source gates


Load all declared bases in full. Preserve the 4 universal outputs and 18 checks, the 5 social-contributions outputs and 12 checks, every mandatory country slot, shared refusals and the exact topical refusals below. This reference leaves the social-contributions country contract incomplete. Actual payroll also requires payroll-workflow-base and a complete Guam payroll companion; no output or country acceptance transfers by implication.

Each calculation concerns one fictional ordinary private employee and one employer, with Medicare-covered employment and all remuneration inclusions/exclusions independently stipulated for the calendar year 2026. Remuneration is paid and received within that same calendar year. There is no predecessor/successor or common-paymaster history, other employer, international agreement, mixed covered/excluded service, excluded employment or disputed wage component. P12 compares 2 separately stipulated employees, each on those conditions. These are explicit fictional premises, never defaults for missing facts. Actual classification, wage inclusion, allocation and the complete applicable law must be established separately before an actual result.

Federal FICA's territorial reach is independent of any income-tax mirroring result. IRC section 3121(e)(1) includes Guam in 'State' and section 3121(e)(2) includes it geographically in 'United States'. Section 3121(b) includes service within the United States irrespective of either party's citizenship or residence, subject to its exceptions. The special Puerto Rico citizen sentence in section 3121(e) is not a rule deeming every resident of this territory a US citizen. This reference establishes neither complete territorial income tax nor every worker's coverage.

Government cases are outside the fictional private-employment branch. Section 3121(b)(7) contains distinct State, territorial and retirement-system branches. Resolve Guam's specific paragraphs (7)(B) and (7)(D) in their complete context; no universal exemption or coverage determination follows from geography alone.

Section 3121(b)(18) contains a specific employment exception for services by a resident of the Republic of the Philippines while temporarily in Guam in the stated immigration status. All such cases are outside this fictional branch. Resolve the exact current status and statutory conditions; do not extend the exception to another visa, nationality or territory.

The [IRS possession FICA summary](https://www.irs.gov/individuals/international-taxpayers/persons-employed-in-a-us-possession-fica) provides administrative corroboration and examples of exceptions. Its summary is not complete statutory intake.

Government hospital-insurance coverage also requires the separate IRC section 3121(u) analysis. An OASDI exemption or State/territorial government label alone does not establish Medicare coverage or exclusion. No government result is computed here.

2. **Keep ordinary shares separate.** E = 0.0145 × M and R = 0.0145 × M. T = E + R is an ordinary Medicare reference total only. No OASDI annual ceiling or employee Additional Medicare withholding reference caps these ordinary shares. 3. **Keep Additional Medicare unresolved in every position.** An employer withholding threshold and annual employee-liability thresholds are different tests. M below, at or above 200000 establishes no Additional Medicare result here. There is no employer-matched 0.9% component and no accepted total Medicare or total FICA result. This reference supplies no annual employee-liability or actual employer-withholding calculation.
4. **Aggregate only the stipulated same-person components.** P11 adds 2 independently Medicare-covered components for one fictional employee/employer/year. P12 compares 2 separately stipulated employees, each receiving a separate reference. Their summed references are not an accepted employer declaration or remittance. Actual multiple-employer, predecessor/successor or common-paymaster treatment remains held.
5. **Preserve exact illustration precision.** Retain M, E, R and T exactly below. Fractional shares are not lawful per-payment withholding, filing or remittance amounts. The identified employee collection fraction-of-a-cent rule does not complete annual allocation, employer-share precision, reconciliation or adjustment authority.
