# Guam (US-GU): tax pack

**Built from the law. Not yet approved. Written by Claude. Not reviewed by an accountant.** Guam is an unincorporated US territory with its own tax administration, the Department of Revenue and Taxation (DRT, guamtax.com). Its income tax is the US Internal Revenue Code applied as the Guam Territorial income tax (48 U.S.C. 1421i), so US rates and forms are used but the tax is paid to Guam. Currency is US dollars. Tax year 2026.

## What Guam does not have

| Tax | Position, and source |
|---|---|
| VAT or retail sales tax | None. The business privilege tax (gross receipts tax) on the seller's gross income takes its place; see `gu-business-privilege-tax` (11 GCA 26202 as restated in P.L. 38-136). |
| Federal unemployment tax (FUTA) | Does not apply to employers in Guam ([IRS Pub. 15 (2026), section 14](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/p15.pdf)). No Guam unemployment contribution was found on the DRT tax calendar; not confirmed. |
| Federal income tax withholding by Guam employers | Replaced by Guam income tax withholding, reported on Form W-2GU ([IRS Pub. 15 (2026)](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/p15.pdf)). |
| Self-employment tax in the Guam mirror | Excluded by 48 U.S.C. 1421i(d)(1); Guam residents pay it to the IRS on Form 1040-SS. |
