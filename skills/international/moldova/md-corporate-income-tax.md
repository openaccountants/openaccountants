# Moldova corporate income tax (2026)

Companies in Moldova pay income tax (impozitul pe venit) under Title II of the Fiscal Code, Codul fiscal nr. 1163/1997. The same title covers individuals, so the rules below are the ones that apply to legal entities. The tax authority is the State Tax Service (Serviciul Fiscal de Stat, SFS). Amounts are in Moldovan lei (MDL). The text used here is the consolidated Fiscal Code on legis.md as last amended by Law LP235 of 2 October 2026 (in force 30 October 2026).

The tax period is the calendar year. For a new company it runs from registration to 31 December (Fiscal Code art. 12^1(1), (2)).

## Who this applies to

- **Resident companies** are taxed on income from sources in Moldova and abroad (art. 13(1)(a), art. 14(1)(a)). A legal entity is resident if its activity is organised or managed in Moldova, or if Moldova is its main place of business (art. 5 pct. 5 lit. b)).
- **Non-resident companies** are taxed on Moldovan-source income listed in art. 71, mostly by withholding (art. 91). A permanent establishment in Moldova files its own return (art. 83(2)(e)). See Cross-border points.
- **Small companies not registered for VAT** may pay 4% on accounting income instead of 12% on profit (Fiscal Code chapter 7^1, arts. 54^1 to 54^4). Excluded: farms, individual entrepreneurs, and companies where more than 60% of last year's sales came from business and management consultancy (CAEM 70.22) (art. 54^1(1)). A company that registers for VAT moves to the standard regime from the date of VAT registration (art. 54^1(4)).
- **IT park residents** pay a single 7% tax on monthly sales revenue instead of income tax, employer contributions and several other taxes (Fiscal Code Title X, chapter 1, arts. 368 to 373). See [moldova-tax-optimization](moldova-tax-optimization.md).
- **Individual entrepreneurs (II) and farms** fall under the same Title II rates (art. 15(a), (c)); their personal rules are in [moldova-income-tax](moldova-income-tax.md).

## The taxable base

**Start point.** Gross income from all sources (art. 18), less allowed deductions and exemptions (art. 14(1)(a)). Ordinary and necessary expenses paid or incurred during the year, strictly for the business, are deductible (art. 24(1)).

**Common adjustments.**
- **Fines and penalties.** Income tax itself, fines and penalties on any tax, and fines for breaking the law are not deductible (art. 30(1)).
- **Interest.** Interest on loans from persons other than financial-sector entities is deductible only up to the weighted average bank lending rate to companies published by the National Bank of Moldova, by term and currency (art. 25(2)).
- **Donations.** Charitable or sponsorship donations to public bodies, non-commercial organisations, trade unions, employers' organisations and family-type children's homes are deductible up to 5% of taxable income (art. 36(1), (2)).
- **Capital gains.** Only 50% of the net capital gain for the year (gains on capital assets minus capital losses) is taxable income (art. 18 lit. e), art. 40(7)). The gain is the amount received less the asset's cost base (art. 40(1)).
- **Dividends received** from another Moldovan company are not taxable, except dividends from profits of 2008 to 2011 (art. 20 lit. z19)).
- **Foreign tax** paid on income also taxed in Moldova is credited, capped at the Moldovan tax on that income, with a certificate from the foreign authority (art. 82(1), (2)).

**Losses.** A tax loss is carried forward and spread over the next five tax periods; older losses are used first (art. 32(1), (3)). There is no carry-back.

**Small-company regime (4%).** The base is accounting income for the year, not profit. Income listed in art. 20 and exchange-rate and amount differences are left out; returns and discounts reduce the base in the year they occur (art. 54^2). The choice is recorded in the accounting policy by 25 April, or by the 25th of the month after the quarter of registration for a new company (art. 54^1(3)). If a shareholder holds more than 25% in more than three small companies, they must pick the ones that move to the standard regime (art. 54^1(3^2)).

Every resident legal entity files a return, whether or not tax is due (art. 83(2)(c)).

**Deferral of tax to the dividend date (2023 to 2026 profits).** A company with at most 249 staff on the last day of the year and either turnover or total assets up to MDL 100 million may pay the tax on 2023 to 2026 profits only when it pays dividends from them (art. 87(1^1)). On each dividend payment, the tax due is the year's tax times a ratio: dividends paid (before withholding) over pre-tax accounting profit for that year, rounded to two decimals. The option is made by filing the return on time. It is not open to individual entrepreneurs, farms, free economic zone, Giurgiulești port and Mărculești airport residents, IT park residents, the 4% regime, financial and insurance companies (CAEM section K), and trade companies (section G) with more than 49 staff and turnover or assets over MDL 50 million. Companies using it do not pay quarterly instalments for 2023 to 2026 (art. 84(6)).

## Penalties and interest

- **Late payment interest** accrues for each day of delay (art. 228(2)). The yearly rate is the National Bank of Moldova base rate set in November of the previous year, rounded up to the next whole percent, plus 5 points, divided by the days in the year (art. 228(3)).
- **Instalments.** Unpaid or short instalments are fined by applying the art. 228(3) rate from the instalment date to the filing date. The shortfall is measured against the smaller of last year's tax and this year's tax (art. 261(6)).
- **Wrong or late return.** MDL 500 to 1,000 per return for companies, capped at MDL 10,000 for all returns (art. 260(1), contributors under art. 232 lit. a) and c)).
- **Under-declaration.** Understating tax in a return with untrue data: 20% to 30% of the amount understated. No fine if the company files a corrected return itself under art. 188 (art. 261(4), (4^1)).
- **Evasion.** Avoiding calculation and payment: 80% to 100% of the tax not declared (art. 261(5)).
- **Deferral regime.** Understating taxable income while using the art. 87(1^1) deferral: MDL 1,000 to 10,000 (art. 260(6)).

## Cross-border points

- **Withholding on payments to non-residents.** 12% on Moldovan-source income in art. 71: management and consultancy services bought by a resident wherever performed, other services in Moldova, interest, royalties, rent, insurance premiums, transport, commissions and more. Sales of goods are excluded (art. 91(1), art. 71(1)). Dividends: 6% (art. 91(1), art. 71(1) lit. e)). Income of a non-resident's permanent establishment is not withheld; the PE files a return (art. 91(2)(a), art. 83(2)(e)).
- **Treaties.** A double tax treaty overrides the Fiscal Code, and the more favourable rate applies (art. 79^3(1)). Check the treaty and its documentation rules before paying at a reduced rate.
- **Foreign tax credit.** Capped at the Moldovan tax on the same income (art. 82(2)).
- **Transfer pricing and permanent establishment rules** are covered in md-transfer-pricing and md-cross-border, which are in review (PR #793).

## Worked examples

### Example 1: SRL on the standard regime, 2026

A Chișinău SRL, VAT-registered, has accounting profit of MDL 900,000 for 2026. The profit includes a MDL 15,000 tax fine and an MDL 80,000 gain on selling shares it held in another company. It has no capital losses. Its 2025 tax was MDL 96,000, and it paid four instalments of MDL 24,000 in 2026 (1/4 of last year's tax, art. 84(1)(b)).

The fine is added back (art. 30(1)). Only 50% of the capital gain is taxable (art. 40(7)), so MDL 40,000 comes off. Taxable income is 900,000 + 15,000 - 40,000 = MDL 875,000. Tax at 12% (art. 15(b)) is MDL 105,000. Instalments paid were MDL 96,000, so MDL 9,000 is due with the return by 25 March 2027 (arts. 83(4)(a), 87(1)). There is no instalment fine, because the company paid the smaller of last year's and this year's tax (art. 261(6)).

```oa-check
name: Moldovan SRL 2026, fine add-back and 50% capital gain
inputs:
  accounting_profit: 900000
  fine: 15000
  capital_gain: 80000
  tax_2025: 96000
steps:
  - gain_excluded: capital_gain * 0.50
  - taxable_income: accounting_profit + fine - gain_excluded
  - tax: taxable_income * 0.12
  - instalment: tax_2025 / 4
  - instalments_paid: instalment * 4
  - balance_due: tax - instalments_paid
expected:
  taxable_income: 875000.00
  tax: 105000.00
  instalment: 24000.00
  balance_due: 9000.00
```

### Example 2: tax deferred until dividends (art. 87(1^1))

An SRL with 20 staff and MDL 30 million turnover opts for the deferral by filing its 2026 return on time. Its 2026 taxable income is MDL 500,000, so the 2026 tax is MDL 60,000. Pre-tax accounting profit for 2026 is MDL 540,000. It paid no quarterly instalments (art. 84(6)). In June 2027 it pays a dividend of MDL 200,000 from 2026 profit to its sole shareholder, a resident individual.

The ratio is 200,000 / 540,000 = 0.370..., rounded to 0.37. Tax due on this payment is 60,000 x 0.37 = MDL 22,200, payable by 25 July 2027 (art. 87(1^1)). The company also withholds 6% from the dividend, MDL 12,000, payable by the same date (arts. 90^1(3^1), 92(1)). MDL 37,800 of the 2026 tax stays deferred until later dividends.

```oa-check
name: Moldovan SRL, 2026 tax deferred to a 2027 dividend
inputs:
  tax_2026: 60000
  pretax_profit_2026: 540000
  dividend_gross: 200000
steps:
  - ratio: round(dividend_gross / pretax_profit_2026, 2)
  - cit_due_now: tax_2026 * ratio
  - dividend_wht: dividend_gross * 0.06
  - cit_still_deferred: tax_2026 - cit_due_now
expected:
  ratio: 0.37
  cit_due_now: 22200.00
  dividend_wht: 12000.00
  cit_still_deferred: 37800.00
```

## Ask the client first

1. Is the company registered for VAT, an IT park resident, or on the 4% regime? Each one changes the tax entirely.
2. On 31 December 2026, how many staff, what turnover and what total assets? This decides whether the art. 87(1^1) deferral is open.
3. Will it pay dividends, interim or final, and to whom (resident individuals, Moldovan companies, non-residents)?
4. What was last year's tax, and what instalments have been paid?
5. Does it pay non-residents for services, interest, royalties or rent, and does a treaty apply?
6. Does it have tax losses from 2021 to 2025, or capital gains or losses this year?
