# Moldova transfer pricing v0.6

2026 writer draft, primary law read 30 October 2026; the current information annex and APA application model read 31 October 2026. The24October draft and its independent correction evidence are preserved; that review does not attest to this expanded country pack. This Guide provides the domestic TP computation/documentation method. Actual comparables, legal facts and any treaty relief must be evidenced before use.

## Who this applies to

Start with the current Fiscal Code TitleV chapter11², arts226¹⁷–226³⁰, and Ministry of Finance Order9/2024 as amended, including Order8/2026. The rules apply to Moldovan-registered business legal/natural persons and foreign legal persons with Moldovan branches/permanent establishments. Art226¹⁹(9) excludes Moldovan-citizen individuals not carrying on business from that article's filing rule. The arm's-length obligation is separate from the information/file threshold. [Code arts226¹⁷–226¹⁹](https://www.legis.md/cautare/getResults?doc_id=157050&lang=ro), [Order9 annex1 points1–2](https://www.legis.md/cautare/getResults?doc_id=152738&lang=ro).

Under226¹⁷(1)(1), affiliation includes spouses or relatives up to the second degree; an individual/legal-person relationship with at least25% direct/indirect voting participation including affiliated holdings or effective control; a legal-person/legal-person relationship on the same basis; and common third-person ownership/control of both. Effective control includes the specified majority board/executive/auditor appointment/removal powers and contractual/constitutional/agreed control. Indirect participation can pass through several people/entities; the legal person and its PE are affiliated. Do not stop the test at a25% share register where another control limb applies. [Code226¹⁷](https://www.legis.md/cautare/getResults?doc_id=157050&lang=ro).

A transaction includes sales, purchases, giving/receiving services, assignments, leases, credit, loans and transfers of interests or rights in tangible/intangible property. A local transaction under226¹⁷(1)(9) concerns residents where either/both have the specified free-zone/IT-park status or different business-income tax rates/bases; a cross-border transaction under(10) is between a resident and a nonresident outside Moldova. Classify the parties, regimes and transaction before applying226¹⁸(2). Do not treat every same-rate domestic intercompany movement as automatically satisfying the statutory local-transaction definition, or omit eligible domestic transactions solely because neither party is foreign. [Code226¹⁷–226¹⁸](https://www.legis.md/cautare/getResults?doc_id=157050&lang=ro).

The current226¹⁹ filing structure applies from fiscal period2025 under Law187/2025 artXXVI(1)(b). It replaces the predecessor two-tier20m/50m system: for2025/2026 periods there is one information threshold and a request-based file. For2024, Law187 artXXVI(3) creates a separate request/120day file transition for its specified former category and disapplies the identified non-submission sanction for the former deadline; do not apply an old automatic filing calendar or treat that transition as the2026 rule. [Law187 artII point19 and artXXVI](https://www.legis.md/cautare/getResults?doc_id=149593&lang=ro).

## The base

### Build the affiliate and threshold reconciliation

Identify ownership/control and fiscal periods; record each party's residence, PE and tax regime. Reconcile transaction values across all eligible affiliates **without VAT**, maintaining sales and purchases separately instead of netting bilateral balances. Tag legal exclusions individually. Art226¹⁹(4) excludes transactions covered by an actual APA from the threshold and its documentation, whereas a pending APA application does not exempt the information filing under Order9 annex3 point4. [Code226¹⁹](https://www.legis.md/cautare/getResults?doc_id=157050&lang=ro), [Order9](https://www.legis.md/cautare/getResults?doc_id=152738&lang=ro).

Art226¹⁹(7) excludes received/paid advances, exchange differences and accounting revaluation differences of assets/liabilities arising from affiliated transactions, except actual currency-exchange transactions. For leases, credit and loans, the threshold includes **only interest calculated during the fiscal period** under art12(7), rather than the funding principal or all instalments. Preserve the bridge from accounting/contract data to eligible amounts; do not use the expired Order9 point52 as if it overrode current statutory exclusions. Foreign-currency conversion mechanics and unusual financing components require the confirmed rule stated below before using an assumed exchange rate. [Code226¹⁹(7)](https://www.legis.md/cautare/getResults?doc_id=157050&lang=ro), [current Order9 annex1 point52 repealed](https://www.legis.md/cautare/getResults?doc_id=152738&lang=ro).

Below20m, the statutory information/request-file obligation under226¹⁹(1) is not triggered solely by value; it does not prove arm's-length pricing or remove ordinary evidence duties. Order9 annex1 point36 permits SFS to require structure/affiliation, markets, competitors, contracts, accounting and transaction-support records from persons without the file obligation; point37 says missing documents do not stop the audit. The old point30 MDL1m verification exclusion is repealed in the current rules. [Code226¹⁸–226²⁰](https://www.legis.md/cautare/getResults?doc_id=157050&lang=ro), [Order9 annex1 points30–37](https://www.legis.md/cautare/getResults?doc_id=152738&lang=ro).

### Delineate and price the actual transaction

The taxpayer bears the burden of demonstrating arm's-length compliance. Compare legal, economic, commercial and financial conditions using characteristics, functions/assets/risks, contractual terms, economic circumstances and business strategy. Record risk control and financial capacity, not only a contract allocating the risk. The actual conduct/conditions prevail where inconsistent with the document. Examine markets, competition, substitutes, supply/demand, purchasing power, regulation and infrastructure; justify a different geographic market and reliable adjustments. [Code226¹⁸(7)–(8),226²⁰(5)](https://www.legis.md/cautare/getResults?doc_id=157050&lang=ro), [Order9 annex1 points4–13](https://www.legis.md/cautare/getResults?doc_id=152738&lang=ro).

Select the most appropriate method and document why the data and comparability support it. The domestic methods are comparable uncontrolled price (CUP), cost-plus, resale-price, transactional net margin (TNMM), profit-split, and another method recognised in the OECD TP Guidelines. Do not invent a universal statutory cost-plus markup, guarantee fee or interest margin. [Code226²⁰(3)–(5)](https://www.legis.md/cautare/getResults?doc_id=157050&lang=ro).

| Method | Computation and evidence to retain | Official provision |
|---|---|---|
|CUP|Compare actual controlled unit price with sufficiently comparable independent prices, adjusting material differences reliably|Order9 annex1 point16|
|Resale price|Independent resale price less the justified resale margin (resale costs and appropriate gross profit), adjusting relevant acquisition costs; keep consistent gross-margin accounting|Point17|
|Cost-plus|Relevant production/service costs plus a justified gross-profit markup; retain cost-pool, allocation and functions/risks/asset analysis|Point18|
|TNMM|Compare a properly delineated tested party's relevant net-profit indicator with reliable independent/internal uncontrolled results; reconcile the chosen denominator and segment accounts|Point19 and21|
|Profit-split|Delineate the relevant combined profit and allocate consistently with evidenced contributions/functions/risks/assets and independent conditions|Point20|
|Other OECD-recognised method|Explain its suitability to the actual case and data under the statutory most-appropriate-method criteria|Code226²⁰(3)(f),(5)|

Table sources: [Order9 annex1 points14–21](https://www.legis.md/cautare/getResults?doc_id=152738&lang=ro), [Code226²⁰](https://www.legis.md/cautare/getResults?doc_id=157050&lang=ro). Resale, cost-plus and TNMM require a tested-party selection under point21. A group policy or a database printout without delineation, comparable selection, adjustments and accounting reconciliation is not the required conclusion.

Build the comparability interval from the justified independent data. Points22–24 use the lower/upper quartiles as the interval bounds. Point25's special rule for at most two comparable values uses the minimum/maximum and arithmetic mean for the median. Preserve the data, rejection log, search date, formula and adjustments; the actual quartile interpolation algorithm for larger samples is not prescribed here and remains a precise gap below. A result within a properly established interval is treated as arm's-length; audit adjustment/estimation uses the median under47. [Order9 annex1 points3,22–25,47](https://www.legis.md/cautare/getResults?doc_id=152738&lang=ro).

### Voluntary and corresponding adjustments

Art226²³ allows voluntary income/expense adjustment; for prior periods submit the required corrected information/file and corrected tax returns under188. Order9 annex1 points26–28 permits adjustment within the interval where pricing is not arm's-length but prevents voluntary correction of the controlled transactions once a TP audit of one of the relevant affiliates has started. SFS can adjust where the principle fails, or estimate where necessary data are absent, and can use another method after demonstrating failure of the taxpayer's method. The amended rules use a thematic tax audit; the former preliminary-review procedure and its protection in repealed point44 must not be reused as a current amnesty. [Code226²⁰,226²³,188](https://www.legis.md/cautare/getResults?doc_id=157050&lang=ro), [Order9 annex1 points26–49](https://www.legis.md/cautare/getResults?doc_id=152738&lang=ro).

For resident affiliates,226²¹ makes the SFS adjustment/estimation opposable to the other party. SFS issues the counterpart decision after the226²¹/252(3) stage, and the other party can amend its income-tax return; where188(4) blocks amendment, request an audit for double-tax elimination. Order9 annex1 point55(1) specifies15working days after the252(3)30day period; its other limb still references the now-repealed point43, so do not invent a surviving notification process for that branch. For foreign-affiliate adjustments, follow the treaty-dependent application and evidence in Cross-border points below. [Code226²¹–226²²,252](https://www.legis.md/cautare/getResults?doc_id=157050&lang=ro), [Order9 annex1 points54–56](https://www.legis.md/cautare/getResults?doc_id=152738&lang=ro).

### Compile the master file and local file

For periods from2025, Order8/2026 points2–4 and current Order9 annex1 point50 prescribe:

- **Master file:** legal/ownership structure and geography; business drivers/value chain, significant intragroup services, markets, group functions/risks/assets and restructuring; intangible strategy, ownership, agreements, DEMPE and transfers; intragroup/independent financing policy and treasury entities; consolidated financial information or the stated equivalent/segment data; APAs and relevant allocation rulings. DEMPE means development, enhancement, maintenance, protection and exploitation, not legal ownership alone.
- **Local file:** local structure, all affiliates/PEs and direct/indirect affiliation; business/markets/pricing and competitors; each controlled transaction's property/service characteristics and contracts/context, restructuring, service cost allocations and cost-contribution arrangements; detailed functional/comparability analysis; method and tested-party selection; internal/external comparables, search method/data; interval, adjustments and the reasoned arm's-length conclusion.

Use the whole-group master information. Point51 permits the relevant group segment if complete information is unavailable **and justified reasons are supplied**; this is not an unconditional master-file waiver. The local file must be Romanian; the master file can be Romanian, Russian or English. Paper and/or electronic files are permitted; electronic local/master files require the specified responsible resident taxpayer signatures, with identification of the master-file preparer. Requested copies of underlying contracts/documents require authorised Romanian translation except English/Russian. The information filing itself carries the responsible electronic signature. [Order9 annex1 points50–53²](https://www.legis.md/cautare/getResults?doc_id=152738&lang=ro), [Order8](https://www.legis.md/cautare/getResults?doc_id=152708&lang=ro).

Retain contracts, invoices, accounting reconciliation, functional interviews, benchmark inputs, signed submissions, the actual SFS request and receipt and review evidence. The old six-years-from-file-presentation assertion came from an earlier226¹⁹(5), which now governs corrected information; it is **not recertified as the current statutory retention trigger**. The current retention rule is expressly unconfirmed below. Preserve the records pending resolution; do not destroy them on an assumed expiry.

### IT-park related-party adjustment return: IUPT25 and IUPT24

IUPT is a single-tax adjustment return for the applicable affiliated transactions, separate from the Order9 TP information form/file and the monthly IU17. The current Order 135 annex 7 is actually labelled **IUPT25**; annex 9 is explicitly **IUPT24 for 2024**. Original forms/instructions 7–10 were fully text-read and visually inspected, including all eight rendered pages. Do not use a 12% ordinary company calculation for a 7% park sales-base adjustment, or rename the approved form because its label contains 25. Establish the correct taxpayer, covered transaction, arm's-length adjustment and fiscal period first. [Current Order 135][parkorder]; [actual IUPT25][iupt25actual] and [instructions][iupt25instructions]; [actual IUPT24][iupt24actual] and [instructions][iupt24instructions]; [Code 373(1^1)](https://www.legis.md/cautare/getResults?doc_id=157050&lang=ro).

**Commencement matters.** Actual Order 106 of **10 October 2025**, published **28 October 2025**, adds the annual and2024-specific annexes. Points **1,3,4,5** take effect on **1 January 2026**; the other provisions take effect on publication. Thus current monthly IU17 no longer includes old rows 011/012, while the annual IUPT25 method explicitly carries the 2025 adjustments that were already reported through monthly IU17. IUPT24 separately isolates2024 adjustments already reported monthly in 013. Preserve those returns and make the annual bridge once. ### APA application and monitoring

An APA requires an application, relevant documents and fee proof, and cannot cover transactions already subject to an ongoing/completed TP audit at application. Residents and registered foreign-person PEs/branches can apply; a bilateral/multilateral APA requires the relevant treaty. An optional written preliminary discussion request is answered with a meeting date within30days. For unilateral requests use Romanian; bilateral/multilateral requests use English, with the annex3 translation requirements. Supply entity/affiliate details, structure, functional/DEMPE analysis, selected method/tested party, comparables/interval, periods, critical assumptions, foreign-authority request where relevant, fee proof, authenticity/no-proceedings declarations and supporting copies. Do not submit duplicate same-object applications already under consideration. [Code226²⁵](https://www.legis.md/cautare/getResults?doc_id=157050&lang=ro), [Order9 annex3 points1–15](https://www.legis.md/cautare/getResults?doc_id=152738&lang=ro).

Track **admissibility separately from substantive resolution**: annex3 points16–19 allows30days for unilateral admissibility and seven months for bilateral/multilateral admissibility, including foreign-authority availability. After admission, point26 allows six/twelve/eighteen months for unilateral/bilateral/multilateral resolution, with the point27 suspension grounds. The Code's foreign refusal/no-response-six-month route gives30days after communication to withdraw/refund or convert the request; do not promise a six-month overall result. The draft decision allows30days for objections and the specified15day response after rejected objections; the decision is communicated within10working days. [Code226²⁵(6)–(7)](https://www.legis.md/cautare/getResults?doc_id=157050&lang=ro), [Order9 annex3 points16–44](https://www.legis.md/cautare/getResults?doc_id=152738&lang=ro).

The APA's scope, transaction types, period, assumptions, method, interval and conditions control protection; an application alone is not approval. Monitor material changes and file the revision request within30days of a point46 condition, observing the statutory pre-expiry request rule as well. Annex3 points45–67 govern extension/revision and decisions. Annually report APA compliance by the **income-tax return deadline** under226²⁹, with electronic signature and the prescribed annex2 reporting content; this is not the sixth-month information deadline. Code226³⁰ and annex3 points70–86 allow audit/termination: “revocarea” operates from conclusion, “anularea” from cancellation, with distinct false-data/noncompliance/changed-assumption/no-report grounds and objection routes. Preserve the actual decision before excluding covered transactions from the threshold. [Code226²⁵–226³⁰](https://www.legis.md/cautare/getResults?doc_id=157050&lang=ro), [Order9 annex3](https://www.legis.md/cautare/getResults?doc_id=152738&lang=ro).

Pay the per-type fee before application/modification. Annex3 points87–90 sends it to the State budget; withdrawal before admission permits requested refund through the statutory process, while withdrawal after admission does not under that ordinary branch. Separately apply the Code's foreign-refusal/no-response and other specific reapplication/refund routes; do not infer a blanket refund entitlement. The approved APA application model is mapped below; technical payment/refund implementation remains unconfirmed. [Code226²⁵(7),226²⁸](https://www.legis.md/cautare/getResults?doc_id=157050&lang=ro), [Order9 annex3 points21–25,55–57,87–90](https://www.legis.md/cautare/getResults?doc_id=152738&lang=ro).

## Penalties and interest

**Calendar-2026 ordinary tax late charge.** Code art. 228(3) and the BNM decision of 6 November 2025, point 1, supply the calculation: the retained **6.00%** short-term policy rate, rounded up to a whole percent, plus **5 percentage points**, divided by **365 days**, gives **0.0301% per delayed day** after rounding the daily percentage to four decimal places. The decimal multiplier is **0.000301**. Apply it to the established outstanding principal for each chargeable day; split changes in principal and calendar year. This calculation concerns debts governed by art. 228 and does not replace a separate statutory regime's rate or establish case-specific relief. [Code art. 228(2)–(3)](https://www.legis.md/cautare/getResults?doc_id=157050&lang=ro); [BNM decision, point 1](https://bnm.md/en/node/69226).

Map the offence and period, rather than multiplying one late-return fine by every missing document:

| Offence | Statutory fine | Provision |
|---|---|---|
|Late annual information|MDL30,000–50,000|260(7)|
|No annual information|MDL100,000–150,000|260(9)|
|Late TP file|MDL30,000–50,000|260(10)|
|No TP file|MDL300,000–500,000|260(12), with applicable transition exceptions|
|False information causing understatement/evasion|MDL60,000–90,000; special commencement below|260(8)|
|False file causing understatement/evasion|MDL150,000–200,000; special commencement below|260(11)|

Figures: [Code260(7)–(12)](https://www.legis.md/cautare/getResults?doc_id=157050&lang=ro). **Law214/2024 artXVII(2)** applies260(8),(11) in SFS audits of fiscal periods beginning with2028; do not charge those two penalties as2026-period sanctions merely because their amounts appear in the Code. This does not postpone the ordinary late/non-submission duties or the underlying tax/interest. The separate2024 file transition is in Law187 artXXVI(3). [Law214 official text](https://www.legis.md/cautare/getResults?doc_id=144601&lang=ro), [Law187](https://www.legis.md/cautare/getResults?doc_id=149593&lang=ro).

For the specific **article228(2^1) self-correction route**, the late charge is capped at the corresponding additional tax obligation. The paragraph covers discovery of errors/omissions followed by a corrected return, and the stated no-return-duty case where erroneous calculation/payment creates an additional obligation. Establish those conditions before applying the cap; it is not a universal cap on every audit debt, sanction or late charge. [Code art.228(2^1)](https://www.legis.md/cautare/getResults?doc_id=157050&lang=ro); exact source context is retained in ledger rowMD-TP-088.

Additional income/expense adjustments can change the relevant income/single tax. General understatement/evasion and self-correction provisions are in261(4)–(5)/188; establish the actual offence and interaction before stacking sanctions. Art228 charges late-payment interest from after due date through payment using its prior-November BNM base-rate-plus-five-points annual formula; case-specific relief and any applicable special single-tax rate must be established. An objection does not automatically suspend collection under252(2). [Code228,252,261,188](https://www.legis.md/cautare/getResults?doc_id=157050&lang=ro).

## Cross-border points

For a foreign corresponding adjustment,226²² requires the applicable double-tax treaty and a resident's application with evidence of the foreign affiliate's arm's-length adjustment/assessment. SFS has30working days to confirm, reject with reasons for insufficient basis/documents, or order an audit. Do not post an automatic equal tax credit or deduction from a foreign assessment. A treaty mutual-agreement route, beneficial ownership and the actual treaty/protocol are distinct questions covered by the cross-border Guide; no universal treaty outcome is assumed. [Code226²²,4](https://www.legis.md/cautare/getResults?doc_id=157050&lang=ro).

A bilateral/multilateral APA needs the treaty/foreign-authority process in226²⁵; a unilateral APA does not compel a foreign authority to accept the price. PE/head-office affiliation does not itself make every head-office charge deductible: test the substantive PE, interest, royalty and service rules in the cross-border/corporate Guides. VAT follows its own base and place rules and is not automatically changed by a TP adjustment. The group master file is not a country-by-country report; current CbC/reporting/exchange applicability remains expressly unconfirmed, not assumed from another jurisdiction's EUR750m threshold. [Code226¹⁷–226¹⁸,226²⁵](https://www.legis.md/cautare/getResults?doc_id=157050&lang=ro).

### 2. Audit CUP adjustment with two reliable comparable prices

Two genuinely comparable uncontrolled unit prices areMDL80 and100 after reliable adjustments; assume the audit has established comparability and non-arm's-length pricing. No extra costs or loss/credit offsets are assumed. This is not a proposed market price for an actual client, and the TP adjustment does not automatically alter VAT. ## Ask the client first

- What are the actual fiscal period, legal entity/branch/PE, residence and tax regimes of every potentially affiliated party? Obtain the complete voting/control/family structure and changes.
- Reconcile all sales/purchases/rights/services/financing flows excluding VAT. Which amounts are advances, interest/principal, currency trades versus FX accounting differences, revaluations or actually APA-covered transactions?
- What do contracts and real conduct show about functions, risk control/financial capacity, assets and DEMPE? Obtain segment accounts, allocation keys and evidence for intragroup services, intangibles, financing and restructurings.
- Which independent/internal comparables, adjustments, methods and tested-party data are available? What supports geography, timing, quantity, terms and the interval calculation?
- Has SFS requested a file, communicated an assessment or started an audit of either relevant affiliate? Obtain actual communications and submission receipts before deciding correction/deadline options.
- Obtain the group master/local files, language/signature authority, prior information filings, current annexes, actual APA decisions/assumptions and annual compliance reports. Is a foreign adjustment or bilateral/multilateral request supported by an actual treaty and foreign-authority evidence?
