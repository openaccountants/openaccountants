# Moldova company formation and entity choice, 2026 v0.2

## Who this applies to

Use this Guide to prepare an ordinary Moldovan SRL incorporation, compare the individual-entrepreneur registration route and screen an SA proposal. It supplies the current registration and continuing-compliance framework as read on 30 October 2026. It does not certify the full securities-registration procedure, regulated-sector approvals, every foreign-document treaty exemption or historical incorporation rules throughout 2026. Whole-country independent review is pending. [Law 135/2007 arts. 2, 9, 11–16][srl]; [Law 220/2007 arts. 7–14, 28, 35][register]; [Law 1134/1997 arts. 2–4, 38][sa].

An SRL has legal personality and capital divided into participation interests. It is formed on State registration; the company answers with its assets, while members' liability has the exceptions in article 9, including unpaid subscribed contributions. One or more permitted individuals or legal entities can found it. An SA divides capital into shares and has its own company/securities requirements. An individual entrepreneur registers the individual under Law 220 article 28. These legal choices do not themselves prove entitlement to a tax incentive. Do not describe one as universally best, safest or most common without evidence about the proposed business. [Law 135 arts. 2, 9, 11][srl]; [Law 1134 arts. 2–4][sa]; [Law 220 art. 28][register].

An ordinary SRL has no general numerical member cap. The article-11(4) exception limits a qualifying crowdfunding investment vehicle to **200 members**. A company board is compulsory with **more than 50 members**; at **50 members or fewer** the statute can provide for one. Build the actual governance arrangement into the statute rather than treating every multi-member SRL identically. [Law 135 arts. 11, 63][srl].

## The base

### Prepare the SRL decision, statute and contributions

Identify founders, proposed name and registered office, actual activities and any required permit, administrator, representation and governance, subscribed capital, each participation interest, contribution type and payment schedule. The full name includes the Romanian legal form and the abbreviated name `S.R.L.`. The statute is approved and signed by all founders or authorised persons and is prepared in Romanian. Its required contents are in article 13, including the founding persons, business activity, capital and interests, contribution method/deadline, office, governing bodies, representation and reorganisation/liquidation arrangements. [Law 135 arts. 3–5, 11–14][srl].

Cash is the presumed contribution unless the statute provides otherwise. Permitted contributions include property, property/intellectual-property rights and money. For in-kind contributions, the statute sets how the members approve the valuation results; ownership passes to the company unless the statute provides otherwise. Members cannot simply demand return of paid-in capital while the company operates. Record amounts subscribed, paid and outstanding separately; a nominal capital entry does not prove payment or satisfy all creditor protections. [Law 135 arts. 21–23][srl].

If State registration has not occurred within **three months from approval of the statute**, current Law 135 article 16(3) refers to Civil Code article 248(2). Do not replace the statutory approval-date trigger with the agency summary’s signing date or certify a founder’s release from contributions without reading the applicable Civil Code provision and actual constitution. That precise civil-law consequence remains a gap. [Law 135 art. 16(3)][srl].

### Assemble and submit the registration file

1. Check the name and office. For online submission, verify name availability before filing. The registered office is entered from the constituting documents; article 10 places responsibility for authenticity on the entity and does not authorise a universal extra premises-proof demand. [Law 220 arts. 9–10][register].
2. Obtain the registration application signed by the founder designated in the formation decision or the person authorised there, the decision and constituting documents approved by the founders, beneficial-owner information, the SA registrar information where relevant, and any additional act/opinion required by the applicable law. For a regulated field, the application declares the required prior approval and the registrar checks it through the prescribed data exchange. Beneficial-owner disclosure is required by article 7(1)(f); this Guide does not certify a complete AML ownership analysis. [Law 220 art. 7(1)–(1^1)][register].
3. For a foreign legal-entity investment, obtain the relevant registry extract and foreign constituting documents. Article 8(2^1) requires authorised Romanian translation and the applicable legalisation/apostille route, subject to actual treaty exemptions. Verify the document's origin and treaty rather than assuming all foreign or CIS documents are exempt. [Law 220 arts. 7(4), 8(2^1)][register].
4. Submit Romanian documents electronically with the prescribed electronic signatures, on paper at the registrar or by registered post. A representative needs the legally valid authenticated power or qualifying electronic-register authorisation. The registrar can obtain official electronic information directly where article 8 permits; keep the receipt listing the application number/date, submitted documents and planned release date. [Law 220 art. 8][register].
5. Pay the applicable registration charge and retain the complete-payment confirmation. Processing follows payment as specified in article 7(5). The registrar checks the file and issues registration or a reasoned refusal. Preserve the decision, statute and register information; the assigned **IDNO is also the entity's fiscal code**. The one-stop procedure transmits registration data for tax, statistical, medical and social records and gives the prescribed notice. This transmission does not prove a separate VAT-registration entitlement or fulfil every later filing. [Law 220 arts. 7(5), 11, 14][register]; [VAT Code art. 112][code].

For individual-entrepreneur registration, article 28 prescribes the application, article-8 submission route, identity/capacity verification and one-stop notifications. Do not apply the company statute/capital checklist mechanically to an individual. A person already registered as an individual entrepreneur, or judicially disqualified from the activity, engages article 30's refusal conditions. The current individual tariff and the ordinary application fields are supplied below; profession-specific authorisations remain separate. [Law 220 arts. 28–30][register].

### Complete the actual individual-registration application

The ASP individual-registration page links the current **3-6 application**, its separate instructions and **BE-8** beneficial-owner declaration. The application’s two pages, all three instruction pages and the declaration’s first page were retrieved and visually checked. These are blank filing templates, not a filed application or proof that an applicant qualifies. The separate legal-entity application and its beneficial-owner forms remain unverified. [ASP page][aspii]; [application][iiform]; [instructions][iiinstructions]; [BE-8][iibe8].

| Application part | Completion method from the actual form/instructions |
|---|---|
| Submission and service | Enter the actual submission date and requested **24-hour or four-hour** track. The ordinary working-day clock is distinct from the date the applicant types on the form |
| Individual identity | Name, personal identification number and valid identity-document data; domicile with postal code, street/building/apartment, locality/district and country; legible valid email and working phone/fax details |
| Legal form and activities | The form states **individual entrepreneur**. Enter the actual CAEM-2 activity code and name; instruction 12 separately addresses the wording for licensed activities. Neither the form nor an activity code grants the required licence |
| Beneficial owner | Complete the named person’s identity, birth and identity-document details, citizenship, domicile, residence country and contact fields using actual evidence. Do not substitute a corporate shareholder’s name for the required natural-person identification |
| Professional office and duration | Complete the professional-office address **only if different from domicile**. Enter the actual expiry date for a fixed term, or unlimited duration as applicable |
| Attachments and signature | List the supporting documents, reconcile the payment proof and beneficial-owner information on the current ASP checklist, and obtain the applicant’s or duly authorised representative’s signature. Apply the actual representation rule; a name typed into the form does not establish authority |

The form acknowledges one-stop fiscal, statistical, medical and social registration and warns about false/incomplete information. Keep the accepted filing/registration confirmation; the acknowledgement is not evidence that this particular applicant has been registered. Form 3-6’s note repeats the **three-day** register-data-change duty already in Law 220 article 29. [Application pages 1–2][iiform]; [instructions points 6–17][iiinstructions]; [Law 220 arts. 28–30][register].

**BE-8 is a separate declaration.** Its first page distinguishes the entrepreneur from a designated declarant and asks for the designation act’s name, number and date where applicable. It identifies the entrepreneur and natural-person beneficial owner, asks the stated PEP/family/close-associate questions and requires signature/date. The form says its annex is not attached when the PEP answer is “No”. Its printed **30-day** beneficial-owner-change undertaking must not be used to extend a separately applicable three-day register-data-change duty. The precise current Law 308/2017 article-14 interaction, designation/ownership tests and current PEP-list applicability remain unverified; the form’s checklist and the instructions’ older AML footnote do not settle them. Only BE-8 page 1 is used here; its five-page PEP annex has not been fully verified. [BE-8 page 1][iibe8]; [application note 3][iiform]; [Law 220 art. 29][register].

The ASP page’s general-information reference says “Law 308/2018”, while its document list and BE-8 identify Law **308/2017**. Retain the inconsistency and use the actual applicable law before completing an ownership analysis. Do not treat that unreconciled year reference or the incomplete footnote as a replacement for current legislation. [ASP checklist/general information][aspii]; [BE-8][iibe8].

## Deadlines and filing

| Continuing obligation | Method and deadline | Primary source |
|---|---|---|
| SRL contribution payment | Track each member's subscribed amount and full payment within six months of registration | [Law 135 art. 22][srl] |
| Ordinary company-register changes | Submit within **30 days** of the decision; article 16(2^1) has distinct triggers for in-kind capital increases, share issues and SA reorganisations | [Law 220 arts. 16–17][register] |
| Individual-entrepreneur changed data | Application and proof within **three days** of the change; registrar decision within **three working days** after submission | [Law 220 art. 29][register] |
| Accounting | Continuous from registration to liquidation; determine the legal entity/category, reporting framework and accounting policy | [Accounting Law arts. 2, 4–5, 18][accounts] |
| Annual individual financial statements | Present to owners and **BNS**: public-interest entities within **120 days**, other covered entities within **150 days** after the period's last day, with management/auditor reports as applicable | [Accounting Law art. 33(1)–(4)][accounts] |
| Consolidated accounts where required | Parent presents/publishes within **180 days** after the period's last day; assess the actual consolidation obligation | [Accounting Law art. 33(6)][accounts] |
| Ordinary annual corporate tax | By the **25th of the third month** after the income-tax period; for the ordinary calendar year, 25 March. Do not substitute the accounts deadline | [Code arts. 12^1, 83(4)(a), 87][code] |
| VAT/payroll and annual payer reports | Apply the actual VAT/payer status, transaction/payment/accrual period and approved form in the connected Guides | [Code arts. 92, 114–115][code] |

Accounting is kept in Romanian and national currency, with foreign-currency transactions also recorded in that currency under the standards. Article 5 distinguishes abbreviated, simplified, complete and IFRS reporting, and the pre-VAT individual cash-accounting route without financial statements. Determine the entity size from article 4's actual asset/sales/employee tests and category-change rules, not the incorporation label alone. New non-public-interest entities have article 21(7)'s first-period choice. The reporting period is ordinarily the calendar year; article 24 lists first-year, parent-aligned, reorganisation/liquidation and Ministry exceptions. [Accounting Law arts. 4–5, 7, 21, 24][accounts].

The responsible person signs before presentation. Mandatory audit includes medium/large entities, public-interest entities, other legally specified entities and the prescribed consolidated accounts. Audited statements and related management/audit reports are published on the entity's website within the article-33 deadline and remain available at least **five years**. The stated suspension exception is in article 33(6^1). A routine tax return is not a substitute for those accounts or audit/publication duties. Actual BNS/SNC templates, public-interest classification and a taxpayer's precise deadline-rollover calculation remain separate checks. [Accounting Law arts. 18, 31–33][accounts].

Use the [corporate](md-corporate-income-tax.md), [individual](moldova-income-tax.md), [VAT](moldova-vat.md), [payroll](moldova-payroll.md) and [social/medical](moldova-social-contributions.md) methods for tax compliance. Obtain the chosen bank's actual account-opening/KYC requirements after establishing the entity and authorised signatories; the predecessor's bank-account step is retained as an implementation requirement, not a verified promise about a bank's timing or acceptance.

## Penalties and interest

Missing required documents, non-compliant constitution, statutory restrictions and the other article-13 grounds can produce a reasoned registration refusal. The registrar cannot refuse merely for inexpediency; after curing the grounds the applicant can reapply, and refusal can be challenged in court. Article 4(8) states the prescribed non-refund rule. False formation information engages Law 135 article 19's joint liability, subject to its knowledge condition; failure to pay a subscribed contribution engages article 9(4) and article 22. General registration/accounting breaches carry the liability specified by the applicable law. This Guide supplies no invented universal registration fine. Tax late charges and filing sanctions follow the relevant tax Guide and actual debt. [Law 220 arts. 4(8), 13, 38][register]; [Law 135 arts. 9, 19, 22][srl]; [Accounting Law art. 34][accounts].

## Cross-border points

Retain the foreign founder's legal identity and authority, beneficial owners, translated/authenticated documents and the actual applicable treaty. Incorporation alone does not determine individual tax residence, foreign-company residence, permanent establishment or treaty entitlement. Screen where management, work and income occur using the [cross-border Guide](md-cross-border.md) and current Code scope; sector approvals and immigration/right-to-work questions need their own applicable rules. [Law 220 arts. 7–8][register]; [Code arts. 5, 13–14, 71–79^3][code].

## Two worked examples that recompute

### 1. Standard and urgent company-registration tariffs

Assume one ordinary company application, no fee relief and no separately priced services. The listed standard service costs 1,149 MDL; the four-times urgent fee is 4,596 MDL, an increase of 3,447 MDL. This is not a professional-service quote or a guarantee that a defective/incomplete file finishes within four hours. [ASP tariff][asp]; [Law 220 arts. 4–5, 7–8][register].

```oa-check
name: Moldova 2026 ordinary versus urgent company-registration tariff
inputs:
  ordinary_fee: 1149
  urgent_multiplier: 4
steps:
  urgent_fee: ordinary_fee * urgent_multiplier
  fee_increase: urgent_fee - ordinary_fee
expected:
  urgent_fee: 4596
  fee_increase: 3447
```

### 2. Subscribed SRL contributions and a separate SA capital screen

Assume an SRL statute subscribes 100,000 MDL; members have paid 40,000 and 35,000. The outstanding amount is 25,000 MDL and remains subject to the six-month rule. Separately, an ordinary SA proposal using only 20,000 MDL of capital is 580,000 MDL below the current 600,000 MDL article-38 floor. These assumptions do not establish the full SA formation/securities procedure or a sector's separate capital requirement. [Law 135 arts. 21–23][srl]; [Law 1134 art. 38][sa].

```oa-check
name: Moldova 2026 subscribed SRL balance and general SA capital screen
inputs:
  srl_subscribed: 100000
  first_paid: 40000
  second_paid: 35000
  sa_proposed: 20000
  sa_minimum: 600000
steps:
  srl_paid: first_paid + second_paid
  srl_outstanding: srl_subscribed - srl_paid
  sa_shortfall: max(0, sa_minimum - sa_proposed)
expected:
  srl_paid: 75000
  srl_outstanding: 25000
  sa_shortfall: 580000
```

## Ask the client first

Obtain the activities and regulated permissions; founders' legal form, countries, ownership/control and authority; actual office, management and work locations; desired governance, investment and liability arrangements; capital, contribution types/payment dates and any in-kind valuation; proposed registration channel/urgency and relief grounds; VAT, employees and existing registrations; accounting assets/sales/headcount/group and public-interest status; bank and foreign-document requirements. Use those facts to select the entity and procedure. Do not select an SRL, SA, individual or incentive solely from a headline tax rate.
