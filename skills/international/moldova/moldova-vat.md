# Moldova: VAT (TVA) (2026) v2.1

Moldova's VAT (taxa pe valoarea adăugată, TVA) is in Title III of the Fiscal Code and is collected by the State Tax Service (SFS). The tax period is the calendar month. Figures below are from the consolidated Fiscal Code on legis.md as amended to Law LP235 of 2 October 2026 (in force 30 October 2026), and from the two 2026 changes to the registration threshold.

## Who this applies to

- **Taxable subjects** (art. 94): businesses and individuals carrying on business, and non-residents with a permanent establishment in Moldova, who are or must be registered for VAT; anyone importing goods (except individuals importing goods for personal use within the legal limit); anyone importing services, registered or not (except social-political organisations); non-residents without a legal form in Moldova who supply electronic services to Moldovan individuals without a business; and buyers of property from VAT-registered businesses in insolvency, or of pledged, mortgaged or seized property.
- **Taxable transactions** (art. 95(1)): supplies of goods and services by taxable subjects as a result of their business in Moldova, imports of goods, imports of services, electronic services by non-residents to Moldovan consumers, and the property purchases above.
- **Registration is compulsory** for a business whose supplies in any 12 consecutive months exceed the threshold (art. 112(1)). Not counted: supplies exempt without the right to deduct and transactions outside the scope of VAT under art. 95(2).
- **Not required to register**: independent entrepreneurs under chapter 10^4 of Title II, and public authorities and institutions under art. 51 other than public education institutions (art. 112(1)).
- **Out of this skill**: excise duties and customs duty; the IT park and small-company regimes, which do not replace VAT (see [md-corporate-income-tax](md-corporate-income-tax.md) and [moldova-tax-optimization](moldova-tax-optimization.md)).

## The taxable base

**Exempt with the right to deduct** (art. 104), the Moldovan equivalent of zero rating: exports of goods and services; international transport of goods and passengers, including forwarding, international gas transport and listed airport and aircraft services; electricity, heat, hot water, water supply and sewerage for residential property; imports of natural gas, electricity and heat through networks; goods and services for technical-assistance and grant-funded projects listed by the Government; supplies into, out of and between free economic zones, the Giurgiulești port and the Mărculești airport, with exceptions for transport services and balancing energy; and listed processing services under inward processing. A tax invoice is not required for supplies under art. 104 a) (art. 117(1)).

**Exempt without the right to deduct** (art. 103(1)), main items: housing, land, residential lettings and land leases (but not agency commissions); listed baby food; privatised state property; education services of public and private institutions; state-fee services; care of the sick and elderly; medical services other than cosmetic, and medical raw materials; financial services; postal services; gambling; funeral services; residential accommodation in hostels and communal services to the population; domestic passenger transport; books and periodicals (not advertising or erotic); listed farm machinery, hail rockets, small hydro turbines and renewable-energy works; and certain cars and other vehicles in tariff headings 8703 as listed. Input VAT on purchases used for these supplies is not deductible and goes to cost (art. 102(2)).

**Imports.** Imports of goods are taxed at customs (art. 95(1) b), 100). Imports of services are taxed in Moldova: the buyer calculates VAT on the service, declares it and pays it (art. 95(1) c), 101(1)). A buyer who is not VAT-registered declares and pays VAT on imported services by the 25th of the month after the month the service was imported or paid, whichever is earlier (art. 115(2) b)). A registered buyer deducts the VAT it calculated on imported services used for taxable supplies (art. 102(1)). Imported services count toward the registration threshold (art. 112(4)).

**Place of supply of services** (art. 111): where the property is, for real-estate services, construction and accommodation; along the route, for transport; where performed, for services on movable goods, culture, education, sport, entertainment and transport-related services; where used, for hire of movable goods; and where the customer is established, for intellectual property, advertising, consulting, engineering, legal, accounting and marketing services, translation, information services, IT services delivered electronically, staffing, and agents acting for these. Electronic communications, broadcasting and radio services are also supplied where the customer is (art. 111(2)).

**Electronic services to consumers.** A non-resident without a legal form in Moldova that supplies services through electronic networks (software and databases, online advertising, marketplaces, hosting, data storage, domain names and similar, art. 95(1^1)) to Moldovan individuals without a business registers under art. 112^1, files quarterly and pays in MDL, USD or EUR (art. 94 d), 114(1^1), 115(1^2), (3)).

**Input VAT deduction** (art. 102). A VAT-registered business deducts VAT paid or payable to VAT-registered suppliers on goods and services bought for taxable supplies in its business. VAT on imported goods is deductible only once paid. VAT on purchases used for both taxable and exempt-without-deduction supplies is deducted by a monthly pro rata, rounded to two decimals: taxable supplies divided by taxable plus exempt-without-deduction supplies, without VAT and excluding advances. A final pro rata on the year's figures is applied in the last return of the year. If the exempt-without-deduction share is below 0.05, all of the common input VAT is deductible (art. 102(3)-(4)). VAT on purchases not used for the business, on goods stolen or lost beyond the limits set by management, and on the undepreciated value of written-off fixed assets, is not deductible (art. 102(8)).

**Deregistration.** When registration is cancelled, the business is treated as supplying its stock and the fixed assets on which it deducted VAT, at market value (for depreciable assets, the higher of book and market value), and pays VAT on that supply (art. 113(3)). The SFS may cancel a registration itself when no returns are filed or the address is false (art. 113(2)).

The SFS may cancel the registration of a business that files no returns (art. 113(2) b)). Assessments are time-barred after 4 years, up to 6 years where no return was filed or a refund is claimed (art. 264).

## Cross-border points

- **Exports** are exempt with the right to deduct (art. 104 a)), so input VAT on them stays deductible.
- **Services bought from abroad** are taxed in Moldova when the place of supply is Moldova under art. 111; the Moldovan buyer self-assesses (art. 95(1) c), 101(1)). For consulting, IT, advertising and similar services, the place of supply is where the customer is established (art. 111(1) e)).
- **Services sold abroad**: the same rule makes consulting, IT, advertising and similar services supplied to a foreign business customer supplied outside Moldova.
- **Non-resident e-service suppliers** to Moldovan consumers register, file quarterly and may pay in MDL, USD or EUR (art. 94 d), 114(1^1), 115(3)).
- **Energy from foreign sellers.** A registered buyer of network gas, electricity or heat from a seller not established in Moldova enters the VAT as both output and input VAT on receipt of the invoice (art. 101(1^1)).
- **Free economic zones, Giurgiulești port and Mărculești airport** have their own exemptions with deduction (art. 104 f), f1), i)).

## Worked examples

**Example 1. Monthly return, October 2026.** A VAT-registered food shop sells MDL 300,000 (net) of household goods at 20% and MDL 50,000 (net) of bread at 8% in October 2026. Its purchases for the business carry MDL 30,000 of VAT on valid tax invoices. Output VAT is MDL 60,000 + MDL 4,000 = MDL 64,000. Input VAT is MDL 30,000. It pays MDL 34,000 and files the October return by 25 November 2026.

```oa-check
name: Moldova monthly VAT return, 20% and 8%, October 2026
inputs:
  sales_standard: 300000
  sales_reduced: 50000
  input_vat: 30000
  standard_rate: 0.20
  reduced_rate: 0.08
steps:
  - output_standard: sales_standard * standard_rate
  - output_reduced: sales_reduced * reduced_rate
  - output_total: output_standard + output_reduced
  - payable: output_total - input_vat
expected:
  output_standard: 60000
  output_reduced: 4000
  output_total: 64000
  payable: 34000
```

**Example 2. Mixed taxable and exempt supplies, with an imported service.** A registered company lets commercial premises (taxable at 20%) for MDL 800,000 and residential flats (exempt without deduction) for MDL 200,000 in a month. Common running costs carry MDL 50,000 of input VAT. The exempt share is 0.20, above 0.05, so the pro rata applies: 800,000 / 1,000,000 = 0.80, and MDL 40,000 is deductible; MDL 10,000 goes to cost (art. 102(4)). The company also buys legal advice for the commercial lettings from a foreign law firm for MDL 10,000. The place of supply is Moldova (art. 111(1) e)), so the company calculates VAT of MDL 2,000 as output and deducts it as input (art. 101(1), 102(1)). Output VAT is MDL 162,000, input VAT MDL 42,000, and MDL 120,000 is payable.

```oa-check
name: Moldova VAT pro rata with imported service, 2026
inputs:
  taxable_supplies: 800000
  exempt_supplies: 200000
  common_input_vat: 50000
  imported_service: 10000
  rate: 0.20
steps:
  - exempt_share: exempt_supplies / (taxable_supplies + exempt_supplies)
  - pro_rata: round(taxable_supplies / (taxable_supplies + exempt_supplies), 2)
  - deductible_common: common_input_vat * pro_rata
  - to_cost: common_input_vat - deductible_common
  - reverse_charge_vat: imported_service * rate
  - output_vat: taxable_supplies * rate + reverse_charge_vat
  - input_vat: deductible_common + reverse_charge_vat
  - payable: output_vat - input_vat
expected:
  exempt_share: 0.20
  pro_rata: 0.80
  deductible_common: 40000
  to_cost: 10000
  reverse_charge_vat: 2000
  output_vat: 162000
  input_vat: 42000
  payable: 120000
```

## Ask the client first

1. Are you VAT-registered? If not, what were your supplies in each of the last 12 months, and do you buy services from abroad?
2. What do you sell: which goods or services, and do any fall under the 8% list, art. 103 (exempt) or art. 104 (exports and other exempt-with-deduction supplies)?
3. Do you sell to or buy from customers and suppliers abroad, and are your customers businesses or consumers?
4. Do you make both taxable and exempt supplies, so that the pro rata applies?
5. Are you on the SFS list for compulsory e-invoices, or do you have public-procurement contracts?
6. Are you in a free economic zone, the Giurgiulești port or the Mărculești airport?
