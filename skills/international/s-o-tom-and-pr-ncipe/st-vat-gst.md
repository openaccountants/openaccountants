# Sao Tome and Principe VAT / GST

## Value-added tax (IVA)

Sao Tome and Principe introduced a value-added tax (Imposto sobre o Valor Acrescentado, IVA) effective 1 June 2023, replacing several prior consumption and service taxes. It operates on standard EU/Portuguese VAT mechanics with input/output credit.

- **VAT in force** — Yes — IVA has applied since 1 June 2023  _(Codigo do Imposto sobre o Valor Acrescentado (IVA) — https://www.vatcalc.com/sao-tome-and-principe/sao-tome-and-principe-implements-15-vat-2023/)_
- **Standard VAT rate** — 15 percent  _(Codigo do Imposto sobre o Valor Acrescentado (IVA) — https://www.vatcalc.com/sao-tome-and-principe/sao-tome-and-principe-implements-15-vat-2023/)_
