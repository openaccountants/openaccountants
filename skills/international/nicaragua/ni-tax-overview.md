# Nicaragua Tax Overview

## Nicaragua tax system at a glance

Nicaragua operates a territorial tax system administered by the Dirección General de Ingresos (DGI), with income tax (Impuesto sobre la Renta, IR) and a 15% value-added tax (IVA) as the main taxes. The framework is set by Law 822, the Ley de Concertación Tributaria, as amended.

- **Tax year** — Calendar year (1 January – 31 December); companies may obtain authorisation for a 31 March, 30 June or 30 September year-end  _([Ley de Concertación Tributaria (Law 822)](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/nicaragua/corporate/tax-administration))_
- **Currency** — Nicaraguan córdoba (NIO / C$)  _(Ley de Concertación Tributaria (Law 822))_
- **Tax authority** — Dirección General de Ingresos (DGI)  _([Ley de Concertación Tributaria (Law 822)](https://www.dgi.gob.ni))_
- **Basis of taxation** — Territorial — only Nicaraguan-source income (income generated in, or causing effects in, Nicaragua) is taxed; foreign-source income is generally not taxed  _([Ley de Concertación Tributaria (Law 822)](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/nicaragua/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income))_
- **Top personal income tax rate (employment/labour income)** — 30 percent  _([Ley de Concertación Tributaria (Law 822)](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/nicaragua/individual/taxes-on-personal-income))_
- **Standard corporate income tax rate** — 30% of net taxable income (or a 1%–3% definitive minimum tax on gross income, whichever is higher) percent  _([Ley de Concertación Tributaria (Law 822)](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/nicaragua/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income))_
- **Value-added tax (IVA)** — Yes — standard rate 15%; exports zero-rated percent  _([Ley de Concertación Tributaria (Law 822)](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/nicaragua/corporate/other-taxes))_
- **Annual individual IR return deadline** — Within 90 days after year-end (Form IR-106)  _([Ley de Concertación Tributaria (Law 822) — Form IR-106](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/nicaragua/individual/tax-administration))_
- **Annual corporate IR return deadline** — Within 3 months (by 31 March) after a 31 December year-end ((approx — confirm; some guides cite 2 months / 28 February for the IR return))  _([Ley de Concertación Tributaria (Law 822)](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/nicaragua/corporate/tax-administration))_
- **Double taxation treaties** — Nicaragua has no income tax treaties in force; statutory WHT rates cannot be reduced by treaty  _([Ley de Concertación Tributaria (Law 822)](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/nicaragua/corporate/withholding-taxes))_
