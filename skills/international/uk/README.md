# United Kingdom: tax pack

Tax year 6 April to 5 April for individuals; companies use their own accounting period. Authority: HM Revenue and Customs (HMRC). Pound sterling. Self assessment for individuals, corporation tax for companies, VAT at 20% with a GBP 90,000 registration threshold, PAYE for payroll, National Insurance as the social contribution.
