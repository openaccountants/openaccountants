## Section 2 — The New FIG Regime (From April 2025)

- **Abolition of remittance basis** — The UK abolished the remittance basis / domicile-based non-dom regime from 6 April 2025.
- **Replacement regime** — The 4-year Foreign Income and Gains (FIG) exemption.
- **Who qualifies for FIG** — Any individual who becomes a UK tax resident on or after 6 April 2025 AND was not UK tax resident in any of the preceding 10 tax years.
- **What is exempt under FIG** — All foreign income (dividends, interest, rent, employment income from foreign employer) arising while the individual is in their first 4 years of UK tax residence; all foreign capital gains arising while in the first 4 years.
- **What is NOT exempt under FIG** — UK-source income and gains — taxed normally regardless of FIG status; foreign income/gains arising AFTER the 4-year window.
- **FIG window is absolute** — No extension, no exceptions. After 4 tax years of UK residence, the individual is taxed on worldwide basis from year 5 onwards.
- **No election needed** — FIG applies automatically. The individual may elect OUT (e.g. if they want to use foreign losses against UK income) but there is no requirement to file a special claim.

## Section 3 — Transitional Provisions: Existing Non-Doms

For individuals who were already using the remittance basis before 6 April 2025:

- **Option 1 — Temporary Repatriation Facility (TRF)** — A 3-year window (2025/26, 2026/27, 2027/28) to bring previously unremitted foreign income and gains to the UK at a reduced flat rate.
- **TRF reduced flat rate 2025/26 and 2026/27** — 12% % (amounts remitted in 2025/26 and 2026/27)  _(https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/residence-domicile-and-remittance-basis/rdrm73400)_
- **TRF reduced flat rate 2027/28** — 15% % (amounts remitted in 2027/28)  _(https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/residence-domicile-and-remittance-basis/rdrm73400)_
- **After TRF window** — After 2027/28, unremitted pre-2025 income remitted to the UK is taxed at full normal rates.
- **Option 2 — Rebasing of foreign capital gains** — Foreign assets (other than non-UK real estate) can be rebased to their 5 April 2019 market value for CGT purposes. Only applies to individuals who claimed the remittance basis in 2016/17 or earlier.

## Section 4 — Before April 2025: Remittance Basis (History)

- **Old regime mechanics** — UK residents who were not domiciled in the UK could elect for the remittance basis. Foreign income and gains were taxed only when remitted to the UK.
- **Annual remittance basis charge (after 7 years UK residence)** — £30,000 GBP (after 7 years UK residence)
- **Annual remittance basis charge (after 12 years UK residence)** — £60,000 GBP (after 12 years UK residence)
- **Deemed domicile after 15 years** — After 15 years UK residence: deemed domiciled — remittance basis no longer available.

This system is now history for new residents. The FIG regime replaced it.

## Section 5 — Overseas Workday Relief (OWR)

- **Overseas Workday Relief (OWR)** — OWR continues under the new regime. OWR allows UK-resident employees to exclude from UK tax the proportion of employment income attributable to non-UK workdays (days physically working outside the UK).
- **OWR availability under FIG regime** — Under the new FIG regime, OWR is available for the first 4 years of UK residence (aligned with FIG), without the remittance condition that applied under the old regime.
- **UK-taxable employment income formula** — UK-taxable employment income = Total employment income × (UK workdays / Total workdays)
- **Recordkeeping requirement** — Requires keeping a contemporaneous workday log.
