# Malawi Tax Overview

## Malawi tax system at a glance

- **National tax authority** — Malawi Revenue Authority (MRA)  _([Malawi Revenue Authority Act](https://www.mra.mw/))_
- **Currency** — Malawi Kwacha (MWK / MK)  _(Reserve Bank of Malawi Act)_
- **Standard tax/charge year** — 1 April to 31 March (Year of assessment; companies may use an approved alternative accounting date)  _(Taxation Act (Chapter 41:01))_
- **Basis of taxation** — Source-based; residents and non-residents taxed on income arising in or derived from Malawi (Malawi taxes primarily on a source basis rather than worldwide income)  _(Taxation Act (Chapter 41:01))_
- **Top personal income tax (PAYE) rate, 2024/2025** — 35 percent (Reform effective 1 Jan 2026 reintroduced a 40% top band (approx — confirm))  _([Taxation Act (Chapter 41:01), Eleventh Schedule (PAYE)](https://www.mra.mw/assets/upload/downloads/PAYE_Brochure_English_April_2024-March_2025.pdf))_
- **Standard corporate income tax rate** — 30 percent  _(Taxation Act (Chapter 41:01))_
- **Does Malawi have VAT?** — Yes — VAT applies; standard rate 16.5% in 2025 (raised to 17.5% from 31 Dec 2025) percent  _([Value Added Tax Act (Cap 42:02)](https://www.mra.mw/individuals/value-added-tax-vat))_
- **Corporate income tax return deadline** — Within 180 days of the company's financial year end  _(Taxation Act (Chapter 41:01))_
- **PAYE remittance deadline** — 14th day of the month following the month of deduction  _([Taxation Act (Chapter 41:01)](https://www.mra.mw/individuals/what-is-pay-as-you-earn-paye))_
- **Withholding tax remittance deadline** — Within 14 days of deduction  _([Taxation Act (Chapter 41:01), Fourteenth Schedule](https://www.mra.mw/individuals/withholding-tax))_
