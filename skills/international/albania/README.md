# Albania: tax pack

Calendar tax year. Authority: the General Directorate of Taxes (Drejtoria e Përgjithshme e Tatimeve), tatime.gov.al; returns are filed on e-Filing. Currency: Albanian lek (ALL). One income tax law, Law No. 29/2023, covers both individuals and companies: corporate income tax at 15%, with a 0% rate until 2029 for businesses with gross income up to ALL 14 million. VAT (TVSH) under Law No. 92/2014: 20% standard rate, registration mandatory above ALL 10 million of turnover, monthly returns. Not an EU member. Procedures and penalties are in Law No. 9920/2008.
