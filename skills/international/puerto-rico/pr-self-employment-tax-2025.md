# Puerto Rico: federal self-employment tax for 2025

## Federal income exclusion is not the end of the calculation

Puerto Rico-source income excluded from federal income tax under section 933 can still carry federal self-employment tax. Publication 570 specifically directs affected Puerto Rico residents to the federal self-employment filing rules. This Guide uses **2025 earnings and the 2026 filing cycle**, not the 2026 social-security wage cap. [IRS Publication 570, Puerto Rico / Self-Employment Tax](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p570); [2025 Form 1040-SS instructions](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i1040ss).

A territory resident without a US income-tax return filing requirement uses **Form 1040-SS**, with **Schedule SE**, for the relevant self-employment obligation. If a federal income-tax return is required, use the applicable income-tax return and Schedule SE rather than assuming 1040-SS replaces it. A local Hacienda return is a separate question. [Form 1040-SS instructions, Purpose and Who Must File](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i1040ss); [Schedule SE instructions](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i1040sse).

## Net earnings and threshold

The ordinary threshold is **USD 400 or more of net earnings from self-employment**, not USD 400 of customer receipts. Under the regular method, Schedule SE line 4a multiplies positive combined net profit by **92.35%**. Line 4c includes any optional-method amount, then applies the USD 400 test. Church-employee income and approved religious exemptions have separate provisions. Do not apply the simple example below to those cases. [2025 Schedule SE, lines 1–6](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/f1040sse.pdf); [Schedule SE instructions](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i1040sse).

For multiple businesses, the Schedule SE instructions combine relevant net profits and losses. Spouses each calculate their own self-employment tax, including on a joint return; one spouse's unused social-security cap is not transferred to the other. Partnership income, rental activity and entity classification require their own analysis. [Schedule SE instructions, More Than One Business and Joint Returns](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i1040sse).

## Ordinary 2025 rates and wage interaction

Schedule SE applies:

- **12.4% Social Security** to the smaller of relevant self-employment earnings and the unused part of the **USD 176,100** combined 2025 wage/earnings limit.
- **2.9% Medicare** to the relevant self-employment earnings, without that Social Security cap.

Social-security wages, tips and specified other compensation reduce the remaining Social Security limit. Reaching the wage cap does not eliminate the Medicare calculation. [Schedule SE, lines 7–12](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/f1040sse.pdf).

Assume ordinary sole-trade net profit of **USD 50,000**, no optional method, no special exemptions and no other self-employment amounts:

| Step | No employee wages | USD 170,000 Social Security wages |
|---|---:|---:|
| Earnings: 50,000 × 92.35% | 46,175.00 | 46,175.00 |
| Remaining Social Security cap | 176,100.00 | 6,100.00 |
| Social Security component | 46,175 × 12.4% = 5,725.70 | 6,100 × 12.4% = 756.40 |
| Medicare component | 46,175 × 2.9% = 1,339.075 | 46,175 × 2.9% = 1,339.075 |
| Schedule SE total, rounded to cents for illustration | **7,064.78** | **2,095.48** |

These are pre-return-rounding illustrations of Schedule SE only. The wage example can also require Additional Medicare Tax depending on filing status and other facts. Do not label either amount the taxpayer's entire federal or Puerto Rico liability. Source calculation: [Schedule SE](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/f1040sse.pdf).

## Additional Medicare Tax

An additional **0.9%** calculation applies under the Form 8959 rules when the relevant earnings exceed the filing-status threshold: **USD 250,000 married filing jointly; USD 125,000 married filing separately; USD 200,000 single, head of household or qualifying surviving spouse**. Relevant wages reduce the remaining threshold for self-employment earnings, not below zero. This is separate from the ordinary 2.9% amount above. [Schedule SE instructions, Additional Medicare Tax](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i1040sse).

## Filing and payment

For calendar-year 2025, the regular Form 1040-SS deadline is **15 April 2026**. The Puerto Rico section of its instructions permits a **six-month filing extension to 15 October 2026** by timely Form 4868. That extension does not extend payment; interest runs from the original due date on unpaid tax. Check any specific disaster relief rather than assume a general postponement. [Form 1040-SS instructions, When To File and Extension of Time To File](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i1040ss).

Retain business income and expense records, the Schedule C/F inputs, all wage statements and the separate Schedule SE for each spouse. Estimated federal payments require their own Form 1040-ES analysis; paying Hacienda does not itself discharge an IRS self-employment liability. [Publication 570, Puerto Rico / Estimated Tax Payments](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p570); [Schedule SE and its instructions](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i1040sse).
