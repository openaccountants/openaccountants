# Ireland: tax pack

Calendar tax year. Authority: the Revenue Commissioners; returns and payments go through ROS and myAccount. Euro. Residents who are Irish domiciled are taxed on worldwide income; residents who are not domiciled here use the remittance basis on foreign income and foreign gains. Individuals pay income tax at 20% and 40%, plus USC and PRSI. Companies pay corporation tax at 12.5% on trading income and 25% on passive income, with the Pillar Two 15% minimum for groups in scope. VAT follows EU rules, standard rate 23%.
