# Dominica: business registration and tax onboarding

## Choose the registration route

CIPO distinguishes a business name from a company's corporate name. A sole trader or partnership using a name different from the proprietor names, or a company trading under a different name, falls within its business-name guidance. CIPO states that these business names must be registered. A business-name application does not establish that a company has been incorporated. Identify the legal owner before selecting forms. [CIPO business-name guidance](https://cipo.gov.dm/businesses-and-companies/business-names/).

This Guide covers domestic business names and the ordinary company application workflow. It does not certify the complete Companies Act amendment chain, beneficial-ownership duties, external-company or international-business-company regimes, all sector permissions, fees or processing times. No registration has been submitted as part of this research.

The former IBC regime was repealed from 1 January 2022. See the [former-IBC Guide](dm-former-ibc-regime-and-records.md) for the primary repeal source, historical transition and surviving records duties. Older registry descriptions do not establish a current IBC incorporation route.

## Business-name application

The Business Names Rules, SRO 26 of 2012, commenced on **1 October 2012**. Rule 6 prescribes:

| Applicant | Form |
|---|---|
| Individual | BN1 |
| Firm or partnership | BN2 |
| Company using a business name | BN3 |

Use the forms in the [Rules' Schedule](https://www.cipo.gov.dm/files/business-names-rules.pdf#page=5), or confirm the current equivalent with CIPO. The registry's narrative page refers to 2011 rules, while its law index links the enacted 2012 instrument; this Guide follows the retrieved instrument.

Rules 3–5 prohibit identical or misleadingly similar registered names and restrict misleading affiliations. Government or recognised university/professional-association connections need the specified written consent; the Rules also prohibit names implying political-party or leader connections. A name registered contrary to the rules can be cancelled. Keep the name decision and required consent documents. [Rules 3–5](https://www.cipo.gov.dm/files/business-names-rules.pdf#page=1).

Rule 7 requires activity descriptions using ISIC. BN1 asks for proprietor identity/contact details, any former names, business status and start date, existing TIN, social-security employer status, intended employees, activity codes and business address. BN2 collects partner details and provides a statutory-declaration procedure if all partners do not sign. Do not omit partners or sign another person's declaration without the prescribed authority. [Rules 6–7 and Schedule](https://www.cipo.gov.dm/files/business-names-rules.pdf#page=3).

Rule 8 permits the Registrar to send application information to Inland Revenue and, where employees are intended, Social Security. That permission is not evidence that either registration has actually completed. [Rule 8 and Schedule](https://www.cipo.gov.dm/files/business-names-rules.pdf#page=4).

## Business-name deadlines and continuing duties

The retrieved Registration of Business Names Act, Chapter 78:46, and Act 20 of 2001 establish the following business-name duties. These are distinct from company annual returns and tax filings. The CIPO law index lists the base Act, the 2001 and 2008 amendments, and the 2001 and 2012 Rules, all read for this topic. This is not proof that no other amendments exist. Act 4 of 2008 changes the definition of Registrar to the Registrar of Companies, with deemed effect from 1 November 2007; it does not alter the deadlines below. [CIPO law index](https://www.cipo.gov.dm/laws/business-names/); [Act 4 of 2008, sections 3–4](https://www.cipo.gov.dm/files/business-names-act4-2008.pdf#page=2).

| Event | Statutory requirement | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Starting a business that requires registration | Furnish the registration particulars within **14 days** after commencement. If a name change triggers registration, count from that change. | [Act, section 7](https://www.cipo.gov.dm/files/business-names-act.pdf#page=7) |
| Change in registered particulars | Furnish a prescribed written statement of the nature and date of the change within **14 days**. The Registrar can allow longer on application made before or after that period expires. Sign and, where required, verify it as for the original registration. | [Act, section 8](https://www.cipo.gov.dm/files/business-names-act.pdf#page=7) |
| Annual business-name return | Every registered firm or business must submit its annual return **on or before 2 April each year**, with the prescribed filing fee. | [Act 20 of 2001, section 5 inserting section 23](https://www.cipo.gov.dm/files/business-name-registration.pdf#page=2) |
| Annual register fee | A firm or business on the register on **31 December** must pay the annual fee **on or before 2 April of the following year**. The annual fee and the return's prescribed filing fee are separate statutory requirements; see the separately identified fees below. | [Act 20 of 2001, section 5 inserting sections 22–23](https://www.cipo.gov.dm/files/business-name-registration.pdf#page=2) |
| Registered firm or individual stops carrying on business | The partners at cessation, the individual, or the deceased individual's personal representatives must deliver the prescribed cessation notice within **three months**. Receipt permits the Registrar to remove the entry. | [Act, section 15](https://www.cipo.gov.dm/files/business-names-act.pdf#page=10) |

The annual return includes partner or proprietor names, residential and business addresses, telephone/fax/email details if any, and a **statement of assets and liabilities**. Retain the submitted return, payment evidence and registry acknowledgement. [Inserted section 23(2)–(3)](https://www.cipo.gov.dm/files/business-name-registration.pdf#page=2).

Do not treat a business-name cessation notice as evidence of tax deregistration or company winding-up. This section establishes only the business-name register procedure. Registration also does not authorise use of a name that could otherwise be prohibited. [Act, sections 15–16](https://www.cipo.gov.dm/files/business-names-act.pdf#page=10).

## Published business-name fees

The CIPO fee page retrieved on 22 October 2026 lists the following amounts in **EC dollars**. The legislation distinguishes the registration application amount from the statement fee, and the annual fee from the annual-return filing fee. Do not present any one line as an all-inclusive registration price.

| Item | EC$ | Legislative basis |
|---|---:|---|
| Application for registration | 70 | Act 20 of 2001, section 3 amending section 5(1) |
| Statement of particulars | 20 | SRO 35 of 2001, rule 3(a) |
| Each deponent to a statutory declaration | 10 | SRO 35 of 2001, rule 3(b) |
| Annual fee | 50 | Inserted section 22 and SRO 35 of 2001, rule 3(c) |
| Annual-return filing | 10 | SRO 35 of 2001, rule 3(d) |

Sources: [CIPO fee page](https://www.cipo.gov.dm/fees/business-names/), [Act 20 of 2001](https://www.cipo.gov.dm/files/business-name-registration.pdf#page=2), and [SRO 35 of 2001](https://www.cipo.gov.dm/files/business-names-rules-2001.pdf). These are registry charges, not professional fees or a quotation for all formation costs. This table does not establish company-incorporation charges or sector-permission fees.

## Ordinary company application

CIPO identifies **Form 1 plus Form 9A for each director** for ordinary incorporation. Its workflow allows electronic preparation but still instructs applicants to print, obtain signatures, deliver the forms and pay the registration fees. Treat data entry as an application stage; retain the issued certificate as completion evidence. [CIPO company instructions](https://www.cipo.gov.dm/businesses-and-companies/companies/).

The retrieved Form 1 requests the company type, proposed name, registered office and separate business address, share classes and authorised/issued numbers, transfer restrictions, directors, activities, proposed start, financial year-end, employee/import-export intentions and incorporator signatures. It says the first financial year must not exceed twelve months. Resolve these facts before signing. This is the published form's requirement, not a completed review of every statutory accounting-period rule. [Form 1](https://www.cipo.gov.dm/forms/bnc/Application-Incorporation-companies-act.pdf).

The form requires confirmation that no incorporator is under eighteen, adjudged of unsound mind or bankrupt, and includes an undertaking to notify Social Security of employees. Its declaration uses “less than 18”, whereas the registry narrative says “over eighteen”; this draft follows the actual form wording without certifying the full eligibility law. [Form 1, declaration](https://www.cipo.gov.dm/forms/bnc/Application-Incorporation-companies-act.pdf#page=2).

The retrieved [Form 9A](https://www.cipo.gov.dm/forms/bnc/From9A-director-of-company.pdf) records director identity, National Insurance number, occupation, address and signed, dated consent to act. It instructs applicants to leave the company number and appointment date blank for a new company. Its fields do not establish director eligibility.

## Tax and employer onboarding

CIPO says company-incorporation information is shared with Inland Revenue for automatic taxpayer registration and that tax confirmation accompanies the incorporation certificate. It separately states that taking on employees still requires **PAYE registration**. Where employees are intended, information is also shared with Social Security for employer registration. Check the actual confirmations and PAYE status rather than treating one certificate as proof of every tax enrolment. [CIPO company instructions, tax and social security](https://www.cipo.gov.dm/businesses-and-companies/companies/).

For each file, retain the legal registration, tax confirmation/TIN, employer registration confirmation, PAYE registration evidence and any required activity permissions. Use the separate [VAT Guide](dm-vat.md) and [social-contribution Guide](dm-social-contributions.md) for their stated scope and unresolved checks. Business-name registration alone does not establish VAT liability or registration.

The IRD's [older startup page](https://www.ird.gov.dm/customer-service/starting-a-business) also describes business registration and activity licences, but contains an older VAT threshold. This Guide does not repeat that threshold or certify licence fees from that page. Determine permissions for the actual activity and premises separately.

## Continuing obligations and remaining work

CIPO identifies ongoing company filings including director/registered-office changes, annual returns and accounts or solvency certification. The [company annual-return Guide](dm-company-annual-returns.md) distinguishes the 1 April return deadline from the 2 April annual fee. Other statutory deadlines, entity-specific exceptions, beneficial ownership and company fees require further checking before a complete compliance calendar is certified. [CIPO company guidance](https://www.cipo.gov.dm/businesses-and-companies/companies/).
