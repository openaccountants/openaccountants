# Gibraltar payroll and social insurance v0.15

> **Source-cited draft.** Professional review is pending. Figures are limited to the periods and assumptions below. This Guide prepares working papers; it is not authority to file or pay.

## Who this applies to

Prepare ordinary monthly payroll controls using the social-insurance schedule effective 1 July 2026. Special contribution classes, partial periods, employment-law entitlements and cross-border coverage require their own determination.

Sources were accessed on 22 October 2026. A dated rate table controls its stated period; an undated guidance page does not prove that all later amendments have been incorporated.

## Rates, bands and thresholds

### Monthly ordinary contribution table

For the ordinary earnings-related class, the published schedule effective **1 July 2026** gives:

| Contributor | Rate | Monthly minimum | Monthly maximum |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Employee | 10% | £62.11 | £176.76 |
| Employer | 18% | £138.55 | £243.65 |
| Self-employed, separate SE class | 20% | £131.95 | £232.05 |

[ITO 2026/27 contribution-class schedule](https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/uploads/Income%20Tax%20Office/Self-Employed/Social%20Insurance%20Contributions%20Class%202026-2027x.pdf). The schedule contains separate pensioner, married-woman, trainee and exemption classes. Do not assign them from age or job title without confirming the issued class and eligibility.

### Other confirmed contribution classes

|---|---:|---:|

Select the issued class and its supporting eligibility evidence. The table is not authority to self-award an exemption. D1 and DW differ; PN employment and XX pension income differ. T2 has no minimum printed in its row. [2026/27 schedule, monthly columns](https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/uploads/Income%20Tax%20Office/Self-Employed/Social%20Insurance%20Contributions%20Class%202026-2027x.pdf).

**Class examples:** with confirmed monthly earnings of £1,000, MW gives £69.12 employee plus **min(£173.98, max(£78.65, £200)) = £173.98** employer. PN gives zero employee and £180 employer. T2 gives **min(£16.99, £100) = £16.99** employee and zero employer. An applicable DW exemption gives zero for both. These compare issued classes, not eligibility choices.

### Use the correct frequency

The ER weekly limits are £14.34–£40.79 employee and £31.98–£56.22 employer, at 10% and 18%. Annual ER limits are £745.29–£2,121.21 employee and £1,662.57–£2,923.83 employer. At confirmed weekly earnings of £300, the amounts are £30 and £54. Do not derive an annual limit from rounded monthly figures: £176.76 × 12 = £2,121.12, whereas the published annual employee maximum is £2,121.21. Use the appropriate published frequency; this difference alone does not establish an entitlement to a year-end adjustment. [2026/27 schedule, ER row](https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/uploads/Income%20Tax%20Office/Self-Employed/Social%20Insurance%20Contributions%20Class%202026-2027x.pdf).

### Selecting the PAYE table and handling irregular payments

The employer page's **2026/27** section links daily, weekly, monthly and annual tables. The daily/monthly files checked here still use a **20252026 URL folder**; preserve both the current linking page and PDF, rather than inferring a document's applicable year from its path alone. The employer notes remain explicitly grouped under 2025/26. Confirm any mismatch affecting the actual payroll with ITO before reliance. [Current employer table index](https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/income-tax-office/employers-and-trusts/employers).

The notes distinguish **Part 1, Allowances Based System**, with the employee's code column, from **Part 2, Gross Income Based System**, with its tax-deduction column. A code identifies a table column; it is not a percentage. Retain the P3, code/system and pay frequency before looking up tax. Their ordinary method uses the frequency of payment, even if wages are calculated at a daily rate. [Employer notes, sections 3–5 and 9–10](https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/uploads/Income%20Tax%20Office/PAYE/20252026/Notes%20for%20guidance%20of%20employers%202025-2026_%20(002).pdf).

For partial weekly/monthly service, the notes divide pay by days worked, look up the daily amount and multiply that daily tax by the days worked. They use weekly tables for fortnightly/four-weekly pay (divide and multiply by two/four), monthly tables for quarterly/half-yearly pay (three/six), and annual tables for annual pay. These are the dated notes' methods, not permission to average arbitrary bonuses or allocate every lump sum over a chosen period. Confirm continued application and the precise day count/rounding for the case. [Sections 4–5](https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/uploads/Income%20Tax%20Office/PAYE/20252026/Notes%20for%20guidance%20of%20employers%202025-2026_%20(002).pdf).

For cessation lump sums, the notes require an **ITO directive before payment**, including loss-of-office compensation, ex-gratia payments, pension commutation and pay in lieu of notice. Do not calculate the deduction using an ordinary monthly example without that direction. Section 8 separately distinguishes part-time/occasional payments and separately paid spare-time commission from commission included with normal monthly salary; uncertain classification is referred to ITO. This Guide does not establish a blanket tax exemption for termination payments. [Sections 6 and 8](https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/uploads/Income%20Tax%20Office/PAYE/20252026/Notes%20for%20guidance%20of%20employers%202025-2026_%20(002).pdf).

## The base

### PAYE deduction and certificate rules

The PAYE Regulations define emoluments to include office/employment income, pensions, cash gratuities and benefits in kind. Payment includes credit to an account on which the employee can draw. Regulation 3 requires withholding even without an assessment and even where the payment relates to another assessment year. Do not postpone withholding solely because a bonus relates to earlier service. [Regulations 2–3](https://www.gibraltarlaws.gov.gi/uploads/legislations/income-tax/1989s114/1989s114(01-07-23).pdf).

Regulation 8(1) uses gross emoluments, the employee's code and the official table on each payday. **If neither the required allowances certificate nor the applicable leaver certificate has been lodged, regulation 8(2) requires code X.** Do not substitute a guessed personal allowance or zero withholding while awaiting a code. Regulation 8(3) adds the proportionate money value of benefits to gross emoluments; valuation and exemptions require separate evidence. A Commissioner direction under 8(4) can specify a different deduction. An agreement not to deduct cannot override the duty. [Regulations 6, 8](https://www.gibraltarlaws.gov.gi/uploads/legislations/income-tax/1989s114/1989s114(01-07-23).pdf).

For an employee taking employment without either relevant certificate, regulation 4(1) requires an allowances declaration within seven days. A change affecting allowances must be declared within 30 days under 4(5). Part II is lodged with the higher-paying employer if there are multiple employers; where the employee receives a pension, the pension payer receives it unless the Commissioner directs otherwise. Do not apply the same allowances independently to every job. [Regulations 4–5](https://www.gibraltarlaws.gov.gi/uploads/legislations/income-tax/1989s114/1989s114(01-07-23).pdf).

On cessation, the employer must furnish the leaver certificate and send part 1 to the Commissioner immediately on cessation. The new employer can use the certificate's code pending the allowances certificate and must send part 3 immediately after engagement where parts 2 and 3 were lodged. Regulation 13 separately requires the annual certificate within one month after year-end for employees employed on 30 June from whose emoluments tax was deducted. [Regulations 13–13A](https://www.gibraltarlaws.gov.gi/uploads/legislations/income-tax/1989s114/1989s114(01-07-23).pdf).

### Contribution liability: wages, absence and employer cost

Section 7(1) places initial payment responsibility for both the employer's and the insured employee's contribution on the employer, unless regulations prescribe otherwise. **The employer's share cannot be deducted from wages or otherwise recovered from the employee, even by agreement.** Recovery of the employee's share is subject to the regulations and section 7(3): where there is no wage or other monetary remuneration from any person in respect of the employment, recovery is prohibited; where the employer pays wages, recovery must be by deduction from them. Do not assume unpaid deductions can be collected in any later payroll without checking the recovery rules. [Social Security (Insurance) Act, section 7(1)–(3)](https://www.gibraltarlaws.gov.gi/uploads/legislations/social-security/1955-14/1955-14(01-07-25).pdf).

Section 5(2) disregards employment for a contribution week only under its combined test: no services are rendered, and no remuneration is paid wholly or partly for a day in the week other than the specified incapacity/non-working-day exceptions. A blank payment run alone does not establish the test. Identify the service dates, remuneration entitlement and period to which payments relate. Sick pay and holiday pay need that analysis; do not treat every absence as exempt. [Section 5(2)](https://www.gibraltarlaws.gov.gi/uploads/legislations/social-security/1955-14/1955-14(01-07-25).pdf).

Section 7(4) separately provides that no contribution is payable in respect of an employee for a week during the whole **or any part** of which she is absent exercising her statutory maternity-leave right. Obtain the leave record and establish that statutory right; this is not a general exemption for every parental absence or a determination of benefit entitlement. [Section 7(4)](https://www.gibraltarlaws.gov.gi/uploads/legislations/social-security/1955-14/1955-14(01-07-25).pdf).

Section 5(1) limits the insured person's and employer's contributions by the weekly maxima in Schedule 1. For multiple employments, establish the exemption/allocation procedure rather than independently treating each job as exempt or silently applying a separate unlimited aggregate cap. That operational allocation remains to be verified. [Section 5(1), (4)](https://www.gibraltarlaws.gov.gi/uploads/legislations/social-security/1955-14/1955-14(01-07-25).pdf).

### Partial months and shared contribution weeks

The 2025/26 employer notes use weekly minima/maxima multiplied by weeks or part-weeks actually worked for a partial month. They combine two paydays falling in the final contribution week before applying its cap. Their rates match the 2026/27 schedule checked here; retain the guidance's stated year and confirm its continued procedural application for the actual payroll. [Employer notes, pages 9–10](https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/uploads/Income%20Tax%20Office/PAYE/20252026/Notes%20for%20guidance%20of%20employers%202025-2026_%20(002).pdf).

Assuming two confirmed contribution weeks and £100 earnings, employee SI is **max(£10, 2 × £14.34) = £28.68** and employer SI **max(£18, 2 × £31.98) = £63.96**. Assuming three weeks and £1,800 earnings, the caps give **3 × £40.79 = £122.37** and **3 × £56.22 = £168.66**. Each assumes ordinary ER liability and no exemption. [Partial-month method and limits](https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/uploads/Income%20Tax%20Office/PAYE/20252026/Notes%20for%20guidance%20of%20employers%202025-2026_%20(002).pdf).

For two £300 paydays in one final contribution week, the first deductions are £30/£54; combined £600 reaches both caps, leaving **£10.79 employee and £2.22 employer** on the second payday. Do not apply a second weekly maximum. [Shared-week method](https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/uploads/Income%20Tax%20Office/PAYE/20252026/Notes%20for%20guidance%20of%20employers%202025-2026_%20(002).pdf).

### Small-employer contribution credit

Regulation 17A permits an employer with **no more than ten employees at any time in the assessment year** to claim £100 per employee, capped at £1,000. This is not the preceding-year average-headcount test used for P8 penalties. A company/business in its first trading year uses twenty employees and a £2,000 cap instead. The per-employee amount remains £100. [Social Insurance (Contributions) Regulations, regulation 17A(1), (5)](https://www.gibraltarlaws.gov.gi/uploads/legislations/social-security/1955.08.04-5/1955.08.04-5(01-07-15).pdf).

The claim requires all SI and PAYE amounts to have been paid on the due date under 17A(2); 17A(6) identifies the due date as on or before the 15th of the following month. Do not treat arrears cleared at year-end as automatically satisfying timely payment. If employer contributions for an employee total less than £100, that employee's credit is restricted to the actual total. Once granted, the credit offsets employer contribution liabilities in the following year. It is not a current employee deduction or an automatic income-tax refund. [Regulation 17A(2)–(6)](https://www.gibraltarlaws.gov.gi/uploads/legislations/social-security/1955.08.04-5/1955.08.04-5(01-07-15).pdf).

**Example:** an eligible employer never exceeds eight employees. Seven employees each generated at least £100 of employer contributions; the eighth generated £60. Subject to timely-payment and claim conditions, the credit is **7 × £100 + £60 = £760**, below the £1,000 cap, for offset in the following year once granted. A continuing business that reached eleven employees fails the stated headcount condition even if it ended the year with eight. A first-year business with fifteen qualifying employees, each with at least £100 employer contributions, has a provisional **£1,500** credit under the enlarged limit. These examples assume confirmed employee counts; staff turnover/counting details and the current claim form/channel remain unverified.


### Classify and value ordinary employment benefits

Schedule 7 paragraph 1 includes salary/wages/fees, money or money's-worth employment profits and other emoluments. Choose the applicable Chapter before valuing a benefit; paragraph 38 excludes from the residual Chapter benefits covered by another Chapter or its stated exception. Keep the provider, recipient/family connection, reason for provision, dates, costs, market/rental evidence and amounts made good. The tax value is not automatically cash wages, a social-insurance earnings base or permission to deduct the employer's cost from wages. [Income Tax Act, Schedule 7 paragraphs 1, 37–38 and 40–46](https://www.gibraltarlaws.gov.gi/uploads/legislations/income-tax/2010-21/2010-21(23-03-26).pdf).

**Expenses, vouchers and cards:** paragraph 6 charges the employment-expense payment to the extent it exceeds expenses paid away wholly, exclusively and necessarily in the course of the employment. Under paragraph 7 a qualifying cash voucher is valued at the money for which it can be exchanged. Paragraph 8 values a qualifying non-cash voucher at provision cost less employee reimbursement; paragraphs 9–10 give the separate credit-token cost rule. Retain the employment/family, receipt/appropriation, public-terms exclusions and statutory timing conditions, including the later-cost/receipt rule and cheque-voucher use/posting rule. Paragraph 11 prevents double counting the resulting money/goods/services, with its employment-expense qualification. Do not value every voucher at face value or tax both voucher and redeemed goods. [Schedule 7 paragraphs 6–11](https://www.gibraltarlaws.gov.gi/uploads/legislations/income-tax/2010-21/2010-21(23-03-26).pdf).

**Living accommodation:** where Chapter 4 applies, paragraphs 14–17 use the rental value for the taxable period, substituting the provider's higher actual rent where required, less the employee's properly attributable payment; the result is nil if that payment meets/exceeds rental value. Annual value is the specified reasonable year-to-year rent with tenant charges and landlord repairs/insurance assumptions. Shared accommodation uses a just-and-reasonable split of one total cash equivalent, not the full value charged to every occupant. Paragraph 18 sends annual-value disputes to the Tribunal. The paragraph 13 specific-employee exception needs the relocation, former-home distance, Commissioner-determined skills and employer-application conditions, change notification and annual benefit declaration. It lasts four years from Gibraltar employment commencement and once per employee; relocation alone is not an exemption. [Schedule 7 paragraphs 12–19](https://www.gibraltarlaws.gov.gi/uploads/legislations/income-tax/2010-21/2010-21(23-03-26).pdf).

**Cars and vans:** first establish employment-related availability without a property transfer and private use under paragraphs 20–23, including the vehicle definitions (a van's design weight is at most 3,500 kilograms). Private use is presumed unless prohibited by the terms and not actually made. Paragraph 26 uses 25% per annum of employer cost for each of the first four years of availability, then 0%; for a vehicle the provider does not purchase, use the higher provision cost/market value, less amounts made good. A cash alternative alone does not trigger earnings under paragraph 24. The pool exception requires all paragraph 27 conditions, including actual multiple use, no ordinarily exclusive user, merely incidental private use and the overnight-location restriction. Paragraph 39 requires a separate calculation then just-and-reasonable reduction for qualifying shared private-use cars. For employment-provided car fuel within paragraph 28’s chargeability condition, value is 90% of employer fuel cost less amounts made good. Provision includes discharged fuel liabilities, voucher/card fuel or money spent on fuel, and fuel-expense payments; retain the paragraph’s printed paragraph-23 reference rather than silently substituting another. No unsupported partial-year convention or fuel/private-use exemption is assumed here. [Schedule 7 paragraphs 20–28 and 39](https://www.gibraltarlaws.gov.gi/uploads/legislations/income-tax/2010-21/2010-21(23-03-26).pdf).

**Employment-related loans:** paragraphs 29–31 include credit and arranging/guaranteeing/facilitating a loan, the defined employer/connected-provider and recipient categories, and the actual outstanding/employment period. The taxable cheap-loan value is comparable interest from an unconnected bank/building society on similar non-interest terms minus interest actually paid. No UK official interest rate or universal loan-size exemption is imported. Paragraph 32 excludes the loan to the extent hypothetical interest qualifies for the specified tax relief/trading deduction. Paragraph 33's necessary/overnight-expense advance exception needs the £1,000 outstanding limit throughout the year, spending within six months and regular accounting, with its expense definitions. Paragraph 34 apportions joint-loan value justly/reasonably. Write-off under paragraph 35 is earnings on release/write-off, subject to paragraph 36's death rules; it is not merely another year's interest benefit. [Schedule 7 paragraphs 29–36](https://www.gibraltarlaws.gov.gi/uploads/legislations/income-tax/2010-21/2010-21(23-03-26).pdf).

**Residual benefits and assets:** paragraphs 40–41 use provision expense, including a proper proportion of shared costs, less the employee's reimbursement. Paragraph 42 separately values an asset made available without transfer at the higher annual-use value or rent/hire, plus additional expenses; annual-use value is rental value for land or 20% of the relevant initial market value for other assets. Paragraph 43 uses market value at transfer for a used/depreciated asset; paragraph 45 defines open-market value. Do not substitute book value or employer cost for that transfer rule, or use the residual asset percentage for a car covered by Chapter 5. A private health-policy benefit and any personal health-insurance deduction need separate charging and deduction analyses. [Schedule 7 paragraphs 37–46](https://www.gibraltarlaws.gov.gi/uploads/legislations/income-tax/2010-21/2010-21(23-03-26).pdf); [Allowances Rules 20A–20B](https://www.gibraltarlaws.gov.gi/uploads/legislations/income-tax/1992s016/1992s016(23-03-26).pdf).

**Pension caution:** paragraph 48 charges employer contributions to its non-approved personal-pension arrangements, without double charging the same income. Its definition points to Allowances rule 3(17), whose current printed text instead concerns approved-fund investment income. The employee FAQ states its administrative distinction between approved occupational/personal schemes and retirement annuity contracts; that statement does not repair the statutory cross-reference or establish every actual scheme's treatment. Keep approval and scheme documents and the exact unresolved reference; do not claim a blanket employee exemption from the employer's deduction or a scheme's label. [Schedule 7 paragraph 48](https://www.gibraltarlaws.gov.gi/uploads/legislations/income-tax/2010-21/2010-21(23-03-26).pdf); [Allowances rule 3(17)](https://www.gibraltarlaws.gov.gi/uploads/legislations/income-tax/1992s016/1992s016(23-03-26).pdf); [ITO pension-contribution FAQ](https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/income-tax-office/individuals-and-employees/paye).

**Independent valuation illustrations:** assume every stated charging, timing and non-exemption condition is established. An expense payment of £2,000 against £1,500 paid away on the qualifying employment expenses leaves £500. A non-cash voucher with £600 provision cost and £100 made good gives £500. A full first-year employer-owned qualifying car costing £40,000 with £2,000 made good gives £8,000. For a separate established paragraph-28 fuel case, £2,000 employer fuel cost and £300 made good give £1,500. A distinct £50,000 full-year loan with independently evidenced 5% comparable interest and £1,000 paid gives £1,500; 5% is an assumed comparable rate, not a statutory rate. With established full-year rental value £18,000, provider rent £24,000 and employee payment £6,000, the accommodation value is £18,000. A separate residual non-car asset with relevant market value £10,000, hire £2,500, additional expense £300 and employee reimbursement £100 gives £2,700. These are separate benefit-base examples before any aggregate threshold, applicable deduction or PAYE calculation.

### Benefit threshold, employer-paid tax and dispensations

Paragraph 73 states that no return/tax is required for total employee benefits **less than/below £250** in an assessment year, subject to any replacement sum specified by legal notice. The current employee FAQ instead says the “first £250” is exempt. Those are different formulations: this Guide does not automatically subtract £250 from every larger benefit package or decide the exact-£250 boundary from the FAQ. Keep the annual aggregate, specific exceptions and the operative primary wording; a case affected by that difference needs the applicable determination. [Schedule 7 paragraph 73](https://www.gibraltarlaws.gov.gi/uploads/legislations/income-tax/2010-21/2010-21(23-03-26).pdf); [ITO benefit FAQ](https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/income-tax-office/individuals-and-employees/paye).

Under paragraph 74 the employer can elect to pay the employee's benefit tax, with no additional employee tax on the employer-paid tax itself. The printed rates apply to the otherwise taxable benefit value: 20% where the value is between £250 and £15,000, and 29% where it is above £15,000. The latter is not described as a marginal rate on only the excess. With independently established otherwise taxable values of £1,000 and, in a separate case, £20,000, tax is £200 and £5,800. These assumptions do not resolve paragraph 73's aggregate/boundary issue or permit deducting the employer's tax from wages. The current employer page lists P10/P10A for benefits and employer-paid benefit tax and a 31 July annual reporting date; retain the actual form year, election, return and payment evidence. [Schedule 7 paragraph 74](https://www.gibraltarlaws.gov.gi/uploads/legislations/income-tax/2010-21/2010-21(23-03-26).pdf); [employer reporting FAQ](https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/income-tax-office/employers-and-trusts/employers).

For a dispensation, paragraphs 70–72 require a statement of the relevant payments/benefits/arrangements and the Commissioner's notice agreeing no additional tax is payable; it is not self-approved. Paragraph 75 limits the relief/reporting effect to covered facts, prevents the specified further employment-expense allowance and keeps the dispensation in force until written revocation. Paragraphs 76–78 permit revocation from its original or a later date, restoring the relevant liabilities and requiring the affected returns within 30 days of the notice unless a longer period is allowed in writing. Keep the statement, notice, covered scope, changes and revocation record. A published FAQ or a requested dispensation is not that notice. [Schedule 7 paragraphs 70–78](https://www.gibraltarlaws.gov.gi/uploads/legislations/income-tax/2010-21/2010-21(23-03-26).pdf).

### Court recovery is not an employee wage deduction

Social Insurance regulation 7 concerns the court-recovery circumstances and related sums payable to the Fund. Paragraph (6) expressly says an employer's payment under those provisions satisfies unpaid contributions and **no part is recoverable from the insured person**. That is a positive restriction for those sums, not a general answer authorising collection of an ordinary missed employee deduction from later wages. The Act's section 7 restrictions and the unresolved ordinary recovery mechanism remain as stated above. Keep the order, contribution period, employer payment and employee deductions distinct. [SI regulation 7(1)–(8), especially 7(6)](https://www.gibraltarlaws.gov.gi/uploads/legislations/social-security/1955.08.04-5/1955.08.04-5(01-07-15).pdf).

### Computation

1. Confirm class, pay frequency, full/partial period, and earnings base before selecting a schedule.
2. For a confirmed full-month ordinary class with an established contribution liability, compute employee `min(176.76, max(62.11, earnings × 10%))` and employer `min(243.65, max(138.55, earnings × 18%))`.
3. Use the employee's tax code and correct-year official tax tables for PAYE. Contribution rates are not income-tax rates.
4. Reconcile gross wages, PAYE, employee contributions, other deductions, net wages and employer cost separately.
5. Reconcile payroll totals to remittances and the annual return. Never multiply a rounded monthly cap to substitute for an official annual table.

**Examples:** full-month ordinary gross earnings £3,000 give employee SI **£176.76**, employer SI **£243.65** and gross-plus-employer-SI **£3,243.65**, before other employer costs. At £1,000, the respective amounts are **£100** and **£180**. A person with an exemption code must not be put through this formula. The full-month formula does not determine liability for zero/very low earnings or unpaid weeks. Apply the statutory absence conditions and partial-month controls above; unresolved eligibility and recovery questions still require review.

These checks use **established liability, issued class and contribution periods**. They do not decide whether contributions are due, which class applies, or how concurrent employments should be allocated. They also do not calculate PAYE from a social-insurance rate.

At £1,000 monthly contribution earnings, MW, PN and T2 give different employee/employer amounts. These checks validate each assigned class; they are not a menu of tax-saving elections. Use confirmed contribution weeks, not days divided by seven or a guessed fraction of the monthly cap. ### Self-employed contribution administration

Regulation 4A requires registration immediately when weekly self-employed contribution liability arises and a contribution schedule. It requires the quarterly schedule to be received within fifteen days after the contribution quarter, with the required signed declaration (including the accompanying declaration for a non-paper schedule). Regulation 4B separately requires monthly recording within seven days after month-end and payment within fifteen days after month-end. A quarterly schedule does not defer the monthly payment. [Regulations 4A–4B](https://www.gibraltarlaws.gov.gi/uploads/legislations/social-security/1955.08.04-5/1955.08.04-5(01-07-15).pdf).

For example, on an ordinary September month-end with no special arrangement, the statutory periods require monthly recording by **7 October** and payment by **15 October**. This example does not establish the contribution-quarter calendar or any weekend/public-holiday extension. Keep registration, schedules and payment receipts separately.

Where an employed contribution is payable and the same person is also liable as self-employed **for the same contribution week**, regulation 4(7) makes only the employed contribution payable. Establish the actual overlapping weeks and liabilities; having a payroll job for part of a year is not an annual exemption from self-employed contributions. Operational coding and supporting evidence still need confirmation. [Regulation 4(7)](https://www.gibraltarlaws.gov.gi/uploads/legislations/social-security/1955.08.04-5/1955.08.04-5(01-07-15).pdf).

The ITO's **SE1** asks for trading commencement, business structure, other paid employment and company directorship/shareholding details. ### Contribution assessments and appeals

The Director can assess unpaid or underpaid contributions under regulation 4C. The notice must identify the amount, payment place and appeal rights. A dispute is made by written notice addressed to the Director **within fourteen days from service**. Late permission requires a specified reasonable impediment and an application without unreasonable delay once it ends. Additional contributions under an amended assessment are payable in full within one month after service of that notice. Keep the notice and evidence of service; do not substitute the 28-day income-tax assessment appeal period. [Regulation 4C(1)–(5)](https://www.gibraltarlaws.gov.gi/uploads/legislations/social-security/1955.08.04-5/1955.08.04-5(01-07-15).pdf).

### Employer contribution records and cessation

Social Insurance (Contributions) regulation 4(1) incorporates Employment Injuries (Contributions) regulations 2–8 except where expressly varied or inconsistent with the Act or the SI regulations. Read those limits before applying the cross-reference. [SI regulation 4(1)](https://www.gibraltarlaws.gov.gi/uploads/legislations/social-security/1955.08.04-5/1955.08.04-5(01-07-15).pdf).

Under the incorporated regulation 5, both weekly-paid and monthly-paid remuneration require recording the contribution class, number and amount within seven days after the **week** in which remuneration is paid. The monthly-paid provision also says week; do not replace it with month. Contributions are payable within fifteen days after the month, and remuneration paid in advance requires contributions in advance for that period. The self-employed monthly-recording rule above is different. [Employment Injuries regulations 5(1)–(4)](https://www.gibraltarlaws.gov.gi/uploads/legislations/social-security/2001s032/2001s032.pdf).

Regulation 4 requires the employer's contribution schedule to reach the Director within fifteen days after each relevant contribution quarter. Regulation 8 separately requires the schedule and contribution remittance forthwith on termination, or within seven days where the employee terminates without notice/intimation; death also requires forthwith return and payment. Do not assume the usual monthly cycle overrides this cessation requirement. Confirm the current electronic process for satisfying it. [Regulations 4(7), 8](https://www.gibraltarlaws.gov.gi/uploads/legislations/social-security/2001s032/2001s032.pdf).

Regulation 7 prohibits unauthorised alteration, erasure, defacement or destruction of the contribution schedule. Preserve the original and obtain the authorised correction route. Regulation 4(8)'s employer-schedule declaration text unexpectedly refers to a self-employed signatory; this drafting mismatch needs administrative confirmation and is not authority to omit a declaration. [Regulations 4(8), 7](https://www.gibraltarlaws.gov.gi/uploads/legislations/social-security/2001s032/2001s032.pdf).

### Contributions paid in error

SI regulation 5 incorporates Employment Injuries regulations 9–12, subject to its express substitution. Regulation 9 requires a written application to the Director for contributions paid under an erroneous belief that they were payable. Regulation 10 gives these periods:

- Paid on the due date: apply within **two years of payment**.
- Paid late: apply within **two years of the due date or twelve months of actual payment, whichever ends later**.

Use actual due/payment dates and the required application form/manner. This is not permission to deduct a disputed amount from the next remittance. SI regulation 6 separately lets the Director allocate wrong-rate payments against the contributions properly payable. [SI regulations 5–6](https://www.gibraltarlaws.gov.gi/uploads/legislations/social-security/1955.08.04-5/1955.08.04-5(01-07-15).pdf); [Employment Injuries regulations 9–10](https://www.gibraltarlaws.gov.gi/uploads/legislations/social-security/2001s032/2001s032.pdf).

**Date example:** a contribution due and paid on 15 August 2025 has a two-year reference endpoint of 15 August 2027. If due on 15 August 2025 but paid on 15 December 2026, the competing endpoints are 15 August 2027 and 15 December 2027; the later endpoint is **15 December 2027**. Submit before the endpoint and confirm exact deadline computation, form and receipt requirements; no holiday extension is assumed.

SI regulation 5(2) substitutes a benefit-deduction provision for incorporated regulation 12: deduct relevant benefits paid because of the erroneous contributions, including amounts paid to another person on that basis. Accordingly, do not simply transplant the Employment Injuries regulation 12 employer/employee refund-recipient rule into an SI claim. Establish recipient, deductions and net refund under the applicable regime. [SI regulation 5(2)](https://www.gibraltarlaws.gov.gi/uploads/legislations/social-security/1955.08.04-5/1955.08.04-5(01-07-15).pdf).

## Penalties and interest

Regulation 11(3) permits the Commissioner, at discretion, to increase late-paid PAYE by **2% for each month of delay, compounded**. This is not an automatic flat 2% penalty, and these PAYE regulations do not establish the SI penalty. Assuming the discretion is exercised and two monthly increments apply to £1,000 with no intervening payment, the amount is **£1,000 × 1.02 × 1.02 = £1,040.40**, including £40.40 penalty. Confirm the actual delay periods, payments and determination before applying the example. Interaction with general Income Tax Act surcharge provisions remains unverified; do not stack penalties without resolving it. [Regulation 11(3)](https://www.gibraltarlaws.gov.gi/uploads/legislations/income-tax/1989s114/1989s114(01-07-23).pdf).

### Payroll records and late production/P8 penalties

Regulation 17 requires production, when called for, of wage sheets and records relating to remuneration calculations/payments, deductions and accounting for deducted tax. Keep the notice and its specified date alongside the records. Regulation 17(2)–(2B) applies the following amounts for missing the specified production or P8 date, based on **average employees in the preceding assessment year**:

| Employer size | Next day after specified date | Additional if failure continues after 30 days | Combined amount at that stage |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Below 10 | £50 | £250 | £300 |
| 10 to below 50 | £100 | £500 | £600 |
| 50 to below 250 | £250 | £1,250 | £1,500 |
| 250 or more | £500 | £2,500 | £3,000 |

Exactly 10 enters the second row; exactly 50 the third; exactly 250 the fourth. Use the preceding-year average, not today's payroll headcount. These administrative amounts are distinct from regulation 19 offences and from payment penalties. The Commissioner has a discretionary power to extend time limits under regulation 16; a requested extension is not an approved extension. [Regulations 16–17](https://www.gibraltarlaws.gov.gi/uploads/legislations/income-tax/1989s114/1989s114(01-07-23).pdf).

This section does not establish the averaging method for unusual staffing histories, applicable standard-scale criminal fine values, or all penalty interactions. Record those as case-specific review questions rather than substituting company-size thresholds from the corporation-tax return rules.

## Cross-border points

This is a **separate social-security agreement**, not the income-tax treaty's 183-day employment rule. Gibraltar's Social Security (United Kingdom) Order 2024 commenced on **1 June 2024** and modifies the listed domestic social-security legislation to implement the scheduled agreement. Article 2 covers qualifying legally resident persons whose situation is not confined wholly to one Party; establish its personal scope and applicable benefit branch before using a coordination rule. This section does not determine all transitional or third-country cases. [Order paragraphs 2–4; agreement Article 2, printed page 10](https://www.gibraltarlaws.gov.gi/uploads/legislations/social-security/2024s086/2024s086.pdf).

### Posting and the ordinary applicable-legislation rule

Article 9(1) assigns a person to one Party's legislation under Part II. Subject to the specified exceptions, Article 9(4)(a) assigns employed/self-employed activity in one Party to that Party's legislation. Article 10 then provides:

- An employee working for an employer normally carrying on activities in Party A, and performing work for that employer in Party B, remains under A's legislation where the **anticipated duration does not exceed 24 months**.
- A person normally self-employed in A who goes to perform a **similar activity** in B remains under A's legislation where the anticipated duration is no more than **24 months**.

The expected duration is a condition at assessment, not an automatic first-24-month allowance for a project known to last longer. Employer activity, the continuing employment relationship and actual assignment facts need evidence. Article 13(1) permits the relevant authorities or designated bodies to agree exceptions in the interests of particular persons/categories; an employer cannot approve its own extension. [Articles 9–10 and 13, printed pages 12–15](https://www.gibraltarlaws.gov.gi/uploads/legislations/social-security/2024s086/2024s086.pdf).

**Boundary examples:** a planned 24-month assignment can meet Article 10's duration limb if all other conditions are satisfied. A planned 25-month assignment does not meet that limb merely because its first payroll occurs within month one; assess the normal rules and any authority-agreed exception. A self-employed person moving to a different activity does not establish Article 10(2) eligibility solely by keeping the stay below 24 months. These are legal-condition examples, not certificates of coverage.

### Normally working in both territories

For employment normally pursued in both Parties, Article 11(1) applies residence-territory legislation if a substantial part of the activity is performed there. If not, paragraph (2) differentiates employer locations: all employers in one Party leads to that Party; employers in both Parties leads to the Party other than residence; the specified cases involving employers outside the agreement lead to residence. Establish the complete employer structure. This Guide has not verified a numerical administrative definition of **substantial**, so it does not invent a percentage. [Article 11(1)–(2), printed page 14](https://www.gibraltarlaws.gov.gi/uploads/legislations/social-security/2024s086/2024s086.pdf).

For self-employment in both Parties, paragraph (3) uses residence where a substantial part is pursued there, otherwise the stated centre-of-interest rule. For employment in one Party alongside self-employment in the other, paragraph (4) selects the employment Party; employment in both requires the provision's further cross-reference and a complete determination. Paragraph (6) treats all relevant activities and income as arising in the selected Party for that legislation. Do not split contributions between jurisdictions merely because there are two payers. [Article 11(3)–(6)](https://www.gibraltarlaws.gov.gi/uploads/legislations/social-security/2024s086/2024s086.pdf).

**Mixed-work example:** assume an in-scope person normally employed in Gibraltar and self-employed in the UK, with no overriding exception. Article 11(4) selects Gibraltar legislation; paragraph (6) brings both activities and income into that selected system. This does not mean the UK self-employment is taxed as salary or that all receipts automatically use the employee contribution rate. Determine the domestic contribution classification/base separately. Income-tax allocation remains a separate analysis.

### Employer obligations and specialist cases

Article 14 requires an employer based in the other Party to meet the selected legislation's obligations, including contributions, as though based in that selected Party. Employer and employee can agree that the employee performs payment obligations on the employer's behalf, with notice to the relevant authority, but the employer's underlying obligations remain. This is not permission to deduct the employer's economic cost from wages. [Article 14, printed page 15](https://www.gibraltarlaws.gov.gi/uploads/legislations/social-security/2024s086/2024s086.pdf).

Civil servants, seafarers and aircrew have separate provisions in Articles 9 and 11. Article 12 treats voluntary/optional continued insurance separately, including a conditional pension exception to the general overlapping-insurance restriction. Do not apply the ordinary posting route to those cases without checking the relevant provision. [Articles 9, 11–12](https://www.gibraltarlaws.gov.gi/uploads/legislations/social-security/2024s086/2024s086.pdf).

### Certificate and payroll evidence

HMRC's [UK inbound guidance](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/social-security-contributions-for-workers-coming-to-the-uk-from-the-eea-or-switzerland) identifies a certificate of coverage, also called a PDA1, as evidence of continued Gibraltar contributions and exemption from UK National Insurance. It directs applicants to the relevant social-security authority and distinguishes the certificate from a work permit. Retain the issued document and covered dates; an application is not an issued certificate. For Gibraltar's local detached-worker enquiries, the ITO publishes **paye.detachedworkers@gibraltar.gov.gi** and **20071071**. [ITO contact](https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/income-tax-office/employers-and-trusts/detached-workers).

Keep assignment dates, expected duration and changes, employer activity evidence, employment/self-employment classification, work locations, residence, other employers, the authority determination and any exception agreement together. Reconcile the decision with the D1/DW payroll categories already shown above; a foreign address alone establishes neither. Gibraltar-issued certificate application mechanics, multiple-territory application routes, transitional history, third-country/Spain situations, benefit entitlement and special-category rulings remain open. No contact has been made or exemption obtained by preparing this Guide.

### Applying to HMRC for UK coverage during work in Gibraltar

**Employees:** HMRC identifies **CA3822** for temporary employment in Gibraltar. Employers must first have HMRC confirmation of business eligibility, requested through **CA3821**; HMRC says this confirmation is needed once. Employer, employee or authorised agent can use the online route. Applications cannot be made more than **12 months in advance**. Prepare identity/nationality details, the sending employer's PAYE reference and applicable residence, work-right and employment-history information. An agent submits the client's **64-8** authority with the application. The current page has removed its postal application option. [CA3822 instructions](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/tell-hmrc-about-employees-going-to-work-in-the-european-economic-area-ca3822).

For an extension, supply the existing expiry date and changed circumstances. Where the combined duration exceeds the permitted period, the instructions require a signed explanation of why continued UK contributions are in the person's interests, subject to the applicable agreement. This is a request, not automatic permission. The page's healthcare paragraph does not list Gibraltar; do not promise an S1 solely from this application. [CA3822 extension and healthcare sections](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/tell-hmrc-about-employees-going-to-work-in-the-european-economic-area-ca3822).

**Self-employed applicants:** use **CA3837** for temporary self-employment in Gibraltar, directly or through an agent. The same **12-month advance-application limit** applies. Prepare identity/nationality information, business address, the work performed in each country, and evidence of how the UK business is maintained, such as premises, equipment, registration or advertising. The form also asks for the UK start date where self-employment began less than **two months** before the overseas start; that question is not itself proof of a universal minimum qualifying period. [CA3837 instructions](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/apply-for-a-certificate-or-document-if-self-employed-in-the-eu-eea-or-switzerland-ca3837).

Agents include **64-8** authority. Apply online with sign-in; identity verification can be requested, and answers can be saved. For extensions, provide current expiry and changes; the stated over-limit route requires the signed explanation under the relevant agreement. The page no longer offers a postal application. Its healthcare paragraph likewise does not establish Gibraltar S1 entitlement. [CA3837 application and extension sections](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/apply-for-a-certificate-or-document-if-self-employed-in-the-eu-eea-or-switzerland-ca3837).

These are UK-coverage application routes for the described temporary work, not Gibraltar-issued certificate forms or a complete multiple-territory work procedure. Use the official form selector where classification differs. Retain the submitted application, acknowledgement, issued certificate, dates, conditions and subsequent changes as distinct records. The Article 10 duration conditions and any Article 13 exception agreement above remain separate from the application's administrative lead time.

## Two worked examples that recompute

### Example 1: confirmed full-month ordinary social insurance

For confirmed full-month ER liability, compare £500, £1,000 and £3,000 of established contribution earnings. £500 tests the floors; £1,000 tests the percentage amounts; £3,000 tests both ceilings. Employer SI is added to employer cost, not withheld as an employee contribution. The £500 example does not establish minimum-wage compliance or liability for unpaid weeks. [2026/27 schedule, ER monthly columns](https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/uploads/Income%20Tax%20Office/Self-Employed/Social%20Insurance%20Contributions%20Class%202026-2027x.pdf).

```oa-check
name: Gibraltar full-month ER floor middle and cap
inputs:
  low_earnings: 500
  middle_earnings: 1000
  high_earnings: 3000
steps:
  - low_employee: min(176.76, max(62.11, low_earnings * 0.10))
  - low_employer: min(243.65, max(138.55, low_earnings * 0.18))
  - middle_employee: min(176.76, max(62.11, middle_earnings * 0.10))
  - middle_employer: min(243.65, max(138.55, middle_earnings * 0.18))
  - high_employee: min(176.76, max(62.11, high_earnings * 0.10))
  - high_employer: min(243.65, max(138.55, high_earnings * 0.18))
  - high_employer_cost_before_other_costs: high_earnings + high_employer
  - high_wages_after_employee_si_before_other_deductions: high_earnings - high_employee
expected:
  low_employee: 62.11
  low_employer: 138.55
  middle_employee: 100
  middle_employer: 180
  high_employee: 176.76
  high_employer: 243.65
  high_employer_cost_before_other_costs: 3243.65
  high_wages_after_employee_si_before_other_deductions: 2823.24
```

The last result is **not net pay**: PAYE and any other authorised deductions still need to be applied. Gross plus employer SI is likewise not the complete cost of employment.

### Example 2: confirmed current-linked monthly PAYE and remittance components

Assume a confirmed full monthly period, code 35/Part 1, ER liability and no other benefits or deductions at gross pay £1,623.50. The table gives PAYE £125.89; SI is £162.35 employee and £243.65 employer. Pay after those deductions is **£1,335.26** and salary plus employer SI is **£1,867.15**. Any further deduction or employer cost must be added separately. [Monthly table, page 14](https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/uploads/Income%20Tax%20Office/PAYE/20252026/Monthly%20Part%201%20Codes%2019%20-%2036.pdf).

```oa-check
name: Gibraltar monthly code35 current-linked table reconciliation
inputs:
  gross_pay: 1623.50
  verified_paye_table_tax: 125.89
steps:
  - employee_si: min(176.76, max(62.11, gross_pay * 0.10))
  - employer_si: min(243.65, max(138.55, gross_pay * 0.18))
  - pay_after_specified_deductions: gross_pay - verified_paye_table_tax - employee_si
  - salary_plus_employer_si: gross_pay + employer_si
expected:
  employee_si: 162.35
  employer_si: 243.65
  pay_after_specified_deductions: 1335.26
  salary_plus_employer_si: 1867.15
```

## Ask the client first

1. Who is the employer, which registration references apply, and what authorises receipt of the employee’s code?

3. What are the paydays, earnings/service periods, contribution weeks and other concurrent employments?
4. What benefits, loans, reimbursements, scheme approvals and employee payments need a separate valuation?
5. Are there starter/leaver dates, termination payments, revised codes/directions, exemption certificates or coverage decisions?
6. What was deducted, paid, reported and acknowledged, including earlier arrears, adjustments and annual form years?

### Questions requiring an operational ruling

Employment Injuries regulation 3(7), incorporated subject to SI regulation 4(1), identifies the first employer in a contribution week where a person has multiple employers. The employer notes instead instruct contributions in each employment unless an ITO exemption certificate has been issued. Their operational relationship, concurrent-job allocation and weekly maximum administration remain unresolved. Obtain the ITO allocation/certificate before ceasing deductions; neither an employer's guess nor the mere existence of a second job settles it.
