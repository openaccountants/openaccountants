# Italy: tax pack

Calendar tax year. Authority: Agenzia delle Entrate. Euro. Residents are taxed on worldwide income under IRPEF (23%, 33% and 43% bands for 2026) plus regional and municipal surcharges; companies pay IRES at 24% plus the regional IRAP; VAT (IVA) follows EU rules, with mandatory e-invoicing (see italy-einvoice). A new income tax code (D.Lgs. 117/2026) replaces the TUIR from 1 January 2027, so 2026 skills cite the old article numbers.
