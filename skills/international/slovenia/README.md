# Slovenia: tax pack

Calendar tax year for individuals; companies may use a business year of up to 12 months. Authority: the Financial Administration (Finančna uprava Republike Slovenije, FURS), with returns filed on eDavki. Euro. Personal income tax (dohodnina) on a five-band scale from 16% to 50%, with capital income and rent taxed separately at 25%. Corporate income tax at 22% for 2024 to 2028 under ZORZFS art. 64 (the ZDDPO-2 rate is 19%). VAT under the EU directive. The main laws are ZDoh-2, ZDDPO-2, ZDavP-2 and ZDDV-1, consolidated on pisrs.si.
