# Slovenia: tax pack

Calendar tax year unless a company keeps a different business year. Authority: the Finančna uprava Republike Slovenije (FURS); tax policy and the recognised interest rates come from the Ministry of Finance. Euro (EUR). Corporate income tax at 22% for 2024 to 2028 (general rate 19%). Personal income tax (dohodnina), VAT and social contributions are in the skills below.
