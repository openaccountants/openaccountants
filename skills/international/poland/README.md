# Poland: tax pack

Calendar tax year for individuals; companies use the calendar year or another 12-month year set in their articles. Authority: Krajowa Administracja Skarbowa (KAS), with social contributions collected by ZUS. Currency: Polish złoty (PLN); EUR thresholds in the law are converted at NBP rates. Individuals pay PIT on a progressive scale, a 19% flat rate or a lump sum on revenue (ryczałt); companies pay CIT at 19%, 9% for small taxpayers, or can elect Estonian CIT, taxed only on distributed profit. VAT follows the EU rules, with JPK_V7 returns and KSeF e-invoicing.
