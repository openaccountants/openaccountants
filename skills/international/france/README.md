# France: tax pack

Calendar tax year for individuals; companies are taxed by financial year. Authority: the Direction générale des finances publiques (DGFiP), with social contributions collected by the URSSAF. Euro. Individuals pay progressive income tax per household (quotient familial) withheld at source; companies pay corporate income tax (IS) at 25%, with 15% on the first EUR 42,500 for qualifying SMEs, on a territorial base; local business taxes (CFE, CVAE) sit on top. VAT at 20% standard rate, EU rules apply.
