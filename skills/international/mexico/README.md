# Mexico: tax pack

Mexico taxes income under the Ley del Impuesto Sobre la Renta (LISR), administered by the Servicio de Administración Tributaria (SAT). Companies pay a flat 30% on the annual fiscal result (LISR Art. 9), with monthly provisional payments by the 17th of the following month (Art. 14). Individuals pay on a progressive annual tariff, or on gross income under RESICO if eligible. Dividends not paid out of CUFIN bear a 30% corporate-level tax on the dividend grossed up by 1.4286 (Art. 10). IVA is the federal value added tax. Social security is IMSS, with the housing contribution to INFONAVIT at 5% of wages (LIFNVT Art. 29); states levy their own payroll taxes. Non-resident income without a permanent establishment is taxed under LISR Title V, mostly at flat rates withheld at source. The currency is the peso (MXN); the tax year is the calendar year.
