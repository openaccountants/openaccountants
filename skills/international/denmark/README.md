# Denmark: tax pack

Calendar tax year for individuals; companies usually use the calendar year but may use a different financial year. Authority: Skattestyrelsen (skat.dk); statutes on retsinformation.dk. Danish kroner (DKK). Individuals pay labour market contribution (AM-bidrag), state and municipal income tax; companies pay 22% corporate tax with mandatory national joint taxation of group companies; VAT (moms) follows the EU VAT Directive.
