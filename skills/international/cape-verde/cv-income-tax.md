# Cape Verde Personal Income Tax

## Personal income tax (IRPS) bands and rates

- **Band 1 — taxable income up to CVE 960,000** — 16.5% %  _(Codigo do IRPS (Personal Income Tax Code) - https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/cabo-verde/individual/taxes-on-personal-income)_
- **Band 2 — taxable income CVE 960,000 to 1,800,000** — 23.1% %  _(Codigo do IRPS (Personal Income Tax Code) - https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/cabo-verde/individual/taxes-on-personal-income)_
- **Band 3 — taxable income over CVE 1,800,000** — 27.5% %  _(Codigo do IRPS (Personal Income Tax Code) - https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/cabo-verde/individual/taxes-on-personal-income)_
- **Tax-free threshold (annual net income exemption)** — CVE 220,000 per year CVE  _(Codigo do IRPS (Personal Income Tax Code) - https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/cabo-verde/individual/taxes-on-personal-income)_
- **Non-resident taxation** — Taxed only on Cape Verde-source income, generally at the same rates as residents on that income  _(Codigo do IRPS (Personal Income Tax Code) - https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/cabo-verde/individual/taxes-on-personal-income)_
- **Resident scope** — Residents taxed on worldwide income  _(Codigo do IRPS (Personal Income Tax Code) - https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/cabo-verde/individual/taxes-on-personal-income)_
