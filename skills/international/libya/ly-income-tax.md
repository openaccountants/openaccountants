# Libya Personal Income Tax

## Personal income tax rates and bands

- **Personal income tax scope and rate basis** — Personal income tax in Libya is charged only on employment income exercised in Libya, at progressive rates of 5% and 10%. Thresholds below are on gross salary after personal exemptions.  _(Income Tax Law No. 7 of 2010)_
- **First band** — 5% on gross salary up to LYD 12,000 per year (LYD 1,000 per month) percent  _([Income Tax Law No. 7 of 2010](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/libya/individual/taxes-on-personal-income))_
- **Second band** — 10% on gross salary over LYD 12,000 per year (over LYD 1,000 per month) percent  _([Income Tax Law No. 7 of 2010](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/libya/individual/taxes-on-personal-income))_
- **Scope of personal income tax** — Only income derived from employment exercised in Libya is taxable; applies to Libyan and foreign nationals alike  _([Income Tax Law No. 7 of 2010](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/libya/individual/taxes-on-personal-income))_
- **Personal exemption — single** — LYD 1,800 per year (LYD 150 per month) LYD  _([Income Tax Law No. 7 of 2010](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/libya/individual/deductions))_
- **Personal exemption — married, no children** — LYD 2,400 per year (LYD 200 per month) LYD  _([Income Tax Law No. 7 of 2010](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/libya/individual/deductions))_
- **Personal exemption — married/widowed/divorced with children** — LYD 2,400 per year plus LYD 300 per child (LYD 200 plus LYD 25 per child per month) LYD  _([Income Tax Law No. 7 of 2010](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/libya/individual/deductions))_
- **Deductible — social security contribution** — The taxpayer's own social security contribution (or equivalent approved scheme) is deductible  _([Income Tax Law No. 7 of 2010](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/libya/individual/deductions))_
- **Deductible — direct work expenses** — Direct expenses incurred by the taxpayer in the performance of work are deductible  _([Income Tax Law No. 7 of 2010](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/libya/individual/deductions))_
- **Deductible — disciplinary fines** — Any amount deducted from the taxpayer as a disciplinary fine is deductible  _([Income Tax Law No. 7 of 2010](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/libya/individual/deductions))_
- **Stamp duty on salaries** — 0.5% assessed on net salaries percent  _([Stamp Duty Law](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/libya/individual/other-taxes))_
- **Social Unity (Solidarity) Fund** — 1% of monthly gross salary, withheld and collected by the Tax Department percent  _([Income Tax Law No. 7 of 2010](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/libya/individual/taxes-on-personal-income))_
- **Collection mechanism** — Personal income tax on salaries is withheld at source by the employer and remitted monthly  _([Income Tax Law No. 7 of 2010](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/libya/individual/taxes-on-personal-income))_
- **PAYE remittance deadline** — Withheld payroll tax is generally payable monthly, within ten days after month-end ((approx — confirm))  _([Income Tax Law No. 7 of 2010](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/libya/individual/taxes-on-personal-income))_
