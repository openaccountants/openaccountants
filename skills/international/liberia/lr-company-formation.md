# Liberia Company Formation & Entity Choice

## Entity types

- **Common entity types** — Sole proprietorship, partnership, limited liability company (LLC), and business corporation (including non-resident/offshore corporations)  _(Associations Law of Liberia (Business Corporation Act) — https://lbr.gov.lr/about/)_
- **Governing law** — Associations Law, Title 5 of the Liberian Code of Laws Revised — incl. the Business Corporation Act  _(Associations Law of Liberia (Business Corporation Act))_
- **Registration authority** — Liberia Business Registry (LBR), Ministry of Commerce and Industry  _(Liberia Business Registry — https://lbr.gov.lr/about/)_

Companies in Liberia are formed under the Associations Law (Business Corporation Act) and registered with the Liberia Business Registry (LBR). Common forms include the business corporation, limited liability company, partnership and sole proprietorship.

## Capital, incorporation steps and compliance

There is generally no statutory minimum paid-up capital for an ordinary domestic company. Incorporation is largely online via the LBR and can be completed within a few working days.

- **Minimum share capital** — No statutory minimum paid-up capital for an ordinary domestic company; a nominal authorised capital is declared (approx — confirm by entity type)  _(Associations Law of Liberia (Business Corporation Act))_
- **Name reservation** — Reserve a unique company name online with the LBR; reservation is valid for 6 months  _(Liberia Business Registry)_
- **Core incorporation documents** — Registration Form (RF-001), Articles of Incorporation, incorporator/shareholder forms, ID documents, and tax-authority (TIN) information form  _(Liberia Business Registry)_
- **Tax registration** — Obtain a Tax Identification Number (TIN) from the Liberia Revenue Authority as part of registration  _(Liberia Revenue Code)_
- **Typical incorporation timeline** — Approximately 1 to 3 working days for domestic registration via the LBR (approx — confirm)  _(Liberia Business Registry)_
- **Indicative registration cost** — Government/agent fees commonly from around USD 700, with annual renewal of around USD 900 (varies by provider and entity type) USD (approx — confirm)  _(Liberia Business Registry)_
- **Core annual compliance** — Annual business-registration renewal, annual income tax return (by 31 March for calendar-year filers), quarterly advance tax, and monthly PAYE/GST/NASSCORP filings where applicable  _(Liberia Revenue Code; Liberia Business Registry)_
