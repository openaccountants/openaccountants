# Brunei Company Formation & Entity Choice

## Entity types and incorporation

- **Registration overview** — Companies in Brunei are registered with the Registry of Companies and Business Names (ROCBN) at the Ministry of Finance and Economy. The most common vehicle is the private limited company (Sendirian Berhad / Sdn Bhd). Several requirements (resident director, share capital) should be confirmed against current ROCBN rules.
- **Private limited company** — Sendirian Berhad (Sdn Bhd) — must include 'Sendirian Berhad' or 'Sdn Bhd' in the name  _(Companies Act (Cap. 39) - https://www.mofe.gov.bn/Divisions/incorporation-of-companies.aspx)_
- **Public limited company** — Berhad (Bhd) — public company able to offer shares to the public  _(Companies Act (Cap. 39))_
- **Other common business forms** — Sole proprietorship, partnership, branch of a foreign company, and registered business name  _(Business Names Act (Cap. 92))_
