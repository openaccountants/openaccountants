# Bulgaria Company Formation & Entity Choice

## Entity types, capital and incorporation

- **OOD (ООД)** — Limited liability company with two or more members  _(Commercial Act (Targovski zakon) — https://www.mi.government.bg/en/business-handbook-for-sme/choice-of-legal-form-registration-documents-and-procedure/)_
- **EOOD (ЕООД)** — Single-member limited liability company  _(Commercial Act (Targovski zakon) — https://www.mi.government.bg/en/business-handbook-for-sme/choice-of-legal-form-registration-documents-and-procedure/)_
- **AD (АД)** — Joint-stock company (used for larger / capital-raising businesses)  _(Commercial Act (Targovski zakon) — https://www.mi.government.bg/en/business-handbook-for-sme/choice-of-legal-form-registration-documents-and-procedure/)_
- **ET (ЕТ)** — Sole trader / sole proprietorship (unlimited liability, taxed at 15%)  _(Commercial Act (Targovski zakon) — https://www.mi.government.bg/en/business-handbook-for-sme/choice-of-legal-form-registration-documents-and-procedure/)_
- **Minimum share capital — OOD/EOOD** — BGN 2 BGN  _(Commercial Act (Targovski zakon) — https://www.mi.government.bg/en/business-handbook-for-sme/choice-of-legal-form-registration-documents-and-procedure/)_
- **Minimum share capital — AD** — BGN 50,000 BGN ((approx — confirm))  _(Commercial Act (Targovski zakon))_
- **Registration authority** — Commercial Register and Register of Non-Profit Legal Entities, Registry Agency (Ministry of Justice)  _(Commercial Register and Register of Non-Profit Legal Entities Act — https://www.mi.government.bg/en/business-handbook-for-sme/choice-of-legal-form-registration-documents-and-procedure/)_
- **Capital deposit** — Share capital deposited into a company bank account before registration; bank confirmation filed with the Commercial Register  _(Commercial Act (Targovski zakon) — https://www.expanship.com/bg/blog/incorporation-requirements-in-bulgaria)_
- **Incorporation timeline** — Approx. 3–7 business days once documents are prepared and submitted ((approx — confirm))  _(Commercial Register and Register of Non-Profit Legal Entities Act — https://www.expanship.com/bg/blog/incorporation-requirements-in-bulgaria)_
- **State registration fee** — approx. BGN 110 on paper / BGN 55 electronic for an OOD BGN ((approx — confirm); Commercial Register filing fee (lower for electronic filing))  _(Tariff of State Fees Collected by the Registry Agency)_
- **Annual financial statements filing** — Annual financial statements published in the Commercial Register, generally by 30 September following the year-end ((approx — confirm))  _(Accountancy Act (Zakon za schetovodstvoto))_
- **Annual corporate tax compliance** — File annual CIT return and pay CIT between 1 March and 30 June following the tax year  _(Corporate Income Tax Act (ZKPO) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/bulgaria/corporate/tax-administration)_
- **VAT registration obligation** — Register for VAT once taxable turnover exceeds BGN 100,000 in 12 months (or voluntarily) BGN  _(Value Added Tax Act (ZDDS) — https://eurofast.eu/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/BulgariaTaxCard2025-5bbc2399-822d-42cb-b32e-cbd12beb4b3c.pdf)_

The most common Bulgarian business vehicle is the limited liability company (OOD, or EOOD if single-member), governed by the Commercial Act and registered with the Commercial Register at the Registry Agency. The statutory minimum share capital is a token BGN 2.
