# Bulgaria: tax pack

Calendar tax year. Authority: the National Revenue Agency (НАП), nra.bg; statutes on lex.bg. Currency: the euro from 1 January 2026 (fixed rate BGN 1.95583); several statutes still state amounts in lev. Flat 10% personal income tax (15% for sole traders) under the Personal Income Taxes Act, flat 10% corporate income tax under the Corporate Income Tax Act, 5% final tax on dividends. VAT (ДДС) at 20% under the VAT Act, following the EU VAT Directive. Procedure, treaty relief and penalties are in the Tax and Social Security Procedure Code (DOPK).
