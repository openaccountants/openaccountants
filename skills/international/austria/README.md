# Austria: tax pack

Calendar tax year. Authorities: the Finanzamt Österreich for most taxpayers and the Finanzamt für Großbetriebe for large businesses and non-resident refunds, under the Federal Ministry of Finance (BMF); filing through FinanzOnline. Euro. Progressive income tax with bands indexed every year, capital income at special flat rates (27.5% and 25%), corporate income tax at 23%, VAT under EU rules, and social insurance run by separate bodies (SVS for the self-employed). EU member, so EU social security coordination applies.
