# Faroe Islands cross-border royalty and dividend review v0.1

> **Source-cited draft.** Professional review is pending. Figures are limited to the periods and assumptions below. This Guide prepares working papers; it is not authority to file or pay.

## Scope

Prepare evidence and claim-timing checks for Faroese royalties and dividend refunds in 2026. This draft does not provide a treaty-rate matrix, a complete PE opinion or an unconditional withholding exemption.

Sources were accessed on 22 October 2026. A dated rate table controls its stated period; an undated guidance page does not prove that all later amendments have been incorporated.

## Royalties

TAKS describes resident royalties as B-income under Tax Act sections 1 and 25. Its nonresident guidance states 25% gross withholding where no relevant DTA applies, and describes treaty relief at 0%. Confirm the actual treaty, royalty definition, beneficial owner, residence, PE connection and relief procedure before using a treaty result. [TAKS royalties](https://www.taks.fo/en/business/tax/tax-and-royalties).

## Computation and evidence

1. Inspect the contract and distinguish a copyright licence from a service, asset sale or employment payment.
2. Identify the legal recipient, beneficial owner, residence certificate and payment date.
3. Identify the exact domestic charging provision, then the applicable treaty article and claim procedure.
4. Determine whether withholding is required at payment or whether relief is authorised. Retain payment and reporting evidence.
5. Reconcile the recipient's gross entitlement, withholding and net payment. Check contractual gross-up separately.

**No-treaty illustration:** established gross royalty DKK 40,000 at 25% gives **10,000 withholding** and **30,000 net**. If the contract instead guarantees DKK 40,000 net and a reviewed gross-up is required, gross is 40,000 / 0.75 = **53,333.33**, with **13,333.33** withholding before applying the authority's rounding rules. Do not gross up a gross-price contract.

## Dividend refund time limit

TAKS states that from **1 May 2026** its administrative practice treats a dividend-refund application as an assessment-amendment request, using **three years after the end of the income year** under Tax Administration Act section 8(2), rather than relying on the general five-year monetary-claim limit. Check transitional circumstances separately. [Dividend-refund notice](https://www.taks.fo/en/business/tax/dividend-tax-refunds).

For a claim within that rule for income year 2026, the ordinary calculation reaches the end of 2029. Confirm the receipt/deadline rules and any exception; do not assume sending a document on the last day secures timely receipt.

## Open review items

Verify treaty eligibility, domestic dividend withholding, participation exemptions, refund documentation and the payment/remittance calendar for the actual case. A treaty's presence in an official list is not evidence that every payment is exempt. Retain an unresolved-rate flag until those checks pass.
