# Faroe Islands company registration and annual reporting v0.1

> **Source-cited draft.** Professional review is pending. Figures are limited to the periods and assumptions below. This Guide prepares working papers; it is not authority to file or pay.

## Scope

Prepare a registration and annual-reporting checklist for ordinary Faroese P/F and Sp/F companies in 2026. Capital minimums, beneficial ownership, audit exemptions, licences and insolvency advice need separate verification.

Sources were accessed on 22 October 2026. A dated rate table controls its stated period; an undated guidance page does not prove that all later amendments have been incorporated.

## Entity and registration

TAKS distinguishes P/F and Sp/F companies, whose finances are separate from owners, from sole proprietorships and partnerships. Companies register with Skráseting Føroya and TAKS's Vinnuskráin. [Entity types](https://www.taks.fo/en/business/vinnuskrain/different-kinds-of-businesses).

TAKS's starting-business checklist requests articles, memorandum, Vinnuskráin form and shareholder register for limited companies. Owner salary from a limited company is A-income; sole-trader/partnership owner income is B-income. [Starting a business](https://www.taks.fo/en/business/starting-a-business/starting-a-business).

## Separate filing obligations

| Filing | Published ordinary requirement |
|---|---|
| Registrar annual accounts | Within six months of financial-year end; a calendar-year company's prior-year accounts before 1 July |
| Company tax return | 1 July, with accounts; exempt companies also file |
| MVG return | Separately determined by VAT registration and the quarterly calendar |

[Registrar annual accounts, referring to Annual Financial Statements Act s138](https://www.skraseting.fo/en/companies/annual-accounts); [TAKS company returns](https://www.taks.fo/en/individuals/tax/tax-return).

## Working-paper process

1. Record the exact entity type and incorporation record. Do not classify a company as a sole trader because an English help page lists overlapping examples.
2. Match registered name, V-tal, shareholder register, directors, financial year and tax registration.
3. Prepare accounts, identify the applicable reporting and audit rules, and preserve the approval evidence.
4. Set separate Registrar, TAKS, payroll and VAT deadlines; one receipt does not discharge the others.
5. Confirm all submitted documents and archive receipts against the relevant year.

**Calendar example:** a 31 December 2025 year-end requires the Registrar's six-month deadline in June 2026 to be distinguished from the TAKS 1 July company-return deadline. A successful tax submission does not establish timely Registrar filing.

## Actual enforcement and boundaries

The Registrar's September 2026 notice concerns companies missing 2025 accounts and refers to compulsory-dissolution proceedings. A dormant or inactive status is not assumed to eliminate filing. [Registrar notice, 1 September 2026](https://www.skraseting.fo/fo/kunning/tidindi/tann-15-september-2026-fer-skraseting-foeroya-at-senda-feloeg-id-ikki-hava-latid-arsfrasoegn-fyri-2025-inn-i-skiftiraettin-til-tvingsilavtoeku).

For foreign businesses, registration and a local VAT representative are separate questions from treaty PE. The TAKS foreign-company page's reference to twelve months must not be turned into a universal safe harbour for every activity or treaty. [Foreign-company guidance](https://www.taks.fo/en/business/vinnuskrain/foreign-companies-in-the-faroe-islands).

**Open scope:** enacted formation provisions, share capital, ownership disclosures, reporting framework, audit thresholds, retention and event-driven filings. This checklist is not comprehensive formation coverage.
