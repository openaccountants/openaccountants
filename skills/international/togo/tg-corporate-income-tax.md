# Togo Corporate Income Tax

## Corporate income tax (Impôt sur les Sociétés)

Companies resident in Togo pay corporate income tax (Impôt sur les Sociétés, IS) on net taxable profit, with a minimum tax floor (Impôt Minimum Forfaitaire). Free-trade-zone enterprises benefit from holidays and reduced rates.

- **Standard corporate income tax rate** — 27 percent  _([Code Général des Impôts (Togo) — Impôt sur les Sociétés](https://www.lloydsbanktrade.com/en/market-potential/togo/taxes))_
- **Minimum tax (Impôt Minimum Forfaitaire)** — Minimum tax floor applies; commonly cited minimum of XOF 3,000 XOF ((approx — confirm; minimum is usually computed as a % of turnover with a floor)) XOF  _([Code Général des Impôts (Togo) — Impôt Minimum Forfaitaire](https://www.lloydsbanktrade.com/en/market-potential/togo/taxes))_
- **Tax base** — Net taxable profit (worldwide for resident companies on Togo-attributable activity)  _(Code Général des Impôts (Togo) — Impôt sur les Sociétés)_
- **Capital gains** — Corporate capital gains taxed as ordinary income at the IS rate  _([Code Général des Impôts (Togo)](https://www.lloydsbanktrade.com/en/market-potential/togo/taxes))_
- **Withholding tax — dividends** — 13% standard; 7% for listed WAEMU companies; 0% for qualifying resident parent/subsidiary percent  _([Code Général des Impôts (Togo)](https://www.lloydsbanktrade.com/en/market-potential/togo/taxes))_
- **Withholding tax — interest** — 13% individuals; 6% companies; 3% medium-term bonds; 0% long-term government bonds percent  _([Code Général des Impôts (Togo)](https://www.lloydsbanktrade.com/en/market-potential/togo/taxes))_
- **Withholding tax — royalties** — 20% to non-residents; 5%/10% to residents (with/without tax number) percent  _([Code Général des Impôts (Togo)](https://www.lloydsbanktrade.com/en/market-potential/togo/taxes))_
- **Employer payroll tax (taxe sur les salaires)** — 3% on gross salaries percent  _([Code Général des Impôts (Togo)](https://www.lloydsbanktrade.com/en/market-potential/togo/taxes))_
- **Registration duty on capital contributions** — 2% on capital contributions ((approx — confirm)) percent  _([Code Général des Impôts (Togo)](https://www.lloydsbanktrade.com/en/market-potential/togo/taxes))_
- **Annual IS return deadline** — 31 March of the following year  _([Code Général des Impôts (Togo)](https://taxatlas.io/country/togo))_
- **Free-trade-zone incentive** — Tax holidays and reduced rates for Free Trade Zone enterprises  _([Togo Free Trade Zone regime](https://www.state.gov/reports/2025-investment-climate-statements/togo))_
