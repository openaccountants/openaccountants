# Hungary: tax pack

Calendar tax year unless a company keeps a different business year. Authority: the Nemzeti Adó- és Vámhivatal (NAV); municipalities run the local business tax. Forint (HUF). Corporate tax at a flat 9%, plus local business tax of up to 2% and a 0.3% innovation contribution for larger companies; small companies can opt into KIVA instead. Flat-rate personal income tax (see hu-income-tax). VAT under EU rules, with real-time invoice reporting to NAV (see hu-vat-return, hungary-vat-return and hungary-einvoice).
