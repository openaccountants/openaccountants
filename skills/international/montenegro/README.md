# Montenegro: tax pack

Calendar tax year. Authority: the Tax Administration (Poreska uprava), with returns filed electronically through IRMS since January 2026. Euro. Personal income tax on salary at 0%, 9% and 15% (monthly bands EUR 700 and 1,000; annual EUR 8,400 and 12,000 for a principal business), 15% on most other income and 12% on gambling winnings from 21 May 2026, plus a municipal surtax of up to 13% of the tax (15% in Podgorica and Cetinje). Corporate profit tax at 9%, 12% and 15% (bands EUR 100,000 and 1,500,000). VAT at 21%, 15%, 7% and 0%, with registration above EUR 30,000. Pension contribution 10% employee and 0% employer since October 2024; no health contribution. Late-payment interest is 0.03% a day to 17 July 2026 and 5.4% a year from 18 July 2026. The CIT changes in Official Gazette 104/26 apply from 1 January 2027 and the new VAT law from EU accession; neither changes 2026.
