# Republic of Congo Personal Income Tax

## Personal income tax (IRPP) bands and rates

- **IRPP overview** — Individuals are taxed under the Impôt sur le Revenu des Personnes Physiques (IRPP), levied on a progressive scale. Residents are taxed on worldwide income; non-residents on Congolese-source income.
- **IRPP rate — taxable income XAF 0 to 464,000** — 1 percent  _(Code Général des Impôts (CGI) — IRPP barème — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/republic-of-congo/individual/taxes-on-personal-income)_
- **IRPP rate — taxable income XAF 464,000 to 1,000,000** — 10 percent  _(Code Général des Impôts (CGI) — IRPP barème — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/republic-of-congo/individual/taxes-on-personal-income)_
- **IRPP rate — taxable income XAF 1,000,000 to 3,000,000** — 25 percent  _(Code Général des Impôts (CGI) — IRPP barème — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/republic-of-congo/individual/taxes-on-personal-income)_
- **IRPP rate — taxable income over XAF 3,000,000** — 40 percent  _(Code Général des Impôts (CGI) — IRPP barème — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/republic-of-congo/individual/taxes-on-personal-income)_
- **Tax-free threshold** — No fully exempt band — income from XAF 0 is taxed at 1%; family-quotient relief reduces tax for dependants ((approx — confirm))  _(Code Général des Impôts (CGI) — IRPP)_
- **Family quotient (quotient familial)** — Tax is computed by dividing taxable income by the number of family shares (parts) based on marital status and dependants ((approx — confirm))  _(Code Général des Impôts (CGI) — quotient familial)_
- **Standard deduction for employment income** — Flat deduction for professional expenses applied to gross salary before applying the barème ((approx — confirm))  _(Code Général des Impôts (CGI) — IRPP)_
- **Tax residence test** — Resident if habitual abode, principal home, or centre of economic interests is in Congo, or present more than 183 days ((approx — confirm))  _(Code Général des Impôts (CGI) — domicile fiscal)_
- **Withholding on non-commercial income of non-residents** — 20 percent  _(Code Général des Impôts (CGI) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/republic-of-congo/individual/taxes-on-personal-income)_
- **Rate for foreign employees on limited secondment** — 20 percent  _(Code Général des Impôts (CGI) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/republic-of-congo/individual/taxes-on-personal-income)_
- **Simplified flat regime for small taxpayers** — 5% of annual turnover or 8% of annual margin for individuals below the threshold with minimal accounting records percent  _(Code Général des Impôts (CGI) — régime forfaitaire — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/republic-of-congo/individual/taxes-on-personal-income)_
- **Annual IRPP return filing deadline** — 20 March following the tax year ((approx — confirm))  _(Code Général des Impôts (CGI))_
- **Employment income collection** — IRPP on salaries is withheld monthly at source by the employer (retenue à la source)  _(Code Général des Impôts (CGI))_
