# Mali Personal Income Tax

## Personal income tax (ITS) bands and rates

- **Tax-free threshold (ITS)** — 0% on annual taxable income up to XOF 330,000 XOF (Tax-free threshold)  _(Code Général des Impôts (Mali) — Barème ITS)_
- **ITS band 2** — 5% on the slice from XOF 330,001 to 578,400 percent (approx — confirm band boundaries)  _(Code Général des Impôts (Mali) — Barème ITS)_
- **ITS band 3** — 12% on the slice from XOF 578,401 to 1,176,400 percent (approx — confirm; some guides show 13% and different boundaries)  _(Code Général des Impôts (Mali) — Barème ITS)_
- **ITS band 4** — 18% on the slice from XOF 1,176,401 to 1,789,733 percent (approx — confirm band boundaries)  _(Code Général des Impôts (Mali) — Barème ITS)_
- **ITS band 5** — 26% on the slice from XOF 1,789,734 to 2,384,195 percent (approx — confirm band boundaries)  _(Code Général des Impôts (Mali) — Barème ITS)_
- **ITS band 6** — 31% on the slice from XOF 2,384,196 to 3,494,130 percent (approx — confirm band boundaries)  _(Code Général des Impôts (Mali) — Barème ITS)_
- **ITS top band** — 37% on the slice above XOF 3,494,130 percent (approx — confirm; some guides show a top rate of 40%)  _(Code Général des Impôts (Mali) — Barème ITS)_
- **Non-resident employment income** — Generally taxed at a flat 30% withholding on Mali-source remuneration percent (approx — confirm; treaty relief may apply)  _(Code Général des Impôts (Mali))_
- **Individual residence test** — Resident if habitual abode in Mali or physical presence of 183+ days in the year (approx — confirm against CGI definition)  _(Code Général des Impôts (Mali))_
- **Key deduction from gross salary** — Mandatory employee social-security (INPS) and AMO contributions are deductible before ITS  _(Code Général des Impôts (Mali) — ITS)_
- **Family situation relief** — ITS computation applies reductions based on number of dependants (approx — confirm the dependant reduction scale with DGI)  _(Code Général des Impôts (Mali) — Barème ITS)_
- **ITS collection method** — Withheld at source monthly by the employer (retenue à la source)  _(Code Général des Impôts (Mali) — ITS)_
- **Annual individual return deadline** — 31 March of the following year (approx — confirm)  _(Code Général des Impôts (Mali))_

Salaries and wages are taxed under the Impôt sur les Traitements et Salaires (ITS), a progressive tax withheld monthly by employers. Published guides disagree on the exact band boundaries and top rate, so the bands below are flagged for accountant confirmation against the current CGI barème.
