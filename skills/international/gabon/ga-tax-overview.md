# Gabon Tax Overview

## The Gabonese tax system at a glance

Gabon is a Central African (CEMAC) state that taxes on a largely territorial/source basis, administered by the Direction Générale des Impôts (DGI) under the Code Général des Impôts (CGI). The figures below are an UNVERIFIED 2025 draft for accountant review.

- **Standard tax year** — Calendar year ending 31 December  _(Code Général des Impôts (Gabon) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/gabon/corporate/tax-administration)_
- **Currency** — Central African CFA franc (XAF) (CEMAC monetary framework)
- **Tax authority** — Direction Générale des Impôts (DGI), Ministère de l'Économie  _(Code Général des Impôts (Gabon))_
- **Basis of taxation** — Largely territorial — income sourced in Gabon is taxed; non-residents are taxed on Gabon-source income only  _(Code Général des Impôts (Gabon) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/gabon/individual/taxes-on-personal-income)_
- **Top personal income tax (IRPP) marginal rate** — 35% %  _(Code Général des Impôts (Gabon) — IRPP — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/gabon/individual/taxes-on-personal-income)_
- **Standard corporate income tax (IS) rate** — 30% %  _(Code Général des Impôts (Gabon) — Impôt sur les Sociétés — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/gabon/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_
- **CIT rate — oil and mining sectors** — 35% %  _(Code Général des Impôts (Gabon) — Impôt sur les Sociétés (https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/gabon/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income))_
- **Does VAT (TVA) apply?** — Yes — VAT (Taxe sur la Valeur Ajoutée) applies; standard rate 18%  _(Code Général des Impôts (Gabon) — TVA — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/gabon/corporate/other-taxes)_
- **Annual CIT return filing deadline** — 30 April of the year following the tax year  _(Code Général des Impôts (Gabon) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/gabon/corporate/tax-administration)_
- **Minimum corporate tax (Minimum de Perception)** — XAF 500,000 minimum annual CIT, even with no profit XAF ((approx — confirm; some guides cite XAF 1,000,000)) XAF  _(Code Général des Impôts (Gabon) — Minimum de Perception — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/gabon/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_
