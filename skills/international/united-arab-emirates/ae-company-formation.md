# UAE company formation: what to set up for tax, and in what order (2026)

## Scope and who this is for ([Corporate Tax Law, consolidated](https://mof.gov.ae/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Federal-Decree-Law-No.-47-of-2022-and-its-amendments-en-v13.1.26.pdf); [FTA Decision No. 3 of 2024](https://tax.gov.ae/Datafolder/Files/Legislation/RE%20FTA%20Decision%20No.%203%20of%202024%20on%20Registration%20Timeline%20for%20Corporate%20Tax%20%20-%20For%20publishing.pdf))

This Guide is the entry point for someone setting up a business in the United Arab Emirates who wants to know what the choice of vehicle means for tax, and which tax registrations to make, by when. It covers tax periods and registrations in 2026, with a short section on returns being filed now for 2025.

It covers:

- the tax difference between a mainland company, a free zone company, a branch and a sole establishment (natural person);
- Corporate Tax registration and the Tax Registration Number (TRN), including the deadline for a new company;
- the Qualifying Free Zone Person (QFZP) regime at the point of set-up: what a free zone company must have in place to get 0% on Qualifying Income;
- Small Business Relief at the point of set-up, and why a QFZP cannot use it;
- the VAT registration thresholds and Designated Zones;
- audited financial statements, record keeping and the first tax period;
- Economic Substance Regulations (ESR), which no longer require reports for financial years ending after 31 December 2022, and Ultimate Beneficial Owner (UBO) filings.

It does **not** cover licensing. The trade licence, legal form, share capital, office lease, visas and fees are set by the Emirate's economic department or the free zone authority, not by the Federal Tax Authority (FTA). Each of those points is marked **check** below and carries no figure: confirm them with the licensing authority before advising.

Once the company exists, use the companion Guides for the detail:

- **ae-corporate-tax**: rates, deductions, losses, transfer pricing, QFZP income tests, returns and penalties.
- **uae-vat** and **ae-vat-return**: VAT rules, invoicing, e-invoicing and the VAT201 return.
- **ae-payroll-social**: employing staff, end-of-service gratuity and GCC nationals' pensions.
- **ae-income-tax**: individuals (there is no personal income tax on wages).

## Ask the client first

- **What will the business actually do, and for whom?** List the activities and the customers: UAE mainland businesses, free zone businesses, consumers (natural persons), or customers abroad. This drives the free zone decision more than anything else.
- **Who will own it?** A UAE or foreign individual, a UAE company, or a foreign company. Is the owner part of a multinational group, and does the group's consolidated revenue reach EUR 750 million? That brings the domestic minimum top-up tax into view.
- **Where will it be managed and controlled from?** A foreign company whose board or senior managers run it from the UAE can be UAE resident for Corporate Tax even without a UAE licence.
- **Company or sole establishment?** Will the business be a juridical person (a company) or will the individual trade in their own name?
- **Mainland or free zone, and which free zone?** If free zone, is the plan to sell mainly to other free zone companies or to carry on a Qualifying Activity, or to sell into the mainland and to consumers? Is the zone also a Designated Zone for VAT (**check** the Cabinet list with the FTA)?
- **Substance plan.** Where will the staff, premises, assets and decision-making sit? Will any core work be outsourced, and to whom?
- **Expected revenue** for the first 12 months and the first financial year, and the expected taxable supplies and taxable expenses for VAT.
- **Financial year end** the company intends to adopt.
- **Dates:** the date of incorporation or licence issue, and the date the first taxable supply is expected.
- **Existing registrations:** does the owner or group already hold a UAE TRN for VAT or Corporate Tax?

## The method, step by step

1. **Choose the vehicle with tax in mind** (the table in "Mainland or free zone" below). A company, whether mainland or free zone, is a Resident Person for Corporate Tax from incorporation. A natural person trading in their own name is taxable only once business turnover exceeds AED 1,000,000 in a calendar year ([Cabinet Decision No. 49 of 2023](https://tax.gov.ae/DataFolder/Files/Legislation/05-06-2023/3/Cabinet%20Decision%20No.%2049%20of%202023%20-%20for%20publishing.pdf)).
2. **Get the licence and incorporate** with the Emirate authority or free zone authority. **Check** the legal form, capital, lease and fees with that authority; this Guide gives no figures for them.
3. **Fix the financial year.** For Corporate Tax the financial year is the Gregorian calendar year, or the 12-month period for which the company prepares financial statements ([Corporate Tax Law, Article 57](https://mof.gov.ae/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Federal-Decree-Law-No.-47-of-2022-and-its-amendments-en-v13.1.26.pdf)). A change needs an FTA application (Article 58). How long a first period may run is set by FTA practice: **check** on EmaraTax before choosing a long or short first year.
4. **Register for Corporate Tax on EmaraTax within 3 months of incorporation**, and obtain a Corporate Tax TRN. This applies to every UAE company incorporated on or after 1 March 2024, **including a free zone company** and a company that expects to claim Small Business Relief ([FTA Decision No. 3 of 2024, Article 3](https://tax.gov.ae/Datafolder/Files/Legislation/RE%20FTA%20Decision%20No.%203%20of%202024%20on%20Registration%20Timeline%20for%20Corporate%20Tax%20%20-%20For%20publishing.pdf)).
5. **Test VAT registration from day one**, month by month: mandatory when taxable supplies in the previous 12 months **exceeded** AED 375,000, or are expected to exceed it in the next 30 days; voluntary when supplies or taxable expenses exceeded AED 187,500 ([VAT Executive Regulation, Articles 7 and 8](https://tax.gov.ae/Datafolder/Files/Legislation/2026/Law-No-8-of-2017-and-its-amendments--09-2026.pdf)). VAT registration is a separate application; it does not register the company for Corporate Tax, and the reverse is also true.
6. **For a free zone company, build the QFZP file before the first period starts:** substance in the free zone, a record of which income will be Qualifying Income, transfer pricing documentation, and an auditor ([Corporate Tax Law, Article 18](https://mof.gov.ae/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Federal-Decree-Law-No.-47-of-2022-and-its-amendments-en-v13.1.26.pdf); [Ministerial Decision No. 84 of 2025](https://tax.gov.ae/Datafolder/Files/Pdf/2025/MD-No-84-of-2025.pdf)).
7. **For a small mainland company, diarise the Small Business Relief election.** It is made in the return for each period, is not automatic, and is not available to a QFZP or to a member of a multinational group ([Ministerial Decision No. 73 of 2023, Article 3](https://tax.gov.ae/Datafolder/Files/Legislation/Corporate%20Tax/Ministerial%20Decision%20No.%2073%20of%202023%20-%20for%20publishing.pdf)).
8. **Set up the books** so that standalone financial statements can be prepared under the accounting standards accepted in the UAE, and keep records for 7 years after the end of each tax period ([Corporate Tax Law, Articles 20 and 56](https://mof.gov.ae/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Federal-Decree-Law-No.-47-of-2022-and-its-amendments-en-v13.1.26.pdf)).
9. **Deal with UBO and other licensing-side filings** with the licensing authority (**check**; outside this Guide). ESR notifications and reports are no longer required for financial years ending after 31 December 2022 ([Ministry of Finance announcement](https://mof.gov.ae/en/news/ministry-of-finance-announces-amendment-to-cabinet-decision-on-economic-substance-requirements/)).
10. **Keep the tax record current.** A registrant quotes its TRN in all dealings with the FTA and others, and tells the FTA of any change to its registered details within 20 Business Days ([Tax Procedures Law, Article 6](https://tax.gov.ae/Datafolder/Files/Legislation/2025/Federal%20Decree-Law%20No.%2028%20of%202022%20-%20publishing%2003%2012%202025.pdf)).
11. **Diarise the first returns:** Corporate Tax return and payment 9 months after the end of the first tax period; VAT returns for the tax periods the FTA assigns.

### Small Business Relief ([Ministerial Decision No. 73 of 2023](https://tax.gov.ae/Datafolder/Files/Legislation/Corporate%20Tax/Ministerial%20Decision%20No.%2073%20of%202023%20-%20for%20publishing.pdf); [Ministerial Decision No. 131 of 2026](https://mof.gov.ae/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Ministerial-Decision-No.-131-of-2026-Amending-Certain-Provisions-of-Ministerial-Decision-No.-73-of-2023-on-Small-Business-Relief-for-the-Purposes-of-Federal-Decree-Law-No.-47-of-2022-EN.pdf))

| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Revenue threshold | AED 3,000,000 for the period **and** every earlier period (does not exceed) |

### VAT registration ([VAT Law, Articles 13 and 17](https://tax.gov.ae/Datafolder/Files/Legislation/2025/Federal%20Decree-Law%20No.%208%20of%202017%20and%20amendments%20-%20publishing%2028%2011%202025.pdf); [VAT Executive Regulation, Articles 7 and 8](https://tax.gov.ae/Datafolder/Files/Legislation/2026/Law-No-8-of-2017-and-its-amendments--09-2026.pdf))

| Item | Figure or rule |
| --- | --- |
| Standard rate | 5% |
| Mandatory Registration Threshold | AED 375,000 of supplies counted under VAT Law Article 19 that **exceeded** it in the previous 12 months, or are expected to in the next 30 days |
| Application deadline | Within 30 days of becoming required to register |
| If the person does not apply | The FTA registers it from the date it first became liable, and imposes penalties |
| Voluntary Registration Threshold | AED 187,500 of supplies **or taxable expenses** |
| Non-resident with no place of residence in the UAE or another implementing state | Registers when it makes supplies on which no one else must account for the VAT; no threshold |

The VAT threshold and the Corporate Tax band are both AED 375,000, but they measure different things: the VAT test counts turnover of taxable supplies; the Corporate Tax band is a slice of taxable profit. Do not confuse them.

## Mainland or free zone: what changes for tax ([Corporate Tax Law, Articles 11, 18 and 19](https://mof.gov.ae/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Federal-Decree-Law-No.-47-of-2022-and-its-amendments-en-v13.1.26.pdf); [FTA Free Zone Persons bulletin](https://tax.gov.ae/Datafolder/Files/Pdf/2024/CT%20Bulletin/Basic%20Tax%20Information%20bulletin-%20Free%20Zone%20Person-English.pdf))

The licensing differences (ownership rules, where the company may trade, office requirements, visas and costs) are **check** items for the licensing authority. For tax the differences are narrower than most set-up material suggests:

| Vehicle | Corporate Tax position | Registration | Main tax point at set-up |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Mainland company (for example an LLC) | Resident Person; 0% up to AED 375,000, 9% above | Within 3 months of incorporation | Small Business Relief may be available if revenue stays at or below AED 3,000,000 |
| Free zone company (FZ-LLC, FZE, FZCO or similar; **check** the forms with the zone) | Resident Person and a Free Zone Person; 0% on Qualifying Income **only** if a QFZP; otherwise the same as mainland | Within 3 months of incorporation, even if it expects 0% | Build substance, income tracking, transfer pricing file and audit from the start |
| Branch of a foreign company | The foreign company is a Non-Resident Person with a permanent establishment, taxed on the branch's profits | 6 months from when the permanent establishment exists | A branch registered in a free zone is a Free Zone Person |
| Branch of a UAE company | Same Taxable Person as the head office (one registration, one return) | Covered by the head office's registration | The FTA bulletin says a UAE company with a branch registered in a free zone will be a Free Zone Person, with the UAE company's own presence treated as a domestic permanent establishment; income of a domestic permanent establishment is taxed at 9% even for a QFZP. Refer |
| Sole establishment or civil (professional) licence held by an individual | Not a juridical person; the individual is taxable only if business turnover exceeds AED 1,000,000 in a calendar year | 31 March of the following year | Wages, personal investment income and unlicensed real estate investment income never count |
| Unincorporated partnership | Transparent: each partner is taxed on its share, unless the FTA approves an application to treat the partnership as taxable | Each partner that is a Taxable Person registers | Refer; see Corporate Tax Law, Article 16 |

**Choosing a free zone does not by itself give 0%.** The 0% rate applies only to Qualifying Income of a Free Zone Person that meets every condition in Article 18, all the time, for the whole tax period.

### What a free zone company must have in place to be a QFZP ([Corporate Tax Law, Articles 18 and 19](https://mof.gov.ae/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Federal-Decree-Law-No.-47-of-2022-and-its-amendments-en-v13.1.26.pdf); [Cabinet Decision No. 100 of 2023](https://tax.gov.ae/Datafolder/Files/Legislation/Cabinet%20Decision%20No.%20100%20of%202023%20on%20Determining%20Qualifying%20Income%20for%20the%20Qualifying%20Free%20Zone%20Person%20-%20for%20publishing.pdf); [Ministerial Decision No. 229 of 2025](https://tax.gov.ae/Datafolder/Files/Pdf/2025/MD-No-229.pdf))

A Free Zone Person is a juridical person incorporated, established or otherwise registered in a free zone, including a branch of a non-resident registered there. An individual cannot be a Free Zone Person. It is a QFZP only while it meets **all** of these:

1. **Adequate substance in the State:** its core income-generating activities are carried on in a free zone, with adequate assets, qualified full-time employees and operating expenditure. Plan the lease, staff and decision-making before trading starts.
2. **Qualifying Income:** income from other Free Zone Persons (except Excluded Activities), income from Qualifying Activities with anyone, and qualifying intellectual property income. Transactions with natural persons are an Excluded Activity for most businesses. The full lists are in **ae-corporate-tax**.
3. **No election** to be taxed at the standard rates. A QFZP may elect into the standard rates from the start of the period in which it elects, or the next period (Article 19).
4. **Arm's length pricing and transfer pricing documentation** (Articles 34 and 55).
5. **Audited financial statements** for every period (Ministerial Decision No. 84 of 2025).
6. **De minimis test:** non-qualifying revenue does not exceed 5% of total revenue or AED 5,000,000, whichever is lower (Ministerial Decision No. 229 of 2025, Article 3).

**Consequence of failing:** a QFZP that fails any condition at any time in a period ceases to be a QFZP from the **beginning** of that period **and for the subsequent 4 tax periods** (Ministerial Decision No. 229 of 2025). In those periods it is taxed at the standard rates. Whether Small Business Relief can then be claimed is not addressed in the sources used here: refer. Treat a planned free zone structure that sells mainly to UAE consumers or mainland customers as a 9% business from the start.

A QFZP that distributes goods in or from a Designated Zone also needs an agreed-upon procedures report from its auditor, for periods starting on or after 1 January 2026 ([FTA Decision No. 6 of 2026](https://tax.gov.ae/Datafolder/Files/Legislation/2026/FTA%20Decision%20No.%206%20of%202026%20on%20Determining%20the%20Additional%20Procedures%20for%20the%20Compliance%20of%20QFZP%20-%20for%20publishing%20-%2007%202026.pdf)).

### Free zones and VAT ([VAT Law, Article 50](https://tax.gov.ae/Datafolder/Files/Legislation/2025/Federal%20Decree-Law%20No.%208%20of%202017%20and%20amendments%20-%20publishing%2028%2011%202025.pdf); [VAT Executive Regulation, Article 51](https://tax.gov.ae/Datafolder/Files/Legislation/2026/Law-No-8-of-2017-and-its-amendments--09-2026.pdf))

- A free zone company is inside the UAE for VAT. It registers on the same thresholds as a mainland company.
- A **Designated Zone** that meets the conditions in the Executive Regulation is treated as outside the State, but only for certain supplies of **goods**. Services supplied in a Designated Zone are taxed as normal. Not every free zone is a Designated Zone: **check** the current Cabinet list with the FTA.
- A person established or registered in a Designated Zone is treated as resident in the UAE for VAT.
- Detail, including goods movements, is in **uae-vat**.

## Economic Substance and UBO ([Ministry of Finance announcement on Cabinet Decision No. 98 of 2024](https://mof.gov.ae/en/news/ministry-of-finance-announces-amendment-to-cabinet-decision-on-economic-substance-requirements/))

- **Economic Substance Regulations (ESR):** the Ministry of Finance announced the cancellation of economic substance reporting requirements "for financial years ending after 31 December 2022", following Cabinet Decision No. 98 of 2024. A company formed now therefore has no ESR notification or report to file. ESR filings for financial years ending on or before 31 December 2022 (and any penalties for them) are a legacy matter: refer.
- **Substance still matters for tax.** For a free zone company, adequate substance is a QFZP condition (above), and a foreign company run from the UAE can become UAE resident through effective management and control.
- **UBO (Ultimate Beneficial Owner) register:** UBO filings are made with the licensing authority, not the FTA, under rules this Guide's official sources do not cover. Mark as **check**: confirm the form, timing and update rules with the Emirate or free zone registrar.
- **Anti-money-laundering registration** for regulated professions and dealers is also outside this Guide (**check** with the relevant supervisor).

## Boundaries and exceptions ([FTA Decision No. 3 of 2024](https://tax.gov.ae/Datafolder/Files/Legislation/RE%20FTA%20Decision%20No.%203%20of%202024%20on%20Registration%20Timeline%20for%20Corporate%20Tax%20%20-%20For%20publishing.pdf); [Cabinet Decision No. 49 of 2023](https://tax.gov.ae/DataFolder/Files/Legislation/05-06-2023/3/Cabinet%20Decision%20No.%2049%20of%202023%20-%20for%20publishing.pdf); [VAT Executive Regulation, Article 7](https://tax.gov.ae/Datafolder/Files/Legislation/2026/Law-No-8-of-2017-and-its-amendments--09-2026.pdf))

| Situation | Answer | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| New free zone company expects all its income to be Qualifying Income | Must still register for Corporate Tax within 3 months and file returns | Every UAE company, including a Free Zone Person, is a Resident Person and a Taxable Person |
| New mainland company expects revenue well under AED 3,000,000 | Must still register within 3 months; Small Business Relief is claimed by election in each return | The relief is an election, not an exemption from registration |
| Individual freelancer with a licence, business turnover of exactly AED 1,000,000 in 2026 | Not subject to Corporate Tax for 2026; no registration | The test is turnover that **exceeds** AED 1,000,000 |
| Individual's business turnover passes AED 1,000,000 in 2026 | Taxable Person for 2026; register by 31 March 2027 | FTA Decision No. 3 of 2024, Article 5 |
| Taxable supplies of exactly AED 375,000 in the last 12 months | Not required to register for VAT | Mandatory only when supplies **exceeded** the threshold |
| Start-up with no sales yet but taxable expenses over AED 187,500 | May register for VAT voluntarily | Expenses count for voluntary registration only |
| Company registered for VAT already | Still has to register separately for Corporate Tax | Separate registrations under separate laws |
| Owner holds two licences or runs several businesses through one company | One Taxable Person, one AED 375,000 band | The band applies per Taxable Person |
| Foreign company with a UAE branch in a free zone | The branch is a Free Zone Person; the foreign parent is treated as having a foreign permanent establishment | FTA Free Zone Persons bulletin |
| Free zone company fails the de minimis test in its second year | Standard rates from the start of that year and the next 4 years | Ministerial Decision No. 229 of 2025 |
| Licence renewal, capital, lease, visa or fee questions | **Check** with the licensing authority | Outside the FTA's rules and this Guide |

## Worked cases ([FTA Decision No. 3 of 2024](https://tax.gov.ae/Datafolder/Files/Legislation/RE%20FTA%20Decision%20No.%203%20of%202024%20on%20Registration%20Timeline%20for%20Corporate%20Tax%20%20-%20For%20publishing.pdf); [Cabinet Decision No. 116 of 2022](https://tax.gov.ae/Datafolder/Files/Legislation/Corporate%20Tax/Cabinet%20Decision%20No.%20116%20of%202022%20-%20for%20publishing.pdf); [Ministerial Decision No. 229 of 2025](https://tax.gov.ae/Datafolder/Files/Pdf/2025/MD-No-229.pdf))

1. **Free zone consultancy that is not a QFZP.**
   - **Facts:** a free zone company is incorporated on 15 February 2026 with a 31 December year end. It gives management consulting to mainland companies and to individuals. Revenue for 2026 is AED 3,400,000, all from those customers; taxable income is AED 900,000.
   - **Registration:** Corporate Tax registration is due within 3 months of incorporation, by 15 May 2026.
   - **QFZP test:** consulting is not a Qualifying Activity, and the customers are not Free Zone Persons, so all revenue is non-qualifying. That is far above 5% of revenue, so the de minimis test fails and the company is not a QFZP for 2026 (or the next 4 periods).
   - **Tax:** standard rates: (900,000 − 375,000) × 9% = AED 47,250. Small Business Relief is not available because revenue exceeds AED 3,000,000.
   - **VAT:** its supplies passed AED 375,000 early in 2026, so it had to apply for VAT registration within 30 days of becoming required to register.
   - **Lesson:** for a business selling services to the mainland and to consumers, the free zone gives no Corporate Tax advantage; choose on licensing grounds (**check**).

2. **Small mainland company using Small Business Relief.**
   - **Facts:** a mainland company is incorporated on 10 March 2026, with its first tax period ending 31 December 2026. Revenue for the period is AED 900,000. It is not part of a multinational group.
   - **Registration:** by 10 June 2026 (3 months from incorporation).
   - **Relief:** revenue does not exceed AED 3,000,000 and the period ends before 31 December 2029, so it may elect Small Business Relief in its 2026 return, due with any payment by 30 September 2027. If it elects, it is treated as having no taxable income, and any loss of the period cannot be carried forward.
   - **VAT:** its supplies exceed AED 375,000 within its first 12 months, so it must apply for VAT registration within 30 days of becoming required to register. Registration for Corporate Tax does not cover this.

3. **Late Corporate Tax registration, penalty waived.**
   - **Facts:** a company incorporated on 10 February 2026, with a 31 December year end, had to register by 10 May 2026. It registered in August 2026.
   - **Penalty:** the late registration penalty is AED 10,000 ([Cabinet Decision No. 75 of 2023](https://tax.gov.ae/Datafolder/Files/Legislation/Cabinet%20Decision%20No.%2075%20of%202023%20and%20amendments%20-%20For%20Publishing.pdf)).
   - **Waiver:** if it files its first return (period ending 31 December 2026) within 7 months of the period end, by 31 July 2027, instead of 9 months, the FTA waives the penalty or refunds it if paid ([CTP006](https://tax.gov.ae/Datafolder/Files/Pdf/2025/CTP006-Waiver-of-Late-Registration-Administrative-Penalty-02-07-2025.pdf)). Any tax is still due by 30 September 2027.

4. **Individual with a sole establishment licence.**
   - **Facts:** a resident designer trades under a sole establishment licence. Business turnover is AED 1,400,000 in 2026.
   - **Corporate Tax:** turnover exceeds AED 1,000,000 in the calendar year, so she is a Taxable Person for 2026 and registers by 31 March 2027. Her return for 2026 is due by 30 September 2027. She may elect Small Business Relief, because revenue is not above AED 3,000,000.
   - **VAT:** separately, her taxable supplies exceeded AED 375,000 during 2026, so VAT registration was due within 30 days of that point, long before the Corporate Tax deadline.
   - **Had turnover been AED 750,000:** no Corporate Tax and no Corporate Tax registration for 2026; VAT registration would still be required because the supplies exceed AED 375,000.

5. **Foreign company opening a UAE branch.**
   - **Facts:** a foreign company opens a branch in the UAE, which becomes a permanent establishment on 1 May 2026.
   - **Registration:** within 6 months from the date the permanent establishment exists, by 1 November 2026. The foreign company is a Non-Resident Person taxed on the profits attributable to the branch.
   - **If the branch is registered in a free zone:** the branch is a Free Zone Person and may be a QFZP only if it meets every condition, including audited statements and substance.
   - **If the parent is in a group with consolidated revenue of EUR 750 million or more:** the domestic minimum top-up tax may apply: refer.

## When to refuse or refer

- **QFZP planning** where activities, customers, substance or outsourcing are unclear, or the de minimis test will be close. Never tell a client a free zone company "pays 0%" without evidence of every condition. Refer to an FTA-registered tax agent ([Corporate Tax Law, Article 18](https://mof.gov.ae/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Federal-Decree-Law-No.-47-of-2022-and-its-amendments-en-v13.1.26.pdf)).
- **Multinational groups** with consolidated revenue of EUR 750 million or more (top-up tax registration and returns), and any group seeking a Tax Group or qualifying group relief ([Cabinet Decision No. 142 of 2024](https://tax.gov.ae/Datafolder/Files/Legislation/Cabinet-Decision-No-142-of-2024-on-Top-up-Tax-on-MNEs.pdf)).
- **Foreign companies** that may be effectively managed and controlled from the UAE, or that may have a permanent establishment or nexus here without meaning to.
- **Unincorporated partnerships, trusts and foundations**, and applications to be treated as a taxable partnership (Article 16).
- **Branches** of UAE companies in free zones, and mixed mainland and free zone operations.
- **Restructurings**: transferring an existing business into a new company, business restructuring relief, or moving from sole establishment to company.
- **Legacy ESR** filings or penalties for financial years ending on or before 31 December 2022.
- **Licensing, capital, UBO, visa, labour and AML questions**: outside this Guide; send the client to the licensing authority or the relevant regulator.
- **Excise tax** (tobacco, energy and sweetened drinks and similar goods): outside this Guide.
- **Refuse** to recommend a structure that splits one business across several licences or entities to multiply the AED 375,000 band or stay under the Small Business Relief threshold. The FTA can treat artificial separation as an avoidance arrangement ([Cabinet Decision No. 116 of 2022](https://tax.gov.ae/Datafolder/Files/Legislation/Corporate%20Tax/Cabinet%20Decision%20No.%20116%20of%202022%20-%20for%20publishing.pdf); [Ministerial Decision No. 73 of 2023](https://tax.gov.ae/Datafolder/Files/Legislation/Corporate%20Tax/Ministerial%20Decision%20No.%2073%20of%202023%20-%20for%20publishing.pdf)).

## Filing and payment

### First filings after formation ([Corporate Tax Law, Articles 51, 53 and 56](https://mof.gov.ae/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Federal-Decree-Law-No.-47-of-2022-and-its-amendments-en-v13.1.26.pdf); [Tax Procedures Law, Article 6](https://tax.gov.ae/Datafolder/Files/Legislation/2025/Federal%20Decree-Law%20No.%2028%20of%202022%20-%20publishing%2003%2012%202025.pdf))

- **Corporate Tax registration** on EmaraTax gives the company a TRN. The FTA can also register a person on its own initiative, effective from the date the person became a Taxable Person (Article 51(3)).
- **VAT registration** is a separate EmaraTax application with its own TRN and effective date; the VAT return periods are those the FTA assigns (see **ae-vat-return**).
- **Corporate Tax return and payment** are due 9 months after the end of each tax period:

| First tax period ends | Return and payment due |
| --- | --- |
| 31 March 2026 | 31 December 2026 |
| 30 June 2026 | 31 March 2027 |
| 30 September 2026 | 30 June 2027 |
| 31 December 2026 | 30 September 2027 |

- **Audited financial statements** for a QFZP (every period) and for any company with revenue exceeding AED 50,000,000.
- **Changes:** tell the FTA within 20 Business Days of any event that changes the information on the tax record (for example a new licence activity, address, owner or manager).
- **Closing down:** a company that ceases business applies to deregister within the FTA's timeline, and cannot be deregistered until all returns (including for the period to cessation), tax and penalties are settled (Article 52).
- **E-invoicing:** the UAE e-invoicing system is being phased in by revenue band; see **uae-vat** for the dates that apply to a new company.

### Returns being filed now: periods ending in 2025 ([Corporate Tax Law, Article 53](https://mof.gov.ae/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Federal-Decree-Law-No.-47-of-2022-and-its-amendments-en-v13.1.26.pdf))

A company formed in 2025 files its first return 9 months after its first period end. A company with a first period ending 31 December 2025 files and pays by 30 September 2026. The same rates, the AED 375,000 band and the Small Business Relief threshold applied to 2025 periods.

### Penalties most often met at formation ([Cabinet Decision No. 75 of 2023 and amendments](https://tax.gov.ae/Datafolder/Files/Legislation/Cabinet%20Decision%20No.%2075%20of%202023%20and%20amendments%20-%20For%20Publishing.pdf); [VAT penalties, Cabinet Decision No. 40 of 2017 as amended](https://tax.gov.ae/Datafolder/Files/Legislation/2025/Cabinet%20Decision%20No.%2040%20of%202017%20and%20its%20amendments%20-%20publishing%2011%202025.pdf))

| Violation | Penalty |
| --- | --- |
| Late Corporate Tax registration | AED 10,000; waived or refunded if the first return is filed within 7 months of the end of the first tax period (first period only) |
| Late VAT registration application | AED 10,000 |
| Late Corporate Tax return | AED 500 for each month or part month in the first 12 months, then AED 1,000 |
| Failure to keep Corporate Tax records | AED 10,000, or AED 20,000 for a repeat within 24 months |
| Late Corporate Tax deregistration application | AED 1,000 a month, up to AED 10,000 |
| Failure to update the Corporate Tax record | AED 1,000, or AED 5,000 for a repeat within 24 months |

The full penalty tables are in **ae-corporate-tax** and **uae-vat**.

## Completion checklist ([FTA Decision No. 3 of 2024](https://tax.gov.ae/Datafolder/Files/Legislation/RE%20FTA%20Decision%20No.%203%20of%202024%20on%20Registration%20Timeline%20for%20Corporate%20Tax%20%20-%20For%20publishing.pdf); [Ministerial Decision No. 84 of 2025](https://tax.gov.ae/Datafolder/Files/Pdf/2025/MD-No-84-of-2025.pdf); [VAT Executive Regulation](https://tax.gov.ae/Datafolder/Files/Legislation/2026/Law-No-8-of-2017-and-its-amendments--09-2026.pdf))

- [ ] Activities, customers and owners recorded; multinational group status and revenue checked.
- [ ] Vehicle chosen with the tax consequences in the "Mainland or free zone" table explained to the client.
- [ ] Licensing, capital, lease, UBO and fees marked **check** and sent to the licensing authority; no figures given for them.
- [ ] Financial year end agreed; first tax period and its return date diarised.
- [ ] Corporate Tax registration made within 3 months of incorporation (or the deadline that fits the person), TRN recorded.
- [ ] VAT tested monthly against AED 375,000 (mandatory, more-than) and AED 187,500 (voluntary); application within 30 days if required.
- [ ] Free zone company: substance, Qualifying Income tracking, de minimis test, transfer pricing file and auditor in place before trading.
- [ ] Mainland small company: Small Business Relief conditions noted and election diarised for each return.
- [ ] Books set up for standalone financial statements; 7-year record retention; audit required if revenue exceeds AED 50,000,000 or QFZP.
- [ ] ESR confirmed as not required for years ending after 31 December 2022.
- [ ] Change-of-details duty (20 Business Days) explained.
- [ ] Referred items (QFZP edge cases, groups, restructurings, partnerships) handed on.
