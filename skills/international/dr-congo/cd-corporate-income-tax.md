# DR Congo Corporate Income Tax

## Corporate income tax rates and base

Corporate income tax (Impôt sur les Bénéfices et Profits, IBP) applies at a standard 30% on DRC-source profits, with turnover-based minimum and simplified regimes for smaller companies. This is an unverified 2025 draft pending accountant review.

- **Standard corporate income tax rate** — 30% percent  _(Code Général des Impôts (RDC) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/democratic-republic-of-the-congo/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_
- **Mining companies** — 30% (same standard CIT rate applies) percent  _(Code Minier (RDC) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/democratic-republic-of-the-congo/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_
- **Minimum tax (loss-making or low-profit companies)** — 1% of yearly turnover percent  _(Code Général des Impôts (RDC) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/democratic-republic-of-the-congo/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_
- **Small company (turnover CDF 25m–300m) — supply of goods** — 1% of turnover percent  _(Code Général des Impôts (RDC) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/democratic-republic-of-the-congo/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_
- **Small company (turnover CDF 25m–300m) — supply of services** — 2% of turnover percent  _(Code Général des Impôts (RDC) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/democratic-republic-of-the-congo/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_
- **Micro company (turnover below CDF 25m)** — Lump-sum amount set by the Minister of Finance  _(Code Général des Impôts (RDC) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/democratic-republic-of-the-congo/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_
- **Rental income tax (Kinshasa province)** — 12% flat, with 10% withheld by the tenant percent  _(Code Général des Impôts (RDC) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/democratic-republic-of-the-congo/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_
- **Tax base** — Net DRC-source profit; turnover includes all profits, interest received, exceptional profits and capital gains  _(Code Général des Impôts (RDC) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/democratic-republic-of-the-congo/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_
- **Withholding tax on dividends** — 20% standard; reduced 10% in the mining sector percent  _(Code Général des Impôts (RDC) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/democratic-republic-of-the-congo/corporate/withholding-taxes)_
- **Withholding tax on interest (non-residents)** — 0–20% (standard 20%) percent  _(Code Général des Impôts (RDC) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/democratic-republic-of-the-congo/corporate/withholding-taxes)_
- **Withholding tax on royalties** — 20% on the net amount (after a 30% deduction from the invoiced total) percent  _(Code Général des Impôts (RDC) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/democratic-republic-of-the-congo/corporate/withholding-taxes)_
- **Withholding tax on service fees (non-residents)** — 14% percent  _(Code Général des Impôts (RDC) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/democratic-republic-of-the-congo/corporate/withholding-taxes)_
- **Annual CIT return deadline** — 30 April of the following year  _(Code Général des Impôts (RDC) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/democratic-republic-of-the-congo/corporate/tax-administration)_
- **First CIT installment (30%)** — Before 1 August  _(Code Général des Impôts (RDC) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/democratic-republic-of-the-congo/corporate/tax-administration)_
- **Second CIT installment (30%)** — Before 1 October  _(Code Général des Impôts (RDC) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/democratic-republic-of-the-congo/corporate/tax-administration)_
- **Third CIT installment (20%)** — Before 1 December  _(Code Général des Impôts (RDC) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/democratic-republic-of-the-congo/corporate/tax-administration)_
