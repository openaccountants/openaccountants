# Palestine: VAT registration, calculation and reporting

## Law and period

Read Decree-Law 26/2024 with Decree-Law 11/2026. The Gazette catalogue gives 30 June 2025 as the original law's commencement; the amending law's article 12 makes it effective from issue, 13 June 2026. Its Gazette publication is dated 25 June 2026. The amendment changes administrative powers, non-resident registration, objections and other procedures. This draft incorporates the specified changes below, but has not reconciled every implementing instruction or subsequent measure. [Original law](https://mjr.ogb.gov.ps/Decrees/Download/?d=قرار-بقانون-رقم-26-لسنة-2024-م-فلسطين&p=e6078fbb-948a-4721-a2cd-48550faba4e0.pdf); [amending law](https://mjr.ogb.gov.ps/Decrees/Download/?d=قرار-بقانون-رقم-11-لسنة-2026-بتعديل-قرار-بقانون-رقم-26-لسنة-2024-بشأن-ضريبة-القيمة-المضافة-فلسطين&p=5ff774ba-26dd-41c9-a5d6-3d5d7dffbb21.pdf).

Actual territorial administration in the West Bank, Gaza and East Jerusalem remains unresolved. No uniform operational treatment is asserted. Confirm location, governing administration, transaction date and applicable instructions before use. This is a research translation; Arabic controls.

## Standard rate and taxable base

Article 2(1) sets **16%** for transactions in the State and goods imports. Article 2(2) permits Cabinet changes or differentiated rates on the Minister's recommendation within the statutory ceiling. An activity's zero-rating, exemption or special treatment must therefore be checked separately. The examples assume an ordinary transaction for which 16% has been established; they are not an exhaustive current rate schedule. [Original law, article 2, printed page 32](https://mjr.ogb.gov.ps/Decrees/Download/?d=قرار-بقانون-رقم-26-لسنة-2024-م-فلسطين&p=e6078fbb-948a-4721-a2cd-48550faba4e0.pdf).

Article 7(5) treats an advertised, offered or otherwise announced price as **tax-inclusive unless explicitly stated otherwise**. Article 8(1) bases goods-import VAT on customs value plus customs duties and other taxes/fees due on import. Do not use the supplier's invoice alone as the import base. Classification, valuation and other import charges require their own evidence. [Articles 7–8, page 33](https://mjr.ogb.gov.ps/Decrees/Download/?d=قرار-بقانون-رقم-26-لسنة-2024-م-فلسطين&p=e6078fbb-948a-4721-a2cd-48550faba4e0.pdf).

## Registration categories

Article 61(4) provides exempt-trader registration, excluding liberal professions, where business transaction turnover does not exceed **JOD20,000**, its equivalent in legally circulating currency, or another amount set under the law. Article 1 defines turnover using the previous financial year's transactions and excludes fixed-asset/equipment sales. This is a statutory registration category, not a general permission for every small business to remain unregistered. Current threshold adjustment and conversion instructions remain to be checked. Article 62 requires a registration certificate identifying number, category and economic activity. [Articles 1, 61–62, pages 31, 48–49](https://mjr.ogb.gov.ps/Decrees/Download/?d=قرار-بقانون-رقم-26-لسنة-2024-م-فلسطين&p=e6078fbb-948a-4721-a2cd-48550faba4e0.pdf).

For a person resident abroad with business activity in the State, article 68 requires a resident agent appointed in writing, notification to the department and the original agency instrument; the agent bears the relevant tax duties. The amended article 69 permits the **Director General** to register residents outside the State wishing to conduct business inside it, under conditions and guarantees set by ministerial instructions. Do not retain the original article 69's allocation of this power to the Director. [Original article 68, page 50](https://mjr.ogb.gov.ps/Decrees/Download/?d=قرار-بقانون-رقم-26-لسنة-2024-م-فلسطين&p=e6078fbb-948a-4721-a2cd-48550faba4e0.pdf); [2026 amendment, article 6, page 6](https://mjr.ogb.gov.ps/Decrees/Download/?d=قرار-بقانون-رقم-11-لسنة-2026-بتعديل-قرار-بقانون-رقم-26-لسنة-2024-بشأن-ضريبة-القيمة-المضافة-فلسطين&p=5ff774ba-26dd-41c9-a5d6-3d5d7dffbb21.pdf).

## Cross-border electronic transactions

Article 22 covers goods bought from abroad through electronic commerce under the customs rules, requires commercial electronic traders to register, and places tax on electronically supplied services from abroad on the recipient. Its detailed operation is delegated to implementing regulations. This text alone does not establish a foreign-platform registration portal or a complete reverse-charge calculation and recovery procedure. [Article 22, page 37](https://mjr.ogb.gov.ps/Decrees/Download/?d=قرار-بقانون-رقم-26-لسنة-2024-م-فلسطين&p=e6078fbb-948a-4721-a2cd-48550faba4e0.pdf).

## Input tax: evidence and restrictions

Article 36(1) allows deduction supported by a compliant tax invoice issued for the trader, an import declaration or another approved document. The import/other document must identify the trader. The provision sets **six months from document issue** and also says the deduction cannot be carried to the following financial year. Do not read the six-month period as overriding that separate year restriction. Article 36(2) denies input deduction for exempt transactions. [Article 36, page 42](https://mjr.ogb.gov.ps/Decrees/Download/?d=قرار-بقانون-رقم-26-لسنة-2024-م-فلسطين&p=e6078fbb-948a-4721-a2cd-48550faba4e0.pdf).

Article 38 normally excludes pre-registration documents but makes an exception for input tax paid by the trader before registration where the trader proves to the Director that the inputs arose during, and were used for, establishing the business. An incorporation date alone does not prove eligibility. Mixed use, private vehicles, invoice defects, year-end timing and the complete July 2025 input-tax instructions still need reconciliation before approving a live claim. [Article 38, page 43](https://mjr.ogb.gov.ps/Decrees/Download/?d=قرار-بقانون-رقم-26-لسنة-2024-م-فلسطين&p=e6078fbb-948a-4721-a2cd-48550faba4e0.pdf).

## Reporting and records

Article 75(1) requires monthly reports from registration, within **15 days of the following month**, including periods with no activity. Article 75(3) permits an authorised period-specific exemption and article 75(6) permits postponement; neither is assumed automatically. Reports include transaction categories, output tax and input tax. Article 76 requires a supplementary report for omitted information. Article 79 requires business books, databases, records and invoices to be retained for **five years** under the applicable bookkeeping rules. Exact retention-start rules and legal holds need separate checking. [Articles 75–79, pages 52–53](https://mjr.ogb.gov.ps/Decrees/Download/?d=قرار-بقانون-رقم-26-لسنة-2024-م-فلسطين&p=e6078fbb-948a-4721-a2cd-48550faba4e0.pdf).

This report deadline is not stated here as the complete payment rule. Payment mechanics, extensions, corrections, refunds and penalties remain open work. No unverified filing portal is supplied.

## Objection procedure changed in 2026

The replacement article 84 requires an objection committee appointed by the Minister on the Director General's recommendation, with at least five first-category employees including a legally qualified member. Members must not have previously examined or participated in the challenged decision. The Director forwards the written objection with the taxpayer file. Undisputed tax must be paid before an objection can be presented. The committee has **one year from submission** to decide; otherwise the objection is treated as accepted. This is not the objection filing deadline or an unconditional payment suspension. Service, filing deadlines and court appeals require the remaining provisions and current procedural rules. [Amending article 7 replacing article 84, pages 6–7](https://mjr.ogb.gov.ps/Decrees/Download/?d=قرار-بقانون-رقم-11-لسنة-2026-بتعديل-قرار-بقانون-رقم-26-لسنة-2024-بشأن-ضريبة-القيمة-المضافة-فلسطين&p=5ff774ba-26dd-41c9-a5d6-3d5d7dffbb21.pdf).

## Example 1: tax-inclusive ordinary price

Assume a price of ILS11,600 includes VAT, the ordinary 16% rate applies, and no exemption/special valuation applies. Net value is ILS10,000 and VAT is ILS1,600. Multiplying the inclusive price directly by 16% would overstate the included VAT.

```oa-check
name: Palestine VAT included in ordinary advertised price
inputs:
  inclusive_price: 11600
  rate: 0.16
steps:
  - net_value: inclusive_price / (1 + rate)
  - vat: inclusive_price - net_value
expected:
  net_value: 10000
  vat: 1600
```

## Example 2: goods import base

Assume an independently verified customs value of ILS50,000, customs duties of ILS5,000 and other import taxes/fees of ILS1,000. Assume all belong in article 8(1)'s base, ordinary 16% VAT applies, and no relief applies. The base is ILS56,000 and import VAT ILS8,960. These assumed charges do not establish tariff rates, and payment does not automatically establish input-credit entitlement.

```oa-check
name: Palestine VAT on established import valuation and charges
inputs:
  customs_value: 50000
  customs_duties: 5000
  other_import_charges: 1000
  rate: 0.16
steps:
  - vat_base: customs_value + customs_duties + other_import_charges
  - import_vat: vat_base * rate
expected:
  vat_base: 56000
  import_vat: 8960
```
