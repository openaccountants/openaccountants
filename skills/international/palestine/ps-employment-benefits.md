# Palestine: employment benefits and work-injury insurance

## Scope before calculation

Labour Law 7/2000 article 3 excludes government/local-authority employees, domestic workers and similar categories subject to a special regulation, and the employer's first-degree family members. Do not apply this Guide to those categories without checking their separate regime. Article 6 treats the law as minimum rights and preserves more favourable provisions under a special employment arrangement. Identify applicable contractual, collective and establishment rules before settling benefits. [Articles 3 and 6](https://mjr.ogb.gov.ps/Decrees/ViewText/26866).

This draft translates selected official Arabic provisions. Later amendments, case law and emergency measures have not been exhaustively reconciled. Actual administration across the West Bank, Gaza and East Jerusalem remains unresolved. It is not a uniform territorial opinion, complete payroll pack or professional attestation.

## Social-security suspension is a separate question

Decree-Law 4/2019 article 1 suspends the operation of Decree-Law 19/2016 on social security and its amendments. Articles 2 and 4 provide for continuing dialogue on the law and its commencement, and effectiveness from issue on **28 January 2019**. Gazette publication was **19 February 2019**. The official catalogue retrieved for this research labels the suspension law in force. This establishes the retrieved suspension instrument, not a comprehensive search of every later measure or every pension/insurance scheme. Do not import historical 2016 contribution rates into a current payroll calculation without resolving legal status. [Suspension law](https://mjr.ogb.gov.ps/Decrees/Download/?d=قرار-بقانون-رقم-4-لسنة-2019-م-بشان-قانون-الضمان-الاجتماعي-فلسطين&p=08062c5b-88ab-4c17-accf-fddd7b58cf79.pdf); [catalogue](https://mjr.ogb.gov.ps/Decrees/Details/29244).

No zero-contribution rate or social-contributions “not applicable” conclusion is made here. Public pensions, sector schemes, cross-border employment and contractual insurance require separate examination. Labour Law benefits and work-injury obligations must be analysed on their own terms.

## End-of-service base and service

Article 45 grants a worker who has completed one year of service one month's wage per service year, using the last wage **excluding overtime**, and counting fractions of a year. Article 1 distinguishes full wage, including allowances, from basic wage; do not automatically substitute basic salary. For piecework or commission workers, article 48 uses the average monthly wage during the final year for end-of-service and unfair-dismissal compensation. Verify the wage components and service period from records. [Articles 1, 45 and 48](https://mjr.ogb.gov.ps/Decrees/ViewText/26866).

## Reason for leaving changes entitlement

Article 42(2) provides one-third of the end-of-service benefit for resignation during the first five years, two-thirds during the following five, and full benefit after ten years or more. Article 42(1) separately preserves rights where the worker leaves after notifying the employer in its specified circumstances, including an employer's failure to meet obligations despite written demand. Do not classify every employee-initiated departure as ordinary resignation. The examples avoid the disputed application of an exact five-year boundary and assume classification is already established. [Article 42](https://mjr.ogb.gov.ps/Decrees/ViewText/26866).

Article 41 preserves notice pay and end-of-service rights for technical reasons or losses requiring workforce reduction, subject to notifying the Ministry. Article 47 separately provides unfair-dismissal compensation of two months' wage per year, capped at two years' wage, while preserving other rights. An employer's description of the termination does not establish its legal validity. Notice, dismissal compensation, leave balances and end-of-service benefit are separate components. [Articles 41, 46–47](https://mjr.ogb.gov.ps/Decrees/ViewText/26866).

## The linked 2018 judgment has a specific context

The Gazette links articles 42(2) and 45 to Constitutional Court interpretation 07/2018. The judgment addresses settlement of accrued rights during transition to the 2016 social-security regime under its article 116(1)(b). It treats that transition as continuation of employment, not resignation, and applies the article 45 basis subject to better worker rights. It also distinguishes calculation from payment arrangements for that transitional settlement. Do not turn this pre-suspension judgment into a claim that ordinary resignation reductions were repealed or that all current termination payments can be deferred. Historic transition settlements need individual review alongside the later suspension. [Judgment, reasoning and operative conclusions, printed pages 150–152](https://mjr.ogb.gov.ps/JudicialDecisions/Download/?d=طلب+تفسير+07-2018.pdf&p=18b3e762-ac48-4aef-86a8-68924e02cd84.pdf).

## Work-injury insurance and reporting

Article 116 requires the employer to insure all its workers against work injuries with licensed providers in Palestine. This is not a statutory percentage contribution schedule. Article 117 requires first aid and transfer to the nearest treatment centre, immediate police reporting for fatal injury or bodily harm preventing continued work, and written notification to the Ministry and insurer within **48 hours** of every work injury, with a copy to the injured worker. [Articles 116–117](https://mjr.ogb.gov.ps/Decrees/ViewText/26866).

Article 125 states that work-injury compensation does not prevent receipt of the end-of-service benefit due. Do not offset one against the other merely because both arise on departure. Insurance premiums, policy coverage, injury classification, compensation rules and claims procedures need separate verification. [Article 125](https://mjr.ogb.gov.ps/Decrees/ViewText/26866).

## Example 1: full entitlement after established service

Assume article 45 applies, the worker has exactly four qualifying years, the independently established final monthly wage excluding overtime is ILS3,000, no resignation reduction applies and no more favourable agreement exists. End-of-service benefit is ILS12,000. This excludes other termination entitlements and does not determine tax treatment.

```oa-check
name: Palestine full end-of-service benefit on assumed eligible service
inputs:
  monthly_wage_excluding_overtime: 3000
  service_years: 4
steps:
  - benefit: monthly_wage_excluding_overtime * service_years
expected:
  benefit: 12000
```

## Example 2: ordinary resignation reduction

Assume an ordinary resignation within article 42(2), exactly three qualifying years, ILS3,000 established monthly wage excluding overtime, no article 42(1) circumstance and no more favourable agreement. The full reference benefit is ILS9,000; one-third gives ILS3,000. If article 42(1) preserves full rights, this reduction must not be used.

```oa-check
name: Palestine ordinary resignation in first five years
inputs:
  monthly_wage_excluding_overtime: 3000
  service_years: 3
  reduction_divisor: 3
steps:
  - full_reference_benefit: monthly_wage_excluding_overtime * service_years
  - resignation_benefit: full_reference_benefit / reduction_divisor
expected:
  full_reference_benefit: 9000
  resignation_benefit: 3000
```

## Employer records and outstanding work

Article 12 requires a monthly statement to the local labour office covering worker names, number, jobs, ages, sex, qualifications, wages, commencement dates and vacancies. Current delivery channels and operational instructions remain unverified. [Article 12](https://mjr.ogb.gov.ps/Decrees/ViewText/26866).

Collect employment category, location, service dates, wage/allowance/overtime history, reason and evidence for departure, notices, agreements and insurance records. Remaining work includes current social-security and pension status by category, territorial application, later cases, special workers, benefit tax treatment, service interruptions, precise boundary cases, work-injury compensation, paid leave and live reporting processes. No payroll or social-contribution topic is closed by this draft.
