# Latvia: tax pack

Calendar tax year for individuals; companies use their financial year as the reporting year. Authority: the State Revenue Service (Valsts ieņēmumu dienests, VID). Euro. Company profit is taxed only when distributed or treated as distributed: corporate income tax is 20% of the distributed amount divided by 0.8, filed monthly or quarterly. Transfer pricing adjustments are one of those deemed distributions. The transfer pricing documentation rules were rewritten from 1 January 2026, for reporting years starting in 2025 onward.
