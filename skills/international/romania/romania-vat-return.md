# Romania VAT return (pointer to ro-vat-return)

This slug used to hold a full copy of the Romanian VAT skill. The two copies were identical and both carried a wrong citation (OUG 59/2025) for the August 2025 rate change. To keep one source of truth, the content now lives only in [ro-vat-return](ro-vat-return.md). Load that skill for any Romanian VAT work.

## Headline figures for 2026

| Item | Rate or amount | Source URL |
|---|---|---|
| Standard rate, Fiscal Code art. 291(1), as rewritten by Legea 141/2025 art. II pt. 42 (in force 1 August 2025, art. VII(1)) | 21% | https://static.anaf.ro/static/10/Anaf/legislatie/L_141_2025.pdf |
| Reduced rate, art. 291(2): medicines, food (with exceptions), water, books and press, museum entry, firewood, heat, social-policy buildings, hotels, restaurants and catering | 11% | https://static.anaf.ro/static/10/Anaf/legislatie/Cod_fiscal_norme_2023.htm |
| Transitional rate for qualifying homes delivered by 31 July 2026, Legea 141/2025 art. III | 9% | https://static.anaf.ro/static/10/Anaf/legislatie/L_141_2025.pdf |
| Small-enterprise exemption threshold, art. 310(1) | 395,000 lei | https://static.anaf.ro/static/10/Anaf/legislatie/Cod_fiscal_norme_2023.htm |
| D300 due date, art. 323(1) and OPANAF 174/2026 | 25th of the month after the period | https://static.anaf.ro/static/10/Anaf/legislatie/OPANAF_174_2026.pdf |
| D394 due date, OPANAF 3769/2015 as amended by OPANAF 77/2022 | 30th of the month after the period | https://static.anaf.ro/static/10/Anaf/legislatie/OPANAF_77_2022.pdf |
| RO e-Factura transmission deadline from 1 January 2026, OUG 120/2021 art. 10(7) as rewritten by OUG 89/2025 | 5 working days | https://static.anaf.ro/static/10/Anaf/legislatie/OUG_89_2025.pdf |

There is no 19%, 9% (other than the transitional homes) or 5% rate in art. 291 since 1 August 2025.
