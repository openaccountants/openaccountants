# Central African Republic Personal Income Tax

## Personal income tax (IRPP) bands and rates

- **IRPP band 1 (tax-free threshold)** — 0% on annual income up to XAF 378,000 XAF ((approx — confirm))  _(Code Général des Impôts (CAR) — IRPP barème — https://remotepeople.com/countries/central-african-republic/employer-of-record/payroll-tax/)_
- **IRPP band 2** — 8% on annual income from XAF 378,001 to 1,680,000 XAF ((approx — confirm))  _(Code Général des Impôts (CAR) — IRPP barème — https://remotepeople.com/countries/central-african-republic/employer-of-record/payroll-tax/)_
- **IRPP band 3** — 15% on annual income from XAF 1,680,001 to 3,360,000 XAF ((approx — confirm))  _(Code Général des Impôts (CAR) — IRPP barème — https://remotepeople.com/countries/central-african-republic/employer-of-record/payroll-tax/)_
- **IRPP band 4** — 28% on annual income from XAF 3,360,001 to 5,040,000 XAF ((approx — confirm))  _(Code Général des Impôts (CAR) — IRPP barème — https://remotepeople.com/countries/central-african-republic/employer-of-record/payroll-tax/)_
- **IRPP band 5 (top rate)** — 40% on annual income above XAF 5,040,000 XAF ((approx — confirm))  _(Code Général des Impôts (CAR) — IRPP barème — https://remotepeople.com/countries/central-african-republic/employer-of-record/payroll-tax/)_
- **Tax-free threshold** — XAF 378,000 of annual income XAF ((approx — confirm))  _(Code Général des Impôts (CAR) — IRPP barème)_
- **Deduction per dependent** — Approximately XAF 50,000 per dependent, up to a maximum of 3 dependents XAF ((approx — confirm))  _(Code Général des Impôts (CAR) — IRPP — https://remotepeople.com/countries/central-african-republic/employer-of-record/payroll-tax/)_
- **Non-resident individual withholding on CAR-source income** — Flat 15% withholding on CAR-source income percent ((approx — confirm))  _(Code Général des Impôts (CAR) — https://remotepeople.com/countries/central-african-republic/employer-of-record/payroll-tax/)_
- **Capital gains tax — residents** — 15% percent ((approx — confirm))  _(Code Général des Impôts (CAR) — https://remotepeople.com/countries/central-african-republic/employer-of-record/payroll-tax/)_
- **Capital gains tax — non-residents** — 20% percent ((approx — confirm))  _(Code Général des Impôts (CAR) — https://remotepeople.com/countries/central-african-republic/employer-of-record/payroll-tax/)_
- **Individual residence test** — Generally tax-resident if domiciled or with principal place of abode/economic interests in CAR (French-code criteria) ((approx — confirm))  _(Code Général des Impôts (CAR))_
- **Annual return filing & payment deadline** — 31 May of the year following the income year ((approx — confirm))  _(Code Général des Impôts (CAR) — https://remotepeople.com/countries/central-african-republic/employer-of-record/payroll-tax/)_

Individuals are subject to the Impôt sur le Revenu des Personnes Physiques (IRPP) on a progressive scale. The bands below are a source-cited draft and must be confirmed against the current CAR finance law (loi de finances), as published bracket amounts vary by source.
