# Central African Republic Corporate Income Tax

## Corporate income tax (Impôt sur les Sociétés)

Companies are subject to the Impôt sur les Sociétés (IS) on taxable profits, with a minimum tax based on turnover where the IS computed is lower. Rates and the minimum tax basis below are a source-cited draft to be verified against the current CAR finance law.

- **Standard corporate income tax rate** — 30% percent  _([Code Général des Impôts (CAR) — Impôt sur les Sociétés](https://taxatlas.io/region/central-africa))_
- **Reduced rate — agricultural activities** — 20% for resident companies engaged in agricultural activities percent (approx — confirm)  _([Code Général des Impôts (CAR) — Impôt sur les Sociétés](https://maxishr.com/en/central-african-republic/company-registration))_
- **Minimum lump-sum tax (IMF) — general businesses** — 1.85% of annual turnover percent (approx — confirm)  _([Code Général des Impôts (CAR) — Impôt Minimum Forfaitaire](https://maxishr.com/en/central-african-republic/company-registration))_
- **Minimum lump-sum tax (IMF) — agriculture** — 3% of turnover for agriculture-related businesses percent (approx — confirm)  _([Code Général des Impôts (CAR) — Impôt Minimum Forfaitaire](https://maxishr.com/en/central-african-republic/company-registration))_
- **Tax base** — Net taxable profit (worldwide profit for resident companies; CAR-source profit for permanent establishments of non-residents) (approx — confirm)  _(Code Général des Impôts (CAR) — Impôt sur les Sociétés)_
- **Withholding tax on dividends** — 15% (non-residents) percent (approx — confirm)  _([Code Général des Impôts (CAR)](https://remotepeople.com/countries/central-african-republic/employer-of-record/payroll-tax/))_
- **Withholding tax on royalties** — 20% (non-residents) percent (approx — confirm)  _([Code Général des Impôts (CAR)](https://remotepeople.com/countries/central-african-republic/employer-of-record/payroll-tax/))_
- **Withholding tax on non-resident service fees** — 15% on CAR-source service payments to non-residents percent (approx — confirm)  _([Code Général des Impôts (CAR)](https://remotepeople.com/countries/central-african-republic/employer-of-record/payroll-tax/))_
- **Withholding tax on interest** — 15% (non-residents) percent (approx — confirm)  _(Code Général des Impôts (CAR))_
- **Annual return filing & payment deadline** — 30 April of the year following the financial year (approx — confirm)  _([Code Général des Impôts (CAR)](https://maxishr.com/en/central-african-republic/company-registration))_
