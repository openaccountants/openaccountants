# Isle of Man Tax Overview

## The Isle of Man tax system at a glance

The Isle of Man is a self-governing British Crown Dependency with its own low-tax regime, administered by the Income Tax Division (Assessor of Taxes). It operates a UK-style VAT through a Customs and Excise revenue-sharing agreement, but its direct-tax rates are markedly lower than the UK's.

- **Personal income tax year** — 6 April to 5 April  _([Income Tax Act 1970](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/isle-of-man/individual/tax-administration))_
- **Currency** — Manx pound (IMP), at par with pound sterling (GBP)  _(Currency Act 1992)_
- **Tax authority (direct tax)** — Income Tax Division of the Treasury (Assessor of Taxes)  _([Income Tax Act 1970](https://www.gov.im/categories/tax-vat-and-your-money/income-tax-and-national-insurance/))_
- **Indirect tax authority (VAT/Customs)** — Isle of Man Customs and Excise Division  _([Value Added Tax Act 1996](https://www.gov.im/categories/tax-vat-and-your-money/customs-and-excise/vat/))_
- **Personal residence basis** — Residents taxed on worldwide income; non-residents taxed on Isle of Man-source income only  _([Income Tax Act 1970](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/isle-of-man/individual/residence))_
- **Headline personal income tax rates (2025/26)** — Standard (lower) rate 10%; upper rate 21% %  _([Income Tax Act 1970](https://www.gov.im/categories/tax-vat-and-your-money/income-tax-and-national-insurance/individuals/residents/rates-and-allowances/))_
- **Annual personal tax cap (election)** — IMP 220,000 per individual; IMP 440,000 per jointly assessed couple IMP  _([Income Tax Act 1970](https://www.pwc.com/im/en/services/tax/isleofman-tax-rates.html))_
- **Headline corporate income tax rate** — 0% standard rate (10%/20% for specified activities; 15% Pillar Two top-up for in-scope groups)  _([Income Tax Act 1970](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/isle-of-man/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income))_
- **Does VAT/GST exist?** — Yes — UK-aligned VAT under a Customs and Excise revenue-sharing agreement with the UK; standard rate 20%  _([Value Added Tax Act 1996](https://www.gov.im/categories/tax-vat-and-your-money/customs-and-excise/vat/))_
- **Capital gains tax and inheritance tax** — No capital gains tax and no inheritance tax in the Isle of Man  _([Income Tax Act 1970](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/isle-of-man/individual/other-taxes))_
- **Personal income tax return deadline** — Before 6 October following the end of the tax year  _([Income Tax Act 1970](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/isle-of-man/individual/tax-administration))_
- **Company income tax return deadline** — Within 1 year and 1 day of the accounting period end ((approx — confirm))  _([Income Tax Act 1970](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/isle-of-man/corporate/tax-administration))_
