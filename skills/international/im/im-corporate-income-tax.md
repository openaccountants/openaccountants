# Isle of Man Corporate Income Tax

## Company income tax rates and base

The Isle of Man taxes most company profits at 0%, with higher rates reserved for banking/large-retail, Isle of Man land income, and (from 2024) certain extraction activities. A 15% Pillar Two top-up applies to in-scope multinational groups. There are no withholding taxes on dividends, interest or royalties.

- **Standard corporate income tax rate** — 0% %  _(Income Tax Act 1970 — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/isle-of-man/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_
- **10% rate (banking and large retail)** — 10% on banking deposit-taking income and on retail profits exceeding IMP 500,000 in the year %  _(Income Tax Act 1970 — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/isle-of-man/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_
- **20% rate (Isle of Man land/property and extraction)** — 20% on income from Isle of Man land and property, and (from 2024) petroleum extraction activities/rights %  _(Income Tax Act 1970 — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/isle-of-man/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_
- **Pillar Two domestic top-up rate** — 15% effective minimum rate for in-scope large multinational groups (OECD Pillar Two) % ((approx — confirm Act title))  _(Global Minimum Tax (Pillar Two) Act 2024 — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/isle-of-man/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_
- **Voluntary election to 10%** — Certain 0%-rate companies may elect to be taxed at 10%  _(Income Tax Act 1970 — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/isle-of-man/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_
- **Tax base** — Worldwide profits of resident companies; Isle of Man-source profits of non-resident companies  _(Income Tax Act 1970 — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/isle-of-man/corporate/income-determination)_
- **Withholding tax on dividends** — 0% (no withholding tax on dividends) %  _(Income Tax Act 1970 — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/isle-of-man/corporate/withholding-taxes)_
- **Withholding tax on interest** — 0% (no withholding tax on interest) %  _(Income Tax Act 1970 — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/isle-of-man/corporate/withholding-taxes)_
- **Withholding tax on royalties** — 0% (no withholding tax on royalties) %  _(Income Tax Act 1970 — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/isle-of-man/corporate/withholding-taxes)_
- **Distribution/loan charge** — Historic 'distributable profits charge' / attribution regimes abolished; relevant Manx-resident shareholders taxed on distributions ((approx — confirm))  _(Income Tax Act 1970 — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/isle-of-man/corporate/income-determination)_
- **Corporate tax return deadline** — Within 1 year and 1 day of the end of the accounting period ((approx — confirm))  _(Income Tax Act 1970 — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/isle-of-man/corporate/tax-administration)_
- **Corporate tax payment deadline** — Tax due within 1 year and 1 day of the accounting period end (alongside the return) ((approx — confirm))  _(Income Tax Act 1970 — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/isle-of-man/corporate/tax-administration)_
