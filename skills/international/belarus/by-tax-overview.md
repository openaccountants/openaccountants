# Belarus Tax Overview

## Belarus tax system at a glance

- **Tax system overview**: Belarus taxes residents on worldwide income and non-residents on Belarus-source income. Personal income tax is a flat 13% with higher bands for very large specified incomes. Companies pay profit tax at 20%. VAT is 20%. Figures below are from the Tax Code as consolidated to Law No. 127-Z of 30 December 2025, read on 31 October 2026. The detailed rules sit in the domain skills linked under each item.
- **Tax year**: the calendar year, for both personal income tax and profit tax _(Special Part of the Tax Code, arts. 185(1), 207)_
- **Currency**: Belarusian ruble (BYN); the Code states its thresholds in Belarusian rubles _(Special Part of the Tax Code, art. 214(1.2))_
- **Tax authority**: Ministry of Taxes and Duties (MNS, nalog.gov.by). Each payer gets one taxpayer number (UNP) for all taxes; for a company or entrepreneur it is the same number as in the Unified State Register (EGR) _(General Part of the Tax Code, art. 69(1))_
- **Basis of taxation**: an individual is resident if physically present in Belarus for more than 183 days in the calendar year. Residents are taxed on income from Belarus and from abroad; non-residents only on Belarus-source income _(General Part of the Tax Code, art. 17(1); Special Part, art. 196(1))_. Detail: [belarus-cross-border-tax](belarus-cross-border-tax.md).
- **Headline personal income tax rate**: 13%. Specified Belarus-source income (salary, civil-contract fees, rent, interest on loans to the agent and similar) is taxed at 25% on the part above BYN 350,000 up to BYN 600,000 for the year, and 30% on the part above BYN 600,000 _(Special Part, arts. 199(8-2), 214(1.1)-(1.3))_. Detail: [belarus-income-tax](belarus-income-tax.md).
- **Dividends received by individuals**: 13%, and 25% on dividends above BYN 350,000 for the year; 0% for a resident when the Belarus company has not distributed profit to resident participants for the five preceding calendar years in a row _(Special Part, art. 214(1.2), (6))_
- **Individual entrepreneurs**: 20% on business income; 30% for the whole year once business income since 1 January exceeds BYN 500,000 _(Special Part, art. 214(3))_
- **Headline corporate profit tax rate**: 20%. The rate is 25% for a Belarus company (and separately for each branch and each permanent establishment) whose profit tax base, cumulative from the start of the year, exceeds BYN 25,000,000 _(Special Part, art. 184(1))_. Detail: [by-corporate-income-tax](by-corporate-income-tax.md).
- **Dividends received by Belarus companies**: 12%; 0% under the same five-year non-distribution condition _(Special Part, art. 184(6), (8))_. Dividends paid to a foreign company without a permanent establishment: 25%, before any treaty relief _(Special Part, arts. 189(1.4), 192(1.4))_
- **Does VAT exist**: yes. The standard rate is 20%, with 10% for listed goods, 25% for telecommunications services and 0% for exports _(Special Part, art. 122)_. Detail: [belarus-vat](belarus-vat.md).
- **Annual personal income tax declaration deadline**: by 31 March of the following year, for income that must be declared (for example foreign-source income and income from payers who are not tax agents). The MNS sends a payment notice by 30 April and the tax is paid by 1 June _(Special Part, arts. 219(2), 222(1))_
- **Entrepreneur returns**: cumulative returns for the quarter, half-year, nine months and year, filed by the 20th and paid by the 22nd of the month after each period _(Special Part, art. 207; see [belarus-income-tax](belarus-income-tax.md))_
- **Profit tax return filing deadline (quarterly periods)**: the return for each quarter is filed by the 20th of the following month and the tax paid by the 22nd. The annual return is filed by 20 March and the balance paid by 22 March of the following year; a fourth-quarter advance of two thirds of the third-quarter tax is due by 22 December _(Special Part, arts. 185, 186(6), (7), (9))_
- **VAT return filing deadline**: by the 20th of the month after the reporting period (a month, or a quarter where elected); payment by the 22nd _(Special Part, art. 136(1)-(2))_
- **Payroll and social contributions**: employers pay 28% pension and 6% social insurance contributions to the Social Protection Fund (FSZN), and withhold 1% from the employee; these are separate from income tax _(Law No. 118-Z, art. 5)_. Detail: [belarus-social-contributions](belarus-social-contributions.md) and [belarus-payroll](belarus-payroll.md).
