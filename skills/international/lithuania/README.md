# Lithuania: tax pack

Calendar tax year for most taxpayers. Authority: the State Tax Inspectorate (Valstybinė mokesčių inspekcija, VMI); social insurance by Sodra. Euro (EUR). Corporate income tax at 17% from 2026 (7% for small entities), personal income tax, VAT under EU rules, and social insurance. The core laws are the Law on Corporate Income Tax (IX-675), the Law on Personal Income Tax (IX-1007), the Law on VAT (IX-751) and the Law on Tax Administration (IX-2112).
