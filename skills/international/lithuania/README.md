# Lithuania: tax pack

Calendar tax year; a company may use another 12 month period with VMI approval. Authority: the State Tax Inspectorate (Valstybinė mokesčių inspekcija, VMI); social insurance is run by Sodra. Euro. Corporate income tax at 17% from 2026 (16% in 2025), with reduced rates for small companies. Personal income tax is progressive from 2026 (20%, 25% and 32% by bands of the average wage), with 15% on dividends. VAT under EU rules.
