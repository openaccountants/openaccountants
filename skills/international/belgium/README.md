# Belgium: tax pack

Calendar income year, named after the following assessment year (income 2026 is assessment year 2027). Authority: FOD Financiën / SPF Finances; corporate returns are filed in Biztax, individual returns in Tax-on-web. Euro. Federal personal income tax with a municipal surcharge for individuals; corporate tax at 25% with a 20% rate on the first EUR 100,000 for small companies that meet a director pay test; VAT under EU rules; social contributions for employees (ONSS / RSZ) and for the self-employed.
