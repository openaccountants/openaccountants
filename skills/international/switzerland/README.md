# Switzerland: tax pack

Calendar tax year. Three levels of income tax: the Confederation (direkte Bundessteuer, collected by the cantons for the Federal Tax Administration, ESTV), the 26 cantons and the communes; cantonal rates, multipliers and procedure differ, so a figure for one canton does not hold for another. Swiss franc. Federal profit tax of 8.5% plus cantonal and communal profit tax. VAT (MWST) is federal. Foreign employees without a settlement permit are taxed at source by the employer. A 35% withholding tax (Verrechnungssteuer) applies to Swiss dividends and interest. Social contributions (AHV/IV/EO) are federal; Switzerland applies EU social security coordination through the Agreement on the Free Movement of Persons.
