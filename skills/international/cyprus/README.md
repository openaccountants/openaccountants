# Cyprus: tax pack

Calendar tax year. Authority: the Tax Department (Τμήμα Φορολογίας) of the Ministry of Finance. Euro. A broad tax reform applies from 1 January 2026: corporate income tax rises from 12.5% to 15%, the personal tax-free band rises to EUR 22,000, losses carry forward 7 years, and Special Defence Contribution (SDC) on dividends to resident domiciled individuals falls to 5% for profits of 2026 onward, with the deemed dividend distribution kept only for 2024 and 2025 profits. Non-domiciled individuals do not pay SDC. VAT follows the EU directive.
