# Kenya: tax pack

The year of income is the calendar year, or a company's accounting year. The authority is the Kenya Revenue Authority (KRA), and returns and payments go through iTax. The currency is the Kenya shilling (KShs).

Residents are taxed on worldwide income. Individuals pay 10% to 35% with a KShs 2 400 monthly personal relief, and resident companies pay 30%. Employers deduct PAYE, NSSF (6% + 6% up to KShs 108 000 from February 2026), SHIF (2.75%, minimum KShs 300) and the Affordable Housing Levy (1.5% + 1.5%) monthly. Most are due by the 9th day of the following month; AHL is due by the 9th working day. Annual Finance Acts amend the rates, most recently the Finance Act 2026 (Act No. 19 of 2026).
