# Kenya Corporate Income Tax

## Corporation tax rates, base and deadlines

- **General taxation approach** — Resident companies are taxed at a flat 30% on worldwide income attributable to Kenya, while branches of non-resident companies pay a higher rate. Tax is paid in quarterly instalments with a balancing payment after year end.
- **Resident company corporation tax rate** — 30 percent  _(Income Tax Act (Cap 470), Third Schedule — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/kenya/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_
- **Branch of a non-resident company (permanent establishment) rate** — 37.5 percent  _(Income Tax Act (Cap 470), Third Schedule — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/kenya/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_
- **Reduced rate — start-ups certified by the Nairobi International Financial Centre Authority** — 15% for the first 3 years, then 20% for the following 4 years percent  _(Income Tax Act (Cap 470) — https://ronalds.co.ke/a-complete-guide-to-taxation-in-kenya-2025/)_
- **Tax base** — Net taxable business profit (accounting profit adjusted for disallowed expenses, capital allowances and exempt income); income accrued in or derived from Kenya  _(Income Tax Act (Cap 470) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/kenya/corporate/income-determination)_
- **Capital Gains Tax (CGT) on transfer of property/securities** — 15% on the net gain percent  _(Income Tax Act (Cap 470), Eighth Schedule — https://njagaadvocates.com/comprehensive-guide-to-business-taxes-in-kenya-2025/)_
- **Withholding tax on dividends to residents** — 5% (exempt where the resident recipient company controls 12.5%+ of the payer) percent  _(Income Tax Act (Cap 470), Third Schedule — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/kenya/corporate/withholding-taxes)_
- **Withholding tax on dividends to non-residents** — 10% (5% for citizens of East African Community member states on listed shares) percent  _(Income Tax Act (Cap 470), Third Schedule — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/kenya/corporate/withholding-taxes)_
- **Withholding tax on interest** — 15% (general rate, residents and non-residents) percent  _(Income Tax Act (Cap 470), Third Schedule — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/kenya/corporate/withholding-taxes)_
- **Withholding tax on royalties — residents** — 5 percent  _(Income Tax Act (Cap 470), Third Schedule — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/kenya/corporate/withholding-taxes)_
- **Withholding tax on royalties — non-residents** — 20 percent  _(Income Tax Act (Cap 470), Third Schedule — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/kenya/corporate/withholding-taxes)_
- **Withholding tax remittance deadline** — By the 20th day of the month following deduction  _(Income Tax Act (Cap 470); Tax Procedures Act, 2015 — https://www.kra.go.ke/individual/filing-paying/types-of-taxes/individual-withholding-tax)_
- **Instalment (advance) tax** — Paid quarterly — by the 20th of the 4th, 6th, 9th and 12th months of the accounting period  _(Income Tax Act (Cap 470); Tax Procedures Act, 2015 — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/kenya/corporate/tax-administration)_
- **Corporation tax return filing deadline** — Within 6 months after the end of the accounting period  _(Tax Procedures Act, 2015 — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/kenya/corporate/tax-administration)_
- **Balance of tax payment deadline** — Within 4 months after the end of the accounting period (by the last day of the 4th month) ((approx — confirm))  _(Tax Procedures Act, 2015 — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/kenya/corporate/tax-administration)_
