# Somalia Corporate Income Tax

## Corporate / business income tax

- **Corporate / business income tax overview** — Company (business profit) tax is payable to the Federal Government of Somalia on a progressive scale by annual income, with USD-denominated brackets. Top-rate and threshold figures below come from the Somalia Investment Promotion Office; a licensed Somali accountant should confirm the intermediate brackets and withholding rates.  _(Somalia Investment Promotion Office — Tax Regime)_
- **Corporate income tax structure** — Progressive rates from 9% to 30% by annual income band (brackets start around USD 2,400) % (approx — confirm)  _(Somalia Investment Promotion Office — Tax Regime (https://sominvest.gov.so/procedures/tax-regime/))_
- **Top corporate rate** — 30% on annual business income above USD 30,000 %  _(Somalia Investment Promotion Office — Tax Regime (https://sominvest.gov.so/procedures/tax-regime/))_
- **Entry threshold for company tax** — Progressive brackets begin around USD 2,400 of annual income USD (approx — confirm)  _(Somalia Investment Promotion Office — Tax Regime (https://sominvest.gov.so/procedures/tax-regime/))_
- **Alternative cited rate range** — Some guides report an effective company tax of 10%–15% for many businesses % (approx — confirm)  _(TaxAtlas — Somalia tax summary (https://taxatlas.io/country/somalia))_
- **Tax base** — Net business profit (income less allowable expenses) arising in Somalia (approx — confirm)  _(Income Tax Law (Law No. 5 of 1966, as amended) (https://sominvest.gov.so/procedures/tax-regime/))_
- **Withholding tax** — Withholding tax is recognised as a direct-tax category; specific statutory rates on dividends, interest and royalties are not uniformly published (approx — confirm)  _(Somalia Investment Promotion Office — Tax Regime (https://sominvest.gov.so/procedures/tax-regime/))_
- **WHT on dividends** — Rate not uniformly published — confirm with the Inland Revenue Department (approx — confirm)  _(Inland Revenue Department (https://www.revenuedirectorate.gov.so/direct-tax))_
- **WHT on interest** — Rate not uniformly published — confirm with the Inland Revenue Department (approx — confirm)  _(Inland Revenue Department (https://www.revenuedirectorate.gov.so/direct-tax))_
- **WHT on royalties** — Rate not uniformly published — confirm with the Inland Revenue Department (approx — confirm)  _(Inland Revenue Department (https://www.revenuedirectorate.gov.so/direct-tax))_
- **Filing / payment deadlines** — Annual corporate filing deadlines not yet uniformly codified; sales tax and payroll withholding remitted monthly (approx — confirm)  _(Inland Revenue Department (https://taxatlas.io/country/somalia))_
