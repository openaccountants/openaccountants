# Eswatini Payroll & Social Contributions

## Payroll withholding and social security (2025)

Employers operate PAYE on employee remuneration and contribute, alongside employees, to the Eswatini National Provident Fund (ENPF). 2025 ENPF figures reflect the multi-year ceiling adjustment under Legal Notice No. 5 of 2025.

- **PAYE (employee tax)** — Employers must withhold income tax from remuneration using the prescribed Employees' Tax Deduction Tables  _([Income Tax Order, 1975](https://www.ers.org.sz/IncomeTax/Paye))_
- **PAYE remittance deadline** — Within 7 days after the end of the month of payment  _([Income Tax Order, 1975](https://www.ers.org.sz/IncomeTax/Paye))_
- **ENPF employee contribution rate** — 5% of pensionable pay (up to the monthly ceiling) percent  _([National Provident Fund Order, 1974 / ENPF Legal Notice No. 5 of 2025](https://enpf.co.sz/employers/))_
- **ENPF employer contribution rate** — 5% of pensionable pay (up to the monthly ceiling) percent  _([National Provident Fund Order, 1974 / ENPF Legal Notice No. 5 of 2025](https://enpf.co.sz/employers/))_
- **ENPF monthly wage ceiling (2025)** — SZL 4,000 per month (increased from SZL 3,700) SZL/month  _([ENPF Legal Notice No. 5 of 2025](https://www.payspace.com/blog/eswatini-enpf-rate-increase-january-2025/))_
- **ENPF maximum monthly contribution per party (2025)** — SZL 200 each from employee and employer (SZL 400 combined) SZL/month  _([ENPF Legal Notice No. 5 of 2025](https://www.payspace.com/blog/eswatini-enpf-rate-increase-january-2025/))_
- **ENPF ceiling adjustment plan** — Ceiling is being raised in stages over 2025–2029 (e.g. SZL 4,300/month and SZL 215 each from January 2026)  _([ENPF Legal Notice No. 5 of 2025](https://www.payspace.com/blog/eswatini-enpf-rate-increase))_
