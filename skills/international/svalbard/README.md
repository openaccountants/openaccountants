# Svalbard: 2026 tax pack

Svalbard has a separate Norwegian tax system, administered by Skatteetaten, using NOK and the calendar year. Gross salary withholding and separately assessed ordinary income are distinct. All six Guides are **source-cited drafts**, not accountant-reviewed. Ordinary scope has been expanded, but the three explicit coverage gaps above keep issue #424 open.

**Territory:** OWNERS.md and issue #424 identify this catalogue entry as Svalbard. The SJ metadata identifier does not establish Jan Mayen coverage. Svalbard Tax Act §1-1 sets this pack's boundary; Jan Mayen needs separate research and is not represented as complete by this work.

Ordinary scope includes residents/nonresidents, employment/pensions, sole traders, capital income and wealth, company profit and allocation, depreciation/losses, membership and contribution classes, employer withholding, standard benefits, related-party pricing/documentation, residence/departure and foreign distributions. Guide presence and passing arithmetic are not proof of full coverage.

Niche exclusions: simplified settlements for foreign research/project groups and Barentsburg; offshore/extractive and shipping regimes; financial institutions; large-multinational minimum tax and Svalbard-specific CbC perimeter implementation; reorganisations and complex hybrid arrangements; specialist pensions/benefits; short-term accommodation; and treaty-rate tables. These do not erase the ordinary gaps declared above. Professional sign-off is a separate trust limit, not a `partial` reason.
