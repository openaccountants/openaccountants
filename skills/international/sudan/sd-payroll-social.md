# Sudan Payroll & Social Contributions

## Social insurance and payroll withholding

Sudan's contributory social insurance is administered by the National Social Insurance Fund (NSIF). Employers and employees both contribute, and employers also withhold employment income tax (PAYE) at source. Rates below are drafts for accountant verification.

- **Total NSIF contribution** — 25% of gross salary percent  _([National Social Insurance Fund (NSIF) regulations — https://www.2interact.us/understanding-sudans-old-age-pension-system/](https://www.2interact.us/understanding-sudans-old-age-pension-system/))_
- **Employer social insurance contribution** — 17% of gross salary percent (approx — confirm)  _([National Social Insurance Fund (NSIF) regulations — https://www.2interact.us/understanding-sudans-old-age-pension-system/](https://www.2interact.us/understanding-sudans-old-age-pension-system/))_
- **Employee social insurance contribution** — 8% of gross salary percent (approx — confirm)  _([National Social Insurance Fund (NSIF) regulations — https://www.2interact.us/understanding-sudans-old-age-pension-system/](https://www.2interact.us/understanding-sudans-old-age-pension-system/))_
- **Minimum contribution wage base** — SDG 12,000 per month (minimum); no stated maximum cap SDG (approx — confirm)  _([National Social Insurance Fund (NSIF) regulations — https://www.2interact.us/understanding-sudans-old-age-pension-system/](https://www.2interact.us/understanding-sudans-old-age-pension-system/))_
- **Self-employed contribution** — 25% on a wage base of SDG 15,000-20,000 percent (approx — confirm)  _([National Social Insurance Fund (NSIF) regulations — https://www.2interact.us/understanding-sudans-old-age-pension-system/](https://www.2interact.us/understanding-sudans-old-age-pension-system/))_
- **Sudanese citizens working abroad** — 23% contribution (voluntary scheme) percent (approx — confirm)  _([National Social Insurance Fund (NSIF) regulations — https://www.2interact.us/understanding-sudans-old-age-pension-system/](https://www.2interact.us/understanding-sudans-old-age-pension-system/))_
- **Employment income tax (PAYE)** — Employer withholds income tax at progressive rates from salaries  _([Income Tax Act 1986 — https://tax.gov.sd/en/income-tax-2/](https://tax.gov.sd/en/income-tax-2/))_
- **PAYE remittance deadline** — On or before the 15th of the month following deduction  _([Income Tax Act 1986 — https://tax.gov.sd/en/income-tax-2/](https://tax.gov.sd/en/income-tax-2/))_
- **NSIF contribution remittance** — Remitted monthly to the National Social Insurance Fund (approx — confirm date)  _(National Social Insurance Fund (NSIF) regulations)_
- **Old-age pension benefit basis** — 30% of average monthly earnings after 180 months (15 years) of contributions, plus increments (approx — confirm)  _(National Social Insurance Fund (NSIF) regulations)_
