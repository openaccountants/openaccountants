# Sudan Corporate Income Tax

## Business profits (corporate) tax rates

- **Standard corporate (business profits) rate** — 15% percent (industrial / general companies)  _([Business Profits Tax (Income Tax Act 1986)](https://orbitax.com/taxhub/corporatetaxrates/SD/Sudan))_
- **Agricultural companies rate** — 0% percent ((approx — confirm))  _([Business Profits Tax (Income Tax Act 1986)](https://orbitax.com/taxhub/corporatetaxrates/SD/Sudan))_
- **Banks and tobacco/cigarette companies rate** — 30% percent ((approx — confirm))  _([Business Profits Tax (Income Tax Act 1986)](https://orbitax.com/taxhub/corporatetaxrates/SD/Sudan))_
- **Oil / petroleum companies rate** — 35% percent ((approx — confirm))  _([Business Profits Tax (Income Tax Act 1986)](https://tradingeconomics.com/sudan/corporate-tax-rate))_
- **Tax base** — Net profit (accounting profit adjusted for tax) of the enterprise  _(Income Tax Act 1986)_
- **Withholding tax on dividends (non-resident)** — No dividend withholding tax percent ((approx — confirm))  _(Income Tax Act 1986)_
- **Withholding tax on interest (non-resident)** — 7% (final) percent ((approx — confirm))  _(Income Tax Act 1986)_
- **Withholding tax on royalties (non-resident)** — 15% (final) percent ((approx — confirm))  _(Income Tax Act 1986)_
- **Withholding tax on technical / management services (non-resident)** — 15% (final) percent ((approx — confirm))  _(Income Tax Act 1986)_
- **Withholding tax on imports of goods (resident payer)** — 2% (creditable) percent ((approx — confirm))  _(Income Tax Act 1986)_
- **Corporate return filing / payment** — Self-assessment; tax payable on submission of the return after year-end (approx — confirm exact date)  _([Income Tax Act 1986](https://tax.gov.sd/en/income-tax-2/))_

Sudan applies business profits tax at sector-differentiated rates: most companies pay a standard rate, while banks, tobacco and oil companies pay higher rates and agriculture is favoured. Figures below are drafts to be confirmed against the current Income Tax Act schedule.
