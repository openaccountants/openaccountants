# Sudan VAT / GST

## Value Added Tax (VAT)

Sudan operates a Value Added Tax under the Value Added Tax Act 1999, administered by the Sudan Taxation Chamber. The standard rate is 17%, with reduced/exempt treatment for certain essential goods and services. Thresholds and exemptions should be confirmed against the current law.

- **Standard VAT rate** — 17 percent  _([Value Added Tax Act 1999](https://sudantribune.com/article/309388))_
- **Reduced VAT rate (essential items)** — 5 percent (e.g. certain food, healthcare, educational materials; (approx — confirm))  _([Value Added Tax Act 1999](https://sd.icalculator.com/vat-calculator.html))_
- **Zero-rated / exempt supplies** — Exports and certain basic goods/services are zero-rated or exempt (confirm list) (approx — confirm)  _(Value Added Tax Act 1999)_
- **VAT registration threshold** — 1,200,000 SDG (annual turnover (confirm — figure predates recent currency changes) (approx — confirm))  _(Value Added Tax Act 1999)_
