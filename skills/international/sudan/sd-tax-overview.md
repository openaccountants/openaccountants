# Sudan Tax Overview

## Sudan tax system at a glance

Sudan's direct taxes are administered by the Sudan Taxation Chamber under the Income Tax Act 1986 (and its 2021 Regulations). The system is self-assessment based, with business profits, employment income and rental income all taxable. These are draft figures for an accountant to verify.

- **National tax authority** — Sudan Taxation Chamber (Diwan Al-Daraib)  _([Income Tax Act 1986](https://tax.gov.sd/en/income-tax-2/))_
- **Currency** — Sudanese pound (SDG)  _(Central Bank of Sudan)_
- **Standard tax year** — Calendar year (1 January to 31 December); accounting period may apply (approx — confirm)  _(Income Tax Act 1986)_
- **Residence / taxation basis** — Residents taxed on income arising in Sudan; broadly territorial / source-based (approx — confirm)  _(Income Tax Act 1986)_
- **Top personal (employment) income tax rate** — 15 percent  _([Income Tax Act 1986](https://tradingeconomics.com/sudan/personal-income-tax-rate))_
- **Standard business profits (corporate) tax rate** — 15% for most companies; higher sector rates apply (banks/tobacco 30%, oil 35%) percent  _(Business Profits Tax (Income Tax Act 1986))_
- **Does Sudan have VAT?** — Yes — Value Added Tax at a standard rate of 17% percent  _([Value Added Tax Act 1999](https://sudantribune.com/article/309388))_
- **Capital gains** — Capital gains tax applies (separate schedule); rate confirm with statute (approx — confirm)  _(Income Tax Act 1986)_
- **Employer PAYE remittance deadline** — On or before the 15th of the month following the deduction  _([Income Tax Act 1986](https://tax.gov.sd/en/income-tax-2/))_
- **Filing model** — Self-assessment; tax due at the time of submitting the return  _([Income Tax Act 1986](https://tax.gov.sd/en/income-tax-2/))_
