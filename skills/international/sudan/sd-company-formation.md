# Sudan Company Formation & Entity Choice

## Entity types, capital and incorporation

- **Governing companies law** — Companies Act 2015  _(Companies Act 2015 (https://chandrawatpartners.com/how-to-incorporate-an-llc-in-sudan-insights-compliances-much-more/))_
- **Registration authority** — Commercial (Trade) Registrar, Ministry of Justice  _(Companies Act 2015)_
- **Common entity types** — Limited liability company (LLC), public/private joint-stock company, branch of a foreign company, partnership, sole proprietorship  _(Companies Act 2015)_
- **Minimum shareholders (LLC)** — 1 shareholder and 1 director (any nationality) ((approx — confirm))  _(Companies Act 2015 (https://chandrawatpartners.com/how-to-incorporate-an-llc-in-sudan-insights-compliances-much-more/))_
- **Minimum share capital (LLC)** — No fixed statutory minimum for an LLC; capital varies by activity SDG ((approx — confirm))  _(Companies Act 2015)_
- **Incorporation steps** — Reserve company name, prepare memorandum & articles of association, submit documents, register with the Commercial Registrar, obtain tax registration and VAT registration ((approx — confirm))  _(Companies Act 2015)_
- **Typical incorporation timeline** — Several weeks (varies with approvals) ((approx — confirm))  _(Companies Act 2015)_
- **Tax registration** — Register with the Sudan Taxation Chamber for income tax (and VAT if above threshold)  _(Income Tax Act 1986 (https://tax.gov.sd/en/income-tax-2/))_
- **Annual financial statements / audit** — Companies must keep accounts and (for most companies) file audited financial statements ((approx — confirm))  _(Companies Act 2015)_
- **Annual return / renewal** — Annual commercial-registry renewal and annual tax return filing required ((approx — confirm))  _(Companies Act 2015)_
- **Social insurance registration** — Employers must register with the National Social Insurance Fund (NSIF)  _(National Social Insurance Fund (NSIF) regulations)_

Companies in Sudan are governed by the Companies Act 2015 and registered with the Commercial (Trade) Registrar under the Ministry of Justice. The limited liability company is the most common vehicle. Capital amounts, timelines and costs vary in practice and should be confirmed locally.
