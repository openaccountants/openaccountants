# Turks and Caicos national-insurance contributions

## Scope

Checked 22 October 2026 for ordinary private-sector employees with established coverage and insurable earnings. The [Board's institutional page](https://www.tcinib.tc/about-us/) identifies a 12% private-sector total effective from 1 April 2024. Public officers and self-employed persons have separate categories; this Guide does not apply the private-sector formula to them.

## Monthly computation

The [current contribution page](https://www.tcinib.tc/contribution-rates/) gives an employee share of 5.5%, employer share of 6.5% and monthly insurable earnings ceiling of $4,000. For established monthly insurable earnings E, use min(E, 4,000) as the contribution base.

| Earnings before ceiling | Base | Employee 5.5% | Employer 6.5% | Total |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| $2,000 | $2,000 | $110 | $130 | $240 |
| $4,000 | $4,000 | $220 | $260 | $480 |
| $6,000 | $4,000 | $220 | $260 | $480 |

If established monthly insurable earnings increase from $3,000 to $3,500, the employee deduction rises from $165 to $192.50, and the employer contribution rises from $195 to $227.50. These are contribution calculations, not net-pay calculations. A wage item being taxable or payable does not, by itself, establish its national-insurance treatment.

Keep national-insurance and health-insurance calculations separate. Their ceilings differ; do not apply one combined percentage to a single capped wage figure.

## Registration and remittance

The [Board FAQ](https://www.tcinib.tc/faq/) requires employer registration and employee registration, and places responsibility for remitting both shares on the employer. It also describes the online payment portal. The [contribution page](https://www.tcinib.tc/contribution-rates/) describes monthly payment with a one-month or 30-day grace period. Its wording does not resolve short-month boundaries precisely; use a month-end payroll close and obtain the applicable statutory deadline before relying on a grace-period extension.

Suggested closing controls: match worker identifiers to the contribution schedule, reconcile the wage base to payroll, separate the two shares in the ledger, and retain the filed schedule and payment receipt. Investigate differences rather than using the receipt total to overwrite the payroll calculation.

## Source conflicts and limits

The contribution page states 11% for self-employed persons, while the institutional page describes 10% from April 2024. The current effective date and legal instrument for that category have not been reconciled. No self-employed calculation is supplied here.

This is an administrative-source draft. The National Insurance Ordinance and subsequent contribution amendments, the exact remittance deadline, weekly limits, earnings inclusions, age boundaries and multiple-employer treatment still need legislative verification. No benefit entitlement is inferred from the examples. Independent accountant review remains pending.
