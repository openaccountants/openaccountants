# Türkiye: tax pack

Türkiye taxes individuals under the Income Tax Law no. 193 (GVK) and companies under the Corporate Tax Law no. 5520 (KVK). VAT is charged under Law 3065 (KDV). The Revenue Administration (Gelir İdaresi Başkanlığı, GİB) collects the taxes; the Social Security Institution (SGK) collects social security premiums under Law 5510. The tax year is the calendar year and the currency is the Turkish lira (TRY). Fixed amounts in the GVK and VUK are raised each year by communiqué.
