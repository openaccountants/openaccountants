# Turkey Company Formation & Entity Choice

Tax year 2026. Every figure below is taken from the consolidated laws on mevzuat.gov.tr or from the Official Gazette (resmigazete.gov.tr), read on 2026-10-31. Corporate tax, VAT and payroll after formation are in [tr-corporate-income-tax](tr-corporate-income-tax.md), [turkey-vat](turkey-vat.md) and [tr-payroll-social](tr-payroll-social.md); the headline figures and filing dates are in [tr-tax-overview](tr-tax-overview.md).

## Company formation in Turkey (2026)

- **Overview of company formation vehicles.** The two vehicles used most are the limited company (limited şirket, Ltd. Şti.) and the joint stock company (anonim şirket, A.Ş.), both under the Turkish Commercial Code No. 6102 (TTK). The minimum capital of both was raised by Presidential Decision No. 7887 (Official Gazette 25 November 2023). Applications go through MERSIS, the Ministry of Trade's central registry system, and the trade registry office where the company has its seat. _(TTK arts. 329, 573; TTK notes 42 and 83; MERSIS)_
- **Limited company (limited şirket).** Formed by one or more natural or legal persons under a trade name, with a fixed capital made up of capital shares (TTK art. 573(1)). It may have no more than 50 partners (art. 574(1)). Partners are not liable for the company's debts; they must pay the capital shares they took up and any additional payment and side duties set in the company contract (art. 573(2)). It can be formed for any lawful business purpose (art. 573(3)). _(TTK arts. 573, 574)_
- **Joint stock company (anonim şirket).** A company with a fixed capital divided into shares, liable for its debts only with its own assets (TTK art. 329(1)). Shareholders are liable only to the company and only for the capital they subscribed (art. 329(2)). One founder who holds shares is enough (art. 338(1)). It can be formed for any lawful economic purpose (art. 331). Some fields of activity named by Ministry communiqué need the Ministry's permission to incorporate (art. 333(1)). The Code treats public (halka açık) and non-public joint stock companies separately, for example for registered capital (art. 332(1)). _(TTK arts. 329, 331, 332, 333, 338)_
- **Minimum capital, limited company.** TRY 50,000. The law sets TRY 10,000 and lets the President raise it up to ten times (TTK art. 580(1), (2)); Decision No. 7887 raised it to TRY 50,000 (art. 580, note 83). _(TTK art. 580 and note 83)_
- **Minimum capital, joint stock company.** TRY 250,000. The law sets TRY 50,000 and lets the President raise it (TTK art. 332(1)); Decision No. 7887 raised it to TRY 250,000 (art. 332, note 42). _(TTK art. 332 and note 42)_
- **Minimum capital, non-public joint stock company in the registered-capital system.** TRY 500,000 initial capital (başlangıç sermayesi). The law sets TRY 100,000; Decision No. 7887 raised it to TRY 500,000 (TTK art. 332(1) and note 42). Initial capital is the capital the company must hold at formation or when it first enters the system (art. 332(2)). _(TTK art. 332 and note 42)_
- **Capital payment timing, joint stock company.** At least 25% of the nominal value of shares subscribed in cash is paid before registration, and the rest within 24 months after registration. Any share premium is paid in full before registration (TTK art. 344(1)). Cash is paid into a special account opened at a bank in the name of the company being formed, usable only by the company, and the bank releases it once the registry confirms the company has legal personality (art. 345(1)). _(TTK arts. 344, 345)_
- **Capital payment timing, limited company.** The rule that 25% of cash shares is paid before registration does not apply to limited companies (TTK art. 585(1)). The other joint stock company rules on paying for capital shares, where to pay and the effects of not paying apply by analogy (art. 585(1)), so the cash capital is paid within 24 months after registration (art. 344(1)). _(TTK arts. 344, 585)_
- **Incorporation process.** A joint stock company is formed when the founders declare their will to form it in articles of association in which they unconditionally undertake to pay the whole capital, with their signatures certified by a notary or signed before the trade registry director or deputy (TTK art. 335(1)). A limited company contract must be in writing and signed by the founders before authorised trade registry staff (arts. 575(1), 585(1)). No valuable-paper charge is taken on the papers containing the articles (arts. 335(1), 575(1)). The articles are registered at the trade registry of the company's seat and announced in the Turkish Trade Registry Gazette within 30 days of formation (art. 354(1)). Foreign investors may invest freely and are treated the same as local investors, unless a treaty or special law says otherwise (Foreign Direct Investment Law No. 4875, art. 3(a)), so foreign persons can own 100% of a limited or joint stock company. _(TTK arts. 335, 354, 575, 585; Law No. 4875 art. 3)_
- **Tax registration.** The trade registry sends a copy of a corporate taxpayer's registration file to the tax office, and the company's duty to notify the start of business is then treated as met (Tax Procedure Law No. 213, art. 153). _(VUK art. 153)_
- **Trade registry fees (2026).** Under tariff (1), part C of the Fees Law No. 492, registering a capital company's business and trade name costs TRY 35,354.50, and registering each person authorised to represent it costs TRY 8,598.80 (amounts for 2026 under Fees Law General Communiqué No. 98). The chambers of commerce (or commerce and industry) collect these fees in advance against a receipt (Law No. 492, art. 132). _(Law No. 492 tariff (1) C/I; Communiqué No. 98)_
- **Competition Authority share.** 0.04% (four in ten thousand) of the capital of every newly formed joint stock and limited company, and of the increase in any later capital increase (Law No. 4054 on the Protection of Competition, art. 39(1)(c)). _(Law No. 4054 art. 39)_
- **Branch of a foreign company.** A Turkish branch of a business whose seat is abroad is registered like a domestic business, subject to the trade-name rules of its home country, and must appoint a fully authorised commercial representative resident in Turkey (TTK art. 40(4)). Every merchant registers its business and trade name at the trade registry within 15 days of opening (art. 40(1)). _(TTK art. 40)_
- **Liaison office.** The Ministry may permit a company formed under foreign law to open a liaison office in Turkey, on condition that it carries on no commercial activity in Turkey (Law No. 4875, art. 3(h)). _(Law No. 4875 art. 3)_
- **Core annual compliance.** Every merchant keeps commercial books that show its business and financial position clearly (TTK art. 64(1)). The ordinary general meeting is held within three months after the end of each financial year: art. 409(1) for a joint stock company, art. 617(1) for a limited company. Tax filing dates (corporate tax return by the end of the fourth month after year-end, quarterly provisional tax by the 17th of the second month after each quarter, VAT by the 28th and the withholding return by the 26th of the following month) are in [tr-tax-overview](tr-tax-overview.md). _(TTK arts. 64, 409, 617; GİB filing calendar)_
- **Capital top-up deadline for existing companies.** Joint stock and limited companies whose capital is below the new minimums had to raise it to the amounts in TTK arts. 332 and 580 by 31 December 2026, or be treated as dissolved (infisah). A non-public registered-capital joint stock company with issued capital of at least TRY 250,000 that does not raise its initial and issued capital to TRY 500,000 leaves the registered-capital system (TTK provisional art. 15(1)). The Ministry of Trade may extend the deadline by one year at a time, at most twice (provisional art. 15(3)). It used that power on 17 October 2026: the deadline is now **31 December 2027** (Ministry of Trade communiqué, Official Gazette 17 October 2026, No. 33403, art. 2). General meetings held to make the increase need no quorum and decide by a majority of the votes present (provisional art. 15(2)). _(TTK provisional art. 15; Official Gazette No. 33403)_

## Worked example

A foreign parent forms a joint stock company in 2026 with the minimum capital of TRY 250,000, all in cash, and one person authorised to represent it. Before registration it pays at least 25% of the capital into the company's blocked bank account: TRY 62,500. The Competition Authority share is 0.04% of TRY 250,000: TRY 100. The trade registry fees are TRY 35,354.50 for registering the business and trade name plus TRY 8,598.80 for the one representative: TRY 43,953.30. Notary, Trade Registry Gazette announcement and chamber registration charges come on top and are not in this example.
