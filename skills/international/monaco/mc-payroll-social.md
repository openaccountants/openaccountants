# Monaco payroll and social contributions

> **Source-cited draft; partial scope.** Checked 24 October 2026. No named Monaco professional has reviewed this Guide. It produces a working paper, not a filing or a final tax position. Read the [country gap register](README.md) before use.

## Who this applies to

This combined payroll and social-contribution Guide covers ordinary professional-employer monthly remuneration **from 1 October 2026**. Do not apply these October CCSS, CAR or CMRC parameters to January–September. Monaco's government describes absence of resident personal income tax within its scope; French treaty and other foreign tax obligations still require a separate decision. This Guide computes social contributions, not a universal zero-withholding conclusion for cross-border staff. [Caisses Sociales, dated contribution tables](https://www.caisses-sociales.mc/accueil/employeur/employeur/employeur-a-titre-professionnel/cotisations); [Government personal-tax scope](https://monservicepublic.gouv.mc/en/themes/tax/information/general-information/tax-in-monaco).

First determine employer affiliation, employee status, remuneration components and social-security coverage. Directors, secondments, domestic employees, construction paid-leave, exempt wages and derogatory CMRC rates need separate rules. The figures below are a selected ordinary schedule, not all employment costs.

## Rates, bands and thresholds

| Component | Employer | Employee | Monthly ceiling/band | Effective date and official source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| CCSS including OMT | 13.40%; 13.45% with CGCS | No employee entry in this schedule | EUR 10,000 | 1 October 2026, [CCSS table](https://www.caisses-sociales.mc/accueil/employeur/employeur/employeur-a-titre-professionnel/cotisations) |
| CAR total rates | 8.39% | 6.85% | EUR 6,276 | 1 October 2026, [CAR table](https://www.caisses-sociales.mc/accueil/employeur/employeur/employeur-a-titre-professionnel/cotisations) |
| Unemployment | 4.00% | 2.40% | EUR 16,020 | 1 January 2026, [Unemployment table](https://www.caisses-sociales.mc/accueil/employeur/employeur/employeur-a-titre-professionnel/cotisations) |
| CMRC tranche A total | 60% of total contribution | 40% of total contribution | Up to EUR 4,067 at 10.02% total | 1 October 2026, [CMRC table](https://www.caisses-sociales.mc/accueil/employeur/employeur/employeur-a-titre-professionnel/cotisations) |
| CMRC tranche B total | 60% of total contribution | 40% of total contribution | Above EUR 4,067 up to EUR 32,536 at 24.29% total | 1 October 2026, [CMRC table](https://www.caisses-sociales.mc/accueil/employeur/employeur/employeur-a-titre-professionnel/cotisations) |
| CAMTI self-employed | EUR 1,128 per quarter | Separate from employee calculation | Exercise 2026–27 only | [Independent contributions](https://www.caisses-sociales.mc/accueil/travailleur-independant/travailleur-independant/cotisations) |

CMRC total A is 7.87% rights-generating plus 2.15% non-rights contribution; B is 21.59% plus 2.70%. The upper limit is eight times A, not eight additional A bands. CCSS totals already include OMT and, for CGCS employers, CGCS: do not add those components twice. [CCSS/CMRC composition](https://www.caisses-sociales.mc/accueil/employeur/employeur/employeur-a-titre-professionnel/cotisations).

## The base

The official schedule starts with gross total remuneration defined by the CCSS internal rules. Confirm includable benefits, any permitted abatements and each scheme's coverage. For a full ordinary covered month, apply each own ceiling. CMRC A base is min(covered pay, 4,067); B is max(0, min(covered pay, 32,536) minus 4,067). Split the resulting CMRC total 60/40. Employee/director unemployment eligibility is not decided by job title alone. [Remuneration, directors and specific scheme links](https://www.caisses-sociales.mc/accueil/employeur/employeur/employeur-a-titre-professionnel/cotisations).

The examples assume identical covered cash pay for all four components, full ordinary coverage, CGCS status, no prorating, derogation, benefits, abatement or additional employment costs. Self-employed CAMTI and CARTI are different schemes. CARTI is class-based; no combined self-employed total or earlier-exercise amount is established here. [CAMTI and CARTI schedules](https://www.caisses-sociales.mc/accueil/travailleur-independant/travailleur-independant/cotisations).

## Deadlines and filing

Professional employers declare monthly remuneration and pay contributions by the **10th of the following month**, using the Caisses Sociales employer services and the authorised employer/accountant access route. The employer letter expressly says the tenth includes weekends and public holidays. Do not import VAT's holiday rule into social contributions. Verify affiliation and employee registration before the declaration; no live onboarding or payroll submission has been tested. [Modalités déclaratives](https://www.caisses-sociales.mc/accueil/employeur/employeur/employeur-a-titre-professionnel/modalites-declaratives); [Employer letter, October 2026](https://www.caisses-sociales.mc/content/download/3439/file/Lettre_information_aux_employeurs_octobre_2025.pdf).

The PDF URL contains “octobre_2025”, but the retrieved two-page document is headed October 2026 and agrees with the dated current tables. The [research report](../../../docs/research/2026-10-24-monaco-research.md) records the mismatch and hashes. Its URL is not evidence for 2025 rates.

## Penalties and interest

The official contribution page states late-payment interest of 1% for each month of delay and a 10% increase in contributions. It also warns of additional penalties from ex officio assessment if declarations are not submitted. This draft has not established the rules for partial months, remission, special schemes or all registration/declaration penalties. Do not reuse ISB or VAT sanction rates. [Payment and late penalties](https://www.caisses-sociales.mc/accueil/employeur/employeur/employeur-a-titre-professionnel/cotisations).

## Cross-border points

Residence, workplace and applicable social coverage must be determined separately. A foreign residence card or no Monaco personal income tax does not establish exemption from contributions. Bilateral social agreements, certificates, remote work, secondments and French income-tax treatment have not been fully researched in this pack. Obtain the applicable-coverage decision before using these contribution formulas. [Employer declaration rules](https://www.caisses-sociales.mc/accueil/employeur/employeur/employeur-a-titre-professionnel/modalites-declaratives); [Government limits on personal-tax statement](https://monservicepublic.gouv.mc/en/themes/tax/information/general-information/tax-in-monaco).

## Two worked examples that recompute

### Ordinary EUR 3,000 month

For the stated ordinary CGCS case, CCSS/OMT/CGCS is EUR 403.50; CAR employer EUR 251.70, employee EUR 205.50; unemployment employer EUR 120, employee EUR 72; total CMRC EUR 300.60 split EUR 180.36/EUR 120.24. Selected employer contributions total EUR 955.56 and selected employee deductions EUR 397.74. Gross minus these selected deductions is EUR 2,602.26. This is not a complete payslip or guaranteed net salary.

```oa-check
name: October 2026 selected ordinary contributions at EUR 3000
inputs:
  pay: 3000
steps:
  - ccss: min(pay, 10000) * 0.1345
  - car_er: min(pay, 6276) * 0.0839
  - car_ee: min(pay, 6276) * 0.0685
  - unemp_er: min(pay, 16020) * 0.04
  - unemp_ee: min(pay, 16020) * 0.024
  - ta: min(pay, 4067)
  - tb: max(0, min(pay, 32536) - 4067)
  - cmrc: ta * 0.1002 + tb * 0.2429
  - selected_er: round(ccss + car_er + unemp_er + cmrc * 0.6, 2)
  - selected_ee: round(car_ee + unemp_ee + cmrc * 0.4, 2)
  - after_selected: pay - selected_ee
expected:
  selected_er: 955.56
  selected_ee: 397.74
  after_selected: 2602.26
```

### Ceiling and CMRC B edge

At EUR 12,000 covered monthly pay, CCSS is capped at EUR 10,000 and CAR at EUR 6,276. Unemployment remains below its own ceiling. CMRC A is EUR 4,067 and B is EUR 7,933, producing EUR 2,334.4391 before split and rounding. Selected employer contributions are EUR 3,752.22 and employee deductions EUR 1,651.68. Gross less only those deductions is EUR 10,348.32. Component rounding on the actual return must follow Caisses rules; this check rounds only the selected totals.

```oa-check
name: October 2026 selected ordinary contributions at EUR 12000
inputs:
  pay: 12000
steps:
  - ccss: min(pay, 10000) * 0.1345
  - car_er: min(pay, 6276) * 0.0839
  - car_ee: min(pay, 6276) * 0.0685
  - unemp_er: min(pay, 16020) * 0.04
  - unemp_ee: min(pay, 16020) * 0.024
  - ta: min(pay, 4067)
  - tb: max(0, min(pay, 32536) - 4067)
  - cmrc: ta * 0.1002 + tb * 0.2429
  - selected_er: round(ccss + car_er + unemp_er + cmrc * 0.6, 2)
  - selected_ee: round(car_ee + unemp_ee + cmrc * 0.4, 2)
  - after_selected: pay - selected_ee
expected:
  selected_er: 3752.22
  selected_ee: 1651.68
  after_selected: 10348.32
```

## Ask the client first

- Which pay months, employer scheme and CGCS/CMRC status apply?
- What are employee/director status, workplace, residence and social-coverage decisions?
- Which pay, benefits, absences, caps and abatements are evidenced?
- Are there derogatory pension rates, construction costs, foreign duties or secondments?
- What affiliation, registration, prior declarations and payment records exist?
