# Gambia Payroll & Social Contributions

## Social security contributions and payroll withholding

Payroll obligations in The Gambia comprise PAYE withholding (remitted to the GRA) and social security contributions administered by the Social Security and Housing Finance Corporation (SSHFC), which runs the National Provident Fund, Federated Pension Scheme and Industrial Injuries Compensation Fund. These figures are a draft to confirm with a licensed Gambian accountant.

- **National Provident Fund (NPF) total contribution** — 15% of basic salary percent  _([National Provident Fund (SSHFC) regulations](https://www.sshfc.gm/national-provident-fund))_
- **NPF employer contribution** — 10% of basic salary percent  _([National Provident Fund (SSHFC) regulations](https://www.sshfc.gm/national-provident-fund))_
- **NPF employee contribution** — 5% of basic salary percent  _([National Provident Fund (SSHFC) regulations](https://www.sshfc.gm/national-provident-fund))_
- **Federated Pension Scheme (FPS) contribution** — 15% of gross salary, paid entirely by the employer percent (approx — applies to covered employers; confirm applicability)  _([Federated Pension Scheme (SSHFC) regulations](https://www.sshfc.gm/federated-pension-scheme))_
- **Industrial Injuries Compensation Fund (IICF) contribution** — 1% of gross salary, capped at D15.00 for salaries of D1,500.00 and above, paid by the employer percent  _([Industrial Injuries Compensation Fund (SSHFC) regulations](https://www.sshfc.gm/))_
- **Contribution wage base** — NPF on basic salary; FPS on gross salary (basic plus fixed allowances) (approx — confirm)  _(National Provident Fund (SSHFC) regulations)_
- **PAYE payroll withholding** — Employers withhold income tax on employment income under the progressive PAYE bands; threshold D3,000/month (D36,000/year)  _([Income and Value Added Tax Act](https://www.gra.gm/calculators/pay-as-you-earn-paye-))_
- **PAYE remittance deadline** — Within 15 days after the end of the month  _([Income and Value Added Tax Act](https://www.gra.gm/domestic-faqs))_
- **Expatriate payroll tax (ECOWAS nationals)** — D10,000 per expatriate GMD  _([Income and Value Added Tax Act](https://www.gra.gm/domestic-faqs))_
- **Expatriate payroll tax (non-ECOWAS nationals)** — D50,000 per expatriate GMD  _([Income and Value Added Tax Act](https://www.gra.gm/domestic-faqs))_
- **SSHFC contribution remittance** — Monthly to the SSHFC (approx — confirm exact due date)  _(Social Security and Housing Finance Corporation Act)_
