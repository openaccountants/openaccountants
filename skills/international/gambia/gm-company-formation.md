# Gambia Company Formation & Entity Choice

## Entity types, incorporation and annual compliance

- **Legal framework and incorporation** — Companies in The Gambia are incorporated under the Companies Act, 2013, with registration handled through the Single Window Business Registry / Registrar of Companies. The private company limited by shares is the most common vehicle. These figures are a draft to confirm with a licensed Gambian professional.  _(Companies Act, 2013)_
- **Common entity types** — Private company limited by shares, public limited company, company limited by guarantee, sole proprietorship, partnership, and registered branch of a foreign company  _(Companies Act, 2013 (https://www.moj.gm/company-registration))_
- **Minimum number of incorporators** — Two or more persons may form and incorporate a company  _(Companies Act, 2013 (https://www.amiebensoudaco.net/corporate-services/registration-of-private-company/))_
- **Minimum share capital** — No statutory minimum; subscribers must take at least 25% of the authorized share capital (typical authorized capital D500,000) GMD (approx — D500,000 is typical practice, not a statutory minimum; confirm)  _(Companies Act, 2013 (https://www.amiebensoudaco.net/corporate-services/registration-of-private-company/))_
- **Constitutional documents** — Memorandum of Association and Articles of Association stating the authorized share capital  _(Companies Act, 2013 (https://www.amiebensoudaco.net/corporate-services/registration-of-private-company/))_
- **Step 1 — Name reservation** — Verify and reserve the proposed company name with the Registrar of Companies  _(Companies Act, 2013 (https://www.moj.gm/company-registration))_
- **Step 2 — Incorporation filing** — Submit incorporation forms (e.g. Form S.V.3) and Articles of Association to the Single Window Registry  _(Single Window Business Registration Act, 2013 (http://lmis.gm/node/86))_
- **Step 3 — Tax registration** — Register with the Gambia Revenue Authority (GRA) to obtain a Tax Identification Number (TIN)  _(Income and Value Added Tax Act (https://www.moj.gm/company-registration))_
- **Step 4 — Trade license and SSHFC** — Obtain a municipal trade license from the local council and register employees with the SSHFC  _(Companies Act, 2013 (http://lmis.gm/node/86))_
- **Incorporation timeline** — Can be completed within a day at the Banjul main office or KMC branch (allow longer for ancillary registrations) (approx — confirm)  _(Single Window Business Registration Act, 2013 (http://lmis.gm/node/86))_
- **Incorporation cost** — Fixed fee bands based on declared share capital, plus name reservation and registration fees (approx — exact fee schedule published by MoJ Companies Division; confirm)  _(Companies Act, 2013 (https://www.moj.gm/companies-division))_
- **Annual compliance — annual return** — File an annual return with the Registrar of Companies (approx — confirm filing date and contents)  _(Companies Act, 2013)_
- **Annual compliance — tax return** — File the corporation tax return with the GRA by 31 March following the tax year  _(Income and Value Added Tax Act (https://www.gra.gm/domestic-faqs))_
- **Annual compliance — accounts and audit** — Maintain accounting records; audited accounts reduce the minimum turnover tax from 2% to 1% (approx — confirm statutory audit thresholds)  _(Companies Act, 2013 (https://www.gra.gm/domestic-faqs))_
