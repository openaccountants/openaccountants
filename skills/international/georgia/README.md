# Georgia: tax pack

Georgia the country (Sakartvelo), not the US state. Calendar tax year; companies on the distributed-profit model account by calendar month (Tax Code art. 135). Currency: Georgian lari (GEL). Authority: the Revenue Service (rs.ge) of the Ministry of Finance; most returns are monthly and due by the 15th of the next month. The main law is the Tax Code of Georgia, consolidated on matsne.gov.ge (publication of 15 October 2026 used across the pack). Individuals pay a flat 20% income tax (art. 81(1)), with a 1% small business regime (art. 90). Companies pay 15% profit tax only when profit is distributed, on the amount grossed up by 0.85 (arts. 97(10), 98(1)); banks and similar lenders pay 20% on annual profit (art. 98(4)). VAT is 18% (art. 166). The only contribution on earnings is the funded pension (Law on Funded Pension). Treaty-by-treaty withholding rate tables are out of scope.
