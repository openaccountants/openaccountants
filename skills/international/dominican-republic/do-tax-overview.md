# Dominican Republic Tax Overview

## The Dominican tax system at a glance

- **Overview of Dominican tax system** — The Dominican Republic operates a territorial tax system administered by the DGII (Dirección General de Impuestos Internos). Personal income tax is progressive (0%-25%), corporate income tax is a flat 27%, and a value-added tax (ITBIS) of 18% applies to most goods and services.
- **Standard tax year** — Calendar year ending 31 December (companies may elect 31 March, 30 June or 30 September fiscal close)  _(Código Tributario (Law 11-92), Title II — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/dominican-republic/corporate/tax-administration)_
- **Currency** — Dominican peso (DOP / RD$)  _(Constitution of the Dominican Republic)_
- **National tax authority** — Dirección General de Impuestos Internos (DGII)  _(Código Tributario (Law 11-92) — https://dgii.gov.do)_
- **Basis of taxation** — Territorial — Dominican-source income is taxed; foreign-source income of residents is generally exempt except foreign investment/financial income (taxable from the third year of residence)  _(Código Tributario (Law 11-92) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/dominican-republic/individual/taxes-on-personal-income)_
- **Top personal income tax rate** — 25 percent  _(Código Tributario (Law 11-92), Art. 296 — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/dominican-republic/individual/taxes-on-personal-income)_
- **Corporate income tax rate** — 27 percent  _(Código Tributario (Law 11-92), Art. 297 — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/dominican-republic/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_
- **Value-added tax (ITBIS) standard rate** — 18 percent (Yes — ITBIS standard rate)  _(Código Tributario (Law 11-92), Title III (ITBIS) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/dominican-republic/corporate/other-taxes)_
- **Annual personal income tax return deadline (IR-1)** — 31 March of the year following the tax year  _(Código Tributario (Law 11-92), Form IR-1 — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/dominican-republic/individual/tax-administration)_
- **Annual corporate income tax return deadline (IR-2)** — Within 120 days after fiscal year-end (e.g. 30 April for a 31 December close)  _(Código Tributario (Law 11-92), Form IR-2 — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/dominican-republic/corporate/tax-administration)_
- **Monthly ITBIS return deadline (IT-1)** — 20th of the month following the operations  _(Código Tributario (Law 11-92), Form IT-1 — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/dominican-republic/corporate/other-taxes)_
- **Asset tax (Impuesto sobre Activos)** — 1 percent (1% of the company's net taxable assets; creditable against corporate income tax (acts as a minimum tax))  _(Código Tributario (Law 11-92), Asset Tax provisions — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/dominican-republic/corporate/other-taxes)_
