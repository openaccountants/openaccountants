# Finland YEL and self-employed social insurance — 2026 editorial draft

**Status:** **Source-cited draft** by the OpenAccountants team. It is not accountant-authored, accountant-verified or an attestation.

**Scope:** Use this method for ordinary Finnish self-employment to screen YEL eligibility, set proposed confirmed income, arrange the policy, calculate the 2026 contribution and route the tax deduction. It does not decide international social-security coordination, disputed worker status, MYEL, benefit entitlement or a provider's individual decision.

## Ask the client first

- When did the self-employment begin and will it continue for at least four months?
- What work does the person actually perform, for how many hours, and what would an equally qualified outsider be paid?
- What are the entity, contract, ownership, voting-right and senior-position facts?
- Which countries are involved, and is there an A1 or applicable-country decision?
- Which pension provider, prior YEL periods, payer and provider quote apply?

## 1. Collect the record

Retain date of birth; work start, duration, hours, role, skills and responsibility; entity and contract facts; shares, votes, position and relevant family ownership; country/A1 facts; pension history; provider quote; payer; outsider-wage evidence; and the field, turnover, work-scope and professional-skill data used for the provider calculation service. Do not set YEL income from invoices, revenue, profit, dividends or a preferred benefit level.

## 2. Eligibility

YEL is required only if the person works in the activity, is self-employed, is at least 18, meets the age end that applies to their birth year, has been self-employed for at least four months, meets the Finnish coverage screen and has 2026 annual confirmed income of at least €9,423.09. The obligation ends at 68 for people born in 1957 or earlier, 69 for 1958–1961, and 70 for 1962 or later. The 2026 upper confirmed-income limit is €214,000. A kiosk operated for three months each year can still require YEL where the seasonal activity continues year to year and the other conditions are met: the YEL policy is continuous and valid year round.

For an Oy shareholder in a senior position, test YEL where the person holds more than 30% alone or more than 50% with family, directly or through an intermediary. An invoicing-platform user still needs an actual employment-relationship screen; an employee belongs in TyEL. Refer multi-country, MYEL, grant, family-work and disputed-status facts.

## 3. Confirmed income and review controls

Confirmed income is the annual value of the work performed, broadly the wage for an equally qualified outsider. Set the value at an annual level even where the business begins part-way through the year; do not prorate the confirmed-income assessment for a midyear start. The provider uses the common calculation service. It considers field median wage, turnover, the value and amount of work, business scope and professional skills. The resulting recommendation has ±30% leeway. Turnover is an input to the recommendation; it is not the determinant of confirmed income.

Record the recommendation, its inputs and the work evidence. Explain any departure with substantiated part-time, hours, staffing, unusual-work, pay or other work-input facts. Do not mechanically choose either leeway boundary. Aggregate work input across companies; one policy can cover it. The provider makes the appealable confirmed-income decision.

Providers review YEL income every three years. In the first two reviews, a provider can increase confirmed income by no more than €4,000 at each review. From the beginning of 2026, that cap also applies at the first review for insurance begun in 2023–2025. It is not an annual cap or an automatic increase. Retain the notice, current evidence and decision.

At the beginning of each year, the provider raises confirmed income using the wage coefficient. Contact the provider when business operations change or when considering a rise in confirmed income. Keep the change facts and new decision: confirmed income cannot be amended retrospectively. This differs from late policy arrangement, which can cover the current and three preceding years.

## The method, step by step

1. Collect the eligibility, ownership, work-input, country, history and payer evidence.
2. Test the ordinary eligibility conditions and refer an international or disputed-status case.
3. Obtain and evidence the provider calculation-service recommendation, then retain the confirmed-income decision.
4. Choose a provider, arrange the policy, calculate the stated contribution and preserve its terms.
5. Route the deduction by payer, preserve paid-contribution evidence and retain review/late-arrangement records.

## 4. Arrange and calculate

Choose an earnings-related pension insurance company, or where eligible an industry pension fund. Obtain its current quote and preserve the provider, policy effective date, payment-frequency choice and terms. Do not use a universal instalment date, late-interest rate or payment plan.

Arrange YEL within six months of starting self-employment. Coverage begins at the start. A person can arrange insurance retroactively for the current and three previous years. If ETK has urged correction and the person does not act, ETK can arrange YEL on that person's behalf and at that person's expense. This draft does not establish a surcharge or provider invoice terms.

For 2026:

    annual standard contribution = confirmed income × 24.40%
    monthly equivalent = annual standard contribution ÷ 12

A newly self-employed person whose self-employment began on or after 1 January 2013 receives a 22% reduction for the initial 48 months. If the first self-employment period is shorter than 48 months, the unused months can be used in a second period; all self-employment periods beginning in 2001 or later count in calculating the period. Confirm the history with the provider before applying it.

Contribution flexibility is temporary: an increase of 10–100% or reduction of 10–20% can apply under the provider’s current route. It does not permanently rewrite confirmed income. Retain the provider confirmation and terms. Pensionable earnings follow contributions actually paid where flexibility, partial payment, non-payment or expiry applies.

## 5. Tax and pension routing

If the person pays contributions, the deduction is in their own or spouse’s taxation; if the company pays, it is a company expense. Claim the contribution once only through the applicable payer route and preserve payer and payment evidence before preparing the return.

For 2026, pension accrues at 1.5% of annual confirmed income for every age group. Ordinarily:

    annual pension accrual = annual confirmed income × 1.5%

The provider record controls actual accrual where flexibility or unpaid contributions applies. Confirmed income also affects sickness, parental and unemployment allowances. The 2026 €15,481 unemployment-security threshold does not alone establish an entitlement.

## 6. Worked checks

**Below threshold:** documented annual work input below €9,423.09 means ordinary mandatory YEL does not arise on these facts. Recheck if scope or hours change.

**Ordinary contribution:** confirmed income of €30,000 gives €30,000 × 24.40% = €7,320 annual standard contribution and €7,320 ÷ 12 = €610 monthly equivalent. The monthly amount is mathematical, not an invoice schedule.

**New entrepreneur:** if the €30,000 case is eligible for the 22% reduction, €7,320 × 78% = €5,709.60. Confirm history before relying on the result.

**Review cap:** a first review in 2026 for insurance begun in 2024 is within the stated 2023–2025 extension, so the €4,000 increase cap applies. Retain the review notice and evidence; no increase is automatic.

**Late arrangement:** check actual work dates. The person may arrange insurance for the current and three prior years; after unheeded ETK admonition, ETK can arrange it at the person's expense. Obtain actual provider terms rather than calculating a surcharge.

**Recurring seasonal kiosk:** a kiosk operates for three months each year. Because the activity continues year to year, test the other YEL gates and, if they are met, arrange continuous year-round YEL rather than treating each season as an isolated three-month activity.

**Midyear start:** a qualifying business begins in July. Estimate confirmed income as the annual value of the work using the outsider-wage/work-input method; do not halve that assessment solely because the start date is midyear. The provider’s decision and actual policy-period terms control.

**Changed business:** the provider has indexed confirmed income at the start of the year, then the business operations materially change. Give the change facts to the provider and obtain its new decision; do not seek a retrospective amendment to confirmed income. A late-policy arrangement is a different rule.

## When to refuse or refer

- Multi-country work, A1 and applicable-country decisions.
- Disputed employment, MYEL, grant and public-sector coverage.
- Retirement, disability or unemployment-pension facts.
- Provider invoice, payment-plan and late-charge terms.
- Exact benefit amount, unemployment-fund membership and income-tax-return postings.
