# Finland tax overview v0.3

## Start with the question and the taxpayer

Use the existing topic Guide and its source/read-date record before applying a figure. Distinguish tax residence, legal entity, income or transaction category and the relevant year. The [pack README](README.md) records coverage and remaining gaps; all seven tax domains remain partial.

| Question | Existing preparation Guide | Remaining boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Resident individual tax or sole-trader income | [Individual income tax](fi-income-tax.md) | The state scale is only one part of the assessment; deductions, local taxes and special income need further review. |
| Company profits, losses or tax return | [Corporate income tax](fi-corporate-income-tax.md) | Complete statutory, financing, group and exemption rules remain open. |
| VAT classification and return | [VAT return](finland-vat-return.md) | Specialist supplies, statutory scope and full review remain open. |
| Self-employed pension insurance | [YEL](fi-yel-social.md) | Establish eligibility and provider decisions; this does not cover all social insurance. |
| Employee deductions and employer reporting | [Payroll](fi-employer-payroll.md) | Detailed benefit, statutory and exceptional cases remain open. |
| Related-party documentation | [Transfer pricing](fi-transfer-pricing.md) | Documentation does not establish an arm's-length price. |
| Arrival/departure and foreign tax relief | [Individual cross-border residence](fi-individual-cross-border-residence.md) | Income-specific treaty and corporate/PE cases remain open. |
| Income-tax payments during the year | [Prepayments](fi-prepayments.md) | The issued decision and applicable taxpayer route control. |
| Multinational reporting | [CbCR](fi-country-by-country-reporting.md) | Threshold, filer role and notification are separate checks. |
| Acquisition of real estate or housing-company shares | [Property-transfer tax](fi-property-transfer-tax.md) | Annual property taxation, rent and disposal gains are separate topics. |
| Entity formation | [Formation draft](fi-company-formation.md) | Current PRH setup/fee and selected statutory checks; broader entity, accounting and specialist review remain open. |

## Workpaper handoff

Record the facts, selected Guide/version, source passages, assumptions, calculations and unresolved points together. Separate preparation from a return submitted or a payment made. File count, executable arithmetic and an HTTP success response do not establish country completeness or named accountant review.
