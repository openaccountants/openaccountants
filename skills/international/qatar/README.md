# Qatar: tax pack

The ordinary income-tax regime is administered by the General Tax Authority
(GTA), with amounts in Qatari riyals (QAR). This batch adds 2026 TP and XB
source-cited drafts. It does not reverify the older 2025 Guides or complete the
country pack. No Guide in this batch has a named professional attestation.

## Domains

| Domain | Guide | State | Remaining work |
|---|---|---|---|
| IT-IND | [qa-tax-overview](qa-tax-overview.md) | Source-cited draft | 2025 overview only; current natural-person business computation missing |
| IT-CORP | [qa-corporate-income-tax](qa-corporate-income-tax.md) | Source-cited draft | Current primary-law base, deductions, losses and foreign income |
| VAT | Not applicable as checked on 2026-10-24 | Not a zero-rated VAT regime | Monitor domestic commencement; existing VAT summaries are legacy material |
| SC | [qa-payroll-social](qa-payroll-social.md) | Source-cited draft | Current primary-law and GCC coordination verification |
| PR | Not applicable to excluded employment remuneration | ITL promulgation art. 2(3) | SC and business-payment WHT remain separate obligations |
| TP | [qa-transfer-pricing](qa-transfer-pricing.md) | Source-cited draft | Subsidiary CbCR activation |
| XB | [qa-cross-border-tax](qa-cross-border-tax.md) | Source-cited draft | Foreign-income/substance/credit computations, PE attribution, migration gains and direct treaty-relief operating conditions |

QFC and free-zone special regimes, petroleum/contractual special rates, detailed
Pillar Two computations and treaty-specific rate tables are outside this ordinary
tax batch. Company formation is part of the later accounting milestone.

## Existing material requiring a separate correction

The six pre-existing Guides are preserved. `qatar-tax.md` contains an unverified
blanket claim that foreign VAT is irrecoverable, old excise summaries and a
different petroleum-rate description. The overview/formation/corporate files
also need reconciliation with the amended law's foreign-income rules. These
legacy statements are not verified by this batch and must not be used as its
legal authority. Correct them in their own focused change under the writing
standard's sibling-contradiction rule.
