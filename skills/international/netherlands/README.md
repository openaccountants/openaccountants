# Netherlands: tax pack

Calendar tax year. Authority: the Belastingdienst. Euro. Individuals are taxed in three boxes: box 1 (work and home, with national insurance contributions collected in the first band), box 2 (substantial interest of 5% or more in a company) and box 3 (deemed return on savings and investments). Corporate tax (vennootschapsbelasting) at 19% and 25.8%; dividend tax at 15%; a conditional withholding tax on payments to low-tax jurisdictions; VAT (btw) under EU rules. Incoming employees may get the expat scheme (30% ruling).
