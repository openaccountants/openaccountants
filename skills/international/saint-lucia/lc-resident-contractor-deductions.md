# Saint Lucia: deductions from resident contractor payments

## Check the statutory contractor definition

Section 78 concerns payments for **labour supply or equipment hire**, whether made directly or through a financial institution. Its contractor definition is a **resident** supplying independent personal services for reward, outside employment. It excludes services as accountants/auditors/tax consultants, business or management consultants, lawyers, doctors/dentists/pharmacists/nurses, civil or mechanical engineers, and funeral undertakings. Do not apply this section merely because an invoice uses the word “contractor”. [Section 78(1), (5)](https://attorneygeneralchambers.com/laws-of-saint-lucia/income-tax-act/section-78).

Employee deductions belong under the payroll rules. For non-residents, consult the separate [non-resident withholding Guide](lc-nonresident-withholding-tax.md); the resident contract exception does not establish a non-resident exemption.

## Rate, contract exception and exemption evidence

The payer or financial institution deducts **10% of the gross covered payment**, subject to the statutory exceptions. Section 78(1A), inserted by section 2 of the retrieved Act 20 of 2023, excludes a **contract valued at no more than EC$10,000**. This tests the contract value, not each instalment. Do not split payments and assume each benefits from a new EC$10,000 exception. The provision also does not describe a tax-free slice of a larger contract. [Section 78(1)–(1A)](https://attorneygeneralchambers.com/laws-of-saint-lucia/income-tax-act/section-78).

A contractor can apply for a Comptroller exemption where this income has been regularly declared and the tax paid or secured to the Comptroller's satisfaction. The payer can pay gross when the contractor produces evidence of that exemption. An application or a contractor's verbal assertion is not the exemption evidence specified by subsection (3). For a contractor operating for less than a year, the Comptroller can request an advance-tax deposit based on the information provided. [Section 78(2)–(4)](https://attorneygeneralchambers.com/laws-of-saint-lucia/income-tax-act/section-78).

Assuming section 78 applies and no exemption is evidenced, an EC$8,000 payment under a contract valued at EC$24,000 produces an **EC$800 deduction** and **EC$7,200 net payment**. The instalment being below EC$10,000 does not satisfy the whole-contract exception. VAT composition, mixed supplies and variations require their own verified treatment before applying this illustration to an invoice.

The enacted amendment bears the date **17 August 2023** and independently confirms the EC$10,000 whole-contract wording. It contains no express retrospective clause or special rule for contracts spanning that date. Do not use the exception to reverse an earlier deduction without checking the relevant transaction dates and applicable commencement rules. [Act 20 of 2023, section 2](https://npc.govt.lc/laws/download/UjVlcXRvZTd3RG93ODVaQ0tsbWNwYll6a2JaWDhXUi9EMWNHcTh2UVVEQ01NVTNldmxWRjE4NWxxcGdkUnNSZjlTZXFrYTlHSitGTldaSWNxTGg3djdlMGIvRnZsK2drVHBKeFZJSnFRYTIyVkRPeUp3NGVQb1dsVkR1Qko2dGU%3D).

## Remit and give credit

Section 116 requires payment of tax **deducted or deductible within 15 days after month-end**. Failure to make the deduction does not move the deadline. Keep the contract, payment calculation, exemption evidence if relevant and remittance proof. [Section 116](https://attorneygeneralchambers.com/laws-of-saint-lucia/income-tax-act/section-116).

Section 81 credits a section 78 deduction against the contractor's section 80(1) income tax for the year to which the income relates. It is therefore not described here as final tax replacing the contractor's income-tax computation. Section 79 protects a payer for the amount actually deducted and accounted for to the Comptroller as if paid to the recipient. [Section 81](https://attorneygeneralchambers.com/laws-of-saint-lucia/income-tax-act/section-81); [section 79](https://attorneygeneralchambers.com/laws-of-saint-lucia/income-tax-act/section-79).

## Failure to deduct is a separate penalty provision

The retrieved section 135 specifies **10% of tax not deducted or not accounted for**, in addition to interest and any personal liability. Act 10 of 2026 changed section 134's late-payment rate; it did not amend section 135. Do not substitute the new section 134 rate into this distinct provision without authority. Historical application, later instruments and remission still need checking. [Section 135](https://attorneygeneralchambers.com/laws-of-saint-lucia/income-tax-act/section-135); [2026 amendment source and analysis](lc-company-tax-filing-and-payments.md).

Section 117 separately governs interest, subject to the prime rate and ministerial orders, and makes it unrecoverable from the person whose tax should have been deducted. This draft does not calculate current interest or establish amnesty eligibility. [Section 117](https://attorneygeneralchambers.com/laws-of-saint-lucia/income-tax-act/section-117).
