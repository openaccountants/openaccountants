# Saint Lucia: beneficial owners, notices and company registers

## Identify the governing company regime

This Guide addresses companies incorporated or continued under the Companies Act. Establish the certificate, company type, incorporation date and applicable regime before using it. International business companies, external companies and other legal arrangements need their own statutory analysis. The annual registry return is covered separately in [company annual reporting](lc-company-annual-reporting.md).

## Identify natural persons, not just registered shareholders

Section 551 defines a beneficial owner as a natural person who ultimately owns or controls the company, exercises ultimate effective control over a legal person or arrangement (such as a senior manager), or on whose behalf a transaction or activity is conducted. Its definition of ultimate ownership/control includes direct or indirect ownership or control of **25% or more** of shares, voting rights or ownership interest. Beneficial ownership includes holdings through trustees, legal representatives, agents and other intermediaries. [Section 551](https://attorneygeneralchambers.com/laws-of-saint-lucia/companies-act/section-551).

The threshold is inclusive: a person with exactly 25% qualifies under that limb. A person below 25% cannot be excluded without examining effective control and the other limbs. Do not stop at a corporate shareholder or assume every senior manager automatically qualifies: assess the statutory control facts.

Collect the share register, voting and appointment rights, ownership chain, nominee/trust arrangements and evidence of who actually controls the company. Document why each person meets a limb. For complex chains, do not treat multiplication of economic interests alone as a complete control test.

## Initial and change notices

| Event | Statutory rule | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Incorporation | Send the prescribed beneficial-owner notice within **90 days**, subject to the initial-notice exceptions; include the prescribed filing fee | [Section 69A](https://attorneygeneralchambers.com/laws-of-saint-lucia/companies-act/section-69a) |
| Change among directors or beneficial owners | Send the prescribed change notice within **15 days** after the change | [Section 77](https://attorneygeneralchambers.com/laws-of-saint-lucia/companies-act/section-77) |

Act 4/2021 section 6 independently confirms the 90-day initial notice and fee requirement; section 7 adds beneficial owners to section 77. Do not wait until the annual return to report an intervening ownership change. Section 69A(3) excludes public companies, multilateral institutions, government entities and pension funds regulated by the government in their country of origin from the subsection (1) initial-notice requirement. This is not stated as a blanket exemption from every company register, change-notice or other transparency obligation. Section 551's public-company definition refers to shares or debentures distributed to the public; do not equate it automatically with stock-exchange listing. [Section 69A](https://attorneygeneralchambers.com/laws-of-saint-lucia/companies-act/section-69a); [section 551](https://attorneygeneralchambers.com/laws-of-saint-lucia/companies-act/section-551).

## Maintain a separate beneficial-owner register

Section 177(3A) requires the company's register to contain the beneficial owner's name and address, the date the person became or changed status as beneficial owner, and the percentage of shares with voting rights held. A company can appoint an agent to maintain the registers; they must be kept at its registered office or another directors-designated place in Saint Lucia. Do not confuse an agent-held register with completion of the Registrar notice. [Section 177(3A), (7)](https://attorneygeneralchambers.com/laws-of-saint-lucia/companies-act/section-177).

For a controller with no shares, retain evidence of the control basis and accurately record the absence of voting shares. The required register field does not erase the alternative control limb in section 551. The accepted form presentation for such a case remains to verify.

## Extensions are applications, not automatic grace periods

Act 4/2021 inserts section 517A, allowing the Registrar, on a company's application, to extend a statutory time limit by a further period of **no more than 90 days**, whether the original time has expired or not. Keep any granted extension and its terms. An application alone does not establish a changed deadline, and this provision does not establish an automatic penalty waiver. [Act 4/2021, section 8](https://npc.govt.lc/laws/download/UjVlcXRvZTd3RG93ODVaQ0tsbWNwVVhzdEZIWE5raDY5TDhxM2VNYnhkazJqT0hSNnVia1lCNUVkWGZlRHIyOElDdGxHUGF5cjQ4akNtaThZUW85c2Z1eW00SnZiaWVrc0lOSGVPYWFKdGVDMHlvc21KaitmT2hhaGtuenZqRkQ=).

## Boundary examples and workflow

- Four individuals each hold 25% directly with no other arrangements: all four meet the ownership threshold; none can be excluded because the holding is not above 25%.
- A person owns 20% but exercises ultimate effective control: examine the control limb rather than treating 20% as an exemption.
- Ownership changes after the initial notice: update the register and assess the 15-day change notice; the next April annual return is a separate obligation.

Check the regime and exemptions, trace natural persons and control, reconcile the register, identify each triggering date, obtain the current prescribed form and fee, file within the applicable deadline, and retain the accepted notice and evidence. Do not substitute a bank's customer-due-diligence questionnaire for the statutory filing.
