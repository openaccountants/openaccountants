# Saint Lucia: land and house tax

## Source scope

**Residential property is exempt for 2026 under section 7(1)(l), as replaced by Act 25 of 2025.** The enacted text expressly lists every year from 2017 through 2026. This is property-tax relief, not an exemption from rental income tax or transaction taxes. [Act 25 of 2025, section 2](https://npc.govt.lc/laws/download/UjVlcXRvZTd3RG93ODVaQ0tsbWNwU0NPSS8vK3JVRWhHTmJjYURkYVRKeVZadTdDWEJwc3JQMGlJY0gwS3VNb241UjlqNzNzRlRrT0QxVzNCMmlVZmlKMkNsbjl5MFhEZGNSekJZdDBVT2w1QTRGdjREZmFQdTNrRlJRMHJFRk0%3D).

This Guide reads the 2023 revised-law text with Acts 2 and 19 of 2024 and Act 25 of 2025. The revised-law site includes a notice that its 2023 laws are not yet authenticated through a Commencement Order; the enacted amendment PDF independently establishes the residential extension. Other later instruments and property-specific classification still require checking. Do not apply the residential relief to commercial property merely because both appear on one assessment. [Official Act index](https://attorneygeneralchambers.com/laws-of-saint-lucia/land-and-house-tax-act/).

## Valuation and rates

Section 12 makes assessments as at **1 January** for the ensuing year. Section 13 uses market value for residential and commercial property. A commercial owner must obtain and submit a recognised valuer's valuation for the period specified in the Comptroller's notice. The older residential annual-rental-value basis must not be carried forward from the 2008 edition. [Sections 12](https://attorneygeneralchambers.com/laws-of-saint-lucia/land-and-house-tax-act/section-12) and [13](https://attorneygeneralchambers.com/laws-of-saint-lucia/land-and-house-tax-act/section-13).

| Classification | Rate in Schedule 2 | Treatment |
|---|---|---|
| Residential property | **0.25%** of open market value | The 2017–2026 exemption overrides a charge for qualifying residential property in 2026. |
| Commercial property | **0.4%** of open market value | Check new-property and other specific relief separately. |
| Combination property | Apportionment determined by the Comptroller | Do not assume a self-selected floor-area split establishes the tax. |

Sources: [Schedule 2](https://attorneygeneralchambers.com/laws-of-saint-lucia/land-and-house-tax-act/schedule-2) and [section 21](https://attorneygeneralchambers.com/laws-of-saint-lucia/land-and-house-tax-act/section-21). Schedule 2 also contains separate acreage-based land rates; this draft does not combine those with building-value rates or certify vacant/agricultural land classification.

## Relief and obsolete form warnings

Section 7(2) exempts an owner whose residential property has assessed market value below **EC$200,000**; at or above that value, it waives tax on the first **EC$200,000** of assessed value. Legal title held singly or jointly is covered by the owner definition. Section 7(1)(k) exempts new commercial property completed after 1 April 2001 for three years. Other institutional and discretionary exemptions require their own facts. [Section 7](https://attorneygeneralchambers.com/laws-of-saint-lucia/land-and-house-tax-act/section-7).

The 2023 edition lists only 2017–2022 in section 7(1)(l). Act 2 of 2024 extended this through 2023, Act 19 of 2024 through 2025, and Act 25 of 2025 through 2026. The last amendment replaces the entire paragraph, so the earlier edition must not be used alone. [Act 2 of 2024](https://npc.govt.lc/laws/download/UjVlcXRvZTd3RG93ODVaQ0tsbWNwYkpaVkVzQUIyeXVXTzVVRWhMQ21tQU9Nck9Jam5ENjBQRUlRSnl4ajlBL3RKOXVuWWdZQUlFMzZIOVlEYkRHMk9ia2dFdk5xcm9XZ3l5WVlVcktrM289); [Act 19 of 2024](https://npc.govt.lc/laws/download/UjVlcXRvZTd3RG93ODVaQ0tsbWNwZUl4bHE1VzdyWWhhODZjMmpLOGRwSWNnNXVHbktKa2Z5VkUvNkl3TE1lajR6VUcrR3VoMkt5Vjg0ekRMMlg3ZFNNTXVrSXlsMEpDWis0VkdYWUJTdVhvdVdqaEVEVURhQ3E0K1lvSkpUUlg%3D); [Act 25 of 2025](https://npc.govt.lc/laws/download/UjVlcXRvZTd3RG93ODVaQ0tsbWNwU0NPSS8vK3JVRWhHTmJjYURkYVRKeVZadTdDWEJwc3JQMGlJY0gwS3VNb241UjlqNzNzRlRrT0QxVzNCMmlVZmlKMkNsbjl5MFhEZGNSekJZdDBVT2w1QTRGdjREZmFQdTNrRlJRMHJFRk0%3D).

The revised section also marks former paragraphs 7(1)(h), (i) and (j) as deleted in 2012. Older forms listing pension age, first-time ownership and low household income do not establish those former categories remain operative. [Section 7](https://attorneygeneralchambers.com/laws-of-saint-lucia/land-and-house-tax-act/section-7).

For a commercial illustration, EC$1,000,000 assessed value × 0.4% gives **EC$4,000 before exemptions or waivers**. Do not use an EC$750 calculation on a residential value of EC$500,000 as a 2026 charge: it would overlook the year-specific residential exemption.

## Historical commercial rent-relief waiver

Section 7A allows a **50%** waiver for a commercial owner who provided tenants either at least **20% rent relief** or a rent moratorium of at least **three months**, for April–June 2020 or July–September 2020, and was compliant with Saint Lucia tax laws when applying. The tax affected is **2019 if unpaid**, or **2020 if 2019 was paid**. This is not a general reduction of 2026 commercial tax. [Section 7A](https://attorneygeneralchambers.com/laws-of-saint-lucia/land-and-house-tax-act/section-7a).

Apply to the Comptroller using Schedule 3 with documents verifying the rent waiver or moratorium. The Comptroller can grant, refuse or request further information; refusal must be notified in writing with reasons. Eligibility does not establish approval. This draft has not verified whether a new historical claim is still accepted, or inspected the current Schedule 3 submission channel. [Section 7B](https://attorneygeneralchambers.com/laws-of-saint-lucia/land-and-house-tax-act/section-7b); [section 7C](https://attorneygeneralchambers.com/laws-of-saint-lucia/land-and-house-tax-act/section-7c).

## Changes, payment and objections

| Event | Statutory action |
|---|---|
| Acquisition or disposal | Notify the Comptroller in writing within **30 days**, with description, location, extent and market value. The specified commercial valuation submission can satisfy the return requirement. |
| Erection, addition or other substantial increase in a house's value | Give a written return within **30 days**, identifying the property and value. |
| Assessment received | Tax is due within **30 days of the date of the notice of assessment**. |
| Assessment disputed | Submit a written objection stating its grounds within **30 days after service** of the assessment/determination notice. The Comptroller can allow further time for good cause. |

Sources: [section 14](https://attorneygeneralchambers.com/laws-of-saint-lucia/land-and-house-tax-act/section-14), [section 35](https://attorneygeneralchambers.com/laws-of-saint-lucia/land-and-house-tax-act/section-35) and [section 28](https://attorneygeneralchambers.com/laws-of-saint-lucia/land-and-house-tax-act/section-28). The payment clock and objection clock use different triggers. This draft does not establish that an objection suspends payment. Additional/reduced assessments do not reopen every earlier issue: section 28(2) limits the further objection right to fresh liability.

Retain the notice and service evidence, title/transfer documents, valuation, property-use evidence, relief decision, written returns, objection grounds and payment receipt. This is a practical evidence checklist, not a statutory expansion of filing requirements.

## From objection to appeal

The Comptroller must consider a valid objection, decide whether to allow it wholly or partly or disallow it, and notify the objector in writing. If the decision changes the assessment, a reduction or increase notice must accompany it or follow as soon as practicable. An objection is not necessarily a route only to a reduction. [Section 29](https://attorneygeneralchambers.com/laws-of-saint-lucia/land-and-house-tax-act/section-29).

An aggrieved person can lodge a **written notice of appeal with the secretary to the Appeal Commissioners**, copying the Comptroller. The deadline is **30 days from service** of the objection decision or other appealable determination; the Commissioners can allow more time for good cause. This is a separate step after an objection, with a different recipient. Preserve evidence of service and lodging. [Section 31](https://attorneygeneralchambers.com/laws-of-saint-lucia/land-and-house-tax-act/section-31).

The appellant bears the burden of proof. The Commissioners can confirm, increase or reduce an assessment or make another appropriate order. Each side normally bears its own costs unless the Commissioners direct otherwise. The secretary must give written hearing notice at least **30 days beforehand**, and both sides can appear personally or through a representative. [Section 32(1)–(5)](https://attorneygeneralchambers.com/laws-of-saint-lucia/land-and-house-tax-act/section-32).

Section 33 provides a further **30-day** appeal route to the High Court, and then from the High Court to the Court of Appeal, for decisions involving law or mixed fact and law. It is not an unrestricted second factual valuation hearing. The retrieved section does not specify the precise service/date trigger for those further periods; applicable court rules and filing requirements must be checked before calculating a case deadline. Either the Comptroller or appellant can appeal, and the court can alter the assessment and award costs. [Section 33](https://attorneygeneralchambers.com/laws-of-saint-lucia/land-and-house-tax-act/section-33).
