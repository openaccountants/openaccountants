# Saint Lucia: Health and Citizen Security Levy

## Scope and taxpayer facts

This source-cited draft covers the separate levy on scheduled imported goods and services. Before computing, record the transaction date, supplier and recipient residence, location and use of the service, VAT registration or registered-recipient status, exact service classification or customs heading, consideration/customs value, related-party status and exemption evidence. It is not a complete customs assessment or VAT return.

For goods, sections 3–5 impose the levy on covered imports, payable to Customs. For services, section 8 covers resident-to-resident supplies, nonresident-to-resident supplies and services from an overseas business to its local branch. Section 2's place-of-service rules include listed services used or enjoyed by a registered recipient in Saint Lucia. Section 10 assigns payment to registered persons or registered recipients. Do not infer that every foreign invoice or every unregistered domestic seller must charge the levy. [Sections 2–5](https://attorneygeneralchambers.com/laws-of-saint-lucia/health-and-citizen-security-levy-act/section-2); [section 8](https://attorneygeneralchambers.com/laws-of-saint-lucia/health-and-citizen-security-levy-act/section-8); [section 10](https://attorneygeneralchambers.com/laws-of-saint-lucia/health-and-citizen-security-levy-act/section-10).

## Classify the supply before using a rate

A service must be in Schedule 2, then survive the Schedule 3 exemption test. The schedule includes accounting/bookkeeping/auditing and tax consultancy, legal activities, management consultancy, advertising, computer programming and consultancy, cleaning, specified real-estate services and other named activities. This list is illustrative, not a replacement for the schedule. SI 95/2023 removed sale of motor vehicles and the two specified financial-service categories; SI 103/2023 added **maintenance and repair of motor vehicles**. A vehicle sale is not thereby classified as a repair service. [Act 16/2023, Schedule 2, printed pages 212–213](https://npc.govt.lc/laws/download/UjVlcXRvZTd3RG93ODVaQ0tsbWNwZWJwMzRIMnorcmMvdkUwMWFsdXY3WHFKY1BlTFRtdW1rakl6VTdWWmFnQWFqUmFWZFRmYjBmMTlZVzhKcHV6bVVuNUV3aTlMU25zbUh5UXVZekV4cTNQZ1VHeGRIZjBkTXQ3UGVmcEVoVkdxZm84ZFhKN0pMcm4wcGM3U2I2YmpRPT0=); [SI 95/2023, section 2(b)](https://npc.govt.lc/laws/download/T1dVbFFjRVYzMVhYdC9lTHNlZ2RrOHcvT3NqZ04yL0VZdXdrTzJPS0lMc050MnoveXFvQTVVUC9BenB3WndyZ0pRMzhDanlVbHBUaGhhamdISEVCdzlTRG9qSktXdXpNeGwyb3BjU0h5RDA9); [SI 103/2023, section 2(b), printed page 1387](https://npc.govt.lc/files/documents/gazettesx/2023/8/Extraordinary%20Gazette%20August%2014%202023.pdf).

Schedule 3 Part 2 exempts services outside Schedule 2 and services exempt or zero-rated under the VAT Act. Establish the actual VAT classification, including its effective period; do not assume all services attract both taxes. Goods need a separate Schedule 1 and Schedule 3 check. SI 103 also changed fuel classification and introduced certified manufacturing/agricultural raw-material and packaging exemptions. Those concessions require the named ministry's certification, not merely an importer describing goods as raw materials. [Act 16/2023, Schedule 3](https://npc.govt.lc/laws/download/UjVlcXRvZTd3RG93ODVaQ0tsbWNwZWJwMzRIMnorcmMvdkUwMWFsdXY3WHFKY1BlTFRtdW1rakl6VTdWWmFnQWFqUmFWZFRmYjBmMTlZVzhKcHV6bVVuNUV3aTlMU25zbUh5UXVZekV4cTNQZ1VHeGRIZjBkTXQ3UGVmcEVoVkdxZm84ZFhKN0pMcm4wcGM3U2I2YmpRPT0=); [SI 103/2023, section 2](https://npc.govt.lc/files/documents/gazettesx/2023/8/Extraordinary%20Gazette%20August%2014%202023.pdf).

For timber imports, SI 165/2025 replaces specified exemption tariff codes. Use the amended heading and description rather than a copied 2023 code list. Detailed customs classification, seasonal barrel concessions and other individual reliefs require transaction-specific verification. [SI 165/2025, section 2](https://npc.govt.lc/laws/download/T1dVbFFjRVYzMVhYdC9lTHNlZ2RreUZFNXcvaTIrV0lNRE1GSzJSYnBXL0c3UUNOeDByRG1OU0ppVU9EUWEyWWdEQXQ0MTkwSUxkNUlINTlIaWhoWDY3L1RTZGkrMTJYUXhSYlNYcXp1OTRqdURVMjRPM2pHQTFLRnRNOFJTZVlLZUZUUGNzMHRZRDJXUW13YVorLzRRPT0=).

## Rates, base and computation

| Supply | Statutory rate and base | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Covered imported goods | 2.5% of customs value determined under customs legislation | [Section 4, as amended by Act 21/2023](https://attorneygeneralchambers.com/laws-of-saint-lucia/health-and-citizen-security-levy-act/section-4) |
| Covered services | 2.5% of consideration, subject to variation orders | [Section 9](https://attorneygeneralchambers.com/laws-of-saint-lucia/health-and-citizen-security-levy-act/section-9) |

The amended service consideration definition includes money or kind and fees/charges, allows the specified discount/rebate reduction, and excludes the levy, VAT, stamp duty and other statutory duties/levies. Related-person consideration uses fair market value. Do not treat the original Act's CIF goods wording as the final amended rule. [Section 2](https://attorneygeneralchambers.com/laws-of-saint-lucia/health-and-citizen-security-levy-act/section-2); [section 4](https://attorneygeneralchambers.com/laws-of-saint-lucia/health-and-citizen-security-levy-act/section-4).

1. Establish coverage, exemption and any applicable rate order for the transaction date.
2. Determine customs value for goods or statutory consideration for services. Document adjustments and related-party valuation.
3. Multiply the applicable base by the levy rate.
4. For services, apply the levy **before VAT**. The government's IRD explanation explicitly calculates VAT on the service charge plus levy. Use the independently verified VAT rate for that supply, not a universal assumed rate. [Section 10(2)](https://attorneygeneralchambers.com/laws-of-saint-lucia/health-and-citizen-security-levy-act/section-10); [IRD explanation, 23 August 2023](https://sports.govt.lc/news/health-and-security-levy-zero-rated-on-services-until-oct-2).

For an ordinary covered service with EC$1,000 consideration and no relief, the levy is **EC$25** and the amount before VAT is **EC$1,025**. For covered goods with an established EC$10,000 customs value, the levy is **EC$250**, before separately assessing other import charges. These are assumed inputs illustrating arithmetic, not valuations or evidence of classification.

If related parties invoice EC$600 but the applicable fair market value is established at EC$1,000, the service levy base is EC$1,000 and levy EC$25. If a service is established as VAT-exempt within Schedule 3 Part 2, levy is zero under that exemption; do not compute 2.5% merely because the service otherwise appears in Schedule 2.

## Administration and payment

Section 12 applies VAT legislation for services administration and assigns collection to Inland Revenue. The government's 23 August 2023 IRD explanation states remittance **21 days after month-end**, giving October collections due by 21 November as its example. This is dated official administrative guidance, not confirmation of a current return form, portal workflow, holiday adjustment or extension. Those operational details remain to verify before filing. Goods payment belongs to Customs under section 5. [Section 12](https://attorneygeneralchambers.com/laws-of-saint-lucia/health-and-citizen-security-levy-act/section-12); [IRD explanation](https://sports.govt.lc/news/health-and-security-levy-zero-rated-on-services-until-oct-2); [section 5](https://attorneygeneralchambers.com/laws-of-saint-lucia/health-and-citizen-security-levy-act/section-5).

Keep the classification, invoice/base calculation, relief evidence, levy ledger and payment receipt together. This recordkeeping workflow does not establish a statutory retention period. Do not net levy paid on purchases against levy collected merely by assuming it follows VAT input-credit rules; levy-credit treatment is not established in this draft.

## Historic and special-rate checks

The original Act commences on **2 August 2023** and contains no general two-year sunset. SI 106/2023's revised-law text specifies a services zero rate from **2 August through 1 October 2023**. The general statutory rate therefore cannot be applied to the entire launch period. The original gazette retrieved for 28 August announces SI 106 but does not reproduce its operative text; the cited revised-law provision is the evidence for the dates. [Act 16/2023, section 1](https://npc.govt.lc/laws/download/UjVlcXRvZTd3RG93ODVaQ0tsbWNwZWJwMzRIMnorcmMvdkUwMWFsdXY3WHFKY1BlTFRtdW1rakl6VTdWWmFnQWFqUmFWZFRmYjBmMTlZVzhKcHV6bVVuNUV3aTlMU25zbUh5UXVZekV4cTNQZ1VHeGRIZjBkTXQ3UGVmcEVoVkdxZm84ZFhKN0pMcm4wcGM3U2I2YmpRPT0=); [SI 106/2023, paragraph 2](https://attorneygeneralchambers.com/laws-of-saint-lucia/health-and-citizen-security-levy-act/section-2-01).

SI 108/2024 sets a zero rate for specified consultants, contractors, subcontractors, suppliers and utility-company goods/services supplied to Rodney Bay City Center Ltd and its contractors for the named approved project. It is not a general construction or tourism concession. Verify both recipient and project connection. [SI 108/2024, paragraph 2](https://npc.govt.lc/laws/download/T1dVbFFjRVYzMVhYdC9lTHNlZ2RrMjFCRGtHSm5HaURDZU9XWEIyZFluQnB4SlZZdkNYeGcrdmtYNEZXa2EvSUZWK29xTGJ6ZXlnWHozL1BXZjFycXZRN2ptZmdHc0h1bWlHajcwYWt1WE09).
