# Grenada: tax records and return controls

## Legal basis and limits

The Tax Administration Act 14 of 2016 commenced on **1 May 2016** under [SRO 34 of 2016, section 2](https://grenadaparliament.gd/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/S.R.O.-34-of-2016-Tax-Administration-Act-Commencement-Order-2016.pdf). Schedule 1 includes income tax, VAT, excise, property tax, property-transfer tax and annual stamp tax. Section 4 preserves the relevant tax law where it conflicts with this administrative Act and excludes collection/recovery vested in the Comptroller of Customs. This is not a single deadline rule for every tax. [Act, section 4 and Schedule 1](https://grenadaparliament.gd/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/Act-No.-14-of-2016-Tax-Administration-Act.pdf).

This Guide covers the identified recordkeeping and return provisions. Act 10 of 2020 commenced on **31 December 2020**, established by [SRO 75 of 2020](https://grenadaparliament.gd/wp-content/uploads/2021/07/S.R.O.-75-of-2020-Tax-Administration-Amendment-Act-2020-Commencement-Order.pdf). The 2025 Beryl special measures are addressed below for their specified historical period. Current penalty calculations and the full 2026 amendment chain are not certified here.

## Corporate registration certificate

The 2020 amendment inserts section 12(5)–(6). A registered body corporate receives an approved-form certificate stating its registration effective date and TIN. Display the original prominently at the principal place of activity and a **Comptroller-certified copy** at each other place of activity. An ordinary photocopy is not the specified certified copy. Check each location and preserve the certificate alongside the registration record. [Act 10 of 2020, section 2](https://grenadaparliament.gd/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/Act-No.-10-of-2020-Tax-Administration-Amendment-Act-2020-1.pdf#page=4).

## Keep transactions and their underlying evidence

Section 20 applies to businesses, independent professionals and persons required to file a tax return. Keep current records in Grenada sufficient to determine gains, profits and losses. Keep the books at the place of business unless the Comptroller approves another place in writing. The printed electronic-record rule also places the equipment/devices holding those records at the business premises. Do not assume a foreign cloud subscription alone satisfies that location requirement; current electronic-storage permissions need verification. [Section 20(1)–(4)](https://grenadaparliament.gd/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/Act-No.-14-of-2016-Tax-Administration-Act.pdf#page=20).

Retain underlying source evidence as well as the ledger. Section 20 identifies invoices, cost documents, orders, delivery notes, bank statements, contracts, bookings and diaries among the relevant documents. For VAT, retain invoices and credit/debit notes issued and received, import/export customs documents, and the specified evidence for imported services. A bank total alone does not document the nature and tax treatment of each transaction. [Section 20(5), (9)](https://grenadaparliament.gd/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/Act-No.-14-of-2016-Tax-Administration-Act.pdf#page=21).

The retention rule is **seven years from the transaction date**, or longer where the assessment time limit for a relevant tax period has not expired. Do not treat seven years as an automatic destruction date. Maintain a retention register identifying the transaction, related tax periods and any unresolved assessment-period question. [Section 20(6)](https://grenadaparliament.gd/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/Act-No.-14-of-2016-Tax-Administration-Act.pdf#page=21).

Financial statements, invoices, books of original entry and written taxpayer/Division communications must be in English. If other required records were prepared in another language, the Comptroller can require an acceptable English translation at the taxpayer's expense. [Section 20(7)–(8)](https://grenadaparliament.gd/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/Act-No.-14-of-2016-Tax-Administration-Act.pdf#page=21).

## Returns, signatures and corrections

Section 22 assigns the form, required information/attachments and manner of filing to the Comptroller. The taxpayer or authorised agent signs to attest accuracy and completeness. A paid preparer other than the taxpayer's full-time employee must also sign. Preserve authority and the submitted version, not merely a draft spreadsheet. [Section 22(1)–(5)](https://grenadaparliament.gd/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/Act-No.-14-of-2016-Tax-Administration-Act.pdf#page=22).

An amended return must state its grounds and be filed no later than **six years after the tax period ends**. Section 22(7) bars that amendment route between issue of a section 30 assessment for the period and its becoming final under section 42. Check assessment status before assuming an amended return is the available remedy. This six-year correction window is not the seven-year document-retention rule. [Section 22(3), (7)](https://grenadaparliament.gd/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/Act-No.-14-of-2016-Tax-Administration-Act.pdf#page=22).

A notice requiring a missing return does not extend the original statutory filing deadline. Information returns about someone else's tax affairs are also subject to the Act's return provisions. [Sections 23 and 25](https://grenadaparliament.gd/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/Act-No.-14-of-2016-Tax-Administration-Act.pdf#page=23).

## Due dates and extensions

If the last day falls on a day when the Division is closed to public business, section 19 treats performance on the next day it opens as timely. The printed provision records receipt-stamping as the filing date, or the postmark for mail. Verify the accepted electronic acknowledgement rule separately; a draft saved in a portal is not evidence of submission. [Section 19](https://grenadaparliament.gd/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/Act-No.-14-of-2016-Tax-Administration-Act.pdf#page=19).

Apply for a filing extension **by the filing due date**. The Comptroller can grant it and can issue a general extension for a specified period following a natural disaster or similar event. An application is not itself a granted extension. A filing extension leaves the tax payment date unchanged unless payment time is **expressly extended too**. Retain the decision and separately diary filing and payment. [Section 26](https://grenadaparliament.gd/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/Act-No.-14-of-2016-Tax-Administration-Act.pdf#page=24).

## Withholding funds

Amounts withheld under Income Tax Act Part VII are held in trust for the Comptroller. Section 65 protects them from attachment for the payer's debts and excludes them from its liquidation, assignment or bankruptcy estate, requiring full payment before property distribution. Reconcile amounts deducted to amounts remitted. The [withholding Guide](gd-withholding-tax.md) separately records the unresolved payment-timing conflict; this trust rule does not settle that deadline. [Section 65](https://grenadaparliament.gd/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/Act-No.-14-of-2016-Tax-Administration-Act.pdf).

## Historical Hurricane Beryl relief

Act 6 of 2025 was published on 20 June 2025 but section 1(2) gives it **deemed commencement on 1 July 2024**. Section 2 separately defines its relevant period as **1 June 2024 through 31 January 2025**. Preserve that statutory distinction; neither the publication date nor the commencement date replaces the expressly defined relief period. [Act 6 of 2025, sections 1–2](https://grenadaparliament.gd/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Act-No.-6-of-2025-Tax-Administration-Post-Hurricane-Beryl-Fiscal-Support-Special-Measures-Act-2025.pdf#page=4).

The Act distinguishes the following measures:

| Tax | Eligibility and relief described in the Act |
|---|---|
| VAT | Registered businesses operating in Carriacou or Petite Martinique; other Grenadian businesses need a written application and evidence satisfying the Comptroller of Beryl damage or other negative impact and any further conditions. Section 4 exempts the specified supplies/imports, returns, remittances and period interest/late fees/penalties. |
| Corporate income tax | Companies registered and operating in Carriacou or Petite Martinique are exempt from section 93 advance monthly payments during the relevant period. Section 5 does not say all final corporate income tax is waived. |
| Annual stamp tax | For qualifying businesses, the specified 2024 return and payment/remittance deadlines extend to 31 January 2025. Eligibility outside the two islands requires the written application and evidence described in section 6. |
| Employee tax deductions | For qualifying employers, the specified 2024 annual deductions return and employee-tax remittance deadlines extend to 31 January 2025. Eligibility outside the two islands requires the section 7 application and evidence. This is not a blanket extension for nonresident supplier withholding. |

[Act 6 of 2025, sections 4–7](https://grenadaparliament.gd/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Act-No.-6-of-2025-Tax-Administration-Post-Hurricane-Beryl-Fiscal-Support-Special-Measures-Act-2025.pdf#page=5).

Each relief section preserves the listed taxes and related charges already due **before the relevant period began**. Sections 6 and 7 also exclude specified interest/late fees/penalties during the relevant period. Retain pre-period arrears separately from liabilities arising within the relief period; do not erase the whole account balance. [Sections 4(5), 5(3), 6(4)–(5), 7(4)–(5)](https://grenadaparliament.gd/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Act-No.-6-of-2025-Tax-Administration-Post-Hurricane-Beryl-Fiscal-Support-Special-Measures-Act-2025.pdf).

Section 8 provides a sunset one year after commencement while saving accrued rights, obligations, liabilities and proceedings relating to the relevant period. These provisions remain relevant to historical reconciliations; the retrieved Act does not establish an ongoing 2026 exemption. Later extensions or amendments and current claim procedures still need verification. For an affected file, retain island/location registration evidence, relevant-period transactions, the application and Comptroller evidence where required, and separate original versus revised due-date calculations. [Section 8](https://grenadaparliament.gd/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Act-No.-6-of-2025-Tax-Administration-Post-Hurricane-Beryl-Fiscal-Support-Special-Measures-Act-2025.pdf#page=11).

## Evidence checklist and remaining work

Keep source records, ledgers, tax reconciliations, signed returns, submission acknowledgements, payment receipts, amendments with grounds, assessments and written extension decisions together. Confirm any approved alternative record location and retain the approval. These are practical controls implementing the cited provisions, not a substitute for the required forms.

Outstanding checks: later amendments or extensions to the Beryl measures, current relief claim procedures, electronic-record/filing directions, specific tax due dates, assessment limitation exceptions and independent review. No current penalty computation is provided.
