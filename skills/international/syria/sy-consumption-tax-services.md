# Syria: consumption tax on services

## Scope and source status

This research draft follows Finance Ministry Decision 938/Q.W of 29 June 2026, implementing Decree 11/2015 as amended by Law 15/2024. The ministry links the primary PDF through its [1 July announcement](https://sana.sy/economy/2515968/). Although the PDF filename mentions imports, its first 20 pages are the executive instructions. Page references below refer to those PDF pages, not the later annex tables. Arabic controls.

This is a service-tax Guide, not a general VAT system or an import tariff. The full base decree, amendment chain, later administrative changes and implementation in the taxpayer's location remain separate verification tasks. Amounts in these instructions are **new Syrian pounds** (section XXII, p.19); do not redenominate them again. Section XXIV (p.20) cancels Decisions 636 of 29 April 2026, 836/Q.W of 20 May 2024 and 1643/1 of 2015 and contrary instructions from issuance. Do not substitute their older procedures for this text. [Official instructions, pp.1, 19–20](https://docs.mof.gov.sy/consumption_tax_on_imports_table.pdf).

## Establish the service and classification first

The instructions add the levy to the invoice. They distinguish these service categories:

| Service | Instruction rate and scope | Locator |
|---|---|---|
| Classified tourist establishments | 10% for four/five stars; 5% for two/three stars. Includes customer-invoiced services such as games, sports, pool, spa, telephone and barber services, and relevant rental/investment contracts | II(a), p.1 |
| Entertainment venues/nightclubs providing artistic programmes | 20%, across the stated classifications; discos fall within this category | II(b), p.1 |
| Fast-food restaurants and tea halls | 5% for the specified two-star groups A/B, including delivery services | II(c), p.2 |
| Electric amusement parks | 5%; sequential admission tickets must show admission price and levy; ticket books require finance-office stamping. Cards/tokens also require separate daily transaction records | II(f), p.3 |
| Mobile/fixed telephone calls | 3% mobile, prepaid or postpaid; 2% fixed-line calls and stated communications. Fixed-line monthly subscriptions and other invoice levies are excluded from this base | II(g), p.3 |
| Tourist vehicle hire, with or without driver | 10% of contract value when signed, whether licensed or unlicensed. Terminating/replacing a contract does not extinguish the levy on the cancelled/amended contract | II(h), p.3 |
| Celebration/wedding halls | 5% of rental value; funeral use excluded. Associated paid event services are also included, whether separately invoiced or on the customer's main invoice | II(i)(1–3), p.3 |

Do not infer the rate merely from a trading name. For tourist establishments the approved classification/licensing decision determines the category and service description until an authorised contrary decision; report changes to the competent finance office within the legally prescribed period (section XVI, p.18). The instructions separately refer discounts/offers to Circular 698 of 27 October 2022 (II(d), p.2); that circular has not been recovered for this Guide, so no discount deduction is assumed.

For whole-establishment letting, II(i)(4) distinguishes the owner's rental value from the new operator's taxable services. For partial letting, II(i)(5) taxes rental value at the establishment's applicable rate. Both address the new operator's commencement declaration. Missing contract consideration can lead to an official valuation, with an objection route (pp.3–4). Establish those facts before calculating a letting transaction. [Instructions, pp.1–4, 18](https://docs.mof.gov.sy/consumption_tax_on_imports_table.pdf).

## Air tickets: sale location, not departure location

Section II(e), p.2 applies **3.5%** to air tickets sold in Syria irrespective of departure point, route or airline nationality. The base is the actual ticket value in the designated fare field, excluding other separately listed taxes/levies and additional travel services, including excess baggage and passenger service extras. The levy has no further additions under this provision.

Registered operating airlines collect directly or through registered agents. Tourist/travel offices selling tickets for these airlines do not themselves submit the article 3(d) declaration. Airlines or their agents supply the declaration and a monthly schedule of direct sales, accredited-agent sales and sales by tourist/travel offices on their behalf.

An electronic ticket booked and sold through foreign sites, paid outside Syria without intervention by a Syrian company/accredited agent, is excluded under II(e)(5). Under II(e)(6), an electronic sale attributable in accounting or law to the airline or its accredited agent inside Syria is treated as a Syrian sale. Payment location or departure point alone does not resolve this distinction.

A cancellation with full/partial fare refund permits the corresponding levy refund under the approved accounting procedures. A no-show without cancellation or fare refund does **not**. A fare increase attracts levy on the increase; a price reduction permits refund of the excess levy. The finance office audits sales and cancelled/refunded tickets; paper tickets or electronic entries must clearly record their status. [Instructions, II(e)(1–11), p.2](https://docs.mof.gov.sy/consumption_tax_on_imports_table.pdf).

## Registration, invoices, filing and payment

Section V (pp.10–11) requires a commencement declaration within **30 days** of starting activity, records showing stock movements/services, and an invoice or contract for each taxable sale/service. Start dates have category-specific tests. For tourism these include classification, temporary qualification or trial opening, whichever is earlier. Other establishments have tests including lease/investment commencement, licensing, activity declarations, transaction documents and opening announcements. Do not simply choose the first cash receipt.

Submit the previous month's sales/service declaration to the finance office where the establishment/activity is located during the **first 10 days of the following month**. For air-ticket sales by airlines/their agents the deadline is **15 working days of the following month**. Pay the levy within the applicable declaration period. The receiving office forwards a copy to the office of the registered head office. These are different deadlines: do not turn the ordinary 10 days into working days or the air-ticket period into 15 calendar days.

A properly declared temporary cessation removes monthly sales declarations for that cessation period; partial cessation leaves reporting for continuing activities. A cessation declaration is not permission to resume silently: written notice is required on resumption (sections V(f), VI(f–g), pp.11–12).

If the invoice/contract is collected in foreign currency, section XIV (p.17) requires levy collection and remittance to the provincial finance directorate account in that currency. Associated penalties follow the stated foreign-currency rules; Syrian-pound minimum penalties use the approved exchange rate at payment. Confirm the applicable account and payment channel with the competent office.

Keep originals and reconcile sales, levies, payments and adjustments. Section XV (pp.17–18) controls records and official destruction procedures, including a full year without destruction measured back from an authorised destruction record or relevant cessation. **This is not a blanket one-year retention limit** or permission for unilateral disposal. Electronic-linkage rules also refer to separate administrative instructions (XVII, pp.18–19), not fully covered here. [Instructions, pp.10–12, 17–19](https://docs.mof.gov.sy/consumption_tax_on_imports_table.pdf).

## Example 1: classified hospitality invoice

Assume a confirmed four-star taxable establishment supplies 10,000 new pounds of covered services before levy, without an unresolved discount or exemption. Levy is 1,000; invoice total 11,000. If the same qualifying service base instead belongs to a confirmed two/three-star category, levy is 500. Classification must be evidenced.

```oa-check
name: Syria classified hospitality service levy
inputs:
  service_base: 10000
  four_star_rate: 0.10
  two_star_rate: 0.05
steps:
  - four_star_levy: service_base * four_star_rate
  - four_star_total: service_base + four_star_levy
  - two_star_levy: service_base * two_star_rate
expected:
  four_star_levy: 1000
  four_star_total: 11000
  two_star_levy: 500
```

## Example 2: ticket base and partial refund

Assume a ticket sale falls within the Syrian rule. Fare is 20,000 new pounds, other separately listed taxes 3,000 and excluded baggage extras 2,000. Levy is 700 on the fare, not 875 on the combined 25,000. Later cancellation refunds 5,000 of fare: corresponding levy refund is 175 under the approved procedures, leaving 525. This is not an automatic deduction without supporting refund records.

```oa-check
name: Syria air ticket base and partial refund
inputs:
  fare: 20000
  other_taxes: 3000
  excluded_baggage: 2000
  fare_refunded: 5000
  rate: 0.035
steps:
  - levy: fare * rate
  - levy_refund: fare_refunded * rate
  - remaining_levy: levy - levy_refund
expected:
  levy: 700
  levy_refund: 175
  remaining_levy: 525
```

## Example 3: no-show is not a refund

On the same 20,000 fare, a passenger does not travel but neither cancels nor receives a fare refund. The 700 levy remains; the no-show alone creates no refund. Establish both cancellation and actual refunded value before using Example 2.

```oa-check
name: Syria ticket no show without cancellation or refund
inputs:
  fare: 20000
  rate: 0.035
  permitted_refund: 0
steps:
  - original_levy: fare * rate
  - retained_levy: original_levy - permitted_refund
expected:
  original_levy: 700
  retained_levy: 700
```

## Penalties, exceptions and remaining checks

Section VI (pp.11–12) provides separate sanctions for missed commencement, invoices, records and returns; do not mistake a penalty computed on sales for a penalty computed on unpaid levy. Late payment is stated as 1% of levy for each delayed day, capped at twice the levy. Section VIII (p.13) provides a seven-day objection period starting the day after notification of the violation record and a 5% security deposit of its total value, with specific consequences based on the objection outcome. This is a violation-record objection, not a universal tax appeal procedure.

General exemption from tax does not automatically remove an establishment's duty to collect a levy borne by customers (XIII, pp.16–17). Section XVIII (p.19) separately addresses international organisations, diplomatic reciprocity/documentation and licensed cinemas/theatres. Resolve exemption evidence before charging or excluding a transaction.
