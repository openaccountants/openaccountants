# Bosnia and Herzegovina Company Formation & Entity Choice

## Entity types, capital and incorporation

The most common business vehicle is the limited liability company (d.o.o.). Registration is done at the competent court / registry in the relevant entity and involves notarisation, capital deposit, and registrations with the tax authority and municipality.

- **Limited liability company** — Drustvo s ogranicenom odgovornoscu (d.o.o.) — the most common form for SMEs and foreign investors  _(FBiH / RS Companies (Business Entities) Laws)_
- **Joint stock company** — Dionicko drustvo (d.d.) — used for larger enterprises and public companies  _(FBiH / RS Companies (Business Entities) Laws)_
- **Sole proprietorship** — Samostalni preduzetnik / obrt — individual trader, no separate legal personality  _(Entity Laws on Crafts / Self-Employment)_
- **Minimum share capital (d.o.o.)** — 2,000 BAM (approx. EUR 1,000)  _(FBiH / RS Companies (Business Entities) Laws (https://www.usemultiplier.com/bosnia-herzegovina/company-registration))_
- **Minimum share capital (d.d.)** — 50,000 BAM (joint stock company (approx — confirm per entity))  _(FBiH / RS Companies (Business Entities) Laws)_
- **Incorporation steps** — Notarise founding act, deposit initial capital, register with the competent court, obtain company stamp, open a bank account, register with the tax administration and municipality  _(FIPA — How to Establish a Business in BiH (https://www.usemultiplier.com/bosnia-herzegovina/company-registration))_
- **Notarisation requirement** — Founding act and registration documents must be certified by a public notary  _(Law on Notaries (https://www.usemultiplier.com/bosnia-herzegovina/company-registration))_
- **Incorporation timeline** — Approximately 10 to 15 business days for court registration  _(FIPA — How to Establish a Business in BiH (https://www.usemultiplier.com/bosnia-herzegovina/company-registration))_
- **VAT registration threshold** — 100,000 BAM (Mandatory once taxable turnover exceeds this amount; voluntary registration possible below the threshold)  _(Law on Value Added Tax (BiH))_
- **Core annual compliance** — Annual financial statements, corporate income tax return (by end of March), monthly VAT returns, and monthly payroll/contribution filings (approx — confirm deadlines per entity)  _(FBiH / RS Accounting and Tax Laws)_
