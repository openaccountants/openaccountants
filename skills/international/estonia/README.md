# Estonia: tax pack

Calendar tax year. Authority: the Estonian Tax and Customs Board (Maksu- ja Tolliamet, ETCB), with the e-MTA e-services. Euro. Income tax is a flat 22% for individuals and companies in 2026 (Income Tax Act § 4(1)); the planned rise to 24% was cancelled and the 2% security tax on income and profits does not apply. Companies pay no tax on retained profit: they pay 22/78 of the net amount when they distribute it, monthly on form TSD, and there is no withholding on dividends. The basic exemption is a flat 700 euros a month from 2026. Standard VAT is 24%.
