# Estonia: tax pack

Calendar tax year. Authority: the Estonian Tax and Customs Board (Maksu- ja Tolliamet, EMTA); returns are filed in the e-MTA portal. Euro. Companies pay no tax on profit they keep: corporate income tax is charged only when profit is distributed or treated as distributed (dividends, fringe benefits, non-business expenses, transfer pricing adjustments), at 22/78 of the net amount, through the monthly TSD return. Individuals pay a flat 22%. Social tax is 33%, paid by the employer. EU member, so EU VAT rules and EU social security coordination apply.
