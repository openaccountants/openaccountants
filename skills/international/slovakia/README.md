# Slovakia: tax pack

Calendar tax year. Authority: the Financial Administration (Finančná správa) and its tax offices, under the Financial Directorate; social insurance by Sociálna poisťovňa, health insurance by the health insurers. Euro (EUR). Corporate income tax at 10%, 21% or 24% depending on taxable revenue; individual income tax in bands from 19%; VAT under EU rules. The law is the Income Tax Act (595/2003 Z. z.), the Tax Code (563/2009 Z. z.) and the VAT Act (222/2004 Z. z.).
