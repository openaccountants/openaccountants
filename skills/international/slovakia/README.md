# Slovakia: tax pack

Calendar tax year. Authority: the Financial Administration (Financna sprava SR); returns are filed electronically through eDane. Euro. VAT (DPH) is 23% standard with reduced rates of 19% and 5%; a business registers once its calendar-year turnover passes EUR 50,000, or at once on passing EUR 62,500.
