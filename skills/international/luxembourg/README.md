# Luxembourg: tax pack

Calendar tax year. Direct taxes (income tax, corporate income tax, municipal business tax, net wealth tax, withholding taxes) are run by the Administration des contributions directes (ACD); VAT by the Administration de l'enregistrement, des domaines et de la TVA (AED); social security by the CCSS. Euro. Companies pay corporate income tax (IRC) at 14% / 16% from 2025, a 7% employment fund contribution on top, municipal business tax (6.75% in Luxembourg City) and an annual net wealth tax with a minimum set by balance sheet size. EU rules apply to VAT and to the parent-subsidiary and anti-avoidance directives.
