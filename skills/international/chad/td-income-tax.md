# Chad Personal Income Tax

## Personal income tax (IRPP) rates and bands

- **IRPP band — XAF 0 to 800,000** — 0% %  _([Code Général des Impôts (Chad) — IRPP scale](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/chad/individual/taxes-on-personal-income))_
- **Tax-free threshold** — XAF 800,000 of annual work income XAF  _([Code Général des Impôts (Chad) — IRPP scale](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/chad/individual/taxes-on-personal-income))_
- **IRPP band — XAF 800,001 to 6,000,000** — 10.5% %  _([Code Général des Impôts (Chad) — IRPP scale](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/chad/individual/taxes-on-personal-income))_
- **IRPP band — XAF 6,000,001 to 7,500,000** — 15% %  _([Code Général des Impôts (Chad) — IRPP scale](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/chad/individual/taxes-on-personal-income))_
- **IRPP band — XAF 7,500,001 to 9,000,000** — 20% %  _([Code Général des Impôts (Chad) — IRPP scale](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/chad/individual/taxes-on-personal-income))_
- **IRPP band — XAF 9,000,001 to 12,000,000** — 25% %  _([Code Général des Impôts (Chad) — IRPP scale](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/chad/individual/taxes-on-personal-income))_
- **IRPP band — above XAF 12,000,000** — 30% %  _([Code Général des Impôts (Chad) — IRPP scale](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/chad/individual/taxes-on-personal-income))_
- **Deductible employee social-security contribution** — 3.5% of salary is deductible before applying the IRPP scale %  _([Code Général des Impôts (Chad) — IRPP income determination](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/chad/individual/taxes-on-personal-income))_
- **Other non-taxable allowances** — Transport allowance and representation/expense allowances are excluded from taxable work income (approx — confirm caps per allowance)  _([Code Général des Impôts (Chad) — IRPP income determination](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/chad/individual/income-determination))_
- **Resident taxable income** — Worldwide work and capital income  _([Code Général des Impôts (Chad) — IRPP](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/chad/individual/taxes-on-personal-income))_
- **Non-resident taxable income** — Only income from activities carried on in Chad (Chad-source)  _([Code Général des Impôts (Chad) — IRPP](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/chad/individual/taxes-on-personal-income))_
- **Residence test** — Individual is resident if they have their habitual residence / home in Chad (approx — confirm 183-day / domicile criteria)  _(Code Général des Impôts (Chad) — IRPP)_
- **Annual IRPP return deadline** — On or before 30 April following the tax year (approx — confirm exact statutory date)  _(Code Général des Impôts (Chad) — Tax administration)_
- **Employment income collection** — IRPP on salaries is withheld monthly at source by the employer (retenue à la source)  _([Code Général des Impôts (Chad) — IRPP withholding on salaries](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/chad/individual/taxes-on-personal-income))_
