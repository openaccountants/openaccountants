# Mongolia Company Formation & Entity Choice

## Entity types and incorporation

- **Common entity types** — Limited liability company (LLC), joint-stock company (JSC), representative office and branch of a foreign company  _(Company Law of Mongolia — https://mongoliainc.com/setting-up-a-business-in-mongolia/establishment-of-llc/)_
- **Minimum share capital (domestic LLC)** — MNT 100,000 MNT ((approx — confirm)) MNT (approx — confirm)  _(Company Law of Mongolia — https://gsl.org/en/offers/incorporation-of-a-company-in-mongolia-formation-of-a-limited-liability-company-with-foreign-investment/)_
- **Minimum capital (foreign-invested LLC)** — USD 100,000 per foreign shareholder holding 25% or more USD USD  _(Investment Law of Mongolia — https://gsl.org/en/offers/incorporation-of-a-company-in-mongolia-formation-of-a-limited-liability-company-with-foreign-investment/)_
- **Registration authority** — General Authority for State Registration (GASR)  _(Law on State Registration of Legal Entities — https://www.investmongolia.gov.mn/taxation/)_
- **Step 1 — name reservation** — Reserve the company name with the state registration authority; the company must be established within 30 days of obtaining the name  _(Law on State Registration of Legal Entities — https://mongoliainc.com/setting-up-a-business-in-mongolia/establishment-of-llc/)_
- **Step 2 — capital deposit** — Open a temporary bank account at a Mongolian commercial bank and deposit the paid-in capital (USD 100,000 or MNT equivalent for a foreign-invested company)  _(Company Law of Mongolia — https://mongoliainc.com/setting-up-a-business-in-mongolia/establishment-of-llc/)_

- **Core annual compliance — CIT** — File the annual corporate income tax return by 10 February, with quarterly returns during the year  _(Corporate Income Tax Law of Mongolia — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/mongolia/corporate/tax-administration)_
