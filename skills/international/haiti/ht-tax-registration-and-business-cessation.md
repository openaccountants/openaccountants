# Haiti: taxpayer registration and cessation notifications

## Scope and commencement

Article 540 brings the replacement Code into force on **1 October 2026** and provides for ministerial implementation decisions. This Guide describes the enacted registration and selected income-tax cessation provisions. It does not establish current application processing times, a complete incorporation procedure, or retrospective treatment of earlier events. Independent source review and accountant attestation are pending. [2026 Code, articles 540](https://mef.gouv.ht/storage/app/uploads/public/6ac/fb4/9bc/6acfb49bca658143176190.pdf#page=169)

## Identify the right applicant

Articles 1–3 require a Matricule Fiscal, called the Numéro d'Immatriculation Fiscale (NIF), for adult natural persons with Haitian tax obligations, sole enterprises operating in Haiti and legal persons operating in Haiti. DGI issues it; presentation is required for tax and customs formalities. Article 4 provides for minors' obligations using the parent's or legal representative's registration with the specified birth/representation evidence. [2026 Code, articles 1–4](https://mef.gouv.ht/storage/app/uploads/public/6ac/fb4/9bc/6acfb49bca658143176190.pdf#page=7)

Under article 5, a person resident abroad who must complete a Haitian tax formality appoints a representative to obtain the registration. The representative presents a proper mandate, their national identification card and their own Matricule Fiscal. This is the article 5 registration procedure, not a conclusion that any person can perform all regulated professional or immigration acts. [2026 Code, articles 5](https://mef.gouv.ht/storage/app/uploads/public/6ac/fb4/9bc/6acfb49bca658143176190.pdf#page=7)

## Application evidence

**Individual:** article 6 requires a Haitian applicant's national identification and birth certificate/extract. For a foreign national it specifies the residence permit issued by the competent ministry or the diplomatic card. The Guide does not invent an alternative document where the applicant lacks the listed evidence; reconcile that case with DGI and article 5. [2026 Code, articles 6](https://mef.gouv.ht/storage/app/uploads/public/6ac/fb4/9bc/6acfb49bca658143176190.pdf#page=7)

**Sole enterprise:** article 7 requires the owner's personal Matricule Fiscal and the completed free application form, with the specified residence/diplomatic evidence for a foreign owner. The enterprise's Matricule Fiscal is expressly **distinct from the owner's personal registration**. [2026 Code, articles 7](https://mef.gouv.ht/storage/app/uploads/public/6ac/fb4/9bc/6acfb49bca658143176190.pdf#page=7)

**Legal person:** article 8 requires the completed free form and the following evidence, subject to its stated alternatives:

- Copy of the official or large-circulation newspaper publishing the statutes; in the absence of the official journal, the original proof of payment of its publication fee.
- Copy of the Ministry of Commerce and Industry notice authorising operation.
- Regulatory authorisation where the activity is regulated or requires prior approval.
- Copy of the statutes where publication is not legally required.
- The mandate empowering the applicant to act for the company and the applicant's personal Matricule Fiscal.
- Opening balance sheet.

Do not describe this list as documents automatically supplied by DGI. Several are evidence of separate formation or authorisation steps. [2026 Code, articles 8](https://mef.gouv.ht/storage/app/uploads/public/6ac/fb4/9bc/6acfb49bca658143176190.pdf#page=8)

Article 9 adds applicant and business particulars: representative/owner identity, personal tax identifier, creation date, business location and activity; and, for legal persons, start date, legal form, capital, object, registered office or principal Haitian establishment where the seat is abroad, and publication/statute details. Check the exact form against the applicable entity category. [2026 Code, articles 9](https://mef.gouv.ht/storage/app/uploads/public/6ac/fb4/9bc/6acfb49bca658143176190.pdf#page=8)

## Permanent number, renewable card and charges

Article 10(2) makes the NIF **unique and permanent**. Article 10(3) makes a natural person's Matricule Fiscal valid for life; article 10(4) requires sole enterprises and legal persons to renew theirs **every five years**. Renewal therefore does not mean assigning a fresh taxpayer identity. [2026 Code, articles 10](https://mef.gouv.ht/storage/app/uploads/public/6ac/fb4/9bc/6acfb49bca658143176190.pdf#page=9)

Article 11 prescribes issuance charges of **HTG 1,000 for individuals** and **HTG 2,500 for sole enterprises/legal persons**. Reprinting is subject to the corresponding charge. Business renewal at expiry attracts the HTG 2,500 charge. Diplomatic and consular missions are exempt from that issuance charge. The application form being free does not remove the issuance charge. [2026 Code, articles 11](https://mef.gouv.ht/storage/app/uploads/public/6ac/fb4/9bc/6acfb49bca658143176190.pdf#page=10)

**Illustration:** an individual needing both a new personal registration and a separate new sole-enterprise registration incurs HTG 1,000 + HTG 2,500 = **HTG 3,500 in these issuance charges**, assuming no exemption. If the personal card already exists and no reprint is required, the new enterprise charge alone is HTG 2,500. These are not total formation costs or professional fees. [2026 Code, articles 7](https://mef.gouv.ht/storage/app/uploads/public/6ac/fb4/9bc/6acfb49bca658143176190.pdf#page=7) [2026 Code, articles 11](https://mef.gouv.ht/storage/app/uploads/public/6ac/fb4/9bc/6acfb49bca658143176190.pdf#page=10)

Maintain the card, permanent NIF, official receipts, supporting documents and renewal date. No general statutory deadline for updating taxpayer particulars has been verified from these articles; do not borrow a social-insurance notification deadline and call it a DGI rule.

## Selected cessation and transfer rules

Articles 128–130 sit within individual income tax. For an industrial, commercial, artisanal or mining business assessed on actual profit, article 128 immediately assesses previously untaxed business profits on total or partial transfer/cessation. The transferor has **30 days** to provide the event date, purchaser details where applicable, net gains with supporting evidence, financial statements, tax return and tax amount. A transformation without a change of object/activity and with assets transferred at book value is not assimilated to a business transfer under article 128(2). This is not a general corporate-reorganisation exemption. [2026 Code, articles 128](https://mef.gouv.ht/storage/app/uploads/public/6ac/fb4/9bc/6acfb49bca658143176190.pdf#page=44)

Article 129 supplies distinct triggers: publication in two large-circulation newspapers for transfer of a business fund; effective takeover of management for transfer of a business; or permanent closure for cessation. On the individual operator's death, article 130 gives successors **six months from death** to supply the required cessation/transfer information. Do not replace those triggers with the contract-signing date in every case. [2026 Code, articles 129–130](https://mef.gouv.ht/storage/app/uploads/public/6ac/fb4/9bc/6acfb49bca658143176190.pdf#page=45)

For a noncommercial profession, article 131 requires notice **within 30 days**, stating the effective cessation date and successor details where applicable. Within the same period, provide a special professional-income declaration including earned but uncollected receivables and the resulting tax. That tax is creditable against the definitive return's liability. These provisions do not establish automatic company deregistration, cancellation of all taxes or a complete corporate-liquidation procedure. [2026 Code, articles 131](https://mef.gouv.ht/storage/app/uploads/public/6ac/fb4/9bc/6acfb49bca658143176190.pdf#page=45)

## Computing day-based deadlines and checks

Article 298 defines “days” as *jours francs*: the period begins the day after the triggering date and expires at midnight on the last day; a Saturday, Sunday or public-holiday expiry moves to the next working day. Use the actual trigger, the full statutory rule and the relevant holiday calendar. This Guide supplies no precomputed deadline without those facts and does not turn fixed-date or month-based provisions into day periods. [2026 Code, articles 298](https://mef.gouv.ht/storage/app/uploads/public/6ac/fb4/9bc/6acfb49bca658143176190.pdf#page=91)

Before filing, confirm applicant category, personal versus business identifiers, permissions, representation, evidence, issuance/renewal status and exact event date. Current forms, amendment notices, transition and corporate closure require further work. Patente, sector licences, social-insurance registration and tax returns remain separate obligations.
