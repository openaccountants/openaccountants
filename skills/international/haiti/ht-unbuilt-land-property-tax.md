# Haiti: unbuilt-land property tax and area minimums

## Scope and effective date

The replacement Code starts on **1 October 2026** under article 540, with implementation arrangements to be established by ministerial decisions. This Guide describes the enacted Contribution Foncière des Propriétés Non Bâties (CFPNB), not a retrospective assessment for earlier years. Current municipal procedures and implementation instruments remain to be confirmed. [Code, article 540](https://mef.gouv.ht/storage/app/uploads/public/6ac/fb4/9bc/6acfb49bca658143176190.pdf#page=169).

Articles 278–279 impose a communal property tax based on the market value of unbuilt land, with the statutory property definition cross-referencing article 55(3). Obtain title, parcel description, area, location, actual land use and the authority's classification. A vacant building is not automatically unbuilt land: article 269 separately includes unoccupied built property within built-property taxation. [Code, articles 278–279](https://mef.gouv.ht/storage/app/uploads/public/6ac/fb4/9bc/6acfb49bca658143176190.pdf#page=85), [article 269](https://mef.gouv.ht/storage/app/uploads/public/6ac/fb4/9bc/6acfb49bca658143176190.pdf#page=83).

## Check exemptions before computing

Article 280(2) exempts **agricultural land**. Document that classification; a rural location by itself is not the same as agricultural use. Article 280(1) also lists land belonging to the State and communes; recognised religious bodies with the stated non-income-producing condition; specified religious, scientific, social or philanthropic nonprofits; congregations, unions or cultural associations with the stated non-income-producing condition; and land owned by embassies or consulates subject to reciprocity. Apply the relevant ownership and conditions rather than assuming that a tenant's status exempts the owner. [Code, article 280](https://mef.gouv.ht/storage/app/uploads/public/6ac/fb4/9bc/6acfb49bca658143176190.pdf#page=85).

The calculations below concern taxable, nonexempt land. Do not apply the area minimum to an exempt parcel merely because its area is known.

## Valuation and enrolment

Article 281 assigns market-value assessment jointly to the tax and communal administrations. Article 282 provides for a general census at least every **five years**, with enrolment or revision also possible during the fiscal year. Inspectors issue an enrolment sheet; the owner must present it to the communal administration within **five working days** for valuation. Failure to attend or supply requested documents permits an assessment using the authorities' available data. [Code, articles 281–282](https://mef.gouv.ht/storage/app/uploads/public/6ac/fb4/9bc/6acfb49bca658143176190.pdf#page=85).

Article 282(5) uses the highest of a reference value, comparable-land value, and **20 times the land's annual rental value**. Article 282(4) allows the authorities to disregard leases affected by special circumstances, related parties, collusion or rents out of line with the market. Use the established assessment and supporting evidence; a private spreadsheet is not an official valuation. [Code, article 282](https://mef.gouv.ht/storage/app/uploads/public/6ac/fb4/9bc/6acfb49bca658143176190.pdf#page=86).

For illustration only, if the three supported values are HTG 2,500,000, HTG 2,800,000 and 20 × HTG 150,000, the highest is HTG 3,000,000. The rental input is annual, not monthly.

## Rate and minimum

Article 283 gives two percentage rates and a separate minimum based on area:

| Classification | Rule |
|---|---|
| Vacant urban land | 0.25% of assessed market value |
| Vacant rural land | 0.10% of assessed market value |
| Commune group 1 under article 21 | Minimum HTG 30 per square metre |
| Commune group 2 under article 21 | Minimum HTG 20 per square metre |
| Other communes | Minimum HTG 10 per square metre |

These are enacted rates from [article 283](https://mef.gouv.ht/storage/app/uploads/public/6ac/fb4/9bc/6acfb49bca658143176190.pdf#page=86). Establish urban/rural classification separately from the commune's group. Article 283 refers to the grouping in article 21; use that current classification, also reproduced in the Haiti business-patente Guide, rather than an older municipality list. [Code, article 21](https://mef.gouv.ht/storage/app/uploads/public/6ac/fb4/9bc/6acfb49bca658143176190.pdf#page=12).

For taxable land:

1. Obtain assessed market value **V** and parcel area **A** in square metres.
2. Select **r = 0.0025** for urban land or **0.0010** for rural land.
3. Select the area minimum **m = HTG 30, 20 or 10** from the commune group.
4. Calculate percentage tax **V × r** and area minimum **A × m**.
5. The tax is the **higher** amount. The minimum is not added to the percentage tax.

**Example where the minimum controls:** taxable urban land assessed at HTG 3,000,000, area 500 m², in group 1. Percentage tax = HTG 7,500; minimum = HTG 15,000. Tax = **HTG 15,000**, not HTG 22,500.

**Example where the percentage controls:** taxable urban land assessed at HTG 20,000,000, area 500 m², group 1. Percentage tax = HTG 50,000; minimum = HTG 15,000. Tax = **HTG 50,000**.

**Rural, nonagricultural example:** assessed value HTG 2,000,000, area 300 m², other-commune category. Percentage tax = HTG 2,000; minimum = HTG 3,000. Tax = **HTG 3,000**. The nonagricultural assumption matters because article 280 separately exempts agricultural land. [Code, articles 280 and 283](https://mef.gouv.ht/storage/app/uploads/public/6ac/fb4/9bc/6acfb49bca658143176190.pdf#page=85).

## Payment and self-checks

Article 284 requires the owner to pay for the current fiscal year **between 1 October and 31 March**. Retain the assessment, classification, area evidence, computation and official receipt. This Guide does not establish a verified electronic filing or payment channel. [Code, article 284](https://mef.gouv.ht/storage/app/uploads/public/6ac/fb4/9bc/6acfb49bca658143176190.pdf#page=86).

Confirm exemption first, use annual rental value in the 20-times comparison, keep urban/rural status distinct from commune group, and compare rather than add the two tax amounts. Mixed built/unbuilt parcels, changes of use, partial-year treatment, assessment disputes and current operational instructions require additional evidence. No general rounding convention has been established here.
