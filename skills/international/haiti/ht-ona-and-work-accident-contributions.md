# Haiti: ONA contributions and occupational-accident insurance

## Sources and scope

This Guide uses [ONA's current insurance guidance](https://ona.ht/index.php/component/sppagebuilder/page/78), its [legal compilation](https://ona.ht/pdf_docs/cadre_legal_ona.pdf), and [OFATMA's published form instructions](https://ofatma.gouv.ht/formulaires.html), accessed 22 October 2026. These social-insurance rules are sourced separately from the replacement Tax Code. Do not infer a new social-insurance commencement date merely from that Code's commencement.

The ONA compilation reproduces historical instruments rather than a clearly dated consolidated current statute. Its original article 200 still prints graduated rates, while ONA's present insurance page states the flat contributions below. The complete amending chain and current operational filing instructions have not been retrieved. Independent source review and accountant attestation are pending.

## ONA coverage and registration

The 1967 law's article 178 covers employees of commercial, industrial and agricultural enterprises and other listed employment, including private teaching and healthcare employment. Article 179 lists exclusions, including specified diplomatic cases and public employees already under civil pensions subject to the stated outside-employment condition. Establish the person's actual employment and pension category before applying the scheme. [ONA compilation, pages 2–3](https://ona.ht/pdf_docs/cadre_legal_ona.pdf#page=2).

The **18 March 1968 decree**, articles 2–6, requires employer registration and separate registration of covered workers. Notify changes in business name, address or activity, mergers, suspension/resumption and worker recruitment/dismissal **within 15 days of the event**. Use the distinct employer and worker registration numbers in documents sent to ONA. Each employer has its own obligations when a worker has several employers. [ONA compilation, page 16](https://ona.ht/pdf_docs/cadre_legal_ona.pdf#page=16).

ONA supplies an [enterprise information form](https://ona.ht/pdf_docs/formulaire_Renseignement_entreprise.pdf). Obtain the completed employer record and worker identifiers before reconciling contributions. This Guide does not certify an online submission route or an application turnaround time.

## ONA calculation and remittance

ONA's current insurance page states **6% withheld from salary and 6% contributed by the employer**, giving **12% monthly** to the employee's account. It describes voluntary insurance at **12% of declared salary**, paid in full by the voluntary insured person. Do not treat voluntary insurance as a substitute for compulsory employment coverage. [ONA insurance guidance](https://ona.ht/index.php/component/sppagebuilder/page/78).

Article 8 of the 1968 decree specifies **basic salary** and prohibits a monthly contribution below the amount calculated on the legal minimum wage. Determine the applicable current minimum wage and relevant employment period separately; no minimum-wage amount or proration rule is established here. The current website's generic word “salary” does not resolve every bonus, allowance or benefit-in-kind question. [ONA compilation, page 17](https://ona.ht/pdf_docs/cadre_legal_ona.pdf#page=17).

Articles 9–10 require monthly withholding and payment of both shares in the **first ten working days of each month**, accompanied by a signed contribution statement in two copies using ONA's model. The quoted article does not itself spell out the preceding-month linkage; confirm the applicable statement period and current submission procedure. Article 12 invalidates an agreement making the employee bear both shares or more than the employer share. [ONA compilation, page 17](https://ona.ht/pdf_docs/cadre_legal_ona.pdf#page=17).

For nonfixed wages, the reproduced 1967 article 213 uses the average monthly wages of the three months preceding declaration. Keep this distinct from guessing a current month cash-receipts base. Articles 211–212 require payroll and accounting records available for inspection. [ONA compilation, pages 6–7](https://ona.ht/pdf_docs/cadre_legal_ona.pdf#page=6).

**Example:** assume a confirmed contributory base of HTG 50,000, above the applicable legal minimum, with the agency's 6% rates applicable. Employee withholding is HTG 3,000; employer cost is HTG 3,000; the remittance is HTG 6,000. Salary remaining after this deduction alone is HTG 47,000, not final take-home pay. Retain separate tax and other-insurance calculations. A voluntary insured person with an accepted HTG 50,000 declared base pays HTG 6,000 themselves. [ONA insurance guidance](https://ona.ht/index.php/component/sppagebuilder/page/78).

## OFATMA occupational accidents: separate employer cost

OFATMA's instructions quote accident contributions of **2% for commercial enterprises**, **3% for agricultural/industrial businesses, construction sites, navigation agencies and export-production businesses**, and **6% for mining**. The stated base is total wages; contributions are exclusively employer-borne and provisionally paid in advance. Food or accommodation increases the stated salary base by **25%**, both by **50%**. Payroll and insured-worker records must be retained for **at least five years**. [OFATMA form instructions, articles 32, 134–135](https://ofatma.gouv.ht/formulaires.html).

On an established HTG 100,000 base, the respective accident contributions are HTG 2,000, 3,000 or 6,000. Confirm the enterprise classification and any benefit uplift first. This is accident insurance only: the source does not establish a complete health/maternity contribution schedule or a universal monthly due date. Do not deduct these employer accident contributions from salary or add rates together without checking each base. [OFATMA form instructions](https://ofatma.gouv.ht/formulaires.html).

## Self-checks and outstanding evidence

Reconcile employee deductions, employer expenses, agency payments and individual insured accounts separately. Confirm employment category, minimum wage, basic-salary elements, nonfixed-pay treatment and employer accident classification. Obtain the complete current amendment history and agency instructions for borderline cases, foreign temporary workers and current payment periods. Health/maternity, public-sector schemes, arrears, pension eligibility and benefits remain outside this Guide.
