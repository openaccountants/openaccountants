# Croatia: tax pack

Calendar tax year. Authority: the Tax Administration (Porezna uprava) of the Ministry of Finance; returns go through ePorezna. Euro since 1 January 2023. Personal income tax has two rates set by each local unit within ranges in the law (default 20% and 30%, band EUR 60,000 a year); capital income is taxed at 12%. Companies pay profit tax at 10% (revenue up to EUR 1,000,000) or 18%. Withholding on payments to non-residents is in Profit Tax Act article 31, with a 25% rate for EU-listed non-cooperative jurisdictions. VAT follows the EU directive.
