# Croatia: tax pack

Calendar tax year unless a company is allowed a different business year. Authority: the Tax Administration (Porezna uprava) of the Ministry of Finance. Euro since 1 January 2023. Profit tax is 10% where revenue in the period is up to EUR 1,000,000 and 18% above it (Profit Tax Act art. 28). VAT follows the EU directive. Laws are published in Narodne novine; the Tax Administration keeps consolidated texts in its register of regulations.
