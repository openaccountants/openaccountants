# Guinea Corporate Income Tax

## Corporate income tax (Impôt sur les Sociétés)

Guinea taxes company profits under the Impôt sur les Sociétés (IS), with the base derived from OHADA/SYSCOHADA financial statements. A minimum tax applies where the IS computed on profit is low. Figures are a source-cited draft for accountant review.

- **Standard corporate income tax rate** — 25% percent  _([Code Général des Impôts (CGI) — Impôt sur les Sociétés (IS); Loi de Finances 2018](https://www.icaew.com/technical/by-country/africa/guinea/tax-in-guinea))_
- **Corporate income tax rate — mining companies** — 30% percent (approx — confirm)  _([Code Minier de la République de Guinée (Mining Code)](https://www.imf.org/-/media/Files/Publications/CR/2021/English/1GINEA2021002.ashx))_
- **Minimum tax (Impôt Minimum Forfaitaire)** — 3% of annual turnover, payable where it exceeds the IS computed on profits percent (approx — confirm)  _([Code Général des Impôts (CGI) — Impôt Minimum Forfaitaire (IMF)](https://taxatlas.io/country/guinea))_
- **Tax base** — Net accounting profit per OHADA/SYSCOHADA accounts, adjusted for non-deductible items and tax rules  _([OHADA Uniform Act on Accounting Law (SYSCOHADA); Code Général des Impôts (CGI)](https://www.icaew.com/technical/by-country/africa/guinea/tax-in-guinea))_
- **Withholding tax on dividends** — 10% percent (before treaty relief)  _([Code Général des Impôts (CGI) — retenue à la source](https://www.icaew.com/technical/by-country/africa/guinea/tax-in-guinea))_
- **Withholding tax on interest** — 10% percent (before treaty relief)  _([Code Général des Impôts (CGI) — retenue à la source](https://www.icaew.com/technical/by-country/africa/guinea/tax-in-guinea))_
- **Withholding tax on royalties (to non-residents)** — 15% percent (before treaty relief)  _([Code Général des Impôts (CGI) — retenue à la source](https://www.icaew.com/technical/by-country/africa/guinea/tax-in-guinea))_
- **Capital gains** — Generally taxed as ordinary corporate income; standalone rate around 15% cited for certain gains percent (approx — confirm)  _([Code Général des Impôts (CGI) de Guinée](https://taxatlas.io/country/guinea))_
- **Annual IS return filing deadline** — By 30 April of the year following the financial year (annual declaration) (approx — confirm)  _(Code Général des Impôts (CGI) de Guinée)_
- **Payment of corporate tax** — Advance installments during the year with balance on filing the annual return (approx — confirm)  _(Code Général des Impôts (CGI) de Guinée)_
