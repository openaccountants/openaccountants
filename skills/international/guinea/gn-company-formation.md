# Guinea Company Formation & Entity Choice

## Entity types and incorporation

Company law in Guinea follows the OHADA Uniform Act on Commercial Companies, with incorporation handled through the one-stop shop at the investment-promotion agency (APIP). The SARL is the most common form for private business. Figures are a source-cited draft for accountant review.

- **Limited liability company** — Société à Responsabilité Limitée (SARL) — most common private entity  _([OHADA Uniform Act on Commercial Companies and Economic Interest Groups](https://c2zadvisory.com/company-incorporation-in-guinea/))_
- **Public limited company** — Société Anonyme (SA) — used for larger operations  _([OHADA Uniform Act on Commercial Companies and Economic Interest Groups](https://c2zadvisory.com/company-incorporation-in-guinea/))_
