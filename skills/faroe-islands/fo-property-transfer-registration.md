# Faroe Islands property-transfer and mortgage registration v0.2

> **General reference only.** This Guide is general tax/accounting reference material for AI-assisted workflows. It has not been reviewed for any specific person's facts, documents, elections, deadlines, residency, filing status, or local procedures. Do not rely on it to file, pay, amend, or take a tax position without review by a qualified professional in the relevant jurisdiction.

## Who this applies to

Use this Guide when real property changes owner by sale, gift or another
transfer, or a mortgage is registered or increased. Registration protects
property rights against competing agreements and enforcement; an unregistered
sale agreement alone does not supply that protection. A deed means an
instrument transferring a specified property or part of it, including a
transfer conditional on payment. [Duty Act §1][duty]; [Registration Act §§1, 6][registration]

Separate **tinglýsingaravgjald**, the percentage duty payable to the Treasury,
from **tinglýsingargjald**, the fixed registry service fee. One transaction
can incur both, and a separate deed and mortgage incur separate document
fees. The applicable Acts are respectively Act 8/2006 and Act 76/1994;
the agency overview interchanges their attribution. Use the operative
Acts below. [Duty Act §§1, 3a][duty]; [Fee Act §§1–2][fee]

This is the property paper-instrument method. Act 128/2024 permits separate
commencement dates for the land, vehicle and personal registers. Regulation
60/2025 commences its provisions for the **vehicle register** on 20 May 2025,
with specified enabling provisions on 29 April. That is not proof that the
land register has moved to mandatory digital filing or invoice settlement.
Do not apply the vehicle rule to a property deed. [Amending Act §§3–5][digital];
[commencement regulation §1][commence]

The [personal-tax](fo-individual-income-tax.md),
[company-tax](fo-corporate-income-tax.md) and
[inheritance-duty](fo-inheritance-duty.md) Guides retain their distinct
income, gain and succession jobs. Vehicle retention of title, floating
business security, cadastral division and a complete survey of property
income taxes are not supplied here.

| Property gift | 1% of the property's assessed value | [Duty Act §§1(2), 3](https://logir.fo/Logtingslog/8-fra-28-02-2006-um-tinglysingaravgjald-sum-broytt-vid-logtingslog-nr-50-fra-2-juni) |
| Equal-value property exchange | No transfer duty if properties balance exactly | [Duty Act §2](https://logir.fo/Logtingslog/8-fra-28-02-2006-um-tinglysingaravgjald-sum-broytt-vid-logtingslog-nr-50-fra-2-juni) |
| Exchange with cash equalisation | 1% of the balancing cash amount | [Duty Act §§1(2), 2](https://logir.fo/Logtingslog/8-fra-28-02-2006-um-tinglysingaravgjald-sum-broytt-vid-logtingslog-nr-50-fra-2-juni) |
| Inheritance division | No transfer duty on the part for which inheritance duty is paid; do not assume every inherited share qualifies | [Duty Act §1](https://logir.fo/Logtingslog/8-fra-28-02-2006-um-tinglysingaravgjald-sum-broytt-vid-logtingslog-nr-50-fra-2-juni) |
| Mortgage, other than a judicial lien | 1% of secured amount, subject to the specific rules below | [Duty Act §3a](https://logir.fo/Logtingslog/8-fra-28-02-2006-um-tinglysingaravgjald-sum-broytt-vid-logtingslog-nr-50-fra-2-juni) |
| Registry service | DKK 1,000 for each document submitted, subject to exemptions and timely resubmission | [Fee Act §§1–4](https://logir.fo/Logtingslog/76-fra-17-05-1994-um-tinglysingargjold-vm-sum-broytt-vid-logtingslog-nr-63-fra-10) |
| Certificate, transcript, extract, photocopy or similar made by the registry | DKK 50 | [Fee Act §6](https://logir.fo/Logtingslog/76-fra-17-05-1994-um-tinglysingargjold-vm-sum-broytt-vid-logtingslog-nr-63-fra-10) |

The percentage entries are duties, not substitutes for the document fee.
A duty exemption does not itself waive the DKK 1,000 fee. The inheritance
exception is expressly tied to the portion on which inheritance duty is
paid; no conclusion is supplied for an inheritance-duty-exempt share.
[Duty Act §§1–3a][duty]; [Fee Act §§1–4, 6][fee]

Fee-free registrations under §4(1) are address changes; majority/guardianship
notices under Registration Act §48; public notices under §13; a request for
separation of property under Marriage Effects Act §43; loss of disposal
power under Probate Act §66; and loss of disposal power under Bankruptcy
Act §26. Fee-free requests under §4(2) are cancellation, removal of a legal
remark, extension or final entry of an instrument registered with a deadline,
and cancellation of rights not satisfied by a forced sale under Procedure
Act §642. These are the specified **service-fee** exceptions; they do not
create a general tax exemption for an underlying transfer. [Fee Act §4][fee]


## The base

**Purchase, gift and exchange.** Retain the deed, consideration schedule,
gift valuation or exchange valuations and balancing-payment evidence. Apply
the appropriate row above. Sections 1–3 do not supply a worked statutory
allocation for a transfer combining a gift, cash and assumed debt. Do not
silently substitute purchase price, net equity or market value for that
unresolved mixed-transfer base. [Duty Act §§1–3][duty]

**Mortgage.** The secured amount is the nominal principal. If the instrument
allows escalation, use the highest principal it can reach. If that maximum
is not stated directly, the taxpayer must state in the instrument a
best-belief maximum over the ten years from registration. Umhvørvisstovan
fixes the amount if the taxpayer does not estimate it or doubt remains.
Do not replace this with the property's value or the cash initially advanced.
[Duty Act §3a(1)][duty]

**Replacing a loan.** Section 3a(2) excludes the part of a qualifying new
loan used to replace a loan with corresponding security in the **same
property**. This applies to real-credit institution loans or bank loans
with an original repayment term of at least ten and at most thirty years.
It excludes owner's mortgages (*ognarveðbræv*) and indemnity bonds
(*skaðaloysisbræv*). Record the lender, original term, outstanding loan
being discharged, same-property security and exact replacement amount.
Relief can apply to partial replacement or one or more new instruments.
[Duty Act §3a(2)][duty]

Apply all the statutory conditions before subtracting the replacement amount:

1. Submit the new security **before** cancelling the old security.
2. Explicitly state and certify that the new submission is conditional on
   cancellation of the old security; cancel it within one year of the new
   submission.
3. If several new instruments replace the old one, keep their security on
   the same property for at least one year after registration of the new
   security.
4. Relief lapses if the new instrument is rejected and will not later be
   registered, or is rejected as not effected within one year of submission.

These conditions concern duty relief. Each chargeable new document still
has its own DKK 1,000 service fee. The Act authorises more detailed evidence
rules; no verified separate current refinancing certificate or complete
implementing-form route was located in this pass. Preserve the evidence
and do not invent a certificate number. [Duty Act §3a(2), (5)][duty]; [Fee Act §§1–2][fee]

**Changes to security.** Adding another property or properties to an existing
mortgage attracts full duty on the secured amount for each such registration.
For a registered increase in secured amount, duty is on the difference
between the outstanding debt and the new secured amount. An old instrument's
original face value is not the outstanding-debt figure. Obtain the current
loan balance and classify an increase separately from adding property.
[Duty Act §3a(3)–(4)][duty]

## Deadlines and filing

**Prepare the instrument.** Use the current title record and the signed
transaction. A private instrument must come from the person authorised by
the register to dispose of the right, or with that person's consent.
An instrument can be time limited or conditional but must have definitive
terms. A deed registered as title can be conditional only on purchase-price
payment within a stated deadline; later registration of the seller's payment
confirmation makes it final. [Registration Act §10][registration]

The following are statutory instrument requirements, **not invented portal
boxes or a reproduced approved blank form**:

| Document or step | Required content or attachment | Authority |
|---|---|---|
| Document and copy, top of first page | Cadastral number; submitter's name, address and telephone. The Act's rejection provision also requires submitter p-tal/v-tal. Deed and mortgage: legal property address. Mortgage: mortgagor and mortgagee addresses, unless the statutory public-address exception applies, or an authorised recipient's address | Registration Act §§9(3)–(4), 14(2) |
| Party identification | Natural-person owner on deed and mortgagor on mortgage: p-tal. Legal-person owner on deed: v-tal. Section 14(2) also requires mortgagor/mortgagee p-tal/v-tal and address, subject to its public-knowledge/representative wording | Registration Act §§9(3), 14(2) |
| Mortgage form | Approved form; particular terms on pages 1–2, fixed text on pages 3–4. Put debt first, then interest/payment/termination terms for an ordinary mortgage, collateral, priority, earlier encumbrances, easements and burdens. Immediately above signature acknowledge the fixed terms. Any additions, deletions or corrections to fixed text on pages 3–4 must also appear in the particular terms on pages 1–2 | Registration Act §50f; Regulation 63/1962 §3(1)–(3) |
| Mortgage endorsement | Continue page numbering and state page count immediately before each signature; use continuation sheets for a preprinted form | Regulation §3(4) |
| Signature | Issuer signs. For a deed or mortgage, a lawyer or two other attesting witnesses certify genuine signature, correct date and legal capacity, giving occupation and address. Public-institution instruments do not require this witness certification | Registration Act §10(2); Regulation §11(1)(3) |
| Copies and authority | Submit instrument and required copy on approved copy paper, legible and faithful to the text. For a typed copy use the first typed or first carbon copy, with a title instrument requiring the first typed copy. With a mortgage attach the latest registered deed, or a written explanation of unavailability and an official supporting document under §9(2). Present necessary powers, corporate/probate authority and other identity evidence; these supporting documents are not themselves independently registered merely as proof | Registration Act §9(1)–(2); Regulation §§4, 6, 11 |
| Deed also creating another right | Expressly request each right. For registration of a deed as creating an easement or mortgage as well, attach the additional extract-copy required by Regulation §5(3), identifying the relevant terms/priority | Registration Act §9(6); Regulation §5(3) |
| New subdivision or condominium | Complete the separate cadastral/registry preconditions. Condominium first title requires the division submission and certified schedule/maps under Regulation §10a; a deed after subdivision requires the certificates under §17 | Regulation §§10a, 17 |

Approved mortgage forms are mandatory; the law permits institution-specific
approved forms and says ordinary approved forms must be obtainable from
the registry. The live official forms index did **not** expose an ordinary
property-mortgage or deed blank on 31 October 2026. Obtain a valid approved
instrument through the lawful preparation route before filing. The table
cannot serve as a substitute blank mortgage form. This remains an ordinary
operational evidence gap. [Regulation §3(1), (6)–(8)][regulation];
[official forms index][forms]

**Screen acquisition permission and declaration** using the next section.
Then have the taxable person calculate and pay the duty to Umhvørvisstovan
before registration. For an ownership change this is the person whose right
is registered; for a mortgage it is the mortgagor. The fixed fee falls due
at submission. The agency instructs submission of the documents with money
or a financial-institution receipt. Keep the calculation, payment proof,
submitted instrument and every attachment together. [Duty Act §4][duty];
[Fee Act §5][fee]; [agency filing instructions][registry]

**Submit by 12:00 on a working day** for that day's journal entry. A later
arrival is entered the next working day. The office must sign a receipt
prepared by the submitter on request. Registration is effective on entry
in the land register, with priority normally dating from the journal
submission day; instruments submitted the same day rank equally. Keep the
receipt and reconcile the returned entry, journal date/number and any
remark or provisional deadline. [Regulation §2][regulation];
[Registration Act §§16(2), 25][registration]

The Act requires examination and final/provisional entry or rejection as
soon as possible and within ten days of journal submission. A provisional
entry for missing proof is removed if the registry's correction deadline
expires without remedy; extensions must be requested and granted. This
ten-day processing rule is not a grace period to defer filing or payment.
[Registration Act §§15(3), 16(3)–(4)][registration]

For cancellation of a mortgage supply the discharged original instrument
or proof that the right ended by court order, subject to the specifically
named institutional receipt exceptions in §11(2). For cancellation under a
power, include the receipt and power copies required by Regulation §6(2).
A cancellation request is service-fee-free. [Registration Act §11(2)][registration];
[Regulation §6(2)][regulation]; [Fee Act §4(2)][fee]

## Penalties and interest

Nonpayment of duty causes rejection from the journal. Duty can be collected
by distraint and collection costs are borne by the taxpayer. Nonpayment of
the fixed fee at submission also causes rejection; that fee obligation then
lapses. Do not treat rejection as a completed registration or preserved
priority. No fixed late-interest percentage is supplied by the cited duty
and fee provisions. [Duty Act §§5, 5a][duty]; [Fee Act §5][fee]

If a document is rejected for another reason and resubmitted within three
months **from first submission**, no new fixed fee is payable. That
concession does not cover a first rejection for nonpayment under §5(3).
The regulation requires refund of percentage duty paid for an instrument
rejected from the journal, land register or personal register. Keep the
rejection and refund reconciliation; do not confuse the duty refund with
the different fixed-fee resubmission rule. [Fee Act §3][fee];
[Regulation §37(2)][regulation]

A duty decision can be appealed in writing, with reasons, to Føroya
Kærustovn within four weeks after notification. A registration decision
under the Registration Act has the same four-week recipient route.
The current Duty Act §5c supersedes the agency overview's older ministry
route. [Duty Act §5c][duty]; [Registration Act §36][registration]

For the **fixed service fee**, Regulation 161/2021 §6 and Annex 4.8 expressly
place Fee Act appeals within the land-matters jurisdiction transferred to
Føroya Kærustovn. Apply Act 147/2021's ordinary four-week notification clock
unless another rule provides otherwise; the authority can admit a late
appeal in special circumstances. Send the appeal in writing with reasons
and supporting documents where possible. An appeal does not automatically
suspend the decision; a stay needs another legal basis or the authority's
special-circumstances decision. Unless other legislation provides otherwise,
its decision can be brought before Føroya Rætt within six months of
notification. [Appeals Act §§2, 10, 15–16][appeal-act];
[Appeal Regulation §6, Annex 4.8][appeal-reg]

## Cross-border points

Permission to acquire and liability to registration duty are different
questions. An individual with permanent residence in the Faroes or at least
five years' aggregate previous residence there can buy without permission.
The same is true for a Danish citizen with five years' aggregate residence
in another part of the realm. A legal person headquartered in the Faroes
can buy without permission. Other individuals/legal persons need ministerial
permission unless a statutory exception applies; foreign public authorities
need permission. [Acquisition Act §§1–2][acquisition]

Section 3 exempts acquisition by inheritance, remaining in an undivided
estate, division of community property and gifts to direct-line ascendants
or descendants from the permission requirement. This does not erase any
separate duty. Permission applications must be lodged no later than six
months after the final transfer agreement, unless someone else has already
taken over the property. A person needing permission also needs it to take
property as a usufructuary pledge for more than six months. [Acquisition Act §§3–5][acquisition]

At registration, the individual certifies the applicable residence/history
or §3 exception on the title instrument, or attaches permission. A legal
person certifies Faroese headquarters or the §3 exception, or attaches
permission. Read §1 together with §7; §7's declaration wording alone is not
an exemption for every non-Danish former realm resident. If no declaration
or permission accompanies the instrument, it is rejected unless the acquirer
certifies that a §4 application has been lodged. In that case the registry
sets a deadline for the permission; absence by that deadline causes removal.
[Acquisition Act §7][acquisition]

When the declaration route is used, put the relevant individual or company/legal-person declaration **before the signatures**, as the registry's published headings require. This placement does not replace attached permission or the statutory pending-application alternative. [Official registry instructions][registry]

Missing the permission-application deadline is punishable by a fine. Refusal,
lapse or failure to apply can lead to an order to sell within a period of
six months to one year. Failure to obey can attract a fine and court-ordered
weekly coercive fines; the court fixes their amount. Permission conditions,
sanctions and declarations require the actual acquisition facts before a
declaration is signed. [Acquisition Act §§6, 9, 12][acquisition]

## Two worked examples that recompute

**1. Ordinary purchase with one new mortgage.** A buyer pays DKK 3,000,000
for a whole property and registers a separate DKK 2,400,000 ordinary mortgage
without escalation or replacement relief. Neither document qualifies for a
fixed-fee exemption. Transfer duty is DKK 30,000, mortgage duty DKK 24,000,
and two document fees DKK 2,000: total DKK 56,000. Legal, valuation and lender
charges are not inputs to this statutory-charge example. [Duty Act §§1, 3a(1)][duty];
[Fee Act §§1–2][fee]

```oa-check
name: Purchase and separate mortgage, no relief (2026)
inputs:
  purchase_price: 3000000
  mortgage_principal: 2400000
  duty_rate: 0.01
  documents: 2
  document_fee: 1000
steps:
  transfer_duty: purchase_price * duty_rate
  mortgage_duty: mortgage_principal * duty_rate
  service_fees: documents * document_fee
  total: transfer_duty + mortgage_duty + service_fees
expected:
  transfer_duty: 30000
  mortgage_duty: 24000
  service_fees: 2000
  total: 56000
```

**2. Refinancing decision before cancellation.** One ordinary bank mortgage
has an original twenty-year term, DKK 1,200,000 of new security and
DKK 1,000,000 replacing the outstanding loan with corresponding security on
the same property. All §3a(2) conditions listed above are evidenced, including
new submission before old cancellation and cancellation within one year.
The taxable secured amount is DKK 200,000; duty DKK 2,000 plus one DKK 1,000
fee gives DKK 3,000. Cancellation has no fixed fee. If instead the instrument
is an owner's mortgage or indemnity bond, the specific replacement relief
does not apply: DKK 12,000 duty plus DKK 1,000 fee gives DKK 13,000. A failure
to prove the conditions cannot be replaced by assuming relief. [Duty Act §3a(2)][duty];
[Fee Act §§1–2, 4(2)][fee]

```oa-check
name: Replacement-loan qualification changes the duty base (2026)
inputs:
  new_security: 1200000
  qualifying_replacement: 1000000
  duty_rate: 0.01
  document_fee: 1000
steps:
  qualifying_base: new_security - qualifying_replacement
  qualifying_duty: qualifying_base * duty_rate
  qualifying_total: qualifying_duty + document_fee
  excluded_instrument_duty: new_security * duty_rate
  excluded_instrument_total: excluded_instrument_duty + document_fee
expected:
  qualifying_base: 200000
  qualifying_duty: 2000
  qualifying_total: 3000
  excluded_instrument_duty: 12000
  excluded_instrument_total: 13000
```

## Ask the client first

1. Is this a sale, gift, exchange, inheritance division or mixed transfer;
   what are the consideration, valuation, debt assumed and relevant duty receipts?
2. Which cadastral property and rights are transferred or secured, who has
   registered authority, and is subdivision or condominium entry required?
3. What instrument type, principal, escalation ceiling and outstanding debt
   apply; is this an increase, replacement or addition of property security?
4. For replacement relief, what lender/original term, same-property evidence,
   submission order and cancellation dates prove every §3a(2) condition?
5. What residence/citizenship/headquarters or family-transfer facts support
   the acquisition declaration or permission, and has an application been lodged?
6. Which approved signed instruments, witnesses, authority documents, copies,
   payment proof and existing registry deadlines are available?
