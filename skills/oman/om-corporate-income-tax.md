# Oman Corporate Income Tax v1.2

> **General reference only.** This Guide is a draft for AI-assisted reference. It has not received independent closeout review or accountant attestation. Do not use it to file, pay or take a position without professional review.

## Who this applies to

Establishments, Omani companies and permanent establishments of foreign persons (arts 2, 112). A permanent establishment is a fixed place of business in Oman, including a branch, office, factory or dependent agent (art. 2). All commercial register holders must register for income tax, whatever the grade, even with losses (https://tms.taxoman.gov.om/portal/web/taxportal/income-tax-faqs, question 1). The Top-up Tax on large groups is in the [overview](om-tax-overview.md).

## The base

Accounts follow the accrual basis and international accounting standards (art. 12). Small enterprises prepare the income statement on a cash basis unless the Authority approves another, subject to arts 13 and 46 (art. 159bis 10); art. 159bis 9 requires supporting books and records. Exempt: dividends from Omani companies and gains on securities listed in Muscat (art. 115). Shipping income of an Omani owner is exempt (art. 116). Losses carry forward for up to five years from the end of the loss year (art. 71); exempt activities cannot carry a loss (art. 72). Depreciation pools: 33 1/3% for machinery, computers, vehicles, fixtures and software; 10% for rigs; 15% for other plant (art. 90). Related-party transactions can be adjusted (art. 125). First accounting period: up to 18 months (art. 50). A register opened from January to June closes that 31 December; from July to December it closes the next 31 December (https://tms.taxoman.gov.om/portal/web/taxportal/income-tax-faqs, question 5).

How to file: sign in to the Tax Authority portal, open income tax services, choose service No. 6 (final returns), complete the return, attach the accounts or the statement of income, submit, then pay through Claims and Payments. Amend registration details with service No. 9. The principal officer gives others access through Representation Rights (https://tms.taxoman.gov.om/portal/web/taxportal/income-tax-faqs, questions 2, 6, 7, 8). The box numbers of the return are not in any page we opened.

## Penalties and interest

Art. 156 additional tax of 1% a month, which the Chairman may relieve. Fines as in the table. Deliberate failure to file by the principal officer: 1 to 6 months in prison and a fine of OMR 500 to 20,000, or either (art. 184). Collection normally lapses after seven years from due payment; statutory notices and legal claims interrupt it, and a final judgment for the Authority gives a new fifteen-year period (art. 157).

## Cross-border points

Withholding covers royalties, research and development, software, and management or service fees paid to a non-resident without an Oman permanent establishment, or to one with a permanent establishment where that payment is not included in its gross income (arts 40, 52; https://tms.taxoman.gov.om/portal/web/taxportal/income-tax-faqs, questions 11, 12, 14). Not subject: conferences and exhibitions, transport, shipping and insurance of goods, training, air tickets and hotels abroad, board meetings, reinsurance, and services tied to activity or property outside Oman (https://tms.taxoman.gov.om/portal/web/taxportal/income-tax-faqs, question 15). Foreign tax credit: apply within two years of the year end (art. 124). Treaties: art. 120 and following. The Tax Authority rate page names interest and the law names dividends in the withholding list, so see the Not yet confirmed line before withholding on either.

## Two worked examples that recompute

**1. Company with taxable income OMR 100,000.** Tax at 15% is OMR 15,000; a late payment of three months adds OMR 450.

```oa-check
name: Standard company with three months late
inputs:
  income: 100000
  months: 3
steps:
  - tax: income * 0.15
  - additional: tax * 0.01 * months
expected:
  tax: 15000
  additional: 450
```

**2. Decision: does a small establishment with profit OMR 40,000 fall at 3%, or 15% once it fails a condition?** If the standard regime applies for the comparison year and no exemption or continuation rule applies, the difference is OMR 4,800. The switch is not automatic on every threshold crossing: arts 159bis 4 and 5 govern the following-year start and permitted excesses.

```oa-check
name: Small enterprise against standard rate
inputs:
  profit: 40000
steps:
  - small: profit * 0.03
  - standard: profit * 0.15
  - cost: standard - small
expected:
  small: 1200
  standard: 6000
  cost: 4800
```

## Ask the client first

- Legal form, and is the owner Omani or foreign?
- Registered capital, gross income, worker count, and activity?
- Does the owner run it full time, and how many Omanis work there?
- Year end, and first period dates?
- Payments to non-residents, and their type?
- Any petroleum, industrial or shipping activity?
