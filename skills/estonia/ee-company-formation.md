# Estonia Company Formation & Entity Choice

## Scope

This skill covers choosing a legal form in Estonia, registering it in 2026, and the first tax and filing duties that follow. The active registration steps use RIK and government e-Residency guidance read on 16 November 2026. Detailed statutory claims not supported by those reads are preserved only in the held archive under Sources. Do not use the archive to choose a fee, capital treatment, liability position or filing obligation. The tax computations sit in the pack's domain skills; where a domain skill and this skill ever differ, the domain skill wins.

Abbreviations: ÄS = Äriseadustik (Commercial Code); RPS = Raamatupidamise seadus (Accounting Act); RLS = Riigilõivuseadus (State Fees Act); TuMS = Tulumaksuseadus (Income Tax Act); KMS = Käibemaksuseadus (Value-Added Tax Act); SMS = Sotsiaalmaksuseadus (Social Tax Act); RIK = Registrite ja Infosüsteemide Keskus (Centre of Registers and Information Systems), which runs the e-Business Register portal.

## Ask the client first

- Which form, founders, ownership percentages and business activities are proposed?
- Can every required signatory use an Estonian ID card, e-Residency card, Smart-ID or Mobile-ID?
- What address, contact person where needed, email, board and beneficial-owner details will be filed?
- What is each founder contributing, in money or in kind, and has it actually been paid?
- Will there be employees, VAT registration, foreign management, regulated activities or a non-calendar financial year?

## The method, step by step

1. Choose the legal form using the entity distinctions below. Refer a notarial, in-kind or other non-standard formation before selecting portal declarations.
2. In the RIK e-Business Register, create the establishment application. Enter the name with the required OÜ/Osaühing suffix, address, email, founders and board. Confirm the email using the portal's link.
3. Enter each contribution, articles, beneficial owners and any contact person. Save the required fields. Add voluntary VAT and employment applications only when actually needed.
4. Review the confirmation screen. Correct validation errors, then obtain every required digital signature. Editing after signature cancels the signatures and requires them again.
5. Follow the portal's capital-payment/deposit procedure. Confirm only contributions actually paid. Pay the state fee using the exact reference supplied by the portal; it must reach the Treasury before submission.
6. Choose automatic submission on fee receipt, or press Submit application after receipt. Record the proceeding term displayed. Respond to any ruling to correct deficiencies through the existing application.
7. Once registered, request return of a court deposit to the company's account if used. Set the annual-report, TSD, VAT and employment duties from the facts below and the relevant domain Guide.

### Entity types, incorporation and annual compliance

- **Private limited company (osaühing, OÜ)**: shares are held by its founders. RIK permits a minimum EUR 0.01 share for each shareholder. Do not infer that the founders have no personal exposure at every capital level; detailed liability conditions are held below. [RIK OÜ procedure](https://abiinfo.rik.ee/en/applications-and-dashboard/establishment-new-legal-person/establishment-private-limited-company).
- **Public limited company (aktsiaselts, AS)**: government e-Residency guidance describes the public-company form and minimum capital of EUR 25,000. Share-transfer and pre-emption conditions are held below. [Government forms guide](https://learn.e-resident.gov.ee/hc/en-gb/articles/360002468417-Forms-of-businesses).
- **Other forms**: an FIE operates in their own name and is not a legal person. A general partnership has at least two personally liable partners; a limited partnership has at least one personally liable partner and one whose exposure is limited to their contribution. A commercial association is another form. A foreign-company branch is not a separate legal person and the foreign company is responsible for it. These are form distinctions, not a complete statutory liability opinion. [Government forms guide](https://learn.e-resident.gov.ee/hc/en-gb/articles/360002468417-Forms-of-businesses).
- **Minimum share capital, OÜ**: EUR 0.01. The current RIK application guide supports the per-shareholder minimum; the historical effective-date statement is held below. RIK: "The minimum capital of a is 0.01€ (if there is one shareholder). The minimum share amount of every shareholder is at least 0.01 €". _(ÄS §§ 136, 148(1); RIK, establishment of a private limited company)_
- **Paying in the capital, OÜ**: RIK requires paid contributions before filing. Its standard electronic flow records off-portal payment and board confirmation for amounts up to EUR 50,000; above that it describes a court-deposit route. Do not substitute an unverified bank-notice rule for that portal procedure. In-kind valuations and audit thresholds require the held statutory conditions below. [RIK OÜ procedure](https://abiinfo.rik.ee/en/applications-and-dashboard/establishment-new-legal-person/establishment-private-limited-company).
- **Minimum share capital, AS**: EUR 25,000. _(ÄS § 222)_
- **Incorporation method**: use the RIK electronic procedure only when all required persons can sign with an accepted Estonian digital identity. Its application uses standard articles. A bespoke or notarial formation is referred rather than treated as the standard portal route. [RIK OÜ procedure](https://abiinfo.rik.ee/en/applications-and-dashboard/establishment-new-legal-person/establishment-private-limited-company).
- **What the application holds**: the name with "Osaühing" or "OÜ" at the start or end; an address chosen from the Estonian address data system (if the company's address is abroad, a contact person must be added); an e-mail address; board members and founders; the share capital per founder; the articles; the beneficial owners. Optional extras sent on to the Tax and Customs Board (EMTA) once the company is entered: voluntary VAT registration and entries in the register of employees. The default financial year is the calendar year. _(RIK, establishment of a private limited company)_
- **Incorporation timeline**: after you submit, the application page shows the proceeding term by which the registrar must review it. The application goes to the register only after the state fee has reached the state treasury. _(RIK)_ The statutory review period is on the "Not yet confirmed" line below.
- **Electronic state fees**: RIK states EUR 265 for the electronic OÜ procedure and EUR 20 for electronic FIE establishment. Use the payment details and reference generated for the application. The other forms, non-electronic route and amendment fees are held below rather than assumed to share these fees. [RIK OÜ procedure](https://abiinfo.rik.ee/en/applications-and-dashboard/establishment-new-legal-person/establishment-private-limited-company); [RIK FIE procedure](https://abiinfo.rik.ee/en/applications-and-dashboard/establishment-new-legal-person/establishment-sole-proprietor-fie).
- **e-Residency**: a government digital identity used for online company registration. It does not determine personal tax residence. An Estonian-established company is resident under Estonia’s domestic test, but foreign management or activity can create taxation abroad; route the actual facts to ee-cross-border. Do not determine the foreign result from the digital card. [Government e-Residency](https://www.e-resident.gov.ee/start-a-company/).
- **Annual report obligation**: companies, including newly established and dormant companies, file an annual report in the e-Business Register within six months after the financial year ends. The government accounting guide describes the annual accounts and management report, and a micro-enterprise exception. Confirm the applicable reporting category rather than assuming every company may omit a management report. Filing uses XBRL and does not replace tax declarations. Detailed statutory attachments and approval conditions are held below. [Government accounting guide](https://learn.e-resident.gov.ee/hc/en-gb/articles/360002569617-Accounting).
- **Ongoing tax compliance**: a resident legal person files the income and social tax return (TSD) on the payments, distributions and other taxable items of a calendar month by the 10th of the following month. A person whose Estonian taxable turnover from the start of the calendar year exceeds EUR 40,000 must register for VAT, applying within three working days of crossing it. A VAT-registered person files the VAT return (KMD) by the 20th of the following month. _(TuMS § 54(2); KMS §§ 19(1), 20(1), 27(1))_ Detail: estonia-vat-return, estonia-payroll.
- **Company tax once formed**: a resident company pays income tax when it distributes profit as dividends or other distributions, not while profit is kept in the company. The rate is 22%, applied to the net amount divided by 0.78. Salaries carry social tax at 33%. _(TuMS §§ 4(1), 4(1^1), 50(1); SMS § 7(1))_ Detail: ee-corporate-income-tax, estonia-social-contributions.

## Worked cases

A hypothetical single-founder OÜ chooses the minimum EUR 0.01 share and the electronic route. Its initial funding is EUR 265.01, consisting of EUR 0.01 belonging to the company and a EUR 265 registration fee. Capital is not an additional tax or a fee. Notary, e-Residency, bank and contact-person costs are excluded. With four founders each holding a minimum EUR 0.01 share, capital is EUR 0.04 and the same electronic state fee gives initial funding of EUR 265.04. [RIK establishment procedure](https://abiinfo.rik.ee/en/applications-and-dashboard/establishment-new-legal-person/establishment-private-limited-company).

## When to refuse or refer

Do not select a standard electronic route when required signatures cannot be supplied, contributions have not actually been paid, or the founders need bespoke notarial or in-kind terms. Refer cross-border tax residence, permanent establishments and regulated licences to the relevant specialist; e-Residency does not determine personal tax residence. Do not quote the unconfirmed review period or notary fees below.

## Filing and payment

The application reaches the registrar only after the fee reaches the Treasury and submission is confirmed. Record the proceeding term from the application, not a promised marketing turnaround. Read and correct any deficiency ruling; registration and tax/VAT registration are separate outcomes. Use the monthly and annual duties stated above only where the company has the relevant activity.

## Completion checklist

- [ ] Form, activity, ownership, board, address/contact person and beneficial owners agreed.
- [ ] All required fields saved and email confirmed.
- [ ] Contributions paid; every declaration and signature correct.
- [ ] Fee/reference and submission confirmed; proceeding term recorded.
- [ ] Registration outcome and any tax applications checked; ongoing duties assigned.

### Held original statutory detail, not instructions

These original passages are preserved for source completion. Their unsupported clauses must not be used as current guidance. Verified subsets are stated in the active procedure above. Historical source dates in the table are provenance claims from the original file, not new reads.

- **Private limited company (osaühing, OÜ)**: a company whose share capital is divided into shares. Shareholders are not personally liable for its debts, and the company answers with all its assets. One or more persons can found it. _(ÄS §§ 135(1)-(3), 137(1))_

- **Public limited company (aktsiaselts, AS)**: a company whose share capital is divided into shares, with share capital of at least EUR 25,000. Shareholders are not personally liable. A registered share is freely transferable. In an OÜ, unless the articles say otherwise, a share can be transferred freely to another shareholder, and the other shareholders have a one-month pre-emption right on a transfer to an outsider. _(ÄS §§ 149(2), 221(1)-(2), 222, 229(1))_

- **Other forms**: a sole proprietor (füüsilisest isikust ettevõtja, FIE), which any natural person can be, must apply to be entered in the commercial register before starting business. A general partnership (täisühing, TÜ) has two or more partners who are jointly liable with all their assets. A limited partnership (usaldusühing, UÜ) has at least one general partner liable with all assets and at least one limited partner liable up to the contribution. The Commercial Code also lists the commercial association (tulundusühistu). A foreign company can register a branch (filiaal), which is not a legal person; the company is liable for it. _(ÄS §§ 2(1), 3(1)-(2), 79, 125(1), 384(1)-(2))_

- **Paying in the capital, OÜ**: founders must pay for their shares in full before the registration application is filed. Founding an OÜ without paying in the contribution (old § 140^1) was repealed from 1 February 2023. Cash goes into a payment account opened in the name of the company being founded; under the expedited procedure it can also be paid as a deposit to the registrar's account. A bank or payment institution notice is needed only when the contribution is over EUR 50,000; up to that amount the management board members confirm in the application that the contributions have been paid. If capital, or a share paid for in kind, is EUR 25,000 or more, an auditor must check the valuation of the in-kind contribution. _(ÄS §§ 140(2), 140^1, 141, 143(3), 144(1) p 3, 144(2^1), 520(4^1); RIK)_

- **Incorporation method**: online through the RIK e-Business Register portal. The application can only be signed with an Estonian ID card (including the e-Residency card), Smart-ID or Mobile-ID, and everyone added to the application must be able to sign it digitally. The expedited procedure (kiirmenetlus) uses standard articles of association set by ministerial regulation. The founding can also be done before a notary: the Code refers to both notarial and non-notarial founding. _(ÄS §§ 139^1(1)-(2), 520(1); RIK)_

- **State fees in 2026**: entering an OÜ, AS, commercial association or foreign company branch costs EUR 200, or EUR 265 when filed under the expedited procedure. RIK states the fee for founding an OÜ electronically is EUR 265. Entering an FIE, general partnership or limited partnership costs EUR 20. Later changes to the entry cost EUR 25 for an OÜ, AS, commercial association or branch, and EUR 10 for an FIE or partnership. The same amounts are in the State Fees Act texts in force from 1 November 2026 and from 1 January 2027. _(RLS §§ 62(1)-(2), 63(1)-(2); RIK)_

- **e-Residency**: a government digital ID. With the e-Residency card you can log in to the e-Business Register and register a company fully online. e-Residency "does not affect your personal tax residency". A company founded under Estonian law is an Estonian tax resident. _(e-Residency, start a company; TuMS § 6(2))_

- **Annual report obligation**: the management board files the approved annual report, with the profit allocation proposal, sales revenue breakdown and the auditor's report where an audit is required, with the commercial register within six months of the end of the financial year. The same six months apply to an OÜ and an AS. The annual report is made up of the annual accounts and a management report; a micro-entity that follows the Estonian financial reporting standard may file the annual accounts alone. _(ÄS §§ 179(4), 334(2); RPS § 14(1), (1^1))_

Held historical effective-date clause, not current instructions: Since 1 February 2023 the Code only says the share capital is stated in euros, and the smallest nominal value of a share is one cent. The underlying statutory commencement was not independently read on 2026-11-16.
