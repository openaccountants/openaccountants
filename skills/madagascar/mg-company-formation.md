# Madagascar Company Formation & Entity Choice

## Entity types and incorporation

- **Société à Responsabilité Limitée (SARL)** — Most common form; 1 to 100 partners, liability limited to capital contributions  _(Loi sur les sociétés commerciales (Madagascar) — https://madagascarinvest.com/how-to-start-a-company-in-madagascar/)_
- **Société Anonyme (SA)** — Joint-stock company for larger ventures; governed by a board / managing director ((approx — confirm))  _(Loi sur les sociétés commerciales (Madagascar) — https://madagascarinvest.com/how-to-start-a-company-in-madagascar/)_
- **Entreprise individuelle / sole trader** — Single-owner business taxed in the owner's hands; commonly under the synthetic regime ((approx — confirm))  _(Code Général des Impôts (Madagascar) — https://madagascarinvest.com/how-to-start-a-company-in-madagascar/)_
- **SARL minimum share capital** — No statutory minimum (commonly a nominal amount, e.g. MGA 100,000 / ~US$1) ((approx — confirm))  _(Loi sur les sociétés commerciales (Madagascar) — https://www.healyconsultants.com/madagascar-company-registration/setup-llc/)_
- **SARL minimum shareholders / directors** — At least 1 shareholder and 1 manager (gérant); partners may be of any nationality  _(Loi sur les sociétés commerciales (Madagascar) — https://madagascarinvest.com/how-to-start-a-company-in-madagascar/)_
- **Incorporation registry / one-stop shop** — Registre du Commerce et des Sociétés (RCS), via the EDBM one-stop shop  _(Loi sur les sociétés commerciales (Madagascar) — https://madagascarinvest.com/how-to-start-a-company-in-madagascar/)_
- **Core incorporation steps** — Reserve company name; deposit capital and obtain certificate (if applicable); sign statutes; file with RCS; register for tax ID (NIF) and VAT where relevant  _(Loi sur les sociétés commerciales (Madagascar) — https://madagascarinvest.com/how-to-start-a-company-in-madagascar/)_
- **Typical incorporation timeline** — Approximately 1 to 3 weeks via the EDBM one-stop shop ((approx — confirm))  _(EDBM company registration guidance (Madagascar) — https://madagascarinvest.com/how-to-start-a-company-in-madagascar/)_
- **Tax identification number** — Numéro d'Identification Fiscale (NIF) obtained at registration with the DGI  _(Code Général des Impôts (Madagascar) — https://madagascarinvest.com/how-to-start-a-company-in-madagascar/)_
- **Core annual compliance** — File annual IR return (15 May for 31 Dec year-end), monthly TVA and IRSA returns, quarterly CNAPS, and file annual financial statements ((approx — confirm))  _(Code Général des Impôts (Madagascar) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/madagascar/corporate/tax-administration)_
- **Local representation** — A SARL must have a resident representative/manager based in Madagascar ((approx — confirm))  _(Loi sur les sociétés commerciales (Madagascar) — https://www.healyconsultants.com/madagascar-company-registration/setup-llc/)_
