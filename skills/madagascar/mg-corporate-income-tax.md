# Madagascar corporate income tax and business-regime routing

> **General reference only.** This Guide is general tax/accounting reference material for AI-assisted workflows. It has not been reviewed for any specific person’s facts, documents, elections, deadlines, residency, filing status, or local procedures. Do not rely on it to file, pay, amend, or take a tax position without review by a qualified professional in Madagascar.

## Who this applies to

Compute ordinary Impôt sur les Revenus (IR) for a company after checking its activity, regime and residence. Mandatory IR starts at annual turnover and/or income of MGA 400,000,000; an IR election is available from MGA 200,000,000. An election for the régime du réel brings both IR and TVA. Cooperatives are expressly brought into IR irrespective of turnover by the July LFR amendment to 01.01.13 VII. Education, agricultural/forest exporters and the other express exceptions need separate routing, even below the threshold (CDI 01.01.02, 01.01.05 V–VII). Residents include companies with a registered office and/or effective management in Madagascar; their income is covered regardless of origin (01.01.04–05). [Official 2026 CDI](https://www.impots.mg/explorer?path=/legislation/Codes%20et%20Manuels/CDI-LFI%202026.pdf).

| Petroleum-company minimum; retail-fuel minimum | MGA 1,000,000 + 0.7%; 0.1% turnover respectively | [CDI 01.01.14 I-A](https://www.impots.mg/explorer?path=/legislation/Codes%20et%20Manuels/CDI-LFI%202026.pdf) |
| Ordinary independent-business IS below mandatory IR threshold, unless exception or election | 5% gross turnover/income; base rounded down to MGA 10,000 | [CDI 01.02.02,04–05](https://www.impots.mg/explorer?path=/legislation/Codes%20et%20Manuels/CDI-LFI%202026.pdf) |
| Qualifying new approved-management-centre members or taxpayers newly registered online by their own clients, first exercises | 3% IS | [CDI 01.02.05](https://www.impots.mg/explorer?path=/legislation/Codes%20et%20Manuels/CDI-LFI%202026.pdf) |

Transport minimum tax is set by regulation; do not put transport into the general sector minimum. IS is not a tax on 70% of turnover. Article 01.02.04 gives a conditional approved-centre allowance of 50% capped at MGA 10,000,000, excluding the new members under 01.02.05. Article 01.02.05 bis gives a 2% reduction on specified documented costs (or documented sales for the named producers/intermediaries/artisans), with tax no lower than 3% turnover; obtain the evidence before applying it. Regulatory minima and implementation conditions remain outside the calculation scope.

## The base

Start from net accounting profit and reconcile necessary business expenses supported by documents, non-deductible items, taxable income and exemptions under CDI 01.01.09–11. Do not calculate IR on turnover. Carryforward of qualifying prior deficits is limited to the following five exercises (01.01.10 11°); track each year separately. The accounting year can be the calendar year, July–June or another twelve-month period. A first period can be shorter or longer, capped at eighteen months absent permission (01.01.09). Round taxable profit, calculate ordinary IR, then compare with the applicable minimum. Deduct evidenced provisional payments from the tax liability, preserving any excess for the applicable credit procedure. [CDI](https://www.impots.mg/explorer?path=/legislation/Codes%20et%20Manuels/CDI-LFI%202026.pdf). Investment reductions, sector regimes and complex deduction limitations require a separate calculation.

## Deadlines and filing

For a 31 December close, declare results and pay by 15 May of the next year. For 30 June, the date is 15 November of that year. Other closes use day 15 of the fourth month after closing. Bimonthly provisional payments fall due by day 15 of the last month of each period (CPF I-11). The amount formula depends on the decision under CDI 01.01.15 and is not assumed here.

Prepare the signed IR declaration, tax reconciliation, provisional-payment schedule and liasse fiscale. CDI 01.01.19 requires the balance sheet, result statements by function and nature, cash flow statement, changes in equity, notes and minimum supporting information under PCG2005; the July LFR amendment expressly includes the fixed-asset/depreciation schedule and provisions/impairment statement in the annexes, plus deposit on the dedicated online financial-statement platform. CPF I-11 directs the signed declaration to the inspector/controller at the competent place of taxation, who issues a receipt. Keep that receipt and payment proof. [CPF I-11](https://www.impots.mg/explorer?path=/legislation/Codes%20et%20Manuels/CPF-LFI%202026.pdf); [CDI 01.01.19](https://www.impots.mg/explorer?path=/legislation/Codes%20et%20Manuels/CDI-LFI%202026.pdf). Current electronic return fields, portal submission sequence and payment-reference process are not verified, so this is a computation and statutory filing checklist, not a portal filing guide.

## Penalties and interest

For turnover at least the mandatory IR threshold, failure to lodge a required return/document attracts MGA 200,000 (CDI 20.01.52). Under 20.01.53 the late-payment scale is 3% for the first month and 1% each later month for this turnover band, with each started month counted and a minimum MGA 10,000 per tax/period. An understatement attracts 40% of additional rights, at least MGA 100,000 (20.01.54). Other turnover bands have different penalties. Do not add these percentages without identifying the actual breach and period. [CDI](https://www.impots.mg/explorer?path=/legislation/Codes%20et%20Manuels/CDI-LFI%202026.pdf).

## Cross-border points

Resident-company income is not limited to Madagascar-source income. Treaty allocation and non-resident service payments go to [cross-border](mg-cross-border.md); related-party adjustments go to [transfer pricing](mg-transfer-pricing.md). Cooperative interest/rebate taxation belongs to the capital-income rules, not a reduced ordinary corporate IR rate. The July LFR adds IR for cooperatives regardless of turnover under 01.01.13 VII; member taxation follows ordinary rules. Merger loss transfers under amended 01.01.11 III require express tax-authority authorization and implementing conditions, so are not an automatic carryforward here. Do not treat a foreign incorporation address as proof of non-residence.

## Two worked examples that recompute

Hypothetical general trading companies existing on 1 January, no reductions; profit already tax-adjusted. Article 01.01.14 applies.

```oa-check
name: Ordinary IR exceeds minimum
inputs:
  profit: 80000999
  turnover: 500000000
  paid: 8000000
steps:
  - rounded: floor(profit / 1000) * 1000
  - ordinary: rounded * 0.20
  - minimum: 1000000 + turnover * 0.01
  - liability: max(ordinary, minimum)
  - balance: liability - paid
expected:
  rounded: 80000000
  liability: 16000000
  balance: 8000000
```

```oa-check
name: Low profit company minimum controls
inputs:
  profit: 5000000
  turnover: 600000000
steps:
  - ordinary: floor(profit / 1000) * 1000 * 0.20
  - minimum: 1000000 + turnover * 0.01
  - liability: max(ordinary, minimum)
expected:
  ordinary: 1000000
  minimum: 7000000
  liability: 7000000
```

## Ask the client first

Get the NIF, regime/election, activities and regulated sector, financial year, residence/effective-management facts, VAT-exclusive turnover, tax-adjusted profit, loss-year schedule, invoices and payment evidence, investment/approved-centre claims, related parties and provisional receipts. Ask about fuel, transport, public contracts, export and free-zone status before using the ordinary result.
