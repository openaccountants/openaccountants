# Curacao: tax pack

Tax year: the calendar year (2026). Curacao has its own tax laws within the Kingdom of the Netherlands. The Belastingdienst Curacao administers income tax, profit tax, turnover tax (OB, a cumulative turnover tax, not a VAT) and wage tax, and collects the AOV/AWW, AVBZ and BVZ premiums; the SVB sets premiums and runs ZV, OV and cessantia. Amounts are Curacao guilders (older pages ANG or NAf, newer ones XCG or Cg).
