# Curaçao payroll: wage tax (loonbelasting), AOV/AWW, AVBZ and BVZ premiums, ZV, OV and cessantia, filing dates and year-end statements (2026, with 2025 notes) v1.0

Figures are for tax year 2026. Premium rates and ceilings come from the Sociale Verzekeringsbank (SVB) table for 2026 ([SVB Tabel 2026](https://svbcur.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Tabel-2026.pdf)); the Belastingdienst rates page still links the 2023 table ([Tax Office Curacao, Belastingtarieven](https://belastingdienst.cw/particulier/themas/belastingtarieven/)), which is out of date. Wage tax uses the 2026 bracket table and the 2026 period tables ([Schijven tarief 2026](https://belastingdienst.cw/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Schijven-tarief-2026.pdf); [Maandtabel 2026](https://belastingdienst.cw/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/lb-maandtabel-2026.pdf)).

Currency: older documents print Antillean guilders (NAf, shown as ANG); documents from 2025 print Caribbean guilders (XCG or Cg). Amounts are quoted in the currency the source prints ([Tax Office Curacao, Lei di Bion](https://belastingdienst.cw/vrijstelling-van-loonbelasting-en-sociale-lasten-mogelijk-dankzij-lei-di-bion/); [Toelichting overwerkregistratie 2026](https://belastingdienst.cw/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Toelichting-overwerkregistratie-2026.pdf)).

## Scope and who this is for

- **Covers:** employers and other withholding agents (inhoudingsplichtigen) in Curaçao. Topics:
  - who withholds, and who is an employee;
  - wage tax (loonbelasting) and the 2026 employee credits;
  - the AOV/AWW, AVBZ and BVZ premiums paid with wage tax;
  - the employer-only ZV, OV and cessantia charges;
  - registration, the monthly return, penalties;
  - the verzamelloonstaat and the Article 45 ALL cards;
  - Lei di Bion, the ex-patriate regime, customary salary for directors, and foreign artists.
- **Does not cover:**
  - the employee's own income tax return;
  - profit tax and turnover tax;
  - SVB benefit claims;
  - labour law (dismissal, severance amounts, minimum wage);
  - the other Dutch Caribbean islands.
- **The withholding agent is wider than the employer.** It includes a pension fund or insurer paying a pension to someone else's former employee, and the parties named under the fictitious-employment rules in step 2 ([Tax Office Curacao, Loonbelasting](https://belastingdienst.cw/ondernemer/themas/loonbelasting/)).

## Ask the client first

- Does the business have a CRIB number, a wage tax and premium registration and a portal account? Are its staff registered with the SVB?
- For each worker:
  - Do they live in Curaçao, and how old are they?
  - Are they a foreign national here temporarily, or insured abroad?
  - Do they hold an Inspector's treaty statement?
- Has each employee given a Loonbelastingverklaring 2026? Does anyone use the credits at another job?
- What does pay include: holiday pay, bonuses, overtime, or in-kind items (car, housing, meals, trips)?
- Does the business pay any of these:
  - directors or supervisory directors, and is any director's interest substantial? What is the turnover?
  - commission agents, agency staff, interns, contractors or artists?
- Is there a pension scheme or a savings fund? Are there staff aged 18 to 30 registered with Kranshi, or regular overtime? Have Lei di Bion requests been filed?
- What is the business's OV risk class? Is it an eigenrisicodrager for BVZ? Does any employee hold an ex-patriate ruling?
- Are any returns, payments, verzamelloonstaten or Article 45 filings late? Were penalties imposed in the last nine periods?

## The method, step by step

1. **Register.** Send the Belastingdienst the documents for your legal form ([Tax Office Curacao, Loonbelasting](https://belastingdienst.cw/ondernemer/themas/loonbelasting/)):
   - N.V., B.V., foundation or association: the deed of incorporation, the Chamber of Commerce extract, the director's details and a valid ID;
   - sole trader: the Chamber of Commerce extract and a valid ID;
   - V.O.F. or maatschap: the extract, the partnership agreement and a valid ID.

   With employees, also give the headcount, the first start date and the gross payroll. Staff are also registered with the SVB ([Tax Office Curacao handbook, 2026](https://belastingdienst.cw/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/FIN_Succesvol_Ondernemen_versie2-1.pdf)). The SVB registration steps are not on the pages read for this skill, so check them with the SVB.
2. **Decide who is an employee.** Withhold, and keep a payroll record, if all three are true ([Tax Office Curacao, Startende ondernemers](https://belastingdienst.cw/ondernemer/themas/startende-ondernemers/)):
   - the person has committed to work for some time;
   - they are paid for it;
   - there is a relationship of authority (gezagsverhouding).

   This covers casual, holiday and Saturday help. For agency or lent staff, the agency or lending employer usually withholds. A supervised freelancer can be an employee. **Fictitious employment** always counts as employment, and the withholding agents are ([Tax Office Curacao, Loonbelasting](https://belastingdienst.cw/ondernemer/themas/loonbelasting/)):
   - the N.V. or B.V., for its directors and supervisory directors;
   - the parent, for a child aged 14 or over;
   - the payer, for people paid on commission;
   - the promoter, for artists and athletes;
   - the principal, for contractors and their helpers;
   - the agency, for temp workers;
   - the host, for interns;
   - the SVB, for its benefit recipients.
3. **Resident or non-resident.** A resident is an employee wherever they work, if the withholding agent is resident or established in Curaçao. A non-resident is an employee only if they:
   - work in Curaçao, or are paid for past work done there;
   - work for a Curaçao public-law body; or
   - are, or were, a director or supervisory director of a company established in Curaçao.

   Withhold unless the employee gives you an Inspector's statement that a tax treaty or the Belastingregeling voor het Koninkrijk rules out wage tax ([Tax Office Curacao, Loonbelasting](https://belastingdienst.cw/ondernemer/themas/loonbelasting/)).
4. **Measure the wage.** Wage is "al hetgeen uit een dienstbetrekking wordt genoten": pay in money (salary, bonuses, holiday pay) and in kind (meals, trips, a company car, free housing). Withhold when the wage is paid or made available ([Tax Office Curacao, Loonbelasting](https://belastingdienst.cw/ondernemer/themas/loonbelasting/)).
5. **Check premium coverage for each person.**
   - AOV: residents aged 15 or older who have not reached 65 ([Tax Office Curacao, Loonbelasting](https://belastingdienst.cw/ondernemer/themas/loonbelasting/)).
   - AWW: residents aged 15 and over. Also covered: a non-resident who works or worked for a Curaçao employer and files income tax as a resident ([Tax Office Curacao, AWW](https://belastingdienst.cw/ondernemer/themas/algemene-weduwen-en-wezenverzekering-aww/)).
   - BVZ: every resident, plus a non-resident working for a Curaçao employer who pays Curaçao wage tax ([Tax Office Curacao, BVZ](https://belastingdienst.cw/particulier/themas/basisverzekering-ziektekosten-bvz/)).
   - AVBZ: insured people aged 15 or over who have income. It does not stop at 65.
   - Not insured: foreign nationals, people with equivalent insurance abroad, and people in Curaçao only temporarily. The Minister of Social Affairs then issues a decision that lets you stop withholding premiums ([Tax Office Curacao, AOV](https://belastingdienst.cw/particulier/themas/algemene-ouderdomsverzekering-aov/)). Without that decision, withhold.
6. **Compute wage tax.** Use the Belastingdienst table for the pay period (month, quincena, week, day, half day or quarter). Then subtract the period's share of the credits (step 7). The basic credit has not been built into the tables since 2018 ([Loonbelastingverklaring 2026](https://belastingdienst.cw/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Loonbelastingverklaring-2026.pdf)). Withholding takes account only of the credits, a fixed ANG 500 for employment costs, pension premiums, up to ANG 840 for a savings fund, and the employee's AOV/AWW premium. Other deductions wait for the income tax assessment ([Tax Office Curacao, Loonbelasting](https://belastingdienst.cw/ondernemer/themas/loonbelasting/)). For 2026 use the 2026 period tables; the monthly table is "Exclusief basiskorting" ([Maandtabel 2026](https://belastingdienst.cw/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/lb-maandtabel-2026.pdf)). The rates page still links the 2023 tables.
7. **Apply the Loonbelastingverklaring 2026.**
   - The employee gives it to the employer:
     - on starting work or a taxable benefit;
     - when their spouse starts earning;
     - at the start of a year after a marriage, divorce or death, or a change in children.
   - The credits apply only once. With overlapping jobs, only one employer applies them, and non-residents get none ([Toelichting Loonbelastingverklaring 2026](https://belastingdienst.cw/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Loonbelastingverklaring-2026-toelichting.docx-1.pdf)).
   - Take 1/12 of the annual total for a month, 1/26 for a quincena, 1/52 for a week, 1/260 for a day and 1/520 for a half day ([Loonbelastingverklaring 2026](https://belastingdienst.cw/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Loonbelastingverklaring-2026.pdf)).
8. **Premiums.** Withhold the employee share and add the employer share, using the 2026 table below. ZV, OV and cessantia are employer-only ([SVB Tabel 2026](https://svbcur.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Tabel-2026.pdf)).
9. **File and pay monthly** through the portal. One return covers wage tax and the AOV/AWW, AVBZ and BVZ premiums. It is due within 15 days after the month ends, moving to the first working day after the 15th if that falls on a weekend or holiday. Pay by bank, not cash ([Tax Office Curacao, Loonbelasting](https://belastingdienst.cw/ondernemer/themas/loonbelasting/)). File even when nothing is due ([Inleverdata 2026](https://belastingdienst.cw/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Inleverdata-aangiften-2026_huisstijl_ME-LB-OB.docx-2.pdf)). ZV, OV and cessantia are not on this return: settle them with the SVB.
10. **Year end.**
    - File the verzamelloonstaat and the Article 45 ALL cards.
    - Give each employee a loonstaat and jaaropgave.
    - Keep records for at least 10 years ([Tax Office Curacao handbook, 2026](https://belastingdienst.cw/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/FIN_Succesvol_Ondernemen_versie2-1.pdf)).

### Wage tax rates, credits and supplements

- **Rate range.** Wage tax is charged at rates from 9,75% up to and including 46,5% of the wage ([Tax Office Curacao, Belastingtarieven](https://belastingdienst.cw/particulier/themas/belastingtarieven/)). The period tables apply the bands to the wage per period; the monthly table excludes the basic credit ([Maandtabel 2026](https://belastingdienst.cw/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/lb-maandtabel-2026.pdf)).
- **2026 bracket schedule** ([Schijven tarief 2026](https://belastingdienst.cw/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Schijven-tarief-2026.pdf)):

|---|---|---|---|---|

- **Credits for 2026, as printed in the employer's calculation box on the 2026 declaration**: basic credit (basiskorting) ANG 2,915 a year; sole-earner supplement (alleenverdienertoeslag) ANG 1,779; elderly supplement (ouderentoeslag) ANG 1,342; transferred elderly supplement ANG 673 ([Loonbelastingverklaring 2026](https://belastingdienst.cw/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Loonbelastingverklaring-2026.pdf)).
- **Source conflict (check).** The 2026 explanatory notes print "NAf 1.739" (sole earner), "NAf 1.312" (elderly) and "658" (transferable) in their text. Those are the 2025 amounts fixed by P.B. 2024 no. 143 ([Ministeriele regeling bedragen inkomstenbelasting 2025](https://belastingdienst.cw/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/143.-MR-vaststelling-bedragen-IB-2025.pdf)), left in the notes. This skill uses the 2026 form ([Toelichting Loonbelastingverklaring 2026](https://belastingdienst.cw/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Loonbelastingverklaring-2026-toelichting.docx-1.pdf)).
- **Who gets the supplements** ([Toelichting Loonbelastingverklaring 2026](https://belastingdienst.cw/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Loonbelastingverklaring-2026-toelichting.docx-1.pdf)):
  - The sole-earner supplement needs all three conditions:
    - married for the whole calendar year;
    - living in Curaçao;
    - the spouse's taxable income for the year is nil or negative.
  - The elderly supplement is for a resident who has reached 60 by the start of the calendar year. A spouse aged 60 or over can transfer the unused part.
  - Someone who is divorced, or permanently separated, is treated as unmarried.
- **Child credit (kindertoeslag) for 2026** ([Loonbelastingverklaring 2026](https://belastingdienst.cw/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Loonbelastingverklaring-2026.pdf); [Toelichting Loonbelastingverklaring 2026](https://belastingdienst.cw/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Loonbelastingverklaring-2026-toelichting.docx-1.pdf)):

|---|---|---|---|
| I (a and b) | aged 16 to 26 and abroad for an MBO, HBO, university or comparable course, or for a course not available in Curaçao | 948 | [Loonbelastingverklaring 2026](https://belastingdienst.cw/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Loonbelastingverklaring-2026.pdf) |

- In wage tax, an unmarried single parent cannot get double child credit, and parents cannot transfer the credit between them. Those choices are made on the income tax return ([Toelichting Loonbelastingverklaring 2026](https://belastingdienst.cw/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Loonbelastingverklaring-2026-toelichting.docx-1.pdf)).

## Worked cases

Premium amounts use the SVB table for 2026. Wage tax uses the 2026 monthly table, which excludes the basic credit.

- **Case 1: monthly premiums for an ordinary employee** ([SVB Tabel 2026](https://svbcur.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Tabel-2026.pdf)). A resident employee aged 35 earns XCG 5,000 gross a month in 2026, with no exemptions.
  - Employee: 6% AOV = XCG 300; 0,5% AWW = XCG 25; 4,3% BVZ = XCG 215; 1.5% AVBZ = XCG 75. Total withheld: XCG 615 ([SVB Tabel 2026](https://svbcur.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Tabel-2026.pdf)).
  - Employer: 9% AOV = XCG 450; 0,5% AWW = XCG 25; 9,3% BVZ = XCG 465; 0.5% AVBZ = XCG 25. Total: XCG 965 ([SVB Tabel 2026](https://svbcur.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Tabel-2026.pdf)).
  - The Belastingdienst return shows XCG 1,580 of premiums plus the wage tax. Separately, the employer owes ZV at 1,9% = XCG 95 (the pay is below the XCG 7,146.10 monthly ceiling) and OV at its risk-class rate, both settled with the SVB ([SVB Tabel 2026](https://svbcur.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Tabel-2026.pdf)).
- **Case 2: credits in the monthly wage-tax calculation.** The same employee is unmarried, with one child aged 10 at home (category IV). Annual credits: XCG 2,915 + XCG 96 = XCG 3,011. The monthly credit is 1/12 of XCG 3,011 = XCG 250.92, deducted from the table tax. With no child it would be 1/12 of XCG 2,915 = XCG 242.92 ([Loonbelastingverklaring 2026](https://belastingdienst.cw/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Loonbelastingverklaring-2026.pdf)). The 2026 monthly table gives XCG 631.48 on a monthly wage of XCG 5,000 ([Maandtabel 2026](https://belastingdienst.cw/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/lb-maandtabel-2026.pdf)); if that is the taxable wage after the AOV/AWW, pension and cost deductions in step 6, wage tax withheld is 631.48 less 250.92 = XCG 380.56.

```oa-check
name: Curacao 2026 monthly premiums and wage tax, XCG 5000
inputs:
  gross: 5000
  table_tax_on_5000: 631.48
  basic_credit_year: 2915
  child_iv_year: 96
steps:
  - employee_premiums: gross * (0.06 + 0.005 + 0.043 + 0.015)
  - employer_premiums: gross * (0.09 + 0.005 + 0.093 + 0.005)
  - premiums_on_return: employee_premiums + employer_premiums
  - zv_employer: min(gross, 7146.10) * 0.019
  - monthly_credit: (basic_credit_year + child_iv_year) / 12
  - wage_tax: table_tax_on_5000 - monthly_credit
expected:
```

- **Case 3: a high earner and the ceilings.** An employee earns XCG 120,000 in 2026 (XCG 10,000 a month) ([SVB Tabel 2026](https://svbcur.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Tabel-2026.pdf)).
  - AOV (annual view): 6% applies up to the XCG 100,000 ceiling, giving XCG 6,000. The table's 1% above the ceiling gives 1% of XCG 20,000 = XCG 200; whether that is withheld in payroll or charged on the assessment is a check item ([SVB Tabel 2026](https://svbcur.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Tabel-2026.pdf)).
  - BVZ: income is below the XCG 150,000 ceiling, so 4,3% of XCG 120,000 = XCG 5,160 (employee) and 9,3% = XCG 11,160 (employer) ([SVB Tabel 2026](https://svbcur.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Tabel-2026.pdf)).
  - ZV each month: 1,9% of the XCG 7,146.10 ceiling = XCG 135.78, not 1,9% of XCG 10,000 ([SVB Tabel 2026](https://svbcur.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Tabel-2026.pdf)).
  - How an annual ceiling is spread over monthly payrolls is not stated on any page read for this skill. Ask the SVB.

## When to refuse or refer

- **A premium question the 2026 SVB table does not answer** (risk class for OV, collection of the 1% AOV above the ceiling, spreading annual ceilings over pay periods). Refer to the SVB ([SVB Tabel 2026](https://svbcur.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Tabel-2026.pdf)).
- **ZV, OV, cessantia registration, risk class or payment mechanics.** Refer to the SVB. They are not on the Belastingdienst return and their procedures could not be read for this skill.
- **Period tables other than the month.** Only the 2026 monthly table was read for this skill. For a quincena, week, day, half-day or quarter payroll, get the Belastingdienst's 2026 table for that period; do not scale the monthly table.
- **Treaty or Kingdom-arrangement relief.** Withhold until the employee produces the Inspector's statement ([Tax Office Curacao, Loonbelasting](https://belastingdienst.cw/ondernemer/themas/loonbelasting/)). Refer the treaty analysis itself.
- **Ex-patriate applications, Lei di Bion disputes, and any recovery for fraud or wrong data.** Refer to a Curaçao tax adviser. These depend on facts the Belastingdienst tests case by case ([Lei di Bion](https://belastingdienst.cw/vrijstelling-van-loonbelasting-en-sociale-lasten-mogelijk-dankzij-lei-di-bion/)).
- **Severance, dismissal, minimum wage or labour disputes.** These are outside this skill. Refer to a labour lawyer or to the ministry the Belastingdienst handbook names as "Ministerie van Sociale Arbeid & Welzijn (SOAW)" ([Tax Office Curacao handbook, 2026](https://belastingdienst.cw/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/FIN_Succesvol_Ondernemen_versie2-1.pdf)).
- **Currency conversion.** For converting an ANG amount to XCG, check the official rate: the Belastingdienst notice ([Invoering Caribische Gulden](https://belastingdienst.cw/invoering-caribische-gulden-xcg/)) is an image and could not be read.

- **Corrections.** File a revised return through the portal. There is one exception: if the wage tax or premiums should be lower than already declared, send the correct return through the contact form and the wage tax department will process the correction ([Tax Office Curacao, Loonbelasting](https://belastingdienst.cw/ondernemer/themas/loonbelasting/)).
- **Late filing or payment** brings an assessment with a penalty ([Inleverdata 2026](https://belastingdienst.cw/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Inleverdata-aangiften-2026_huisstijl_ME-LB-OB.docx-2.pdf)). The Belastingdienst lists these penalties ([Tax Office Curacao, Loonbelasting](https://belastingdienst.cw/ondernemer/themas/loonbelasting/)):

| Default | Penalty | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Not paid or paid late, first default | 5% of the additional assessment (naheffingsaanslag), at least ANG 50, at most ANG 2,500 | [Tax Office Curacao, Loonbelasting](https://belastingdienst.cw/ondernemer/themas/loonbelasting/) |
| Second default | 10%, at least ANG 100, at most ANG 5,000 | same |
| Third or later default | 15%, at least ANG 150, at most ANG 10,000 | same |
| Systematic failure to file, or to file on time | at most ANG 2,500 | same |
| Underpayment because too little was declared | 15%, at least ANG 25, at most ANG 10,000 | same |

  - To count the defaults, the Inspector looks at the nine preceding periods.
  - These amounts are printed in ANG on the Belastingdienst page. Check whether they have been restated in XCG.
- **Annual verzamelloonstaat.**
  - File one summary of each employee's annual pay online, with any corrections, through the portal. A CSV import is available ([Tax Office Curacao, Loonbelasting](https://belastingdienst.cw/ondernemer/themas/loonbelasting/)).
  - **Deadline: file by 31 January.** The Belastingdienst gave 31 January 2025 as the "wettelijke termijn" for the 2024 statement ([Tax Office Curacao news, verzamelloonstaten 2024](https://belastingdienst.cw/deadline-verzamelloonstaten-2024-uiterlijk-31-januari-2025/)). In 2025 it wrote that employers must file "vóór 1 februari" ([Tax Office Curacao news, aanmaning](https://belastingdienst.cw/ruim-2-700-werkgevers-ontvangen-aanmaning-van-belastingdienst/)). The loonbelasting theme page still says "vóór 1 Maart" ([Tax Office Curacao, Loonbelasting](https://belastingdienst.cw/ondernemer/themas/loonbelasting/)); treat that as out of date.
  - **Penalty:** employers that still had not filed the 2024 statement after 11 August 2025 received a penalty assessment of XCG 5,000 (printed "Cg 5.000"). The duty to file remains after the penalty ([Tax Office Curacao news, aanmaning](https://belastingdienst.cw/ruim-2-700-werkgevers-ontvangen-aanmaning-van-belastingdienst/)).
  - Every employee's CRIB number is needed. Without them the statement cannot be filed ([Tax Office Curacao news, verzamelloonstaten 2023](https://belastingdienst.cw/indienen-verzamelloonstaten-2023-voor-1-maart-2024-anders-naf-5-000-boete/)).
- **Article 45 ALL cards.** Every January, report the third parties who worked or supplied services for you in the past year without being employed by you: for example accountants, lawyers, car repairers, doctors, contractors and painters. File online ([Tax Office Curacao, Loonbelasting](https://belastingdienst.cw/ondernemer/themas/loonbelasting/)).
- **Reduction of wage tax (employee request).** An employee can ask the Belastingdienst to reduce withholding. Two conditions apply: they filed an income tax return for the past year, and their final income tax is expected to be more than a quarter, or more than ANG 1,000, below the wage tax withheld. Requests go through the contact form, not in person ([Tax Office Curacao, Loonbelasting](https://belastingdienst.cw/ondernemer/themas/loonbelasting/)).
- **Year-end settlement of premiums (employee).** Too much or too little premium withheld is settled on the employee's income tax assessment. For BVZ this applies only to the employee's share: "het werkgeversdeel krijgt u niet terug" ([Tax Office Curacao, BVZ](https://belastingdienst.cw/particulier/themas/basisverzekering-ziektekosten-bvz/)). On that assessment, no BVZ premium is due on annual income of ANG 12,000 or less; a gliding-scale discount applies between ANG 12,000 and ANG 18,000; and the rate is 6,5% for someone no longer insured for AOV ([Tax Office Curacao, BVZ](https://belastingdienst.cw/particulier/themas/basisverzekering-ziektekosten-bvz/)). The page describes the premium on the assessment. It does not say whether the employer applies the floor or the gliding scale in payroll, so check with the SVB before you depart from the table rates.

### 2025 returns and statements still being filed or corrected

- **Monthly 2025 periods.** Each 2025 month was due on the date in the Belastingdienst's 2025 table: for example January 2025 by 17 February 2025, and December 2025 by 15 January 2026. The Belastingdienst news item prints the date as "uiterlijk 2 februari 2025", which appears to be a misprint for 2026. Employers that did apply Lei di Bion in 2025 received an automatic extension to 1 April 2026, because the new format was delayed. Until the new format is ready, the overtime registration Excel file is enough for them ([Tax Office Curacao news, overuren Lei di Bion](https://belastingdienst.cw/vrijstelling-loonbelasting-en-sociale-premies-over-overuren-lei-di-bion/)).
- **Overtime exemption for 2026.** Requests were due by Monday 2 February 2026, and the 2025 verzamelloonstaat had to be filed first ([Tax Office Curacao news, overuren Lei di Bion](https://belastingdienst.cw/vrijstelling-loonbelasting-en-sociale-premies-over-overuren-lei-di-bion/)). A late request is a refer item.

## Completion checklist

- [ ] CRIB number, wage tax and premium registration, a working portal account, and SVB registration of staff.
- [ ] Each worker classified (employee, fictitious employment, agency staff or contractor on the Article 45 cards), with residence status and any Inspector's treaty statement on file.
- [ ] Premium coverage checked for each person, with any exemption decision on file.
- [ ] A Loonbelastingverklaring 2026 on file for each employee; credits applied by one employer only, and none for non-residents.
- [ ] Premiums at the SVB 2026 rates and ceilings ([SVB Tabel 2026](https://svbcur.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Tabel-2026.pdf)); wage tax from the 2026 period table, with credits deducted.
- [ ] Lei di Bion, ex-patriate and customary-salary positions documented.
- [ ] Every monthly return filed and paid by bank by the 2026 date (nil returns included); ZV, OV and cessantia settled with the SVB.
- [ ] Verzamelloonstaat by 31 January, Article 45 cards in January, annual statements to employees, and records kept 10 years ([Tax Office Curacao handbook, 2026](https://belastingdienst.cw/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/FIN_Succesvol_Ondernemen_versie2-1.pdf)).
