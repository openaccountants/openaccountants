# Moldova: social and health contributions (2026) v0.2

Moldova has two compulsory systems. State social insurance (asigurări sociale de stat, run by the National Social Insurance House, CNAS) is set by Law 489/1999 on the public social insurance system and the yearly state social insurance budget law (for 2026, Law LP320 of 29 December 2025). Compulsory health insurance (asigurare obligatorie de asistenţă medicală, run by the National Health Insurance Company, CNAM) is set by Law 1593/2002 and the yearly health insurance funds law (for 2026, Law LP321 of 29 December 2025). The State Tax Service (SFS) collects the percentage contributions and premiums.

## Who this applies to

- **Employers** pay state social insurance on salaries and other rewards of employees and of people working under civil contracts for works or services (Law 489/1999 annex 1 pt. 1.1).
- **Employees** pay no state social insurance contribution; the annex has employer lines only (annex 1 pt. 1.1). Employees and other people receiving rewards pay the 9% health premium, which the payer withholds (Law 1593/2002 annex 1 pt. 2 and 4; Law LP321/2025 art. 4(1)).
- **People insured on their own** pay fixed annual amounts: founders of individual enterprises and farms, independent retail traders, plant-product buyers and patent holders (annex 1 pt. 1.6, 1.7), justice professionals (pt. 1.6^1), and people under an individual contract with CNAS (LP320/2025 art. 3(3)). Pensioners and people with disabilities are outside pt. 1.6, 1.6^1 and 1.7.
- **Health premium in a fixed sum**: unemployed residents who own or rent agricultural land, founders of individual enterprises, patent holders, independent retail traders, people renting out property, plant-product buyers, justice professionals, family doctors in independent practice, and other residents who are neither employed nor insured by the Government (Law 1593/2002 annex 2).
- **Carve-outs.** IT park residents pay social insurance under Law 77/2016 inside their single tax (annex 1 pt. 1.4), and neither IT park employers nor their employees pay the percentage health premium on salaries (Law 1593/2002 art. 4(1^1)). Independent entrepreneurs under chapter 10^4 of the Fiscal Code pay both inside their 15% / 35% single tax (annex 1 pt. 1.10; Fiscal Code art. 69^25).
- Income tax is in [moldova-income-tax](moldova-income-tax.md); the monthly payroll run is in [moldova-payroll](moldova-payroll.md).

## The taxable base

**Social insurance.** The employer rate applies to the total of salaries and rewards calculated each month for all employees (annex 1 pt. 1.1). The monthly base for each employee may not be lower than the minimum wage (MDL 6,300 in 2026), in proportion to the time worked. For an employee on part-time or reduced hours, the contribution may not be less than 25% of the contribution on the minimum wage: at 24%, MDL 1,512 a month at full time and MDL 378 at the 25% floor. Article 22 sets a minimum base only; its former paragraphs (2) and (3) are repealed (Law 489/1999 art. 22(1)). No contribution is calculated on the items in annex 3 to Law 489/1999, which include: dividends, interest and other income from property (pt. 21); sick pay (pt. 19); social benefits from public budgets (pt. 20); business-trip and other compensation, except pay for unused leave on dismissal (pt. 3); severance pay within Labour Code limits (pt. 8); copyright payments and civil contracts other than contracts for works or services (pt. 26); grants and free financial aid (pt. 25); union or employer-body aid up to one average monthly wage a year (pt. 28); and employer payments for child care up to MDL 2,500 a month per child under 3 (pt. 42).

**Health insurance.** The 9% premium is calculated on salary and other rewards before taxes and other deductions are withheld (Law 1593/2002 art. 6). "Other rewards" means any sum other than salary paid by the employer to the employee, and other rights and income paid to individuals, except income under Fiscal Code art. 20, 89, 90 and 90^1 (art. 3). The employer does not pay a health premium of its own; the employee is the payer (annex 1 pt. 2).

**Fixed amounts.** The fixed social contribution is paid as 1/12 each month (annex 1 pt. 1.6, 1.6^1). Patent holders pay at least 1/12 for each month of activity (pt. 1.7). The fixed health premium covers people who are in an annex 2 category on 1 January; someone who joins a category after 31 March pays a pro rata premium within 30 days (Law 1593/2002 art. 22(1), 23(1)). A person who paid the fixed health premium and then pays more than that amount as the 9% premium in the same year may ask CNAM for a refund of the fixed premium (art. 23(2), (2^1)). Moldovan citizens outside every category who prove more than 183 days abroad become insured after paying the full MDL 12,636 (LP321/2025 art. 4(5)).

**Income tax link.** Health premiums and social contributions paid by an individual are deductible for income tax (Fiscal Code art. 35^2(1)-(2)).

## Penalties and interest

| Breach | Penalty | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Social contributions paid late | 0.1% of the debt for each day of delay, to the day of payment | LP320/2025 art. 4; Law 489/1999 art. 28(1) |
| Contributions or premiums reduced by false data in a return | 20% to 30% of the amount reduced (not applied to a self-corrected return) | Fiscal Code art. 261(4), (4^1) |
| Evading calculation and payment of contributions or premiums | 80% to 100% of the undeclared amount | Fiscal Code art. 261(5) |
| Return not filed or filed wrongly | MDL 200 to 400 or MDL 500 to 1,000 per return (caps MDL 2,000 and MDL 10,000) | Fiscal Code art. 260(1) |

No late penalty is charged on social contributions while a transfer or offset request is being processed, or on debts written off and held on special record by the SFS (Law 489/1999 art. 28(1^1)). The SFS collects unpaid percentage health premiums like tax arrears; CNAM's territorial agencies track the fixed premiums and may agree a payment plan (Law 1593/2002 art. 17). Health premiums and their penalties are time-barred after 3 years (art. 17(4)).

## Cross-border points

- **Foreign workers.** Foreigners with a Moldovan employment contract, permanent residents, beneficiaries of protection and asylum seekers pay health premiums like Moldovan citizens, unless a treaty provides otherwise. Foreigners with temporary residence for family reunion, study, volunteering, religious work or as digital nomads pay like individually insured citizens (Law 1593/2002 art. 10(1)-(2)). Digital nomads become insured after paying the full fixed premium (LP321/2025 art. 4(6)).
- **Moldovans working abroad.** Citizens outside every payer category who prove more than 183 days abroad in the year may insure by paying MDL 12,636 (LP321/2025 art. 4(5)).
- **International projects.** Moldovan citizens employed by international organisations and projects are in pt. 1.1 unless a treaty exempts them (annex 1 pt. 1.1; annex 3 pt. 6).
- **Social security agreements.** Bilateral agreements decide which country's system applies to posted workers; they are not covered in this pack yet (see [md-cross-border](md-cross-border.md) for income tax).

## Worked examples

**Example 1. Private-sector employee, MDL 18,000 a month.** A private company pays an employee MDL 18,000 gross in October 2026. The company owes 24% social insurance, MDL 4,320, on top of the salary. It withholds the employee's 9% health premium, MDL 1,620. Both, with the income tax withheld, are paid and reported by 25 November 2026. The company's cost is MDL 22,320, and MDL 5,940 goes to the two funds.

```oa-check
name: Moldova employer social and employee health, MDL 18,000, 2026
inputs:
  gross: 18000
  employer_social_rate: 0.24
  health_rate: 0.09
steps:
  - employer_social: gross * employer_social_rate
  - employee_health: gross * health_rate
  - employer_cost: gross + employer_social
  - to_funds: employer_social + employee_health
expected:
  employer_social: 4320
  employee_health: 1620
  employer_cost: 22320
  to_funds: 5940
```

**Example 2. Founder of an individual enterprise, paying early or late.** A sole trader who founded an individual enterprise is not a pensioner and has no disability. For 2026 she owes the fixed social contribution of MDL 22,878, paid as MDL 1,906.50 a month by the 25th of the following month. For health insurance she is in annex 2 pt. 1 b). If she pays the fixed premium by 31 March 2026 it is MDL 2,527; paid later it is MDL 12,636. Paying on time saves MDL 10,109. With the early premium her two fixed amounts total MDL 25,405 for the year.

```oa-check
name: Moldova IE founder fixed contributions, 2026
inputs:
  social_fixed: 22878
  health_fixed_full: 12636
  health_fixed_by_31_march: 2527
steps:
  - social_monthly: social_fixed / 12
  - saving_if_paid_by_31_march: health_fixed_full - health_fixed_by_31_march
  - total_paid_on_time: social_fixed + health_fixed_by_31_march
expected:
  social_monthly: 1906.50
  saving_if_paid_by_31_march: 10109
  total_paid_on_time: 25405
```

## Ask the client first

1. Are you an employer, an employee, or self-employed? If self-employed, under which form: individual enterprise, farm, patent, retail trader, justice profession, family doctor, independent entrepreneur?
2. Is the employer private or a public body, and does any job fall under the special working conditions list (annex 2 to Law 489/1999)?
3. Is the business agricultural, with at least 70% of its activity in CAEM groups 01.1 to 01.6?
4. Are you a pensioner or a person with a disability?
5. Is the business an IT park resident?
6. Did you pay the fixed health premium by 31 March?
