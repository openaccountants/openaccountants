# Moldova Company Formation & Entity Choice

## Entity types, capital and annual compliance

The most common Moldovan business vehicle is the limited liability company (SRL). Registration is handled by the Public Services Agency (Agenția Servicii Publice, ASP). Figures below are from Law 135/2007 on limited liability companies (as amended to LP41/2026), the Fiscal Code (as amended to LP235/2026) and the ASP service pages, all read on 30 October 2026.

- **Most common entity type**: Societate cu Răspundere Limitată (SRL), a commercial company with legal personality whose capital is divided into shares (părți sociale) and whose debts are backed by the company's own assets _(Law 135/2007 art. 2(1))_. The full name must include the words "societate cu răspundere limitată" _(ASP, state registration of a legal entity, specific requirements)_
- **Founders and members of an SRL**: one or more individuals and/or legal entities; the number of members is not limited (200 at most for a crowdfunding investment vehicle) _(Law 135/2007 art. 11(1), (3), (4))_. A board is compulsory above 50 members _(Law 135/2007 art. 63(1))_
- **Joint-stock company**: Societate pe Acțiuni (SA), for larger or public capital raising; registered by ASP like an SRL _(ASP, state registration of a legal entity: online filing for SRL and SA)_
- **Sole trader vehicle**: Întreprinzător Individual (II), activity on one's own account without forming a legal entity, taxed at 12% on income _(ASP, registration of an individual entrepreneur; Fiscal Code art. 15(a))_
- **Independent entrepreneur (antreprenor independent)**: a registration for individuals providing services only, recorded by ASP within 24 hours, paying one monthly single tax of 15% of service income up to MDL 1,200,000 a year and 35% above, which includes income tax, social and health contributions and local taxes _(Fiscal Code arts. 69^20, 69^21, 69^25, 69^26)_. Detail: [moldova-income-tax](moldova-income-tax.md).
- **Minimum share capital for an SRL**: no statutory minimum. The capital is set in the articles and/or entered in the state register; the former minimum (art. 21(3)) is repealed _(Law 135/2007 art. 21(1)-(3))_
- **Paying in SRL capital**: each member pays its subscribed contribution in full within 6 months of registration; contributions are presumed to be cash unless the articles say otherwise _(Law 135/2007 art. 22(1), (2))_
- **Minimum share capital for an SA (joint-stock company)**: MDL 600,000 _(Law 1134/1997 on joint-stock companies, art. 38(2))_. The old figure of MDL 20,000 is no longer the law.
- **Registration authority**: Public Services Agency (ASP), State Register of legal units _(ASP, state registration of a legal entity)_
- **Incorporation timeline**: registration within 24 hours, counted from the working day after the documents are filed; 4 hours on the urgent track _(ASP, state registration of a legal entity)_. If the company is not registered within 3 months of the articles being signed, the founders are released from their share obligations unless the articles say otherwise _(ASP, specific requirements for SRL)_
- **State registration fee, legal entity (SRL, SA)**: MDL 1,149 for 24 hours; MDL 4,596 for 4 hours; free for applicants who qualify for the reliefs in Law 220/2007 art. 4 and Law 179/2016 art. 15 _(ASP, state registration of a legal entity, terms and fees)_
- **State registration fee, individual entrepreneur (II)**: MDL 364 for 24 hours; MDL 1,456 for 4 hours _(ASP, registration of an individual entrepreneur, terms and fees)_
- **Name check and reservation**: free in 5 working days; MDL 71 in 3 working days; MDL 142 in 24 hours; MDL 213 in 4 hours; MDL 284 in 1 hour. A reserved name is required before applying _(ASP, name check and reservation, terms and fees)_
- **Core incorporation steps**: reserve the name at ASP; sign the articles (statut) by all founders; file the registration application with founders' and administrator's IDs and pay the fee (MPay or bank transfer); receive the registration decision, articles and, on request, a register extract. The state identification number (IDNO) is also the tax code _(ASP, state registration of a legal entity; Law 135/2007 art. 12(1))_. Open a bank account and register for VAT separately when required (next items).
- **VAT registration**: required once supplies exceed MDL 1.7 million in any 12 consecutive months (from 1 March 2026; MDL 1.5 million before that date); notify the SFS by the last day of the month in which the threshold is passed _(Fiscal Code art. 112(1); Law LP12/2026)_
- **Income tax regime choice in year one**: 12% of taxable income on the standard regime, or 4% of accounting income for a company not registered for VAT, chosen in the accounting policy by the 25th of the month after the quarter of registration _(Fiscal Code arts. 15(b), 54^1(3)(c), 54^3)_. Detail: [md-corporate-income-tax](md-corporate-income-tax.md).
- **Core annual compliance**: annual income tax return by 25 March (25th of the third month after year end), with quarterly instalments on 25 March, June, September and December; VAT returns monthly by the 25th; tax withheld from salaries and other payments, and the monthly withholding report, by the 25th of the following month _(Fiscal Code arts. 83(4)(a), 84(1), 114(1), 115(1), 92(1), (2))_. Annual financial statements (with the management report and, where required, the auditor's report) go to the owners and the National Bureau of Statistics within 150 days of year end, so by 30 May 2027 for 2026; a public-interest entity has 120 days _(Law 287/2017 on accounting and financial reporting, art. 33(1), (3))_.
