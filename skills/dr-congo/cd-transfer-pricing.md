# DR Congo transfer pricing v0.1

> **General reference only.** This Guide is general tax reference material for AI-assisted workflows. It has not been reviewed for a particular taxpayer. Obtain review by a qualified professional before filing, paying or taking a tax position.

## Who this applies to

Use this method for an enterprise operated in the **Democratic Republic of the Congo**, not the Republic of Congo. For the 2026 income year, Law 23/053 article 53 governs indirect transfers of profits to foreign controlled or controlling enterprises. It compares the actual profits with those that would have arisen without dependence or control. The documentation and reporting populations are separate tests under Law 004/2003, as amended by Finance Act 25/060. [Law 23/053, arts. 53, 153](https://dgi.gouv.cd/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/loi-is-et-Irpp.pdf); [Finance Act 25/060, arts. 25–28](https://www.budget.gouv.cd/wp-content/uploads/budget2026/lf_2026_recettes.pdf).

A documentation threshold is **not** an exemption from article 53. A small enterprise can receive a targeted audit information request under article 29 bis even when it is outside article 24 bis. The August rectifying Act preserves unamended provisions of the original Finance Act. [Finance Act 25/060, art. 28](https://www.budget.gouv.cd/wp-content/uploads/budget2026/lf_2026_recettes.pdf); [Rectifying Act 26/032, art. 49](https://www.budget.gouv.cd/wp-content/uploads/budget2026/lfr_2026/lfr_2026_recettes.pdf).

## The base

1. **Identify the link before looking at value.** Article 53 includes relative majority capital ownership, indirect ownership chains, actual decision power, voting rights sufficient for effective control, common management or shared interests, contractual/commercial/financial dependence, and common control by a third entity. Its entity definition extends to enterprises, companies, associations, joint ventures, trusts and fiduciary arrangements. A fixed majority percentage is not the only substantive test. [Law 23/053, art. 53](https://dgi.gouv.cd/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/loi-is-et-Irpp.pdf).
2. **Identify every way value moves.** Reconcile goods, services, financing, royalties, waived income, free services, debt/commission waivers and other group benefits. Article 53 expressly reaches more than mispriced invoices. [Law 23/053, art. 53](https://dgi.gouv.cd/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/loi-is-et-Irpp.pdf).
3. **Test the independent outcome.** Assemble the contracts, actual functions, assets, risks, delivery evidence, comparable terms and pricing calculation. The statutory comparison is with the profits without dependence or control. An article 29 bis audit request can require complete functional and comparability analyses and the method used. Do not invent a statutory safe harbour, preferred method or accepted range from this Guide. [Law 23/053, art. 53](https://dgi.gouv.cd/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/loi-is-et-Irpp.pdf); [Finance Act 25/060, art. 28](https://www.budget.gouv.cd/wp-content/uploads/budget2026/lf_2026_recettes.pdf).
4. **Compute the identified transfer.** Record the difference between the actual and supported independent outcome, the affected transaction and the resulting profit adjustment. Then reconcile the company's taxable result separately. A calculation does not establish the reliability of the benchmark or authorise a unilateral treaty/corresponding adjustment. [Law 23/053, art. 53](https://dgi.gouv.cd/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/loi-is-et-Irpp.pdf).
5. **Check deduction restrictions separately.** Related-party pricing does not establish deductibility. Article 46 separately requires an actual service, inability to obtain it from a person established in DR Congo, and an independent remuneration amount for the payments within that provision. Article 47 separately tests payments to an unrelated person in a privileged-tax jurisdiction; article 48 denies deductions for payments to listed non-cooperative jurisdictions. Do not substitute an OECD or EU list for the Congolese ministerial list. Current list identification is a declared gap. [Law 23/053, arts. 46–48](https://dgi.gouv.cd/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/loi-is-et-Irpp.pdf).
6. **Retain transaction evidence even with group files.** The statutory master/local-file requirement does not replace invoices, contracts and other evidence for each transaction. The detailed ministerial file specifications remain unconfirmed here. [Finance Act 25/060, art. 25](https://www.budget.gouv.cd/wp-content/uploads/budget2026/lf_2026_recettes.pdf).

## Deadlines and filing

Create three separate workpapers: the article 53 transaction/adjustment analysis; the article 24 bis/24 ter documentation and annual-return screening; and the article 24 quinquies CbC screening. Keep each test's own taxpayer, period, ownership chain, amounts and evidence. [Law 23/053, art. 53](https://dgi.gouv.cd/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/loi-is-et-Irpp.pdf); [Finance Act 25/060, arts. 25–28](https://www.budget.gouv.cd/wp-content/uploads/budget2026/lf_2026_recettes.pdf).

The electronic annual TP declaration contains group identity and ultimate-parent address; activities; group pricing policy and changes; intangible owners and their jurisdictions; restructurings affecting functions, risks or assets; and the local enterprise's activities, controlled transactions, loans/borrowings, free or non-cash transactions, and foreign advance-pricing agreements or rulings. Reconcile it to the accounts and documentation rather than submitting the master file as a substitute return. [Finance Act 25/060, art. 26 / procedure art. 24 ter](https://www.budget.gouv.cd/wp-content/uploads/budget2026/lf_2026_recettes.pdf).

For CbC, screen ultimate-parent consolidation, turnover and ownership conditions; foreign-parent local-filing branches; systemic-failure notification; and the cumulative substitute-filing conditions. Those conditions include an equivalent foreign filing obligation, an effective automatic-exchange agreement, no notified systemic failure, actual exchange, foreign notification of the designated filer and Congolese notification identifying the filer and its tax residence. A foreign filing alone does not prove local exemption. The required exchange list, notification model and electronic schema are not verified here. [Finance Act 25/060, art. 27 / procedure art. 24 quinquies](https://www.budget.gouv.cd/wp-content/uploads/budget2026/lf_2026_recettes.pdf).

The DGI links [i-Impôts](https://i-impots.dgirdc.cd/) as its online declaration/payment entry point on its [IS page](https://dgi.gouv.cd/impot-sur-les-societes/). The TP form number, authenticated menu, field identifiers, upload schema, submission receipt and payment reference have **not** been inspected. This Guide cannot supply a completed electronic filing. Stop before submission until the current form and receipt process are confirmed. Do not invent fields to fill the gap.

## Penalties and interest

Use the distinct statutory penalties in the table only when their triggering facts exist. Retain the formal notice and its identified deficiencies before calculating a documentation-default fine. For a below-threshold article 29 bis request, keep the precise request, response deadline and actual communication date. Do not count a five-day documentation deadline as a 15-day information request. [Finance Act 25/060, arts. 25, 28, 33–34](https://www.budget.gouv.cd/wp-content/uploads/budget2026/lf_2026_recettes.pdf).

These amounts do not certify the entire penalty assessment. The general late-payment interest, additional tax, procedure for disputing an assessment and interaction between concurrent defaults still need current-law verification. They remain outside the supplied computation.

## Cross-border points

Article 53 removes the dependence/control condition for transfers to enterprises in a privileged-tax or non-cooperative foreign jurisdiction. Article 47's privileged-tax test compares the foreign profit/income tax amount with the amount that would arise under ordinary Congolese IS if the enterprise were operated there: foreign tax must be **more than half lower**. It is an amount-based statutory comparison, not a blanket residence-country headline-rate shortcut. Article 48 requires the Congolese ministerial non-cooperative list. [Law 23/053, arts. 47–48, 53](https://dgi.gouv.cd/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/loi-is-et-Irpp.pdf).

Withholding, foreign permanent-establishment attribution and any treaty or mutual-agreement relief are separate work. Do not assume an arm's-length price supplies a withholding exemption, treaty residence, a corresponding foreign adjustment or CbC exchange relief. This Guide does not determine those results.

## Two worked examples that recompute

### 1. Supported independent sales price

Assume an article 53 cross-border controlled sale of 10,000 identical units. Actual revenue is CDF 980 per unit. A completed comparability analysis, taken as an input to this example, supports CDF 1,000 per unit on the same terms. The identified transfer is CDF 200,000. If the other conditions for the adjustment are established, add that amount to the recorded result. The example proves arithmetic, not that a real transaction's comparable is acceptable. [Law 23/053, art. 53](https://dgi.gouv.cd/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/loi-is-et-Irpp.pdf).

```oa-check
name: Controlled sale compared with an independently supported price
inputs:
  units: 10000
  actual_unit_price: 980
  supported_independent_unit_price: 1000
  recorded_profit: 5000000
steps:
  recorded_revenue: units * actual_unit_price
  independent_revenue: units * supported_independent_unit_price
  identified_profit_transfer: independent_revenue - recorded_revenue
  adjusted_profit_before_other_tax_rules: recorded_profit + identified_profit_transfer
expected:
  recorded_revenue: 9800000
  independent_revenue: 10000000
  identified_profit_transfer: 200000
  adjusted_profit_before_other_tax_rules: 5200000
```

### 2. Own figures below the threshold, qualifying foreign parent

Assume a Congolese enterprise has annual turnover of CDF 6 billion and gross assets of CDF 8 billion. At year end a foreign enterprise majority-holds it, directly, and has gross assets of exactly CDF 10 billion. The local enterprise fails its own-size test but meets the upstream ownership test. Prepare the files and annual declaration; choosing only the local-size route would give the wrong filing decision. The second calculation shows a conditional documentation-default exposure **only if** an unanswered/incomplete formal notice covers CDF 8 billion of transactions for that audited year. The percentage amount exceeds the statutory minimum. It is not the annual-declaration penalty. [Finance Act 25/060, arts. 25, 34](https://www.budget.gouv.cd/wp-content/uploads/budget2026/lf_2026_recettes.pdf).

```oa-check
name: Parent threshold equality and conditional documentation default
inputs:
  own_turnover: 6000000000
  own_gross_assets: 8000000000
  parent_gross_assets: 10000000000
  documentation_threshold: 10000000000
  affected_transactions: 8000000000
  penalty_rate: 0.02
  minimum_penalty: 100000000
steps:
  own_size_shortfall: documentation_threshold - max(own_turnover, own_gross_assets)
  parent_threshold_margin: parent_gross_assets - documentation_threshold
  percentage_penalty: affected_transactions * penalty_rate
  conditional_documentation_penalty: max(percentage_penalty, minimum_penalty)
expected:
  own_size_shortfall: 2000000000
  parent_threshold_margin: 0
  percentage_penalty: 160000000
  conditional_documentation_penalty: 160000000
```

## Ask the client first

1. What entity, income year, tax residence and actual Congolese operations are involved?
2. What are the direct/indirect capital, votes, actual control and common-management relationships at year end?
3. What are the enterprise's and relevant parent/subsidiaries' annual turnover excluding taxes and **gross** assets?
4. What controlled transactions, contracts, actual functions/risks, pricing evidence and preferential-jurisdiction payments exist?
5. Is there an audit notice, precise information request or annual declaration already filed, and on what dates?
6. What is the ultimate parent's prior-year consolidated turnover, filing jurisdiction, designated CbC filer and exchange/notification evidence?
