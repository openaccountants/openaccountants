# Italy: tax pack

Calendar tax year for individuals; companies are taxed on their financial year. Authority: Agenzia delle Entrate; social contributions are collected by INPS. Euro. Individuals pay progressive IRPEF plus regional and municipal surcharges; companies pay IRES at 24% plus IRAP, a regional tax on the value of production at a standard 3.9% that regions may vary. VAT (IVA) follows the EU rules, with mandatory e-invoicing through the SdI. From 1 January 2027 the income tax, payment and penalty rules move into new consolidated codes (D.Lgs. 117/2026, D.Lgs. 33/2025, D.Lgs. 173/2024), with new article numbers.
