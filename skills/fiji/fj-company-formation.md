# Fiji Company Formation and Entity Choice v0.1

## Scope

Use this for choosing and registering a Fiji company and setting up its first compliance calendar. It does not incorporate a sole trader, partnership, trust or foreign branch by treating it as a local company. Those structures remain alternatives, with their own registration and liability rules. A public company can raise funds only under the applicable offering rules, not merely because it has registered as public. [Companies Act 2015, sections 14-16 and Part 22](https://www.parliament.gov.fj/wp-content/uploads/2017/03/Act-No-3-Companies.pdf?ver=1794354122).

## Ask the client first

Ask for the activity and licences, whether any owner is a foreign investor, every proposed member and ultimate natural-person owner, share classes and transfer restrictions, directors and ordinary residence, proposed secretary, registered office and principal business address, articles, financial year, group revenue and intended public fundraising. Obtain identity and consent documents and establish who may lodge the application.

## The method, step by step

1. **Choose the structure.** For a private company, test the articles against all three section 16 restrictions. A public company includes a company limited by shares that does not qualify as private, a listed company, a company limited by guarantee, a company limited by shares and guarantee, or an unlimited-liability company. Do not describe a branch, partnership, sole trader or trust as a private company. [Companies Act, sections 14-16](https://www.parliament.gov.fj/wp-content/uploads/2017/03/Act-No-3-Companies.pdf?ver=1794354122).
2. **Check and choose the name.** Follow the Registrar's Entity Name Search and name-reservation service. Section 25 excludes identical, unacceptable and undesirable names; section 26 sets the legal ending. Do not promise that the reservation lasts for the period quoted on an old checklist: the Act and current webpage differ, as recorded below. [Registrar's company service](https://www.justice.gov.fj/company/); [Companies Act, sections 25-27](https://www.parliament.gov.fj/wp-content/uploads/2017/03/Act-No-3-Companies.pdf?ver=1794354122).
3. **Prepare the application.** Use the Registrar's current company-registration service and Form A2 checklist. Record the company type and name, directors, members and share details, registered office and principal business street addresses. Provide the officeholder identity and TIN documents requested by the Registrar. Lodge proposed articles unless adopting the permitted standard articles without amendment; identify any modifications. Have the required consents before lodging and retain them in the registered company. [Companies Act, section 20](https://www.parliament.gov.fj/wp-content/uploads/2017/03/Act-No-3-Companies.pdf?ver=1794354122); [Registrar's company service, registration checklist](https://www.justice.gov.fj/company/).
4. **Check foreign investment separately.** The Investment Act requires company/business registration, any other licences and relevant immigration permission, and reporting to the investment agency. It repealed the Foreign Investment Act 1999 and removed the former certificate attachments from the company forms. Do not require a newly issued Foreign Investment Registration Certificate simply because the Registrar's webpage still mentions it. Existing certificates continue according to their terms and cannot be extended under the transitional provision. Check reserved and restricted activities before establishment; exact investment amounts and reporting deadlines are held below pending the regulations. [Investment Act 2021, sections 5-6 and 23-25](https://www.parliament.gov.fj/wp-content/uploads/2021/06/Act-5-Investment.pdf?ver=1794354122); [Investment Fiji, change from 1 August 2022](https://www.investmentfiji.org.fj/article/investment-fiji-embarks-on-a-new-journey).
5. **Lodge and retain the result.** Submit the accurate application and documents through the Registrar's linked digital service, using its displayed fee and payment reference. Do not quote the old webpage fee as the current payable amount. Once approved, download and retain the registration certificate; registration creates the body corporate, and the certificate records company type and member liability. [Companies Act, sections 20-23](https://www.parliament.gov.fj/wp-content/uploads/2017/03/Act-No-3-Companies.pdf?ver=1794354122); [Registrar's company service, steps 2-3](https://www.justice.gov.fj/company/).
6. **Set up tax and employment obligations.** Establish the company's FRCS TIN and tax-service registrations through the current official service. Load [corporate income tax](fj-corporate-income-tax.md), [VAT](fiji-vat.md) and, before hiring, [payroll and FNPF](fj-payroll-social.md). Registration alone does not settle whether VAT, PAYE or FNPF applies. Exact TIN application fields are not confirmed in this skill.
7. **Keep company and ownership records.** Maintain the member register and notify relevant changes under sections 82 and 89-90. Separately, the Registrar or Reserve Bank may require the beneficial-owner particulars under the 2026 Companies Regulations. This is distinct from the FRCS special register and notification deadlines in the table. Do not assume there is a universal percentage threshold for identifying a natural person exercising ultimate effective control. [Companies Act](https://www.parliament.gov.fj/wp-content/uploads/2017/03/Act-No-3-Companies.pdf?ver=1794354122); [LN 53/2026, regulations 2-3](https://www.justice.gov.fj/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/LN-53-Companies-Amendment-Regulations-2026.pdf); [TAA sections 37O-37Q inserted by Act 13/2026](https://www.parliament.gov.fj/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/Act-13-Tax-Administration-Budget-Amendment-Act-2026.pdf?ver=1794354122).
8. **Build the annual calendar.** Record the registration anniversary fee and income-tax return. Small-company status is not a general exemption from accounts, tax or ownership records: member/Registrar directions and foreign control can trigger financial reporting. Public and large private companies prepare financial statements and directors' reports; medium private companies prepare proforma statements, subject to member direction for full statements. Charitable-company reporting needs a separate check of the 2020 amendment and its appointed commencement notice, held below. Apply the reporting/audit provisions and amendments to the actual structure rather than treating the size test as an audit opinion. [Companies Act, sections 19, 24 and 388-393](https://www.parliament.gov.fj/wp-content/uploads/2017/03/Act-No-3-Companies.pdf?ver=1794354122); [2016 amendment, sections 14-17](https://www.parliament.gov.fj/wp-content/uploads/2017/03/Act-3-Companies-Amendment-Act.pdf?ver=1794354122); [2020 amendment, section 2](https://www.parliament.gov.fj/wp-content/uploads/2020/09/Act-33-Companies-Amendment.pdf?ver=1794354122).

## Worked cases

**Revenue boundary.** A private company has FJD 4,000,000 revenue and a controlled entity has FJD 1,000,000, assumed to be the consolidated amounts after appropriate eliminations. Total FJD 5,000,000 meets the section 19 small-company revenue boundary, which is inclusive. This calculation alone does not remove foreign-control, direction or charitable-company reporting requirements. [Companies Act, sections 19 and 388-389](https://www.parliament.gov.fj/wp-content/uploads/2017/03/Act-No-3-Companies.pdf?ver=1794354122).

```oa-check
name: Fiji small-company inclusive revenue boundary
inputs:
  company_revenue: 4000000
  controlled_entity_revenue: 1000000
steps:
  consolidated_revenue: company_revenue + controlled_entity_revenue
  excess_over_small_limit: max(consolidated_revenue - 5000000, 0)
expected:
  consolidated_revenue: 5000000
  excess_over_small_limit: 0
```

## When to refuse or refer

Do not lodge without owner identity, valid consents, the required resident officers or authority to act. Do not promise unrestricted foreign ownership, a zero capital requirement, eligibility for an investment waiver, automatic limited liability for another structure, or exemption from financial reporting merely from turnover. Refer public fundraising, foreign branches, regulated activities, trust structures, nominee/control disputes and special tax incentives to the corresponding statutory process before proceeding.
