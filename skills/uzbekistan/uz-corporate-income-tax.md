# Uzbekistan Corporate Income Tax v0.1

> **General reference only.** Produce an estimated computation and review brief, not a filed return or an accountant's approval. All monetary computations use UZS; obtain the actual period, entity, regime, income, deductions and payments before calculating.

## Who this applies to

Covers an Uzbek resident legal entity on the general profit-tax regime, with routing for foreign legal entities' permanent establishments (PEs), Uzbek-source nonresident income and turnover-tax alternatives. Article 294 also covers individual entrepreneurs exceeding the applicable general-regime boundary or electing profit tax, consolidated-group responsible participants and simple-partnership responsible persons; these require the relevant special rules. Turnover-tax payers are generally outside ordinary profit tax unless a provision separately requires it. [Tax Code, arts. 294–296](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/4674902).

Ask first: resident company or nonresident/PE; sector and actual activity; tax period and regime history; income by source; documentary expenses; special relief; distributions; prior/current-year turnover; prior advances and withholding. Do not use a headline rate before classifying the payer and income.

Do not confuse the turnover/general-regime threshold with the separate UZS 20,000,000,000 advance-payment threshold.

## The base

1. Confirm the payer under article 294. A resident legal entity's aggregate income includes domestic and foreign sources; a PE uses attributable income/expenses under its special chapter; a nonresident without a PE may instead face gross-source withholding. Ordinary CIT is profit, not bank credits or turnover. [Tax Code, arts. 294–297](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/4674902).
2. Build aggregate income under the Code, normally excluding VAT/excise as prescribed, and reconcile it to the books and primary documents. Separate income with distinct treatment. Foreign tax credit, CFC, related-party and PE adjustments require their own supporting schedules; do not assume that an overseas bank account removes income from scope.
3. Deduct economically justified, documented income-related expenses under article 305, subject to the specific deduction/non-deduction and timing rules. Do not deduct creditable VAT twice or expense depreciable capital automatically. Carry forward losses only under the applicable chapter; no automatic loss offset is computed by this short skill. [Tax Code, arts. 295–296 and 305](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/4674902).
4. Apply the correct rate to each taxable base, on a cumulative year-to-date basis for reporting. Reconcile prior-period assessed tax, advance payments, withholding/credits and payments; the quarterly amount is not a second charge on the same year-to-date profit. [Tax Code, arts. 296 and 339–340](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/4674902).

### Exceptions that change the ordinary computation

Article 337 contains separate agricultural/fishery, social-sector, budget, disability-association, pension-account, renewable-electricity, high-technology and dividend rules. Do not apply ordinary CIT without checking whether the identified entity/income belongs there. Their eligibility is not established by its trading name.

First-time turnover taxpayers moving to profit tax starting in 2026 have the article 337 exemption for the **1 tax period following the transition year**, excluding dividend/interest income and reorganised taxpayers; do not claim the exemption in the transition year. The simplified base of **25% of aggregate income** refers to the specified transition category. A separate first-time income crossing above **UZS 10,000,000,000** after 1 September 2022 can allow a **50% rate reduction** for the current and following tax periods while the relevant periods' income does not exceed **UZS 100,000,000,000**. The Code excludes subsoil/excise taxpayers and liquidation/artificial income-splitting cases from the stated reliefs. Obtain the complete article-specific eligibility before using either relief. [Tax Code, art. 337 and neighbours](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/4674902).

The old export-profit 0% row was removed by ZRU-1014 effective 1 January 2025. An ordinary export does not itself make 2026 corporate profit zero-rated; VAT export treatment is a different tax. [ZRU-1014, amendment to art. 337](https://lex.uz/docs/7279923); [current art. 337](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/4674902).

## Deadlines and filing

For tax filing, payment and notice deadlines expressed in days, count working days unless the provision expressly says calendar days. If the final date is a statutory nonworking day, apply the next-working-day rule. [Tax Code, art. 5](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/4674902).

| Obligation | Deadline or method | Source |
|---|---|---|
| CIT period | Tax year is calendar year; reporting period is quarter. | Tax Code, art. 338: https://lex.uz/ru/docs/4674902 |
| Quarterly report/payment | By the 20th of the month after the reporting period; use cumulative profit/tax and reconcile earlier payments. | Tax Code, arts. 339–340: https://lex.uz/ru/docs/4674902 |
| Annual report/payment | By 1 March of the following year. Budget/non-state nonprofit organisations, except consumer cooperatives, have the annual-only reporting rule; no report where the statutory no-aggregate-income condition is met. | Tax Code, arts. 339–340: https://lex.uz/ru/docs/4674902 |
| Monthly advances when required | By the 23rd of each month. First quarter repeats the previous last-quarter monthly advance; later quarters use 1/3 of the relevant prior quarter's incremental assessed tax. No advance where the computed amount is nonpositive. | Tax Code, art. 340: https://lex.uz/ru/docs/4674902 |
| Current-year threshold crossing | Including newly created payers: start advances after the full quarter in which adjusted aggregate income crosses UZS 20,000,000,000 (in words on the page). A split/spin-off pays monthly advances for the 2 subsequent tax periods, regardless of income, if its predecessor calculated monthly advances in the tax period of reorganisation. Under article 338, tax periods are calendar years. | Tax Code, art. 340: https://lex.uz/ru/docs/4674902 |
| Revised anticipated-profit schedule | May submit by the 15th of the first month of the next quarter; the authority can correct unjustified understatement. Annual-only reporters do not make monthly advances; liquidation/cessation/revival have their own suspension/payment rules. | Tax Code, art. 340: https://lex.uz/ru/docs/4674902 |

Use the taxpayer's actual registration, return and payment history in its tax cabinet. Preserve the submitted return, acceptance record, payment reference, bank settlement and tax-account reconciliation. The precise current electronic form fields are not established by this skill; do not fabricate box numbers or treat a draft working paper as accepted filing. Apply the statutory nonworking-day rule to the calendar deadline, rather than assuming the named date is always a business day.

## Penalties and interest

Tax arrears attract statutory daily interest, in addition to tax and any applicable sanction. Article 110 uses **1/300 of the Central Bank refinancing rate effective at the time** for each calendar day from the day after the due date, with specified suspension/reliance and other exceptions. Retrieve the actual rate history and debt/payment dates before quantifying; no fixed daily percentage is hard-coded here. Violation-specific filing, withholding and understatement fines require the applicable provision, rather than a generic percentage. [Tax Code, art. 110](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/4674902).

## Cross-border points

Distinguish residents' worldwide profit, PE-attributable profit and gross Uzbek-source nonresident payments. For treaty relief, establish the applicable treaty, beneficial entitlement and residence documentation under articles 6, 354 and 358. A residence certificate alone does not create an exemption where the treaty's substantive conditions fail. Do not rely on the old unverified count of **55 treaties**; select the actual in-force treaty for the recipient's country. [Tax Code, arts. 6, 351–354 and 358](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/4674902).

No general treaty-rate table, full PE allocation, CFC, transfer-pricing or sector return is computed here. Corporate recipients do not inherit the individual share-dividend exemption. Separately investigate the actual share/bond character and statutory window before applying the article 483 exceptions.

## Two worked examples that recompute

### Example 1: ordinary profit and prior advances

Assume an ordinary resident company on the general regime, no relief, separately taxed income, losses or other adjustments. Annual Code income is UZS 2,000,000,000; admissible documented expenses are UZS 1,200,000,000; advances already paid are UZS 90,000,000. These are illustrative inputs, not eligibility thresholds. [Tax Code, arts. 295, 305, 337 and 340](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/4674902).

```oa-check
name: uz-cit-ordinary-profit
inputs:
  income: 2000000000
  deductions: 1200000000
  rate: 0.15
  advances_paid: 90000000
steps:
  taxable_profit: income - deductions
  annual_tax: taxable_profit * rate
  balance_due: annual_tax - advances_paid
expected:
  taxable_profit: 800000000
  annual_tax: 120000000
  balance_due: 30000000
```

Annual assessed CIT is UZS 120,000,000; the balance after the given advances is UZS 30,000,000. Do not pay the advances again.

### Example 2: rate category changes profit tax, not turnover

Assume UZS 1,000,000,000 taxable profit in an ordinary company, a qualifying row-2 mobile-service company and a fully eligible garment manufacturer meeting every UP-229 condition for the quarter. Inputs deliberately hold profit constant to isolate rate selection. [Tax Code, art. 337](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/4674902); [UP-229, para. 2](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/7283088).

```oa-check
name: uz-cit-sector-choice
inputs:
  profit: 1000000000
  ordinary_rate: 0.15
  mobile_rate: 0.20
  garment_rate: 0.02
steps:
  ordinary_tax: profit * ordinary_rate
  mobile_tax: profit * mobile_rate
  eligible_garment_tax: profit * garment_rate
  relief_difference: ordinary_tax - eligible_garment_tax
expected:
  ordinary_tax: 150000000
  mobile_tax: 200000000
  eligible_garment_tax: 20000000
  relief_difference: 130000000
```

The garment amount is UZS 20,000,000, not UZS 10,000,000. If its eligibility fails, this case cannot be used; recalculate under the applicable ordinary rule and any clawback.

## Ask the client first

Obtain legal form/residence and PE facts; tax period and prior regime; actual sector/products and relief documents; income and expenses reconciled to books; excluded/separately taxed items; prior-year adjusted income and current crossings; previous quarter assessments; advances/payments; withholding recipients, residency/beneficial ownership and treaty; share versus LLC distribution and bond type. Refuse a computation from turnover alone, a claimed relief without conditions or an assumed treaty count.
