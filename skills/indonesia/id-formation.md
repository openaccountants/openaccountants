# Indonesia Business Formation and Entity Choice v2.0

## Scope

Compare an Indonesian sole trader/UD, CV, ordinary PT, micro/small single-founder PT Perorangan and foreign-investment PT/PMA for a proposed 2026 business. Produce an evidence-based choice, ownership/activity/licensing map, capital plan and compliance handoff. This method does not incorporate a company, create an account, pay a fee or submit an OSS/AHU/tax application. It does not draft a full shareholders' agreement, give an immigration entitlement or guarantee a bank account.

Use [AHU](https://ahu.go.id) for the applicable legal-entity/business registration, [OSS](https://oss.go.id) for business identity and risk-based licensing, Ministry of Investment and Downstream Industry/BKPM for investment administration and [DJP](https://www.pajak.go.id) for tax. A registered legal person, a NIB, an effective sector licence, a bank account, a tax identity and PKP status are separate outcomes. Legacy names Kementerian Hukum dan HAM, Menkumham, SIUP/TDP/API-U, DNI and BKPM Regulation4/2021 remain useful to locate old records, not to establish current entitlement.

**Current framework:** PP28/2025 replaces PP5/2021 for risk-based licensing. Investment/BKPM Regulation5/2025 repeals Regulations3,4 and5of2021, with its own transition protections for existing permissions. The Job Creation changes are now in the UU6/2023 framework; UU11/2020 alone is not a sufficient current-law citation. Read the actual current sector/KBLI restriction and licence conditions before recommending an ownership structure. [PP28 art.550](https://jdih.kemenkeu.go.id/api/download/b71d04f2-c2be-4eaa-9637-c944ae584f46/2025PP028.pdf); [BKPM5 arts.391-400](https://jdih-storage.bkpm.go.id/jdih/jdih/2025Permeninvesthil005-.pdf).

## Ask the client first

Read the existing business plan, founder records, passports/identity evidence, cap table, shareholder agreements, actual activity/contracts, projected accounts and previous registrations first. Ask only for missing facts:

1. Founders/shareholders, nationality, residence, direct/indirect owners and beneficial ownership; foreign participation, actual funding and any nominee arrangement. Citizenship for the special domestic single-founder route is not the tax-residence test.
2. Number of founders and changes expected; age/legal capacity and any earlier single-founder incorporation in the relevant year; required liability protection and actual management/control.
3. Each real business activity and current KBLI, project location/city/regency and zoning/spatial rights (RDTR/KKPR), land/building arrangements, environmental and building approvals, product/sector regulator and intended operational start.
4. Available paid-up capital, wider investment budget, asset acquisitions and working capital; distinguish capital deposited in the company from total investment and revenue. Ask for evidence of the source of funds and existing licence/capital commitments.
5. Actual and projected turnover, costs/margins, assets, staff, customer mix, domestic/export transactions, related parties, losses and distributions; any tax elections/facilities and existing final-tax clock or connected businesses.
6. Whether a founder will work in Indonesia, who signs and opens the bank account, and whether the bank has provided its own KYC requirements. KITAS/RPTKA/foreign-worker permissions and full bank onboarding are specialist work, not a formation benefit promised by this method.

Do not choose a business code, ownership percentage, risk class, corporate tax regime or paid-up amount from the founder's preferred outcome. A software developer is not automatically eligible for final turnover tax and a low turnover figure is not a legal finding that an entity meets all micro/small criteria. Obtain the exact activity and status before computing tax.

### 1. Establish the legal form before tax optimisation

An **Orang Pribadi/UD** trades as the individual, not a separate limited-liability taxpayer. The owner's personal tax identity/income and business records stay distinguishable from a company. The original UD topics remain: trade-name use, possible local business documentation, NIB Perseorangan, personal liability, simple administration and the inability to sell company shares in a business that is not a company. A local Surat Keterangan Usaha or notarised practice is not automatically a statutory formation requirement. The precise current local documentation and bank requirements need confirmation before giving a checklist.

This domestic sole-business lane does not authorise foreign investment outside the permitted legal route. Investment Law art.5 allows domestic legal/non-legal/individual forms but ordinarily requires foreign investment through an Indonesian PT unless another law provides otherwise. Do not equate a foreign founder's tax residence or immigration status with permission to run any UD. [Investment Law art.5](https://www.pajak.go.id/id/peraturan/penanaman-modal). Use this route only when the actual domestic-founder/activity conditions are satisfied and the owner knowingly accepts liability. Avoid recommending it for liability-sensitive activity, foreign equity or outside fundraising merely because the forecast tax looks lower.

A **CV** is the commanditaire partnership with active/komplementer and silent/komanditer roles. Its active-partner liability, passive-partner protection and management powers require the actual deed and applicable partnership law; this tax/formation method is not a guarantee of limited liability or silent-partner immunity if they manage the business. Retain the notarial deed, actual AHU/SABU business registration, tax identity, OSS identity and licences. The exact current CV registration regulation, partnership capital rule and foreign-partner eligibility are not certified here; have the Indonesian notary confirm them before acting. The original “no fixed minimum capital” claim is an unresolved legal check, not a guessed cash requirement.

For income tax a CV falls within the statutory broad Badan category. The exclusion for distributions by the specified partnerships whose capital is not divided into shares is not identical to the rules for PT dividends. It does not exempt salary, disguised fees or every distribution under every instrument merely because the entity is called a CV. [Income Tax Law arts.2(1)(b),4(3)(i)](https://www.pajak.go.id/sites/default/files/2023-05/SDSN%202023%207.1_0.pdf). A domestic family business may value this form, but the active partner must understand liability and investors/counterparties may require a PT. Do not promise a universal cross-border recognition result.

An **ordinary PT** is a distinct legal person with shares and shareholder, director and commissioner organs. Record founders, legal ownership, deed, articles, management, approvals and actual legal-person registration. Limited liability has statutory exceptions; a shareholder's personal guarantee, abuse or unpaid subscribed capital is not erased by a company label. The normal multiple-founder route and the special eligible single-founder route must not be mixed. Refer deed drafting, governance and exceptions to the notary/advocate. [Company Law arts.1,3,7-15](https://www.pajak.go.id/index.php/id/peraturan/perseroan-terbatas-0); [PP8 arts.2-7](https://jdih.kemenkeu.go.id/api/download/680a952b-3f39-4e9c-86ea-4e4e1d1470e4/8TAHUN2021PP.pdf).

Founders do not obtain a universally capital-free company merely because the authorised capital amount can be agreed. PP8 requires at least one-quarter subscribed and fully paid and timely valid evidence, with sector-specific minima. Keep authorised, issued/subscribed and paid-up capital separate. For a PMA the special paid-up floor is a further constraint, not a per-KBLI subscription amount. [PP8 arts.3-5](https://jdih.kemenkeu.go.id/api/download/680a952b-3f39-4e9c-86ea-4e4e1d1470e4/8TAHUN2021PP.pdf); [BKPM5 art.26](https://jdih-storage.bkpm.go.id/jdih/jdih/2025Permeninvesthil005-.pdf).

### 2. Check the single-founder PT Perorangan route

Confirm one Indonesian citizen founder who is at least seventeen and legally capable, and the actual micro/small criteria. Electronic Indonesian-language **Pernyataan Pendirian** includes name/domicile, duration, purpose/activity, authorised/subscribed/paid capital, share data, address and founder/director/shareholder particulars. Legal-person status follows registration with the minister and the electronic certificate, not simply saving an online draft. The original benefit of no notarial incorporation deed on this qualifying route remains; it does not waive tax, licence, capital-evidence or financial-report obligations. [PP8 arts.6-7](https://jdih.kemenkeu.go.id/api/download/680a952b-3f39-4e9c-86ea-4e4e1d1470e4/8TAHUN2021PP.pdf).

The original one-new-single-founder-company-per-year restriction must be checked under the **current amended Company Law art.153E**, not attributed to PP8 art.6, which addresses nationality/age/capacity. **Not yet confirmed:** that current consolidated article was not obtained in this reading. Preserve the earlier-incorporation intake and stop a second-formation entitlement until the notary/AHU applies the current rule; do not silently discard the restriction or state it as a verified annual allowance.

Keep the actual change/conversion history. More than one shareholder **or** ceasing to meet the micro/small criteria requires conversion to the ordinary company route with a notarial deed and electronic registration. Do not invent a universal conversion grace period. Avoid starting here if the planned second investor or actual scale makes the shortcut unsuitable. [PP8 arts.8-9](https://jdih.kemenkeu.go.id/api/download/680a952b-3f39-4e9c-86ea-4e4e1d1470e4/8TAHUN2021PP.pdf).

Maintain annual financial statements and submit the electronic financial report within six months after the accounting period. It contains financial position, profit/loss and current-year notes; retain the electronic receipt. Non-compliance has administrative consequences including warnings, access suspension or legal-person-status revocation under the applicable procedure. This is different from the entity's annual income-tax return. No universal audit exemption is granted merely by the words PT Perorangan; sector/size/special-law obligations must still be checked. [PP8 arts.10-12](https://jdih.kemenkeu.go.id/api/download/680a952b-3f39-4e9c-86ea-4e4e1d1470e4/8TAHUN2021PP.pdf); [Company Law art.68](https://www.pajak.go.id/index.php/id/peraturan/perseroan-terbatas-0).

The micro/small capital/sales figures in the table are licensing classifications, not the IDR4.8b final-tax/PKP test. The current BKPM wording uses alternatives and particular land/building exceptions for property, accommodation, farming/plantation/livestock/aquaculture. Apply the relevant classification law for the actual formation and licence; do not mix one table's criteria with another regime to manufacture eligibility. [BKPM5 art.25](https://jdih-storage.bkpm.go.id/jdih/jdih/2025Permeninvesthil005-.pdf).

### 3. Map foreign ownership before a PMA recommendation

An ordinary foreign-investment company is an Indonesian PT with the actual foreign participation recorded, subject to Investment Law, current business-field restrictions, sector regulation and investment administration. Portfolio/special-law exceptions need their own analysis. Foreign individuals or foreign parent entities do not obtain an automatic single-founder micro-company shortcut. Establish the actual required shareholders, management, authorised signers and beneficial owners; do not fabricate a domestic second shareholder. [Investment Law art.5](https://www.pajak.go.id/id/peraturan/penanaman-modal); [PP8 arts.2,6](https://jdih.kemenkeu.go.id/api/download/680a952b-3f39-4e9c-86ea-4e4e1d1470e4/8TAHUN2021PP.pdf).

The Positive Investment List framework associated with Perpres10/2021 as amended by Perpres49/2021 displaced the older DNI framework. The original open/closed, cooperatives/MSME-reserved, partnership and conditional-cap concepts remain required checks. **Not yet confirmed:** the current applicable annex/sector amendments, ownership cap and any special investment conditions for each proposed KBLI. The annex could not be read from the attempted official download. Do not claim every sector is open to full foreign ownership, repeat an unchecked closed-sector example, or confirm that a particular software/retail/education/mining activity is unrestricted. Record exact actual activity, owner and location and obtain the operative current annex/sector rule before advising.

**Nominees are not a workaround.** Investment Law art.33 prohibits agreements/declarations that shares are held for and on behalf of another and declares them void. Refuse a nominee structure designed to evade restrictions; retain the actual beneficial-ownership record and refer to an Indonesian advocate. [Investment Law art.33](https://www.pajak.go.id/id/peraturan/penanaman-modal). An unsupported local shareholder on paper does not satisfy substantive ownership rules.

### 4. Separate PMA investment, capital and bank evidence

For the ordinary current rule, total investment must be **more than IDR10b** excluding land/buildings per five-digit KBLI per project location, while minimum subscribed/paid-up capital is **IDR2.5b per PT**. These are different tests, and a plan of exactly IDR10b is not “more than” that amount. A sector-specific statutory minimum can override the general capital floor. [BKPM5 art.26(1)-(2),(9)-(10)](https://jdih-storage.bkpm.go.id/jdih/jdih/2025Permeninvesthil005-.pdf).

Preserve all exceptions rather than treating the earlier special-sector note as merely a higher floor: wholesale uses the first four KBLI digits; food/beverage services use the first two digits per location point with the regency/city rule; construction uses the first four digits; products in one production line have a specific grouping. Property, accommodation, agriculture, plantations, livestock and aquaculture can include land/buildings in the investment criterion, with distinct property-project/unit conditions. Electric-vehicle charging has the provincial grouping; KEK cases refer to their own presidential business-field rule. Do not multiply or consolidate investment budgets without selecting the applicable article. [BKPM5 art.26(3)-(8)](https://jdih-storage.bkpm.go.id/jdih/jdih/2025Permeninvesthil005-.pdf).

The old IDR10m minimum **per shareholder** is not certified under the new rule and is not retained as a mandatory payment instruction. Check current share nominal-value/deed/sector requirements rather than inventing a replacement minimum. Retain actual subscription, proof of payment, authorised bank account and the source/use of funds; no nominee deposits or fictional capital injection.

Article27 provides a twelve-month restriction on moving deposited/subscribed capital out of the business account with exceptions for asset purchases, building construction and operations, a self-declaration and administrative consequences. The printed cross-reference points to art.26(6), although the explicit general paid-up floor is art.26(10). Preserve the exact text and obtain the applicable OSS declaration/notarial interpretation for this case; do not silently repair the law's cross-reference, promise unrestricted withdrawals or call all the money frozen against genuine operating use. [BKPM5 art.27](https://jdih-storage.bkpm.go.id/jdih/jdih/2025Permeninvesthil005-.pdf).

Existing permissions, renewals, location changes or expansion can have transition protections retaining earlier capital/investment conditions. A new lower general floor does not automatically authorise a capital reduction, release an old licence commitment or change the articles of an existing company. Establish the actual prior permission, validity, project and transition provision. [BKPM5 arts.394-395](https://jdih-storage.bkpm.go.id/jdih/jdih/2025Permeninvesthil005-.pdf).

Bank KYC is a separate task. Obtain the named bank's checklist for the actual directors/owners, source of funds, NIB, tax identity, deed and approval, and confirm lawful remote/physical/PoA options with that bank. The old four-to-eight-week bank and four-to-twelve-week PMA estimates are not verified service commitments. Do not guarantee remote foreign-owner onboarding, director attendance rules for all banks or a fixed capital-injection sequence that conflicts with lawful evidence requirements.

### 5. Run the actual activity and location through current OSS licensing

Under PP28, low, medium-low, medium-high and high risk determine different permissions. A low-risk NIB is different from an unverified certificate or a high-risk preparatory NIB. Basic spatial, forest-use, environmental and building/function requirements, plus supporting PB UMKU, may still apply to the actual project. The presence of a NIB does not mean every activity can operate immediately. [PP28 arts.128-133](https://jdih.kemenkeu.go.id/api/download/b71d04f2-c2be-4eaa-9637-c944ae584f46/2025PP028.pdf); [BKPM5 arts.185-186](https://jdih-storage.bkpm.go.id/jdih/jdih/2025Permeninvesthil005-.pdf).

- Low risk: NIB is the business identity and legal permission, with actual required declarations/basic conditions.
- Medium-low: NIB plus standard certificate based on the prescribed undertaking; meet operating standards and remain subject to oversight.
- Medium-high: NIB plus standard certificate; an unverified certificate allows preparation, while the verified certificate is needed for operational/commercial activity.
- High: NIB plus actual licence; before the licence, NIB supports preparation rather than full operations.

These four rules are from [PP28 arts.130-133](https://jdih.kemenkeu.go.id/api/download/b71d04f2-c2be-4eaa-9637-c944ae584f46/2025PP028.pdf) and [BKPM5 art.186](https://jdih-storage.bkpm.go.id/jdih/jdih/2025Permeninvesthil005-.pdf), not repealed PP5/2021. The precise risk class is activity/scale/location and current annex-specific. Do not certify a five-digit software code/risk class simply because the founder says “developer”.

Prepare the full ownership/activity/location data first; complete actual legal-person/deed registration where relevant; have the authorised person establish OSS access; select the real entity and current KBLI/project; validate the authority's risk/basic/sector requirements; obtain actual NIB and required verified certificates/licences; reconcile tax identity and business-location identifiers with DJP; and maintain the issued permissions and obligations register. Current portal menus, automatic NPWP integration success, current NIB format and BPJS enrolment completion must be observed or documented, not assumed from an old screen sequence. NIB is not proof the employer's BPJS accounts are active.

For PMA the original name reservation, Indonesian notary deed, legal-person approval, NIB, tax identity, bank/capital evidence, LKPM and sector-licence steps all remain checkpoints. For CV keep its actual business registration and active/silent roles. For PT Perorangan keep its statement/certificate route. Never substitute a historical “Izin Usaha equivalent” for the actual medium-high verification or high-risk licence.

Regulated financial activities need more than incorporation. Crypto/digital-financial-asset supervision is now with **OJK**, and OJK's January2026 announcement confirms the end of the Bappebti transition; the old claim that Bappebti is the current crypto regulator is obsolete. Payments and other financial activities require the actual OJK/Bank Indonesia or other sector permission. No fintech, lending, payment, crypto, education, mining or other specialised operation is authorised by a generic PT/NIB. [OJK transition announcement](https://ojk.go.id/id/berita-dan-kegiatan/siaran-pers/Pages/Nota-Kesepahaman-OJK-Bappebti-2026.aspx).

### 6. Choose and document the actual tax regime

An individual business uses the actual ordinary progressive computation unless it qualifies for an applicable final business regime. The individual annual untaxed IDR500m turnover portion is not an expense deduction, a PT allowance or eligibility for professional services. An ordinary PT, CV and PT Perorangan are Badan taxpayers, with their actual ordinary/final treatment determined separately from legal registration. [Income Tax Law arts.2,17,31E](https://www.pajak.go.id/sites/default/files/2023-05/SDSN%202023%207.1_0.pdf); [PP55 art.60](https://www.pajak.go.id/peraturan/penyesuaian-pengaturan-di-bidang-pajak-penghasilan).

PP20/2026 deletes the old universal three/four/seven-year eligibility grid and changes eligible persons, exclusions, owner/company aggregation, permanent exit and transition rights. A new ordinary PT/CV is not granted a new final-tax clock by copying its former type into a table. Qualifying individuals and single-founder PTs use the amended rule, cooperatives their stated period, and earlier ordinary PT/CV/firma/BUMDes rights only the exact transition. Professional-company and individual professional exclusions, foreign income and related-owner aggregation must be tested. Use `id-pph-final-umkm` with actual history. [PP20 amended arts.56-59 and art.II](https://www.pajak.go.id/id/peraturan/perubahan-atas-peraturan-pemerintah-nomor-55-tahun-2022-tentang-penyesuaian-pengaturan-0).

The new general-rate election is not an annual toggle: establish any earlier permanent exit and the current notice/effect. Never default every new entity into final tax. Compare lawful ordinary profit/expense/loss treatment and final tax only after both are available; a high-cost/loss business may not prefer a gross-turnover tax. The original low-tax recommendation for all solo developers was unsupported because activity eligibility was not established. [PP20 amended art.57](https://www.pajak.go.id/id/peraturan/perubahan-atas-peraturan-pemerintah-nomor-55-tahun-2022-tentang-penyesuaian-pengaturan-0); [PMK164 arts.5,16](https://www.pajak.go.id/id/peraturan/tata-cara-pengenaan-pajak-penghasilan-atas-penghasilan-dari-usaha-yang-diterima-atau).

For Article31E, compute the eligible taxable income in proportion to qualifying TURNOVER. Do not apply eleven percent to the first IDR4.8b of profit or mechanically stack a public-company reduction with this facility. For foreign shareholders, identify the actual dividend/beneficial owner/treaty requirements; the ordinary twenty-percent PPh26 statutory starting point is not a promised final treaty rate or a complete distribution analysis. Domestic individual/entity dividend exemptions and CV distributions have distinct conditions. [Income Tax Law arts.4,17,26,31E](https://www.pajak.go.id/sites/default/files/2023-05/SDSN%202023%207.1_0.pdf).

Tax holiday and allowance remain specialist alternatives, not automatic PMA privileges. The read PMK130 grants a qualifying IDR100b-to-under500b investment a half reduction for five years followed by a quarter reduction for two years; qualifying larger investments use full reduction and investment-dependent five-to-twenty-year periods followed by a half reduction for two years. PMK81's allowance provisions include thirty-percent investment reduction over six years, with separately conditional accelerated deductions, loss extension and foreign-dividend treatment. **Not yet confirmed:** the current 2026 application window and this project's eligibility/grant. Do not sell an expired application period as open or a maximum incentive as awarded. [PMK130 arts.2-5](https://pajak.go.id/id/peraturan/pemberian-fasilitas-pengurangan-pajak-penghasilan-badan); [PMK81 arts.407-418](https://pajak.go.id/en/node/113110).

VAT status is separate: ordinary non-luxury PMK131 transactions can use twelve percent on eleven-twelfths of price, while luxury and separately regulated supplies differ. Crossing the applicable turnover threshold requires the actual PKP process; a voluntary registration choice needs input-credit/customer economics and ongoing compliance, not simply “customers want invoices”. Withholding obligations depend on actual payer, recipient, type and transaction, not every article for every entity. Route to the VAT, payroll and withholding methods, taking account of recorded gaps in the protected withholding guide. [PMK131 arts.2-4](https://jdih.kemenkeu.go.id/api/download/f128868e-3cf6-4596-8407-c34eeca0e7be/2024pmkeuangan131.pdf); [PMK164 arts.17-19](https://www.pajak.go.id/id/peraturan/tata-cara-pengenaan-pajak-penghasilan-atas-penghasilan-dari-usaha-yang-diterima-atau).

### 7. Build the ongoing obligations and evidence register

Keep the deed or incorporation statement, ministerial certificate/approval and amendments, actual shareholders/beneficial owners, roles/authority, capital subscription/payment evidence, NIB, current tax identity/location record, basic approvals, standard certificates/verification, sector licences, bank authority/KYC and source-of-funds documents, BPJS/employer enrolment evidence, financial statements and accepted reports. A bank statement is not a legal-person approval and an AHU certificate is not an operating licence.

For medium/large operations, LKPM is quarterly with preparation versus operational/commercial phase, for each applicable activity/location; zero commercial sales do not by themselves remove a preparation-report duty. Small business is semiannual and micro has the stated exemption. Actual due dates, holiday notice, complete investment/workforce/production/conditions/problems and OSS receipt must be retained. Use the actual current rule and status, not the old shorthand “all PMA from day one no exceptions”. PMA is ordinarily large under the current investment rule, but obtain any specific exception. [BKPM5 arts.26,285-286](https://jdih-storage.bkpm.go.id/jdih/jdih/2025Permeninvesthil005-.pdf).

Calendar-year ordinary entity tax returns are ordinarily due by the end of April following year end; individual returns by the end of March, with actual extension rules separately checked. PT Perorangan's AHU financial report has its distinct six-month deadline. Employer payroll/BPJS, PPh agent returns, VAT and other obligations arise only where applicable and retain their own calendar. Registration is not the end of compliance. [KUP art.3](https://www.pajak.go.id/sites/default/files/2023-05/SDSN%202023%207.1_0.pdf); [PP8 art.10](https://jdih.kemenkeu.go.id/api/download/680a952b-3f39-4e9c-86ea-4e4e1d1470e4/8TAHUN2021PP.pdf).

A company may require a statutory audit under Company Law art.68, including specified public-funds/securities/public/state-company categories, assets and/or turnover of at least IDR50b, or another law. Do not grant a blanket UD/CV/single-founder exemption against a sector-specific rule. The exact applicability and any later sector/threshold change require the current law and entity facts. Related-party rules use PMK172/2023, not superseded PMK213/2016: keep the actual prior-turnover, related-transactions, lower-tax and group/CbCR tests, availability dates and disclosure obligations in the corporate/TP handoff. [Company Law art.68](https://www.pajak.go.id/index.php/id/peraturan/perseroan-terbatas-0); [PMK172 arts.16-19](https://pajak.go.id/index.php/id/peraturan/penerapan-prinsip-kewajaran-dan-kelaziman-usaha-dalam-transaksi-yang-dipengaruhi-hubungan).

## Worked cases

### Case1. Andi's developer comparison is conditional, not an election recommendation

Andi, an Indonesian citizen aged28, wants limited liability, expects calendar2026 gross IDR900m, has no employees and works from home in Jakarta. The earlier version asserted final-tax eligibility from “software developer” and quoted an automatic short company clock. That is not enough: identify the actual activity/professional exclusion, owner/other-company turnover, foreign income, earlier election/transition and micro/small legal criteria first. The old KBLI62019/risk-class assumption is not a verified code selection.

**Only if** the upstream final-tax method establishes both alternatives qualify, individual final tax is (900m minus500m)times0.5%=IDR2m; a qualifying PT Perorangan has no individual allowance and pays IDR4.5m; differenceIDR2.5m. This is a conditional comparison, not advice that Andi's actual professional income qualifies. If ineligible, obtain actual ordinary profit, deductions/NPPN and individual/PT tax. Limited liability, governance, licensing, costs, distribution and growth still matter. Illustrative authorised capitalIDR50m requires at least IDR12.5m subscribed/paid under the general25%rule, plus actual sector/formation conditions; it is not a PMA minimum. [PP55 art.60](https://www.pajak.go.id/peraturan/penyesuaian-pengaturan-di-bidang-pajak-penghasilan); [PP20 arts.I-II](https://www.pajak.go.id/id/peraturan/perubahan-atas-peraturan-pemerintah-nomor-55-tahun-2022-tentang-penyesuaian-pengaturan-0); [PP8 arts.3-6](https://jdih.kemenkeu.go.id/api/download/680a952b-3f39-4e9c-86ea-4e4e1d1470e4/8TAHUN2021PP.pdf).

### Case2. Paid-up PMA capital is not the investment-plan minimum

Assume a new ordinary PMA project with one five-digit KBLI and location, no special-sector/zone or existing-permission transition, a documented investment planIDR12b excluding land/buildings and paid-up capitalIDR2.5b. ## When to refuse or refer

- **R-ID-F1, nominees:** refuse a structure hiding foreign beneficial ownership to evade restrictions; document and refer under Investment Law art.33.
- **R-ID-F2, closed/restricted sector:** no incorporation workaround; obtain the current annex and actual sector permission before a favourable recommendation. Earlier example sectors are not a substitute for reading the current list.
- **R-ID-F3, immigration:** KITAS/RPTKA/foreign-worker permissions require current immigration/employment specialist review; a director/shareholder title alone is not a work entitlement.
- **R-ID-F4, banking:** no guaranteed foreign-owner remote onboarding or universal attendance/PoA rule; obtain the actual bank requirement.
- **R-ID-F5, regulated sector:** no operating advice for crypto/fintech/payments/lending or other regulated activity until the competent regulator and current permissions are mapped.
- **R-ID-F6, tax/substance mismatch:** refuse false turnover splitting, fictitious owners, nominee companies, backdating or a form chosen to conceal the actual business; document the real activity and lawful options.

### Conservative choices and deliverable

Do not incorporate by default. For a solo domestic founder compare individual liability/tax with a qualifying single-founder company; multiple domestic founders ordinarily require the appropriate partnership or multiple-founder company route; foreign capital requires the actual permitted investment structure. Do not default a new company into final tax, presume voluntary PKP is always cheaper or quote unsupported formation/bank/sector timelines. The old one-to-three-day/week and extra-two-to-eight-week estimates were not sourced legal completion guarantees and are removed as promises.

Produce: confirmed facts/missing facts; one recommended legal route with alternatives and reasons; actual ownership/KBLI/location/sector checks; separate capital/investment/source-of-funds plan; legal registration, tax identity, NIB/basic/sector permission checkpoints; bank/immigration referrals where required; actual tax eligibility and conditional comparison; ongoing AHU/tax/LKPM/payroll/VAT/TP calendar; required documents and named blocking unknowns. Preserve a current permission's transition basis and accepted receipts. No filing, payment, professional sign-off or accountant approval is invented.
