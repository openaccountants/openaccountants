# Libya Payroll and Social Contributions v1.0

> **General reference only.** This skill is general tax/accounting reference material for AI-assisted workflows. It has not been reviewed for any specific person's facts, documents, elections, deadlines, residency, filing status, or local procedures. Do not rely on it to file, pay, amend, or take a tax position without review by a qualified professional in the relevant jurisdiction.

**2026 research edition, incomplete currentness check.** The domestic rules below are transcribed from the Tax Authority's official 2016 compilation. Its live legislation catalogue and history still identify Income Tax Law 7/2010 and Regulation 592/2010. That establishes the source text, not a complete amendment chain through 2026. Use the calculations as statutory research working papers; obtain the applicable later instruments and current filing instructions before acting. This pack remains partial and has no accountant attestation.

## Who this applies to

Employers and taxable workers under Income Tax Law 7/2010 arts. 55-60, plus contribution participants. Public-budget administrative salary exemptions are addressed in [Individual income tax](ly-income-tax.md). Social Security Law 13/1980 art. 31 includes public employees, contract workers and self-employed workers; non-Libyan residents participate within regulatory limits and international agreements. Do not replace that conditional scope with "every expatriate always pays".

## The base

First classify taxable versus exempt employment and prepare the art. 55 income-tax base, including non-cash benefits and documented statutory exclusions. Obtain the family-status evidence and other-employer declaration before allocating the personal exemption. Income-tax bands and exclusions have one home in [Individual income tax](ly-income-tax.md).

For social security, art. 32 of Law 13/1980 distinguishes employees' actual salary/wage, including stable fixed regular allowances and financial benefits, from partners' and self-employed participants' selected prescribed income. A gross-pay label alone is insufficient. Obtain the current contribution regulation, participant registration, applicable total rate, salary-base classification and any agreement exemption. Only then allocate the confirmed contribution between employee, employer and treasury using the applicable category.

**Rate conflict requiring resolution.** The tax authority's 2016 compilation, printed p. 208, reproduces a historical 1991 table using a 15% total and different salary percentages from the legacy Guide's 5.125%, 14.350%, 15.375% and 1.025%. Neither an old table nor the old secondary-source Guide closes the 2026 rate chain. Do not insert either set into a live payroll. The allocation examples below start from an independently confirmed total contribution amount and do not assert a salary rate.

The separate Solidarity Fund contribution uses total income in its source. Its income-tax deductibility and current collection practice require a confirmed instrument; do not silently deduct it from the income-tax base merely because a deduction appears on the payslip. Salary stamp duty also requires the right schedule item and payment context; no universal net-salary rate is certified here.

## Deadlines and filing

The read Income Tax Regulation 592/2010 provides these routes, which replace the old unsupported ten-day monthly statement:

1. Create a worker file with appointment, pay, benefits, deductions and family-status support (art. 52). Complete Form 7 with employee names, taxable income, exemption and tax withheld. Initial statements and changes are due within 60 days of joining/entitlement or the change (arts. 52-53).
2. Deduct and remit taxable employment income tax within 60 days after payment of income, with the Form 7 statement (art. 53). This is the timetable in the read text; current amendments and any operative acceleration must be checked before setting a 2026 payment calendar.
3. If the foreign payer has no Libyan branch, agency or representative, or cannot be compelled to remit, the worker submits Form 7 within 60 days of joining/entitlement and pays within 15 days of receiving income (art. 54; Law art. 59). A foreign payer does not make the salary automatically exempt.
4. Submit against a receipt or registered-post acknowledgment and retain payment evidence under Reg. arts. 29 and 69. The public https://services.tax.gov.ly/ login page was inspected; private fields and payment references were not.
5. Treat social-security registration and remittance to the Social Security Fund as a separate liability and workflow. The current prescribed rate, forms, filing channel and deadline have not been established. Do not reuse the income-tax deadline for the Fund.
6. A bank's deposit-interest withholding uses Form 8 within 60 days of interest entitlement under Reg. art. 55; this is a bank duty, not an employer's salary deduction.

## Penalties and interest

Income-tax late-payment and withholding penalties are those in [Individual income tax, Penalties and interest](ly-income-tax.md), including Law art. 76's separate withholding/remittance rule. Social Security Law art. 41 permits recovery by administrative attachment and gives the Fund's claims priority after legal costs. Current social-security late-payment charges are unresolved: the Fund publication includes the actual Law 16/2013, arts. 1-3, repealing art. 46 and barring unpaid charges under that former provision, so an older automatic late-charge statement must not be reused without checking that chain.

## Cross-border points

For a non-Libyan worker obtain nationality, residence, social-agreement status, employer type and work location. Law 13/1980 art. 31 expressly makes non-Libyan coverage conditional on regulations and agreements. Tax treaty employment relief is separate from social-security relief. The UK treaty's all-condition employment test is in [Cross-border](ly-cross-border-tax.md).

## Two worked examples that recompute

**Allocate a confirmed national-employer contribution.** Assume the Fund has independently confirmed LYD 200 total contribution for the period and that Decision 182's national-employer allocation applies. Employee LYD 50, employer LYD 140 and treasury LYD 10 sum to LYD 200. The input is an assessed contribution, not an assumed percentage of salary.

```oa-check
name: Allocate independently confirmed national-employer contribution
inputs:
  confirmed_contribution: 200
steps:
  - employee: confirmed_contribution * 0.25
  - employer: confirmed_contribution * 0.70
  - treasury: confirmed_contribution * 0.05
  - reconciled_total: employee + employer + treasury
expected:
  employee: 50
  employer: 140
  treasury: 10
  reconciled_total: 200
```

**Employer-category decision.** On the same independently confirmed LYD 200 total, a non-national employer pays LYD 150 rather than LYD 140. The employee still pays LYD 50; there is no treasury allocation under that category. Determine the employer's legal classification before approving the extra LYD 10 employer cost.

```oa-check
name: National versus non-national employer allocation decision
inputs:
  confirmed_contribution: 200
steps:
  - national_employer: confirmed_contribution * 0.70
  - non_national_employer: confirmed_contribution * 0.75
  - employee: confirmed_contribution * 0.25
  - extra_employer_cost: non_national_employer - national_employer
  - non_national_total: non_national_employer + employee
expected:
  national_employer: 140
  non_national_employer: 150
  extra_employer_cost: 10
  non_national_total: 200
```

## Ask the client first

- What employer category, worker registration, nationality, work location and social agreement apply?
- What current instrument or Fund assessment confirms the total contribution, base, cap and deadline?
- Which pay, benefits, reimbursements, pension deductions and family-status documents support tax withholding?
- Are there other employers or prior personal-exemption claims?
- Who pays the income, where is the payer represented, and when was each payment received?
