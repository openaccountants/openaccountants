## Who this applies to

Founders choosing a Libyan LLC or joint stock company and foreign enterprises planning a branch. Company law and tax registration are separate approvals. The original Commercial Activity Law 23/2010 text below does not certify a current sector licence or foreign-ownership permission. The Commercial Register's 2023 amendment page was read; it changes competition-council provisions, not the capital rules discussed here. Other later decisions are not fully traced.

## The base

An LLC is not a public share subscription vehicle. Its interests are not shares under art. 271; art. 274 prohibits public subscription and loan-bond issuance. Article 273 excludes banking and insurance and permits other activity restrictions by the competent authority. The name must state limited liability (art. 272).

Article 276 requires a formal formation instrument with partner identity and nationality, name, registered office, purpose, capital/contributions, profit/loss rules and duration. All interests must be allocated; non-cash contributions need the JSC valuation rules through art. 277. Article 101 similarly requires a formal JSC contract, corporate purpose, subscribed and paid capital, board and supervisory body particulars and duration. Do not treat the JSC hypothetical capital in an example as the operative minimum.

Keep legal formation, sector approval, commercial registration and tax notification as separate tasks. Form 1 of the tax regulations asks for taxpayer/trade name, national ID or passport/residence reference, legal form, business purpose, main address, contact details, establishment/start date, legal representative and commercial-register number/date. It does not itself establish a complete incorporation checklist.

LLC governance includes an annual general meeting during the three months after financial year-end (art. 284), partner and decision records (art. 289), and balance-sheet preparation (art. 290). A supervisory body is required by art. 288 if capital exceeds LYD 100,000. Use [Corporate income tax](ly-corporate-income-tax.md) for the certified tax return and tax-clearance steps. The old automatic claim that every company needs the same audited annual accounts is broader than the formation provisions read here.

## Deadlines and filing

1. Confirm founders, nationality, sector and whether the vehicle is an LLC, JSC or foreign branch. Obtain the applicable later capital, foreign-participation and licensing decisions before finalising documents or funding.
2. Have the prescribed formal instrument prepared with the statutory contents. Confirm cash payment and any independent valuation under arts. 101, 104 and 275-277, according to the vehicle and capital level. Commercial Register authority: https://www.cca.gov.ly/ . Current name reservation, document upload, chamber registration and branch-approval screens were not tested.
3. Obtain the commercial-register evidence and the relevant business authorisation. Do not promise the legacy four-to-twelve-week formation estimate.
4. Notify the tax office on Form 1 under Reg. 592 art. 2. Complete the identity, legal form, business purpose, address, activity-start date, representative and register-reference labels in the printed form. The company representative/branch manager is responsible for notification. Retain receipt evidence under Reg. art. 69.
5. Set up accounting and separate corporate and payroll compliance. Tax return/certification timing is owned by [Corporate income tax](ly-corporate-income-tax.md); worker and contribution controls are in [Payroll](ly-payroll-social.md). Stamp treatment of cash capital and instruments is in [VAT status and transaction stamp duties](ly-vat-gst.md). Do not reuse a formation payment as proof that tax registration or every licence is complete.

## Penalties and interest

Commercial Activity Law art. 102 makes persons acting in a JSC's name before registration jointly and unlimitedly liable unless the company assumes the obligations under the statutory mechanism. An LLC name that omits its limited-liability designation exposes managers to compensation for resulting harm (art. 272). Tax notification and record penalties are separate and linked in the income-tax Guides. Current registrar fees and statutory penalties for other formation breaches have not been established.

## Cross-border points

Foreign participation and branch activity need the current sector and ownership instruments. The legacy Decree 944/2022 and Decree 207/2012 claims have not been checked against their actual operative texts and amendments. A foreign branch is nevertheless within the tax scope described in Law 7/2010 art. 63. Incorporation does not settle residence or treaty eligibility; consult [Cross-border](ly-cross-border-tax.md).

## Two worked examples that recompute

**LLC capital allocation working paper.** Assume a locally permitted LLC with LYD 4,000 agreed capital, no later capital requirement and two equal founders. At LYD 20 per interest it has 200 equal interests, 100 for each founder, LYD 2,000 each. Full payment is assumed because the JSC-threshold exception is not engaged. This tests the original statute, not approval of the current minimum.

```oa-check
name: LLC equal-interest allocation under original statutory text
inputs:
  capital: 4000
  interest_value: 20
  founders: 2
steps:
  - interests: capital / interest_value
  - interests_each: interests / founders
  - cash_each: capital / founders
  - total_cash: cash_each * founders
expected:
  interests: 200
  interests_each: 100
  cash_each: 2000
  total_cash: 4000
```

**Payment-structure decision.** Assume a competent adviser confirms a JSC can be formed with LYD 1,000,000 cash capital. The art. 101 minimum paid portion is LYD 300,000, leaving LYD 700,000 to be paid under the applicable terms. Compare full funding with the minimum paid portion: LYD 700,000 is deferred, not forgiven. LYD 1,000,000 is an assumption, not a sourced JSC minimum, and no five-year payment limit is inferred here.

```oa-check
name: JSC confirmed capital minimum payment versus full funding
inputs:
  confirmed_subscribed_cash: 1000000
steps:
  - minimum_initial_payment: confirmed_subscribed_cash * 0.30
  - unpaid_commitment: confirmed_subscribed_cash - minimum_initial_payment
  - full_initial_payment: confirmed_subscribed_cash
  - cash_difference: full_initial_payment - minimum_initial_payment
expected:
  minimum_initial_payment: 300000
  unpaid_commitment: 700000
  cash_difference: 700000
```

## Ask the client first

- Who are the founders, their nationalities, intended interests and legal vehicle?
- What activities, municipality and foreign-participation or sector approvals apply?
- Which current instrument sets the operative capital and cash-payment requirements?
- What contributions are cash, property or rights, and how are they valued?
- What start date, registered address, commercial-register reference and representative can be documented?
