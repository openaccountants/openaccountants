## Who this applies to

Natural persons and the specified partnerships with Libyan-source income under Law 7/2010 arts. 1 and 34-62. Company-taxed profits and their distributions are outside this chapter under art. 34, subject to art. 64. Employees, traders and professionals use different bases and collection routes. Oil-law cases and special investment approvals require their own instruments; art. 100 preserves petroleum legislation.

## The base

Classify each source separately under Law 7/2010 art. 35. The old salary-only description was wrong. Article 1 brings Libyan-source activity and assets into charge; foreign income enters only where the law specifies it. Article 33 exempts, among other categories, savings-account income, pure agriculture, specified pensions, foreign income of Libyans and resident foreigners, and income from work of employees in public administrative units financed by the public budget. Prove that the exact exemption covers the pay or income; being connected with government is not the statutory test.

For a trader, deduct evidenced costs of earning income under art. 39. Do not deduct personal/family spending, the proprietor's own salary, or enduring capital improvements as revenue expenses (art. 40). Capital assets use regulatory depreciation; formation costs are spread over five years (Reg. arts. 35-39). Bad-debt recoveries are income. Qualifying charitable gifts are capped at 2% of net income before the gift deduction (Law art. 39(e); Reg. art. 38). Losses carry into the following five years; a purchaser does not inherit the seller's losses (Law art. 42; Reg. art. 40). Independent professionals report receipts including advances and recoveries and actual earning costs (Reg. art. 50).

Employment includes money and benefits for Libyan services, plus the specified overseas-service case in art. 55. Exclude the employee's qualifying pension contribution, actual reimbursed work expenses, disciplinary deductions, accumulated-leave cash at termination and termination gratuity (art. 55); employer pension payments are outside the employee's base (art. 56). Article 36 also exempts qualifying life-insurance premiums for a spouse or dependants, general insurance premiums for the taxpayer, and health-insurance premiums. Apply the personal exemption once, to the lowest-taxed source if several sources exist. Family changes apply from the following month (arts. 36-37). Do not subtract the exemption separately in every job. Payroll-specific contributions are owned by [Payroll and social contributions](ly-payroll-social.md).

A disposal of business assets uses sale proceeds less depreciated cost; substitute market value if proceeds are below market, including the statutory legal-form-change rule (art. 45).

## Deadlines and filing

1. Identify the taxpayer, income category, accounting period and responsible tax office. Individuals generally use the calendar tax year; a different accounting year needs the art. 22 conditions and approval.
2. Traders and craftspeople prepare Form 4, professionals Form 5, including losses and supporting records, within 90 days after year-end (Law art. 38; Reg. art. 33). Partnerships need the prescribed accountant certification (Law art. 49; Reg. arts. 43, 50). Disclose other income and confirm the personal exemption has not been used elsewhere (Reg. arts. 34, 66).
3. Attach income and expense support. For regular business accounts, Reg. arts. 45-46 require the financial statements, depreciation and expense schedules and double-entry records supported by documents. Do not invent form box numbers: the printed forms use labels.
4. Submit to the office against a receipt or by registered post with acknowledgment, retaining Form 19 receipt evidence (Reg. art. 69). The public entry point https://services.tax.gov.ly/ redirects to login; current online form fields and acknowledgments were not inspected.
5. Pay under Law art. 19 unless a category has its own collection timetable. For assessment above LYD 100, the text provides four instalments with windows from the 10th to the 25th of March, June, September and December, beginning at the first window following the due date; up to LYD 100 is one payment. Obtain the assessment and current payment instructions; Reg. art. 29 requires an official Form 20 receipt. Employment and deposit withholding have separate routes, linked in payroll.
6. Notify cessation or a business transfer within 60 days (arts. 43-44). Business-transfer buyer and seller have the specified joint liability; obtain the authority's debt statement rather than assuming a clean transfer.

## Penalties and interest

Law 7/2010 art. 20 imposes a late-payment charge of 1% for each month or part of at least 15 days, capped at 12%. Article 72 penalises an unexcused failure to provide the listed return, notice or statement by an amount equal to the finally assessed tax. Article 74 separately provides LYD 500-10,000 for payment failure or negligent delayed remittance. Deliberate evasion attracts at least four times unpaid tax under art. 75; failure to withhold/remit attracts at least three times under art. 76. These are distinct provisions, not one interchangeable late fee. Cite https://tax.gov.ly/assets/m2016.pdf and obtain a local review of the facts and applicable enforcement instruments.

## Cross-border points

Nationality does not by itself remove Libyan-source income from art. 1. The employment source rule includes non-residents and the specified overseas services in art. 55. A foreign payer without a Libyan representative can move direct payment duties to the employee under art. 59 and Reg. art. 54. Treaty protection requires the particular convention and residence evidence, not a general foreigner exemption; see [Cross-border](ly-cross-border-tax.md).

## Two worked examples that recompute

**Employment working paper.** Assume a full-year single employee in taxable private employment, LYD 30,000 pay already net of all verified art. 55 exclusions. The personal exemption leaves LYD 28,200. The statutory bands produce LYD 2,220 income tax. Contributions and stamp classification must be settled separately.

```oa-check
name: Taxable employment after verified exclusions
inputs:
  adjusted_pay: 30000
  personal_exemption: 1800
steps:
  - base: max(0, adjusted_pay - personal_exemption)
  - tax: min(base, 12000) * 0.05 + max(0, base - 12000) * 0.10
expected:
  base: 28200
  tax: 2220
```

**Classification decision.** A full-year single person has LYD 50,000 evidenced receipts and LYD 20,000 deductible costs, no other income or losses. Compare a trading activity with a qualifying craft activity. The base after the single exemption is LYD 28,200. Trade tax is LYD 4,230; craft tax is LYD 2,820. The difference is LYD 1,410. The actual activity determines the category; this is not an election to call trade a craft.

```oa-check
name: Trade versus genuine craft classification
inputs:
  receipts: 50000
  costs: 20000
  exemption: 1800
steps:
  - base: max(0, receipts - costs - exemption)
  - trade_tax: base * 0.15
  - craft_tax: base * 0.10
  - difference: trade_tax - craft_tax
expected:
  trade_tax: 4230
  craft_tax: 2820
  difference: 1410
```

## Ask the client first

- Which income categories, legal entity and tax period apply, and where was each activity performed?
- What proves family status, dependent minor children and any change date?
- What other source or employer has already used the personal exemption?
- Which costs, pension deductions, benefits, losses and disposal values have documentary support?
- Is any pay public-budget administrative pay, or income covered by a specific exemption or treaty?
