## Who this applies to

Individuals working across borders, foreign payers, national companies and foreign enterprises with Libyan activity. Start with Law 7/2010 art. 1 source and statutory foreign-income rules, not a global resident/non-resident slogan. Article 33(10)'s foreign-income exemption for Libyans and resident foreigners must be read with the relevant taxpayer chapter; art. 63 expressly taxes national-company and branch income in Libya and abroad. Do not apply the natural-person wording to every corporation.

The detailed treaty example here is only the UK-Libya convention, listed by HMRC as in force. Other corridors require their actual text and effective dates.

## The base

For each receipt establish source, legal taxpayer, residence, activity, payer and beneficial owner. Libya-source services can be taxable even when the worker lives elsewhere. Article 55 additionally reaches specified work abroad unless the payer has the separate overseas organisation and accounts described there. Article 23 treats the manager/holder as representative where the taxpayer is non-resident or prevented from managing.

Under the UK convention arts. 1 and 4 establish treaty residence first. A person liable only on local-source income is excluded from the defined resident test. For dual-resident individuals use permanent home, centre of vital interests, habitual abode, nationality, then competent-authority agreement in that order. The original corporate tie-breaker is effective management; check any later operative modification before applying it.

For business, art. 8 restricts host taxation to PE-attributable profits, but art. 5 includes fixed place, construction and agency tests. Do not infer no PE solely because a project is short or a subsidiary is legally separate. For employment, art. 16 requires the whole short-stay test; directors, government service, pensions, artistes and independent services use their own articles rather than that test.

Investment-income relief needs beneficial ownership, the precise definition, connection to a PE/fixed base and the article's anti-purpose provision. The property-investment dividend exception prevents a blanket dividend-zero statement. Special relationships limit interest and royalty relief to the independent amount. The treaty does not create a domestic charge where none exists; a ceiling is a limit, not a tax rate to charge automatically.

Article 23 provides UK credit subject to its domestic-law conditions and generally Libyan exemption for income the UK can tax, with its property-investment-dividend credit and progression exceptions. Do not claim a universal foreign tax credit. Read the relevant paragraph for the item and taxpayer.

## Deadlines and filing

1. Collect dated residence evidence, travel log, workdays, contracts, payer residence and cost-bearing records. Identify the actual treaty and effective date. The tax authority's ET.pdf is an index of named conventions and entry dates, not a source of article-level relief or proof that no other treaty exists.
2. Compute the domestic liability using the income or company Guide, then document how the operative convention limits it. Keep the domestic calculation even where treaty relief removes the charge.
3. For an unrepresented foreign employer follow the direct employee declaration/payment route in [Payroll](ly-payroll-social.md). For a company/branch follow its certified Form 9 route. Current residence-certificate, treaty application/refund forms and online boxes were not verified.
4. Law art. 84 requires non-national taxpayers' tax-payment clearance before the specified exit authorisation; it includes company-responsible persons and an exception for foreign state/public-body employees except final departure. Reg. art. 60 identifies responsible managers/representatives. Current immigration administration and exceptions require confirmation; do not call this a mark-to-market exit tax.
5. Tax clearance request/issue Forms 16/17 and payment restrictions are linked in the corporate Guide. If double taxation conflicts with the UK convention, consider art. 26 competent-authority procedure alongside domestic remedies; no invented filing deadline or automatic refund follows.

## Penalties and interest

Use the domestic category's actual penalties and appeal rules from the income and corporate Guides. Treaty relief does not itself excuse a missed declaration or payment procedure. Separate treaty disagreement, unsupported residence and deliberate misstatement. Current penalties specific to a treaty-relief application have not been verified.

## Cross-border points

The UK corridor above is not a template of numerical treaty limits for Malta, France, India or the other indexed partners. Obtain each original convention, protocol and current status. Social-security agreements, stamp duties, customs and foreign-exchange controls have distinct scopes. ## Two worked examples that recompute

**UK short assignment.** Assume a UK treaty-resident employee spends 120 days in Libya in the relevant rolling period, has a UK-resident employer and no Libyan PE/fixed base bearing the pay. Employment pay attributable to Libya is LYD 20,000. If all art. 16(2) conditions hold, the employment article leaves LYD 0 of that pay taxable in Libya. This is a treaty-base allocation, not a full payroll exemption from every levy.

```oa-check
name: UK employee all short-stay conditions satisfied
inputs:
  libya_work_pay: 20000
  host_taxable_fraction: 0
steps:
  - libya_treaty_base: libya_work_pay * host_taxable_fraction
  - residence_state_allocated_pay: libya_work_pay - libya_treaty_base
expected:
  libya_treaty_base: 0
  residence_state_allocated_pay: 20000
```

**Cost-bearing decision.** The same UK resident spends only 120 days, but a Libyan PE bears the whole LYD 20,000 employment remuneration. The third condition fails. The domestic employment computation must now be done on the LYD 20,000 attributable pay, subject to actual exclusions and period exemption. Compare treaty-base routing with and without the PE cost charge: LYD 20,000 changes route. Day count alone does not decide relief.

```oa-check
name: Short stay with versus without host PE cost bearing
inputs:
  libya_work_pay: 20000
  fraction_when_all_conditions_pass: 0
  fraction_when_pe_bears_pay: 1
steps:
  - all_conditions_base: libya_work_pay * fraction_when_all_conditions_pass
  - pe_bearing_base: libya_work_pay * fraction_when_pe_bears_pay
  - base_difference: pe_bearing_base - all_conditions_base
expected:
  all_conditions_base: 0
  pe_bearing_base: 20000
  base_difference: 20000
```

## Ask the client first

- What taxpayer type, domestic residence and treaty-residence evidence apply for each period?
- Where were services, assets, management and project activities located, and for how long?
- Who is the payer, beneficial owner and actual bearer of remuneration or branch expenses?
- Which treaty, protocol, effective dates and specific income article govern?
- What local filing, clearance, foreign assessment and payment documents already exist?
