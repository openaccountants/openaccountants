# Libya Corporate Income Tax v1.0

> **General reference only.** This skill is general tax/accounting reference material for AI-assisted workflows. Do not rely on it to file, pay, amend, or take a tax position without review by a qualified professional in the relevant jurisdiction.

**2026 research edition, incomplete currentness check.** The domestic rules below are transcribed from the Tax Authority's official 2016 compilation. Its live legislation catalogue and history still identify Income Tax Law 7/2010 and Regulation 592/2010. That establishes the source text, not a complete amendment chain through 2026. Use the calculations as statutory research working papers; obtain the applicable later instruments and current filing instructions before acting. This pack remains partial and has no accountant attestation.

## Who this applies to

Law 7/2010 arts. 63-71 covers national companies and foreign branches, regardless of object, plus other legal persons carrying on commercial, industrial, craft or real-estate-investment activity under art. 64. Article 63 expressly refers to income in Libya and abroad and defines foreign branches broadly by their activity and capital. Do not replace that wording with an assumed corporate-residence test. Liquidation remains taxable until final distribution (art. 65).

## The base

Start with all operations and income in the statutory scope, using the selected cash or accrual basis permitted by art. 66 and the specific regulatory conditions. Deduct costs proved to earn that income. Maintain a tax reconciliation rather than applying the rate to turnover. Articles 39-45 apply through art. 71 where consistent with the company chapter: tax depreciation, qualifying bad debts and subsequent recoveries, employee pension contributions, activity-related taxes other than this income tax, restricted gifts, loss carryforwards and capital disallowances.

Capital improvements increase asset cost rather than immediately reducing profit. Use asset-specific straight-line rates from Reg. art. 35, not accounting depreciation copied without checking. Formation costs follow the table above. Loss use needs a year-by-year schedule; do not carry forward losses beyond the statutory period or transfer them to a purchaser without authority. Reg. arts. 40 and 58 govern continuity.

For foreign branches separately test head-office allocations and the ceiling in art. 66. The authority can estimate a branch's income as a proportion of the foreign enterprise's total revenue commensurate with branch activity under art. 67, subject to the comparable statutory computation condition. Outbound transport and telecommunications income has a special source rule in art. 68.

A legal-form change counts as cessation. A merger counts as a transfer: art. 69 taxes the difference between book value and the value represented in the new company's capital. Article 45 also supplies market-value disposal rules. Before a liquidation distribution or payment of other liabilities, the liquidator must settle the company's tax obligations under art. 65. Oil legislation is preserved by art. 100. Do not assume that an investment licence or listing automatically removes ordinary tax.

## Deadlines and filing

1. Establish the company's financial year: art. 71 uses its financial year, expressly departing from the general art. 22 year rule. Obtain the approved balance sheet date.
2. Prepare Form 9, signed by the legal representative and certified by a practising accountant/auditor on the Libyan register, within one month after approval of the balance sheet and no later than four months after financial year-end (Law art. 71; Reg. arts. 56-58). The trigger is balance-sheet approval, not an assumed audit-report date.
3. File one return for all activity even if exempt, loss-making or without regular books (Reg. art. 57). Attach income statement, balance sheet, expense/depreciation schedules and manufacturing-cost statement where relevant (Reg. arts. 45, 58). Foreign-company head-office statements can be required. Reconcile taxable profits, losses, allowances and exempt sources to the labelled Form 9 schedules.
4. Submit against an official receipt or by registered post with acknowledgment (Reg. art. 69); keep the authority's Form 19 receipt. Current electronic screens at https://services.tax.gov.ly/ were not accessible without login.
5. General assessment payment windows and the small-assessment rule are set out in [Individual income tax, Deadlines and filing](ly-income-tax.md). Obtain the assessment, due date and official Form 20 payment receipt (Law art. 19; Reg. art. 29).
6. Obtain tax clearance before affected contract payments and licence renewals. Law arts. 88-90 and Reg. arts. 71-72 govern this control; Form 16 requests the certificate and Form 17 is the issued certificate. Its statutory validity cannot exceed 60 days. The certificate is a compliance control, not a general percentage withholding tax.

## Penalties and interest

Use the distinct income-tax late-payment, non-filing, payment and evasion provisions in [Individual income tax, Penalties and interest](ly-income-tax.md), Law arts. 20 and 72-79. Accounting-record failures are separately subject to LYD 1,000-50,000 under art. 73. A tax assessment can be disputed within 45 days of notification under art. 5; the primary-committee filing fee is 0.5% of disputed tax, minimum LYD 10 (art. 8). The appeal period is 15 days with a 1% fee, minimum LYD 20 (arts. 11, 13). Keep notification evidence; a dispute does not automatically suspend payment (art. 10).

## Cross-border points

Do not assert zero withholding on every dividend, interest or royalty. Article 34 excludes distributions of company-taxed profits from the individual chapter, which is narrower than a complete cross-border withholding conclusion. Source, recipient status, treaty article and clearance conditions still need testing. [Transfer pricing](ly-transfer-pricing.md) owns related-enterprise and branch allocation analysis; [Cross-border](ly-cross-border-tax.md) owns treaty routing. Stamp, import-service fees and the claimed Jihad-tax abolition are separate questions, not components silently included in the ordinary company rate.

## Two worked examples that recompute

**Ordinary profit.** Assume ordinary company receipts of LYD 500,000, supported operating deductions of LYD 320,000, verified tax depreciation of LYD 20,000 and an unexpired eligible loss of LYD 30,000. The statutory working-paper base is LYD 130,000 and tax LYD 26,000.

```oa-check
name: Company profit with evidenced deductions and eligible loss
inputs:
  receipts: 500000
  costs: 320000
  tax_depreciation: 20000
  eligible_loss: 30000
steps:
  - profit_before_loss: receipts - costs - tax_depreciation
  - base: max(0, profit_before_loss - eligible_loss)
  - tax: base * 0.20
expected:
  base: 130000
  tax: 26000
```

**Deduct or capitalise decision.** An ordinary company has LYD 100,000 profit before a LYD 50,000 formation-cost item. Immediate expensing would leave LYD 50,000; the statutory five-year deduction is LYD 10,000, leaving LYD 90,000. The correct working-paper tax is LYD 18,000, versus LYD 10,000 under the rejected immediate deduction. This assumes the whole amount qualifies as formation cost and no other adjustments.

```oa-check
name: Formation costs spread rather than immediate expense
inputs:
  profit_before_cost: 100000
  formation_cost: 50000
  years: 5
steps:
  - annual_deduction: formation_cost / years
  - statutory_base: profit_before_cost - annual_deduction
  - statutory_tax: statutory_base * 0.20
  - rejected_immediate_tax: (profit_before_cost - formation_cost) * 0.20
  - understatement: statutory_tax - rejected_immediate_tax
expected:
  statutory_tax: 18000
  rejected_immediate_tax: 10000
  understatement: 8000
```

## Ask the client first

- What is the legal person or branch, and is petroleum or a specific investment approval involved?
- What financial year and balance-sheet approval date are documented?
- Which revenue, tax adjustments, asset classes and unexpired losses reconcile to the books?
- Which head-office or connected-party charges are allocated, and how are necessity and value proved?
- Which certificate, assessment, contract payment, merger or liquidation is pending?
