# Thailand: tax pack

Thailand uses Thai baht and a calendar tax year for individuals; companies use their accounting periods. The Revenue Department administers the taxes covered here. The seven updated files address 2026 and distinguish statutory rules from historical examples, working-paper settings and items still unconfirmed. They are not accountant-reviewed.
