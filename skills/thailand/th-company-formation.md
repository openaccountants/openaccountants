# Thailand company formation and entity choice v1.0

> **General reference only.** This skill is general tax/accounting reference material for AI-assisted workflows. It has not been reviewed for any specific person's facts, documents, elections, deadlines, residency, filing status, or local procedures. Do not rely on it to file, pay, amend, or take a tax position without review by a qualified professional in the relevant jurisdiction.

## Who this applies to

**2026 answer layer:** prepare a Thai private limited company formation and a comparison with partnership, public company, branch or representative-office alternatives. Do not treat incorporation as foreign-business permission or a work permit. Choose the activity and ownership route before submitting the registration.

A private company has at least 2 promoters. This is the amended Civil and Commercial Code §1097, not a rule effective merely when the amendment was signed: Act No. 23 B.E. 2565 §2 supplies the delayed commencement and §6 changes the promoter provision. Each promoter must take at least a share. The DBD procedure distinguishes promoters, shareholders and directors; they are not interchangeable labels.

## The base

### Entity choice and foreign-business permission

Keep the original alternatives in the decision: private limited company, public limited company, ordinary or limited partnership, branch and representative office. A branch is an operation of the foreign legal entity; a representative-office label does not authorise earning all forms of Thai revenue. Public companies, partnership liability and representative-office permitted activities need their specific law and registration requirements before recommending that form. The ordinary private-company workflow below does not substitute for them.

Under [Foreign Business Act §§4 and 8-14](https://www.boi.go.th/upload/Foreign%20Business%20Act_5dd766122ff27.pdf), first classify the real beneficial ownership and business. The definition of foreigner includes a Thai-registered entity with at least half its capital held by relevant foreign persons and certain foreign-managed partnerships; tracing and indirect holdings matter. List One is prohibited, List Two has its ministerial/Cabinet route, and List Three has the Director-General/Commission route. Treaty and investment-promotion routes require their own conditions and certificates. Check other sector-specific laws that prevail under §13. Section 14 does not apply where the foreigner uses money or property derived as revenue from an original business already operating in Thailand to commence another business, subscribe for shares or invest in another undertaking or juristic person. Establish that actual revenue/funding history before claiming this exception; it does not by itself remove a separate business-permission requirement. Section 14's stated floors do not establish the detailed minimum-capital regulation, remittance timetable, sector approval or employment/work-permit capital test.

### Preparation and registration sequence

1. Record the business activity, owners, directors, registered office, proposed names, capital structure and who will sign. Obtain the genuine ownership and funding evidence; a nominal Thai shareholding is not a substitute for the actual legal test.
2. Reserve the name and prepare the memorandum, with the name, office, objects, capital/share structure and promoters. Arrange subscription of all shares. The DBD one-day procedure permits the preparatory steps to be completed together; it is not a promise that every foreign-business permission will finish the same day.
3. Hold the statutory meeting, deal with the articles, appointments and establishment matters, appoint the directors and auditor as applicable, and hand the business over to the directors. Directors call for the initial share payments. Verify the actual payments and keep the evidence for the registration review.
4. Use DBD Biz Regist via the DBD website's registration route. Prepare the required documents, verify the authorised signers' identities through the offered ordinary process, sign the application, pay the portal's actual assessed registration fee, and retain the application, receipt, registrar's result and company certificate. This source check did not create an account or test a signed-in registration. The older one-day leaflet's walk-in/e-Registration channels, quoted fee discounts and service time are historical, superseded for current new-company channels by the rendered 2026 DBD notice.
5. After registration, establish the accounting records, shareholder register, bank mandate, tax and VAT position, employer registration and any separate activity licences. Load the [corporate tax](th-corporate-income-tax.md), [VAT](thailand-vat.md) and [payroll](th-payroll-social.md) methods for those duties. Do not assume company registration automatically completes them.

## Deadlines and filing

Accounting starts from the legal registration date for a Thai company. The first closure is within the statutory accounting period and later closures follow the recurring period, subject to authorised changes. [Accounting Act §§8-11](https://tfac.or.th/media/573076803015544832/2026/09/wSPof648jJbpV1Q0MyqkTYEx.pdf) requires annual statements and a certified auditor's opinion; the small registered-partnership exception is not an exemption for a dormant private limited company. No trading does not erase a company's accounting duty.

Arrange the audit and company approval in time for the approval and DBD filing periods in the table. Use DBD e-Filing for the statements and retain the signed auditor's report, approval minutes and filing acknowledgement. [Section 11](https://tfac.or.th/media/573076803015544832/2026/09/wSPof648jJbpV1Q0MyqkTYEx.pdf) allows a case-specific extension for an unavoidable cause; do not assume an extension. The separate PND.50 tax-return deadline is in [corporate tax](th-corporate-income-tax.md); it is not measured from the AGM.

## Penalties and interest

Late registration, deficient documents, unlawful foreign-business operation, missing accounts and late financial-statement filing have different consequences. Refer the specific offence and responsible persons before computing a penalty. This skill does not quote an unverified generic incorporation or dormant-company fine. Current filing fees, detailed sanctions and mitigation remain under Not yet confirmed.

## Cross-border points

Test foreign-business restrictions separately from immigration, employment permission and tax residence. The old THB 2,000,000 per foreign employee and THB 1,000,000 for a Thai spouse claims were vendor-sourced and are not usable work-permit rules here. A foreign investor's treaty, BOI status or listed activity can change the permission route and capital conditions; obtain the specific legal instrument before comparing entity costs. A branch's tax and profit-disposal obligations differ from those of a Thai company.

## Two worked examples that recompute

**Private-company share funding.** Hypothetical registered capital THB 100,000 consists of 20,000 shares of THB 5. If all shares are subscribed and directors call the minimum initial fraction, payment is THB 25,000 and the unpaid balance THB 75,000. This establishes the share-payment arithmetic only; it is not the funding needed for a foreign business, licence or work permit.

```oa-check
name: Thailand private company initial share payment
inputs:
  shares: 20000
  par: 5
  paid_fraction: 0.25
steps:
  - capital: shares * par
  - initial_paid: capital * paid_fraction
  - unpaid: capital - initial_paid
expected:
  capital: 100000
  initial_paid: 25000
  unpaid: 75000
```

**Domestic formation versus foreign-business capital screen.** Compare the hypothetical capital above with the statutory foreign-business floor for a new entrant carrying on a listed business requiring permission, with no qualifying existing-business-revenue funding under §14 final paragraph. THB 100,000 is THB 2,900,000 below that floor. Even THB 3,000,000 does not itself provide permission or prove a higher regulation-based amount has been satisfied. The decision is to stop the domestic-only incorporation budget and obtain the business-specific approval and funding plan.

```oa-check
name: Thailand foreign business capital screen
inputs:
  planned_capital: 100000
  statutory_floor: 3000000
steps:
  - shortfall: max(statutory_floor - planned_capital, 0)
expected:
  shortfall: 2900000
```

## Ask the client first

- Actual business activity, customer locations and intended revenue-generating activities?
- Owners, indirect ownership, nationalities, directors and signing authority?
- Private company, public company, partnership, branch or representative office, and why?
- Paid-up funding, evidence of subscription/payment, and any BOI/treaty/activity permission?
- Registered office, accounting year end, first transactions and expected employees?
- Foreign employees and their separate immigration/employment-permit position?
