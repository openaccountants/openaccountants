# Somalia related-party pricing working paper v0.1

> **General reference only.** This skill is general tax/accounting reference material for AI-assisted workflows. It has not been reviewed for any specific person's facts, documents, elections, deadlines, residency, filing status, or local procedures. Do not rely on it to file, pay, amend, or take a tax position without review by a qualified professional in the relevant jurisdiction.

## Who this applies to

**Statutory-baseline draft for 2026 preparation, not confirmed operative 2026 coverage.** The enacted Act was read, but Income Tax Regulations 0141/2025 and any transitional directions were not. Article 105(7) permits transitional measures that delay or modify provisions; article 37(3) leaves arm's-length exceptions and international-guideline choices to regulations. Prepare the working paper below, then stop before treating it as a final tax position until those instruments and the relevant period have been checked. An unread regulation is not evidence that there is no exception. [Act 37 arts. 37(3), 105(7), PDF pages 57 and 125](https://sag.gov.so/media/documents/bulletins-contents/legislation/2025/Sharciyeedka_Canshuurta_Dakhliga.pdf).

The statutory job concerns a **Somali resident person transacting with an associated non-resident person**. Article 37(1) requires the resident to compute income and tax using the arm's-length standard; article 37(2) refers to the amounts independent persons would calculate, characterise and allocate. Do not silently extend that specific rule to every domestic transaction, or treat a foreign address as proof of non-residence. Use the [residence working paper](so-cross-border-residence.md) for the separate article 77 screen. [Act 37 art.37(1)–(2), PDF pages 56–57](https://sag.gov.so/media/documents/bulletins-contents/legislation/2025/Sharciyeedka_Canshuurta_Dakhliga.pdf).

This is a working-paper job for ordinary goods/service transactions where the independent-party amount is supported. It does not select an unverified statutory method, prescribe a markup, approve a loan/royalty structure or prepare a local file, master file, CbCR or APA application.

## The base

Prepare these internal working papers. The labels are recommendations for organising evidence, not mandatory form fields or a statutory documentation checklist.

1. **Confirm period and the regulatory gate.** Identify the assessment year and any special accounting period, relevant transitional instrument and pricing exception before finalising applicability. Article 105 preserves earlier-period rules and permits additional transitional measures; this draft does not settle 2025 commencement or mixed old/new periods. [Act 37 arts.25,105–106, PDF pages 48,124–125](https://sag.gov.so/media/documents/bulletins-contents/legislation/2025/Sharciyeedka_Canshuurta_Dakhliga.pdf).
2. **Map the people and rights.** Record the legal entities, all relevant ownership chains, income/capital/voting rights, control arrangements, related holders and residency evidence. Test each article 4 limb. Below 50% is not an exemption when control, aggregation or another association ground applies. Record why the counterparty is associated and non-resident. [Arts.4,37(1)](https://sag.gov.so/media/documents/bulletins-contents/legislation/2025/Sharciyeedka_Canshuurta_Dakhliga.pdf).
3. **Describe the actual transaction.** Reconcile contracts, invoices, delivery/service evidence, payment terms, currencies and accounting entries. Explain the goods/services supplied and differences from the proposed independent comparison. Do not assume a group invoice proves that a service occurred.
4. **Support the independent-party amount.** Retain the actual independent transaction/quotation and its source, date, product/service, volume, market, functions, risk and payment-term facts. Explain comparability adjustments and why the remaining comparison answers the actual transaction. These are evidence recommendations for applying the independent-person standard, not a claim that a particular OECD method is legislatively selected. If no defensible comparison or regulation-confirmed approach is available, stop before asserting an arm's-length price. [Art.37(2)–(3)](https://sag.gov.so/media/documents/bulletins-contents/legislation/2025/Sharciyeedka_Canshuurta_Dakhliga.pdf).
5. **Compute the reconciliation.** Show recorded revenue/cost, supported independent-party revenue/cost and difference. An underpriced sale raises the income figure when replaced by the supported higher amount; an overpriced purchase reduces the cost figure when replaced by the supported lower amount. Keep the computation separate from the tax-rate, deduction-limit and loss calculations. Do not automatically reverse the difference in another entity's return. [Art.37(1)–(2),(4)](https://sag.gov.so/media/documents/bulletins-contents/legislation/2025/Sharciyeedka_Canshuurta_Dakhliga.pdf).
6. **Complete the review record.** Link each input to evidence; state the pricing conclusion, unresolved regulatory questions and which person must resolve them. A supported price does not itself establish deductibility, source, withholding or treaty treatment. Preserve the original and adjusted entries so an assessment can be traced to the books.

The Ministry's statutory adjustment power includes recharacterising the transaction and the source/nature of income, loss, amounts or payments, and allocating expenses based on turnover. It is broader than replacing an invoice's unit price. [Art.37(4)](https://sag.gov.so/media/documents/bulletins-contents/legislation/2025/Sharciyeedka_Canshuurta_Dakhliga.pdf).

## Cross-border points

The specific article 37(1) condition is a resident dealing with an associated non-resident. Establish residence rather than equating incorporation abroad with non-residence. Dual residence, treaty relief, PE attribution, corresponding adjustments and withholding require separate analysis; this draft grants no treaty or foreign-tax credit. [Arts.37(1),77](https://sag.gov.so/media/documents/bulletins-contents/legislation/2025/Sharciyeedka_Canshuurta_Dakhliga.pdf).

## Two worked examples that recompute

**Underpriced sale.** Assume the regulatory gate is resolved, the Somali seller is resident, its foreign parent is an associated non-resident, and a properly supported independent sale establishes a comparable price ofUSD 100 per unit for the actual terms. The seller recorded 1,000 units atUSD 80. The revenue reconciliation isUSD 80,000 toUSD 100,000, an increase ofUSD 20,000. The input price is fictional evidence assumed established, not a Somali safe harbour. No tax is computed.

```oa-check
name: Supported sales-price adjustment under statutory baseline
inputs:
  quantity: 1000
  recorded_unit_price: 80
  supported_unit_price: 100
steps:
  recorded_revenue: quantity * recorded_unit_price
  supported_revenue: quantity * supported_unit_price
  income_increase: supported_revenue - recorded_revenue
expected:
  recorded_revenue: 80000
  supported_revenue: 100000
  income_increase: 20000
```

**Association boundary and purchase direction.** Assume a non-resident holds 49% of the Somali buyer's voting rights and an associated holder holds another 1%; their aggregated 50% meets article 4(2). All other statutory and regulatory conditions are established. For 500 units, the buyer bookedUSD 120 each while the supported independent price isUSD 100. Replacing theUSD 60,000 recorded cost withUSD 50,000 raises income byUSD 10,000 before other tax rules. If only 49% were counted, the numerical threshold would not be met, but that would still require testing control and every other association limb. Do not apply the adjustment simply from the percentage without transaction evidence.

```oa-check
name: Aggregated association boundary and overpriced purchase
inputs:
  direct_voting_percent: 49
  associated_holder_percent: 1
  threshold_percent: 50
  quantity: 500
  recorded_unit_cost: 120
  supported_unit_cost: 100
steps:
  aggregated_percent: direct_voting_percent + associated_holder_percent
  threshold_margin: aggregated_percent - threshold_percent
  recorded_cost: quantity * recorded_unit_cost
  supported_cost: quantity * supported_unit_cost
  income_increase: recorded_cost - supported_cost
expected:
  aggregated_percent: 50
  threshold_margin: 0
  recorded_cost: 60000
  supported_cost: 50000
  income_increase: 10000
```

The checker verifies arithmetic. It does not prove association, comparability, regulatory applicability or the tax effect.

## Ask the client first

- Which assessment period, transactions and entities are under review, and is there an audit notice?
- What supports each party's residence, ownership, voting/income/capital rights, control and related-holder links?
- What contracts, delivery/service evidence, invoices and ledger entries establish the actual transaction?
- Which independent evidence supports price and terms, and what differences require adjustment?
- Which applicable regulation/transition direction and filing instructions have been obtained?
