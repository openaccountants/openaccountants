# Myanmar Corporate Income Tax v1.0

> **General reference only.** This skill is general tax/accounting reference material for AI-assisted workflows. It has not been reviewed for any specific person's facts, documents, elections, deadlines, residency, filing status, or local procedures. Do not rely on it to file, pay, amend, or take a tax position without review by a qualified professional in the relevant jurisdiction.

## Who this applies to

Companies registered under the Myanmar Companies Law or the Special Companies Act 1950, businesses operating with Myanmar Investment Commission (MIC) permits, State-owned economic organisations, cooperative societies and non-resident aliens with non-salary Myanmar income, for the financial year 2026-27 (income year 1 April 2026 to 31 March 2027). Rates: Union Taxation Law 2026 (in force 1 April 2026). Rules: the Income Tax Law. Both are published by the Internal Revenue Department (IRD).

**Residence (Income Tax Law s 3(k)).** A company formed under the Myanmar Companies Act or any other existing law wholly or partly with foreign shareholders is a resident foreigner; residents are taxed on income within and outside Myanmar.

**Out of scope, refer:** associations in oil and gas exploration and production (25% on net profit, and capital gains at 40%, 45% or 50% by size, UTL 2026 ss 24(a), 27(a)); undisclosed income (s 25); Special Economic Zone incentives; MIC incentive certificates.

## The base

1. **Total net profit** for the income year, computed under the Income Tax Law: business income less expenditure incurred in earning that income and the depreciation allowance prescribed (s 3 and the heads of income).
2. **No reliefs:** the section 6 basic relief does not apply to companies at the 22% rate.
3. **Losses** from one source are set off against other sources in the year; the rest is carried forward 3 years. Capital asset losses and a share of an association's loss are excluded.
4. **Capital gains** are computed and taxed separately at 10% (sale value less cost and net written-down value, per the Income Tax Rules), unless the year's total sale value is 10 million kyats or less.
5. **Foreign currency income** (other than capital gains) is taxed in that currency (s 30).
6. **Tax** = 22% (or 17% if listed) of total net profit, less any MSME exemption, less advance tax paid.

## Deadlines and filing

1. **Register.** After DICA approval of the company, register electronically on the IRD e-Registration system for a taxpayer identification number (TIN), then use the e-Filing and e-Payment systems (IRD notice, 28 April 2026). The IRD companies page also names the Large Taxpayer Office or Medium Taxpayer Offices for registration.
2. **Advance tax.** Pay quarterly within 10 days after each quarter of the income year (July, October, January, April), with challan Form (WinNga)-18.
3. **Annual return.** Form PaTaKha (WinNga)-1 (or the Self-Assessment System forms where the office uses SAS), within 3 months after year end: 30 June 2027 for FY 2026-27. Large and Medium Taxpayer Office companies must file electronically. Dormant and loss-making companies still file.
4. **Capital gains.** Separate return and payment within 30 days of each disposal.
5. **Assessment.** Under SAS, pay as declared once the confirmation letter is received; an audit can raise a demand (SAS-3) for additional tax and fines.
6. **Commercial tax and specific goods tax** run alongside: [myanmar-ct](myanmar-ct.md).

## Penalties and interest

- Late return: greater of 5% of tax owing plus 1% a month (or part) until assessment, and K 100,000.
- Advance tax short paid: 10% of the shortfall.
- Failure to file: assessed by the Township Revenue Officer on the evidence available (s 19(d)).

## Cross-border points

- A company with any foreign shareholders formed under Myanmar law is a resident foreigner taxed on worldwide income.
- Non-resident aliens' non-salary Myanmar income: 22%.
- Non-residents entitled to treaty relief on income subject to withholding apply on Form IRD (IT)-11-Q1 with the checklist and authorised representative letter (IRD Notification 108/2023); the tax office issues a statement of entitlement (Form IRD (IT)-11-Q2) or of ineligibility (Q3).
- Withholding tax rates on payments to non-residents are set by Ministry notification under s 16(b) and 16-B and are not restated here (see Not yet confirmed).

## Two worked examples that recompute

**Example 1. Private company, net profit 100,000,000 kyats, FY 2026-27, advance tax paid 18,000,000.** Tax 22% = 22,000,000. Balance due with the return 4,000,000. If listed on the Yangon Stock Exchange, 17% = 17,000,000.

```oa-check
name: Myanmar company, net profit 100m kyats, FY 2026-27
inputs:
  profit: 100000000
  advance: 18000000
steps:
  - tax: profit * 0.22
  - balance: tax - advance
  - tax_if_listed: profit * 0.17
expected:
  tax: 22000000.00
  balance: 4000000.00
  tax_if_listed: 17000000.00
```

**Example 2 (decision). Does the new-MSME exemption apply? Net profit 30,000,000 kyats in the second year of a new small manufacturer.** If it qualifies under s 29, the first 20,000,000 is exempt and tax is 22% of 10,000,000 = 2,200,000. If it does not (for example it is a trading company, not domestic production, or it is in its fourth year), tax is 22% of 30,000,000 = 6,600,000. The figure that decides is whether the year is within the 3 consecutive years from commencement and the business is in the s 29 classes; the difference is 4,400,000.

```oa-check
name: Myanmar new MSME exemption, net profit 30m kyats
inputs:
  profit: 30000000
steps:
  - tax_msme: max(profit - 20000000, 0) * 0.22
  - tax_normal: profit * 0.22
  - saving: tax_normal - tax_msme
expected:
  tax_msme: 2200000.00
  tax_normal: 6600000.00
  saving: 4400000.00
```

## Ask the client first

1. What kind of entity: Companies Law company, MIC-permitted business, listed company, cooperative, branch of a foreign company?
2. When did the business commence, and is it a micro, small or medium enterprise in domestic production?
3. Is it in oil and gas or a Special Economic Zone?
4. Net profit, losses brought forward and advance tax paid in the year.
5. Any capital assets sold, and their total value?
6. Payments to non-residents (withholding and treaty claims)?
