# Myanmar: tax pack

The financial year runs 1 April to 31 March. Rates are set each year by the Union Taxation Law; FY 2026-27 uses the Union Taxation Law 2026, in force 1 April 2026. The Internal Revenue Department (IRD) runs income tax, commercial tax and specific goods tax; the Social Security Board (SSB) runs social security; DICA registers companies. Myanmar has no VAT or GST: commercial tax takes its place. Amounts are kyats. This map covers FY 2026-27.
