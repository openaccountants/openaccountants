# Sierra Leone Tax Intake and Figure Map v1.0

**General reference only.** This skill is general tax/accounting reference material for AI-assisted workflows. It has not been reviewed for any specific person's facts, documents, elections, deadlines, residency, filing status, or local procedures. Do not rely on it to file, pay, amend, or take a tax position without review by a qualified professional in the relevant jurisdiction.

## Who this applies to

Use this intake map for a person, employer, company or overseas business with a Sierra Leone connection. It chooses the correct workpaper and evidence requirements; it does not repeat a headline rate as if it settles a filing. **2026 is the research intake year, not approval of every operative tax provision.** The six underlying tax Guides retain their exact accepted source-review receipts and explicit unresolved conditions.

First identify individual/company residence, source and classification, including temporary residence. The opened Income Tax Act baseline distinguishes ordinary residents, temporarily resident individuals and non-residents; effective management/control and majority operations are alternative company-residence grounds alongside incorporation. Calling everything “worldwide resident taxation” loses those conditions. The consolidation is expressly unofficial and stops at 2017. Use [cross-border](https://github.com/openaccountants/openaccountants/blob/9166cb1accc12699a0e0d14858acefd4b26138fc/skills/sierra-leone/sl-cross-border.md) before calculating income. [Income Tax Act ss.10-16, 2017 baseline](https://webtestcms.nra.gov.sl/uploads/Income_Tax_Act_2000_as_amended_to_Finance_Act_2017_1_83760e046c.pdf).

## The base

The National Revenue Authority continues under the **NRA Act 2022**, with revenue-law administration and collection functions; that Act repeals the 2002 Act subject to saved instruments. Company registration has a different authority and legal question, dealt with in [formation](sl-company-formation.md). [NRA Act ss.2,15,42](https://webtestcms.nra.gov.sl/uploads/THE_NATIONAL_REVENUE_AUTHORITY_ACT_2022_73b1f19cd7.pdf).

Record the unit on every source and ledger. The Bank's 11 August 2021 notice is prospective and gives **one New Leone for 1,000 Old Leones**; it does not prove the currency became legal tender during 2021. Convert source amounts explicitly, never read an old-Leone figure as new currency or redenominate a later statute a second time. Actual legal introduction/withdrawal dates and transaction exchange rates are not established by this notice. [Bank notice, denomination table and paragraphs 1,3](https://bsl.gov.sl/Public%20Notice%20on%20Redenomination.pdf).

The opened income-tax baseline uses an ordinary twelve-month assessment year ending 31 December, but permits a different twelve-month period by written Commissioner permission; transitional periods need a separate check. Obtain the actual permission and relevant accounting period rather than assuming every entity files for a calendar year. [Income Tax Act ss.45-47](https://webtestcms.nra.gov.sl/uploads/Income_Tax_Act_2000_as_amended_to_Finance_Act_2017_1_83760e046c.pdf).

Build separate reconciliations for income receipts, allowable costs, employee basic wages/benefits, GST supplies/inputs, associated transactions and foreign-source receipts. Use the six figure homes above with the taxpayer's documents. Source review closes identified source checks; it does not remove each Guide's stated currentness and procedure holds.

## Deadlines and filing

Start a separate obligation calendar for each tax and legal filing. The [company Guide](https://github.com/openaccountants/openaccountants/blob/9166cb1accc12699a0e0d14858acefd4b26138fc/skills/sierra-leone/sl-corporate-income-tax.md) owns the annual-return/instalment baseline; [income tax](https://github.com/openaccountants/openaccountants/blob/9166cb1accc12699a0e0d14858acefd4b26138fc/skills/sierra-leone/sl-income-tax.md) owns actual PAYE timing and statement conditions; [payroll](https://github.com/openaccountants/openaccountants/blob/9166cb1accc12699a0e0d14858acefd4b26138fc/skills/sierra-leone/sl-payroll-social.md) owns NASSIT; [GST](https://github.com/openaccountants/openaccountants/blob/9166cb1accc12699a0e0d14858acefd4b26138fc/skills/sierra-leone/sl-vat-gst.md) distinguishes its return deadline from payment; [TP](https://github.com/openaccountants/openaccountants/blob/9166cb1accc12699a0e0d14858acefd4b26138fc/skills/sierra-leone/sl-transfer-pricing.md) distinguishes preparation, annual declarations, written-request responses and submissions. Company-law annual returns are a different job in [formation](sl-company-formation.md).

The RAA 2017 s.21 baseline requires a person deriving potentially primary-tax-liable Sierra Leone income to apply for a TIN within 15 days of the relevant activity/action, without replacing GST registration or requiring an existing holder to apply twice. Do not wait mechanically for incorporation before considering that trigger. The live method and complete amendment chain are held. [RAA s.21](https://webtestcms.nra.gov.sl/uploads/Revenue_Administration_Act_2017_a505c603d7.pdf).

Retain authentic returns, supporting schedules, acknowledgements, assessments, payment evidence and notices for the actual period. A downloaded law, drafted Guide or successful local arithmetic check is not proof that a return was filed or paid.

## Penalties and interest

Open the correct Guide for the actual default, taxpayer classification and legal period. Do not add competing historical charges automatically; GST penalty priority and current interest implementation remain explicit holds in their figure homes. No current payable penalty or interest rate is independently established by this overview.

## Two worked examples that recompute

An old-source figure of Old Leones 12,000,000 converts, using the Bank's stated denomination relationship, to New Leones 12,000. A separate modern-source figure already expressed as New Leones 12,000 stays 12,000. Dividing it again would give 12 and understate it by 11,988. This is a unit-control case, not an entitlement to a particular deduction or threshold.

```oa-check
name: Record the source currency and avoid double redenomination
inputs:
  old_source_amount: 12000000
  old_per_new: 1000
  modern_new_amount: 12000
steps:
  - converted_old_source: old_source_amount / old_per_new
  - incorrect_double_conversion: modern_new_amount / old_per_new
  - understatement: modern_new_amount - incorrect_double_conversion
expected:
  converted_old_source: 12000
  incorrect_double_conversion: 12
  understatement: 11988
```

A second intake case is purely a fictional cash-to-profit reconciliation, with no tax deduction or rate implied: sales receipts 100,000, operating cash costs 40,000 and owner capital 20,000 enter a bank account. Closing cash increase is 80,000; trading cash surplus before any tax adjustments is 60,000. Treating owner capital as sales would overstate that preliminary surplus by 20,000. The corporate Guide must still classify accruals, capital items, statutory inclusions and deductions before any tax computation.

```oa-check
name: Separate owner funding from the preliminary trading reconciliation
inputs:
  sales_receipts: 100000
  operating_cash_costs: 40000
  owner_capital: 20000
steps:
  - cash_increase: sales_receipts + owner_capital - operating_cash_costs
  - preliminary_trading_surplus: sales_receipts - operating_cash_costs
  - wrong_surplus: cash_increase
  - overstatement: wrong_surplus - preliminary_trading_surplus
expected:
  cash_increase: 80000
  preliminary_trading_surplus: 60000
  overstatement: 20000
```

## Ask the client first

Confirm person/entity, activity, ownership/control and relevant countries; residence calendar and income sources; actual year/permission; source currency and amounts; workers and benefits; taxable/exempt/digital supplies; related-party transactions; foreign assessments and credits; existing registrations, submitted returns, notices and payment evidence. Ask about the specific facts that determine the relevant Guide and obligation.
