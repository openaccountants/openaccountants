# Barbados Payroll & Social Contributions

## National Insurance and PAYE

- **Employer PAYE and NIS withholding overview** — Employers withhold income tax under PAYE and deduct National Insurance Scheme (NIS) contributions from employees, remitting both to the BRA/NIS. Contribution rates rose from 1 April 2025.
- **NIS employee contribution rate** — 11% of insurable earnings %  _(National Insurance and Social Security Act, Cap. 47 — https://www.nis.gov.bb/contribution-rates/)_
- **NIS employer contribution rate** — 12.75% of insurable earnings %  _(National Insurance and Social Security Act, Cap. 47 — https://www.nis.gov.bb/contribution-rates/)_
- **Maximum insurable earnings (monthly)** — 5,280 BBD per month  _(National Insurance and Social Security Act, Cap. 47 — https://www.nis.gov.bb/nis-insurable-earnings/)_
- **Maximum insurable earnings (weekly)** — 1,219 BBD per week  _(National Insurance and Social Security Act, Cap. 47 — https://www.nis.gov.bb/nis-insurable-earnings/)_
- **Resilience and Regeneration Fund (employee)** — 0.25% of gross earnings (increased from 0.1% effective 1 April 2025) %  _(National Insurance and Social Security Act, Cap. 47 — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/barbados/individual/other-taxes)_
- **Resilience and Regeneration Fund (employer / self-employed)** — 0.25% of gross earnings %  _(National Insurance and Social Security Act, Cap. 47 — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/barbados/individual/other-taxes)_
- **PAYE income tax withholding** — Employers withhold income tax from salaries under the PAYE system at the personal income tax rates (12.5% / 28.5% after allowances)  _(Income Tax Act, Cap. 73 — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/barbados/individual/tax-administration)_
- **PAYE remittance deadline** — On or before the 15th of the month following the month of deduction  _(Income Tax Act, Cap. 73 — https://bra.gov.bb/Popular-Topics/Companies/Filing-and-Payment)_
- **NIS remittance deadline** — Contributions due by the 15th of the month following the contribution month ((confirm exact NIS due date))  _(National Insurance and Social Security Act, Cap. 47 — https://www.nis.gov.bb/contribution-rates/)_
