# Mauritius Corporate Income Tax v0.2

> **General reference only.** This skill is general tax/accounting reference material for AI-assisted workflows. It has not been reviewed for any specific person's facts, documents, elections, deadlines, residency, filing status, or local procedures. Do not rely on it to file, pay, amend, or take a tax position without review by a qualified professional in the relevant jurisdiction.

Tax year covered: Income year 1 July 2026 to 30 June 2027 (year of assessment 2027/28).

## Who this applies to

Companies, and through the Advance Payment System also unit trust schemes, collective investment schemes, protected cells, foundations, most trusts and non-resident sociétés (Income Tax Act s.50A). A company is resident if incorporated in Mauritius or centrally managed and controlled there (s.73(1)(b)). A Mauritius company managed and controlled abroad is treated as non-resident and still files under s.116 (s.73A). Global business questions start in [Cross-border](mu-cross-border.md).

## The base

Start from accounting profit and adjust to net income under the Act. Expenditure is deductible to the extent exclusively incurred in producing gross income (s.18). Annual allowance replaces depreciation (s.24). A loss carries forward 5 income years; the part from annual allowance on capital expenditure from 1 July 2006 has no time limit (s.59). Export formula: gross export income times chargeable income, divided by total gross income (s.44B(2)). From the year of assessment starting 1 July 2027, the exemption at Sub-part C item 57 and ss.67H and 67Q go (Finance Act 2026 s.28(10)). "Export of goods" now excludes live animals and includes shipping without landing in Mauritius and aviation fuel sold to an airline (s.2).

From 19 June 2026 a start-up set up on or after that date in the National SME Incubator Scheme, managed in Mauritius with turnover up to Rs 100 million, is exempt for 10 income years (Second Schedule, Sub-part C item 63).

## Deadlines and filing

1. APS (s.50B): log in to [MRA e-Services](https://www.mra.mu/index.php/e-services) under File and Pay, Corporate. Each of the first three quarters is due within 3 months of the end of the month the quarter ends, with tax. A quarter ending in September or March is due 2 days before the end of December or June, excluding Saturdays and public holidays.
2. June year ends may opt for a fourth APS statement due 30 September (s.50B(5), (6)); the next first-quarter statement then falls due 31 January.
3. Annual return, IT Form 3 family on the [forms page](https://www.mra.mu/index.php/downloadable-forms/income-tax/company-others): within 6 months of the end of the month the accounting period ends, with tax (s.116(1)).
4. June year end: 2 days before the end of December, excluding Saturdays and public holidays; 15 January if no tax is payable or a loss is declared; 31 January if a fourth APS statement was filed (s.116(2) to (2B)).
5. December year end: 2 days before the end of June (s.116(2C)).
6. FSC statement (VAT Act s.53P): same quarterly pattern, fourth statement within 6 months of year end.
7. CCR levy: 25% with each of the first three APS statements after the s.161A(76) cut, balance with the annual return (s.50O(2)).
8. QDMT: notify the designated person within 6 months of fiscal year end; return and payment within 15 months of the end of the month the fiscal year ends (s.50W).

The MRA calendar printed 29 December 2025 for June 2026 year ends, an apparent slip; s.116(2) gives the rule.

## Two worked examples that recompute

**Example 1.** Domestic company, chargeable income Rs 30,000,000, prior-year chargeable income the same, turnover Rs 120,000,000. Tax Rs 4,500,000, FSC Rs 1,500,000, CSR Fund Rs 600,000, CCR before any APS reduction Rs 600,000: Rs 7,200,000.

```oa-check
name: Rs 30m chargeable income, above FSC and CCR limits
inputs:
  ci: 30000000
steps:
  - tax: ci * 0.15
  - fsc: ci * 0.05
  - csr: ci * 0.02
  - ccr: ci * 0.02
  - total: tax + fsc + csr + ccr
expected:
  total: 7200000.00
```

**Example 2. Decision: does export income cut the tax?** Gross income Rs 100,000,000 with Rs 40,000,000 from exports of goods, chargeable income Rs 10,000,000. Export share Rs 4,000,000 at 3% is Rs 120,000. Rest Rs 6,000,000 at 15% is Rs 900,000. Tax Rs 1,020,000, against Rs 1,500,000 with no export routing: Rs 480,000 less.

```oa-check
name: Export formula against flat 15 percent
inputs:
  a: 40000000
  b: 100000000
  c: 10000000
steps:
  - export_ci: a * c / b
  - tax: export_ci * 0.03 + (c - export_ci) * 0.15
  - flat: c * 0.15
  - saving: flat - tax
expected:
  tax: 1020000.00
  saving: 480000.00
```

## Ask the client first

1. Accounting year end?
2. Gross income, turnover and chargeable income, this year and last?
3. Exports of goods, or another reduced-rate activity?
4. Global Business Licence, bank, insurer, hotel, real estate or telecoms?
5. Part of a group with EUR 750 million revenue?
6. Foreign dividends or interest, and substance in Mauritius?
