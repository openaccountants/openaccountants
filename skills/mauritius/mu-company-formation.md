# Mauritius Company Formation & Entity Choice

## Entity types and incorporation

- **Private company limited by shares** — Most common domestic vehicle; cannot have more than 25 shareholders  _(Companies Act 2001 (https://www.nexus.ua/data/files/Legal_DB/Mauritius/Mauritius_Guide.pdf))_
- **Public company** — May offer shares to the public and have unlimited shareholders  _(Companies Act 2001 (https://www.nexus.ua/data/files/Legal_DB/Mauritius/Mauritius_Guide.pdf))_
- **Global Business Company (GBC) / Authorised Company (AC)** — Licensed vehicles for cross-border/international business, regulated by the Financial Services Commission  _(Financial Services Act (https://www.finsburey.com/products/ac/))_
- **Foreign ownership** — A domestic company may be 100% foreign-owned  _(Companies Act 2001 (https://www.sovereigngroup.com/mauritius/domestic-companies/))_
- **Minimum share capital** — No statutory minimum share capital for a standard private company (approx — GBC/licensed entities may have capital/substance conditions; confirm)  _(Companies Act 2001 (https://www.sovereigngroup.com/mauritius/company-formation/))_
- **Minimum directors** — At least one director (a private company commonly requires a resident director) (approx — confirm minimum and residency requirement)  _(Companies Act 2001 (https://www.nexus.ua/data/files/Legal_DB/Mauritius/Mauritius_Guide.pdf))_
- **Company secretary** — A qualified/resident company secretary is generally required (small private companies may be exempt) (approx — confirm exemption thresholds)  _(Companies Act 2001 (https://www.nexus.ua/data/files/Legal_DB/Mauritius/Mauritius_Guide.pdf))_
- **Incorporation step — name reservation** — Company name must be approved/reserved by the Registrar of Companies before incorporation  _(Companies Act 2001 (https://www.nexus.ua/data/files/Legal_DB/Mauritius/Mauritius_Guide.pdf))_
- **Incorporation step — application** — File the incorporation application with the CBRD, including director/secretary consents and shareholder details  _(Companies Act 2001 (https://www.nexus.ua/data/files/Legal_DB/Mauritius/Mauritius_Guide.pdf))_
- **Incorporation timeline** — Typically about 1 to 3 weeks for a domestic company (faster online) (approx — confirm current CBRD processing times)  _(Companies Act 2001 (https://www.sovereigngroup.com/mauritius/company-formation/))_
- **Annual compliance — annual return** — File an annual return with the Registrar of Companies  _(Companies Act 2001 (https://www.nexus.ua/data/files/Legal_DB/Mauritius/Mauritius_Guide.pdf))_
- **Annual compliance — financial statements** — Prepare annual financial statements; audit required depending on company size/type (approx — confirm audit-exemption thresholds for small private companies)  _(Companies Act 2001 (https://www.nexus.ua/data/files/Legal_DB/Mauritius/Mauritius_Guide.pdf))_

Companies are formed under the Companies Act 2001 and registered with the Corporate and Business Registration Department (CBRD). The most common vehicle is a private company limited by shares; Global Business and Authorised Companies are used for international structuring.
