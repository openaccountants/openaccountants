# Kosovo: tax pack

Calendar tax year. Authority: the Tax Administration of Kosovo (Administrata Tatimore e Kosovës, TAK), atk-ks.org; returns are filed on TAK EDI. Currency: euro. Individuals pay personal income tax under Law 05/L-028 at 0%, 8% and 10% (bands set by Law 08/L-142); sole traders with annual gross income up to EUR 50,000 pay 3% or 9% of quarterly gross receipts instead. Companies pay 10% corporate income tax under Law 06/L-105, or 3% or 9% of gross receipts up to EUR 30,000. VAT under Law 05/L-037 (amended by 08/L-276): 18% standard, 8% reduced, registration above EUR 30,000 of annual turnover. Employees and employers each pay 5% to the Kosovo Pension Savings Trust. Procedures and penalties are in Law 08/L-257. Not an EU member.
