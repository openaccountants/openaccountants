# Senegal Corporate Income Tax

## Corporate income tax (Impôt sur les sociétés)

- **Standard corporate income tax rate** — 30% percent  _(Code Général des Impôts (CGI) — Impôt sur les sociétés — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/senegal/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_
- **Reduced rate — Free Export Enterprise (Entreprise Franche d'Exportation)** — 15% percent ((approx — confirm current regime status))  _(Code Général des Impôts (CGI) / Free Export Enterprise regime — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/senegal/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_
- **Minimum corporate tax (Impôt Minimum Forfaitaire, IMF)** — 0.5% of annual turnover for loss-makers or those with taxable income below XOF 5 million, capped at XOF 5 million percent  _(Code Général des Impôts (CGI) — Impôt Minimum Forfaitaire — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/senegal/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_
- **Tax base** — Profits of businesses operating in Senegal; non-residents taxed on Senegal-source profits via a permanent establishment  _(Code Général des Impôts (CGI) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/senegal/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_
- **Withholding tax on dividends (to non-residents)** — 10% percent (Unless reduced by tax treaty)  _(Code Général des Impôts (CGI) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/senegal/corporate/withholding-taxes)_
- **Withholding tax on interest** — 16% percent (Unless reduced by tax treaty)  _(Code Général des Impôts (CGI) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/senegal/corporate/withholding-taxes)_
- **Withholding tax on royalties / services paid to non-residents** — 20% percent (Unless reduced by tax treaty)  _(Code Général des Impôts (CGI) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/senegal/corporate/withholding-taxes)_
- **Withholding tax on services by certain residents not subject to CIT** — 5% percent  _(Code Général des Impôts (CGI) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/senegal/corporate/withholding-taxes)_
- **Annual CIT return + financial statements filing deadline** — 30 April of the year following the financial year  _(Code Général des Impôts (CGI) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/senegal/corporate/tax-administration)_
- **CIT first installment (acompte)** — 1/3 of prior year's tax, due 15 February  _(Code Général des Impôts (CGI) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/senegal/corporate/tax-administration)_
- **CIT second installment (acompte)** — 1/3 of prior year's tax, due 30 April  _(Code Général des Impôts (CGI) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/senegal/corporate/tax-administration)_
- **CIT final balance payment** — Due 15 June  _(Code Général des Impôts (CGI) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/senegal/corporate/tax-administration)_
- **Newly incorporated companies** — Exempt from installments in the first year; full CIT due by 15 June following the first financial year  _(Code Général des Impôts (CGI) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/senegal/corporate/tax-administration)_
