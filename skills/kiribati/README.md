# Kiribati (KI): tax pack

**Built from the law. Not yet approved. Written by Ryan Duguid. Not reviewed by an accountant.** Kiribati uses the Australian dollar. Taxes are administered by the Tax Division of the Ministry of Finance and Economic Development (tax.gov.ki); laws and gazettes are published by the Office of the President (president.gov.ki). VAT at 12.5% has applied since 1 January 2014, with amendments from 1 January 2026. Tax year 2026.
