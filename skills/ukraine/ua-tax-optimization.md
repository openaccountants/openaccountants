# Ukraine: lawful FOP tax planning and Diia City, 2026

> **General reference only.** Prepare an evidence-based comparison for a qualified Ukrainian accountant or tax lawyer. This is not an election, filing, payment instruction or approval of a person's structure. Never design evasion, sham employment, artificial splitting or false expenses.

## Scope

Compare eligible Ukrainian **ФОП / FOP Group 3** single tax with the general system, assess **VAT**, legitimate own-**ЄСВ** choices/exemptions and expense evidence, and compare an eligible **Diia City company/gig** offer. Currency: UAH. Authorities: **ДПС** and NBU; portals **[Електронний кабінет](https://cabinet.tax.gov.ua)** and **[Diia City](https://city.diia.gov.ua/)**. Contributor remains **Open Accountants Community**. Ordinary resident FOPs are the main scope; Groups 1/2, residence/treaties, e-residents, farms, regulated sectors and company distributions require their owning skills/specialists.

**Answer layer:** Group 3 ordinary non-VAT tax is **5% plus a separate 1% levy on article292 income**, plus the actual own-ЄСВ obligation. General-system ordinary tax is **18% PIT plus5% levy on net taxable business income**, with its separate monthly contribution base/floor/cap and documented paid-contribution deductions. A universal **45%-of-profit bill** or **26% margin break-even** is wrong. The VAT million threshold has a **Groups1-3 carveout**. Diia City is a **company regime**, not a FOP election; ordinary eligible specialist income is **5% PIT plus5% levy**, with the qualifying company's ЄСВ on top. [167,177,181,291-293,170.14-1;XX.10.16-1](https://tax.gov.ua/nk/); [2026 contribution figures](https://tax.gov.ua/media-tsentr/novini/999526.html).

Sources were read in the exact scopes dated below, including **16 November2026** current reads and identified preserved15November evidence. No authenticated submission or actual client transaction was tested. Military-levy provisions have their own sunset: current ordinary single-tax levy continues through **31December of the third calendar year following the martial-law-end year**; do not infer an immediate end or a fixed future date. [XX.10.16-1(1.1-1.3)](https://tax.gov.ua/nk/).

## Ask the client first

Reuse intake/records. Establish residence/legal form, actual regime/group/VAT effective dates, activity and customer eligibility, KVED and debt/bar flags, income history/cap headroom, receipt dates/FX, commercial forecasts and change dates. Obtain documented **paid/matched business expenses**, depreciation records, recoverable VAT, monthly taxable-income/own-ЄСВ schedule, actual own-ЄСВ payments and any employer/pension/disability/military relief evidence. Distinguish accrued contribution liability from amounts deductible by payment.

For an offer or company, obtain gross remuneration versus client/employer budget, who pays ЄСВ, ordinary employment versus genuine FOP contract versus statutory gig contract, VAT-inclusive/exclusive pricing, benefits, equipment, subordination and IP terms. For Diia City obtain the actual register entry/date, ordinary/startup/other status, legal qualification/activity/income/headcount/remuneration tests, company tax election and compliance records, specialist annual remuneration, actual January NBU EUR rate, other income and recapture facts. No unknown input becomes zero or an exemption.

Test legal eligibility **before** optimizing an arithmetic result.

### 2. Calculate the bases before comparing the tax

For ordinary Group3, start with statutory **article292 income**, not every bank credit or accounting profit. Apply actual receipt dates, refund/loan/agency/passive-income exclusions and nonmonetary restrictions through `ua-single-tax`. Expenses do not reduce that turnover-tax base; they still reduce business cash/profit. Registered3% VAT, cap-excess/barred income and regime changes require their distinct rules. [292,293](https://tax.gov.ua/nk/).

For general system, **177.2** uses gross taxable activity receipts minus documented directly connected costs. **177.3** excludes input/output VAT for a registered VAT payer; do not compare a VAT-inclusive turnover with VAT-exclusive profit. **177.4.1-.4** covers eligible materials/goods, wages/civil-law fees, permitted business taxes/own ЄСВ, and specified connected services/rent/communications/bank/transport costs. An expense must meet its statutory category, linkage and evidence/timing; a payment alone is insufficient. Undocumented/personal costs cannot be inserted to achieve a desired margin. [177.1-.4](https://tax.gov.ua/nk/).

Keep contracts, supplier invoices, acts/other valid primary evidence, goods/payment records and the prescribed income/expense record. Software/licence/subcontractor costs need their actual classification; buying a fixed asset/intangible is not automatically an immediate deduction. Elective straight-line depreciation has separate asset records, useful lives and exclusions; land, residential property and passenger vehicles have specified restrictions. Do not advise all equipment or software can be expensed. Route the detailed catalogue/depreciation to `ua-income-tax` and records to `ua-bookkeeping`. [177.4.5-.9](https://tax.gov.ua/nk/).

**Paid own ЄСВ is deductible where177.4.3 permits it.** The2026 DPS explanation records it in **column8** of the Order261 record **on actual payment with documentary support**. Accrued current-year contribution and payment-date expense are not interchangeable, especially fourth-quarter/January payments. Get the monthly contribution schedule and actual paid/allocable amounts from the qualified contribution/tax owner; do not solve an invented annual22% equation or deduct an unpaid liability. No own PIT/levy deduction is assumed in the cases below. [177.4.3](https://tax.gov.ua/nk/); [full payment-date explanation](https://cv.tax.gov.ua/media-ark/news-ark/977945.html).

General-system ЄСВ uses taxable activity income/profit with its monthly floor/cap and applicable exceptions, rather than an uncapped22% of annual gross profit. In a no-profit period the cited official explanation provides a self-determined-base branch; the complete current no-income/relief interaction must be assessed rather than inferred from the simplified-system minimum. An employer-paid exemption requires the **actual minimum paid for that person in each month**, not annual pay or merely being “also employed”. Pension/disability/military and other exceptions need their specific evidence. [Bases](https://sumy.tax.gov.ua/media-ark/news-ark/977379.html); [current employer-paid test](https://zir.tax.gov.ua/main/bz/view/?id=43912&src=ques).

### 3. Replace the old margin shortcut with a real decision

The old **0.06÷0.23≈26.09%** compares turnover tax with PIT/levy **while omitting contribution differences and their deduction**. It is a mathematical ratio, not a lawful universal switch point. The old statement that adding ЄСВ only “slightly” shifts it is withdrawn. Costs, monthly floors/caps, documented payments, exemptions, VAT, the real tax base and compliance costs can change direction. At fixed confirmed comparable bases, use:

**Group3 known tax/contribution allocation =0.06×eligible article292 income +actual own ЄСВ.**

**General known tax/contribution allocation =0.18×confirmed PIT base +0.05×confirmed levy base +actual own ЄСВ.**

Use a shared general-system PIT/levy base only where the tax owner establishes it. Deduct paid own ЄСВ correctly in that base and avoid counting it twice as an operating cost. Test low/high forecast and expense-admissibility sensitivity; uncertainty in claimed costs may overwhelm a small saving. A high-margin software freelancer may find Group3 cheaper, but “almost always optimal” does not replace actual eligibility/math. A low-margin reseller/agency can prefer general system, but a whole-million VAT/pricing issue must be evaluated before a recommendation.

### 4. Assess VAT on the real transactions and price

**181.1 currently carves out Groups1-3** from the ordinary turnover-based mandatory-registration test. A non-VAT Group3 freelancer does **not** need to stay below one million solely to preserve that carveout. General-system businesses must monitor actual taxable supplies over the rolling twelve months; ordinary application is due **by day10 of the following month** after the threshold event. Special import/nonresident-service liabilities can still arise without ordinary registration. [181-183,208](https://tax.gov.ua/nk/).

For an eligible **3%+VAT** route, model actual output VAT, recoverable input VAT, customer credit/pricing and cash-flow/СЕА capacity; **3% versus5% alone** does not decide. A pure-labour seller with little input credit and a fixed VAT-inclusive price may bear a different economic cost from one who can add VAT. VAT is not automatically20% of every cross-border receipt: establish actual place, taxable/exempt/outside-scope category and rate. Many listed B2B services use **186.3 recipient-place** rules, but not every service exported to any nonresident does. [183,186.3,193-195,293.3/.8](https://tax.gov.ua/nk/); `ukraine-vat`/`ua-einvoice`.

**Future reform held:** the original late2025 proposal, **1January2027** mandatory VAT for single-tax taxpayers and **10January2027** application date are preserved as **unconfirmed proposal/history**, not an enacted calendar or planning instruction. Current181.1 is the confirmed2026 rule; the full later proposal/legislative history and operative2027 amendment were not recovered here. Obtain an enacted source before using a future change. Do not split one real business across relatives/FOPs to multiply either VAT or income limits.

### 5. Test the Diia City company and the specialist separately

Diia City resident status belongs to a **Ukrainian-registered legal entity**; an individual FOP does not elect it. The company may use employees, statutory gig contracts or genuine FOP suppliers according to their actual law/tax conditions. A qualifying gig relationship is a recognized form, but the words “gig contract” do not cure an invalid agreement or guarantee no legal/reclassification risk. [Official resident-service explanation](https://diia.gov.ua/services/zayava-pro-nabuttya-statusu-rezidenta-diya-city); [170.14-1](https://tax.gov.ua/nk/).

| Ordinary resident test | Actual measurement and exception boundary | Official source |
|---|---|---|
| Qualified activities/income | Perform a listed qualified activity, recorded in charter/EDR. Qualified income at least **90%** of total for the first **three calendar months after acquisition month**, and each resident calendar year where total income exists; not a generic profit-margin test | [Law1667-IX art5 as explained in official guide](https://guide.diia.gov.ua/view/nabuttia-statusu-rezydenta-diia-siti) |
| Average monthly remuneration | At least **EUR1200** equivalent using NBU rate on the **first day of the relevant calendar month**, every month from the month after resident acquisition. Not January1 for every month | [Official measurement rule](https://guide.diia.gov.ua/view/nabuttia-statusu-rezydenta-diia-siti) |
| Average employee/gig count | At least **nine persons**, monthly from the month after acquisition, with actual statutory counting | [Official measurement rule](https://guide.diia.gov.ua/view/nabuttia-statusu-rezydenta-diia-siti) |
| Startup route | Official service describes registration no earlier than **24calendar months** before application and prior-year income not exceeding **1167minimum wages**, plus the other legal conditions. The portal's old approximate2021 startup hryvnia amount is **not** the2026 threshold. Full current art5 excluded-entity/clean-history/ownership/sanctions/regulated-activity tests and startup conditions were not recovered, so the table is a screening framework, **not resident-status approval**. The service/guide summaries contain inconsistent headline processing labels; no guaranteed processing date is issued here. Confirm actual register decision, reporting/compliance duties and tax election with counsel/accountant, including any special wartime relief.

Under **170.14-1.2**, qualifying **5% PIT** starts **the calendar month after resident acquisition**; acquisition-month remuneration follows ordinary18%. The provision covers the stated salary/gig/qualifying author-remuneration categories, not every payment or sick benefit. It excludes the simultaneous **Defence City** status specified in the current text. **The5% military levy is separate**: do not falsely postpone levy to the following month merely because PIT relief starts then. [170.14-1.1-.2,167.1-.2;XX.10.16-1](https://tax.gov.ua/nk/).

The **EUR240000** cap is an annual remuneration test at actual **1January NBU EUR rate**; the statutory already18%-taxed amounts are excluded. On excess the individual reports and pays18% PIT with the actual prior withholding credit reconciled; it is not18% **plus another5% PIT on the same excess**. No actual EUR rate or hryvnia cap is invented here. [170.14-1.3](https://tax.gov.ua/nk/); [DPS3April2026 credit explanation](https://lg.tax.gov.ua/media-ark/news-ark/997647.html).

Loss of status following the specified unsuccessful judicial challenge and failure of monthly remuneration/headcount conditions can trigger company-funded18% PIT recapture. **170.14-1.5** deducts tax already remitted; **170.14-1.6** gives a particular startup **headcount** exception and later recapture mechanism, not blanket startup immunity for every salary test. These liabilities require separate calendar/payroll review and cannot be deducted arbitrarily from the specialist. Full current ЄСВ preferential eligibility/recapture conditions also require the payroll owner; the minimum-company-contribution case assumes those conditions are satisfied. [170.14-1.4-.6](https://tax.gov.ua/nk/); `ua-payroll`/`ua-social-contributions`.

The **company** independently chooses ordinary18% CIT or elected special9% under **136.8** on its defined **135.2/141.9-1 transaction base**. The Code includes specified distributions/returns and other chargeable operations; it is not merely a dividend-only tax and not a blanket0% rate for all “reinvested profit”. Full transaction exclusions, adjustments and election conditions are a corporate specialist's work; do not calculate the company bill from retained profit alone. Founder dividends and cross-border payments have separate tax questions. [135.2 opening/distribution/return provisions,136.8,141.9-1](https://tax.gov.ua/nk/); `ua-corporate-income-tax`.

### 6. Use legitimate contribution and FX choices

An ordinary liable single-tax FOP can select the minimum permitted own-ЄСВ base; choosing more changes contribution/pension-cover economics, not turnover tax. Document the client's social-cover preference rather than recommending automatic overpayment. Verified statutory exemption can lawfully reduce the obligation; the old “no legitimate way to pay less” is withdrawn. Do not transfer the FOP minimum rule to general-system profit months or assume pension/foreign coverage without a source. [Law2464 art7, official bases](https://sumy.tax.gov.ua/media-ark/news-ark/977379.html); [monthly employer-paid test](https://zir.tax.gov.ua/main/bz/view/?id=43912&src=ques).

Foreign-client income is included in the appropriate Group3 cap/base at the **NBU rate on receipt** under292.5/.6, subject to actual statutory classification. Preserve contract/invoice/act or equivalent evidence, bank/SWIFT/PSP records and actual rate/date; a later bank conversion is not automatically a second receipt. Use the correct business account and current NBU rules. The old blanket mandatory-conversion or universal settlement deadline is **not confirmed** here; current service-category, date, transaction, war and account rules need the NBU/cross-border owner. No off-channel concealed proceeds. [292.5-.6](https://tax.gov.ua/nk/); [NBU Instruction162 paragraphs10/24](https://bank.gov.ua/admin_uploads/law/29072022_162.pdf?v=4); `ua-cross-border`/`ua-bookkeeping`.

### 7. Preserve the anti-avoidance and classification boundaries

The original risk list remains a refusal list, not a planning menu. Flag fixed hours, subordination, employer equipment/premises, employee-like leave and a single economically dependent client for an actual employment-law review; no one fact automatically decides all cases. Ordinary payroll includes PIT/levy and employer ЄСВ, with its own bases/caps, not a universal uncapped “23%+22%” assessment. Use genuine independent contracting, lawful employment or an actually eligible statutory gig form; do not manufacture multi-client paperwork to disguise employment. [Salary taxation168/171 and gig170.14-1](https://tax.gov.ua/nk/); `ua-payroll`.

Refuse artificial FOP splitting to multiply limits, hidden/backdated receipts, fabricated/personal expenses, false primary documents, mischaracterized VAT place/rate or unlawful FX receipt/conversion. Preserve actual commercial agreements and transaction substance; if tax advantage depends on invented facts, stop. The exact employment reclassification test, fines, other anti-abuse doctrines and client exposure are held for the qualified practitioner, not asserted from an unsourced general warning. Diia eligibility failure, missing documents and unreconciled annual income likewise block a final recommendation.
