# Bangladesh: tax pack

The pack distinguishes the income year from the assessment year, uses BDT and cites NBR and enacted Bangladesh legislation. The current rebuild preserves all existing slugs and keeps unconfirmed rules outside usable instructions. These skills remain tier 2 and have no accountant approval.

| Domain/job | Skill | Scope |
|---|---|---|
| Individual income tax | [PIT](bangladesh-pit.md) | Ordinary AY 2026-2027 computation and filing windows |
| Company tax | [Corporate income tax](bd-corporate-income-tax.md) | Ordinary classes, selected withholding and filing |
| VAT | [VAT working papers](bangladesh-vat.md) | Registration, classification, credit and return preparation boundaries |
| Self-employed pension | [Social contributions](bd-social-contributions.md) | Universal Pension choices |
| Salary withholding | [Payroll](bd-payroll.md) | Annual estimate, deduction adjustments, deposits, certificates and quarterly reporting |
| Employer funds | [Payroll and social duties](bd-payroll-social.md) | Provident fund/Progoti and WPPF conditions |
| Services relief | [IT/freelancer tax](bd-it-freelancer-tax.md) | Conditional successor exemption and remittance evidence |
| Setup | [Business registrations](bd-formation.md), [Company incorporation](bd-company-formation.md) | Distinct registration and incorporation jobs |
| Accounting evidence | [Bookkeeping](bd-bookkeeping.md), [Financial statements](bd-financial-statements.md), [VAT invoices](bd-einvoice.md) | Records, reporting and fiscal documents |

| Restricted assets/provenance | [Crypto referral](bd-crypto-tax.md), [References](references.md) | Refusal and source selection |

Each skill's Sources table states the provisions actually read on 14 November 2026 and its unresolved boundaries. The partial entries above are coverage gaps; passing structural checks does not remove them. Transfer pricing and cross-border remain tracked in [the Bangladesh pack issue](https://github.com/openaccountants/openaccountants/issues/501).
