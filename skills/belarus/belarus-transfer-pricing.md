# Belarus transfer pricing, 2026 v0.1

## Who this applies to

2026 profit-tax payers undertaking transactions in General Code art. 88. Identify related persons under art. 20, including specified direct/indirect participation, common control/ownership, management, employment and family relationships. A court can recognise other influential relationships. Multiply participation along each chain and sum the relevant chains under art. 20(2); ownership percentage is not the only test. Interposed parties without substantial functions/assets/risks and offshore residents are treated as related under art. 88(2). [General Code arts. 20, 87-88](https://pravo.by/document/?guid=3871&p0=hk0200166).

Chapter 11 adjusts profit tax/loss where nonmarket conditions reduce Belarus profit tax or inflate loss. Do not compute an automatic TP charge on transaction value or use it to lower Belarus tax. Test the transaction class before its threshold and exclusions. [Arts. 87(4)-(5), 88](https://pravo.by/document/?guid=3871&p0=hk0200166).

## The base

**Control screen.** Classify related foreign trade; domestic transactions with a related Belarus legal entity entitled to profit-tax nonpayment/exemption; the specific property/share/loan category; and strategic-goods foreign trade. The 2026 art. 88(1.3) category expressly includes the listed zone/preferential-regime counterparties and foreign related transactions. Do not send its real estate through the ordinary large-taxpayer threshold. Check art. 88(5) exclusions, including treaty-set prices, specified organised-market securities, qualifying short interbank loans and the other enumerated transactions. [Art. 88](https://pravo.by/document/?guid=3871&p0=hk0200166).

Aggregate sales and purchases separately per counterparty for the calendar year, excluding VAT/excise. For loans, transaction price for threshold purposes is principal; include annual new advances/tranches plus the opening outstanding balance where the year's loan transaction occurs. This is not the interest-income base, and an unchanged historical loan is not automatically a new annual advance. [Art. 88(4)](https://pravo.by/document/?guid=3871&p0=hk0200166); [MNS loan clarification](https://nalog.gov.by/organizations/transfer_pricing/questions/).

**Comparability.** Record contract and actual conduct, product/service/IP features, functions, assets, risks, market/economic conditions and business strategy under art. 89. Adjust material differences with evidence. Use official/public market sources or documented third-party data under art. 90, not a made-up markup. For loans examine currency, term/tranche, security, credit quality, interest type and repayment. [Arts. 89-90, 92](https://pravo.by/document/?guid=3871&p0=hk0200166).

**Methods and calculation.** Art. 91 prefers the comparable uncontrolled price (CUP) method where it and another method can be used. Otherwise select the most reliable supported resale-price, cost-plus, comparable-profitability or profit-split method; combining methods is permitted. A less complex tested party with fewer risks and no valuable influencing intangibles is the preferred profitability tested party under art. 91(5). [Arts. 91-96](https://pravo.by/document/?guid=3871&p0=hk0200166).

- CUP compares adjusted comparable prices with the controlled price. A single comparable can set both bounds only if conditions are comparable/adjustable and the comparable seller is not dominant (art. 92(6)).
- Resale price compares gross margin on resale to an independent buyer; cost-plus compares gross profit/cost. Use the art. 91 financial definitions consistently, without mixing gross and operating margins (arts. 93-94).
- Comparable profitability uses the supported operating profit/sales, costs, assets or other permitted indicator under arts. 91(8), 95. For an out-of-range result use the relevant closest range boundary under the statute, subject to the upward-tax rule.
- Profit split allocates the combined actual profit/loss according to the parties' evidenced functional/asset/risk contributions or other statutory comparable basis; it is not an automatic equal split (art. 96).

For ordinary price and profitability ranges, sort comparable values ascending, keep duplicates and exclude the controlled transaction. For `n` observations, lower rank is `n/4` and upper rank `0.75*n`: at an integer rank average that observation and the next; otherwise take the next integer rank. Index observations starting at one. Profitability uses the transaction year or permitted three preceding years, with weighted averages and working-capital adjustments where required. Special exchange/price-agency and loan rules in art. 92(4)-(4-2) override the ordinary range. Prices inside the range are accepted; below/above use the respective boundary. [Arts. 91(10)-(12), 92(4)-(6)](https://pravo.by/document/?guid=3871&p0=hk0200166).

For qualifying open-ended/demand loans or missing term/currency comparables, art. 92(4-2) uses the specified currency benchmark with a 20% decrease/increase; BYN lending minimum is refinancing rate less 50%, BYN borrowing maximum is refinancing rate plus 20%. Fixed rates use the contract-date benchmark; variable cases use the income/cost-recognition date; repayment requires the statutory actual-term comparison. These are relative multipliers, not percentage-point shifts. Retrieve the actual dated benchmark, rather than quoting a fixed annual rate. [Art. 92(4-2)](https://pravo.by/document/?guid=3871&p0=hk0200166).

**Adjustment.** Calculate the revenue shortfall or excess deductible expense against the lawful boundary; adjust taxable profit/loss only if it does not reduce profit tax. Recalculate cumulative rate and tax under the corporate skill, credit tax already paid, and preserve transaction-level bridge/evidence. A contractual price amendment alone does not discharge a tax adjustment. [Art. 87(4)-(5)](https://pravo.by/document/?guid=3871&p0=hk0200166); [Special Code arts. 184, 186](https://pravo.by/document/?guid=3871&p0=hk0900071).

Prepare records contemporaneously; the August restriction on requesting formal documentation does not postpone quarterly CUP correction or invoice disclosure. Corporate returns and payments follow Special Code art. 186. ## Penalties and interest

Late-payment interest under General Tax Code art. 55 is computed separately for each period of constant unpaid tax and National Bank refinancing rate: `unpaid tax × overdue calendar days × annual rate / 360 / 100`. Count from the day after the due date through payment; interest reflected in the audit decision on audit-assessed tax is capped at that assessed tax amount; apply art. 55(3) exclusions and art. 55(4)'s four-decimal interest-rate precision. Use the rate actually in force in each period, not a single assumed 2026 rate. [General Code art. 55](https://pravo.by/document/?guid=3871&p0=hk0200166).

For a business/agent return, Administrative Code art. 14.2(1)-(2) sets up to 1 basic unit for no more than 3 working days late; otherwise 2 basic units plus 0.5 per complete month, capped at 10. Repetition within a year invokes art. 14.2(4), 3 per complete or incomplete month, capped at 20. Under art. 14.4(1), business tax underpayment carries 40% of unpaid tax, minimum 2 basic units for an entrepreneur or 10 for a company, subject to the statutory notes, including timely voluntary correction/payment. Do not apply these amounts automatically to an ordinary individual or to a withholding default. [Administrative Code arts. 14.2 and 14.4](https://pravo.by/document/?guid=3871&p0=hk2100091). The basic unit is BYN 45 from 1 January 2026 ([Council Resolution 651 of 20 November 2025](https://pravo.by/document/?guid=12551&p0=C22500651)).

No standalone TP percentage penalty is inferred here. Inaccurate tax returns, withholding and failure to supply requested information require classification under the applicable Administrative Code provision; formal-documentation timing does not create immunity from additional tax or interest. [General Code arts. 55, 79, 97](https://pravo.by/document/?guid=3871&p0=hk0200166); [Administrative Code ch. 14](https://pravo.by/document/?guid=3871&p0=hk2100091).

## Cross-border points

Domestic TP, profit-tax controlled-debt limits and foreign withholding are separate tests. Apply each relevant rule and avoid deducting more interest merely because the price is arm's length. A treaty corresponding adjustment/MAP requires the specific operative treaty and suspension analysis; unilateral Belarus upward correction does not itself establish foreign relief. See `belarus-cross-border-tax`. [General Code arts. 87-97](https://pravo.by/document/?guid=3871&p0=hk0200166); [Special Code arts. 172, 187-194](https://pravo.by/document/?guid=3871&p0=hk0900071).

## Two worked examples that recompute

### 1. Underpriced related export

A non-large ordinary company sells 10,000 units to a foreign related buyer at BYN 50 each, so annual sales of 500,000 exceed the ordinary threshold. A single fully comparable independent sale at 60 meets art. 92(6), including the nondominant-seller condition. Revenue adjustment is 100,000, producing 20,000 extra profit tax assuming no losses, exemptions or increased-rate trigger. The comparable price is an example fact, not a statutory safe harbour.

```oa-check
name: BY 2026 CUP related export adjustment
inputs:
  units: 10000
  charged_price: 50
  comparable_price: 60
steps:
  sales: units * charged_price
  market_revenue: units * comparable_price
  adjustment: max(0, market_revenue - sales)
  extra_tax: adjustment * 0.20
expected:
  sales: 500000
  adjustment: 100000
  extra_tax: 20000
```

### 2. Loan principal threshold and interest shortfall

A covered related-party loan of BYN 500,000 is newly advanced and outstanding for the example's full interest year. It passes the principal threshold even though interest is small. A documented single comparable under art. 92(6), rather than a statutory benchmark, establishes 4%; actual interest is 1%. Taxable interest adjustment is 15,000 and extra ordinary profit tax is 3,000. In practice use the contract's day-count, payment and recognition terms; the full-year fraction here is stipulated.

```oa-check
name: BY 2026 related loan principal and CUP interest
inputs:
  principal: 500000
  actual_rate: 0.01
  comparable_rate: 0.04
  year_fraction: 1
steps:
  actual_interest: principal * actual_rate * year_fraction
  market_interest: principal * comparable_rate * year_fraction
  adjustment: max(0, market_interest - actual_interest)
  extra_tax: adjustment * 0.20
expected:
  actual_interest: 5000
  adjustment: 15000
  extra_tax: 3000
```

## Ask the client first

- What are all direct/indirect holdings, common controllers, managers and family links?
- Is the payer listed as large, and what are the counterparty country, offshore and exempt-regime statuses?
- What are annual sales, purchases, property/share transactions and loan advances/opening balances per counterparty?
- What contracts, actual conduct, functions, assets, risks and comparable data support the price?
- Which invoices, documentation/economic-justification requests and adjustment returns have been filed?
- Are there audits, APAs, foreign adjustments or requests for treaty relief?
