# Belarus cross-border tax, 2026 v0.1

## Who this applies to

Calendar-year 2026, BYN. This covers ordinary inbound/outbound individuals and business payments, foreign-company permanent establishments (PEs) and cessation. Identify the legal recipient, tax residence, income source, actual beneficial owner, activity and payment date before selecting the tax. A foreign individual is subject to PIT, not the foreign-company chapter. Belarus-resident individuals and organisations include qualifying foreign-source income; nonresident individuals and non-PE foreign companies are taxed on the respective Belarus-source objects. [General Code arts. 14-17](https://pravo.by/document/?guid=3871&p0=hk0200166); [Special Code arts. 166-169, 188-198](https://pravo.by/document/?guid=3871&p0=hk0900071).

These are domestic rates, not treaty rates. A certificate alone does not establish an available treaty article or beneficial ownership. Specific government-debt and other art. 192 relief requires its own facts; there is no universal zero rate for foreign-company interest.

## The base

**Residence and individuals.** Under General Code art. 17, count from the day after arrival through departure. Statutory temporary treatment/business absences count; holiday absences count only within the stated aggregate 60-day condition. Prior-year status is provisional for current withholding. Resolve dual residence using the actual operative treaty. Without a resolving treaty, the citizenship/permanent-residence fallback in art. 17(5) applies. Residence permits and nationality alone do not replace this procedure. Residents include foreign receipts under arts. 195-203; the income-tax skill supplies exemptions and deductions. Convert foreign-currency individual receipts at the National Bank rate on actual receipt, with the separate employment and entrepreneur dates in art. 199(11). [General Code art. 17](https://pravo.by/document/?guid=3871&p0=hk0200166); [Special Code arts. 195-214](https://pravo.by/document/?guid=3871&p0=hk0900071).

**Foreign-company withholding.** Test the closed Belarus-source income classes in art. 189. Ordinary foreign business turnover is not automatically subject to 15%, and performance abroad does not automatically remove listed consultancy/royalty income. Art. 190(1.3) uses gross income for the residual classes. The specific documented deductions in art. 190(1.1) cover qualifying leasing reimbursement/insurance/financing, specified merchandise operations, real-estate/security/share disposal costs and other listed classes. The agent must possess the documents. For property and company-interest disposals apply the specified historical USD conversions and reconversion at the liability date; do not deduct an unsupported accounting carrying value. Dividends use art. 182(6)'s base via art. 190(1.2). [Arts. 189-190](https://pravo.by/document/?guid=3871&p0=hk0900071).

Art. 191 generally recognises the earlier of accounting recognition/completion of the obligation and payment, including noncash settlement. For specified debt interest and contractual sanctions, use the permitted accrual/payment election consistently for the year and mark the return. Convert currency on the applicable liability date under that article. If a contract promises a net amount and requires the payer to bear withholding, gross up the taxable amount before applying the rate, rather than multiplying only the promised net by the rate. [Arts. 190-191](https://pravo.by/document/?guid=3871&p0=hk0900071).

**PE profit.** Art. 180 covers a fixed place/branch, a dependent contracting agent, aggregated places performing functions toward a common result, and the service/construction duration tests. Independent ordinary-course agents and exclusively preparatory/auxiliary activities qualify for the stated exceptions only on their actual conduct. Aggregate service days across sites; construction unrelated sites have the separate rule. A PE is recognised from commencement once the duration condition is met. Calculate attributable revenue less evidenced allowable costs and losses using the corporate skill; head-office costs require art. 180(9)-(13)'s allocation, currency and evidence rules, including the prescribed foreign auditor/tax-consultant conclusion unless the local-record exception applies. If profit cannot be evidenced, art. 180(7) provides an agreed allocation or analogous-business assessment, not zero profit. [Arts. 180, 183-186](https://pravo.by/document/?guid=3871&p0=hk0900071).

**Foreign-tax credits.** For a Belarus company, art. 187 covers qualifying foreign income tax actually paid/withheld, within Belarus tax on that same income, the tax paid for its income calendar year and the operative treaty amount. Translate at payment date, with art. 187(3)'s certificate-date alternatives. File foreign-authority evidence; art. 187(5) permits specified alternative documents and supplemental contracts/primary records for missing details. Credit normally belongs to the period evidence is filed; evidence filed before the prior annual return deadline can support that annual return or the current period. No unrestricted credit for foreign tax on unrelated income. [Art. 187](https://pravo.by/document/?guid=3871&p0=hk0900071).

Resident individual credit under art. 224 is similarly limited to Belarus tax on the qualifying foreign income, subject to the statutory offshore exclusion and any operative treaty. Obtain foreign-authority proof or the permitted declaration/payment evidence. Temporary payer evidence requires the final evidence within the statutory one-year follow-up, otherwise reassessment and interest follow. Entrepreneurs have the separate evidence-filing period, exchange-rate and treaty-cap rules in art. 224(3). [Art. 224(1)-(3)](https://pravo.by/document/?guid=3871&p0=hk0900071).

**Offshore levy.** Belarus organisations/entrepreneurs separately test art. 263: payments to offshore-registered recipients or offshore accounts, obligations to those persons, and the specified noncash/assignment transactions. Screen the recipient registration and account jurisdiction against the actual current list in Presidential Decree 353 of 25 May 2006, including subnational entries such as Delaware and Wyoming, rather than treating every US payment identically. [Decree 353](https://pravo.by/document/?guid=3871&p0=P30600353). Apply the exact art. 264 exemptions, including qualifying returned loan principal/interest and specified maritime freight/forwarding conditions, before using the 15% art. 265 transfer/contract base. A withholding-treaty reduction does not by itself remove this separate levy. [Arts. 262-267](https://pravo.by/document/?guid=3871&p0=hk0900071).

## Deadlines and filing

| Duty | Procedure/deadline | Official act/article |
|---|---|---|
| Foreign-company withholding | Agent's quarterly return by 20th, payment by 22nd after quarter | [Art. 193](https://pravo.by/document/?guid=3871&p0=hk0900071) |
| Treaty relief before withholding | Provide competent foreign-authority residence certificate and required beneficial-owner evidence by statutory time; verify actual operative treaty/article | [Art. 194](https://pravo.by/document/?guid=3871&p0=hk0900071) |
| Treaty refund after withholding | Submit certificate, application and required contract/payment records through agent's tax office under art. 194; no assumed automatic refund | [Art. 194(4)-(6)](https://pravo.by/document/?guid=3871&p0=hk0900071) |
| Representative office/branch registration | Apply at local tax office within 10 working days of opening; dependent-agent activity before commencement | [General Code art. 70(1.1),(1.5)-(1.6)](https://pravo.by/document/?guid=3871&p0=hk0200166) |
| Property registration | Foreign owner registers for tax before state registration of relevant property right | [General Code art. 70(1.2)](https://pravo.by/document/?guid=3871&p0=hk0200166) |
| Resident individual foreign/high income | Return by 31 March following year; authority notice by 30 April; pay by 1 June | [Special Code art. 219](https://pravo.by/document/?guid=3871&p0=hk0900071) |
| Nonresident individual expected taxable activity/disposal | Declare/pay before activity, with real-estate payment within 5 working days after notice; cessation reconciliation and extra payment have separate 5-working-day rules | [Art. 218](https://pravo.by/document/?guid=3871&p0=hk0900071) |
| Belarus citizen permanently departing | Declare current-year receipts through declaration date, pay within 15 calendar days after notice and before departure; later receipts need additional declaration | [Art. 222](https://pravo.by/document/?guid=3871&p0=hk0900071) |
| Offshore levy | Monthly return by 20th after month; cash transfer tax before transfer, noncash generally next day; specified card/LC/direct-debit cases by 22nd after month | [Art. 267](https://pravo.by/document/?guid=3871&p0=hk0900071) |

For a PE, use the quarterly/annual corporate return and payment calendar in the corporate skill. Register/notify the actual activity and preserve local records rather than waiting until day 181. A foreign office/branch registration does not itself prove that every payment is PE-attributable. The agent obtains the Belarus tax office's PE confirmation for the art. 193 exemption by the applicable deadline; otherwise the existence of another PE does not justify withholding nothing. [Arts. 180, 186, 193(4)](https://pravo.by/document/?guid=3871&p0=hk0900071); [General Code arts. 69-70](https://pravo.by/document/?guid=3871&p0=hk0200166).

On foreign-company/PE cessation, file the final return at least five working days before cessation and settle tax/interest no later than one working day after filing (General Code art. 44(4)). Special Code art. 186(2) uses actual gross profit through cessation. For Belarus-entity liquidation, art. 44(1) requires an initial return within five working days after the liquidation application, tax/interest by the 22nd of the following month, and a final return at least ten working days before the liquidation balance if taxable objects arise during liquidation; the latter settlement is within five working days after that return. Ordinary intervening returns continue unless the paragraph's no-further-object condition applies. The liquidator settles the company's liabilities under art. 44(2). A liquidated branch's return is due by the 20th following the liquidation month and payment by the 22nd of the filing month (art. 44(3)). Use the corporate skill's actual-profit base/rate and separately classify shareholder distributions under arts. 189-190; settlement procedure is not a deemed-disposal exemption. Foreign-entity deregistration under General Code art. 70(4) requires the specified twelve-month interval, clearance of liabilities/penalties and completion of pending audit conditions, followed by the ten-working-day action period; it is not automatic on closing a bank account. This pack does not establish every corporate migration, deemed-disposal or liquidation charge. Do not infer an exemption or zero exit tax from that limit. [General Code arts. 44, 70(4)](https://pravo.by/document/?guid=3871&p0=hk0200166); [Special Code art. 186(2)](https://pravo.by/document/?guid=3871&p0=hk0900071).

## Penalties and interest

Late-payment interest under General Tax Code art. 55 is computed separately for each period of constant unpaid tax and National Bank refinancing rate: `unpaid tax × overdue calendar days × annual rate / 360 / 100`. Count from the day after the due date through payment; interest reflected in the audit decision on audit-assessed tax is capped at that assessed tax amount; apply art. 55(3) exclusions and art. 55(4)'s four-decimal interest-rate precision. Use the rate actually in force in each period, not a single assumed 2026 rate. [General Code art. 55](https://pravo.by/document/?guid=3871&p0=hk0200166).

Late agent returns use Administrative Code art. 14.2. Under art. 14.5, the lower withholding-default bracket requires both the amount ceiling (4 basic units for an entrepreneur, 10 for a company) and no more than 5% of the period amount. Fines are 0.4-4 basic units / 2-20 basic units respectively; exceeding either condition invokes 40% of the unpaid amount, minimum 2 / 10 basic units. Responsible-person and intentional-default sanctions are separate. Preserve voluntary-correction/payment facts and assess statutory exceptions before imposing a fine. [Administrative Code arts. 14.2, 14.5](https://pravo.by/document/?guid=3871&p0=hk2100091). The basic unit is BYN 45 from 1 January 2026 ([Council Resolution 651 of 20 November 2025](https://pravo.by/document/?guid=12551&p0=C22500651)).

## Cross-border points

For each payment, document the recipient country, legal and beneficial owner, income class, treaty's entry/termination status and exact article's suspension status at the relevant date. Council Resolution 164 of 7 March 2024 suspends specified dividend, interest and capital-gain provisions with listed countries for 1 June 2024-31 December 2026; the exact country/article conditions and subsequent changes require checking, not a claim that every treaty with those countries is wholly suspended. The Lithuania treaty terminated from 1 January 2026. Use domestic law where the relevant treaty relief is unavailable. [Resolution 164, para. 2 and annex](https://pravo.by/document/?guid=3871&p0=C22400164); [MNS commencement notice](https://nalog.gov.by/news/23386/); [MNS Lithuania termination](https://nalog.gov.by/news/29303/); [MNS current treaty directory](https://nalog.gov.by/international/double_taxation/).

Art. 194 requires the residence confirmation for the relevant period, with its specified annual/timing exceptions. Beneficial-owner documentation is a separate requirement; the BYN 40,000 aggregate unclaimed-tax documentation exception does not create substantive treaty entitlement. If evidence arrives late, withhold domestically then use the documented refund route. Individuals' residence certificates and refunds use art. 224(4). For outbound corporate claims, obtain a Belarus residence certificate under art. 187(7), naming the foreign country and available income details. Individuals use the prescribed Belarus residence certificate under art. 224(5); foreign residence evidence and treaty refunds remain under art. 224(4). [Arts. 187, 194, 224](https://pravo.by/document/?guid=3871&p0=hk0900071).

VAT place/reverse charge/imports, transfer pricing, controlled debt and social-security coverage are separate tests. A direct-tax business-profit exemption does not settle any of them. This skill supplies the verification procedure, not a comprehensive country-by-country treaty-rate matrix or treaty-specific MAP/APA analysis.

## Two worked examples that recompute

**1. Gross royalty paid to a non-PE foreign company.** A documented art. 189 royalty of BYN 100,000 gross, no art. 190 deduction, no available treaty relief and no offshore-levy trigger. Domestic 15% withholding is 15,000; net payment is 85,000. The agent reports by the 20th and pays by the 22nd after the quarter. [Arts. 189-193](https://pravo.by/document/?guid=3871&p0=hk0900071).

```oa-check
name: BY 2026 non-PE foreign-company royalty withholding
inputs:
  gross_royalty: 100000
  withholding_rate: 0.15
steps:
- withholding: gross_royalty * withholding_rate
- net_payment: gross_royalty - withholding
expected:
  withholding: 15000
  net_payment: 85000
```

**2. Resident's capped foreign-tax credit.** A resident receives BYN 10,000 of qualifying foreign income, after currency conversion. It is taxable at ordinary 13%, has no deductions, is outside the specified Belarus high-income pool, and is not excluded offshore income. Qualifying foreign tax of 2,000 is evidenced in time; assume the operative treaty permits that foreign tax. Belarus tax is 1,300, credit 1,300 and additional Belarus tax zero. The excess 700 is not a credit against unrelated income. [Arts. 214(1.1), 224](https://pravo.by/document/?guid=3871&p0=hk0900071).

```oa-check
name: BY 2026 resident foreign-tax credit cap
inputs:
  foreign_taxable_income: 10000
  belarus_rate: 0.13
  qualifying_foreign_tax: 2000
steps:
- belarus_tax: foreign_taxable_income * belarus_rate
- credit: min(belarus_tax, qualifying_foreign_tax)
- balance: belarus_tax - credit
- uncredited_foreign_tax: qualifying_foreign_tax - credit
expected:
  belarus_tax: 1300
  credit: 1300
  balance: 0
  uncredited_foreign_tax: 700
```

## Ask the client first

- What are each person's travel days, home/family links, prior residence and intended departure/cessation dates?
- Who are the legal and beneficial recipients, their countries, PE locations and actual functions?
- What are the contract class, gross/net clause, accrual/payment dates, currencies and noncash settlements?
- Which residence/beneficial-owner certificates, treaty-status checks and foreign-tax proofs are available?
- Which Belarus registrations, returns, payments, audits and earlier credits/refunds exist?
- Are offshore accounts, related parties, asset/share transfers, services or construction sites involved?
