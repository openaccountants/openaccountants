# Cambodia Corporate Income Tax v1.0

> **General reference only.** This skill is general tax/accounting reference material for AI-assisted workflows. It has not been reviewed for any specific person's facts, documents, elections, deadlines, residency, filing status, or local procedures. Do not rely on it to file, pay, amend, or take a tax position without review by a qualified professional in the relevant jurisdiction.

## Scope

Prepare a 2026 Cambodian Tax on Income working paper for an ordinary resident company, distinguishing annual income tax, minimum tax, monthly prepayments, advance tax on dividend distributions and withholding. It is a statutory computation outline, not a complete return or a company-law distribution approval. The English reference translation of the 2023 Law is used below; the promulgated Khmer text prevails. Do not use a bank balance as taxable profit.

Resident taxpayers are within the worldwide-income scope; non-residents are within the Cambodian-source scope. A legal person or partnership organized or managed in Cambodia, or having its principal place of business there, is resident under Article 5. A permanent establishment has its own statutory treatment; it is not simply a foreign company with the ordinary worldwide base. Sole proprietorships and partnerships are not legal persons for this chapter's tax definition: use [individual income tax](kh-income-tax.md). Source: [Law Articles 4-5](https://www.tax.gov.kh/u6rhf7ogbi6/gdtstream/816f7e7b-e4be-4d8f-a789-e587b92dbe2e).

## Ask the client first

1. Legal form, residence, permanent establishments, financial year and GDT taxpayer classification?
2. Accounting profit, tax reconciliation, turnover excluding VAT but including other taxes, and evidence of proper accounting records?
3. QIP certificate, exemption period, independent audit and any sector-specific incentive or suspension?
4. Losses and credit schedules, prepayment receipts, tax withheld, foreign income and certified foreign tax paid?
5. Any distribution, its profit year, whether annual income tax has been paid, recipient residence and proposed treaty claim?
6. Resident/non-resident service, royalty, rental, interest and insurance payments, recipient registration status, gross/net contract and supporting documents?

## The method, step by step

### Establish the applicable base and regime [Law Articles 4-11, 19-21](https://www.tax.gov.kh/u6rhf7ogbi6/gdtstream/816f7e7b-e4be-4d8f-a789-e587b92dbe2e)

Use accounting results adjusted under the tax law, rather than treating every booked expense as deductible. Trace business purpose and amount; distinguish capital costs from current expenses and identify related-party payments. Article 19 excludes entertainment/recreation, private living/family costs except the stated salary-taxed fringe-benefit exception, income tax and salary withholding tax, losses on sales or exchanges of property directly or indirectly between related persons, and expenses without the required incurred amount and business purpose. Detailed depreciation, interest limitations, charitable deductions and related-party pricing are not computed here.

The self-assessment regime has small, medium and large taxpayer categories. Do not revive the old estimated-regime language or infer classification from an entity suffix. A calendar tax year is the baseline; an enterprise controlled by a legal non-resident may apply for a different fiscal year. New and closing enterprises require the Article 8 period rules. Confirm the approved year before deriving a deadline.

### Rates and separate taxes [Law Articles 20-28](https://www.tax.gov.kh/u6rhf7ogbi6/gdtstream/816f7e7b-e4be-4d8f-a789-e587b92dbe2e)

All amounts below are statutory baseline rules used for the 2026 outline, subject to confirmed incentives and implementing instruments. Percentages appear in numerals and words in the source.

| Item | Rate and complete population boundary | Official source |
|---|---|---|
| Ordinary legal-person income tax | 20% of taxable income, not turnover | [Article 20(1)](https://www.tax.gov.kh/u6rhf7ogbi6/gdtstream/816f7e7b-e4be-4d8f-a789-e587b92dbe2e) |
| Petroleum/natural-resource income | 30% for income under a petroleum agreement or exploitation of natural resources, including timber, ore, gold and precious stones; specialist computation | [Article 20(2)](https://www.tax.gov.kh/u6rhf7ogbi6/gdtstream/816f7e7b-e4be-4d8f-a789-e587b92dbe2e) |
| QIP exemption period | 0% only during the qualifying income-tax exemption period under the Investment Law; do not grant it merely because a business invests | [Article 20(3)](https://www.tax.gov.kh/u6rhf7ogbi6/gdtstream/816f7e7b-e4be-4d8f-a789-e587b92dbe2e) |
| Property/other-risk insurance or reinsurance | 5% of gross premiums received in the tax year for the stated activities in Cambodia; life insurance with savings and other activities follow Article 20 instead | [Article 21](https://www.tax.gov.kh/u6rhf7ogbi6/gdtstream/816f7e7b-e4be-4d8f-a789-e587b92dbe2e) |
| Minimum tax | 1% of annual turnover including all taxes except VAT, imposed where proper accounting records are not maintained; QIP exemption requires the stated independent audit report; reconcile with income tax, never add the full amount twice | [Articles 24, 39](https://www.tax.gov.kh/u6rhf7ogbi6/gdtstream/816f7e7b-e4be-4d8f-a789-e587b92dbe2e) |
| Monthly income-tax prepayment | 1% of the previous month's turnover including all taxes except VAT for an enterprise liable under self-assessment, including a QIP after its exemption period; credit at annual liquidation; check actual incentive/suspension separately | [Article 28](https://www.tax.gov.kh/u6rhf7ogbi6/gdtstream/816f7e7b-e4be-4d8f-a789-e587b92dbe2e) |
| Resident royalties and qualifying non-bank interest | 15% on the gross payment by a resident business payer under Article 25; royalties include intangible assets and equity interests in mineral resources; interest paid to domestic banks and Article 9 exempt income are exceptions | [Article 25(1)(a), (2)](https://www.tax.gov.kh/u6rhf7ogbi6/gdtstream/816f7e7b-e4be-4d8f-a789-e587b92dbe2e) |
| Resident services | 15% on gross service income paid by a resident taxpayer carrying on business to a resident recipient outside self-assessment, including management/consultancy/similar services, subject to the Article 25(2) exempt-income exception; do not replace this population test with a supplier-name match | [Article 25(1)(a), (2)](https://www.tax.gov.kh/u6rhf7ogbi6/gdtstream/816f7e7b-e4be-4d8f-a789-e587b92dbe2e) |
| Resident property rental | 10% on gross movable/immovable-property rental income within the resident business-payer rule; check the Article 25(2) exempt-income exception | [Article 25(1)(b), (2)](https://www.tax.gov.kh/u6rhf7ogbi6/gdtstream/816f7e7b-e4be-4d8f-a789-e587b92dbe2e) |
| Domestic bank deposit interest | 6% fixed-term and 4% non-fixed-term interest paid by domestic banks to a resident taxpayer, subject to Article 25(2) | [Article 25(1)(c)-(d), (2)](https://www.tax.gov.kh/u6rhf7ogbi6/gdtstream/816f7e7b-e4be-4d8f-a789-e587b92dbe2e) |
| Non-resident payment | 14% on gross Cambodian-source income within Article 33 paid by a resident business payer, including a non-resident's PE; gross reinsurance premiums for Cambodian property/other risks are excepted; treaty application is separate | [Articles 26, 33](https://www.tax.gov.kh/u6rhf7ogbi6/gdtstream/816f7e7b-e4be-4d8f-a789-e587b92dbe2e) |

For non-resident withholding, test source before the rate. Article 33's non-exhaustive list covers resident-paid interest/dividends, services performed in Cambodia, management/technical services paid by a resident, Cambodian-situated property income and relevant gains, specified resident/PE-paid royalties, Cambodian-risk premiums, PE asset gains and PE business income. Do not apply the rule to every foreign transfer. A treaty rate needs the actual treaty, beneficial-owner/residence evidence and the current GDT relief procedure. The old generic “typically 10%” treaty proposal is not a rate instruction.

Article 27 makes Article 26 withholding and Article 25 withholding on recipients outside self-assessment final for those recipients. This does not turn income-tax prepayments into expenses.

### Reconcile annual tax and credits [Law Articles 24, 36-39](https://www.tax.gov.kh/u6rhf7ogbi6/gdtstream/816f7e7b-e4be-4d8f-a789-e587b92dbe2e)

1. Determine taxable income and apply the applicable Article 20/21 rule. Substantiate loss use; Article 17 allows successive carryforward through the fifth following tax year in loss order, not an unrestricted carryback. Detailed loss eligibility/changes of ownership require current implementing review.
2. Deduct allowable advance-distribution tax and foreign-tax credit under Article 37, no further than that tax liability. Article 36 foreign credit is per foreign country, limited to the lower of certified foreign tax and the proportionate Cambodian income tax; document procedural conditions. The statute permits eligible excess foreign credit through the fifth following year, in origin order.
3. Reconcile eligible resident withholding and income-tax prepayments against the Article 37 result. Retain receipts and a separate ledger for each credit. Article 38 contemplates a refund application or following-year prepayment carryforward after minimum-tax reconciliation; a negative balance is not an automatically approved refund.
4. Determine whether minimum tax applies under Article 24 and reconcile Article 39. For an uncomplicated case with no special credits, annual burden is the higher of income tax and applicable minimum tax, less eligible prepayments. Do not use that shortcut for special credit/incentive cases.

### Check distributions independently [Law Articles 22-23, 26](https://www.tax.gov.kh/u6rhf7ogbi6/gdtstream/816f7e7b-e4be-4d8f-a789-e587b92dbe2e)

The old wording about additional tax determined only by the historic profit-tax rate is incomplete. Article 23 addresses a dividend distributed before annual income tax has been paid: subject to its QIP exemption-period exception, gross up the distribution at the appropriate annual income-tax rate and apply that rate. This advance tax becomes an annual income-tax credit; any excess over annual income tax carries forward against income tax in subsequent tax years under Article 23(2). Qualifying subsequent redistribution is not charged again under the same Article. Property/other-risk insurance premium income under Article 21(1) is excepted. Separate the recipient's non-resident withholding from the company's advance tax. Branch remittances require Article 22, including whether transferred income has already borne Article 20 tax and the Article 26 question. Do not compute a distribution from unidentified profit pools.

### Filing, payments and enforcement [Law Articles 8, 29-32, 223-233](https://www.tax.gov.kh/u6rhf7ogbi6/gdtstream/816f7e7b-e4be-4d8f-a789-e587b92dbe2e)

Annual returns and payment are due within 3 months after year-end, including a loss return. For the ordinary calendar 2026 year, 31 March 2027 is the derived baseline date; verify any later applicable official notice or extension before filing. Small taxpayers have the annual obligation in Article 30, with their own accounting rules.

The Law sets the 20th of the following month for withholding, advance-distribution tax and income-tax prepayment. The [live GDT calendar](https://www.tax.gov.kh/en/), read on 14 November 2026, specifically shows October 2026 monthly returns due 20 November 2026 manually and 25 November 2026 online. Use that actual period/channel evidence; do not silently treat the statutory 20th as the universal e-filing deadline or extrapolate a future holiday calendar.

Escalate enforcement under Articles 223-225 and 233. The negligence, serious-negligence, unilateral-assessment, reminder-letter and interest rules have different triggers, bases and timing exclusions. Do not produce a penalty bill from bank data; this skill does not compute penalties.

## Boundary and exception table

| Situation | Action |
|---|---|
| Petroleum, natural resources, insurance or QIP | Retain the rate/population above, obtain the sector computation and complete incentive conditions; ordinary-company examples do not apply automatically. |
| Resident registered service supplier | Establish self-assessment status and documents. The former VAT-invoice-only exception is not a substitute for Article 25 and current implementing requirements. |
| Royalty, interest, rent, dividend or foreign payment | Identify legal nature, gross/net basis, residence, source and applicable exemption before withholding. |
| Treaty proposed | Obtain the actual treaty and relief procedure; no generic reduced rate. |
| Loss, foreign tax, advance distribution or minimum tax | Keep separate schedules and apply statutory credit ordering. |
| No proper books or accounting/tax difference unexplained | Do not assume minimum-tax exemption or convert cash profit into the annual return. |

## Worked cases

All KHR amounts are hypothetical inputs, not additional thresholds. No incentives, foreign taxes or special credits are assumed.

### Ordinary company with proper accounting records

Taxable income 500,000,000 gives 100,000,000 income tax. Turnover 2,000,000,000 does not create minimum tax where the assumed proper-records condition is met. Eligible prepayments 70,000,000 leave 30,000,000 payable. The prepayments reduce tax; deducting them from profit would be wrong.

```oa-check
name: Ordinary resident company and credited prepayments
inputs:
  taxable_income: 500000000
  prepayments: 70000000
steps:
  income_tax: taxable_income * 0.20
  balance: income_tax - prepayments
expected:
  income_tax: 100000000
  balance: 30000000
```

### Minimum-tax decision where books fail the required standard

Assume Article 24 minimum tax applies: turnover 1,000,000,000 produces 10,000,000 minimum tax, while taxable income 20,000,000 produces 4,000,000 income tax. The annual burden is 10,000,000, including a 6,000,000 minimum-tax difference. Eligible prepayments 7,000,000 leave 3,000,000 payable. If proper-records exemption actually applies instead, the burden is 4,000,000 and the 3,000,000 excess prepayment goes to the Article 38 refund/carryforward process, not an automatic cash refund. Establishing record status changes the outcome by 6,000,000.

```oa-check
name: Minimum-tax exposure versus proper-records exemption
inputs:
  turnover: 1000000000
  taxable_income: 20000000
  prepayments: 7000000
steps:
  income_tax: taxable_income * 0.20
  minimum_tax: turnover * 0.01
  burden_if_applicable: max(income_tax, minimum_tax)
  minimum_difference: burden_if_applicable - income_tax
  balance_if_applicable: burden_if_applicable - prepayments
  excess_if_exempt: prepayments - income_tax
expected:
  income_tax: 4000000
  minimum_tax: 10000000
  minimum_difference: 6000000
  balance_if_applicable: 3000000
  excess_if_exempt: 3000000
```

## When to refuse or refer

Refer QIP/special sectors, disputed PE/source/residence, transfer pricing, unproven loss eligibility, treaty relief, mixed dividend pools, branch remittances, deficient books and enforcement to a qualified Cambodian practitioner. Hold a withholding computation if payee status, tax source or gross/net contract is unknown. This skill does not authorize filing, payment, an incentive election, or distribution.
