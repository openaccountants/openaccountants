# Cambodia Corporate Income Tax

## Tax on Income — rates and base (2025)

- **Standard Tax on Income rate** — 20% percent  _(Law on Taxation — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/cambodia/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_
- **Oil, gas and mineral exploitation rate** — 30% percent  _(Law on Taxation — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/cambodia/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_
- **Insurance/reinsurance of general (non-life) risks rate** — 5% of gross premium income percent  _(Law on Taxation — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/cambodia/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_
- **Tax base** — Net taxable profit (gross income less allowable deductions); residents on worldwide income, non-residents on Cambodia-source income  _(Law on Taxation — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/cambodia/corporate/income-determination)_
- **Minimum Tax** — 1% of annual turnover inclusive of all taxes except VAT; payable if it exceeds TOI, unless the taxpayer maintains proper accounting records and is exempt percent  _(Law on Taxation — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/cambodia/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_
- **Monthly Prepayment of Tax on Income (PTOI)** — 1% of monthly turnover inclusive of all taxes except VAT, creditable against annual TOI percent  _(Law on Taxation — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/cambodia/corporate/tax-administration)_
- **Withholding tax on dividends (additional profit tax on distribution)** — Additional tax on dividend distribution applies depending on the rate at which profits were taxed (e.g. 0% if already taxed at 20%); WHT of 14% on dividends paid to non-residents percent  _(Law on Taxation — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/cambodia/corporate/withholding-taxes)_
- **Withholding tax on payments to non-residents (general)** — 14% on Cambodia-source interest, royalties, rent, dividends, management/technical service fees percent  _(Law on Taxation — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/cambodia/corporate/withholding-taxes)_
- **Domestic WHT on royalties (resident payee)** — 15% percent  _(Law on Taxation — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/cambodia/corporate/withholding-taxes)_
- **Domestic WHT on services (resident payee)** — 15% (exempt where paid to a registered taxpayer supported by a valid VAT invoice) percent  _(Law on Taxation — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/cambodia/corporate/withholding-taxes)_
- **Domestic WHT on rental of movable/immovable property (resident payee)** — 10% percent  _(Law on Taxation — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/cambodia/corporate/withholding-taxes)_
- **Domestic WHT on interest paid to residents** — 15% (6% on interest on fixed-term deposits and 4% on savings deposits paid by domestic banks) percent  _(Law on Taxation — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/cambodia/corporate/withholding-taxes)_
- **Reduced WHT under double tax agreements** — Typically reduced to 10% for dividends, interest, royalties and technical/management fees under most Cambodian DTAs percent  _(Applicable Double Taxation Agreement — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/cambodia/corporate/withholding-taxes)_
- **Annual TOI return and payment deadline** — Within 3 months after financial year-end (31 March for calendar-year taxpayers)  _(Law on Taxation — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/cambodia/corporate/tax-administration)_
