# Sint Maarten: tax pack

Tax year: the calendar year (2026). Dutch Sint Maarten (SX) is a country within the Kingdom of the Netherlands, separate from French Saint-Martin (MF). Authority: the Inspectorate of Taxes, Sint Maarten Tax Administration (tax.sx). Sint Maarten levies a 5% turnover tax, not a VAT. Amounts are Caribbean guilders (Cg or XCG).
