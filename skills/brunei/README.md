# Brunei: tax pack

Brunei Darussalam taxes companies only: corporate income tax at 18.5% after a small-company threshold, on a preceding-year basis (year of assessment 2026 taxes the basis period ending in 2025), with withholding tax on certain payments to non-residents. There is no personal income tax, no capital gains tax and no VAT or GST. Employers and citizen or permanent resident employees pay SPK retirement contributions. The Revenue Division of the Ministry of Finance and Economy runs income tax, withholding and stamp duty; TAP runs SPK; ROCBN registers companies. Amounts are BND. This map covers 2026.
