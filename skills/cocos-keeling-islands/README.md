# Cocos (Keeling) Islands (CC): tax pack

**Built from the law. Not yet approved. Written by Ryan Duguid; imported from his fork and brought to the standard by Claude on 14 November 2026. Not reviewed by an accountant.** Cocos (Keeling) Islands are an Australian external territory. Commonwealth Acts extend there of their own force (Cocos (Keeling) Islands Act 1955, section 8E), and geographical Australia for income tax includes the territory (ITAA 1997 section 960-505(1)(d)-(e)), so Australian income tax, Medicare levy, PAYG withholding and the super guarantee apply under the ATO. GST does not: the territory is outside the indirect tax zone. Currency is the Australian dollar. Income year 2026-27 (1 July 2026 to 30 June 2027).

Australian figures have one home in `skills/australia/`: the rates card `au-rates-2026-27`, `au-medicare-levy`, `au-super-guarantee`, `australia-payroll` and `au-company-tax`.
