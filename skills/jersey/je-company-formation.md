# Jersey company formation and reporting

## Scope and period

This partial reference covers selected ordinary company formation and reporting rules. It does not fill the formation base's complete entity catalogue, capital, cost and licensing slots, or the complete financial-statements companion. Keep entity recommendations, total engagement costs and the complete output package held until those requirements and the applicant's facts are established. Hypothetical source examples remain usable as references.

The reused [*Companies (Jersey) Law 1991*](https://www.jerseylaw.je/laws/current/l_30_1991) snapshot applies from 30 September 2026; current business-licence Order from 2 October 2026, status Regulations from 14 September 2026 and disclosure Regulations from 1 June 2026. The JFSC fee notice covers 1 January to 31 December 2026. Earlier events and later fees require their own sources. An editing date does not establish an operative period.

## Company form, documents and submission

[Companies Law Articles 3 to 9](https://www.jerseylaw.je/laws/current/l_30_1991) distinguish public/private, par value/no par value/guarantee, limited/unlimited, cell and limited-life forms. Member liability follows the actual share/guarantee/unlimited provisions. Do not infer an entity recommendation, public/private status or capital requirement from a generic portal option or member count alone. Full special-form and deemed-public analysis remains open.

The memorandum states the name, public/private status, par/no-par/guarantee form and subscriber particulars, with the applicable share/guarantee provisions. Articles and subscriber execution must meet Articles 4 to 7. Article 6 permits appropriate adoption of the Standard Table. The [JFSC incorporation procedure](https://www.jerseyfsc.org/registry/register-or-make-a-change/incorporate-a-company/) says fully adopting that table removes the need to upload its text; the memorandum and subscriber page still accompany the submission. Part adoption needs the relevant replacement articles. The preset portal capital is not evidence of a universal statutory minimum.

The [JFSC About companies guidance](https://www.jerseyfsc.org/registry/register-or-make-a-change/about-companies/), issued 14 July 2025, describes submission by a Jersey-resident beneficial owner where beneficial ownership is wholly Jersey resident, or by a regulated provider licensed for formation services. Verify submitter and nominated-person eligibility; do not present incorporation as unrestricted overseas self-service. The process includes an application for share-issue consent under the Control of Borrowing Order, with the applicable regulatory conditions.

For an established ordinary company route, the incorporation checklist requires:

1. Proposed name, company form and capital structure consistent with its memorandum. Advance name reservation is optional; acceptance is still subject to the registrar.
2. A Jersey registered office, with its occupier's authorisation, and accurate business activity/Sound Business Policy information.
3. Required memorandum, articles/adoption and subscriber execution, associated-party particulars and required identification through the authorised official process.
4. A structure chart when beneficial owners differ from subscribers, the entity is in a group, or activity is sensitive; clarify ownership/control and nominated-person arrangements.
5. An authorised myRegistry submission, chosen processing service, document review and payment of the applicable fee. Follow returned-submission actions; rejected applications cannot simply be resubmitted as that application.
6. Registrar approval and certificate. [Article 9](https://www.jerseylaw.je/laws/current/l_30_1991) establishes existence from the incorporation date stated in the certificate. Do not assume a company exists because its application or name is accepted; pre-incorporation contracts need separate liability analysis.

The procedure's service times are processing options. Sensitive activity can prevent guaranteed priority processing, and fees are non-refundable. No application, identity document or payment is submitted by this guide.

## Published 2026 fees

The **final** [2026 JFSC Fees Notice](https://www.jerseyfsc.org/media/kdpn4ow1/2026-fees-notices.pdf), Part I sections 1, 3 and 6, supplies these selected fees. They are registry charges, not a total provider/legal/office/audit cost quotation.

| Selected service or fee category | GBP |
|---|---:|
| Ordinary company incorporation within five business days | 205 |
| Within three business days | 308 |
| Within two business days | 395 |
| Within one business day | 543 |
| Within two hours | 856 |
| Out of hours, by agreement, minimum | 1,548 |
| Optional online name reservation | 10 |
| Annual confirmation: entity administered by trust company or fund services business | 339, including government levy 175 |
| Annual confirmation: ordinarily administered entity | 220, including government levy 145 |

Confirm the annual fee category. [Disclosure Regulations, Regulation 7](https://www.jerseylaw.je/laws/current/ro_138_2020) separately sets the levy at GBP 175 for relevant-business administered entities and GBP 145 for others; relevant business uses the statutory fund/trust definitions and excludes class O-only trust-company services. This settles the levy rule, not the JFSC notice's undefined ordinarily administered base-fee category. Hold a disputed class O-only total rather than choosing a total from the levy alone. No 2027 amount is inferred.

## Business and staffing licences

The [government startup sequence](https://www.gov.je/Industry/BusinessAdviceHub/StartingABusiness/Pages/home.aspx) puts limited-company incorporation before its business-licence application. Incorporation automatically initiates corporate tax registration, with tax references and filing information; it does not establish GST, employer, industry or data-protection registration completion. Check each stream separately, with je-gst, je-payroll and je-corporate-income-tax as applicable.

[CHWL Articles 22 to 25](https://www.jerseylaw.je/laws/current/l_31_2012) and the [2026 Licences and Exemptions Order](https://www.jerseylaw.je/laws/current/ro_40_2026) distinguish resident/non-resident business, business licences and staffing permissions. Incorporation abroad, a short visit or regulated status is not a general exemption. Article 23(2)'s unpaid-work definition boundary is distinct; the Article 22 payment definition includes specified benefits and receipts, not salary alone.

Order Article 5(4)'s small-hours exemption from the business-licence requirement needs **all** of:

- A resident business carried on by a sole trader or company with only one member.
- That individual holds Permanent Entitled, Entitled, Licensed, Licensed for Lease Only or Entitled for Work Only status.
- That individual is the only person working in or for the business.
- That individual works in or for it for **less than eight hours a week**.

Exactly eight hours fails this exemption; no averaging qualification appears in the provision. The [detailed licensing guidance](https://www.gov.je/Industry/BusinessAdviceHub/IndustryRegulationsResponsibilities/Pages/ControlHousingWorkBusinessGuidance.aspx) omits the company one-member condition, so use the complete statutory test. Failure of this particular exemption still requires checking other lawful exemptions before deciding the overall licence position.

[Status Regulations, Regulation 24](https://www.jerseylaw.je/laws/current/ro_16_2025) replaces existing Registered status with Standard status from 14 September 2026. Standard is not in Article 5(4)'s eligible list. Do not replace the separate term registered housing or disregard the transition's conditions for other statuses.

Order Article 6 separately addresses staffing licences and maximum permissions for Licensed, Licensed for Lease Only and Standard workers, with its exempt-worker rules. A business-licence exemption alone is not an answer to every employment/status/immigration requirement. The current licensing guidance states no fee for a resident business licence and ordinary processing targets of five working days resident/ten working days non-resident, with complex cases taking longer. Staffing permission fees and non-resident/industry charges remain separate. Do not label an entire staffing or company-formation process free.

The startup guidance also identifies data-protection registration, employer combined returns and industry permissions. Direct data-protection exceptions and complete licensing/worker branches remain to be verified before an engagement checklist is complete.

## Registry obligations

[JFSC annual confirmation guidance](https://www.jerseyfsc.org/registry/annual-confirmation/) covers entities registered in or before the year preceding that confirmation. Submit from 1 January to the last day of February through an authorised nominated person. Confirm registered office and associated parties and update company share-capital information first. Entities dissolved or placed into winding up before or during that confirmation period do not have to submit; Jersey registered charities have no fee. Verify the actual entity and exemption.

Notify beneficial-owner, controller or significant-person information changes within 21 days of knowledge. Annual confirmation does not replace those updates or tax returns. LLPs have additional solvency/accounting confirmations, and relevant special-purpose vehicle COBO consents have separate confirmation conditions; neither branch is completed by the ordinary company steps above.

## Accounts, records and audit

The current [Companies Law, Articles 103 to 105, 108 and 113](https://www.jerseylaw.je/laws/current/l_30_1991) supplies the statutory framework:

| Ordinary company obligation | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Accounting records | Explain transactions and disclose financial position with reasonable accuracy |
| Record retention | At least 10 years from creation, subject to winding-up provisions |
| Accounts period | No more than 18 months, subject to the holding-company consolidated accounts provision |
| Accounts preparation | Identify the adopted generally accepted accounting principles; directors approve and sign |
| Public company accounts actions | Ordinarily within 7 months after period end |
| Private company accounts actions | Ordinarily within 10 months after period end |

Article 105 has exceptions for laying accounts before a general meeting and member requests. Article 108 imposes public-company registration obligations and exceptions, separately from the private-company timetable. Do not turn this table into a filing instruction without checking status and the applicable provisions.

Article 113 ordinarily requires an auditor for a public company, where the articles require one, or where members resolve to require one. It also contains qualified disapplication and other exceptions. Market traded companies need a recognised auditor. Regulated entities can have further obligations. A private-company label alone does not settle audit requirements.

Revenue Jersey separately requires the relevant signed financial accounts or statements with corporate returns, including dormant resident companies and Jersey branch activity within the return's scope. See the [corporate return notes](https://www.gov.je/TaxesMoney/IncomeTax/Companies/CompanyTaxGuidance/pages/companytaxreturnnotes.aspx).

First establish whether an ordinary company return is required for the year or period. [Income Tax Law, Article 120AC](https://www.jerseylaw.je/laws/current/l_29_1961) disapplies specified ordinary returns for relevant MNE group entities, with conditions in Article 120AB. See `je-corporate-income-tax` and `je-pillar-two`; this tax-return exception does not establish an exemption from company accounting or registry duties.

## Economic substance

[Companies Economic Substance Law, Articles 3 to 5A](https://www.jerseylaw.je/laws/current/l_3_2019) identifies relevant activities, core income-generating activities and the substance tests. Relevant company activities include banking, insurance, fund management, finance and leasing, headquarters, shipping, holding, intellectual property, and distribution or service centres, subject to definitions and exclusions.

For an ordinary resident company with gross income from a relevant activity, assess Jersey direction and management, adequate people, expenditure and physical assets, Jersey core income-generating activities and control of permitted outsourcing. Self-managed funds have a separate provision. Retain contemporaneous evidence and use the published guidance; a registered office alone does not satisfy these tests. Partnership substance requirements require separate legislation and remain outside this guide.

## Hypothetical worked positions

These are source illustrations for established facts, not a complete entity recommendation or first-year package.

1. **Selected incorporation charges.** An eligible ordinary 2026 company uses the five-business-day service and optional online name reservation. GBP 205 + GBP 10 = GBP 215 for those two registry items. Provider, capital, office and other applicable costs remain separate. Source: final 2026 JFSC Fees Notice.
2. **Service choice.** On the same established non-sensitive 2026 facts, the one-business-day fee GBP 543 exceeds the five-business-day fee GBP 205 by GBP 338. Choosing the higher service does not override the sensitive-activity processing qualification. Sources: fee notice and incorporation procedure.
3. **Annual fee period.** An established ordinarily administered company registered in 2025 and still subject to confirmation in 2026 uses the 2026 GBP 220 total, including GBP 145 levy. A company first incorporated in 2026 has its first ordinary annual confirmation in 2027; this notice supplies no verified 2027 fee. Sources: annual confirmation guidance and 2026 fee notice.
4. **One-member exemption.** An established resident company has one Entitled member, who is its only worker and works 7.5 hours each week. All Article 5(4) conditions are met. At eight hours, the same branch fails; another exemption and any staffing obligations still need separate analysis. Source: Licences and Exemptions Order Article 5(4).
5. **Two-member company.** A resident company with two members and seven hours' weekly work fails Article 5(4)'s one-member condition even if it has no employees. Do not adopt the broader guidance shorthand. No overall licence conclusion follows without checking other exemptions. Source: the same provision.
6. **Status and disputed fee.** A sole trader with Standard status working seven hours fails Article 5(4)'s eligible-status condition. Separately, an entity receiving only class O trust-company services is outside Regulation 7(2)(b)'s higher-levy treatment; this does not establish its full JFSC fee category. Hold the disputed total. Sources: Order Article 5(4), Status Regulations and Disclosure Regulations Regulation 7.
