# Jersey Class 2 contributions: 2026

## Scope and evidence

Establish the person's insured status, liability months, contribution notice and applicable exception or concession before calculating Class 2 contributions. This guide describes the statutory decision procedure and conditional arithmetic. An official assessment determines the notified liability; the illustrations below do not determine a payment notice or benefit entitlement.

Use [je-social-contributions](je-social-contributions.md) for the ordinary Class 1 calculation and long-term care (LTC) overview. Class 2 relief does not generally apply to LTC: [Social Security (Contributions) (Jersey) Order 1975, Article A4](https://www.jerseylaw.je/laws/current/ro_6108) preserves only its specified late-payment and erroneous-payment provisions for LTC. Keep LTC assessment separate.

The following procedure assumes non-negative, independently classified income and an ordinary adult case. Negative or disputed income, pension-age elections, education and other credits, overseas coverage, special classifications and benefit calculations need their applicable provisions before calculation. Do not substitute zero for missing information.

## Insured status and classification

[Social Security (Jersey) Law 1974, Article 3](https://www.jerseylaw.je/laws/current/l_22_1974) uses school leaving age and prescribed residence conditions. It divides insured people into Class 1 employment under a contract of service and Class 2, subject to classification orders. Age 18 and elapsed time since arrival are not universal tests.

[Residence Order 1974, Article 2](https://www.jerseylaw.je/laws/current/ro_6081) ordinarily measures 6 continuous months beginning with the month after arrival and ending after school leaving age. It includes immediate Class 1 employment coverage, a Minister-allowed Class 2 waiver after notification, and a full-time education/training exception for a person above school leaving age and under 18. Its special foreign-employer and foreign-contribution conditions can substitute 12 months. Verify the relevant branch and dates.

Two [Classification Order 1974, Schedule 1 Part 2](https://www.jerseylaw.je/laws/current/ro_6107) provisions need particular care:

- Paragraph 14 treats employment with monthly earnings below the lower monthly earnings limit as Class 2. Equality does not satisfy its strict comparison. Confirm the person's other employments and actual contributions.
- Paragraph 25 applies where the Minister is satisfied that the person, spouse, civil partner, nominee or agent beneficially holds at least half the company's shares, or otherwise has substantial control. Neither being a director alone nor holding less than half settles the classification. Paragraph 24 separately addresses specified avoidance or inequality cases.

Under [Residence Order, Article 4](https://www.jerseylaw.je/laws/current/ro_6081), an ordinary Class 2 person wholly outside Jersey for a month is not liable for that month. Voluntary full-rate payments have contribution-factor or previous-residence conditions. Article 4(3) excludes the continuing-overseas-employment case in Article 3(2)(b); refer that case separately. Do not prorate an annual illustration across months without verifying the applicable periods and rules.

## 2026 limits and contribution components

The [2026 Class 2 guidance](https://www.gov.je/Working/Contributions/Payments/pages/class2contributionsstatement.aspx) supplies SEL, UEL and published payment amounts. The [LTIA guidance](https://www.gov.je/Benefits/SicknessInjury/pages/longtermincapacityallowance.aspx) explicitly labels the monthly LEL for 2026. Annual equivalents below are twelve times the monthly limits.

| 2026 limit | Monthly GBP | Annual equivalent GBP |
|---|---:|---:|
| Lower earnings limit, LEL or L | 1,296 | 15,552 |
| Standard earnings limit, SEL or S | 6,062 | 72,744 |
| Upper earnings limit, UEL or U | 27,632 | 331,584 |

[Law, Schedule 1B paragraphs 3 and 4](https://www.jerseylaw.je/laws/current/l_22_1974) combine 2% for the Health Insurance Fund and 10.5% for Social Security on the lower band, plus 2.5% for Social Security between SEL and UEL. The combined lower rate is 12.5%. The printed nominal limits in paragraph 2 are subject to annual indexation; use the dated 2026 figures.

The published full monthly amount is GBP 1,297: GBP 757.75 on SEL plus GBP 539.25 on the upper band. [Article 8](https://www.jerseylaw.je/laws/current/l_22_1974) requires full rate where the person is not entitled to reduced rate or is entitled but does not apply. A payable reduced amount requires the applicable entitlement, application and assessment evidence. GBP 757.75 is the published amount at SEL; the statutory term 'standard contribution' in Schedule 1B paragraph 4A describes a separate Social Security Fund amount and excludes the Health Insurance allocation.

## Establish both statutory income inputs

[Contributions Order, Schedule 2](https://www.jerseylaw.je/laws/current/ro_6108) supplies the income definitions. Reconcile each component to the relevant assessment and underlying evidence before applying a branch:

1. Establish **income from self-employment** under paragraph 1. It includes Schedule 1B earnings; qualifying self-employment-derived company dividends, including specified family or household arrangements; partnership distributions; the specified company or partnership pension contributions for the person or family/household; self-employment-derived trust income; the specified pension-related Schedule D income; and Case IIA income. Apply each provision's conditions.
2. Set **E** to that self-employment income plus Class 1 earnings.
3. Establish the paragraph 3(2) amount: income-tax total income plus the approved holder pension contributions actually deducted under Article 131I(1), and applicable Article 135A deemed income for a high value resident. Reconcile those statutory additions; do not add all pension payments merely because another pension test uses them.
4. Set **T** to the greater of the paragraph 3(2) amount and E. E is a comparator, not a further addition to the tax-based amount. T must be at least E.

Gross salary, business turnover, net trading profit and pension Article 130C relevant earnings are not interchangeable with E or T. The captured sources do not settle every gross-fee/net-profit mapping. An assessment showing only taxable total income does not establish all the self-employment components. Obtain the statutory reconciliation or an official Class 2 assessment before deriving the inputs; otherwise stop the independent contribution calculation.

[Article 13B](https://www.jerseylaw.je/laws/current/ro_6108) ordinarily uses **2024 income for 2026 Articles 13 and 13A**, against **2026 limits**. The prior-full-time-education/training estimate exception requires Comptroller acceptance. Article 12 instead uses estimated current-year income, and Article 13E uses actual concession-year income.

## Applications and approved periods

[Contributions Order, Articles 16A and 16B](https://www.jerseylaw.je/laws/current/ro_6108) govern applications for exceptions and concessions under Part 1. Apply in writing in the form and manner required by the Minister or Comptroller. Supply the required earnings or income information and evidence in the required manner and timeframe. Article 16A(3) requires an application within 6 months of the date from which it seeks the exception or concession. Under Article 16A(4), the Minister or Comptroller may accept a late application if satisfied that circumstances justify acceptance.

Record the requested start date, application date and supporting evidence. For a late application, retain the authority's acceptance separately from the decision establishing relief and its approved period. Article 16B permits the authority to extend an exception, concession or credited-contribution period and set conditions for exercising those powers. Neither late acceptance nor an extension is automatic. Read these general provisions alongside the specific Article 13D startup cut-offs below; do not infer an earlier startup period from the six-month rule.

These synthetic cases illustrate the general application procedure. They do not establish income eligibility, an approved period or payable contributions:

| Requested relief start | Application date and evidence | Procedural result |
|---|---|---|
| 1 January 2026 | 15 June 2026; required form and evidence supplied | Within the general application period; establish applicable relief and its approved period separately |
| 1 January 2026 | 1 August 2026; no late-acceptance decision | Outside the general application period; do not assume relief applies |
| 1 January 2026 | 1 August 2026; actual written late-acceptance decision | Late application accepted; obtain the relief decision, approved period and conditions |

## Exceptions and the four reduced-rate branches

[Article 12](https://www.jerseylaw.je/laws/current/ro_6108) makes a Class 2 person eligible for low-income relief if estimated **2026 T does not exceed S/3**, or GBP 24,248. Equality satisfies the income condition. The Comptroller determines the exception's period and may impose conditions. Notify a failed condition immediately; the exception ends when a condition ceases to be fulfilled. No contributions are credited while this exception applies. Previously paid or credited contributions may still support benefits under Article 12(10).

The [protecting-records page](https://www.gov.je/Working/Contributions/Payments/pages/protectingcontributionsrecords.aspx) describes applicants who are not working and have income less than GBP 24,248. That wording does not settle the Comptroller's grant conditions for working applicants or equality. Show statutory income eligibility separately from an actual grant; do not promise relief.

For ordinary assessments, test [Article 13](https://www.jerseylaw.je/laws/current/ro_6108) first: **E is below L and T is below 2S/3**, or GBP 48,496. Both comparisons are strict. Establish the special exception and its application to the relevant year, rather than treating numerical eligibility as an automatic zero notice.

Where that exception does not apply, [Article 13A and Schedule 2 paragraph 4](https://www.jerseylaw.je/laws/current/ro_6108) identify deemed earnings B for an applicable reduced rate:

| Condition | Article 13A branch | B |
|---|---|---|
| E below L; T below S | (1) | S |
| E below L; T at least S | (2) | T |
| E at least L; T below 4S/3 | (3) | E |
| E at least L; T at least 4S/3 | (4) | T |

Four-thirds S is GBP 96,992. The branch can change abruptly at a boundary. Do not replace these rules with one percentage of T or smooth the discontinuity.

For twelve otherwise qualifying ordinary months and an applicable reduced rate, the [published annual bands](https://www.gov.je/Working/Contributions/Payments/pages/class2contributionsstatement.aspx) support this **illustrative annual percentage arithmetic**:

`C(B) = 0.125 × min(B, 72,744) + 0.025 × max(0, min(B, 331,584) − 72,744)`

The law expresses monthly contributions. C(B) divided by twelve is an illustrative monthly equivalent, not a verified penny payment rule. Eligible months, notices, credits and offsets control the actual amount. **Do not use the workflow base's generic two-decimal fallback to generate a payable Class 2 amount.** Final rounding and monthly allocation remain unverified; use the official notice for notified amounts.

## Class 1 offset: a specific classification

[Article 13C](https://www.jerseylaw.je/laws/current/ro_6108) reduces the month's Class 2 contribution by any Class 1 contributions paid in respect of a person treated as Class 2 under Classification Order Schedule 1 Part 2 paragraph 14. [Law, Article 4](https://www.jerseylaw.je/laws/current/l_22_1974) defines Class 1 contributions as primary and secondary components. Record both actually paid components for that person and month.

This provision does not establish an offset for every person who combines employment and self-employment, or every paragraph 25 company owner. Do not infer Class 1 payments from wages alone. Disputed classification, broader administrative top-ups and an offset exceeding the Class 2 amount require an official determination; do not create a negative liability or refund.

## Startup concession

[Article 13D](https://www.jerseylaw.je/laws/current/ro_6108) covers a Class 2 person commencing a new business or joining an existing business otherwise than as an employee. Before commencement the person must have been Class 1 insured, in full-time education, or unemployed and available for employed-person employment, with the required evidence. Apply in writing and supply the required earnings evidence, ordinarily for the second preceding assessment year. The education/training estimate exception requires acceptance.

Opening years depend on the business start date: a start on any January day covers that year and the next; a February-to-December start covers that year and the following 2 years. A January 2026 start therefore has opening years 2026 and 2027; a February 2026 start has 2026, 2027 and 2028. This identifies eligible years, not an automatic grant for every month.

The [Article 13D(8) application cut-offs](https://www.jerseylaw.je/laws/current/ro_6108) establish the concession period to year end:

| Application for concession year 2026 | Concession-period start |
|---|---|
| On or before 15 June 2026 | 1 January 2026 |
| After that cut-off, on or before 15 September 2026 | 1 April 2026 |
| After that cut-off, on or before 15 December 2026 | 1 July 2026 |
| After that cut-off, on or before 15 March 2027 | 1 October 2026 |

A period beginning on 1 January does not create January liability for a person who first becomes Class 2 in February. Establish liability months separately. The Comptroller must grant an eligible, evidenced application subject to the specified special-rate, anti-avoidance and repeat-application provisions. A granted concession for another business triggers a 5-year restriction beginning on the day after the concession period. A person's termination notice ends the concession immediately and bars another application for the same concession period.

The ordinary approved amount is one third of the contribution on monthly SEL. The [2026 published startup amount](https://www.gov.je/Working/Contributions/Payments/pages/class2contributionsstatement.aspx) is GBP 252.58. Twelve actual payments of GBP 252.58 total GBP 3,030.96. One-third arithmetic without monthly rounding gives GBP 3,031 annually; reconcile actual payments. For the Article 13D(7) restricted-registration one-child day-carer or share-fisherman branch, the amount is based on monthly LEL: 12.5% of GBP 1,296 is GBP 162, subject to the grant and covered months.

The official startup page gives annual reapplication and business-licence instructions. Retain that administrative evidence alongside the statutory conditions and grant; it does not replace them.

## Startup reconciliation

[Article 13E(2) and (2A)](https://www.jerseylaw.je/laws/current/ro_6108) require the Comptroller to determine the year's liability without the startup concession. Articles 13 and 13A then use **actual concession-year E and T**, overriding the ordinary 2-year lookback. For a 2026 concession, use actual 2026 income, applicable liability months, credits, exceptions and offsets. Compare the determination with the actual payments credited to that year.

- If there would have been no liability, the person can elect a refund, after deduction of the assessed relevant benefit and recalculation of contribution credits, or elect to retain payments. If relevant benefit exceeds retained payments, the notified excess is payable no later than 28 days after notification.
- If payments exceed a positive liability, the Comptroller refunds the excess and recalculates credits.
- If payments fall short, the Comptroller notifies the shortfall. It is payable no later than 28 days after notification; credits are recalculated taking account of what is paid.

'Relevant benefit' in Article 13E(5A) is the specified benefit amount calculated by reference to refunded or paid contributions. Obtain the official amount; do not use every benefit received or independently calculate benefit recovery. Article 13E(8) preserves benefits already paid during the year against the listed credit recalculations. Future entitlement is not calculated here.

For the no-liability election, separately assume the Comptroller determines zero liability and assesses relevant benefit at GBP 150. The government's GBP 250 incremental example uses unrounded annual startup arithmetic. The GBP 250.04 comparison above uses twelve actual published rounded payments. That difference does not establish an error on an individual billing notice. Total income alone also cannot establish the Article 13E branch.

## Income-tax expense treatment

[Income Tax (Jersey) Law 1961, Article 70A](https://www.jerseylaw.je/laws/current/l_29_1961) permits the relevant expense in computing Schedule D trade or professional profits, including the individuals carrying on a partnership trade or profession: 52% of Class 2 contributions outside the SEL-to-UEL component and 100% of that upper-band component. It is a profit deduction, not a contribution rebate or an automatic deduction against employment income.

For a qualifying trade with twelve full-rate liability months, no adjustments and established annual components of GBP 9,093 lower and GBP 6,471 upper, the illustrative expense is `0.52 × 9,093 + 6,471 = GBP 11,199.36`. Confirm eligibility and the actual liability components before transferring an expense to [je-income-tax](je-income-tax.md).

Article 70A refers to contributions the individual is liable to pay in the year. The [2025 self-employment return help](https://www.gov.je/taxesmoney/incometax/individuals/yourtaxreturn/helpguides/pages/selfemploymenttaxformhelp.aspx) asks for monthly paid amounts from statements. Reconcile liability-year and cash-payment differences, especially deferred startup amounts, with the applicable assessment instructions or Revenue Jersey before a filing deduction. The 2025 form does not establish a 2026 cash-paid rule.

## Registration, notices and payment

[Collection Order 2013, Articles 1 to 3, 6 and 11](https://www.jerseylaw.je/laws/current/ro_89_2013) establish these duties:

- Apply for a social security registration number and contribution liability notice by the end of the month in which insured status begins. FR1 means liable; XR1 means not liable.
- On becoming liable with XR1, apply for FR1 within 7 days. When liability ends with FR1, apply for XR1 as soon as reasonably practicable. An employee gives the number and notice to the employer within 2 working days of starting employment.
- A person whose liability ceased during absence and who returns to ordinary Jersey residence notifies the Minister within 7 days of becoming ordinarily resident.
- Pay Class 2 by the 15th of the following month with the required information. The Comptroller may extend the due date by up to 2 months for a particular case or type of case; verify the extension.
- An employer must notify the Comptroller within 7 days of a shareholding/control change if it knows or suspects that the change reclassifies an insured person. This is the employer's specified duty, not a universal share-change rule for every worker.

Retain registration, classification and relief evidence, E/T reconciliation, contribution and credit statements, actual payment dates/references, the determination and notification date. Unmatched payments remain unresolved. Bank-reference formats, general penalties, collection enforcement, monthly penny rounding and complete benefit effects need further primary checks.
