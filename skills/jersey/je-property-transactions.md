## Classify the charge and value

| Transaction | Rule to establish before calculation |
|---|---|
| Share transfer conferring occupation rights, relevant trust declaration or security over those shares | LTT Article 3. Schedule paragraph 2 ordinarily uses the higher of consideration and gross market value; a gift uses gross value. Qualifying off-plan contracts use consideration. Sole/joint changes have specific half-gross-value comparisons |
| Freehold or other registrable immovable-property contract | Stamp Schedule 1 item 13. Ordinary sale item 13(a) uses the higher of consideration and gross value; qualifying off-plan item 13(aa) uses consideration. Exchanges, gifts, partages and sole/joint changes have separate bases and conditions |
| Acquisition of control of a property-owning structure | EPTT Articles 1–7. A transaction within the LTT Law is excluded by Article 3(8); do not charge both merely because both concern shares |
| Borrowing or lease | Identify its own security document or lease item. Purchase tax does not include every borrowing charge, premium or registration fee |
| Death or estate administration | Separate recovery authority, sworn net personal estate, probate duty and other court fees |

LTT domestic/non-domestic use follows the actual parish Rates List at the transaction date (Schedule paragraph 2(1A)). Stamp duty's dwelling test concerns land on which a dwelling is or is to be constructed (Schedule 1 paragraph 2). EPTT uses the parish listing subject to Article 5's mixed-property rule. Obtain evidence for the applicable test.

LTT's off-plan exception needs every Schedule paragraph 2(2A) condition: a dwelling constructed or to be constructed, non-transferable contract, non-completion penalty of at least 10% of consideration, consideration at least notional market value when signed, and signature on or after 1 January 2017. Stamp Article 1 defines its relevant off-plan contract similarly. An off-plan label establishes neither exception.

## First-buyer relief and borrowing

LTT Schedule paragraph 4 requires a request, specified Entitled or qualifying spouse/civil-partner status, no previous worldwide accommodation ownership/occupation rights within its expanded definition, consideration not less or not substantially less than gross value, and occupation by the purchaser. Licensed status alone does not satisfy this paragraph. Stamp item 13(b) has its own conditions, including joint-transferee wording for a qualifying spouse/civil partner; item 13(ba) separately covers qualifying off-plan contracts. Check every applicable condition.

Eligible LTT first-buyer value is gross or completed notional gross value under paragraph 4(4). The charge is GBP 90 plus zero through GBP 350,000, then GBP 1 per GBP 100 or part above GBP 350,000 through GBP 600,000. From over GBP 600,000 through GBP 700,000, the published method reduces basic LTT by GBP 8,500 minus 8.5% of the excess above GBP 600,000. Paragraph 4(3)(c) literally refers to paragraph '(2)'; government examples use basic paragraph 2 tax. Preserve that qualification and hold disputed applications. Stamp items 13(b)/(ba) have corresponding bands but their own valuation/eligibility conditions. [LTT Schedule](https://www.jerseylaw.je/laws/current/l_10_2009); [published examples](https://www.gov.je/TaxesMoney/PropertyTransactionTaxes/LandTransaction/Pages/LandTransactionTaxRates.aspx); [Stamp item 13](https://www.jerseylaw.je/laws/current/l_8_1998).

Stamp Schedule 1 item 13(l)(1) uses annual rent and possession term including optional extensions. Count a fraction of a year as a full year and cap the period at 21 years. Transfer/extension normally uses current rent; below-market rent requires Greffier agreement. Rent increases and previously taxed extensions have their own rules. These provisions do not settle fractional years under EPTT.

## EPTT: control, valuation and 2026 computation

EPTT Articles 1–6 require a transfer conferring **more than 50%** ownership/control, directly or through specified controlled entities, and beneficial ownership of enveloped Jersey property with market value **exceeding** GBP 500,000 domestic or GBP 700,000 non-domestic. Exactly 50% control and exactly the value threshold do not satisfy those respective tests. Overseas incorporation does not itself exclude an entity. Article 4 can aggregate connected holdings; establish connection under Income Tax Law Article 3A. Do not charge a percentage of property tax merely equal to the share percentage purchased.

Article 5 deems qualifying physically attached/immediately adjacent, separately listed mixed domestic and non-domestic units all non-domestic. Check every condition. Schedule 1 excludes specified succession, court-order, nominee, listed-share, fund-unit, option, security, already-taxed connected-transfer, exempt-transferee and connected-company transactions. The subsequent connected-transfer exclusion needs both its earlier taxed-and-paid transaction and later acquisition conditions. Check the precise paragraph; connection alone is not a general exemption. [EPTT Articles 1–6 and Schedule 1](https://www.jerseylaw.je/laws/current/l_18_2022).

Non-lease property uses unencumbered market value at the transaction date. Article 7(10) forbids deducting specified entity/group debts and liabilities. Once scope and classification are established:

- Non-domestic non-lease property uses Schedule 2 Table A: the non-domestic table above restricted to values over GBP 700,000.
- Standard domestic non-lease property uses Schedule 2 Table B column 2: the standard dwelling table above restricted to values over GBP 500,000.
- Higher domestic non-lease property in 2026 uses Article 7(12)(a)/Schedule 4: the higher 2026 dwelling table above restricted to values over GBP 500,000. The older Table B higher column is not the selected 2026 schedule.
- Add GBP 90 under Article 7(1). Connected persons' allocation follows Article 7(5)–(6), based on the significant interest acquired; establish holdings and the series before allocating a total.

The higher dwelling test/trading-stock exception is in Article 7(4A); main residence can be outside Jersey. **Drafting qualification:** Article 7(1) still refers generally to column 2 while paragraphs (3)/(4) distinguish standard/higher columns. The explicit 2026 override and specific tables support these dated branches; this guide does not represent the wording as formally corrected. [EPTT Article 7 and Schedules 2/4](https://www.jerseylaw.je/laws/current/l_18_2022); [official guidance](https://www.gov.je/TaxesMoney/PropertyTransactionTaxes/EnvelopedPropertyTransactionTax/pages/epttrates.aspx).

## Statements, payment and records

LTT Article 4 requires the occupier or secured party, as applicable, to deliver an electronic statement, prescribed material and payment by the due date: the 28th day following the transaction day. Its declaration concerns truth, completeness and correctness. [LTT Articles 1, 3–4](https://www.jerseylaw.je/laws/current/l_10_2009).

The [LTT Statements Order, Articles 2–3](https://www.jerseylaw.je/laws/current/ro_131_2019) distinguishes occupation and security statements. Record company identity, parties/property, date, property value, tax branch, tax/surcharge/total and declaration; occupation statements also address trust, main residence and trading stock. Security statements identify secured party/shareholder and basic, low-value, remortgage or charitable branch. Receipts can be electronic or paper; replacement costs GBP 50 under Articles 4–5.

EPTT Article 8 requires the acquirer to deliver prescribed statement/material and tax within 28 days after the transaction. Article 9 gives a qualifying Jersey-established/registered/present entity a separate 28-day statement duty, disapplied if an Article 8 statement has been delivered. The [EPTT Statements Order, Articles 2–3](https://www.jerseylaw.je/laws/current/ro_32_2022) specifies entity, transferor, acquirer/connected person, property, market value, parish-use, main-residence/trading-stock, date, tax/surcharge/total and declaration fields; Article 9 uses its prescribed electronic form or Comptroller-provided alternative.

LTT Article 7 and EPTT Article 13 impose a 10% late-payment surcharge in their stated ordinary-payment cases, with discretionary waiver/reduction only under specified conditions. Written applications ordinarily have a 40-day notice-based limit. Each Law's Article 11 has its own 28-day assessment-payment route. Ordinary appeals use LTT Article 17 or EPTT Article 19 and their 40-day notice-based rules, subject to late-admission conditions; LTT Article 8(2) expressly gives no right of appeal against an Article 8(1) remission decision. Six-year records apply within LTT Article 10's and EPTT Article 15's scopes. [LTT Law](https://www.jerseylaw.je/laws/current/l_10_2009); [EPTT Law](https://www.jerseylaw.je/laws/current/l_18_2022).

Stamp Article 8 conditions registration on the Greffier's satisfaction about correct duty paid or to be paid. Article 8A requires main-residence/trading-stock declarations for its enumerated item 13(a), (c), (h), (j), (k) and (l) transactions. Do not infer a universal 28-day stamp deadline or extend that list without authority. [Stamp Articles 8–8A](https://www.jerseylaw.je/laws/current/l_8_1998).

## Probate duty and recovery authority

Establish domicile, personal estate within the applicable scope and statutory **net value**. Stamp Articles 1 and 5 use worldwide personal estate for Jersey domicile and personal estate within the court's jurisdiction for other domicile. Net value is the statutory market-value measure less specified charges/taxes, not an unsupported deduction of every debt. Article 5(3) permits an overpayment corrective affidavit within six months after true net value is ascertained; refund requires the Greffier’s certificate and its presentation under paragraph (4). Paragraph (5) requires an underpayment corrective affidavit within that six-month period and payment of the difference. Article 8(4) governs sworn value and duty before grant.

| Sworn net personal estate | Schedule 3 item 9(1) variable duty |
|---|---|
| No more than GBP 10,000 | Nil |
| Over GBP 10,000, no more than GBP 100,000 | GBP 50 per GBP 10,000 or part of the **whole** estate |
| Over GBP 100,000, no more than GBP 13,360,000 | GBP 500 plus GBP 75 per additional GBP 10,000 or part above GBP 100,000 |
| Over GBP 13,360,000 | GBP 100,000 |

Add item 9(2): GBP 107 for a professional application or GBP 268 otherwise. These are identified item 9 components, not all grant costs. Other services can add fees; item 7 is discretionary up to GBP 670. [Stamp Schedule 3 items 7 and 9](https://www.jerseylaw.je/laws/current/l_8_1998); [Courts fee guidance](https://www.courts.je/judicial-greffe/probate-and-protection/probate-registry/probate-registry-services-and-fees/). Exactly GBP 13,360,000 has variable duty GBP 99,950; the next penny enters the GBP 100,000 row.

Probate Articles 19–19F and 23 separate recovery authority from duty. A holder **may** release under an applicable exception; an exception does not compel a bank to release assets. A caveat, entitlement or declaration issue needs legal resolution.

- Article 19A: for a non-Jersey-domiciled deceased, statutory gross Jersey movable estate **held by that holder** must not exceed GBP 30,000. Jersey domicile also requires the applicant's declaration that worldwide gross movable estate does not exceed GBP 30,000. Both need written liability acknowledgement, apparent entitlement and no caveat.
- The Courts summary instead describes total Jersey movable estate for the non-Jersey branch. Preserve that scope difference. Do not split assets between holders to infer release entitlement; disputed multi-holder cases remain held.
- Article 19B: only designated particular holders can use this route. The Law requires the worldwide GBP 30,000 declaration without a domicile qualification, plus its other conditions. Order Article 3(7)(b) describes that form declaration only for Jersey domicile; it does not remove the Law's condition. Article 2 designates specified care providers/managers or nominees, Chief Nurse or nominee, social-housing manager, Prison Governor or nominee, and Viscount. Verify exact designation and form requirements.
- Article 19C distinguishes funeral-director authorisation for an item no more than GBP 1,000 from an application to release items for burial/cremation subject to GBP 10,000 and its conditions/form. Items must have been on/with or worn by the person at death. Multiple-item applications use the Order's aggregate-value declaration.
- Article 19D's Viscount route requires its appointment, worldwide-value and permitted properly invoiced-payment conditions. Article 19E concerns release to the Receiver-General after reasonable efforts fail to identify the entitled person. Neither is a general family-release power.

Sources: [Probate Articles 19–23](https://www.jerseylaw.je/laws/current/l_16_1998); [Forms/Particular Holders Order Articles 2–4](https://www.jerseylaw.je/laws/current/ro_90_2023); [Courts summary](https://www.courts.je/judicial-greffe/probate-and-protection/probate-registry/exemptions-under-the-probate-law/). Route disputes and succession/distribution questions to a qualified Jersey reviewer. Prepare redacted references to checks/forms; do not collect or submit identity documents, bank details, declarations, payments or estate releases.

## Intake, defaults and refusals

Add date, legal instrument, title/share/trust chain, before/after control and connected holdings, consideration, independent gross/unencumbered value, parish listing/dwelling evidence, main residence/trading stock, relief request/status/history, loan document/unpaid balance/discharge evidence, lease rent/market rent/term/options/premium, and estate domicile/statutory net-value/recovery-authority evidence to base intake. Collect redacted references; keep real identity documents and bank details outside this workflow. Ask only for facts needed by the branch, following the base's question form and pacing.

| Missing fact or source condition | Permitted default |
|---|---|
| Unknown law, period, instrument or statutory interpretation | None; R-JE-PROP-SOURCE |
| Missing valuation, use, main residence, control, connection or relief evidence | None; Needs Input. Do not choose higher tax to replace unknown classification |
| Unresolved rounding or lease period/rent method | None; R-JE-PROP-METHOD |
| Uncertain estate entitlement, caveat or release authority | None; R-JE-PROP-ESTATE |

Use these exact messages and record code/affected position in the base refusal trace:

| Code | Trigger | Verbatim output message |
|---|---|---|
| R-JE-PROP-SOURCE | Unverified period/source chain or disputed drafting, classification or allocation | Needs Input: the applicable Jersey property source or interpretation is unresolved. Hold the affected amount for a qualified Jersey reviewer. |
| R-JE-PROP-METHOD | Unsupported rounding, fractional EPTT years, variable rent, exceptional valuation or specialist charge | Needs Input: this Jersey property calculation method is not established for the supplied facts. No payable amount is produced for that position. |
| R-JE-PROP-ESTATE | Unresolved recovery, entitlement, caveat, multi-holder scope or succession question | Needs Input: Jersey estate authority or eligibility is unresolved. Do not release or distribute assets on this output. |
| R-JE-PROP-REVIEW | Filing-ready, payment, declaration, identity submission or unreviewed advice requested | This workflow prepares material for a qualified reviewer. It cannot submit a declaration, payment or identity document, release assets, or replace professional review. |

Reviewer attention policy: flag every proposed amount above GBP 0 and every nil/exempt position, relief, valuation/classification judgment, source qualification or missed deadline, regardless of cash amount. This is preparation policy, not statutory materiality. Unknown amounts are not zero.
