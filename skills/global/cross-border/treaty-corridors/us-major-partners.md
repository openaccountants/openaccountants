# US Major Partners

## Quick Reference

Use the applicable treaty ceiling only after the recipient, income and payment meet that treaty’s conditions. These are US-source payment rules; a reverse payment needs its own domestic-source, relief and filing analysis.

The original fourteen partner corridors remain: UK, Germany, France, Canada, Australia, Japan, India, Netherlands, Ireland, Switzerland, Singapore, Mexico, South Korea and Israel. There are thirteen comprehensive income-tax treaty partners in that list, plus the Singapore domestic-law corridor. Hong Kong is a separate no-extension caution, not another treaty in the count. The IRS list and the income-specific treaty texts decide coverage, not the original “14 treaties” label.
[IRS current treaty list](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/international-businesses/united-states-income-tax-treaties-a-to-z); [IRS Publication 901](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p901)

The ordinary US chapter 3 default is 30% of gross taxable US-source FDAP paid to a foreign payee unless a domestic exemption, valid treaty reduction or another applicable rule applies. No treaty does not mean no domestic exemption: portfolio interest is one example. FATCA/chapter 4 status, effectively connected income and personal-service withholding need their own treatment. A treaty ceiling does not create tax that domestic law does not impose.
[IRS 2026 Publication 515](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p515)

## Ask the client first

1. What is the direction, payment date, gross amount and actual source of each payment? Where were services performed or intellectual-property rights used?
2. Who is the beneficial owner, what is its treaty residence and legal/tax classification, and is it a US citizen, resident, transparent entity, pension, fund, RIC or REIT?
3. What rights or work does the contract actually supply: dividends, ordinary or contingent debt, equipment, software/copyright, patents, know-how, franchise, included services, enterprise services or an individual’s work?
4. What voting ownership, direct/indirect chain, holding dates, payer income and special fund facts establish the precise rate class?
5. Which treaty-specific LOB route is evidenced? Are there a third-country PE, source PE/fixed base, related-party excess, conduit/back-to-back arrangement or special incentive-period facts?
6. What signed documentation and any required residence/eligibility evidence will the payer hold, and is this a withholding working paper or a separate foreign refund/return job?

## The method, step by step

1. **Confirm coverage and the operative edition.** Start with the current IRS country index, then read the actual treaty, integral protocol and relevant later arrangement. Signing, entry into force and the date a particular tax provision applies are different. IRS Table 3 gives general effective dates; the operative commencement clause controls a payment. The Sources table distinguishes reused completed reads from newly read dependencies.
[IRS current treaty list](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/international-businesses/united-states-income-tax-treaties-a-to-z); [IRS Table 3](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-lbi/table-3-list-of-tax-treaties.pdf)

2. **Determine source and classify the payment.** For ordinary personal-service income, source generally follows where the services are performed; for royalties it follows where the property is used. A US payer alone does not decide both questions. Split contracts and mixed performance need allocation and separate classification. India’s included-services rules, and an individual’s independent-services rules, cannot be replaced with a generic enterprise “no PE” result.
[IRS income-source rules](https://www.irs.gov/individuals/international-taxpayers/nonresident-aliens-source-of-income); [India Convention, Protocol and MOU](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-trty/india.pdf)

3. **Establish the claimant.** Apply the actual residence, beneficial-owner and fiscally transparent-entity rules. Then apply that treaty’s saving clause and its exceptions: a non-US resident’s treaty benefit is not a blanket exemption for a US citizen or resident. State taxes and US residence taxation are separate from a source-country withholding ceiling.
[IRS 2026 Publication 515](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p515); [IRS current treaty list](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/international-businesses/united-states-income-tax-treaties-a-to-z)

4. **Prove the particular eligibility route.** Use the LOB table below, the full cited article and any required competent-authority determination. A general LOB pass may not satisfy a parent-dividend exemption. There is no single ownership/base-erosion formula across these treaties. Do not import an MLI change merely from the partner’s MLI participation: the United States is not an MLI signatory.
[Canada Finance MLI explanation](https://www.canada.ca/en/department-finance/programs/tax-policy/tax-treaties.html)

5. **Select the rate with its exceptions.** Read the ownership and timing conditions on the row, special fund classes, debt/royalty definitions, PE/fixed-base connection, related-party excess, contingent/securitisation rules and relevant anti-abuse provisions. “0%” means qualifying treaty relief for the stated class, not that all receipts with that label escape source tax.

6. **Apply domestic law and the treaty limitation in the right order.** Establish domestic tax and documentation treatment, then apply the available treaty restriction. Enterprise business-profits relief is conditional on classification, eligibility and no relevant source PE. In the India corridor, the integral Protocol expressly addresses US net-income/branch taxation of included services within the aggregate gross-fee treaty ceiling; do not turn the ceiling into an unqualified gross withholding instruction.
[IRS 2026 Publication 515](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p515); [India Convention, Protocol and MOU](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-trty/india.pdf)

7. **Document the US claim.** Obtain the applicable signed beneficial-owner certificate: Form W-8BEN for an individual or W-8BEN-E for an entity, with the required treaty-residence, derivation, LOB and special-rate certifications as applicable. Transparent entities/intermediaries and effectively connected income may require another form. An individual claiming a treaty exemption for compensation generally uses Form 8233 rather than treating every service payment as an ordinary W-8BEN claim. This method does not replace the forms’ current instructions or a full return/refund procedure.
[IRS 2026 Publication 515](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p515); [IRS W-8 instructions](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/iw8)

8. **Record the result and the boundary.** Keep the article/version, source and gross base, claimant facts, exact rate class, eligibility evidence, exclusions tested, documentation and any unresolved point. If a material fact or operative condition is missing, hold the reduced-rate claim; do not fill the gap with the old table or a generic treaty summary.

## Worked cases

Each calculation assumes treaty residence, beneficial ownership, the required LOB route, valid documentation, no connected PE/fixed base and no excess/conduit exception; those assumptions must be established in an actual claim.

**Case 1 — Canada: identify the royalty, not just the payee.** A qualifying Canadian beneficial owner receives USD 10,000 of US-source royalties for use of computer software that falls within Article XII(3). The source-tax ceiling is zero. If the same USD 10,000 instead is a trademark/franchise royalty outside the exempt classes, the Article XII(2) ceiling is USD 1,000. This is a classification comparison, not a rule that a contract can elect its label.
[Canada-US consolidated Convention](https://www.canada.ca/en/department-finance/programs/tax-policy/tax-treaties/country/united-states-america-convention-consolidated-1980-1983-1984-1995-1997-2007.html)

```oa-check
name: Canada US-source software versus non-exempt franchise royalty
inputs:
  gross: 10000
steps:
  - software_ceiling: gross * 0
  - franchise_ceiling: gross * 0.10
  - difference: franchise_ceiling - software_ceiling
expected:
  software_ceiling: 0
  franchise_ceiling: 1000
  difference: 1000
```

**Case 2 — Japan: distinguish the holding condition for 5% from the parent exemption.** On the entitlement date a qualifying Japanese beneficial-owner company holds 10% voting power and has held it for only one month. On a USD 10,000 US dividend the corporate ceiling is USD 500: the 5% class does not have the old general six-month requirement. By comparison, a qualifying portfolio owner has a USD 1,000 ceiling. In a separate parent scenario, holding at least 50% through the permitted ownership chain for the required six months **and** satisfying a specifically accepted Article 22 route can give a zero ceiling. The ownership percentage alone does not establish it.
[Japan 2003 Convention](https://home.treasury.gov/system/files/131/Treaty-Japan-11-6-2003.pdf); [Japan 2013 Protocol](https://home.treasury.gov/system/files/131/Treaty-Japan-Pr2-1-24-2013.pdf)

```oa-check
name: Japan distinct corporate portfolio and conditional parent dividend classes
inputs:
  gross: 10000
steps:
  - corporate_ceiling: gross * 0.05
  - portfolio_ceiling: gross * 0.10
  - parent_ceiling_when_all_conditions_met: gross * 0
  - corporate_vs_portfolio: portfolio_ceiling - corporate_ceiling
expected:
  corporate_ceiling: 500
  portfolio_ceiling: 1000
  parent_ceiling_when_all_conditions_met: 0
  corporate_vs_portfolio: 500
```

## When to refuse or refer

- Hold treaty relief where source, residence, beneficial ownership, the exact LOB route, ownership/timing or a material exception is unresolved. A missing form or missing proof is not cured by a table rate.
- Refer individual employment, entertainers, real-property income, transparent entities, retirement arrangements, transport income and connected PE/fixed-base income to their applicable provisions before using a passive-income or enterprise-services row.
- Refer reverse-direction domestic liability and full foreign filing/refund work to the actual local rules and current forms. The selected reverse context below does not make these US-source rows bidirectional.
- The specific unsupported legacy assertions under Sources are non-usable. They remain visible as preservation records and must never supply a calculation or a filing instruction.
