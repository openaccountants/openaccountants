# Wealth tax: regimes, valuation and cross-border limits (2026)

Figures are for tax year 2026 unless a row expressly identifies an earlier tax period. This source-cited draft separates current legal schedules from prior-year amounts published during 2026. It does not certify that every country with a possible asset levy has been exhaustively covered. Actual primary bodies and material neighbours were personally read on 16 November 2026 in the scopes in Sources; an access response or search result is not evidence of reading.

Load `cross-border-workflow-base` first. This Guide covers Spain, Norway, Switzerland, Argentina, Colombia, Uruguay, French real-estate-only IFI and Dutch Box 3. Box 3 taxes a computed return, rather than imposing the same charge as a net wealth tax. Italy's IVIE/IVAFE and Belgium's securities-account tax are separate asset-specific alerts. Keep inheritance, estate and gift tax, property transfers, capital gains and pension income taxation in their respective country Guides; pension inclusion or exemption from a wealth base remains relevant here.

## The method, step by step

1. Establish the taxpayer, household, residence and treaty residence separately for each relevant date. Obtain acquisition dates, legal and beneficial ownership, trusts, companies, pensions and asset locations.
2. Identify the actual tax and year. A return filed during 2026 may concern a 2025 year-end. Do not apply December valuations to a January snapshot, or an account-average tax to either.
3. Build a gross asset register using each law's valuation method. Record exemptions before subtracting only permitted, documented liabilities. Allocate debts to exempt or foreign assets where required.
4. Apply the correct personal allowance, regional tariff, household status and marginal bands. Then apply each cap, rebate, foreign-tax credit and credit for another wealth tax in its prescribed sequence.
5. Determine filing independently of liability. A nil bill does not universally remove a return requirement. Recover the form, campaign, payment and direct-debit deadlines for the actual period before execution.
6. Read each applicable bilateral capital article, covered-tax definition, protocol and domestic relief rule. A model article, a residence certificate or foreign payment alone does not settle allocation or credit.

## Spain: IP, regional rules and solidarity tax

IP under Ley 19/1991 is an individual tax. Habitually resident persons generally report worldwide wealth; nonresidents report Spanish-situs or exercisable/performable rights, including the specified unlisted property-rich entity rule. Residence and any special election require separate testing. The net base deducts real charges and substantiated debts; nonresidents deduct only qualifying charges and debts invested in taxable Spanish assets. There is no blanket prohibition on a debt merely because the lender is a foreign bank. [Official source](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-1991-14392).

Value debts at nominal amount on the accrual date and substantiate them. A mortgage is not an extra deduction in addition to its underlying loan. Guarantees are generally excluded until the statutory demand/insolvency conditions apply. Debt used to acquire exempt assets is excluded, proportionately where exemption is partial. [Official source](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-1991-14392).

The state-default IP allowance is EUR 700,000 where the competent region has not replaced it; the habitual-home exemption is up to EUR 300,000 per taxpayer, separately. IP accrues on 31 December. Do not equate gross assets, net wealth, the allowance-adjusted base and the final bill. [Official source](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-1991-14392).

The family-share exemption is conditional: the entity must not principally manage securities or other assets where for more than 90 days of the year more than half its assets are securities or non-business assets, subject to the defined management-holding and operating-profit exceptions; the individual holds at least 5%, or a group with spouse, ascendants, descendants or second-degree collateral relatives by blood, affinity or adoption holds at least 20%. Effective management must earn more than 50% of the relevant business, professional and employment income, with statutory exclusions; in a family group one qualifying member can meet the management/pay test. Relief covers only the proportion attributable to necessary business assets net of business liabilities, including the corresponding subsidiary analysis. Active ownership alone is insufficient. [Official source](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-1991-14392).

Check each classified historical/artistic asset against its actual registration, value, museum-deposit or creator conditions; a family heirloom is not automatically exempt. Household effects have their own definition and exclusions. Author intellectual property is exempt while remaining in the author’s estate, and industrial property only outside the stated business setting; inalienability is not the test. Only the defined pension, insurance, social-provision and PEPP rights qualify, rather than every retirement asset. [Official source](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-1991-14392).

ITSGF is complementary, not a flat charge on every euro above a gross threshold. The statutory wealth trigger is net wealth strictly exceeding EUR 3,000,000; IP exemptions apply, and the personal minimum is EUR 700,000 for both personal and real obligations after the amendment. Accrual is 31 December. Residence, territorial exceptions and any representative requirement must be checked. [Official source](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2022-22684).

RDL 8/2023 extended ITSGF until wealth taxation is reviewed in the autonomous-financing reform. The old attribution to RDL 9/2024 and a fixed 2025 sunset is not the operative extension. [Official source](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2023-26452).

For the statutory personal-obligation route, the combined IRPF/IP/ITSGF cap uses 60% of the defined IRPF bases, subject to the incorporated exclusions and a maximum 80% ITSGF reduction. IP has its own corresponding cap and 80% reduction ceiling. The non-income-producing asset component is excluded from the cap calculation; this is not a rule that the entire cap disappears whenever assets are mostly nonproductive. Joint-income-return allocations must be respected. Apply qualifying foreign-tax relief and other reductions in order, then credit IP actually paid. Do not infer automatic nonresident cap entitlement from a form alone; the current procedural/judicial basis must be established for that separate route. Catalonia’s published statutory minimum is EUR 500,000. While ITSGF remains in force, its transitional tariff runs from 0.210% to 3.480%, including the 2.205% band starting at EUR 5,347,998.03 with prior quota EUR 74,930.46; 2.750% starts at EUR 10,695,996.06, and 3.480% above EUR 20,000,000. The old 2.75% ceiling is incomplete. The retained official consolidation is updated 18 December 2024; confirm subsequent regional changes before an actual 2026 year-end filing. [Official source](https://economia.gencat.cat/web/.content/70_tributs/Normativa/Refoses/IP.pdf).

Madrid’s current authority guidance suspends its general 100% IP rebate while ITSGF is in force and replaces it with the positive difference between IP after its cap and the corresponding ITSGF after its cap. Compute both charges and credit actual IP; do not begin with an assumed zero IP bill. The exact IP tariff and all case-specific caps remain prerequisites to a final Madrid computation. [Official source](https://www.comunidad.madrid/atencion-contribuyente/patrimonio).

Madrid fixes the IP minimum at EUR 700,000. Its current IP chapter contains that minimum and the general rebate; DT7 suspends the rebate while ITSGF is in force and replaces it by the positive capped-IP/shadow-ITSGF difference. Apply the state supplementary tariff where no regional scale is approved. The current read legal version is updated 10 July 2026. [Official source](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOCM-m-2010-90068).

The state fallback IP tariff applies absent an approved regional tariff. In the supplied Madrid case, the relevant base band starts at EUR 5,347,998.03 with prior quota EUR 71,362.33 and marginal rate 2.1%. This supports the computed pre-rebate amount; actual cap/credit changes require recomputation. [Official source](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-1991-14392).

For personal-obligation IP, foreign personal tax on included foreign assets is credited up to the lower of actual foreign tax and the Spanish average effective rate applied to the corresponding foreign-taxed base. The statutory average-rate precision and treaty provisions matter; a treaty capital article is not a prerequisite for this domestic route. [Official source](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-1991-14392).

Andalusia’s current DT5 suspends its regional tariff and general 100% rebate while ITSGF remains in force. It substitutes the positive difference between IP after its cap and shadow ITSGF after its cap. The state supplementary tariff therefore matters; the old two-choice arrangement belongs to the prior version. This does not support an unconditional zero wealth-tax result. [Official source](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2021-17915).

Galicia’s guidance distinguishes its ordinary 50% rebate and ordinary tariff from the temporary ITSGF overlay: the current temporary tariff reaches 3.5%, and the rebate is reduced by the ITSGF amount that would otherwise be payable, floored at zero, with the stated corresponding treatment of other regional deductions. Do not use the old 0.2%–2.5% comparison as the current final tariff. [Official source](https://www.atriga.gal/informacion-tributaria/tributos/patrimonio/informacion-del-tributo/guia-del-imposto-sobre-o-patrimonio).


### The original EUR 8 million examples, corrected

Both examples supply EUR 8,000,000 of net assets after asset-specific exemptions and deductible debts but before personal minima, eligible individual residence, no cap reduction, no foreign credit, no special regional relief and actual payment of any IP credited. They are illustrations, not filing outputs. The original rounded EUR 95,612 formula is arithmetically consistent with its rounded thresholds, but used the wrong pre-allowance base.

- Madrid example: the ITSGF shadow base is EUR 8,000,000 − EUR 700,000 = EUR 7,300,000. Using the statutory prior quota, EUR 39,915.97 + (EUR 7,300,000 − EUR 5,347,998.03) × 2.1% = **EUR 80,908.01** before caps/credits. Madrid’s EUR 700,000 minimum and state fallback tariff give pre-rebate IP EUR 71,362.33 + (EUR 7,300,000 − EUR 5,347,998.03) × 2.1% = EUR 112,354.37. The replacement rebate is EUR 31,446.36, leaving **EUR 80,908.01 IP**, and its actual payment leaves **EUR 0 ITSGF** in these supplied no-cap/no-other-relief facts. **EUR 0 IP plus EUR 95,612 solidarity is not a supported result**. Source: https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2022-22684 ; https://www.comunidad.madrid/atencion-contribuyente/patrimonio ; https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOCM-m-2010-90068 ; https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-1991-14392 .
- Catalonia example: the IP base is EUR 8,000,000 − EUR 500,000 = EUR 7,500,000. EUR 74,930.46 + (EUR 7,500,000 − EUR 5,347,998.03) × 2.205% = **EUR 122,382.10** before caps/credits. Crediting that IP actually paid against shadow ITSGF EUR 80,908.01 gives **EUR 0 ITSGF** in the supplied facts. This replaces the unsupported EUR 130,000 estimate. Source: https://economia.gencat.cat/web/.content/70_tributs/Normativa/Refoses/IP.pdf ; https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2022-22684 .

IP filing is required when a positive amount is payable after deductions/rebates, or when the law-valued assets/rights exceed EUR 2,000,000 even if no tax is payable. Form 714 and the precise annual campaign/electronic procedure require that period’s implementing order; the old universal May–June window is not a confirmed 2026-year deadline. [Official source](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-1991-14392).

Form 718 is electronic; the ordinary presentation window is 1–31 July following accrual, with a separate direct-debit deadline. Filing is tied to the statutory payable-amount rule and exceptions. Do not substitute the IP campaign dates. A future amendment, calendar or a special nonresident route must be checked before execution. [Official source](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2023-13800).


## Norway: 2026 wealth and valuation

For a single taxpayer the 2026 municipal schedule is zero through NOK 1,900,000 and 0.35% above; state tax is zero through NOK 1,900,000, 0.65% up to NOK 21,500,000 and 0.75% above. The combined marginal rates are 1.00% and 1.10%. The published thresholds double for spouses, registered partners or qualifying cohabitants jointly assessed on combined wealth: NOK 3,800,000 and NOK 43,000,000. These are marginal bands, not a charge on exempt wealth. [Official source](https://www.skatteetaten.no/satser/formuesskatt/?year=2026).

Published valuation discounts concern directly owned assets and the corresponding debt where specified: primary home 75% discount up to NOK 14 million and 30% discount on the excess; secondary home no discount; shares including commercial property 20% discount; other operating assets 30% discount. Thus the corresponding taxable fractions are 25%, 70%, 100%, 80% and 70%. Do not treat operating assets as an undifferentiated 80% class or reduce assets without checking debt allocation. Cash is not one of the listed discounted classes. [Official source](https://www.skatteetaten.no/satser/formuesskatt/?year=2026).

For the home, use the authority’s computed residential value and the documented market-value correction rules where applicable; the 25%/70% taxable fractions change at NOK 14 million, not the old NOK 10 million. Secondary homes use the published 100% fraction. These rules concern income year 2026, not a 2025 return filed in 2026. [Official source](https://www.skatteetaten.no/satser/formuesverdi-bolig/?year=2026).

Norwegian tax residence generally brings foreign assets into reporting; limited liability does not generally include wealth abroad. Report the tax value of foreign financial assets at 31 December and convert currencies correctly. The authority permits a documented credit claim for foreign tax on the same wealth subject to Norwegian tax; treaty residence and the actual treaty can limit the charge. Do not assume that relief requires a treaty capital article, or that any Swiss payment is automatically creditable. [Official source](https://www.skatteetaten.no/en/person/to-from-norway/to/work/the-tax-return/wealth-other-countries/).


## Switzerland: canton, municipality and allocation

There is no direct federal wealth tax on individuals; cantons and municipalities levy it. The current ESTV report states law as at 1 January 2026. Rates, tax-free amounts and deductions are cantonal; municipality and sometimes church affiliation affect the result. A single canton-wide headline percentage and threshold cannot establish a bill. [Official source](https://www.estv2.admin.ch/stp/ds/d-vermoegenssteuer-np-de.pdf).

Declare the whole wealth register, including foreign movable assets, ownership and usufruct. Unlimited and economic-connection liability, treaties and allocation rules decide the taxable share and rate-determining wealth. The old universal exclusion for foreign movable assets is incorrect. Value assets under the particular cantonal rules; there is no universal 60%–80% real-property rule. Subtract only proven debts under the applicable allocation rules; guarantees and contingent obligations are not universally deductible. Use the year-end reference and any change-of-liability rules. [Official source](https://www.estv2.admin.ch/stp/ds/d-vermoegenssteuer-np-de.pdf).

Household goods/personal-use effects and qualifying second-pillar/3a retirement rights before payment have the stated exclusions. Annuity rights depend on their legal type and cantonal rule, not a universal exemption. Personal/family deductions and minimum wealth differ from a zero-rate threshold. The ten original cantons remain comparison candidates below, but their old approximate rates and allowances are individually unusable until the actual municipality, household and current cantonal schedule are supplied. [Official source](https://www.estv2.admin.ch/stp/ds/d-vermoegenssteuer-np-de.pdf).

## Argentina: annual BP versus an existing REIBP election

Bienes Personales generally measures assets at 31 December: residents report Argentine and foreign assets, nonresidents Argentine assets, with the estate/substitute-taxpayer rules separately checked. The authority’s latest recovered indexed table is tax period 2025: general minimum ARS 384,728,044.57 and home amount ARS 1,346,548,155.99. Those are 2025 amounts published for the corresponding return, not verified 2026 indexed peso values. The old ARS 100 million/350 million figures belong to 2023. [Official source](https://www.arca.gob.ar/gananciasYBienes/bienes-personales/conceptos-basicos/que-es.asp).

The enacted ordinary 2026 schedule is 0.50% then 0.75% on the allowance-adjusted base, with the annually indexed band boundary; do not substitute the unindexed statutory peso boundary or the unrecovered 2026 indexed amount. The reformed table covers domestic and foreign property together. The old separate foreign surcharge, 50%–100% multiplier and 5% repatriation route are not the current ordinary schedule. The compliant-taxpayer 0.50-percentage-point reduction in article 64 covers 2023–2025, not automatically 2026. [Official source](https://biblioteca.arca.gob.ar/dcp/LEY_C_027743_2024_06_27).

Specified bank deposits, public securities and certain qualifying investment instruments are exemptions, not a general lower rate for liquid assets. Check each statutory instrument, currency, institution and underlying-asset condition; some funds require at least 75% qualifying assets. Other listed categories include defined pension rights, cooperative interests, intellectual property, qualifying rural land and registered rental-home conditions. Exempt reporting can still be required. [Official source](https://arca.gob.ar/gananciasYBienes/bienes-personales/conceptos-basicos/bienes-exentos.asp).

REIBP is the special advance-payment regime under Ley 27.743, not a regime named Plan Aporte Especial. Existing eligible elections distinguish ordinary 2023–2027 coverage, a fivefold defined 2023 base at 0.45%, from regularised 2024–2027 coverage, a fourfold defined base at 0.50%, with its own currency/base rules. Eligibility, credits and original election deadlines matter; this Guide does not establish that a fresh 2026 election remains available. Specified gifts from related non-electors can trigger additional tax and require the complete special rules. [Official source](https://biblioteca.arca.gob.ar/dcp/LEY_C_027743_2024_06_27).

REIBP’s defined fiscal-stability protection extends through 2038, with the statutory 0.45%/0.50% ceiling through 2027 and 0.25% for 2028–2038 under its specified base and aggregate-national-asset-tax rules. It is not simply a guarantee ending in 2027 or an approximate 1.5% prepayment. The ordinary law separately provides a 0.25% 2027 schedule; future application still requires that year’s operative law. [Official source](https://biblioteca.arca.gob.ar/dcp/LEY_C_027743_2024_06_27).


## Colombia: the ordinary individual tax in 2026

The ordinary law targets resident individuals and qualifying estates, nonresident individuals/estates with Colombian exposure, and specified foreign non-filing entities subject to the detailed asset/portfolio/receivable/lease exclusions. It is not a general charge on Colombian companies. The charge arises on 1 January where net wealth reaches at least 72,000 UVT; retain the UVT base until the correct year’s official UVT is recovered rather than recycling the old COP 3,388 million or USD 850,000 conversions. [Official source](https://normograma.dian.gov.co/dian/compilacion/docs/ley_2277_2022.htm).

Deduct the statutory liabilities and exclusions. The first 12,000 UVT of the qualifying habitual home is an exclusion from the taxable base, using the statutory net-to-gross proportion; it does not replace the initial net-wealth trigger. Permanent-establishment attribution, shares and foreign trust/foundation rights have separate valuation/look-through rules. The innovative-startup provisions require all specified conditions and change valuation; they are not a blanket active-business-share exemption. No general cultural/heirloom exemption is established by this source. [Official source](https://normograma.dian.gov.co/dian/compilacion/docs/ley_2277_2022.htm).

This is not a judgment deleting the separate ordinary Ley 2277 individual tariff above. Any different 2026 emergency corporate charge needs its own current law and judicial status; it is outside this individual computation. [Official source](https://www.corteconstitucional.gov.co/relatoria/2026/C-079-26.htm).

That dated notice is not a complete annual return calendar. Form 420 is the original requested form topic, but the recovered live application supplies only a loading shell; current form/version and other deadlines remain execution prerequisites. [Official source](https://www.dian.gov.co/Paginas/Vencimientos-tributarios-septiembre-2026.aspx).


## Uruguay: do not roll a 2025 table into year-end 2026

The recovered DGI guide expressly concerns assets at 31 December 2025: minimum UYU 6,653,000 for an individual/estate and UYU 13,306,000 for a qualifying family unit. Spouses can elect the stated joint treatment or separate ownership/community allocation. These supersede the old approximate UYU 5.6 million for that period, but are not a verified year-end 2026 indexed allowance. Use the actual local asset valuations and household-effects computation; it is not a universal worldwide fair-market-value tax. [Official source](https://www.gub.uy/direccion-general-impositiva/sites/direccion-general-impositiva/files/documentos/publicaciones/PATRIMONIO%20PF%202025.pdf).

The qualifying home receives a 50% deduction up to the applicable minimum, with the actual-home and estate exclusions. Debt relief is restricted to the defined domestic financial creditors, generally average month-end balances supported by creditor certification, and is absorbed first by specified foreign/exempt/noncomputable assets. Do not deduct all face-value liabilities or assume a foreign-bank loan qualifies. [Official source](https://www.gub.uy/direccion-general-impositiva/sites/direccion-general-impositiva/files/documentos/publicaciones/PATRIMONIO%20PF%202025.pdf).

For the recovered 2025 period, DGI publishes 0.10% on taxable excess for the general population and a separate 0.70%/1.10%/1.40%/1.50% progressive schedule for nonresidents who do not pay IRNR. Thus neither the old 0.2%–0.7% resident schedule nor a flat 1.5% for every nonresident is reliable. The page URL’s historical suffix does not override its explicit 2025 title and table. The 2026 base, rate applicability and filing calendar must be confirmed from the current period’s instruments before execution; the old universal August filing claim is not established. [Official source](https://www.gub.uy/direccion-general-impositiva/comunicacion/publicaciones/tasas-del-impuesto-patrimonio-personas-fisicas-2023).


## France: IFI is an immovable-wealth tax

The real-estate-only CGI 964 structure is effective from 1 January 2018, replacing the former ISF framework. IFI applies when the household’s net taxable immovable wealth strictly exceeds EUR 1,300,000 on 1 January. Residents generally include worldwide taxable immovables/indirect property interests; nonresidents French exposure. A qualifying new resident not resident in any of the five preceding calendar years has the specified temporary French-property-only treatment through the fifth year following arrival. Household aggregation and exceptions must be checked. [Official source](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000036384999/2026-03-20).

Include the taxable property fraction of company, SCPI or OPCI interests, rather than automatically the full security value. Active-company, minority-holding, control and enjoyment exceptions require the actual statutory facts; own property management is not automatically an operating business. Use the statutory property valuation and ownership conditions. [Official source](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/section_lc/LEGITEXT000006069577/LEGISCTA000036384995/).

The authority explains a 30% value reduction for the qualifying principal home. The legal ownership and household conditions must be checked; this is not a 30% reduction of all immovable holdings. [Official source](https://www.impots.gouv.fr/particulier/calcul-de-lifi).

Only after the strict scope threshold is crossed do all applicable slices, including the EUR 800,000–1,300,000 slice, enter the tariff. For wealth in the statutory EUR 1.3–1.4 million range, subtract EUR 17,500 − 1.25% × taxable wealth. At exactly EUR 1,300,000 there is no IFI under the strict scope test; at EUR 1,350,000 the gross tariff is EUR 2,850, décote EUR 625, and IFI EUR 2,225 before other relief. [Official source](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000036385041).

Debt must exist at 1 January, be borne by the taxpayer and concern taxable assets, subject to the statutory acquisition/repair/tax categories and proportional inclusion. Bullet/open-ended loans, family and controlled-entity borrowing have special limits and proof exceptions. If taxable assets exceed EUR 5 million and debts exceed 60% of their value, only half the excess debt is ordinarily deductible, subject to the non-mainly-tax-purpose exception. [Official source](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/section_lc/LEGITEXT000006069577/LEGISCTA000036384995/2026-07-20/).

Professional-property relief has distinct own-business, partnership, corporate-director/pay/holding and leasing conditions; it is not triggered by a business label. Conditional 75% forestry relief and specific long-term rural-lease relief exist, but their incorporated certificate/management/ownership conditions must be recovered before use. Do not infer a general rent-capped-property exemption. The 2026 amendment’s separate relief for assets subject to article 235 ter C depends on its financial-year application; no automatic 2026 IFI exemption is assumed here. [Official source](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/section_lc/LEGITEXT000006069577/LEGISCTA000036384995/2026-07-20/).

For the resident route, the statutory 75% cap compares the defined IFI and income-related taxes with prior-year worldwide income under its own inclusions and anti-abuse rule. It reduces IFI; it is not a promise that all excess taxes are refunded. Foreign similar-property-tax credit is limited to the corresponding foreign taxable-property fraction. File the prescribed immovable-wealth annex with the income return, commonly Form 2042-IFI, including gross/net values and supporting schedules; recover that campaign’s exact deadline. [Official source](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/section_lc/LEGITEXT000006069577/LEGISCTA000036384995/2026-07-20/).


## Netherlands: Box 3 return calculation, not a flat net-worth rate

The 2026 provisional-assessment method uses 1 January asset values, a 1.28% provisional bank yield, fixed 6.00% other-asset yield and 2.70% provisional debt yield. Bank/debt percentages become final after year-end; do not present them as final statutory investment performance. The rate is 36%; allowance EUR 59,357 per person or EUR 118,714 for fiscal partners, with a EUR 3,800 single debt threshold before eligible debt enters the method. [Official source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2026).

Compute bank and other-asset yields, subtract yield on eligible debt, then apply the taxable-ground/net-ground proportion after the allowance, and tax that apportioned return. Thus 6.00% × 36% = 2.16% is only a pre-allowance illustration for the other-asset class, not a universal effective wealth-tax rate. The old 6.04% × 36% = 2.1744% arithmetic does not supply current parameters. The authority says a lower actual return can be reflected in the 2026 return; this does not mean every taxpayer is automatically assessed solely on actual return or that a particular 2027–2028 reform date is enacted. [Official source](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/box-3/content/berekening-box-3-inkomen-2026).
