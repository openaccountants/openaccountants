# Togo VAT / GST

## Value added tax (TVA)

- **Standard VAT rate** — 18% percent  _(Code Général des Impôts (Togo) — TVA (https://www.lloydsbanktrade.com/en/market-potential/togo/taxes))_
- **Reduced VAT rate** — 10% on accommodation, catering, hotel/restaurant and licensed tour-operator services percent  _(Code Général des Impôts (Togo) — TVA (https://www.lloydsbanktrade.com/en/market-potential/togo/taxes))_
- **Exports** — Exports are zero-rated / exempt percent  _(Code Général des Impôts (Togo) — TVA (https://taxatlas.io/country/togo))_
- **Exempt supplies** — Financial, insurance, medical and transport services; agriculture, fishing and livestock  _(Code Général des Impôts (Togo) — TVA (https://www.lloydsbanktrade.com/en/market-potential/togo/taxes))_
- **VAT registration threshold** — Annual turnover above XOF 60,000,000 (commonly cited WAEMU/Togo threshold) XOF ((approx — confirm))  _(Code Général des Impôts (Togo) — TVA)_
- **Filing frequency** — Monthly VAT return, due by the 15th of the following month  _(Code Général des Impôts (Togo) — TVA (https://rivermate.com/guides/togo/taxes))_
- **Reverse charge** — Reverse charge applies to services supplied by non-resident providers to Togo-registered customers ((approx — confirm))  _(Code Général des Impôts (Togo) — TVA)_
- **2025 VAT reforms** — Recent VAT reforms have increased compliance/administrative obligations for businesses  _(Loi de finances (Togo) 2025 (https://wts.com/global/publishing-article/20250930-togo-vat-reforms~publishing-article))_
