# Togo annual property-tax working paper v0.1

## Who this applies to

Prepare the annual taxe foncière assessment for a property in Togo, separately from income earned by renting or selling it. CGI article 257 creates tax on built and unbuilt property. Article 258 includes buildings, fixed shelter/storage installations subject to its movable-equipment exception, private infrastructure, fixed accommodation/commercial boats, indispensable building land, specified commercial/industrial land and specified advertising land. Article 259 defines taxable urban unbuilt land and land intended for nonagricultural construction. Do not treat every undeveloped plot as taxable urban land. [CGI 257–259](https://web.archive.org/web/20260702000000id_/https://www.otr.tg/index.php/fr/documentation/sur-les-impots/code-general-des-impots/600-code-general-des-impots-livre-des-procedures-fiscales-mis-a-jour-2025/file.html)

Identify the holder before computing: property or superficie right holder, usufructuary, emphyteutic lessee, or construction/rehabilitation lessee. For the specific article 260 construction-at-tenant-cost arrangement, calculate the tenant's constructed-property assessment and the landowner's separate rent/prestation assessment under that article; joint liability applies. Its amortisation-based tenant value is a distinct rule, so hold that calculation until the full lease and works cost are available. [CGI 260](https://web.archive.org/web/20260702000000id_/https://www.otr.tg/index.php/fr/documentation/sur-les-impots/code-general-des-impots/600-code-general-des-impots-livre-des-procedures-fiscales-mis-a-jour-2025/file.html)

This is a bounded statutory working paper. The original 2025 consolidation and actual 2026 finance-law overlay were read. The law applies its other fiscal measures from 1 January 2026, subject to contrary provisions; article 21 replaces CGI 271. Later amendment continuity is not proved by the 2026 label. [LF 2026 arts. 2, 21](https://finances.gouv.tg/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Loi-de-finances-de-lannee-exercice-2026.pdf)

## The base

1. Establish the property, its location, rights holder, use and condition at 1 January. The annual tax is due for the whole year; special relief is a separate claim. Built-property land that is indispensable to the construction is included in its cadastral rental estimate, rather than taxed again as unbuilt land. [CGI 258(6), 268(4), 270; LPF 85](https://web.archive.org/web/20260702000000id_/https://www.otr.tg/index.php/fr/documentation/sur-les-impots/code-general-des-impots/600-code-general-des-impots-livre-des-procedures-fiscales-mis-a-jour-2025/file.html)
2. Screen the full permanent exemptions. Article 261 covers qualifying non-income-producing public-service property, specified public concession/water/energy assets, public worship, non-income-producing school/university property, medical/social assistance property, rural livestock/crop-storage buildings, the single principal home actually and exclusively inhabited by the owner or specified immediate family, state railway assets, official foreign diplomatic/consular mission residences and qualifying approved mutual entities. The conditions matter; ownership by a public body or an unoccupied second home alone is insufficient. [CGI 261](https://web.archive.org/web/20260702000000id_/https://www.otr.tg/index.php/fr/documentation/sur-les-impots/code-general-des-impots/600-code-general-des-impots-livre-des-procedures-fiscales-mis-a-jour-2025/file.html)
3. For unbuilt property, article 268 exempts the specified non-income-producing public land/ways, official or collective agricultural selection nurseries, school land, already built-taxable land, qualifying cultivation in use at 1 January and state railway ways. Article 269 exempts rural immovables. Do not infer rural status just from nondevelopment. [CGI 268–269](https://web.archive.org/web/20260702000000id_/https://www.otr.tg/index.php/fr/documentation/sur-les-impots/code-general-des-impots/600-code-general-des-impots-livre-des-procedures-fiscales-mis-a-jour-2025/file.html)
4. Apply the temporary exemption only after checking declarations. Article 262 also has a separate five-year rule for property acquired by approved financial institutions or economic public enterprises through their own mortgage enforcement or dation, intended for resale or rental to recover debts. Article 263 grants five years for the stated rural-building conversion. Commercial/industrial or specified advertising land has no temporary exemption; irregular subdivisions receive only the remaining window once regularised. [CGI 262–265](https://web.archive.org/web/20260702000000id_/https://www.otr.tg/index.php/fr/documentation/sur-les-impots/code-general-des-impots/600-code-general-des-impots-livre-des-procedures-fiscales-mis-a-jour-2025/file.html)
5. Retain the assessed gross rental value, the statutory reduction, net cadastral income and rate as separate lines. For unbuilt land, retain the cadastre's market-value support, recent comparable conveyances or direct estimate. An owner's advertised asking price is not a substituted cadastral determination. [CGI 270, 272–275](https://web.archive.org/web/20260702000000id_/https://www.otr.tg/index.php/fr/documentation/sur-les-impots/code-general-des-impots/600-code-general-des-impots-livre-des-procedures-fiscales-mis-a-jour-2025/file.html)
6. Reconcile any rental-payment tax credit only against proof of its payment and the assessment bulletins. LPF 100 allocates the withholding between property tax and income tax; do not deduct the entire rental withholding from this property assessment, assume a refund or combine the separate taxes into one final rate. The full rental withholding/advance computation remains outside this Guide. [LPF 100–101](https://web.archive.org/web/20260702000000id_/https://www.otr.tg/index.php/fr/documentation/sur-les-impots/code-general-des-impots/600-code-general-des-impots-livre-des-procedures-fiscales-mis-a-jour-2025/file.html)

## Deadlines and filing

Pay the annual tax spontaneously to the tax receiver in two equal instalments, by 31 May and 31 October. LPF 85 also states joint liability of landowner and tenant. Do not replace the statutory instalment calendar with an unverified portal instruction. [LPF 85](https://web.archive.org/web/20260702000000id_/https://www.otr.tg/index.php/fr/documentation/sur-les-impots/code-general-des-impots/600-code-general-des-impots-livre-des-procedures-fiscales-mis-a-jour-2025/file.html)

By 31 May, owners, principal tenants, or property managers acting in their place declare each tenant's identity, premises, principal rent and charges; rent-free occupants and their premises; self-occupied premises; vacant premises; and exact taxable unbuilt-land area. New construction, reconstruction, additions and changes of size/use are notified within four months of definitive completion. Keep submission and payment evidence separately. Current form identifier, boxes, electronic route and payment reference format were not established. [LPF 86–87](https://web.archive.org/web/20260702000000id_/https://www.otr.tg/index.php/fr/documentation/sur-les-impots/code-general-des-impots/600-code-general-des-impots-livre-des-procedures-fiscales-mis-a-jour-2025/file.html)

For the new-building exemption, provide a building permit or the article 266 commencement declaration within four months of works opening, with building nature/use, footprint, cadastral/land identification and sketch/plan; additionally submit the completion declaration within four months after completion. The text requires written administration-issued forms. A late declaration can recover only the unexpired exemption fraction from 1 January after its submission; it never exempts the first year after completion. [CGI 266–267](https://web.archive.org/web/20260702000000id_/https://www.otr.tg/index.php/fr/documentation/sur-les-impots/code-general-des-impots/600-code-general-des-impots-livre-des-procedures-fiscales-mis-a-jour-2025/file.html)

Property is assessed in the prefecture/commune where it lies. Arrange the cadastral mutation after a property transfer: pending mutation the former owner can remain assessed and liable, subject to recourse against the buyer. A titled parcel's assessment-name change depends on transcription of the transfer act/decision in the land register. Ownership disputes go to the civil courts and suspend the assessment-name decision until final judgment; correction decisions also affect later years until the rolls are corrected. This is an assessment-record rule, not a calculation of transfer duty. [CGI 281–285](https://web.archive.org/web/20260702000000id_/https://www.otr.tg/index.php/fr/documentation/sur-les-impots/code-general-des-impots/600-code-general-des-impots-livre-des-procedures-fiscales-mis-a-jour-2025/file.html)

## Penalties and interest

Under CGI 266, missing the required construction declarations causes assessment from 1 January after completion. In the first assessment-roll year, the addition counts the years between completion and discovery, including discovery, capped at five times that year's contribution. Preserve dates and the actual notice before computing that addition. LPF 87 separately addresses undeclared construction/change using presumed completion. Do not invent a universal late-payment percentage from these provisions; general administration and sanctions are routed to [the overview](tg-tax-overview.md), and a property-specific current penalty/interest assessment remains a hold. [CGI 266–267; LPF 87](https://web.archive.org/web/20260702000000id_/https://www.otr.tg/index.php/fr/documentation/sur-les-impots/code-general-des-impots/600-code-general-des-impots-livre-des-procedures-fiscales-mis-a-jour-2025/file.html)

Commercial/industrial vacancy or non-use relief requires all three article 278 conditions: involuntary, at least three months, and the whole property or an independently rentable/usable part. The potential window begins the first day of the month after vacancy starts and ends the last day of the month in which it ends. Built-property destruction/demolition and extraordinary disappearance of unbuilt property have their separate next-month rules. Relief requires a claim, not unilateral nonpayment. [CGI 278–280](https://web.archive.org/web/20260702000000id_/https://www.otr.tg/index.php/fr/documentation/sur-les-impots/code-general-des-impots/600-code-general-des-impots-livre-des-procedures-fiscales-mis-a-jour-2025/file.html)

LPF 374 normally sets 31 December of the year after the relevant listed event as the claim deadline, including the year the minimum vacancy duration is met. Record the actual triggering event; LPF 375 provides a separate reassessment-period rule. Claims under LPF 376 identify every disputed assessment, grounds and requested result, taxpayer contact/NIF, date, handwritten signature and supporting notices/payment/withholding evidence. A representative needs the article 377 mandate unless an exception applies; a claimant domiciled abroad elects a Togo domicile. No filing/payment stay is inferred. [LPF 374–378](https://web.archive.org/web/20260702000000id_/https://www.otr.tg/index.php/fr/documentation/sur-les-impots/code-general-des-impots/600-code-general-des-impots-livre-des-procedures-fiscales-mis-a-jour-2025/file.html)

## Cross-border points

The property-location and enumerated-right-holder tests above control this domestic assessment. A foreign owner's salary-residence classification does not determine the cadastral base. Article 261(10)'s official foreign mission residence exemption is specific; do not apply it to every foreign-owned property. A claimant domiciled outside Togo must elect a domestic domicile for its claim. Treaty-specific effects, succession, foreign-owner conveyance restrictions and foreign tax credits remain unconfirmed. Route residence and withholding questions to [cross-border tax](tg-cross-border.md), and rental-income computation to [income tax](tg-income-tax.md). [CGI 258–261; LPF 378](https://web.archive.org/web/20260702000000id_/https://www.otr.tg/index.php/fr/documentation/sur-les-impots/code-general-des-impots/600-code-general-des-impots-livre-des-procedures-fiscales-mis-a-jour-2025/file.html)

## Two worked examples that recompute

**Commercial asset valuation.** Assume premises and incorporated installations are qualifying company balance-sheet assets, with evidenced market value CFA 100 million, and no exemption or credit. Replacement CGI 271 gives annual rental value CFA 6 million. CGI 270 reduces it to CFA 3 million net cadastral income; CGI 275 gives CFA 225,000 annual tax, with two CFA 112,500 instalments under LPF 85. This is not 6% tax on the property and does not establish a market value for another parcel. [CGI 270, 275; LPF 85](https://web.archive.org/web/20260702000000id_/https://www.otr.tg/index.php/fr/documentation/sur-les-impots/code-general-des-impots/600-code-general-des-impots-livre-des-procedures-fiscales-mis-a-jour-2025/file.html), [replacement CGI 271](https://finances.gouv.tg/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Loi-de-finances-de-lannee-exercice-2026.pdf).

```oa-check
name: Qualifying commercial balance-sheet property
inputs:
  market_value: 100000000
steps:
  rental_value: market_value * 0.06
  net_cadastral_income: rental_value * (1 - 0.50)
  annual_tax: net_cadastral_income * 0.075
  each_instalment: annual_tax / 2
expected:
  rental_value: 6000000
  net_cadastral_income: 3000000
  annual_tax: 225000
  each_instalment: 112500
```

**Temporary-exemption decision.** Assume completion in 2023, fully timely article 266 declarations, regular subdivision, no use change, no permanent exemption and an independently established gross cadastral rental value CFA 2 million. For assessment 2026, three years have elapsed after completion. A qualifying commercial building's two-year exemption covered 2024–2025, so its 2026 tax is CFA 75,000. A qualifying residential building's five-year exemption covers 2024–2028, so 2026 tax is zero and three exempt assessment years remain including 2026. Use follows the evidence; the owner cannot elect the residential exemption for a commercial building. Without timely declarations this example cannot be applied. [CGI 262, 265–267, 270, 275](https://web.archive.org/web/20260702000000id_/https://www.otr.tg/index.php/fr/documentation/sur-les-impots/code-general-des-impots/600-code-general-des-impots-livre-des-procedures-fiscales-mis-a-jour-2025/file.html).

```oa-check
name: Two-year versus five-year construction exemption in 2026
inputs:
  completion_year: 2023
  assessment_year: 2026
  gross_rental_value: 2000000
steps:
  years_after_completion: assessment_year - completion_year
  ordinary_tax: gross_rental_value * 0.50 * 0.075
  commercial_tax: ordinary_tax * clamp(years_after_completion - 2, 0, 1)
  residential_tax: ordinary_tax * clamp(years_after_completion - 5, 0, 1)
  residential_exempt_years_left: max(0, 5 - years_after_completion + 1)
expected:
  ordinary_tax: 75000
  commercial_tax: 75000
  residential_tax: 0
  residential_exempt_years_left: 3
```

## Ask the client first

- Which parcel, location, cadastral/title reference and rights holder applied at 1 January, and has any transfer been transcribed?
- What is the actual use and occupancy, including principal-home/family use, rural status, commercial/industrial balance-sheet classification and every tenant?
- Which cadastral valuation and market-value evidence exists, and what is included in land and incorporated installations?
- When did works begin/end, when were permits/declarations delivered, and is the subdivision regular?
- Which assessment bulletins, paid rental-withholding credits, instalment receipts and claimed exemptions already exist?
- Did vacancy, destruction, a later law or a special approval alter this case, and what dated evidence proves it?
