## Who this applies to

**Source-baseline draft for 2026 preparation; operative 2026 coverage is not confirmed.** The consolidated Taxation Act (amendments through June 2021), the enacted FY 2024/25 Financial Act and the cited official guidance were opened. Intervening annual Financial Acts after June 2021 have not been reconciled in full. The public sources checked did not establish the complete enacted FY 2025/26 and FY 2026/27 amendment chain. A ministry publication labelled Draft Finance Bill 2025/26 is not treated as enacted law. Prepare and review the evidence below, then stop before a final 2026 tax position or submission until the later-law chain and relevant directions are resolved. The FY 2024/25 tables describe that opened enactment, not a silently rolled-forward 2026 rate. [SSRA public catalogue](https://cms.nra.gov.ss/resources); [ministry public library](https://mofp.gov.ss/wp-json/wp/v2/dlp_document?per_page=100).

Ordinary company formation under the opened Companies Act. The statute was actually read from a 9 June 2026 archive of the official Justice PDF after direct and normal-browser certificate failures. This is a document-preparation and compliance handoff, not a claim that incorporation or tax registration has occurred. Sole proprietorship, partnership, LLP and foreign-branch procedures require their own legal route.

## The base

1. Identify the intended company type, real subscribers, joint holdings and beneficial/control arrangements. The opened ordinary private-company minimum is not the legacy one-shareholder claim. Separate legal membership from a director appointment. [ss.7–9, PDF 26–27](https://web.archive.org/web/20260609050816/https://mojca.gov.ss/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/Companies-Act-2012.pdf)
2. Prepare the English memorandum with the required name, office, objects, liability and share capital/division. Record each subscription and the witness/signature particulars. Do not treat nominal declared capital as proof that every sector or foreign investment has no minimum. [ss.10–11, PDF 27–28](https://web.archive.org/web/20260609050816/https://mojca.gov.ss/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/Companies-Act-2012.pdf)
3. Check director residence, natural-person status, consent, age and disqualifications. No person should be appointed merely to fill a numeric box without meeting the Act. [s.241, PDF 156–157](https://web.archive.org/web/20260609050816/https://mojca.gov.ss/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/Companies-Act-2012.pdf)
4. Deliver the statutory documents to the registrar, obtain the actual certificate and registration number, and verify that they match the approved documents. Establish any required declaration of compliance. The archived Act supplies the legal sequence; it does not establish today’s online screens or processing time. [ss.21–23, PDF 32–33](https://web.archive.org/web/20260609050816/https://mojca.gov.ss/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/Companies-Act-2012.pdf)
5. Prepare the separate business-TIN handoff using the official manual, then check current instructions before submitting. Registration, tax clearance, investment certification, work permits and trade licences are distinct jobs; a certificate for one does not complete the others. [PDF 26–37](https://cms.nra.gov.ss/uploads/NRA_e_Tax_Training_Manual_V_001_Registration_eae52f383f.pdf)
6. Establish the annual-return calendar and package. Section 177 has incorporation-year/AGM-related exceptions; the return is not simply due on the tax return date. Under s.179, complete and forward the copy within 42 days after the AGM, signed by both a director and secretary. Apply s.180(1) balance-sheet/auditor/director-report attachments only after checking s.180(5): a private company is excluded unless at least one shareholder is a public company. Where required, copies are certified by both a director and secretary; retain required annexes, certified English translations and any required corrections. Separately, a private company must endorse or accompany its s.177 return with the s.181 certificate, signed by both officers, confirming no public share/debenture invitation since incorporation. If the return shows more than 50 members, add the similarly signed certificate that the excess consists wholly of persons excluded from that limit. [ss.177–181, PDF 111–115](https://web.archive.org/web/20260609050816/https://mojca.gov.ss/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/Companies-Act-2012.pdf)

## Deadlines and filing

The registration manual documents https://etax.nra.gov.ss/ and business.eservices.gov.ss validation. Its business route selects Business or Company, enters director TIN and business number, verifies phone/email, handles an existing TIN certificate, optionally adds an agent, reviews the declaration and completes registration. Retain the verification reference and resulting TIN certificate; an application is not automatic approval. Current registry uploads, fee invoice, payment path and certificate acknowledgement were not tested. Do not state the old 2–6 week timeline as a legal service promise.

## Cross-border points

A foreign-owned company, foreign-company branch and individual investor are different legal persons/routes. Foreign investment minimum, certificate and incentive eligibility are not inferred from company capital wording. The [cross-border Guide](ss-cross-border.md) owns tax residence/effective management; the [BPT Guide](ss-corporate-income-tax.md) owns the tax computation. Non-tax investment legislation is not a standalone ordinary BPT exemption under the opened s.69(3). [Taxation Act s.69(3), PDF 65–66](https://cms.nra.gov.ss/uploads/TAXATION_ACT_2021_R_E_CONSOLIDATED_f4f9ed8876.pdf).

## Two worked examples that recompute

The inputs are fictional evidence assumed established. The arithmetic does not establish a client fact, current-law applicability or completion of a filing.

**Joint holders are not two members for this limit.** Assume A and B jointly hold a single shareholding and no one else subscribes. The statutory member count is 1, short of the ordinary private-company minimum 2. Adding C as a genuine separate subscriber gives 2. This is not an instruction to invent a nominal subscriber.

```oa-check
name: Private-company member-count decision
inputs:
  joint_group_count: 1
  separate_member_a: 0
  separate_member_b: 1
  minimum: 2
steps:
  count_a: joint_group_count + separate_member_a
  count_b: joint_group_count + separate_member_b
  shortfall_a: max(minimum - count_a, 0)
expected:
  count_a: 1
  count_b: 2
  shortfall_a: 1
```

**Declared shares must reconcile.** Assume the memorandum proposes 100 shares at SSP 1,000 each, subscribed 60 and 40 between two genuine members. Declared capital is SSP 100,000, with no unallocated shares. This fictional amount is not a minimum capital rule or a company-registration fee.

```oa-check
name: Memorandum share reconciliation
inputs:
  shares: 100
  nominal_value: 1000
  subscriber_a: 60
  subscriber_b: 40
steps:
  declared_capital: shares * nominal_value
  allocated: subscriber_a + subscriber_b
  unallocated: shares - allocated
expected:
  declared_capital: 100000
  allocated: 100
  unallocated: 0
```

## Ask the client first

- What legal vehicle, activities, nationality/ownership and regulated-sector requirements apply?
- Who are the real subscribers, joint holders, directors and proposed local resident director?
- What English memorandum/articles, share allocation and witness information are ready?
- Is there an existing business number, director TIN, certificate or previous tax registration?
- Which current registry form, fee schedule and certificate workflow applies?
- What investment certificate, branch, work-permit or sector licence job is separately required?
