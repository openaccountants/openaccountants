# South Sudan business profit tax source-baseline working paper v0.1

> **General reference only.** This skill is general tax/accounting reference material for AI-assisted workflows. It has not been reviewed for any specific person's facts, documents, elections, deadlines, residency, filing status, or local procedures. Do not rely on it to file, pay, amend, or take a tax position without review by a qualified professional in the relevant jurisdiction.

## Who this applies to

**Source-baseline draft for 2026 preparation; operative 2026 coverage is not confirmed.** The consolidated Taxation Act (amendments through June 2021), the enacted FY 2024/25 Financial Act and the cited official guidance were opened. Intervening annual Financial Acts after June 2021 have not been reconciled in full. The public sources checked did not establish the complete enacted FY 2025/26 and FY 2026/27 amendment chain. A ministry publication labelled Draft Finance Bill 2025/26 is not treated as enacted law. Prepare and review the evidence below, then stop before a final 2026 tax position or submission until the later-law chain and relevant directions are resolved. The FY 2024/25 tables describe that opened enactment, not a silently rolled-forward 2026 rate. [SSRA public catalogue](https://cms.nra.gov.ss/resources); [ministry public library](https://mofp.gov.ss/wp-json/wp/v2/dlp_document?per_page=100).

Ordinary business organisations within Chapter XII. This Guide owns company BPT, deductions and advance reconciliation. Sole proprietors belong in the [income-tax Guide](ss-income-tax.md), and payment withholding in the [cross-border Guide](ss-cross-border.md). Insurance, oil/mining fiscal regimes and special agreements require separate work.

All figures below retain the period and conditions of the opened source. Read the stop gate before using a value.

## The base

1. Establish the legal taxpayer, residence/source and calendar tax period. Reconcile gross receipts and accrued income to the financial statements. Do not classify a business by the government’s fiscal year or resurrect old small/medium rate tiers. [ss.64–68, PDF 62–64](https://cms.nra.gov.ss/uploads/TAXATION_ACT_2021_R_E_CONSOLIDATED_f4f9ed8876.pdf)
2. Separate ordinary costs from land/capital assets, fines and profit tax. Apply representation and bad-debt conditions, not only an accounting expense balance. Category capital-account, placed-in-service, repairs and disposal rules need their detailed regulations and evidence before a final allowance. [s.70(1)–(2), PDF 66](https://cms.nra.gov.ss/uploads/TAXATION_ACT_2021_R_E_CONSOLIDATED_f4f9ed8876.pdf); [s.74, PDF 68–69](https://cms.nra.gov.ss/uploads/TAXATION_ACT_2021_R_E_CONSOLIDATED_f4f9ed8876.pdf)
3. Obtain the origin year and remaining life of each loss; track the statutory carryforward window. Do not substitute an unexplained opening loss balance. [s.78, PDF 71–72](https://cms.nra.gov.ss/uploads/TAXATION_ACT_2021_R_E_CONSOLIDATED_f4f9ed8876.pdf)
4. Reconcile gains and losses under s.77, including its special Category 2/3 proceeds rule, rather than using the old blanket capital-gains percentage. Related-party deductions and pricing must pass the [TP Guide](ss-transfer-pricing.md). [s.77, PDF 70–71](https://cms.nra.gov.ss/uploads/TAXATION_ACT_2021_R_E_CONSOLIDATED_f4f9ed8876.pdf)
5. Compute ordinary BPT only after the law-selection and deduction gates are resolved. Reconcile evidenced import advances and withholding to the right taxpayer and period; the opened FY 2024/25 s.96 amendment rejects automatic finality. This is a credit reconciliation, not an automatic refund instruction. [s.49 approved amendment 4, PDF 39–40](https://cms.nra.gov.ss/uploads/Financial_Act_2024_2025_464925c180.pdf)

## Two worked examples that recompute

The inputs are fictional evidence assumed established. The arithmetic does not establish a client fact, current-law applicability or completion of a filing.

**Representation cost requires a capped deduction.** Assume all law-selection questions are resolved, income is SSP 1,000,000, other allowable costs SSP 600,000 and representation costs SSP 40,000. Only SSP 20,000 representation is allowed under the opened cap. Taxable profit is SSP 380,000 and ordinary baseline BPT SSP 114,000. No depreciation, loss, credit or special-regime issue is assumed.

```oa-check
name: Ordinary BPT representation adjustment
inputs:
  income: 1000000
  other_costs: 600000
  representation_cost: 40000
  cap_rate: 0.02
  bpt_rate: 0.3
steps:
  allowed_representation: min(representation_cost, income * cap_rate)
  taxable_profit: income - other_costs - allowed_representation
  baseline_bpt: taxable_profit * bpt_rate
expected:
  allowed_representation: 20000
  taxable_profit: 380000
  baseline_bpt: 114000
```

**An import advance is a credit question, not the final tax.** Assume a reviewed ordinary BPT liability of SSP 300,000 and properly matched, eligible import advances of SSP 240,000. The balance is SSP 60,000. If matched eligible credits were SSP 320,000, the workpaper shows SSP 20,000 excess for the credit/refund process, not an instruction to receive cash automatically.

```oa-check
name: Import advance reconciliation branches
inputs:
  reviewed_bpt: 300000
  credits_a: 240000
  credits_b: 320000
steps:
  balance_a: max(reviewed_bpt - credits_a, 0)
  excess_b: max(credits_b - reviewed_bpt, 0)
expected:
  balance_a: 60000
  excess_b: 20000
```

## Ask the client first

- Which legal entity, residence, accounting period and activity/special agreement apply?
- What financial statements and underlying income/cost records reconcile to the return?
- What supports representations, bad debts, capital accounts, disposals and prior-year losses?
- What related-party transactions need a pricing or deductibility adjustment?
- What import advances and withholding certificates belong to this taxpayer/period?
- Which later law, current BPT form and filing/payment directions apply?
