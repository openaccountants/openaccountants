# South Sudan related-party pricing source-baseline working paper v0.1

> **General reference only.** This skill is general tax/accounting reference material for AI-assisted workflows. It has not been reviewed for any specific person's facts, documents, elections, deadlines, residency, filing status, or local procedures. Do not rely on it to file, pay, amend, or take a tax position without review by a qualified professional in the relevant jurisdiction.

## Who this applies to

**Source-baseline draft for 2026 preparation; operative 2026 coverage is not confirmed.** The consolidated Taxation Act (amendments through June 2021), the enacted FY 2024/25 Financial Act and the cited official guidance were opened. Intervening annual Financial Acts after June 2021 have not been reconciled in full. The public sources checked did not establish the complete enacted FY 2025/26 and FY 2026/27 amendment chain. A ministry publication labelled Draft Finance Bill 2025/26 is not treated as enacted law. Prepare and review the evidence below, then stop before a final 2026 tax position or submission until the later-law chain and relevant directions are resolved. The FY 2024/25 tables describe that opened enactment, not a silently rolled-forward 2026 rate. [SSRA public catalogue](https://cms.nra.gov.ss/resources); [ministry public library](https://mofp.gov.ss/wp-json/wp/v2/dlp_document?per_page=100).

Related-person asset transactions or contract obligations and related-payment deductions under ss.5,59,73,81. The statutory wording does not impose a nonresident-only condition. This Guide supplies a working-paper sequence, not an unverified mandatory local/master-file checklist.

## The base

1. Map each person, control chain, voting/share rights, officers/directors, partnership/employment relationships and family connection. A below-threshold shareholding is not safe when another limb applies. [s.5, PDF 18](https://cms.nra.gov.ss/uploads/TAXATION_ACT_2021_R_E_CONSOLIDATED_f4f9ed8876.pdf)
2. Reconcile the actual contract, goods/services, delivery, invoice, payment and accounting entry. Label these as evidence recommendations, not a prescribed filing form. Establish the transaction before pricing it.
3. Look first for a defensible comparable uncontrolled price. Document product/service, volume, market, functions, risk and terms, and support any comparison adjustments. Only when that method is not possible does s.81(2) direct resale-price or cost-plus. No unverified OECD method, profit split, TNMM or safe harbour is added. [s.81, PDF 73](https://cms.nra.gov.ss/uploads/TAXATION_ACT_2021_R_E_CONSOLIDATED_f4f9ed8876.pdf)
4. Reconcile booked and supported amounts and the taxable-profit effect, with a separate check of the fair-market deduction restrictions. Do not invent a corresponding adjustment in another person’s return. [ss.73,59(3)–(4), PDF 68,57](https://cms.nra.gov.ss/uploads/TAXATION_ACT_2021_R_E_CONSOLIDATED_f4f9ed8876.pdf); [s.81, PDF 73](https://cms.nra.gov.ss/uploads/TAXATION_ACT_2021_R_E_CONSOLIDATED_f4f9ed8876.pdf)
5. Record any scheme/anti-avoidance issue separately. The printed FY 2024/25 s.8 A condition uses unusual wording, so this Guide does not silently replace that wording with a broader modern GAAR test. Refer a scheme case for the complete legal test, rather than equate every pricing difference with tax avoidance. [s.48 B(iv), PDF 32](https://cms.nra.gov.ss/uploads/Financial_Act_2024_2025_464925c180.pdf)

## Two worked examples that recompute

The inputs are fictional evidence assumed established. The arithmetic does not establish a client fact, current-law applicability or completion of a filing.

**An underpriced associated sale.** Assume association and all operative rules are established and reliable CUP evidence supports SSP 100 per unit. A seller booked 1,000 units at SSP 80. The supported revenue is SSP 100,000 rather than SSP 80,000, raising the working-paper profit figure by SSP 20,000 before other rules. No tax rate or markup safe harbour is invented.

```oa-check
name: CUP sales reconciliation
inputs:
  quantity: 1000
  booked_price: 80
  supported_cup: 100
steps:
  booked_revenue: quantity * booked_price
  supported_revenue: quantity * supported_cup
  profit_difference: supported_revenue - booked_revenue
expected:
  booked_revenue: 80000
  supported_revenue: 100000
  profit_difference: 20000
```

**Numerical ownership is only one association branch.** A hypothetical holder at 49% does not meet the numerical 50% limb; at 50% it does. The arithmetic below shows the boundary, not a conclusion that 49% is unrelated. Control, common control, office/director status, employment, partnership and family links must still be checked in both cases.

```oa-check
name: Association threshold branch
inputs:
  holding_a: 49
  holding_b: 50
  threshold: 50
steps:
  a_shortfall: max(threshold - holding_a, 0)
  b_shortfall: max(threshold - holding_b, 0)
expected:
  a_shortfall: 1
  b_shortfall: 0
```

## Ask the client first

- Who are the parties and what ownership, control, employment, partnership or family links exist?
- What transaction, assessment period, contracts and actual delivery/payment evidence apply?
- What independent comparable exists, and why is it comparable?
- If CUP is not possible, what evidence supports the permitted alternative and its costs/margin?
- What was booked and what deduction/source/withholding treatment is proposed?
- What later TP instrument, notice, documentation rule or scheme issue needs resolution?
