# South Sudan residence, source and withholding source-baseline working paper v0.1

> **General reference only.** This skill is general tax/accounting reference material for AI-assisted workflows. It has not been reviewed for any specific person's facts, documents, elections, deadlines, residency, filing status, or local procedures. Do not rely on it to file, pay, amend, or take a tax position without review by a qualified professional in the relevant jurisdiction.

## Who this applies to

**Source-baseline draft for 2026 preparation; operative 2026 coverage is not confirmed.** The consolidated Taxation Act (amendments through June 2021), the enacted FY 2024/25 Financial Act and the cited official guidance were opened. Intervening annual Financial Acts after June 2021 have not been reconciled in full. The public sources checked did not establish the complete enacted FY 2025/26 and FY 2026/27 amendment chain. A ministry publication labelled Draft Finance Bill 2025/26 is not treated as enacted law. Prepare and review the evidence below, then stop before a final 2026 tax position or submission until the later-law chain and relevant directions are resolved. The FY 2024/25 tables describe that opened enactment, not a silently rolled-forward 2026 rate. [SSRA public catalogue](https://cms.nra.gov.ss/resources); [ministry public library](https://mofp.gov.ss/wp-json/wp/v2/dlp_document?per_page=100).

Individuals and business organisations with foreign connections, foreign-source income or payments in the withholding categories. This Guide owns domestic residence/source and the opened withholding table. It does not determine immigration residence, treaty status or every special tax agreement.

## The base

1. Identify the legal person and calendar tax period. For an individual, document domicile and actual presence dates. The opened Act uses 183 days in a tax period, not a rolling 12-month test. For an entity, establishment and effective management are alternatives; a foreign registry alone does not settle residence. [s.5, PDF 19](https://cms.nra.gov.ss/uploads/TAXATION_ACT_2021_R_E_CONSOLIDATED_f4f9ed8876.pdf); [s.5, PDF 19](https://cms.nra.gov.ss/uploads/TAXATION_ACT_2021_R_E_CONSOLIDATED_f4f9ed8876.pdf)
2. Map each income item to local/foreign source and the individual/business chapter. The statutory PE definition is workplace-based and gives examples; do not import an unsupported treaty construction/service day threshold. [s.64, PDF 62–63](https://cms.nra.gov.ss/uploads/TAXATION_ACT_2021_R_E_CONSOLIDATED_f4f9ed8876.pdf); [s.64, PDF 63](https://cms.nra.gov.ss/uploads/TAXATION_ACT_2021_R_E_CONSOLIDATED_f4f9ed8876.pdf)
3. Identify the payment, legal payer, recipient, residence, contract and gross amount. The government-contract rates do not apply automatically to private contracts. Retain the precise scope of nonresident technical fees when reading the later rate schedule. [s.49 Schedule 1(A), PDF 40](https://cms.nra.gov.ss/uploads/Financial_Act_2024_2025_464925c180.pdf); [s.49 Schedule 1(A), PDF 40; Taxation Act ss.92(g),94(6), PDF 77–78](https://cms.nra.gov.ss/uploads/Financial_Act_2024_2025_464925c180.pdf)
4. Keep rent withholding on gross payment apart from liability on rental/investment income. The opened Act’s body and schedule conflict on that liability; no single rental-income rate or “real-estate-only” scope is selected. [s.4(2)(f), PDF 11 and Schedule 1(A), PDF 41](https://cms.nra.gov.ss/uploads/Financial_Act_2024_2025_464925c180.pdf)
5. Reconcile withholding certificates and income-tax liability under the replacement s.96. Older consolidated exemptions/finality wording must be reconciled with that amendment before granting a final settlement, exemption or refund. For foreign credit, establish foreign tax paid, reciprocal treatment, relevant income and both average rates; a foreign tax invoice alone is insufficient. [s.49 approved amendment 4, PDF 39–40](https://cms.nra.gov.ss/uploads/Financial_Act_2024_2025_464925c180.pdf); [s.62(d)–(e), PDF 61](https://cms.nra.gov.ss/uploads/TAXATION_ACT_2021_R_E_CONSOLIDATED_f4f9ed8876.pdf)

## Two worked examples that recompute

The inputs are fictional evidence assumed established. The arithmetic does not establish a client fact, current-law applicability or completion of a filing.

**The presence threshold is not the only residence ground.** Verified non-overlapping visits of 90, 70 and 23 days total 183 in the calendar tax period. A second person totals 182. The former meets the opened presence limb. The latter still needs a domicile check; proven South Sudan domicile can establish residence independently of days. Neither arithmetic result settles a treaty.

```oa-check
name: Domestic presence boundary
inputs:
  visit_a: 90
  visit_b: 70
  visit_c: 23
  short_c: 22
  threshold: 183
steps:
  days_a: visit_a + visit_b + visit_c
  days_b: visit_a + visit_b + short_c
  shortfall_b: threshold - days_b
expected:
  days_a: 183
  days_b: 182
  shortfall_b: 1
```

**A foreign credit has an income-specific ceiling.** Assume reciprocity and all substantive conditions are proven, the relevant foreign income is SSP 1,000,000, its foreign average rate 12% and its South Sudan average rate 10%. The permitted comparison is SSP 120,000 against SSP 100,000, with SSP 100,000 as the workpaper credit ceiling. These fictional rates are not South Sudan headline tax rates or a treaty promise.

```oa-check
name: Reciprocal foreign credit limit
inputs:
  relevant_income: 1000000
  foreign_average: 0.12
  south_sudan_average: 0.1
steps:
  foreign_measure: relevant_income * foreign_average
  domestic_measure: relevant_income * south_sudan_average
  credit_ceiling: min(foreign_measure, domestic_measure)
expected:
  foreign_measure: 120000
  domestic_measure: 100000
  credit_ceiling: 100000
```

## Ask the client first

- Who is the individual/entity and what domicile, establishment and effective-management facts apply?
- What verified presence dates and local work/property/service connections exist?
- What payment category, payer type, recipient residence, gross amount and contract apply?
- What foreign tax, income and reciprocal-credit evidence exists?
- What treaty text, entry-into-force dates and entitlement facts support any relief claim?
- Which later law resolves current withholding/finality and rental-income treatment?
