# South Sudan sales tax and excise source-conflict working paper v0.1

> **General reference only.** This skill is general tax/accounting reference material for AI-assisted workflows. It has not been reviewed for any specific person's facts, documents, elections, deadlines, residency, filing status, or local procedures. Do not rely on it to file, pay, amend, or take a tax position without review by a qualified professional in the relevant jurisdiction.

## Who this applies to

**Source-baseline draft for 2026 preparation; operative 2026 coverage is not confirmed.** The consolidated Taxation Act (amendments through June 2021), the enacted FY 2024/25 Financial Act and the cited official guidance were opened. Intervening annual Financial Acts after June 2021 have not been reconciled in full. The public sources checked did not establish the complete enacted FY 2025/26 and FY 2026/27 amendment chain. A ministry publication labelled Draft Finance Bill 2025/26 is not treated as enacted law. Prepare and review the evidence below, then stop before a final 2026 tax position or submission until the later-law chain and relevant directions are resolved. The FY 2024/25 tables describe that opened enactment, not a silently rolled-forward 2026 rate. [SSRA public catalogue](https://cms.nra.gov.ss/resources); [ministry public library](https://mofp.gov.ss/wp-json/wp/v2/dlp_document?per_page=100).

Importers, local producers and specified service providers. The opened law has sales-tax and excise chapters; that does not prove that no later VAT enactment exists. This job produces a classification and conflict schedule. Stop before invoicing, filing or paying on an unresolved rate/base.

## The base

1. Identify the goods/service, HS code where relevant, import/local-production facts, supply date and any exemption agreement. Do not apply a headline percentage to every service or vehicle. [ss.105–107, PDF 85–86](https://cms.nra.gov.ss/uploads/TAXATION_ACT_2021_R_E_CONSOLIDATED_f4f9ed8876.pdf)
2. Retain the actual invoice, customs value, freight/insurance, customs duty, excise and fair-market evidence. Compare the inclusive valuation in s.108 and FY 2024/25 body s.14(2) with Schedule 1(A) paragraph 5. The latter describes customs value for imports without the same additions. The source conflict is real; no unverified precedence rule is supplied. [s.108, PDF 86; Financial Act Schedule 1(A) para 5 PDF 40](https://cms.nra.gov.ss/uploads/TAXATION_ACT_2021_R_E_CONSOLIDATED_f4f9ed8876.pdf)
3. Compare the enacted body’s rate text with replacement Schedule 1(A), recording both PDF pages. The ministry copy independently repeats the conflict. Do not select a rate because an old Guide or a portal estimate uses it. [Ministry copy, PDF 14,40–41](https://mofp.gov.ss/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/Finance-Act-Book__2024-2025.pdf).
4. Keep excise distinct from sales tax and customs duty. Identify the exact tariff entry, not just the label “vehicles” or “cosmetics”. The selected historical excise entries above are a source locator; a complete customs/excise calculation also needs classification, valuation and later amendments. [Schedule 1(A) excise table PDF 41–42; Schedule 1(C) PDF 44](https://cms.nra.gov.ss/uploads/Financial_Act_2024_2025_464925c180.pdf)
5. Reconcile tax liability, invoice and remittance only after the conflict and operative-law gates close. No VAT-style input credit or cross-border reverse-charge entitlement is inferred from this sales-tax chapter. Those old negative assertions need current law, not reasoning from the tax’s name.

## Cross-border points

Imports require customs/source valuation and exemption analysis. The opened FY 2024/25 body limits donor/diplomatic relief to the documented agreement/project use and excludes alcohol/tobacco; subsequent private disposal/change of use can bring tax into charge. No treaty, customs-union preference or general input-credit result is assumed. [s.15, PDF 15](https://cms.nra.gov.ss/uploads/Financial_Act_2024_2025_464925c180.pdf)

## Two worked examples that recompute

The inputs are fictional evidence assumed established. The arithmetic does not establish a client fact, current-law applicability or completion of a filing.

**The rate dispute changes a service calculation.** Assume a separately established tax-exclusive service base of SSP 100,000. The body 20% reading produces SSP 20,000; the schedule 18% reading SSP 18,000. The unresolved difference is SSP 2,000. Neither is selected as payable tax; this comparison is the reason to stop before filing.

```oa-check
name: Unresolved service rate sensitivity
inputs:
  agreed_base: 100000
  body_rate: 0.2
  schedule_rate: 0.18
steps:
  body_amount: agreed_base * body_rate
  schedule_amount: agreed_base * schedule_rate
  difference: body_amount - schedule_amount
expected:
  body_amount: 20000
  schedule_amount: 18000
  difference: 2000
```

**Two valuation readings also matter.** Assume customs value SSP 100,000, duty SSP 10,000 and excise SSP 5,000. The inclusive base in s.108 and body s.14(2) is SSP 115,000; the replacement-schedule wording identifies customs value SSP 100,000. For sensitivity only, holding a hypothetical 18% rate constant gives SSP 20,700 versus SSP 18,000. The base difference is SSP 15,000 and tax sensitivity SSP 2,700; no legal rate/base choice has been made.

```oa-check
name: Unresolved import valuation sensitivity
inputs:
  customs_value: 100000
  duty: 10000
  excise: 5000
  comparison_rate: 0.18
steps:
  inclusive_base: customs_value + duty + excise
  schedule_base: customs_value
  base_difference: inclusive_base - schedule_base
  tax_difference: base_difference * comparison_rate
expected:
  inclusive_base: 115000
  schedule_base: 100000
  base_difference: 15000
  tax_difference: 2700
```

## Ask the client first

- What exact goods/HS classification or specified service, and which transaction date?
- Is the transaction an import, local release from production or local service?
- What invoice, customs value, excise/customs duty and fair-market evidence supports the base?
- Which exemption agreement, use and subsequent disposal facts apply?
- What written authority resolves the competing rate and valuation text?
- Which later law and actual sales/excise/customs filing workflow applies?
