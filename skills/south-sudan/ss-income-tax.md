# South Sudan personal income tax source-baseline working paper v0.1

> **General reference only.** This skill is general tax/accounting reference material for AI-assisted workflows. It has not been reviewed for any specific person's facts, documents, elections, deadlines, residency, filing status, or local procedures. Do not rely on it to file, pay, amend, or take a tax position without review by a qualified professional in the relevant jurisdiction.

## Who this applies to

**Source-baseline draft for 2026 preparation; operative 2026 coverage is not confirmed.** The consolidated Taxation Act (amendments through June 2021), the enacted FY 2024/25 Financial Act and the cited official guidance were opened. Intervening annual Financial Acts after June 2021 have not been reconciled in full. The public sources checked did not establish the complete enacted FY 2025/26 and FY 2026/27 amendment chain. A ministry publication labelled Draft Finance Bill 2025/26 is not treated as enacted law. Prepare and review the evidence below, then stop before a final 2026 tax position or submission until the later-law chain and relevant directions are resolved. The FY 2024/25 tables describe that opened enactment, not a silently rolled-forward 2026 rate. [SSRA public catalogue](https://cms.nra.gov.ss/resources); [ministry public library](https://mofp.gov.ss/wp-json/wp/v2/dlp_document?per_page=100).

Individuals with employment or entrepreneurial income. This Guide owns the opened wage bands and unaudited sole-proprietor turnover schedule. It is not a company BPT computation. Establish residence and source through the [cross-border Guide](ss-cross-border.md).

## The base

1. Classify each receipt as wages, entrepreneurial income, property/investment or another category; obtain contracts, ledgers and withholding evidence. Do not label every receipt salary. The consolidated Act includes capital gains in gross income, so the old blanket capital-gains rate is not a substitute for classification. [s.54, PDF 53](https://cms.nra.gov.ss/uploads/TAXATION_ACT_2021_R_E_CONSOLIDATED_f4f9ed8876.pdf); [s.57(1)(g), PDF 54](https://cms.nra.gov.ss/uploads/TAXATION_ACT_2021_R_E_CONSOLIDATED_f4f9ed8876.pdf)
2. Reconcile gross wages to the [payroll Guide](ss-payroll-social.md), which owns wage inclusions, approved pension deductions and employer withholding. Use taxable monthly wages, not gross pay, for the bands above. The 2012 principal-employer regulation demonstrates marginal band computation, but its old amounts must not be reused. [Regulation 1.94(1)(2), PDF 68](https://cms.nra.gov.ss/uploads/Regulations_2012_05491867ea.pdf).
3. For an entrepreneur, record audited-statement status and annual turnover before choosing the separate unaudited schedule. A NIL amount in that schedule does not waive registration, payroll or other taxes. Detailed depreciation, capital-account and deduction calculations require the full applicable rules; do not combine a presumptive turnover charge with a profit method by guesswork. [s.49 Schedule 1(A), PDF 40](https://cms.nra.gov.ss/uploads/Financial_Act_2024_2025_464925c180.pdf)
4. Reconcile tax withheld and advance payments against the correctly classified annual liability. Preserve credits separately; do not treat a withholding payment as final by default. [s.49 approved amendment 4, PDF 39–40](https://cms.nra.gov.ss/uploads/Financial_Act_2024_2025_464925c180.pdf)

## Two worked examples that recompute

The inputs are fictional evidence assumed established. The arithmetic does not establish a client fact, current-law applicability or completion of a filing.

**Monthly band computation, not a 2026 payroll approval.** Assume the law-selection gate has been resolved and supported taxable monthly wages, after separately established deductions, are SSP 100,000. Marginal slices using the opened schedule give SSP 1,000 +1,700 +4,950 +2,000 =SSP 9,650 base PIT. State surtax and any other job are excluded pending their own rules. This is not gross-to-net pay.

```oa-check
name: Opened wage schedule arithmetic
inputs:
  taxable: 100000
steps:
  band5: min(max(taxable - 20000, 0), 20000) * 0.05
  band10: min(max(taxable - 40000, 0), 17000) * 0.10
  band15: min(max(taxable - 57000, 0), 33000) * 0.15
  band20: max(taxable - 90000, 0) * 0.20
  base_pit: band5 + band10 + band15 + band20
expected:
  base_pit: 9650
```

**Unaudited turnover boundary changes the route.** Assume a sole proprietor has no audited financial statements and the opened schedule applies. At SSP 4,000,000 annual turnover the schedule amount is SSP 400,000; at SSP 4,000,001 it is SSP 800,000. The SSP 1 turnover difference selects a different fixed amount. A company cannot use this example simply because its turnover is similar.

```oa-check
name: Unaudited sole proprietor boundary
inputs:
  at_boundary: 4000000
  above_boundary: 4000001
  lower_charge: 400000
  upper_charge: 800000
steps:
  turnover_difference: above_boundary - at_boundary
  charge_difference: upper_charge - lower_charge
expected:
  turnover_difference: 1
  charge_difference: 400000
```

## Ask the client first

- Which person, receipt categories, assessment year and residence/source facts apply?
- What are gross wages, benefits, reimbursements and qualifying pension contributions?
- What are annual business receipts, allowable costs and audited financial-statement status?
- What tax was withheld or paid in advance, with whose certificate and for which period?
- Which later enacted Finance Act, amendment or administrative direction applies to the proposed filing date?
