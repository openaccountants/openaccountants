# South Sudan payroll and social-contribution source-baseline working paper v0.1

> **General reference only.** This skill is general tax/accounting reference material for AI-assisted workflows. It has not been reviewed for any specific person's facts, documents, elections, deadlines, residency, filing status, or local procedures. Do not rely on it to file, pay, amend, or take a tax position without review by a qualified professional in the relevant jurisdiction.

## Who this applies to

**Source-baseline draft for 2026 preparation; operative 2026 coverage is not confirmed.** The consolidated Taxation Act (amendments through June 2021), the enacted FY 2024/25 Financial Act and the cited official guidance were opened. Intervening annual Financial Acts after June 2021 have not been reconciled in full. The public sources checked did not establish the complete enacted FY 2025/26 and FY 2026/27 amendment chain. A ministry publication labelled Draft Finance Bill 2025/26 is not treated as enacted law. Prepare and review the evidence below, then stop before a final 2026 tax position or submission until the later-law chain and relevant directions are resolved. The FY 2024/25 tables describe that opened enactment, not a silently rolled-forward 2026 rate. [SSRA public catalogue](https://cms.nra.gov.ss/resources); [ministry public library](https://mofp.gov.ss/wp-json/wp/v2/dlp_document?per_page=100).

Employers and third parties financing payroll. This Guide owns wage inclusions, qualifying employee pension deductions, withholding deadlines and employer reconciliation. It does not certify NSIF coverage or a final net-pay calculation.

## Rates, bands and thresholds

All figures below retain the period and conditions of the opened source. Read the stop gate before using a value.

| Item and conditions | Opened statutory baseline, not certified operative 2026 law | Official source |
|---|---|---|
| Wages | Wage base includes salary, bonuses, cash/in-kind compensation, temporary work, pensions, debt forgiveness/personal expenses; s.58(2) excludes specified employer pension and actual travel/medical reimbursements. | [s.58(1)–(2), PDF 55–56](https://cms.nra.gov.ss/uploads/TAXATION_ACT_2021_R_E_CONSOLIDATED_f4f9ed8876.pdf) |
| Approved pension deduction | Opened FY 2024/25 employee deduction is actual qualifying contribution up to 5% of gross wages to approved funded pension scheme; not an automatic 5% deduction or a contribution rate. | [s.5(2), PDF 11](https://cms.nra.gov.ss/uploads/Financial_Act_2024_2025_464925c180.pdf) |
| Withhold date | Withholding statement/payment within 15 days after month-end; if withholding exceeds SSP 300,000 in a particular payroll/payment period, remit within five business days of withholding. | [s.95(1), PDF 79](https://cms.nra.gov.ss/uploads/TAXATION_ACT_2021_R_E_CONSOLIDATED_f4f9ed8876.pdf) |
| Payroll annual | Employee statement by 31 January on request; employer reconciliation within 60 days after year-end. | [s.95(3)–(4), PDF 79–80](https://cms.nra.gov.ss/uploads/TAXATION_ACT_2021_R_E_CONSOLIDATED_f4f9ed8876.pdf) |
| Withhold liability | Failure to withhold creates liability for tax, penalties and interest and denial of otherwise-allowable payment deduction. | [s.39, PDF 41](https://cms.nra.gov.ss/uploads/TAXATION_ACT_2021_R_E_CONSOLIDATED_f4f9ed8876.pdf) |

## The base

1. Reconcile each employee’s cash and in-kind items, temporary-work payments, debt forgiveness and paid personal expenses to the books. Separately evidence the s.58(2) exclusions; do not deduct every reimbursement without its statutory condition. [s.58(1)–(2), PDF 55–56](https://cms.nra.gov.ss/uploads/TAXATION_ACT_2021_R_E_CONSOLIDATED_f4f9ed8876.pdf)
2. Use the [income-tax Guide](ss-income-tax.md) for the standard expense allowance and wage bands. For qualifying employee pension payments, deduct the lesser of the actual approved-scheme contribution and the stated wage-based cap. A cap alone does not prove payment or approval. [s.5(2), PDF 11](https://cms.nra.gov.ss/uploads/Financial_Act_2024_2025_464925c180.pdf)
3. Identify the principal employer and any other job before using a wage calculation. The opened 2012 regulations contain a distinct non-principal-employer method and older figures; their current interaction with later Financial Acts was not resolved. Stop on multiple-employer facts rather than apply the principal example automatically. [Regulation 1.94, PDF 68–70](https://cms.nra.gov.ss/uploads/Regulations_2012_05491867ea.pdf).
4. Reconcile payroll withholding, remittances, employees and the annual reconciliation. If a third party directly finances payroll, inspect s.95(2); the employer is not relieved of return obligations. [s.95(1), PDF 79](https://cms.nra.gov.ss/uploads/TAXATION_ACT_2021_R_E_CONSOLIDATED_f4f9ed8876.pdf)
5. Keep social-insurance liabilities and payments in a separate evidence schedule. The old private-hosted assertions about NSIF rates are not treated as current law. Obtain the applicable enactment, coverage category, contribution base, authority and remittance instruction before calculating or releasing net pay.
6. The official Labour Ministry hosts a 2 March 2021 NRA pension/social-insurance withholding suspension circular, addressed to NGOs through RRC. Preserve its scope and check subsequent instruments before applying it to any current pension payment; it is not a general social-contribution rate law. [Circular RSS/NRA/DCG/J/19, PDF 1](https://mol.gov.ss/sites/default/files/2021-05/NRA_Suspension%20of%20Withholding%20Tax%20on%20Pension%20Fund_Social%20Insurance%20Fund.pdf)

## Deadlines and filing

The statement/remittance trigger is in the table. The consolidated Act’s five-business-day rule runs from withholding when the particular payroll/payment-period threshold is exceeded. It is not permission to wait for the following month. The historical eTax sequence is documented in the [overview](ss-tax-overview.md); the manual shows generic withholding fields and bulk templates, not a verified current payroll form. Retain the tax invoice, payment evidence and employee-level reconciliation. Historical paper Forms 95-1/95-1(a) in the regulations do not prove the current electronic route.

## Two worked examples that recompute

The inputs are fictional evidence assumed established. The arithmetic does not establish a client fact, current-law applicability or completion of a filing.

**A pension contribution exceeds the tax-deduction cap.** Assume gross qualifying wages are SSP 100,000 and an actual SSP 8,000 contribution to an approved funded scheme is documented. Under the opened FY 2024/25 deduction provision, the cap is SSP 5,000 and the non-deducted excess is SSP 3,000. The hypothetical SSP 8,000 is not a statutory contribution rate.

```oa-check
name: Approved pension deduction cap
inputs:
  gross_wages: 100000
  actual_contribution: 8000
  deduction_cap_rate: 0.05
steps:
  cap: gross_wages * deduction_cap_rate
  deductible: min(actual_contribution, cap)
  excess: actual_contribution - deductible
expected:
  cap: 5000
  deductible: 5000
  excess: 3000
```

**One SSP changes the remittance branch.** Assume withholding is SSP 300,000 in one payroll/payment period, versus SSP 300,001 in another. Only the latter exceeds the opened Act’s trigger and enters the accelerated five-business-day branch. This arithmetic does not compute a real due date or alter any earlier obligation.

```oa-check
name: Accelerated withholding threshold
inputs:
  boundary: 300000
  withheld_a: 300000
  withheld_b: 300001
steps:
  a_above: max(withheld_a - boundary, 0)
  b_above: max(withheld_b - boundary, 0)
expected:
  a_above: 0
  b_above: 1
```

## Ask the client first

- Who is the legal employer, who funds payroll and which employees have more than one employer?
- What cash, benefits, reimbursements and approved funded-pension payments are evidenced?
- What withholding amount and date arise in each payroll/payment period?
- What residence, work-location and foreign-currency facts apply?
- What social-insurance law, employee coverage, contribution base and payment instruction have been obtained?
- Which later enacted law and current return/template governs the actual payroll date?
