# South Sudan: tax pack

Eight source-baseline Guides prepare evidence and calculations for review. All seven tax domains remain partial. Neither the 2026 metadata nor passing mechanical checks establishes operative 2026 law or country completion. The opened consolidated Taxation Act defines the tax period as the calendar year. The enacted FY 2024/25 Financial Act, two official copies and its operationalisation circular were read at the cited provisions. Intervening annual Financial Acts after June 2021 have not been fully reconciled. An official publication titled Finance Bill FY 2025/2026 is a draft; an enacted FY 2025/26 and FY 2026/27 amendment chain was not established.
