# South Sudan tax source map and administration working paper v0.1

> **General reference only.** This skill is general tax/accounting reference material for AI-assisted workflows. It has not been reviewed for any specific person's facts, documents, elections, deadlines, residency, filing status, or local procedures. Do not rely on it to file, pay, amend, or take a tax position without review by a qualified professional in the relevant jurisdiction.

## Who this applies to

**Source-baseline draft for 2026 preparation; operative 2026 coverage is not confirmed.** The consolidated Taxation Act (amendments through June 2021), the enacted FY 2024/25 Financial Act and the cited official guidance were opened. Intervening annual Financial Acts after June 2021 have not been reconciled in full. The public sources checked did not establish the complete enacted FY 2025/26 and FY 2026/27 amendment chain. A ministry publication labelled Draft Finance Bill 2025/26 is not treated as enacted law. Prepare and review the evidence below, then stop before a final 2026 tax position or submission until the later-law chain and relevant directions are resolved. The FY 2024/25 tables describe that opened enactment, not a silently rolled-forward 2026 rate. [SSRA public catalogue](https://cms.nra.gov.ss/resources); [ministry public library](https://mofp.gov.ss/wp-json/wp/v2/dlp_document?per_page=100).

Use this source and administration map before individual income tax, business profit tax, payroll, sales/excise, cross-border or company work. It owns general tax-period, registration, records, currency and administration rules. The job-specific Guides own their figures; no second conflicting headline-rate table is maintained here.

## Rates, bands and thresholds

All figures below retain the period and conditions of the opened source. Read the stop gate before using a value.

## The base

1. Identify the legal person, actual activity and period. A government Financial Act fiscal-year title is not a redefinition of the consolidated Act’s calendar tax period. [s.5, PDF 19](https://cms.nra.gov.ss/uploads/TAXATION_ACT_2021_R_E_CONSOLIDATED_f4f9ed8876.pdf)
2. Route individual/entrepreneur work to [PIT](ss-income-tax.md), company profits to [BPT](ss-corporate-income-tax.md), wage withholding/social evidence to [payroll](ss-payroll-social.md), imports/production/services to [sales tax/excise](ss-vat-gst.md), foreign connections/payments to [cross-border](ss-cross-border.md) and associated transactions to [TP](ss-transfer-pricing.md). Formation is a separate [company-law job](ss-company-formation.md), not a tax filing.
3. Obtain the enacted law and amendment chain applying on the transaction date. The revenue-authority circular says the Financial Act 2024/25 was signed 25 November 2024 and operationalised 2 December 2024, continuing until repealed/replaced. That wording does not establish that no later replacement exists. [Circular, PDF 1–2](https://cms.nra.gov.ss/uploads/Operationalization_of_the_Financial_Act_2024_2025_53a1891f10.pdf).
4. Preserve the actual source, provision, read date, unresolved conflict and input documents. Never select the draft Finance Bill 2025/26’s proposed figures as law. A missing later Act is a research gap, not proof of no change.
5. Reconcile ledger amounts, currency conversions, tax calculations, return fields, invoice and remittance. Separate the filing acknowledgement from payment evidence; neither an unchecked draft nor an invoice alone proves both steps are complete.

## Deadlines and filing

The opened official eTax manual documents this sequence, subject to a fresh operational check before real filing:

1. Open https://etax.nra.gov.ss/ via the authority site. The taxpayer uses its own TIN and SMS verification. Choose the appropriate individual or business profile.
2. Select activity, currency and filing period. For the manual’s withholding illustration choose Add Withholding Tax, then type and taxable amount, Add Item. For a bulk list, download the current Excel and tax-code templates, fill and upload them.
3. Review entries, use Edit for corrections and verify the declaration summary/tax payable. Only then check the declaration and File Returns.
4. The manual generates a tax invoice, which can be downloaded for integrated-bank payment or its card option. Retain the submitted return, acknowledgement/reference, invoice and payment confirmation and reconcile all four. These are evidence recommendations; actual current screen/acknowledgement wording and bank/card availability were not tested. [PDF 3–6,8–19](https://cms.nra.gov.ss/uploads/NRA_e_Tax_Filing_Declaration_Manual_7425326530.pdf)

## Penalties and interest

The baseline penalty and appeal rules are in the table. The actual published underpayment interest rate was not obtained;120% is a multiplier of the statutory prime-commercial-rate measure, not an annual interest rate of 120%. Do not apply the late-filing charge to every information return. The later Financial Act includes amended offence provisions and a customs-value penalty; those are distinct from ordinary monthly filing/payment charges. Obtain the notice and current law before computing a real sanction. [s.36, PDF 38–39](https://cms.nra.gov.ss/uploads/TAXATION_ACT_2021_R_E_CONSOLIDATED_f4f9ed8876.pdf); [s.50, PDF 164](https://cms.nra.gov.ss/uploads/Financial_Act_2024_2025_464925c180.pdf)

## Two worked examples that recompute

The inputs are fictional evidence assumed established. The arithmetic does not establish a client fact, current-law applicability or completion of a filing.

**Late-filing cap changes the calculation.** For illustration under the opened s.38(1) only, assume reportable tax SSP 100,000, a valid whole-month count and no waiver. Three late months give SSP 15,000; seven months hit the SSP 25,000 cap. Interest, late-payment and other offences are separate and are not added by this example.

```oa-check
name: Historical late-filing cap
inputs:
  reportable_tax: 100000
  monthly_rate: 0.05
  cap_rate: 0.25
  months_a: 3
  months_b: 7
steps:
  charge_a: reportable_tax * min(monthly_rate * months_a, cap_rate)
  charge_b: reportable_tax * min(monthly_rate * months_b, cap_rate)
expected:
  charge_a: 15000
  charge_b: 25000
```

**A fiscal-year file contains two calendar buckets.** Assume a July 2025–June 2026 ledger contains SSP 60,000 from July–December 2025 and SSP 80,000 from January–June 2026. The file totals SSP 140,000 but the two amounts belong in separate calendar-year workpapers, each needing the other half of that calendar year. This is a completeness decision, not an annual return assembled from a government fiscal label.

```oa-check
name: Calendar-period completeness
inputs:
  jul_dec_2025: 60000
  jan_jun_2026: 80000
steps:
  fiscal_file_total: jul_dec_2025 + jan_jun_2026
  calendar_2025_known: jul_dec_2025
  calendar_2026_known: jan_jun_2026
expected:
  fiscal_file_total: 140000
  calendar_2025_known: 60000
  calendar_2026_known: 80000
```

## Ask the client first

- Who is the taxpayer, what activity and what calendar/transaction period are under review?
- Which enacted Act/amendments and current authority instructions apply?
- What ledgers, currency evidence and source documents support the calculation?
- Which existing TIN/profile, tax activity, return, invoice and payment references exist?
- Is there a notice, assessment, dispute or time-sensitive compliance event?
