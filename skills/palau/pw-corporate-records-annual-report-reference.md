## Intake and legal boundaries

Reuse relevant redacted records before asking grouped questions. Establish the actual legal entity and regime, original incorporation and registration evidence separately, transition/re-registration history, report year and current status, year-end, requested record category, requester capacity and purpose, notice/request delivery and effective dates, electronic permissions, current forms/instructions, fees/waivers, filing acceptance and reviewer scope. Keep personal identifiers, beneficial-owner birthdates, signatures, account credentials and bank details with the professional. No live registration, account creation, identity upload, payment or message is authorised by this guide.

L1 section 1601(a) makes the listed governance records permanent: shareholder/board minutes, board resolutions, actions without meetings, shareholder communications/notices, articles/bylaws and amendments and specified committee actions. Section 1601(b) requires appropriate accounting records sufficient for a balance sheet and profit-and-loss statement, the most recent Registrar annual report and prepared annual financial statements for the last 3 fiscal years or shorter existence. Section 1601(c) separately requires the share/member record in Palau, capable of listing names, addresses and holdings, reflecting transactions during the preceding 5 years. These are different obligations. Neither 3 nor 5 years is a universal retention period or permission to destroy other records. Current directors/officers, beneficial ownership and reasonably inspectable paper-convertible form are separately required by sections 1601(d)-(g).

L1 section 1603 gives a qualifying shareholder of a domestic for profit corporation a conditional inspection/copying route. The signed written notice must be given at least 5 days before the requested date; good faith, proper purpose, reasonable particularity and direct connection are all required. Board meeting minutes and records of board actions without meetings are excluded from this route; do not exclude every board document or decide all other rights. The section permits reasonable confidentiality/use/distribution restrictions and copying charges; articles/bylaws cannot abolish or limit its right. Agent/attorney and specified beneficial-owner rights remain. Section 1604 is a separate director route at reasonable times to the extent reasonably related to director duties, with its own limits and court protections. It does not inherit the shareholder's 5-day procedure.

L1 section 1605(a) requires annual balance sheet, income statement and changes in equity unless that information appears elsewhere, with appropriate consolidated/combined presentation allowed. GAAP applies on the stated condition that the corporation's statements are prepared on that basis; this is not a blanket GAAP or audit mandate. Section 1605(b) requires availability to shareholders within 120 days after fiscal year-end. Thereafter, a written request engages the 5-business-day sending provision measured from delivery. Electronic delivery requires articles/bylaws permission or shareholder agreement. Section 1605(c)'s reasonable determination of lack of good faith or proper purpose remains relevant. Do not replace 5 business days with 5 days, omit the sequence or promise every request must be fulfilled. The nonprofit section 1606 is a different branch and remains outside this illustration. Preparing actual financial statements requires the relevant complete financial-statements-workflow-base and country content; this guide does not prepare them.

L1 section 1607(a) requires a prescribed annual report with name/registration number, local registered office/agent, principal office, confirmation of shareholder/director/principal-officer names and business addresses then on the register, business description, for-profit authorized/issued/outstanding shares by class/series and any other prescribed information. Subsection (b) separates incorporation-year exclusion, registration-anniversary delivery month and the report's own in-month information date. Subsection (c) treats a corrected information-deficient report as timely only if corrected and delivered within 30 days after the notice's effective date. This is not a general extension or an automatic cure for late submission. Notice effectiveness requires section 110's relevant method and facts, not merely when someone read an email. Exact day counts, holidays and actual relief remain held.

L1 sections 103, 105, 107 and 109 and L4 Part III require the prescribed format, appropriate signature, applicable fee and Registrar acceptance. A submission is deemed unsubmitted unless accepted; an upload, payment or draft calendar does not establish filing. CR01's default one-month period applies only if no period is prescribed, so it cannot replace section 1607(b). Changes, corrections, beneficial ownership requests, foreign and re-registration processes have separate requirements. L1 sections 1701-1708 do not permit treating re-registration as creation of a new entity or silently resetting its anniversary; all transition cases remain held.

## Conditional reference mechanics

1. **Establish both dates and the fictional branch.** I and R must each be expressly established complete Gregorian dates, expressly equal for this illustration, in 2025 or 2026. Y must expressly be 2026. Missing, conflicting, malformed or unsupported dates stay held. These are fictional premises only; they cannot establish actual applicable law or entity status.
2. **Apply the incorporation-year exclusion separately.** If the fictional I is in 2026, the reference is no section 1607 annual report in calendar 2026. This is not exemption from records, statements, tax, payroll, licensing, beneficial ownership, change notices or all compliance. R does not determine this exclusion. [L1 section 1607(b); L2 section 27.]
3. **Return an anniversary month only.** If fictional I is in 2025 and Y is 2026, return 2026 with R's month. Preserve I and R separately even though these examples expressly make them equal. Do not return an anniversary day, year-end deadline, 120-day annual-report deadline, holiday-adjusted deadline or filing acceptance. The report must separately be dated within its delivery month with information compiled as of that date. [L1 section 1607(b); L2 section 27.]
4. **Preserve categories and request conditions.** Apply only the specific retained-source distinctions for governance records, accounting records, prepared statements, share transactions, shareholder/director capacity, request purpose and delivery sequence. A category label or elapsed period does not clear actual access, destruction, statement preparation or filing. [L1 sections 1601-1606.]
5. **Stop before actual-company output.** Keep actual law/commencement, classification, date evidence, exact day counting, form, fee, submission/acceptance, professional review and complete engagement outputs held. No reference month, zero, website acknowledgement or user confirmation waives those gates. The external fixture checks this bounded illustration only; live workflow and refusal enforcement remain untested.
