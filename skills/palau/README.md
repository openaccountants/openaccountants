# Palau (PW): tax pack

**Built from the law. Not yet approved. Written by Ryan Duguid. Not reviewed by an accountant.** The Republic of Palau uses the US dollar. Taxes are administered by the Bureau of Revenue and Taxation (BRT) in the Ministry of Finance. Since 1 January 2023 Palau has a 10% goods and services tax (PGST) and a 12% Business Profits Tax for PGST-registered businesses. Corporations are registered with the Registrar under Title 12 of the Palau National Code. Tax year 2026.

## What is not covered yet

- Wages and salary tax, Business Profits Tax, social security and the Healthcare Fund, and import and excise taxes: no skill yet.
- PGST: registration, zero-rated and exempt classification, input credits, returns and payment. Two Senate bills introduced on 14 September 2026 (SB 12-83, a 5% rate, and SB 12-82, food exemption) were still in progress on 14 November 2026.
- Corporations: foreign, nonprofit and Division 3 entities, re-registration, and beneficial ownership filings.
