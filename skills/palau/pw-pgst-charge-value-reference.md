### Scope and period

The subjects are the selected PGST charging rule in section 1211, value-of-supply rules in section 1235 and deemed output on first application of goods to private or exempt use in section 1236. Their complete text was read from ministry-hosted page images. Definitions, registration, taxable and exempt categories, place and time of supply, imported services, input-credit entitlement, returns, adjustments and administration require further complete content. A rate or value formula does not resolve those dependencies. [S1]

RPPL 11-11 section 21(a) states an effective date of **1 January 2023**, except where the Act explicitly provides otherwise. Section 21(b) directs implementation regulations before 1 January 2022; that deadline does not establish when a particular regulation was made or came into force. Section 21(c) sets a transitional registration period commencing 3 calendar months before the effective date and ending the day before it. It addresses applications for persons meeting section 1221, registration taking effect at commencement, specified purported earlier applications/registrations/certificates and registration by the Director where required persons did not apply. This historical transition is not a current registration test, threshold or deadline. [S1, printed pp 105–106]

The last page records passage on 29 September 2021 and a visible approval annotation dated 29 September 2021. These observations identify the published electronic copy; they are not signature authentication or proof of an exhaustive amendment chain. The gallery has 107 ordinals, whilst the Act's printed pages run to 106. Gallery page 11 is printed page 10; do not interchange these locators. No original PDF byte hash or complete-PDF retrieval is claimed. [S1]

### Selected charging and value rules [S1](https://ministryofhrctd.pw/regulations/RPPL%20No.%2011-11/)

| Dated statutory unit | What it states | Condition retained |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **1211(a)–(b)** | A registered person is assessed and must pay PGST on the value of taxable supplies made by that person and imported services supplied to that person. The stated rates are **0% for a taxable supply that is zero-rated** and **10% for any other taxable supply**. | Establish the person, registration, supply classification, applicable period and current rate first. 'Other taxable' does not mean every transaction. Zero-rated and exempt are distinct classifications.  [S1](https://ministryofhrctd.pw/regulations/RPPL%20No.%2011-11/) |
| **1211(c)** | Liability on the registered person's taxable supply arises at the time of supply, is accounted for under section 1282 and is recoverable from the recipient as specified. | This does not establish the complete time-of-supply rule, a return/payment deadline or how a particular contract is invoiced.  [S1](https://ministryofhrctd.pw/regulations/RPPL%20No.%2011-11/) |
| **1211(d)** | Liability on imported services supplied to a registered person arises at the time of supply and is accounted for by the recipient under section 1282. | Imported-services definitions, place rules, the complete section 1237 value/invoice rule and administration remain held. Do not apply the ordinary supply example to imported services by analogy.  [S1](https://ministryofhrctd.pw/regulations/RPPL%20No.%2011-11/) |
| **1235(a)–(c)** | Subject to the chapter, value is the price. The associate/below-market rule applies only with both 1235(b) conditions. Except as provided in the chapter, value is zero where no price is charged. | Complete definitions and chapter overrides are not qualified here. A gift, associate transaction or zero price is not automatically exempt, zero-rated or a nil actual liability.  [S1](https://ministryofhrctd.pw/regulations/RPPL%20No.%2011-11/) |
| **1235(d)–(f)** | Where a registered person makes a taxable supply without separately identifying PGST, value is `A − (A × B)`. `A` is the total charged; `B = r/(1+r)`, with `r` the applicable section 1211 rate. Subsection (e) addresses the input tax payable by a registered recipient by reference to that value. | Use a decimal rate: 10% is `0.10`. The rule's prerequisites and current rate must be established. Subsection (e) does not itself grant an input credit, deduction, refund or net liability.  [S1](https://ministryofhrctd.pw/regulations/RPPL%20No.%2011-11/) |
| **1236(a)–(b)** | For a registered person, application of goods to private or exempt use is deemed taxable only if input credit was allowed on their acquisition or import. At first application, deemed output equals the input credit allowed for those goods. | Establish the goods, actual credit allowed, qualifying application and timing. This is not a new fair-market-value-times-rate computation. Other adjustments and net return treatment require complete content.  [S1](https://ministryofhrctd.pw/regulations/RPPL%20No.%2011-11/) |

Section 1235(b)(1) concerns a registered person's taxable supply to an associate below fair market value, including zero price. Its second condition is literally **'the recipient is not entitled to an input tax credit for the whole or part of the input tax payable in respect of the supply.'** Preserve both conditions. This reference does not resolve the effect of partial entitlement or replace that wording with 'any related party' or a general 'not fully creditable' rule. Fair market value is determined at the time of supply when the provision applies. [S1, printed p 22]

A 10% tax fraction is `0.10 / 1.10 = 1/11`. The total charged, statutory value, tax component, input tax payable, credit actually allowed and net return position are separate fields. Do not use 10% of an inclusive total as the included component, or treat the component as the amount to remit. Statutory precision, rounding unit, currency conversion and any adjustment are not established here. [S1, section 1235]

## Worked cases

### Hypothetical component positions

All inputs below are fabricated and anonymous. Assume the dated S1 provisions apply, the stated person and classification are legally established and all stated section conditions hold. These assumptions are for arithmetic illustration only. They authorise no factual or legal default for an actual engagement. Amounts are illustrative units; no current currency, rounding convention or whole-return liability is selected.

| Position | Assumed facts and calculation | Component and limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **A. Separately established value** | A registered person makes a taxable supply falling in the 10% branch. Legally established statutory value is 1,000, with tax separately identified. `1,000 × 0.10 = 100`. | Output component **100**. This does not establish an actual invoice price, payable balance or input credit. |
| **B. Total without separate PGST** | Same established 10% branch; total charged `A = 1,100`, no separate PGST. `B = 0.10/1.10 = 1/11`; `A × B = 100`; `value = 1,100 − 100 = 1,000`. | Included component **100**, value **1,000**. Cross-check `1,000 + 100 = 1,100`. No 10%-of-total shortcut or currency rounding is applied. |
| **C. Established zero-rated supply** | A registered person's supply has independently established zero-rated status; total 2,200, no separate PGST. `B = 0/(1+0) = 0`; `value = 2,200 − (2,200 × 0) = 2,200`; `2,200 × 0 = 0`. | Explicit nil component **0**. This does not establish any real zero-rated category, exemption or credit/refund entitlement. Retain the nil row. |
| **D. Both associate-rule conditions met** | Assume a qualifying associate supply in the 10% branch, charged price 200 below independently established fair market value 1,000, and recipient with no input-credit entitlement. Both 1235(b) conditions are expressly assumed. Value 1,000; `1,000 × 0.10 = 100`. | Output component **100**. The actual associate status, market value and entitlement condition remain subject to evidence; partial entitlement is not decided. |
| **E. First private/exempt application** | A registered person's identified goods first enter qualifying private or exempt use; input credit actually allowed on their acquisition was 75. Under 1236(b), deemed output equals that allowed credit: **75**. | Deemed output component **75**. No market value, new percentage charge, second application, credit availability or net return amount is inferred. |

Reconcile all 5 synthetic positions separately. They are alternative illustrations, not a single tax period or a schedule to sum and remit. An actual zero-price or partially creditable associate transaction remains held under the source and scope gates.

### Recompute the retained hypothetical positions

The following blocks recompute 2 of the positions above using exactly their supplied premises. They are separate arithmetic exercises. The original classification, period, source, currency, precision and operational holds remain applicable. A nil or negative arithmetic component does not resolve an unknown liability or establish an exemption, refund or amount to pay.

```oa-check

inputs:
  value: 1000
  rate: 0.1
steps:
  - component: value * rate

  component: 100
tolerance: 0.000001
```

```oa-check

  total: 1100
  rate: 0.1

  - component: total * rate / (1 + rate)
  - value: total - component
  - reconciled: value + component

  component: 100
  value: 1000
  reconciled: 1100
```
