# Bhutan Corporate and Business Income Tax (2026) | v2.0

For income year 2026, companies use the new Act’s company rate; an unincorporated individual business uses the individual or qualifying presumptive rules. The former flat BIT label does not determine the new individual liability. [ss.2-5](https://www.drc.gov.bt/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Income-Tax-Act-of-Bhutan-2025.pdf); [Schedule 1 paragraphs 2-6](https://www.drc.gov.bt/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Income-Tax-Act-of-Bhutan-2025.pdf); [Schedule 2](https://www.drc.gov.bt/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Income-Tax-Act-of-Bhutan-2025.pdf)

## Who this applies to

A company or body of persons is resident if formed or registered under Bhutan law, has its principal office in Bhutan, or has management and control exercised in Bhutan at any time in the year. Resident business income is worldwide; non-resident business income must have a Bhutan source. Distinguish a company, individual, partnership, trust and body of persons before choosing a rate. [ss.226-232](https://www.drc.gov.bt/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Income-Tax-Act-of-Bhutan-2025.pdf); [ss.16-20](https://www.drc.gov.bt/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Income-Tax-Act-of-Bhutan-2025.pdf); [Schedule 1 paragraphs 2-6](https://www.drc.gov.bt/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Income-Tax-Act-of-Bhutan-2025.pdf)

## The base

Start with business gains and profits, trading stock, business-asset realizations, revenue subsidies and business-connected investment receipts. Exclude amounts classified as employment, exempt or final-withholding income and the other s.28 exclusions. Deduct only qualifying wholly and exclusively incurred business expenditure with documentation; accounting profit alone is not taxable income. Capital costs and benefits lasting beyond 12 months do not become ordinary deductions merely because paid. Use the Act’s depreciation and stock rules. [ss.25-28,39-61](https://www.drc.gov.bt/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Income-Tax-Act-of-Bhutan-2025.pdf); [ss.41-43](https://www.drc.gov.bt/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Income-Tax-Act-of-Bhutan-2025.pdf)

Interest requires the qualifying borrowing/payer conditions in ss.45-50. The general deduction is limited to 30% of EBITDA (in words on the page), with the statutory financial-institution exceptions; disallowed interest can be carried forward within the later-year limit. Do not treat the limit as permission to deduct private, non-qualifying or undocumented borrowing costs. [ss.45-50](https://www.drc.gov.bt/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Income-Tax-Act-of-Bhutan-2025.pdf)

Unrelieved business losses can be carried forward without a fixed expiry under ss.58-61, but remain subject to the business, reduced/exempt-income and foreign-loss restrictions. Foreign business losses offset foreign business income only; business losses do not offset employment, investment or other income. A change of underlying entity ownership by more than 50% compared with any time in the previous 3 years blocks pre-change losses and carried financial costs (quantities in words on the page). Bankruptcy has separate loss restrictions. Retain loss evidence until fully offset, in addition to the normal record period. [ss.58-61,204-207,239-240](https://www.drc.gov.bt/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Income-Tax-Act-of-Bhutan-2025.pdf); [ss.324-326](https://www.drc.gov.bt/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Income-Tax-Act-of-Bhutan-2025.pdf)

Presumptive taxation excludes partnerships, individuals with the professional qualifications described in Rule 152 and other prescribed businesses. Crossing the threshold leads to ordinary assessment for the whole year, credit for presumptive tax already paid, and no later return to presumptive taxation after an ordinary assessment. DRC’s dated Public Ruling 01/2026 interprets other salary or investment income as not, by itself, disqualifying an otherwise eligible business and aggregates multiple businesses for the turnover test; the other statutory conditions still apply. [Schedule 2](https://www.drc.gov.bt/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Income-Tax-Act-of-Bhutan-2025.pdf); [rr.151-152](https://www.drc.gov.bt/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Rules-on-the-Income-Tax-Act-of-Bhutan-2025.pdf); [Public Ruling 01/2026,entire ruling](https://www.drc.gov.bt/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Rulings-on-Presumptive-Tax.pdf)

For withholding, first identify the payer and recipient, income head, Bhutan source, payment type and exemptions. Rule 158 includes a payment-below-Nu.20,000 exception, except interest/dividends, with anti-splitting under Rule 159; valid exemption certificates, Government payees and specified compensation, authorized-dealer goods, air/rail tickets, in-kind consideration and utilities have distinct conditions. Exclude GST from the withholding base. An exception from withholding does not itself exempt the recipient’s income. [ss.266-284;Schedule 1 paragraph 7](https://www.drc.gov.bt/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Income-Tax-Act-of-Bhutan-2025.pdf); [rr.154-164](https://www.drc.gov.bt/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Rules-on-the-Income-Tax-Act-of-Bhutan-2025.pdf)

Do not retain the old blanket fixed-deposit rate. Individual investment interest/dividends use [the personal skill](bt-income-tax.md); intercorporate dividends are exempt under Schedule 3 and Rule 164. Business-connected investment amounts are classified under s.27(8) and Rule 154 and are not final under Rule 163. For non-individual interest, Rule 154’s “person” wording and Schedule 1’s individual-specific branch require a documented legal reconciliation before certifying a rate; no unconditional non-individual interest rate is provided here. The old bonus-share exclusion is not automatically imported into the new Act. [Schedule 3 paragraphs 5,7-9,12,14](https://www.drc.gov.bt/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Income-Tax-Act-of-Bhutan-2025.pdf); [ss.266-284;Schedule 1 paragraph 7](https://www.drc.gov.bt/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Income-Tax-Act-of-Bhutan-2025.pdf); [rr.154-164](https://www.drc.gov.bt/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Rules-on-the-Income-Tax-Act-of-Bhutan-2025.pdf)

Historical 2025-2026 filing cycle only: the old Act’s PartI s.45 distinguished non-state companies at 25% from state enterprises at 30%; PartII s.12 used 30% for full-liability businesses (rates in words on the page). The old skill’s universal company rate was wrong. Its non-resident contractor/consultant rate of 3% of contract value belonged to the former PartI s.45.2 (3% is in words on the page); current withholding is governed by the new branches above. Historical loss periods and transaction-specific treatment require the applicable year’s complete rules. [PartI 45;PartII 12](https://www.drc.gov.bt/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/Income-Tax-Act-of-the-Kingdom-of-Bhutan-2001_Fourth-Edition.pdf); [ss.2-5](https://www.drc.gov.bt/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Income-Tax-Act-of-Bhutan-2025.pdf); [ss.266-284;Schedule 1 paragraph 7](https://www.drc.gov.bt/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Income-Tax-Act-of-Bhutan-2025.pdf)

## Deadlines and filing

Companies and non-presumptive business taxpayers must prepare semiannual accounts and prepay by the last day of August. Use the net profit shown in the half-year accounts; where those accounts are unavailable use 50% of the previous positive annual net profit shown in the financial accounts (50% is in words on the page), or the DRC reasonable-amount branch where applicable. The July notice confirms 31 August 2026. Credit the prepayment in the annual computation. [ss.285-289](https://www.drc.gov.bt/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Income-Tax-Act-of-Bhutan-2025.pdf); [whole notification](https://www.drc.gov.bt/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/Notification-on-Prepayment-of-Tax-for-the-IY-2026.pdf)

Annual returns are due by the last day of April following the year, with assessment tax payable on that date; 30 April 2027 is derived for calendar-year 2026. Declare inactivity under s.291 rather than assuming no business means no compliance. Withholding statements and deposits are separately due within 10 days after each month. For historical 2025 BIT/CIT, the DRC window ended 31 March 2026. [ss.263,290-295](https://www.drc.gov.bt/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Income-Tax-Act-of-Bhutan-2025.pdf); [ss.273-279](https://www.drc.gov.bt/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Income-Tax-Act-of-Bhutan-2025.pdf); [paragraphs 1-2](https://www.drc.gov.bt/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Tax-Filing-Notification_IY-2025_final.pdf)

Apply for a TPN unless already issued or within a statutory exception; s.299 generally requires application within 30 days of first becoming subject to tax, subject to further permitted time. The July notice requires non-resident PE/SEP taxpayers to register separately for income tax in RAMIS rather than reuse the GST BITS number. [ss.296-300](https://www.drc.gov.bt/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Income-Tax-Act-of-Bhutan-2025.pdf); [whole notification](https://www.drc.gov.bt/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/Notification-on-Prepayment-of-Tax-for-the-IY-2026.pdf)

For the current prepayment route, the July notice names RAMIS “Return and Assessment” then “Half-Yearly Return Filing” for a PE; do not apply the PE screen to the SEP branch. Prepare accounts, tax adjustments, losses and credits. AnnexureIV’s company full-return method begins with net profit before tax, adds other taxable income/disallowances, adjusts interest/depreciation and brought-forward losses, then computes income tax and subtracts prepayment/withholding. Keep the company financial summary and supporting schedules. Obtain the actual submission acknowledgement and reconcile the payment to the filed period. [whole notification](https://www.drc.gov.bt/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/Notification-on-Prepayment-of-Tax-for-the-IY-2026.pdf); [AnnexureIV,individualPartB/companyPartsB-C](https://www.drc.gov.bt/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Rules-on-the-Income-Tax-Act-of-Bhutan-2025.pdf)

The income module moves from RAMIS to BITS from 1 January 2027 under the November notice. Preserve old/new TPN correspondence; the future annual-return screens and bank/reference workflow have not been read live. Do not copy 2025 BETA or RAMIS fields into a claimed 2027 how-to. [both image pages](https://www.drc.gov.bt/transition-to-the-bhutan-integrated-taxation-system-income-tax-module/); [paragraphs 1-2](https://www.drc.gov.bt/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Tax-Filing-Notification_IY-2025_final.pdf)

Keep Dzongkha or English records for 6 years after the relevant period; amended-assessment, unrelieved-loss and audit/court rules can require longer retention. [ss.324-326](https://www.drc.gov.bt/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Income-Tax-Act-of-Bhutan-2025.pdf)

## Penalties and interest

Late filing is Nu.300 per day for a company and Nu.100 per day for another person under s.664, with the extension and no-duplicate-notice rules in ss.664-665. Unpaid tax bears 15% per annum simple interest (in words on the page), capped at the underlying liability. A withholding agent remains liable for tax it should have withheld; its late interest is borne personally and cannot be recovered from the taxpayer. [ss.442-450,664-666](https://www.drc.gov.bt/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Income-Tax-Act-of-Bhutan-2025.pdf); [ss.273-279](https://www.drc.gov.bt/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Income-Tax-Act-of-Bhutan-2025.pdf)

## Cross-border points

Separate resident worldwide business income, non-resident Bhutan-source income, PE, SEP and Bhutan-asset income. The non-resident final-withholding rule excludes income derived through those PE/SEP/asset branches. Royalties and other investment payments require s.266 classification, statutory exemptions and any properly established treaty entitlement. The old reference to Bhutan-India treaty rates does not supply a current treaty article or reduced rate; hold that result until the operative treaty and claimant facts are established. [ss.16-20](https://www.drc.gov.bt/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Income-Tax-Act-of-Bhutan-2025.pdf); [ss.266-284;Schedule 1 paragraph 7](https://www.drc.gov.bt/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Income-Tax-Act-of-Bhutan-2025.pdf); [Schedule 1 paragraphs 2-6](https://www.drc.gov.bt/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Income-Tax-Act-of-Bhutan-2025.pdf)

## Two worked examples that recompute

A resident ordinary company has Nu.2,000,000 taxable income after all adjustments, Nu.200,000 prepayment and Nu.40,000 creditable non-final withholding. Tax is Nu.440,000 and balance Nu.200,000, derived. No exemption, ownership change, SEP or final-income item is assumed. [Schedule 1 paragraphs 2-6](https://www.drc.gov.bt/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Income-Tax-Act-of-Bhutan-2025.pdf); [ss.285-289](https://www.drc.gov.bt/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Income-Tax-Act-of-Bhutan-2025.pdf); [ss.266-284;Schedule 1 paragraph 7](https://www.drc.gov.bt/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Income-Tax-Act-of-Bhutan-2025.pdf)

```oa-check
name: Company annual tax and credits
inputs:
  taxable_income: 2000000
  prepayment: 200000
  creditable_withholding: 40000
steps:
  tax: taxable_income * 0.22
  balance: tax - prepayment - creditable_withholding
expected:
  tax: 440000
  balance: 200000
```

A first-time resident individual retailer has Nu.4,000,000 Bhutan-source business income and Nu.800,000 ordinary taxable profit, is not GST registered and meets every presumptive condition and exclusion test. Presumptive taxable business income is Nu.600,000 and tax Nu.20,000. Ordinary assessment on the stated profit would be Nu.42,500, a derived Nu.22,500 difference. This is a comparison of conditional outcomes, not an unrestricted annual election: previous ordinary assessment prevents presumptive use. [Schedule 2](https://www.drc.gov.bt/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Income-Tax-Act-of-Bhutan-2025.pdf); [rr.151-152](https://www.drc.gov.bt/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Rules-on-the-Income-Tax-Act-of-Bhutan-2025.pdf); [Public Ruling 01/2026,entire ruling](https://www.drc.gov.bt/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Rulings-on-Presumptive-Tax.pdf); [Schedule 1 paragraph 1](https://www.drc.gov.bt/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Income-Tax-Act-of-Bhutan-2025.pdf)

```oa-check
name: Presumptive eligibility compared with ordinary assessment
inputs:
  business_income: 4000000
  ordinary_taxable_profit: 800000
steps:
  presumptive_taxable: business_income * 0.15
  presumptive_tax: 10000 + (presumptive_taxable - 500000) * 0.10
  ordinary_tax: 35000 + (ordinary_taxable_profit - 750000) * 0.15
  difference: ordinary_tax - presumptive_tax
expected:
  presumptive_taxable: 600000
  presumptive_tax: 20000
  ordinary_tax: 42500
  difference: 22500
```

## Ask the client first

- Entity class, formation/control, residence and income year?
- Bhutan/foreign receipts, PE/SEP and each payment’s business connection?
- Accounts, adjustments, stock, assets, borrowing and proof of payment?
- Prior ordinary assessment, all businesses, professional qualifications and GST status?
- Loss history, ownership changes, bankruptcy and exemption periods?
- TPN, withholding certificates, prepayments, notices and filing acknowledgements?
