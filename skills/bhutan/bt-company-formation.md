# Bhutan Company Formation and Entity Choice (2026) | v2.1

Use Corporate Regulatory Authority’s current company-registration route and the Companies Act 2016, then obtain the separate licences, FDI approvals and tax registration that the activity needs. A company certificate alone does not authorize every proposed business. [ss.21-37](https://www.cra.gov.bt/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/The-Companies-Act-of-Bhutan-2016.pdf); [whole operative registration page](https://www.cra.gov.bt/guide-item/to-register-domestic-company/); [rr.1-16,58-85,108,138(22);SchedulesI-III](https://www.moice.gov.bt/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/FDI-Rules-Regulations-2025.docx.pdf)

## Scope

This skill covers entity choice and ordinary Bhutan company incorporation, plus the approval gates for foreign investment. All companies under the Act are limited by shares; shareholder liability is the amount unpaid on the shares, not an unlimited guarantee of the company’s debts. Sole proprietorships and partnerships are distinct unincorporated arrangements; do not describe their trade licence as a company certificate or assume the former BIT rate is their current individual tax. [ss.4-14](https://www.cra.gov.bt/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/The-Companies-Act-of-Bhutan-2016.pdf); [ss.21-37](https://www.cra.gov.bt/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/The-Companies-Act-of-Bhutan-2016.pdf); [Schedule 1 paragraphs 2-6](https://www.drc.gov.bt/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Income-Tax-Act-of-Bhutan-2025.pdf)

## Ask the client first

- Proposed business and location, existing licence or new activity?
- Owners, citizenship, foreign individual/institutional investment and beneficial interests?
- Private/public choice, public fundraising plans and available directors/CEO?
- Project cost, paid-up/authorized capital, asset contributions and funding?
- Existing loans, tax clearance, accounts and conversion obligations?
- Filing agent, licences, FDI certificate/clearances and tax registration status?

## The method, step by step

Choose the legal form before the tax comparison. A private company cannot offer shares to the public and must have at least 2 directors; a public company may offer securities under the Act’s issuance rules and needs at least 3 directors and a company secretary (director quantities in words on the page). Public capital requirements are prescribed, not a figure to invent. The Act permits a one-person company with a consenting nominee, but Corporate Regulatory Authority says OPC regulations/service are not yet adopted: do not promise a live single-person incorporation. [ss.4-14](https://www.cra.gov.bt/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/The-Companies-Act-of-Bhutan-2016.pdf); [ss.10-12](https://www.cra.gov.bt/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/The-Companies-Act-of-Bhutan-2016.pdf); [whole operative paragraph](https://www.cra.gov.bt/guide-item/to-register-one-person-company-opc/)

Corporate Regulatory Authority’s ordinary domestic route states 2-49 shareholders for a private company and at least 2 for a public company. Read that operational requirement with the statutory OPC provision and pending OPC service, rather than asserting that the Act universally requires 2 members. Corporate Regulatory Authority also requires a CEO who is not CEO of another company. Use a certified filing agent/company secretary for the application route. [whole operative registration page](https://www.cra.gov.bt/guide-item/to-register-domestic-company/); [whole operative paragraph](https://www.cra.gov.bt/guide-item/to-register-one-person-company-opc/); [ss.4-14](https://www.cra.gov.bt/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/The-Companies-Act-of-Bhutan-2016.pdf)

For foreign capital, classify the activity under the FDI Rules 2025 before committing the project. Check the negative list, the priority schedules and any special Economic Development Board terms. Ordinary “Other Activities” use minimum project cost Nu.50,000,000 for manufacturing or Nu.25,000,000 for services and maximum foreign equity 74%; project cost means total capital cost excluding working capital under r.138(22). Manufacturing must meet at least 30% value addition, with positive foreign-reserve impact and quality jobs for these activities. Foreign investors generally need at least 20% equity, or 10% for a foreign institutional investor. Submit a separate application for each business activity; apply the project-cost minimum and foreign-equity maximum separately for the same activity in different locations. These are project/classification rules, not a universal minimum share capital for all private companies. [rr.1-16,58-85,108,138(22);SchedulesI-III](https://www.moice.gov.bt/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/FDI-Rules-Regulations-2025.docx.pdf)

Apply online for an FDI Registration Certificate with investor identity/company authorization, local-partner details and the applicable land/existing-business valuation evidence. Registration is not final project approval. Obtain the certificate before incorporation, then the required business plan, sectoral clearances and project approval; obtain the online business licence within 1 month of approval and renew its 1-year validity (periods in words on the page). The FDI Regulations/Policy 2019 were repealed by the 2025 Rules, so do not use their old entry conditions automatically. [rr.1-16,58-85,108,138(22);SchedulesI-III](https://www.moice.gov.bt/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/FDI-Rules-Regulations-2025.docx.pdf)

FDI must be made in convertible currency; Indian investors may also use INR. Investors must fully subscribe the equity and inject total equity into company accounts by inward remittance, or foreign-currency-account transfer for downstream projects, by the earlier of 3 years from project approval (in words on the page) and commencement of project operation. Notify the Royal Monetary Authority and the Department within 30 working days of receiving foreign equity in the company’s foreign-currency account. Registration or project approval does not itself complete these funding and notification obligations. [rr.1-16,58-85,108,138(22);SchedulesI-III](https://www.moice.gov.bt/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/FDI-Rules-Regulations-2025.docx.pdf)

For the domestic route, the filing agent opens Corporate Regulatory Authority → E-Services → Company Registration and the name-approval application. Enter the proposed name without the company suffix, use the agent’s valid security clearance and CFA number, and save the applicant/company details. Choose “New Business Activity (For Bhutanese)” or “Existing Business” correctly. Upload authorization; conversion of an existing licensed proprietorship/partnership also requires the stated financial-institution no-objection, tax clearance and licences. Retain the name-approval email. [whole operative registration page](https://www.cra.gov.bt/guide-item/to-register-domestic-company/)

Use the approved-name email link to establish company credentials, then Corporate Regulatory Authority E-Services → “Company Incorporation”. Supply directors/CEO identification, contacts, photograph, signature and address, witness details, and the applicable existing-business accounts/assets/bank/licences. The Act requires subscriber application, director consent and non-model Articles where used. Do not add a separate memorandum requirement from the old draft without checking the actual form. Submit and retain the approval, Certificate and Articles of Incorporation. [whole operative registration page](https://www.cra.gov.bt/guide-item/to-register-domestic-company/); [ss.21-37](https://www.cra.gov.bt/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/The-Companies-Act-of-Bhutan-2016.pdf)

For Corporate Regulatory Authority’s FDI route, choose the individual or institutional application type. Its incorporation form includes “Share Capital” for paid-up/authorized capital and “Subscribers to Article of Incorporation” for shareholder identity and percentage. Upload the FDI certificate, notarized passports/authorization and the applicable investor-company and joint-venture documents. Reconcile the percentages to the approved project and contributed capital. [Share Capital/Subscribers tables and documents](https://www.cra.gov.bt/guide-item/to-setup-foreign-direct-investment-company/); [rr.1-16,58-85,108,138(22);SchedulesI-III](https://www.moice.gov.bt/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/FDI-Rules-Regulations-2025.docx.pdf)

Pay only the fee actually requested for the chosen route and retain the receipt. The current Corporate Regulatory Authority page lists a name fee and alternative registration fees without fully resolving every route’s allocation; this skill does not certify a single universal incorporation total. Obtain the certificate/Articles and separately establish trade/industrial licensing and DRC registration before treating the business as ready to operate. [whole operative registration page](https://www.cra.gov.bt/guide-item/to-register-domestic-company/); [ss.21-37](https://www.cra.gov.bt/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/The-Companies-Act-of-Bhutan-2016.pdf)

## Worked cases

A shareholder subscribes Nu.100,000 of shares and pays Nu.75,000. Subject to the share terms and ordinary limited-liability rule, the unpaid subscription is Nu.25,000, derived. Fully paying the subscription reduces that unpaid-share exposure to nil; this does not remove a separately signed personal guarantee, wrongdoing liability or another legal obligation. [ss.4-14](https://www.cra.gov.bt/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/The-Companies-Act-of-Bhutan-2016.pdf)

```oa-check
name: Limited liability tracks unpaid shares
inputs:
  subscribed: 100000
  paid: 75000
steps:
  unpaid: subscribed - paid
  fully_paid_unpaid: subscribed - subscribed
expected:
  unpaid: 25000
  fully_paid_unpaid: 0
```

A proposed service business is classified as an ordinary “Other Activity”, is outside the negative/priority lists, satisfies the foreign-reserve/job conditions and has no special Board variation. Its project cost and fully equity-funded capital are Nu.30,000,000. A foreign individual asks for 80% (Nu.24,000,000); the ordinary 74% ceiling gives Nu.22,200,000 foreign and Nu.7,800,000 local equity. The proposed structure exceeds the ceiling by Nu.1,800,000. Restructure or obtain a supported different approval before filing; passing the numerical project-cost test is not project approval. All amounts are derived. [rr.1-16,58-85,108,138(22);SchedulesI-III](https://www.moice.gov.bt/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/FDI-Rules-Regulations-2025.docx.pdf)

```oa-check
name: FDI ordinary service equity ceiling
inputs:
  project_equity: 30000000
  proposed_foreign_share: 0.80
steps:
  proposed_foreign: project_equity * proposed_foreign_share
  maximum_foreign: project_equity * 0.74
  required_local: project_equity - maximum_foreign
  excess_foreign: proposed_foreign - maximum_foreign
  project_cost_margin: project_equity - 25000000
expected:
  proposed_foreign: 24000000
  maximum_foreign: 22200000
  required_local: 7800000
  excess_foreign: 1800000
  project_cost_margin: 5000000
```

## When to refuse or refer

Stop if the activity or foreign ownership is unclassified, the current OPC route is assumed available, capital/fee requirements are unsupported, or licences/clearances are missing. Refer public securities offers, special-zone regimes, disputed ownership, regulated sectors and guarantees outside this ordinary incorporation job. Do not certify an audit exemption or treaty benefit from the company’s registration alone.

## Filing and payment

Under ss.267-268, prepare an annual return for the year ended 31 December, with financial statements, auditor’s report where applicable and directors’ report authenticated by at least 1 director (in words on the page) and the CEO. Listed companies file by 31 May; unlisted public and private companies by 31 July. These are corporate-registry deadlines, not the income-tax deadline. Corporate Regulatory Authority’s compliance page confirms the audited-statement package and RAA-panel auditor. The final annual-return form fields, submission/payment screen and current penalty allocation are not certified here. [ss.251-255,267-268](https://www.cra.gov.bt/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/The-Companies-Act-of-Bhutan-2016.pdf); [whole annual-compliance page](https://www.cra.gov.bt/guide-item/annual-compliance/)

Obtain or confirm the DRC TPN and use [corporate/business tax](bt-corporate-income-tax.md), [personal tax](bt-income-tax.md), [GST](bt-vat-gst.md) and [payroll](bt-payroll-social.md) as applicable. The new Act’s TPN timing and annual return apply to 2026; the prior March tax filing date belongs to the historical business/company cycle, not every new return. Preserve BITS transition correspondence. [ss.296-300](https://www.drc.gov.bt/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Income-Tax-Act-of-Bhutan-2025.pdf); [ss.263,290-295](https://www.drc.gov.bt/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Income-Tax-Act-of-Bhutan-2025.pdf); [paragraphs 1-2](https://www.drc.gov.bt/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Tax-Filing-Notification_IY-2025_final.pdf); [both image pages](https://www.drc.gov.bt/transition-to-the-bhutan-integrated-taxation-system-income-tax-module/)

## Completion checklist

Record entity choice and owners, directors/CEO and filing agent, capital and any FDI classification, funding currency/subscription, equity-injection deadline and RMA/Department notice, approvals/licences, certificate/Articles, tax registrations, audit appointment, annual calendar and receipts. A pending item stays pending; do not describe the company as fully operational merely because the certificate arrived.
