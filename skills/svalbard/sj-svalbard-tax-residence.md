# Svalbard: individual tax residence and temporary work

## Scope

This Guide covers Svalbard under [Svalbard Tax Act section 1-1](https://lovdata.no/dokument/NL/lov/1996-11-29-68). It does not cover Jan Mayen despite the shared SJ catalogue identifier. Determine residence and source separately before applying a tax rate. This is a source-cited draft checked on 22 October 2026.

## Residence starts before the twelve-month anniversary

Section 2-1 treats a person taking permanent residence in Svalbard as resident. A stay lasting **at least twelve months** counts as residence from the beginning of that stay. The statutory starting point is taxation of all wealth and income, subject to the Act's restrictions and exceptions. It is not a rule that taxation begins only in month thirteen. [Section 2-1](https://lovdata.no/dokument/NL/lov/1996-11-29-68).

The administration explains that genuine residential attachment must be assessed on the facts; brief holiday or business absences do not necessarily break residential continuity. Keep accommodation, employment, travel and actual-living records. [Tax Administration residence guidance](https://www.skatteetaten.no/person/skatt/hjelp-til-riktig-skatt/svalbard/hvem-er-skattemessig-bosatt-pa-svalbard/).

## Newcomer restriction is conditional

A person who was not tax resident in Norway, including Svalbard, during the preceding ten years has liability restricted by reference to section 2-2 for the first five years of Svalbard residence. This includes the relevant local work/business income, specified benefits and local real property. It is not an unconditional five-year exemption. [Sections 2-1 and 2-2](https://lovdata.no/dokument/NL/lov/1996-11-29-68).

Section 2-1 also permits the tax office to set aside this restriction for a year if it excludes a significant amount from Svalbard tax; the statute defines the significant amount as **twelve times the National Insurance basic amount**. Do not promise that foreign investment income is always outside tax during the newcomer period. The applicable basic amount and the office's exercise of this power require a case-specific calculation. [Section 2-1, second paragraph](https://lovdata.no/dokument/NL/lov/1996-11-29-68).

## Temporary work: thirty continuous days

A nonresident temporarily working or conducting business on Svalbard is liable on that local income if the stay lasts **at least thirty continuous days**. Exactly thirty satisfies the statutory test. Section 2-2 also contains a separate rule for specified benefits, requiring active work when entitlement arose, and a separate local-property rule. [Section 2-2](https://lovdata.no/dokument/NL/lov/1996-11-29-68).

For this temporary-stay test, the administration excludes travel days to and from Svalbard and treats an interruption as ending the period. Its example of fourteen days, a weekend away and twenty further days produces two nonqualifying periods, not thirty-four continuous days. [Official wage guidance](https://www.skatteetaten.no/person/skatt/hjelp-til-riktig-skatt/svalbard/skatt-lonn/).

A qualifying continuous stay establishes limited liability on the relevant work income; it does not establish general residence merely because thirty days have elapsed. Conversely, a resident's brief holiday does not automatically impose this temporary-worker test on every later work period. Apply the correct statutory category. [Sections 2-1 and 2-2](https://lovdata.no/dokument/NL/lov/1996-11-29-68).

## Departure and income outside Svalbard

Residence-based liability ends from the income year in which time outside Svalbard exceeds **183 days in a twelve-month period**. The statute says more than 183, not at least 183. The administration counts whole or partial calendar days outside and explains that the period need not be continuous. Therefore review a rolling travel history, not just a calendar-year total. [Section 2-1](https://lovdata.no/dokument/NL/lov/1996-11-29-68); [residence guidance](https://www.skatteetaten.no/person/skatt/hjelp-til-riktig-skatt/svalbard/hvem-er-skattemessig-bosatt-pa-svalbard/).

When residence-based liability ceases, test any remaining Svalbard work under section 2-2. Loss of resident status does not itself exempt local work or local property. [Sections 2-1 and 2-2](https://lovdata.no/dokument/NL/lov/1996-11-29-68).

Section 2-1 excludes pay for work outside Svalbard and business income earned outside Svalbard where it is demonstrated that the income is taxable under other Norwegian taxation or in a foreign state. Property and installations outside Svalbard, and income from them, have a separate exclusion. A foreign payer alone does not establish where work was performed or prove these conditions. [Section 2-1](https://lovdata.no/dokument/NL/lov/1996-11-29-68).

## Review file and limits

Record residence history for the preceding ten years, exact arrival/departure dates, travel-day treatment, work locations, property locations and evidence of foreign/mainland tax liability. Keep the newcomer exception assessment distinct from the ordinary residence decision.

Mainland Norwegian residence and departure, treaty scope, medical-absence exceptions, special benefits, offshore work and appeals require additional review. This Guide does not determine immigration permission or social-insurance membership. Independent accountant review remains outstanding.
