# Svalbard: transfer pricing in 2026 v0.1

> **Source-cited draft.** Tax year 2026, checked 24 October 2026. General reference for working papers; a qualified professional must review the facts and filing position. Independent AI review does not confer accountant review.

## Who this applies to

[Svalbard Act §3-2](https://lovdata.no/dokument/NL/lov/1996-11-29-68) imports the Income Tax Act with express exclusions; **§13-1 is not excluded**. Income/wealth reduced through direct or indirect common interests can be adjusted to what independent parties would have agreed. This applies even where a documentation exemption applies ([Income Tax Act §13-1](https://lovdata.no/dokument/NL/lov/1999-03-26-14/KAPITTEL_13)).

[Administration Act §8-11](https://lovdata.no/dokument/NL/lov/2016-05-27-14), applied through Svalbard Act §4-1, separately requires controlled-transaction reporting and documentation from companies/entities with relevant returns. Its **50% direct/indirect ownership or control** definition is a compliance rule, not a universal minimum ownership for substantive §13-1 adjustment. Include relevant permanent-establishment/head-office dealings under §8-11(5), and screen each mainland/Svalbard allocation. Svalbard only; no Jan Mayen coverage.

## Rates, bands and thresholds

| Item | 2026 rule | Official source |
|---|---|---|
| Related-party reporting definition | At least 50% direct/indirect ownership/control, with statutory common-control/family scope | [Administration Act §8-11(4)](https://lovdata.no/dokument/NL/lov/2016-05-27-14) |
| Small-transaction/balance exemption | Transactions strictly below NOK 10m **and** year-end balances strictly below NOK 25m | [Administration regulation §8-11-1](https://lovdata.no/dokument/SF/forskrift/2016-11-23-1360/KAPITTEL_7) |
| Separate small-group documentation exemption | Fewer than 250 employees **and** either turnover no more than NOK 400m **or** balance sheet no more than NOK 350m | [Administration Act §8-11(3)](https://lovdata.no/dokument/NL/lov/2016-05-27-14) |
| Documentation response / retention | 45 days after request, starting no earlier than the return deadline / at least 10 years after period end | [Administration Act §8-11(2)](https://lovdata.no/dokument/NL/lov/2016-05-27-14); [regulation §8-11-15(1)](https://lovdata.no/dokument/SF/forskrift/2016-11-23-1360/KAPITTEL_7) |
| CbC reporting group threshold / filing | Prior-year consolidated revenue at least NOK 6.5bn / within 12 months after year end | [Administration Act §8-12](https://lovdata.no/dokument/NL/lov/2016-05-27-14); [regulation §8-12-7](https://lovdata.no/dokument/SF/forskrift/2016-11-23-1360/KAPITTEL_7) |
| Ordinary company tax effect | 16%, or 22% for the statutory higher slice | [2026 Svalbard decision §3(b)–(c)](https://lovdata.no/dokument/STV/forskrift/2025-12-19-2780) |

Strict inequalities matter: exactly NOK 10m transactions or NOK 25m balances fails the first exemption. Exactly 250 employees fails the employee condition; turnover of exactly NOK 400m can satisfy that limb.

## The base

Identify all related transactions, actual conduct, contracts, functions, assets and risks. Establish economically comparable independent terms, document the pricing method/comparables and reconcile the result to the Svalbard tax base. A service requires evidence of what was supplied and how the recipient benefited; a loan requires its amount, term, currency, security and credit circumstances. Do not treat a group policy or an unsupported percentage as arm's-length evidence. Under [§13-1(4)](https://lovdata.no/dokument/NL/lov/1999-03-26-14/KAPITTEL_13), the OECD guidance is taken into account as the statutory conditions provide; the rule also recommends corresponding consideration in other cases. This does not import an unsupported fixed safe-harbour margin.

Compute a price correction from supported independent terms and adjust profit once, then apply the corporate guide's loss and 16%/22% slice rules. §13-1 permits discretionary assessment where the relationship reduced the taxpayer's income/wealth. The specified information-exchange circumstances in §13-1(2) affect the presumption; distinguish these from the separate documentation exceptions.

For the small-transaction exemption, aggregate **fair value** of controlled transactions for the period, and year-end receivables, liabilities and guarantees as defined by regulation §8-11-1. Both limits must be met. Otherwise report nature/extent and balances under §8-11(1), then separately test formal documentation under §8-11(2)–(3). The small-group exemption requires group-wide employee and turnover/balance data. Its exemption does not cover the stated transactions with a related counterparty in a state from which Norway cannot demand income/wealth information under an international agreement; petroleum taxpayers have a further statutory exception. Do not infer absence of an information-exchange agreement from absence of an income-tax treaty.

Documentation under [regulation §§8-11-3–16](https://lovdata.no/dokument/SF/forskrift/2016-11-23-1360/KAPITTEL_7) includes enterprise/group/business, financial information, controlled dealings, functional analysis, central services, intangibles, method and comparability analysis, relevant agreements and reconciliation. Use Norwegian, Swedish, Danish or English. Retain sufficient evidence even when formal relief applies, because tax returns still must be correct.

## Deadlines and filing

Controlled-transaction information accompanies the relevant annual tax return, ordinarily **31 May after the income year** for companies and business owners ([regulation §8-2-3](https://lovdata.no/dokument/SF/forskrift/2016-11-23-1360/KAPITTEL_7); [Administration Act §§8-1,8-11](https://lovdata.no/dokument/NL/lov/2016-05-27-14)). Use the electronic business return and current controlled-transaction disclosure. Keep the computation tied to the reported numbers.

Produce formal documentation within **45 days of an authority request**, but that response period starts **no earlier than expiry of the tax-return filing deadline** (regulation §8-11-15(1)). Retain it for at least **10 years after the period**. If the formal documentation lacks a comparability analysis and the authority requests one under §8-11-15(2), the stated response period is **60–90 days**; that does not replace the ordinary 45-day rule or limit general information-request powers. Do not wait for the request to begin gathering evidence. CbC §8-12 includes parent and defined secondary-filing duties, identity-of-filer notification and the **NOK 6.5bn** prior-year threshold; where in scope the report is in English and due within **12 months** of financial-year end. The application of that regime's “Norway/foreign” location terms to a Svalbard-only group perimeter has not been independently established; that large-group detail is excluded from ordinary coverage and must be checked separately.

Tax due from a correction uses ordinary Payment Act §§10-20–10-21 and, for amended assessments, §10-53. A filing extension does not itself extend tax-payment dates.

## Penalties and interest

Under [Svalbard Act §4-1](https://lovdata.no/dokument/NL/lov/1996-11-29-68), the Tax Administration Act applies. Missing mandatory information can trigger a daily coercive fine, ordinarily **0.5 court-fee units per day**, capped at **50 units** for the ordinary information failure ([Administration Act §14-1](https://lovdata.no/dokument/NL/lov/2016-05-27-14); [regulation §14-1-1](https://lovdata.no/dokument/SF/forskrift/2016-11-23-1360/KAPITTEL_13)). The authority's notice sets the start date; this is not an automatic charge on every late return.

Incorrect or incomplete information producing a tax advantage attracts **20%** additional tax, reduced to **10%** for the specified third-party-reported information. Intentional or grossly negligent failures can attract a further **20% or 40%**. Excusable circumstances, qualifying voluntary correction and other statutory exceptions must be tested ([Administration Act §§14-3 to 14-6](https://lovdata.no/dokument/NL/lov/2016-05-27-14)). These percentages apply to the tax advantage, not turnover.

Unpaid tax bears interest from the due date under [Payment Act §§11-1 and 11-6](https://lovdata.no/dokument/NL/lov/2005-06-17-67). The [authority's 2026 interest table](https://www.skatteetaten.no/satser/forsinkelsesrenter/) gives **12% a year for 1 January–30 June 2026** and **12.25% for 1 July–31 December 2026**. Split arrears at the change date. A payment falling due in 2027 needs the rate then in force; do not extrapolate 2026 rates.

## Cross-border points

Income Tax Act §2-35 and Svalbard Act §§2-1–2-4 govern which territory taxes an item. Keep source allocation and arm's-length pricing as two evidenced steps. Norway's income-tax treaties do not apply to Svalbard ([current authority S-23-1.5](https://oppslag.rettskilder.skatteetaten.no/rettskilder2/type/handboker/skatte-abc/gjeldende/skatteabc-S-23)); do not promise treaty MAP, corresponding adjustment or a treaty safe harbour. Domestic amendment/appeal rights remain separate. Foreign-tax and departure questions are mapped in the XB guide.

## Two worked examples that recompute

**Supported service-price correction.** A resident company has stated taxable profit NOK 4m after a NOK 1.2m related-party service fee. Assume the services are deductible but independent evidence supports only NOK 1m. Add back NOK 200,000: taxable profit NOK 4.2m, income tax NOK 672,000, increase NOK 32,000. The supported NOK 1m is a hypothetical fact, not a statutory safe harbour.

```oa-check
name: Svalbard supported service fee correction 2026
inputs:
  reported_profit: 4000000
  charged_fee: 1200000
  supported_fee: 1000000
steps:
  adjustment: max(0,charged_fee-supported_fee)
  corrected_profit: reported_profit+adjustment
  tax: corrected_profit*0.16
  increase: adjustment*0.16
expected:
  adjustment: 200000
  corrected_profit: 4200000
  tax: 672000
  increase: 32000
```

**Adjustment crosses the corporate floor.** Stated profit NOK 14m, qualifying payroll NOK 500,000 and physical-asset tax value NOK 5m. A supported NOK 3m correction produces NOK 17m profit. Substance threshold is NOK 6m, so the NOK 15m floor governs. Tax is NOK 2.84m, compared with NOK 2.24m before correction: increase NOK 600,000. No losses or other changes are assumed.

```oa-check
name: Svalbard pricing correction crosses company rate band 2026
inputs:
  profit: 14000000
  adjustment: 3000000
  payroll: 500000
  assets: 5000000
steps:
  corrected: profit+adjustment
  threshold: max(15000000,10*payroll+0.20*assets)
  high_slice: max(0,corrected-threshold)
  tax: (corrected-high_slice)*0.16+high_slice*0.22
  previous_tax: profit*0.16
  increase: tax-previous_tax
expected:
  threshold: 15000000
  high_slice: 2000000
  tax: 2840000
  increase: 600000
```

## Ask the client first

- What direct/indirect ownership, control and family links exist, including head office/branches?
- What are fair-value transaction totals, year-end balances, guarantees and counterparties' locations?
- What group employee, turnover and balance-sheet figures support an exemption?
- What contracts, actual services, functions/risks, financial data and independent comparisons support prices?
- Have disclosure, documentation requests, CbC notifications or assessment corrections arisen?
