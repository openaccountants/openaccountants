# Saint Helena island: company income tax v0.3

> **General reference only.** This skill is general tax reference material for AI-assisted workflows. It has not been reviewed for any specific person's facts. It covers Saint Helena island only, not Ascension or Tristan da Cunha, which have separate laws.

## Who this applies to

Companies and other bodies of persons with income earned, accrued or derived in or from Saint Helena. A company incorporated in Saint Helena is resident, and so is a body whose control and management was exercised there in the tax year (Ordinance s.2). The tax year runs 1 April to 31 March; tax year 2026 here is 1 April 2026 to 31 March 2027.

## The base

Section 8 and the source definitions decide taxable income: income earned, accrued or derived in or from Saint Helena. Residence does not replace that source test. Section 7 then gives chargeable income after deductions, floored at nil. A company operating in a broadly related sector does not qualify for 15% on all its income: only income from its own primary production in a listed activity does.

If a company has both standard and qualifying income, deductions attributable to both are apportioned in the ratio of the two income sources to total taxable income (Schedule 1 para 1(3)(a)). Dividends and gains are not blended into the 25% base.

A company gets no personal allowance; it pays on every pound of profit (Tax Guide 12). Records: books or accounts under s.52. A person required to register under s.36 must obtain a tax invoice from resident suppliers (s.52(2)).

## Deadlines and filing

Return for the year to 31 March 2026: 30 June 2026 (official reminder). For the year to 31 March 2027: 30 June 2027, payment 30 September 2027 (ss.39(1)(b), 43; Income Tax Office key dates). An approved substituted accounting period (s.53) runs on its own calendar.

How to file:
1. Register before the activity starts, if s.36 applies. A company gives its registered name, registration number, place of incorporation, the activity, its start date, places of business and correspondence address (Regulation 20). The Income Tax Office issues a certificate with the tax registration number. Report changes within one month.
2. Download the Company Income Tax Return (P50 COM) from the Relevant Forms section of the [Income Tax page](https://www.sainthelena.gov.sh/public-services/income-tax/). The current form is the 2025/26 spreadsheet.
3. Complete it, with a depreciation schedule where applicable, and include end of year accounts. The form calculates tax in its tax calculation table.
4. Send it to the Income Tax Office, 1st Floor, Post Office Building, Jamestown, by the due date. The office lists Mondays to Fridays 8.30am to 4pm, telephone (290) 22825 for companies. Section 37(5) lets the Commissioner approve electronic filing; the arrangement in force was not stated on the page.
5. Returns are processed on the figures given. Checks and audits come afterwards (Tax Guide 3).

Filing extension: request in writing before the due date with a full statement of reasons (s.39(3)). It does not move the payment date (s.43(2)), which stays 3 months after the original return date. No late filing penalty applies within an extended date (Tax Guide 5). Earlier instalments still apply.

Instalments: within 15 days after the end of months 3, 6, 9 and 12 of the tax year or substituted period (Regulation 21(1)). For the normal year that is 15 July, 15 October, 15 January and 15 April. Each is one quarter of the previous year's tax, less tax deducted under s.26 (Regulation 21(2)). If that figure is unknown, use the latest assessed year or an estimate (21(3)). A reduced estimate is not self-authorising: apply by the third instalment date and get approval (21(4)). If an approved estimate is under 75% of actual tax, the difference is treated as a tax shortfall under s.68 (21(5), (6)). The test concerns taxable income from the specified sources, not final tax or turnover.

If the company cannot pay, it may ask the Commissioner for an arrangement under s.46; that does not change the due date for penalties.

## Penalties and interest

Late filing: £100 plus £10 for each complete month (s.66). The penalty is mandatory, and an extension before the due date avoids it (Tax Guide 5). Late payment: 10%, plus 1% a month after the first month (s.67). The Commissioner may remit it where the cause was beyond the person's control or it is reasonable to do so (s.67(4)). False or misleading statement: 75% of the shortfall if knowing or reckless, 20% otherwise (s.68(2)), up 10 points on a repeat. Voluntary disclosure before discovery or audit cuts the rate by 20 points under s.68(3)(b). Tax Guide 5 says 10%; the statute wins. Failure to register: offence, plus £100 and £10 a month on conviction (s.36).

## Cross-border points

A non-resident is taxed on business income attributable to activity in Saint Helena, including through a permanent establishment (s.2). The Commissioner can reallocate income between associates to an arm's length result (s.78). See [cross-border points](sh-cross-border.md).

## Two worked examples that recompute

Example 1: £80,000 ordinary and £20,000 qualifying chargeable income, after allocation and with no credits.

```oa-check
name: Mixed company income
inputs:
  ordinary: 80000
  qualifying: 20000
steps:
  - tax: ordinary * 0.25 + qualifying * 0.15
expected:
  tax: 23000.00
```

On £100,000 of either kind alone, tax is £25,000 or £15,000. This example starts after allocation and does not establish an incentive entitlement.

Example 2: prior-year tax £20,000, no s.26 withholding, no variation, final current-year tax £25,000.

```oa-check
name: Instalments and balance
inputs:
  prior: 20000
  final: 25000
steps:
  - instalment: prior / 4
  - balance: final - instalment * 4
expected:
  instalment: 5000.00
  balance: 5000.00
```


## Ask the client first

- Is the company incorporated on the island, or controlled and managed there?
- Which income is from primary production in a listed activity?
- Previous-year taxable income from business and property?
- Prior-year tax, and any approved accounting period?
- Any new depreciable assets bought or imported?
