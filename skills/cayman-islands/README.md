# Cayman Islands: tax pack

The Cayman Islands has no income tax, corporate tax, capital gains tax, VAT, sales tax, payroll tax or withholding tax. What exists is import duty (22% on most goods), stamp duty on land (7.5%, and 10% from 1 January 2026 where the price is CI$2 million or more, with Caymanian first and second property reliefs), the 13% tourist accommodation tax, employer pension (5% plus 5% to CI$87,000) and health insurance duties, the CI$8.75 minimum wage, registry and work permit fees, and economic substance reporting. Amounts are CI$; the General Registry converts at US$1 = CI$0.82. This map covers 2026.
