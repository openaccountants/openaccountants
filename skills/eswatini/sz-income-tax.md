# Eswatini Personal Income Tax

## Personal income tax bands and rates (2025)

- **Band 1 — SZL 0 to 100,000** — 20% of taxable income (SZL 0 plus 20% of the excess over SZL 0) percent (Eswatini applies the same graduated rate table to residents and non-residents, with relief delivered through annual tax rebates rather than a zero-rate band. Applied for the 2025 tax year per the ERS / PwC summary.)  _([Income Tax Order, 1975](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/eswatini/individual/taxes-on-personal-income))_
- **Band 2 — SZL 100,001 to 150,000** — SZL 20,000 plus 25% of the excess over SZL 100,000  _([Income Tax Order, 1975](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/eswatini/individual/taxes-on-personal-income))_
- **Band 3 — SZL 150,001 to 200,000** — SZL 32,500 plus 30% of the excess over SZL 150,000  _([Income Tax Order, 1975](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/eswatini/individual/taxes-on-personal-income))_
- **Band 4 — above SZL 200,000** — SZL 47,500 plus 33% of the excess over SZL 200,000  _([Income Tax Order, 1975](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/eswatini/individual/taxes-on-personal-income))_
- **Standard annual tax rebate (primary)** — SZL 8,200 per year SZL/year  _([Income Tax Order, 1975](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/eswatini/individual/taxes-on-personal-income))_
- **Senior citizen rebate (age 60+)** — SZL 10,900 per year SZL/year  _([Income Tax Order, 1975](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/eswatini/individual/taxes-on-personal-income))_
- **Effective tax-free threshold** — Approximately SZL 41,000 of annual taxable income (point at which the standard rebate fully offsets tax) SZL/year  _([Income Tax Order, 1975](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/eswatini/individual/taxes-on-personal-income))_
- **Non-resident rate treatment** — Non-residents are taxed at the same graduated rates on Eswatini-source income  _([Income Tax Order, 1975](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/eswatini/individual/taxes-on-personal-income))_
- **Residence concept** — Eswatini taxes on source rather than residence; an individual is broadly treated as resident if ordinarily resident or physically present in Eswatini for a substantial part of the year (approx — confirm day-count test)  _(Income Tax Order, 1975)_
- **Employment income collection** — Employment income is collected through monthly PAYE withholding by employers  _([Income Tax (Employees' Tax) provisions / Income Tax Order, 1975](https://www.ers.org.sz/IncomeTax/Paye))_
- **PAYE remittance deadline** — Within 7 days after the end of the month in which remuneration was paid  _([Income Tax Order, 1975](https://www.ers.org.sz/IncomeTax/Paye))_
- **Annual return filing** — Individuals with income beyond fully-taxed employment must file an annual income tax return with the ERS after 30 June (approx — confirm exact deadline)  _([Income Tax Order, 1975](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/eswatini/individual/tax-administration))_
