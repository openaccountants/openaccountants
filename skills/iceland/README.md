# Iceland: tax pack

Iceland taxes individuals on the calendar year. A combined state and municipal income tax is collected monthly through withholding (staðgreiðsla), and the annual return filed in March reports the previous year's income. Companies pay income tax at a flat rate set by legal form (20% for hf. and ehf., 37.6% for other taxable entities) and also file in the year after the income year. VAT (virðisaukaskattur, VSK) is 24% with an 11% reduced rate. Employers pay the social security levy (tryggingagjald) and mandatory pension contributions. Iceland Revenue and Customs (Skatturinn) runs all of these. Currency: Icelandic króna (ISK).
