# Gambia Personal Income Tax

## Personal income tax bands and rates

- **Annual tax-free threshold** — D36,000 per year (D3,000 per month) GMD  _(Income and Value Added Tax Act (First Schedule) https://www.igrowventure.com/gambia-in-the-2025-tax-reforms/)_
- **Band 1: D0 to D36,000** — 0 percent  _(Income and Value Added Tax Act (First Schedule) https://www.igrowventure.com/gambia-in-the-2025-tax-reforms/)_
- **Band 2: D36,001 to D46,000** — 5 percent  _(Income and Value Added Tax Act (First Schedule) https://www.igrowventure.com/gambia-in-the-2025-tax-reforms/)_
- **Band 3: D46,001 to D56,000** — 10 percent  _(Income and Value Added Tax Act (First Schedule) https://www.igrowventure.com/gambia-in-the-2025-tax-reforms/)_
- **Band 4: D56,001 to D66,000** — 15 percent  _(Income and Value Added Tax Act (First Schedule) https://www.igrowventure.com/gambia-in-the-2025-tax-reforms/)_
- **Band 5: D66,001 to D76,000** — 20 percent  _(Income and Value Added Tax Act (First Schedule) https://www.igrowventure.com/gambia-in-the-2025-tax-reforms/)_
- **Top band: above D76,000** — 25 percent ((approx — confirm; some guides still list a 30% top rate above D86,000))  _(Income and Value Added Tax Act (First Schedule) https://www.igrowventure.com/gambia-in-the-2025-tax-reforms/)_
- **PAYE employment income threshold** — Employees earning above D3,000 per month (D36,000 per year) are subject to employment income tax GMD  _(Income and Value Added Tax Act https://www.gra.gm/domestic-faqs)_
- **Minimum tax for individual business income** — Higher of the schedule rate or 1% of turnover (audited accounts) / 2% of turnover (otherwise)  _(Income and Value Added Tax Act https://www.gra.gm/domestic-faqs)_
- **Small business turnover tax** — 3% for businesses with annual turnover under D500,000 percent ((approx — confirm))  _(Income and Value Added Tax Act https://taxatlas.io/country/gambia)_
- **Capital gains tax (individuals)** — Higher of 15% of the gain or 5% of the consideration  _(Income and Value Added Tax Act https://www.gra.gm/domestic-faqs)_
- **Residence test** — Present in The Gambia for more than 183 days in the tax year  _(Income and Value Added Tax Act https://taxatlas.io/country/gambia)_
- **Individual annual return deadline** — 31 March of the following year  _(Income and Value Added Tax Act https://www.gra.gm/domestic-faqs)_
- **PAYE remittance deadline** — Within 15 days after the end of the month  _(Income and Value Added Tax Act https://www.gra.gm/domestic-faqs)_
