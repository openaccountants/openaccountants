# Laos Payroll & Social Contributions

## Social security contributions

The Lao Social Security Organisation (LSSO) lists the revised Social Security Law No. 54/NA of 27 June 2018 as current, with a register date of 1 May 2019. Instruction No. 0060/MLSW of 11 January 2023 applies from 1 May 2023 and replaced Instruction No. 1206/MLSW. [LSSO legislation register](https://www.lsso.gov.la/index.php/lo/download-la/legislation-la/90-law-la/new-low) · [Notice No. 1356](https://www.lsso.gov.la/images/stories/Notices/notices_khnn_0060.pdf).

- **Enterprise contribution rates.** The employer pays 6% and the employee pays 5.5% of monthly insurable earnings, for a combined 11.5%. The base includes basic salary, pay by output, overtime and other earnings declared for income tax. For workers paid by the day or week, aggregate their earnings for the month. [Social Security Law, art 74(2)](https://www.lsso.gov.la/Legislation/nssf%20law%2054.pdf#page=27).
- **Insurable earnings limits.** The government minimum wage for the period determines the floor. The ceiling is determined separately based on economic conditions and actuarial assessment. Decision No. 1740/MLSW and Notice No. 0824 raised the ceiling to LAK 4,500,000 from 1 January 2017 under the previous law. Confirm the ceiling with the LSSO for the contribution month before calculating a maximum contribution. [Social Security Law, arts 74 and 77](https://www.lsso.gov.la/Legislation/nssf%20law%2054.pdf#page=28) · [Instruction No. 0060, item 13](https://www.lsso.gov.la/Legislation/knn_new0060.pdf#page=8) · [Decision No. 1740](https://www.lsso.gov.la/Legislation/agreement1740.pdf) · [Notice No. 0824](https://www.lsso.gov.la/images/stories/Notices/notices_phadan_4500000.pdf).
- **Payment and return.** The employer deducts the employee share and remits both shares monthly with a detailed contribution list. Confirm the enterprise payment day with the LSSO. The 15th of the following month in item 13 of Instruction No. 0060 applies to voluntary contributors. [Social Security Law, art 75(2.1)](https://www.lsso.gov.la/Legislation/nssf%20law%2054.pdf#page=28) · [Instruction No. 0060, item 13](https://www.lsso.gov.la/Legislation/knn_new0060.pdf#page=8).
- **Registration.** An enterprise with at least one employee must register with the LSSO. Employee registration includes covered foreign employees. Submit employee registration forms within 30 days of signing the employment contract. [Social Security Law, arts 90–92](https://www.lsso.gov.la/Legislation/nssf%20law%2054.pdf#page=35) · [Notice No. 3185 of 16 September 2022](https://www.lsso.gov.la/images/stories/Notices/notices_regis.pdf).

## Payroll withholding and remittance

> **Income-tax period.** The Official Gazette lists the revised Income Tax Law as current. Article 74 names 1 September 2025 as commencement and refers to promulgation and publication. The Gazette records publication on 19 June 2026. Confirm which rules govern any pay period affected by those dates with a Lao practitioner. The salary rules below follow the revised Law. [Law on Income Tax (Revised), arts 73–74](https://laoofficialgazette.gov.la/kcfinder/upload/files/88-25-6-2025_0001.pdf) · [Lao Official Gazette register](https://laoofficialgazette.gov.la/index.php?r=site/listlegistioncp&agencies_id=2&old=0).

- **PAYE-equivalent — the 0%–25% range is confirmed, and the bands can now be stated** — **Article 38(1)** of the revised Law sets a progressive scale *"ແຕ່ ສູນສ່ວນຮ້ອຍ (0%) ຫາ ຊາວຫ້າສ່ວນຮ້ອຍ (25%)"* — **from zero per cent (0%) to twenty-five per cent (25%)**, given in words and digits together — on the income defined in **article 33(1)**: salary, wages, overtime, time allowances, position and duty allowances, bonuses, board- and management-committee meeting fees, and other benefits **in cash or in kind**. The range this guide carried on a commercial source is borne out; what it never gave is the scale, which the article sets out **per month** (*ຖານລາຍໄດ້ແຕ່ລະເດືອນ*), in kip  _([Law on Income Tax (Revised), arts. 33(1), 38(1)](https://laoofficialgazette.gov.la/kcfinder/upload/files/88-25-6-2025_0001.pdf))_

| Band | Monthly income (LAK) | Slice taxed | Rate | Tax on the slice | Cumulative tax |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | up to 2,500,000 | 2,500,000 | 0% | 0 | 0 |
| 2 | over 2,500,000 to 5,000,000 | 2,500,000 | 5% | 125,000 | 125,000 |
| 3 | over 5,000,000 to 15,000,000 | 10,000,000 | 10% | 1,000,000 | 1,125,000 |
| 4 | over 15,000,000 to 25,000,000 | 10,000,000 | 15% | 1,500,000 | 2,625,000 |
| 5 | over 25,000,000 to 65,000,000 | 40,000,000 | 20% | 8,000,000 | 10,625,000 |
| 6 | over 65,000,000 | — | 25% | — | — |

- **⚠ The bands are indexed to the minimum wage, so a printed scale dates** — The sentence immediately under article 38(1)'s table provides that the monthly income base in each band ***"ຈະມີການປັບປຸງ ຕາມຄ່າແຮງງານຂັ້ນຕໍ່າ ທີ່ລັດຖະບານກໍານົດ ໃນແຕ່ລະໄລຍະ"*** — **will be adjusted in line with the minimum wage set by the Government from time to time**. The rates are statutory; the money columns are not fixed by the Law and move with a government instrument this guide does not track. **Check the current minimum wage before applying the figures above**  _([Law on Income Tax (Revised), art. 38(1)](https://laoofficialgazette.gov.la/kcfinder/upload/files/88-25-6-2025_0001.pdf))_
- **Pay at or below the minimum wage is exempt outright** — **Article 34(1)** puts *"ເງິນເດືອນ ແລະ ເງິນລາຍໄດ້ອື່ນ ຕາມຄ່າແຮງງານຂັ້ນຕໍ່າ ທີ່ລັດຖະບານ ກໍານົດໃນແຕ່ລະໄລຍະ"* — salary and other income **up to the government-set minimum wage** — outside income tax altogether, alongside (art. 34(2)) the salaries of diplomatic and international-organisation staff and foreign experts working in Lao PDR under an agreement. This is a separate relief from band 1 above, and the guide stated neither  _([Law on Income Tax (Revised), art. 34](https://laoofficialgazette.gov.la/kcfinder/upload/files/88-25-6-2025_0001.pdf))_
- **Payroll PIT remittance deadline — the 20th is confirmed, and a withholding certificate goes with it** — **Article 39 (ປັບປຸງ)**: a person, legal entity or organisation paying salary to its staff, to soldiers, police and workers, or to others under contract or another obligation, must withhold and declare the tax on article 33(1) income to the tax office it belongs to or where its branch is located ***"ບໍ່ໃຫ້ກາຍ ວັນທີ 20 ຂອງເດືອນຖັດໄປ"*** — **no later than the 20th of the following month**. The "(approx — confirm)" is resolved. The same article adds an obligation the guide omits: the payer must **issue the person taxed a certificate of the tax withheld**, on the Tax Department's form, **certified by a tax officer**  _([Law on Income Tax (Revised), art. 39](https://laoofficialgazette.gov.la/kcfinder/upload/files/88-25-6-2025_0001.pdf))_
- **Monthly withholding is not the end of it — there is an annual reconciliation** — **Article 50 (ປັບປຸງ)** reconciles article 33(1) income annually against a scale set out in the same terms as article 38's. The two are **the same scale**: every band edge in the annual table is **exactly twelve times** the monthly one — 30,000,000 / 60,000,000 / 180,000,000 / 300,000,000 / 780,000,000 against 2,500,000 / 5,000,000 / 15,000,000 / 25,000,000 / 65,000,000 — at the identical 0 / 5 / 10 / 15 / 20 / 25%, and it carries the same minimum-wage indexation sentence. So the annual table is the monthly one restated, **not a second regime**; an employee paid unevenly through the year can still have over- or under-withheld against it. Article 50 also requires individuals with article 35 income to declare total income received and total tax paid for the year **no later than 31 March of the following year**  _([Law on Income Tax (Revised), art. 50](https://laoofficialgazette.gov.la/kcfinder/upload/files/88-25-6-2025_0001.pdf))_
- **⚠ Non-domiciled staff — same salary scale, a much shorter clock** — Articles 38–39 above sit in the chapter for persons **with** a Lao domicile (*ທີ່ມີພູມລໍາເນົາ*). **Article 48(1)** gives persons **without** one the same progressive **0%–25%** on salary, wages, overtime, position and duty allowances, bonuses and the like under article 45(1) — so on pay the treatment does not differ. **Article 49 (ປັບປຸງ) does differ**: the payer must declare and remit *"ພາຍໃນເວລາ ສິບຫ້າວັນ ນັບແຕ່ວັນໄດ້ເບີກຈ່າຍເງິນ"* — **within fifteen days of the date the payment was made** — not by the 20th of the following month. **The Law gives that number in words only**, with no digits beside it; it is legible but has no second form to check against  _([Law on Income Tax (Revised), arts. 48(1), 49](https://laoofficialgazette.gov.la/kcfinder/upload/files/88-25-6-2025_0001.pdf))_
