# Federated States of Micronesia (FM): tax pack

**Built from the law. Not yet approved. Written by Ryan Duguid. Not reviewed by an accountant.** The Federated States of Micronesia uses the US dollar. National taxes (wage and salary tax, gross revenue tax) are in Title 54 of the FSM Code and are administered by the Department of Finance and Administration (dofa.gov.fm); social security is run by the FSM Social Security Administration (fsmssa.fm) under Title 53. The four states (Chuuk, Kosrae, Pohnpei, Yap) levy their own taxes. Tax year 2026.
