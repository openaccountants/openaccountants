# Kuwait Corporate Income Tax

Tax year 2026. Income tax, Zakat, NLST and DMTT for companies.

## Who this applies to

Foreign corporate bodies trading in Kuwait, Kuwaiti shareholding companies (Zakat), listed companies (NLST) and multinational groups with EUR 750m revenue (DMTT). A group in DMTT scope leaves the other three regimes for periods from 1 January 2025. Individuals are out.

## The base

Income tax falls on gross income less necessary, real, documented costs (reg. art. 3). Kuwait-source income includes work done wholly or partly in Kuwait wherever the contract was signed, sale or licence of intellectual property, agency commissions, gains on assets including shares in a company holding mainly Kuwaiti real estate, rent on property used in Kuwait, services performed in Kuwait, and activity on the stock exchange (reg. art. 2). A permanent office in Kuwait where sales contracts are concluded is included. The default period is the calendar year; the first period may run to 18 months with approval (reg. art. 10).

## Deadlines and filing

1. Register with the tax administration within 30 days and apply for a tax card with the memorandum, agency contract and address (reg. arts. 11 and 12). Government bodies and companies may not deal with a corporate body lacking a valid card.
2. Log in to the MOF electronic tax services, reached from https://www.mof.gov.kw under corporate tax services.
3. File the Arabic return on the form attached to the executive rules, with the auditor report from an auditor on the MOF approved list, balance sheet and final accounts, asset schedule, subcontractor schedule with retention held, closing stock, contracts and trial balance (reg. art. 13).
4. Pay in one sum or the first instalment.
5. MOF assesses. Pay within 30 days of the letter or object within 60 days.
6. Ask for the clearance certificate. Retained money is released only with MOF approval: not taxable, exempt or a loss; all tax paid; or a bank guarantee (reg. art. 38).

DMTT: register within 120 days, file and pay within 15 months of period end, and send questions to DMTT@mof.gov.kw as named on the MOF DMTT page.

## Penalties and interest

Income tax: 1% per 30 days or part. A payer that fails to retain or remit is liable for the tax itself (reg. art. 39), and unreported subcontractor payments are disallowed (reg. art. 37). MOF may assess on an estimated basis if no return is filed (reg. art. 19). DMTT: see the rates table; tax evasion carries up to three years in prison and a fine up to three times the tax (art. 36).

## Cross-border points

Tax is territorial as set out under The base. Exemptions under international agreements stand for their term (reg. art. 8). Non-residents are caught through agents. For DMTT a permanent establishment includes a site or services lasting over six months in any twelve (art. 1). Equipment may not be exported until a clearance certificate is shown (reg. art. 46).

## Two worked examples that recompute

**Example 1.** A foreign branch has net taxable income of KWD 100,000 and files 70 days late. Tax is KWD 15,000. Seventy days is three 30-day blocks or parts, so the fine is 3% of the tax, KWD 450.

```oa-check
name: Foreign branch, net taxable income KWD 100,000, filed 70 days late
inputs:
  income: 100000
  blocks: 3
steps:
  - tax: income * 0.15
  - fine: tax * 0.01 * blocks
  - total: tax + fine
expected:
  tax: 15000.00
  fine: 450.00
  total: 15450.00
```

**Example 2 (decision).** Tax per return is KWD 40,000. Paying in one sum puts KWD 40,000 out on the first date. Paying by instalments puts KWD 10,000 out on each of the four dates, with no fine if each date is met. Instalments win on cash unless the company prefers to close the liability. Where an extension is granted, all instalments already due are paid together when the return goes in (reg. art. 28).

```oa-check
name: Decision: pay in one sum or four instalments, tax KWD 40,000
inputs:
  tax: 40000
steps:
  - one_sum: tax
  - instalment: tax / 4
  - first_due: instalment
expected:
  one_sum: 40000.00
  instalment: 10000.00
  first_due: 10000.00
```

## Ask the client first

- Is the group's consolidated revenue EUR 750m or more in two of the last four periods?
- Is the entity foreign, Kuwaiti or Gulf owned, and in what shares?
- Is it a public or closed shareholding company, and is it listed?
- What is the period end, and was an extension requested in time?
- Does it hold a valid tax card, and what retention is held by customers?
- Does it hold a Law 116 of 2013 exemption?
