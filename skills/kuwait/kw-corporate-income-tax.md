# Kuwait Corporate Income Tax v0.4

Tax year 2026.

> **General reference only.** This skill is general tax/accounting reference material for AI-assisted workflows. It has not been reviewed for any specific person's facts, documents, elections, deadlines, residency, filing status, or local procedures. Do not rely on it to file, pay, amend, or take a tax position without review by a qualified professional in the relevant jurisdiction.

## Who this applies to

Corporate bodies trading in Kuwait, public and closed Kuwaiti shareholding companies, listed Kuwaiti companies and multinational groups. Determine the regime before computing. This Guide computes ordinary income tax only after foreign corporate-body liability is established and no exemption applies. Kuwaiti/Gulf and mixed-ownership income-tax liability is a NON-OPERATIVE HOLD: do not infer it from the broad statutory corporate-body definition. Natural-person own-work income is exempt subject to the corporate-share exception in art. 8. DMTT scope includes the revenue test and exclusions; it is not a blanket tax on every group member.

## The base

After liability and exemptions are settled, income tax falls on gross income less necessary, real, documented costs allowed under the law (reg. art. 3). Kuwait-source income includes work done wholly or partly in Kuwait wherever the contract was signed, sale or licence of intellectual property, agency commissions, income from lending money in Kuwait, gains on assets including shares in a company holding mainly Kuwaiti real estate, rent on property used in Kuwait, services performed in Kuwait, and activity on the stock exchange (reg. art. 2). A permanent office in Kuwait where sales contracts are concluded is included. The default period is the calendar year; the first period may run to 18 months with approval (reg. art. 10).

## Deadlines and filing

1. Register with the tax administration within 30 days and apply for a tax card with the memorandum, agency contract and address (reg. arts. 11 and 12). Government bodies and companies may not deal with a corporate body lacking a valid card, except registration and licensing procedures needed to start activity (art. 12).
2. MOF electronic-tax service entry point is https://www.mof.gov.kw. The authenticated portal screens, form field numbers, acknowledgement and payment-reference steps have not been read. Do not treat this as a complete electronic filing walkthrough; obtain the current form and instructions from MOF before submission.
3. File the Arabic return on the form attached to the executive rules, with the auditor report from an auditor on the MOF approved list, balance sheet and final accounts, asset schedule, subcontractor schedule with retention held, closing stock, contracts and trial balance (reg. art. 13).
4. Pay in one sum or the first instalment.
5. MOF assesses. Determine the payment amount/date under arts. 20 and 29 to 31 and the separate 60-day objection right. An objection is not presented here as an automatic payment suspension.
6. Ask for the clearance certificate. Retained money is released only with MOF approval: not taxable, exempt or a loss; all tax paid; or a bank guarantee (reg. art. 38).

DMTT: register within 120 days, file and pay within 15 months of period end, and send questions to DMTT@mof.gov.kw as named on the MOF DMTT page.

## Penalties and interest

Income tax: 1% per 30 days or part on the applicable tax/instalment base and starting date in art. 34. Filing and payment are separate breaches. A payer that fails to retain or remit is liable for the tax itself (reg. art. 39), and unreported subcontractor payments are disallowed (reg. art. 37). MOF may assess on an estimated basis if no return is filed (reg. art. 19). DMTT: see the rates table; tax evasion by falsifying, concealing or destroying tax-relevant records carries up to three years in prison and/or a fine up to three times evaded tax; repeat offences within five years after completion or expiry of the earlier punishment carry up to five years and/or five times tax (art. 36).

## Cross-border points

Tax is territorial as set out under The base. Exemptions under international agreements stand for their term (reg. art. 8). Non-residents are caught through agents. For DMTT, a construction/assembly/installation site or related supervision lasting over six months in twelve, and non-resident services aggregating over six months in twelve, are among the PE cases (art. 2). This is not the ordinary income-tax PE rule. Equipment may not be exported until a clearance certificate is shown (reg. art. 46).

## Two worked examples that recompute

**Example 1.** A foreign branch outside DMTT with confirmed income-tax liability, no exemption or losses, has net taxable income of KWD 100,000 and files 70 days late. Assume its tax payments were timely and no statutory excuse or remission applies; only the filing fine is calculated. Tax is KWD 15,000. Seventy days is three 30-day blocks or parts, so the fine is 3% of the tax, KWD 450.

```oa-check
name: Foreign branch, net taxable income KWD 100,000, filed 70 days late
inputs:
  income: 100000
  late_days: 70
steps:
  - blocks: ceil(late_days / 30)
  - tax: income * 0.15
  - fine: tax * 0.01 * blocks
  - total: tax + fine
expected:
  blocks: 3
  tax: 15000.00
  fine: 450.00
  total: 15450.00
```

**Example 2 (decision).** Tax per return is KWD 40,000. Paying in one sum puts KWD 40,000 out on the first date. Paying by instalments puts KWD 10,000 out on each of the four dates, with no fine if each date is met. Instalments win on cash unless the company prefers to close the liability. Where an extension is granted, all instalments already due are paid together when the return goes in (reg. art. 28).

```oa-check
name: Decision: pay in one sum or four instalments, tax KWD 40,000
inputs:
  tax: 40000
steps:
  - one_sum: tax
  - instalment: tax / 4
  - first_due: instalment
expected:
  one_sum: 40000.00
  instalment: 10000.00
  first_due: 10000.00
```

## Ask the client first

- Is the group's consolidated revenue EUR 750m or more in two of the last four periods?
- Is the entity foreign, Kuwaiti or Gulf owned, and in what shares?
- Is it a public or closed shareholding company, and is it listed?
- What is the period end, and was an extension requested in time?
- Does it hold a valid tax card, and what retention is held by customers?
- Does it hold a Law 116 of 2013 exemption?
