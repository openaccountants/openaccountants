# French Polynesia salary CST reference v0.1

This conditional reference supplies selected salary CST rules and raw components for a qualified reviewer's working paper. CST-S is a tax collected through the DICP/Recette des impôts; this file does not establish CPS social-insurance liabilities. Load both declared bases. Complete all mandatory country slots, outputs, checks, tools and professional review before delivering an actual financial result.

## Scope and source dates

The principal source is the tax code displaying a version in force at 3 July 2026. Its full 249-page provider extraction was captured on 30 October 2026; original PDF bytes and native/rendered verification of that compilation remain unavailable. Selected provisions are corroborated by the separately downloaded original law, official forms and rate note below. Later amendments have not been comprehensively established. An HTTP 200 loading shell on the official amendment index proves no absence of amendments.

This guide covers selected treatment of salary, pension and related CST income, ordinary progressive arithmetic, multiple-source monthly aggregation and captured declaration interfaces. It does not supply a complete personal tax return, CST-NS business tax, CPS contributions, business formation, reporting, property, cross-border entitlement or net payroll. Actual residence, category, exemptions, benefit valuation, temporal allocation, precision, assessment and settlement remain evidence gates. Source acquisition and guide presence do not complete French Polynesia coverage.

## Taxable persons and base

Article 193-1 covers the named income categories and persons domiciled in French Polynesia. Its domicile branches include a home/principal place of stay or professional activity in French Polynesia. Do not substitute metropolitan residence tests or infer cross-border relief from this wording. Establish the applicable jurisdiction and requested period first. [S1]

LP.193-5(1) uses total gross remuneration, including fixed pay, commissions, profit-related remuneration, bonuses, allowances, index/majoration components and benefits in kind. S1 expressly includes remuneration paid to directors of legal persons. Benefits in kind use the CPS employee-regime valuation rules; only in the absence of a forfait valuation rule does the provision use actual value, defined by the normal tax-inclusive cost at effective availability. Obtain the applicable valuation rules before supplying an amount. A CPS contribution ceiling does not become a CST ceiling. [S1 LP.193-5; S4]

Preserve the detailed inclusion and exclusion branches. LP.193-5(2) addresses specified company officers, pensions/annuities, elected/public office remuneration and termination payments. LP.193-5(3) excludes specified family/assistance benefits, maintenance payments, professional-expense indemnities, occupational-injury and military-disability payments, certain non-salaried directors' remuneration, specified damages and prejudice components and compulsory-retirement indemnities. Each exclusion needs its exact statutory conditions and supporting facts. Economic-redundancy/social-plan relief depends on the separate order and its limit; do not invent that limit. LP.193-6 requires an express allocation by nature for the specified global termination payments. [S1 LP.193-5 and LP.193-6]

LP.193-7's participation/profit-sharing exemption requires the qualifying agreement, coverage/seniority, decision timing and non-substitution conditions. For an employee shareholder, its additional dividend comparison applies. Exempt sums must still appear on the annex. S4's exemption labels do not prove eligibility for exceptional purchasing-power bonuses or any other exemption. Voluntary-departure taxation under LP.193-10-1 is a separate branch, with its own 20-month maximum and downward month-count rounding; it supplies no ordinary CST product-rounding rule. Hold these special branches until all conditions are established. [S1; S4]

## Selected rates, thresholds and computation order

The following progressive bands are printed in S1 LP.193-15, S2 and S3. S2 starts them on 1 January 2022. S1's stability guarantee through income paid for 2024 does not itself establish either expiry at that date or a complete later amendment search. Select the requested-period operative schedule before actual use.

| Monthly income fraction, F CFP | Marginal rate |
|---|---|
| Up to 150,000 | 0.5% |
| 150,001–250,000 | 3% |
| 250,001–400,000 | 5% |
| 400,001–700,000 | 9% |
| 700,001–1,000,000 | 11% |
| 1,000,001–1,250,000 | 15% |
| 1,250,001–1,500,000 | 18% |
| 1,500,001–1,750,000 | 21% |
| 1,750,001–2,000,000 | 24% |
| 2,000,001–2,500,000 | 26% |
| Above 2,500,000 | 28% |

For the integer-franc fictional bases below, allocate each marginal fraction once. A higher band taxes the excess above the preceding upper limit; do not subtract an extra franc because the printed next band starts at 150,001. Do not apply the top marginal rate to the whole salary, annualise the monthly bands or replace actual monthly income by a semester average. Preserve exact raw amounts until each legally required precision stage is established. [S1 LP.193-10, LP.193-11 and LP.193-15; S3]

Article 193-26, subject to article 193-11, provides no withholding by the payer and no recovery where the monthly contribution per person is strictly below 750 F CFP. This is a non-collection rule, not a minimum charge or a 750 F CFP allowance. At an exactly 150,000 F CFP ordinary monthly base, the raw contribution is 750 F CFP and is outside that strict-below rule. S3 separately identifies income below 150,000 and taxable income at or above it. Keep the multiple-source exception, operative precision and any other recovery rule separate. [S1 193-26; S3]

For 2 or more payers, LP.193-11 calculates on the person's total taxable income received monthly, deducting contributions actually withheld by each payer. Preserve income and evidenced withholding by person, payer and month; no credit for an unevidenced amount and no combining different people's incomes. Declarations are semestrial, while assessment remains monthly. Article 193-25 assigns collection to the paierie and LP.193-28 provides regularisation by assessment roll. A raw reconciliation is not the issued assessment or permission to self-pay a guessed balance. [S1; S5]

For deferred or complementary income, LP.193-10 reallocates income to the months to which it relates, or to the last non-prescribed month as applicable, recomputes the supplementary CST and adds the complement(s) to CST on current income. Do not tax every arrears payment solely in the payment month. Establish the income character, attribution, prescription, original bases/withholding and precision before using this branch. [S1]

## Declaration, annex and settlement interfaces

LP.193-10 requires a monthly declaration of the preceding month's income, accompanied by payment within the first 15 days of each month, subject to the quarterly provision. LP.193-12 allows a calendar-quarter return where global annual deductions are at most 240,000 F CFP; it expressly provides creation-year time proration. Establish the applicable year, basis, account regime and proration without assuming a preceding-year test, a day-count formula or automatic approval. Declarations/payment are due no later than the 15th of the month following the relevant period. S3 has 3 separate month columns; quarterly filing does not make the bands quarterly or permit averaging. Weekend/holiday adjustments, submission timestamps and actual acceptance remain unestablished. [S1 LP.193-10 and LP.193-12; S3]

S4's annex identifies the payer and period, then each beneficiary, function/income nature, cash gross excluding benefits and exempt income, benefits in kind, CST, exempt-income nature/amount and regime-exit date. It expressly includes beneficiaries with income below 150,000 F CFP. Keep all beneficiaries and the monthly register; no automatic nil-return or reporting exemption follows from non-collection. LP.193-10's declaration dispensation for payers whose monthly incomes are exclusively below 150,000 requires an express request and starts on the first day of the month following receipt of the request. Establish actual eligibility, request and administrative facts. [S1; S4]

**Annual-annex exception repealed.** S4 page 2 still reproduces the former fourth paragraph allowing electronic declarants to deposit all monthly annexes together by 15 January of the following year. S7 LP6 deletes that paragraph; LP12 and S8 establish effect from 2 July 2026. Do not use that reproduced exception as authority for a post-commencement annual annex timetable. S1 LP.193-12 still literally refers to LP.193-10's fourth paragraph for quarterly electronic annexes; after the deletion, that reference is unresolved. Do not silently renumber it, invent a replacement exception or decide transitional obligations for earlier months. Obtain the complete requested-period law and administrative instructions before an actual annex deadline. [S1; S4; S7; S8]

LP.193-11 states multiple-income filing no later than 31 January and 31 July. S5 instead prints 'avant le 31' of the month after each semester. Record the discrepancy and establish the operative deadline/inclusivity before advising on a last-day filing. A low-income multiple-source dispensation is request-based where each monthly total throughout the semester is below 150,000; the request has its own statutory deadline. Continuing registration requires declarations until an express deregistration request, subject to the administration's statutory power of deregistration. S5 includes an exit request and says to return a declaration even without multiple income during the period. Do not treat cessation or missing paper as automatic deregistration. [S1 LP.193-11; S5]

For a payer outside French Polynesia, LP.193-10 places the obligations personally on the income recipient unless the payer has formally undertaken the specified declaration/remittance obligations to the administration. Establish the undertaking and applicable cross-border law; do not assume an exemption or import a metropolitan collection route. [S1]

S3 describes cash, cheque to Trésor Public or transfer to the Recette des impôts, with the Tahiti number, tax and period identified for a transfer. S5 instead refers to payment of the assessment roll to the competent public accountant. Verify current payment instructions, references and actual settlement evidence before any action. The printed interest/penalty descriptions do not establish complete current triggers, calculation, contest procedure or assessment. No bank-description pattern, automatic offset/refund or payment acceptance is inferred. [S3; S5]
