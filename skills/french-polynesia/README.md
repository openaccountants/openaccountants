# French Polynesia (PF): tax pack

**Built from the law. Not yet approved. Written by Ryan Duguid. Not reviewed by an accountant.** French Polynesia is a French overseas collectivity with its own tax code and tax authority, the Direction des impôts et des contributions publiques (DICP, service-public.pf/dicp). Laws are published on Lexpol and by the Assemblée de la Polynésie française. Currency is the CFP franc (XPF). There is no general personal income tax; salaries bear the contribution de solidarité territoriale (CST). Tax year 2026.
