# Guyana: tax pack

Calendar year of income; tax for a year of assessment is charged on the previous year's income, so 2026 income is year of assessment 2027, with returns due on 30 April 2027. Authority: the Guyana Revenue Authority (GRA); returns are filed through GRA eServices. Guyana dollar (G$). Residents are taxed on worldwide income (foreign earned income, and foreign income of people not ordinarily resident or not domiciled, only when received in Guyana). For 2026 individuals pay 25% up to G$3,360,000 of chargeable income and 35% above, after an allowance of G$1,680,000 or one third of income. Companies pay 25%, 40% (commercial) or 45% (telephone), with a 2% of turnover floor for commercial companies. VAT is 14%. Payments to non-residents bear 20% withholding tax.
