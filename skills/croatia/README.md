# Croatia: tax pack

Calendar tax year unless a company is allowed a different business year. Authority: the Tax Administration (Porezna uprava) of the Ministry of Finance; returns go through ePorezna. Euro since 1 January 2023. Personal income tax has two rates set by each local unit within ranges in the law (default 20% and 30%, band EUR 60,000 a year); capital income is taxed at 12%. Profit tax is 10% where revenue in the period is up to EUR 1,000,000 and 18% above it (Profit Tax Act art. 28). Withholding on payments to non-residents is in Profit Tax Act article 31, with a 25% rate for EU-listed non-cooperative jurisdictions. VAT follows the EU directive. Laws are published in Narodne novine; the Tax Administration keeps consolidated texts in its register of regulations.
