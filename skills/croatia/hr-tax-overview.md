## Croatian tax system at a glance

- **Croatian tax system overview.** Croatia is an EU member state. The main taxes are personal income tax (porez na dohodak), profit tax (porez na dobit) and VAT (porez na dodanu vrijednost, PDV), plus mandatory social contributions (doprinosi). Laws are published in Narodne novine; the Tax Administration keeps consolidated texts in its register of regulations. Detail: [croatia-income-tax](croatia-income-tax.md), [hr-corporate-income-tax](hr-corporate-income-tax.md), [croatia-vat-return](croatia-vat-return.md), [croatia-social-contributions](croatia-social-contributions.md).
- **Standard tax year.** Income tax is set and paid for the calendar year (Income Tax Act art. 7(1)). Profit tax is set for a period that is as a rule the calendar year; the Tax Administration can, on request, approve a different period of no more than 12 months, which the company then cannot change for three years (Profit Tax Act art. 29(1), (2)).
- **Currency.** Euro (EUR). The kuna was replaced by the euro under the Act on the Introduction of the Euro as the Official Currency (Zakon o uvođenju eura kao službene valute u Republici Hrvatskoj, NN 57/22). The euro became the official currency and legal tender on 1 January 2023, at a fixed conversion rate of 7.53450 kuna to one euro (Government decision NN 85/22, point II). All amounts in the 2026 tax laws are in euro.
- **Tax authority.** Assessment and collection of taxes, and the application of tax treaties, are carried out by the Ministry of Finance through its administrative bodies (General Tax Act art. 51(1)); for these taxes that is the Tax Administration (Porezna uprava). Returns go through ePorezna.
- **Basis of taxation.** An individual is resident if he or she has a domicile (prebivalište) or habitual abode (uobičajeno boravište) in Croatia (Income Tax Act art. 3(1)); a person with neither who is employed in the Croatian state service and paid on that basis is also resident (art. 3(2)). A resident is taxed on income earned in Croatia and abroad (worldwide income); a non-resident only on income earned in Croatia (art. 6(1), (2)). Company residence, permanent establishment and withholding on payments abroad are in [hr-cross-border](hr-cross-border.md).
- **Who pays profit tax.** Companies and other resident persons carrying on business independently and for profit (Profit Tax Act art. 2(1)). A sole trader who keeps books may choose profit tax instead of income tax (art. 2(3)), and must pay profit tax if total receipts in the previous period were above EUR 1,000,000 (art. 2(4)).

## Deadlines for 2026

- **Corporate profit tax return deadline.** The PD return is filed no later than four months after the end of the tax period, and the tax shown is due on the last day of that period (Profit Tax Act art. 35(1), (2)). For a calendar-year company that is 30 April 2027 for 2026. The return is filed electronically (art. 35(3)), with the balance sheet and profit and loss account (art. 35(6)). Advances are paid monthly by the end of the month for the month before (art. 34(1)).
- **Annual personal income tax return deadline.** The self-employed who keep books (and seafarers in international shipping) must file the annual return (DOH) (art. 48(1)) by the end of February for the previous year (art. 50(2)). When the last day falls on a Sunday or holiday the deadline moves to the next working day (General Administrative Procedure Act art. 81(2), applied by General Tax Act art. 4), so for 2026 the DOH is due Monday 1 March 2027 (28 February 2027 is a Sunday). For employees and others not obliged to file, the Tax Administration runs a special procedure on its own data (art. 51(1)) and sends a provisional decision by 30 June (art. 54(2)); an objection can be filed by 31 July (art. 54(3)).
- **Lump-sum trades (paušalni obrt).** A trade not in VAT with receipts not above the VAT registration amount can choose to pay income tax at 12% on a deemed income set by bands (art. 82(1), (6)). Its annual report (PO-SD) is due within 15 days after the year ends (art. 82(6)), so by 15 January 2027 for 2026. The bands are in [croatia-income-tax](croatia-income-tax.md) and [croatia-tax-optimization](croatia-tax-optimization.md).
- **Contributions of the self-employed.** Due by the 15th of the month for the month before (Contributions Act art. 67, 71).
- **Annual financial statements filing (FINA).** Companies, branches of foreign businesses, credit unions and any person that pays profit tax on its whole activity (Accounting Act art. 4(2), (3)) file the balance sheet, profit and loss account and additional data for statistical purposes with the Financial Agency (FINA) by 30 April for the previous calendar year (art. 50(1)). An obrt taxed under income tax is not in that list. Companies, branches and credit unions also file their statements for public disclosure, with the audit report where one is required, no later than six months after the end of the business year; consolidated statements are due within nine months (art. 47(1), (5)). A calendar-year company that files the public-disclosure statements with the additional data by 30 April meets both duties at once (art. 50(2)).

## Worked example

**Resident with a high income, in a local unit with no rate decision.** A resident sole trader on books, no dependants, has annual income of EUR 82,200 after business expenses and contributions. The allowance is EUR 7,200 (12 x EUR 600, ZPD art. 14(1)), so the base is EUR 75,000. The first EUR 60,000 is taxed at the default lower rate of 20% and the remaining EUR 15,000 at the default higher rate of 30% (art. 19, 19.a(5)): EUR 12,000 + EUR 4,500 = EUR 16,500. In Zagreb at the top of its ranges (23% and 33%) the same base would cost more; check the unit's own decision in [croatia-income-tax](croatia-income-tax.md).

```oa-check
name: Croatia resident, income EUR 82,200, default rates 20% and 30%, 2026
inputs:
  income: 82200
  allowance_month: 600
steps:
  - allowance: allowance_month * 12
  - base: income - allowance
  - lower: min(base, 60000) * 0.20
  - higher: max(base - 60000, 0) * 0.30
  - tax: lower + higher
expected:
  allowance: 7200.00
  base: 75000.00
  tax: 16500.00
```
