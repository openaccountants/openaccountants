# Qatar: tax pack

Tax year **2026**, checked **24 October 2026**. Ordinary mainland income tax
is administered by the General Tax Authority (GTA), in Qatari riyals (QAR).
Eight Guides cover the five applicable ordinary tax domains, current VAT status
and mainland LLC entity choice. All are **source-cited drafts, tier 2**;
none carries a licensed Qatar professional's attestation.

## Domains

| Tax | Guide | Status and ordinary coverage |
|---|---|---|
| Income tax | [qa-tax-overview](qa-tax-overview.md) | Source-cited draft: taxable individual activities, exemptions, actual/elected expenses, losses and filing |
| Company tax | [qa-corporate-income-tax](qa-corporate-income-tax.md) | Source-cited draft: ownership tests, tax reconciliation, deduction limits, assets, losses and audit/return duties |
| VAT or sales tax | [qa-vat-gst](qa-vat-gst.md), [qatar-tax](qatar-tax.md) | Not applicable: VAT not implemented; both current summaries use the same rule and foreign-tax routing |
| Social contributions | [qa-payroll-social](qa-payroll-social.md) | Source-cited draft: Qatari employee base/caps, 2025 ER, leave, reporting and GCC home-scheme coordination |
| Payroll | Not applicable to excluded employment remuneration | ITL promulgation art. 2(3); SC and business-payment WHT remain separate obligations |
| Transfer pricing | [qa-transfer-pricing](qa-transfer-pricing.md) | Source-cited draft: arm's length, amended files/deadlines, mandatory CbCR and current local-filing suspension, BAPA procedure |
| Cross-border | [qa-cross-border-tax](qa-cross-border-tax.md) | Source-cited draft: WHT, foreign income/exemptions/substance/credit, PE attribution, actual disposals/cessation and approved-payer treaty relief |

[Company formation](qa-company-formation.md) is also reconciled to current
primary law: mainland LLC partners/capital, foreign-ownership approval and tax
handover. The pack preserves existing slugs. The seven tax Guides each have
two executable examples; formation is an entity-choice Guide.

## Scope and source conflicts

Specialist QFC/free-zone regimes, petroleum/government contractual rates,
detailed Pillar Two, regulated-bank/insurer special calculations, voluntary
self-employed income-slice enrolment, pension benefits, excise/customs
computations, specific reorganisations, voluntary surrogate CbCR arrangements
and treaty-rate tables require separate sources and are outside this ordinary
pack. Ordinary GCC employee coordination is covered, with actual home-state
rates/base supplied by that authority rather than a fictional GCC-wide rate.

Use the current Arabic law where the older English compilation mistranslates
exempt-income costs or the government-payer WHT accrual exception, or misnumbers
exemptions. The Guides identify the audit-threshold difference between ER art. 33
and the GTA investor FAQ, and apply the statute rather than silently replacing
it. The legal register omits ER art. 37(5)–(6); the GTA Arabic compilation
preserves them and the amending decision changes only paragraphs (3)–(4).
