# UK–Gibraltar: employment, pensions and related income

> **General reference only.** This Guide is general tax/accounting reference material for AI-assisted workflows. It has not been reviewed for any specific person's facts, documents, elections, deadlines, residency, filing status, or local procedures. Do not rely on it to file, pay, amend, or take a tax position without review by a qualified professional in the relevant jurisdiction.

## Who this applies to

### Establish residence, period and income type


This Guide applies the agreement's allocation rules to individuals in the UK–Gibraltar corridor in 2026. Establish domestic liabilities and Article 4 treaty residence before applying them. For dual-resident individuals the sequence is permanent home, centre of vital interests where both have a home, habitual abode where required, then competent-authority agreement. Do not use a nationality tie-breaker absent from Article 4. The [investment-income Guide](gi-uk-investment-income-treaty.md) gives the fuller entitlement and commencement analysis. Withholding provisions apply from 1 May 2020; the relevant income-tax assessment years start from 6 April 2020 in the UK and 1 July 2020 in Gibraltar. [Articles 4 and 29; official commencement note](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/gibraltar-tax-treaties/2019-uk-gibraltar-double-taxation-agreement-in-force).

Identify ordinary employment, board fees, personal entertainment/sport, government service, pensions and student maintenance separately. Payment into a Gibraltar bank account does not by itself determine where employment is exercised or a pension arises. Article 1(3) preserves resident taxation subject to its listed exceptions, including relief under Article 22. Consequently, permission for one territory to tax does not by itself exempt the income in the other. [Articles 1(3), 14–19 and 22](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/gibraltar-tax-treaties/2019-uk-gibraltar-double-taxation-agreement-in-force).

## The base

### Ordinary employment and all three short-stay conditions


Subject to Articles 15, 17 and 18, Article 14(1) ordinarily allocates employment remuneration to the residence territory alone unless employment is exercised in the other territory. That other territory can tax remuneration derived from the employment exercised there. Analyse the duties and their location; a foreign employer or foreign payroll alone does not exempt local work. [Article 14(1)](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/gibraltar-tax-treaties/2019-uk-gibraltar-double-taxation-agreement-in-force#article-14-income-from-employment).

Article 14(2) restores exclusive residence-territory taxation for work exercised in the other territory only when **all three** conditions hold:

1. Presence in the other territory does not exceed **183 days in aggregate in any twelve-month period commencing or ending in the fiscal year concerned**.
2. Remuneration is paid by, or on behalf of, an employer not resident in that other territory.
3. Remuneration is not borne by the employer's permanent establishment in that other territory.

Exactly 183 days passes only the first limb. The test is not a single calendar-year or tax-year total, not 183 workdays, and not a domestic residence test. Reconcile travel records across year boundaries and document the employer and cost-bearing arrangements. The agreement text does not supply a complete arrival/departure-day counting method or decide every economic-employer/recharge question; verify those before applying a real case. [Article 14(2)](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/gibraltar-tax-treaties/2019-uk-gibraltar-double-taxation-agreement-in-force#article-14-income-from-employment).




### Directors, performers and transport crew


Article 15 allows the company's residence territory to tax fees and similar payments received in the capacity of a member of its board. Distinguish board remuneration from a director's separate employment duties; Article 14's short-stay exception is not a blanket exemption for board fees. [Article 15](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/gibraltar-tax-treaties/2019-uk-gibraltar-double-taxation-agreement-in-force#article-15-directors-fees).

Article 16 allows the territory where an entertainer or sportsperson performs personal activities to tax the resulting income notwithstanding Article 14. Paragraph (2) also covers income accruing to another person instead of the performer, overriding Articles 7 and 14. A short performance or payment through a company does not itself establish treaty exemption. [Article 16](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/gibraltar-tax-treaties/2019-uk-gibraltar-double-taxation-agreement-in-force#article-16-artistes-and-sportsmen).

Article 14(3) gives exclusive residence-territory taxation for remuneration of the regular complement of a ship or aircraft for employment aboard it in international traffic, excluding operation solely within the other territory. Establish the actual vessel/aircraft operations and duties; do not apply the rule to every transport-company worker or office employee. Article 3(1)(h) defines international traffic. [Articles 3 and 14(3)](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/gibraltar-tax-treaties/2019-uk-gibraltar-double-taxation-agreement-in-force).


### Government service and the Regiment exception


Article 18(1)(a) allocates qualifying salaries and similar remuneration, **other than pensions**, paid by a territory, political subdivision or local authority for services to it exclusively to the paying territory. Paragraph (1)(b) instead allocates exclusively to the other territory when services are rendered there and the individual is resident there without having become resident solely to render those services. Do not replace that actual test with a nationality condition. [Article 18](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/gibraltar-tax-treaties/2019-uk-gibraltar-double-taxation-agreement-in-force#article-18-government-service).

Protocol paragraph 3 disapplies Article 18(1)(b) for remuneration for service as a member of the **Royal Gibraltar Regiment**. It is not a blanket exemption for all service-connected receipts. For services connected with a business carried on by a territory, subdivision or authority, Article 18(2) routes remuneration and pensions to Articles 14–17. Identify the payer and whether the services concern a government business before applying Article 18. [Article 18(2); Protocol paragraph 3](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/gibraltar-tax-treaties/2019-uk-gibraltar-double-taxation-agreement-in-force).


### Pensions: source taxation and a conflicting summary


Article 17 permits the territory **where a pension or similar remuneration arises** to tax it. It does not say that pensions are taxable only in the recipient's residence territory. Article 18(1) expressly excludes pensions. The same language appears in Gibraltar's official PDF at printed page 23. Verify pension character, source, domestic exemptions and both liabilities separately; neither a transfer of residence nor the existence of the agreement proves a zero UK withholding entitlement. Lump sums, transfers and scheme classifications require separate domestic analysis. [Articles 17–18](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/gibraltar-tax-treaties/2019-uk-gibraltar-double-taxation-agreement-in-force#article-17-pensions); [Gibraltar official copy](https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/uploads/Income%20Tax%20Office/docs/UK-Gibraltar%20Double%20Taxation%20Agreement.pdf).

**Unresolved official-summary conflict:** HMRC DT8152's table, framed for UK-resident beneficial owners, says other pensions are taxable only in the UK, describes government pensions under Article 18 and says there is no arbitration. Those entries do not match Article 17's source-tax permission, Article 18(1)'s exclusion of pensions, or Article 24(5)'s requested arbitration provision in both published agreement copies. This Guide follows the agreement text and records the discrepancy; the reason for the summary entries and any relevant separate arrangements have not been established. Obtain a case-specific resolution before relying on the conflicting entries for a pension exemption or a dispute route. Do not silently present the table as settled law. [DT8152](https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/double-taxation-relief/dt8152); [Articles 17–18 and 24](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/gibraltar-tax-treaties/2019-uk-gibraltar-double-taxation-agreement-in-force).


### Offshore employment has a separate rule


Article 21 takes priority over other agreement provisions and defines offshore activities by reference to exploration or exploitation of the seabed, subsoil and their natural resources. Under paragraph (7), the offshore territory can tax employment remuneration to the extent duties are performed offshore there. The ordinary 183-day exception is not the controlling test. Paragraph (8) instead allocates exclusively to employee residence for employment aboard ships/aircraft transporting supplies or personnel to/between offshore activity locations, or ships undertaking the specified towing/anchor-handling operations. Verify the actual operation. [Article 21(1)–(2), (5), (7)–(8)](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/gibraltar-tax-treaties/2019-uk-gibraltar-double-taxation-agreement-in-force#article-21-miscellaneous-rules-applicable-to-certain-offshore-activities).

The separate **30-day** exception in Article 21(4) concerns the enterprise's deemed permanent establishment under paragraph (3), including aggregation for associated enterprises; it is not an employee income-tax exemption. A short offshore assignment must be assessed under paragraphs (7)–(8), not by importing that enterprise threshold. [Article 21(3)–(8)](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/gibraltar-tax-treaties/2019-uk-gibraltar-double-taxation-agreement-in-force).


### Student and apprentice maintenance


Article 19 exempts qualifying maintenance, education or training payments in the visited territory when a student or business apprentice is there solely for education/training, is or immediately before visiting was resident in the other territory, and the payments arise from outside the visited territory. This does not exempt every local part-time wage merely because the worker is a student. Document prior residence, purpose and payment source. [Article 19](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/gibraltar-tax-treaties/2019-uk-gibraltar-double-taxation-agreement-in-force#article-19-students).


### Double-tax relief: Gibraltar-resident example


Article 22(1) permits Gibraltar to include income that the agreement allows the UK to tax in a resident's tax base, then requires a deduction from tax equal to the lesser of Gibraltar tax due or UK tax due **on that same income**. Article 22(3) excludes this provision where UK tax is permitted under the agreement solely because the income is also derived by a UK resident. Establish the UK taxing right and same-income amounts; do not credit all UK deductions without checking that basis. [Article 22(1), (3)](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/gibraltar-tax-treaties/2019-uk-gibraltar-double-taxation-agreement-in-force#article-22-elimination-of-double-taxation).



For UK residents, Article 22(2)(a) provides credit for qualifying Gibraltar tax on the same profits, income or gains subject to UK credit law and the agreement; paragraph (4) excludes tax justified solely by Gibraltar residence. Do not simply reverse the worked example without checking those UK rules. The [domestic Gibraltar foreign-tax-relief Guide](gi-foreign-tax-relief.md) addresses section 37 separately; its domestic six-year claim rule is not established here as a universal treaty claim deadline. [Article 22(2), (4)](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/gibraltar-tax-treaties/2019-uk-gibraltar-double-taxation-agreement-in-force).

Prepare a residence and income-classification memo, travel log across relevant twelve-month windows, duties/remuneration allocation, contracts, employer residence and permanent-establishment cost evidence, pension source/scheme documents, both tax computations and payment/assessment evidence. Record authority correspondence and every domestic filing, payment and appeal deadline separately. This is a recommended evidence file, not a prescribed form list.

## Two worked examples that recompute

**Example: cross-year presence.** Assume verified countable presence of 100 days followed by 83 days, all within a relevant twelve-month window. The total is 183. If every relevant window passes and both employer limbs are met, the short-stay exception applies. One additional countable day makes 184 and fails the presence limb; it does not mean only the 184th day's remuneration becomes taxable. If remuneration is borne by the employer's host permanent establishment, even 60 days does not satisfy the three-limb exception. These examples determine the exception, not the amount assessable under domestic law.

```oa-check
name: UK Gibraltar employment presence boundary
inputs:
  first_visit_counted_days: 100
  second_visit_counted_days: 83
steps:
  - window_days: first_visit_counted_days + second_visit_counted_days
  - boundary_headroom: 183 - window_days
  - with_extra_day: window_days + 1
expected:
  window_days: 183
  boundary_headroom: 0
  with_extra_day: 184
```
**Example: confirmed UK-source pension.** Assume a Gibraltar treaty resident, pension classification and UK source established, UK tax due on that income £2,000 and Gibraltar tax due on it before relief £3,000, with no paragraph (3) exclusion. The credit is **£2,000**, leaving Gibraltar tax **£1,000**. If UK tax due is £4,000 on the same assumed income, the credit is capped at £3,000 and leaves zero Gibraltar tax. The £1,000 above that cap is not a Gibraltar refund. These are assumed liabilities, not pension rates, and do not establish that this person's pension is actually taxable in Gibraltar.

```oa-check
name: UK Gibraltar same pension income credit ceiling
inputs:
  gibraltar_tax_before_relief: 3000
  uk_tax_first_case: 2000
  uk_tax_second_case: 4000
steps:
  - first_credit: min(gibraltar_tax_before_relief, uk_tax_first_case)
  - first_gibraltar_balance: gibraltar_tax_before_relief - first_credit
  - second_credit: min(gibraltar_tax_before_relief, uk_tax_second_case)
  - second_gibraltar_balance: gibraltar_tax_before_relief - second_credit
  - second_uk_tax_above_credit: uk_tax_second_case - second_credit
expected:
  first_credit: 2000
  first_gibraltar_balance: 1000
  second_credit: 3000
  second_gibraltar_balance: 0
  second_uk_tax_above_credit: 1000
```

## Ask the client first

1. Which domestic/treaty residence and exact assessment periods apply?
2. What kind of income, source, duty location and relevant 12-month travel windows are evidenced?
3. Who employs/pays, where are they resident, and who bears the cost through any PE?
4. What pension scheme/source documents and same-income tax liabilities exist?
5. Which payroll authorisations, claims, payments, appeals and special-status decisions already exist?
