# Gibraltar Category 2 certificate, income tax and IT1C v0.1

> **General reference only.** This Guide is general tax/accounting reference material for AI-assisted workflows. It has not been reviewed for any specific person's facts, documents, elections, deadlines, residency, filing status, or local procedures. Do not rely on it to file, pay, amend, or take a tax position without review by a qualified professional in the relevant jurisdiction.

## Who this applies to

This is the distinct Category 2 job excluded from the [ordinary individual comparison](gi-individual-income-tax.md). It addresses certificates under the current Qualifying (Category 2) Individuals Rules 2004, their family elections and the actual IT1C 2026 return for **1 July 2025–30 June 2026**, including its separate 2026/27 payment section. Keep immigration, work-permission and treaty-residence evidence separate; this calculation does not establish those outcomes. Legacy Qualifying/HNWI status and HEPSS are different certificate routes; do not select their IT1C certificate boxes merely because they also use that form. [Rules 2–3, 5, 11 and 14](https://www.gibraltarlaws.gov.gi/uploads/legislations/income-tax/2004s070/2004s070(22-06-26).pdf); [IT1C pages 1 and 3](https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/uploads/Income%20Tax%20Office/Self-Employed/IT1C_2026.pdf).

### Qualification decision before calculation

1. Record the application assessment year and each of the five preceding assessment years. Rule 4 excludes presence **exceeding 183 days in any one** of those years or **an average exceeding 90 days in any three** of them. It is not the ordinary current-year residence test or a requirement that the selected three years be consecutive. Exactly 183 days does not cross that particular limb; other limbs still apply. Record previous Gibraltar trade, business, profession, vocation and employment separately. The incidental-to-an-overseas-activity exception applies to the activity limb, not automatically to the day tests. Only the Director can exercise the rule 4(3) economic-development waiver. [Rule 4](https://www.gibraltarlaws.gov.gi/uploads/legislations/income-tax/2004s070/2004s070(22-06-26).pdf).
2. Obtain approved accommodation for exclusive residential use. The definition includes the stated family and occasional non-paying guests, but excludes letting or leasing of any sort. Rule 3 requires availability for the whole assessment year, with the application-year requirement running from certificate issue for the remaining part of that year. An unapproved property or occasional hotel stay is not substituted for the approval requirement. Record application, issue and effective dates separately: rule 6 makes effectiveness run from when the Director is satisfied that the conditions were first met. [Rules 2–3 and 6](https://www.gibraltarlaws.gov.gi/uploads/legislations/income-tax/2004s070/2004s070(22-06-26).pdf).
3. Establish the Director's rule 6 decision on substantial and sound financial standing, good character and Gibraltar's reputation. The current application requires proof of financial standing and the documents below. The **18 June 2026 announcement** states an intention to move new applicants' minimum net wealth from £2m to £5m and grandfather existing holders. The enacted 22 June amendment changes the fee only; neither it nor the current application/checklist establishes the effective implementation or full measurement of that announced wealth policy. Do not certify eligibility solely by applying either headline wealth number. Retain the actual current criteria and approval decision for the applicant. [Rule 6](https://www.gibraltarlaws.gov.gi/uploads/legislations/income-tax/2004s070/2004s070(22-06-26).pdf); [announcement](https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/press-releases/government-announces-changes-to-category-2-regime-4692026-12107); [enacted amendment](https://www.gibraltarlaws.gov.gi/uploads/legislations/income-tax/2004s070/2026=127.pdf); [current application](https://www.gibraltarfinance.gi/uploads/forms/Cat%202%20application%20form%20-%20wef%20June%2026.pdf).
4. Identify every proposed Gibraltar activity before signing the declaration. The Director can permit the listed activities on the economic-development condition. Permitted Gibraltar-source activity income remains taxable under rule 10(4); permission does not place it inside the cap. The rule 5(3) incidental-overseas-activity exception has its own facts. Do not claim that all work is forbidden or that any directorship is automatically permitted. [Rule 5 and rule 10(4)](https://www.gibraltarlaws.gov.gi/uploads/legislations/income-tax/2004s070/2004s070(22-06-26).pdf).

## The base

### Derive the income columns before applying the cap

Make a transaction-level schedule showing source/payer, income type, accrual and receipt location, gross amount, applicable Act charge or exemption, supporting document, any election and the destination column below. Section 11 and the applicable Schedule 1 class are the starting point; a bank receipt or certificate label alone does not establish chargeable income. A benefit equivalent to foreign income can constitute Gibraltar receipt under section 11(3). Retain the ordinary [individual method](gi-individual-income-tax.md) for the applicable income/deduction rules, without importing its GIBS election into this job. [Act 11 and 25](https://www.gibraltarlaws.gov.gi/uploads/legislations/income-tax/2010-21/2010-21(23-03-26).pdf); [Rules 9(1)–(2)](https://www.gibraltarlaws.gov.gi/uploads/legislations/income-tax/2004s070/2004s070(22-06-26).pdf).

- **Excluded foreign income:** rule 10(1) excludes the specified foreign dividends, pensions and emoluments of office from Category 2 assessable income. Rule 10(2) permits an election to include all or part for the particular assessment year. Record the income and election expressly; completing the optional outside-Gibraltar section is not proof that a particular election was accepted. Do not describe every overseas receipt, every salary or every trust distribution as automatically excluded. Apply its actual charging/exemption provisions first. [Rule 10(1)–(2)](https://www.gibraltarlaws.gov.gi/uploads/legislations/income-tax/2004s070/2004s070(22-06-26).pdf); [Act 11](https://www.gibraltarlaws.gov.gi/uploads/legislations/income-tax/2010-21/2010-21(23-03-26).pdf).
- **Certificate-capped column:** include chargeable amounts remaining within rule 9(4)(a) after exclusions and elections. Rule 9(2) adopts the rules made under section 25 for taxable income. Support each expense, exemption and ABS deduction with its eligibility rule and allocate it once; do not deduct a personal allowance again from the other column. This Guide does not create a new allowance or make a payment eligible merely because the form lacks an allowance box. Attach the calculation supporting taxable income. [Rules 9(1)–(4)](https://www.gibraltarlaws.gov.gi/uploads/legislations/income-tax/2004s070/2004s070(22-06-26).pdf); [Act 25](https://www.gibraltarlaws.gov.gi/uploads/legislations/income-tax/2010-21/2010-21(23-03-26).pdf).
- **Uncapped Gibraltar column:** separately derive chargeable property income from Gibraltar rents, premiums and other property profits under rule 10(3), and permitted Gibraltar-source activity income under rule 10(4). Reconcile gross rent/business receipts to the supported taxable profit, distinguishing revenue costs from capital/outlay and non-deductible items under Schedule 3. Rule 10(5) applies ABS rates; rule 10(6) includes the £118,000 amount in assessable income when determining the rate on this additional income. Under the current full bands that puts positive additional taxable income above the £16,000 lower-band boundary, at 39%; it is not sheltered by unused capped-income headroom. Preserve the Schedule 3 profit computation and deduction allocation rather than taxing gross property proceeds. [Rule 10(3)–(6)](https://www.gibraltarlaws.gov.gi/uploads/legislations/income-tax/2004s070/2004s070(22-06-26).pdf); [Act 25 and Schedule 3](https://www.gibraltarlaws.gov.gi/uploads/legislations/income-tax/2010-21/2010-21(23-03-26).pdf); [Rates rule 2](https://www.gibraltarlaws.gov.gi/uploads/legislations/income-tax/1989s096/1989s096(01-07-24).pdf).

The following is a statutory working-paper reading of rules 9–10. For a full-band calculation, calculate the capped tax on the smaller of its supported taxable column and £118,000, then add the rule 10(3)/(4) component at its applicable rate. Record any legally available credit separately. Section 60(6) denies the usual tax credit to a Category 2 individual except for its stated dividends assessable because of rule 10(4); do not automatically copy a company's tax credit into the capped column. Where that exception is proved, apply section 60's gross-up/credit rules with the company's evidence. Apply the relevant rule 9 minimum to the resulting tax payable. The floor is not a deduction, additional income or a second charge added to the computed liability. Shared-deduction allocation and the minimum/credit ordering in mixed uncapped-income or credit cases remain review points; do not finalise those disputed cases from this draft. [Rules 9–10](https://www.gibraltarlaws.gov.gi/uploads/legislations/income-tax/2004s070/2004s070(22-06-26).pdf); [Act 60(1)–(6)](https://www.gibraltarlaws.gov.gi/uploads/legislations/income-tax/2010-21/2010-21(23-03-26).pdf).

Only the minimum is expressly determined by complete/part certificate months in rule 9(4)(c)–(d). Use actual-year income, not an automatic fraction of £42,380. The printed £3,083.33 gives £36,999.96 for twelve months under those first/cessation-year provisions; do not silently replace that printed multiplication with an invented rounding rule. Part-year ABS band/deduction eligibility, including the Rates rule 2(2) legacy section reference, needs the applicable ordinary method and exact facts before a non-zero part-year calculation is final. The zero-income minimum example below does not depend on resolving that reference. [Rule 9(4)](https://www.gibraltarlaws.gov.gi/uploads/legislations/income-tax/2004s070/2004s070(22-06-26).pdf); [Rates rule 2(2)](https://www.gibraltarlaws.gov.gi/uploads/legislations/income-tax/1989s096/1989s096(01-07-24).pdf).

### Family and continuing status

A written rule 11 election can attribute a spouse/civil partner's or qualifying child's income to the certificate holder. The family member must not be excluded by rule 4. A child is unmarried and meets the stated under-18-at-year-start, full-time-education or articles/indentures limb; do not impose an unstated age limit on the latter limbs. Keep the election, identity/relationship evidence and income schedule. Withdrawal is effective from written notice; the specified commencement of disqualifying activity has its own deemed-withdrawal rule. A family member can instead apply in their own right with the shared-property exclusive-use deeming. Do not claim two separate individual caps/floors for income attributed to one holder. [Rule 11](https://www.gibraltarlaws.gov.gi/uploads/legislations/income-tax/2004s070/2004s070(22-06-26).pdf).

The certificate is indefinite subject to continuing conditions and the endorsement cycle. Retain evidence of accommodation, permitted activities, financial standing, character and the rule 7 tax-payment conditions as printed. Revocation can run from the Director's identified failure date; a declaration of no chargeability triggers immediate revocation under rule 7(4). Rule 8 advance-tax refunds apply on rejection or relinquishment/surrender, with outstanding tax deducted on surrender. A refundable advance is different from the non-refundable application fee. Rule 14 preserves specified older certificates/legacy elections; do not treat a legacy HNWI certificate as a new Category 2 application or apply its historic 2002–04 two-year transition to a 2026 applicant. [Rules 6–8 and 12–14](https://www.gibraltarlaws.gov.gi/uploads/legislations/income-tax/2004s070/2004s070(22-06-26).pdf).

## Deadlines and filing

### Apply using the current Finance Centre pack

Use the June 2026 application and checklist linked by Gibraltar Finance. Complete block capitals: personal/passport/nationality/occupation/address details; family name, birth date, relationship and Rule 11 election column; the accommodation, prior-presence/activity and rule 5 declarations; signature, date and data-consent declaration. Supply the cover letter, passport, CV, character reference, bank letter confirming account-opening date, proof of financial standing and Gibraltar-regulated-intermediary reliance letter. Explain intended Gibraltar business activity for approval. For a family election supply the requested passport, marriage/birth and school-enrolment evidence. These are the authority's published application requirements, not a promise of approval. [Application pages 1–3](https://www.gibraltarfinance.gi/uploads/forms/Cat%202%20application%20form%20-%20wef%20June%2026.pdf); [checklist page 1](https://www.gibraltarfinance.gi/uploads/forms/Cat%202%20application%20checklist%20-%20wef%20June%2026%20(2).pdf).

The checklist says submit by email to **fcd.category2applications@financecentre.gov.gi**; processing awaits the non-refundable fee and all required documents. Keep separate evidence of the £5,000 fee and the rule 8 application advance. Do not send the fee to a guessed tax-payment category or treat either remittance as the certificate itself. Retain the applicable Finance Centre payment instructions and actual receipts. [Checklist page 1](https://www.gibraltarfinance.gi/uploads/forms/Cat%202%20application%20checklist%20-%20wef%20June%2026%20(2).pdf); [rule 8](https://www.gibraltarlaws.gov.gi/uploads/legislations/income-tax/2004s070/2004s070(22-06-26).pdf).

Identify the property and obtain approval **before** entering a purchase/rental agreement. The checklist requires notification and the accommodation form plus rental agreement/deed within two months of application approval; the application declaration uses certificate-approval wording. Record the actual approval letter and both triggers rather than waiting for formal issue where the checklist says issue awaits the deed/contract. The accommodation form asks for estate agent, property address, offer price/rent, proposed buyer/tenant, occupants/relationships, internal/external area, original-plan bedrooms, bathrooms and signed contact details. Future address changes require advance approval under the checklist. [Checklist page 2](https://www.gibraltarfinance.gi/uploads/forms/Cat%202%20application%20checklist%20-%20wef%20June%2026%20(2).pdf); [application page 2](https://www.gibraltarfinance.gi/uploads/forms/Cat%202%20application%20form%20-%20wef%20June%2026.pdf); [accommodation form](https://www.gibraltarfinance.gi/uploads/forms/CAT%202%20Approval%20of%20Residential%20Accommodation%20Form.pdf).

The checklist also requires the medical-insurance certificate within two months of certificate approval, Gibraltar coverage, at least £100,000 policy value per insured person and the listed inpatient/outpatient, professional fees, diagnostic, therapy, surgical/drug/dressing and European evacuation/repatriation cover. Include elected family members. These are checklist conditions; do not relabel the insurance premium as an automatically deductible expense. [Checklist page 2](https://www.gibraltarfinance.gi/uploads/forms/Cat%202%20application%20checklist%20-%20wef%20June%2026%20(2).pdf).

### Reconcile three cash obligations, then make the actual transfer

Keep separate columns for (a) the rule 8 application/annual maximum-tax advance, (b) statutory payments on account and (c) the return's balance. Rule 8(2) specifies **30 November in the assessment year** for its advance. Section 39(2)(b) restricts the statutory payment-on-account basis to its named income classes, whereas IT1C prints a same-as-box-1 estimate. Its two form instalments do not repeal rule 8, prove how an existing advance was credited, or authorise paying the same liability twice. Reconcile the actual tax account by year and payment type before deriving cash still payable; keep the assessment/account allocation and any section 39(5) reduction decision. This cash-allocation interaction is not settled by adding the form's boxes. [Rule 8](https://www.gibraltarlaws.gov.gi/uploads/legislations/income-tax/2004s070/2004s070(22-06-26).pdf); [Act 39(2)–(6)](https://www.gibraltarlaws.gov.gi/uploads/legislations/income-tax/2010-21/2010-21(23-03-26).pdf); [IT1C page 1](https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/uploads/Income%20Tax%20Office/Self-Employed/IT1C_2026.pdf).

Open the [official payment page](https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/income-tax-office/report-changes), follow **Income Tax Online Payment Portal** to `https://tax.egov.gi/`, then **Fill in a payment form online**. The public blank form begins with contact **Name, Phone, Email** and **Next**. The published service guide requires the taxpayer's name/TIN where paying for someone else and a plan identifying payment type, period and amount. Prepare separate lines for the correct taxpayer/year/type; check the totals before **Finalize and Review**. The guide says completion provides a payment confirmation with bank details and an emailed copy. Quote its generated **PAYMENT REFERENCE** in the actual bank transfer. A payment plan or email confirmation does not itself transfer money. Retain the plan/reference, bank execution evidence and resulting tax-account allocation. The public entry and instructions were read without entering taxpayer data, creating a plan or making a transfer. [Payment service guide](https://tax.egov.gi/Downloads/Tax_Portal_Payment_Service_Guide.pdf); [official payment route](https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/income-tax-office/report-changes).

The downloads page still links May 2011 qualifying-individual notes. Their December instalment date and old surcharge/penalty structure are historical, not the current timetable. Current section 39 and IT1C 2026 specify January/June instalments. [Historical notes](https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/new/sites/default/files/1/01/Guidance_notes-Qualifying_Individuals.pdf); [current section 39](https://www.gibraltarlaws.gov.gi/uploads/legislations/income-tax/2010-21/2010-21(23-03-26).pdf); [current form](https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/uploads/Income%20Tax%20Office/Self-Employed/IT1C_2026.pdf).

## Penalties and interest

Section 65(2) provides £50 for a section 28 return failure, then further £300 after three months and £500 after six months if it continues. Section 64 adds 10% of unpaid tax immediately after the due date; its further 20% applies to tax **and surcharge** still unpaid at the statutory 90-day point. IT1C's sentence saying 90 days after “the date of payment” does not replace the Act's due-date/surcharge trigger. Apply actual dates and intervening payments, not an invented flat interest charge. Certificate revocation/payment conditions are a separate rule 7 issue. [Act 64–65](https://www.gibraltarlaws.gov.gi/uploads/legislations/income-tax/2010-21/2010-21(23-03-26).pdf); [IT1C page 1](https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/uploads/Income%20Tax%20Office/Self-Employed/IT1C_2026.pdf); [rule 7](https://www.gibraltarlaws.gov.gi/uploads/legislations/income-tax/2004s070/2004s070(22-06-26).pdf).

## Cross-border points

A certificate and approved home do not determine ordinary residence under every statute, immigration rights, source, foreign tax or treaty entitlement. Retain residence/travel and income-source evidence, and use the [Spain residence](gi-spain-tax-residence.md), [UK treaty](gi-uk-employment-pensions-treaty.md) and [foreign-relief](gi-foreign-tax-relief.md) jobs where relevant. Do not assume the foreign dividends/pension/office-income exclusion is a foreign-country exemption, or that a voluntary inclusion election creates a foreign tax credit. The rule 11 family election changes this computation's attribution; it does not itself change another country's tax result. [Rules 4–5 and 10–11](https://www.gibraltarlaws.gov.gi/uploads/legislations/income-tax/2004s070/2004s070(22-06-26).pdf); [Act 11](https://www.gibraltarlaws.gov.gi/uploads/legislations/income-tax/2010-21/2010-21(23-03-26).pdf).

## Two worked examples that recompute

### 1. Capped income, separate property profit and the full-year floor

Assume a full-year continuing certificate, full ABS bands, a documented rule 10(2) election including £150,000 foreign income within that rule, £4,000 of eligible deductions allocated once to that component, and no available credit. The £4,000 is a hypothetical verified deduction total, not a new statutory allowance. Assume Gibraltar rental receipts £12,000 and £2,000 allowable revenue costs, with no further deductions. The capped taxable component is £146,000, limited to £118,000; property profit is £10,000 outside the cap, taxed above the lower-band boundary. Total is **£46,280**, so the £37,000 floor does not increase it. The maximum £42,380 is therefore not a ceiling on this person's total liability. Without the inclusion election, the foreign-income exclusion must be recorded before recomputing; do not keep the elected amount in the base by habit. [Rules 9–10](https://www.gibraltarlaws.gov.gi/uploads/legislations/income-tax/2004s070/2004s070(22-06-26).pdf); [Act 25](https://www.gibraltarlaws.gov.gi/uploads/legislations/income-tax/2010-21/2010-21(23-03-26).pdf); [Rates rule 2](https://www.gibraltarlaws.gov.gi/uploads/legislations/income-tax/1989s096/1989s096(01-07-24).pdf).

```oa-check
name: Category 2 capped elected income and uncapped Gibraltar rental profit
inputs:
  elected_foreign_income: 150000
  eligible_deductions_allocated_once: 4000
  covered_cap: 118000
  rental_receipts: 12000
  allowable_revenue_costs: 2000
  continuing_year_floor: 37000
steps:
  - covered_taxable: max(elected_foreign_income - eligible_deductions_allocated_once, 0)
  - capped_taxable: min(covered_taxable, covered_cap)
  - capped_tax: min(capped_taxable, 4000) * 0.14 + min(max(capped_taxable - 4000, 0), 12000) * 0.17 + max(capped_taxable - 16000, 0) * 0.39
  - uncapped_property_profit: rental_receipts - allowable_revenue_costs
  - uncapped_tax: uncapped_property_profit * 0.39
  - total_before_floor: capped_tax + uncapped_tax
  - tax_payable: max(total_before_floor, continuing_year_floor)
expected:
  covered_taxable: 146000
  capped_taxable: 118000
  capped_tax: 42380
  uncapped_property_profit: 10000
  uncapped_tax: 3900
  total_before_floor: 46280
  tax_payable: 46280
```

### 2. First-year minimum and the distinct application cash columns

Assume the certificate is first in force **20 March 2027 through 30 June 2027**, with no chargeable income after the actual exclusions, no inclusion election, no additional Gibraltar property/activity income and no credit. Four complete/part months give **£12,333.32**, not four months of the £42,380 maximum. The application requires a separate £5,000 non-refundable fee and the rule 8 maximum-tax advance; their arithmetic sum is £47,380, but that is not the first-year tax liability. The advance's allocation and any later refund require the actual account/certificate events. [Rules 8–10](https://www.gibraltarlaws.gov.gi/uploads/legislations/income-tax/2004s070/2004s070(22-06-26).pdf); [Rates rule 2](https://www.gibraltarlaws.gov.gi/uploads/legislations/income-tax/1989s096/1989s096(01-07-24).pdf).

```oa-check
name: Category 2 first certificate year monthly minimum and separate application cash
inputs:
  complete_or_part_certificate_months: 4
  monthly_minimum: 3083.33
  computed_income_tax: 0
  application_fee: 5000
steps:
  - certificate_minimum: complete_or_part_certificate_months * monthly_minimum
  - tax_payable: max(computed_income_tax, certificate_minimum)
  - maximum_tax_advance: 4000 * 0.14 + 12000 * 0.17 + (118000 - 16000) * 0.39
  - fee_plus_advance_distinct_from_liability: application_fee + maximum_tax_advance
expected:
  certificate_minimum: 12333.32
  tax_payable: 12333.32
  maximum_tax_advance: 42380
  fee_plus_advance_distinct_from_liability: 47380
```
