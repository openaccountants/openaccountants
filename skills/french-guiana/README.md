# French Guiana: tax pack

Guyane applies French income and corporation tax with local reliefs, uses EUR and generally a calendar-year individual income period. VAT does not apply; goods imports and qualifying local production instead encounter octroi de mer and regional octroi. The 2026 filing season assesses 2025 individual income; do not represent those bands as the final 2027 assessment schedule. These eight source-cited tier 2 Guides carry no accountant approval.

## Coverage limits

The octroi Guide does not yet supply a complete pre-July tariff crosswalk or a full post-July amendment-chain audit. Its live import-procedure field numbers and pre-May customs sanction crosswalk remain unconfirmed. The space child verifies one illustrative annex entry, not all five pages. No unconfirmed general rate is supplied. Future 2027 individual-return forms/calendar/indexed limits have not been issued or verified; the applicable future check is explicit in each Guide.

## Out of scope

Regulated-profession pension-fund schedules, agricultural/public/maritime/artist-author social regimes, pre-2020 micro entrants, tourist-accommodation computations, occupation-specific benefits and special mobility reliefs; specialist high-income, disposals, investment incentives, consolidated/holding/restructuring/Pillar Two and exceptional large-company tax; specialist transfer-pricing/APA/CbCR work; full exit/share-plan/artist/sport withholding procedures and treaty-by-treaty tables. Actual employer accident/mobility/contractual rates must come from its notification. These boundaries do not imply an exemption.
