# French Guiana personal income tax 2026 v0.1

> **General reference only.** This Guide is general tax/accounting reference material for AI-assisted workflows. It has not been reviewed for any specific person's facts, documents, elections, deadlines, residency, filing status, or local procedures. Do not rely on it to file, pay, amend, or take a tax position without review by a qualified professional in the relevant jurisdiction.

## Who this applies to

This is the ordinary individual income-tax Guide for **Guyane (French Guiana), part of France**, not Guyana. A person fiscally domiciled there is within French resident taxation on worldwide income. CGI article 4 B tests the home/principal abode, a non-accessory professional activity, or the centre of economic interests; meeting a domestic test remains subject to treaty residence. Do not substitute a universal days-only test. [CGI art.4 B](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000051202565/); [DGFiP DOM guidance](https://www.impots.gouv.fr/professionnel/impots-professionnels-dom).

Start with the income year, household members, residence and move dates, income categories, documented expenses, previous losses, relief elections and tax already withheld. Married/PACS couples generally file jointly; single individuals normally have one part and jointly assessed couples two, with child/dependant and shared-custody adjustments. Ordinary dependent children add half a part for each of the first two and one part from the third; shared-custody increments are halved and children with exclusive charge are counted first. A single parent living alone has separate first-child rules; disability, widowhood, adult attachment and changing family status require the special table. Compute the actual household's parts and family-quotient cap from its status, not from the salary of one spouse. [DGFiP brochure, household pp.75-84 and calculation pp.369-372](https://www.impots.gouv.fr/www2/fichiers/documentation/brochure/ir_2026/pdf_integral/Brochure-IR-2026.pdf).

**Year boundary:** the 2026 filing campaign assesses **2025 income**. The article 197 bands below are enacted for 2025 and following years as the law stands on the read date. They are a current-law planning basis for 2026 income, not an assertion that Parliament has fixed the 2027 assessment's future indexed figures or filing calendar. Every example explicitly uses 2025 income assessed in 2026. [CGI art.197, commencement note](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000006308332/).

The personal progressive-tax reduction belongs here. ZFANG business-profit relief belongs in [gf-corporate-income-tax](gf-corporate-income-tax.md), including its application to eligible individual businesses; social contributions and payroll calculations belong in [gf-social-contributions](gf-social-contributions.md) and [gf-payroll](gf-payroll.md). Non-resident computation, foreign withholding and treaties belong in [gf-cross-border-tax](gf-cross-border-tax.md). Specialist investment incentives, stock awards, life-insurance withdrawals, exceptional/high-income contributions and business or property disposal reliefs need their own current-law computation.

Unfurnished property income and statutory excluded products retain the lower CSG rate. ## The base

1. **Classify receipts before combining them.** Salaries and benefits use net taxable pay, pensions their own deduction, BIC commercial/accrual profits, BNC professional receipts less expenses in the default receipts/payment system (or a valid accrual election), BA agricultural rules, unfurnished rentals property rules and investment income its relevant flat/scale treatment. Partnership income may be taxed to partners even when cash is retained. [DGFiP brochure, income chapters](https://www.impots.gouv.fr/www2/fichiers/documentation/brochure/ir_2026/pdf_integral/Brochure-IR-2026.pdf); [independent professionals](https://www.impots.gouv.fr/particulier/professions-independantes).
2. **Salary expenses:** report salary before the automatic expense deduction. For each employee compare the standard deduction with necessary, documented actual employment costs. An actual-expense election requires the relevant employer expense allowances/reimbursements to be included in taxable pay under the brochure's reimbursement rules; do not deduct the same cost twice. Private expenditure is excluded. Salary boxes 1AJ/1BJ and actual-expense boxes 1AK/1BK distinguish the choices. [Brochure pp.107-111](https://www.impots.gouv.fr/www2/fichiers/documentation/brochure/ir_2026/pdf_integral/Brochure-IR-2026.pdf).
3. **Business:** micro means gross receipts on 2042-C-PRO and the statutory allowance, not profit after expenses. Under actual BIC/BA compute allowable business costs, stock/cut-off, depreciation and justified provisions; personal expenses, drawings, capital purchases deducted as current expenses and disallowed fines need adjustment. BNC normally deducts expenses necessarily incurred for the profession when paid, with capital assets handled through depreciation. Retain invoices and reconcile the results return to 2042-C-PRO. [DGFiP independent professionals](https://www.impots.gouv.fr/particulier/professions-independantes); [CGI art.39 ordinary expense restrictions](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000053542680/). A valid versement libératoire option changes income-tax collection for qualifying micro income; do not apply the progressive calculation to it as though no election existed. Its income still enters annual reporting and the effective-rate calculation for other income. For an existing business elect through Urssaf by30September of the preceding year; new businesses may elect by the last day of the third month following creation. Report the receipts in 2042-C-PRO5TA/5TB/5TE as appropriate, even after periodic tax was paid. If eligibility has ceased, the special credit/reclassification rules apply instead of pretending the payment remains final. The relevant current option conditions are [DGFiP](https://www.impots.gouv.fr/professionnel/questions/en-tant-que-micro-entrepreneur-sous-quelles-conditions-puis-je-opter-pour-l); also see [brochure micro chapter](https://www.impots.gouv.fr/www2/fichiers/documentation/brochure/ir_2026/pdf_integral/Brochure-IR-2026.pdf).
4. **Property:** micro-foncier uses gross rent and no additional actual expenses. Under the real regime use 2044: deductible management, qualifying repairs/maintenance/improvement, insurance, property taxes and loan interest subject to each rule. Construction, reconstruction or enlargement is not an ordinary repair deduction. For a deficit isolate interest first; interest cannot create a global-income deduction. A global deduction normally requires letting through 31 December of the third year after the deduction year. Special energy-renovation and statutory rental incentives have separate conditions. [DGFiP rental guidance](https://www.impots.gouv.fr/particulier/location-vide-de-meubles); [2044 instructions](https://www.impots.gouv.fr/sites/default/files/formulaires/2044/2026/2044_5486.pdf).
5. **Losses:** genuine professional BIC/BNC deficits generally offset other household income and unabsorbed global losses carry forward as above. Non-professional BIC and non-professional BNC losses are ringfenced to qualifying same-category income, normally for six following years; furnished non-professional rental losses use their separate ten-year ringfence and cannot offset salary. Capital/securities losses do not become general salary deductions. Keep schedules by origin, category and expiry year, use oldest available loss first and never reuse it. [CGI art.156; BOI-IR-BASE-10-20](https://bofip.impots.gouv.fr/bofip/6451-PGP.html); [BNC loss guidance](https://bofip.impots.gouv.fr/bofip/4614-PGP.html); [brochure business-loss chapters](https://www.impots.gouv.fr/www2/fichiers/documentation/brochure/ir_2026/pdf_integral/Brochure-IR-2026.pdf).
6. **Household taxable net income:** combine only progressive-tax categories after their lawful allowances/losses, then deduct eligible global charges once. Maintenance payments require a legal/support obligation and need/evidence; the same child cannot generate both attachment and a maintenance deduction. For 2025 income, ordinary maintenance to an adult child is capped at EUR 6,855, with special doubled limits only where the statutory household/dependency conditions apply. Retirement-saving deductions depend on the individual's available pension ceiling and previous carry-forwards, shown on the tax notice; do not deduct employer-relieved contributions twice. For 2025 voluntary contributions deductible globally, the ordinary current ceiling starts at 10% of 2024 net professional income capped at 8 times the relevant social ceiling, a maximum EUR 37,094, or the floor EUR 4,637 if higher, then prescribed previous-year pension adjustments and unused ceilings. The brochure separately confirms 2026 contribution ceiling maximum EUR 37,680/floor EUR 4,710 based on 2025 net professional income, before required adjustments. The 2026 law extends new unused ceiling to the next five years; the transition preserves only unused 2023-25 ceilings for deductions in 2026, and later years phase in the extension. Use the current-year ceiling first, then valid older amounts oldest first; do not resurrect expired pre 2023 amounts. From 1January 2026 voluntary PER payments from the seventieth birthday cease to qualify for the relevant deduction. [CGI art.163 quatervicies](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000053542827/); [BOI-IR-BASE-20-50-30 §§30-80](https://bofip.impots.gouv.fr/bofip/1117-PGP.html). Contributions beyond available ceiling are not themselves carry-forward deductions. Jointly assessed spouses can elect ceiling pooling via 6QR. New French residents and occupational/self-employed plans have specific rules. Use the dedicated maintenance and retirement fields, not salary-expense boxes. [Brochure pp.227-236](https://www.impots.gouv.fr/www2/fichiers/documentation/brochure/ir_2026/pdf_integral/Brochure-IR-2026.pdf).
7. **Compute in order:** divide net progressive income by actual parts; apply bands; multiply by parts. Apply the family-quotient advantage limit by comparison with the base-parts computation, including special-status relief where relevant. Apply Guyane reduction, then décote, then non-refundable reductions, then refundable credits and creditable withholding/advances. A non-refundable reduction cannot by itself create a refund. Separately compute flat-tax income and any special contribution. [CGI art.197](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000006308332/).

**Donations:** check the recipient's legal eligibility and retain its tax receipt; a payment with a material personal benefit is not automatically a qualifying gift. The enhanced rate covers the statutory free meals, accommodation or principal medical-care activities and qualifying free support/rehousing of domestic-violence victims. For the 2026 return, ordinary French-body gifts use 7UF; enhanced gifts through 13 October 2025 use 7UD and later 2025 gifts 7UQ, with the aggregate ceilings applied once. EU/qualifying EEA recipients have separate eligibility/evidence and 2042-RICI fields. The carried amount is the unused gift above the income ceiling; an otherwise allowable reduction lost because tax is insufficient is not a refundable credit or a general carry-forward. [DGFiP donation reporting](https://www.impots.gouv.fr/particulier/questions/jai-fait-des-dons-une-association-que-puis-je-deduire); [BOI §§90-150](https://bofip.impots.gouv.fr/bofip/5873-PGP.html).

**Home services:** a French-tax-resident household may claim eligible domestic/family services at its French main or second home, through direct employment or a qualifying declared/authorised provider. Costs at a qualifying ascendant's home require the statutory APA conditions; the same ascendant cannot also generate a maintenance deduction for the claimant. Work-related or rental costs already deducted in an income category cannot be claimed again. Deduct APA/PCH, employer-funded CESU and other assistance. Off-premises services need the statutory bundled-service conditions and relevant spending limit; do not treat any delivery as eligible domestic work. For the 2026 return, report gross qualifying expenses in 2042 7DB and assistance in 7DR; complete 2042-RICI service-type details BDA-BEA, provider identity/nature and intervention method. Claim first-direct-employment status in 7DQ or qualifying disability in 7DG where applicable. Reconcile prefilled immediate credit advance 7HB and annual advance 8EA to avoid receiving the credit twice. [CGI art.199 sexdecies](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000054251333/2026-06-14); [DGFiP home-service workflow](https://www.impots.gouv.fr/particulier/emploi-domicile); [brochure pp.237-240](https://www.impots.gouv.fr/www2/fichiers/documentation/brochure/ir_2026/pdf_integral/Brochure-IR-2026.pdf).

**Childcare:** use an approved childminder or qualifying nursery/daycare, including eligible supervised time outside school lessons; the cost is for care outside the claimant's home. Exclude food and exceptional non-care extras, deduct CMG and employer/other childcare aid, and retain the provider's itemised statement. For 2025 care, the authority allows the specified childminder maintenance-cost element at EUR2.65 per care day; do not project that administrative daily amount to unissued 2027 instructions. Use 2042-RICI 7GA/7GB/7GC for exclusive/principal charge or 7GE/7GF/7GG for shared custody, one child per field. In-home childcare instead follows the home-service rules, with no double claim for the same expense. [DGFiP childcare conditions and fields](https://www.impots.gouv.fr/particulier/questions/je-fais-garder-mon-jeune-enfant-lexterieur-du-domicile-que-puis-je-deduire); [brochure p.247](https://www.impots.gouv.fr/www2/fichiers/documentation/brochure/ir_2026/pdf_integral/Brochure-IR-2026.pdf).

| Domiciled in Guyane at 31 December of the income year | Guyane reduction applies to the year's qualifying progressive tax, including earlier mainland income; address must reflect actual domicile |
| Guyane domicile on 1 January, then move to mainland during the year | Reduction only on the tax attributable to income received while domiciled in Guyane; retain dated income and move evidence |
| Micro business also eligible for ZFANG | Test the business relief separately using the corporate Guide; no assumption that all Guyane activities qualify |
| Flat-rate investment income | The Guyane reduction is a reduction of progressive tax; do not automatically reduce every flat withholding or capital levy |
| Treaty non-resident | Do not grant resident progressive/Guyane relief automatically; route to the cross-border Guide |

Sources: [DGFiP DOM address and move rules](https://www.impots.gouv.fr/professionnel/impots-professionnels-dom); [CGI art.197](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000006308332/); [ZFANG §100](https://bofip.impots.gouv.fr/bofip/11833-PGP.html).

## Deadlines and filing

**Registration and access:** business commencement is declared through the INPI guichet unique; a micro-business social account does not replace the annual tax return. Obtain a numéro fiscal and personal Finances publiques access. A new filer without access uses the DGFiP registration/first-return procedure, rather than submitting an unprocessable scan through messaging. [DGFiP micro guidance](https://www.impots.gouv.fr/particulier/questions/comment-declarer-les-revenus-provenant-de-mon-activite-dauto-entrepreneur); [2026 campaign](https://www.impots.gouv.fr/les-modalites-de-la-declaration-de-revenus-en-2026).

**Annual workflow:** log in at impots.gouv.fr → espace Finances publiques → declare income. Confirm civil status, household, dependant/shared custody and addresses/move dates. Review every prefilled taxable-income and withholding line against annual statements. Select annexes before entering additional categories. The 2026 forms for 2025 income use 2042 salary 1AJ/1BJ (actual expenses 1AK/1BK), pensions 1AS/1BS, micro-foncier 4BE or real property result 4BA from 2044; capital income is in section 2, with 2OP for the global progressive-scale election. Micro BIC ordinary sales use 2042-C-PRO 5KO/5LO, services 5KP/5LP, and professional micro-BNC 5HQ/5IQ; use the correct taxpayer and professional-status column. Foreign income needs 2047 and then the appropriate 2042 income and credit/exemption fields, not only an amount on the annex. Review 3916/3916-bis foreign-account reporting where applicable. [2026 brochure, reproduced forms and category instructions](https://www.impots.gouv.fr/www2/fichiers/documentation/brochure/ir_2026/pdf_integral/Brochure-IR-2026.pdf).

**Actual business returns first:** BIC uses 2031 and 2033-A to G or 2050 to 2059-G; BNC uses 2035 and its annexes; BA uses 2139 simplified or 2143 normal with its annexes. Submit electronically in the professional space/EDI as available. The normal results deadline is the second working day after 1 May following the relevant income/closing year, with the electronic extension where applicable. Reconcile any prefilled 2042-C-PRO result to the signed results return. A micro taxpayer normally reports receipts directly on 2042-C-PRO. [DGFiP independent professionals](https://www.impots.gouv.fr/particulier/professions-independantes); [results filing](https://www.impots.gouv.fr/professionnel/imposition-des-resultats).

For the **2026 return of 2025 income**, the Guyane online deadline was **4 June 2026 at 23:59**, paper when legally needed **19 May 2026 at midnight**. Non-residents used the separate earlier deadline in the cross-border Guide. These dates are not the 2027 deadlines. [2026 calendar](https://www.impots.gouv.fr/les-modalites-de-la-declaration-de-revenus-en-2026).

Sign and submit, save the acknowledgement and ASDIR where issued, then check the final assessment. The annual liability reconciles source deductions and self-employed/rental advances already paid; it is not cancelled by prélèvement à la source. Use “Gérer mon prélèvement à la source” to update circumstances and check bank details. On the 2026 campaign DGFiP schedules balances below EUR 300 in September and above EUR 300 across September-December; use the actual notice for the debit schedule and exact boundary. Refunds go to the recorded bank account or the administration's alternate method. The 2027 assessment's forms, indexed limits and administrative dates must be checked when issued. [2026 campaign](https://www.impots.gouv.fr/les-modalites-de-la-declaration-de-revenus-en-2026).

## Penalties and interest

Late annual IR filing generally brings **10%** of tax; filing within **30 days** after formal notice raises it to **20%**; failing to file within those 30 days brings **40%**; a discovered hidden activity can bring **80%** without prior notice. The filing penalty base is the total tax before crediting advances/source deductions, whereas interest takes relevant payments into account. Interest is generally **0.20% per month**, with statutory start/stop rules. [CGI arts.1727, 1728, 1758 A; official sanctions guidance](https://www.service-public.gouv.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/F34175).

An ordinary inaccurate/omitted IR declaration can attract the article 1758 A **10%** surcharge subject to spontaneous/notice-correction exceptions; deliberate understatement attracts article 1729 **40%**, fraudulent manoeuvres **80%**, subject to the distinct abuse rules. Do not stack incompatible penalties for the same rights. Personal tax paid late is normally subject to **10%** under article 1730, rather than importing the company-payment surcharge. Statutory correction and collection exceptions may remove or reduce a penalty. [CGI art.1758A](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/id/LEGISCTA000006147258/); [CGI art.1729](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000020051904/); [CGI art.1730; BOI-CF-INF-10-30 II](https://bofip.impots.gouv.fr/bofip/2428-PGP.html).

## Cross-border points

Residence generally exposes worldwide income, but treaty allocation and credit/exemption methods must be applied income by income. A Brazilian or Surinamese payer does not establish an exemption by itself. Foreign tax may require gross income reporting, evidence and an explicit credit claim; overseas accounts can trigger a separate reporting duty even with no taxable receipt. See [gf-cross-border-tax](gf-cross-border-tax.md). [CGI art.4 B](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000051202565/); [2026 brochure, 2047 and foreign-account instructions](https://www.impots.gouv.fr/www2/fichiers/documentation/brochure/ir_2026/pdf_integral/Brochure-IR-2026.pdf).

Mainland France and Guyane are part of the same income-tax system. The distinct VAT/octroi border does not make a mainland salary foreign-source treaty income. Moves into/out of France require a residence-period split and possibly 2042-NR; a move between Guyane and mainland instead triggers the DOM-reduction rules above. [DGFiP DOM guidance](https://www.impots.gouv.fr/professionnel/impots-professionnels-dom); [brochure moves chapter](https://www.impots.gouv.fr/www2/fichiers/documentation/brochure/ir_2026/pdf_integral/Brochure-IR-2026.pdf).

## Two worked examples that recompute

### Example 1: one employee, Guyane reduction before décote

For **2025 income assessed in 2026**, a single person with one part is domiciled in Guyane all year, has taxable salary EUR 50,000 before the standard expense deduction, and has no other income, special status, credits or reductions. Deduction = EUR 5,000; net income = EUR 45,000. Tax = (29,579 − 11,600) × 11% + (45,000 − 29,579) × 30% = EUR 6,603.99. Guyane reduction = EUR 2,641.596, below its cap. Remaining tax = EUR 3,962.394; décote is nil because its formula is negative. The illustration rounds final liability to EUR 3,962 before source-payment credits. [CGI art.197](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000006308332/); [salary deduction](https://www.impots.gouv.fr/www2/fichiers/documentation/brochure/ir_2026/pdf_integral/Brochure-IR-2026.pdf).

```oa-check
name: Guyane single salary, 2025 income assessed in 2026
inputs:
  salary: 50000
steps:
  - expense_deduction: min(salary, max(509, min(salary * 0.10, 14555)))
  - net_income: salary - expense_deduction
  - band11: (min(net_income, 29579) - 11600) * 0.11
  - band30: (net_income - 29579) * 0.30
  - gross_tax: band11 + band30
  - guyane_reduction: min(gross_tax * 0.40, 4050)
  - after_guyane: gross_tax - guyane_reduction
  - decote: min(after_guyane, max(0, 897 - after_guyane * 0.4525))
  - final_tax: round(after_guyane - decote, 0)
expected:
  net_income: 45000
  gross_tax: 6603.99
  guyane_reduction: 2641.596
  final_tax: 3962
```

### Example 2: choose actual employee expenses only after checking reimbursements

Same year and household, salary EUR 50,000, with EUR 8,000 qualifying actual costs and **no employer expense reimbursements requiring add-back**. Standard deduction yields the preceding EUR 3,962 rounded tax. Actual expenses instead give EUR 42,000 taxable income, gross tax EUR 5,703.99, Guyane reduction EUR 2,281.596 and EUR 3,422 rounded tax. Actual expenses save EUR 540 income tax here, subject to evidence; do not apply both deductions. Different reimbursements or disallowed/private costs can reverse the choice. [Brochure pp.107-111](https://www.impots.gouv.fr/www2/fichiers/documentation/brochure/ir_2026/pdf_integral/Brochure-IR-2026.pdf); [CGI art.197](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000006308332/).

```oa-check
name: Actual versus standard employee expenses, 2025 income assessed in 2026
inputs:
  salary: 50000
  actual_expenses: 8000
steps:
  - standard_net: salary - salary * 0.10
  - actual_net: salary - actual_expenses
  - standard_gross: (29579 - 11600) * 0.11 + (standard_net - 29579) * 0.30
  - actual_gross: (29579 - 11600) * 0.11 + (actual_net - 29579) * 0.30
  - standard_after: standard_gross - min(4050, standard_gross * 0.40)
  - actual_after: actual_gross - min(4050, actual_gross * 0.40)
  - standard_tax: round(standard_after - min(standard_after, max(0, 897 - standard_after * 0.4525)), 0)
  - actual_tax: round(actual_after - min(actual_after, max(0, 897 - actual_after * 0.4525)), 0)
  - saving: standard_tax - actual_tax
expected:
  standard_net: 45000
  actual_net: 42000
  standard_tax: 3962
  actual_tax: 3422
  saving: 540
```

## Ask the client first

- What is the income year, actual residence and treaty status, and every move date/address?
- Who belongs to the household, with what marriage/PACS, children, custody, disability or other special-part facts?
- What income, foreign receipts, employer benefits/reimbursements, bank statements and withholding certificates support each category?
- Which micro/actual/flat-payment elections exist, and what prior turnover, loss schedules, property history and ZFANG evidence apply?
- Which documented expenses, maintenance, retirement ceilings, reliefs and foreign-tax credits are being claimed, and what tax was already paid?
- What returns/notices were filed, received or left overdue, including business and foreign-account annexes?
