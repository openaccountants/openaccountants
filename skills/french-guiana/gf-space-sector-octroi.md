# French Guiana space-sector import octroi 2026 v0.3

> **General reference only.** This Guide is general tax/accounting reference material for AI-assisted workflows. It has not been reviewed for any specific person's facts, documents, elections, deadlines, residency, filing status, or local procedures. Do not rely on it to file, pay, amend, or take a tax position without review by a qualified professional in the relevant jurisdiction.

## Who this applies to

For imports of qualifying space-sector goods into Guyane from **1 August 2026**, under AP-2026-56 and AP-2026-57. This is a narrow child of [gf-import-octroi-and-vat](gf-import-octroi-and-vat.md): use the parent for VAT non-application, customs value, declaration and ordinary compliance. A contract with a space customer alone does not qualify the supplier. [AP-2026-57 arts. 1-8](https://www.ctguyane.fr/www/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/delib.ctg-ap-2026-57-regime-exo-partielle-activites-spatiales.pdf).

Quick reference: prove the date, actual Guyane activity/NAF, exact annex goods code, own/exclusive needs, direct launch-chain use and absence of alternative use before applying the residual rates. If a condition fails, determine the otherwise applicable tariff and any independently valid relief; never assume zero tax.

## Rates, bands and thresholds

| Item | Rule and conditions | Official source |
|---|---|---|
| Residual external OM | 7% of confirmed customs value for qualifying imports from 1 August 2026 | [AP-2026-57 arts. 6, 8](https://www.ctguyane.fr/www/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/delib.ctg-ap-2026-57-regime-exo-partielle-activites-spatiales.pdf#page=3), [AP-2026-56 art. 1](https://www.ctguyane.fr/www/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/delib.ctg-ap-2026-56-fixation-tx-om-reduit-activites-spatiales-1.pdf#page=2) |
| Continuing regional OMR | 3% on the same base, even where partial OM relief qualifies | [AP-2026-57 art. 6](https://www.ctguyane.fr/www/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/delib.ctg-ap-2026-57-regime-exo-partielle-activites-spatiales.pdf#page=3) |
| Verified illustrative annex entry | NAF 30.30Z, code 73082000, iron/steel towers and lattice masts, subject to all use restrictions; not every metal product | [Enacted annex p1/5](https://www.ctguyane.fr/www/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/delib.ctg-ap-2026-57-annexe-1.pdf#page=1) |

The 4.5% proposal mentioned in AP-2026-56's recitals is not the enacted rate. AP-2026-56 article 1 enacts 7%. Rates do not establish eligibility.

## The base

The importer must exercise the relevant economic activity in Guyane, match the annex activity and goods row, and use the goods for that activity's own exclusive needs. Goods must directly serve operational launching, including assembly, integration, positioning and launch, with no alternative use. The annex restricts habitual sale/rental of beneficiary-use goods. Read the complete applicable row and use restrictions, not just a chapter heading. Only the illustrative mast row has been visually verified in this Guide; the full five-page classification crosswalk is not asserted. [AP-2026-57 arts. 1-2](https://www.ctguyane.fr/www/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/delib.ctg-ap-2026-57-regime-exo-partielle-activites-spatiales.pdf); [annex p1](https://www.ctguyane.fr/www/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/delib.ctg-ap-2026-57-annexe-1.pdf).

For service operators article 5 requires ownership or an operating contract assigning use of the equipment, and proof of direct exclusive use inseparable from launch operations. If the operator's principal activity is outside the space sector, listed equipment must be technically inseparable from launch; ordinary transport, handling, warehousing and logistics equipment is excluded. [Art. 5](https://www.ctguyane.fr/www/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/delib.ctg-ap-2026-57-regime-exo-partielle-activites-spatiales.pdf#page=3).

Article 3 substitutes this regime for AP-2023-33 annex relief where the goods/activity and direct exclusive launch use conditions apply. Article 4 prevents simultaneously combining another OM exemption on the same goods. Determine the regime from actual destination, principal use and operational role. Article 7 permits only simple nomenclature correspondence for the same originally covered products, without changing nature/destination or adding goods, until a new deliberation. Document the correspondence. [Arts. 3-7](https://www.ctguyane.fr/www/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/delib.ctg-ap-2026-57-regime-exo-partielle-activites-spatiales.pdf).

Both levies use confirmed customs value. OM is base × 7%; OMR is base × 3%, separately, without compounding. A reduction in import OM is not an income-tax loss or VAT input credit. The parent explains producer input deduction where relevant. [Law arts. 9, 37](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/loda/id/JORFTEXT000000253374); [Customs](https://www.douane.gouv.fr/demarche/importer-un-bien-dans-un-drom).

## Deadlines and filing

1. Assemble import date, activity/NAF evidence, specifications/code, ownership or operating contract, technical launch-chain description and proof of no alternative/habitual resale or rental use.
2. Obtain the current enacted annex and match the whole row; preserve it and any simple-nomenclature correspondence. Check the CTG published index for a later amendment before declaring. No pre-August transaction uses this decision retrospectively.
3. Give the declarant confirmed customs value, classification and relief support. The parent describes the article 6 attestation, goods/end-use and conditional investment retention procedure; a private evidence checklist does not replace prescribed customs support.
4. Declare OM and OMR separately on the import customs declaration and retain acceptance, reference, payment/release evidence and the legal/technical relief file. No separate monthly space-sector VAT return is created by these decisions.
5. Monitor actual destination and changed use; failure of a condition requires reassessment under the applicable law rather than silent continuation of relief. Seek a written customs determination for disputed classification/use before relying on the illustration.

[AP-2026-57 arts. 1-9 and annex](https://www.ctguyane.fr/www/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/delib.ctg-ap-2026-57-regime-exo-partielle-activites-spatiales.pdf); [annex](https://www.ctguyane.fr/www/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/delib.ctg-ap-2026-57-annexe-1.pdf); [Customs](https://www.douane.gouv.fr/demarche/importer-un-bien-dans-un-drom); [CTG index](https://www.ctguyane.fr/octroi-de-mer-tarif-exoneration/). Customs declaration clocks and procedure-specific fields belong to the parent; no invented space-only filing date is supplied.

## Penalties and interest

Relief obtained without the goods/activity/end-use conditions can leave OM underpaid; ordinary customs recovery/sanctions apply under law article 42. Use the parent's [penalties and interest](gf-import-octroi-and-vat.md#penalties-and-interest) for current interest and offence bases, rather than importing France's ordinary VAT penalties. AP-2026-57 does not create a bespoke flat penalty in its articles 1-9. The decision itself states a two-month administrative-court challenge period from notification/publication/display; that is not a general customs reassessment appeal deadline. [Decision final page](https://www.ctguyane.fr/www/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/delib.ctg-ap-2026-57-regime-exo-partielle-activites-spatiales.pdf#page=4); [law art. 42](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/loda/id/JORFTEXT000000253374).

## Cross-border points

Imports from mainland France and other EU territories can be OM imports. Guyane VAT non-application does not cancel external or regional OM. Provenance alone does not establish the space relief. Parent rules govern valuation, ordinary exemptions, Antilles/Guyane exceptions and customs declaration. Services and ordinary logistics remain outside this goods-specific concession where article 5 excludes their equipment. [Customs](https://www.douane.gouv.fr/demarche/importer-un-bien-dans-un-drom); [AP-2026-57 art. 5](https://www.ctguyane.fr/www/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/delib.ctg-ap-2026-57-regime-exo-partielle-activites-spatiales.pdf).

| Boundary | Result |
|---|---|
| Listed mast with an alternative non-launch use | Article 2 exclusive/no-alternative condition fails |
| Ordinary warehouse equipment used for launch cargo | Customer identity does not override article 5 exclusion |
| Import before 1 August 2026 | Use the earlier applicable regime |
| Two exemption claims on one item | Article 4 prohibits simultaneous combination |
| Simple updated code for identical covered goods | Article 7 correspondence can preserve relief; retain proof |

## Two worked examples that recompute

**Qualifying import.** Assume all activity, code and use tests proved, no competing relief, customs value EUR 100,000 and import after commencement. OM EUR 7,000 plus OMR EUR 3,000 is EUR 10,000. This excludes other import charges and does not compound OMR. [Art. 6](https://www.ctguyane.fr/www/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/delib.ctg-ap-2026-57-regime-exo-partielle-activites-spatiales.pdf).

```oa-check
name: Qualifying space import
inputs:
  customs_value: 100000
steps:
  om: customs_value*0.07
  omr: customs_value*0.03
  total: om+omr
expected:
  om: 7000
  omr: 3000
  total: 10000
```

**Eligibility decision.** Assume a technically qualifying launch mast costing EUR 100,000; all other conditions are proved. If direct exclusive launch use without alternative use is established, the eligible base is EUR 100,000. If the same item is also ordinary warehousing equipment, the eligible base under this regime is zero. That is not zero tax: use its verified ordinary tariff instead. No unsupported mast ordinary rate is invented. [Arts. 2, 5](https://www.ctguyane.fr/www/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/delib.ctg-ap-2026-57-regime-exo-partielle-activites-spatiales.pdf).

```oa-check
name: Exclusive launch use versus ordinary warehouse
inputs:
  customs_value: 100000
steps:
  qualifying_relief_base: customs_value
  warehouse_relief_base: 0
  excluded_from_relief: customs_value-warehouse_relief_base
expected:
  qualifying_relief_base: 100000
  warehouse_relief_base: 0
  excluded_from_relief: 100000
```

## Ask the client first

- Entry/release date and customs procedure.
- Actual principal activity and the annex's NAF code.
- Exact goods code/specifications, full annex row and any correspondence.
- Ownership or contract assigning equipment operation.
- Technical evidence of direct exclusive launching and no alternative use.
- Actual destination, habitual sale/rental and competing relief claims.
- Customs value, declaration, payment and retention evidence.
