# Vanuatu Company Formation & Entity Choice

## Incorporating a local company

Apply to the Registrar through the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC) under section 6 of the Companies Act No. 25 of 2012. The prescribed application identifies the company name and type (private, public or community), rules, directors and their consents, shareholders and share allocations, registered office and postal address. Include any non-model rules and the prescribed fee. From 5 January 2018, section 6 also requires details of the nominators of any nominee shareholders and nominee directors. The Registrar enters the company on the register and issues the incorporation certificate when the application complies. [Companies Act, ss 6–9](https://www.vfsc.vu/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Companies-Act-2012-Official-Gazetted-Version-1.pdf), [Companies Amendment Act No. 32 of 2017, s 2 and Sch item 2](https://www.vfsc.vu/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Companies-Amendment-Act-No.-32-of-2017-1.pdf)

Overseas companies carrying on business in Vanuatu and international companies have separate registration routes. [VFSC entity guidance](https://www.vfsc.vu/learn-about-companies-other-entities/), [VFSC legislation index](https://www.vfsc.vu/legislation/)

## Local registration and annual return fees

The VFSC schedule checked on 11 November 2026 lists VUV 35,000 for each of local registration under section 6 and the annual return under section 119, online or offline. The original regulation, which commenced on 31 August 2015, lists VUV 30,000 online and VUV 35,000 offline for each. The instrument supporting the online increase remains unverified. Confirm the applicable online fee with the Registrar before paying. [VFSC fees and penalties](https://www.vfsc.vu/companies-fees-and-penalties/), [Companies Regulations Order No. 114 of 2015, commencement and Schedule 2 Part 1](https://www.vfsc.vu/regulations/)

## Annual returns and accounting records

- **Annual return.** File during the company's allocated month with the prescribed information, signature and fee. The Registrar allocates the month at registration and can change it by written notice. The calendar year of incorporation is exempt. A subsidiary can use its holding company's month with written approval. [Companies Act, s 119](https://www.vfsc.vu/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Companies-Act-2012-Official-Gazetted-Version-1.pdf), [VFSC annual return process](https://www.vfsc.vu/company-annual-returns/)
- **Missed return.** From 5 January 2018, section 140(1)(a) requires removal if a company fails to file its annual return within 6 months after the allocated date, subject to section 142. Section 119 still sets the filing obligation in the allocated month. [Companies Act, ss 119 and 142](https://www.vfsc.vu/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Companies-Act-2012-Official-Gazetted-Version-1.pdf), [Companies Amendment Act No. 32 of 2017, s 2 and Sch item 16](https://www.vfsc.vu/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Companies-Amendment-Act-No.-32-of-2017-1.pdf)
- **Accounts.** Directors must ensure that accounting records explain company transactions, enable its financial position to be determined with reasonable accuracy and support compliant and auditable financial statements. Sections 125–129 set separate financial statement and audit rules. Check the applicable class and audit triggers, including the triggers for some private companies. [Companies Act, ss 124–129](https://www.vfsc.vu/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Companies-Act-2012-Official-Gazetted-Version-1.pdf)

## Business licensing

DCIR's 2026 guidance requires a separate business licence application for a company carrying on a business for profit, subject to the exemptions described there. The application uses a taxpayer identification number and, for a new company licence, a valid VFSC incorporation certificate. DCIR also asks for a valid Vanuatu Foreign Investment Promotion Authority certificate for new or renewal applications from businesses with foreign ownership. [DCIR 2026 business licence guidance](https://vanuatucustoms.gov.vu/taxes-and-licensing/licensing/business-licencing)

DCIR says a new licence runs from the business start date to 31 December. Existing businesses renew by 31 January each calendar year. Use the applicable form and activity and turnover category to determine the fee. This guide leaves detailed fee bands, sector exemptions, business-name registration and the separate overseas and international company reporting regimes for further verification. [DCIR 2026 business licence guidance](https://vanuatucustoms.gov.vu/taxes-and-licensing/licensing/business-licencing)
