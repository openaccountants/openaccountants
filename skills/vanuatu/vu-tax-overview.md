# Vanuatu Tax Overview

## The Vanuatu tax system at a glance

Vanuatu has no ordinary personal or corporate income tax. Its separate regimes include VAT, rent tax, business licensing and import duties. [Vanuatu Competent Authority guide, 20 August 2024](https://vanuatucustoms.gov.vu/images/Forms/VCA/VU_AEOI_Guide_v4.pdf), [Global Forum assessment, para 23, adopted 23 April 2026](https://www.oecd.org/content/dam/oecd/en/publications/reports/2026/04/global-forum-on-transparency-and-exchange-of-information-for-tax-purposes-vanuatu-2026-second-round-supplementary-report_7a20b670/75b3bc85-en.pdf), [DCIR legislation index](https://vanuatucustoms.gov.vu/taxes-and-licensing/legislations)
- **National currency** — Vanuatu vatu (VUV / VT)  _(Reserve Bank of Vanuatu Act)_
- **Principal tax authority** — Department of Customs and Inland Revenue (DCIR). [DCIR legislation index](https://vanuatucustoms.gov.vu/taxes-and-licensing/legislations)
- **Ordinary personal income tax** — No domestic charge, rate schedule or annual return. See the [personal income tax guide](vu-income-tax.md).
- **Ordinary corporate income tax** — No domestic profits charge, rate schedule or annual return. See the [corporate income tax guide](vu-corporate-income-tax.md).
- **Property and asset taxes** — Identify the transaction and regime separately. Property, estate and stamp duties await full reconciliation.
- **Value Added Tax (VAT)** — Standard rate 15% in the 2020 consolidation and the DCIR guidance checked on 11 November 2026. [Value Added Tax Act, s 11](https://vanuatucustoms.gov.vu/images/Laws_Regulations/VAT/247_Value_Added_Tax_Consolidated_2020.pdf), [DCIR VAT guidance](https://vanuatucustoms.gov.vu/taxes-and-licensing/taxes/value-added-tax-vat/introduction.html)
- **Rent tax** — 12.5% of the excess over VUV 200,000 for a natural person or of all chargeable rent for other persons, per 6-month period under the 2020 consolidation. Check the VAT exclusion. [Rent Taxation Act, ss 1 and 4 and Sch 1](https://vanuatucustoms.gov.vu/images/TAA/196_Rent_Taxation_Act_Consolidated_2020.pdf)
- **Vanuatu National Provident Fund** — 4% each for employer and employee through December 2025. From January 2026 each pays 6% of covered remuneration. [VNPF commencement notice](https://www.vnpf.com.vu/news.html), [VNPF Act, s 25](https://www.vnpf.com.vu/uploads/9/5/0/5/95052148/vanuatu_national_provident_fund_consolidated_2026.pdf)
- **Ordinary VAT filing and payment** — Due on 5 January for periods ending 30 November and on the 27th of the following month for other periods. Use the next working day if the statutory date is a non-working day. See the [VAT guide](vu-vat-gst.md) for exceptions. [Value Added Tax Act, ss 16 and 20](https://vanuatucustoms.gov.vu/images/Laws_Regulations/VAT/247_Value_Added_Tax_Consolidated_2020.pdf)
- **Other regimes** — Source-based withholding, foreign tax and sector-specific obligations require separate checks.

## Financial account reporting

Under the Applied Common Reporting Standard provisions that commenced on 1 January 2020, a Reporting Financial Institution applies the prescribed due diligence and files an electronic return for each calendar year, including a nil return if it identifies no reportable accounts. The ordinary deadline is 31 May of the following year, unless the Competent Authority authorises another date. [Tax Administration Regulation Order No. 154 of 2019, reg 53 and Sch 2 Part 1 cls 1, 4 and 6–7](https://vanuatucustoms.gov.vu/images/TAA/Tax_Administration_Order_no154_of_2019.pdf)

For the calendar 2025 return, the Competent Authority's opening notice gives 31 May 2026 and directs filers to MDES. Its 2026 reporting plan gives 31 July 2026. Which date applied to that cycle still needs confirmation. [2026 portal opening notice](https://vanuatucustoms.gov.vu/images/VCA/Public%20Notice%20-%20CRS%20Reporting%20%202026%20English.pdf), [2026 reporting plan](https://vanuatucustoms.gov.vu/images/VCA/MDES-CRS_Reporting_plan_2026.pdf)

The 2020 amendment requires retention of covered records for 5 years after the end of the calendar year in which the return for the last relevant reporting period falls due. Where a record's last relevant period is calendar 2025 and that return falls due in calendar 2026, the amended rule runs through 31 December 2031. The effect of the 2024 amendment on the current rules still needs checking. [Tax Administration Regulation (Amendment) Order No. 156 of 2020, cl 2 and Sch items 8–9](https://doft.gov.vu/images/2020/Tax_Policy/Tax_Administration_Regualtion_Amendment_Order_No_156_of_2020.pdf)
