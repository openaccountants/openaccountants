# Vanuatu payroll and social contributions

Vanuatu has no ordinary domestic income tax withholding on wages. Eligible employment can still trigger employer and employee contributions to the Vanuatu National Provident Fund (VNPF). [Vanuatu Competent Authority guide, 20 August 2024](https://vanuatucustoms.gov.vu/images/Forms/VCA/VU_AEOI_Guide_v4.pdf), [Global Forum assessment, para 23, adopted 23 April 2026](https://www.oecd.org/content/dam/oecd/en/publications/reports/2026/04/global-forum-on-transparency-and-exchange-of-information-for-tax-purposes-vanuatu-2026-second-round-supplementary-report_7a20b670/75b3bc85-en.pdf)

## Registration and covered remuneration

An employer with a worker for whom contributions are payable must apply to register with the Board. The employer must register employees with the Board in the approved manner. Check the statutory definition of employee, the Schedule and any applicable exemption before deciding coverage. Self-employed persons can apply for membership. [VNPF Act, ss 1 and 22–23A](https://www.vnpf.com.vu/uploads/9/5/0/5/95052148/vanuatu_national_provident_fund_consolidated_2026.pdf)

Start with gross cash remuneration before deductions, including bonuses and incentives. Section 1 excludes specified benefits in kind approved in writing under the Employment Act, necessary employment reimbursements, housing allowances, statutory severance and employment gratuities. Check the facts of the payment against the statutory definitions. [VNPF Act, s 1](https://www.vnpf.com.vu/uploads/9/5/0/5/95052148/vanuatu_national_provident_fund_consolidated_2026.pdf)

## Contributions by period

The ordinary rate through the December 2025 contribution is 8% of covered remuneration, split 4% each. The 2025 rate still applies to arrears for those periods. From the January 2026 contribution, payable in February 2026, the ordinary rate is 12%, split 6% each. The Fund identifies Act 25 of 2025 and Extraordinary Gazette 11 of 2025 in its commencement notice. [2021 consolidation, s 25](https://www.vnpf.com.vu/uploads/9/5/0/5/95052148/vnpf_act_%5Bcap_189%5D_amended.pdf), [2026 consolidation, s 25(1)–(1A)](https://www.vnpf.com.vu/uploads/9/5/0/5/95052148/vanuatu_national_provident_fund_consolidated_2026.pdf), [VNPF commencement notice](https://www.vnpf.com.vu/news.html)

Check total remuneration per employer each month. Below VUV 3,000 from that employer, no contribution is payable for that month. At VUV 3,000 or more, apply the rate to covered remuneration. In a January 2026 case at exactly VUV 3,000 with covered employment and no exemption, the employer and employee each owe VUV 180. Section 25 does not specify an earnings cap. Deduct the employee's share when paying the remuneration. If the employer misses the deduction, liability for that share passes to the employer. [VNPF Act, s 25](https://www.vnpf.com.vu/uploads/9/5/0/5/95052148/vanuatu_national_provident_fund_consolidated_2026.pdf)

## Remittance and records

Remit the contributions payable for the month by the end of the following month in the prescribed form and manner. If contributions remain unpaid after the due date, s 26(2) applies 5% of the unpaid contribution amount for each month or part of a month. If the surcharge calculated under that rule is below VUV 50, the payable surcharge is VUV 100. The Board may remit it in whole or part. [VNPF Act, s 26](https://www.vnpf.com.vu/uploads/9/5/0/5/95052148/vanuatu_national_provident_fund_consolidated_2026.pdf)

For contributions from 1 January 2008, the Fund credits 50% to retirement, 25% to investment and 25% to medi-save under s 30. The Fund's income, specified interest, withdrawals and instruments are exempt under s 46. Employee bankruptcy does not stop contributions from remuneration under s 47. [VNPF Act, ss 30 and 46–47](https://www.vnpf.com.vu/uploads/9/5/0/5/95052148/vanuatu_national_provident_fund_consolidated_2026.pdf)

Check the worker category, registration, monthly base and filing evidence. Exempt and cross-border workers, the complete rounding rules, particular registration and remittance procedures and qualified review remain open. [VNPF employer guidance](https://www.vnpf.com.vu/employers.html)
