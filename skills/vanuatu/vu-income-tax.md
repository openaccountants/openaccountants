# Vanuatu personal income tax

## Ordinary personal income tax

Vanuatu has no ordinary domestic personal income tax. An ordinary personal income tax rate, threshold, residence test and annual return therefore do not apply. The Competent Authority's residence criteria for the Common Reporting Standard serve a separate reporting purpose. [Vanuatu Competent Authority guide, 20 August 2024](https://vanuatucustoms.gov.vu/images/Forms/VCA/VU_AEOI_Guide_v4.pdf), [Global Forum assessment, para 23, adopted 23 April 2026](https://www.oecd.org/content/dam/oecd/en/publications/reports/2026/04/global-forum-on-transparency-and-exchange-of-information-for-tax-purposes-vanuatu-2026-second-round-supplementary-report_7a20b670/75b3bc85-en.pdf)

## Separate rent tax

Identify the taxpayer and the 6-month chargeable period. Establish chargeable rent from leases of land in Vanuatu. Apply the exclusion to the extent that a VAT-registered person derives rent subject to VAT. For a natural person, apply 12.5% to chargeable rent above VUV 200,000 for the period. If chargeable rent is at or below that threshold, the Schedule 1 charge is zero. Check the Act's separate rules for trustees, joint or family arrangements, subleases, deemed rent, exemptions and bad debts before fixing the chargeable amount. [Rent Taxation Act, ss 1, 3–11A and Sch 1](https://vanuatucustoms.gov.vu/images/TAA/196_Rent_Taxation_Act_Consolidated_2020.pdf)

For example, VUV 300,000 in chargeable rent for a natural person with no special adjustment gives (VUV 300,000 − VUV 200,000) × 12.5% = VUV 12,500. [Rent Taxation Act, Sch 1](https://vanuatucustoms.gov.vu/images/TAA/196_Rent_Taxation_Act_Consolidated_2020.pdf), [DCIR rent-tax guide](https://vanuatucustoms.gov.vu/images/Brochures/IRD/eng/Rent_Tax_Guide.pdf)

The periods run from 1 December to 31 May and 1 June to 30 November. Under ss 11 and 14 as substituted from 1 January 2020, the taxpayer returns all rent within 28 days after period end and pays by the return date. The unadjusted dates are 28 June and 28 December. Check the DCIR calendar and applicable date adjustments for the actual period. [Rent Taxation Act, ss 1, 11 and 14](https://vanuatucustoms.gov.vu/images/TAA/196_Rent_Taxation_Act_Consolidated_2020.pdf), [Act 7 of 2019, s 2 and Schedule](https://vanuatucustoms.gov.vu/images/TAA/Rent_Taxation_Amendment_Act_No_7_of_2019.pdf), [DCIR dates](https://vanuatucustoms.gov.vu/index.php/important-dates)

## Checks and limits

Confirm the taxpayer, lease and VAT status, then check the return form and any period-specific notice. This guide covers the ordinary computation and filing rule in the published 2020 consolidation. Special rent cases, penalties, current submission channels and qualified review remain open. [DCIR rent-tax return and instructions](https://vanuatucustoms.gov.vu/images/Forms/rates_taxes_forms/Approved-Rent-Taxation-Return-Form-2021.pdf)
