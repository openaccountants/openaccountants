# Bosnia and Herzegovina: tax pack

Bosnia and Herzegovina has three tax jurisdictions for direct taxes: the Federation of BiH (FBiH), Republika Srpska (RS) and Brčko District (BD). Each has its own income tax, contributions, corporate tax and tax administration (Porezna uprava FBiH, Poreska uprava RS, Porezna uprava BD). VAT (PDV, 17%) is a single state-level tax run by the Indirect Taxation Authority (UIO, uino.gov.ba). The tax year is the calendar year. Currency: convertible mark (KM, BAM), pegged to the euro.
