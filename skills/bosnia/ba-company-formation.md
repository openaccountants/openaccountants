# Bosnia and Herzegovina Company Formation & Entity Choice v0.3

> **General reference only.** This skill is general tax/accounting reference material for AI-assisted workflows. It has not been reviewed for any specific person's facts, documents, elections, deadlines, residency, filing status, or local procedures. Do not rely on it to file, pay, amend, or take a tax position without review by a qualified professional in the relevant jurisdiction.

## Who this applies to

Use this skill to choose a legal form and prepare the formation and first-compliance checklist for a business with its seat in the Federation of Bosnia and Herzegovina (FBiH), Republika Srpska (RS) or Brčko District (BD), in 2026. Choose the seat first: company law, registry court and direct-tax administration differ. VAT is a state tax administered by the Indirect Taxation Authority (UIO). A branch operating in another part of BiH needs a separate direct-tax analysis. [FBiH Company Law arts. 1–4, 8](https://www.pufbih.ba/v1/public/upload/zakoni/e2370-zakon-o-privrednim-drustvima-precisceni.pdf); [BD Companies Law arts. 1–4](https://skupstinabd.ba/3-zakon/ba/Zakon%20o%20preduzec-ima%20Brc--ko%20Distrikta%20BiH/000%2049-11%20Zakon%20o%20preduzec-ima%20Brc--ko%20distrikta%20BiH,%20prec--is--c-en%20tekst.pdf); [VAT Law arts. 1–3](https://www.uino.gov.ba/portal/wp-content/uploads/PROPISI/2_Porezi/1_PDV/1_Zakon_o_PDV/B/B-1-Zakon_o_PDV-b.pdf).

All three systems provide a general partnership, limited partnership, limited liability company (d.o.o.) and joint-stock company (FBiH/BD d.d.; RS a.d., open or closed). Domestic and foreign natural and legal persons may found companies, subject to special legislation. The FBiH general partnership and the general partners of a limited partnership answer jointly with all their assets. FBiH limited partners, shareholders and d.o.o. members normally have limited liability, but cannot use it to shelter personal purposes contrary to the company and other members, company assets treated as their own, creditor fraud or harm, or asset diversion/assumption of obligations where they knew or should have known that the company could not pay. Do not assume that choosing a d.o.o. removes personal exposure. [Company Law arts. 2–5](https://www.pufbih.ba/v1/public/upload/zakoni/e2370-zakon-o-privrednim-drustvima-precisceni.pdf); [RS Company Law arts. 2–6](https://www.vladars.net/sr-SP-Cyrl/Vlada/Ministarstva/mpp/pravniokvir/Documents/%d0%97%d0%b0%d0%ba%d0%be%d0%bd%20%d0%be%20%d0%bf%d1%80%d0%b8%d0%b2%d1%80%d0%b5%d0%b4%d0%bd%d0%b8%d0%bc%20%d0%b4%d1%80%d1%83%d1%88%d1%82%d0%b2%d0%b8%d0%bc%d0%b0%20%d0%a1%d0%bb%d1%83%d0%b6%d0%b1%d0%b5%d0%bd%d0%b8%20%d0%b3%d0%bb%d0%b0%d1%81%d0%bd%d0%b8%d0%ba%20%d0%b1%d1%80%d0%be%d1%98%2045%2025.pdf); [BD Companies Law arts. 2–3](https://skupstinabd.ba/3-zakon/ba/Zakon%20o%20preduzec-ima%20Brc--ko%20Distrikta%20BiH/000%2049-11%20Zakon%20o%20preduzec-ima%20Brc--ko%20distrikta%20BiH,%20prec--is--c-en%20tekst.pdf).

A sole trader acts in their own name and for their own account; it is not a separate company. In the FBiH an **obrtnik** is entered in the **Obrtni registar**, maintained by the Federal Ministry of Development, Entrepreneurship and Crafts, with the competent municipal/city authority handling the crafts-law procedure. In RS and BD the individual business is a **preduzetnik**; BD expressly imposes full-asset liability. Double-entry bookkeeping does not itself turn an entrepreneur into a company. Route personal taxation to [bosnia-income-tax](bosnia-income-tax.md), rather than applying corporate profit-tax rules merely because accounts are kept. [Crafts Law arts. 1–3](https://www.pufbih.ba/v1/public/upload/zakoni/7f411-zakon-o-obrtu-i-srodnim-djelatnostima-u-fbih-75-21-novi.pdf); [BD Companies Law art. 2](https://skupstinabd.ba/3-zakon/ba/Zakon%20o%20preduzec-ima%20Brc--ko%20Distrikta%20BiH/000%2049-11%20Zakon%20o%20preduzec-ima%20Brc--ko%20distrikta%20BiH,%20prec--is--c-en%20tekst.pdf); [RS Accounting Law arts. 2, 6](https://www.narodnaskupstinars.net/sites/default/files/upload/dokumenti/akti/usvojeni_zakoni/ci/Zak%20o%20rac%20i%20reviziji.zip).

## The base

### Capital and funding

For an FBiH d.o.o., separate subscribed cash, contributions in kind/rights, and formation costs. Make all in-kind/rights contributions available in full before the registration application. Cash paid before that application must be at least the greater of half the subscribed cash and KM 1,000. Check the aggregate-cash and per-contribution minima in the table separately. Founders bear formation costs in proportion to their shares unless they agree otherwise; costs cannot be paid from, or counted towards, share capital. [Company Law arts. 307, 309](https://www.pufbih.ba/v1/public/upload/zakoni/e2370-zakon-o-privrednim-drustvima-precisceni.pdf).

For an RS d.o.o., check the minimum per founder and the subscribed-cash amount separately. The minimum cash capital is paid to the temporary account before registration. If subscribed cash is at least KM 5,000, pay at least half to that account before registration and put the balance-payment dates in the founding act, no later than 2 years after registration. Do not assume that the KM 1 minimum means an arbitrary larger subscribed amount can remain wholly unpaid. [Company Law art. 107, as amended by 17/23](https://www.vladars.net/sr-SP-Cyrl/Vlada/Ministarstva/mpp/pravniokvir/Documents/%d0%97%d0%b0%d0%ba%d0%be%d0%bd%20%d0%be%20%d0%bf%d1%80%d0%b8%d0%b2%d1%80%d0%b5%d0%b4%d0%bd%d0%b8%d0%bc%20%d0%b4%d1%80%d1%83%d1%88%d1%82%d0%b2%d0%b8%d0%bc%d0%b0%20%d0%a1%d0%bb%d1%83%d0%b6%d0%b1%d0%b5%d0%bd%d0%b8%20%d0%b3%d0%bb%d0%b0%d1%81%d0%bd%d0%b8%d0%ba%20%d0%b1%d1%80%d0%be%d1%98%2045%2025.pdf).

### Founding documents

An FBiH d.o.o. uses a written founding contract with signatures certified as prescribed; a single founder adopts a founding decision. The act may be an electronic document signed under the applicable electronic-signature rules. A proxy needs an express power of attorney with a certified signature, or the prescribed electronic alternative. The mandatory contents include founder identity/address or name/seat; company name, seat and activity; capital, cash and in-kind/right values, members' shares; rights and duties; default on contributions/other obligations; formation costs and how paid; managers/representatives and registration applicant; failed-formation consequences; and any fixed duration. Adopt the company statute within the founding-act period, at most 60 days after registration. [Company Law arts. 304–306, 310](https://www.pufbih.ba/v1/public/upload/zakoni/e2370-zakon-o-privrednim-drustvima-precisceni.pdf). Obtain the registry's current document-authentication requirements before execution; this skill does not resolve every interaction between registration-law notarial wording and subsequent company-law/signature rules.

In RS the founding act is ordinarily a notarial deed. A single-member d.o.o. paying only the statutory minimum cash capital uses notarial signature certification instead. For electronic registration of that single-member/minimum-cash case, the statutory authentication and electronic-signature method replaces notarial signature certification; do not assume the exception covers a multi-member or higher-capital company. [Company Law art. 7, replaced by 82/19](https://www.vladars.net/sr-SP-Cyrl/Vlada/Ministarstva/mpp/pravniokvir/Documents/%d0%97%d0%b0%d0%ba%d0%be%d0%bd%20%d0%be%20%d0%bf%d1%80%d0%b8%d0%b2%d1%80%d0%b5%d0%b4%d0%bd%d0%b8%d0%bc%20%d0%b4%d1%80%d1%83%d1%88%d1%82%d0%b2%d0%b8%d0%bc%d0%b0%20%d0%a1%d0%bb%d1%83%d0%b6%d0%b1%d0%b5%d0%bd%d0%b8%20%d0%b3%d0%bb%d0%b0%d1%81%d0%bd%d0%b8%d0%ba%20%d0%b1%d1%80%d0%be%d1%98%2045%2025.pdf).

APIF's public d.o.o. checklist lists: founding decision; director appointment; notarised director acceptance; notarised specimen-signature form (**OP obrazac**); certified founder/director IDs; bank confirmation of paid contribution; electronically completed registration application; proof of founder/director residence; and the founder's direct-tax clearance. A foreign director needs a temporary residence permit. The court may request additional documents. [Registar poslovnih subjekata](https://www.apif.net/index.php?option=com_content&view=article&id=7:registar-poslovnih-subjekata&catid=9:registri&Itemid=118&lang=sr). For several founders, use the appropriate founding contract rather than treating the single-founder decision label as universal.

The RS registration amendment **1/26** adds the company email address. For a foreign corporate founder whose founder is another legal person, obtain registry extracts for each ownership-chain legal person with an interest of at least 20%, through to the individual ultimate owner, with competent-state certification and apostille as prescribed. The provision also requires the relevant residence and RS direct-tax evidence. Where the notary obtains domestic individuals' residence and founder tax-clearance data through official electronic exchange **and records them in the notarial instrument**, original/certified copies of those particular documents need not be supplied again. Where the ultimate owner cannot objectively be identified under the extract procedure, the foreign legal founder's authorised representative supplies a written statement identifying the ultimate owner. This is a conditional alternative, not permission to omit ownership information. [1/26 art. 9, replacing registration art. 32](https://www.vladars.net/sr-SP-Cyrl/Vlada/Ministarstva/muls/PAO/PublishingImages/Pages/Splash/%D0%97%D0%B0%D0%BA%D0%BE%D0%BD%20%D0%BE%20%D0%BE%D0%B1%D0%B8%D1%99%D0%B5%D0%B6%D0%B0%D0%B2%D0%B0%D1%9A%D1%83%20%D0%B4%D0%B0%D0%BD%D0%B0%20%D0%B6%D0%B0%D0%BB%D0%BE%D1%81%D1%82%D0%B8.pdf).

### Accounting setup

In FBiH, classify a newly established entity using its first accounting period's data. Apply the asset/revenue/staff tests together: average business assets are opening plus closing net book value divided by 2; average staff is the sum of month-end employee counts, including staff outside FBiH, divided by the number of months in the period. Banks, insurers, leasing companies and the other financial organisations specified in art. 5(8) are large regardless of numerical size. Medium and large entities and public-interest entities require an audit; special legislation may impose it on others. Do not infer a universal small-company audit exemption. [Accounting Law arts. 5, 57](https://www.pufbih.ba/v1/public/upload/zakoni/4ffff-zak-o-racunovodstvu-i-reviziji-u-fbih-bos..pdf).

## Deadlines and filing

### Registration workflow

**FBiH.** File with the registry court for the seat before trading. Legal personality arises on entry; regulated activities still need the required permission. Paper and electronic applications are legally possible. The electronic route requires the prescribed account, documents and signature checks. The court's **3-day document examination** is not a promise that incorporation finishes within that period. If ordered to cure deficiencies, comply within the court-set period, at most 30 days. Electronic transmission to the tax authority, statistics office and, when needed, customs supports tax/customs identification; obtain the resulting identifiers before using them. [Company Law arts. 4, 6](https://www.pufbih.ba/v1/public/upload/zakoni/e2370-zakon-o-privrednim-drustvima-precisceni.pdf); [Registration Law arts. 3–5, 48–50, 54](https://www.pufbih.ba/v1/public/upload/zakoni/af930-zakon-o-registraciji-preciscen.pdf).

The public [BiH registration portal](https://www.bizreg.ba/) presently describes implementation at the Municipal Courts in **Sarajevo and Travnik**, with other courts forthcoming. Its observed route is **Login → Register → email/password → verify the emailed link → log in and complete user details → Confirm → Start registration**. It also describes an unsigned route through **Frames / Applicant → Browse application**, followed by printing and taking the uniquely coded application to court. These are the public instructions read on 15 November 2026; no account or application was created for this research. Do not promise a fully online end-to-end service in every court.

**RS.** Start by filing the registration application through APIF's one-stop counters, operating since 1 December 2013. APIF lists 11 units: **Banja Luka, Prijedor, Gradiška, Modriča, Doboj, Bijeljina, Zvornik, Foča, Trebinje, Sokolac and Istočno Sarajevo**. The registration-law chain is 67/13, 15/16, 84/19 and 1/26. [APIF public registration instructions](https://www.apif.net/index.php?option=com_content&view=article&id=7:registar-poslovnih-subjekata&catid=9:registri&Itemid=118&lang=sr); [1/26 amending preamble and arts. 1–13](https://www.vladars.net/sr-SP-Cyrl/Vlada/Ministarstva/muls/PAO/PublishingImages/Pages/Splash/%D0%97%D0%B0%D0%BA%D0%BE%D0%BD%20%D0%BE%20%D0%BE%D0%B1%D0%B8%D1%99%D0%B5%D0%B6%D0%B0%D0%B2%D0%B0%D1%9A%D1%83%20%D0%B4%D0%B0%D0%BD%D0%B0%20%D0%B6%D0%B0%D0%BB%D0%BE%D1%81%D1%82%D0%B8.pdf). The 2026 amendment provides a name check/reservation before filing. Where the court identifies an identical or similar reserved/registered name and orders correction, change it within 3 days of receipt; noncompliance leads to rejection. An electronic identical-name match produces a system warning to choose another name; a similar-name court order carries the same correction requirement. [1/26 art. 4, new registration art. 12(4)–(9)](https://www.vladars.net/sr-SP-Cyrl/Vlada/Ministarstva/muls/PAO/PublishingImages/Pages/Splash/%D0%97%D0%B0%D0%BA%D0%BE%D0%BD%20%D0%BE%20%D0%BE%D0%B1%D0%B8%D1%99%D0%B5%D0%B6%D0%B0%D0%B2%D0%B0%D1%9A%D1%83%20%D0%B4%D0%B0%D0%BD%D0%B0%20%D0%B6%D0%B0%D0%BB%D0%BE%D1%81%D1%82%D0%B8.pdf).

**BD.** Personality arises when the registry court enters the company. A foreign company's branch is registered where that branch has its seat. Do not substitute an FBiH or RS checklist for BD court requirements. [11/20 arts. 1–2, amending company arts. 4, 9](https://skupstinabd.ba/3-zakon/ba/Zakon%20o%20preduzec-ima%20Brc--ko%20Distrikta%20BiH/010B11-20%20Zakon%20o%20izmjenama%20i%20dopuni%20Zakona%20o%20preduzecima%20Brcko%20distrikta%20BiH.pdf).

### VAT and first direct-tax filings

For VAT, test taxable supplies plus exempt supplies carrying credit, aggregate commonly controlled businesses, and consider **likely** turnover rather than waiting for actual receipts alone. A person first becoming, or likely to become, liable applies to UIO by the **20th of the month following the month** in which the threshold was exceeded or likely to be exceeded. Art. 57 also states a general application requirement at least **8 days before taxable business starts**; identify the applicable route with UIO rather than treating the crossing deadline as permission to delay a known pre-start registration. UIO has **15 days after receipt** to decide and fixes the effective registration date in its decision. [VAT Law art. 57](https://www.uino.gov.ba/portal/wp-content/uploads/PROPISI/2_Porezi/1_PDV/1_Zakon_o_PDV/B/B-1-Zakon_o_PDV-b.pdf); [80/23 threshold amendment](https://www.uino.gov.ba/portal/wp-content/uploads/PROPISI/2_Porezi/1_PDV/1_Zakon_o_PDV/B/B-H-S-1-5-Z-o-PDV-sl-list-80-23-24-11-23.pdf).

The below-threshold small-business exclusion does not automatically cover someone already registered or previously above the threshold. Voluntary application is possible at any time, but registration lasts at least 60 months. After registration, the VAT period is a calendar month; file by the **10th of the following month**, including nil returns. For forms, payment and detailed exceptions use [bosnia-vat](bosnia-vat.md). [VAT Law arts. 38–39, 44](https://www.uino.gov.ba/portal/wp-content/uploads/PROPISI/2_Porezi/1_PDV/1_Zakon_o_PDV/B/B-1-Zakon_o_PDV-b.pdf).

- **FBiH corporate tax:** the ordinary calendar-year annual return is due **31 March, or the last working day in March**. For modified periods under art. 47(2), Rulebook art. 101(2) gives a **30-day** return period from registration, cessation or status change according to the registry decision, or from the Minister-authorised period end. Returns after **1 January 2026** require qualified electronic certification. Final annual tax is due within **20 working days after filing**. First-time business has the art. 52(7) advance-payment exception; later monthly advances are paid by the end of the current month for the previous month. [Rulebook art. 101](https://pufbih.ba/v1/public/upload/zakoni/03062026%20Pravilnik%20o%20primjeni%20Zakona%20o%20porezu%20na%20dobit%2039-26.pdf); [Corporate Tax Law arts. 47–53](https://www.pufbih.ba/v1/public/upload/zakoni/9080b-zakon-o-porezu-na-dobit-precisceni.pdf).
- **RS corporate tax:** ordinary annual return within **90 days after the tax year**, and for a calendar year no later than **31 March**; the balance is paid by the return deadline. A Ministry-approved different tax year is possible. Monthly advances, based on the previous annual return, are due by the **10th of the following month**. A first registered taxpayer must submit its end-of-year profit estimate within **30 days after starting business** to establish advances; do not grant it the FBiH exception. The separate annual withholding return is due within **30 days after year end**. [Corporate Tax Law arts. 37–39](https://www.vladars.net/sr-SP-Cyrl/Vlada/Ministarstva/mf/Documents/%D0%97%D0%B0%D0%BA%D0%BE%D0%BD%20%D0%BE%20%D0%BF%D0%BE%D1%80%D0%B5%D0%B7%D1%83%20%D0%BD%D0%B0%20%D0%B4%D0%BE%D0%B1%D0%B8%D1%82%20%D0%A1%D0%93%2094-15.pdf); [1/17 art. 13](https://www.narodnaskupstinars.net/sites/default/files/upload/dokumenti/akti/usvojeni_zakoni/Zakon%20o%20izmj%20i%20dop%20Zakona%20o%20porezu%20na%20dobit_la.zip); [58/19 art. 9](https://www.narodnaskupstinars.net/sites/default/files/upload/dokumenti/akti/usvojeni_zakoni/Zak%20izmj%20dop%20Zak%20porezu%20na%20dobit_la.zip). The later [48/24 amendment](https://www.narodnaskupstinars.net/sites/default/files/upload/dokumenti/akti/usvojeni_zakoni/lat_zakon_o_izmj_i_dop_zakona_o_porezu_na_dobit_hp_la.zip) and [114/25 amendment, effective 1 January 2026](https://www.narodnaskupstinars.net/sites/default/files/upload/dokumenti/akti/usvojeni_zakoni/la/Zak%20o%20izmj%20i%20dop%20Zak%20o%20porezu%20na%20dobit.zip) do not change these ordinary filing/advance clauses.
- **BD corporate tax:** return within **90 days after year end**, and for a calendar year by **31 March**; pay the annual balance by the return deadline. Monthly advances based on the preceding annual return are due by the **10th for the previous month**. Art. 24 does not itself grant a first-year holiday; obtain the first-year treatment before assuming no advance is due. [Corporate Tax Law arts. 20–24](https://skupstinabd.ba/3-zakon/ba/Zakon%20o%20porezu%20na%20dobit/05B17-22%20Zakon%20o%20porezu%20na%20dobit%20-%20prec--is--eni%20tekst.pdf); [38/22 amendment, unchanged arts. 22 and 24](https://skupstinabd.ba/3-zakon/ba/Zakon%20o%20porezu%20na%20dobit/06B38-22%20Zakon%20o%20izmjeni%20i%20dopunama%20Zakona%20o%20porezu%20na%20dobit.pdf).

These initial filing reminders route to [ba-corporate-income-tax](ba-corporate-income-tax.md) for computations and forms. Employer withholding and contributions follow separate FBiH, RS and BD rules: build the payroll calendar from [bosnia-payroll](bosnia-payroll.md) and [bosnia-social-contributions](bosnia-social-contributions.md) before hiring. The old general description of all employer obligations as monthly is not a complete payment-timing rule.

### Annual financial statements and audit

**FBiH:** file annual financial statements, statistical reports and the classification notice with **FIA by the last day of February** for the previous year. Management report, adoption decision and profit-allocation decision follow by **30 June**. Consolidated statements are due by the **end of April**. Art. 44 requires electronic **and paper** submission, signature/seal of the certified accountant including name and valid licence number, and the authorised representative's signature and company seal. A different financial year still requires calendar-year statistical reporting under art. 44(3). A company formed in the year whose account has had no movements through 31 December other than the mandatory deposit has the specific financial-statement exemption, but must file its inactivity statement by the end of February. Do not assume the exemption where costs or other transactions have affected the account; refer unclear accrual cases. [Accounting Law art. 44](https://www.pufbih.ba/v1/public/upload/zakoni/4ffff-zak-o-racunovodstvu-i-reviziji-u-fbih-bos..pdf).

**RS:** annual financial statements go to APIF's Register of Financial Statements by the **last day of February** for the previous year; consolidated statements by the **end of April**. These are the rules of Accounting Law **115/25**, effective **1 January 2026**, not the parliamentary upload date. [Accounting Law arts. 41–43, 127](https://www.narodnaskupstinars.net/sites/default/files/upload/dokumenti/akti/usvojeni_zakoni/ci/Zak%20o%20rac%20i%20reviziji.zip).

**BD:** annual financial statements and annual management reports are due within **60 days after financial-year end**; consolidated reports within **120 days**. The newly formed, mandatory-capital-only inactivity exception requires an inactivity statement by the end of February. It does not turn the ordinary deadline into an end-of-February deadline. [Accounting Law art. 45](https://skupstinabd.ba/3-zakon/ba/Zakon%20o%20rac--unovodstvu%20i%20reviziji/003%2022-16%20Zakon%20o%20rac+unovodstvu%20i%20reviziji%20u%20Brc+ko%20distriktu%20Bosne%20i%20Hercegovine%20B.pdf); [50/18 amendment, unchanged art. 45](https://skupstinabd.ba/3-zakon/ba/Zakon%20o%20rac--unovodstvu%20i%20reviziji/004%2050-18%20Zakon%20o%20izmjeni%20Zakona%20o%20racunovodstvu%20i%20reviziji%20B.pdf).

## Two worked examples that recompute

### FBiH: cash, in-kind capital and separate costs

Assume an ordinary d.o.o. with subscribed cash **KM 6,000**, in-kind capital **KM 4,000**, every contribution at least the statutory minimum, and separate formation costs **KM 600**. Before applying, cash capital is max(half of 6,000, 1,000) = **KM 3,000**; all **KM 4,000** in-kind capital must be available. The separate cash budget is **KM 3,600**, with **KM 3,000** subscribed cash still to fund. Costs do not increase or discharge capital. These are illustrative inputs, not statutory fees. [Company Law arts. 307, 309](https://www.pufbih.ba/v1/public/upload/zakoni/e2370-zakon-o-privrednim-drustvima-precisceni.pdf).

```oa-check
name: FBiH subscribed capital and separate formation cash
inputs:
  subscribed_cash: 6000
  in_kind: 4000
  formation_costs: 600
steps:
  cash_before_application: max(subscribed_cash * 0.5, 1000)
  total_capital: subscribed_cash + in_kind
  capital_delivered_before_application: cash_before_application + in_kind
  separate_cash_budget: cash_before_application + formation_costs
  cash_remaining: subscribed_cash - cash_before_application
expected:
  cash_before_application: 3000
  total_capital: 10000
  capital_delivered_before_application: 7000
  separate_cash_budget: 3600
  cash_remaining: 3000
```

### Two founders: minimum-capital comparison and an RS funding choice

For two founders, ordinary RS minimum cash is **2 × KM 1 = KM 2**, compared with the FBiH aggregate-cash minimum **KM 1,000**, a **KM 998** difference. That compares legal minima only. If the two founders instead choose an RS d.o.o. with subscribed cash **KM 6,000 + KM 2,000 = KM 8,000**, the at-least-KM-5,000 rule requires at least **KM 4,000** before registration and leaves **KM 4,000** payable under the founding act within **2 years**. The multi-founder act requires the ordinary notarial form; no fee is assumed. Assess seat, liability and operating facts alongside capital before making the entity decision. [Company Law arts. 7, 107](https://www.vladars.net/sr-SP-Cyrl/Vlada/Ministarstva/mpp/pravniokvir/Documents/%d0%97%d0%b0%d0%ba%d0%be%d0%bd%20%d0%be%20%d0%bf%d1%80%d0%b8%d0%b2%d1%80%d0%b5%d0%b4%d0%bd%d0%b8%d0%bc%20%d0%b4%d1%80%d1%83%d1%88%d1%82%d0%b2%d0%b8%d0%bc%d0%b0%20%d0%a1%d0%bb%d1%83%d0%b6%d0%b1%d0%b5%d0%bd%d0%b8%20%d0%b3%d0%bb%d0%b0%d1%81%d0%bd%d0%b8%d0%ba%20%d0%b1%d1%80%d0%be%d1%98%2045%2025.pdf); [FBiH Company Law art. 307](https://www.pufbih.ba/v1/public/upload/zakoni/e2370-zakon-o-privrednim-drustvima-precisceni.pdf).

```oa-check
name: Two-founder minimum comparison and elected RS capital
inputs:
  founders: 2
  rs_minimum_per_founder: 1
  fbih_minimum_cash: 1000
  founder_one_cash: 6000
  founder_two_cash: 2000
steps:
  rs_minimum_cash: founders * rs_minimum_per_founder
  minimum_cash_difference: fbih_minimum_cash - rs_minimum_cash
  subscribed_cash: founder_one_cash + founder_two_cash
  cash_before_registration: subscribed_cash * 0.5
  cash_remaining: subscribed_cash - cash_before_registration
expected:
  rs_minimum_cash: 2
  minimum_cash_difference: 998
  subscribed_cash: 8000
  cash_before_registration: 4000
  cash_remaining: 4000
```

## Ask the client first

Obtain the intended seat and actual operating locations; company or sole-trader choice; activity and special-sector permissions; founder count, nationality, legal status and complete ownership chain; director identity/residence/permit; cash and in-kind subscriptions and evidence of payment/valuation; cost budget; name/email; accounting year and projected turnover including common-control businesses; prior VAT history; employees and hiring dates; group/consolidation/public-interest status; and the first-period financial classification inputs.

Prepare a reviewable founding-document checklist, capital-funding reconciliation, registry submission pack and first-deadline calendar. Stop before signing or filing if the competent court's current required fields, authentication, fees, bank evidence or local procedural requirements have not been established. Do not guarantee the old **10–15 business-day** completion estimate, assume a universal stamp or municipal-company-registration step, or infer a beneficial-owner filing deadline from the new RS registration-document provisions.
