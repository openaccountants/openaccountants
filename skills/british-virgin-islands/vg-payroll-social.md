# British Virgin Islands Payroll & Social Contributions

## Payroll Tax (the main employment tax)

- **Overview of Payroll Tax** — Payroll Tax is the principal direct tax in the BVI, charged on remuneration paid by employers and on self-employed persons carrying on business in the territory. The total rate is split between an employer share and a fixed 8% employee share that the employer withholds. The first US$10,000 of annual remuneration per employee is exempt.
- **Total Payroll Tax rate — Class 1 (small) employers** — 10% of remuneration (8% employee + 2% employer) percent  _([Payroll Taxes Act, 2004](https://bvi.gov.vg/content/payroll-tax))_
- **Total Payroll Tax rate — Class 2 (larger) employers** — 14% of remuneration (8% employee + 6% employer) percent  _([Payroll Taxes Act, 2004](https://bvi.gov.vg/content/payroll-tax))_
- **Employee share of Payroll Tax** — 8% (same for Class 1 and Class 2), withheld by the employer percent  _(Payroll Taxes Act, 2004)_
- **Payroll Tax annual exemption per employee** — First US$10,000 of annual remuneration is exempt USD  _(Payroll Taxes Act, 2004)_
- **Class 1 employer qualification (must meet all)** — Annual payroll ≤ US$150,000, annual turnover ≤ US$300,000, and ≤ 7 employees USD  _(Payroll Taxes Act, 2004)_
- **Class 2 employer trigger** — An employer exceeding any one Class 1 threshold (payroll > US$150,000, turnover > US$300,000, or > 7 employees) is a Class 2 employer  _(Payroll Taxes Act, 2004)_
- **Payroll Tax filing & remittance deadline** — Monthly return and payment due by the 21st of the following month ((approx — confirm))  _(Payroll Taxes Act, 2004)_
- **Social Security contribution — total rate** — 8.5% of insurable earnings (4% employee + 4.5% employer) percent  _([Social Security Act](https://www.vissb.vg/contributions/))_
- **Social Security — employee share** — 4% of insurable earnings percent  _(Social Security Act)_
- **Social Security — employer share** — 4.5% of insurable earnings percent  _(Social Security Act)_
- **Social Security maximum insurable earnings** — US$51,000 per year (2025); rising to US$53,400 per year from 1 January 2026 USD  _([Social Security Act](https://www.vissb.vg/contributions/))_
- **National Health Insurance (NHI) — total rate** — 7.5% of insurable earnings (3.75% employee + 3.75% employer) percent  _([National Health Insurance Regulations](https://www.vinhi.vg/nhi-contribution-breakdown/))_
- **NHI maximum insurable earnings** — US$102,000 per year (2025); rising to US$106,800 per year from 1 January 2026 USD  _([National Health Insurance Regulations](https://www.vinhi.vg/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/NHI-Maximum-Insurable-Earnings-2025-13.09.2024.pdf))_
- **Self-employed Payroll Tax** — Self-employed persons pay Payroll Tax at the applicable class rate (10% Class 1 / 14% Class 2) on their deemed remuneration percent  _(Payroll Taxes Act, 2004)_
