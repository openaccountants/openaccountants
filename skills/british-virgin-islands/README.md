# British Virgin Islands: tax pack

The British Virgin Islands collect no income tax, corporate income tax, capital gains tax or VAT. The Inland Revenue Department collects payroll tax, stamp duty, property tax, hotel accommodation tax and other levies, filed on eTax (efiling.gov.vg) from 2 November 2026. The Social Security Board collects social security and National Health Insurance. Amounts are US dollars.
