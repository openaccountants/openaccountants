# Turkmenistan VAT / GST

## Turkmenistan VAT / GST

## Value-added tax (VAT)

Turkmenistan levies VAT under the Tax Code of Turkmenistan at a standard rate of 15%, with a 0% rate for qualifying exports and international transport. VAT is administered and remitted to the State Tax Service.

> **The Tax Code has been read, and the two rate rows were right.** The Tax
> Directorate publishes the ***«Salgytlar hakynda» Türkmenistanyň bitewi Kanuny***
> (Unified Law of Turkmenistan "On Taxes") at
> [tax.gov.tm](https://tax.gov.tm/hukuknamalar). Article 103 confirms **15%** and the
> **0%** band, and article 105 confirms that **oil and gas are carved out of the export
> zero rate** — a claim this guide carried on a commercial source and which the statute
> bears out. What article 105 adds is **eight further zero-rated limbs** the guide did
> not list, and a definition of international carriage that excludes a case most
> readers would assume is inside it.
>
> _([«Salgytlar hakynda» Türkmenistanyň bitewi Kanuny, arts. 103, 105](https://tax.gov.tm/pdf/ckeditor_assets/attachments/10/2.pdf))_

- **VAT rates.** Article 103 applies 0% to article 105 transactions and 15% to other taxable transactions. For state-fixed prices and certain assessments using VAT-inclusive market prices, extract the VAT using the fraction rate / (100 + rate). At 15%, that is 15/115 of the VAT-inclusive price. ([Tax Code art 103](https://mejlis.gov.tm/single-code/10?lang=tm))
- **Exports of goods.** The zero rate covers exports of own-produced or purchased goods where crossing the Turkmen customs border is confirmed in the prescribed manner. The general export rule excludes natural and associated gas, oil and products of their processing. ([Tax Code art 105](https://mejlis.gov.tm/single-code/10?lang=tm))
- **Processed hydrocarbon exports.** A Turkmen resident legal entity can zero-rate its direct exports of products that it processed from crude oil and gas condensate purchased from non-residents. Confirm the customs crossing. The third paragraph of article 105 originally applied from 1 June to 31 December 2021. The law of 30 November 2024 restored it from official publication. ([Tax Code art 105](https://mejlis.gov.tm/single-code/10?lang=tm) · [2024 amending law parts I(2)(3) and II](https://turkmenistan.gov.tm/ru/post/90313/zakon-turkmenistana-o-vnesenii-izmenenij-i-dopolneniya-v-nekotorye-zakonodatelnye-akty-turkmenistana-7))
- **International carriage.** Article 105 zero-rates qualifying passenger and goods transport. Journeys whose origin and destination are both in Turkmenistan fall outside this category, even if the vehicle crosses the border en route. ([Tax Code art 105](https://mejlis.gov.tm/single-code/10?lang=tm))
- **Textile sales to exporters.** Producers can zero-rate sales of their own textile products to Turkmen resident individuals in business and resident legal entities who export the goods directly, with the required customs confirmation. ([Tax Code art 105](https://mejlis.gov.tm/single-code/10?lang=tm))
- **Other zero-rated supplies.** Article 105 includes export freight and handling, transit of foreign cargo, qualifying international vessel and aircraft supplies and servicing, duty-free shop sales, supplies for official use by eligible diplomatic missions and international organisations, qualifying fund-financed supplies and sales of own-produced agricultural goods. The November 2026 text includes the Government foreign-currency reserve, the Stabilisation Fund for its charitable funds and the named charitable fund for children needing guardianship. ([Tax Code art 105](https://mejlis.gov.tm/single-code/10?lang=tm))
- **Petroleum work supplies.** The zero rate can apply to supplies to qualifying contractors or subcontractors for petroleum work. It excludes supplies for other activities, those whose use cannot be determined in advance and inseparable mixed-use supplies. The recipient must notify its supplier before invoicing. Check the closing paragraph of article 105 for its contractor and subcontractor limits. ([Tax Code art 105](https://mejlis.gov.tm/single-code/10?lang=tm))
- **⚠ Privately-owned legal persons are not VAT payers** — Article 95(1) makes legal persons and private entrepreneurs VAT payers when they carry out the article 96 taxable transactions. **Article 95(2) then excludes four categories outright**, and the third is the striking one: *"**hususy eýeçilikdäki edara görnüşli taraplar**"* — **legal persons in private ownership**. Also excluded: the **Central Bank of Turkmenistan**; **contractors and subcontractors under the Law "On Hydrocarbon Resources"** (who **are** brought back in by paragraph 1 for taxable transactions **not** relating to petroleum operations); and **private entrepreneurs, for activity under the simplified taxation system**. Given a 50% profit-tax band on state-owned banks and insurers and an 8% band for residents, the Code consistently treats private ownership as the favoured category — but a scope rule this wide should be put to a practitioner before it is relied on. **Read twice: from the text layer and from the rendered page**  _([Bitewi Kanun, art. 95](https://tax.gov.tm/pdf/ckeditor_assets/attachments/10/2.pdf))_
- **Taxable transactions** — Article 96(2): supplying goods, performing works, providing services, **and transferring property rights**; and **construction-installation works carried out for one's own needs** — which expressly **does not** include works connected with installing or mounting purchased or self-produced equipment, or repairing it. Article 96(1) locates the charge by reference to the place-of-supply rules in articles 98 and 99  _([Bitewi Kanun, art. 96](https://tax.gov.tm/pdf/ckeditor_assets/attachments/10/2.pdf))_
- **⚠ VAT registration threshold — the Code sets none** — This row asked for *"a precise turnover threshold … confirmed against the Tax Code"*. **Article 95 contains no turnover threshold.** Liability turns on **what the person is** (legal person, private entrepreneur, and which of the four excluded categories they fall in) and **what they do** (an article 96 transaction), not on how much they turn over. The question this row poses does not have the kind of answer it assumes  _([Bitewi Kanun, arts. 95–96](https://tax.gov.tm/pdf/ckeditor_assets/attachments/10/2.pdf))_
- **Exempt supplies.** Article 106 includes education, insurance and reinsurance by insurers, and healthcare and dental prosthetics. Non-therapeutic cosmetic services fall outside the healthcare exemption. Check the specific conditions and exclusions for the supply. ([Tax Code art 106(1)](https://mejlis.gov.tm/single-code/10?lang=tm))
- **⚠ VAT periods — monthly for companies, but half-yearly for sole traders** — Article 104: for **legal persons**, and for parties in partnerships that include at least one legal person, the tax and reporting periods are **the calendar month**. For **private entrepreneurs**, and parties in partnerships with no legal person in them, the **tax period is the calendar year** and the reporting periods are **1 January – 30 June** and **1 July – 31 December**. This guide said "Monthly" without qualification, which is wrong for every sole trader  _([Bitewi Kanun, art. 104](https://tax.gov.tm/pdf/ckeditor_assets/attachments/10/2.pdf))_
- **Ordinary filing and payment.** File by the 20th of the month following the reporting period. Ordinary declarants pay by the 25th. Article 111 sets separate payment rules for the individuals and partnerships below and for tax agents. ([Tax Code art 111(1)–(5)](https://mejlis.gov.tm/single-code/10?lang=tm))
- **Half-yearly VAT payment.** VAT-paying individual entrepreneurs and members of partnerships with no legal entity as a partner pay by 1 September for January–June and by 1 March of the following year for July–December. The tax authority calculates the VAT from the return and sends the payment notices by 1 August and 1 February respectively. ([Tax Code arts 104(2), 108(3) and 111(5)](https://mejlis.gov.tm/single-code/10?lang=tm))
- **Non-resident tax agent report.** Where an agent withholds and pays VAT, the foreign legal entity or foreign individual entrepreneur does not file a VAT declaration for the transactions covered. The payer reports to its own tax authority by the 20th of the month after payment. The report identifies the payees, the amounts calculated, withheld and remitted, and payees from whom VAT was not withheld. ([Tax Code art 111(2)](https://mejlis.gov.tm/single-code/10?lang=tm))
- **Tax agent withholding.** A statutory tax agent generally withholds VAT at payment for taxable Turkmen transactions by a foreign legal entity or foreign individual entrepreneur who is unregistered for tax. Article 108(2) names resident legal entities, locally tax-registered individual entrepreneurs and foreign legal entities operating through a local branch, representative office or permanent establishment that make the relevant purchases. A payer receiving proof of local tax registration does not withhold under this rule. Where withholding cannot be performed or there is no eligible agent, the foreign supplier must register and account for VAT. ([Tax Code art 108(2)](https://mejlis.gov.tm/single-code/10?lang=tm))
- **Tax agent payment.** Remit at the withholding deadline, or within 15 days of that deadline if the underlying payment is made abroad. If the underlying payment is after the end of the tax period in which the transaction occurred, the agent funds the VAT, remits within 15 days after that period ends and recovers it through withholding later. In that case calculate VAT as at the transaction date or, for long-term contracts, the last day of that tax period. ([Tax Code arts 108(2) and 111(4)](https://mejlis.gov.tm/single-code/10?lang=tm))
- **Special Ministry deadlines.** For designated taxpayer categories the Ministry can set different filing and payment deadlines, within 30 days after the corresponding deadlines in article 111(1), (3), (4) and (5). Check the category and implementing instrument before using a different deadline. ([Tax Code art 111(6)](https://mejlis.gov.tm/single-code/10?lang=tm))
- **Input VAT recovery** — Registered taxpayers may offset input VAT on business purchases against output VAT  _(Tax Code of Turkmenistan)_
