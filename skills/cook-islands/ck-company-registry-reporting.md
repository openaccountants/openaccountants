# Cook Islands: domestic company registry and reporting

## Scope and period gate

This reference establishes selected registry prerequisites, company records,
annual return month and statutory financial statement/audit classifications
for an ordinary domestic company genuinely first incorporated after
1 January 2026. It provides a component working paper for a credentialed
reviewer. It does not establish that a company is the right entity or that
the business may start operating.

Load workflow-base and company-formation-workflow-base. Retain their source
conditions, profile confirmation, substantive entity-choice checkpoint,
refusal messages, outputs and checks. This reference does not fill every
mandatory formation country slot. A complete formation engagement remains
held until each applicable slot and output is established. Unknown facts
remain Needs Input; unresolved law remains on hold. No factual assumption
is authorised merely to finish a checklist.

Obtain the incorporation certificate and actual history. Re-registration,
continuation, restoration, overseas/international entities, groups,
regulated activities, insolvency and disputed ownership need separate
sources and analysis. An application or a historic registration number
does not prove this new-company scope. [Incorporation certificate, section
9][CERT]; [2021 amendment, sections 29 and 31][AMEND21]; [2025 amendment][AMEND25]

The 2021 transitional audit rule concerns periods ended before its
27 November 2021 commencement. Inserted section 412A concerns a small
company period already underway then and its special 31 March 2022 notice
cutoff. Neither becomes the ordinary rule for a genuinely new 2026 company,
whose first accounting period starts at incorporation. The 2025 amendment
changes legacy restoration provisions in sections 408/411B. It does not
establish new-company eligibility. [2021 amendment, sections 2, 29 and 31][AMEND21];
[Schedule 1 clause 1][DEFS]; [section 412A][TRANSITION]; [2025 amendment][AMEND25]

## Intake and source library

Confirm the purpose, activities, founder situation and reviewer under the
bases before selecting an entity. For this component record:

- Exact incorporation date and evidence that the company is genuinely new,
  domestic, ordinary, unregulated and outside the excluded cases.
- Current constitution, all changes, share register, share classes and
  voting shares. Distinguish registered holders from beneficial owners.
  Establish whether holder numbers and rights stayed fixed throughout the
  illustrated period. Joint holdings, changes and disputed entries are held.
- Each director's natural-person status, age, residence, consent,
  disqualification review and constitution qualifications. For the NZ branch,
  establish the qualifying NZ company directorship separately.
- Proposed registered office and postal address, both in Cook Islands;
  statutory records location, English accessibility and integrity controls.
- Registrar's allocated annual return month and any approved change;
  incorporation year, actual filing status and applicable period.
- Balance date, accounting period and any valid adoption of another balance
  date; written shareholder notice, its delivery date and voting-share numerator
  and denominator; statement preparation and audit evidence separately.
- Auditor recognition, current status, independence and applicable statutory
  exception; any appointment, resignation or removal event.
- Actual event dates and a checked statutory/declared holiday calendar for
  a final deadline. Record location events and notice registration are
  different events. Do not collect credentials or submit identity documents.

### Registration prerequisites

Apply section 6 to the complete structure, not just its director count.
A corporate director, person under 18, undischarged bankrupt in any
jurisdiction, prohibited person, person within the specified mental-health
order or person failing the constitution's qualifications cannot pass
section 75 merely by signing a consent. A disqualified person acting as
director can still incur statutory duties. [Sections 6 and 75][ESSENTIAL] [DIRECTOR]

The name must end with Limited or Ltd and pass section 11's enactment,
identical/almost-identical name, offensiveness and false-connection tests.
An available-looking name is not proof that the Registrar will accept it.
[Section 11][NAME]

The section 7 application is in the prescribed form, signed and filed with
the proposed name, constitution/change information, required director and
shareholder details, share allocation, registered office, postal address
and fee. Supply a non-default constitution where required. Retain the
prescribed signed director consent under section 76 and Form 1; the ordinary
Form 1 is held in company records and produced if the Registrar requires
it. Form 1 expressly distinguishes amalgamation submission, which is
outside this scope. Do not infer that every supporting item is routinely
uploaded. [Application][APPLICATION]; [consent][CONSENT]; [Form 1][FORM1]

Regulations 4-5 generally require filing through the approved online
registry format and recognise specified prescribed-form exceptions.
Physical delivery generally has no effect, subject to the regulation's
court-order service exception. Verify the current approved fields and
procedure before actual filing. Fees are due when the transaction occurs;
a waiver requires the Registrar's written exercise of discretion.
[Online filing][ONLINE]; [forms][FORMS]; [payment][PAYMENT]; [waiver][WAIVER]

Section 8 requires the Registrar to enter the company and issue a certificate
as soon as the Registrar receives an application that complies with section 7.
No fixed administrative turnaround has been independently verified.
The certificate establishes incorporation and
its stated date. Separate legal personality and capacity under section 10
do not replace tax, employer, foreign-investment, land or activity-specific
permissions. [Registration][REGISTER]; [certificate][CERT]; [personality][PERSON]

### Annual return and records

Use the Registrar's allocated month, not incorporation anniversary or
balance date. A changed month or subsidiary alignment requires the
statutory approval/notice; it is not inferred from a request. Section 153
requires prescribed information dated within the allocated month, the
required signature and fee. Its incorporation-calendar-year exception
does not establish an inactivity exemption. [Sections 153-154][RETURN] [MONTH]

Keep category A records including certificate, constitution, share register,
current director details and office/postal details. Category B contains the
specified shareholder records for 7 years and required statements for the
last 7 completed periods. Category C includes specified director records
and consents for 7 years and accounting records for the current plus last
7 completed periods. These are different retention measures. Written
Registrar approval of a shorter period is discretionary. [Section 144][RECORDS]

Records must be at the registered office or another place in Cook Islands,
with the section 145 location notices. Section 146 requires accessible
English form and measures preventing and detecting falsification. Online
accessibility alone does not establish that offshore/cloud storage meets
the statutory local-location duty. [Sections 145-146][LOCATION] [FORMAT]

Directors must ensure accounting records correctly record and explain
transactions, permit determination of the financial position with reasonable
accuracy and enable statements and audit. Section 159 specifies daily
receipts/payments, assets/liabilities and the relevant goods, stock and
services records. Its narrow retail cash daily-aggregate exception does
not waive other record or VAT duties. [Sections 158-159][DIRECTOR-RECORDS] [ACCOUNTING]

### Statements and audit classification

Count registered shareholders using Schedule 1. For the ordinary examples,
the register, rights and count remain fixed throughout the period. A change
during the period, nominee/joint arrangement or disputed entry requires
separate interpretation and evidence; do not choose a favourable test date.
[Definitions][DEFS]

At 10 or more shareholders, apply section 161(2)-(4). With fewer than 10,
compute the notice holders' qualifying voting shares divided by total
voting shares. Keep the exact fraction and compare with 10% before rounding.
A qualifying timely written notice engages section 161(2)-(4) for that period.
Without it, section 163(4) still requires any balance sheet prepared for
that period to state that its underlying accounting records have not been
audited. This classification does not remove recordkeeping or duties under
the constitution or another regime. [Sections 161/163][STATEMENTS] [NOTICE]

The applicable statements must give a true and fair view, comply with
applicable regulations and be dated and signed by the directors as specified.
The default balance date is 31 March; another date needs valid directors'
adoption under applicable regulations. Establish the framework and complete
statement content separately before producing accounts. [Section 161][STATEMENTS];
[definitions][DEFS]

Section 162 requires a company required to prepare statements to have them
audited by a qualified auditor. The 4-month statement-preparation window is
not, on the sources established here, an expressly verified audit-completion
deadline. Appointment and AGM timing, removal procedure, vacancy and audit
completion need their complete applicable chain. Sections 164-165A add
appointment and Registrar intervention/notice duties; this reference does
not replace them with a single calendar date. [Audit][AUDIT]; [appointment][APPOINT];
[Registrar appointment][REGISTRAR-AUDITOR]; [resignation/removal notice][AUDITOR-NOTICE]

For the ordinary section 169(1) route, verify current written Registrar
recognition and absence of disqualification. Directors, employees,
accounting-record keepers and the specified connected persons, liquidators,
receivers and related-company cases require the statutory checks. A
professional designation alone is insufficient. Sections 169(6)-(7) give
the defined Director of the Office of the Public Expenditure Review Committee
and Audit a separate statutory qualification; do not apply the ordinary
recognition-letter test to that exception without examining it. [Section 169][AUDITOR]

### Ownership information and calendar holds

Section 52 requires sufficient beneficial-ownership information, including
nominee/nominator information, through its FTR section 8 and associated
regulations chain. Registered shareholder counting is not this classification.
Regulation 6 includes direct/indirect and joint ownership, joint and several
liability, other control, legal arrangements, foundations and specified
professional-capacity branches. Its paragraphs (2)(b)/(c) reference
subclause (1)(a)/(b), although (1) has no such paragraphs, in both the
current text and original 2024 amendment. Preserve that ambiguity for legal
review rather than silently correcting the reference. Do not reduce all
cases to a 25% share test. Regulation 6A imposes additional duties on a
reporting institution; that does not automatically classify every company
as one. BO class selection and completed BO filing remain held here.
[Section 52][BO]; [FTR section 8][ULTIMATE]; [regulations 6/6A][BO-REG] [CDD];
[2024 amendment][AMEND24]

Cook Islands Act section 622 applies the Acts Interpretation Act 1924,
subject to its terms. A month is a calendar month; the general rule excludes
the triggering day. Its section 25(a) extends an expiry on a holiday to
the next non-holiday. The Companies Act working-day definition excludes
Saturday, Sunday and statutory/declared public holidays. These are distinct
tests: Saturday alone does not automatically extend a month deadline.
Resolve additional holiday declarations, unusual corresponding-date cases
and the particular provision before issuing a final calendar. Do not import
NZ's modern general time-computation law or all NZ holidays.
[Section 622][INTERPRET-BRIDGE]; [definitions][INTERPRET-DEFS]; [time][TIME];
[Companies definitions][DEFS]; [Public Holidays Act][HOLIDAY-DEFS] [HOLIDAYS] [HOLIDAY-LIST]

For the conditional April illustration below, Cook Islands section 2
incorporates the NZ-observed dates of Good Friday and Easter Monday:
3 and 6 April 2026 respectively. It does not import NZ Anzac Mondayisation,
Matariki or every other NZ holiday. Any additional applicable holiday
declaration must be checked before turning the illustration into an actual
deadline. [Holiday definitions][HOLIDAY-DEFS]; [NZ Government dates][NZ-DATES]
