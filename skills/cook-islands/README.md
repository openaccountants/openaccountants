# Cook Islands (CK): tax pack

**Built from the law. Not yet approved. Written by Ryan Duguid; imported from his fork and brought to the standard by Claude on 14 November 2026. Not reviewed by an accountant.** The Cook Islands are self-governing in free association with New Zealand and have their own tax law, administered by the Revenue Management Division of the Ministry of Finance and Economic Management (MFEM). The main statutes are the Income Tax Act 1997, the Value Added Tax Act 1997 (VAT at 15%), the Companies Act 2017 and the Cook Islands National Superannuation Act 2000 (CINSF). The New Zealand dollar is the currency in use. The income year is the calendar year for an ordinary December balance date.
