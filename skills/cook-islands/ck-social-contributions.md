## Eligibility, exclusions and registration

Section 36(1) covers local employment and employers in respect of that employment, subject to the Act and class application. Do not add a citizenship, 183-day or company-tax residence requirement. Overseas employment has distinct language and exclusions and is held here. The 2016 Order extends application to all classes of persons in local employment while preserving s 37. [S 36](https://cookislandslaws.gov.ck/api/display_pages/sect_LOCI_NSA_NSA00_S36_htm), [Order cl 4](https://cookislandslaws.gov.ck/api/display_pages/clause_LOCI_NSA_CINSFO16_CL4_htm)

Under s 37(1), compulsory contributions are excluded for an employee under 18 or aged 60 or more, and in the other specified election/existing-fund circumstances. For the historic age-55 route, establish that the employee had attained 55 when the Act/Part began to apply to the class, written notices to Board and employer and the Board-notification date. A person currently 55 does not qualify merely by that current age; liability continues until the Board is notified under s 37(4). Existing-fund membership alone is insufficient: the cessation restriction must be as determined by the Board. [S 37](https://cookislandslaws.gov.ck/api/display_pages/sect_LOCI_NSA_NSA00_S37_htm)

An excluded employee may elect to contribute. Section 37(2) makes the employer liable if the employee contribution is paid on the earnings. Do not infer the matching share from an election alone. Additional voluntary contributions under s 36(2) require Board consent and the Trust Deed minimum and are a different route; this guide supplies no current voluntary minimum, withdrawal or investment decision. [Ss 36–37](https://cookislandslaws.gov.ck/api/display_pages/sect_LOCI_NSA_NSA00_S37_htm)

New employers apply within 14 days of becoming an employer; new employees apply within 14 days of becoming an employee, using the Board-prescribed manner/form. The Board allots an employer number and certificate. The historical commencement wording in ss 28(1) and 31(1) is literally 'not less than 4 weeks'; do not silently replace it with 'within 4 weeks' or use it for an ordinary new-person deadline. Do not invent the prescribed fields or treat a website processing estimate as a liability deferral. [S 28](https://cookislandslaws.gov.ck/api/display_pages/sect_LOCI_NSA_NSA00_S28_htm), [s 31](https://cookislandslaws.gov.ck/api/display_pages/sect_LOCI_NSA_NSA00_S31_htm)

## Earnings before rates

For the selected ordinary employee base, s 40(2) includes wages, salary, allowances, holiday pay, overtime, long-service leave, maternity leave whether employer or Crown funded, bonuses, gratuities, extra salary, commissions, directors' fees, honoraria, emoluments and remuneration of any kind paid or payable in relation to employment, cash or otherwise, before income tax. This is a legal classification, not permission to value a non-cash item or allocate a Crown-funded payer without authority. [S 40](https://cookislandslaws.gov.ck/api/display_pages/sect_LOCI_NSA_NSA00_S40_htm)

The termination lump-sum exclusion in s 40(2)(a) is conditional on its statutory character and occasion. It does **not** remove accrued holiday pay, accrued long-service leave or maternity leave pay, whether funded by employer or Crown. An ordinary in-service bonus is not a qualifying termination gratuity merely because both are labelled 'bonus'. Preserve the other exclusions for past-employment pension/annuity, specified dividends and qualifying outside-Cook-Islands employment. Self-employed and shareholder-employee bases are distinct s 40(3)–(4) rules and are held here. [S 40(2)–(4)](https://cookislandslaws.gov.ck/api/display_pages/sect_LOCI_NSA_NSA00_S40_htm)

Do not reduce this base by PAYE or by the employee contribution itself. The employee and employer shares are separate obligations calculated from the established base; the employer share is not another deduction from the employee's wages. A complete payroll still requires its own country rules and output controls. [Ss 39–41](https://cookislandslaws.gov.ck/api/display_pages/sect_LOCI_NSA_NSA00_S39_htm)

## Conditional shares and timing

Section 39(2) states 3% for the first year after commencement, 4% for the next year and 5% thereafter, with the same employer rate under s 39(1)(b), subject to the s 39(3) Order variation power. These historic phases must not be restarted merely because an employer registers a new employee. The current fund pages corroborate 5% employee plus 5% employer, but are not a complete current Order chain. [S 39](https://cookislandslaws.gov.ck/api/display_pages/sect_LOCI_NSA_NSA00_S39_htm), [fund FAQ](https://cinsf.com/members/faqs/)

Once the actual base, rate, currency, limits and rounding are established, compute each share independently and total the rounded lines under the prescribed rule. This guide provides no established share rounding, currency conversion, floor, ceiling, pro-ration or multiple-employment aggregation rule. Keep raw precision separate; never infer a two-decimal or whole-dollar convention from the examples.

Section 41 requires deduction at payment of earnings. Section 44(1) addresses an employer who pays earnings. Section 48(1) requires, not later than the 20th of the following month after earnings became payable, the employer contribution and the employee contribution deducted therefrom, with a Board-prescribed monthly statement. Preserve all 3 formulations. Same-month wholly paid/payable cash cases avoid a cross-month bridge; unpaid accruals, delayed payment, part payment and arrears do not establish both remittable shares through this reference. Section 67(3) deems deduction when earnings are paid for its specified penalty provision, not from an unpaid accrual. [S 41](https://cookislandslaws.gov.ck/api/display_pages/sect_LOCI_NSA_NSA00_S41_htm), [s 44](https://cookislandslaws.gov.ck/api/display_pages/sect_LOCI_NSA_NSA00_S44_htm), [s 48](https://cookislandslaws.gov.ck/api/display_pages/sect_LOCI_NSA_NSA00_S48_htm), [s 67](https://cookislandslaws.gov.ck/api/display_pages/sect_LOCI_NSA_NSA00_S67_htm)

Retain the unadjusted statutory month/day separately from any actual calendar result. Time-computation, holidays, operative notices and facts must be established before a final date. Section 49 concerns statements/certificates and reporting relief; its captured wording does not authorise a general contribution-payment extension. Section 48(2)'s annual certificate refers to Income Tax Act tax-deduction-certificate timing and excludes amounts previously certified; that complete timing chain is held. [Ss 48–49](https://cookislandslaws.gov.ck/api/display_pages/sect_LOCI_NSA_NSA00_S49_htm)

Section 44(5) concerns employer contributions **paid** in accordance with its specified subsections and refers to Income Tax Act s 68. Do not turn an unpaid accrual into a tax deduction or infer employee deductibility here. Complete the income-tax companion and primary-law chain separately. [S 44(5)](https://cookislandslaws.gov.ck/api/display_pages/sect_LOCI_NSA_NSA00_S44_htm)
