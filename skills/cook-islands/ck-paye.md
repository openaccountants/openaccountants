# Cook Islands ordinary PAYE reference v0.1

> **General reference only.** This is a bounded Tier 2 draft for professional review. It does not authorise a payslip, tax payment, return, payroll journal or final employee liability. Guide presence, primary-source preparation and passing component checks do not establish comprehensive country acceptance.

## Scope and source gate

Load both mandatory bases in full. Preserve all 4 universal outputs and 18 checks, all 5 payroll outputs and 12 checks, every mandatory country slot and all loaded refusal catalogues. This guide is not the complete country payroll companion. Its conditional examples explain selected statutory mechanics; they do not waive source, profile-confirmation, factual, reviewer or runtime gates.

The selected reference covers ordinary domestic employee cash wages, an established Rarotonga primary-income classification, exact supported whole-unit weekly table rows, the prescribed longer-period method where the notional weekly amount is an unconflicted whole-unit row, an expressly established no-declaration status, payment/credit timing, separate-sum aggregation and selected reporting reconciliation. Salary entitlement, minimum wage, overtime entitlement, all programme contributions and actual net pay must be established separately. CINSF earnings and payment rules do not establish PAYE earnings, withholding or remittance periods.

Before any engagement calculation, establish the requested pay period, payment/deemed-payment date, tax year, actual employee and employer facts, complete operative Act/amendment/commencement/regulation chain and any applicable Collector requirement under sections 149(9), 153 or 155(3). Verify actual declarations, table amounts and regional status. Neither a 2021 table title, the 1 December 2025 consolidation label, this guide's 2026 metadata nor an unsuccessful search establishes current law. Unknown law is a source hold, not a conservative assumption.

The source conflict register below is binding. A conflicting or unsupported row, fractional selection, secondary-income method, Pa Enua calculation, changed-rate period, benefit, retirement payment, extra emolument, withholding payment, correction or special certificate is outside the numeric component. Return Needs Input or the applicable refusal. Do not repair a published number from neighbouring rows or an annual-tax formula. Do not deduct the annual supplemental deduction again from a table amount or use annual income tax divided by 52, 26 or 12 as PAYE.

## Intake and position rules

Record stable redacted employer/employee references, employment type, contracts, lawful gross components, dates and pay frequency, regular full-time/part-period facts, separate sums for the same period, actual cash/credit/application evidence, all employment and other income needed for classification, home/presence facts, actual declaration/certificate delivery and effective dates, Collector decisions, prior certificates, prior gross/withholding/remittances and their allocation, forms, relevant country slots and reviewer identity. Keep names, tax identifiers, bank details and signatures with the reviewer. No default establishes unknown law, code, residence, benefit value, entitlement, currency or rounding.

1. **Classify the payment.** Section 2 distinguishes salary/wages, extra emoluments and withholding payments. A label such as bonus or backpay is not sufficient. Sections 46, 147 and 148 govern benefit/retirement and insufficient-cash issues; keep those cases held here. Do not issue a negative bank payment.
2. **Establish residence.** Section 44(3) expressly applies to Schedule 2 cl 2: the person made their home in Rarotonga and was present at least 183 cumulative days in the relevant year, including the specific deemed-presence cases in s 44(4). Citizenship, the current work location or an unsupported prediction of days is insufficient. Section 44A and Schedule 1AA provide the distinct Pa Enua route. Permanent arrival/departure and partial-year annual deductions do not establish a prorated weekly PAYE allowance.
3. **Establish income and code separately.** Schedule 2 cl 1 includes investment and rental income in primary income and defines majority non-investment income with a strict more-than-50% test and an alternative when the largest source is 50% or less. Section 149 defines no declaration, P and S and excludes extra emoluments and withholding payments from this code provision. A P entry alone does not resolve all Schedule 2 income facts. Do not default an unknown code to P or equate uncertain records with an established no-declaration state. Equal competing payments and classification mismatches remain held.
4. **Apply declaration dates.** Under s 149(4), delivery generally does not cover a pay period commencing before delivery, with the stated first-period-of-new-employment exception. Preserve year-end expiry, the current-period protection and unchanged-code renewal within 10 days after 1 January in s 149(5). Apply cessation, replacement and notice rules in subsections (6)–(7), including their distinct 3-day and 4-day requirements. A reduced deduction is not generally portable to another employer; s 149(8) states the successor exception. Actual subsection (9) variations must be checked.
5. **Apply payment and period rules.** Section 145 deducts when payment is made; s 2 includes distribution, crediting or dealing on a person's behalf and s 146 expressly covers credit/application on the employee's account. Mere accrual does not establish a payment event. Section 145(2) treats wages for part of a regular full-time pay period as for the whole period. Section 145(4) aggregates separate sums for the same pay period. Its delayed-overtime exception requires all stated conditions; a later transfer alone does not satisfy it. Section 150 has Saturday-week aggregation and a specific sequential regular full-time employment exception. Hold multi-employment and piecework computations here.
6. **Calculate only the supported reference.** For an established ordinary Rarotonga primary weekly case, use the exact unconflicted Schedule 2A amount for its supported wage row or the express below-281 note. For a period longer than 1 week, Schedule 2 cl 3 requires uniform daily accrual of overtime and the balance, a notional weekly amount, the relevant table deduction and multiplication by total payment divided by notional weekly payment. Use this component only when that weekly amount is a supported whole-unit row and the result is exact without an invented rounding rule. Do not divide by zero. Clause 4's other-period method and changed-rate cases under s 152 remain held. An annual-tax calculation is not a substitute.
7. **Distinguish no declaration.** Once s 149 no-declaration status is expressly established, Schedule 2 cl 5 requires 30 cents for every dollar or part of a dollar of the payment, irrespective of primary or secondary income. For a positive amount in hundredths of a source-table unit, the reference arithmetic is 0.30 times the number of whole units rounded upward. This express quantum does not settle the primary-table fractional method, general currency or rounding. Clause 6's separate extra-emolument rule is outside this component.
8. **Keep payroll and reporting separate.** PAYE is one employee deduction. Establish other deductions, employer charges and lawful gross before actual cash net wages. Use the CINSF companion only within its own bounds. Employer contributions are additional costs, never employee deductions. No actual payroll output is complete whilst the country slots below remain unresolved.

### Remittance, records and year-end controls

Section 154 requires a record of source payments and deductions when paid and retention for at least 5 years after those payments, subject to the specified Collector notification. Section 217(3) supplies the records definition, including electronic records. Do not substitute CINSF's 7-year transaction basis or apply this PAYE rule to every employment record indiscriminately.

Section 155(1)(a) requires the signed authorised monthly certificate and deductions by the 20th day after the month in which source payments were made. That is distinct from CINSF's payable-month wording. MFEM identifies RM205 and directs a nil RM205 where no wages and no PAYE were paid/deducted; zero PAYE with positive gross is not zero gross. Verify actual due dates, holidays, current calendar and any lawful variation. An apparent online submission, bank debit or equal amount alone does not establish a matched liability or accepted filing.

Reconcile period gross and PAYE from payroll to RM205, actual attributed payments, RM101 certificates and RM106. Keep VAT, CINSF, other periods, credits, unallocated funds, overpayments and penalties separate. A combined payment requires an evidenced split; do not offset a VAT refund or CINSF payment against PAYE without authority. Show amounts due, matched, outstanding and separately unmatched. Explain every difference without deleting it.

Section 155(1)(b) states annual employee certificates by 31 January; paragraph (c) states within 7 days of cessation. Exclude amounts already included in a certificate previously delivered. Paragraph (e) requires reconciliation and its stated documents/explanation; preserve its unusual accompanying-document wording and the conflict register. Paragraph (f) has a distinct business-cessation rule. Do not infer a final shortfall due date from the form. Actual authorised electronic requirements under s 228A and ss 149(9)/155(3) decisions remain necessary.

Sections 156 and 165 distinguish employee missing-deduction duties and employer debt/concurrent recovery. Track the same shortfall and actual recovery once. Section 151 concerns a specified advance-payment deficiency; it is not general permission to recover every old error in current wages. Sections 157–163 do not make PAYE final for every employee or authorise an automatic employer refund. Penalties under ss 168–169 are outside this reference; do not use a mandatory flat treble charge or invent compounding mechanics.
