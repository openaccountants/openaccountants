# Montserrat property tax, landholding licence and stamp duty

> **General reference only.** This skill is general tax/accounting reference material for AI-assisted workflows. It has not been reviewed for any specific person's facts, documents, elections, deadlines, residency, filing status, or local procedures. Do not rely on it to file, pay, amend, or take a tax position without review by a qualified professional in the relevant jurisdiction.

## Scope

Annual property tax on land and buildings, and the charges on buying, leasing or transferring land in Montserrat, in 2026: the Property Tax Act, Cap. 17.16; the Landholding Control Act, Cap. 8.02; the Stamp Act, Cap. 17.02; and the Registered Land Act, Cap. 8.01 and its Rules, all in the revised edition showing the law at 1 January 2025. Amounts are EC$. Income tax on a seller's gain: [ms-income-tax-individuals](ms-income-tax-individuals.md) and [ms-corporate-income-tax](ms-corporate-income-tax.md).

## Ask the client first

1. The property's classification (residential, agricultural, commercial, institutional, industrial, hotel, cultural, historical, recreational) and the site and building values on the valuation list.
2. Who owned it on 1 January? Separate owners of land and building, co-owners, condominium unit?
3. Is the buyer or lessee a person belonging to Montserrat (Cap. 8.02 s.2), or a national covered by an exemption order (Antigua and Barbuda, St Vincent and the Grenadines)?
4. Consideration, lease term and rent, and any debts or future payments taken over.
5. Any exemption: new dwelling, new commercial building, certified agricultural land, unsafe area?

### The base

- **Property tax.** The valuation officer assigns each taxable property unit (hereditament) its class and market value, with site and building values recorded separately (ss.3 to 6 and 22). Liability falls on the owner on 1 January; separate owners of land and buildings and co-owners are dealt with in ss.7 to 11; condominium units are assessed separately. Unpaid tax on a transfer is an encumbrance recoverable from the new owner.
- **Rates.** Section 4 calls for rates by 15 December of the preceding year and carries existing rates forward where none are set. SRO 27/2016 directs SRO 22/2008 into the 2013 edition and identifies Columns II and III as the 2009 rates; the printed schedule also has columns headed "Current Rate" (residential 0.80% and 0.25%, commercial 2.00% and 0.60%). Invest Montserrat gives 0.03% and the Tourism Authority gives 0.05% for residential land and 0.025% for buildings. This skill uses Columns II and III; establish the Gazette chain before applying them to a 2026 bill.
- **Exemptions (ss.12 to 21).** Ownership- and use-based exemptions; a qualifying new dwelling has a 3-year building exemption from the year after it becomes habitable, with the valuation officer's Form F certificate; s.17 cuts the next year's tax by 50% for qualifying agricultural land certified by 15 December; unsafe-area relief depends on the area order, timing and use; tax due of EC$10 or less is deemed remitted (s.21(2)).
- **Who needs a landholding licence.** A person who does not belong to Montserrat (s.2, including the Immigration Act reference, corporate control tests and the treaty exception). Sections 3 to 5 cover ownership, leases and mortgages; the s.3(1) proviso allows specified short interests for residence, trade or business up to 5 acres in total. The Act also covers inherited land, mortgages, general mortgage licences, licensed corporations and restricted shares and debentures; s.4 covers registration, Form A and licence conditions.
- **Stamp duty.** Consideration includes specified debts and future payments (s.55). A voluntary transfer uses the property's value (s.61, with its exclusions and the Accountant General's opinion). Leases: premium and rent are separate, with a rent scale by term and rent. Stock, mortgages and settlements have their own entries.

### Steps

1. **Annual tax:** land value x land rate, plus (building value less EC$150,000 for a dwelling) x building rate; apply exemptions and any surcharge; take 5% off for payment by the due date.
2. **On a purchase by a non-belonger:** licence fee (greater of 5% and EC$2,500), conveyance stamp duty (EC$5 per EC$250 or part), registration fee (greater of EC$20 and 0.75%).
3. **Before registration:** the Comptroller's Form E property tax certificate (Cap. 17.16 s.9) and stamping (Cap. 8.01 s.109).

## Worked cases

### Case 1: annual tax on a home

Site value EC$120,000 and dwelling EC$270,000, residential, no other relief or surcharge. Land tax 600; the building is taxed on 120,000 after the EC$150,000 deduction, giving 300. Total 900, or 855 paid by the due date.

```oa-check
name: Montserrat property tax, site 120,000 and dwelling 270,000
inputs:
  site: 120000
  dwelling: 270000
steps:
  - land_tax: site * 0.005
  - building_tax: max(dwelling - 150000, 0) * 0.0025
  - total: land_tax + building_tax
  - paid_early: total * 0.95
expected:
  land_tax: 600
  building_tax: 300
  total: 900
  paid_early: 855
```

### Case 2 (decision): what does a non-belonger pay to buy at EC$260,100?

An ordinary sale with an ownership licence needed and no relief: licence 13,005, conveyance stamp 5,205 (1,041 units of EC$250, rounded up), registration 1,950.75. Total 20,160.75 (derived), about 7.75% of the price. A buyer who belongs to Montserrat, or a covered Antigua and Barbuda or St Vincent national, avoids the 13,005 licence (an exempt national pays EC$500 per parcel instead).

```oa-check
name: Montserrat purchase charges on 260,100, licence needed
inputs:
  price: 260100
steps:
  - licence: max(price * 0.05, 2500)
  - units: ceil(price / 250)
  - stamp: units * 5
  - registration: max(price * 0.0075, 20)
  - total: licence + stamp + registration
expected:
  licence: 13005
  units: 1041
  stamp: 5205
  registration: 1950.75
  total: 20160.75
```

## When to refuse or refer

- Which rate column applies to a 2026 bill until the Gazette chain is confirmed.
- Surcharge cases, unsafe-area relief and agricultural certification.
- Licence applications for corporations, mortgages, shares and debentures.
- Leases (premium and rent scale), settlements, gifts, estates and indirect transfers.
- Arrears and penalties until the Cap. 17.16 and Cap. 17.26 rules are reconciled.

## Filing and payment

1. Property tax: pay the bill by the due date to get the 5% rebate (MCRS portal).
2. Purchase by a non-belonger: apply to Lands and Survey for the licence before acquiring; pay the fee in the manner set for stamp duty (s.13(1)).
3. Pay the stamp duty and have the instrument stamped; obtain the Form E property tax certificate; register the transfer and pay the 0.75% fee.

## Completion checklist

- Class and values from the list; EC$150,000 off a dwelling; rebate if paid on time.
- Belonger status tested; licence, stamp and registration computed separately.
- Form E and stamping before registration.
