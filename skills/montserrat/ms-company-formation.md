# Montserrat: forming a company

> **General reference only.** This skill is general tax/accounting reference material for AI-assisted workflows. It has not been reviewed for any specific person's facts, documents, elections, deadlines, residency, filing status, or local procedures. Do not rely on it to file, pay, amend, or take a tax position without review by a qualified professional in the relevant jurisdiction.

## Scope and who this is for

People setting up a domestic company limited by shares in Montserrat, or registering a foreign company, in 2026, under the Companies Act 2023, No. 15 of 2023 (commenced 1 April 2024 by SRO 14/2024; revised as Cap. 11.12 at 1 January 2025), the Persons with Significant Control (Registration) Regulations (SRO 15/2024) and the Companies Regulations (SRO 16/2024), both in force 22 March 2024. Amounts are EC$. Tax registration after incorporation: [ms-corporate-income-tax](ms-corporate-income-tax.md). Business names and trade licences are separate regimes and outside this skill.

## Ask the client first

1. Which entity: company limited by shares (public or not), limited by guarantee (with or without shares), or non-profit (needs the Governor's prior written consent, s.5(5))?
2. Will it carry on activity outside Montserrat, or be a reporting company (large or public)? That decides whether the agent must be a licensed company manager.
3. Who will be the registered agent and where is the registered office?
4. Who are the proposed directors and shareholders, and who holds more than 25% of shares or votes, or other control?
5. Is it a foreign company with a place of business in Montserrat?

### Steps in order

1. **Classify the entity** (ss.3 and 4).
2. **Registered office and agent** (ss.78 to 80): a physical registered office in Montserrat and a locally eligible registered agent. Reporting companies and (subject to prescribed exemptions) companies active outside Montserrat need a licensed company manager; other companies may use an individual director normally and habitually resident in Montserrat. If the office is at the agent's office, say so in the address.
3. **Reserve the name** (s.20, 45 days; form C-NR in the original SRO 16 Schedule 3; the Commission's forms page lists Form 27 and Invest Montserrat lists Form 26: confirm with the registry).
4. **File the application** (s.5): only the proposed agent files, through CIPO. Include the approved application (C-IN1), articles complying with s.7, the agent's signed consent, entity type and public status, office and agent details, proposed directors and their consents, proposed shareholders and allocations, and the control disclosures. Pay EC$400 plus EC$400.
5. **Certificate** (s.6): the Registrar registers compliant documents and issues the certificate; its date is the incorporation date.
6. **Within 14 days:** the s.95 beneficial ownership notice (C-PSC-1), separate from the s.5(3)(i) initial disclosure in the application, applying the tests in ss.85 to 89 and SRO 15 (more than 25% of shares or votes, appointment rights, other control, indirect structures); and the member and director particulars (s.373, C-MD).
7. **Tax registration:** notify the Comptroller of the resident principal officer or authorised agent within one month of starting business (Cap. 17.26 s.46(4)); TIN within 30 days of starting a covered activity (Cap. 17.26 ss.9 and 10).
8. **Each year:** annual return and EC$200 by 1 April (s.106(3), Form 25 on the Commission's list); reporting companies prepare audited financial statements (ss.107 to 112).

### Foreign company

A place of business in Montserrat triggers Part 12 (s.216); a bank account or holding property alone does not. The company may carry on business once registered or while its application is undecided (s.217). Apply with incorporation evidence, certified constitutional documents, member and director particulars, the agent's consent (a licensed company manager) and certified English translations (ss.218 to 221, form C-IN-O). File prescribed financial statements and returns (s.230).

### What happens after

- **Documents (s.100):** articles, member and director registers and 10 years of filings at the registered office (where a director is the agent) or the agent's office; s.101 covers minutes and resolutions.
- **Records (s.105):** documents that explain transactions and show the financial position with reasonable accuracy, kept 7 years. A company with a licensed company manager may keep them elsewhere (even outside Montserrat) if the agent's office holds records showing the position at intervals of at most 3 months and a written location record.
- **Audit (ss.119 and 120):** a company other than a reporting company may dispense with an auditor with all members' consent; check the timing language in s.120(2).

## When to refuse or refer

- Continuation, transitions under s.388 and Schedule 2, and the remaining foreign-company rules.
- Reporting company classification: reg.21 cross-refers to s.2(8), which deals with preferential creditors in both the enacted and revised Acts; resolve before classifying.
- Business names (Cap. 11.11, 2019 revision) and trade licences (Cap. 15.02, 2013 revision).
- Steps taken between 22 and 31 March 2024 (Interpretation Act ss.11 and 50 anticipatory exercise; Gazette exhibition rules).
- International banking and trust companies.
