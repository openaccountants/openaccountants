## Selected territorial rules

| Question | Qualified distinction and held application |
|---|---|
| **HIMI reception** | S1 s5(1) receives ACT non-criminal law from time to time only so far as applicable and not inconsistent with an Ordinance, as if HM formed part of ACT. Section 5(2) has a separate Jervis Bay criminal-law path. Sections 6/8 preserve authority/redirection and Ordinance modification mechanisms. Do not assume no modifying Ordinance or direction exists. |
| **Act versus enactment** | S1 s2 says Act does not include enactment; s5(3)'s Act exclusion retains named Seat of Government exceptions. S2's enactment definition includes Assembly-made law however titled and the s34 conversion path. A modern ACT law titled Act is not excluded merely by that title. Full interpretation, reception and modification analysis remains required. |
| **Commonwealth reach** | S1 s7 preserves both the otherwise-provided-by-this-or-another-Act path and express extension; own-force Commonwealth application cannot be altered by Ordinance. S1 s10 retains Gazette-notice commencement unless contrary intention. These are source mechanisms, not proof of any later instrument's commencement. |
| **Income tax geography** | S3 s1-4 and 960-505(1)(f) expressly include HM in the ITAA 1997 extension/definition bridge. S4 s6(1) adopts that meaning for ITAA 1936; this entry alone is not a newly qualified stand-alone extension clause for the entire 1936 Act. Residence, source and rates remain separate. |
| **FBT and SG** | S5 contains express external-territory extension through Australia/ITAA 1997. FBT s2B retains contrary-intention limits for outside-Australia matters and binds the Crown. SG s5(1)'s Commonwealth/Department/untaxable-authority charge exception must be read with all s5(2)–(5) administration/modification rules and s5A. Neither extension nor a government label decides an actual employer's liability or duties. |
| **PAYG employee payments** | S6 s3 expressly extends through Australia. Schedule 1 s12-35 concerns listed salary, wage, commission, bonus and allowance payments to an individual as employee. Read complete 12-1 exceptions, 12-5 priority/one-withholding rule, 12-7 alienated-PSI exceptions and 12-10 noncash rule with its Division 14 cross-reference. Extension does not supply an amount, schedule, STP, registration or remittance result. |
| **GST** | S7 s195-1 excludes external Territories from indirect tax zone, retaining the specified offshore/Customs installation qualifications. Complete 9-25 distinguishes goods, removal/import, offshore low-value supplies, real property, other things and Australian-consumer connections. Subsection 6's specified installation/assembly case treats 2 supplies separately. Subsection 7 does not treat residence solely through the external-territory inclusion as sufficient Australian-consumer residence and retains registration/acquisition conditions. Neither all HM-connected supplies being outside GST nor all being GST-free follows. |
| **Medicare** | S4 Levy Act ss4–5 incorporates ITAA 1936 and ties levy to its Part VIIB. Complete 251R–251VA preserves prescribed-person and dependency conditions and separate surcharge rules. The certification branch needs the specified Health Minister certificate; lack of access to services or a Medicare card is not an automatic exemption. No levy or surcharge amount is computed. |
| **ACT payroll** | S9 ss6–11C supplies taxable-wage/service/payment/employee/employer connections. Apply these only after establishing S1/S2 reception, interpretation, inconsistency, modifications and applicable exceptions. No ACT printed due date or general tax result is converted into an HM instruction. |
| **Corporations** | HM is absent from S8 s9's six-territory Territory list, but s5(4) applies provisions outside this jurisdiction according to their tenor, subject to s5(8). Section 5(7) likewise reaches natural persons/bodies according to tenor regardless of the listed residence/formation connections; 5(9) has a regulatory extension mechanism. No blanket nonapplication or formation/foreign-company clearance follows. |

This guide supplies no tax rate, monetary threshold, levy/contribution calculation, filing/payment deadline, conversion or rounding rule. All current HM Ordinances/directions, incorporated definitions, delegated and application/saving instruments remain required for actual treatment. Specific unread dependencies include ACT s34/interpretation and named Seat of Government provisions; ITAA Division 950/offshore definitions; Medicare/health/dependency/certification rules; SG modifications and general charging bodies; TAA Division 14 and schedules; GST 9-5/9-26/Divisions 38/84 and registration; Corporations regulations and particular registration provisions; criminal, marine, fishery and environment rules.

## Fictional scope positions

These invented controls resolve the stated territorial question only. They do not produce a tax, contribution, permission or filing result.

| Case | Facts, rule and result |
|---|---|
| **1. Expedition location** | An individual works on HM but supplies no residence/source evidence. S3 includes HM geographically for its Act, not automatically as proof of residence or a taxable amount. R-HM-FACTS and R-HM-CALC hold the actual result. |
| **2. ACT title** | A reviewer excludes ACT payroll law solely because its title says Act. S1 s2/s5(3) and S2 require the enactment distinction. Reject the title shortcut; reception, connections, exemptions and modifications remain held. |
| **3. Supply connected elsewhere** | A business assumes a supply involving HM cannot have any GST connection to the indirect tax zone. Complete S7 s9-25 requires the actual goods/service, movement, property or consumer branch. R-HM-FACTS holds the decision until those facts and incorporated rules are established. |
| **4. Commonwealth employer** | A Department invokes SG s5(1) as proof that all duties disappear. Read s5(2)–(5)/s5A separately; the charge exception does not erase every employment-administration provision. Actual modifications and treatment remain held. |
| **5. No Medicare card** | A worker claims levy exemption solely because no card is held. S4's prescribed-person/dependency/certification rules do not make that fact sufficient. R-HM-CALC holds the claim without inventing a certificate or zero levy. |
| **6. Company geography** | A company assumes no registration/foreign-company issue because HM is absent from the six-territory list. S8 s5 preserves tenor-dependent outside-jurisdiction operation. R-HM-CALC fires pending the particular provision and actual connections. |
| **7. Superseded source** | A payroll review has only August TAA C2026C00393. The independently checked current target is October C2026C00468. Replace the source unit and retain currentness/application checks; neither compilation identity determines withholding amount. |

## Intake, defaults and reviewer policy

Obtain the requested period and obligation, exact island/location, taxpayer type, citizenship and residence evidence, payer/employer identity and legal status, place and dates of work, contract and income source, transaction direction and supporting documents. For each Commonwealth obligation obtain the actual charging provision and its own definition. For ACT law obtain the enactment/Act classification, reception connections, current Ordinances, directions and modifications. For GST obtain the supply category, movement, supplier, recipient and consumer/registration facts.

No default is authorised for unknown law, commencement, residence, employer status, source, exemptions or territorial reach. Keep the affected decision as Needs Input. Missing evidence is not a zero liability, an exemption or a negative registration requirement. Ask the base's structured questions for missing facts and put missing legal sources in the action list.

Reviewer policy, not a statutory cash threshold: flag every territorial, employer and commencement decision regardless of amount. An unresolved source condition stops the affected classification; an attention flag does not resolve it.
