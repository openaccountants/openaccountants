# Spain: foral investment, business and gift credits in personal income tax, 2026

This Guide holds part of [es-foral-income-tax](es-foral-income-tax.md), moved here unchanged on 6 November 2026 so that each Guide stays within the size the site can deliver. Built from the law; not yet approved by an accountant.

## Who this applies to

An ordinary resident of Bizkaia, Álava, Gipuzkoa or Navarra under the residence tests in [es-foral-income-tax](es-foral-income-tax.md#who-this-applies-to) who invests in a company, carries on a business with investment credits, or gives to a qualifying entity. Compute the bases and quotas in es-foral-income-tax first; the corporate-tax credit rules referred to below are in [es-foral-corporate-tax](es-foral-corporate-tax.md).

### Territorial business-investment credits: calculate first, then limit use

For each qualifying business investment or activity, first calculate the credit under the relevant territory's corporate-tax provision, using its rate, eligible expenditure, exclusions, timing and documentary requirements. The PIT incorporation provisions then determine where the credit can be used. An incorporated corporate credit is not a deduction from business receipts and is not a common-territory IRPF credit.

In Bizkaia, Álava and Gipuzkoa, art 88 incorporates the local corporate-law investment/activity credits with their percentages and limits. Apply the limits to IRPF gross quota attributable to the general taxable-base portion consisting of business income, rather than the whole return's salary and savings quota. Art 88.3 carries unused amounts arising from insufficient gross quota for thirty following tax periods with the same limits. The corporate art 67 ordinary limit is 35%; the separately enumerated R&D/innovation group has a 70% limit and the specified creative-production group a 50% limit, with the statutory sequential residual-quota ordering. Use the local article's actual credit list and any applicable five-period block election; these groups are not interchangeable.

Bizkaia art 88.1 overrides the listed 64 bis/65 bis/66 quinquies financing credits to a 25% PIT limit and extends that treatment to its specified AIE 66 quater case. Art 88.4 requires the funding contract and communications within its prescribed pre-return-period timetable. Álava art 88.4 instead refers to the contract/communication timetables in its corporate arts 64 bis, 65 bis and 66 quater. Gipuzkoa art 88.1 also incorporates corporate DAs 26–28; its art 88.4 is repealed. Do not transplant one province's special financing rule to the other two.

### Mixed salary and business income: Bizkaia and Álava credit pool

For the ordinary **positive general-BI case** in **Bizkaia and Álava**, the published territorial instructions give the allocation explicitly: `business_quota_pool = general_gross_quota ×business_income_in_general_BI /total_general_BI`. The denominator is **base imponible general**, not base liquidable after pension/joint reductions, and not general-plus-savings income. The numerator is the actual admitted business-income component in that general BI, aggregated over the person's relevant businesses, not gross turnover. Use the general quota after the applicable article77 minoración and statutory general-quota adjustments; savings quota and the separate listed-share3% levy are outside this pool. Establish the annual tax calculation first rather than taxing the business profit alone through a new copy of the progressive bands.

Bizkaia's [Instruction1/2026,15.9.1, page162](https://www.bizkaia.eus/instrucciones/instruccion_1_2026_75_es.pdf) expressly supplies the proportional allocation. Álava's [IRPF2024 Instruction14.7.1, page70](https://web.araba.eus/documents/1247685/1249666/Instrucci%C3%B3n%2B%2BIRPF%2B2024.pdf/8f47b0d8-fbb0-2644-fbb7-c18fe0e92938?t=1744182672921) prints that formula; read it with current Álava88.2's unchanged attribution target and the [2025 official manual, pages192–193/230–231](https://web.araba.eus/documents/d/araba/manual_renta_renta2025_es-pdf), which define general quota after minoración and the credit pool. The older instruction/manual are evidence of those unchanged methods, **not sources for2026rates or2026personal amounts**. Current territorial74/75/77/88 and the2026scales supplied here control those amounts.

A hypothetical2026 individual in either territory has admitted net salary24,000 and net business income16,000, making general BI40,000; no signed offset, prior business-loss compensation, business-asset gain, exempt-progression income or Álava rural extra minoración applies. A proved allowable general-base reduction5,000 gives liquidable general base35,000. The2026scale yields `18,080 ×23% +16,920 ×28% =8,896`; ordinary minoración1,615 leaves general gross quota7,281. The business pool is `7,281 ×16,000 /40,000 =2,912.40`, **not** `7,281 ×16,000 /35,000`. Under the ordinary annual35% group, with an already entitled2,000 credit, no earlier credit priority and no five-period-block election, the present cap is `2,912.40 ×35% =1,019.34`; use1,019.34 and retain980.66 under the actual carry rules. Other savings tax does not enlarge that pool. These inputs avoid a disputed intermediate-cent boundary; the calculation does not assert a universal filing-program rounding sequence.

This sourced example covers `total_general_BI >0` with `0 ≤business_income_in_general_BI ≤total_general_BI` and the stated ordinary adjustments. The ordinary positive-income **Gipuzkoa** branch is supplied below as an expressly labelled inference from its own statutory target and own FAQ attribution formula; an explicit ordinary88 instruction is not claimed. Attribution where signed offsets make the business numerator exceed total general BI or the denominator is nonpositive remains precisely unconfirmed here. Do not insert an unsupported zero denominator, clamp or cross-territory formula. The five-period-block option and special25/50/70% groups retain their own actual rules; this is the ordinary annual35% route. ### The text's own controls: Gipuzkoa positive-income business-credit pool and the 35% use limits

These are the controls stated in this Guide, recomputed. Net salary **24,000** and business income **16,000** give general BI **40,000**; a **5,000** pension reduction gives general BL **35,000**. Gipuzkoa art. 75: `18,080 × 23% + 16,920 × 28% = 8,896`; the art. 77 minoración **1,615** leaves **7,281**. The pool is `7,281 × 16,000 / 40,000 = 2,912.40`. A **2,000** credit is capped at 35% of the pool, **1,019.34**, carrying **980.66**.
