# Macau VAT GST and Other Indirect Tax Boundaries v0.1

> **General reference only.** These instructions support working papers. Obtain qualified local review before filing, paying or adopting a position for particular facts.


## Who this applies to

For identifying the absence of a general Macau VAT/GST/sales-tax regime and routing a transaction to its actual indirect tax. No Macau VAT registration threshold, periodic VAT return or VAT reverse-charge mechanism follows from the framework checked. This is an absence of a regime, not a zero-rated sale or permission to ignore another jurisdiction’s VAT. The direct official no-VAT statement is in the 2020 WTO review; the current official tax catalogue and amended consumption/stamp laws were checked separately, rather than relabeling that statement as a new 2026 enactment. [2020 Trade Policy Review paragraph 3.49](https://www.wto.org/english/tratop_e/tpr_e/s402_e.pdf); [current official taxonomy, income and indirect tax headings](https://www.ipim.gov.mo/en/invest-here/taxation); [article 2](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/99/50/lei04.asp?printer=1); [article 1](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/88/26/lei17.asp?printer=1).

The old narrow consumption-tax description is retained with its real scope: covered goods can be taxed on production or entry into Macau, with statutory exclusions. Free-port status does not eliminate excise, motor-vehicle/tourism/gaming taxes, licensing or stamp duty. Detailed gaming, vehicle and tourism computations are outside this skill. [article 2](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/99/50/lei04.asp?printer=1); [article 3](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/99/50/lei04.asp?printer=1); [article 4](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/99/50/lei04.asp?printer=1); [article 5](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/99/50/lei04.asp?printer=1); [free port, excise and licensing](https://www.ipim.gov.mo/en/invest-here/foreign-trade); [2020 Trade Policy Review paragraph 3.49](https://www.wto.org/english/tratop_e/tpr_e/s402_e.pdf).

## The base

Classify the transaction before calculating. The no-VAT conclusion gives no right to add a fictitious VAT percentage, claim a VAT input credit or submit a Macau VAT return. For imported/manufactured goods check the actual consumption-tax schedule, product strength/classification, importer/producer and release event, then the exclusions, duty-free/transit/temporary arrangements and other licensed controls. The spirits description is not a tax on every alcoholic drink. No excise amount or exemption is cleared merely because general customs duties are absent. [2020 Trade Policy Review paragraph 3.49](https://www.wto.org/english/tratop_e/tpr_e/s402_e.pdf); [article 2](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/99/50/lei04.asp?printer=1); [article 3](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/99/50/lei04.asp?printer=1); [article 4](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/99/50/lei04.asp?printer=1); [article 5](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/99/50/lei04.asp?printer=1); [current Groups I-IV and exclusions](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/99/50/lei04.asp?printer=1); [free port, excise and licensing](https://www.ipim.gov.mo/en/invest-here/foreign-trade).

For an ordinary direct immovable-property transfer, establish the lawful taxable value, instrument, buyer status, purpose and relief before using the progressive schedule. Compute the duty by bands and then the additional percentage of duty. Do not charge the top marginal combined rate on the whole value. Other document charges and special transactions require their own classification. The old generic 0.2%-10% stamp range is not a substitute for the General Schedule. [article 1](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/88/26/lei17.asp?printer=1); [General Schedule item 42, immovable transfers](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/88/26/lei17.asp?printer=1); [General Schedule item 15, additional duty](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/88/26/lei17.asp?printer=1).

The 2026 residential exemption requires an adult Macau resident who has never used this relief, with special co-acquirer/family allocation rules. The application and proof are required within 30 days of the relevant instrument; declaring remains obligatory despite relief. Where value exceeds the relief cap, ordinary rules apply to the remainder. Do not assume a commercial property or every co-owner qualifies, and do not infer future-year relief from this annual law. [2026 Budget Law 13/2025 article 12](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/2025/52/lei13.asp?printer=1).

For a fixed lease, use total contractual rent and the correct lessor’s liability, not one month’s rent. Check the minimum and valid arbitration reduction. Variable rent, increases, reductions, early termination and renewal have separate reporting/refund rules. Annual instalments require timely application and DSF approval; the approved annual route uses prior-year rent, July assessment and September payment notice. A revoked/expired/invalid arbitration agreement or contrary court action can trigger the statutory difference payment and reporting obligations. Do not use this simple case for a variable/concession contract. [General Schedule item 6, lease duty](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/88/26/lei17.asp?printer=1); [article 27](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/88/26/lei17.asp?printer=1); [article 27-A](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/88/26/lei17.asp?printer=1); [article 27-B](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/88/26/lei17.asp?printer=1); [article 27-C](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/88/26/lei17.asp?printer=1).


## Deadlines and filing

No Macau VAT registration or VAT return is prescribed by the framework checked. Follow the actual tax instead: consumption-tax import/production procedures, property-transfer declaration, or lease declaration/payment with the executed contract and any relief/arbitration/instalment evidence. Use the current DSF service/form applicable to that instrument and retain assessment, payment reference and receipt. Detailed live-box mapping and complete import-permit procedures are not cleared here. [2020 Trade Policy Review paragraph 3.49](https://www.wto.org/english/tratop_e/tpr_e/s402_e.pdf); [article 2](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/99/50/lei04.asp?printer=1); [article 5](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/99/50/lei04.asp?printer=1); [free port, excise and licensing](https://www.ipim.gov.mo/en/invest-here/foreign-trade); [article 27](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/88/26/lei17.asp?printer=1); [2026 Budget Law 13/2025 article 12](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/2025/52/lei13.asp?printer=1).

Lease duty is normally paid by the lessor within the execution-based period above; the annual-instalment route is conditional. Reporting for variable rent, rent changes, early termination and extensions follows the relevant statutory provisions, rather than an invented VAT return cycle. Do not treat a foreign customer’s VAT requirement as a Macau tax-registration rule. [article 27](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/88/26/lei17.asp?printer=1); [article 27-A](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/88/26/lei17.asp?printer=1); [article 27-B](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/88/26/lei17.asp?printer=1); [article 27-C](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/88/26/lei17.asp?printer=1).


## Penalties and interest

A missing VAT regime does not waive stamp, excise or licensing duties. Confirm the actual breach, taxpayer, notice and due date before computing sanctions. Relief can preserve declarations and arbitration reductions can be lost; a blanket no-tax statement is not a filing conclusion. Numeric sanctions are not cleared here. [2026 Budget Law 13/2025 article 12](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/2025/52/lei13.asp?printer=1); [article 27](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/88/26/lei17.asp?printer=1); [article 5](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/99/50/lei04.asp?printer=1); [free port, excise and licensing](https://www.ipim.gov.mo/en/invest-here/foreign-trade).


## Cross-border points

Macau is a separate customs territory/free port, while import permits and specific excises can still apply. Foreign VAT/GST, distance-sales, place-of-supply and foreign registration obligations require the other jurisdiction’s skill and facts. A Macau no-VAT conclusion does not decide foreign VAT or whether an overseas invoice must show a foreign tax. [free port, excise and licensing](https://www.ipim.gov.mo/en/invest-here/foreign-trade); [2020 Trade Policy Review paragraph 3.49](https://www.wto.org/english/tratop_e/tpr_e/s402_e.pdf).


## Two worked examples that recompute

### Progressive property duty is not a flat top rate

Assume an ordinary commercial-property transfer, agreed taxable value MOP 8,000,000, no exemption or other charge applicable to this limited computation. Duty is MOP 20,000 on the first band, MOP 40,000 on the second and MOP 120,000 on the remainder: MOP 180,000. The 5% additional duty is MOP 9,000, giving MOP 189,000, derived. This is not a residential-relief or complete conveyance-tax clearance. [General Schedule item 42, immovable transfers](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/88/26/lei17.asp?printer=1); [General Schedule item 15, additional duty](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/88/26/lei17.asp?printer=1).

```oa-check
name: Progressive property duty is not a flat top rate
inputs:
  taxable_value: 8000000
  first_band_cap: 2000000
  second_band_cap: 4000000
  additional_rate: 0.05
steps:
  - first_duty: min(taxable_value, first_band_cap) * 0.01
  - second_duty: min(max(0, taxable_value-first_band_cap), second_band_cap-first_band_cap) * 0.02
  - top_duty: max(0, taxable_value-second_band_cap) * 0.03
  - base_duty: first_duty + second_duty + top_duty
  - additional_duty: base_duty * additional_rate
  - total: base_duty + additional_duty
expected:
  first_duty: 20000
  second_duty: 40000
  top_duty: 120000
  base_duty: 180000
  additional_duty: 9000
  total: 189000
```

### Ordinary lease versus qualifying arbitration clause

Assume a fixed-rent private lease, MOP 10,000 per month for 24 months, no rent-free/variable element, other charge or relief. Total rent is MOP 240,000; ordinary duty is MOP 1,200, above the minimum. A valid written agreement satisfying article 27(3) reduces it to MOP 600; without that agreement, use MOP 1,200. Derived saving is MOP 600. Neither result establishes eligibility for annual instalments; both are below its duty boundary. [General Schedule item 6, lease duty](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/88/26/lei17.asp?printer=1); [article 27](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/88/26/lei17.asp?printer=1); [article 27-B](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/88/26/lei17.asp?printer=1).

```oa-check
name: Ordinary lease versus qualifying arbitration clause
inputs:
  monthly_rent: 10000
  months: 24
  rate: 0.005
  minimum_duty: 50
  arbitration_factor: 0.5
steps:
  - total_rent: monthly_rent * months
  - ordinary_duty: max(minimum_duty, total_rent * rate)
  - qualified_arbitration_duty: ordinary_duty * arbitration_factor
  - saving: ordinary_duty - qualified_arbitration_duty
expected:
  total_rent: 240000
  ordinary_duty: 1200
  qualified_arbitration_duty: 600
  saving: 600
```


## Ask the client first

- What goods/services, production/import event, strength/classification, permits and foreign jurisdictions?
- Which executed instrument, lawful property/lease value, rent-free/variable term and document form?
- Buyer residence/age/previous benefit, co-owners, arbitration wording and approved instalment request?
- What actual declaration, tax notice, payment receipt and foreign VAT request?
