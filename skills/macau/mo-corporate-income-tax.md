# Macau Corporate Income Tax v0.1

> **General reference only.** These instructions support working papers. Obtain qualified local review before filing, paying or adopting a position for particular facts.


## Who this applies to

Complementary/profits tax on Macau industrial or commercial income of individuals and legal persons, irrespective of residence or domicile. This skill covers the ordinary company assessment framework and the latest cleared annual profits relief; a sole proprietor may also be within complementary tax, while listed self-employed professions use professional tax. It does not clear special-sector incentives, every adjustment or an actual return. [article 2](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/78/36/lei21.asp?printer=1); [article 3](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/78/36/lei21.asp?printer=1); [article 5](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/78/08/lei02.asp?printer=1).

Collect income year, residence/actual management, legal form, capital, each prior taxable-profit assessment, ultimate-parent/MNE status, accounting election, source of each receipt, proposed distributions and assessment notices before calculating.

## The base

Start from the annual net commercial/industrial profit, not gross turnover. Group A uses accounting profit adjusted under the regulation; Group B is assessed using presumed net results or statutory indicia. Identify non-taxable items, allowable supported costs, gains, financial income and distributions separately. Business capital gains form part of the ordinary profit analysis, including the defined non-inventory asset gains; a capital label does not itself exempt them. Urban-property income and profit/dividend allocation have specific statutory treatment, so do not count the same amount twice or treat every owner distribution as already tax-free. [article 3](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/78/36/lei21.asp?printer=1); [article 19](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/78/36/lei21.asp?printer=1); [article 20](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/78/36/lei21.asp?printer=1); [article 21](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/78/36/lei21.asp?printer=1); [article 1-A definitions](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/78/36/lei21.asp?printer=1).

Classify Group A before using an accounts-based estimate: S.A./partnership limited by shares, qualifying capital/profit companies, ultimate parent entities and properly elected other taxpayers are included. Other taxpayers fall into Group B. Article 4 says the average taxable profit must be strictly above the boundary; the DSF summary says no less than it. Follow the statute, and check other independent inclusion criteria and actual classification. The IPIM FAQ’s threshold/filing shorthand is not substituted for this law. [article 4](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/78/36/lei21.asp?printer=1); [current GroupA obligations, inclusion and normal payment](https://www.dsf.gov.mo/en/tax/tax_introduction/profits_tax_a).

The current-law source exception to territorial shorthand matters: from the Tax Code amendments, a tax-resident MNE constituent entity defined in article 1-A(4) also falls within complementary tax on foreign dividends, interest, royalties and capital gains. This is not a rule taxing every foreign receipt of every company. Determine residence under the Tax Code and the actual constituent-entity definition before applying it. Detailed credits and treaty relief are not computed here. [article 1-A definitions](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/78/36/lei21.asp?printer=1); [article 2](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/78/36/lei21.asp?printer=1); [article 24](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/2024/53/lei24.asp?printer=1); [Law24/2024 main-law article29, Entry into force](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/2024/53/lei24.asp?printer=1).

For the cleared annual-relief case, compute max(0, agreed taxable income minus the income-year exemption) times the excess rate. Keep annual relief separate from permanent bands and special regimes. The 2026 Budget Law expressly gives its enhanced profits exemption to 2025 income; do not silently apply it to 2026 profits or present the permanent base table as a final 2026 return calculation before checking the applicable annual measure. [2026 Budget Law 13/2025 article 22](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/2025/52/lei13.asp?printer=1); [annexed tax table](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/78/36/lei21.asp?printer=1).


## Deadlines and filing

Submit the complementary-tax M/1 annually for the preceding year: Group B in January-March, Group A in April-July. The amended law is effective from 1 January 2026, and the current DSF Group A page agrees with July. The old 30 June end-date, still repeated by IPIM, is superseded. Apply the legal non-working-day rules and any actual extension before stating an exact final calendar date. [article 10](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/78/36/lei21.asp?printer=1); [Law24/2024 main-law article29, Entry into force](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/2024/53/lei24.asp?printer=1); [current GroupA obligations, inclusion and normal payment](https://www.dsf.gov.mo/en/tax/tax_introduction/profits_tax_a); [current GroupB obligations and normal payment](https://www.dsf.gov.mo/en/tax/tax_introduction/profits_tax_b).

Use the DSF Electronic Services or accepted DSF counter channel. Group A M/1 is signed by the taxpayer/representative and responsible accountant/auditor and accompanied by accounts and required supporting schedules, including the controlled-foreign-related-party summary when applicable. Group B submits for each establishment even with no operation. Article 18(2) normally requires purchase/sales/service books where accounts are not regularly organized, but article 18(3) exempts operators working alone or assisted by no more than 4 persons in industry or 3 in commerce from that specific books requirement. Other applicable supporting-document and tax duties remain; do not treat that exemption as complete recordlessness. Retain the return receipt and the distinct M/5 assessment and M/6 or M/7 collection notice; a declaration is not proof of payment. Current final-year form fields and full schedules are not mapped here. [article 10](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/78/36/lei21.asp?printer=1); [article 13](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/78/36/lei21.asp?printer=1); [article 18](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/78/36/lei21.asp?printer=1); [current GroupA obligations, inclusion and normal payment](https://www.dsf.gov.mo/en/tax/tax_introduction/profits_tax_a); [current GroupB obligations and normal payment](https://www.dsf.gov.mo/en/tax/tax_introduction/profits_tax_b); [article 58](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/78/36/lei21.asp?printer=1).

Use the normal payment split above only for the ordinary assessment cycle. Late/additional assessments can carry a 30-day notification-based payment period. The current DSF guidance says failure to pay the first instalment makes the second immediately due. Follow the actual valid notice; do not turn September/November into universal payment dates for all late assessments. [article 57](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/78/36/lei21.asp?printer=1); [article 58](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/78/36/lei21.asp?printer=1); [article 60](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/78/36/lei21.asp?printer=1); [current GroupA obligations, inclusion and normal payment](https://www.dsf.gov.mo/en/tax/tax_introduction/profits_tax_a).

Declare the tax domicile by the current transitional deadline using the accepted DSF channels; see [formation](mo-company-formation.md) for the date and identity/address checklist. [2026 tax-domicile declaration and channels](https://nhp.dsf.gov.mo/topic/declare_tax_domicile/en/home).


## Penalties and interest

Non-declaration, late/inaccurate declaration and late/non-payment can attract fines and interest. Resolve the actual notice, statutory breach, due date and extension before quoting a charge. Detailed penalty amounts and assessment-appeal periods are not cleared here; DSF summaries and amended statutory procedures should not be mixed into an unsupported deadline. [current GroupA obligations, inclusion and normal payment](https://www.dsf.gov.mo/en/tax/tax_introduction/profits_tax_a); [current GroupB obligations and normal payment](https://www.dsf.gov.mo/en/tax/tax_introduction/profits_tax_b).


## Cross-border points

Separate recipient tax liability, a payer withholding mechanism and treaty relief. The old blanket statements of no dividend, interest or royalty withholding are not treated as a complete tax exemption or payment clearance. Foreign-source MNE passive income is a separate inclusion rule, and special regimes/distributions need their own analysis. Do not invent a gross withholding rate from the ordinary company rate. [article 2](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/78/36/lei21.asp?printer=1); [article 3](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/78/36/lei21.asp?printer=1); [article 20](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/78/36/lei21.asp?printer=1).


## Two worked examples that recompute

### Cleared filing-cycle profit and two instalments

Assume ordinary agreed 2025 taxable profit of MOP 1,000,000, no special regime, credit or further adjustment, assessed in the normal 2026 cycle. The cleared exemption leaves MOP 400,000, giving MOP 48,000 tax and two MOP 24,000 instalments. This example does not clear 2026-profit relief. All results are derived. [2026 Budget Law 13/2025 article 22](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/2025/52/lei13.asp?printer=1); [article 57](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/78/36/lei21.asp?printer=1).

```oa-check
name: Cleared filing-cycle profit and two instalments
inputs:
  taxable_profit: 1000000
  exemption: 600000
  rate: 0.12
steps:
  - chargeable_excess: max(0, taxable_profit - exemption)
  - tax: chargeable_excess * rate
  - september: tax / 2
  - november: tax / 2
expected:
  chargeable_excess: 400000
  tax: 48000
  september: 24000
  november: 24000
```

### Small assessment uses one payment

Assume ordinary agreed 2025 taxable profit of MOP 620,000 with the same conditions. Tax is MOP 2,400; because it does not exceed the normal small-assessment boundary, it is payable once in September, rather than split. The notice still controls the actual normal-cycle payment. All results are derived. [2026 Budget Law 13/2025 article 22](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/2025/52/lei13.asp?printer=1); [article 57](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/78/36/lei21.asp?printer=1); [current GroupA obligations, inclusion and normal payment](https://www.dsf.gov.mo/en/tax/tax_introduction/profits_tax_a).

```oa-check
name: Small assessment uses one payment
inputs:
  taxable_profit: 620000
  exemption: 600000
  rate: 0.12
  single_payment_limit: 3000
steps:
  - chargeable_excess: max(0, taxable_profit - exemption)
  - tax: chargeable_excess * rate
  - single_payment_headroom: single_payment_limit - tax
  - september: tax
  - november: tax - september
expected:
  chargeable_excess: 20000
  tax: 2400
  single_payment_headroom: 600
  september: 2400
  november: 0
```


## Ask the client first

- Which income year and final annual measure, actual residence/source, ultimate-parent/MNE status and special regime?
- Which capital and prior assessed taxable profits; what Group A election/classification?
- Which accounting adjustments, gains, distributions and foreign taxes are proved?
- Which filed M/1, supporting schedules, assessment, collection notice and accepted extensions?
