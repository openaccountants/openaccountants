# Turks and Caicos Islands: tax pack

The Turks and Caicos Islands have no income tax, corporate tax or VAT. The Revenue Department collects five sales-type taxes (hotel and tourism 12%, communications 12%, domestic financial services 12%, insurance premiums 2.5%, vehicle hire 12%) and business licence fees. Employers and employees pay National Insurance (NIB) and National Health Insurance (NHIP) contributions. Amounts are US dollars.
