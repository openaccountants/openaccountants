# Åland: tax-law scope for 2026

Establish the applicable national and Åland rules before classifying a receipt,
expense, employment relationship or property transaction. This guide supports
that scope decision for a credentialed reviewer. It supplies no tax amount,
rate table, filing deadline or permission to file, pay, register or acquire land.
Every calculation needs its applicable domain base and a verified topical
companion. The existing Finland pack alone does not establish Åland's exceptions.

## Legal and territorial boundaries

The [Self-Government Act 1144/1991](https://www.finlex.fi/sv/lagstiftning/1991/1144)
section 1 establishes Åland's autonomy. Section 18(5) assigns specified regional
taxes and municipal taxes to regional legislative competence; section 27(36)
reserves other taxes and charges to the state. Verify the particular tax and
period. Autonomy is not evidence of a general exemption from Finnish taxation.

For municipal income tax, [Åland Act 2011:119](https://www.regeringen.ax/alandsk-lagstiftning/alex/2011119)
section 1 applies national income-tax legislation subject to regional deviations.
[Vero's Åland guidance, sections 1–2](https://www.vero.fi/sv/Detaljerade_skatteanvisningar/anvisningar/48911/beskattningen-pa-aland8/)
explains that state and municipal taxable-income calculations remain separate.
An accepted state-tax deduction does not establish the municipal deduction.
Home municipality, residence, income source and the tax year need their own
evidence; an Åland postal address alone does not settle them.

The [European Commission's territorial table](https://taxation-customs.ec.europa.eu/taxation/vat/vat-directive/how-does-vat-work/territorial-scope_en)
includes Åland in EU territory and EU customs rules, while excluding it from
EU VAT and excise rules. For Finnish tax, [Act 1266/1996](https://www.finlex.fi/sv/lagstiftning/1996/1266)
section 1 applies VAT and excise legislation subject to that Act's exceptions.
EU fiscal-territory exclusion does not establish a zero rate or an exemption.
Goods movements require their applicable tax-border import/export rules.

Services require a separate analysis. [Vero's tax-border guidance, sections 4,
4.4 and 4.9](https://www.vero.fi/syventavat-vero-ohjeet/ohje-hakusivu/48654/ahvenanmaan-veroraja--arvonlisaverotuksessa3/)
applies national service place-of-supply rules subject to the separate Act.
Section 7 of Act 1266/1996 modifies the treatment of the intangible services
identified in national VAT Act section 69h(2), including a specified other-EU
supplier to Åland non-business-customer branch. Section 4.4 of the guidance also
sets separate FI VAT-number and EU-services reporting rules, with mainland
fixed-establishment exceptions. Establish the actual supplying and receiving
establishments, customer status and service category before selecting a tax
treatment or return field. This scope guide supplies no return-box mapping.

Company law and accounting fall within state competence under section 27(8)
of the Self-Government Act; the trade-register allocation appears in section
29(1)(2). Regional business rights require a separate check under sections 11
and 18(22). Property acquisition also requires a separate check under section
10. Incorporation or a national business identifier does not establish those
regional permissions. Current regional implementing legislation and the
particular entitlement are required before either conclusion.

## Intake, defaults and reviewer policy

Obtain the requested period, taxpayer/entity type, residence and home-municipality
evidence, income/work/property locations, transaction documents, relevant
establishments, customer business status and the tax or permission being assessed.
For a service, obtain its contractual description and the actual supplying and
receiving establishments. For a regional business/property permission, obtain
the relevant status or decision and current implementing law.

No factual default is authorised for unknown residence, municipality, source,
establishment, customer status, service category, tax period or legal permission.
Record Needs Input and hold the dependent classification. Do not estimate a
cash difference where the legal base or applicable calculation is unknown.
Ask the base's structured factual questions only when the answer is not already
in the documents; source and permission evidence belongs in the action list.

Reviewer policy, not a statutory threshold: flag every territorial, competence,
commencement and permission decision, regardless of amount. All unresolved source
conditions require Needs Input; a reviewer flag cannot cure unknown law.

## Hypothetical scope positions

These examples resolve only the stated scope question. They are fabricated
reference cases, with no tax computation, filing-ready result or workbook claim.

1. **Separate tax bases.** A reviewer has established a 2026 Åland home municipality
   and a national state-tax deduction. The municipal deduction has no source.
   Apply Municipal Act section 1 and Vero section 2.2: keep separate bases. Hold
   the municipal deduction under R-AX-SCOPE pending its topical rules. No amount
   is transferred from the state computation.
2. **Specified intangible service.** An other-EU supplier has no Finnish
   establishment; a documented section 69h(2) service is supplied to a private
   customer resident in Åland. Section 7's special branch must be checked, so
   generic supplier-country B2C treatment is insufficient. Record the branch
   and source, then R-AX-SCOPE for registration, rate and return treatment.
3. **Unclear establishment.** A business operates in Åland and mainland Finland
   and purchases a service from another EU country. The receiving establishment
   is undocumented. Vero section 4.4 distinguishes that fact. R-AX-FACTS fires;
   hold VAT-number/reporting classification without assuming the Åland or
   mainland establishment.
4. **Future municipal change.** A 2026 pension-deduction review is presented with
   amendment 2026:70. Its commencement clause begins in 2027. Keep that amendment
   out of the 2026 position and obtain the applicable 2026 rules under
   R-AX-SCOPE. The date decision does not determine deduction eligibility.
5. **Business identifier and land.** A company presents a national business
   identifier as its only evidence for trading in Åland and acquiring land.
   Self-Government Act sections 10–11 require separate regional checks.
   R-AX-PERMISSION fires for the proposed permissions; no entitlement is inferred.
6. **Conflicting healthcare annotation.** A 2026 healthcare review sees the
   Swedish heading's 2027 annotation. The two official amendment PDFs and the
   commencement entry establish 1 July 2024 for amendment 316/2024. Record both
   the conflict and publication evidence. R-AX-SCOPE still holds any exemption
   until the actual statutory care/provider conditions are verified.

## Outputs and self-checks

For an engagement that clears the scope checks, retain all four universal
outputs: Excel working paper, reviewer brief, action list and review checklist.
The applicable domain adds its own required outputs. Record each source item
once in Transactions using the base's A–L fields and cite its scope decision.
Use separate linked scope records where an item needs more than one topic;
do not duplicate its amount in Transactions. Keep Needs Input distinct from
an exclusion or a zero tax result. Retain formula summaries and successful
recalculation as the base requires; this publication has not executed them.

Add to the reviewer brief: tax period and requested obligation; relevant
national/regional competence; residence and municipality evidence; supplying
and receiving establishments where relevant; operative sources and commencement;
each unresolved topical companion or source condition; and the full refusal
trace. No liability or filing-ready figure is produced by this scope guide.
The action list names the missing evidence, its owner and any verified deadline.
When a refusal occurs before computation, deliver its exact message and scope
trace under the base's refusal path.
