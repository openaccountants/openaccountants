# Central African Republic Company Formation & Entity Choice

## Entity types and incorporation

Companies in the Central African Republic are formed under OHADA company law and registered with the Registre du Commerce et du Crédit Mobilier (RCCM). The SARL and SA are the most common forms. Capital figures and process details below are a source-cited draft to confirm against the OHADA Uniform Act and local practice.

- **Common entity — limited liability company** — Société à Responsabilité Limitée (SARL)  _(OHADA Uniform Act on Commercial Companies and Economic Interest Groups)_
- **Common entity — public limited company** — Société Anonyme (SA)  _(OHADA Uniform Act on Commercial Companies and Economic Interest Groups)_
