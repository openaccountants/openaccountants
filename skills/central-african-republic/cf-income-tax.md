**Source status:** For resident employees and non-salaried individuals under the stated CGI baseline. Tax year 2026 is the research year; the source is not represented as a complete operative 2026 consolidation. Independent pack review is pending. ## Who this applies to

Individual fiscal residence is established by a dwelling available as owner, usufructuary or tenant; otherwise principal stay; or the centre of vital economic/professional interests. No automatic day-count test is established here. Treaty allocation and the additional persons in article 4 must be considered. [CGI arts. 3–4](https://www.finances.gouv.cf/sites/default/files/2023-05/CGI%20RCA%202017%20mise%20%C3%A0%20jour%202023.pdf) (ledger I01).

## The base

The resident net global income baseline combines category net income without distinguishing domestic from foreign source, subject to treaty provisions. Do not extend this personal rule to companies. [CGI art. 66](https://www.finances.gouv.cf/sites/default/files/2023-05/CGI%20RCA%202017%20mise%20%C3%A0%20jour%202023.pdf) (ledger I02).

Salary base includes cash and benefits. Article 38 allows a 30% professional-expense deduction from gross pay and benefits; article 39 also deducts family allowances and qualifying pension/social contributions. Establish the actual gross, exempt items and deductions before applying the net-income bands. [CGI arts. 36, 38–39, 86](https://www.finances.gouv.cf/sites/default/files/2023-05/CGI%20RCA%202017%20mise%20%C3%A0%20jour%202023.pdf) (ledger I05).

Article 64 removes income subject to final levies before combining categories. A category loss carries against that category through the third following year; losses from pleasure/vacation properties are excluded. No cross-category loss netting is inferred. [CGI arts. 64–65](https://www.finances.gouv.cf/sites/default/files/2023-05/CGI%20RCA%202017%20mise%20%C3%A0%20jour%202023.pdf) (ledger I08).

For a resident individual receiving distributed investment income, article 84 applies 15% to the gross distribution. Source withholding and its remittance are addressed in the cross-border Guide. This does not verify an undifferentiated royalty rate. [CGI art. 84](https://www.finances.gouv.cf/sites/default/files/2023-05/CGI%20RCA%202017%20mise%20%C3%A0%20jour%202023.pdf) (ledger I10).

Personal capital gains are a separate category under articles 55–57, including movable/immovable rights and real-estate-rich shares. The general base is disposal/market value less acquisition cost; qualifying undeducted capital improvements increase real-estate cost. Enumerated exemptions include the principal residence. This reading does not establish the old separate resident/non-resident CGT rates. [CGI arts. 55–57](https://www.finances.gouv.cf/sites/default/files/2023-05/CGI%20RCA%202017%20mise%20%C3%A0%20jour%202023.pdf) (ledger I11).

Classify the person and each income category. For salary, identify the net annual taxable amount under the cited base rules, then apply each band only to its slice. For business income, compute category profit and compare the applicable profit tax against the turnover minimum. Segregate final-levy income and retain category loss schedules. Apply the personal arrival/departure rules through [cross-border](cf-cross-border.md).

## Deadlines and filing

Annual detailed signed return goes to the competent residence/designated tax office by 30 April following the income year; obtain an acknowledgement. Foreign movable assets require the article 88 detailed nature/value/income statement by the same date. [CGI arts. 87–88](https://www.finances.gouv.cf/sites/default/files/2023-05/CGI%20RCA%202017%20mise%20%C3%A0%20jour%202023.pdf) (ledger I09).

Employee withholding and the small-employer calendar are in the payroll Guide. Non-salaried taxpayers follow the corporate instalment/payment mechanism by article 115. Excess paid instalments are carried to following instalments under article 119; this is not an automatic cash-refund promise. [CGI arts. 115, 119](https://www.finances.gouv.cf/sites/default/files/2023-05/CGI%20RCA%202017%20mise%20%C3%A0%20jour%202023.pdf) (ledger I12).

Use the detailed income statement required by the cited provisions, classified by category, and retain the office acknowledgement. Current forms, boxes, electronic portal, rounding reconciliation and payment reference were not opened and remain a stop for execution.

## Two worked examples that recompute

All amounts below are XAF. Inputs are fictional. Code-baseline cases are conditional on confirming the cited provisions remain applicable to the actual 2026 case. Arithmetic checks do not verify current law.

### 1. Annual salary bands on a supplied net base

Assume the source-currentness hold is cleared and annual net taxable salary is XAF 2,000,000 after verified deductions. The first slice is exempt; 1,302,000 at 8% gives 104,160 and the next 320,000 at 15% gives 48,000. Total: XAF 152,160. This starts from net taxable salary and does not guess social contributions.

```oa-check
name: Annual salary bands on a supplied net base
inputs:
  net: 2000000
steps:
  - band8: (min(net,1680000)-378000)*0.08
  - band15: max(0,min(net,3360000)-1680000)*0.15
  - tax: band8+band15
expected:
  tax: 152160
```

### 2. Business profit tax or minimum

For a non-agricultural individual business, assume XAF 120,000,000 covered turnover, XAF 4,000,000 taxable profit and no exemption. Profit tax is 1,200,000, turnover tax is 2,220,000 and exceeds both profit tax and the 1,850,000 floor. The minimum controls. This is a comparison of liabilities, not a choice of regime.

```oa-check
name: Business profit tax or minimum
inputs:
  turnover: 120000000
  profit: 4000000
steps:
  - profit_tax: profit*0.30
  - turnover_min: turnover*0.0185
  - liability: max(profit_tax,turnover_min,1850000)
expected:
  liability: 2220000
```

## Ask the client first

- Residence facts and any treaty entitlement?
- Employee, business, investment or capital-gain income, and in which period?
- Gross pay, benefits, exempt items and actual deductions?
- Turnover split by activity, prior losses and instalment credits?
- Foreign property, income and departure date?
