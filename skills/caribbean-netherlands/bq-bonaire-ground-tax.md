# Bonaire local ground tax in 2026

Establish whether the property falls within Bonaire's local *grondbelasting*
before calculating an assessment component. A national *vastgoedbelasting*
exemption does not establish that local ground tax is absent. The local levy
has its own valuation period, threshold, rounding and payment provisions.
[L1; F1, articles 43–52]

Load `workflow-base` and preserve its profile confirmation, source conditions,
refusals, four outputs, recalculation and professional review. This Tier 2
reference has no accountant sign-off. Its fictional component examples do
not resolve the operational completion conditions for an actual property.

## Scope and completion conditions

This guide covers selected Bonaire local charging, valuation, rate, co-holder
and collection provisions. It does not establish an operative local ground
tax for Saba or Sint Eustatius, or cover national property tax, transfer tax,
ground lease payments or every other property levy.

The official registry displays the ordinance as current from 1 January 2011.
Its metadata explains the linked FinBES commencement and shows one published
version. The text heading identifies 29 November 2010 no. 6; the publication
metadata identifies A.B. 2010 no. 4. The authentic original and an exhaustive
amendment/continuation chain were not acquired. Preserve that provenance gap
before treating this reference as a complete operative-law opinion. [L1–L2]

## Charge, object and valuation

Article 1 charges the person enjoying the qualifying property through
ownership, possession or limited right at the calendar year's start.
Cadastral registration is presumptive and can be displaced by the actual
right. Establish either the article 4 own-main-home branch or the enterprise
asset branch referring to article 6 income in the *Wet inkomstenbelasting
BES*. Test the actual local wording and classification; company ownership,
commercial use or receipts alone do not establish the enterprise branch.
[L1, article 1; F1, article 43; I1, articles 4 and 6]

Article 4's main home must be available to the taxpayer as a main residence
other than temporarily, with ownership of the dwelling, its land held in
*erfpacht* or *huur*, or usufruct acquired under inheritance law. The land
branch does not establish a general exemption or charging rule for every
rented dwelling. Resolve the right, residence, enterprise-asset attribution
and any mixed use for the actual object. [I1, article 4; L1, article 1]

The 2020 own-home policy separately describes construction-related own-home
treatment and conditional personal deductions for an empty or unfinished
future main home. It predates the 2025 main-residence amendment to article 4.
Those income-tax approvals do not by themselves establish the local own-home
charging branch, which expressly requires main-residence availability. For a
case relying on that policy, resolve its post-amendment interaction before
selecting an assessment or nil levy. Do not import the policy's deduction
window as a local valuation or payment period. [L1, article 1; I1, article 4;
O1–O2, sections 2.1 and 3.1]

An object can be built property, unbuilt property, an independently usable
part, or a qualifying combination used by the same taxpayer and belonging
together in the circumstances. Do not treat a parcel identifier as a complete
object classification. [L1, article 2; F1, article 45]

The valuation premise is full, unencumbered ownership transferable to an
acquirer who can immediately use the property fully in its existing state.
Use replacement value if higher, with the stated nature, use, technical and
functional obsolescence factors. Accounting value or purchase price alone
does not establish that statutory value. [L1, article 3; F1, article 46]

The local reference date lies **one year before** the five-year period starts.
This differs from the national property tax's reference date at its period's
start. This guide has not established the actual local period/decision for
2026. Specified building, improvement, demolition or destruction changes use
the property's state at the start of the following calendar year. Do not
import national investment relief into this local calculation. [L1,
article 3(3)–(7); F1, articles 47–48]

Article 4 excludes the stated minerals and naturally occurring fertilisers;
qualifying worship property; qualifying nature reserves; public roads and
waterways; qualifying public water-management and utility works; and
qualifying removable machinery. The worship and specified works categories
exclude their residential parts from those exclusions. Verify every use,
holder and removal condition. A national exemption or a general charity
description does not replace this local list. [L1, article 4; F1, article 49]

## Rate, threshold and assessment rounding

| Rule | Figure or method | Authority |
|---|---|---|
| Main-home branch | 0.345% of the established local base | L1, article 5(1)(a) |
| Enterprise-property branch | 0.345% of the established local base | L1, article 5(1)(b) |
| Base threshold | No tax when the base is **below** USD 10,000 | L1, article 5(2) |
| Assessment rounding | Round down to whole United States dollars per assessment | L1, article 5(3) |
| Collection concession | The text says amounts 'tot 10 USD'; aggregate amounts combined on one bill for this rule | L1, article 5(4); exact USD 10 boundary remains unverified |

The USD 10,000 threshold is not a deduction from every taxable base. FinBES
permits a threshold up to USD 12,000, but Bonaire's enacted threshold is USD
10,000. Use the local ordinance's figure. Apply the rate after the base and
classification are established, then round the assessment down once. [L1,
article 5; F1, articles 50–51]

Keep no levy, assessment rounding and collection relief separate. Do not
apply national property tax's USD 70,000 independent-dwelling branch here.
The old fifteen-opcenten ground-tax instrument is not established as an
additional charge on this post-2010 levy; no extra 15% is included. A current
portal label alone cannot settle the legacy transition. [L1; F1,
articles 101a–101e; L5]

The government's product guidance gives USD 862.50 for a USD 250,000 base.
That simplified example retains a fractional dollar despite article 5(3).
Preserve the statutory downward assessment rounding and record the guidance
conflict. The example does not establish the actual taxpayer's classification
or value. [L1, article 5(3); G1, cost panel]

## Co-holders, collection and procedure

FinBES article 83 allows an assessment to name one of multiple liable persons.
For the specified property-right case, recovery can reach the whole property;
the payer has proportionate recourse against other liable persons, subject
to an agreement departing from that recourse rule. An unnamed liable person
retains the stated objection and appeal rights. An internal ownership share
does not by itself establish separate assessments or separate rounding.
[F1, article 83]

Apply Bonaire's selection policy, including its categories, preference order,
exceptions and discretion. It first distinguishes limited-right holders,
owners and other enjoyment, with further rules within categories. Prior
payment history and a suitable person's request can affect the selection;
the policy also permits justified departures. Do not replace the complete
policy with a blanket oldest-owner or largest-share rule. The local official
makes the assessment decision. [P1, article I(1)–(9)]

The levy is by assessment. Four equal instalments fall on the last day of
each calendar quarter, with no instalment collectible within one month after
the bill date. The General Term Limits Act does not apply to these periods.
Resolve late-issued bills, the actual instalment dates and allocation before
preparing a payment schedule. Do not import national property tax's ordinary
two-month collection term. [L1, articles 6 and 8]

For whole or partial destruction of buildings by unforeseen disasters,
article 9 provides a requested proportional reduction or refund reflecting
the remaining tax year and loss in value. Establish the event, value change
and accepted request; the provision does not supply a general monthly
ownership proration. [L1, article 9; F1, article 52]

The local procedure uses the applicable Belastingwet BES chapters through
FinBES, with the local executive and designated levy/collection officials in
the national officials' places. Establish the actual notice, decision under
challenge, competent official, calendar and local delegated rules. This guide
does not establish complete objection, deferral, remission or enforcement
procedures. The implementing decision's listed forms concern other specified
levies; no ground-tax return form or automatic no-return procedure is inferred.
[L1, articles 7 and 10; F1, articles 67 and 84; L4]

## Hypothetical component positions

These are fictional arithmetic positions. The statutory object, taxable
classification, full local base and absence of other exclusions or reductions
are stipulated as established. They do not determine an actual assessment,
payment schedule or final amount after unresolved procedural conditions.

1. **Fractional result.** A qualifying local base of USD 250,000 produces
   250,000 × 0.345% = 862.50. Article 5(3) gives an assessment component of
   USD 862 after downward whole-dollar rounding. Do not select USD 862.50
   from the simplified guidance example. [L1; G1]
2. **Threshold boundary.** At an established base of USD 9,999 there is no
   tax under article 5(2). Exactly USD 10,000 is outside the 'below' branch:
   10,000 × 0.345% = 34.50, rounded down to USD 34. Do not deduct USD
   10,000 from the base or substitute FinBES's enabling ceiling. [L1; F1]
3. **Rounding boundary.** A base of USD 199,999 gives 689.99655 and an
   assessment component of USD 689. A base of USD 200,000 gives exactly
   USD 690; USD 200,001 gives 690.00345, rounded down to USD 690. Preserve
   precision until the single per-assessment rounding step. [L1]
4. **Stipulated local period.** If the local authority actually establishes
   a five-year period starting on 1 January 2026, the statutory reference
   date is 1 January 2025. That conditional illustration does not establish
   Bonaire's actual 2026 period or import the national 2026 reference date.
   [L1, article 3(4)–(6); F1, article 47]
5. **Unresolved enterprise status.** A company owns a building and receives
   payments for its use. Those facts alone do not establish the local
   enterprise-asset/Article 6 branch, the national exemption, or their
   interaction. Record Needs Input and obtain the classification authority
   before selecting this rate or producing engagement outputs. [L1,
   article 1; F1, article 43; I1, article 6]
