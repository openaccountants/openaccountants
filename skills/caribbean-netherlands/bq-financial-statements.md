# Bonaire, Sint Eustatius and Saba: company reporting in 2026

Determine the legal form, articles, financial year and applicable reporting
framework before preparing accounts. Ordinary NV/BV accounts, the large-NV
regime and a company's articles-based opt-in have different rules. This Tier 2
reference has no accountant sign-off and does not yet support a complete
operational financial-statements engagement.

Load `workflow-base` and `financial-statements-workflow-base`. Preserve the
universal profile confirmation, source conditions, refusals, four outputs and
recalculation requirements, together with the domain's six outputs and fourteen
checks. The full-IFRS statement extensions and depreciation reconciliation
below are necessary when those substantive rules apply. A source hold prevents
delivery of a partial set labelled complete or IFRS compliant.

Combine regime/framework confirmation with the universal profile round trip.
The domain's later adjustment checkpoint confirms supporting facts and estimate
inputs; it cannot waive a required entry or an unresolved source condition.

## Scope, period and completion conditions

This guide covers selected Book 2 accounts, examination, approval, inspection
and retention duties for NVs and BVs, with routing notes for other legal
persons. It does not establish all recognition, measurement, disclosure or tax
rules for a particular framework or engagement. Financial institutions,
insurers, pension entities, listed entities and other regulated or subsidised
bodies require their sector rules. Group relief is conditional; this guide
does not calculate consolidated accounts.

The [1 January 2026 snapshot][J1] and [1 July 2026 consolidation][J2] have the
same selected reporting provisions. The July change deletes article 105(6)
and (7), concerning bearer shares, under [Staatsblad 2019/107][H1] and its
[commencement order][H2]. It does not create a new accounts or audit threshold.
The [article 119 information page][M1] lists no delegated threshold regulation;
the displayed statutory amounts below remain the verified reference. This
bounded check does not certify every historical or unindexed instrument.

For an IASB-framework engagement, verify the operative standards, amendments,
elections and reporting-period dates. The [IFRS Foundation's IFRS 18 overview][A2]
sets mandatory application for periods beginning on or after 1 January 2027,
with early application permitted. Do not impose its new presentation model on
an ordinary 2026 period without an established early-adoption basis.

Public overviews, educational material and identified standard quotations
support only the selected framework points below. Direct full-standard links

operative 2026 editions were not accepted as read. Resolve those source

## Statutory 2026 regimes, amounts and periods

The statute's NV test is cumulative. All of the following must hold under
article 119(2); a two-out-of-three test is incorrect. [J2]

| Large-NV condition | Statutory test |
|---|---|
| Workforce | At some time within one month before or after the balance date, at least 20 workers together making at least 20 labour days, working in BES, Curaçao or Sint Maarten through the company, a group company, an employment agency or similar institution |
| Assets | More than USD 2.8 million, or its equivalent in other currency, on the balance sheet prepared under article 120(3) |
| Annual net turnover | More than USD 5.6 million, or its equivalent in other currency, calculated under article 120(3) |

Equality with either monetary boundary fails that condition. Do not substitute
European Netherlands, EU micro/small/medium or two-year tests for the displayed
BES rule. A smaller NV may opt into articles 120–122, optionally with 123–124,
through its articles. Article 219 makes the BV route an articles-based opt-in;
the NV size test alone does not move a BV into this regime. [J2, articles
119 and 219]

| Matter | Ordinary NV/BV | Large NV or relevant opt-in | Authority |
|---|---|---|---|
| Accounts preparation | Within eight months after year end | Within six months after year end, with annual report | J2, articles 116, 120, 216 |
| Extension | General meeting may grant at most six extra months for special circumstances | Same maximum and condition | Same articles; not an automatic extension |
| Basis | Socially accepted norms giving the prescribed insight | IASB standards; justified, identified alternative internationally accepted standards permitted | J2, articles 116(4), 120(3), 216(4) |
| Signatures | All directors and serving supervisors; explain a missing signature | Same | J2, articles 116(2), 120(4), 216(2) |
| Approval | Submit accounts to general meeting | General meeting must first be able to consider attached expert statement | J2, articles 116(3), 121(7), 216(3) |
| External expert | Articles 117/217 permit appointment; no blanket sector exemption follows | Qualified external examination required, subject to article 124 conditions | J2, articles 117, 121, 124, 217 |
| Ordinary inspection right | Every shareholder and bearer-debenture holder may inspect retained documents for two years after preparation or approval, respectively | Establish the applicable article 120 and 122 duties separately | J2, articles 116(5)–(6), 216(5)–(6) |
| Public availability | Do not infer article 122 from the ordinary regime | Within eight days of approval; copy available for two years; unapproved-accounts branch below | J2, article 122 |
| Accounting retention | Ten years | Ten years, including annual report and associated documents | J2, articles 15(3), 116(5), 120(7), 216(5) |

Minimum ordinary NV/BV accounts comprise a balance sheet, profit-and-loss
account and notes. They must give the prescribed insight into assets, result,
solvency and liquidity, to the extent appropriate to accounts. The generic
workflow cannot itself establish a different mandatory local framework.
[J2, articles 116 and 216]

For ordinary NVs and BVs, preserve the inspection right of every shareholder
and every bearer-debenture holder over the documents retained under articles
116(5) and 216(5). The two-year period runs from preparation for the prepared
accounts and from approval for the approved accounts, respectively. Record
both events and the associated documents. This access right does not depend
on an article 219 opt-in or impose article 122's eight-day public-availability
duty on an ordinary BV. The ten-year retention duty is separate from the
two-year inspection periods. [J2, articles 15(3), 116(5)–(6) and 216(5)–(6)]

Other legal persons require their own provisions. Article 15 gives the general
records and eight-month accounts duty. Article 89 covers associations, including
annual report and a committee of at least two non-board members where its
specified supervisor/expert conditions apply. Article 94 covers cooperative
and mutual accounts, with its own articles-based opt-in. Do not apply NV/BV
approval or audit rules to these forms without completing their analysis. [J2]

## Preparation, examination and publication rules

### Establish the regime and framework first

1. Read the actual deed/articles, year-end and any amendments. The calendar
   year is the default only where the articles designate no other financial
   year. Establish individual versus group accounts and any sector rules.
   [J2, article 15(5)]
2. For an NV, document each article 119 condition using the statute's geography,
   timing, workers and financial measurements. For a BV or smaller NV, inspect
   the relevant opt-in. Do not presume an opt-in from management's preferred
   accounting label. [J2, articles 119 and 219]
3. Select and document the permitted framework. Article 120(3)'s alternative
   requires grounded reasons and identification in the notes. Ordinary accepted
   norms do not automatically mandate full IFRS, mainland Dutch GAAP or
   EU-endorsed IFRS. Any claimed simplified framework or reduced disclosures
   needs its own established eligibility and legal basis. [J2]
4. Verify the complete current policies, statement set and disclosures for
   that framework and period. Hold unresolved recognition, measurement, tax or
   source conditions before constructing the engagement output package.

### Prepare the accounts, report and expert evidence

Under article 120, prepare the accounts and annual report in the articles'
language unless the general meeting has decided otherwise beforehand. Add
the latest accounts, expert statement where applicable and annual report of
unconsolidated subsidiaries unless their omission is justified as immaterial
to the prescribed insight in the notes. The annual report addresses balance-date
condition, the year's business, significant subsequent events and expected
developments; it must agree with the accounts. [J2, article 120]

Within the applicable article 120(1) preparation period, including any lawful
extension, make both the accounts and annual report available to all
shareholders at the company office. Without delay after that period expires,
offer the annual report to the general meeting and present the prepared
accounts for approval at the same time. Include each event in the filing
checklist. These article 120(1) and (6) duties are separate from article 122's
public-availability events. [J2]

The external expert examines article 120(3) compliance and the annual report's
compatibility. Article 121(6) defines eligible Dutch register-accountants,
qualifying accountant-administratieconsulenten, US certified public accountants
and persons admitted by the finance minister against equivalent competence.
Verify the actual person's entitlement; a generic accountant title does not
establish eligibility. Attach the written statement before meeting approval.
[J2, article 121]

### Distinguish office availability, registry notice and deposit

Article 122 normally requires a full accounts copy, dated with approval, to be
available to interested parties at the company office within eight days of
approval and for two years thereafter. If accounts remain unapproved two months
after the preparation deadline, make the prepared accounts available immediately.
Do not wait indefinitely for approval or add two months to every other duty.

Notify the Commercial Register promptly of availability and, where applicable,
unapproved status and required representation by a qualified expert. Supply
eligible requesters a copy at cost. Alternatively, article 122(6) permits
public inspection at the Commercial Register under the same timing and
additional conditions. The [Commercial Register Decree, article 23][R1]
confirms the notice and applicable group-declaration duties. These are not a
universal annual-accounts deposit rule for every BES entity. [J2, article 122]

### Check every group-relief condition separately

Article 123 relief from article 122 requires qualifying consolidation under
article 120(3), the specified written joint-liability undertaking, deposit of
that undertaking within six months after the subsidiary year end, and timely
availability of the consolidated accounts. Verify the undertaking's exact
debt scope, dates and signatory authority against article 123(b); do not
replace it with a generic parent-support letter. Article 123(d) has its own
consolidated balance-date timing and article 122 qualifications. [J2]

Article 124's expert-examination relief additionally requires written consent
from every shareholder during the financial year or within six months after
it ends. Only that fully qualifying branch also removes the annual-report
requirement and permits the specified minimum accounts in place of article
120(3). A consolidated parent return, majority consent or a late declaration
does not establish all conditions. [J2, articles 123–124]

Preserve ten-year legal-person accounting retention and the applicable paper
accounts and data-transfer conditions in article 15. Public inspection periods
and a different tax-retention period do not shorten that duty. Late or
misleading accounts can engage articles 125–126 and other provisions; no
complete penalty calculation or litigation deadline advice is provided here.

## Selected framework controls

Apply these points only after establishing the relevant framework. They do
not supply its complete standards or an IFRS compliance conclusion.

### Statement set, presentation and notes

The [IAS 1 overview][A1] identifies financial position, profit or loss and
other comprehensive income (OCI), changes in equity, cash flows, notes and
preceding-period comparatives as parts of the set. An additional opening
financial-position statement has a conditional retrospective-change branch.
Verify its material-effect test. Add every required statement to the domain's
outputs; an income statement and balance sheet alone cannot be labelled a
complete IFRS set.

The [IASB reference material][A3], reproducing selected IAS 1 paragraphs with
old/new markup, supports flexible financial-position ordering, the liquidity
exception to current/non-current presentation, and disclosure of presentation
currency and rounding scale. It also supports systematic notes and related
statement cross-references. It is not the full operative standard or a source
for every format or classification rule. Do not copy its concatenated markup
as statutory wording.

Use material accounting policy information under the [effective policy
disclosure amendment][A4]. Establish the complete framework checklist for
policies, judgements, estimates, tax, assets, debt, equity, related parties,
events and other material items. No micro/small/medium notes exemption is
established by this reference.

The [IAS 21 overview][A5] distinguishes functional currency, based on the
primary economic environment, from presentation currency. It does not impose
USD on every BES company's statements. Retain source precision and establish
conversion and display conventions; tax-return whole-dollar rules are not
financial-statement rounding rules.

### Depreciation and tax allocation

Verify each asset's applicable recognition, component, useful-life, residual,
method and timing rules before posting depreciation. This reference supplies
no default lives, rates or first-year convention. The [identified IAS 16
paragraphs quoted in the 2015 staff paper][A6] illustrate that a charge may
enter profit or loss or another asset's carrying amount. Reconcile total
depreciation to its initial destinations and separately track later releases
from capitalised balances. Apply the domain base's allocation check without
double counting amounts subsequently expensed. The paper's separate
testing-proceeds discussion and proposals are not adopted here.

Determine each levy's scope before preparing a tax provision. The [IAS 12
overview][A7] concerns taxes based on taxable profits. BES incorporation and
the existence of a distribution or property levy do not establish a generic
corporate-rate-times-accounting-profit calculation. Verify tax residence,
levy treatment, taxable bases, recognition exceptions and measurement separately.

Keep current and deferred tax schedules distinct. The [identified IAS 12
paragraphs quoted in the 2015 staff paper][A8] allocate tax with the underlying
item to profit or loss, OCI or equity, with a separate business-combination
branch. A tax balance alone cannot identify its corresponding statement
location. This selected historical quotation does not establish every current
tax rule or the paper's disputed instrument treatments. Hold the engagement
entry until the applicable current authority and facts are verified.

### Going concern

The [IFRS Foundation's 2025 educational material][A9] explains the IAS 1
assessment using all available future information, looking at least twelve
months from reporting date without a twelve-month cap and updating through
authorisation for issue. Consider material uncertainties and significant
close-call judgements. If going concern is inappropriate, disclose the basis
used and reasons; IAS 1 does not itself prescribe a replacement basis. Separate
audit-standard or local-law requirements need their own applicability check.

Map each actual ledger account to a justified statement line, note and
recognition location. An account description cannot establish the applicable
accounting policy. The following are internal mapping examples, not official

| Current tax and deferred tax | Separate tax schedules and statement lines | Profit or loss, OCI, equity or other applicable location |

checklist, together with all six domain components: income statement, balance

brief. Where the established framework requires them, add OCI, changes in

## Hypothetical component positions

These 2026 positions stipulate the relevant facts and authority. They do not
constitute complete financial statements, tax computations or audit opinions.
Amounts are USD unless stated otherwise.

1. **Strict monetary boundaries.** An NV meets the complete workforce test,
   has assets of 2,800,000 and net turnover of 5,700,000. Assets equal the
   2,800,000 boundary, so that condition fails and the cumulative test fails.
   Increasing established assets by 1 to 2,800,001 makes that condition pass;
   with the other stipulated conditions, the large-NV test then passes.
   Conversely, turnover exactly 5,600,000 fails even with higher assets.
   Check opt-in and sector rules separately. [J2, article 119]
2. **BV size does not create an opt-in.** A BV has the same established
   workforce, assets of 3,000,000 and turnover of 6,000,000. Its articles have
   no article 219 opt-in. Those figures alone do not activate articles
   120–122; the selected ordinary BV preparation period remains eight months
   under article 216, subject to any separately established sector rule.
   Hold an overall audit conclusion until that separate check is complete.
3. **Unapproved large-NV accounts.** A qualifying NV's year ends on
   31 December 2025. Under the stipulated unchanged rules and with no
   extension, preparation is due within six months, ending 30 June 2026.
   Both accounts and annual report must be available to all shareholders
   at the office within that period. Without delay after it expires, submit
   the annual report and prepared accounts to the meeting under article 120(6).
   Two further months end on 31 August 2026. If still unapproved then, the
   prepared accounts must be available immediately under article 122(2),
   with prompt registry notice. It cannot wait for a planned September
   approval. Any calendar-law adjustment needs its own verified authority.
4. **Approval and timely availability.** A company's applicable article 122
   accounts are approved on 1 September 2026 and first made available on
   5 September, four elapsed days later. That is within eight days. The full
   copy carries the approval date, stays available for the required two-year
   period and triggers prompt registry notice. This does not cure an earlier
   failure of the unapproved-accounts branch or establish tax-return filing.
5. **Group relief and dissent.** Every article 123 condition is established,
   including consolidation, the exact undertaking, timely deposit and accounts
   availability. Of four shareholders, three consent in writing to audit
   relief and one does not. Three out of four is 75%, not every shareholder;
   article 124's additional condition fails. Article 123 inspection relief
   and article 124 examination relief must not be combined into one assumed
   exemption. [J2, articles 123–124]
6. **Depreciation destinations.** Independently verified framework rules and
   asset schedules establish total current depreciation of 10,000: 6,000
   expensed and 4,000 included in qualifying assets still held. Reconcile
   10,000 = 6,000 + 4,000. Requiring the whole 10,000 to equal this period's
   profit-or-loss depreciation would misstate the stipulated allocation by
   4,000. This is a reconciliation example, not a decision that an actual
   asset qualifies for capitalisation. [A6]
7. **Tax recognition locations.** Verified tax schedules establish a 6,000
   profit-or-loss charge, 2,000 OCI charge and 500 direct-equity charge, with
   no other movements. Total specified tax movement is 6,000 + 2,000 + 500 =
   8,500. Deduct 6,000 in deriving profit after tax; reconcile 2,000 and 500
   separately to OCI and equity. Deducting 8,500 from profit would overstate
   that charge by 2,500. These are stipulated established amounts, not rates
   or a BES levy-scope decision. [A8]
