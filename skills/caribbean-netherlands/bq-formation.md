# Bonaire, Sint Eustatius and Saba: company formation in 2026

Establish the island, legal form, planned activities and requested date before
preparing formation advice. Incorporation, commercial registration, permission
to operate, admission to work or reside, and tax residence have separate tests.
This Tier 2 reference supports a qualified professional's examination of selected
rules. It has no accountant sign-off and does not yet support the complete
formation workflow.

Load `workflow-base` and `company-formation-workflow-base`. The universal base
governs source conditions, inferred-profile confirmation, refusals and its four
outputs. Combine its profile confirmation with the domain's situation check,
then retain the separate entity choice before the registration checklist.
Preserve the domain's five outputs and ten checks as well. Reference examples below do not constitute an engagement
recommendation, a complete registration checklist or a filing.

## Scope, period and completion conditions

The detailed rules cover ordinary *naamloze vennootschap* (NV, public limited
company) and *besloten vennootschap* (BV, private limited company) incorporation,
selected capital and register duties, and common establishment licensing. Other
forms are identified to prevent an incomplete choice being presented as a
complete entity comparison. Their full constitutions, tax treatments and
registration procedures remain outside this version's verified treatment.

The [1 January 2026 Book 2 snapshot][J1] is valid through 30 June. The
[consolidation from 1 July 2026][J2] deletes article 105(6) and (7), concerning
bearer-share evidence and transferees. [Staatsblad 2019/107, articles IV and V][H1]
and the [1 July 2019 commencement order][H2] establish the seven-year delay.
The selected incorporation and reporting provisions used here are unchanged
between those snapshots. This comparison does not determine every bearer-share
transaction or transitional right.

The establishment statute displays a pending-amendment annotation at article 5.
Its [official history][L2] identifies an amendment that cannot be implemented;
the [2012 commencement order][L3] expressly excludes that article. Apply the
displayed operative grounds in [articles 4a–6][L1], subject to verification of
the requested period. Do not infer an additional refusal ground from the flag.

Before an engagement, check applicable amendments, delegated rules and local
decisions for its period. An editing date does not establish operative law.

## Legal forms and entity routing

Book 2 recognises associations, cooperatives, mutual insurers, NVs, BVs,
foundations and private fund foundations. Its general legal-person rule normally
separates member, shareholder, director and supervisor liability from the
entity's obligations, subject to statutory exceptions. For example, BV articles
can impose shareholder liability under article 202(5)–(7). Do not describe any
form as unconditional personal-asset protection. [J2, articles 1 and 3]

| Local form and English description | Verified formation distinction | Route or limit |
|---|---|---|
| NV, public limited company | One or more incorporators; notarial deed; prescribed initial voting/profit shares | Detailed rules below; Book 2, articles 100–102 |
| BV, private limited company | One or more incorporators; notarial deed; registered shares only | Detailed rules below; articles 200–202 |
| Stichting, foundation | Notarial deed; no members or shareholders; restrictions on distributions | Specialist constitution and purpose review; articles 50–51 |
| Stichting particulier fonds, private fund foundation | No members or shareholders; cannot carry on a business, subject to specified investment, holding and limited-partner exclusions | Do not recommend it as an ordinary trading vehicle; article 50 |
| Vereniging, association | Non-notarial articles restrict registered-property and inheritance capacity and add director liability | Establish the required legal capacity and member rules; article 73 |
| Coöperatie, cooperative | Notarial deed; member needs met through the specified business arrangements | Verify full member, capital, liability and sector conditions; article 90 |
| Onderlinge waarborgmaatschappij, mutual insurer | Notarial deed; member insurance purpose | Insurance supervision and specialist review required; article 90 |
| Eenmanszaak, sole proprietorship; VOF, general partnership; CV, limited partnership | Commercial Register Act article 3 recognises natural-person businesses and the two partnerships | Their complete constitution, partner liability and income-tax treatment are not established here; obtain the relevant commercial and tax law |
| Foreign entity's branch | An island branch requires commercial-register analysis; licensing treats branches separately | Branch disclosure, foreign law, local representative and tax consequences require separate sources |

The legal-form catalogue alone cannot establish an entity's tax treatment.
Belastingwet BES article 5.2 generally deems a BES-established body to be in
European Netherlands for chapter V, with specified exceptions and inspector
decisions. Incorporation does not itself establish a qualifying BES tax status.
Resolve the full residence, substance, activity and ownership conditions before
comparing tax costs. Do not apply a generic zero corporate-profit rate or a
European Netherlands rate to every BES company. [T1, articles 1.5 and 5.1–5.2]

## Formation and registration rules

### Establish the constitution and contributions

Use a notary practising in BES. The notarial instrument includes the articles
and initial officers. Founders choose a language the notary understands; a
sworn Dutch translation is required for a language other than Papiamentu,
Dutch, English or Spanish. The notary arranges prompt commercial registration,
deposit of the authentic instrument and the Staatscourant formation notice.
The Commercial Register Act also assigns duties to directors. [J2, articles
4–5; R1, article 5]

NV/BV articles specify the name, island seat and purpose. The statutory name
must include the prescribed NV or BV designation and use Latin characters.
Record share classes, initial holders and contributions. Cash-only formation
requires all founders' equity declaration. In-kind formation requires their
signed opening balance. Shares may have nominal values, including in foreign
currency; do not import a mainland minimum-capital figure. [J2, articles
101–102 and 201–202]

Each subscriber owes the consideration specified in the deed or issue decision;
for nominal shares its value must at least equal nominal value. State a
non-cash contribution's value in the instrument. A permitted deferred call does
not automatically count towards statutory equity: articles 107(3) and 207(3)
exclude a claim not unconditionally due before one year after issue. Retain the
valuation basis and all enforceable contribution terms for the notary. [J2]

Pre-incorporation acts need their own ratification and liability analysis under
article 6. Do not assume the later company's existence removes the actor's
exposure. Post-formation share transfers, bearer-share transitions,
restructurings and distributions require their complete applicable provisions.

### Separate registration from permission to operate

Register island businesses and branches at the competent Chamber. NVs, BVs,
cooperatives, mutuals, foundations, private fund foundations and fully capable
associations are also registered as legal persons. Associations without full
capacity may register. The Act assigns owner, director, notary and specified
local daily-manager duties; another authorised person's completed notification
can discharge the same notification duty. [R1, articles 3–8]

The Executive Council's establishment permission is a separate stream.
Article 2 covers establishment, operation, acquisition, continuation, public
relocation and a change in the business's nature. Branches are separate
businesses for this statute. Article 1a also expressly includes NV directors
and specified other actors. Check the licence required for both the business
and the person; an extract from the Chamber is not permission to trade. [L1]

Article 3 exceptions concern public bodies, market or street-vending activities,
specified agriculture and fishing activities, craftspeople subject to locally
set worker limits, and small traders covered by an Executive Council decision.
Obtain the actual local decision before applying a conditional exemption.
Undated guidance about foundations does not establish a blanket exemption for
a trading foundation. [L1, articles 1a–3]

The eight-week decision period starts when the application is submitted in the
Executive Council's prescribed manner. A written extension of up to eight weeks
is permitted when article 5(2)'s public-interest refusal is contemplated.
For a late decision, the statutory licence takes effect on the third day after
the period expires; publication is required within two weeks and further
conditions can follow within eight weeks after expiry. This is not an
unqualified promise of permission eight weeks after any incomplete submission.
[L1, articles 4a–4c]

Refusal grounds include manifestly inadequate business skills or financial
resources. Public-interest refusal requires prior ministerial agreement.
Conditions and withdrawal provisions also apply. Operating without the
required licence can lead to closure and article 10 penalties; a complete
penalty assessment needs the applicable offence and enforcement rules.
[L1, articles 5–8, 10–11 and 15]

The [Bonaire official application guidance][G2] describes an online route and
six working days for complete applications; that is administrative guidance,
not a universal statutory deadline. [Statia guidance][G3] directs applicants
through Chamber registration and the licensing unit. Verify current form and
document requirements without submitting an application during research.

### Check officers, ownership and continuing obligations

Book 2 normally requires a board; a shareholder-managed BV is a specified
exception. Representation, conflicts, temporary vacancies and duties depend on
the statute and articles. Do not infer a universal secretary requirement or a
residence exemption from silence in the incorporation clauses. [J2, articles
8, 10–14 and 239]

Business/director permission, work authorisation and residence are separate.
The [current CTU-BES policy][I1] addresses self-employed entrepreneurs and
directors; the [work-permit decree, article 8(1)(d)][I2] has an exception for a
foreign holder of BES director permission. That exception does not itself grant
residence. Verify nationality, actual work, ownership, admission and any treaty
branch with the immigration professional before giving an individual answer.

The NV board maintains the registered-shareholder register under article 109;
the BV board maintains its shareholder register under article 209. Record the
prescribed share, holder, voting, contribution and mutation details. Foundation
article 51a separately specifies an internal payment-recipient register. These
records do not necessarily reveal every ultimate indirect owner. [J2]

Covered service providers must identify and adequately verify ultimate
beneficial owners (UBOs), understand ownership/control and keep information
current under [Wwft BES, articles 2.2–2.3][U1]. Article 2.13 prescribes their
record duty and five-year retention after the relationship or transaction.
This is distinct from an entity's internal register or a central filing duty.
The [government's February 2026 letter][U2] accompanied a draft whose
[explanatory section 7.1.6][U3] says BES had no UBO registers then. No central
BES filing form or deadline was established in the captured operative register
rules. Recheck subsequent developments and sector rules before an engagement;
the draft does not enact a permanent exemption. The reviewer supplies actual
identity data securely to the regulated provider, outside agent inputs.

Plan annual accounts from the governing legal form and articles. Ordinary NV/BV
accounts use the eight-month regime. The mandatory large-NV regime requires
the workforce condition, assets above USD 2.8 million and turnover above
USD 5.6 million together. A BV does not enter that regime merely by exceeding
NV size figures; article 219 requires an articles-based opt-in. Sector duties
can apply separately. Group relief requires every condition in articles
123–124, not merely ownership by a parent. [J2, articles 116–124 and 216–219]

Preserve ordinary NV/BV shareholders' and bearer-debenture holders' access to
retained accounts documents for two years from preparation and approval,
respectively. Track those events separately from ten-year retention and the
article 122 public-availability regime. Use `bq-financial-statements` for the
selected reporting distinctions; its full operational conditions remain open.
[J2, articles 116(5)–(6) and 216(5)–(6)]

Keep legal-person accounting records for ten years under article 15. Maintain
the event-notification calendar and actual tax/employer invitations separately.
Do not turn a preparation deadline into a universal Chamber accounts-filing
deadline, or treat the general seven-year tax record rule as permission to
destroy records still required by company law. [J2; R1, articles 7–8]

## Hypothetical component positions

These 2026 examples stipulate the facts and applicable provisions. They are
selected legal and arithmetic positions, not complete formation advice.

1. **Cash-only BV contribution.** One founder subscribes the required share
   rights and contributes USD 2,000 cash. The opening balance has assets of
   2,000, no liabilities and no nominal capital. Equity is 2,000 − 0 = 2,000,
   satisfying the non-negative component of article 201. All founders' signed
   declaration, the rest of the deed, permissions and registration remain
   required. The result is not proof that total formation costs are 2,000.
2. **In-kind contribution and nominal capital.** Founders contribute assets
   with a substantiated value of 8,000 and assumed liabilities of 1,000.
   Opening equity is 8,000 − 1,000 = 7,000. It is non-negative, but an intended
   nominal capital of 8,000 exceeds it by 1,000. The component fails articles
   101/201's nominal-capital condition. Obtain a legally valid contribution or
   capital revision and the signed opening balance; do not certify the deed.
3. **Deferred contribution excluded.** A share-issue position has 2,000 cash
   and a 4,000 contribution claim that first becomes unconditionally due more
   than one year after issue. With no other assets or liabilities, the amount
   counted for the specified statutory equity calculation is 2,000, excluding
   4,000 under article 107(3) or 207(3). It is not 6,000. Determine the actual
   contract and nominal-capital obligations before accepting a formation.
4. **Statia application fee components.** A verified business application and
   sole-director application give 300 + 200 = 500 under F2. A business with
   two directors gives 300 + (2 × 250) = 800. The difference is 300. These
   exclude Chamber, notary, publication, sector and professional costs. A
   grant-year annual charge cannot be invented; item 11.2 begins in the next
   calendar year and its scope must be applied to each actual licence.
5. **Bonaire foreign-inquiry costs.** The establishment application component
   is 140. Suppose the competent authority establishes additional inquiry
   costs of 35 and asks for the statutory 8 advance towards those costs.
   Total components are 140 + 35 = 175; after the advance, the remaining
   inquiry cost is 35 − 8 = 27. The advance is part of the inquiry cost, not
   a second 8 charge. Without an actual cost assessment, the total is unknown.
   [F1, tariff 10.2; L1, article 4(2)]
6. **Turnover does not establish BV relief.** An ordinary trading BV expects
   qualifying sales of 20,000, below 32,000 by 12,000. That comparison alone
   does not establish ABB relief: article 6.22's ordinary route concerns
   natural persons and requires a request and decision. The separate
   social/cultural-body route needs its own facts. Record Needs Input for
   the applicable tax/relief position and use `bq-abb`; do not mark the BV
   exempt or outside tax registration merely from expected turnover.
