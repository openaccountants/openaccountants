# Argentina: tax pack

The individual tax year is the calendar year; companies use their fiscal year. The national authority is ARCA, which replaced AFIP. Amounts are Argentine pesos unless stated otherwise. Provincial turnover tax and municipal charges are separate from national income tax and IVA. This map covers 2026 and retains clearly dated 2025 return material where still relevant.

## Out of scope

Provincial and municipal filing procedures; complete treaty-by-treaty relief and financial/CFC matrices; complex restructurings and special investment regimes; detailed customs/export-refund and withholding-agent procedures; regulated-industry formation and full provincial registry workflows. These specialist cases do not make an otherwise covered domain partial. Ordinary unresolved items are named above. An unconfirmed figure is not a zero, exemption or permission to use an old period's value.
