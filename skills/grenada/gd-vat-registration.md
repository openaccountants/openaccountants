# Grenada VAT registration and returns

> **General reference only.** This skill is general tax/accounting reference material for AI-assisted workflows. It has not been reviewed for any specific person's facts, documents, elections, deadlines, residency, filing status, or local procedures. Do not rely on it to file, pay, amend, or take a tax position without review by a qualified professional in the relevant jurisdiction.

## Scope

Registration for and returns under the Value Added Tax Act, Chapter 333A, in Grenada for 2026, with the rates in section 8(3). Amounts are EC$. The Inland Revenue Division (IRD) site now redirects to a social media page; its June 2026 VAT page is read from a web.archive.org copy. Input tax rules, imports, reverse charge, exemptions and refunds are outside this skill.

## Ask the client first

1. Taxable supplies (standard-rated and zero-rated) in the last 12 months, the last 4 months, and a supportable forecast for the next 12.
2. Is the person a promoter of public entertainment or the licensee or proprietor of a place of public entertainment?
3. Is it making a taxable supply through an investment under the Grenada Investment Promotion Act?
4. Does it run several activities, branches or divisions?
5. What does it supply: hotel or holiday accommodation, dive packages, ordinary goods and services?

## The method, step by step

### Steps

1. **Test monthly.** On the last day of each month check the three tests in s.9(1): the 12 months to that day exceed the threshold; the 4 months to that day exceed one third of it; or there are reasonable grounds to expect the next 12 months to exceed it.
2. **Count** standard-rated and zero-rated taxable supplies of the person across all activities and branches. Leave out exempt supplies, sales of capital assets and supplies made because the business is sold or closing (s.9(3)).
3. **Special cases:** public entertainment promoters and venues (s.10) and investors under the Investment Promotion Act (s.12) register whatever their turnover.
4. **Apply** on the prescribed form within 14 days of becoming required to register. A late applicant is treated as a taxable person from the day it became required to register.
5. **One registration** in the name of the person, not of each activity, branch or division. Display the original registration certificate.
6. **Returns:** lodge for each tax period within 20 days of its end; pay on time or interest runs at 1.5% a month.
7. **Cancellation:** a written request to the Comptroller; falling turnover is a reason to apply, not a cancellation.

## Worked cases

### Case 1: four-month test for a new business

A business opened on 1 March 2026 and made taxable supplies of EC$110,000 by 30 June. One third of 300,000 is 100,000, so at 30 June it is 10,000 over the four-month limit and must register unless it can show the four months plus the next eight will not exceed 300,000. It applies by 14 July 2026. Its standard-rated sales of 10,000 a month then carry output VAT of 1,500 a month, and a hotel room night of 400 carries 40.

## When to refuse or refer

- The threshold is EC$300,000 under s.9(2) as amended by Act No. 21 of 2015; the consolidated Cap. 333A copy on the Parliament site predates that amendment and still shows EC$120,000.
- Exemptions, zero-rating schedules, input tax, imports, reverse charge and refunds.
- Late filing penalties: not stated on the IRD page read.

## Filing and payment

1. Apply for registration on the prescribed form within 14 days (s.9(4)); the Comptroller notifies within 21 days (s.14).
2. Lodge the VAT return in the prescribed form within 20 days after each tax period (s.44); online through GTAX.
3. Pay with the return; interest is 1.5% a month on late amounts ([IRD VAT page, archived](https://web.archive.org/web/20260606100448/https://www.ird.gd/taxes/value-added-tax)).
4. Keep the return calculation and submission receipt; a portal account alone is not proof of filing.

## Completion checklist

- Three tests checked at each month end, all activities together.
- Applied within 14 days; certificate displayed.
- 15% or 10% applied by supply type.
- Returns within 20 days.
