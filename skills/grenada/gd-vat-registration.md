# Grenada VAT registration and returns

## Scope and evidence

Source-cited draft checked 22 October 2026. This Guide addresses registration and return administration under the VAT framework. It does not establish VAT rates or a transaction's exemption. The official IRD VAT page returned an HTTP 500 error directly; its text was accessible in the search index. Treat this as indexed official guidance pending direct verification.

## Registration decision

IRD states that annual taxable supplies **in excess of EC$300,000** require registration. A promoter of public entertainment or licensee/proprietor of a public-entertainment venue must register regardless of that threshold. Multiple taxable activities, branches and divisions are registered under the person conducting them, not separately. [IRD VAT guidance, referring to section 14](https://www.ird.gd/taxes/value-added-tax).

The [IRD G-TAX registration FAQ](https://www.ird.gd/gtax/index.html) describes both a look-back test at the end of twelve months or less and a forward-looking test where there are reasonable grounds to expect taxable supplies above EC$300,000. Its ordinary threshold description includes standard-rated and zero-rated supplies. Do not test each branch separately or exclude zero-rated supplies just because their output rate is zero.

## Worked registration checks

These illustrations assume the receipts have already been correctly classified as taxable supplies:

- One person has EC$180,000 from one activity and EC$140,000 from another within the relevant period. Combined supplies are EC$320,000, above the ordinary threshold.
- A new operator has a supported forecast of EC$360,000. Review the forward-looking registration obligation without waiting for actual receipts to cross the threshold.
- A public-entertainment promoter cannot rely solely on a forecast below EC$300,000 to avoid registration.

Record the period, legal person, activity classification, actual turnover and forecast evidence. The application deadline and effective registration date require checking against the operative Act before advising when charging begins.

## Return and cessation controls

IRD says a taxable person must lodge a VAT return for each tax period no later than twenty days after its end. Retain the return calculation and submission receipt; a portal account alone does not demonstrate compliance. [IRD VAT return guidance](https://www.ird.gd/taxes/value-added-tax).

The G-TAX FAQ directs a person seeking cancellation to submit a written request to the Comptroller. Falling turnover is a reason to apply for deregistration, not evidence that cancellation has already taken effect. [IRD G-TAX FAQ](https://www.ird.gd/gtax/index.html).

## Unresolved scope

Confirm registration timing, payment deadlines, rates, zero-rating and exemption schedules, invoices, deductions, imports, refunds and special-sector rules before completing a return. The retrieved VAT page describes tourism/accommodation at 10% in an ambiguously worded general-rate answer; it is not used to establish a universal rate. Do not substitute an assumed VAT percentage for research. Independent review and direct source verification remain pending.
