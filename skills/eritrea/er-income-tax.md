# Eritrea Personal Income Tax

> **General reference only.** This skill is general tax/accounting reference material for AI-assisted workflows. It has not been reviewed for any specific person's facts, documents, elections, deadlines, residency, filing status, or local procedures. Do not rely on it to file, pay, amend, or take a tax position without review by a qualified professional in the relevant jurisdiction.

Tax year 2026. The newest income tax text we could open is Legal Notice 119/2012 and the newest sales tax text is Proclamation 117/2001. The Ministry of Justice list dated 2023 shows no later amendment to either. Nothing dated 2023 to 2026 could be opened, so check with the Inland Revenue Department that no later change exists.

## Who this applies to

Any person with income that has its origin in Eritrea, wherever paid (Proc 62/1994 arts 2(8), 5). Employment, rent, business and commercial farming income are computed separately (art 6). Sales tax is in [VAT or GST](er-vat-gst.md), companies in [Corporate income tax](er-corporate-income-tax.md).

## The base

Employment income covers salaries, wages, allowances, pension contributions, directors' fees and benefits in kind (arts 7, 9, 10). A payment covering more than a month is prorated over the months (art 8(1)).

Rent: gross includes cash and benefits in kind and furniture rent. Deduct non-income taxes on the house and one quarter of gross for repairs, maintenance and depreciation (art 18). Rent paid for over a year falls in the year of payment, with tax computed by prorating (Legal Notice 20/1995 art 14).

Business: deduct expenses wholly, necessarily and exclusively paid for the activity (art 24). Interest is deductible only on loans from approved credit institutions (art 25). Losses carry forward 5 years, earliest first (art 25A, added by Proc 116/2001).

Exempt (art 31): rural self-employed craft workers with no employees; casual day labourers, who become taxable if they work over 15 days in a month for one employer (Legal Notice 20/1995 art 7); pay from abroad for a foreign representative present under 183 days in a year, with the whole period taxable once that is passed (art 8 of the Notice); bank interest; dividends from Eritrean bodies; diplomats on reciprocity; income exempted by law, treaty or ministerial agreement.

## Deadlines and filing

Employer: declare, withhold and pay within one month from the end of the month (arts 13, 14). An employer who fails is personally liable. A person with two employers in a month, or a non-exempt embassy employee, declares and pays personally within one month of the next month (arts 11, 12).

Rent: within one month from the end of the fiscal year (art 19).

Business (art 26): 4 months after year end if balance sheet and profit and loss are required, 2 months if only a daily income and expense summary, 1 month if no books are required. Regulations category test (Legal Notice 119/2012): A is any body, or a person with gross income over 1,000,000 Nakfa. B is a person over 200,000 up to 1,000,000, a professional over 30,000, or an importer, exporter, wholesaler or distributor up to 1,000,000. C is a person up to 200,000, or a professional up to 30,000. Category A keeps a journal, pre-numbered invoices, stock cards, and cost and depreciation records; B a daily book and the same documents (Legal Notice 20/1995 art 16). Receipts must be issued without being asked (Legal Notice 81/2003).

Steps: fill the declaration, deliver it with payment to the Inland Revenue Department office, keep the receipt. An assessment can be appealed to the Tax Appeal Commission within 30 days with a cash deposit (art 55). A business pays the lesser of 75% of the tax assessed and 75% of the tax finally assessed for the last preceding year; on a first assessment it pays 30%; in all other cases 50%; further appeal to the High Court on law within 30 days (art 58).

## Penalties and interest

| Failure | Penalty | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Not declaring and paying on time | 20% of tax finally assessed | Proc 62/1994 art 63 |
| Late payment | 5% a month, up to 50% | art 64 |
| No acceptable books | 20% of tax finally assessed | art 65 |
| No receipt issued | 125% of the amount, at least 500 Nakfa, licence suspension possible above 8,000 | Proc 138/2003 art 2 |

Penalties count as tax and can be waived by the Department (art 66).

## Cross-border points

Income is taxed on Eritrean origin, not worldwide residence (art 2(8)). Income from services by persons abroad is under the 10% rate, withheld by the payer (art 26(2)(b)). For citizens abroad the 2% tax is collected by embassies and consular offices (Proc 67/1995 art 3).

## Two worked examples that recompute

**Case 1: salary of 5,000 Nakfa a month.** 4 + 100 + 260 + 250 + 450 = 1,064 Nakfa.

```oa-check
name: Monthly pay 5000 Nakfa
inputs:
  pay: 5000
steps:
  - band_1: min(pay, 200) * 0.02
  - band_2: (min(pay, 1200) - min(pay, 200)) * 0.10
  - band_3: (min(pay, 2500) - min(pay, 1200)) * 0.20
  - band_4: (min(pay, 3500) - min(pay, 2500)) * 0.25
  - band_5: (pay - min(pay, 3500)) * 0.30
  - tax: band_1 + band_2 + band_3 + band_4 + band_5
expected:
  tax: 1064.00
```

**Case 2, a decision: should a landlord take furnished rent of 60,000 a year as one payment for one year or two?** One year, income after the 25% allowance is 45,000.

```oa-check
name: Rent income 45000 after allowance
inputs:
  income: 45000
steps:
  - band_1: min(income, 2400) * 0.02
  - band_2: (min(income, 14400) - min(income, 2400)) * 0.10
  - band_3: (min(income, 30000) - min(income, 14400)) * 0.20
  - band_4: (min(income, 42000) - min(income, 30000)) * 0.25
  - band_5: (income - min(income, 42000)) * 0.30
  - tax: band_1 + band_2 + band_3 + band_4 + band_5
expected:
  tax: 8268.00
```

Tax is 8,268 Nakfa. If the same sum covers two years, it is prorated (Notice art 14), so each year is taxed on 22,500 and falls in lower bands.

## Ask the client first

- Citizen, and living in Eritrea or abroad?
- Income type: employment, rent, business, farming?
- One employer or several in the month?
- Annual gross income and occupation (sets the category)?
- Fiscal year used, if approved as different?
