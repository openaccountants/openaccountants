# Falkland Islands: tax pack

The Falklands are a separate tax jurisdiction using GBP and FIGTO income/corporation tax. Individuals use calendar-year income with assessment in the following year; companies use accounting periods. Do not import UK allowances, National Insurance or treaties. The UK arrangement must be tested on its own conditions. Five source-cited tier 2 Guides cover the six applicable domains, with the ordinary gaps above left visible.

## Out of scope

Petroleum/ring-fence asset and financing computations, full Schedule 6 administration, specialist reorganisations/group surrenders, defence designations and foreign-jurisdiction consequences. No specialist omission is disguised as an assertion of exemption. The unissued 2026-income personal return's 2027 fields and live base lending rate are not invented.
