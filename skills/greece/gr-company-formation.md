# Greece company formation and entity choice v0.3

## Scope

Form an IKE, EPE or AE in Greece, with the legal form, founders, capital/contribution choices, lawful formation route, fees and post-formation handoff made explicit. This is a formation workflow for 2026, not a tax computation or a claim that a special activity licence has been obtained. The source consolidations and unresolved later-law questions are stated below.

Before choosing a route, apply Decision 38164/2025 art. 2: ordinary YMS/e-YMS covers original formations within its scope. It excludes companies formed by merger, division (including partial division or branch spin-off) or conversion; formations requiring administrative permission, including insurance/reinsurance undertakings, credit/financial institutions, credit/mutual-insurance cooperatives, sports AE (PAE/KAE) and entities licensed by the Capital Market Commission; and companies with a main or secondary activity under the pre-authorisation KADs 56.10.11.06, 56.30.10.09, 56.30.10.10, 56.30.10.11, 56.30.10.12, 90.04.10.09 or 93.21.10.01. These activities require the art. 80 Law 3463/2006 establishment pre-approval specified in the decision. Use the applicable transformation or administrative-authorisation formation procedure for an excluded case; ordinary YMS acceptance is not sufficient. [Decision 38164/2025 arts. 2-3](https://gge.mindev.gov.gr/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/ΝΕΑ-ΥΜΣ.pdf).

## Ask the client first

1. Which legal form and activity, founders and managers, residence/nationality and active Greek tax numbers are involved?
2. What capital, in-kind property, work or guarantee contributions, and member liability are intended?
3. Does an asset transfer, special law or founder choice require a notarial deed?
4. Is the name already reserved, how many founders are there, and what written fee quotation does the chosen route produce?
5. Will the entity employ staff, trade across borders or require sector authorisation, and who will manage its books and filing calendar?

## The method, step by step


### Entity choice and capital

- **Common entity types overview.** The most common Greek business vehicles are the private company (ΙΚΕ), the limited liability company (ΕΠΕ) and the société anonyme (ΑΕ). The Ministry of Development calls the ΙΚΕ the most widespread company form in Greece and the one most founders choose. Each gains legal personality when it is registered in GEMI, the General Commercial Registry (Γ.Ε.ΜΗ.).  _(Ministry of Development, basic features of companies, https://gge.mindev.gov.gr/tomeas-emporiou/vasika-xaraktiristika-etaireion/)_
- **Private company (ΙΚΕ, Ιδιωτική Κεφαλαιουχική Εταιρεία).** A commercial company with legal personality. Subject to the personal liability accepted for guarantee contributions under art. 79, only the company is liable for its debts, with its own assets (Law 4072/2012 art. 43(1), (2)). One person can form it, or it can become single-member (art. 43(4)). The name must contain "Ιδιωτική Κεφαλαιουχική Εταιρεία" or "Ι.Κ.Ε.", or "Private Company" / "P.C." in international dealings; a single-member company adds "Μονοπρόσωπη" / "Single Member" (art. 44(2), (3)). It does not need its real seat in Greece (art. 45(3)).  _(Law 4072/2012 arts. 43-120, ministry text updated to 28.4.2023, https://gge.mindev.gov.gr/tomeas-emporiou/δίκαιο-της-ιδιωτικής-κεφαλαιουχικής/)_
- **ΙΚΕ share capital.** The law sets no minimum. The partners set the capital freely and it may be zero (Law 4072/2012 art. 43(3), as replaced by Law 4155/2013 art. 11(1)). Law 4155/2013 art. 11(2) repealed art. 77(5), the rule that at least one share had to stand for a capital contribution. Partners take shares for capital contributions in cash or in kind (art. 77), non-capital contributions such as work or services (art. 78), or guarantee contributions, meaning personal liability to creditors up to an amount set in the articles (art. 79). Every share, of any kind, has the same nominal value of at least EUR 1 (art. 75(3)). The Ministry of Development's company page states a minimum of EUR 1 of capital, and its consolidated text of Law 4072/2012 still prints the repealed art. 77(5); the law in the Government Gazette allows zero capital.  _(Law 4155/2013 art. 11, ΦΕΚ Α' 120/29.5.2013; Law 4072/2012; Ministry of Development, [ΙΚΕ basic features](https://gge.mindev.gov.gr/tomeas-emporiou/vasika-xaraktiristika-etaireion/%ce%b9%ce%b4%ce%b9%cf%89%cf%84%ce%b9%ce%ba%ce%b7-%ce%ba%ce%b5%cf%86%ce%b1%ce%bb%ce%b1%ce%b9%ce%bf%cf%85%cf%87%ce%b9%ce%ba%ce%b7-%ce%b5%cf%84%ce%b1%ce%b9%cf%81%ce%b5%ce%b9%ce%b1-%ce%b9%ce%ba%ce%b5/))_
- **ΙΚΕ capital paid in full.** The capital must be paid in full at formation or at a capital increase. Within one month the manager certifies full payment by a deed registered in GEMI. Any unpaid part is cancelled by reducing capital (Law 4072/2012 art. 77(4)). A contribution in kind needs no valuation if the articles value it at no more than EUR 5,000 (art. 77(2)).  _(Law 4072/2012)_
- **ΙΚΕ website.** Within one month of formation the company must have a website showing the partners' names and addresses, each partner's type of contribution, the manager, the capital and the GEMI number (Law 4072/2012 art. 47(1), (2)).  _(Law 4072/2012)_
- **Limited liability company (ΕΠΕ, Εταιρεία Περιορισμένης Ευθύνης).** Formed with minimum capital of EUR 1, set freely by the partners, in cash or in kind. Partners have limited liability. Governed by Law 3190/1955. The EUR 4,500 figure in the earlier version of this skill no longer applies.  _(Ministry of Development, [ΕΠΕ basic features](https://gge.mindev.gov.gr/tomeas-emporiou/vasika-xaraktiristika-etaireion/%ce%b5%cf%84%ce%b1%ce%b9%cf%81%ce%b5%ce%b9%ce%b5%cf%83-%cf%80%ce%b5%cf%81%ce%b9%ce%bf%cf%81%ce%b9%cf%83%ce%bc%ce%b5%ce%bd%ce%b7%cf%83-%ce%b5%cf%85%ce%b8%cf%85%ce%bd%ce%b7%cf%83-%ce%b5%cf%80%ce%b5/), https://gge.mindev.gov.gr/tomeas-emporiou/vasika-xaraktiristika-etaireion/)_
- **Société anonyme (ΑΕ, Ανώνυμη Εταιρεία).** A commercial company for larger ventures, governed by Law 4548/2018. Shareholders have limited liability. It can issue ordinary and preference shares, bonds and other securities.  _(Ministry of Development, [ΑΕ basic features](https://gge.mindev.gov.gr/tomeas-emporiou/vasika-xaraktiristika-etaireion/%ce%b1%ce%bd%cf%89%ce%bd%cf%85%ce%bc%ce%b5%cf%83-%ce%b5%cf%84%ce%b1%ce%b9%cf%81%ce%b5%ce%b9%ce%b5%cf%83-%ce%b1%ce%b5-%ce%b2%ce%b1%cf%83%ce%b9%ce%ba%ce%b1-%cf%87%ce%b1%cf%81%ce%b1%ce%ba/), https://gge.mindev.gov.gr/tomeas-emporiou/vasika-xaraktiristika-etaireion/)_
- **ΑΕ minimum share capital.** EUR 25,000.  _(Ministry of Development, [ΑΕ basic features](https://gge.mindev.gov.gr/tomeas-emporiou/vasika-xaraktiristika-etaireion/%ce%b1%ce%bd%cf%89%ce%bd%cf%85%ce%bc%ce%b5%cf%83-%ce%b5%cf%84%ce%b1%ce%b9%cf%81%ce%b5%ce%b9%ce%b5%cf%83-%ce%b1%ce%b5-%ce%b2%ce%b1%cf%83%ce%b9%ce%ba%ce%b1-%cf%87%ce%b1%cf%81%ce%b1%ce%ba/))_
- **Audit.** An ΙΚΕ or ΕΠΕ publishes annual financial statements each year and elects statutory auditors only if it is a medium or large entity. It may choose a voluntary audit.  _(Ministry of Development, ΙΚΕ and [ΕΠΕ basic features](https://gge.mindev.gov.gr/tomeas-emporiou/vasika-xaraktiristika-etaireion/%ce%b5%cf%84%ce%b1%ce%b9%cf%81%ce%b5%ce%b9%ce%b5%cf%83-%cf%80%ce%b5%cf%81%ce%b9%ce%bf%cf%81%ce%b9%cf%83%ce%bc%ce%b5%ce%bd%ce%b7%cf%83-%ce%b5%cf%85%ce%b8%cf%85%ce%bd%ce%b7%cf%83-%ce%b5%cf%80%ce%b5/))_


### Incorporation route, documents, timeline and cost



For original formations within that scope, companies are formed through a One-Stop Shop (Υπηρεσία Μιας Στάσης, ΥΜΣ) at a chamber of commerce or a certified notary, or fully online through the e-ΥΜΣ at https://eyms.businessportal.gr (Law 4919/2022 arts. 8, 9 and 12).

- **Registration body.** GEMI (Γενικό Εμπορικό Μητρώο). Registration is compulsory for the ΑΕ, ΕΠΕ, ΙΚΕ and the general and limited partnerships, among others (Law 4919/2022 art. 16(1)). An ΙΚΕ is formed by its registration in GEMI (Law 4072/2012 art. 52(1)).  _(Law 4919/2022, ministry codification after Law 5164/2024, https://gge.mindev.gov.gr/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/ΚΩΔΙΚΟΠΟΙΗΣΗ-ΝΟΜΟΥ-4919.22-μετά-την-τροπ.-με-Ν.-5164.24-1-2.pdf)_
- **Founder tax number (ΑΦΜ) and other founder conditions.** Every individual founder must have, at the time of formation: legal capacity; an active Greek tax number (Α.Φ.Μ.) whose use is not suspended; TAXISnet codes or an eIDAS electronic identity; and, for a non-EU national who lives or will live in Greece, a residence permit or proof of a pending application (Joint Ministerial Decision 38164/2025 art. 6(1)). A foreign legal person that joins as partner or board member and has no Greek ΑΦΜ gets one from the ΥΜΣ through TAXIS (Law 4919/2022 art. 9(2)).  _(ΚΥΑ 38164/2025, ΦΕΚ Β' 2639/29.5.2025, https://gge.mindev.gov.gr/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/ΝΕΑ-ΥΜΣ.pdf)_
- **Formation document.** An ΙΚΕ is formed by a document containing its articles. It must be notarial only if a special law requires it, if assets whose transfer needs a notarial deed are contributed, or if the founders choose it (Law 4072/2012 art. 49(2)). Outside those cases an ΙΚΕ is formed only online (Law 4072/2012 art. 51(α); ΚΥΑ 38164/2025 art. 3(6)). Where a special law requires the notarial form, for the activity or for the transfer of a contributed asset, formation goes through a certified notary acting as ΥΜΣ, whatever the company form (ΚΥΑ 38164/2025 art. 3(5)). Template articles are on the GEMI website, and the ministry publishes templates for the ΑΕ, ΕΠΕ, ΙΚΕ, ΟΕ and ΕΕ; a template cannot be used where a notarial deed is required (Law 4919/2022 art. 13(1), (3), (4)).  _(Law 4072/2012; Law 4919/2022; ΚΥΑ 38164/2025; Ministry of Development, legal framework of the ΥΜΣ, https://gge.mindev.gov.gr/tomeas-emporiou/thesmiko-plaisio-genikes-plirofories/)_
- **What the ΥΜΣ does in one step.** After its checks the ΥΜΣ system issues the EUID and GEMI number, gives the company a tax number (ΑΦΜ) and enters it in the tax register, provides the TAXIS registration/key procedure under art. 10(3), registers the company with e-EFKA as an employer where needed, registers it with the competent chamber and issues the digitally signed formation certificate. The company then exists and operates lawfully (Law 4919/2022 art. 9(6), (7)). It may then ask a bank of its choice to open an account through the ΥΜΣ system (art. 9(8)).  _(Law 4919/2022)_
- **Incorporation timeline.** The ΥΜΣ checks the application, the articles, the name and the fee payment on the working day it receives them, or at the latest the next working day (Law 4919/2022 art. 9(4)). If something is missing, the founders have five working days to correct it; otherwise the application is rejected (art. 9(5)). Time to obtain founder tax numbers and residence permits comes on top.  _(Law 4919/2022)_
- **Incorporation cost.** Set by ΚΥΑ 38164/2025 art. 5 and its Annex I. Lawyer and notary fees are not included (Law 4919/2022 art. 14(2)(α); ΚΥΑ art. 5(4)). The fee is not refunded if formation fails for reasons on the founders' side (Law 4919/2022 art. 14(3)).

So an ΑΕ with EUR 25,000 capital, formed at a chamber ΥΜΣ that also checks the name, pays EUR 60 + 10 + 10 = EUR 80, plus EUR 25 (25,000 x 0.1%) to the Competition Commission: EUR 105 in all. An ΙΚΕ, which is formed online, pays the EUR 18 online formation fee in place of the EUR 60.


### Execute the selected route

1. First apply the art. 2 exclusions above. For an eligible ordinary formation, start from the public e-YMS service, https://eyms.businessportal.gr, or the competent certified notary/One-Stop Shop. Choose the legal form and confirm the current template or required notarial instrument. No live submission is part of this skill.
2. Assemble the legally signed application, articles, founder/manager identification, contribution details, registered address evidence, authorisations and applicable residence documents. Follow the form's actual fields; do not invent current screen labels. Law 4919/2022 art. 9(1) and decision arts. 6-9 govern the documents and checks.
3. Complete the name check/reservation, signatures and route-specific fee payment. Preserve the application and receipts. Respond to any deficiency notice within the actual statutory cure period above.
4. After acceptance, retrieve the digitally signed formation notice and articles, GEMI number/EUID, company tax identity, TAXIS activation instructions and employer number where required. Verify these records rather than assuming every interoperability step succeeded. Formation and post-formation tax activation are distinct checks.
5. Calendar IKE capital certification and company-website publication, then hand the records to the relevant tax, invoicing and payroll owners below. Requesting a bank account after formation is not proof that a bank has opened it.

## Worked cases

### Chamber formation fee calculation

For an AE with EUR 25,000 capital, formed at a chamber One-Stop Shop that also checks the name, the statutory components are EUR 60 formation, EUR 10 registration, EUR 10 name check and EUR 25 Competition Commission charge, totaling EUR 105. Capital itself is not a fee, and notary/lawyer charges are additional where applicable. For an IKE online, the decision states EUR 18 formation instead of EUR 60; do not assume that this settles every other fee. These are route comparisons, not a recommendation to choose a legal form purely on its fee. [Decision art. 5](https://gge.mindev.gov.gr/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/ΝΕΑ-ΥΜΣ.pdf).

```oa-check
name: Greek AE chamber fees, capital EUR 25,000 and name check

  name_check: 10

  - competition_charge: capital * competition_rate
  - total_fees: chamber_fee + registration + name_check + competition_charge
  - online_ike_formation_component: chamber_fee * 0.30
expected:
  competition_charge: 25
  total_fees: 105
  online_ike_formation_component: 18
```

## When to refuse or refer

Use the certified notary route where required. Refer unresolved property valuations, guarantee liability, regulated activities, immigration eligibility and founder identity defects before submission. Do not promise the older practical formation times or an all-in online fee without the current official quotation. An IKE's ability to have its real seat elsewhere does not decide tax residence; use gr-cross-border.

## Filing and payment


Greek companies keep statutory accounting records, publish annual financial statements in GEMI, file the corporate income tax return, and meet VAT and payroll obligations.

- **Annual financial statements.** Prepared under the Greek Accounting Standards (Law 4308/2014) and published in GEMI. Failing to file financial statements in GEMI carries a fine of EUR 1,000 to EUR 100,000, set by seriousness, company form and size category (Law 4919/2022 art. 50(1)(ζ)). The fine is halved if it is paid and the breach put right within 30 days of notice; payment also waives the right to challenge that assessment (art. 50(4)). It doubles for a repeat breach within three years and triples for further repeats (art. 50(2)).  _(Law 4919/2022 art. 50, as replaced by Law 5164/2024 art. 55)_
- **Other GEMI fines.** Not registering in GEMI: EUR 1,000 to EUR 10,000 (Law 4919/2022 art. 50(1)(β)). Late application for a GEMI entry: EUR 100 to EUR 500 (art. 50(1)(δ)). Missing company details on documents and letters: EUR 100 to EUR 500 (art. 50(1)(γ)).  _(Law 4919/2022)_
- **Annual corporate income tax return.** Prepare form N in myAADE with the accounts and tax adjustments, using [gr-corporate-income-tax](gr-corporate-income-tax.md) for the actual rate, filing window, instalments and annual trade fee. Those former headline figures have one home there.
- **VAT compliance.** Check registration and the EL-prefixed VAT identity for intra-EU work. Use [greece-vat-return](greece-vat-return.md) for VAT registration, the small-business exemption and the applicable monthly/quarterly rules, rather than inferring a period from incorporation alone.
- **myDATA e-books.** Revenue, expense and classification data go electronically to AADE's myDATA platform. See greece-einvoice.  _(greece-einvoice)_
- **Payroll filings.** Check employer registration and hiring through ERGANI II, monthly APD and contribution payments, and payroll income-tax withholding. Use [gr-payroll](gr-payroll.md) and [gr-employee-contributions](gr-employee-contributions.md) for the actual filing and payment calendars.


## Completion checklist

Keep the signed formation instruments, registry notice/EUID, tax/employer registrations, payment receipts, actual outstanding deficiency notices, capital-payment certification and a named owner for each ongoing filing. Tax rates, trade fees and return dates previously repeated here are now handed to their existing owners, not dropped from the workflow.
