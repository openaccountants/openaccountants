| 87 | The three inputs | - **Total acquisition price.** Everything paid or given for the portfolio, including consideration in kind and soultes paid. An asset received by inheritance or gift takes the value used for the droits de mutation à titre gratuit, or failing that its real value when it entered the client's estate. Where the client cannot produce the supporting documents for a price or value of acquisition, those assets are deemed acquired for a nil value.
