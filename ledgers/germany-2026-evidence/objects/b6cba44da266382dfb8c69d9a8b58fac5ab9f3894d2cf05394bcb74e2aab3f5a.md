# DE-R05: ordinary de minimis exception to partnership contamination

**Material correction required.** Current partners Guide LF34 and intake LF221, and trade Guide LF35, treat any own commercial side activity as contaminating the whole partnership. The statute prints no threshold, but that does not settle its judicial interpretation. The actual current2025 BMF handbook expressly applies the BFH de minimis interpretation. This is an ordinary professional partnership classification boundary, not a hypothetical specialist intersection.

Own separate commercial activity must be distinguished from commercial income received through another partnership. For separable own-origin commercial activities, both annual tests are inclusive: commercial net receipts must not exceed3% of all partnership net receipts and must not exceed24,500. Gains versus losses do not remove the threshold. This does not exempt the commercial side activity itself from its proper classification, nor does it make inseparably commercial activity a free profession.
