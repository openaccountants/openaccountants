## 866 at-vat-return

Words: old 6,248 / new 4,751. Severity: **low**

LOST:
1. "Food rate rules (from 1 July 2026)": "A down payment received before 1 July 2026 for food delivered later is in principle taxed at the old rate, though the supplier may already apply the new one." This is the transition rule for the new 4.9% rate, and it matters for grocers' Q3 2026 returns. The new file has no transition rule.
2. "Classification rules > Input VAT": "Down payments are deductible once paid and invoiced. Start-up costs before the first sale are deductible." Neither point is in the new file. Pre-trading input VAT is a common question from new businesses.
3. "UVA: who files and how often": "A new business estimates its first-year turnover and files monthly from the start if it expects more than EUR 100,000." The new file gives no period rule for a start-up year.
4. "Classification rules > Reverse charge": "Reverse-charge invoices ... issued by the 15th of the month after the supply." The new file keeps what the invoice must show (§ 11 Abs 1a) but drops this deadline.

OK-REMOVED: the step-by-step method list, the separate boundary table, worked cases 1 to 7 and the checklist were cut. Their rules now sit in the base and deadline sections and in two new worked examples. The EUR 40 gift threshold moved to "Not yet confirmed". The income-tax GWG note was dropped. Some client questions were cut (Airbnb, UID format, U12 form name). Two rules were corrected: the old "shortfall due within a month" became "no new due date (§ 21 Abs 5)", and the old 30% self-disclosure maximum is replaced by the 5/15/20/30% scale. The bank-pattern table was shortened.

## 868 dk-income-tax

Words: old 7,544 / new 3,817. Severity: **low**

LOST:
1. "Personal allowance and calculation sequence": "If spouses live together and one cannot use all of their personal allowance, SKAT transfers the unused part to the other spouse automatically." The new file covers the spouse transfer of the DKK 55,000 capital-income amount and the PSL § 9 order, but not the transfer of the personfradrag between spouses. This changes a couple's computation.
2. "Middle-bracket tax" row: "The couple's combined positive net capital income is calculated together only if they have been married and living together for the whole year." The joint-calculation condition is gone. The new file keeps only the condition for transferring the unused DKK 55,000 at year end.
3. "1. Classify the taxpayer and activity": the four-way split (employment, fee income/B-income, commercial self-employment, non-commercial activity) "affects whether expenses and losses are deductible". The new file covers business versus employment only. The hobby (non-commercial) loss restriction is not mentioned.
4. "Senior employment allowance": "Because the rule starts in 2026, a person who reaches pension age in 2027 gets it for 2026 only", plus the full-at amounts (DKK 435,715 senior, DKK 440,000 single parent). This is a minor edge case.
5. "Business phone/internet": "enter it in field 462", and "If both phone and internet are taxable, the value is still DKK 3,500 once, not twice." The new file states DKK 3,500 for each, so the once-only cap and the field number are gone.

OK-REMOVED: the 100-row "figures" table, which was mostly worked-example amounts mislabelled as official figures, was cut along with the bank transaction-type table, the step-by-step method and the operational sequence, which duplicated the new computation. Thirteen worked cases were cut; their rules (entertainment 25%, mixed-use DKK 16,900, minor asset DKK 36,000, 25% pool, car rate, late-filing penalty, under-18 AM gate, skatteloft) are all stated in the new text. The completion checklist and the "explicit omissions" paragraph were also cut. The restskat details remain in the new file.

## 873 de-estimated-tax (Einkommensteuer-Vorauszahlungen)

Words: old 7,142, new 2,498. Severity: **medium**

LOST:
1. 5.1 "Always left out": "In the cases of § 31 EStG where child benefit does not fully achieve the required tax relief, the child allowances of § 32(6) EStG and the child benefit to be offset are also left out (§ 37(3) sentence 12)." This base exclusion is missing. It changes the prepayment base for parents.
2. Example 5 / bank classification: "Not a deductible business expense ... (§ 12 number 3 EStG) ... Church tax paid is the exception ... a special expense under § 10(1) number 4 EStG". The deductibility rules for prepayments and the late-payment surcharge (not deductible), and for church tax (deductible as a special expense), are gone. Users commonly ask this.
3. When to refuse or refer: "A client who cannot pay a prepayment. Deferral (Stundung, § 222 AO) is at the tax office's discretion, as a rule granted only on application and against security, and carries its own interest." The new file has no route for a client who cannot pay.
4. 5.1 rental losses: "If the building is bought before the calendar year in which it is completed, completion takes the place of purchase (§ 37(3) sentence 9) ... another let asset within § 21(1) numbers 1 to 3 ... start of use in place of purchase (sentence 11)." The new file keeps only the general rule from sentences 8 to 10. Low impact.
5. 7.3 "Risk of reduction": the § 370 AO warning (tax evasion for incorrect or incomplete facts in a reduction application) and the prohibition on helping a client do that. The new file says only "must state facts truthfully". Low to medium.
6. 7.1: an adjustment is at the tax office's discretion ("kann"), and the current notice stays payable until replaced (§ 124(2) AO). The new file keeps "pay the notice until a new notice arrives" but drops the discretion point and the legal basis. Low.
7. 6.3: deferral interest (§ 234 AO) follows a different rate (§ 238(1), half a percent a month) and must not be mixed with the § 233a rate. Also dropped: the longer 2019 to 2024 interest-free period (Art. 97 § 36 EGAO). Low.

OK-REMOVED: the whole bank-statement payment pattern library (Section 3, working conventions with no official source; noted in "Not yet confirmed"), the quick-reference metadata, the self-checks, the test suite and prohibitions (duplicates of rules that are still stated), the generic disclaimer, and the corporations, partnership and trade-tax refusals (moved to the sibling skills de-corporate-tax, de-partners-and-part-year and de-trade-tax). The founders'-portal advice was moved to "Not yet confirmed".

## 874 fr-cfe

Words: old 7,800, new 3,187. Severity: **medium**

LOST:
1. EC6 Closing business mid-year: "On a cessation during the year, the business does not owe the CFE for the months left to run." The new file names cessation only as a 1447-M-SD trigger and lists "how the cessation relief is claimed" as not confirmed. The substantive rule (no CFE for the remaining months) is not stated. The new file also does not say this relief must not be applied to a change of operator.
2. Step 5: "new businesses are not liable to the CVAE in the year of their creation, except where an activity is transferred". This is missing.
3. EC6: "A classified installation for the environment (ICPE) still owes the CFE during the rehabilitation of the site." Missing. Low.
4. CVAE filing: a small single-establishment business may use the CVAE box of its results return (2033-E, 2035-E, 2059-E or 2072-E) instead of 1330-CVAE, if it meets every condition and is not micro. Missing. Low.
5. 6.5 CET cap: the chamber taxes are kept, but the exclusion of the ZRD tax credit and the cessation relief from the cap computation was dropped; so was the 1.438% cap rate for CET 2028. Low.
6. Step 5 optional exemptions: the 3-year creation or extension exemption "from the year after the creation (or the 2nd year after the extension) ... before 5 May of the year following the extension". The new file keeps the exemption but drops its timing and deadline. Low.
7. Step 7 First Year After Creation: there is no June acompte in the first year a notice is due, because the prior-year CFE was nil. This can be derived from the acompte rule, but it is no longer stated. Low.

OK-REMOVED: the skill metadata and T1/T2/T3 tier definitions, Step 0 onboarding (duplicated "Ask the client first"), the verbatim French quote columns, the edge-case registry and the test suite (Tests 2 to 6 duplicated the body rules; Tests 3 and 7 survive as worked examples), the prohibitions (restatements of rules kept in the body), the notes on "earlier versions removed X", the espace professionnel menu path, the generic disclaimer, and the sibling pointers (fr-income-tax and others), which were partly dropped (fr-cross-border is kept).

## 878 netherlands-30-percent-ruling

Words: old 5,495 / new 3,192. Severity: **medium**

LOST:
1. Figures > Salary norm: "The lower norm applies only while the person is under 30. From the month after the month in which they turn 30 the general norm applies" (plus master's must be academic: NVAO accreditation or Nuffic equivalence). New file gives the under-30 norm but not when it switches off or what degree qualifies. Changes the norm test for anyone aged 29-30.
2. Boundaries + Method step 6/10: relocation costs and an employee familiarisation (orientation) visit to the business can be paid tax-free ON TOP of the ruling. New says the only add-on is school fees ("except school fees"), which is narrower and would make users tax qualifying relocation costs.
3. Method step 9 / Boundaries: "It cannot be used during a period in which the employee is suspended or released from work on full pay, except in the first pay period after..." Absent in new. Matters for garden leave / termination.
4. Method step 9: ruling applies only to wage enjoyed for tax purposes on or before the decision end date; "Do not substitute the period to which a late payment relates for its fiscal enjoyment date." Absent. Matters for bonuses paid after the end date.
5. Method step 7: base uses current-employment wage; "do not automatically include payments classified as wage from former employment." Absent.
6. Figures > WNT cap: "If the employee changes employer during the year, the norm is split between employers by the number of pay periods at each." Absent; new only pro-rates for part years.
7. Boundaries: transfer of undertaking (art. 7:662 BW) - employee moves by law, no new decision needed. Absent.
8. Boundaries (PhD row): the two extra PhD situations (Dutch employment alongside PhD research; contract before obtaining doctorate) where the ruling starts only when the doctorate is obtained. New keeps only the basic 1-year PhD exception.
9. Worked cases: "The employer may spread the annual maximum evenly over the pay periods, or optimise it with a year-end calculation (KG:041:2024:22)." Absent.
10. Worked cases 6-9 and 13 (2025-start ruling at 27% in 2027 failing the announced 2027 norm; Dec-2023 ruling keeping 30% in 2027; late application 1 Feb / 15 Jun -> start 1 Jul with actual-costs-only gap; annual-test failure at EUR 47,500; wage exactly at norm). The rules survive in prose, but the scenarios are gone; low individually.

OK-REMOVED: CTA block, completion checklist (duplicates method), repeated Handboek citations, 2027 norms (51,899 / 39,450) moved to "Not yet confirmed", 2027 cap-at-27% inference dropped as unconfirmed, refer list condensed into one paragraph, scarcity-test footballer example, actual-cost reimbursement from 2026 (cost of living / calls home) kept in shorter form, 2025-return section kept under Cross-border points. New adds statutory citations, penalties, belastingrente, AWR deadlines.
