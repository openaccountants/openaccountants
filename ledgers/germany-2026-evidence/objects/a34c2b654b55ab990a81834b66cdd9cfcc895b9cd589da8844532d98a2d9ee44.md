## DE-R32: income-producing private assets

Current LF30/49/95 states the one-year rule for all other private assets. Own §23(1) no.2 sentence4 extends the period to ten years where an asset was used as an income source in at least one calendar year. BMF paragraph63 separately excludes Currency/Payment Tokens from that extension. It does not exclude every other private asset.

Source: [EStG §23](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/estg/__23.html), DEB004 native LF10; BMF 6 March2025, DEB176 LF785–788. Both full selected quotations were read.

- Option A, recommended: qualify the table/body with the statutory ten-year branch and preserve the specific token exception. Ask about income-producing use and asset classification.
- Option B: limit the positive one-year method to non-income-producing assets and the separately supported tokens; route other income-producing assets to the ten-year check.

Control: an independently established non-business other asset used as an income source and sold after two years cannot be declared exempt solely because one year elapsed. No tax amount is invented without its actual base.
