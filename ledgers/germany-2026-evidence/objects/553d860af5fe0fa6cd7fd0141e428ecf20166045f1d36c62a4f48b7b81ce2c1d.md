# DE-B01: property-fund quota preservation

The pre-refresh Guide expressly supplied both >50% quota tests at LF109–110. Current LF77 keeps the60%/80% exemptions but omits those tests. No contradictory source was found.

Option A (recommended), append to current LF77:

A property fund must, under its investment terms, continuously invest more than 50% of its assets in property and property companies; for a foreign property fund, more than 50% must be in foreign property and foreign property companies. Apply the classification, exclusions and asset-value rules in [§ 2(9)–(9b) InvStG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/invstg_2018/__2.html).

Option B, append to current LF77:

For the 60% and 80% exemptions, obtain the fund’s classification under [§ 2(9)–(9b) InvStG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/invstg_2018/__2.html): property funds require a continuous investment-terms quota of more than 50% in qualifying property/property companies, and foreign property funds more than 50% in the foreign equivalents. Exactly 50% does not meet either quota.

Official source: https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/invstg_2018/__2.html, §2(9)–(9b), actually read29October2026; exact quote and raw/reader hashes in two-options-and-evidence.json. No Guide edit made by this writer.
