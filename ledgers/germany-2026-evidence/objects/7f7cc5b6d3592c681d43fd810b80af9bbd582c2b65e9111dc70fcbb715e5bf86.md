## Headline figures (all read 2026-10-24)
- Allowance EUR 24,500 (individuals, partnerships), EUR 5,000 (§ 2(3) bodies); none for companies: § 11(1) GewStG
- Base rate 3.5%: § 11(2) GewStG; home workers 56% of it: § 11(3)
- Multiplier floor 200% for 2026; 280% from 2027: BGBl. 2026 I Nr. 197 Art. 8 (changes "200" to "280") + § 36(5b) GewStG; BMF "Steuern von A bis Z" 2025
- Examples: Munich 490% (stadt.muenchen.de), national average 2025 410% (Destatis PD26_369)
- § 8 no. 1: one quarter of the financing items above EUR 200,000
- § 9 no. 1: the Grundsteuer booked as an expense. This replaced the 1.2% of Einheitswert from 2025 (§ 36(4b)). The brief's "1.2% of property value" is no longer the law, so the skill explains the change.
- § 10a: EUR 1m in full, then 60%. BVerfG 23 Jul 2025 upheld it (statute footnote)
- § 35 EStG: 4.0x the base amount, capped by the trade tax actually payable and by the Ermäßigungshöchstbetrag
- Prepayments due 15 Feb/May/Aug/Nov, minimum EUR 50: § 19 GewStG
- Return deadline: 2 Aug 2027 without an adviser, 29 Feb 2028 with one (§ 149 AO, § 108(3) AO)
- Late filing EUR 25 per started month, at most EUR 25,000 (§ 152(6), (10) AO); late payment 1% per month (§ 240); interest 0.15% per month (§ 233a, § 238(1a))
