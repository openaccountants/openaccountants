# DE-R14 and DE-R15: two retained-source scope conditions

## DE-R14: §7g own-use and letting branches

The freelancer Guide's frozen LF219 says `§7g needs the asset used in a domestic establishment (§7g(4))`. This erases the expressly separate letting alternative, despite the correct letting-or-own-use explanation in its own LF159. Own actual B quote FR.IAB, source DEB033, readerSHA `859ac461ec65fe96438195a0605e0981a06e5b3ef2849a872236ab00f7c5f841`, literalLF7–22, includes §7g(1)/(4)/(6): `vermietet oder in einer inländischen Betriebsstätte des Betriebes ausschließlich oder fast ausschließlich betrieblich genutzt`. The actual2026 EÜR line89 also repeats the alternatives (DEB183 literalLF1566–1585, SHA `c0af7243fc4b4a3d52b51838076f3283a209d8c023999148c5bd739e9f06d5e3`). Both full selected quotations have been independently actually read.

Official sources: https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/estg/__7g.html and the Guide's already cited2026 EÜR instructions.

**Option A (recommended):** align only the cross-border summary with the source and main method: own-use branch requires qualifying use in a domestic establishment; letting is a separate statutory alternative for the required period. Preserve all other profit/timing/use/reporting gates. This does not declare an untested particular foreign leasing arrangement eligible.

**Option B:** remove the universal domestic-use sentence and refer to the two qualified branches already supplied in the main method. No new source search is needed.

Separating condition: a stipulated qualifying letting case is tested under its letting branch, rather than rejected solely for failing the own-use-location predicate. No new cross-border exemption or tax amount is inferred.

## DE-R15: filing threshold for the Guide's expressly covered other bodies

Trade Guide frozen LF125's list omits the EUR5,000 filing branch for other private-law legal persons/associations, public-law-person businesses and the listed partly exempt businesses. Its scope table expressly includes the first population; its allowance table already supplies5000. This is not a request to add a new charity domain.

Own actual B quote GT.filing, source DEB148, literalLF7–21, SHA `dbb29495b02df477985d87eead52d0bfc21a0a4f4bfa44f9ece1af6c58d94829`, https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/gewstdv_1955/__25.html, has full §25(1)3–5. The decisive wording for other private-law bodies is: `deren Gewerbeertrag im Erhebungszeitraum den Betrag von 5 000 Euro überstiegen hat`. The whole selected provision has been independently actually read.

**Option A (recommended):** add the exact5000 branch and its actual entity/partly-exempt-activity gates to the filing population, preserving the24500 ordinary sole-trader branch, all-amount capital-company/cooperative branch and loss/request overrides. Keep the original two examples unchanged.

**Option B:** narrow the Guide's express scope so those bodies are referred for their filing method, rather than giving them the24500-only list. A is preferable because the exact current source is already retained.

Separating fictional control: an otherwise in-scope other private-law body with taxable commercial-operation trade incomeEUR6,000 is above5000 and must be tested for filing although below24500. Exactly5000 does not satisfy that strict branch, without prejudice to loss/request overrides. No new income-tax exemption is inferred.
