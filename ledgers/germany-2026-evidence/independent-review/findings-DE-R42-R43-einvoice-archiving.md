**DE-R42: outgoing-copy waiver lacks its conditions (Guide435).** Same-content reconstruction alone does not supply all of GoBD76. Official annex native254–265 says the actually processed format must have the highest machine evaluability and full content; otherwise both formats must be kept. It then says, among other conditions, master data and terms must be historised, documentation must identify terms used, original layout saved and historised with original format ascertainable, and invoicing data retained machine-evaluable and immutable. Native260 matches the current2025 waiver.

- Option A (recommended): preserve the waiver but add these actual conditions, including 'among other requirements' and the full-content/highest-evaluability prerequisite. If they are not met, do not claim reconstruction alone permits discarding the image copy. Keep the separate incomingXML rule intact.
- Option B: keep image copies as the default and describe this same conditional waiver in a bounded exception. Do not turn a failure of waiver conditions into an invented fine.

Separators: software that reproduces the same current-looking invoice but overwrites historical customer/terms/layout data does not establish the waiver; a system meeting the full statutory-administrative record conditions can use it. Neither test requires running a particular vendor system in this review.

**DE-R43: available main-letter conditions resolve two expressly unread ordinary archiving instructions (Guide438/441 and corresponding scope limits).** Official GoBD135/native492–499 gives the dual-version/index/converted-marker default, but permits retaining the converted version alone if there is no image/content change, no loss of other retention-required information, documented proper lossless conversion, and no restriction of machine evaluation or fiscal data access. This is more precise than the legacy 'original must also be kept'. Preserve the independently applicable e-invoice structured-original/noXML-loss requirements:135 must not be presented as permission to delete the XML prohibited by GoBD125 and the VAT original-structured-part rule.

GoBD150–155/native545–557 expressly requires intelligible process documentation sufficient for a knowledgeable third party, with scope proportionate to system/business complexity, processes from creation to storage/retrieval/evaluation/security/reproduction, actual programme-version correspondence and traceable version history. Paragraph155 says missing/inadequate documentation is not a substantive formal defect warranting rejecting books where it does not impair traceability/verifiability. Do not imply an automatic penalty or universal certification duty.

- Option A (recommended): replace the 'not read/carried over' procedural/conversion sentences with these narrow actual methods and conditions. Narrow only those corresponding Sources/referral reservations now supplied; archive-system choice and actual implementation evidence remain claimant facts.
- Option B: state the safe dual-retention conversion default plus the expressly conditioned135exception and the scoped documentation duty in a short paired paragraph, preservingXML requirements and the155qualification.
