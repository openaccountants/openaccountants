**Depreciation (§ 26 and § 27).** Tangible assets go into one of seven groups. Straight-line annual depreciation (§ 27) is entry price divided by the period. In the first year only the months from the month the asset is put into use count. Assets in groups 2 and 3 may instead be depreciated by the accelerated method (§ 28); the method is chosen per new asset and cannot be changed (§ 26(3)). sk-corporate-income-tax main unit18 LF[85, 87]
**Interest: two separate limits.**
- **§ 21a (thin capitalisation).** Interest and related costs on loans from a related person (závislá osoba) are not deductible to the extent they exceed 25% of EBITDA. EBITDA here is accounting profit before tax plus the depreciation and interest expense in it. Banks, insurers and leasing companies are out. § 21a does not apply where the company has already added back interest under § 17k.
- **§ 17k (ATAD rule).** If net interest expense is above EUR 3,000,000, the excess over 30% of the tax base (increased by net interest and tax depreciation) is added back. Most owner-managed companies never reach the EUR 3,000,000 floor.

sk-payroll-social main unit9 LF[29, 33]
**Self-employed (SZČO).** From 1 January 2026 a person aged 18 or over is an SZČO for social insurance if they hold a licence to do business or, by an honest declaration, carry on self-employed work that needs no licence (for example an artist or author). Income no longer decides this status (Sociálna poisťovňa, changes from 1 January 2026, item 22).
- **New entrants** (no licence in the last 60 months) become compulsorily insured from the first day of the sixth calendar month after the month the licence starts.
- **Returning entrants** (a licence ended less than 60 months ago) are insured from the day the licence starts.
- Insurance now lasts until the licence ends. The old income test at 1 July no longer ends it.
- **Transition:** to 30 June 2026 an SZČO who held a licence before 1 January 2026 stays under the old rules. Under those rules insurance started on 1 July 2025 (1 October 2025 with an extended tax deadline) if 2024 income was above EUR 8,580 (Sociálna poisťovňa, 2026 tables, note 3).
